# Engineering context by affected behavior

For executable design, implementation, review, or diagnosis, identify affected owners and consumers and read only the matching contracts below. Reuse current loaded context. These routes apply to new files and cross-layer callers; they do not authorize implementation or unrelated qualification. Questions load only contracts needed to answer them. Prose-only mentions do not trigger executable workflows.

Each row is conditional; a matching row does not require every other row.

- Action behavior design, implementation or review, including new commands/families, equipment capabilities/compatibility, prerequisites, availability, execution, offered timing/cost/range facts or menu/NPC/plan admission: read [Action availability and temporary execution](../../docs/action-capabilities.md#action-availability-and-temporary-execution) before planning, implementing or reviewing those paths. A prose-only mention of an action does not trigger this read.
- Changes to selected world rules, installation/defaults or their executable wording/projections in any layer: read [Selected-world consumers](../../docs/engine-and-world-boundaries.md#trace-selected-world-behavior-through-its-consumers) before planning, implementation or review, even when the engine contract is unchanged. This includes inventory/entity/equipped choices, world-authored icons, activity labels, help/parser/schema examples and AI instructions; prose-only mentions do not trigger it.
- Physical/spatial behavior: before designing, implementing or reviewing placement, attachment, support, reach, body bounds/clearance, coverage, candidate selection, collision, movement, pathfinding or navigation preparation in any layer, including new mechanics outside the spatial package, read [Spatial behavior](../../docs/spatial-world.md), its [physical-interaction design checks](../../docs/spatial-world.md#physical-interaction-design-checks), [body anchors and conservative candidates](../../docs/spatial-world.md#what-a-location-means) for whole-interval bounds/clearance, and applicable [movement](../../docs/spatial-world.md#movement) sections. Query/preparation changes also use Performance. Presentation-only camera work follows UI/PlayCanvas; prose-only mentions do not trigger these reads.
- TypeScript/tooling design, planning, implementation or review, including runtime record/definition representation changes in TypeScript owners: read [TypeScript](../../.agents/rules/typescript.md) before choosing the representation or changing code. Prose-only mentions do not trigger this read.
- Identity allocation, construction or resolution changes, including authored/application IDs coexisting with native sequence IDs; composite identities, multi-field index/cache keys, record joins or persisted references: read the [identity contract](../../docs/identity-and-references.md#contract) before designing, implementing or reviewing these paths and consumers in any layer, including new files. Display-only names, exact lookup by an unchanged single ID and prose-only mentions do not trigger this read.
- State/lifecycle changes, including timed work, cancellation, effects continuing after participant loss, equipment/body replacement, durable-operation/receipt recovery and player presence, pause/resume or reconnect: read [Continuation and identity](../../docs/save-and-load.md#continuation-and-identity) and the affected save contract before planning or changing those paths in any layer, even without a schema or storage change. Receipt-recovery callers also read the affected durable-operation contract even when the stored schema is unchanged. Presentation-only or prose mentions do not trigger this read.
- Recording, retaining, correcting/forgetting or presenting character knowledge/experience, including native discovery, container/item inspection disclosure and remembered-information UI: read applicable [Memory architecture](../../docs/memory-architecture.md) sections even without AI. Action-attempt records, produced-output bindings, learned-method representation or sequence continuation also require the relevant [Action records and learned activities](../../docs/action-experience.md) sections. Introduction/story-selection changes also read [Replaceable story selection](../../docs/narration-and-conversations.md#replaceable-story-selection). Prose-only mentions do not trigger these reads.
- Event/history tracking, action outcomes, awareness, memories, recall, summaries or personal wording: read [Precise history and general causes](../../docs/engine-and-world-boundaries.md#preserve-precise-history-and-solve-the-general-cause) and the affected event/memory contract before changing producers or consumers. Private evidence/history inspection, including native progress UI without AI, reads [private retrieval fencing](../../docs/memory-architecture.md#private-retrieval-fencing).
- Permission/grant selection, protected resource transfers, exact approval dependencies, operator configuration affecting approved behavior/spending or authorization during multi-phase work: read [Complete-operation authorization](../../docs/engine-and-world-boundaries.md#complete-operation-authorization) before planning, implementing or reviewing those paths in any layer. Prose-only mentions do not trigger this read.
- New/changed persisted records, definitions, schemas, restoration or current-format validators: read [Current-format validation](../../docs/save-and-load.md#current-format-validation) and applicable save/load sections before planning, implementing or reviewing them. A prose-only mention of saving does not trigger this read.
- Character creation/editing, backstories, personality/disposition, character behavior or changes to their cognition/context: read [Character authorship and changing personality](../../docs/projects/compelling-characters-feature-spec.md#character-authorship-and-changing-personality); for implementation or architecture work also read its [technical design](../../docs/projects/compelling-characters-tech-design.md). Apply the shared evidence/perspective boundaries to new producers and consumers.
- Character decisions, Jev/TypeSafe, LLMs, prompts, cognition context, embeddings, provider behavior or model-evaluation corpora, grading and comparisons: [AI](../../.agents/skills/openlegend-ai/SKILL.md)
- Local development or verification that needs Macrofold: follow [local server reuse, startup and cleanup](../../docs/live-ai-setup.md#local-macrofold-server-lifecycle) before connecting or starting services.
- Scene asset authoring, import/export, inspection or render measurement (including offline 3D/Blender tools and new files), PlayCanvas, camera, picking or render lifecycle: [PlayCanvas](../../.agents/skills/openlegend-playcanvas/SKILL.md). Prose-only mentions do not trigger it.

### Authored-reality design principles

Before planning, implementing or reviewing fictional laws, authored content or producers/consumers of installed world definitions in any layer, including new files, read the [boundary decision procedure](../../docs/engine-and-world-boundaries.md#5-a-repeatable-boundary-decision) and [boundary principles](../../docs/engine-and-world-boundaries.md#design-principles-for-every-feature). This includes routine fixes under an unchanged contract; prose-only mentions do not trigger these reads or the full design workflow.

For architecture and implementation, diagnose new specific cases against the overall design before adding exceptions or duplicate representations; follow [precise history and general causes](../../docs/engine-and-world-boundaries.md#preserve-precise-history-and-solve-the-general-cause). Localize justified v1 specificity with its owner, limitation, seam and expansion trigger. Preserve the external-world-package seam without an unused loader.

Before planning, implementing or reviewing saved state and its references, ownership of saved active work, serialization, restoration or current-format validation, read the applicable [save/load contract](../../docs/save-and-load.md), including [Continuation and identity](../../docs/save-and-load.md#continuation-and-identity) for relationships. This includes new files and validator changes that leave the schema unchanged. The [root development save policy](../../AGENTS.md#development-save-policy) remains controlling; prose-only mentions do not trigger the read.
