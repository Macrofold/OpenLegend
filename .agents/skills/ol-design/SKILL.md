# /ol-design

Treat the text after `/ol-design` (or `$ol-design`) and the conversation as the art request, then follow the [art workflow skill](../openlegend-art/SKILL.md) exactly. It owns settings, defaults, questions, tools, review, spending and preservation.

The result is actual artwork or design files: prompts, plans or a record alone do not fulfil the request. An exploration ends with reviewable artwork and a saved study; a direct artwork request ends with the requested files; adopting a direction or putting art in the game needs explicit authorization.
