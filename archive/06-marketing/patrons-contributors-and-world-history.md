# Patrons, contributors, and world history

Recorded September 19, 2026. **Accepted direction:** recognize financial supporters and contributors, offer meaningful world dedications, give both groups a voice, and explore a creator fund. The following mechanics are proposals; no membership, entitlement, grant, or permanent-service promise has been sold.

## Patronage with concrete benefits

Candidate message: **Help bring this world into existence, and become part of its history.**

| Offering | Example | Duration / proposed treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Recurring patron tier | Gold recognition, previews, community participation | Active benefits depend on current subscription; exact tiers/prices open |
| Founding recognition | Display name in the official founding chronicle or an engraving | One-time recognition retained in the historical record under stated service terms |
| Special dedication | Name a dragon, constellation, or monument | A naming entitlement redeemed once, with names reviewed before publication |
| Contributor recognition | Badge or attribution for useful packs, code, art, or community work | Earned personal history, distinct from purchases |

Budget recurring hosting/AI benefits explicitly. A one-time purchase must not accidentally promise unlimited lifetime compute. Recognition does not confer god powers in another person's world or override ordinary player protections.

## Engravings, dragons, and permanence

Separate the historical dedication from its current physical representation. A monument can crumble and a named dragon can die while the chronicle retains who sponsored the dedication and what happened. This supports a living world without rewriting its origin story.

“Cannot be unengraved” should become a precise product commitment about the official history, persistence, and recovery policy. Do not promise perpetual server operation, mandatory display in every fork, or literal unchangeability merely because an NFT exists. Define service retirement/export, moderation, corrections, privacy requests, and consent to public display names before selling recognition.

Proposed records include a dedication ID, world/branch ID, original supporter or contributor, approved display name, created/redeemed time, target identity, benefit terms/version, and any current collectible owner. An append-only event history with recorded corrections is a design option, not yet an implemented guarantee. Entitlement redemption must survive retries and world rollback without granting a second naming claim.

## Ownership transfers do not rewrite attribution

If Alice sponsors a dragon's naming and later transfers an associated collectible to Bob, the historical record still credits Alice. Bob receives only the collectible and any explicitly transferable unconsumed benefits. A used naming claim cannot be redeemed again; Bob does not become the original sponsor. Earned contributor credentials should remain attached to the contributor.

Transfers can be gifts or sales if the product permits them. Recurring membership validity and permanent possession of a collectible are different records. Ordinary account benefits are sufficient for a first implementation; [NFTs and portable credentials](tokens-and-community-funding.md) remain optional.

## A voice through contribution and financial support

Both useful work and funding can earn participation. A proposed model gives patrons and contributors defined portions of influence over a community budget. Exact weights, thresholds, terms, elections, identity checks, and overlap between groups remain open.

Start with choosing among feasible creator-fund proposals or advising on community priorities. Publish what a vote can decide, what is advisory, and how final decisions are made. Larger purchases should not silently become unlimited votes. Recognize useful contributions rather than raw commit counts, AI output volume, or manufactured engagement. Address duplicate identities and require disclosure/recusal when a voter is seeking a grant.

Community participation is not company equity, entitlement to profits, or control over independently owned worlds. Engine contributor licensing is a separate topic in [LICENSING.md](../../LICENSING.md).

## Creator fund: the platform pays creators

This is an expense/grant program, not another product creators must buy to participate in. Fund it from an approved platform budget and any disclosed allocation of patron purchases or dedicated sponsorship. Determine commitments after accounting for operating costs and delivery obligations; no percentage is selected.

Creators propose useful worlds, systems, accessible interfaces, tools, or content. A proposed grant workflow uses a reviewable scope, agreed milestones, payment terms, license/access terms, and evidence of delivery. Publishing grant results can show patrons what their support enabled. Membership should not be required merely to apply unless a later policy explicitly justifies that restriction.

Distinguish three flows: pack buyers pay for existing products; subscriber allocations reward qualifying world participation; the creator fund finances new work. A creator may receive more than one under transparent rules. Patron voting does not give patrons a financial return from grantees.

## Why history matters

A developed world accumulates relationships, institutions, inventions, and shared experiences. Its provenance and community may give it value beyond a snapshot that someone copies. This is a product hypothesis, not cryptocurrency proof of work, a financial valuation, or a guarantee that more simulation time creates more demand.

Record branches, parent checkpoints, approved mechanics, and significant public events where useful. Preserve private memories and conversations separately. A signature or blockchain commitment can attest to a recorded version; it does not independently prove that every narrated event occurred or require other communities to recognize a world as canonical.
