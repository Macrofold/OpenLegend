# Marketing workspace

## Player experience and commercial purpose

**Create a world by playing it. Share what you discover.**

Lead with a compelling browser survival-adventure: build a home from an empty-handed start, form chosen relationships, invent useful alternatives and encounter independent danger and consequence. The [world specification](../../docs/worlds/base/playable-openings.md#first-playable-game-target) owns the complete target; [gameplay priorities](../../docs/repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md) governs selection. Show what the actual build supports and label planned content honestly.

Player enjoyment, authoring interest, developer contribution, patron support and willingness to buy managed worlds are distinct questions. Test game, audience and business in parallel. Compare the managed-world candidate with a credible browser alternative rather than assume the offer is proven. Actual prices, allocations and operating commitments belong in the commercial decision process.

AGPL-3.0-only is selected for the repository; [LICENSING.md](../../LICENSING.md) owns the terms. Accepted creator-world, subscription and premium-entry directions do not make a marketplace, patron offer or separately adopted creator fund prerequisites for the first game.

## Working principles

Demonstrate a worthwhile activity and a real instance of the premise before promising a universe of possibilities. Separate ordinary player abilities from creator authority. Show representative gameplay, disclose important waits/limits and label concepts. Technical explanations serve developer audiences rather than substitute for the player experience.

Make substantial reveals, demos and releases deliberate while keeping routine work sustainable. Measure usability, voluntary enjoyment, broader demand and actual payment separately. Cost coverage, founder income and studio growth are different objectives. Repeated qualified response should justify deliberate expansion, not permanent small-test mode.

Keep the open platform useful independently and identify separately licensed content/services. Preserve supported creator examples, including refinement of the magic-tree spell's use restrictions, without implying that arbitrary requests instantly become working mechanics.
