# Tokens, collectibles, and community funding

Recorded September 19, 2026. **Exploration only.** No blockchain, ICO, token, NFT collection, investment offering, redeemable in-game currency, or payment provider is selected. The accepted near-term direction is concrete patron benefits and community recognition; tokenization is optional. This document preserves the discussion, not legal clearance or an offering announcement.

## Distinct purposes

| Approach | What supporters receive | Design implication |
|---|---|---|
| Membership / founder package | Specified access, credits, experiences, and recognition | Can be an ordinary account entitlement; bound delivery and compute obligations |
| Collectible NFT | A unique record with explicitly stated associated rights | Ownership of a token does not automatically confer company equity, copyright, or perpetual service |
| Community investment | Actual defined financial rights | Explore a properly structured offering if the goal is a financial stake; tokenization is optional |
| Native tradable token | Whatever utility, governance, or financial rights are actually specified | Game success does not automatically create holder value; no issuance decision |
| Crypto payments | An alternative settlement method | Distinct from creating our own speculative asset |

Tokens are sometimes used for financing, service access, governance, contribution incentives, or speculative community identity. Choose an actual user benefit before choosing a blockchain. Buybacks spend operating cash; newly issued staking rewards do not themselves create business income; token market capitalization is not available treasury cash.

## Portability, transfers, and issuance

Portability means that someone can prove ownership to an independent service that chooses to recognize the credential. It does not automatically grant benefits in every world. User-controlled transfer means a holder can gift or sell a transferable token under its rules without a manual ownership change by Open Legend. Game benefits still require an entitlement system and service cooperation.

There is no technical requirement for a single ICO. Contracts can support ongoing membership issuance, seasonal collections, or fixed editions. Supply limits, future editions, redemption, transfer restrictions, subscription expiry, and any administrative controls must be disclosed accurately. A collectible can remain owned after a subscription expires. Historical attribution and consumed naming rights do not transfer automatically; see the [dedication proposal](patrons-contributors-and-world-history.md).

NFT ownership does not guarantee immutable metadata, continued hosting, an indestructible in-world object, or recognition by forks. Blockchain records and optional public commitments are separate from the private simulation database. No minting or wallet integration is implemented. [Ethereum NFT overview](https://ethereum.org/nft/).

## Financial and in-game value

The preferred product hypothesis is a real creator economy around memberships, packs, hosting, and grants. A market for speculative tokens is not evidence of demand for the game. A collectible should ideally be worth owning to its buyer even if resale is unavailable.

If redeemable cryptocurrency is considered later, real balances must be isolated from editable world rules. God powers, conjured gold, accelerated NPC labor, snapshots, and forks cannot duplicate an external financial balance. Rewards would need an actual funded budget and authorized settlement. That is a separate financial product design, not an ordinary survival-economy feature.

## U.S. offering distinction, checked September 19, 2026

The SEC's March 2026 interpretation distinguishes certain digital collectibles, including meme coins, from investment contracts. A collectible may be purchased in hopes of resale appreciation without itself being a security. A sale can still involve an investment contract when the issuer's representations about essential managerial work create a reasonable expectation of profits and the other elements are met. Funding development with merchandise revenue alone is not conclusive; the offering's full economic circumstances matter. An NFT label or a “no guaranteed returns” disclaimer is not clearance. [SEC interpretation](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/interp/2026/33-11412.pdf), [SEC explanation](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/capital-raising-building-blocks/transactions-involving-crypto-assets).

For actual community investment, U.S. Regulation Crowdfunding is one avenue for eligible issuers through registered intermediaries, with disclosures and investment/resale restrictions. It is not selected here. The SEC's August 2026 Regulation Crypto Assets release is a **proposal**, not an adopted exemption to rely on. Recheck current rules and obtain offering-specific advice before any public sale intended as investment financing. Non-security status also does not remove other applicable fraud, consumer, tax, or financial-service obligations. [Regulation Crowdfunding](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/exempt-offerings/regulation-crowdfunding), [proposed crypto rules](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2026/08/s7-2026-27), [SEC discussion of other laws](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/staff-statement-meme-coins).

There is no evidence supporting “99% of crypto is speculation.” In the Federal Reserve's 2025 U.S. household survey, 9% of adults bought/held crypto as investment and 2% used it for financial transactions; categories overlap, and investment holding is not synonymous with short-term speculation. These figures do not forecast Open Legend sales. [Federal Reserve survey](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2026-economic-well-being-of-us-households-in-2025-banking.htm).

## Open questions before a product decision

Is wallet-held ownership or transfer useful enough to justify the additional account/recovery experience? Which benefits survive transfers or cancellation? What must remain private? How are editions and redeemed benefits tracked? Does community governance need independent operators or an ordinary account-based vote? What is the actual funding need, delivery budget, and distribution channel? Do the proposed rights require an investment-offering structure?

The current recommendation is patron tiers and creator grants first, with collectibles or genuine community investment evaluated on their own merits. This preserves room for future choices without making speculative token demand part of the game's operating plan.
