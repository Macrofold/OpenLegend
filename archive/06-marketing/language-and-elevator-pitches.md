## Purpose and scope

This is the canonical reference for the language Mike selected in the October 17, 2026 positioning conversation. The quoted copy below preserves those selections. Audience labels, suggested ordering and explanations are editorial guidance; no exclusive tagline winner has been chosen.

The terminology reference also retains considered wording and the reasoning behind it. A term’s inclusion in the glossary does not make it another selected description or a new product requirement.

Use this language to communicate what Open Legend is being built to become. For a project introduction, establish that context with “We’re building…” before using a selected description. Public descriptions of an available build must match [current architecture](../../docs/architecture.md) and [verification evidence](../../docs/verification.md). Selecting a pitch does not establish completed features, measured MMO scale, a release date or tested audience response.

The game and the wider engine have related propositions. The game offers survival-adventure, autonomous inhabitants, invention and lasting consequences. The engine supports authored realities with their own rules and premises. Ordinary player invention and authorized creator control remain distinct.

[Positioning and copy](positioning-and-copy.md) owns the wider positioning hypothesis, audience experiments and demonstration concepts. [Gameplay priorities](../../docs/repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md) and the [product baseline](../01-requirements/product-baseline.md) own the intended experience and requirements.

## Selected game descriptions

### Primary game description

> A persistent, AI-native survival sandbox MMORPG with autonomous inhabitants, emergent stories, and a world that evolves through invention.

Suggested general-purpose opening. It combines recognizable genre language with character autonomy, emergence and invention.

### Living-world description for gamers

> A living-world survival MMO where the inhabitants remember what happens, pursue lives of their own, and respond to the world you help shape.

Use when personal continuity and independent lives are the most useful way to introduce the game. Follow with something the player can do and a consequence they can encounter later.

### Concise professional description

> An AI-native, systemic sandbox MMORPG combining autonomous character simulation, generative mechanics, and persistent world consequences.

Use with an audience comfortable with design terminology. Be ready to explain [systemic](#what-systemic-means), autonomous character simulation and generative mechanics in concrete terms.

## Selected elevator pitches

### Professional elevator pitch

> Open Legend is an AI-native, persistent-world survival sandbox MMORPG. It combines autonomous characters with memory and independent goals, systemic gameplay, and player invention that can become reusable additions to the world—allowing new stories, relationships, and eventually societies to emerge through play.

Use for a short professional introduction, project overview or discussion with a potential collaborator. The final clause expresses the longer arc from individual encounters toward societies.

### Gamer elevator pitch

> Open Legend is a living-world survival MMO where the people have lives of their own. Explore, fight, build, form relationships, and invent useful things that become a lasting part of the world and change what happens next.

Use for a conversational introduction to the intended player experience. It gives recognizable activities and a reason that creations and relationships matter.

## Selected ambition descriptions

### Long-term ambition

> A persistent survival-adventure MMO where players and autonomous AI inhabitants develop societies, create history, and expand the world’s possibilities through discovery and invention.

Use when discussing the larger destination. Society development and shared history extend the smaller survival-adventure experience; this wording does not make every larger system a prerequisite for enjoyable initial play.

### Expanded genre and ambition

> An AI-native, persistent open-world survival and life-simulation MMORPG with autonomous inhabitants, generative mechanics, emergent societies, and player-driven world evolution.

Use when a deliberate genre blend is useful, such as a vision slide or an industry conversation with room to unpack each term.

### Dense genre descriptor

> Persistent open-world AI-native survival sandbox MMORPG with autonomous inhabitants, generative mechanics, emergent societies, and player-driven invention.

Use when a compact list of genre and capability terms is specifically wanted. In ordinary conversation, one of the elevator pitches is easier to follow.

## Engine and creator descriptions

### Engine description

> An open engine for authored realities, supporting persistent worlds, autonomous inhabitants, and AI-assisted creation.

Use for the open engine and its broader technical proposition. “Authored realities” means worlds with deliberately chosen laws and premises; survival rules need not apply to every world.

### Creator-platform description

> A platform for creating living multiplayer worlds with their own rules, inhabitants, and histories.

Use for a world creator or game master audience. Explain the chosen world’s creation capabilities and the experience available to its players.

### Connecting the game and engine

> A survival sandbox MMORPG built on an open engine for living worlds, autonomous characters, and gameplay that can evolve through invention

Use when introducing both the playable game and the foundation that supports it. The game gives people an experience to care about; the engine enables different authored worlds.

## Selected taglines

### Imagination and consequence

> Your imagination, your consequences.

Emphasizes creative agency and consequential action. Consequences include useful discoveries, relationships, beauty and achievement as well as danger or loss.

### Memory and agency

> A world that remembers. A future you shape.

Connects continuity with the player’s ability to influence what happens next. “Remembers” refers to meaningful retained experience and world consequences.

### Personal life and wider influence

> Build a life. Shape a world.

Connects inhabiting the world personally with contributing to its wider development.

The existing endorsed line, **“Create a world by playing it. Share what you discover.”**, also remains available in [Positioning and copy](positioning-and-copy.md#positioning-hypothesis). The three selections above add options; this conversation did not choose a replacement for every use.

For reuse, retain Mike’s selected wording: “your consequences” and “you shape.” Earlier brainstorming used different variants, which are not interchangeable with these selections.

## Explain what makes the combination distinctive

The positioning rests on how **autonomous lives, useful invention and persistent consequences** reinforce one another. Describe that combination and its intended experience; claiming that every ingredient is unprecedented would require separate evidence.

1. **Autonomous lives:** inhabitants have needs, personality, motives, memories and relationships that can shape what they pursue. What they perceive and know matters to their choices. Their independence becomes meaningful through what they actually do, including initiating, refusing and reconsidering.
2. **Useful invention:** a discovery can become a working addition that people can use again. Invention can expand the available possibilities: a tool, technique or other supported capability can change how a later problem can be approached.
3. **Persistent consequences:** creations and meaningful outcomes carry forward. An encounter can change a relationship; an invention can create an opportunity; repeated choices can affect a community; their accumulated consequences can become history.

Together, these support the explanatory idea **“a world whose possibilities expand through play.”** This is editorial framing for the selected descriptions. The world can evolve both in what happens and in what its inhabitants are able to do. Communities and history describe possible larger consequences, without prescribing a compulsory path from survival to civilization. The [gameplay priorities](../../docs/repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md) keep enjoyable initial play central.

## What “systemic” means

**Systemic gameplay uses consistent, interacting rules. Those interactions can produce emergent situations, solutions, and stories.**

Use **systemic** to describe the design of the interacting rules and **emergent** to describe the situations and possibilities that arise through play. The earlier definition combined both ideas; separating them makes the distinction clearer.

| Term           | What it describes                                          | Open Legend meaning                                                                                               |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Systemic**   | How general rules and behaviors interact                   | Perception, needs, knowledge, actions and consequences connect through consistent rules within an authored world. |
| **Emergent**   | What can arise from interactions and participants’ choices | Situations, solutions, behaviors, relationships and stories develop through play.                                 |
| **Generative** | How new content or capabilities can be created             | An invention can become a validated, usable and reusable addition to the world’s supported possibilities.         |

A rule applies wherever its relevant conditions hold within the chosen world. Mechanics can connect through their actual effects: something happens, a character perceives it, that experience informs a decision, and the resulting action changes another situation. Individual choices can vary with knowledge, circumstances, needs and personality while the world’s laws remain coherent.

**Hypothetical Open Legend illustration:** noise from a fight reaches a nearby resident. What they can hear and recognize affects what they know; their memories, goals and current circumstances influence whether they help, flee, intervene or continue their own activity. If they act and remember the encounter, it may affect a later interaction. This illustrates how hearing, knowledge, motivation, action and memory can connect. It is not a claim that this entire sequence has been implemented or tested.

**A simple industry example:** if enemies pursue the player and weight activates a trap, a player can lure an enemy onto its trigger. The reusable rules are systemic; the resulting tactic is emergent. Randy Smith describes such a tactic as anticipated emergence in [his February 2004 Game Developer interview, “Emerging from the Tunnel” (p. 14)](https://media.gdcvault.com/GD_Mag_Archives/Game.Developer.2004.02.pdf#page=8).

Several distinctions matter when explaining these terms:

- **Emergence can be anticipated.** Designers can deliberately enable combinations. A solution need not surprise its designer to be emergent, and emergence is not synonymous with randomness.
- **Generative creation is a separate dimension.** Existing mechanics can support emergent play. Generative invention can expand the available mechanics, but generating a description or an object does not by itself establish meaningful emergence.
- **Simple rules can suffice.** Systemic design does not require exhaustive realism, extra complexity or identical laws in every authored world. Interacting systems alone do not guarantee rich or enjoyable emergent play; the resulting experience needs to demonstrate that value.
- **Authored content can coexist with systemic play.** Designers can author places, characters, encounters and particular story moments without prescribing every possible combination. Naughty Dog’s [GDC session overview on authored and systemic behavior in Uncharted 4](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1024467/Authored-vs-Systemic-Finding-a) discusses balancing the two. These industry sources explain terminology, not Open Legend’s implementation.

These are useful distinctions for this guide; industry usage overlaps rather than forming a rigid taxonomy. For a short gamer introduction, **emergent gameplay** often better names the experience being offered. Explain the benefit as **“the world’s rules work together, so your ideas and actions can have meaningful knock-on effects.”** For professionals, **systemic gameplay** names the design approach. Retain it in the selected professional descriptions while explaining the emergent outcomes it is intended to enable.

## Meaning of the other key terms

These definitions preserve the conversation’s substantive vocabulary, including related aliases and optional terms. Choose the terms that help the particular audience understand the intended experience; the glossary is not a request to put every term in one pitch. Project meaning follows the [product baseline](../01-requirements/product-baseline.md) and [gameplay priorities](../../docs/repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md); current capability remains owned by architecture and verification.

### Genre, structure and simulation

| Term                                           | Intended meaning and usage                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Open world**                                 | Spatial freedom to explore and approach places or activities in different ways. Keep “open world” together when describing the game. Engine openness concerns source and creation rights; it is a separate meaning of “open.” This term alone does not specify infinite space or one seamless server.                                      |
| **Persistent world / persistent consequences** | Meaningful state, creations and outcomes carry forward and can affect later play. Persistence communicates continuity; it does not alone promise continuous simulation while everyone is offline or perfect retention of every event.                                                                                                      |
| **Sandbox / systemic sandbox**                 | Players have meaningful room to choose goals, experiment and pursue different approaches within the world’s rules. “Systemic sandbox” adds the interacting-rules emphasis. Sandbox describes freedom of activity and approach; open world describes spatial freedom. Goals, challenges and authored content can still give play direction. |
| **Survival / survival-adventure**              | Survival supplies stakes through needs, hazards and vulnerability. “Survival-adventure” foregrounds the broader intended appeal: exploration, combat, useful rewards, building, invention and relationships. Explain what is enjoyable as well as what must be endured.                                                                    |
| **Life simulation**                            | Physical needs, personality, motives, memories, relationships and everyday activity can shape inhabitants’ lives. It broadens the description beyond combat and survival without committing every authored world to the same model of life.                                                                                                |
| **World simulation / survival simulator**      | “World simulation” emphasizes connected models of the world and its inhabitants. “Survival simulator” concentrates attention on modeled survival conditions and may suggest detailed realism or routine maintenance. Explain which interactions matter to the experience; simulation does not require an exhaustive model of reality.      |
| **MMO / MMORPG / sandbox MMORPG**              | Communicates the intended large shared-world game and role-playing ambition. In Open Legend copy, it does not select fixed classes or a conventional quest-and-raid structure. “Sandbox MMORPG” foregrounds player-chosen activity within that shared world. Target scale and demonstrated capacity remain separate.                       |

### Inhabitants, AI and interaction

| Term                                                                             | Intended meaning and usage                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **AI-driven**                                                                    | Broadly says AI influences the experience, leaving its role unspecified. Explain whether the relevant contribution is dialogue, character decisions, invention, events or creation. The label alone does not communicate why someone would want to play.                                                                                                                |
| **AI-native**                                                                    | AI is central to the intended character and invention experience. Ordinary world rules and actions can run through native code; the phrase does not require a model call for every action. Pair it with a concrete benefit such as independent inhabitants or useful invention.                                                                                         |
| **Living world**                                                                 | Independent activity, remembered experience and connected consequences make the world feel inhabited. It is a useful player introduction to lives that extend beyond the player’s immediate request. Explain the behavior behind the phrase; persistence and offline activity still have their own meanings.                                                            |
| **Autonomous inhabitants / living characters / autonomous character simulation** | Inhabitants’ needs, personality, motives, memories and relationships influence their independent purposes and choices. They can initiate, refuse and reconsider; they need not cooperate with the player. “Autonomous character simulation” emphasizes this connected model for professionals, while “people with lives of their own” makes the benefit accessible.     |
| **Persistent minds**                                                             | Shorthand for continuity of relevant character memory, knowledge, goals and relationships across encounters. It should describe retained character state and its effects, without implying perfect recall, human consciousness or infallible understanding.                                                                                                             |
| **Embodied AI agents**                                                           | Agents act as situated inhabitants with bodies, senses, locations and physical circumstances that affect what they can know and do. Use when their grounding in the shared world matters to a technical explanation.                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Agentic simulation**                                                           | Professional AI vocabulary for agents pursuing goals and taking actions within a simulation. In Open Legend, explain the inhabitants’ autonomy and their world’s rules. For gamers, “inhabitants with lives of their own” is usually clearer editorial wording.                                                                                                         |
| **Natural-language interaction**                                                 | People can express dialogue, intentions or ideas in ordinary words through supported interfaces. Interpreting a request and realizing its effect are separate steps governed by available capabilities, knowledge and world conditions. Specify supported input when discussing a build; the term alone does not establish voice input or the success of every command. |

### Emergence, society and history

| Term                                                  | Intended meaning and usage                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Emergent gameplay**                                 | Situations, challenges, solutions and strategies arise through interacting rules and participants’ choices. It includes both helpful solutions and new complications. See [the systemic/emergent distinction](#what-systemic-means); designer surprise is optional.                                              |
| **Emergent stories / emergent storytelling**          | Events, goals, relationships and consequences accumulate into stories people can experience and tell. Authored moments and generated narration can contribute, while the meaningful sequence of events gives the story its substance.                                                                            |
| **Emergent relationships / emergent social dynamics** | Trust, cooperation, rivalry, disagreement and other relationships can develop through encounters, memory and choices. This describes meaningful social interaction at a smaller scale than entire societies; it does not assume automatic friendship or compliance.                                              |
| **Emergent societies**                                | Collective organization, roles, norms and institutions can develop through repeated interactions. This expresses a larger ambition supported by individual lives and shared consequences, without making civilization development a prerequisite for enjoyable initial play or a required goal for every player. |
| **Emergent history**                                  | Accumulated events, discoveries, relationships, cooperation and conflict can leave lasting changes in a world’s shared past. “Create history” is strongest when a past event affects later circumstances. It does not by itself specify an exhaustive chronicle or who remembers each event.                     |

### Invention, progression and worldbuilding

| Term                                                                         | Intended meaning and usage                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Player-driven invention / generative mechanics**                           | Ideas can become supported, usable and reusable additions to the world. “Generative mechanics” emphasizes the ambition to expand what can be done through new gameplay capabilities. Realization, character knowledge and permissions determine what becomes available; a narrated idea alone does not establish a working mechanic. |
| **Generative crafting**                                                      | A concrete entry point into invention through supported new recipes, tools or crafted objects. It is useful when making and using something explains the experience quickly. The broader invention ambition can extend beyond crafting.                                                                                              |
| **Discovery-driven progression**                                             | Learning something useful, discovering a method or making an invention can open new opportunities and approaches. The phrase communicates progress through discovery without selecting a policy for experience points, levels or classes.                                                                                            |
| **Open-ended technological development**                                     | A long-term ambition for discoveries and inventions to influence a world’s development beyond an entirely preauthored technology path. Use when technological development fits the world’s premise, and explain the supported means of extending its possibilities. It does not promise unlimited automatic research.                |
| **Evolving gameplay / player-shaped worlds / player-driven world evolution** | Actions change circumstances, while discovery and invention can expand the opportunities available within them. Players influence that development alongside autonomous inhabitants with their own agency. This can describe both lasting world change and growth in what its participants can do.                                   |
| **Worldbuilding through play / participatory worldbuilding**                 | Players contribute to a world through inhabiting it: making things, forming relationships and shaping shared practices or places. It connects ordinary participation to a larger world’s development. Defining foundational rules as an authorized creator remains a separate capability.                                            |

### Engine and creator language

| Term                                                | Intended meaning and usage                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Authored realities / creator-authored realities** | Creators choose coherent foundations and premises for distinct worlds. Physical, social or magical rules can vary by setting; survival is not a universal engine requirement. Ordinary players inhabit the rules of the world they join.                                                                       |
| **Open engine / open-source world simulation**      | Describes the repository’s open-source proposition and its value for inspecting and extending world simulation. It is useful developer language alongside a clear explanation of the playable game. [Licensing](../../LICENSING.md) owns the actual terms and boundaries for code, world content and services. |
| **AI-assisted creation**                            | AI helps people propose or refine world content and supported mechanics. In creator material, explain the author’s actual controls and how ideas become usable additions. Keep that authoring context clear when also discussing ordinary player invention.                                                    |

## Terms to use deliberately

These are positioning judgments about the expectations a label can create. They retain the earlier discussion’s tradeoffs without promoting the labels into selected copy.

| Term or label                                      | How to use it for Open Legend                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Immersive sim / immersive-sim-inspired**         | Explain the intended connection to consistent systems and multiple approaches to problems. “Immersive-sim-inspired” can identify a design influence; “systemic sandbox” directly names the relevant approach. Describing an immersive atmosphere alone does not communicate that design proposition.                               |
| **Self-evolving world**                            | Identify what drives the change: player actions, autonomous inhabitants, invention or creator activity. The selected “a world that evolves through invention” supplies a concrete cause. Do not leave the audience to infer automatic, unconstrained rewriting of the game’s rules.                                                |
| **Player-driven economy / emergent economy**       | “Player-driven” foregrounds human influence but can obscure inhabitants’ participation. When describing the broader ambition, “emergent economy” or “an economy shaped by players and autonomous inhabitants” can make the participants clearer. Explain the actual production, exchange or resource interactions being discussed. |
| **Civilization simulator / colony sim / god game** | These labels can suggest directing a population from above. “Emergent societies” communicates the larger social ambition while keeping the ordinary player’s role as an inhabitant legible. Creator or game-master controls belong in their own authoring context.                                                                 |
| **Infinite / unlimited / anything is possible**    | These imply guarantees broader than the intended proposition. For ambition, use “open-ended,” “extensible,” “generative” or “evolving,” and say what can expand while the chosen world’s rules remain coherent.                                                                                                                    |

## Communication principles

The original draft, **“Open persistent world AI-driven survival simulator MMORPG,”** captures the intended blend but compresses several kinds of information into one list. The selected descriptions clarify the game’s form and then explain the distinctive experience. “Open world” concerns exploration, “persistent” continuity and “AI-native” the role of AI; the inhabitants, invention and consequences give people reasons to care.

1. **Connect the three defining ideas.** Use [autonomous lives, useful invention and persistent consequences](#explain-what-makes-the-combination-distinctive) together with a concrete example. Their interaction carries the proposition.
2. **Give players activities and reasons to care.** Exploration, combat, making, relationships, discovery, beauty and accomplishment can all matter. Explain how the chosen example becomes enjoyable and consequential.
3. **Explain invention as something usable.** A new tool or technique can create an opportunity and remain useful later. Coherent world rules, supported realization and character knowledge determine what can actually happen.
4. **Match the level of ambition to the conversation.** Use the large vision freely when introducing what we are building. When showing a specific build, identify the demonstrated slice and use its actual evidence.
5. **Keep player agency and creator authority legible.** Describe changing a world through participation separately from defining its rules as an authorized creator.
6. **Use the selected copy as the wording reference.** Keep newly suggested alternatives visibly separate until selected. Do not silently rewrite the chosen taglines or promote every earlier brainstorming term into the preferred vocabulary.

For demonstration structure and broader public-claim guidance, use [Positioning and copy](positioning-and-copy.md#hero-demo-concept). For what to build and what is currently supported, use the existing product, architecture and verification owners linked above.
