# NPC cognition research — source request

**Requested by Mike, October 20, 2026.** Research and documentation only; full designs and experiments are separate work.

## Supplied material

Mike supplied extensive transcripts of [Kevin in Halo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EOfm3h4xYU8) and [the Skyrim NPC experiment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IngsybtbBeg). The questions concern perception, fast reactions, combat controls, expressive speech, character fidelity and memory. The Skyrim transcript's “Mandela” likely refers to Mantella; its exact installed build/model/settings remain unresolved. The complete transcripts remain conversation evidence, not independently frame-timed observations.

## Research scope

Explain the mechanisms from source code where available: native execution versus model decisions, event cadence, actor-visible information, grounded references, action feedback, memory and speech. Survey relevant architectures, papers, downloadable models, current hosted availability and firsthand implementation reports.

Compare more than Kevin's stack. Distinguish actual community use, maintainer recommendations, model-card claims and measured suitability. Treat unnecessary refusals and quiet sanitization of permitted fiction as important model-selection concerns without confusing them with authentic character disagreement or changing the accepted service boundary.

Connect the findings to OpenLegend's survival-adventure, invention and independent-character goals. Consider cooperation, conflict, discovery, expression and remembered consequences alongside reliability and cost. Preserve useful conclusions in the archive and route unresolved design/test preparation to existing owners; do not duplicate a roadmap or turn each promising technique into a requirement.
