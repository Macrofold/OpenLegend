Recorded September 28, 2026. The user's request is preserved verbatim below. It asks for open ideation, not implementation: the resulting [Wellbeing Vision](../08-wellbeing-vision/README.md) collects ideas, research and cautions, and creates no requirement, decision or task.

## User request

Help me brainstorm ways that a game like Open Legend could impact, aid, engage, and enable people in real tangible ways that interact with their daily lives, goals, social lives, etc beyond just being a video game? I don't want this to be a dystopian vision where the best thing Open Legend becomes is another reason to keep people glued to their screens, at home, alone, interacting with AI instead  of other humans. I'm very excited about the potential for this game, and I'm incredibly inspired and motivated by the creative aspects of this game, but I also seek to build things that are useful and that improve the well-being of humans overall. While I'm passionate about this project for the creative pursuit in its own right (and I think it will be incredibly special and engaging for people of all kinds once it's finished), I am concerned that, in its best form, it will be making it more likely that people engage less and escape more into fantasy rather than engaging with their real lives. I'd like you to do deep research, think hard, find ideas and inspiration and precedent and nuggets of wisdom and ideas from the web, across the web, throughout history, in books, literature, and sci-fi, and imbue your own ideas into this. I would like you to find anything that you can use to help me think about this aspect of what I'm building. Think about ways to build this such that it will actually promote human well-being and will promote people to engage more with their lives to be more successful, to be happier and more content and more connected and more full of joy and love, and more wise (rather than just being a game that people play to escape their realities). Create a set of docs under archive/, probably under a new folder. Maybe call it Wellbeing Vision or something like that, and just put a ton of ideas in there. This doesn't have to be one doc. You could create multiple docs, but just put a ton of ideas in there for me to peruse through and bubble up some of the ideas that you think would be most interesting to me. These can be incredibly ambitious. They can be theoretical, abstract, or very pragmatic and practical and down-to-earth. I want the full spectrum. Don't be afraid to think hard and long, and do a ton of research, and go much deeper than you think is necessary.
