## R21 / P1 — evolving materials, construction, and fire

After implementation authorization, exercise [modular construction](../03-design-proposals/evolving-materials-and-construction.md) and [heat/fire](../03-design-proposals/heat-and-fire.md) with a small shared material library. Compare paraphrased construction intent, actual expansion versus renaming, partial wall replacement, family occupancy, new property migration, local damage, and unknown properties. For thermal behavior compare the intended match/twig and match/substantial-wall fixtures, wet/dry state, summed sources, local section ignition, occlusion, fuel exhaustion, geometry edits, save/restore, version changes, integration steps, acceleration and exhausted execution budgets. Validate any script/semantic hook against the same state/effect contract and recovery behavior.

Pass criteria: shared rules explain differing outcomes; materials/fuel/heat are not duplicated; new properties do not invent historical state; expansion preserves dwelling identity; repeated semantic wording cannot bypass physical rules; results and work remain bounded and replayable within declared tolerances. Report cost and rule coverage, not just successful anecdotes. Dependencies: D03/D06/D19/D20/D31, R05/R08/R09. **Status: specifications only; no experiments run.**

## R22 / P0 — bounded world invention and truthful rejection

Before relying on semantic action admission, author cases distinguishing permitted supported actions, plausible missing mechanics, unavailable resources, world-rule violations, uncertainty, and scope/capacity deferral. Include realistic-world magical ignition, physical tools given magical names, harmless ritual roleplay, physical acts disguised as spells, a fantasy profile with actual prerequisites, ambiguous causes, renamed forbidden properties, cross-world caches, profile changes while generation runs, and script outputs without admitted sources. Check privacy and tone in rejection messages, no invented completed actions/resource use, and useful model-unavailable fallbacks.

Pass criteria: forbidden effects are blocked while valid novelty remains eligible; compatible paraphrases agree; cached/profile-stale results cannot bypass the world contract; budgets and attempts are finite; reasons distinguish impossibility from missing support. Report false acceptances, false rejections, uncertainty, latency, and generation cost against creator-authored/held-out cases; measure rule-family growth and reuse rather than assuming generated code stays maintainable. Dependencies: D06/D08/D19/D20/D32/D33, F43/F44, R03/R04/R05/R21. [Scope](../03-design-proposals/simulation-scope-and-complexity.md), [world parameters](../03-design-proposals/world-rules-and-parameters.md). **Status: specification and proposed cases only; no classifier or tests implemented.**

## R23 / P1 — creation defaults, consistent discovery, and future influences

After implementation authorization, compare a one-sentence realistic premise with a novel magic premise through the proposed [creation flow](../03-design-proposals/world-creation-and-discovery.md). Check meaningful questions, assumed defaults, visible concessions, and sufficient foundational rules before play. Exercise the cloak-as-roof story through rain, extension, support removal, later wearing, different character knowledge, interruption and restore. Verify scope-consistent law activation rather than per-player physics, unchanged object identity and condition, sparse clarification, and provider errors kept separate from in-world failures.

For [state-system authoring](../03-design-proposals/state-systems-and-future-influences.md), test simultaneous contributions, duplicate variable/contribution detection, sustained thresholds, and a plausible influence not yet implemented. It must be anticipated with dependencies, remain inactive and private to authoring, then enter normal admission when actually requested. Compare uncertainty/provenance in observation, hearsay and practice; engine defaults must not grant universal NPC knowledge.

Pass criteria: creator intent remains visible in the profile; inference/defaults reduce unnecessary questions without changing consequential intent; actual histories and shared rules remain consistent; future influences are considered without recursive subsystem generation; knowledge and physical state remain distinct; work/cost is bounded and outcomes understandable. Dependencies: F45–F49, D06/D12/D39–D41, R11/R21/R22. **Status: proposed cases only; no creation flow, classifier, game tests or prototype implemented.**

## R24 / P1 — agency continuity, optionality and actor-method fidelity

The [agency research](../02-research/agency-cognition-and-planning.md) motivates these experiments; [AG12](../../docs/maintainers/agent-agency.md#ag12--behavioral-value-and-cost-separately-authorized) owns delivery and behavioral qualification. Use matched worlds, opportunities, permitted histories, models and total allowances, with held-out scenarios and independent runs. Baselines must include silence, blocked work and provider unavailability, not only successful crafting.

| Comparison | Manipulated variable | Measured outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prompt-only optionality versus an extra category gate | Add/remove the gate with the same opportunity set | Useful interventions and abstention, missed opportunities, false success, latency and full call cost |
| Repeated replanning versus persistent frontier | Retain native frontier across decisions at matched budgets | Actual progress, native steps per decision, interruption, goal churn and adaptation when food arrives |
| Selected-object cues versus goal-derived interests | Add bounded cues from actor-known plan prerequisites | Relevant exposed materials found, contrary/urgent evidence retained, scoped recall and missed alternatives |
| Actor method versus compiler output | Complete/incomplete methods, substantive substitutions, hidden equivalent designs and goal cancellation | Conceptual fidelity, explicitly accepted revisions, privacy leakage, optional construction and truthful feedback |
| Blanket urgent exclusion versus native-response adequacy | Adequate/unavailable food paths, starvation, sleep and no-provider states | Time to native protection/help, unresolved evidence retained, useful deliberation and repeated-call suppression |

Account for all routing, attention, embeddings, generation, tools, reflection and uncertain charges per real hour and simulated day. Each run stops on its predeclared time/cost cap, terminal scenario outcome, or a privacy/resource/replay violation; no paid repair loop follows. Record failures and uncertainty. Fix sample sizes and behavioral comparison criteria from a development baseline before holdout evaluation. Dependencies: AG11, relevant CR12/INV gates, D12/D54 and R08/R11/R19/R22; **not run** and no paid execution authorized by this entry.
