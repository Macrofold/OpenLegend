Text under “Current direction / proposed default” is a proposal unless the row explicitly identifies it as accepted. Record each decision by changing its status and adding rationale, date, owner, evidence, and affected documents. Preserve superseded decisions in the history section. Do not silently convert a recommendation into a requirement.

## Decisions requiring creator taste rather than more browsing

The user has chosen the wilderness premise, tolerance for meaningful NPC death, grounded modern pixel art, PlayCanvas and ownership of the simulation. Remaining consequential preferences are whether the camera must freely follow a character in 3D, whether spoken interaction is essential immediately, the acceptable spend ceiling, the exact pace of life/aging, and how dangerous human-to-human interaction should be. Research can clarify consequences, but cannot decide those values.

Resolve the remaining parts of D01–D04 through focused discussion and, after implementation authorization, pacing experiments. The proposed one-hour game day can anchor comparison scenarios without being locked in. This archive uses explicit provisional assumptions to make the other proposals concrete; nothing has been provisioned on those assumptions.
