# Wellbeing Vision: there and back again

Status: **open ideation** · 2026-09-28 · Written at Mike's request ([source request](../00-source/wellbeing-vision-followup.md)). Nothing in this folder is an accepted requirement, decision, specification or task. It is a library of ideas, evidence and cautions to choose from.

## The question

Could a game like Open Legend make real, tangible differences to people's daily lives, goals and relationships — and help them become more connected, capable, joyful and wise — without becoming one more reason to stay home, alone, talking to AI instead of people?

## The short answer

**Yes, but not by default.** The research supports the worry about the *default* path of engagement-optimised AI products. Heavy, voluntary emotional use of AI companions tracks with more loneliness and less socialising. Manipulative goodbyes measurably keep people chatting. Lost sleep is the most common everyday harm of play. And hours are the wrong thing to count: why and how people play predicts their wellbeing far better than how long.

The same research shows the outcome turns on a small number of levers: what the team measures, how sessions end, what residents may and may not do, whether the business rewards hours or meaning, and whether play leads people to other people. Pulled the right way, the engine that could isolate people can connect them.

Open Legend starts unusually well placed. The world already pauses when the player leaves. The Narrator already keeps a private journal. Residents remember, age and die. Invention is the core loop. The proposed Threewater March turns on who governs a shared river crossing, and one of its residents is written as "the host learning to close the door." The engine is open source. Each of these can become a bridge back to life rather than a reason to stay. [The worry, examined](01-the-worry-examined.md) sets out the evidence.

## The vision in one sentence

> **Open Legend succeeds when its players' lives go better — and it is proud of the ways they go better away from the screen.**

The frame for the whole folder is the oldest answer to this worry: *there and back again*. Traditions that never met — the hero's return, Plato's cave, the Zen ox-herding pictures, Odysseus leaving Calypso, *The Phantom Tollbooth* — agree that a journey into another world is complete only when the traveller comes home changed and brings something back. Open Legend can make *leaving well* the climax of a session rather than a failure of retention. Games should be "important but not predominant," in Kurt Hahn's phrase, and each world a trailhead rather than a destination. [There and back again](02-the-return-principles.md) turns this into twelve organising ideas, a 22-principle charter and design tests for reviews.

## Start here: the top picks

[Top picks](00-top-picks.md) bubbles up fifteen ideas, each with a short scene, the evidence, a first experiment and what to watch out for. If only three things happen soon:

1. **Adopt the Resident Code, with a 0% farewell-manipulation audit as a release gate.** It protects the most-abused moment in AI companionship, it's cheap to automate, and new companion-chatbot laws already require much of it.
2. **Turn the pause into a ritual.** The Return Rite, chapter endings, and a camp that grows sleepy near the player's chosen bedtime.
3. **Measure "worth it" and "with whom" from day one.** Whatever a team measures becomes the product.

## How to read these docs

- **Status.** Everything here is open ideation. "Should" means "the research suggests," not "the project has decided."
- **Horizon tags** on ideas: **Now** fits the current prototype or first playable; **Next** needs planned systems (the Narrator, shared worlds, Threewater March); **Later** needs new systems; **Moonshot** is a long-range bet.
- **Evidence tags:** **[Strong]** replicated findings, meta-analyses or large, well-designed studies; **[Moderate]** at least one good study or consistent observational evidence; **[Weak]** small, early or contested evidence; **[Wisdom]** a tradition, philosophy or literary insight rather than an empirical finding; **[Idea]** original design thinking with no direct evidence; **[Law]** a legal requirement rather than research evidence. Combined tags such as **[Moderate] + [Wisdom]** mean both kinds of support.
- **Two local grading schemes.** [Precedents](10-precedents.md) grades *sources* A–D, from randomised studies to anecdote. [Measuring what matters](12-measuring-what-matters.md#6-evidence-labels-for-features) labels *features* A–D by the strength of the evidence behind them.
- **† (dagger)** marks a quotation's exact wording, or a detail, that could not be re-checked against a primary source during this research. Verify before quoting publicly.
- **Worlds and residents.** Scenes use the project's proposed settings — the wilderness start with Ada, [Threewater March](../../docs/repertoires/worlds/medieval-survival.md) and [Linden Reach](../../docs/repertoires/worlds/modern-life.md) — which are themselves proposals, not canon. Named residents come from those proposals; other characters are illustrative.
- **Names** such as "the Return Rite" and "the Resident Code" are working names; [questions and first experiments](14-questions-and-first-experiments.md#3-naming) collects them.
- **Law.** Legal summaries are research notes as of September 2026, not legal advice.

## Research, sources and limits

The folder synthesises ten research briefs written for it on 2026-09-28 with AI-assisted web research: games and wellbeing science; AI companions and human connection; precedents for play crossing into life; philosophy, myth and wisdom; science fiction and literature; humane design and ethics; community, civic life and behaviour change; learning and skill transfer; the 2025–26 frontier in law, research and industry; and nature, craft, body and ritual. They are kept in [research](research/README.md) as the evidence base. [Sources](sources.md) gathers the most important sources by theme.

**Limits.** The research budget ran out before every item could be re-checked, and a few pages could not be fetched. Items that could not be confirmed are marked † here or flagged in the briefs. Study figures are quoted as reported; check the primary source before using any number externally. Much of the most beautiful material is [Wisdom] or [Idea] — fine for ideation, not for marketing claims.
