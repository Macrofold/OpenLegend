This is a separate evidence archive from [game-design inspiration](../game-inspiration/). It records individual development, community, funding and launch histories; it does **not** prescribe an Open Legend marketing plan or synthesize principles across games.

## Scope and reading conventions

The collection meets the requested minimum of 20 successful games and 20 unsuccessful cases, prioritizing solo developers and core teams smaller than ten. A small core team is not the same as a small total production: publishers, outsourced studios, freelancers, platform partners and inherited audiences are recorded separately. Some historical headcounts remain unverified, notably Midora and The Black Glove; they are not silently presented as confirmed under-ten or solo precedents. Other limitations and later studio growth are identified within each dossier.

Outcome labels refer to a stated period and dimension:

- **Success:** documented sales, revenue, commercial rankings or an explicit successful commercial outcome. The cohort includes modest sustainable products and major breakouts; awards and positive reviews alone are insufficient. Success does not automatically establish audited profitability.
- **Unsuccessful outcome:** documented weak discovery, failed fundraising, poor sales, unsustainable returns or stalled/canceled funded delivery. These are different failure modes, identified in the table and dossier, not a judgment of artistic quality.
- **Mixed:** completion or reception and commercial sustainability point in different directions. These two extra cases are not counted toward either minimum. An eventual recovery is not also counted as a second unsuccessful game: Among Us belongs only to the success cohort here.

Dossiers distinguish **documented facts**, **attributed explanations**, **case-specific interpretation**, and **not established**. The last term means the reviewed public sources do not establish the answer; it does not mean the activity never happened. No private analytics, new developer interviews conducted for this project, purchased data or game playtesting are represented here.

Historical follower counts, Discord membership, conversion rates, paid-media spending and hiring contracts are especially incomplete. Present-day follower/review counts are not backdated to launch. Backers, wishlists, downloads, paid copies, concurrent players, gross revenue, developer receipts and profit remain different quantities. Currency amounts retain the source currency. Kickstarter periods use the campaign's displayed dates and duration where recoverable; timezone-dependent dates in retrospective articles can differ by one day. Failed-campaign pledges are not reported as money collected.

Each dossier records the earliest **verified** public touchpoint, not necessarily the first-ever post. A trailer does not prove that a public downloadable demo existed. Kickstarter metadata can remain readable when the original description or updates do not; access limits are recorded rather than filled with remembered details. A funded but canceled game is not mislabeled as a failed fundraising campaign, and a later source-code release is not automatically completed delivery.

## Dossier structure and evidence limits

Each game includes its outcome and evidence window; origin and team; development/public-exposure timeline; crowdfunding or other financing; channel-by-channel community evidence; launch, reception and commercial results; collaborators/publishing/marketing; bounded positive and negative observations; and unresolved evidence with a source register. Where a requested field is unavailable, the dossier identifies the gap rather than manufacturing an answer.

Sources are linked beside claims and listed with author/organization, date where established, source type and purpose. Developer recollections and marketing claims are attributed. Reviewers' opinions are reception evidence, not independent financial audits. Restricted, missing and partially indexed sources are described accordingly; no claim is made that every linked old build, video or campaign update could be opened in full.

This is a deliberately selected comparison set, subject to survivorship, disclosure and retrospective-reporting bias. It is not a representative sample from which a success rate can be calculated. Historical promotion routes and platform mechanisms are not automatically current policy. **The requested deliverable ends at these dossiers; synthesis remains separate.**
