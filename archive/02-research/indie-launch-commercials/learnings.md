This document is the main synthesis for **community, launch and commercial research**, not a replacement for game-design research or an approved Open Legend business/implementation plan. The October 16 requests expanded the earlier dossiers-only scope and required a complete reread. Recommendations are research judgments to test, not commitments to a platform, release date, budget or team size. Individual dossiers own the detailed timelines, figures and source registers; the review map records a contribution and qualification for every case.

## The central distinction

Do not ask one number to answer four questions: **Do people understand and want the game? Do strangers actually enjoy playing it? Will enough customers pay? Can the proceeds support delivery and continued work?** A project can answer one positively and another negatively.

[Rec Room](games/rec-room-platform.md) is an extreme audience/funding-versus-sustainability comparison. [Maze Mice](games/maze-mice.md) exposes the difference between a selected production budget and all costs. [Regency Solitaire](games/regency-solitaire.md) separates one storefront from the whole business. [Brigador](games/brigador.md) separates a poor early period from later improvement. These are not interchangeable varieties of “failure.”

### Best current recommendation for Open Legend

Use the [working business plan](../../06-marketing/business-plan.md) as the commercial starting point: a small hosted-world experience that is worthwhile alone, a bounded paid pilot, and evidence of renewal, operating margin and manageable support. Do not replace that with Steam-first, Kickstarter-first or marketplace-first merely because those routes appear in successful cases. The existing proposed price and cost figures are hypotheses, not measured economics.

The immediate priority is **one complete experience strangers can understand and enjoy**, alongside modest, truthful public communication and recruiting. Then measure the cost and responsibility of delivering the actual paid promise. Increase audience, spending, scope and ongoing obligations only when the corresponding evidence improves. The [execution guide](execution-playbook.md) specifies work products, go/rework/stop conditions, ownership, metrics and conditional Steam/crowdfunding routes. Its completion does not mean any game or commercial gate has passed.

### How much confidence to place in this collection

This is a purposively selected set, **not a random sample**. Adding obscure cases reduces dependence on famous winners but does not establish industry success rates. A developer willing to publish accounts is different from one who says nothing; a memorable comeback is more likely to be discussed than a quiet closure. Several games share a developer or publisher, so repeated patterns are not independent statistical replications.

Prefer dated original posts and explicit financial definitions to retrospective slogans. Developer interviews provide valuable process evidence but often lack a counterfactual. Store records establish release and publishing facts, not profit. Missing follower history means unknown, not that no audience existed. Roblox and Rec Room are platform/service comparisons, and their mature organizations are not under-ten-person studios.

**Strongest confidence:** distinctions supported directly by records or accounting—reach is not purchase, pledges are not profit, a later source release is not completed delivery, and launch is not lifetime outcome. **Moderate confidence:** recurring mechanisms worth testing—representative play, relevant creator access, purposeful community activity, costed scope and specific specialist responsibilities. **Low confidence:** a universal posting frequency, “best” channel, wishlist threshold, optimal launch month, publisher uplift or guaranteed recovery formula. The strongest findings concern distinctions and observable sequences, not isolated proof of sales causation.

## 1. Publicity timing depends on what is ready to show

“Start immediately” versus “work privately for three years” is an unhelpful binary. A technical diary, a compelling visual reveal, an unassisted playable session and a request for money create different expectations.

[Minecraft](games/minecraft.md) offers an early usable experiment; [Eastshade](games/eastshade.md) separates public writing, increasingly coherent playtests and a later official trailer. [Spilled!](games/spilled.md) separates a pre-campaign demo from post-campaign visual improvement. [The Ember Guardian](games/the-ember-guardian.md) distinguishes the reveal from the playable discovery event. None requires treating every early post as a full commercial announcement.

**Research judgment:** begin learning from people before betting the whole production on private assumptions. Promote broadly when there is a truthful, comprehensible artifact that can satisfy the expectation it creates. The question is not whether every asset is finished, but whether the part being shown demonstrates why someone would care.

**Counterexample and limit:** a campaign can raise money without an already public playable build; [Limit Theory](games/limit-theory.md) documents backer playability after fundraising. That proves possibility, not adequate funding or eventual delivery. Conversely, [PONCHO](games/poncho.md) shows that playable material does not guarantee a campaign succeeds. [FTL](games/ftl-faster-than-light.md), [Hollow Knight](games/hollow-knight.md) and [Undertale](games/undertale.md) offer useful credible-playable sequences, not proof of a mandatory amount of pre-campaign polish.

For Open Legend, test the promise of the intended first player experience, rather than assuming excitement about a flexible engine will become demand for a particular game. An illustrative candidate—not a claim of current implemented gameplay—would be a short situation in which a player's choice produces an understandable, memorable response from the world.

## 2. The visible starting audience often has a history

A first Steam release is not necessarily a first public creation. [Lethal Company](games/lethal-company.md), [Choo-Choo Charles](games/choo-choo-charles.md), [A Short Hike](games/a-short-hike.md) and [BattleBit Remastered](games/battlebit-remastered.md) expose different inherited advantages: earlier games, shared work, professional relationships or mod communities. The TrampolineTales dossiers preserve catalog continuity rather than treating each game as an independent zero-audience debut.

There is also a direction-of-causation trap: the dedicated game account in [Among Us](games/among-us.md) followed the breakout. A large account observed afterward cannot establish that amassing followers was a prerequisite.

Separate **the developer's own audience**, **other creators' audiences**, **a platform's customers**, and **active players who invite others**. A developer's YouTube channel can provide motivation, credibility and testers without being the largest source of buyers. [Only Way is Down](games/only-way-is-down.md) and [Knife To Meet You](games/knife-to-meet-you.md) warn against equating entertaining video reach with purchase intent. [Luck be a Landlord](games/luck-be-a-landlord.md) documents recurring creator contact, while [Mortal Glory](games/mortal-glory.md) distinguishes relevant outreach from social posts mainly reaching developers.

**Research judgment:** do not make becoming a successful YouTuber a prerequisite for developing Open Legend. A sustainable public record may be valuable, but it should have a specific job: recruit tests, explain a changed experience, show something worth playing, or reach a relevant creator. Track the result of that job instead of optimizing views alone.

**Limit:** the archive does not contain enough comparable historical channel data to prescribe a universal weekly upload schedule or prove one social network has the best return on developer time. [SNKRX](games/snkrx.md), [shapez](games/shapez.md) and Mortal Glory also prevent treating a small prelaunch wishlist count as automatic commercial failure; their other discovery routes and later visibility matter. They are counterexamples, not reasons to deliberately neglect preparation.

## 3. A demo should test the paid promise, not merely generate a flattering metric

The supplied [Ember Guardian](games/the-ember-guardian.md) account directly raises the danger of attracting people to one structure and selling another. [Only Way is Down](games/only-way-is-down.md) raises the related question of visual appeal versus actual difficulty. [Crumbling World](games/crumbling-world.md) had an early public build missing the defining crumbling mechanic. These are more useful than the generic instruction to make a polished trailer.

A strong free experience can support payment rather than replace it: [Backpack Battles](games/backpack-battles.md) and [Pawnbarian](games/pawnbarian.md) document substantial pre-purchase play. [Balatro](games/balatro.md) changed its sampling design rather than treating any demo as equivalent. That does not establish that unlimited demos are appropriate for a short narrative game, nor that every returning free player will buy.

**Research judgment:** investigate whether people who like the marketing also like the actual intended activity. Observe first-time players without explaining away confusing parts. Ask what they thought they were buying and what would bring them back. Measure representative enjoyment separately from successful installation, a long session caused by confusion, or repeated retries after a bug.

The aim is not to make all players stay indefinitely. A satisfying short game and an unsustainable live service have different success criteria. [Spooky Bonus](games/spooky-bonus.md) and [What's the Password?](games/whats-the-password.md) are useful scope comparisons, not arguments that every game needs a recurring daily habit. [Slay the Spire](games/slay-the-spire.md) shows the value of expert feedback; that does not replace observing newcomers. [Where the Water Tastes Like Wine](games/where-the-water-tastes-like-wine.md) makes the risk of late complete-experience testing explicit.

## 4. A useful community has an activity, not just a membership count

[Descenders](games/descenders.md) gives prerelease participants reasons to act. [Coregrounds](games/coregrounds.md) supplies a community-to-collaborator route. These differ from counting inactive signups as ready customers. [Dynasty Feud](games/dynasty-feud.md) and [The Flock](games/the-flock.md) expose the gap between enthusiasm and a sustainable population available to play.

**Research judgment:** open a community space when it has a job and someone can reliably support it. A test session, feedback cycle, player-created challenge or release-support area is more concrete than a general demand to “build hype.” Choose the smallest manageable set of destinations rather than scattering a tiny audience across empty channels.

For synchronous multiplayer, track whether compatible players can actually meet at the same time. [BattleBit Remastered](games/battlebit-remastered.md) used scheduled tests to concentrate participation. For an asynchronous or independently playable product, use the relevant alternative: returning testers, completed sessions or understandable player stories. Backpack Battles' asynchronous opponents do not impose the same launch-population requirement. Do not copy a competitive multiplayer server-population target into a product that does not need one.

**Limit:** a small committed group can reveal serious issues and still fail to represent the eventual audience. Community affection is not independent evidence of product readiness; unfamiliar players and varied circumstances remain important. A volunteer contribution also needs clear responsibilities, permission and maintenance rather than assumed perpetual free labor. Factorio, Core Defense and Descenders preserve different examples of community expertise and its coordination costs.

## 5. A successful launch can create a delivery problem immediately

In [The Ember Guardian](games/the-ember-guardian.md), promotion, testing and emergency fixes intersected in damaging ways. [BattleBit Remastered](games/battlebit-remastered.md) supplies a different timescale: extraordinary first sales did not eliminate later communication and major-update difficulties. [Valheim](games/valheim.md) shows success changing support and roadmap demands. None establishes that bad reviews caused a particular measured loss in revenue.

**Research judgment:** promotion should be paired with a realistic ability to support the attention it earns. Protect the actual release candidate from temporary recording changes. Recheck urgent fixes in the situations they affect, and retain a practical recovery path. Software rollback and restoration of customer data are not automatically interchangeable. Treat this as a future commercial-release consideration, not an instruction to change Open Legend's present development-save policy or its current verification rules.

Ten trusted testers and thousands of purchasers answer different questions. The release plan should make room for unfamiliar machines, later-game situations, repeated transitions and support volume, rather than assuming a successful demo session qualifies the whole product.

A launch should not depend on a solo creator simultaneously debugging, answering every community message and conducting all press outreach. Dividing responsibilities can be valuable even when it does not add a measurable new buyer. When a second person is unavailable, reducing the pace of admission is a valid alternative to pretending there is a full launch team.

## 6. Revenue, cost recovery and a sustainable livelihood are different claims

The archive contains several incompatible uses of “profitable.” [Maze Mice](games/maze-mice.md) excludes a disclosed PR expense and founder labor from its celebratory budget calculation. [What's the Password?](games/whats-the-password.md) reports a small cash surplus, not a fully priced salary. [Shadowhand](games/shadowhand.md) distinguishes respectable sales from return on years of work. [The Ember Guardian](games/the-ember-guardian.md) supplies a creator-defined net amount whose intermediate deductions are not fully itemized.

**Research judgment:** keep at least three views of the money: cash spent and received; the economic cost including founder labor and reused work; and the cash remaining to support the next period. Do not subtract the same fee twice, assume a publisher's recoup order, or treat a planned advertising budget as confirmed expenditure. A gross-to-net waterfall is only reproducible when each deduction and period is known.

The useful launch question is not merely whether first-month receipts divided by all development months look attractive. It is also how much runway remains, what obligations are unpaid, and what evidence supports continued sales. Future income is possible; it is not already cash in the bank. [Basingstoke](games/basingstoke.md), [Lemma](games/lemma.md) and [Only Way is Down](games/only-way-is-down.md) illustrate different gaps between real sales and sufficient return.

For Open Legend's hosted offer, each renewing customer can also create continuing AI, storage, hosting and support obligations. A one-time premium game's revenue pattern cannot justify unlimited service use. Measure costly customers and outstanding prepaid obligations as well as averages. The existing business plan already distinguishes operating cash flow from founder compensation; the execution guide makes those measurements explicit.

**Counterpoint:** refusing to recognize any success until an audited lifetime ledger exists would discard useful evidence. Attribute the creator's assessment and preserve its definition. A small product can be worthwhile without becoming a famous hit, while a large audience can remain expensive to serve.

## 7. Hiring and publishing are separate decisions with different timing

The dossier set contains several concrete entry points, not one universal order:

| Evidence route | What the comparison helps decide |
|---|---|
| [PONCHO](games/poncho.md): applications through pixel-art communities | Whether a clearly defined visual need can be commissioned before asking a wider audience for money. |
| [Choo-Choo Charles](games/choo-choo-charles.md): a focused storefront-art commission | Whether a specialist should own one important promotional deliverable rather than an entire permanent role. |
| [Spilled!](games/spilled.md): technical-art help after fundraising | Whether funding purchases a specific production improvement instead of vaguely extending runway. |
| [Coregrounds](games/coregrounds.md): a contributor emerging from the community | Whether demonstrated interest and ability can lead to collaboration, with responsibilities made explicit. |
| [The Ember Guardian](games/the-ember-guardian.md): a publisher after early traction | Whether freeing founder development time is part of the partnership's value, not only promised reach. |
| [Eastshade](games/eastshade.md) versus [Maze Mice](games/maze-mice.md) | Why the scope and performance of outside PR matter more than the label “hired marketing.” |
| [Balatro](games/balatro.md) and [Valheim](games/valheim.md): early publisher contact | Why a huge existing community is not a universal prerequisite for a useful partnership. |
| [Where the Water Tastes Like Wine](games/where-the-water-tastes-like-wine.md): event relationships and late editorial help | Why production coordination and editorial ownership are distinct from promotional reach. |

**Research judgment:** recruit against a defined output or responsibility. A practical evaluation is a relevant portfolio, a bounded paid trial, reliable communication and a clear handoff. Agree on deliverables, revisions, ownership/licensing and ongoing support before depending on the work. These are proposed evaluation criteria, not legal conclusions about any historical contract.

A publisher may finance production, coordinate platforms, arrange distribution, provide marketing or perform some subset. An agency may distribute assets without owning positioning. Ask which party does what, how success will be evaluated and what work remains with the founder. A large contact list is not proof of those services. Compare references, rights, recoupment, cash timing and capacity as well as the headline split. The record cannot calculate which past games would have earned more without their publisher.

For Mike, the evidence does not support hiring a full team immediately, nor a rule to avoid collaborators until launch. The narrower proposal is to buy help when the bottleneck is clear, the output can be evaluated and the commitment is affordable under the project's actual budget. No provider named in an old dossier is thereby recommended as a current vendor.

## 8. Recoveries need an explanation of what changed

[Brigador](games/brigador.md) supports a genuine weak-period-to-improved-business comparison. [Among Us](games/among-us.md) provides an unusually large later breakout. [Descenders](games/descenders.md), by contrast, should not be described as a rescued failure merely because it grew further after a good launch. [Luck be a Landlord](games/luck-be-a-landlord.md) also separates a successful product from an unsuccessful promotional event.

[Sunset](games/sunset.md) adds an important qualification: later receipts, grants and crowdfunding allowed reported break-even and debt repayment by August 2015, while the creators still rejected their previous commercial model. That is financial repair without proof that the intended business became sustainable. Core Defense is a successful different product after Coregrounds, not restoration of the failed service. Echoes' small later sales increase is not a proven full recovery.

**Research judgment:** before extending a struggling product, name a testable reason the next period could differ: a demonstrated retention problem to fix, a better introduction, a relevant language audience, a new distribution partner or a validated new use. Define what evidence would justify more work and what would make the experiment stop. Avoid both “launch week determines everything” and “keep updating and success eventually arrives.”

Discounts, relaunches and new platforms can change units, money per sale and cost at the same time. A late sales spike is not automatically better profit. A fall in concurrency is not automatically commercial failure, especially for a finite single-player game. Missing later accounts should stay missing rather than being filled with a plausible comeback story. Respect obligations to existing customers even when the decision is not to expand further.

## 9. The market and platform are part of the comparison

[Regency Solitaire](games/regency-solitaire.md) and [Spooky Bonus](games/spooky-bonus.md) show why Steam alone may be an incomplete business lens. [Eastshade](games/eastshade.md) provides the opposite kind of platform disappointment. [Backpack Battles](games/backpack-battles.md) adds a regional-distribution comparison. These observations do not establish that the same historical channels or deal terms remain available today. Localization can involve interface, font, testing and support work, not simply translating a trailer.

[Minecraft](games/minecraft.md), [Adopt Me!](games/adopt-me.md) and [Welcome to Bloxburg](games/welcome-to-bloxburg.md) are useful examples of activities players can show one another. **Inference:** an experience that generates player-made stories or creations can provide distribution material beyond the developer's own production diary. This is a mechanism to investigate, not proof that adding construction or sharing features guarantees growth. [Escape Simulator](games/escape-simulator.md) supplies a related room-creation example; the value of creating and sharing is a product question before it is a marketing tactic.

[Roblox](games/roblox-platform.md) supplies an existing creation/distribution environment to its game developers. Independently operating a comparable environment would put a different set of costs and responsibilities on the studio. [Rec Room](games/rec-room-platform.md) is the explicit warning against treating a populated platform as proof of sustainable economics. A functioning export of some data is not necessarily a functioning substitute for a discontinued service.

For Open Legend, distinguish the audience for a first game from the audience for tools to create worlds. They may overlap, but their desired experiences, acquisition channels and support needs need not be identical. This is a research question, not a decision to ship two products or build a creator marketplace. Preserve the current narrow commercial test rather than inheriting a mass platform's scale and operating duties from its origin story.

## 10. Fund the deliverable, not the campaign headline

[Shovel Knight](games/shovel-knight.md) demonstrates that an apparently successful campaign and real sales can coexist with unpaid production time. [Haunts](games/haunts-the-manse-macabre.md), [Midora](games/midora.md), [Limit Theory](games/limit-theory.md) and [Unwritten](games/unwritten-that-which-happened.md) distinguish supporter demand from secured finishing capacity. [The Black Glove](games/the-black-glove.md) demonstrates that a well-publicized proposition can still fall short of the budget its creators believe necessary.

**Research judgment:** crowdfunding is an optional financing and community event, not a mandatory stage. A campaign should state what exists, what remains, who can deliver it, and what resources cover that work if only the minimum goal is reached. Include specialist labor, founder capacity, rewards, marketing, payment costs, testing, contingency and continuing obligations. An attractive goal is not a sound budget when it silently requires a second uncommitted funder.

The original Kickstarter guidance likewise asks whether the project can be delivered at exactly its goal. It uses all-or-nothing funding and tells creators to account for costs and fees. [K1] This is not evidence that every campaign must finance all historical sunk work; it means the remaining promises need an honest, secured financing plan.

**Counterpoint:** funding can buy important polish and specialists after a credible prototype, as in Spilled! and Hollow Knight. It is unnecessary to finish everything privately to avoid all risk. The distinction is between an honestly bounded unfinished product and an underfinanced promise. Stretch goals are new obligations, not free advertising. An editor, additional ports or lifelong hosted AI can create costs well beyond the pledge that attracts them.

Personal availability and a team's ability to continue also matter. Unwritten's circumstances should not be reduced to laziness or failed promotion. Secure critical responsibilities and a feasible reduced-scope fallback rather than assuming uninterrupted founder labor.

## 11. Economical scope uses existing strengths without making smallness a formula

[Bunny Park](games/bunny-park.md), [Mortal Glory](games/mortal-glory.md), [The First Tree](games/the-first-tree.md), [Spooky Bonus](games/spooky-bonus.md) and [A Short Hike](games/a-short-hike.md) have different forms of scope advantage: existing skills, reusable technology, purchased assets, a compact proposition or a concrete commissioning deadline. Their prior experience and support are part of the economics.

**Research judgment:** bound the next complete player experience and use the current project's strengths. Buy or reuse a well-defined contribution when it saves meaningful time without diluting the promise. Evaluate added work by the player value and evidence it buys, not the number of systems, geographic size or novelty of the technology. A short project is not cheap if its custom tooling, content or service obligations remain large.

**Counterexamples:** [Monkey Labour](games/monkey-labour.md) and [Rock Paper SHIFT](games/rock-paper-shift.md) show that compact production can still fail commercially. [Stardew Valley](games/stardew-valley.md), Factorio and RimWorld show that longer production can work. The recommendation is not “all games should take three months”; it is to control the exposure of the actual bet and learn before increasing it.

Founder sustainability is also a product constraint. The first-game learning years, family support, cheap living arrangements and unpaid time in success stories are not automatically available to Mike. Balatro's developer described severe pressure; expectations about ceaseless public work are not justified by its financial result. Backpack Battles' frequent-update workload should not become a compulsory solo cadence. The guide therefore uses adjustable experiment batches and explicit time ceilings, not a daily content treadmill.

## 12. Measure the next decision, and keep player value separate from manipulation

[Dynasty Feud](games/dynasty-feud.md), [Knife To Meet You](games/knife-to-meet-you.md), [Only Way is Down](games/only-way-is-down.md) and [I Am Overburdened](games/i-am-overburdened.md) show why invitations, keys, activations, videos, views and purchases cannot substitute for one another. [Neon Climber](games/neon-climber.md) shows the risk of delaying the relevant commercial test while improving other aspects. [Luck be a Landlord](games/luck-be-a-landlord.md) separates an event's financial result from its networking or feedback value.

**Research judgment:** give every material experiment a question, audience, version, cost/time cap, observable outcome and stopping decision. Where possible change one important thing; when changes overlap, preserve that limitation. A demo redesign, festival, creator wave and advertising campaign occurring together do not isolate advertising return. Absence of tracked conversions is not proof of no influence, but neither justifies claiming all untracked sales.

Distinguish customer cohorts, acquisition periods and stock versus additions. Measure a renewal only after the customer is eligible. Count a bundle purchase once. Review-driven unit estimates are not disclosed sales, downloads are not necessarily people, and a rising wishlist remainder is not promised future income. Record useful dated snapshots now rather than trying to reconstruct them years later.

Player satisfaction, trust and a sustainable desire to return are separate from maximizing compulsive activity. Do not adopt manipulative scarcity, false urgency, review pressure or obligations to log in merely because a popular game uses them. A community that enjoys sharing creations is a different objective from a community pressured to defend the product or buy repeatedly. This is a normative product recommendation, not a measured claim that every ethical choice maximizes short-term revenue.

## Current Steam documentation: do not turn anecdotes into rules

Rechecked **October 16, 2026**. Valve says there is **no minimum wishlist count** before Steam begins showing a game, and no formula that accurately predicts sales from wishlists. Notifications also depend on account/email conditions. Therefore the 20,000-wishlist goal in the supplied video is a team's planning target, not a universal platform guarantee. [V2]

Valve describes purchases and play as important visibility signals. Store-page traffic alone is not one; wishlists are mostly excluded from algorithmic visibility except areas such as Popular Upcoming. It also says review score is not an algorithmic visibility factor while a game remains Mixed or above, with a reduction in featuring below 40%. Thus an 82%-to-72% decline is not evidence of crossing an 80% algorithmic cutoff. Buyers may still react to reviews, which is a different mechanism. [V1]

The distinction is causal, not advice to ignore wishlists, reviews or presentation. Historical claims that a creator “triggered the algorithm” describe an observed sequence with an interpretation attached, not a verified account of every ranking input. Store documentation can change; recheck the relevant official rules when planning an actual release. The [execution guide](execution-playbook.md) separately records testing, festival, release, measurement and generated-content requirements.

## A conditional sequence to test for Open Legend

The [execution guide](execution-playbook.md) is the owner of concrete work products, gates, role assignments and stop/rework decisions. This overview preserves the original sequence, now reconciled with the hosted-world business plan. It is a proposed decision sequence, not an accepted release calendar.

| Stage | What to establish before increasing commitment |
|---|---|
| Define the first promise | A prospective player can explain what they would do and why that interests them. Keep the engine's future possibilities separate from the available experience. |
| Test a small playable | Unfamiliar players can begin without constant explanation and show the intended enjoyment. Separate bugs, comprehension problems and a weak underlying activity. |
| Start focused public communication | There is an honest visual/playable artifact, one clear next action and enough capacity to respond. Choose channels according to the audience being sought. |
| Broaden sampling | The demo represents the intended paid experience; creator outreach targets people whose audiences plausibly want that experience. Record results, not just sends. |
| Choose financing and specialist help | Remaining work, desired founder runway and the exact service being purchased are clear. A campaign target covers its promises; a publishing deal has defined responsibilities. |
| Prepare the actual commercial offer | Exercise first experience, later progression, persistence, costs and support. A small hosted pilot is the working track; a Steam release is conditional rather than assumed. |
| Evaluate the next period | Separate payment, refunds, returning play, eligible renewals, support costs and money retained. Continue investment for an evidenced reason rather than a hoped-for viral rescue. |

A publishing opportunity or tightly bounded commission could move financing earlier. A small finite game might not need a large ongoing Discord at all. The sequence is meant to expose dependencies, not force every project through identical activities. Public learning can run alongside production rather than waiting until every gate has finished.

### What to record from the beginning

| Question | More useful record | Misleading substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Did the message reach plausible buyers? | Source, date, artifact, audience fit and meaningful next actions. | Total views without knowing why people watched. |
| Did they enjoy the intended experience? | Observed first sessions, completion/return appropriate to the game, and reasons for stopping. | Installation, launch or membership counts alone. |
| Did interest become payment? | Cohort-specific wishlist/demo purchases where available, direct sales, eligible renewals and refunds kept separate. | All release sales divided by the prelaunch wishlist total. |
| Did marketing help enough? | Attributable outcomes where possible, costs, founder hours saved and the activity's original objective. | Assuming an event caused every sale occurring afterward. |
| Can the work continue? | Actual cash, unpaid obligations, operating costs, costly-user cases and founder runway. | Gross receipts, valuation, or costs defined to omit an inconvenient expense. |

Use aggregated, proportionate measurement rather than collecting unnecessary personal information. Record dated snapshots and changes now: years later, the absence of those records is exactly what prevents a reliable reconstruction in many dossiers. The guide's dashboard supplies operational definitions without requiring a large analytics system before the first test.

## Open evidence questions, not hidden conclusions

The collection does not establish an optimal YouTube cadence, a universal Discord size, guaranteed wishlist conversion, the counterfactual value of a publisher, or an industry probability of success. Many contractors' discovery routes and terms remain private. Some financial windows are only weeks long, while others span years. Those gaps limit comparison; they do not justify substituting present-day follower counts or review-based sales estimates.

Rereading every dossier is not the same as independently reopening every original external source or verifying all later lifetime results. The [review coverage](review-coverage.md) identifies the exact corpus and targeted source refreshes. Old cohorts remain historical classifications, not 2026 profitability claims.

The most defensible working principle remains **increase commitment as the relevant evidence improves**. Do not demand perfect information before showing a small useful experience, and do not let a large number at one stage stand in for proof at every later stage.
