## The recommendation

**Control risk around an ambitious player experience; do not reduce the experience until only a low-risk business experiment remains.** Make a compact, genuinely enjoyable Open Legend adventure in which useful invention and independent characters produce consequences the player can encounter again. Develop the game, its audience and its business together, while asking each for different evidence.

The [playable-game priority](../../../docs/repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md) owns the intended survival-adventure: worthwhile exploration, opposition, rewards, progression, useful crafting and building, with a real instance of practical invention and independent character consequence in the opening experience. Hosting is a delivery arrangement, not a replacement genre. A general observer simulation, an engine demonstration and a generic survival game that postpones its distinguishing features do not satisfy that product direction.

The positive question is **why someone would choose their next gaming session here instead of in a game they already enjoy**. Test three things: an understandable attraction, rewarding actual decisions and outcomes, and enough variation or consequence to make another session worthwhile. Art and sound help communicate that attraction, not only make controls readable. Scope should permit a small amount of unusually good play, not merely a small amount of acceptable play.

The [business plan](../../06-marketing/business-plan.md) retains managed private worlds as a concrete offer to test. It is not an outcome established by this mostly premium-game research set. Compare it with the strongest feasible alternative for the same intended experience before committing to substantial packaging or billing work. An AI bill constrains delivery; it does not establish that customers want a subscription. Actual terms and a viable alternative remain decisions to investigate, not offerings already selected or implemented.

## The central distinction

Separate **understanding and desire**, **enjoyment**, **payment**, and **sustainable delivery**. A project can answer one positively and another negatively. [Rec Room](games/rec-room-platform.md), [Maze Mice](games/maze-mice.md), [Regency Solitaire](games/regency-solitaire.md) and [Brigador](games/brigador.md) expose different combinations of audience, selected-cost recovery, storefront performance and later business improvement. None is adequately described by one permanent success/failure label.

The collection is a broad, reviewed evidence base, not all possible learnings or a representative industry sample. [Evidence weighting](review-coverage.md#how-to-weight-the-evidence) separates observed records, participant explanations and our inferences, and compares cases by product, model, resources, era and ongoing service burden. Repeated observations suggest experiments; they do not establish causal success rates. The strongest conclusions concern distinctions and dependencies. Universal posting schedules, audience thresholds, publisher uplift and guaranteed recoveries remain unsupported.

## 1. Publicity timing depends on what is ready to show

A technical diary, a visual reveal, an unassisted playable session and a request for money create different expectations. [Minecraft](games/minecraft.md) supplies an early usable experiment; [Eastshade](games/eastshade.md) separates early writing, coherent private play and a later trailer. [Spilled!](games/spilled.md) had a public demo before crowdfunding and financed further visual improvement afterward. [The Ember Guardian](games/the-ember-guardian.md) distinguishes a visual reveal from playable discovery.

**Principle:** learn from people early and promote broadly when an honest artifact demonstrates why they should care. The whole game need not be finished, but the feature central to the demonstrated promise must exist. Label concept work and avoid presenting final polish as a prerequisite that necessarily existed before funding.

[FTL](games/ftl-faster-than-light.md), [Hollow Knight](games/hollow-knight.md) and [Undertale](games/undertale.md) support studying credible prototype/demo preparation. [PONCHO](games/poncho.md) shows that playability does not guarantee crowdfunding success; [Limit Theory](games/limit-theory.md) shows that funding can precede backer playability without securing finished delivery.

Creative exploration still needs room. A bounded period spent discovering a distinctive interaction is legitimate work, not a failure to validate immediately. [Balatro](games/balatro.md) began as a hobby experiment before a public commercial process. Player evidence should diagnose whether our idea works, not replace creative direction with a feature vote. Define the question and affordable exposure of an experiment without requiring external approval for every creative choice.

## 2. The visible starting audience often has a history

A first commercial release is not necessarily a first public creation. [Lethal Company](games/lethal-company.md), [Choo-Choo Charles](games/choo-choo-charles.md), [A Short Hike](games/a-short-hike.md) and [BattleBit Remastered](games/battlebit-remastered.md) bring earlier projects, shared work, professional relationships or mod communities. The TrampolineTales cases preserve catalog continuity. Conversely, the dedicated game account in [Among Us](games/among-us.md) followed the breakout; a large later account was not necessarily a prelaunch prerequisite.

**Principle:** distinguish our own followers, other creators' audiences, prior customers, platform discovery and players inviting others. A developer channel can provide motivation, credibility and testers without being the largest buyer source. Do not make becoming a successful YouTuber a prerequisite for building Open Legend.

[Only Way is Down](games/only-way-is-down.md) and [Knife To Meet You](games/knife-to-meet-you.md) show substantial watchable reach with limited associated purchase activity. [Mortal Glory](games/mortal-glory.md) separates genre-matched creator contact from posts mainly reaching developers. [Luck be a Landlord](games/luck-be-a-landlord.md) documents continued creator relationships around meaningful updates.

Use a sustainable communication routine and deliberate larger moments: reveal, representative demo, relevant event, creator access and release. A post should have a job and a reachable next action. Once multiple relevant audiences respond well, increase outreach intentionally rather than staying indefinitely in tiny tests. Small launches that later grew, such as [SNKRX](games/snkrx.md) and [shapez](games/shapez.md), rebut fatalistic wishlist rules; they do not justify neglecting preparation.

## 3. A demo should test the paid promise, not merely generate a flattering metric

The Ember Guardian account raises a demo-versus-campaign concern. Only Way is Down raises the distinction between cute visual appeal and punishing play. [Crumbling World](games/crumbling-world.md) initially offered an alpha without the defining crumbling mechanic. A popular sample can therefore test a different promise from the eventual paid game.

**Principle:** the attraction shown in marketing, the activity in the sample and the value of the paid experience should agree. [Backpack Battles](games/backpack-battles.md) and [Pawnbarian](games/pawnbarian.md) demonstrate substantial free play before payment; Balatro changed its sampling design. These do not establish an ideal demo length or that every genre benefits from unlimited access.

Keep four tests distinct: observed usability, voluntary repeated enjoyment, broader audience response, and payment under realistic terms. A small newcomer group can expose serious confusion without estimating market size. Specialist testers, as in [Slay the Spire](games/slay-the-spire.md), can assess depth without representing newcomers. Friends can provide valuable evidence; distinguish supportive words from freely chosen behavior. Asking to play again, trying another approach or seeking the next build is encouraging, not proof of a business.

Observe before explaining. Record expectations, hesitation, choices, payoff and stopping reasons. Long sessions caused by confusion, crashes or grinding are not enjoyment. [Where the Water Tastes Like Wine](games/where-the-water-tastes-like-wine.md) warns against postponing complete-experience testing. A satisfying finite game need not generate daily return; the appropriate test depends on the promise.

## 4. A useful community has an activity, not just a membership count

[Descenders](games/descenders.md) gave prerelease participants beta access and recurring activities. [Coregrounds](games/coregrounds.md) produced deeply committed fans and a collaborator yet lacked a sustainable multiplayer population. [Dynasty Feud](games/dynasty-feud.md) and [The Flock](games/the-flock.md) separate registration and short-event enthusiasm from actual repeated play.

**Principle:** open a community space when there is a purpose and capacity to support it: scheduled tests, discoveries, creations, challenges or help. Keep destinations manageable and provide an opt-in contact route for people who do not want chat. Track active participation and return rather than total members.

For synchronous multiplayer, compatible players must arrive together. BattleBit used scheduled tests; Backpack Battles' asynchronous opposition avoids that same scheduling dependency. An independently enjoyable first Open Legend experience need not wait for a mass simultaneous audience. This is a first-experience recommendation, not removal of the shared-world vision.

Community contributions require consent, clear responsibility, credit and maintenance expectations. Factorio's guide collaboration, Coregrounds' programmer recruitment and Core Defense's translation coordination illustrate different arrangements. A fan is not automatically a perpetual unpaid employee. Trusted community tests remain valuable but cannot qualify every unfamiliar player, machine or later-game state.

## 5. A successful launch can create a delivery problem immediately

Ember Guardian's recording change and patch regressions, [Eastshade](games/eastshade.md)'s operational problems, [Valheim](games/valheim.md)'s support/roadmap pressure and BattleBit's later communication gap show that acquisition and reliable continuing delivery are separate constraints. These accounts do not isolate a precise sales loss caused by reviews or bugs.

**Principle:** qualify the build and conditions people will actually receive, and scale exposure with the ability to support them. Protect release configuration from recording-only changes; exercise progression, demanding situations and recovery, not just the introduction. Divide communication and repair responsibilities where possible. When staffing cannot expand, limit initial admissions instead of assuming a solo developer can perform every launch role simultaneously.

For Open Legend, assess the defining experience and its cost together. Does waiting interrupt decisions? Does an invention become useful behavior rather than a generated name? Do characters respond consistently to information they actually perceived? Does ordinary play remain enjoyable and honest when optional generation is unavailable or spending is exhausted? These are qualification questions, not newly asserted implementation results or universal numeric latency targets.

A software rollback is not necessarily a safe reversal of customer data changes. Release policy must handle that deliberately. The [current development compatibility rule](../../../AGENTS.md#development-save-policy) remains authoritative; planning for real customers does not authorize legacy support or claim that readiness has been verified.

## 6. Revenue, cost recovery and a sustainable livelihood are different claims

Maze Mice's reported budget recovery excludes disclosed PR expenditure and founder labor. [What's the Password?](games/whats-the-password.md) reports a small cash surplus, not a fully paid salary. [Shadowhand](games/shadowhand.md) distinguishes respectable sales from labor return. Ember Guardian supplies creator-defined net receipts with an incomplete public deduction breakdown. [Basingstoke](games/basingstoke.md), [Lemma](games/lemma.md) and Only Way is Down expose further gaps between sales and sufficient income.

**Principle:** maintain three views: actual cash and obligations; economic effort including founder labor and reused work; and resources available to continue. Preserve reporting periods, taxes/fees already deducted, recoupment order and planned versus actual spending. Future sales are possible, not present cash. Grants, patronage, bundles and subscriptions need their own accounting boundaries.

Define the intended business outcome: operating-cost coverage, manageable side income, founder livelihood or studio growth require different evidence. The business plan's ten-renewing-customer target is a proposed early milestone for a hosted offer, not the destination or proof of a large market. Its illustrative ten-customer calculation leaves only $140 per month before founder compensation and other excluded expenses. The inputs are not measurements.

For hosted worlds, ordinary and costly usage, support, stored history and prepaid obligations matter. Popularity can increase unfunded costs. Test both whether we can afford the service and whether customers prefer that offer. Recognize attributed modest successes without demanding an unavailable audit, but do not turn selected-cost recovery into full profitability.

## 7. Hiring and publishing are separate decisions with different timing

| Evidence route | Decision it informs |
|---|---|
| [PONCHO](games/poncho.md), applications through pixel-art communities | A defined visual need can be commissioned before crowdfunding. |
| [Choo-Choo Charles](games/choo-choo-charles.md), focused storefront art | A specialist can own a single important deliverable rather than a permanent department. |
| [Spilled!](games/spilled.md), funded technical-art work | Funding can purchase a specific production improvement after a credible sample. |
| [Coregrounds](games/coregrounds.md), community contributor | Demonstrated interest can lead to collaboration, with explicit responsibilities. |
| [Balatro](games/balatro.md) and [Valheim](games/valheim.md), early publisher contact | Large existing audiences are not a universal prerequisite for partnership. |
| [The Ember Guardian](games/the-ember-guardian.md), later publisher agreement | Founder development time saved can be part of commercial value. |
| [Eastshade](games/eastshade.md), [Maze Mice](games/maze-mice.md), [Dynasty Feud](games/dynasty-feud.md) | Strategy, outreach distribution and execution are different agency services. |
| [Where the Water Tastes Like Wine](games/where-the-water-tastes-like-wine.md) | Editorial/production coordination is distinct from promotional reach. |

**Principle:** hire against an observable bottleneck and assess a relevant portfolio, references, bounded paid trial, communication and handoff. Specify outputs, acceptance, revisions, rights/licensing, credit, payment and continuing support. Appropriate professional review is needed for consequential contracts; these are evaluation criteria, not legal conclusions.

Compare self-publishing, contractors, agencies and publishers on actual responsibilities, cash timing, recoupment, rights and capacity, not only the headline revenue split. No marketplace or agency named in an old case is a current endorsement. Solo-led production can use specialist help; self-publishing does not mean no marketer. Obtain help neither reflexively at the start nor automatically only after release.

## 8. Recoveries need an explanation of what changed

[Brigador](games/brigador.md) documents improved onboarding, relaunch/localization and later capacity to fund another project, without large reserves. Among Us is a much larger later discovery outlier. Descenders was already successful before further growth; Core Defense is a new product after Coregrounds, not restoration of that service. [Echoes](games/echoes-nova-box.md)' small later increase is not proven full recovery.

[Sunset](games/sunset.md) achieved reported debt repayment and subsidy-inclusive break-even while its creators still rejected the intended commercial model. Luck be a Landlord's unprofitable convention was an activity-level result, not failure of the game.

**Principle:** continue investment for a testable reason the next period can differ, with a cost/time limit and a decision afterward. Possible reasons include a diagnosed introduction or repeat-play problem, demonstrated audience mismatch, relevant localization or distribution opportunity. Neither launch-week fatalism nor indefinite updates awaiting virality is justified.

Discounts and ports change units, price and costs together. Declining concurrency does not establish a finite single-player game's commercial failure. Separate customer obligations from optional expansion, and preserve both early and later evidence rather than selecting the more dramatic headline.

## 9. The market and platform are part of the comparison

Regency Solitaire and [Spooky Bonus](games/spooky-bonus.md) succeeded substantially outside Steam; Eastshade's strong PC business did not ensure strong console sales. Backpack Battles' regional demand and bundles, and Brigador's localization, suggest specific distribution questions—not a guarantee that the same channels, deal terms or language priorities apply now.

**Principle:** distinguish the game experience, the purchase offer and the delivery platform. Compare plausible player demand, friction, service obligations, discoverability and costs for the actual product. Subscription preference cannot be inferred from premium-game sales. A Steam page, a browser trial and a managed private world are not automatically interchangeable offerings.

[Minecraft](games/minecraft.md), [Adopt Me!](games/adopt-me.md), [Welcome to Bloxburg](games/welcome-to-bloxburg.md) and [Escape Simulator](games/escape-simulator.md) give players creations and experiences to show others. **Inference:** player-made stories could become meaningful distribution for Open Legend beyond developer diaries. First establish something worth sharing; sharing tools alone do not supply that value.

[Roblox](games/roblox-platform.md) supplies services and a market to its creators. Independently operating a comparable platform entails different responsibilities. Rec Room demonstrates audience without sustainable company economics, not a verdict on Open Legend's business model. Mature staffing and later billions of views must not be backdated to a small founding team. Preserve the open/self-hosted direction without making a creator marketplace or mass platform a prerequisite for the first game.

## 10. Fund the deliverable, not the campaign headline

[Shovel Knight](games/shovel-knight.md) combined successful crowdfunding and sales with unpaid production time. [Haunts](games/haunts-the-manse-macabre.md), [Midora](games/midora.md), Limit Theory and [Unwritten](games/unwritten-that-which-happened.md) separate supporter enthusiasm from finishing capacity. [The Black Glove](games/the-black-glove.md) shows professional credits and visibility still falling short of required funds.

**Principle:** crowdfunding is optional. The minimum funded plan must cover its remaining promises using secured resources, including critical labor, testing, rewards, fees and contingency. Kickstarter's guidance explicitly tests deliverability at the goal and explains all-or-nothing funding. [K1] Prior sunk work need not all be reimbursed by the campaign, but a hypothetical later publisher cannot silently cover missing essential work.

Spilled! and Hollow Knight show that funding can buy polish and expertise after a credible prototype. Ports, an editor, more content or lifelong hosted AI are costed obligations, not free stretch-goal promotion. Haunts and Unwritten also warn that specialist availability and personal circumstances matter; their outcomes cannot fairly be reduced to laziness or bad marketing. Secure responsibilities and a feasible fallback rather than assuming unlimited uninterrupted founder effort.

## 11. Economical scope uses existing strengths without making smallness a formula

[Bunny Park](games/bunny-park.md), Mortal Glory, [The First Tree](games/the-first-tree.md), Spooky Bonus and A Short Hike benefited from different combinations of skills, reusable technology, bought assets, a compact proposition and concrete deadlines. Short measured production excludes some earlier learning and work.

**Principle:** narrow the amount of production while preserving the reason to play. Select a connected experience and compare the value of adding to it against the strongest alternative use of effort. Art can express identity and desirability early; efficient production is not a mandate for an indistinct presentation. Elaborate infrastructure or service obligations can make a visually small game expensive.

[Monkey Labour](games/monkey-labour.md) and [Rock Paper SHIFT](games/rock-paper-shift.md) show that compact games can fail commercially. [Stardew Valley](games/stardew-valley.md), [Factorio](games/factorio.md) and [RimWorld](games/rimworld.md) show longer paths that worked. There is no universal three-month rule. Bound the actual bet, allow focused creative exploration, and increase commitment when the experience earns it.

Founder time, support from others and living costs are real constraints, not hidden free resources. Backpack Battles' demanding update cadence need not become a solo obligation. Maintain enough capacity to think, make, communicate and support; small experiments should not become permanent avoidance of a larger opportunity when evidence is strong.

## 12. Measure the next decision, and keep player value separate from manipulation

Dynasty Feud, Knife To Meet You, Only Way is Down and [I Am Overburdened](games/i-am-overburdened.md) distinguish invitations, activations, videos, viewers and purchases. [Neon Climber](games/neon-climber.md) shows the risk of postponing the relevant commercial test. Luck be a Landlord distinguishes event sales from networking and feedback value.

**Principle:** record a question, competing explanation, audience, build, intervention, cost/time limit, observation period, denominator and decision. No universal threshold does not mean no targets: set project-specific downside, expected and stronger scenarios, showing their assumptions. Small tests diagnose and select the next experiment; broader repeatable results justify larger spending or production.

Imperfect attribution is useful when its limits are visible. Combine tagged links, timing, player responses and repeated experiments. Steam UTM results cover eligible tracked visits and a limited conversion window, not every influenced purchase. [V8] When a demo redesign, festival, ads and creator wave overlap, report the combined event rather than claiming one caused all the improvement. Lack of tracked conversion proves neither no effect nor entitlement to claim every untracked sale.

Keep stocks versus additions, unique people versus repeated activity, eligible renewal cohorts and bundle purchases distinct. Dividing total sales by prelaunch wishlists is not wishlist conversion. Review-derived sales estimates are not disclosed units. Collect proportionate aggregate data; private conversations are not required for commercial measurement.

Player value and a sustainable desire to return are the objective, not compulsive activity. Avoid false urgency, coercive scarcity, review pressure or mandatory daily habits as substitutes for enjoyment. This is a product-value recommendation, not a claim that every ethical choice maximizes immediate revenue.

## Current Steam documentation: do not turn anecdotes into rules

Checked **October 17, 2026**. Valve says there is no minimum wishlist count before a game can be shown, and no formula that accurately predicts sales from wishlists. It nevertheless recommends a Coming Soon page when ready, continued engagement and reviewing promotion results. [V2] Set useful project targets without presenting them as guaranteed featuring thresholds.

Valve describes purchases and play as visibility signals, says store traffic alone is not one, largely excludes wishlists except placements such as Popular Upcoming, and distinguishes review scores below 40% from Mixed or above. An 82%-to-72% change is not proof of an 80% algorithmic cutoff; buyers can still respond to reviews. [V1]

Check distribution feasibility while comparing offers. Valve requires disclosure of player-consumed generated content and safeguards for live generation; its external-service FAQ requires managing access on players' behalf and using Steam-supported payment methods. These are relevant to packaging, not just final paperwork. Survey completion does not establish product acceptance. [V7] The execution guide owns the remaining platform checklist.

## A conditional sequence to test for Open Legend

Work in parallel on **game**, **audience** and **business**, as described in the [execution guide](execution-playbook.md). The [one-page brief](execution-brief.md) keeps the current hypothesis, evidence, uncertainty, next experiment and commitment limit visible.

Build one unmistakably Open Legend adventure; observe usability and voluntary play; improve and demonstrate it; test broader response; compare realistic offers and their delivery constraints; qualify the selected release or pilot; then review money, satisfaction, repeat value and support. Public learning and commercial feasibility work start alongside production. Major commitments depend on evidence, not on completion of a universal serial checklist.

No gameplay test, price adoption, contractor engagement or commercial release is asserted by this research. Existing gameplay, licensing, privacy, spending and verification owners remain authoritative. The dossier map records a complete read of the corpus, not independently refreshed lifetime accounts. Maintain it when evidence changes a consequential conclusion; add research to answer a specific decision, not merely increase the case count.
