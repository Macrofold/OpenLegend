# Echoes (Nova-box, 2024 remaster) — launch and commercial dossier

[Collection index](../README.md) · **Read and written: 2026-10-15** · **Cohort: unsuccessful commercial launch, with modest later improvement**

## Identity, origin and evidence quality

This dossier concerns **Nova-box's Echoes**, Steam app 1775220, not another game sharing the title. Steam identifies Nova-box as both developer and publisher and records **May 29, 2024** as release. The game is a 2D investigative narrative about Ricky Fox and Greenhearth, with English and French support. [S2]

The commercial account comes from **Chanel**, posting as `leftypower04in` and identifying as a marketing/communication staff member who joined Nova-box in September 2025. It describes a remaster of the studio's 2012 mobile title and **fewer than 300 sales** by the retrospective. This is an attributed staff report, not an audited statement or an interview conducted for this research. Exact contemporaneous headcount is not independently established; the source calls Nova-box a small studio. [S1]

## Commercial measurements and interventions

The staff member reports an increase from roughly **60 to 180 daily store visits**, **300 to 1,300 daily impressions**, **5 to 25 monthly sales**, and **10 to 80 monthly wishlists** after revised capsules, copy/tags, localization of store text and social activity. These are small absolute volumes. The before/after comparison does not isolate the effect of each change or control for other events. [S1]

The same account describes the original release as a shadowdrop without marketing or Next Fest participation. That is stronger evidence of a compressed prelaunch window than merely observing a small current review count. [S1]

## Funding, publishing and hiring

Kickstarter financing is not established. Campaign goals, dates and pledges are consequently not available for this case. The official release is self-published. The staff account shows marketing/communications work being added well after the paid debut; employment terms, budget and the earlier allocation of marketing duties are undisclosed. [S1], [S2]

No reliable artist-recruitment history, contractor marketplace, compensation schedule or remaster production budget was recovered. The game page can establish product presentation, not how those assets were commissioned.

## Case-specific observations

**Positive interpretation:** reporting visits, impressions, wishlists and purchases separately makes the small improvement intelligible. A fivefold sales increase from five monthly copies remains a very different business result from a fivefold increase from thousands.

**Negative interpretation:** releasing a remaster does not automatically reactivate the audience for earlier products. A storefront created shortly before shipping also provides little time to learn whether strangers understand the proposition.

The staff post includes categorical advice about shadowdrops and trailer length. This dossier does **not** adopt those as universal rules. A single before/after case with tiny volumes cannot establish an optimal video duration or prove one launch pattern always fails.

## Remaining gaps

Precise post publication day was not exposed by the retrieved Reddit rendering; the text anchors the measured period to September–October 2025. Staff identity and internal metrics were not independently audited. Exact team size, launch budget, net revenue, historical social series and full later recovery remain unknown. The current Steam review count is intentionally not converted into estimated unit sales.
