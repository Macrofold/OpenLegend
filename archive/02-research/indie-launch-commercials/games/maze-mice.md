# Maze Mice — launch and commercial dossier

[Collection index](../README.md) · **Read and written: 2026-10-16** · **Outcome: weaker Early Access expectations followed by reported production-budget recovery; full-cost profitability not established**

## Origin and inherited audience

TrampolineTales is Dan DiIorio's solo-development business. Maze Mice followed Luck be a Landlord; the store explicitly markets that connection and the returning composer. It is a maze-based survival game in which time advances with movement. **This is not a first release starting from zero professional relationships or customers.** [S2, S3]

Steam records **May 2, 2025 Early Access** and **July 11, 2025 full release**. A demo is offered, but its exact original publication date and the game's pre-announcement production duration were not recovered. [S2]

## The money claim, kept within its actual definition

On **July 15, 2025**, DiIorio reported recovering a rounded **US$23,000** production budget after passing approximately **6,000 copies**. Its categories were art US$12,000, soundtrack US$2,000, effects US$1,500, localization US$5,000 and key art US$2,500. **He expressly excluded US$15,000 spent on an unsatisfactory PR company and his own programming time.** [S1]

Including that disclosed PR spend gives **US$38,000 of identified external costs**, before founder labor. That arithmetic is this dossier's calculation, not the creator's budget definition. Consequently the headline cannot establish full-cost break-even. It also cannot establish that the game never recovered those costs later.

## Marketing and release sequence

The creator says he ended the agency relationship, resumed direct press/influencer email, benefited from LocalThunk's endorsement, and used a **30% full-release discount** to build momentum. He reports New & Trending placement after previously worrying about the Early Access trajectory. No channel-by-channel sales attribution or counterfactual is provided. [S1]

His separately documented outreach process uses a maintained creator list, individual keys, a concise visual description and update-based follow-ups. It treats recurring coverage as a relationship, not a one-time mass announcement. That is a studio practice, **not proof that every listed step or every past contact was used for Maze Mice**. [S3]

## Reception, later sales and case-specific observations

The accessed store shows favorable player reception, but its contemporary review count is not a July 2025 snapshot or a profit ledger. The current bundle with other TrampolineTales games confirms catalog cross-promotion; it does not quantify transferred customers. [S2]

**Interpretation:** this case is a particularly useful correction to the binary “success/failure” label. Production costs recovered under the author's definition, a disappointing marketing purchase, unpaid founder time and a valuable customer base can coexist.

The documentary question for a future project is not whether to trust an upbeat title, but **which costs are included, which sales period is measured and what remains unpaid**. One unhappy agency engagement does not establish that agencies in general underperform founder-led outreach; the source lacks a contract and matched comparison. Later lifetime sales and full-cost recoupment remain unknown.
