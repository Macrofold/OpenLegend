# Knife To Meet You — launch and commercial dossier

[Collection index](../README.md) · **Read and written: 2026-10-15** · **Cohort: unsuccessful 2021 Early Access launch and influencer-conversion disappointment; later full release**

## Origin, team and sequence

Mate Magyar describes a **two-person team**, nine months of work before the 2021 account, and an Early Access debut that sold **nine copies in its first promotional week**. Several small creators played it without a noticeable commercial change. This is not a no-publicity case or an abandoned concept. [S1]

Steam confirms **July 21, 2021 Early Access** and **March 13, 2024 full release**, with Mate Magyar listed as developer and publisher. The physics-based throwing game includes trick shots, scenarios and community features in its later store description; that complete current feature list is not assumed to have existed in the first build. [S2]

## The quantified creator event

In late August 2021, Paluten covered the game. Magyar's retrospective reports the following sequence:

| Measure | Reported amount |
|---|---|
| Creator subscribers | About 4 million |
| Video views / likes | About 400,000 / 20,000 |
| Demo installations | About 500 |
| Copies sold during the associated event | 15 |
| Android installations | Increased from about 200 to 800; active users subsequently fell |

The developer says the attention spike faded after roughly three days. These are associated observations, **not an instrumented causal funnel** proving each purchaser watched the video. Nor are 15 copies the game's lifetime sales. [S1]

## Financing, specialists and marketing assistance

No Kickstarter or publisher-financing route is established. Campaign dates, goal and pledges are not applicable to the documented release. The founder ended the retrospective by asking for referrals to marketing experts and trailer makers; that is a **request for help, not evidence of a completed hire**. [S1]

The other team member's detailed role, art sourcing, compensation, contractor terms and total production budget remain unverified. Self-publishing and a two-person core should not be interpreted as evidence of zero purchased assets or services.

## Reception and bounded observations

The discussion includes substantial criticism of presentation and pricing. Those comments describe some readers' impressions, not a representative customer survey. In particular, commenters used different currencies and speculative counterfactual sales totals; the dossier does not adopt their asserted optimal price or promised results. [S1]

**Interpretation:** the case challenges a specific belief—large reach necessarily rescues a weak launch. It does not show that influencer coverage is useless. Audience fit, demo experience, positioning and willingness to pay remain separate questions, and the disclosed metrics cannot isolate them.

The later 2024 completion is important context. No verified full-release sales or lifetime profitability statement was recovered, so the negative classification remains confined to the documented 2021 period.
