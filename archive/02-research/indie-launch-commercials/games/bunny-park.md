# Bunny Park — launch and commercial dossier

[Collection index](../README.md) · **Read and written: 2026-10-15** · **Cohort: modest commercial success; solo creator with purchased music**

## Origin and what the headline leaves out

Éloïse / `elliebeanzz` describes Bunny Park as her **second Steam game**, made in **six weeks**, not her first exposure to game development. The post title rounds this to a month; the body supplies the more precise duration. She reports approximately **US$30,000 revenue after a year**, a US$5 product and roughly five hours to complete its principal goals. She made the visual assets herself and bought the soundtrack from an asset store. [S1]

This is a small-product commercial success according to its creator, not a verified US$30,000 profit. Prior skill, previous customers and the time spent learning tools are not included in the six-week figure.

## Development, community and release sequence

The creator **streamed development on Twitch** and frequently shared GIFs on Twitter during production. She also describes keeping successive games within a similar cute-simulation niche so an existing audience could remain interested. The account therefore supplies both concurrent publicity and a prior-product audience, rather than evidence of starting from zero followers. It also discloses unusually long workweeks, so the short calendar schedule should not be treated as a normal-workweek labor estimate. [S1]

Steam independently confirms **January 14, 2020**. The current listing names **Cozy Bee Games** as both developer and publisher. Its presentation foregrounds decorating a park, caring for rabbits and completing a collection; the commercial proposition is visibly compact. Current branding and bundle membership do not establish the exact original company branding or every launch promotion. [S2]

## Funding, publishing and specialist work

No Kickstarter campaign is documented. Campaign dates, backer count, goal and pledges are not applicable to the evidenced route. The retrospective does not identify a funding publisher, hired launch PR agency or separate marketing employee. It does identify a purchased-music route instead of a custom soundtrack commission. [S1]

The account is useful for deciding which production work can be omitted or purchased, but it does not disclose music license terms, the particular marketplace, asset cost or whether a composer relationship from another game affected this project.

## Case-specific observations

**Interpretation:** this case is useful precisely because a commercially satisfactory project did not require years of additional content production. It is not evidence that a beginner can reproduce the same result in six weeks: the creator's production ability and audience relationship predate the measured project.

The unresolved comparison is revenue per **total career effort**, not just per week in this game's short production window. The source does not supply that denominator. It also does not isolate Twitch, Twitter, prior buyers or Steam discovery as the cause of the revenue.

No lifetime profitability claim, console-launch reconstruction or present-day follower estimate is made here. Those would require additional evidence rather than extrapolation from the 2021 retrospective.
