# Escape Simulator — launch and commercial dossier

[Collection index](../README.md) · **Read and written: 2026-10-15** · **Cohort: substantial commercial success; small project team inside a larger studio**

## Team boundary, origin and result

Pine Studio's project lead Tom reports a **five-to-six-person core**, with other contributors assisting in art, audio and testing. The company had **14 people** by the retrospective and nine years of prior experience. This is not a first-time solo production or a studio with fewer than ten total employees. The team initially explored a mobile augmented-reality game before changing direction. [S1]

A contemporaneous first-week post reports **more than 50,000 players**, with nearly **60,000 wishlists before launch**. The separate December 2021 postmortem reports **over US$4 million gross sales in under two months**. The much higher total install figure in the first post includes alleged pirated copies and is not a paid-unit count or a measured quantity of lost sales. [S1], [S2]

## Marketing, community and collaborations

The team used a professional PR firm and trailer production; marketing represented **6% of the reported development budget**. Tom describes contacting developers of related games for cross-promotion and tailoring material to creators. These are concrete business-development activities, not a claim that a large founder YouTube channel was necessary. [S1]

The first-week discussion includes players inviting friends and highlighting the room editor as a purchase attraction. Those are individual reception anecdotes, not a representative survey. The official Steam page supports both cooperative play and sharing user-created rooms, giving the community an activity beyond following announcements. [S2], [S3]

## Funding and specialist recruitment

The evidenced release is self-published, with purchased commercial services and a larger studio behind the small core. No Kickstarter campaign is established; goal, dates and pledges are therefore not applicable to the documented route. The 6% ratio should not be converted into a cash budget without a disclosed denominator. [S1], [S3]

No verified agency fee, artist recruitment marketplace, employment agreement or contractor sourcing chronology was recovered. The presence of experienced coworkers changes the relevance of the case to a solo founder assembling a team from scratch.

## Case-specific observations

**Positive interpretation:** a cooperative product and a room-making tool supply repeatable reasons for existing players to bring in others and produce new material. The observed financial result supports studying this mechanism, not asserting that every editor or cooperative game will grow organically.

**Caution:** the near equality of a reported wishlist total and early sales/player totals is not proof of 100% wishlist conversion. Buyers without prior wishlists, refunds and measurement windows matter. Similarly, the studio's previous experience and specialist access must not disappear behind the phrase “small team.”

The record does not isolate how much demand came from creators, Steam discovery, existing fans or peer promotion. Profitability after all studio costs, retention cohorts and historical Discord growth remain unverified.
