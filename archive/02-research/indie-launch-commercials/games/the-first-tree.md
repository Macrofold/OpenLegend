# The First Tree — launch and commercial dossier

[Collection index](../README.md) · **Read and written: 2026-10-15** · **Cohort: commercial success; solo-led with licensed assets and porting help**

## Origin, team and commercial evidence

David Wehle's original interview describes a deliberately personal, low-budget narrative game, building on his earlier release **Home Is Where One Starts**. He made an exploration experience rather than a conventional action game, using a fox and environmental storytelling to connect two family narratives. Prior production experience matters when interpreting the later short production schedule. [S1]

Chris Zukowski's notes from Wehle's 2019 GDC presentation report approximately **18 months / 1,000 hours**, **US$10,000 in assets and show travel**, **US$150,000 revenue**, and a further **US$150,000 from console ports**. These are an attendee's account of the talk, not independently audited finances or a transcript. The notes incorrectly identify THE VOID as Wehle's previous game; that claim is excluded. [S3]

## Art, contractors, publishing and creator access

The official FAQ states that Wehle licensed assets from artists and programmers rather than making everything himself. It also names **DO Games** as a technical/certification collaborator, while describing the game as self-published. This is a specific division of labor: acquiring production components and buying porting expertise did not require handing all publishing responsibility to another company. Exact suppliers, fees and selection criteria are not disclosed. [S2]

The website grants permission to stream the game but warns that licensed music can trigger automated claims and offers contact help. That is a tangible creator-support issue hidden by the phrase “send keys to YouTubers.” The current FAQ's price and languages are not treated as original-launch metadata. [S2]

## Case-specific interpretation

The strongest documented collaboration lesson is narrow specialist support: the creator could retain the game's direction while licensing components and obtaining help with console requirements. The record does not show the legal or financial terms that would make an equivalent arrangement sensible for another project.

A visual post's ability to attract attention and a complete game's ability to satisfy buyers are separate questions. The revenue report supports commercial interest, but it cannot isolate the effect of Reddit from the product's presentation, press, prior audience or platform discovery. The April interview's selected reader comments are reception anecdotes, not a representative audience survey.
