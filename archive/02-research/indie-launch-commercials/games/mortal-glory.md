# Mortal Glory — launch and commercial dossier

[Collection index](../README.md) · **Read and written: 2026-10-15** · **Cohort: successful modest-scale solo commercial launch**

## Origin, team and measured result

AuroDev / Redbeak Games describes learning Unity in April 2019 and producing the game alongside a full-time job in roughly nine months, with approximately 1,000 development hours. His first-year account reports **17,525 copies, US$128,884 gross revenue, US$76,592 received by the company, and US$3,174 direct development expenditure**. These are different accounting quantities. His hypothetical personal after-tax calculation is not an additional cost actually paid or a tax rule transferable to another developer. [S1]

Steam confirms **January 29, 2020** and identifies Redbeak Games as both developer and publisher. The product is a single-player tactical gladiator-management roguelike, not an audience-dependent multiplayer service. [S3]

## How the audience was acquired

The marketing retrospective describes Twitter GIFs that mostly reached other developers, occasional Reddit activity, and manual outreach to relevant video creators. A **US$60 month of Woovit** was the disclosed cash marketing purchase; this excludes the founder's substantial unpaid time. The author contacted 16 press outlets, received three pieces of coverage, and describes an approximately 100-person mailing list built later around a DLC giveaway. Postlaunch Imgur attention is not credited as the cause of the original debut. [S2]

The first-year post records roughly **1,700 launch wishlists**. The much larger first-week unit total does not mean more than 100% of wishlisters converted: new buyers could arrive directly during release. [S1]

## Specialists and financing

Art used a mixture of purchased assets and Fiverr commissions; audio came from asset purchases. The creator also used selected utility assets rather than writing every supporting component himself. The published budget is useful evidence of a low-cash production route, but it does not give individual artist rates, licensing negotiations or first-contact dates. [S1]

No Kickstarter or financing publisher is established in the documented route. Kickstarter opening, closing, goal and pledges are therefore not applicable to the evidenced launch. Self-publishing did not require personally making all art or audio. [S1], [S3]

## Case-specific observations

**Positive interpretation:** this is a commercially meaningful outcome below the multimillion-copy outlier tier, with a disclosed cash budget and creator labor. Its relevance is strongest for a compact single-player product that can be explained and distributed to genre-matched creators.

**Caution:** subtracting asset expenditure from gross revenue would overstate personal earnings and ignore labor, platform deductions and taxes. The developer's belief that particular outreach helped is not a controlled marketing experiment. Later teaching videos should not be read backward as the source of the original game's audience.

Net lifetime profit, refund details, launch-demo maturity, contractor compensation and a full historical follower series remain unresolved.
