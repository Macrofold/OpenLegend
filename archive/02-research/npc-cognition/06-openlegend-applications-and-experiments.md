# OpenLegend applications and evidence-producing experiments

**October 20, 2026 — proposed comparisons; none executed.** This dossier translates the [source reconstructions](01-kevin-source-reconstruction.md), [architecture panel](03-architectures-and-papers.md), [model catalogue](04-models-local-hosted-openrouter.md) and [voice investigation](05-realtime-voice-and-serving.md) into concrete questions for existing owners. It is not a full implementation design, a new test framework or permission to spend.

## 1. What would make a tangible difference

The target is not an NPC that continuously talks about being intelligent. It is a person who can notice a relevant change, make a coherent choice, actually do it, and remain intelligible later. A silent continuation can be more responsive than another spoken plan. A fast, wrong action is not an improvement over a slower correct one.

| Player situation | Research-derived opportunity | Existing boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A resident has already chosen to walk somewhere or finish a finite task | Continue admitted native work without repeatedly asking a model for the next primitive | AG/IS continuation; existing physical interruption and outcome rules. |
| An actual threat interrupts work | Make complete current choices available and prioritize useful response, independently of spoken justification | CTR's selected two-stage strategy and voluntary choice; no automatic counterattack script. |
| The player refers to “that rock” or “the object beside you” | Ground a semantic referent in permitted exact candidates rather than free-form coordinates | Spatial perception, recognition and action admission; ambiguity remains possible. |
| A person says they gave an item but did not transfer it | Supply source-attributed claims separately from actual inventory and observed transfer | Existing knowledge, experience and native resource authority. |
| A character says they are not fighting while their attack continues | Include actual own activity, transition status and recent outcomes in the required context | Current action/experience owners, not a second narrative truth store. |
| A later conversation concerns an old insult, promise or shared pleasure | Retrieve relevant permitted evidence without dumping every past event | Required versus optional recall, independent interpretation and correction/forgetting. |
| Several people converse | Share common computation where genuinely identical while preserving private evidence and voluntary turns | SPK/attention-scene contracts; a shared scene-writer is a different comparison mode. |
| A short reply feels slow or lifeless | Compare useful phrase streaming and lawful expressive synthesis | NST's text contract and HE07's separately selected media work. |

These opportunities reinforce existing accepted directions. They do not establish that a new framework is required, nor that each missing behavior is a new design gap. The [focused map](../../../docs/maintainers/npc-cognition-research-needs-design.md) distinguishes reuse, evaluation and genuinely new policy questions.

## 2. Common evaluation discipline

Use the currently delivered ordinary path as the baseline, at a newly pinned revision when experiments are authorized. Hold world rules, actor identity, permitted observations, available actions, prior choices, model parameters and total allowance constant except for the named variable. Preserve accepted About me and required evidence under the current contract. A smaller prompt obtained by removing a material commitment is not an efficiency improvement.

Record the actual submitted context and request-bound options, every dispatched attempt, native admission and actual effects. Separate independent willingness, mechanically valid choice, physical completion, useful player experience and articulate self-report. Retain silence, refusal, inconclusive results and service failures. A fixture containing the desired answer can test plumbing, not voluntary behavior.

Choose development cases and assessment rules before examining held-out results. Include independently authored natural situations as well as variants of the existing DB01 synthetic corpus. Correlated paraphrases of one scene are not independent full episodes. Do not infer a causal improvement from simultaneously changing model, context, biography and voice.

Measure time from the meaningful input, not only from provider dispatch. Attribute acquisition, preparation/retrieval, queue, each decision, generation, native commit, first useful action, first useful text/audio and final completion. Report real-time and simulation-time consequences at the actual offered clock. Include cold starts, quiet periods, burst load and more than one active actor. Report distributions and severe failures, not only a mean.

Account for all preparation, embeddings, judging, generation, reasoning, synthesis, summaries, storage and uncertain charges. An incomplete invoice is not zero cost. Local compute is not economically free. Experiments stop under a predeclared finite allowance and the existing privacy/resource/authority boundaries; failure does not authorize an automatic paid repair.

The labels E1–E8 below are local comparison names, **not new maintainer task IDs**. Delivery, permission and acceptance remain with the named existing owners.

## E1 — Determine what really blocks a fast response

**Hypothesis:** some visible delay comes from context preparation, unnecessary serial gates or waiting for speech, rather than the chosen model's raw inference time.

Trace an ordinary request, a short supported action, an interrupted task, a meaningful danger and an unchanged continuing activity. Compare current operation with the smallest permitted change to redundant preparation or staging. Do not change character intent or action scope to manufacture speed. Include a completed task, a blocked path and a changed prerequisite so “continue cheaply” cannot hide invalid work.

Evidence must show zero new semantic requests for unchanged admitted primitives where the existing policy allows continuation, and a timely opportunity when relevant evidence changes. A queue can coalesce wakeups without discarding required harm, testimony or outcomes. Check that later action attempts do not claim old state is still current.

**Decision affected:** whether native/preparation work should be improved before changing models. **Owners:** PF, EPR, AG12/CR12, IS01/IS03/IS08 and the relevant CTR gate. This comparison is not permission to remove the selected two-stage threat strategy or to add a new scheduler.

## E2 — Compare Kev with the actual bounded-decision baseline

**Hypothesis:** a local prefill-only decision model can preserve useful judgment while lowering whole-decision delay or marginal cost.

Compare the existing decision provider with pinned Kev Q8 and a smaller-memory quantization, using the same source state, questions, option order and admissible outcomes. Add a compact general instruct model only where it can answer the same task without changing meaning. Record runtime, weight hash, quantization, context/template, hardware, concurrency and exact endpoint support. Local and hosted Kev are separate serving conditions.

Cover direct requests, negation, quoted speech, hypothetical instructions, ambiguous targets, conflicting prior commitments, competing needs, incomplete evidence, refusal and no-change. Include dynamic offered options and unrelated names; a fixed-label demonstration alone cannot qualify general action selection. Arithmetic and exact prerequisites remain native.

Measure accepted errors versus coverage, abstention, unnecessary escalation, missed opportunities, p50/p95/p99 complete latency and total work. Confidence is a feature to calibrate, not a guarantee of correctness. Preserve provider-specific score meaning; do not silently normalize away the current raw-score contract.

**Decision affected:** whether a new adapter/model is worth a scoped adoption design. **Owners:** R03, IS03, CR12 and existing AI-provider/accounting owners. No default model changes follow from a benchmark win alone.

## E3 — Test characterization without the voice halo effect

**Hypothesis:** a different model or prompt can improve coherent characterization, including permitted conflict, without sacrificing evidence fidelity or native action validity.

Compare stock Gemma 4 12B Instruct and the exact Kevin HauhauCS artifact at matched context and, where feasible, equivalent quantization. Add one verified hosted larger model and the current OpenLegend generator as baselines. Explicitly record unavoidable differences in training ancestry or deployment rather than attributing all gains to reduced refusal behavior.

Use coherent authored people with different values and histories. Include an ordinary afternoon, solitude, a declined invitation, actual useful help, sufficient food ending a food-seeking reason, an interruption, a disagreement, and a later return. Do not put the desired action sequence in biography or supply a hidden instruction to be more varied.

Evaluate appropriate independent choice, continued interests, stopping, factual grounding, stable voice in text, delayed continuity, needless refusals and service-boundary violations. A high swear-word count is not stronger personality. A cooperative reply is not proof of genuine help; a threatening reply is not actual retaliation.

**Decision affected:** candidate model/prompt comparison within the existing character design. **Owners:** CE05/AG12/CR12 and R10/R24; content admission remains with the established safety/release owners. Test only permitted fictional content; no real-world harmful-instruction demonstration is needed.

## E4 — Diagnose perception gaps before adding continuous vision

**Hypothesis:** complete actor-scoped structured evidence resolves most grounding failures more cheaply and reliably than screenshot inference; selected visual interpretation may help with genuinely unrepresented appearance.

Compare current context with corrected native projection of the same lawful evidence. Only then add an on-demand image arm for a specific visual task. The candidate image must come from a selected authorized viewpoint, without human-only UI or hidden labels. A player pointing at something supplies a referent cue, not automatic access to the player's entire camera.

Cases include two similar rocks, an object behind a wall, a moved target, an identical-looking stranger, a claimed gift without transfer, a real gift with exact count, a spawned object outside the exported roster, an observed attack and an unseen attack. Use an explicit no-match/uncertain result. Marked candidates can select exact native places; route feasibility and action currentness remain separate.

Trace whether a false answer came from absent bridge coverage, stale input, improper identity, failed retrieval or model error despite sufficient evidence. The system must not convert “not represented” into “definitely absent.” A model-generated visual description remains an interpretation of a captured image, not authority to rewrite the world.

**Decision affected:** native projection repair versus a narrowly justified visual capability. **Owners:** SPK/CR03/CR04, EPR/SW, required experience and existing knowledge/action owners. No new always-on vision cadence is proposed.

## E5 — Compare memory methods on demonstrated consequential omissions

**Hypothesis:** complementary lexical, temporal or associative candidates improve useful later recall without replacing canonical experience.

Start with current recall, then a modest native hybrid/rank-fusion improvement, then an optional richer service or method justified by the observed misses. Reuse the prior Hindsight three-arm logic. Recent methods in the [paper panel](03-architectures-and-papers.md#4-memory-useful-advances-without-a-second-canonical-mind) are candidates for specific components, not a mandatory combined graph/tree/codebook system.

Include an old promise, changed belief, repeated-source rumor, important one-off incident among routine activity, an unheard exchange, similar names, delayed indexing, correction, forgetting and restoring an earlier timeline. Protect required recent outcomes independently of optional retrieval. Record candidate omission separately from the reader model ignoring a supplied record.

Evaluate provenance, useful downstream choice, false certainty, coverage, index lag, extraction loss and complete write/read/consolidation cost. Shared semantic summaries are permissible only where the actual source/audience meaning is preserved; identical text does not establish identical personal interpretation. Reject cross-actor and abandoned-future leakage rather than averaging it into a quality score.

**Decision affected:** improve native retrieval, add a replaceable candidate source, or keep the baseline. **Owners:** DG04/ND20, R11/R19, CR12/CR13 and current memory/lifecycle owners. Longer-term outlook change remains DG23, not an automatic effect of installing retrieval.

## E6 — Qualify economical expressive speech

**Hypothesis:** useful phrase streaming and stable voice conditioning improve the encounter more than an extra prosody-model call, within the actual device and audience constraints.

Compare a small plain-voice baseline, Pocket TTS and the suitable Qwen3-TTS mode. Breeze is a reference only where its non-commercial/research terms actually permit the experiment; otherwise obtain an appropriate license or omit that arm. A licensed hosted path is a separate deployment condition. Use lawful reference voices, exact committed words and matched scenes before testing richer delivery.

Measure end-of-input to first useful audio, complete delivery, underruns, interruption, lexical/name/number fidelity, identity stability, expression appropriateness and interference with game rendering and other models. Compare text-only play so audio is not mistaken for cognitive improvement. Include quiet speech, overlap, a late listener, partial hearing, unknown identity, reconnect and output-off.

**Decision affected:** a selected optional media slice and deployable model/runtime. **Owners:** R07, DG26/HE07, NST where consuming committed text, and release/content/privacy owners. The existing microphone proposal remains an editable unsent draft. Direct speech-to-action requires a separate policy decision, not an experimental shortcut silently shipped to players.

## E7 — Preserve independence while sharing computation

**Hypothesis:** common prepared facts and compatible prefix computation can be reused across actors without combining their private contexts or forcing their replies.

Compare independent baseline requests with shared immutable preparation or exact prefix caching. Keep each actor's private memories, beliefs, permissions and action authority separate. A Mantella-style single shared scene-writer can be studied as an explicitly different diagnostic mode, but cannot be reported as semantically equivalent independent minds.

Use divergent knowledge, a secret shared with only one person, a late arrival, a partially heard utterance, one character refusing and another agreeing. Account for all eligible participants; reducing the audience or always picking the top speaker is not a valid speedup. Test pressure on queue age and useful turns without compulsory chatter.

**Decision affected:** a real shared-computation optimization or no change. **Owners:** IS05/IS06/IS08, DG18, SPK, PS/PF and existing dialogue/agency contracts. No new omniscient social director or parallel scene state is selected.

## E8 — Determine whether learned routines add value

**Hypothesis:** a person's repeatedly chosen, source-supported routine can reduce repeated planning while remaining revisable.

First establish that existing admitted methods, intentions and continuation fail a concrete case. Compare them with a proposed actor-owned routine only after its authority and lifetime are designed. A journal-derived opportunity for a fresh decision is different from immediate execution; neither is model-weight training.

Include a changed resource, competing concern, withdrawn consent, former friend, corrected memory, restored save and a purpose already completed. A remembered intention cannot authorize indefinite violence or automatically renew itself. Generated habits must not become developer-authored conditional biography scripts that masquerade as voluntary choices.

**Decision affected:** whether a further routine-policy design is justified at all. **Owners:** existing AE/AG/CR, CE and DG23 where lasting outlook changes; R24. Weight fine-tuning, reinforcement learning and a new executable-policy language are not prerequisites and remain unselected.

## 3. Recommended sequence and useful stopping points

E1 and the native-evidence portion of E4 are the highest-information starting work. E2 and E3 can then isolate model improvements without changing the world. E5 starts only after a meaningful omission is identified. E6 remains a separate optional-experience comparison. E7 follows a measured repeated workload; E8 requires a demonstrated gap in existing continuation.

A valid outcome can be “keep the current model,” “repair a missing own-action field,” “use native geometry rather than vision,” or “a small lexical/time retrieval improvement is sufficient.” Rejecting an unnecessary dependency is a useful research result. Conversely, a more advanced model or memory method should remain eligible when its complete benefit survives these comparisons.

This task produced desk research and this experimental plan only. No comparison results, player-quality judgments, deployment capacity, runtime changes or paid model calls are claimed.
