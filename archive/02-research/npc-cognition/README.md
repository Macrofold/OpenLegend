# NPC cognition research

**October 20, 2026 — desk research; experiments and model adoption remain open.** Findings refer to the repository and external-source snapshots in the [source register](07-source-register-and-open-questions.md). The [source request](../../00-source/npc-cognition-research-followup.md) defines the scope.

Use this research alongside [gameplay priorities](../../../docs/repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md), not as a replacement roadmap. Architecture and model choices should support useful invention, independent people and enjoyable play; popularity, eloquent dialogue and low refusal alone do not establish that value. Existing gameplay, privacy and service-content contracts remain authoritative.
