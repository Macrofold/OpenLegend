## Evidence methods and remaining uncertainty

### Reception and commercial evidence

- The five principal written bodies come from **Try Hard Guides, MMORPG.com, Wccftech, The Tank Club and Bits N' Pixels**. They span alpha and Early Access and different exposure depths; they are not five mature 1.0 reviews. Developer-guided observations and accompanying interview promises are separated.
- Historical Steam accounts were selected for contrasting, substantive explanations. They are a purposive qualitative sample, not a random survey or prevalence estimate. The later fixed-window coding has its own evidence owner below. Specific allegations remain attributed rather than verified facts.
- Freeze the November 14 Steam capture: **69% positive of 2,731 English reviews** and **52% of 101 recent reviews**. Different SteamDB aggregates and capture times are retained separately. Recent reviews include prelaunch time.
- Monthly concurrency is not cohort retention, daily users, distinct humans, income or technical capacity. June 2025 is a partial launch month; launch-return activity does not establish a lasting recovery.
- The release is one day old at this cut. The old and new worlds, inherited cosmetics and fresh gameplay progress have different continuity rules. The old F2P promise, paid functional empire design and cascading refined-material chain have been superseded in important respects.
- Commercial research checked official company, monetization and release accounts, a developer-reported sales milestone, a designer portfolio and third-party estimators. Missing realized-price, refund, platform, premium-spend and cost data preclude a defensible total-revenue/profit range. The absence of disclosure is not proof of failure.

### Technical and visual evidence

- BitCraft server code was inspected at **85c972d8d9433560fb51a906424b74b7958ddf99**, the November 13 1.0 release. Its 2.10.0 build dependencies are distinct from the platform's inspected 2.15.0 release and unknown live hosting configuration.
- Regional source, transfer/relay routines, scheduled activity and subscription behavior support bounded findings. They do not establish universal exactly-once handoffs, unlimited dense populations, stopped-region catch-up, comprehensive confidentiality or rich autonomous NPC cognition.
- Vendor throughput and reported memory savings are attributed. No gameplay session, independent load benchmark, production deployment, actual hosting-bill calculation, code import or automated test suite occurred.
- Official forestry/waterfall compositions and the UI designer's inventory, map and shop images were actually inspected. Promotional composition and design mockups are distinguished from live camera behavior and measured usability. The detailed art-director transcript was unavailable.
- Named composers and official music references were verified; music was not auditioned. Original lore bodies were incompletely extractable. The graphical 2026–2027 roadmap failed extraction; no unseen milestones or story events were inferred.
