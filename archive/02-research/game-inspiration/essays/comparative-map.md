## 2. Comparative map

The “lesson” column is an analytical recommendation. Sources in the case studies support the underlying mechanics and outcomes.

| Case | Main promise | Outcome lens used here | Most useful lesson for OpenLegend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minecraft | Make a place and do things your way | Dated mass-market and cultural milestones | Simple reusable primitives; personal projects; a visible sharing unit |
| RimWorld | Keep particular people alive through unpredictable events | Developer account and strong published reception | Traits and relationships matter when they change choices and consequences |
| Roblox | Play and create experiences with a social audience | Platform discovery and audited operating results | Tools require distribution, trust, operating capacity, and reasons to return |
| Hades | Master an escape while relationships continue | Developer interviews and published reception | Failure can advance character investment instead of resetting it |
| Dwarf Fortress | Participate in a world with deep independent history | Long-lived product and reception | Persistent causality and specific detail; complexity must be accessible |
| The Sims | Author and inhabit recognizable human lives | Cultural/critical and player-story evidence | Familiar stakes, expression, and space for interpretation |
| Wildermyth | Grow attached to heroes who change | Mechanics and original criticism | Authored story structures can make procedural people feel particular |
| Baldur’s Gate 3 | Make consequential choices with memorable companions | Mechanics and strong reception | Reactivity and authored identity complement systemic freedom |
| Factorio / Satisfactory | Build a system that works better | Mechanics and developer retrospective | Visible bottlenecks, compositional mastery, and earned automation |
| Terraria | Explore, fight, build, and grow stronger | Mechanics and reception | Sandbox freedom can coexist with clear progression destinations |
| Stardew Valley | Build a personally satisfying life and home | Dated commercial milestones and mechanics | Multiple gentle goals, accumulated ownership, and emotional rhythm |
| Valheim | Venture out and build a home with others | Dated launch milestone and official design | Preparation, expedition, recovery; survival need not be starvation management |
| Noita | Experiment with dangerous interacting systems | Mechanics reference | Consistent composition makes surprising outcomes feel discoverable |
| Caves of Qud | Inhabit a strange, systemic science-fantasy world | Mechanics and reception | Shared capabilities need a distinctive culture, not a generic ontology |
| Outer Wilds | Understand a mysterious changing world | Mechanics and developer prototype | Knowledge itself can be progression; finite worlds can sustain discovery |
| Garry’s Mod | Play with a toolbox and community-made modes | Mechanics and community examples | Reusable tools and finished playable experiences reinforce one another |
| Kenshi | Become someone in a world that does not favor you | Mechanics and reception | Setbacks can become identity and rescue stories |
| Project Zomboid | Survive through practical judgment | Mechanics and reception | Perception and mundane interactions become interesting under stakes |
| Among Us / Lethal Company | Coordinate, suspect, panic, and laugh together | Developer account and mechanics | Information asymmetry plus a simple shared objective produces stories |
| Dreams / Project Spark | Create and share interactive work | Support/closure evidence | An impressive creator tool does not guarantee a sustainable ecosystem |
| EverQuest Next | A more dynamic next-generation online world | Cancellation statement | Technical ambition and integrated fun are separate milestones |
| Worlds Adrift | Build ships and live in a persistent online world | Founder closure retrospective | Online physics, growth, continuity, and technical debt can compound |
| Spore | Create life and explore its possibilities | Contrasting contemporary criticism | Creativity and strategic depth are separate kinds of value |
| No Man’s Sky | Explore a vast generated universe | Launch criticism, updates, current reception | Variety becomes stronger when attached to activities and persistent projects |
| Ultima Online ecology | A simulated environmental economy | Developer account of a failed subsystem | Player incentives can overwhelm a beautiful model |
| Scribblenauts | Solve problems by describing objects | Official mechanics and criticism | Natural-language freedom needs challenge, feedback, and interaction quality |
| AI Dungeon | Create an open-ended story through language | Product positioning; outcome unknown | Textual possibility is useful, but continuity and rule authority remain separate problems |
| Hades II | Extend a beloved combat/story loop | Early access, review disagreement and dated Steam history | Craft and narrative payoff must evolve together |
| Grow a Garden / Steal a Brainrot | Accumulate, return, and participate in social events or risk | Creator accounts, platform milestones and contrasting comments | Separate accessible play from exploitative obligation or loss |
| Path of Exile / Path of Exile 2 / Diablo IV | Build mastery and return for fresh progression | Developer cadence accounts, review friction and Steam history | Rule changes affect competence and perceived ownership |
| League of Legends / Dota 2 | Master a shared overhead competitive space | Developer clarity guidance, original criticism and Steam reach | Readable state and combinatorial depth; camera is not an audience ceiling |
| Fortnite | Join a recognizable social destination | Dated company events and creator-platform reporting | The invitation and return occasion need value beyond novelty |
| Balatro | Combine familiar cards into surprising systems | Creator demo/wishlist diary and original criticism | Small operators can create large meaningful variety |
| Vampire Survivors | Focus on positioning and builds while routine attacks automate | Creator account and original criticism | Relocate agency rather than removing every decision |
| Slay the Spire | Construct and operate a coherent deck | Historical development and original criticism | More options are not always better; subtraction can be strategy |
| Palworld | Collect creatures who help and express emotion | Producer interview, player testimony and player milestone | Useful autonomy plus character is a close OpenLegend comparison |
| PEAK | Solve a simple risky predicament together | Rapid sales and accessible interview excerpt | One legible social problem can produce many stories |
| Core Keeper | Explore danger and return to a growing underground home | Launch milestones and detailed criticism | Readable top-down play can be warm, spectacular and systemic |
| Against the Storm | Repeatedly solve constrained settlement problems | Developer account and criticism | Renew local problems without necessarily erasing the main world |
| Animal Crossing: New Horizons | Inhabit and personalize a place | Nintendo unit disclosure; lower-depth comparison | Large non-combat appeal does not require FPS or tactical mastery |
