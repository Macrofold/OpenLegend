# MMO persistence, trust and durable worlds

**Research synthesis; evidence checked October 26–27, 2026.** The [scoped ledger](../research-progress-mmos-2026-10-26.md) owns the 22-subject research pass and its completion state. Individual dossiers retain mechanics, concrete situations, lore, reception and source qualifications. This essay owns the comparison and route into OpenLegend; it is not a new MMO feature specification or evidence of implemented capacity.

## Read success and failure precisely

The sample combines commercial scale, sustained service and different ways of making a shared world worthwhile. It is not a current population league table. Subscriber counts, registered accounts, concurrent connections, copies sold, gross revenue and profit answer different questions. A publisher's franchise total cannot be assigned to one regional PC service. A Steam decline does not measure an entire game distributed through its own launcher and other platforms. Concurrency trends are not cohort-retention rates.

The regional comparisons matter. Nexon's investor page reports more than US$22 billion lifetime revenue for the Dungeon&Fighter **franchise** and more than US$5 billion for the MapleStory **franchise**, whose scope includes PC and mobile. Its undated milestone page does not establish current activity or standalone PC profit. NC separately reports KRW269.1 billion for **Lineage Classic's first 140 days through June 30, 2026**. These justify inclusion, not an apples-to-apples ranking. [C01–C02]

Separate at least six outcomes when asking what went wrong: disappointing play, population decline, damaged trust, stopped content development, a regional withdrawal, and closure. Distinguish observed outcomes, participants' explanations, publisher statements and our causal hypotheses. The same game can recover in one respect while retaining another problem. Commercial durability is not proof that every mechanic serves player enjoyment.

## What a persistent open world promises

Yes, MMOs can maintain shared worlds and lasting player consequences. The useful question for OpenLegend is what a person can rely on finding when they return, who shares those consequences, and how absence changes their opportunities. The following properties must be selected separately.

| Property                   | Practical question                                                                        | Frequent mistaken inference                                               |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Retained character/account | Do skills, possessions, knowledge, relationships and collections survive logout?          | Every public object and quest outcome must also persist.                  |
| Shared continuity          | Can other people continue acting while I am absent?                                       | Every absent creature must receive continuous full-rate computation.      |
| Mutable public state       | Can ownership, buildings, prices, infrastructure or political outcomes outlast a session? | Every player must receive the same personal quest history.                |
| Spatial continuity         | What travel crosses a loading boundary, region or separate map?                           | Seamless travel implies one economy, one server or unlimited co-presence. |
| Population topology        | Which realm, region, channel, map copy or instance shares this event?                     | Cross-platform access means worldwide shared state.                       |
| Reset and recovery         | What changes on death, logout, maintenance, seasonal ending, abandonment or shutdown?     | Persistence promises no resets, no downtime or perpetual service.         |

Wurm Online is a particularly direct reference for altered shared geography: its official description includes player construction, terrain shaping and canals, alongside separate PvE/PvP servers. Foxhole offers a different persistent unit: a weeks-long war with player production, logistics, construction and territory, followed by another war. Its FAQ acknowledges multiple shards during population spikes and continuing infrastructure costs. These are **supporting comparisons**, not completed full-game dossiers in this pass. Neither publisher's advertised capacity establishes OpenLegend capacity. [C03–C05]

OpenLegend's [worlds and belonging](../../../../docs/product-scalability/worlds-and-belonging.md) already separates community, place and computational area. Its [hosted-world proposal](../../../../docs/game-ideas/hosted-worlds-and-rule-profiles.md) separates retained storage, availability, concurrency, background simulation and authoring allowances. The research supports evaluating those promises explicitly; it does not choose a new topology or unlimited autonomous life.

## Payment and practical advantage

Assess what the purchase changes in the selected world. Direct combat power, faster production, avoided repetition, more market supply, access to group requirements, inventory relief and authoring capacity can have different effects. “Convenience” and “cosmetic” are descriptions to investigate, not sufficient fairness findings. Conversely, a paid subscription, expansion or creator allowance is not by itself evidence that play is unfair or that payment caused a game's decline.

For OpenLegend, the accepted direction includes finite free inventions, larger paid allowances, platform membership and premium-world entry. Paid authoring still obeys native validation, materials, skills, time and consent. Preserve that direction. Compare an included-capacity participant with someone buying additional capacity, including indirect economic and social effects, effort on the ordinary route and useful participation after the authoring allowance is exhausted. The selected world's competitive and cooperative promises determine which differences are acceptable. [The initial-offer brief](../../../../docs/projects/initial-commercial-offer-decision-brief.md) and CO01 own that decision; this essay supplies evidence and alternatives.

## Positive opportunities worth carrying forward

The comparison should help us choose appealing experiences, as well as recognize failure conditions. These are design hypotheses for the existing first playable, not a promise to reproduce each source game's systems.

- **Make ordinary action satisfying.** [New World](../dossiers/new-world.md) and [Black Desert](../dossiers/black-desert-online.md) are useful references for the immediate experience of gathering, moving and fighting. A consequential world still needs understandable contact, timing, feedback and payoff. Observe the whole outing, including carrying, preparation and cleanup; an attractive animation cannot redeem an unwanted routine.
- **Make improvement open useful possibilities.** Compare [Guild Wars 2](../dossiers/guild-wars-2.md) and [OSRS](../dossiers/old-school-runescape.md) when asking whether a learned capability changes where someone can go, what they can make or how they can help. Let a desired expedition, commission or personal project give preparation a purpose. Neither permanent escalation nor a universal prohibition on power growth follows.
- **Give people a place they can make their own.** [ESO](../dossiers/elder-scrolls-online.md), [FFXIV](../dossiers/final-fantasy-xiv.md) and OSRS offer different property and creation comparisons. OpenLegend can connect a useful, revisable home to gifts, work, display and remembered events. Peaceful expression can be worthwhile in itself; property should not require compulsory attendance to justify its existence.
- **Let another person's contribution matter.** [Albion](../dossiers/albion-online.md), [WoW](../dossiers/world-of-warcraft.md) and [Lost Ark](../dossiers/lost-ark.md) help compare shared purpose with barriers to joining. An attainable useful role, understandable terms and the freedom to leave can make cooperation desirable. Mere proximity, forced dependencies or an incumbent's permission to participate are weaker evidence of a good social experience.
- **Make history available through interesting activity.** [LOTRO](../dossiers/lord-of-the-rings-online.md), FFXIV and [SWTOR](../dossiers/star-wars-the-old-republic.md) supply contrasting ways to connect place, culture, people and action. OpenLegend's discoveries should be worthwhile in different orders, with enough local meaning to engage a newcomer. A coherent world need not demand that everyone finish the same backlog before they can care.
- **Make the distinctive OpenLegend promise observable.** The project's opportunity is a useful place inhabited by particular people with independent purposes, where invention and actual events change later possibilities. MMO scale or content volume does not prove that experience. [CP10](../../../../docs/maintainers/first-playable-cooperation.md) and [CE05](../../../../docs/maintainers/character-experience.md) already ask whether encounters, choices, refusal, remembered consequences and human pacing work in ordinary episodes. These are the nearer tests of distinctiveness and fun.

An additional transfer limit concerns human attention. The [OSRS](../dossiers/old-school-runescape.md) and [modern RuneScape](../dossiers/runescape.md) comparisons distinguish learned capability from repeated labor and chosen goals. For OpenLegend, cheaply delegated work could change scarcity, remove a useful human contribution or reduce cooperation to issuing orders. Those are hypotheses to test, not arguments for automatic production caps. Existing competence, economy and character-experience owners should evaluate the selected world's costs, motives and willingness together; an inhabitant's independent purpose and ability to decline remain part of the experience.

## Route into OpenLegend

The most promising product direction remains a place people care about, with useful capabilities, independent inhabitants and consequences they can understand and influence. The research should improve the quality of that experience before it increases map size, simultaneous population or obligation to return. ## Integration and critique

Keep three layers: full evidence in the per-game dossiers; comparison and tradeoffs here; selected behavior and delivery work in their existing canonical owners. The research index and needs-design entries provide entry points. Do not paste the same rules into every specification.

The small selected refinements are an included-versus-paid participation comparison in the initial-offer brief/CO01, and an explicit quiet/mature-community play-value observation in PT06 using PS08's existing workloads. ND02 and ND22 link to the relevant evidence. The other major questions already have owners. A separate MMO feature/technical pair, new progression framework, live-events service or new needs-design ID is not justified by the research category alone.

Do not overlearn a recent failure or a successful incumbent. An audience may actively enjoy danger, long preparation, economic specialization or scheduled teamwork that another audience rejects. Preserve the tradeoff and its conditions. A large catalog of chores, mandatory catch-up, permanent escalation, forced mergers or unlimited generated content does not follow from wanting a compelling lasting world. Existing first-playable delivery and observed player value remain the nearer decision gates.
