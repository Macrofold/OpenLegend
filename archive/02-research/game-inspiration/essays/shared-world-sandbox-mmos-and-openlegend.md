# Shared-world sandbox MMOs and OpenLegend

**Research comparison in progress — November 14, 2026.** [Batch scope and completion](../research-progress-shared-world-sandbox-mmos-2026-11-14.md). This document records analysis and proposed positioning, not accepted new product scope or proven market demand.

## Question and comparison baseline

Mike asks which of nine shared-world sandboxes succeed, what players actually value and reject, how graphics affect the experience, and whether OpenLegend's documented vision offers a compelling reason to choose it with a smaller team and graphics budget. Success must distinguish durable community, broad reach and sustainable economics.

OpenLegend's [gameplay priorities](../../../../docs/repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md) already select a connected survival-adventure: useful making, a home, danger, discoveries, independent people, practical invention and persistent consequences. Its [selected language](../../../06-marketing/language-and-elevator-pitches.md) distinguishes this playable game from the wider engine and creator proposition. The [current art direction](../../../../art-direction/decisions.md#current-direction-at-a-glance) requires believable grounded stylization and rejects visible facets, blockiness and toy proportions. Research does not reopen those decisions by convenience.

## First completed case: SEED

The [SEED dossier](../dossiers/seed.md) establishes substantial overlap with autonomous characters, personality-conditioned LLM dialogue, remembered relationships, offline work, generational histories and social institutions. “We have AI characters” is insufficient differentiation. SEED's player-managed society and OpenLegend's intended direct adventure have different centers of play, but that is a hypothesis about experiential distinction that must survive an ordinary playable demonstration.

The reception sample gives both positive and negative explanations of the same design: becoming a useful contributor can be satisfying; configuring and maintaining that contribution can feel like employment. Its funding and staffing establish production investment, not sales success. Steam's absent review corpus does not mean there are no standalone players.

The other eight dossier passes, common comparisons, application map and final recommendations remain unfinished at this checkpoint.
