### 4.20 Vampire Survivors — automation can relocate agency rather than eliminate it

**The useful contrast.** A game can automate attacks yet still ask for meaningful decisions about positioning, pickups and builds. The 2022 review praises the escalation and accessible loop; its low historical price is part of the contemporary value proposition, not a current price recommendation. [CRIT-VS](../references/sources-03.md#source-crit-vs)

**Creator perspective.** Luca Galante’s comments emphasize affordability and that enjoyable value need not require indefinitely long games. Other reporting examines how the resulting genre spawned both superficial copies and games that made their own design changes. [VS1](../references/sources-03.md#source-vs1) [VS2](../references/sources-03.md#source-vs2) This brief does not infer a precise present sales or profit figure from its cultural influence.

**My interpretation.** The player is not relieved of all choice; routine execution is reduced so a different layer becomes salient. That is a very useful analogy for OpenLegend’s native agent continuation. A player may enjoy deciding where a settlement grows and who should work together without manually reissuing every known step.

**The risk.** Once automation makes the remaining choices obvious, the player may be watching an outcome rather than shaping it. Escalating audiovisual feedback can conceal that thinning decision space temporarily. Repeated play needs new problems or useful uncertainty, not only larger quantities.

**For OpenLegend.** Automate the tasks the player has already meaningfully delegated, while preserving intervention points around purpose, scarce resources, risk and social consequences. Let an NPC carry out a known plan without another model call for every action. Evaluate the result by the quality of decisions freed up—not by the percentage of activity performed autonomously.

**Growth lesson.** Low entry cost and an immediately communicable loop can help experimentation and sharing. That does not imply every cheap, small-scope game becomes a hit. The important product test is whether a newcomer can experience the distinctive reward almost immediately, without studying a large system first.
