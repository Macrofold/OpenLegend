### 4.23 PEAK — one shared predicament can be enough

**Observed growth.** Launch reporting records 100,000 copies in 24 hours and an update to one million within six days. A January 2026 Game File interview introduction reports more than ten million sales in 2025; only its accessible excerpt was inspected. [PEAK1](../references/sources-03.md#source-peak1) [PEAK2](../references/sources-03.md#source-peak2)

**Why include a first-person game?** The research is not an anti-FPS argument. A cooperative climbing predicament benefits from bodily immediacy, visibility limits and the understandable possibility of helping or failing someone. The correct camera depends on the activity being designed. A successful first-person case and a successful overhead case can support the same principle: make the important interaction readable and felt.

**My interpretation.** A simple shared objective can create rich group variation because participants differ in position, resources, judgment and willingness to help. A rescue or mishap is immediately interpretable. The world does not need a separately generated plot for the group to tell a story afterward.

**Evidence limit.** The publicly accessible material in this pass is stronger on growth and the pitch than on a representative set of player complaints or the full creator retrospective. The report does not claim comprehensive review coding for PEAK. Frustration over dependence on friends, repeated failure or inaccessible group coordination remains a research question for analogous OpenLegend design.

**For OpenLegend.** Prototype one constrained cooperative problem before assuming a vast world is necessary for emergent stories. Can an AI inhabitant supply a useful observation, take a risk, keep a promise or require rescue in a way a player understands? A good episode might validate perception, agency and memory together. It does not require launching public multiplayer first: native/fixture companions can help evaluate the causal structure, with actual model quality tested separately.

**Economic caution.** Rapid sales do not establish tiny development cost, easy replication or predictable marketing. A low-friction invitation and a legible shared activity can support word of mouth, but channel contribution remains unknown. Do not design a business that only works if it reproduces a ten-million-copy exception.

**Detailed system study:** [Shared burdens, rescue, and spatial tools](../mechanics/peak-shared-burdens-rescue-and-spatial-tools.md) adds stamina and carrying interactions, backpacks, Pitons, Rope and Anti-Rope, temporary boosts, proximity communication, selected contrasting player testimony, and the documented production history. Its later research extends the earlier evidence limits above without retroactively claiming that the original pass inspected those sources.
