### 5.8 AI Dungeon — useful adjacent evidence, not a solved blueprint

**Evidence.** Latitude positions AI Dungeon as an open-ended AI text adventure. Its product updates specifically include memory and Story Card improvements. This establishes continuity as an actual product concern, but does not establish public retention, profitability, or the quality of any particular model. [AI1](../references/sources-01.md#source-ai1) [AI2](../references/sources-01.md#source-ai2)

**Interpretation.** Free-form narrative interaction can be compelling as collaborative imagination. It is a different promise from a shared authoritative simulation. A player may enjoy rewriting the fiction in a story tool; the same flexibility can make a tactical or persistent-world accomplishment feel unearned.

**OpenLegend implication.** Keep three boundaries visible: proposed action, admitted action, and actual consequence. A model’s willingness to narrate success must not decide whether a resource was consumed or a character learned something. Persistent memory should improve continuity, while native state prevents contradiction.

**Product risk to test.** Novelty may attract experimentation without creating a reliable return loop. Model changes, latency, uncertain outcomes, and inconsistent characterization can affect the experience even when the underlying engine is unchanged. Treat these as design and operating risks to measure, not as proven explanations of AI Dungeon’s commercial performance.

**Outcome classification.** Ongoing adjacent product; no unsupported declaration of success or failure. The useful comparison is its interaction promise and documented emphasis on memory, not an imagined revenue figure.

#### Gameplay field guide — AI Dungeon

**What you actually do.** A text-first collaboration: you supply an action, dialogue, or story direction and a model continues the fiction. You can also revise or retry text and maintain contextual information. This makes authorial freedom much broader than a conventional command menu, but it does not automatically establish an independently simulated inventory, physics, or verified causal history. [FG-AID-O](../references/sources-04.md#source-fg-aid-o)

**What the reviewed accounts value.** The distinctive pleasure is responsive improvisation: a player can pursue a premise or detour without waiting for a developer to author a matching quest. The independent reflection recognizes this promise, while the official tools explicitly support co-authoring rather than one fixed win condition. Enjoyment can come from jointly constructing the fiction, not defeating a consistent simulated obstacle. [FG-AID-C](../references/sources-04.md#source-fg-aid-c) [FG-AID-O](../references/sources-04.md#source-fg-aid-o)

**What they dislike or find limiting.** The 2026 discussion reports abrupt scene changes, repetitive continuation, and a model taking unwanted control of a character or plot. Another participant does not reproduce every complaint but does recognize repetition. One specific account jumps from a shopping scene to an unrelated home conversation. Treat these as setup-dependent reported failures, not a controlled verdict on all available models. [FG-AID-P](../references/sources-04.md#source-fg-aid-p)

**The design tension.** A story tool can legitimately let the author repair or redirect canon. In a persistent simulation, the same power must not silently rewrite completed effects, another character’s knowledge, or real resource accounting.

**Watch to understand the game**

1. **[[Vinesauce] Vinny — AI Dungeon 2 #1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7M-IVNQS2t0)** — 2019 historical entertainment/playthrough. Observe the basic human-proposal/model-continuation loop: surprise, steering, inconsistency, and the human effort needed to make it a story. **Caution:** Historical model and interface, mature language, and entertainment framing. It is not evidence of current AI Dungeon quality, memory, pricing, or tool behavior.

**Three concrete in-game situations**

1. **Action, speech, and story authoring are different inputs.** *Documented interface.* Do frames what the character attempts; Say frames dialogue; Story lets the player supply narrative text more directly. These modes make the user’s role partly authorial. For OpenLegend, that distinction suggests explicit separation between embodied intent and creator control rather than one ambiguous chat box. [FG-AID-O](../references/sources-04.md#source-fg-aid-o)

2. **An unwanted continuation can be edited or retried.** *Documented authoring workflow.* The player can change text or request another continuation, effectively helping select the story’s next version. That is useful for collaborative fiction. It would be a different promise if retrying also changed authoritative damage, transferred items, or another player’s completed action. [FG-AID-O](../references/sources-04.md#source-fg-aid-o)

3. **Remembered essentials still compete for context.** *Documented context mechanism.* Plot Essentials supplies recurring facts and Story Cards can introduce relevant contextual material. Those tools can help maintain a premise, but adding more text uses context and is not proof the model will apply every fact. Persistent prompt content and reliable behavioral memory are distinct goals. [FG-AID-M](../references/sources-04.md#source-fg-aid-m)

**What to borrow for OpenLegend.** Use AI Dungeon to study language affordances and the pleasure of co-authorship, not as proof that unrestricted narration is sufficient world authority. OpenLegend should retain actual outcome receipts, scoped knowledge, and useful correction tools while making technical failures distinct from fictional choices.

*Scope/evidence note:* Base-game systems unless noted; examples describe documented mechanics, not a play session conducted for this research.
