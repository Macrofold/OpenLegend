## Scope and ownership

Mike requested an exceptionally deep study of **G75 Elden Ring**: the base game, Shadow of the Erdtree and relevant released content, including all major gameplay systems, story, distinctive design choices, player praise and criticism, commercial outcomes and community creation. [The established dossier](dossiers/elden-ring.md) remains the entry point and preserves the earlier September evidence. Nightreign is a separate product and is mentioned only to prevent scope and financial confusion.

This pass applies [R01–R14 and conditional R15](research-requirements.md), including the [economics, enjoyment, lore and creation amendment](research-economics-engagement-and-creation.md). It does not recertify other games or close separate whole-library preservation gates. Research hypotheses do not authorize OpenLegend product or runtime changes.

## Evidence limits retained

The $2 billion figure is an attributed Alinea Analytics estimate, not an audited income statement or profit figure. Its exact DLC/accounting perimeter, project costs, revenue shares and creator earnings are not established by the examined public evidence. Price sensitivity is illustrative rather than a confidence interval.

Player accounts establish specific experiences and disagreements; they do not measure population sentiment or isolate sales causality. Historical reviews retain their version/platform context. Current PC/PlayStation/Xbox patch evidence identifies App 1.17.2 and Regulation 1.17.3; no independent Switch 2 technical baseline is inferred from it.

Lore distinguishes released evidence, interested character testimony, interpretation and unresolved questions. The work covers all major system families and research dimensions, rather than reproducing every item statistic, dialogue line or possible quest ordering. Detailed source-access limits remain beside the affected claims.
