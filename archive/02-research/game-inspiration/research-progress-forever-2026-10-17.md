## Evidence boundaries

The subject is unreleased at this capture. A complete prelaunch study can address documented designs, observed beta experience and reception of the promise. It cannot certify launch quality, long-term retention, a stable economy or future service longevity. Private revenue and cost figures require defensible disclosure or inputs; missing data is not a commercial failure.

Completion means substantive coverage and review of the available evidence, with limitations recorded in the dossier. It does not mean personal gameplay, a complete spell database, accepted OpenLegend features or completion of other research.
