Awakening and the major smaller DLC receive separate substantive treatment. **R09 — further research needed:** the production section supplies valuable moral-choice intent, voice work and DLC experimentation, but not a substantive history of the **base game's** origins and development: initial creative direction, development sequence, documented iteration and production constraints. Do not fill this with unsourced engine names, staff counts or a presumed schedule. The existing interviews can remain; they do not by themselves supply that missing history.

**Reporting correction applied to the ledger:** the actual worked-situation section is §17, **seven examples**, not twelve. The earlier ledger's additional claims of eighteen individually enumerated mechanics and sixteen reception patterns were not supported by the dossier's structure. Seven substantive examples can satisfy R05; this is a correction of reporting, not a newly imposed numerical threshold. Canonical dossier text has not been changed by this correction.

**Established coverage:** Power versus Influence and advisor timers; equipment/material crafting; respecialization; Guard versus Barrier; consumable preparation; veilfire and institutional lock access; companions, judgments and romance conditions; home/camps/collections; ten causal cases; three independently treated expansions; primary production, audio and promotional evidence; and correctly defined commercial history. **R02–R04 — further research needed:** the ordinary class/build progression is much thinner than the unusual-item examples. Because multiplayer is explicitly in this dossier's scope, stating that it is separate from the campaign is necessary but insufficient.

**R12 — preserve the evidence boundary:** actual helpful Steam bodies were inaccessible after recorded attempts; direct discussion/player alternatives are labelled. No current matchmaking or reliability test is implied.

## G55 — Pillars of Eternity II: Deadfire

**Established coverage:** multiclass opportunity costs, class-resource differences, defenses/penetration, inspirations/afflictions, Empower gates, Party Assist, fixed grimoires, named-item/enchantment behavior, preparation, faction/companion relationships, combat policy editing, narrative scale, ten specific cases and three expansions. Five full independent reviews and a genuine helpful Steam sample are present, with a negative alternative. The turn-based change is correctly distinguished from the first game's 2026 update; crowdfunding and attributed eventual profitability are not conflated with units or audited finances.

**R02/R04 — further research needed:** naval encounters are central to the scope and reception, yet their operating rules are scarcely explained. The reader learns that ships, crew, supplies, morale and boarding exist, but not how the ship encounter's commands, positioning/range, guns, crew tasks, damage/repair and boarding choice actually interact. A list of nautical nouns and disagreement among reviewers do not discharge the mechanics inventory. Add a named, rules-based naval situation rather than another generic travel lesson.

**R06 — further research needed:** companion-to-companion reactions are well explained, but romance and its eligibility/continuity boundaries are not substantively treated. The assignment explicitly includes romance where applicable. Expand the actual relationship modes rather than implying the approval/disposition discussion covers them all.

**R04 improvement:** make the precise distinction between ordinary knockout, accumulating injury and irreversible death usable to the reader; currently it is emphasized as a distinction without fully explaining the recovery contract.

## G57 — Divinity: Original Sin II

**Established coverage:** the armor/status design, Source acquisition versus replenishment, attributes and prepared skills, objects/surfaces, theft/speaker ownership, spirit/animal information, campaign progression, origin/companion relationships, co-op continuity, six critic assessments and actual contrasting helpful Steam testimony are substantive. **R02/R03 — further research needed:** the build inventory omits substantive **talent** treatment and important racial rule differences. **R14 improvement:** the Gift Bag source S05 is a general news feed. A stable title/date-specific announcement would better support the important baseline-versus-optional-rule distinctions. Existing inherited access notes should be retained.

**Established coverage:** edition boundaries, six-person party, kingdom/adviser connection, camping, named companions, parallel Varnhold history, all three major expansions, development/authorship evidence, dated commercial definitions, six publications' full reviews plus a reassessment, and actual GOG helpful alternatives. Counting labels alone overstates specificity.

**R14/evidence:** inaccessible mechanics sites are properly disclosed, but this does not make the still-general rules account complete. Seek accessible guide/manual/developer alternatives rather than inventing rules from tabletop memory. The critic minimum itself is satisfied by the documented bodies.
