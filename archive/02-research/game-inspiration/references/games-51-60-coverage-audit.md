The substantive dossier bodies and source registers of **all G51–G60 were read in full**. A requirement judged addressed means substantive coverage was found; it does not certify every inherited factual claim.

## G51 — Dragon Age: Origins

**Established coverage:** created origins, attributes, class/talent/skill distinctions, equipment and fatigue, crafting, ordered tactics, status combinations, noncombat access, companion approval and romance, story consequences, failure/recovery and replay. Awakening and the major smaller DLC receive separate substantive treatment. Five attributable full written assessments, actual helpful Steam testimony and a qualified negative alternative are present. Redcliffe, the Landsmeet and the other worked examples connect named game situations to consequences rather than only discussing generic agency.

**R09 — further research needed:** the production section supplies valuable moral-choice intent, voice work and DLC experimentation, but not a substantive history of the **base game's** origins and development: initial creative direction, development sequence, documented iteration and production constraints. Do not fill this with unsourced engine names, staff counts or a presumed schedule. The existing interviews can remain; they do not by themselves supply that missing history. Missing private budgets are not a failure of coverage; the gap concerns the publicly researchable development account.

**Reporting correction applied to the ledger:** the actual worked-situation section is §17, **seven examples**, not twelve. The earlier ledger's additional claims of eighteen individually enumerated mechanics and sixteen reception patterns were not supported by the dossier's structure. Seven substantive examples can satisfy R05; this is a correction of reporting, not a newly imposed numerical threshold. Canonical dossier text has not been changed by this correction.

The claims of eighteen enumerated mechanics, eighteen reception patterns and ten video routes were likewise not established by the inspected structure. Existing evidence and viewing/reading routes remain intact.

**R02–R04 — further research needed:** the ordinary class/build progression is much thinner than the unusual-item examples. Because multiplayer is explicitly in this dossier's scope, stating that it is separate from the campaign is necessary but insufficient.

**R12 — preserve the evidence boundary:** actual helpful Steam bodies were inaccessible after recorded attempts; direct discussion/player alternatives are labelled. No current matchmaking or reliability test is implied.

## G55 — Pillars of Eternity II: Deadfire

**Established coverage:** multiclass opportunity costs, class-resource differences, defenses/penetration, inspirations/afflictions, Empower gates, Party Assist, fixed grimoires, named-item/enchantment behavior, preparation, faction/companion relationships, combat policy editing, narrative scale, ten specific cases and three expansions. Five full independent reviews and a genuine helpful Steam sample are present, with a negative alternative. The turn-based change is correctly distinguished from the first game's 2026 update; crowdfunding and attributed eventual profitability are not conflated with units or audited finances.

**R02/R04 — further research needed:** naval encounters are central to the scope and reception, yet their operating rules are scarcely explained. The reader learns that ships, crew, supplies, morale and boarding exist, but not how the ship encounter's commands, positioning/range, guns, crew tasks, damage/repair and boarding choice actually interact. A list of nautical nouns and disagreement among reviewers do not discharge the mechanics inventory. Add a named, rules-based naval situation rather than another generic travel lesson.

**R06 — further research needed:** companion-to-companion reactions are well explained, but romance and its eligibility/continuity boundaries are not substantively treated. The assignment explicitly includes romance where applicable. Expand the actual relationship modes rather than implying the approval/disposition discussion covers them all.

**R04 improvement:** make the precise distinction between ordinary knockout, accumulating injury and irreversible death usable to the reader; currently it is emphasized as a distinction without fully explaining the recovery contract.

## G57 — Divinity: Original Sin II

**Established coverage:** the armor/status design, Source acquisition versus replenishment, attributes and prepared skills, objects/surfaces, theft/speaker ownership, spirit/animal information, campaign progression, origin/companion relationships, co-op continuity, six critic assessments and actual contrasting helpful Steam testimony are substantive. **R14 improvement:** the Gift Bag source S05 is a general news feed. A stable title/date-specific announcement would better support the important baseline-versus-optional-rule distinctions. Existing inherited access notes should be retained.

## G58 — Pathfinder: Kingmaker

**Established coverage:** edition boundaries, six-person party, kingdom/adviser connection, camping, named companions, parallel Varnhold history, all three major expansions, development/authorship evidence, dated commercial definitions, six publications' full reviews plus a reassessment, and actual GOG helpful alternatives. Adviser assignment is correctly not equated with removing the person from all adventuring activity. Native turn-based editions are correctly distinguished from earlier mod advice.

**R02–R04 — further research needed:** the core rules account is unusually general. It says classes, feats, skills, spells, attacks and defenses matter but rarely explains their operating relationships. Supply the actual action economy; prepared versus spontaneous resources; attack/defense/save distinctions; major equipment/proficiency and exploration-skill constraints; and meaningful recovery/loss rules. The phrase “not a universal mana-only system” is not an explanation of the actual alternatives. No exhaustive coefficient table is needed.

**R02/R04 — further research needed:** kingdom projects, ranks, adviser checks, unrest/failure, expansion and construction need an operational explanation. Existing paragraphs explain why administration might be interesting more thoroughly than how a player actually performs it.

**R05 — strengthen concrete grounding:** several of the nine cases have the requested causal labels but use unspecified prerequisites and objects (“compatible teamwork investments,” “a desirable weapon,” “a relevant artisan”). Replace some abstraction with named game-specific interactions and actual eligibility/cost conditions. Preserve the two attributed reviewer experiences and their launch-versus-reassessment limits. Counting labels alone overstates specificity.

**R14/evidence:** inaccessible mechanics sites are properly disclosed, but this does not make the still-general rules account complete. Seek accessible guide/manual/developer alternatives rather than inventing rules from tabletop memory. The critic minimum itself is satisfied by the documented bodies.

## G59 — Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous

**Established coverage:** much stronger rules detail than G58, including action types, defense categories, bonus stacking, Precise Shot/Outflank, class/character/mythic advancement, prepared casting, skill roles, consumable production, corruption/rest, mounts, equipment, companions and crusade resources. All six premium expansions have individual accounts. Ten situations, five full independent critic bodies, a genuinely attempted Steam route and actual GOG alternatives are present. Development, audio and commercial evidence retain source and time boundaries.

**R02/R03 — further research needed:** the ten-path table explains fantasies and several important late-path costs, but several **early paths' distinctive mechanics** are still mostly names. Demon rage, Aeon, Trickster and Azata need enough concrete rules to explain how their activity/build decisions differ; Angel's merge and late Legend/Devil/Swarm costs cannot substitute for all of them. Do not attempt a copy of every path ability or assume equal narrative length.

**R02/R04 — deepen the principal crusade battle loop:** councils, resources, buildings and adviser outcomes are covered, but army-stack combat, general capabilities, losses/recovery and the morale/infirmary relationships need a more direct operational account. The dossier's own critics repeatedly discuss this separate mode, so it should be understandable without opening an external walkthrough.

**Withdrawn link suspicion:** M10's Reddit URL repeats the post ID in a `/comment/` path and initially looked malformed. The audit opened the [DevTrackers mirror](https://devtrackers.gg/pathfinder/p/830eca8f-spoiler-free-new-player-guide-to-wrath-of-the-righteous-part-1-fundamentals-mechanics-and-adventuring), followed its original-post link and successfully retrieved the [same Reddit target](https://www.reddit.com/r/Pathfinder_Kingmaker/comments/14r1roy/comment/14r1roy/). **Established coverage:** no major missing R01–R14 dimension was identified in the full manuscript and source-register inspection. It explains attack speed and weight, triangle and regional effectiveness, class roles and fixed promotions, weapon proficiency, staff/consumable functions, physical rescue versus the Rescue staff, convoy access, map/terrain/fog actions, recruitment, support thresholds and budgets, development, rankings, money, native suspension and loss exceptions.

Presentation has concrete map/portrait/animation, sound-cue and music evidence. Three contemporary Hobonichi interview installments, production/localization credits and promotional-distribution records support R09–R11; unknown private budget, profit and sales metrics are explicitly not fabricated. Five substantive independent written reviews include both archived GameSpy pages and an archived Eurogamer original. Actual Nintendo Life player comments provide contrasting testimony. This non-Steam title correctly has no invented Steam sample.

**Limits to retain:** conflicting specialist descriptions of support edge cases and a calculator's arithmetic are not silently resolved; native cartridge rules remain separate from emulator conveniences. The oversized Nintendo manual PDF and unavailable Nintendo Online Magazine interview are not represented as read. Relevant HTML manual text and the separately accessible interviews are explicitly distinguished from those unavailable originals. A fresh gameplay reproduction, full re-opening of every external source, or packet-wide reconciliation was not performed in this audit.
