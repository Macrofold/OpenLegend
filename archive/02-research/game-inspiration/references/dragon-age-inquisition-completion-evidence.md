## Newly inspected sources

### Trespasser: power after victory

Nicholas Tan, [GameRevolution review](https://www.gamerevolution.com/review/72685-dragon-age-inquisition-trespasser-review), September 10, 2015; substantive PC review body inspected. The Exalted Council asks what legitimacy the Inquisition retains after defeating Corypheus. Ferelden worries about sovereignty; Orlais wants influence over the organization. The epilogue advances two years and closes access to the base campaign's open activities. Tan values the character farewells, interlocking levels and stronger resolution, while criticizing a consequential betrayal's abbreviated reaction and the Anchor's tendency to dominate combat. His roughly ten-hour experience is an individual review duration, not a completion-time distribution.

**Research interpretation:** gaining authority and surrendering authority are different playable problems. A leadership system needs a constituency, jurisdiction and sunset condition, not merely a larger resource bar. This interpretation does not establish that Inquisition simulates independent states or that OpenLegend implements such a system.

### Sound and promotion

Conal Pierse, [Inside the Temple of Dragons with Composer Trevor Morris](https://blog.bioware.com/2014/11/12/inside-the-temple-of-dragons-with-composer-trevor-morris/), BioWare, November 12, 2014; page text inspected. The official account identifies Ocean Way Studios, Nashville, and strings, brass and choir. It also says the behind-the-scenes video was first shown to Dragon Age newsletter subscribers. This establishes a documented promotional touchpoint and orchestral production, not an audience-conversion rate. Video footage has not been watched in this pass.

### Commerce and product boundaries

[EA's current Inquisition product page](https://www.ea.com/games/dragon-age/dragon-age-inquisition), retrieved September 26, 2026, describes the Game of the Year package, storefront links, EA-account/app requirements, optional virtual-currency purchases and paid random items. Its displayed October 6, 2015 date is a package date, not the original 2014 release. The former `/dlc/single-player/trespasser` URL now redirects to this general product page and is not a freshly inspected detailed Trespasser specification.

[WN Hub's September 18, 2024 report](https://wnhub.io/news/investment/item-45517) reproduces Mark Darrah's September 17 statement that Inquisition had sold over 12 million units and exceeded EA's internal projections. Treat this as an attributed developer statement relayed by reporting, not an independently audited 2026 financial disclosure. Do not convert lifetime units into active users, full-price revenue or profit; platform and discount mix are not supplied.

### Player evidence access

The store returned an age gate; the community root returned a sparse shell; the filtered and JSON endpoints did not return review bodies through the browsing tool. A container network fallback also failed DNS resolution. These attempts do **not** count as inspected helpful-review bodies or a measured Steam sample.

The [May 2–3, 2024 Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1222690/discussions/0/4364627785841867224/) supplies contrasting firsthand access reports: the opening poster asks about crashes and audio/EA-app complaints; two respondents report working installations, one with a small number of crashes over substantial play. These do not prove that either universal failure or universal reliability is correct. The dossier must distinguish this discussion from helpful-review sampling and seek additional directly accessible player accounts.
