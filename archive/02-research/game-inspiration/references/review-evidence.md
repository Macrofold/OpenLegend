# Review evidence notebook

**Research date: September 23, 2026.** Companion to the [master brief](../README.md). These are selected qualitative observations, not a sentiment-analysis dataset or a representative ranking of complaints.

## Method and guardrails

Read accessible original criticism, most-helpful Steam lists (including positive/negative filters), creator responses and selected discussions. Prefer substantive mixed evaluations and specific play stories over one-line jokes. Paraphrase rather than reproduce reviews. Note source/version dates; later edits may appear under an original posting date. Do not treat displayed hours as hours at posting. Helpfulness ranking favors material that circulated, not a random population.

A Steam recommendation is a binary purchase recommendation with free text, not a separate score for each mechanic. A negative review can praise art, sound and combat while objecting to one important issue. A positive review can describe a serious late-game problem. Those tensions are more useful than flattening the response into positive/negative totals.

## Developer responses that clarify—not automatically settle—reception

**Hades II:** the May 2026 Kasavin/Korb interview describes adjusting an ending after feedback. A responsive process does not require satisfying every opposing preference, nor does it prove that the revised outcome pleased everyone. [HD7](sources-02.md#source-hd7)

**Project Zomboid:** the July 2026 stable-release announcement separates newly shipped systems from continuing late-game and support work. Current status is not inferred from an old complaint. [PZ2](sources-02.md#source-pz2)

**PoE2:** reporting of the director’s 2025 response identifies a disappointing update period and intended repairs. It is not independent verification of every subsequent fix. [POE4](sources-02.md#source-poe4)

**Balatro:** a creator diary gives a specific record of demo changes, creator exposure and feedback. This supports an iterative development account, not a universal recipe for streamer success. [BA1](sources-03.md#source-ba1)

## Interpretation for OpenLegend

The useful output of review research is a set of testable conflicts: comfort versus pressure; autonomy versus lost agency; knowledge versus opacity; consequences versus arbitrary punishment; novelty versus stable competence; expressive authorship versus tedious configuration; social occasion versus attendance obligation.

Do not resolve these conflicts by taking a vote over whichever comments are easiest to retrieve. Choose an intended experience, test it with relevant people, and preserve alternative modes only when they have coherent consequences. Public reception helps discover the questions; it does not decide the answer for this game.

## Evidence distinctions

- **Critic review:** a particular critic’s experience of a specified release/version.
- **Player account:** an attributed experience, sometimes edited after initial posting or affected by mods and settings.
- **Developer description:** evidence for the intended/published mechanic, not independent proof that players enjoy it.
- **Mechanics reference:** documentation of a rule or interaction, not a verified recording of a particular session.
- **Video recommendation:** metadata/official-link validation only; no claim of full playback analysis.
- **OpenLegend interpretation:** a proposed design lesson rather than a demonstrated causal result.
