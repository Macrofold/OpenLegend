# Source-packet provenance and reading map

This page identifies the seven Markdown files supplied with the continuation of the game-inspiration research on September 25, 2026. Their internal research dates are September 22–23. It is an archival reading aid, not a task tracker, an implementation specification, or a claim that historical external facts were reverified today.

The library already separates the packet into game chapters, cross-game essays, and evidence references. New granular studies supplement those owners. Keep the original distinctions between documented mechanics, attributed play stories, developer accounts, criticism, and our interpretation. Preserve disagreement and version qualifications rather than replacing the research with a single recommendation.
