## Examples that sharpen the existing interpretation

### Rewards should serve the intended early activity

Blizzard describes caster weapons with useful damage/healing bonuses, healing equipment that also contributes damage, and quest rewards serving more roles. The transcript's precise level-10 claim is not needed to establish the design point and is not independently certified here as beta behavior. [Official Deep Dive — itemization](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303313/world-of-warcraft-forever-deep-dive-panel-recap)

**Interpretation:** a reward table can be extensive while a chosen approach has little worth anticipating. Examine whether an improvement helps its recipient do something they actually want to do while that opportunity still matters. Support specialization can retain practical solo usefulness without making every role equally capable.

For OpenLegend, this applies to the intended ways of engaging with a selected activity. It does not require identical reward timing, a separate progression track for every supported action, or numerical growth for peaceful creation, discovery, expression or company. Those experiences can supply their own worthwhile outcomes. Earlier availability is not automatically better: anticipation and earned competence can be part of the appeal.

### An optional choice should not conceal an already-promised prerequisite

The Paladin article identifies former talents made baseline, including Blessing of Kings and improved buff effects. It also preserves distinct class limitations. [Official Paladin deep dive](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302099/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-paladin)

**Interpretation:** moving a benefit into ordinary capabilities can free choices for more distinctive alternatives. This does not demonstrate that every removed talent was mandatory, that passive improvements lack value, or that the revised choices have no dominant option.

The useful question is whether an option changes how someone plays or merely repairs a capability the activity already promises at that stage. Learning a necessary skill, unlocking a specialization or deliberately depending on companions may be meaningful progression. A commonly selected option alone establishes neither a bad choice nor a need to remove it.

### Separate repeated instructions from valued execution

Judgement no longer consumes the active Seal, while an optional Seal Twisting route retains timed switching. Consecration concentrates useful effectiveness on the first four enemies; Blizzard explicitly connects that limit to preserving tactical group combat. [Official Paladin deep dive](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302099/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-paladin)

**Interpretation:** examine which repeated inputs sustain timing, resource decisions or a valued rhythm, and which simply restate an unchanged intention. The existing dossier records disagreement about class rhythm; reducing input count is not automatically an improvement for every player.

Similarly, ask which surrounding decisions remain when a powerful capability is used well. An improvement may intentionally retire a mastered obstacle, open another approach, or remove a choice the game still needs. That consequence matters more than copying a four-target limit into OpenLegend. Existing [execution and presentation guidance](../../../../docs/repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md#make-execution-and-presentation-earn-their-attention) owns the broader selection question.

## Correcting the camping claim without overclaiming the result

The transcript says camp bonuses stack with class buffs. Blizzard's written Deep Dive says the corresponding effects do **not** stack; its sharpening-wheel example increases Attack Power rather than Strength. The inspected prior dossier makes no stacking claim, so this qualifies the new transcript rather than repairing a recorded dossier error. [Official Deep Dive — Camping and Professions](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303313/world-of-warcraft-forever-deep-dive-panel-recap)

Non-stacking limits accumulation of equivalent bonuses. It does not establish that a camp is optional for every party or challenge: a party lacking the corresponding class may still strongly value the benefit. Neither a contribution limit nor a rest bonus proves inclusion, conversation or a lasting relationship.

The [existing social synthesis](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/14ac519648f4dd0c681db8fa168479fb9ed4e387/archive/02-research/game-inspiration/essays/wow-forever-and-a-world-worth-returning-to.md#shared-activity-can-lead-to-relationships-without-requiring-friendship) already distinguishes useful help, pleasant company and voluntarily meeting again, including silent bonus collection. Its counterexamples remain part of the lesson. OpenLegend's [cooperation work](../../../../docs/maintainers/first-playable-cooperation.md) owns the selected cast, independent consent and useful shared activities; this supplement adds no camp-buff mechanic.

## Keep the other conclusions proportional to the evidence

- **Legacy already has stronger coverage in the prior dossier.** Official guidance allows 16 points to be spent per character at launch and enough earning opportunities on one character to make alts optional. Account-wide progression can still confer advantages; its label does not establish fairness or harmless economic effects. These details are context, not a proposal for OpenLegend account progression. [Official Legacy explanation](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24307383/get-to-know-the-world-of-warcraft-forever-legacy-system)
- **Studio experience is a reason to inspect the reasoning.** Blizzard describes 22 years of learning about convenience and social effects, and acknowledges overextending class changes in Season of Discovery. Those interview accounts are not public controlled comparisons proving each Forever choice or its long-term effect. [Convenience interview, October 9](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/blizzard-explains-why-wow-forever-wont-have-summoning-stones-says-easy-quick-access-to-teleports-shrink-the-world-and-easily-erodes-its-core-design-pillars) · [Iteration interview, October 12](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/the-wow-forever-team-says-it-learned-a-lot-from-classic-season-of-discovery-including-managing-the-in-game-economy-and-rmt-but-blizzard-will-be-more-careful-and-cautious-with-class-changes-as-it-knew-we-were-going-too-far)
- **Existing transfer boundaries remain.** The full dossier and synthesis retain the distinctions between durable investment and permanently optimal gear, surprise and repeat value, and appearance credit and physical objects. For OpenLegend, familiar encounters do not establish a safe method for migrating people between worlds with different histories, and shared rewards must still respect what each character could learn. Those are application boundaries, not Forever findings or authorization for new mechanics. This source does not justify a new server system, universal travel prohibition or mandatory group progression.
