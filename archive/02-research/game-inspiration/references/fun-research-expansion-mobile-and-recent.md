# Beyond the dossiers — mobile play and recent games

**Research expansion, October 14, 2026.** Companion to the [main synthesis](../essays/fun-and-sustainable-engagement.md), [theory critique](fun-research-expansion-theory.md) and [AI-era research](fun-research-expansion-ai.md). These targeted cases broaden the earlier library; they are not new full-category dossiers, a representative market survey or a ranking of the best games. Official descriptions establish mechanics and intended experiences, not proof that every player enjoys them. No gameplay or video playback was performed.

## 1. Mobile is a context of participation, not a synonym for shallow design

A narrow input vocabulary can support precise execution, an expressive puzzle, meaningful uncertainty or a collection. Short activities need not have trivial decisions. Conversely, a short match does not establish a short total obligation: collection, invitations, event schedules and rewards may operate on another timescale. The following cases deliberately separate those layers rather than treating all mobile games as one retention formula.

<a id="m01"></a>
### M01 — Poinpy: one-handed action with a finish

**Source:** Devolver's [current App Store description](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/poinpy/id1529163722), checked October 14, 2026. **Inspected:** developer-supplied description, not a device test or representative review sample.

The vertical climber uses one-handed control to collect fruit, avoid threats and feed a pursuing creature. Abilities change later attempts; a puzzle mode and an endless mode provide different purposes, with the latter following completion. The current listing explicitly says play requires neither ads nor purchases, with optional developer tipping. That is a dated product declaration, not an audit of its entire distribution history.

**Our interpretation:** approachability can come from how an action is entered, not from removing every skill demand. A complete game and an optional repeat mode can coexist. For OpenLegend, the useful question is whether a core action can be performed directly and understood immediately; adopting mobile monetization or endless progress is not required to learn from mobile control design.

<a id="m02"></a>
### M02 — Monument Valley 3: perception and atmosphere are the activity

**Sources:** ustwo games' [official game page](https://ustwogames.co.uk/our-games/monument-valley-3/) and [supported-device guidance](https://ustwogames.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/23497457933341-On-which-devices-can-I-play-Monument-Valley-3), the latter dated February 11, 2026. **Inspected:** official text, not every platform build.

Noor's journey joins manipulation of perspective and architecture with sailing and a home/community premise. This is an authored visual and spatial experience, not a survival equipment ladder. Later platform information must not be replaced with an assumption that an earlier distribution arrangement remains its sole form.

**Our interpretation:** a place can reward observation, movement and aesthetic attention without becoming a material mine or a list of combat encounters. That does not prove every attractive landscape is good exploration. Ask what the player notices, can manipulate and newly understands. For OpenLegend, an unusual crossing or building can communicate a world rule through space; it need not acquire an arbitrary collectible simply to make visiting it count as progress.

<a id="m03"></a>
### M03 — Pokémon TCG Pocket: collection, play and appointment pressure are distinct

**Sources:** The Pokémon Company's [product overview](https://www.pokemon.com/uk/pokemon-video-games/pokemon-trading-card-game-pocket), checked October 14, 2026, and [Gible drop-event announcement](https://www.pokemon.com/uk/pokemon-news/pokemon-trading-card-game-pocket-gible-drop-event), March 3, 2025. **Inspected:** official descriptions; no account, purchase or pack-opening test.

The overview describes two free daily booster packs, artwork-oriented collecting, Wonder Pick, 20-card battles, and assistance including rental decks and auto-build. The historical event separately uses a limited date window, regenerating event stamina and chance-based repeat rewards. Those March 2025 event details are not asserted to be today's schedule or a universal current event rule.

**Our interpretation:** appreciating and showing a collection can be a genuine activity rather than failed competitive play. The match, collection access and appointment economy still need separate evaluation. A helpful onboarding feature does not prove every acquisition burden is benign; uncertain rewards do not by themselves diagnose harm. For OpenLegend, distinguish an expressive possession, an immediately useful tool and access to the next activity instead of assuming every reward needs a higher power number.

<a id="m04"></a>
### M04 — Marvel SNAP: changing stakes changes what players are willing to try

**Sources:** Second Dinner's [High Voltage design explanation](https://marvelsnap.com/new-limited-time-mode-high-voltage/) and [Battle Mode design account](https://staging.marvelsnap.com/battle-mode-1-0-is-coming-soon/). **Inspected:** developer text. These are historical mode accounts; their relative launch language and event deadlines are not current availability promises.

High Voltage condenses play to three turns, changes energy availability and removes Cube/snapping stakes. Its designers explicitly contrast discovering unusual combinations with formats centered on reading an opponent and retreating. Battle Mode instead retains decks across multiple rounds, making revealed information and remaining health consequential. Developer accounts of internal iteration are qualitative production evidence, not controlled population experiments.

**Our interpretation:** the same familiar pieces can support different pleasures through a changed horizon, risk and information structure. Protecting experimentation can be appropriate in one activity without abolishing consequential retreat elsewhere. However, a no-loss match can still sit within timed reward access; it does not certify the entire surrounding economy. For OpenLegend, choose which uncertainty and commitment the present activity should preserve rather than assuming one failure rule must serve every mode.

<a id="m05"></a>
### M05 — Asynchronous social play can decouple togetherness from simultaneous attendance

**Source:** [*Principles for Engineering Social Interaction in Asynchronous Mobile Games*](https://www.sciencedirect.com/org/science/article/pii/S1942388821000034), *International Journal of Gaming and Computer-Mediated Simulations* 13(3), 2021, DOI `10.4018/IJGCMS.290306`.

**Inspected:** indexed article abstract and introduction; direct rendering failed. The authors derive design guidance from launching an asynchronous question-and-answer guessing game. Their discussion concerns participation during available intervals and social ties that do not require simultaneous presence. This is a genre-specific deployed-design account, not proof that asynchronous interaction improves every social game.

**Our use:** inspect whether a player's absence actually prevents another person from enjoying the activity. Sharing a completed creation, leaving a useful result or returning to a common project are candidate forms of connection, not reasons to demand continuous attendance. This is not permission to add offline simulation or alter OpenLegend's documented pause behavior; any such feature would need its own design and authorization.

<a id="m06"></a>
### M06 — Accessibility separates the intended challenge from the barrier to entering it

**Sources:** Microsoft's [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 107: Input](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/107) and [108: Game Difficulty Options](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/108), checked October 14, 2026. **Inspected:** guideline text, including mobile input provisions. **Type:** expert/community-informed design guidance, not an enjoyment experiment or legal certification checklist.

XAG 107 addresses alternative input mechanisms and mobile-native assistance, rather than assuming every person can perform the same gesture. XAG 108 treats experienced difficulty as the relation between a person's capabilities and the barriers presented. Changing a motor or perceptual demand need not remove a tactical or interpretive problem.

**Our use:** identify what a challenge is meant to test before selecting an aid. Timed aiming, recognizing a threat, planning a route and reading dialogue impose different demands. A larger target can improve access without revealing a puzzle's solution; optional non-audio feedback can communicate a threat without making it harmless. This does not import every platform recommendation or a fixed number of difficulty presets as an OpenLegend requirement.

<a id="mobile-lessons"></a>
## 2. Mobile lessons worth transferring

These are **our design hypotheses**, informed by M01–M06 rather than universal findings about phone users.

**Minimize interaction overhead, not necessarily strategic depth.** A player should not need a long text composition to perform an ordinary action. Reserve richer input for an intention that benefits from it. A compact action can still have a large consequence; short input is not synonymous with automatic success.

**Make re-entry inexpensive.** After an interruption, the player should be able to recover their purpose, immediate circumstances and remaining commitment without rereading a long transcript. A recognizable project and concise relevant history may help. This concerns communicating already-supported state, not automatically advancing the world while absent.

**Design for silent and constrained contexts.** Voice can be expressive but unavailable in public, inaccessible to some players, or simply unwanted. A phone-sized panel is not evidence that every action is touch-usable. Inspect targeting, scrolling, readable text, accidental activation and whether information survives without sound or a hover. These are proposed evaluation conditions, not claims that OpenLegend already passes them.

**Offer meaningful small completions.** A local puzzle, conversation, useful craft or exploration insight can conclude inside a larger journey. Do not create an artificial timer solely to force that shape. The player can choose another activity without every completed unit immediately generating another obligation.

**Keep the activity and its business/access system separately legible.** Collection, cosmetic expression and routine can be legitimate values. Test whether the person endorses the recurring commitment and understands any costs, rather than inferring approval from daily attendance. A quiet optional visit should not be scored as inferior to an anxious repeated check. [Incentive analysis](fun-research-expansion-theory.md#t09)

<a id="recent-games"></a>
## 3. Additional 2025–2026 games that challenge or refine the synthesis

These cases were chosen for contrasts, not because release recency or review scores prove quality. Each is outside the earlier 148-subject evidence map. Release facts come from official storefronts or the developer's current press material; promotional claims such as inexhaustible novelty are not adopted.

<a id="c01"></a>
### C01 — Blue Prince: variable routes around learnable pieces

**Sources:** Dogubomb/Raw Fury's [official game description](https://www.blueprincegame.com/) and [Steam release record](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1569580/Blue_Prince/), 2025 release. **Inspected:** product text; no puzzle solution walkthrough or playthrough.

The player chooses rooms behind closed doors while searching a shifting manor. The floor plan resets between days; selected permanent estate upgrades have a different persistence horizon. Room choice and tools shape the route.

**Our interpretation:** variability can renew planning while knowledge of pieces remains valuable. The useful contrast is between a changing arrangement and a world whose rules arbitrarily change when relied upon. A reset can also delay a player's preferred investigation; this is a risk to examine, not a complaint attributed to an uninspected reviewer. For OpenLegend, a new situation using an already learned invention is a better initial test than generating an unrelated new rule at every obstacle. The mystery premise is retained without revealing its solutions.

<a id="c02"></a>
### C02 — Is This Seat Taken?: understandable people as a compact constraint problem

**Source:** Poti Poti Studio/Wholesome Games Presents, [Steam product description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3035120/Is_This_Seat_Taken/), released August 7, 2025. **Inspected:** developer description, not a representative reception study.

People have recognizable preferences and aversions; arranging them at a cinema, on transport or at a gathering creates a logic puzzle. The advertised puzzle mode has no timers or leaderboards. The cast's particularity is the problem, not an enormous simulated biography.

**Our interpretation:** modest combinations can become compelling when their meaning is immediately legible. Social detail can explain a constraint without requiring an autonomous society. OpenLegend should not reduce every person to a puzzle to optimize, but this case suggests testing whether one understandable preference changes a practical encounter before expanding hidden personality variables. A richly acting NPC and a compact authored puzzle remain different achievements.

<a id="c03"></a>
### C03 — Baby Steps: intentional control difficulty is not an interface accident

**Sources:** Maxi Boch's [developer account](https://blog.playstation.com/2023/09/14/wonderfully-weird-walking-sim-baby-steps-toddles-onto-playstation-next-year/), September 2023; [current PlayStation product record](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/concept/10008588/), September 23, 2025 release. The old announcement's 2024 target is not treated as its actual release date.

The player deliberately lifts and places individual feet; learning to walk over terrain is central. Boch connects this to physical comedy and progressing from isolated steps toward a hiking rhythm. **Our interpretation:** automating the difficulty could remove the intended performance. This is not a justification for missed clicks, illegible feedback or accidental navigation failures in another game. For OpenLegend, routine movement should support its adventure unless an explicitly chosen activity makes precise bodily performance the point. Mechanical complexity must serve the intended experience rather than borrow prestige from a difficult game.

<a id="c04"></a>
### C04 — Wanderstop: a game can question the desire to optimize

**Sources:** Ivy Road's [press kit](https://www.wanderstopgame.com/press-kit), March 11, 2025 release, and Jenny Windom's [developer article](https://blog.playstation.com/2024/12/05/wanderstop-a-cozy-game-about-change-and-tea-coming-to-ps5-march-11-2025/), December 5, 2024. **Inspected:** written accounts, not the embedded video.

Alta is a fighter uneasy with tending a tea shop. Growing, brewing, talking, decorating and sometimes doing nothing support an authored tension around rest and relentless growth. The team describes personal experiences with burnout as inspiration, not a clinical treatment claim.

**Our interpretation:** an inconvenient or uncomfortable activity can contribute to expression when the whole experience makes that purpose intelligible. Neither “cozy” nor a calm-looking setting guarantees that every participant feels relaxed. For OpenLegend, home and ordinary companionship need not exist only to raise combat readiness; equally, this reference does not justify forcing domestic administration before the adventure the product promises.

<a id="c05"></a>
### C05 — Mewgenics: variation can come from inherited consequences

**Source:** Edmund McMillen and Tyler Glaiel's [Steam product description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/686060/Mewgenics/), released February 10, 2026. **Inspected:** developer description and release metadata; no current balance audit or gameplay.

Teams use class-linked abilities in positional combat. Returning cats carry experience and bodily changes, while breeding connects one expedition to future teams. Home decisions also compete with retaining particular cats. The listing's large content counts and “never the same” rhetoric are not taken as proof of endless meaningful variety.

**Our interpretation:** lasting consequences can renew composition without preserving one perfect individual forever. The attraction might be tactical recombination, attachment, comedy or a mixture; these require player evidence rather than inference from sales or a genre label. OpenLegend can test a small changed relationship or capability affecting a second encounter without first implementing breeding, dynasties or hundreds of mutations. This is a 2026 design reference, not evidence that runtime generative AI is required for contemporary novelty.

## 4. What these cases change in the central argument

The main essay's pluralism should do practical work. **Poinpy** changes the relationship between input simplicity and mastery; **Monument Valley 3** foregrounds perceptual and aesthetic pleasure; **Pocket** separates collection from battle and attendance; **SNAP's modes** separate speculative combinations from repeated-opponent stakes; **Blue Prince** distinguishes variable layouts from learned rules; **Is This Seat Taken?** makes small understandable constraints expressive; **Baby Steps** turns a particular friction into performance; **Wanderstop** questions relentless growth; **Mewgenics** renews composition through consequence. These are our comparative readings, not causal verdicts about commercial success.

A better question than “how many interesting choices are there?” is: **Which kind of participation is desirable here, what makes it possible, and which burdens do not contribute?** Sometimes the answer is a strategic alternative; sometimes it is a gesture, an amusing failure, an image, an act of care or a clean ending. Choice and progression remain useful tools, not universal units of fun.

## 5. OpenLegend application and limits

The current [product-priority snapshot](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/1700f3b23a39acfdd14f3a6ab34c8ce330847bc3/docs/repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md) asks for a complete adventure with a real invention and consequential character, not a sequence of household prerequisites. Preserve that center of gravity. The [README snapshot](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/1700f3b23a39acfdd14f3a6ab34c8ce330847bc3/README.md) documents narrow-screen panels and some presentation preferences; those are not evidence of a fully qualified mobile experience.

Proposed observations are concrete: can a player inspect a threat, choose and perform an action without a hover; identify why a tool or companion helped; complete a small worthwhile activity; stop; and recover their purpose later? Do they value a quiet place without an attached bonus? Does a second encounter let them reuse what they learned? Does a useful aid preserve the intended problem rather than solve it for them? These questions extend the existing evaluation target, not the implementation backlog.

**Evidence limits:** most game cases use developer material, with its marketing incentives and incomplete edge-case rules. They do not establish average enjoyment, effectiveness of particular onboarding, current monetization costs, or accessibility in every build. The asynchronous study was read through indexed sections and is not generalized to every mobile genre. Platform guidance is not proof of measured player benefit in OpenLegend. No mobile port, service test, clinical result or commercial forecast is claimed.
