# Annotated source register

Original research: **September 22, 2026**. Expanded pass: **September 23, 2026**. Companion to `research-brief.md`. The original 55 entries retain their access notes; 69 additional entries below specify the new evidence. This is not a scraped representative player-review dataset or a claim to have watched every linked talk. Accessible excerpts and secondary-only limits are marked.

**How to read the evidence:** official pages establish supported mechanics and announced outcomes; developer interviews establish their account of intent/tradeoffs; original criticism and player stories establish particular reported experiences. None alone identifies the causal contribution of an individual feature to sales or retention.

Historical milestone dates are preserved. Current store pages are snapshots, not stable measurements. Financial figures use the report’s accounting categories. No sales are estimated from review counts.
