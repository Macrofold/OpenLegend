## 4. Economic models are different design constraints

| Model | Examples | Product advantage | Ongoing obligation / risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Premium finite experience | Hades, BG3 | A satisfying ending can be success; no need for infinite daily attendance | Production must be recovered through units and pricing; post-launch sales are not guaranteed |
| Premium long-tail sandbox | Minecraft, Terraria, Stardew, Zomboid | Projects, mods, friends and updates can reactivate ownership | Ongoing support and compatibility expectations; old owners do not automatically generate fresh revenue |
| Premium survival/co-op breakout | Palworld, PEAK, Core Keeper | Clear invitation and visible shared progress | Viral traffic, support, content expectations and technical load can arrive together |
| Recurring character/build cycles | Path of Exile, Diablo | New seasons create shared return moments and fresh choices | Content cadence, balance trust and commercial friction |
| Competitive free-entry ecosystem | League, Dota | Low entry barrier, repeat mastery and existing groups | Match quality, onboarding, community safety, service operation |
| Creator/social platform | Roblox, Fortnite | Content and audience can reinforce each other | Discovery, creator economics, moderation, trust, safety and infrastructure |

The model labels are analytical groupings, not a claim that each example uses only one revenue stream. Never derive net profit by multiplying current players or reviews by list price. Regional prices, refunds, taxes, platform shares, bundles, subscriptions, sales and business costs all intervene.

## 5. What data would materially improve the decision

For OpenLegend, the missing high-value evidence is: target-audience first-session completion, voluntary return cohorts, actual costs per meaningful interaction, friend-invitation conversion, successful pack reuse, and whether the preferred camera prevents or helps comprehension. Public competitor peaks cannot supply those answers.

Use commercial research as a constraint on the hypothesis, not a substitute for playing and observing the product. None of these data selects a pricing model or authorizes spending by itself.
