## Audit standard and limits

A completed label, filename, section heading or requirement map is not proof of substantive coverage. The audit inspected the mechanics, examples, production, commercial definitions, actual review summaries and source/access notes. It distinguishes missing research from legitimately inaccessible evidence. Material disputed/version-sensitive claims received selected external checks; this is not a new playtest or an exhaustive reread of every external source.

The previous range verification explicitly said it was not a fresh factual re-audit of G40–G58. This audit addresses manuscript coverage across that range rather than trusting its inherited labels. Previous completion commits and source qualifications remain historical evidence, not automatic passes. Recording a correction here does **not** mean its canonical dossier has already been edited.

The standard does not require every statistic, every item, private budgets, proprietary code or a fabricated representative survey. It does require enough explanation to understand the principal activity, its prerequisites, resources, consequences and limits. Similarly, five critic names or links to an aggregate are not five substantive review bodies. A genuinely substantive unscored essay can qualify when its type and scope are clear. A documented failed Steam attempt plus strong alternative player testimony is different from never attempting the required helpful surface.

The dossier covers capture, work, needs, base production, breeding, technology, traversal, combat, multiplayer, current-versus-launch reception and dated commercial definitions. However, the following require correction or deeper evidence:

- **R09/R14:** section 20's later GDC/human-cost statement points to the January 2024 producer interview. The older chapter already preserves the intended source as [PAL3](sources-03.md#source-pal3): Game Developer's March 17, 2025 GDC reporting, not the 2024 interview. Restore that attribution without describing the GDC account as a fresh gameplay test.
- **R05 correction now supported:** case B's generic furnace “fuel” premise should be replaced with a named recipe and actual work requirement. This is a community data reference, explicitly unaffiliated with Pocketpair, not proprietary source-code verification.

The fresh PC Gamer body confirms the July 10, 2026 1.0 date; it would be wrong to reject that edition merely from older model knowledge. Earlier partner-skill examples, the Mammorest encounter and historical worker-assignment complaints remain in the preserved mechanics owner; they need not be duplicated wholesale.
