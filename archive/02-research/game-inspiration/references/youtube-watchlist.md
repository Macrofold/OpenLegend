# YouTube viewing guide for OpenLegend research

**Selection date: September 23, 2026.** Companion to the [master brief](../README.md) and [game field guide](field-guide.md).

## Verification and viewing policy

These 28 direct recommendations cover 22 games. Search-indexed titles/descriptions or official-page links establish video identity and relevance; **full videos and transcripts were not reviewed**. The prompts are questions for your viewing, not verified claims about particular scenes. No invented durations, timestamps, or clip-by-clip analyses are supplied. Availability, regional access, and age restrictions can change.

A trailer gives efficient orientation but understates troubleshooting. A tutorial exposes actual controls and work but can overstate how easily a newcomer understands them. An edited playthrough shows possible stories but not their frequency. Read each selection accordingly. Gameplay mods, expansions, historical versions, and spoilers are marked where relevant or not independently established.
