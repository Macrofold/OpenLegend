**Use and limitation:** More than four billion related views in September 2020, roughly two years after release; creator sizes, formats and geographic spread. Primary viewership evidence, not unique-player counts or sales attribution.

## Research limits and follow-up evidence that would improve confidence

The biggest missing datasets are comparable retention cohorts, actual newcomer onboarding recordings, marketing attribution by channel, creator earnings distributions net of labor, and production budgets. Public store reviews do not replace these.

The research deliberately avoids unsupported creator-revenue promises, rumored sales totals, the claim that every canceled project was intrinsically unfun, and the claim that a highly reviewed game serves all player types. Sources with inaccessible primary archives are identified as contemporaneous reporting instead.

For OpenLegend, the highest-value next evidence is observed player behavior in a small playable world—not another market-size estimate. The final section of the brief specifies falsifiable product tests.
