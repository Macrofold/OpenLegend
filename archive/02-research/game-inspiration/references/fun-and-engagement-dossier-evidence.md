| ✓ G14 | [Project Spark](../dossiers/project-spark.md), §§5–10 | An example campaign and editable behaviors can teach a practical creation vocabulary. Historical mechanics and constructed examples are not evidence of a currently operating service. |
| ✓ G16 | [Spore](../dossiers/spore.md), §§8–9 and detailed study 5 | Authored forms, layout constraints and different acquisition methods support expression and planning. A solved repeated layout can lose its decision value. |
| ✓ G17 | [No Man's Sky](../dossiers/no-mans-sky.md), §§3–5 | Place-specific preparation and technology access can make exploration purposeful; separate difficulty dimensions permit different desired levels of upkeep and risk. | Constant refueling can interrupt the exploration fantasy. | ✓ G18 | [EverQuest Next](../dossiers/everquest-next.md), §§4–7 | Its proposed discoveries, faction-linked learning and limited active set illustrate a hypothesis: broad world possibilities can coexist with bounded moment-to-moment choices. | **Unreleased design proposal**, not observed enjoyment or retention. Complete crafting, loot and endgame outcomes were never established; ambition must not be counted as successful execution. |
| ✓ G19 | [Ultima Online](../dossiers/ultima-online.md), §§3–6 | Practicing a relevant action can make becoming a specialist legible; noncombat capabilities give people useful identities beyond a combat level. | Use-based growth can reward repetitive training or macro-like routines instead of enjoyable practice. Shared PvE/PvP abilities also create consent and balance tradeoffs. |
| ✓ G20 | [Minecraft](../dossiers/minecraft.md), §§3–6 | Identity can grow from projects and expertise rather than a class label. Material progress changes which parts of the world can be manipulated; compact survival rules supply concrete reasons to prepare. | More physiological detail is not automatically more interesting. Automation and creator authority should not silently take over the embodied player's purpose. |
