## G01–G20 — action, social play, creator platforms and systemic progression

| Read | Subject and dossier | Contribution to the synthesis | Counterweight / evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ✓ G01 | [Hades II](../dossiers/hades-ii.md), §§1–9, 12–14 | Handling differences, limited active configurations, relationship-linked objects and specific callbacks make repeated attempts meaningfully different. Failure can advance a chosen home project without pretending the attempt succeeded. | A material checklist, changed handling or unsatisfying ending can weaken an otherwise loved game. More dialogue and more rewards do not automatically resolve those objections. |
| ✓ G02 | [Among Us](../dossiers/among-us.md), §§9–14 | A small predicament plus unequal evidence and responsibilities lets people create fresh stories. Matching players to compatible group rules is part of delivering the fun. | More roles cannot supply friends or repair an unwelcome lobby. A bounded betrayal round does not justify the same loss policy for a long-lived personal world. |
| ✓ G03 | [Animal Crossing: New Horizons](../dossiers/animal-crossing-new-horizons.md), §§6–14 | Collections become places; furniture, letters and gradual renovation support personal meaning. Guidance can expose possibilities while leaving room to stop optimizing a rating. | Decorating is not every player's favorite activity. Cozy presentation can coexist with tedious interactions; an occasional return can still fulfill the purchase's promise. |
| ✓ G04 | [Roblox](../dossiers/roblox.md), especially §§2, 7, 12–14 | Separate the platform's discovery/compensation incentives from a particular game's value. The supplied transcript contrasts strategy and authored surprise with hollow catch-up rewards and pressure not to leave. | Platform scale, video views and the narrator's session length are not measurements of fun or clinical addiction. Experience-specific rules do not become universal Roblox behavior. |
| ✓ G05 | [Grow a Garden](../dossiers/grow-a-garden.md), §§7–13 | A collection, useful helpers, ordinary companionship and shared occasions can provide different reasons to return. Abundance needs a purpose beyond another inflated cost. | Some players enjoy quiet conversation without chasing stock; others find only waiting after solving the economy. Neither experience can be inferred from an event attendance peak. |
| ✓ G06 | [Steal a Brainrot](../dossiers/steal-a-brainrot.md), §§7–13 | Ownership changes, escape, interference and negotiated stakes generate legible social episodes. Consent changes the meaning of otherwise similar losses. | A critic's valued possessions discouraged returning because they were vulnerable. Attachment may produce avoidance, not more attendance; spectacle can also become uncomfortable. |
| ✓ G07 | [Fortnite](../dossiers/fortnite.md), §§9–15 | Different finished activities and social routes fit different motivations. A useful reusable device still needs an appealing activity around it; discovery and account rewards are separate systems. | Pass completion, an event visit and returning to play with friends can look alike in playtime. Tool compatibility, operating viability and enduring player value are not interchangeable. |
| ✓ G08 | [League of Legends](../dossiers/league-of-legends.md), §§8–16 | Mastery, recognizable character identities, scoped information and complementary abilities can sustain repeated contests without persistent match power. | Knowledge burden and dependence on hostile or uncooperative teammates can coexist with excellent mechanics. Feeling trapped in a long match is not evidence of enjoyment. |
| ✓ G09 | [Dota 2](../dossiers/dota-2.md), §§7–16 | Multipurpose resources, abilities with explicit dependencies and costly rescue opportunities create consequential combinations and shared learning. | Depth can be socially or cognitively inaccessible. Valve's production shift away from pass-centered work illustrates that reward production can compete with improving the underlying activity. |
| ✓ G10 | [Path of Exile](../dossiers/path-of-exile.md), §§7–11 | Actions as items, rule-changing upgrades and currencies with executable uses make loot relevant to a build. Trade and self-found play give the same drop different meanings. | First-time discovery can become a repeated prerequisite. A new endgame interface or a balance change can slow familiar work or disrupt a learned method. |
| ✓ G11 | [Path of Exile 2](../dossiers/path-of-exile-2.md), §§5–12 | Crafting, configuration, risk and retry opportunities form distinct commitments. Allowing a trial to be paused can remove a scheduling burden without removing its tactical problem. | Preserving a character record is not preserving its effective build. Deliberate combat differs from opaque punishment; edition and Early Access boundaries remain explicit. |
| ✓ G12 | [Diablo IV](../dossiers/diablo-iv.md), §§11–16 | Build identity, atmospheric contrast, explicit seasonal restart and chosen activity chains supply several forms of engagement. Returning with a familiar identity need not preserve all power. | Forced activity, expansion ownership, repeated investment and access failures are different objections. More loot does not fix each; finite campaign completion is not automatic failure. |
| ✓ G13 | [Dreams](../dossiers/dreams.md), §§8–11 and detailed studies 6, 8, 12, 14 | Playable examples, small shared projects, visible budgets and specialization help creativity become an achievable contribution. Discovery gives that contribution a destination. | Expressive tools can coexist with a noisy audience experience. Permission, curation, compatibility and support affect whether investment feels safe or worthwhile. |
| ✓ G14 | [Project Spark](../dossiers/project-spark.md), §§5–10 | An example campaign and editable behaviors can teach a practical creation vocabulary. A contributor's useful project need not be an entire game. | Learning to express an idea is different from entitlement gates that withhold its basic grammar. A technical demonstration can disappoint someone seeking an adventure. |
| ✓ G15 | [Worlds Adrift](../dossiers/worlds-adrift.md), §§5–7 and detailed study 4 | A ship joins construction, preparation, navigation, repair and teamwork. Local component damage and material tradeoffs change actual capabilities, supporting distinctive expedition stories. | Knowledge points differ from practical understanding; scanning can become a checklist. Historical mechanics and constructed examples are not evidence of a currently operating service. |
| ✓ G16 | [Spore](../dossiers/spore.md), §§8–9 and detailed study 5 | Authored forms, layout constraints and different acquisition methods support expression and planning. Earlier behavior can unlock a different action vocabulary. | Visual freedom can exceed mechanical difference: spacecraft appearance and operational tools have separate rules. A solved repeated layout can lose its decision value. |
| ✓ G17 | [No Man's Sky](../dossiers/no-mans-sky.md), §§3–5 | Place-specific preparation and technology access can make exploration purposeful; separate difficulty dimensions permit different desired levels of upkeep and risk. | Constant refueling can interrupt the exploration fantasy. Reducing it does not erase all challenge; a single power number cannot describe orthogonal tools and projects. |
| ✓ G18 | [EverQuest Next](../dossiers/everquest-next.md), §§4–7 | Its proposed discoveries, faction-linked learning and limited active set illustrate a hypothesis: broad world possibilities can coexist with bounded moment-to-moment choices. | **Unreleased design proposal**, not observed enjoyment or retention. Complete crafting, loot and endgame outcomes were never established; ambition must not be counted as successful execution. |
| ✓ G19 | [Ultima Online](../dossiers/ultima-online.md), §§3–6 | Practicing a relevant action can make becoming a specialist legible; noncombat capabilities give people useful identities beyond a combat level. | Use-based growth can reward repetitive training or macro-like routines instead of enjoyable practice. Shared PvE/PvP abilities also create consent and balance tradeoffs. |
| ✓ G20 | [Minecraft](../dossiers/minecraft.md), §§3–6 | Identity can grow from projects and expertise rather than a class label. Material progress changes which parts of the world can be manipulated; compact survival rules supply concrete reasons to prepare. | More physiological detail is not automatically more interesting. Automation and creator authority should not silently take over the embodied player's purpose. |
