## Evidence limits

The complete supplied transcript was read; direct YouTube retrieval failed. No footage was watched and no timestamps are inferred. This is source research, not a report of firsthand gameplay, installed mods or executed authoring workflows.

Published sales disclosures, publisher reach claims, crowdfunding, modeled Steam spending, studio receipts and profit remain distinct. Unknown costs and contracts prevent many profitability conclusions. Player samples establish specific experiences and disagreements, not their prevalence or causal effects on sales. Those evidence boundaries do not leave an assigned research category unfinished.
