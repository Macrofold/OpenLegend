## Method and evidence boundaries

Apply [R01–R14 and conditional R15](research-requirements.md) plus the [economics/enjoyment/lore/creation amendment](research-economics-engagement-and-creation.md). Finish and commit each subject before starting the next. Specialist researchers may investigate independent dimensions of the same active game. Five substantive independent written reviews are required where genuine reviews exist; previews, community testimony and inaccessible sources retain their actual labels. Steam reviews are qualitative samples unless an explicit sampling method supports a count.

Distinguish released systems, announcements, deprecated rules and observed failures. Separate funding, paid units, active accounts, concurrent connections, gross spending, developer receipts and profit. Steam-only activity cannot rank the entire audience of a multi-launcher or non-Steam service. A lower population is not automatically commercial failure. Hypotheses about causes are labeled as analysis.
