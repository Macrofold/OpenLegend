# Resident Evil Requiem / Resident Evil 9 — research dossier

> G195. Research checkpoint, 22 November 2026. This file is in progress; mechanics, cross-system examples, source review, and the final coverage map are still being completed. It is not yet marked complete in the research ledger.

## Identity and scope

Resident Evil Requiem is the ninth mainline Resident Evil game, developed and published by Capcom. Its campaign alternates between Grace Ashcroft’s survival horror and Leon S. Kennedy’s combat, with investigation, puzzles, and resource management joining the two. This dossier distinguishes the launch campaign from subsequent updates, especially the free Leon Must Die Forever mode; it also separates official modes from community mods. [Official product page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/resident-evil-requiem/).

## Story, people and the past encountered through play

**Research checkpoint; November 22, 2026.** Opening spoilers below; the completed section will label major revelations separately and distinguish in-world reports from established events.

**Grace Ashcroft** is an FBI analyst investigating deaths at the abandoned **Wrenwood Hotel**, where her journalist mother **Alyssa** was murdered. Alyssa survived Raccoon City and continued investigating Umbrella. **Leon S. Kennedy**, now a veteran DSO agent, approaches the same mystery with decades of outbreak experience. These backgrounds place personal bereavement beside a surviving professional's accumulated burden. [Official character descriptions](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/resident-evil-requiem/)

## Presentation and accessibility

### Faces and hair carry the protagonists' contrast

Capcom's Masaru Ijuin describes automatically changing hair segmentation according to its projected screen length, improving near-camera curves without spending equal work on distant strands. The hair simulation was also rewritten to give artists more control. Skin, eyes and teeth received related improvements, including subsurface scattering and greater polygon density. The interview ties these advances to the visible difference between Grace's youthful skin and Leon's weathered face. This is a concrete rendering explanation, not an independent performance benchmark. [AUTOMATON's original Capcom/Sony interview, June 12, 2026](https://automaton-media.com/en/interviews/why-was-resident-evil-requiem-the-first-game-to-adopt-the-ps5-pros-upgraded-pssr-we-talk-to-capcom-and-sie/)

### Camera choice helps, with important exceptions

Laura Dale's March 6 accessibility review describes choosing either perspective before play and substantially less motion sickness with third-person Grace. However, selected cutscenes, combat and repeated-input sequences force first person, including the hotel escape and an early police-officer encounter. Therefore, support for both perspectives should not become an unqualified promise that every moment respects the chosen camera. This is a reported access experience, not a guarantee for all players. [Access-Ability review](https://access-ability.uk/2026/03/06/resident-evil-9-accessibility-review-access-ability/?amp=1)

Can I Play That's May 11 PC review finds remappable keyboard/controller inputs, stick sensitivity/deadzones, hold alternatives, automatic walk/run, adjustable aim snapping/following, subtitle backgrounds, speaker names and captions. Its specific limitations include unavailable menu narration and a mouse wheel that remained tied to weapon switching. Motion-blur and camera-wobble adjustments, HUD scaling and a persistent center dot offer separate ways to reduce strain. These are useful options with concrete gaps; the favorable verdict is the reviewer's assessment. [Mike Matlock's accessibility review](https://caniplaythat.com/2026/05/11/resident-evil-requiem-accessibility-review/)

The official title is **Resident Evil Requiem**, Steam application [3764200](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3764200/Resident_Evil_Requiem/). This section examines original accounts rather than promotional extracts or an aggregate score. The initial critic accounts concern launch review builds and cannot certify the state of every platform after later updates.

### What the disagreement explains

**A strong activity can lose its appeal when its context stops changing.** Push Square's complaint is not that Leon's combat is poorly made: sustained action eventually weakened the rhythm that previously made it welcome. The transferable Open Legend question is whether intense sequences, traversal, conversation and quiet companionship give each other room to matter. Alternation should serve the experience rather than become a rigid scheduling rule. ([Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/reviews/ps5/resident-evil-requiem))

**Surprise and mastery provide different reasons to return.** GamesRadar documents a second run becoming more fluent while less emotionally overwhelming. For Open Legend, a moving discovery should be assessed separately from a repeatable decision space. A scene may succeed completely on first encounter without needing to remain shocking indefinitely; repeat enjoyment can instead come from interpretation, relationships or more expressive execution. That is a design hypothesis, not an instruction to add replay systems. ([GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/resident-evil/resident-evil-requiem-review/))

**Clarity matters when the game changes what success means.** GameSpot describes deaths caused by unknowingly blocking a necessary exit in an encounter that otherwise encouraged environmental destruction. The lesson is to make the relevant constraint intelligible before punishing the player's plausible interpretation. Failure that reveals a tactical mistake and failure that finally discloses the objective are different learning experiences. ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/resident-evil-requiem-review-two-headed-mutant/1900-6418464/))

**A rescue option changes perceived danger before it is used.** VGC felt safer merely carrying the powerful revolver. The analogous question is whether a reserve ability should protect the player from intolerable frustration, preserve a sense of vulnerability, or do some of both. Its psychological effect depends on what the player believes it can solve, as well as its actual resource cost. ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/review/resident-evil-requiem-review/))

**The recommendation button conceals different experiences.** Meds wants harder decisions and demonstrates an actual return to play, while also criticizing puzzles and pacing. ♥ItsV can appreciate individual performances yet dislike how the surrounding tone undermines them. Neither recommendation direction tells us which ingredients succeeded. For Open Legend, the useful question is what the player wanted to keep doing, what interrupted that desire, and whether the cause was the activity itself, its ordering, or a mismatch with the promise that drew them in. ([Meds](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561199258081218/recommended/3764200); [♥ItsV](https://steamcommunity.com/id/ItsVeee/recommended/3764200/))

The sample supports enjoyment, boredom, replay attempts and reluctance to return. It **does not establish a rate of abandonment or retention**. Most of these players accumulated substantial recorded hours; that can include replay, idling, retries or additional modes. A negative recommendation is not evidence of quitting, just as a long playtime is not proof of satisfaction. Claims that somebody stopped before the ending require an explicit statement or a defined behavioral measure that these records do not consistently provide.

### Launch experience versus the November game

Official notices materially qualify the launch accounts. **March 26** added photo mode and reported progress-blocking, crash and driver-related visual fixes. **May 7** added **Leon Must Die Forever**, unlocked after completing the story, plus PC DualSense functions. **June 24** adjusted that mode's lower Forever ranks and strengthened selected abilities. Consequently, a launch complaint that there is no additional mode cannot describe the November feature set. These notices establish announced changes, not how much any particular player's enjoyment or reliability improved. ([Capcom's Steam announcements](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3764200/allnews/), accessed November 22, 2026.)

A campaign pacing criticism and an inability to launch offline also require different follow-up. The former asks how the game structures experience; the latter needs a reproducible platform, build and connectivity check. Sasha Nexis's review supplies a dated allegation, not proof of a permanent always-online requirement. No current offline launch test was performed for this dossier. Likewise, official crash fixes do not establish that every configuration is now free of crashes. ([Sasha Nexis in Steam's negative listing](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3764200/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated); [official updates](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3764200/allnews/))

### Evidence boundary

The review table is an annotated source register: original author, exposed date, platform, available play context, and concrete praise and criticism. These are five separately authored editorial accounts; some outlets share owners, so they are not five wholly independent corporate samples. All were accessed November 22, 2026. Their scores are attributed judgments, not inputs to a new average. Individual completion times differ in counting method and playstyle and should not be turned into an average duration.

All ten player bodies were read, with adjacent source links and available date, hours and helpfulness metadata recorded above. The November account supplies a later opinion, but publication date alone does not prove the player revisited the newest build. Exact current executable versions are not established by this reception sample. Its strongest conclusion is about contrasting experiences: carefully placed vulnerability, agency and recognition can sustain engagement, while repetition, tonal interruption or unclear encounter requirements can weaken it. The application hypotheses remain research inputs, not adopted Open Legend design decisions.


## Production, iteration and support

**Research cutoff: November 22, 2026.** The requested “Resident Evil 9” is Capcom's released **Resident Evil Requiem**, launched February 27, 2026. Capcom's March announcement identifies its proprietary **RE ENGINE** and the combination of two protagonists and selectable viewpoints. This is a premium entry within an established franchise and publisher infrastructure, not an independent team's first product. [Capcom, March 4](https://www.capcom.co.jp/ir/english/news/html/e260304.html)

Director **Koshi Nakanishi** dates small-scale work to roughly six years before January 2026 and the present design to about three years before that interview. He frames the project as a return toward the main series storyline after Ethan's arc. Development duration therefore includes exploration, not six identical years of full production. [Original director interview, January 29](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=414601)

The development process included discarded online/open-world experiments, according to Nakanishi's creator message as reported by VGC. Producer **Masachika Kawata** presented the intended product as single-player/offline. The extended original video was restricted to the Capcom portal membership route, so the abandoned-prototype account is attributed reporting rather than a video personally inspected for this dossier. It demonstrates iteration and scope rejection; it does not establish a surviving multiplayer feature. [VGC's June 27, 2025 report](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/capcom-reveals-resident-evil-requiem-started-as-an-online-game-but-we-realised-fans-didnt-want-it/)

Capcom's engine team describes two years of rendering work shared with PRAGMATA: build a reference path tracer, compare it with offline rendering tools, then optimize a game version around DLSS reconstruction. Technical artists coordinated integration and surfaced content problems across teams. The production lesson is that rendering advances require authoring practices and expertise, as well as algorithms. Hardware-intensive options are not evidence that every platform renders identically. [Original NVIDIA/RE ENGINE Q&A, July 16](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/?p=119888)

Support is already delivered, rather than solely promised: official Steam notices record a March photo mode/fixes update and the May **Leon Must Die Forever** minigame/controller update. The main mechanics section covers their rules. A separate story expansion was announced in March; an announcement must not be counted as released content without a dated release record. [Official Steam notices](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3764200/allnews/) · [Announcement reproducing the director's message](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/03/resident-evil-requiem-content-updates-story-expansion-announced)

No verified title-specific budget, full production headcount, outsourcing allocation or independent labor audit was found. Corporate profitability and RE ENGINE reuse do not reveal this project's cost or staff conditions. **Production inference:** Requiem's useful precedent is a team rejecting a less coherent direction, then combining contrasting player experiences inside a common production system. OpenLegend should study the decision boundaries, not inherit Capcom's financial or staffing assumptions.

## Distribution, marketing and creator exposure

The campaign progressed from the June 2025 announcement to public hands-on exposure: Capcom says Gamescom 2025 hosted the game's first playable demo and awarded it four honors. That establishes an event demo, not a home-downloadable prerelease build. [Capcom's June announcement](https://www.capcom.co.jp/ir/english/news/html/e250609.html) · [Capcom's August 25 Gamescom record](https://www.capcom.co.jp/ir/english/news/html/e250825.html)

The January 15, 2026 showcase combined gameplay with Porsche and Hamilton collaborations, PC graphics positioning and GeForce NOW availability. Capcom's press material lists PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2, Steam and Epic at launch; cloud play requires the separate service membership and a supported store copy. The collaboration vehicles/watches and anniversary concerts extend visibility beyond the core game's storefront. They do not establish a measurable acquisition cost or conversion uplift. [Publisher showcase release](https://www.gamespress.com/it/Resident-Evil-Showcase-Uncovers-a-Deeper-Look-at-the-Terror-and-Action)

The public demo story changed after release: the June 3 Steam notice confirms a downloadable demo existed and explains replacement of a Japanese build accidentally distributed globally in May. Retrospective accounts should not repeat the earlier “no public demo planned” claim as the current state. [Official demo correction](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3764200/allnews/)

Creator exposure was substantial. Streams Charts reports **43.69 million hours watched across covered livestreaming services during the release week**, making Requiem its largest new launch in the first half of 2026 by that measure. These are viewing hours rather than unique people, purchases or proof of an advertising contract. The campaign's horror reactions, recognizable characters and replay challenges plausibly fit streaming, but causal contribution to sales remains unknown. [Original streaming analysis](https://streamscharts.com/news/top-h1-2026-game-launches-publisher)

The commercial proposition combines a returning action protagonist with a vulnerable newcomer, giving longtime fans recognition and new players an entry point. **Analysis:** contrasting protagonists make a large sequel easier to explain without asking a trailer to teach every mechanic. This is a plausible acquisition advantage, not a controlled comparison. No verified title-specific marketing spend, paid-versus-organic creator split, wishlist conversion cohort or customer-acquisition cost was identified.
