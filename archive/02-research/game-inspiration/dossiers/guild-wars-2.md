# Guild Wars 2 — enduring capability, cooperative discovery and chosen goals

**G189 · Research date: October 26, 2026.** [Scoped ledger](../research-progress-mmos-2026-10-26.md). ArenaNet's MMO offers a persistent account, distinctive movement and spontaneous cooperation across an expanding authored world. It is a strong positive case for preserving useful accomplishments while giving players new reasons to explore. Stable equipment does not eliminate preparation, exclusion or tedious work.

## Current service and persistence boundary

Guild Wars 2 remains active. _Visions of Eternity_ launched October 28, 2025; its _Code of Creation_ finale shipped September 15, 2026. ArenaNet has paused expansions for foundational improvements and Hall of Monuments work. Continuing development is documented; a firm next-expansion release date is not established here. Guild Wars 3 is a separate case. [G13, G29]

**Persistent open world: qualified yes.** Character/account progress and social organizations persist, while megaservers create regional copies of shared PvE maps. Story, homes and group encounters also use instances. Leyspring Hollows explicitly repeats its meta-event and closes the completed map; local event state is not indefinitely permanent. [G30, G31] WvW's guild-oriented restructuring supersedes old home-world team identity. These boundaries distinguish durable belonging from uninterrupted geography. [G32]

**Failure and reset:** depleted health can enter a downed state, where allies revive or a credited defeat of an opponent can rally the player; competitive opponents can interrupt rescue or finish the downed character. [G16] Equipment damage on defeat was removed in June 2022. [G17] Encounters, event maps and recurring reward opportunities reset without erasing the account's accumulated capabilities. The practical stakes include lost position, failed objectives, another attempt and teammates' time—not simply a repair invoice. [G20, G23, G31]

The progression arc shifts from learning a profession and exploring while leveling to choosing level-80 capabilities, builds and projects. A returning player may retain useful equipment yet still need an intelligible intermediate goal; preserved possessions alone do not supply motivation. [G01, G12, G18, G45]

## Seven worked interactions

Constructed situations below illustrate documented rules; none was personally reproduced.

1. **Create a shared combat effect.** An ally places a water combo field; another player uses a blast finisher within it to heal nearby allies. The same finisher in a different field produces another result. Field priority and timing make cooperation a compositional action rather than merely simultaneous damage. [G15]
2. **Open a route through learned movement.** Cross a canyon on a trained raptor, use a springer's charged jump to inspect a ledge, then return with a jackal's portal mastery to investigate a sand gateway. The reward is newly reachable space and the pleasure of controlling movement, not just faster commuting. [G18]
3. **Contribute to a public campaign.** In the Silverwastes, defend a captured fort, escort supplies and carry rubble to restore defenses. Failure can return it to Mordrem control. Later, spread across forts for the Breach and lanes for Vinewrath. Local jobs visibly advance a shared operation; the map eventually repeats. [G20]
4. **Choose a useful wartime role.** Escort a WvW dolyak so a camp's supply reaches a tower, or raid the opposing caravan to impede its building and repairs. Guild-authorized upgrades add another layer. A small group's logistical contribution can matter without winning a direct clash against the largest army. [G21]
5. **Earn a symbolic object through an economic choice.** The End of Dragons legendary route combines a precursor and gifts at Zommoros's Mystic Forge. Some fishing inputs can be bought from other players, so someone who dislikes fishing can earn their contribution elsewhere. The finished weapon joins a continuing account collection and carries dragon symbolism. [G09]
6. **Enter harder play with a bounded commitment.** Use raid Quickplay for a curated encounter, accept the queue and cooperate without a preassigned commander. The documented design supplies moderate assistance rather than matching roles; players can mark and ready-check. Afterwards choose to stay together or leave. This removes some organization costs without proving all learning barriers solved. [G23, G24]
7. **Make a place another person can reuse.** Craft decorations, arrange and test a home, then save its layout. Share the XML or permit a visitor to save it. Importing places only decorations the recipient owns and lists missing pieces. Shared design therefore remains distinct from material ownership, account access and permission to copy. [G07, G13, G14]

## Lore, people and motives

**Story spoilers.** Tyria's founding playable cultures provide different relationships to its crises: asuran competitive magitech, sylvari experience shared through the Dream, humanity rebuilding amid silent gods, displaced norn pursuing worthy legends, and charr military society learning to rely on former enemies. This describes the core setting; later campaigns alter those circumstances. [G22]

Biography and race determine an initial personal story. The Durmand Priory, Order of Whispers and Vigil supply alternative allegiances and information before the campaign reaches Zhaitan and the dead nation of Orr. Friends may accompany the owner, whose choices govern the instance. Dungeons develop Destiny's Edge through encounters such as Caithe confronting the Nightmare Court in Twilight Arbor. Authored branching, communal encounters and personal choices coexist without giving every player a different shared-world history. [G04, G05]

The commander protects and raises **Aurene**, teaching her concern for Tyria's inhabitants. Her ability to filter absorbed dragon magics gives the relationship a world-scale purpose; prismatic legendary weapons turn that history into recognizable objects. [G09] Resolving the dragon war creates new damage: **Soo-Won's death** fractures magical barriers, including **Isgarren's World Spire**, enabling the Kryptis intrusion. Aurene restores stability, but the secretive Astral Ward's earlier inaction remains politically questionable after **Eparch** falls. The Tyrian Alliance responds to repeated crises through cooperation between nations. [G10]

Janthir's lowland kodan emphasize environmental stewardship and mutual obligation. Their Claw is advised by a council, contrasting with northern kodan's Voice/Claw leadership. The player's diplomatic assignment requires learning from neighbors rather than assuming they need a conqueror. Regional techniques become usable Masteries, while White Mantle history and ruined settlements complicate the peaceful homestead. [G08, G10] Visions later pursues the Inquest's experiments into isolated Castora; its allies, discoveries and antagonists belong to this continuing game, not to unreleased GW3 systems. [G13]

These connections are useful because a place's culture affects what the player learns and does. The limit matters too: this evidence concerns authored people, dialogue and events; it does not demonstrate OpenLegend-style independent character deliberation.

## Presentation, production and sustained delivery

Movement, readable attacks and painted environments form a recognizable identity. Daniel Dociu's 2010 interview describes more than 70 artists across six disciplines, cross-department prototyping and reuse of concept art in cinematics. End of Dragons' audio team combined orchestral and traditional Korean musicians; these are concrete production practices, not proof every player experiences the same atmosphere. [G40, G41]

Development was announced in 2007 and the game launched August 28, 2012 after public testing. [G39] Its 2022 Steam arrival combined free core access, partner streams, bundles and Twitch Drops. ArenaNet acknowledged confusing Living World entitlements and excluded linking existing accounts to Steam. Acquisition and account continuity were separate problems. [G43]

A productive critique comes from ArenaNet itself. The 2024 Secrets of the Obscure retrospective acknowledges excessive narrative scope, rushed relationships, constrained map production and insufficient upkeep of overlapping features. [G42] Likewise, combat leadership found that strikes had not become the hoped-for bridge into raids: inexperienced engagement was low, and difficulty/group formation remained obstacles. The 2026 consolidation is a response, not measured proof of success. [G25] A distinctive system still needs clear goals, maintained interfaces and enough production capacity to finish its promise.

## Commercial success and practical access

The commercial evidence supports a sustained success, with limits on what can be inferred.

| Evidence                                                                   | Scope                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| More than five million copies sold by August 2015                          | Publisher's historical paid-sales milestone, before the core became free. [G38]                                       |
| KRW1.6 trillion cumulative IP revenue; more than 20 million users acquired | NC's May 2026 GW2 presentation card. IP boundary/cutoff are unspecified; acquired users are not active players. [G36] |
| Reported annual revenue, 2023 / 2024 / 2025                                | **KRW101.146bn / 96.956bn / 85.612bn**, using the consistent presentation that includes royalties. [G36]              |
| Q1 / Q2 2026 revenue                                                       | **KRW32.625bn / 23.689bn**, recognized revenue in an unaudited release, not profit or retention. [G37]                |

**Exact title-only lifetime consumer spending, cost and profit remain unestimable** from the inspected disclosures. The IP milestone cannot substitute for a reconciled lifetime title revenue/cost series. Recent contraction and continuing substantial revenue can coexist; no causal retention rate follows from either.

The paid route includes **money → Gems → Gold**; earned Gold can also purchase Gems. Thus the model sells time substitution and convenience as well as appearance, while expansions grant playable content and abilities. [G33, G34] The October 13, 2026 revision included Living World S2 with the base game, S3–4 with Heart of Thorns/Path of Fire, and S5 with End of Dragons; missing episodes unlock for relevant owners. Separate episode charges are now historical criticism. [G35]

A stable statistical ceiling protects some prior investment. It does not ensure equal movement, builds, storage, knowledge or admission to a particular group. Conversely, retaining past Wizard's Vault cosmetics in a Legacy section preserves reward access across rotations. [G29] For OpenLegend, compare actual desired participation and effort under an offer, rather than treating “horizontal” or “cosmetic” as a complete fairness verdict.

The evidence supports several paths back: attainable intermediate goals, new movement, friends and encounters, a meaningful collection, or a home. It also preserves a design disagreement: coordinated maps can make strangers useful, while less staged spaces can better reward wandering. Neither preference should erase the other.

## Native creation and the R15 ecosystem

**R15 applies to a substantial overlay/addon and marker-pack ecosystem**, with a narrower authority than server-owned world creation. Blish HUD reports a community exceeding 13,000 Discord members; the inspected TacO catalog lists multiple independent pack makers. These are bounded participation/diversity proxies, not audited active-user shares. [G53, G56]

| Tool or authored contribution | Maker → distribution → player; limit                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Blish HUD                     | Developers build modules against its framework; users acquire them through its repository. A separate overlay reads positional data and optional API access. Permission-limited subtokens restrict account data, not necessarily arbitrary module execution. [G53, G54]                                                         |
| TacO / Pathing packs          | Authors define XML markers, record trails and supply textures; publish a pack; players download, toggle categories and choose updates. Inspected entries include achievements, guild missions, hero-point routes and scenic tours. Lua extensions mean identical package formats do not guarantee identical behavior. [G55–G57] |
| ArcDPS                        | The author's client-side addon, extension interface and combat logs support measurement and analysis. Installation/removal and game-update compatibility remain player responsibilities; it does not author server quests or rules. [G58]                                                                                       |

The documented author workflow includes placing/repositioning markers in TacO's editor, recording `.trl` trails, then saving and reloading to inspect edits. Creators fix required XML/path fields and duplicate entries, package XML/trails/textures in a ZIP-format `.taco` file, and provide a channel for reported problems. This is an editing, preview, distribution and maintenance loop, not authority to change shared quests. [G55, G57, G62]

ArenaNet's policy prohibits automation and unfair advantage while declining blanket approval, endorsement or support for third-party tools. Availability is not permission. No software was installed and pack asset rights were not audited. [G59]

Native homestead layout sharing is separately permissioned and ownership-aware, as the worked example shows. These contribution types suggest useful community roles beyond adding more game systems: teaching, interpretation, scenic routes and reusable arrangements. **Inference:** overlays can improve access while replacing discovery with instructions; metrics can help learning while aiding exclusion. Evaluate those outcomes separately, and do not make community tools responsible for comprehensible basic play.

The distinctive opportunity is a world worth revisiting because new knowledge, relationships and creations make it more useful and personal. GW2 demonstrates several authored ways to achieve that. OpenLegend can pursue its own persistent causal world while retaining the approachable cooperation, expressive movement and recognizable accomplishments that made those experiences compelling.
