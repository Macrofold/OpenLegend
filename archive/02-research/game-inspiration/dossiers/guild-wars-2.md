# Guild Wars 2 — enduring capability, cooperative discovery and chosen goals

**G188 · Research date: October 26, 2026.** Guild Wars 2 combines a persistent character/account with shared event maps, private story and group instances, competitive arenas and territorial warfare. Its strongest research question is how exploration, cooperation and expressive long-term goals remain valuable when advancement need not continually replace yesterday's equipment.

## Actions, composition and progression

| System                   | What the player does; consequential boundary                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Moment-to-moment combat  | Move while attacking, read telegraphs, spend endurance to dodge, heal and revive allies. The skill bar combines five weapon skills, a heal, three utilities, an elite and profession-specific controls. Weapon choice therefore changes available actions rather than merely damage numbers. [G01, G02]                                                                            |
| Open-world participation | Join an event without formally joining a party; individual experience/rewards reduce competition over another player's presence. Success and failure can lead to different follow-up events. This is authored branching and scaling, not evidence that every resident runs an autonomous life. [G03]                                                                               |
| Personal story           | Race and biography establish different beginnings; later choices lead through one of three orders toward the campaign against Zhaitan. Friends can accompany the owner, whose story decisions govern that instance. Private narrative state and shared map state are distinct. [G04]                                                                                               |
| Group encounters         | Five-player dungeons connect story and alternative explorable paths. Fractals increase difficulty through scales, environmental modifiers and agony resistance requirements. Ten-player raids demand coordinated movement, builds and encounter execution. The absence of an ever-rising character level does not remove equipment preparation or social entry requirements. [G05] |
| Account capabilities     | The 2015 Mastery design explicitly replaced further levels with account abilities earned through experience and region-specific points: gliding, local knowledge and traversal opened new routes. This is an access/proficiency ladder, even when it preserves equipment investment. Later expansions extend that model into different activities. [G06, G08]                      |
| Home and craft           | Janthir Wilds delivered an account-wide homestead, handiworker decoration crafting, visitors through a conjured doorway, existing home resource nodes and alternate characters displayed as NPCs. Homestead growth uses Masteries; unlocked characters and furniture do not become an unrestricted simulation or scripting language. [G08]                                         |

**Creation has a dependency chain.** The Homestead designers explained why the personal decoration discipline advances through expansion experience rather than repeated furniture manufacture, and why each decorative copy consumes materials. Friends may help decorate with permission; placed resource nodes remain constrained for functionality. This ties creative reach to play, collection and access, which can provide a goal or become an obstacle for someone who primarily wants to build. [G07]

## Lore: alliances, inherited damage and a world after its central war

**Setting and story spoilers.** The core story takes a character from their racial home to one of the Durmand Priory, Order of Whispers or Vigil, then to the dead nation of Orr and the Elder Dragon Zhaitan. The orders provide different allies and information on the route. Dungeons add a parallel relationship story: Destiny's Edge reunites through adventures such as Caithe's confrontation with the Nightmare Court in Twilight Arbor. Narrative is delivered through private chapters, shared places and optional group routes rather than one uniform quest sequence. [G04, G05]

The bond with **Aurene** turns the dragon conflict into a responsibility. The commander protects her egg, raises her and teaches her concern for Tyria's inhabitants. As an Elder Dragon, she can filter the different magics released by the others' deaths. Her prismatic identity also becomes a material reward language: legendary weapons express different absorbed dragon magics through related appearances. The prop and effects artists explicitly balanced recognizable character symbolism against combat readability. [G09]

The apparent resolution creates further consequences. In the narrative team's account, **Soo-Won's death** fractures magical barriers, including Isgarren's World Spire; Aurene restores stability, but the breach exposes Tyria to the Kryptis and the secretive Astral Ward. Defeating Eparch does not answer why the wizards withheld help or settle the political consequences of their secrecy. Janthir's Tyrian Alliance therefore begins from nations seeking cooperation after repeated disasters. [G10]

The lowland kodan make that shift concrete. Their culture emphasizes stewardship and obligations to a harsh environment; unlike northern kodan's Voice/Claw partnership, they follow a Claw advised by a village council. The player's initial assignment is diplomatic contact, not automatic conquest. Learning regional practices through Masteries connects cultural acquaintance to usable abilities, while the nearby ruins and White Mantle history disturb the promise of a peaceful new home. These are authored societies with contrasting institutions; the dossier does not infer simulated politics from their presentation. [G08, G10]
