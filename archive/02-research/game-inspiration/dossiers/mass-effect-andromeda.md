# G104 — Mass Effect: Andromeda

[Research roster](../research-roster.md) · [Progress](../research-progress.md) · [Requirements](../research-requirements.md) · [Library](../README.md)

**Research date:** September 26, 2026. **Pass:** R01–R14 complete, with access and interpretation limits below; the requested second-pass audit is recorded after the requirement map. Documentary research, not a playtest. **Spoilers:** sections 5–6 discuss Kadara's leadership, the Archon's prisoners, Meridian and colonization outcomes. No gameplay footage, benchmark or personally observed session is claimed.

**Central finding — interpretation:** Andromeda tries to make an expedition's work visible through safer environments, new settlements, improved equipment and a more capable Pathfinder. Its strongest systems let the player change a practical situation. Its weakness is that an activity can award progress without making the activity itself interesting: more scanning, driving and errands do not necessarily produce more discovery. The reception disagreement also warns against treating tactical squad command and personal combat freedom as the same kind of depth.

## 1. Identity, editions and the opening experience

BioWare and Electronic Arts released Andromeda on Windows, PlayStation 4 and Xbox One on March 21, 2017 in North America and March 23 in Europe. The new expedition travels more than six centuries from the Milky Way to the Heleus Cluster. It is a new protagonist's campaign, not Mass Effect 4 continuing Shepard's save history. Later patches and deluxe packaging change specific capabilities or entitlements; Andromeda is not one of Legendary Edition's three remastered Shepard campaigns. [S13](#s13) [S10](#s10)

The single-player campaign directly controls Sara or Scott Ryder, normally accompanied by two AI squadmates. Creation chooses training and appearance and also permits customization of the twin. The historical choice of Shepard's gender is not an import of the trilogy's complete decision tree. Habitat 7 introduces the failed settlement expectation, hazardous exploration and a leadership crisis; SAM subsequently provides a fictional basis for Ryder's unusual adaptability. [S14](#s14)

Established play alternates between the Tempest, inhabited hubs, open planetary areas and more directed missions. The Tempest is a travel and social home, not a player-flown dogfighting spacecraft. The Nexus is an expedition's institutional center, not a city whose buildings the player freely places. A separate cooperative multiplayer mode uses its own characters and progression; it is not a shared version of Ryder's campaign. [S13](#s13) [S15](#s15)

**Interpretation:** the first hours promise an explorer's authority before the player has earned the confidence associated with Shepard. That difference can support vulnerability and learning. It can also make casual jokes or effortless solutions feel mismatched with the expedition's danger. A new setting does not remove the need to establish why this particular person should be trusted.

## 2. What the player does and what constrains it

Ryder can mix combat, technology and biotic skills rather than remain permanently locked to a traditional class. Investment unlocks stronger **profiles**: Soldier, Engineer, Adept, Sentinel, Vanguard, Infiltrator and Explorer. Only one profile is active at a time. These packages improve particular styles; learning broadly does not make every passive package operate simultaneously. Respecializing skills also changes which developed profiles remain available. [S16](#s16)

Four **Favorites** each store a profile and up to three active skills. Changing to another Favorite puts its powers on cooldown. The player therefore has flexible access over an encounter, not twelve simultaneously ready buttons. **Interpretation:** preparation can replace a rigid class prohibition, but switching needs a legible cost if specialization is still to matter. [S6](#s6)

| System | Player decisions | Constraints and failure feedback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Movement and gunplay | Jump, hover while aiming, dash, mantle, move around automatic cover, shoot, reload and use melee. | Height can expose Ryder; mobility is not invulnerability. An overloaded or unpredictable cover interaction can frustrate the intended movement. |
| Powers and loadout | Choose active skills, a profile, weapons, armor and consumables. | Cooldowns, power-cell charges, weapon weight and compatible equipment matter. A power learned but not in the current set is not immediately usable. |
| Squad | Choose two companions and develop their skills; give attack, position and rally commands. | Companion powers are largely selected by their AI rather than through the trilogy's manual tactical casting. Presence and competence do not guarantee perfect positioning. |
| Investigation | Use SAM-assisted scanning, inspect clues, converse and solve or bypass supported decryption puzzles. | An eligible target or discovered glyph can be required. Scanning everything is not the same as making a difficult inference. |
| Travel and recovery | Drive the Nomad, establish forward stations, return to safe areas and use fast travel. | Life support, vehicle damage and location-specific restrictions limit expeditions. A distant waypoint does not establish a worthwhile encounter. |
| Material preparation | Research blueprints, develop equipment, fit mods, sell or deconstruct surplus and buy from merchants. | Knowledge, materials, credits and compatible slots are separate prerequisites; one cannot substitute for all the others. |

The table condenses documented controls and systems, not a promise that every scene supports every action. The manual's transcribed controller symbols contain inconsistencies, so exact button diagrams are deliberately not reproduced. [S14](#s14) [S7](#s7) [S9](#s9)

Named abilities such as Pull, Charge, Concussive Shot and Turbocharge answer different combat needs; the profile does not itself replace the need to invest in the relevant skills. Priming and detonating target states, choosing a defense-appropriate attack and moving to a usable line of fire remain separate decisions. Power ranks and passive investments make two characters with similar equipment behave differently. Multiplayer kits must not be assumed to possess Ryder's entire flexible skill library. [S17](#s17)

**Remnant interactions are more specific than “scan and solve.”** The scanner reveals links between consoles and devices; operating a console toggles its linked active/inactive states. Glyph puzzles first require discovering missing symbols, then arranging the grid without repeating a glyph in a row, column or outlined region. This separates gathering information from applying a rule. An incorrect submission normally summons defenders, but some puzzles have no combat penalty. A consumable decryption key bypasses most, not all, grids: the Elaaden vault's skill-point puzzle is a documented exception despite the item's broader in-game description. Keys also compete with keeping resources for later obstacles. These are authored puzzles, not arbitrary code-breaking or a general crafting language. [S33](#s33)

**Interpretation:** a useful comparison with the trilogy is not simply whether the newer game has more powers. It is whether players can express a plan through the available controls. Andromeda places more of that expression in Ryder's immediate motion and loadout, and less in manually orchestrating each companion's next cast.

## 3. Objects, crafting, resources and the expedition economy

Research and development are distinct. **Milky Way, Heleus and Remnant Research Data** unlock blueprints in their respective families. Development consumes minerals and other materials to produce equipment. A discovered design does not automatically become a finished gun. Higher-tier research and manufacture create progression, while finite data encourages choosing which equipment families to pursue rather than purchasing every possible blueprint immediately. [S7](#s7)

**Augmentations** are selected during development; ordinary weapon mods can be changed afterward. Deconstruction returns augmentations and part of the material investment, not necessarily every spent resource. Equipped gear must first be removed before it can be deconstructed. The Innovation cryo-pod benefit can add a crafting augmentation slot; an already manufactured extra-slot item retains that property through New Game Plus even though the perk itself resets. [S7](#s7) [S22](#s22)

Concrete equipment shows why these distinctions matter. The **Scattershot** is a Remnant shotgun using heat rather than an ordinary magazine. The **Rozerad** is categorized as a pistol but behaves like a rapid-fire weapon. A **Krogan Hammer** offers a different melee approach from a gun upgrade. These items draw on different research/material families; treating the inventory as a single ladder of larger damage numbers misses handling, resource and range choices. [S18](#s18)

The **Bio-Converter** augmentation exchanges health for an automatic magazine refill. A **Shield Oscillator** has different effects when applied to a gun or chest armor. These are not permission to place every desirable special augmentation on one object: compatibility and special-slot restrictions remain important. The **Beam Emitter** is another behavior-changing augmentation, and patch 1.08 specifically revises its operation. A launch build should not silently receive the patched behavior. [S19](#s19) [S10](#s10)

**Modification has several distinct owners.** Firearms normally have two mod sockets; a development-time Mod Slot Extension or Double Mod Extension adds capacity. Barrels, stocks, scopes and other types have weapon-specific compatibility, and enhanced variants can impose penalties. Melee weapons do not accept those gun mods. Purchased/found weapon tiers and researched blueprint tiers also unlock on slightly different schedules, so Ryder's level alone does not mean every equipment route has advanced identically. Regular companions' weapons are fixed rather than separately replaceable/upgradable through Ryder's inventory. [S34](#s34)

**Fusion mods are a separate armor system.** Only Ryder's eligible chest pieces accept them. The Fusion Mod of Shielding trades half maximum health for 50% more maximum shields; Rapid Deployment improves power recharge at a weapon-damage cost. Fusion Mod Support, a commerce cryo-pod perk, halves their penalties rather than erasing them. They are swappable through loadout configuration, unlike a development-time augmentation. A reward from a vault or Architect can therefore change an entire build's tradeoff, not merely its armor number. Some special chest sets cannot accept fusion mods. [S35](#s35)

**Inventory and patch boundaries.** Weapons, armor and ordinary weapon mods consume individual spaces; consumables stack, and research/material categories are not counted as individual carried gear. Patch 1.05 increased default capacity from 50 to 100 and the two Trade Capacity tiers to 150 and 200. Thus a launch complaint about an 80-item fully expanded bag should not be repeated as the patched limit. The two community inventories disagree about whether fusion mods count toward that limit; this audit does not certify that disputed edge case without a direct implementation check. It does distinguish the well-supported broad capacity change from blanket claims that every acquired object fills a slot. [S36](#s36)

Minerals can be found, mined or purchased; surplus equipment can become credits or recovered materials. Research, personal combat improvement and Nomad upgrades compete for some of the same effort. The result is a resource-supported expedition, not an autonomous industrial economy: the player does not establish labor contracts, operate a production line or trade in a simulated interstellar commodity market. **Interpretation:** the interesting question is whether a material shortage sends the player toward a meaningful decision or merely another round of routine collection.

## 4. Viability, time, progression and recovery

A planet's local **viability percentage** differs from overall **Andromeda Viability Points** and the **Nexus level**. Forty percent local viability is a minimum for an outpost, but location-specific quest conditions can still block settlement. Improving the Nexus grants cryo-pod choices whose science, commerce or military benefits support the expedition. This is not proof that every awakened person becomes an individually scheduled worker. [S8](#s8)

Cryo-pod choices are more committed than ordinary skill respecialization: they are not freely reset in the same campaign. Benefits such as Lab Technicians, Mining Operations and Grey Market Connections support different resource priorities. Some recurring benefits accrue through active play and require collection at the relevant interface. Their timers are not the same as the real-world timers used by strike teams. A reviewer describing every side quest as mandatory for full viability overstates the rule; progress can reach its local cap without exhausting every activity. [S8](#s8)

The Nomad's mobility and protection make it an exploration tool. Driving modes trade speed for climbing, and upgrades include improved boost, suspension, shields and life support. It is not the Mako's armed combat vehicle. Forward stations replenish supplies, permit loadout changes, create travel anchors and support the Nomad. Havarl and H-047c have important vehicle/access exceptions, so no one planet is a universal template for all exploration. [S9](#s9) [S20](#s20)

Ryder's experience and skill points, equipment tiers, profile ranks and the Nexus's development are separate progression tracks. Skill allocation can be reset aboard the Tempest. Combat defeat generally returns the player to a save or checkpoint; authored decisions and losses instead remain part of that chosen history. A hazardous environment consumes life support before exposing the character to further harm, making retreat a practical action rather than only a story description. [S14](#s14) [S17](#s17)

The main story can be completed without clearing every optional task. A completed career can seed **New Game Plus**: character development, equipment and most research persist, while missions, relationships, local story progress and Nexus/cryo-pod progression restart. Strike teams belong to the account rather than one Ryder. This is a fresh campaign with retained capabilities, not an extension in which all prior settlements remain built and all romances remain committed. [S22](#s22)

Ordinary post-finale continuation is a different option from New Game Plus. After Meridian and its epilogue, much unfinished exploration remains possible, but some Hyperion-related missions become unavailable. The main resolution does not automatically complete unactivated vaults or maximize every planet's viability. That is a particularly important limit on inferring game state from a cinematic image of flourishing worlds. [S38](#s38)

**Interpretation:** several overlapping progress bars can make a long expedition feel productive, but they can also obscure the player's actual purpose. A meaningful milestone should answer “what can we now do, or whom did this help?” rather than only “which percentage increased?”

## 5. People, dialogue and an inhabited frontier

Cora, Liam, Vetra, Drack, Peebee and Jaal supply the regular field-team possibilities. They are not interchangeable representatives of their species: their personal missions foreground obligations, family, curiosity, disappointment or trust. Loyalty missions unlock the upper tier of companion skills, connecting time spent with a person to a combat-development opportunity. That link is not Mass Effect 2's complete suicide-mission survival formula transplanted unchanged. [S14](#s14) [S23](#s23)

Dialogue emphasizes Emotional, Logical, Casual and Professional tones rather than a Paragon/Renegade points contest. Narrative-action prompts can be accepted or ignored, and conversation icons distinguish investigation, friendship and flirtation. The game records a style and selected decisions without promising that every phrasing creates a new plot branch. Jaal's friendship route is especially useful evidence that not every developing relationship announces itself immediately through an obvious romance icon. [S24](#s24)

Native romance is bounded. Cora and Gil are Scott-specific routes; Liam and Suvi are Sara-specific. Peebee, Vetra and Reyes can be pursued by either Ryder, with route-specific conditions. Keri's limited relationship should not be equated with every fully committed companion arc. Jaal's availability to Scott was added by patch 1.08; presenting it as a launch feature would erase a meaningful post-release change. These are authored relationships, not a universal gift, marriage or family simulation. [S25](#s25) [S10](#s10)

The Tempest's conversations and messages provide continuity, while the Nexus expresses administrative conflict and the consequences of the earlier expedition's failures. Angaran settlements, the kett, Remnant structures and the Scourge create overlapping histories rather than an empty frontier awaiting the newcomers. **Interpretation:** the setting is most convincing when the player encounters other people's existing purposes. Calling a place “new” from Ryder's perspective should not imply nobody already lives there. [S13](#s13) [S26](#s26)

**Kadara spoilers.** Saving Sloane from the sniper in High Noon preserves her rule; allowing her death enables Reyes's different arrangement through angaran intermediaries. Either broad outcome can still permit the Initiative's outpost after its other conditions are met. This is a political difference within a reconverging settlement path, not proof that the choice has no consequence or that it creates two wholly separate campaigns. [S26](#s26)

**Archon-prisoner spoilers.** The choice between rescuing the salarian pathfinder Raeka's group and the krogan scouts affects who escapes. Leaving the scouts also allows further Behemoths—exalted krogan—to appear later in the campaign. That connection gives an apparent rescue choice a future combat consequence. It does not make every later multiplayer Behemoth the result of this individual Ryder's decision. [S27](#s27)

**Meridian spoilers.** The Archon severs Ryder's SAM connection and exploits the twin's implant. The player briefly controls the other sibling, with a limited weapon/ability set, to restore a life-saving connection; this is a scripted exception, not free character switching. The final battle combines defense, movement and console interaction with the twin's help. The objective is to disrupt the Archon's control, not permanently kill every endlessly appearing machine. Prior allies and rescued groups can assist, while securing the other Pathfinders affects Captain Dunn's survival. These are recognizable preparations paying off within an authored sequence, not an independent fleet-war simulation. [S38](#s38)

## 6. Six worked interactions

These are **constructed, rules-based illustrations**, not observed play sessions. They assume the stated skills, equipment, mission state and edition. They demonstrate dependencies rather than prescribe one optimal career.

### A. Change a plan without receiving twelve ready powers

**Intention:** replace a close-range approach with a safer response to a new threat. **Conditions:** two prepared Favorites with different skills and profiles. **Actions:** move to a defensible position, switch and wait for the new powers to become available. **Interaction:** flexible preparation is limited by a transition cost. **Result:** Ryder can adapt without restarting the character. **Next decision:** remain in the new role or accept another switching interval. **Limit:** changing Favorites while exposed does not provide an instant emergency cast. The interface should make that unavailable interval clear. [S6](#s6)

### B. Convert a reload interruption into a health decision

**Intention:** sustain fire through a dangerous moment. **Conditions:** a compatible gun developed with Bio-Converter and enough remaining health. **Actions:** keep firing until the magazine empties and the augmentation spends health to refill it. **Interaction:** the weapon now consumes a survival resource instead of merely demanding reload time. **Result:** a longer firing opportunity with a real cost. **Next decision:** seek healing, change weapons or stop firing. **Limit:** this is not free infinite ammunition, and it does not imply that another special augmentation can occupy the same restricted slot. [S19](#s19)

### C. Make a dangerous route repeatable

**Intention:** reach a distant objective without exhausting life support. **Conditions:** an accessible forward-station site, the Nomad where permitted and an environmental hazard. **Actions:** establish the station, replenish, adjust the loadout and travel in the vehicle before making a short on-foot excursion. **Interaction:** a support location changes the cost of repeating the journey. **Result:** a temporary frontier becomes more navigable. **Next decision:** pursue the objective or return before the protection fails. **Limit:** a station does not eliminate every hazard, and vehicle restrictions differ by world. [S9](#s9) [S20](#s20)

### D. Distinguish enough capacity from permission to settle

**Intention:** establish an outpost on Kadara. **Conditions:** adequate local viability but unresolved required local tasks. **Actions:** examine the remaining settlement conditions instead of repeatedly collecting generic progress; resolve the necessary political and practical work. **Interaction:** a numerical threshold and an authored permission state must both be satisfied. **Result:** the outpost becomes possible. **Next decision:** use its services or continue improving the area. **Limit:** forty percent is not a universal “build anywhere now” command, and the resulting town is not freely laid out by the player. [S8](#s8) [S26](#s26)

### E. Let a converging campaign remember different authorities

**High Noon spoilers. Intention:** decide whom to trust with Kadara. **Conditions:** the confrontation and its timed intervention. **Actions:** prevent the assassination or allow it. **Interaction:** personal judgment changes the local leadership while leaving the broader Initiative settlement goal achievable. **Result:** a specific political history, not a completely separate ending tree. **Next decision:** deal with the surviving authority and its limitations. **Limit:** a shared later objective does not erase the earlier choice, but neither does one altered leader establish fully autonomous diplomacy. [S26](#s26)

### F. Understand why a rescued person and an avoided enemy can be the same outcome

**Archon spoilers. Intention:** choose which prisoners to save. **Conditions:** the mutually exclusive rescue opportunity. **Actions:** prioritize the krogan scouts or Raeka's group. **Interaction:** those left behind can become part of the enemy's future force. **Result:** an immediate humanitarian choice changes later opposition as well as dialogue. **Next decision:** live with the saved group and the loss of the other. **Limit:** this is an authored causal connection, not a simulation that can transform any captured NPC into any enemy type. [S27](#s27)

## 7. Multiplayer, distant teams and distinct economies

Cooperative multiplayer normally runs through seven waves, with special objectives and a final extraction. Its characters have defined kits and their own development, rather than sharing Ryder's unconstrained profile selection. Credits purchase randomized card packs; Mission Funds can buy specific items and also support strike-team recruitment or equipment. **Andromeda Points** are the paid currency, not another name for Mission Funds. No current server-population or payment-availability claim is made. [S15](#s15) [S28](#s28)

Strike teams are dispatched rather than directly controlled. Their level, traits and equipment affect the presented mission prospects; even failure can yield experience. Standard tasks use this automated route, while eligible APEX tasks can also be played in multiplayer. The displayed agents' species composition is cosmetic rather than a hidden species-combination bonus. Choosing a suitable trait is therefore a real preparation action; inventing tactical differences from portraits is not. [S29](#s29)

The risk is more persistent than one missed reward. A team starts with a positive trait and can acquire lasting positive or negative modifiers. Daring and Timid apply in opposite directions to High-Risk, High-Reward missions; other traits favor particular enemies or conditions. A failure may leave the team less attractive for future work, creating a choice between retaining experience and recruiting a replacement. The dedicated and general wikis disagree about the maximum number of negative traits and the exact rule for gaining positive ones, so no universal cap or guaranteed learning rule is asserted here. The useful supported distinction is persistent team condition versus a single randomized result. [S29](#s29)

Rewards can support Ryder, but the cooperative mode is optional rather than a required readiness multiplier for the single-player ending. Timed remote work, an active cooperative match and a campaign mission are three different activities. **Interpretation:** tying them through rewards can provide flexibility, but can also create pressure to service timers unrelated to the story. The right research question is whether the connection increases useful choice or merely adds another obligation.

## 8. Art, audio, interface and feel

The visual promise comes from dramatic planetary color, distant structures and changing atmospheric conditions. The Nomad gives those spaces a bodily scale, while the Tempest provides familiarity. RPGFan particularly values the landscapes, ambient sound and crew-message humor but finds story and task repetition less persuasive. These are critical judgments, not a measured claim that every visual effect or performance succeeds. [S2](#s2)

**Audio has a documented production layer.** Composer John Paesano's own site identifies his Andromeda score. In a March 29, 2017 interview, additional musical sound designer Nate Brenholdt explains enhancing arpeggios, sequences and string sounds with hardware synthesizers, including the ARP 2600 and Serge modular. He describes adding dynamics and liveliness to existing composer material as well as creating new sounds. This is evidence of complementary production roles and an electronic texture-making process, not proof that every sound effect used those instruments or that their names predict a listener's response. No linked music or footage was played for the audit. [S37](#s37)

The player repeatedly moves between travel animations, menus, scanning overlays, the map and crafting interfaces. A distinction that is sound as a rule—research versus manufacture, for example—can still be poorly explained at the moment it matters. **Interpretation:** system depth and interface complexity should be evaluated separately. A clear explanation may preserve a useful dependency; deleting the dependency is not the only way to reduce confusion.

Launch-era facial animation, dialogue staging and movement bugs drew substantial criticism. Later patch notes explicitly address some presentation and customization issues, but that does not justify declaring all problems fixed or all later players' reports identical. Patch 1.08 adds appearance editing aboard the Tempest and revises particular interactions and equipment. No formal accessibility audit, frame-time benchmark, complete soundtrack listening session or before/after animation test was performed here. [S10](#s10)

## 9. Production and the difference between a plan and a shipped system

In GameSpot's interview, producer Michael Gamble describes both new and experienced contributors, BioWare Montreal's central role and the substantial tools work involved in moving to Frostbite. The conversation wheel required a supporting authoring system, not merely a new renderer. He frames the project as a fresh start that still draws on the trilogy's exploration and personal-mission strengths. These are primary developer accounts, not independently audited staffing or budget figures. [S30](#s30)

Ian Frazier's IBTimes interview explains that an earlier, much larger procedural-world approach was reduced in favor of hand-built major planets. He also identifies a surviving **Storyteller** director that adjusts the likelihood of small encounters according to recent activity. This is more specific than either “everything is procedural” or “nothing is procedural.” It is an account of a limited encounter-selection system, not evidence that the shipped game synthesizes arbitrary stories or civilizations. [S31](#s31)

**Interpretation:** scope reduction can preserve some useful tools without preserving the original product promise. The pertinent research lesson is to ask which player experiences a generator actually improves. Increasing generated terrain alone does not guarantee meaningful uncertainty, and a handcrafted main world can still benefit from bounded variation in encounters.

## 10. Promotion, patches, distribution and commercial evidence

EA's launch announcement presents the game as a new home and an entry point for a new generation of players. Interviews, trailers and the Andromeda Initiative fiction provide concrete promotional surfaces; the existing franchise community supplies context but also expectations. Gamble explicitly contrasts new characters with companions whose appeal accumulated over three games. That is a developer's explanation of the challenge, not proof that every unfavorable comparison is unfair. [S13](#s13) [S30](#s30)

The August 19, 2017 studio update identifies patch 1.10 as the final single-player update and states that no further single-player patches or in-game story content were planned. It distinguishes multiplayer support and stories in comics or novels. An unresolved quarian-ark hook is therefore not a shipped story expansion. Deluxe gear, cosmetic content and multiplayer additions are not equivalent to another campaign. The statement describes the 2017 support decision, not a live promise of current services. [S11](#s11)

EA's Form 10-Q for the quarter ended **June 30, 2017** identifies Andromeda and Battlefield 1 as important contributors to growth in full-game download revenue. The reported **$72 million increase** is a category-wide year-over-year figure, not Andromeda's individual revenue. It supports the narrower conclusion that EA credited the game with contributing to business growth. No audited title-specific unit sales, budget, profit or lifetime active-player total was established in this pass. Third-party estimates are not silently promoted into actual sales. [S32](#s32)

The business model combines a premium single-player game, edition-specific extras and a multiplayer acquisition economy with optional real-money purchases. Campaign credits, Mission Funds, paid points and corporate revenue are distinct. **Marketing interpretation:** clips of awkward presentation are unusually easy to circulate, while a gradual appreciation of exploration or a companion is harder to compress. This is a plausible sharing mechanism, not a measured attribution of the game's sales or reputation to particular creators.

## 12. Conditional OpenLegend research lessons

**Show what progress makes possible.** An outpost, a safer route or a recovered specialist can communicate advancement more meaningfully than an abstract completion total. A small experiment could compare identical rewards presented only numerically against rewards with a visible change in available actions. The risk is decorative change that promises functionality the settlement does not actually have.

**Separate knowledge, manufacture and modification only when each choice matters.** Crafting can support invention when discovering a method differs from paying to produce it. Compatibility and recoverable investments permit experimentation without making every mistake free. The failure mode is several currencies and screens that all serve the same undifferentiated grind.

**Use adaptable roles with understandable transition costs.** Favorite-style preparation can let one character respond to changing circumstances without permanently excluding other approaches. The important dependency is readable availability. A player who believes an emergency power is ready should not discover an unexplained switching cooldown only after committing to danger.

**Let local consequences coexist with a shared main path.** Kadara demonstrates that reconvergence need not eliminate all difference. A changed authority, rescued group or future enemy can preserve a particular history. The limitation should remain honest: a few authored branches are not an autonomous political simulation, and promotional language should not imply otherwise.

**Treat traversal support as an action-enabling system.** A station can change how far someone can safely travel and what they can carry or attempt. This only remains interesting if the journey contains decisions worth enabling. Widely spaced chores do not become exploration simply because travel has resource costs.

**Prefer a coherent small encounter director to unsupported universal generation.** The documented Storyteller suggests testing limited variation around recent activity while keeping important places legible and authored. Such a director would need diversity of meaningful events and safeguards against obvious repetition; varying spawn timing alone will not create a living culture.

**Give new characters room to become particular.** A cast cannot inherit three games of attachment by sharing familiar species. Repeated conversation, competent action, conflicting obligations and remembered assistance offer testable foundations. More biography text is not automatically a substitute for an experience shared with the player.

These are conditional research hypotheses, not accepted OpenLegend requirements, implementation tasks or permission to reuse BioWare's fiction.

## 13. Reading route, requirement map and preservation
