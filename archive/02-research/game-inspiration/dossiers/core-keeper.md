# Core Keeper — full research dossier

**G46 · Research and identified audit remedies completed September 26, 2026.** The principal current-version reference is **1.3.0.1 — Riders of the Underground, September 21, 2026**, not a guarantee that no subsequent hotfix exists. **Void & Voltage** launched February 25, 2026 after an earlier January 28 plan. Historical Early Access, 1.0 and prerelease review experiences remain version-qualified. No gameplay, watched footage, soundtrack audition or current-network test is claimed. [CK-B](#ck-b) [CK-C](#ck-c) [CK-O](#ck-o)

[Preserved overview](../games/core-keeper.md) · [Detailed mechanics study](../mechanics/core-keeper-resource-circuits-food-and-inhabited-discovery.md) · [Requirements](../research-requirements.md) · [Progress](../research-progress.md)

Core Keeper's strongest OpenLegend lesson is the relationship between **place, resource, automation and return**. A location is not consumed when first discovered: a cavern can become a road, an ore deposit an extraction site, a biome a farm source, and a base a means of converting discoveries into durable capability. These are research interpretations, not accepted OpenLegend requirements.

## 1. Identity and current scope

Core Keeper is Pugstorm's top-down mining/survival sandbox, published by Fireshine Games, for one to eight players on its larger-platform configurations. Early Access began March 8, 2022; PC/PS5/Xbox Series 1.0 launched August 27, 2024, with additional console versions following. [CK-A](#ck-a)

The player creates an explorer, awakens around the mysterious Core, mines and reshapes terrain, gathers resources, crafts equipment, farms, cooks, fishes, builds and decorates, automates production, fights bosses, establishes merchant services, explores generated biomes and authored Scenes, raises animals and pets, and progresses toward the Core's larger mystery. Solo and cooperative play share that activity vocabulary.

**1.3 Riders of the Underground** adds the Moss sub-biome, Orex cattle, Saddles/Racing Saddles, cattle mounts and further pets/tools. Its summoning and mana changes are explained in §7. The pre-release presentation also discusses hydras and a mysterious door, but hydras as a category were not invented by this patch; preview content must not all be labelled newly added. [CK-B](#ck-b) [CK-I](#ck-i)

**1.2 Void & Voltage** adds Breaker's Reach and its Void sub-biome, advanced machinery, equipment, Echo-Maps, the Otherworldly Merchant, S.A.H.A.B.A.R. and Oblidra. Its final February release and earlier January announcement are different evidence states. [CK-C](#ck-c) [CK-O](#ck-o)

## 2. Character creation and backgrounds

Players create an appearance and choose a starting **background**. Backgrounds supply initial items and an early direction, not an irreversible class. The Nomad offers an especially uncommitted start. A player can subsequently develop different skills and equipment styles instead of rerolling to become a different combat archetype. [CK-D](#ck-d)

**Interpretation:** an initial identity can make the first session legible without permanently limiting the person. The important distinction is between a helpful starting configuration and a locked profession.

## 3. Skills level through use

Skills cover Mining, Running, Melee Combat, Vitality, Crafting, Ranged Combat, Gardening, Fishing, Cooking, Magic and Summoning, with later systems adding further specialization. Performing relevant activities develops the corresponding expertise and opens talent choices. Skill growth and equipment tiers are separate sources of capability. [CK-D](#ck-d)

This makes expertise behavior-linked: mining and cooking improve different competencies. But a progress bar can also turn a pleasant activity into a repetitive obligation. The inherited negative-review evidence objects to shallow trees, slow later development and uneven magic/summoning breadth. Those are selected experiences, not a measured ranking of all skills. [CK-E](#ck-e)

The September 2026 patch changes combat-skill experience to account for attack speed. Older advice about maximizing experience through a particular rapid-hit routine is therefore not a timeless rule. [CK-B](#ck-b)

## 4. Health, food and survival

The player manages health, food, damage/status effects, protection and recovery. Cooked food provides more than relief from hunger: ingredients can supply expedition-specific benefits. Farming, fishing and foraging consequently connect home production with combat and exploration preparation. [CK-G](#ck-g)

As infrastructure improves, survival pressure can become easier to handle and attention can move toward building, exploration and bosses. **Interpretation:** that is a useful progression outcome. A successful food supply should free the player for another purpose rather than automatically generate more maintenance.

## 5. Mining and terrain transformation

Mining reveals rooms, harvests ore, opens shortcuts and carves usable space. Players place walls, floors, bridges and objects, making a route something they can deliberately improve. Better tools change both efficiency and which material can be manipulated. [CK-A](#ck-a) [CK-G](#ck-g)

An expedition tunnel can later become a lit road, a rail line, a conveyor route or a protected corridor. **Interpretation:** geography acquires history through use. The reward is not only reaching a new place but changing what a familiar journey requires.

## 6. Material tiers, crafting, repair and upgrading

New ore and materials unlock workbenches, tools, armor, weapons, stations and machinery. The familiar sequence is harder region → new material → better production/tooling → access to another obstacle. The new material changes what the player can manipulate, not merely the number on the character sheet. [CK-D](#ck-d)

### 6.1 Four different verbs apply to an existing item

The **Salvage and Repair Station** distinguishes repairing, reinforcing and salvaging. Repair restores missing durability with Scrap Parts. Reinforcement requires an eligible fully repaired item and additional materials; it adds a second durability layer and an equipment benefit, rather than permanently creating an arbitrary enchantment. Legendary and other special-item exceptions matter. Salvage destroys an item for returned materials, so it is not another way to preserve the same possession. [CK-P](#ck-p)

The **Upgrade Station**, introduced through the Scarlet-tier crafting progression, instead raises an eligible item's level at a resource cost. This lets an earlier tool or preferred piece remain useful beyond its original tier. It does not make repairing, reinforcing and upgrading interchangeable operations. The original 0.7 announcement is the historical introduction, not a complete current price table. [CK-Q](#ck-q)

**Interpretation:** the player has several reasons to keep an object: restore it, improve its immediate endurance, raise its future relevance, or reclaim its components. The interface should identify which action preserves identity and which consumes it. More item generation is less valuable when an existing favorite can never grow with the player.

## 7. Weapons, armor, magic and summoning

Broad combat styles are melee, ranged, magic and summoning. Weapons, armor, accessories, offhands, unusual equipment and pets support different combinations of damage, critical effects, survival, movement, mining and resource acquisition. Use-based skills and matching gear reward commitment without a rigid class lock. [CK-F](#ck-f)

### 7.1 Casting has a resource and a rhythm

Magic uses Mana rather than only a weapon's ordinary attack timing. Equipment, skills and recovery affect how long the player can sustain it. Void & Voltage adds ordinary and Greater Mana Potions, made at different production stations, connecting the casting budget to preparation. The **Tome of Breach** also shows that a summoning tool can provide a mining helper, not only another combat projectile. [CK-C](#ck-c) [CK-R](#ck-r)

**Current correction:** 1.3 no longer stops mana regeneration completely after casting. Regeneration continues at **50% effectiveness during magic attacks and for one second afterward**, before returning to its ordinary rate. Several gear/talent effects were changed to match. Old wiki language about a complete post-cast regeneration pause is historical, not the current rule. [CK-B](#ck-b)

### 7.2 Summons are maintained participants, not an expiring cast queue

Summoning uses the appropriate tome and available capacity; gear and talents alter the supported force. In 1.3, minions no longer expire automatically, spawning cooldown is removed, and the player can summon the permitted group together or dismiss it. Scepters provide another way to support minion damage. Older lifespan/despawn talent descriptions must not be combined with these new rules as though both remain active. [CK-B](#ck-b)

**Interpretation:** this changes the player's work from repeatedly renewing a short timer toward selecting, positioning and supporting a continuing force. It does not establish general strategic intelligence in each minion. A helper's usable role still depends on targeting, travel and the actual effect it can perform.

## 8. Combat, secondary actions and bosses

Combat is real-time. The player moves, aims, attacks, uses magic and summons, kites, deploys healing/buffs and exploits terrain. Some weapons have charged secondary actions: a spear thrust and an axe's wider strike ask for different positioning. Shield timing can create a parry opportunity. Those verbs coexist with equipment and preparation rather than replacing them. [CK-S](#ck-s)

Bosses gate important progression. Early slime and worm encounters pose different spatial problems; later Titans, hydras and mechanical threats expand the repertoire. TheSixthAxis values the challenge but notes that particular bosses can become progression walls. **Interpretation:** stronger equipment should make a plan more viable without removing the need to read what the encounter demands. [CK-F](#ck-f)

## 9. Death, recovery and difficulty

World difficulty and character death rules are different choices. Under the documented ordinary character rules, death respawns the explorer at the Core or a set bed and leaves ordinary backpack contents in a tombstone. Hotbar and worn equipment are retained, with equipment wear among the costs. Recovery is therefore a return expedition, not a reset of all learned skills or deletion of the world. [CK-T](#ck-t)

Hardcore instead risks permanent loss of that character. The world and its infrastructure are separate; retrieving goods with another character does not resurrect the dead character's accumulated development. The consulted references do not consistently describe every hardcore equipment exception, so no universal all-items recovery promise is made here. [CK-T](#ck-t) [CK-U](#ck-u)

Version-specific exceptions matter. In 1.3, vehicles are no longer sent to the grave in the old manner when the player dies while riding; the item is returned to inventory. That is not evidence that every possession avoids ordinary death consequences. [CK-B](#ck-b)

**Interpretation:** separate a lost inventory, a damaged tool, a lost person and an enduring world. Each supports a different kind of recovery story. A configurable contract is more honest than describing every mode as either consequence-free or universally permanent death.

## 10. Farming and renewable resources

Players till soil, plant, grow, harvest and replant. Gardening connects discovered seeds with continuing food, consumables, livestock and trade. A single expedition discovery can therefore become home infrastructure rather than one consumed reward. [CK-A](#ck-a) [CK-G](#ck-g)

Later automation can take over repeated handling. **Interpretation:** the useful question is whether the farm frees attention and serves a project, not merely whether it produces a larger stockpile.

## 11. Cooking: two ingredients compose properties

Cooking combines two supported ingredients into meals. The detailed study preserves Heart Berry's health-oriented value, Glow Tulip's illumination and a meal connecting the two. Duplicate effects do not simply add without rules. Both ingredients can also compete with other crafting uses. [CK-G](#ck-g)

**Interpretation:** recognizable ingredient properties support plausible experimentation. The generated meal name is less important than understanding why this preparation fits the expedition. A recipe catalog should describe behavioral differences, not merely every possible label.

## 12. Fishing and water

The player equips a rod, casts into eligible water, responds to a bite and retrieves a catch. Biome, water type, fishing capability and optional bait affect available results; visibly active fish spots differ from undisturbed water's broader catch possibilities. Fish become food and preparation ingredients, while other catches can be resources or collectibles. A better rod is useful because it changes accessible opportunities, not just collection speed. [CK-V](#ck-v)

The fishing minigame is **optional in the documented current settings**. Advice written during the brief period when it was removed must not be presented as a permanent absence, and an old mandatory-minigame description is equally incomplete. The alternative allows the catch interaction without that extra input challenge. The page also documents explosives interacting with fishing, but that is not a guarantee that every explosive or water situation yields an optimal catch. [CK-V](#ck-v)

**Interpretation:** optional execution difficulty can preserve an activity's production role for players with different preferences. The system still needs useful outputs and geographical distinctions; removing an input minigame does not itself make fishing either deep or pointless.

## 13. Cattle, pets and mounts

These are related living entities but not identical systems. Cattle consume suitable food and produce resources. Pets occupy an equipment-associated companion role and either attack or supply benefits. In 1.3, Saddles turn eligible cattle into mounts; naming and follow controls make the same creature useful as a recognizable traveling companion. [CK-B](#ck-b) [CK-S](#ck-s)

### Pet development

Find or acquire an egg, incubate it, hatch it and equip the pet. Combat-related experience or Pet Candy raises it toward level 10; talent points arrive at intervals, up to five. Individual talent offerings create choices within the pet's role rather than letting one creature learn every benefit simultaneously. This is a second development track, not another copy of the explorer's own Summoning skill. [CK-W](#ck-w) [CK-S](#ck-s)

A support pet need not attack to justify its slot. **Earie** improves a minion-oriented setup, whereas **Arcane Symbiote** supports magical protection and the current casting-regeneration system. The relevant contribution depends on the owner's build; appearance and attachment can still be legitimate selection reasons. [CK-X](#ck-x)

**Interpretation:** keep production animals, equipment-like helpers and individually developing companions conceptually distinct even when one being participates in several roles. A creature should not require an unrelated duplicate object merely because the player now rides it.

## 14. Base building, lighting and home

Players create rooms, workshops, farms, storage, machines, rail/conveyor routes, decorative spaces and merchant accommodation. The pixel-art view makes illumination consequential: PC Gamer describes warm home spaces against the dangerous underground darkness. [CK-H](#ck-h)

Home becomes meaningful because it is safe, productive, authored and revisited. **Interpretation:** attachment can emerge from use and history without a long generated backstory. More furniture types are not automatically more belonging.

## 15. Automation: source, transfer, process, destination

A common ore chain is a finite boulder, powered Drill or Crude Drill, loose output, conveyor/transfer stages, a compatible smelter, finished-bar removal and storage. The preserved study explains spatial orientation, the Robot Arm and processing compatibility. Extracting ore does not prove usable bars reach the workshop. [CK-G](#ck-g)

**Interpretation:** delegation should describe an end state. Source, transport, transformation, storage, replenishment and a stop condition are separate responsibilities. A helper that finishes only the first stage has not necessarily satisfied the player's request.

## 16. Finite automation creates a natural ending

Large ore boulders deplete. An installation can complete its purpose, become obsolete in that location and be reused elsewhere. [CK-G](#ck-g)

**Interpretation:** this is healthy project structure. Not every machine needs infinite inputs or perpetual attention. “Harvest enough for winter,” “repair the damaged homes” and “survey the valley” can end successfully and release people for other work. Moving an installation is only a new decision when the new context matters; otherwise it is repeated setup labor.

## 17. Blocked output: inspectable failures

Production can stall through missing inputs or power, incompatible processing, full output, missing storage or broken transfer. The mining study specifically documents an output-side blockage that more drills do not solve. [CK-G](#ck-g)

**Interpretation:** an agent should report the failed prerequisite. “Could not complete the task” is insufficient when the actual issue is a full chest, missing permission or inaccessible route. Fluent narration cannot repair an opaque physical chain.

## 18. Void & Voltage: deepening an existing vocabulary

The February 25, 2026 release adds machinery as well as a destination. The **Advanced Automation Table** opens tools including a Robot Farm Arm, Seed Extractor, Item Collector and sensors; these extend familiar farming and handling work. Breaker's Reach, its new equipment and the two named bosses give the production expansion an expedition purpose. [CK-O](#ck-o)

Its Titan Shrines connect guidance, scanners, summoning items and waypoints. The patch explains a Content Bundle route for introducing shrines to older worlds. This is a specific compatibility mechanism, not a guarantee that every old world automatically contains every new feature. [CK-C](#ck-c)

**Interpretation:** expansion can deepen transfer, processing and discovery rather than introduce an unrelated minigame. Compatibility is part of that content design: the player needs to know which old progress persists and how new opportunities become accessible.

## 19. Merchants, Cavelings and inhabited-world cues

### 19.1 A service is connected to a place

Most merchants deal in **Ancient Coins** and require appropriate accommodation to move to a base: an enclosed room, a crafted bed, usable floor space and the relevant summoning object. Ghorm's **Mysterious Idol**, for example, connects a boss reward to attracting the Cloaked Merchant. Some merchants can also be encountered naturally before obtaining their relocation item. Their offerings have stock and access conditions, not unlimited player-authored prices. [CK-Y](#ck-y)

The **Spirit Merchant** is an important exception: an Ancient Hologram Pod supplies an immobile, repeatable material-exchange interface rather than an ordinary resident needing the same bedroom. This prevents treating every visible humanoid service as the same kind of simulated person. [CK-Y](#ck-y)

**Interpretation:** home construction can create useful access without a general NPC economy. A bed and idol are authored admission conditions; they do not establish wages, household consumption, friendship or consent-driven migration.

### 19.2 A modest routine changes interpretation

PC Gamer reports Cavelings mining, sleeping and tending crops. Entering their space can consequently feel like an intrusion rather than encountering objects waiting to be attacked. [CK-H](#ck-h)

This does not establish autobiographical memory, complex offscreen goals or conversational cognition. **Interpretation:** practical routines and physical continuity can make life perceptible before dialogue. A richer agent system would have to add actual knowledge, interruption and relationship behavior rather than merely infer it from an animation.

## 20. Procedural world plus authored Scenes

The generated underground contains predesigned **Scenes** with particular arrangements, loot, creatures or environmental stories. Their placement rules have changed across versions; an old description of infinite terrain is not necessarily a current world-generation contract. [CK-G](#ck-g)

**Interpretation:** procedural macro variation and authored local structure serve different purposes. Recognizing a former worksite gives a player something to interpret. Repeated templates can become obvious, so variation in what happens there may matter more than randomly changing decoration.

## 21. Exploration and discovery

Biomes, resources, bosses, ruins, dungeons, merchants, equipment, Scenes and secrets can all change an expedition's purpose. PC Gamer repeatedly values the surprise of an unfamiliar discovery. The strongest loop is discovery → capability → preparation → farther discovery. [CK-H](#ck-h)

The inherited criticism identifies the converse: very large regions can feel empty, and a particular randomly placed structure can become tedious to locate. Echo-Maps turn selected searches into directed journeys; they do not reveal every secret or eliminate all randomness. [CK-E](#ck-e) [CK-C](#ck-c)

## 22. Narrative and boss progression

The ancient Core provides the framing mystery. Restoring its associated progression, confronting bosses and Titans, and exploring old structures move the player from immediate survival toward larger threats. The story is primarily environmental and progression-driven, not a relationship-heavy dialogue campaign. [CK-A](#ck-a) [CK-H](#ck-h)

Road to the Maw and the Riders preview preserve an ongoing secret/door hook. A preview showing a hydra is not proof the entire hydra system is new, and a roadmap's destination is not automatically released content. [CK-I](#ck-i)

**Interpretation:** mysterious structures are valuable when they give curiosity a direction and later become usable. A gate that only postpones the same fight with larger numbers is a weaker discovery reward.

## 23. Multiplayer, character persistence and world persistence

A host selects a world and character, then shares a Game ID. Joining a place is not equivalent to creating one fused party identity. Character skills and carried possessions are stored separately from terrain, buildings and other world state. Published player accounts specifically describe taking the same developed explorer and inventory into different friends' worlds; those are dated observations, not a promise of arbitrary cross-console save transfer. [CK-Z](#ck-z) [CK-U](#ck-u) [CK-AA](#ck-aa)

**Interpretation:** a visitor can bring capability without bringing their home. The host's world can retain shared construction while the visitor continues elsewhere. That requires explicit group norms around imported equipment, common storage and who maintains an installation. Independent progress is not automatically fair or understandable progress.

### Platform and access limits

The publisher's 1.0-era FAQ identifies up to eight players for PC and PlayStation/Xbox families, versus four on the original Switch, and distinguishes PC-storefront crossplay from within-console-family access. The later Switch 2 announcement adds eight-player hosting and says original Switch users can join such sessions. Thus the older four-player statement must not be generalized to every Nintendo-hosted configuration. A dated announcement/FAQ is not a September 2026 live connection test or a guarantee of universal crossplay. [CK-Z](#ck-z) [CK-AB](#ck-ab)

Players can divide exploration, mining, construction, cooking, farming, automation and boss preparation without hard MMO class roles. The useful social outcome is one person's infrastructure benefiting another. Historical player reports of server rubberbanding and the original Switch review's lack of split-screen/local-wireless access remain specific to their setup and period, not universal multiplayer failure. [CK-E](#ck-e) [CK-J](#ck-j)

## 24. Art, audio, interface and feel

The visual vocabulary combines top-down pixel rendering, dynamic light, dark caves, glowing materials, water effects and distinct biome palettes. Jonathan Geer's music supplies another identity layer; playable instruments also let players make a shared base audible. These are credited/described features, not a new soundtrack audition. [CK-D](#ck-d)

Crafting stations, maps, skill trees, placement tools and quick deposit can make complex activity manageable. Reviewers nevertheless identify controller navigation, weak initial instruction and inventory trips. A useful shortcut does not remove friction for someone who never discovers it. [CK-D](#ck-d) [CK-J](#ck-j)

**Interpretation:** the home/exploration contrast depends on readability as well as decoration. A rich lighting effect can orient a player, while overlapping combat effects can conceal the same target. More visual detail does not settle that tradeoff.

## 25. Production and community contribution

Pugstorm is a Swedish indie studio. Fredrik Präntare's December 2022 interview describes combining familiar influences and building on the team's **Radical Rabbit Stew** code. A wooden spoon initially stood in for a pickaxe, and darkness was used to invite the player to seek light. Cooperative play was a deliberate pillar. These are attributed creator accounts, not a reconstructed private budget or source-code audit. [CK-G](#ck-g)

Early Access provided feedback and time to expand the systems before 1.0. The **Scene Makers** initiative in 2024 invited constrained community scene submissions for possible inclusion, with selection and credit rules. Submission did not guarantee inclusion. [CK-G](#ck-g)

**Interpretation:** a concrete requested artifact with an actual route into play can be more useful than a general creator marketplace without a purpose. Prior team experience also changes what a rapid-production success story means: this was not an inexperienced team creating everything from an empty slate.

## 26. Distribution, community and live support

The product has PC and console storefronts, digital and physical releases, period-specific subscription distribution, free major updates, mod support and community events. The roadmap extends the discovery loop; it is not evidence of an introduced seasonal battle-pass economy. [CK-A](#ck-a) [CK-Z](#ck-z)

The 1.2 material supplies an official modding-guide route. A mod ecosystem's existence does not prove every mod works on every platform. Gamereactor reports some unsuccessful mod attempts in its tested 2024 setup; that is useful compatibility testimony, not a current service audit. [CK-C](#ck-c) [CK-AC](#ck-ac)

**Interpretation:** ongoing access, compatibility and useful community creations are separate jobs. A feature list is not proof that a newcomer can find and use the particular extension they need.

## 27. Commercial and participation context

The earlier chapter preserves **250,000 copies in the first Early Access week** and **500,000 in two weeks**. A September 2024 publisher announcement subsequently reported **three million players worldwide** after 1.0. Those measures are different: players can include subscription access, while paid units describe purchases. [CK-K](#ck-k) [CK-L](#ck-l)

The previous pass also observed substantial positive Steam review volume. That is a dated reception observation, not a new active-user count, revenue estimate or profit calculation. No title-level budget, retention dataset, audited profitability or channel-attribution analysis is established here. [CK-A](#ck-a)

Negative findings included shallow late repetition, decorative/vendor clutter, random-location searches, skill grind, thin magic/summon options and some server/input concerns. ## 30. Concrete situations

The following preserve the five earlier situations and add three operational examples. Except the explicitly attributed Caveling observation, they are **constructed illustrations**, not newly played tests.

### Situation A — turn discovery into infrastructure

**Goal:** exploit a distant ore boulder. **Action:** secure/light the route, power drills, transfer ore, feed a compatible smelter and store the bars. **Result:** discovery becomes a supply node. **Next decision:** improve the bottleneck or work elsewhere. **Limit/ending:** the finite boulder depletes; a successful installation need not run forever. [CK-G](#ck-g)

### Situation B — diagnose the wrong bottleneck

**Observation:** ore accumulates despite ample extraction. **Actual issue:** the smelter's output or destination is full. **Action:** clear or extend the downstream stage, not the drills. **Result:** flow resumes. **Next decision/limit:** confirm the next stage has capacity rather than assuming one repair makes the whole chain permanently reliable. [CK-G](#ck-g)

### Situation C — cook for the expedition

**Goal:** prepare for darkness. **Action:** combine Heart Berry and Glow Tulip under the cooking rules. **Result:** a meal connects bodily preparation with illumination. **Next decision:** consume or reserve the ingredients for another supported use. **Limit:** compatible properties combine under rules; duplicate effects are not arbitrary addition. [CK-G](#ck-g)

### Situation D — routine changes the meaning of intrusion

**Attributed observation:** Livingston sees Cavelings working and sleeping. **Result:** entering their space feels more like intruding into habitation than clearing a spawn room. **Next research question:** what happens when the routine is interrupted? **Limit:** visible work does not establish private memory or a complete society. [CK-H](#ck-h)

### Situation E — the familiar animal becomes transport

**Goal:** change the cost of repeated travel. **Action:** use the supported saddle/mount route and name the animal. **Result:** a being associated with the base gains another practical role. **Next decision:** travel, bring it along or keep it at home. **Limit:** a mount is still a particular entity with supported controls, not an unrestricted vehicle-creation tool. [CK-B](#ck-b)

### Situation F — preserve a useful object through the right operation

A player likes an earlier weapon but needs greater capability. Repairing only restores wear; reinforcing supplies another kind of benefit; the Upgrade Station addresses item level. The player compares those costs before spending material. **Limit:** the actions have different eligibility rules, and salvaging would destroy the object rather than improve it. [CK-P](#ck-p) [CK-Q](#ck-q)

### Situation G — a boss reward becomes a service at home

After obtaining the Mysterious Idol, a player creates suitable accommodation for the Cloaked Merchant instead of treating the reward as miscellaneous loot. **Result:** an expedition changes future shopping access. **Next choice:** spend coins, relocate the service or retain resources. **Limit:** room conditions are authored; this is not a negotiated household simulation. [CK-Y](#ck-y)

### Situation H — choose a helper for the whole build

An explorer using minions compares an attacking pet with Earie's support. The latter can improve the group's contribution without personally supplying another attack pattern. **Next choice:** develop that pet or choose a different companion for a different loadout. **Limit:** a benefit tied to minions has less value when the owner is not using them; affection and appearance can still justify another choice. [CK-X](#ck-x)

## 31. Transferable inspiration for OpenLegend

### A. Let places change roles

Discovery can become home, mine, shrine, road hub, farm or a ruin under restoration. Novelty need not end at first sight.

### B. Define delegation by outcome

“Mine” differs from “maintain 100 iron bars at the forge.” The latter implies a chain, a destination and a stop condition.

### C. Make bottlenecks explainable

Missing input, blocked output, absent destination, no power, no permission and an unreachable route need different responses. Do not collapse them into a generic failure line.

### D. Give automation natural completion

Finite projects free attention. Successful delegation should enable another purpose rather than manufacture endless chores.

### E. Let familiar ingredients carry properties

Compositional preparation can be rich without arbitrary natural-language alchemy. Preserve understandable transformations and exceptions.

### F. Use small routines to imply life, then deepen where useful

Working, resting and eating can make a place feel inhabited. More ambitious cognition must build on actual continuity, not a description of it.

### G. Mix procedural macro-space with authored local situations

Pure randomness risks semantic emptiness. A recognizable situation gives the player something to infer and change.

### H. Avoid inventory entropy

Generated objects are not automatically meaningful if most are clutter. Repair, upgrading, reuse and deliberate reclamation can preserve identity better than endless replacement.

### I. Separate the traveler from the place

Character-held knowledge and goods are not the host world's buildings and history. Shared activity needs explicit ownership and import rules rather than silently treating all progress as one save.
