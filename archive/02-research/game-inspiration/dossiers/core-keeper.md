# Core Keeper — full research dossier

**G46 · Complete research pass, September 26, 2026.** This dossier uses **Core Keeper 1.3.0.1 — Riders of the Underground (September 21, 2026)** as the current boundary. It also incorporates the February 25, 2026 **Void & Voltage** 1.2 expansion of machinery, Breaker's Reach, Echo Maps and late-game equipment. Historical Early Access and 1.0 criticism is kept version-qualified.

[Preserved overview](../games/core-keeper.md) · [Detailed mechanics study](../mechanics/core-keeper-resource-circuits-food-and-inhabited-discovery.md) · [Requirements](../research-requirements.md) · [Progress](../research-progress.md)

Core Keeper's strongest OpenLegend lesson is the relationship between **place, resource, automation and return**. A location is not consumed when first discovered: a cavern can become a road, an ore deposit can become an extraction site, a biome can become a farm/ranch source, and a base can turn expedition discoveries into durable capability.

## 1. Identity and current scope

Core Keeper is Pugstorm's top-down mining/survival sandbox, published by Fireshine Games, for one to eight players on its larger-platform configurations. Early Access began March 8, 2022; PC/PS5/Xbox Series 1.0 launched August 27, 2024, with additional console versions following. [CK-A](#ck-a)

The player:
- creates an explorer;
- awakens around the mysterious Core;
- mines and shapes terrain;
- gathers ores/resources;
- crafts tools/equipment;
- farms, cooks and fishes;
- builds and decorates a base;
- automates extraction/processing;
- fights creatures and bosses;
- unlocks merchants/NPC support;
- explores generated biomes and authored scene pockets;
- raises animals/pets;
- plays solo or cooperatively;
- progresses toward Titans and the Core's larger mystery.

The current **1.3 Riders of the Underground** update adds:
- the Moss sub-biome in Forgotten Ruins;
- Orex cattle;
- Saddles/Racing Saddles;
- rideable/tameable cattle mounts;
- mount naming/follow behavior;
- further hydra/secret progression;
- Summoning changes so minions persist rather than automatically expiring. [CK-B](#ck-b)

The February 2026 **1.2 Void & Voltage** update added:
- Breaker's Reach;
- a Void sub-biome;
- advanced machinery/automation;
- new tools/weapons/equipment/armor;
- another minion tome;
- Echo Maps that reveal points of interest;
- S.A.H.A.B.A.R. and other late-game content. [CK-C](#ck-c)

## 2. Character creation and backgrounds

Players create a visual character and choose a starting **background**.

Backgrounds primarily determine:
- starting equipment/items;
- early thematic direction.

They are not irreversible RPG classes.

A player can later:
- use any supported weapon style;
- train any skill;
- equip any compatible gear.

This is a good compromise between:
- readable first-session identity;
- long-term freedom.

The Nomad background can begin with essentially no special equipment, while other backgrounds ease a chosen activity. [CK-D](#ck-d)

## 3. Skills level through use

Core Keeper progression includes skills tied to repeated activity, including categories around:
- Mining;
- Running;
- Melee Combat;
- Vitality;
- Crafting;
- Ranged Combat;
- Gardening;
- Fishing;
- Cooking;
- Magic;
- Summoning;
- other update-specific systems.

Using an activity earns experience in that domain.

Skill levels grant talent points at thresholds, then the player chooses bonuses from relatively compact trees.

### Strength

This makes expertise feel behavior-linked:
> mine to become a better miner; cook to become a better cook.

### Friction

Current negative reviews repeatedly argue that:
- some skill trees are shallow;
- maxing certain skills can require excessive repetition;
- magic/summoning options feel thinner than melee/ranged for some players. [CK-E](#ck-e)

OpenLegend should preserve “practice improves relevant skill” while avoiding grind that exists merely to move a bar.

## 4. Health, food and survival

The player manages:
- health;
- food/hunger;
- damage/status effects;
- armor/resistance;
- healing.

Food is not merely a starvation timer.

Cooked meals can provide:
- healing;
- temporary buffs;
- exploration/combat utility.

Because food ingredients are farmed/fished/foraged, survival connects:
- home economy;
- expedition preparation.

The survival burden is comparatively forgiving once infrastructure develops, letting later attention shift toward exploration/building/combat.

## 5. Mining and terrain transformation

Mining is the core world verb.

Players:
- break walls/terrain;
- reveal caves/rooms;
- harvest ore blocks;
- open shortcuts;
- create tunnels;
- carve base space;
- place walls/floors/bridges/objects.

Tool progression changes:
- mining damage;
- access to harder materials;
- efficiency.

This makes the map **editable history**.

A winding expedition path can later become:
- a lit road;
- minecart line;
- conveyor route;
- safe corridor.

OpenLegend should similarly let traversal infrastructure transform repeated journeys instead of forcing the same friction forever.

## 6. Material tiers and crafting progression

Progression follows ore/material tiers.

New materials unlock:
- workbenches;
- tools;
- armor;
- weapons;
- automation components;
- stations;
- advanced equipment.

A common loop:
1. enter harder biome;
2. discover new ore;
3. craft better station/tools;
4. mine harder material;
5. improve combat/mining;
6. reach new biome/boss.

The 2026 content continues this ladder with Void-related and later current materials/equipment.

The critical pattern:
> the new material changes **what the world can be manipulated with**, not only a character stat.

## 7. Weapons, armor and build styles

Combat supports several broad styles:
- melee;
- ranged;
- magic;
- summoning.

Equipment includes:
- weapons;
- armor;
- accessories;
- offhands;
- special/legendary items;
- pets/companions;
- update-specific gear.

Items can support:
- damage;
- crit;
- survivability;
- movement;
- mining;
- summon capacity;
- resource acquisition.

TheSixthAxis specifically notes distinct weapon/armor paths for melee, ranged, magic and summons. [CK-F](#ck-f)

A player can switch styles rather than being class-locked, although use-based skills and specialized gear reward commitment.

### Current Summoning boundary

1.3 changed Summoning so minions persist until deliberately despawned rather than expiring automatically, and adjusted the Summoning talent tree accordingly. [CK-B](#ck-b)

This is a meaningful current-version change; historical complaints about minion duration must be read against it.

## 8. Combat and bosses

Combat is real-time.

Player actions include:
- move;
- aim;
- melee;
- fire projectiles;
- cast/use magic;
- summon;
- dodge/kite;
- consume healing/buffs;
- exploit terrain.

Bosses are major progression gates.

Early examples show different problems:
- giant slime controls terrain;
- giant worm moves too quickly to approach conventionally.

Later bosses include:
- Titans;
- hydras;
- Void/robot encounters.

The important design feature is that better gear helps but does not fully replace:
- reading attack patterns;
- movement;
- preparation.

TheSixthAxis praises boss challenge while noting some can feel like walls. [CK-F](#ck-f)

## 9. Death and difficulty

World creation supports configurable difficulty/world-generation options.

Death consequences vary by mode/settings; standard play is recoverable rather than an automatic permanent-world failure.

Hardcore-style options increase stakes.

The game supports a useful separation:
- world persistence;
- character death pressure.

OpenLegend should likewise make death a world-rule contract:
- injury;
- dropped property;
- respawn;
- revival;
- permadeath;
- successor

rather than one engine-universal behavior.

## 10. Farming and renewable resources

Players can:
- till soil;
- plant seeds;
- grow crops;
- harvest/replant;
- scale food/ingredient supply.

Gardening develops through use.

Farming supports:
- cooking;
- potion/consumable supply;
- livestock;
- sale/trade.

It turns one expedition discovery—a seed—into durable home infrastructure.

## 11. Cooking: two ingredients compose properties

Cooking combines two supported ingredients into meals.

The detailed study preserves:
- Heart Berry as a health-oriented ingredient;
- Glow Tulip as an illumination-oriented ingredient;
- a meal combining the two as expedition preparation. [CK-G](#ck-g)

Effects do not simply add every duplicate property without rules.

This gives the player:
- recognizable ingredients;
- plausible experimentation;
- bounded transformation.

OpenLegend can use the same pattern:
> objects carry semantic properties that transformations preserve/modify according to explicit rules.

A generated meal name is less important than why it helps.

## 12. Fishing and water

Fishing provides another resource/collection activity.

Its outputs support:
- food;
- rare ingredients;
- collection/progression.

Water and biome presentation also create:
- environmental identity;
- exploration barriers/routes;
- fishing regions.

The activity is optional enough that current player criticism can reasonably find it underdeveloped without making it a blocker for all playstyles. [CK-E](#ck-e)

## 13. Cattle, pets and current mounts

Animals can be raised/cared for and integrated into the base.

The current **Riders of the Underground** update substantially expands this:
- all cattle can be converted into mounts via Saddle items;
- Orex appears in the Moss sub-biome;
- mounts can be named;
- mount follow behavior can be controlled;
- mount acquisition changes travel. [CK-B](#ck-b)

This creates another strong OpenLegend pattern:
> the same living entity can be livestock, companion, transport and named possession without being duplicated into separate object systems.

Pets/companions also support combat/build variety.

## 14. Base building, lighting and home

Players construct:
- rooms;
- walls/floors;
- crafting areas;
- farms;
- storage;
- automation;
- rail/conveyor infrastructure;
- decorative spaces;
- NPC rooms.

The top-down pixel-art presentation makes lighting especially important.

PC Gamer repeatedly describes the warmth of illuminated home spaces against the dangerous dark underground. [CK-H](#ck-h)

The base becomes emotionally useful because it is:
- safe;
- lit;
- productive;
- authored;
- repeatedly revisited.

OpenLegend should favor places that become meaningful through use/history rather than solely generated backstory.

## 15. Automation: source, transfer, process, destination

Core Keeper's automation is especially instructive.

A common ore chain:
1. locate a large finite ore boulder;
2. power Drill/Crude Drill;
3. extract loose ore;
4. move output via spatial machinery/conveyors;
5. use Robot Arm to transfer;
6. feed compatible smelter;
7. remove finished bars;
8. store at destination.

The detailed study emphasizes:
> “drill ore” is not the same request as “keep usable bars available at my workshop.” [CK-G](#ck-g)

Each stage has a different failure.

This maps directly to OpenLegend delegation:
- source;
- transport;
- transformation;
- storage;
- replenishment;
- stop condition.

## 16. Finite automation creates a natural ending

Large ore boulders are finite.

A mining installation therefore eventually:
- completes its task;
- becomes obsolete;
- can be moved/reused.

That is healthy automation design.

Not every machine needs:
- infinite input;
- infinite output;
- permanent attention.

OpenLegend projects can likewise terminate:
- “harvest this grove until winter reserve is full.”
- “repair every house damaged by the storm.”
- “survey this valley.”

Then actors become available for something new.

## 17. Blocked output: inspectable failures

Automation can stall because:
- input absent;
- power absent;
- processing station incompatible;
- output full;
- destination missing;
- path/transfer broken.

The mining reference specifically warns that a full smelter/output chain can halt progress. [CK-G](#ck-g)

This is an excellent agent UX standard:
> failure should identify the blocking precondition.

A worker saying “I couldn't complete it” is insufficient if the system knows:
- storage full;
- no permission;
- missing tool;
- route blocked.

## 18. Void & Voltage: automation as expansion theme

The February 25, 2026 **1.2 Void & Voltage** update makes machinery itself a major content theme.

It adds:
- advanced machines/automation;
- Breaker's Reach and Void content;
- new tools/weapons/armor;
- Echo Maps;
- new bosses;
- official modding guidance. [CK-C](#ck-c)

The game can therefore expand by deepening an existing vocabulary:
- electricity;
- transfer;
- industrial processing;

rather than inventing an unrelated minigame.

## 19. NPCs, Cavelings and inhabited-world cues

Core Keeper has NPC/merchant support and Caveling inhabitants.

PC Gamer reports seeing Cavelings:
- mine;
- sleep;
- tend crops. [CK-H](#ck-h)

That modest routine changes the interpretation:
> entering their area feels more like entering someone's place than encountering spawned attack targets.

But the evidence does **not** establish:
- rich autobiographical memory;
- complex offscreen goals;
- conversational cognition.

OpenLegend should combine:
- practical routines;
- physical continuity;
- richer cognition

without assuming dialogue alone creates life.

## 20. Procedural world plus authored scenes

The underground is procedurally generated.

Inside it, the game also places authored/predesigned **Scenes**:
- distinctive arrangements;
- loot;
- creatures;
- environmental storytelling.

This hybrid is valuable:
- procedural macro variation;
- authored local readability.

A fully generative system risks producing places that are technically unique but semantically shapeless.

OpenLegend can similarly use authored situation templates as constraints/seed structures while allowing:
- actors;
- history;
- resources;
- damage;
- ownership

to make each occurrence truly different.

## 21. Exploration and discovery

Exploration reveals:
- biomes;
- resources;
- bosses;
- dungeons;
- ruins;
- scenes;
- NPCs;
- equipment;
- crafting materials;
- secrets;
- current Echo Map targets.

PC Gamer's review repeatedly celebrates encounters that produced “what is that?” surprise. [CK-H](#ck-h)

The strongest loop is:
> discovery → capability → preparation → farther discovery.

Current negative reviews identify the failure case:
- huge procedural regions can feel empty;
- RNG can make specific structures/items tedious to locate. [CK-E](#ck-e)

Echo Maps in 1.2 partly address the problem by turning some late exploration into directed search. [CK-C](#ck-c)

## 22. Narrative and boss progression

The framing mystery is the ancient Core.

The player:
- restores/activates Core-linked progression;
- defeats bosses/Titans;
- opens access to further regions;
- discovers ruins/lore;
- advances toward deeper current mysteries such as Road to the Maw content.

Narrative is mostly:
- environmental;
- boss/progression-driven;
- exploration-based.

It is not a relationship-heavy authored RPG.

Current 1.3's “mysterious door”/hydra progression shows the live game still uses environmental gates/secrets to pull exploration forward. [CK-I](#ck-i)

## 23. Multiplayer

PC supports up to eight players in cooperative worlds; platform limits differ. [CK-A](#ck-a)

Players can specialize organically:
- explore;
- mine;
- build;
- farm;
- cook;
- automate;
- boss hunt.

There are no hard MMO-style class roles.

The cooperative strength is that one player's infrastructure becomes useful to others.

### Current limitations

Historical/current Steam feedback includes:
- server performance/rubberbanding for some larger/dispersed groups;
- platform-specific player-count limits;
- no split-screen/local wireless on Switch;
- no universal cross-play assumption. [CK-E](#ck-e) [CK-J](#ck-j)

These are version/platform-sensitive; do not generalize one player's server issue to all multiplayer.

## 25. Production

Pugstorm is a Swedish indie studio.

A 2022 creator interview describes:
- deliberately combining familiar survival/crafting influences;
- reusing technical experience/code from **Radical Rabbit Stew**;
- an early wooden-spoon placeholder that became a pickaxe;
- designing the dark opening to make seeking light intuitive;
- choosing co-op as a major pillar. [CK-G](#ck-g)

This matters because the product was not created from an empty slate by a team with no reusable code/judgment.

### Early Access

Early Access gave Pugstorm:
- real player feedback;
- time to expand bosses/biomes/systems;
- community visibility.

1.0 then reframed a much larger, more polished game rather than merely removing an Early Access label.

### Scene Makers

In 2024, the Scene Makers initiative invited constrained community scene submissions for possible inclusion in the full game. [CK-G](#ck-g)

This is a useful creator-economy lesson:
> ask communities for a specific artifact with a real distribution path.

## 30. Concrete situations

### Situation A — turn discovery into infrastructure

**Goal:** exploit a distant ore boulder.

**Action:** light/secure path, place powered drills, route output, feed smelter, store bars.

**Result:** one expedition location becomes a durable supply node.

**Next decision:** improve throughput or spend time elsewhere.

**Ending:** the finite boulder eventually depletes.

**Lesson:** infrastructure can have a successful completion state.

### Situation B — diagnose the wrong bottleneck

**Observed:** ore piles up even though drill count is high.

**Actual issue:** smelter output/storage is full.

**Action:** clear/add output stage rather than adding extraction.

**Lesson:** automation must expose stage-level blockers.

### Situation C — cook for the expedition

**Goal:** prepare for dark exploration.

**Action:** combine a health-oriented ingredient with Glow Tulip.

**Result:** one meal can carry a useful combination such as sustain + illumination under current cooking rules.

**Lesson:** transformations should preserve recognizable ingredient semantics.

### Situation D — inhabitant routine changes moral interpretation

**Observation:** Cavelings are seen farming/mining/sleeping.

**Result:** attacking/entering can feel like intruding into inhabited space rather than clearing a monster room.

**Lesson:** simple world routines can create social meaning before dialogue.

### Situation E — current mount changes traversal

**Goal:** accelerate long journeys.

**Action:** saddle/tame cattle in 1.3, name it and use it as a mount.

**Result:** a previously production/ecology-oriented creature also becomes transport.

**Lesson:** reuse living entities across systems instead of spawning a separate vehicle ontology.

## 31. Transferable inspiration for OpenLegend

### A. Let places change roles

Discovery should sometimes become:
- home;
- mine;
- shrine;
- road hub;
- farm;
- ruin under restoration.

### B. Define delegation by outcome

“Mine” differs from:
> maintain 100 iron bars at the forge.

The latter implies a chain and stop condition.

### C. Make bottlenecks explainable

Agents/machines should report:
- missing input;
- blocked output;
- no destination;
- no power;
- no permission;
- unreachable path.

### D. Give automation natural completion

Finite projects free attention.

### E. Let familiar ingredients carry properties

Compositional crafting can be rich without arbitrary natural-language alchemy.

### F. Use small routines to imply life, then deepen where valuable

Work/sleep/eat first; autobiographical cognition can build on physical continuity.

### G. Mix procedural macro-space with authored local situations

Pure randomness often produces semantic emptiness.

### H. Avoid inventory entropy

More generated objects are not more meaningful if most are:
- vendor trash;
- decoration noise;
- duplicate clutter.
