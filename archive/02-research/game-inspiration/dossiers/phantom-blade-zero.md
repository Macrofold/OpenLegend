# Phantom Blade Zero — G186 full research dossier

**Evidence cutoff: 31 October 2026. Research in progress.** This is a source-based study of the released PC/PS5 game. It is not a personal playthrough, performance benchmark or approved Open Legend feature specification. The [scoped record](../research-progress-phantom-blade-zero-2026-10-31.md) owns completion and outstanding work.

## Confirmed scope and commercial distinction

S-GAME's official site gives **29 October 2026** as the global launch date. Its Steam announcement places release at **28 October, 7 p.m. PDT**, equivalent to 10 p.m. in New York and 29 October in UTC. The prior September 9 announcement is superseded. [Official game site](https://pbz.s-game.com/en-US/) · [Official Steam announcements](https://steamcommunity.com/app/4115450/allnews/).

The widely repeated million-plus prelaunch figure is an **estimate**, not a publisher-certified sales announcement. Alinea's October 27 account modeled 850,000 digital purchases across Steam and PS5 and inferred a total above one million after adding unreported WeGame sales. Its separate 80,000-plus purchases after reviews is a part-period observation and must not be added to the reported total without a clearly disjoint period. [Original analyst account](https://alineaanalytics.substack.com/p/phantom-blade-zero-rises-while-final).

That claim also differs from the **one million wishlists in fifteen days** announced in December 2025. Wishlisting is not a purchase, and store/platform totals may include the same person more than once. Preorders opened in August 2026; the full estimated volume did not accumulate solely during launch week. [Official wishlist and preorder announcements](https://steamcommunity.com/app/4115450/allnews/).

## Research scope

The finished dossier will cover major actions and their interactions; equipment, resources, progression and loss; exploration, people and lore; production and S-GAME's earlier portfolio; at least five substantive written reviews and direct contrasting Steam accounts; the dated campaign, advertising placements and audience evidence; commercial definitions and unknowns; creation/modding applicability; and lessons grounded in Open Legend's existing owners.

All major claims will distinguish released mechanics, dated developer intentions, reviewer observations, sampled player accounts, analyst estimates and interpretation. It will not invent private ad spend, channel attribution, audited profitability, long-term retention or unseen mechanics.

## Current evidence limit

The game has been publicly available for only about two days. Launch purchasers' early reactions and pre-release critics' fuller playthroughs can answer different questions. Neither establishes durable retention or the eventual commercial outcome. Unknown financial data and inaccessible source passages remain explicit limits rather than fabricated conclusions.
