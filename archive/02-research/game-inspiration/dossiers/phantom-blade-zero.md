# Phantom Blade Zero — G186 full research dossier

**Evidence cutoff: 31 October 2026.** A source-based study of the released PC/PS5 game, launch reception, S-GAME and the campaign behind early demand. It is not a personal playthrough, independent benchmark or accepted Open Legend feature design. The [scoped completion record](../research-progress-phantom-blade-zero-2026-10-31.md) owns the review and evidence limits.

## Research judgment

**The central lesson is a distinctive promise made credible through repeated opportunities to see and play it.** The campaign gave people a recognizable action fantasy; public demonstrations and longer previews supplied evidence beyond an edited reveal. The [marketing chapter](phantom-blade-zero/04-marketing-business.md) distinguishes that interpretation from measured sales attribution.

The finished game then exposes a useful tension. Spectacular execution can make someone feel highly capable, while another person wants a clearer relationship between each input and the resulting action. The [reception chapter](phantom-blade-zero/02-reception.md) preserves this disagreement alongside praise for combat, weapons and bosses, criticism of narrative interruptions and restricted traversal, and platform-specific technical reports.

For Open Legend, the useful comparison is **recognizable player purpose, credible ordinary play, readable control and consequences that make the next activity worthwhile**. The [application chapter](phantom-blade-zero/05-openlegend-applications.md) connects those questions to existing owners. It selects no new gameplay system, budget or campaign.

## The release and million-copy question

The global release was **29 October 2026**, with the simultaneous US launch on **28 October**. [Official game page](https://pbz.s-game.com/en-US/) · [Official launch announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/app/4115450/allnews/).

The million-plus prelaunch story is a third-party estimate, combining modeled Steam/PS5 purchases with an unquantified WeGame extrapolation. It is distinct from the publisher's older announcement of a million wishlists in fifteen days. The [commercial evidence table](phantom-blade-zero/04-marketing-business.md#commercial-signals-what-each-number-actually-measures) supplies exact dates, quantities, platforms, definitions and source limitations.

Preorders had been available since August. The study does not claim the whole estimated total arrived within a few days, or turn gross sales revenue into studio profit. Its documented launch advertisements also cannot automatically explain purchases measured before those advertisements were reported.

## What the evidence can and cannot answer

The dossier directly examines substantive review bodies, public player testimony, original developer statements, contemporary Chinese reporting and analyst publications. The sources support an extensive account of major mechanics, the campaign sequence and early reactions. They do not supply every hidden interaction, final audited cost, channel-attributed sales, refund rate or long-term retention.

The English and Simplified Chinese Steam samples are visible, helpful-ranked examples, not randomized demographic surveys. Review votes can coexist with criticism, and displayed hours can exceed hours at original posting. Critics' review-period play and launch purchasers' first sessions answer different questions. Neither group should be presented as the sole player experience.

Patch announcements establish intended corrections, not independently verified fixes. Early unofficial mods establish available projects, not yet a sustained popular ecosystem. Dated previews and prior studio games are kept separate from released-game behavior.
