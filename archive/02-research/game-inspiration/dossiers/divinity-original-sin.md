## Written review evidence inspected

**Kyle E. Miller, RPGFan, July 30, 2014:** [full review](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/divinity-original-sin/) praises the first half's exploratory freedom and interaction, but says the second becomes repetitive, easier and narratively less interesting. His cooperative session illustrates separate activities, crime consequences and two protagonists' disagreement; he dislikes the rock-paper-scissors implementation. This is the original game, not an assessment of Enhanced balancing.

**Chris Thursten, PC Gamer, July 11, 2014:** [review](https://www.pcgamer.com/divinity-original-sin-review/) describes a lightning spell conducting through blood into his own party and using rain to save Cyseal's burning ship. He values broad action applicability, enemy access to tools and varied combat. Caveats include weak setting attachment, inventory friction and a slow, poorly explained opening. The beginning and later body were inspected; do not turn his scenario into firsthand researcher play.

**Kevin VanOrd, GameSpot, July 19, 2014:** [full review](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/divinity-original-sin-review/1900-6415819/) finds small material actions unusually meaningful. His grave-digging anecdote ends in a civilian death and a worthless reward, creating regret without a major cinematic branch. He values roleplaying both heroes and rock-paper-scissors conversations, unlike Miller, while criticizing inventory and opening pace. His praise for natural consequences is not proof that every story line reacts correctly; he explicitly reports stale dialogue.

**Stephen Tailby, Push Square, October 27, 2015:** [full PS4 review](https://www.pushsquare.com/reviews/ps4/divinity_original_sin_-_enhanced_edition) values reactive attitudes, bright art, elemental tactics and dynamic local split-screen. A disliked protagonist can lose access to a suspect's conversation. He finds the UI crowded but workable. Exact party-XP generalizations and every inferred damage formula are not adopted without a rule source.

**Dominic Leighton, TheSixthAxis, October 27, 2015:** [PS4 review](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2015/10/27/divinity-original-sin-enhanced-edition-review/) body covers flexible creation, independent partners, action points, surfaces, slow pacing and controller learning. He likes the soundtrack more consistently than the newly voiced NPC performances. Byline and opening section must be verified before final completion; main body and conclusion were read.

**Stephen Meyerink, RPGFan, January 14, 2016:** [Enhanced Edition review](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/divinity-original-sin-enhanced-edition/) supplies a version-specific contrast: stronger later balancing and revised skills, but remaining quest opacity, input/UI glitches and irritating repeated speech. He and his partner preferred permanently split screens to dynamic switching. This extra review complements, rather than replaces, the five independent publications/assessments above; finish reading its remaining middle before describing complete access.

## Actual helpful Steam sample

[English Most Helpful, All Time](https://steamcommunity.com/app/373420/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated) returned complete displayed bodies. **blizzardmetal (June 3, 2017)** describes initial rejection followed by enjoyment once the systems became intelligible. **lindell (December 4, 2015)** praises tactics but dislikes slow animations, repeated barks and sparse quest guidance. **laurelj (November 18, 2020)** enjoys difficult fights while relying on a walkthrough and disliking inventory sorting. The selection is qualitative, not representative; current displayed hours are not hours at publication. Another displayed co-op review makes an incorrect or at least unverified blanket claim that Honour forbids resurrection; do not repeat it. A direct [Metacritic player account](https://www.metacritic.com/game/divinity-original-sin-enhanced-edition/) by **pale81, July 21, 2025**, criticizes late puzzles and hard quest gates despite liking the first half.

## Concrete rule leads already established

TheThirdRace's [Enhanced Edition character/party guide](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/pc/170514-divinity-original-sin-enhanced-edition/faqs/72764) gives nails-plus-boots slipping immunity and recipes using actual stations, ingredients and eligible equipment. Adding an elemental weapon effect can replace the old one rather than stack all elements. [The Teleporter Pyramids](https://divinity.fandom.com/wiki/The_Teleporter_Pyramids) identifies different original/Enhanced acquisition sequences. A [firsthand achievement solution](https://www.trueachievements.com/a209012/hey-hey-do-the-zombie-stomp-achievement) documents throwing a pyramid onto Nick's ship to cross a gap. These are bounded rules and examples, not permission to assume all physics interactions or all objects compose arbitrarily.
