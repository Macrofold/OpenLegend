## Verified release boundary and a consequential qualification

The primary announcement feed identifies **Version 80 — The Blooming Update**, April 5, 2026, as out of beta. Its earlier beta announcement is not the latest delivery state. The same feed describes Version 85 as hoped-for future work; this checkpoint does not count that forecast as a release. The store still presents a paid Early Access game, originally released October 23, 2023. The old approximate six-month development estimate is historical, not a current promise. [P1] [P2]

The new **utility slot** accepts most non-scrap, shop-purchased tools, excluding shovels. Zeekerss explicitly explains that making tools compete with scrap slots had encouraged players to avoid tools altogether. This qualifies the preserved study's four-slot opportunity-cost account: it remains an accurate historical design case, but not the complete current inventory rule. **Interpretation:** a theoretical tradeoff can suppress use of the very interactions that make a game interesting. The useful lesson includes the revision, not only the original scarcity rule. [P2]

The announcement also establishes delivered additions to forest moons and accessibility/graphics settings. The full detailed change list and creature references still need reading before precise behavioral claims are written. Current accessibility work must be kept alongside, not used to erase, the earlier review's criticism of missing hearing alternatives. [P2]
