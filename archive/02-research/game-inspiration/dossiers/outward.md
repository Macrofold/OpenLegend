# Outward — full-game research dossier

**Subject:** G188. **Evidence date:** November 7, 2026. **Status:** In progress; the [scoped research record](../research-progress-outward-2026-11-07.md) owns completion.

This is a research reference, not an accepted Open Legend feature specification. The scope covers the original game, The Soroboreans, The Three Brothers and Definitive Edition. Outward 2 is separate sequel context, with its status to be verified against current primary sources.

## Starting question

How can preparing for, undertaking and returning from an expedition make an ordinary person's adventure feel important—and where do the same demands become repetitive or hostile to players?

The [supplied player thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/outwardgame/comments/1sez96v/36_hours_in_and_im_absolutely_hooked/) provides a useful qualitative case. Its author reports repeated installation and abandonment before a successful playthrough, then describes growing interest in lore and a hybrid magic build. The thread also contains enjoyment of navigation, cooperation and risk, alongside uncertainty about goals and disagreement between friends over hardcore play. This is evidence of differing experiences, not a representative player survey or proof that friction causes enjoyment.

The research will test two linked hypotheses: that connected preparations make achievements personally meaningful, and that the cost of reaching that understanding can exclude people who might otherwise enjoy the game. It will also examine the value of discovery, quiet travel, relationships, fictional institutions, aesthetic pleasure and finite completion without reducing every activity to combat preparation.

## Research scope

The full pass will cover R01–R14 and screen R15: complete mechanics and named interactions; progression, economy, failure and replay; story and lore; people and cooperative state; art/audio/interface; production and marketing; commercial evidence and modeled limits; at least five substantive reviews plus contrasting Steam/player testimony; sustained modding if supported; and applications through existing Open Legend design owners.

Detailed research and source review are underway. No completion is claimed at this checkpoint.

## Applications under review

The [Open Legend applications](outward/04-openlegend-lessons.md) connect expedition payoff, knowledge, recovery, cooperation and continuing play to existing owners. They remain research hypotheses while the supporting source chapters and complete evidence review are finished.
