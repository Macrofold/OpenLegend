## Game, mechanics and player-created content

### 1. Core loop: an RPG race inside a battle royale

The First Soldier combined a seventy-five-player battle royale with melee weapons, firearms and spellcasting. Players landed with their Style’s melee weapon, searched for equipment and fought for survival as the safe area contracted. Monsters offered another way to develop strength before confronting other candidates. The resulting choice was not simply where to find a gun: engaging a monster could produce useful growth while exposing the player to an opportunistic human attacker. [Omer Khan, Gamition, January 28, 2022; full guide body read; unsupported popularity claims excluded](https://gamition.com/final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier-beginners-guide/)

The launch had five Styles: **Warrior, Sorcerer, Monk, Ranger and Ninja**, specializing respectively in melee pressure, area magic, durability and recovery, scouting and ranged combat, and stealth/mobility. The publisher explicitly connected defeating monsters to experience and leveling. Later seasons expanded that choice to eight Styles. [Square Enix launch announcement, November 19, 2021; full primary body read](https://na.finalfantasy.com/news/1861)

**Main Match** offered solo play and teams of three without ranked gains/losses. Style Mastery still advanced there, unlocking further abilities/skills; collection items and Chocobo food could also be earned. Kamigame reports more bots in this mode than in Ranked, but gives no reliable population percentage. Thus a seventy-five-candidate match should not automatically be counted as seventy-five human opponents. [Kamigame Main Match guide; complete body read](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/181803976130302551.html)

The design joins persistent specialization to a match’s temporary equipment and survival constraints. Preparing a preferred Style matters, then the player must find resources and remain alive long enough to exploit them.

### 2. Input and carried tools

The documented mobile shooting options were **Auto Fire**, a **Fixed Fire** button and a **Floating Fire** button. Their purpose was to separate aiming from the choice of how a touch press triggered a shot. The inventory supported two firearms alongside the Style-specific melee weapon, plus three Materia slots. A player therefore could not retain every useful weapon or spell found. The tutorial/training area provided a place to practise attacks and looting. [Mohit Sahi, Mobile Gaming Hub, December 18, 2021; full body read; its unreliable Style summaries and broad marketing judgments are excluded](https://mobilegaminghub.com/ffvii-the-first-soldier-beginners-guide-tips-and-tricks/)

Controller support was added before launch, following the June 2021 beta. Ateam also described a revised tutorial and a training mode for experimenting with firearms and Materia. This means beta criticism of touch-only access cannot be treated as the released product’s permanent input boundary. [Ateam, October 3, 2021; full primary body read](https://www.ateam-entertainment.com/en/news/700/)

Parkour let candidates jump toward walls, climb through windows and cross obstacles to reach elevated firing positions or shorten routes. These movement options interacted with Style-specific traversal rather than requiring every candidate to use a Ninja’s additional jump. [Kamigame parkour guide; full Japanese body read](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/184714459678310068.html)

The separate **dodge roll** used a quick double tap of crouch. It could break a reload to take another action or move into cover. This is a repositioning/cancellation tool; the written guide does not establish universal invulnerability during a roll. [Kamigame rolling guide; full body read](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/187577533045201763.html)

#### Weapons, spells and recovery

Damage also built a separate **Burst** gauge, with melee contributing more quickly than gunfire in the guide. A burst candidate temporarily lost attacking ability and moved slowly; burst monsters stopped acting, including airborne targets falling down. This supplied a reason to sustain pressure beyond simply reducing HP. [Kamigame Burst guide; complete body read](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/186019632627335658.html)

The ordinary firearm families were **assault rifles, light machine guns, submachine guns, sniper rifles, shotguns and handguns**. Ammunition was not interchangeable across all guns: the **Lethal Gaze** and **Shinra Type B 3B** used heavy rounds, **MP-60 Type A** light rounds, and **Model S-7** shotgun shells. Their automatic, burst or single-shot behavior changed exposure and reload opportunities. [Kamigame weapon inventory; complete equipment tables read, not a final balance-stat claim](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/182398813749681936.html)

Magic used **MP** and cooldowns. Repeated copies upgraded equipped Materia, improving their effects. **Fire** exploded on impact; **Blizzard** placed an automatic ice attacker; **Thunder** struck an area; **Comet** sustained a larger bombardment. **Bio** left poison, **Gravity** controlled movement, **Blind** obscured vision, and **Aero** supplied vertical movement. **Cure** restored nearby HP; **Raise** served ally recovery. Finding another strong spell therefore required considering both MP demand and the opportunity cost of replacing one of three slots. [Kamigame Materia guide; full body read; its obvious Blind-row label error is excluded](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/183545646404386138.html)

**Potions** and stronger **Hi-Potions** were consumable recovery, with restoration occurring over time in the launch guide. The item method and repeatable MP-funded healing created distinct reserves: carrying medicine could leave magic available for attack or movement, while keeping Cure reduced dependence on finding more consumables. [Justin Joy, Pro Game Guides, November 17, 2021; full healing article read; its unverified self-revive speculation is excluded](https://progameguides.com/final-fantasy/how-to-heal-in-final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier/)

Defensive and offensive **accessories** altered particular threats and weapon combinations. The Season 3 record describes elemental protection, **Star Pendant** against poison, **Talisman** for MP recovery, **Shiva Bangle** slowing targets hit by assault rifles and **Drain Belt** recovering HP through light-machine-gun hits. **Ifrit Belt** rewarded SMG/shotgun kills with a reload and temporary damage improvement. **Reraise Ring** allowed a self-revival, including in solo. Accessory chests increased access to these builds; they were functional equipment, separate from cosmetic appearance. [Kamigame accessory guide, including June 2 changes; full body read; stale lower-table values are excluded](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/184729690789914286.html)

#### Match growth and the fictional economy

Experience came from monsters, rival candidates, chest-found **Orders** and surviving into subsequent phases. An Order supplied a timed objective such as a monster kill; only one could be active. Leveling increased maximum HP/MP and strengthened selected skills. The documented growth thresholds upgraded skills at levels four and seven. Small **Hedgehog Pies** and **Slug-Rays** offered different time/risk commitments from a **Guard Scorpion**. [Kamigame match-level guide; full body read; this is match growth, not account mastery](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/185398872011483903.html)

**Gil** from candidates, monsters and chests bought items and weapon upgrades from vending machines. This made detours economically meaningful: a battle’s rewards could be converted into a more reliable loadout, but reaching the machine consumed time and could expose the survivor. Chocobos and vehicles changed travel choices; an injured bird’s defeat dismounted its rider rather than automatically eliminating the candidate. [Gamition’s January 2022 guide](https://gamition.com/final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier-beginners-guide/)

A scout’s information can support a teammate’s attack; it does not directly replace ammunition, cover or aim.

**Dragoon**, released with Season 2, used a lance and **Sky Predator** for gliding and downward strikes. Ability choices were **High Jump** or **Dragon’s Favor**, the latter combining speed with melee MP absorption, bleed and kill recovery. Skills were **Aerial Wall** against fire from below, **Dragon’s Ire** to mark attackers and drain their MP, and **Wide Swing** for wider melee combos. [GameSpot’s separate Season 2 guide, February 28, 2022; full body read](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/final-fantasy-7-the-first-soldier-season-2-guide-bahamut-summon-dragoon-style-and-more-big-changes/1100-6501094/)

**Machinist**, introduced June 2, used a hammer. **Support Gear** generated protective sub-gadgets after the main gadget broke. **Air Gadget** offered riding, missiles and an abandoned vehicle’s explosive collision; **Jamming Gadget** created an area that disabled firearms and inflicted continuing damage, including interference with its owner. Selectable **Shot Gear** followed up gun hits, **Heal Gear** sacrificed itself at low health, and **Blast Gear** exploded against a nearby attacker after damage. [Kamigame’s dedicated Machinist guide, including July 19 adjustment history; full Japanese body read and translated; later maintenance stopped](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/212819964648800524.html)

**Trickster**, added in Season 4, fought with cards. **Gold Trick** awarded gil on leveling and scaled melee critical chance/ability recovery with total gil collected. **Decoy Card** created a fighting double; **Marking Card** stored half the user’s HP and returned them to that location with the stored health when reduced to zero while active. Skills were **Seize** for monster-kill gil, **Drain Shot** for headshot healing and **Soul Drain Deck** for MP after using an ability. [Veerender Singh Jubbal, GameSpot, September 2, 2022; substantive article text read through the search index, direct article fetch failed](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/how-to-play-the-new-trickster-style-in-final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier/1100-6507105/)

Style Mastery grew through playing that Style, kills, challenges and wins. The launch guide documented sixty mastery levels and rewards across matches. This was a persistent reason to specialize even though a match’s weapons and survival position had to be rebuilt. It did not lock the player permanently into their initial Style. [Justin Joy, Pro Game Guides, November 17, 2021; full body read; its inaccurate story chronology is excluded](https://progameguides.com/final-fantasy/how-to-improve-your-style-mastery-in-final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier/)

**Later community-documented replacements:** Warrior’s **Critical Ending** increased critical chance on the final melee-combo hit, replacing Countershield; Sorcerer’s **Staggering Touch** let attack Materia stagger candidates, replacing Reinvigorate; Dragoon’s **Urgency** reduced ability cooldown after melee damage, more strongly for divebomb damage, replacing Aerial Wall. Each dedicated wiki page labels old/new skills, but supplies no patch date or primary changelog citation. These are documented later alternatives with a dating limitation, not a fully verified final-build balance sheet. ### 4. Verified later-season mechanics

#### Dominate the Sky — Season 2

**Dragoon** introduced lance attacks and aerial movement: **High Jump** launched the character upward, **Sky Predator** enabled gliding, and **Dragon’s Favor** added MP absorption to melee and recovery after defeating an enemy. **Teleport Materia** linked a targeted location to a portal at the caster’s feet, and other players could use the portals. Defeating the roaming **Bahamut** supplied summon Materia that transformed the successful candidate into Bahamut, rather than merely adding an ordinary gun. [Square Enix, March 1, 2022; full primary Season 2 body read](https://na.finalfantasy.com/news/1934)

Summons had distinct control relationships. **Ifrit** was called from supply-drop Materia to fight autonomously, using attacks such as **Crimson Dive**; the guide warns that holding a summon until the endgame can leave it unusable. [Dedicated Ifrit guide, including August 31 change; full body read](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/184871419996289387.html) **Shiva** likewise supplied an autonomous ally, thematically using **Diamond Dust**. [Dedicated historical Shiva guide; full body read](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/184871384361482603.html) Bahamut’s transformation instead gave its user the creature’s attack kit, including **Flare Breath**, **Divebomb**, **Spin Attack**, **Umbral Inferno** and **Megaflare**. [GameSpot’s Season 2 attack inventory](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/final-fantasy-7-the-first-soldier-season-2-guide-bahamut-summon-dragoon-style-and-more-big-changes/1100-6501094/)

#### Highway Star — Season 3

Season 3 added **Midgar Plateside**, with locations such as Sector 8, the highway and Mako Reactor Core 1. **Machinist** used an Air Gadget for hovering. **Water Materia** provided homing attacks, while the **Serpent Launcher** added a long-range explosive weapon. These were actual gameplay additions in the publisher’s released-season report, not mechanics inferred from the locations’ appearance in Remake. [Square Enix, June 16, 2022; full primary body read](https://www.square-enix-games.com/en_US/home/season-3-highway-star-final-fantasy-vii-first-soldier)

The June 2 announcement distinguishes the launch **Midgar Undercity** map from the added Plateside map. Plateside could use randomly selected day/night presentation, and falling off the plate caused defeat. Season 3 also introduced a map-exploration mode for roaming and taking screenshots with friends, as well as aim-assist improvements. [Ateam, June 2, 2022; full primary body read](https://www.ateam-entertainment.com/en/news/1068/)

The **Dual Wheeler** assault rifle consumed MP for charging, putting a gun into competition with spell use. This was different from choosing between two conventional ammunition types. The Season 3 hands-on report separately describes Water tracking enemies behind cover and the Serpent Launcher’s rockets. [Erren Van Duine, Nova Crystallis, June 6, 2022; full original written body read](https://novacrystallis.com/2022/06/final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier-season-3-all-new-content-explained/)

#### Feast at the Gold Saucer — Season 4

The August 31 update added **Trickster**, Gold Saucer attractions to Undercity, and six more Plateside locations. **GS Battle Square** contained guards/monsters; **GS Wonder Square** supplied breakable reward boxes. **Chance Reels** turned matching symbols into items. **Tornado** cost one MP and explicitly dealt no damage: it pushed candidates, including allies, and removed placed magic such as Blizzard, Gravity and Teleport. The new Sports Car supplied another traversal option. [Ateam’s August 31, 2022 Japanese primary announcement; complete body read and translated](https://www.ateam-entertainment.com/ja/news/1685/)

#### Variant Mode

This released limited mode began August 24, 2022 with **three Variants against nine candidates**. Variants could become **Sephiroth, Bahamut, Cactuar, Bomb or Slug-Ray**. It was not the normal seventy-five-player last-survivor ruleset. [Ateam, August 25, 2022; full primary Japanese body read](https://www.ateam-entertainment.com/ja/news/1677/)

Both sides depleted the opposing power gauge. Candidates could damage two objectives guarded by the Variants; disabled objectives later reactivated. Defeated players respawned, with Variants choosing another form rather than repeating their immediately previous one or duplicating a teammate. Orders could award **Demolition Rocket Launchers** effective against objectives. The fifteen-minute limit rewarded the larger remaining gauge, with ties favoring Variants. Earned tickets prioritized a Variant place without guaranteeing one. [Tony Garsow, Nova Crystallis, August 25, 2022; complete reproduced publisher rules read](https://novacrystallis.com/2022/08/the-first-soldier-reveals-season-4-teaser-and-launches-time-asymmetrical-variant-mode/)

#### Final Season and relevant rule changes

The **Final Season: To Each Battlefield**, recorded on November 30, returned previous collection items and added a system-run competition with qualification and finals. This documents actual activity after the four numbered content seasons rather than assuming development ended with August’s update. [Nova Crystallis’s November 28–December 2 publisher-social roundup; complete First Soldier subsection read](https://novacrystallis.com/2022/11/this-week-in-events-week-48-2022/)

Historical rules changed within seasons: June 2 stopped Shiva drops and airborne potion use; June 17 stopped team-mode Reraise Ring drops; June 30 removed Water’s friendly fire; July 19 improved Ranger reload reduction. These dated changes prevent applying every launch interaction throughout the final service. [Kamigame patch-history record; relevant entries read in full](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/185397285843141319.html)

### 5. Social organization and persistence

Friends could be found by name/ID or recent matches, then accepted through a request. Friendship supported invitations, chat and online-status visibility; settings could hide online status or reject non-friend invitations. These tools supported recurring teams instead of relying exclusively on random encounters. [Kamigame friend-system guide; complete body read](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/184750442914277910.html)

In a match, contextual **signals** marked enemies, items, intended movement, attack or defensive positions. A wheel selected the message; map markers, free text, preset text and in-game voice supplied other coordination channels. A player could therefore communicate a resource or retreat route without needing to describe its location in prose. [Kamigame signal guide; complete body read](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/186880412155436984.html)

In team play, a candidate reaching zero HP first became **downed**. A teammate could approach and complete the `+` interaction, or use **Raise** in range. Expiry of downed HP meant elimination. An eliminated teammate instead needed a map **PHS** and gil to return. These are different recovery states; an ordinary healing potion is not equivalent to buying back an eliminated ally. [Kamigame recovery guide; full body read](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/185405724262700999.html)

**Ranked** maintained separate solo and team standings, with placement and kills contributing. April 14 changes penalized some premature team departures, including quitting while a PHS return remained possible, and protected some matches that began understaffed. The guide’s conflicting rank-count/table presentation prevents reconstructing a dependable final scoring formula. [Kamigame Ranked guide; full body read](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/181797850651337038.html)

The earned economy persisted selectively: **part of match gil** was received afterward, while challenges and mastery also rewarded the account. Neither an entire match wallet carryover nor arbitrary preloaded battle equipment is established. [Kamigame gil guide; full body read](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/184712947849815451.html) Blue vending machines supplied medicine/ammo, green supplied Materia/accessories, yellow sold weapons, and red upgraded held weapons for gil until their rarity ceiling. [Kamigame vending guide; full body read](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/184710304448428628.html)

The **Moogle Shop** appeared after finding a **Mog Bell** or winning. Its gil-priced stock included collection materials, banners, music and Chocobo supplies. Special equipped items imposed a **handicap in the next match**, with achievements for winning under that disadvantage. This is a documented way an account-side choice affected a later battle; it does not establish purchasing stronger starting weapons. The complete final item list and consumption rules remain unverified. [Kamigame Moogle Shop guide; full body read](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/185412546834990859.html)

The match ended with victory or elimination under its mode’s rules; looted combat equipment and match growth were rebuilt on the next deployment. Mastery, unlocked choices, recorded ranking, account collections and specifically supported preparations formed the continuing loop. An unsuccessful battle could still produce progression without making the next match a continuation of the same battlefield. Main Match, Ranked and Variant Mode had different death and scoring rules.

### 6. Six constructed systems interactions

These are worked examples derived from the written rules, not observed play sessions or promised optimal strategies.

1. **Turn a manageable PvE detour into match growth.** A candidate finds a timed monster Order near a small-monster group. Completing the objective yields growth while avoiding a lengthy Guard Scorpion commitment; crossing the next phase also contributes XP. Reaching a skill threshold improves the equipped skill before the next human fight. The detour fails strategically if its time cost leaves the candidate badly positioned when the safe area changes. [Match-growth rules](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/185398872011483903.html)

2. **Choose which resource pays for the next engagement.** A player has the Dual Wheeler, limited MP and a Hi-Potion. Charging the rifle now reduces the reserve available for a spell; taking cover and using the consumable preserves that magic budget but delays re-engagement. The useful comparison is ammunition, MP, healing time and exposure together. A powerful MP-powered gun does not make resource management disappear. [Dual Wheeler report](https://novacrystallis.com/2022/06/final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier-season-3-all-new-content-explained/) · [Consumable recovery](https://progameguides.com/final-fantasy/how-to-heal-in-final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier/)

3. **Avoid being trapped by your own gadget.** A Machinist throws Jamming Gadget onto a contested position, then shoots from outside its interference area. Walking into it would disable the Machinist’s gun too. Once a broken gadget has generated Support Gear, Shot Gear supplies follow-up pressure after hits; Heal Gear instead reserves recovery. The earlier loadout choice and later position determine whether the same gadget supports an attack or obstructs its user. [Machinist rules](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/212819964648800524.html)

4. **Clear a spell-controlled route without expecting damage.** An opponent protects an approach with Blizzard and a Teleport escape. A Season 4 player casts Tornado to remove the placed effects, then attacks with their weapon. Because Tornado itself deals no damage and can push allies, the caster coordinates position before using it. The outcome is an opening and disrupted movement, not a guaranteed kill from the spell alone. [Tornado’s published rules](https://www.ateam-entertainment.com/ja/news/1685/)

5. **Choose between an immediate rescue and a later return.** One teammate is downed beside an exposed lane. Another marks the threat and covers the approach while the rescuer completes the recovery interaction. If the downed timer expires, running over with Cure no longer solves the problem: the survivors need gil and access to a PHS. Leaving prematurely can additionally affect Ranked play. [Recovery states](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/185405724262700999.html) · [Signals](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/186880412155436984.html) · [Ranked departure rule](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/181797850651337038.html)

6. **Win an objective exchange despite a personal defeat.** In Variant Mode, candidates complete an Order for a Demolition Rocket Launcher and attack a defended installation. Reducing the installation’s HP damages the Variant team’s power even if a candidate is then killed. That candidate can respawn, while the installation later reactivates for another attack. The team compares this repeatable objective damage with the remaining match clock rather than treating every death as permanent elimination. [Variant Mode rules](https://novacrystallis.com/2022/08/the-first-soldier-reveals-season-4-teaser-and-launches-time-asymmetrical-variant-mode/)

### Shinra's selection program and the timeline

The player is a candidate seeking entry to **Shinra's SOLDIER unit**, fighting in Midgar with guns, melee weapons and magic. That institutional purpose makes competitive survival a selection exercise within the fiction. [Ateam's November 11, 2021 feature reveal](https://www.ateam-entertainment.com/en/news/772/).

The precise timeline needs care. Producer **Shoichi Ichikawa** explained in March 2022 that **Project 0** began about thirty years before Final Fantasy VII, while this game occurs about ten years after that—approximately **twenty years before the original game**. [Direct producer interview, Unreal Engine](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier-blends-rpg-sensibilities-with-battle-royale-action?lang=en-US). This distinction qualifies the widespread thirty-years-before shorthand. The separately released _Ever Crisis_ story also called _The First Soldier_ is not this closed battle royale service.

### A persistent creature raised for temporary matches

The launch **Chocobo Farm** incubated eggs and held up to seven chocobos. Feeding improved attributes including speed and HP; separate skins changed appearance. This made a persistent creature relevant to later combat without preserving the whole previous match's loadout. [Ateam feature reveal](https://www.ateam-entertainment.com/en/news/772/).

Justin Joy's release guide describes eggs found in special chests or bought from the **Moogle Emporium**, incubation, differently colored hatchlings, feeding vegetables and selecting which bird would be called in a match. Breeding was another farm action. Its golden-chocobo suggestion was speculation, so it is not evidence that the original FFVII's complete breeding quest existed here. [Pro Game Guides, November 18, 2021](https://progameguides.com/final-fantasy/how-to-use-the-chocobo-farm-in-final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier/).

**Constructed creature workflow:** obtain an egg and retain it for incubation, hatch it at the farm, obtain feed from match chests or the shop, improve the desired attributes and designate a mount for later play. The activity connects collection outside direct fighting to traversal in the next match. It is constrained selection and development inside an authored creature system, rather than importing a new species or writing its behavior. [Jamie Moorcroft-Sharp's launch farm guide](https://www.gamepur.com/guides/what-is-the-chocobo-farm-in-final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier).

The **Egg Hunter** event gave the creature another purpose. Between March 31 and April 26, 2022, riders searched the training field for event eggs, then returned them to the farm. Seasonal enemies, including a bunny-eared Cactuar, supplied special materia for challenges. This connected a familiar creature and seasonal visual theme to actual collection and combat tasks. ### Cosmetic expression and organized private matches

The **Collection** feature exchanged data from monsters and special boxes for themed cosmetics. Gil-bought weapon appearances included a **Nail Bat** sword skin and **Umbrella** staff skin. ### Blitz Mode: a shorter competitive loop

**Blitz Mode: Shinra Building** launched May 25, 2022 as a limited event. Two teams of three alternated attacking and defending; the first to win two rounds won the match. Attackers needed both capture points, and a defender inside a point blocked its capture. Defenders won by retaining at least one. Defeated candidates returned after a timer, while healing zones restored HP and MP. A Style determined starting equipment, and gil awarded after rounds funded additional purchases. Challenges supplied season-level stars. These rules supported repeated close encounters and equipment decisions within one match, with a different failure cost from battle royale elimination. **Constructed situation:** an attacker delays a capture until a teammate removes its defender, then retreats to recover before contesting the second point. Respawning permits another attempt, but surrendering time can still lose the round. [Square Enix's complete May 25 rules announcement](https://na.finalfantasy.com/news/2006).

Blitz returned July 7 with ranking and accomplishment rewards, available through July 19. Associated login bonuses and challenges offered up to forty Premium Shinra Pack Tickets. The same update introduced **V. Holo emotes** using Remake character voice clips, including Cloud, Tifa and Aerith. These allowed players to perform an authored voice-and-gesture identity; they did not supply editable dialogue or new character behavior. [Ateam's July 7, 2022 update; full written body read](https://www.ateam-entertainment.com/en/news/1105/).

All amounts below describe historical offers in the closed service. They are not purchasable today, and regional storefront taxes and currency conversion should not be inferred from one launch report.

**Direct cosmetics and the season pass.** Siliconera's November 17, 2021 store account describes a 1,000-Shinra-Credit Season 1 pass, a 3,600-credit Cloud bundle and a 2,800-credit Tifa bundle. The bundles combined avatar presentation with related weapon or vehicle skins and other accessories; the pass required progression for later rewards, including its level-100 Rufus outfit. The report lists 1,300 credits at $9.99 and 2,800 at $19.99 in its observed dollar storefront. These are dated examples, not universal regional prices. ### Commercial explanation, alternatives and transferable questions

**Interpretation, not a measured causal decomposition:** the product tried to join recognizable Final Fantasy presentation with a competitive action loop, then monetize identity and continued participation. That combination needs several separate things to work: players must enjoy the actual matches, feel attached to their persistent identity, encounter attractive offers and keep returning long enough for the content operation to be paid for. Familiar characters can make an offer intelligible; they cannot by themselves demonstrate willingness to play this genre every week.

Three hypotheses merit testing: **technical friction weakened repeat play; platform selection limited the reachable audience; and the offers failed to support sufficient spending per active player**. Tests would require crash-linked retention, regional/device conversion and offer-level lifetime-value data respectively. Management's retrospective suggests those questions; it does not measure each factor's contribution or establish that a PC release alone would have solved the business. [C06](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/20221215-17265/)

The counterpoint is useful: free tickets and an earnable next pass can improve perceived value, while simultaneously reducing repeat cash purchases from some committed players. Removing those benefits would not automatically improve the business; it could also weaken retention or trust. The defensible lesson for Open Legend is to test the relationship between the recurring play experience, the desired identity or collection and the operating cost of supplying it. A large franchise and a working shop are inputs to that test, not substitutes for its results.

### Research questions for OpenLegend

These are hypotheses to investigate, not instructions to copy systems:

- **Can players execute the interesting combinations they understand?** R1 suggests testing whether expressive combat survives the available camera and input method.

- **What does a failure teach when movement is unreliable?** P1 distinguishes losing a difficult fight from losing confidence that an input will work.
- **Who is available to share the activity?** P6 suggests examining friends' access and the range of shared activities alongside individual enthusiasm.
