It combines documented mechanics, concrete constructed situations, original commercial evidence, production context and independent reception. It grants no OpenLegend implementation or monetization decision. Detailed access and version limitations remain beside the claims.

## Game, mechanics and player-created content

### 1. Core loop: an RPG race inside a battle royale

The First Soldier combined a seventy-five-player battle royale with melee weapons, firearms and spellcasting. Players landed with their Style’s melee weapon, searched for equipment and fought for survival as the safe area contracted. Monsters offered another way to develop strength before confronting other candidates. The resulting choice was not simply where to find a gun: engaging a monster could produce useful growth while exposing the player to an opportunistic human attacker. [Omer Khan, Gamition, January 28, 2022; full guide body read; unsupported popularity claims excluded](https://gamition.com/final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier-beginners-guide/)

The launch had five Styles: **Warrior, Sorcerer, Monk, Ranger and Ninja**, specializing respectively in melee pressure, area magic, durability and recovery, scouting and ranged combat, and stealth/mobility. The publisher explicitly connected defeating monsters to experience and leveling. Later seasons expanded that choice to eight Styles. [Square Enix launch announcement, November 19, 2021; full primary body read](https://na.finalfantasy.com/news/1861)

This combines two different kinds of preparation. A player chooses a persistent character specialization before the match, then builds the actual fighting resources under the match’s time and survival constraints. A progression advantage is therefore conditional on remaining alive long enough to use it. Exact mastery unlocks and loss boundaries are being verified separately from this opening account.

### 2. Input and carried tools

The documented mobile shooting options were **Auto Fire**, a **Fixed Fire** button and a **Floating Fire** button. Their purpose was to separate aiming from the choice of how a touch press triggered a shot. The inventory supported two firearms alongside the Style-specific melee weapon, plus three Materia slots. A player therefore could not retain every useful weapon or spell found. The tutorial/training area provided a place to practise attacks and looting. [Mohit Sahi, Mobile Gaming Hub, December 18, 2021; full body read; its unreliable Style summaries and broad marketing judgments are excluded](https://mobilegaminghub.com/ffvii-the-first-soldier-beginners-guide-tips-and-tricks/)

Selecting an automatically triggered shot did not decide when to expose the character, whether to pursue a melee target or which spell deserved a limited slot. Those remained strategic choices. The complete equipment section will distinguish ammunition, MP, consumable healing, defensive equipment and earned gil rather than treating all of them as one power meter.

### 3. Styles: persistent specialization, selective loadout

A candidate selected a Style for the next match. Each had a trait, a choice of active abilities and a choice of skills; the February 2022 guide specifies **one ability and one skill equipped simultaneously**. These are alternative builds, not a character carrying every listed effect at once. The table preserves that guide’s date because later patches replaced some skills. [Jason Fanelli, GameSpot, February 28, 2022; complete six-Style body read](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/final-fantasy-7-the-first-soldier-style-guide-choosing-what-style-fits-best/1100-6501091/)

Only the Ranger/Ninja sections of [Ruel Butler’s November 23, 2021 QooApp guide](https://news.qoo-app.com/en/post/76855/ffvii-the-first-soldier-guide-how-to-choose-fighting-style) are used here: its other Style sections contain older skill names conflicting with the later GameSpot snapshot. A scout’s information can support a teammate’s attack; it does not directly replace ammunition, cover or aim.

**Dragoon**, released with Season 2, used a lance and **Sky Predator** for gliding and downward strikes. Ability choices were **High Jump** or **Dragon’s Favor**, the latter combining speed with melee MP absorption, bleed and kill recovery. Skills were **Aerial Wall** against fire from below, **Dragon’s Ire** to mark attackers and drain their MP, and **Wide Swing** for wider melee combos. [GameSpot’s separate Season 2 guide, February 28, 2022; full body read](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/final-fantasy-7-the-first-soldier-season-2-guide-bahamut-summon-dragoon-style-and-more-big-changes/1100-6501094/)

**Machinist**, introduced June 2, used a hammer. **Support Gear** generated protective sub-gadgets after the main gadget broke. **Air Gadget** offered riding, missiles and an abandoned vehicle’s explosive collision; **Jamming Gadget** created an area that disabled firearms and inflicted continuing damage, including interference with its owner. Selectable **Shot Gear** followed up gun hits, **Heal Gear** sacrificed itself at low health, and **Blast Gear** exploded against a nearby attacker after damage. [Kamigame’s dedicated Machinist guide, including July 19 adjustment history; full Japanese body read and translated; later maintenance stopped](https://kamigame.jp/FF7FS/page/212819964648800524.html)

**Trickster**, added in Season 4, fought with cards. **Gold Trick** awarded gil on leveling and scaled melee critical chance/ability recovery with total gil collected. **Decoy Card** created a fighting double; **Marking Card** stored half the user’s HP and returned them to that location with the stored health when reduced to zero while active. Skills were **Seize** for monster-kill gil, **Drain Shot** for headshot healing and **Soul Drain Deck** for MP after using an ability. [Veerender Singh Jubbal, GameSpot, September 2, 2022; substantive article text read through the search index, direct article fetch failed](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/how-to-play-the-new-trickster-style-in-final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier/1100-6507105/)

Style Mastery grew through playing that Style, kills, challenges and wins. The launch guide documented sixty mastery levels and rewards across matches. This was a persistent reason to specialize even though a match’s weapons and survival position had to be rebuilt. It did not lock the player permanently into their initial Style. [Justin Joy, Pro Game Guides, November 17, 2021; full body read; its inaccurate story chronology is excluded](https://progameguides.com/final-fantasy/how-to-improve-your-style-mastery-in-final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier/)

### 4. Verified later-season mechanics

#### Dominate the Sky — Season 2

**Dragoon** introduced lance attacks and aerial movement: **High Jump** launched the character upward, **Sky Predator** enabled gliding, and **Dragon’s Favor** added MP absorption to melee and recovery after defeating an enemy. **Teleport Materia** linked a targeted location to a portal at the caster’s feet, and other players could use the portals. Defeating the roaming **Bahamut** supplied summon Materia that transformed the successful candidate into Bahamut, rather than merely adding an ordinary gun. [Square Enix, March 1, 2022; full primary Season 2 body read](https://na.finalfantasy.com/news/1934)

#### Highway Star — Season 3

Season 3 added **Midgar Plateside**, with locations such as Sector 8, the highway and Mako Reactor Core 1. **Machinist** used an Air Gadget for hovering. **Water Materia** provided homing attacks, while the **Serpent Launcher** added a long-range explosive weapon. These were actual gameplay additions in the publisher’s released-season report, not mechanics inferred from the locations’ appearance in Remake. [Square Enix, June 16, 2022; full primary body read](https://www.square-enix-games.com/en_US/home/season-3-highway-star-final-fantasy-vii-first-soldier)

### Shinra's selection program and the timeline

The player is a candidate seeking entry to **Shinra's SOLDIER unit**, fighting in Midgar with guns, melee weapons and magic. That institutional purpose makes competitive survival a selection exercise within the fiction. [Ateam's November 11, 2021 feature reveal](https://www.ateam-entertainment.com/en/news/772/).

The precise timeline needs care. Producer **Shoichi Ichikawa** explained in March 2022 that **Project 0** began about thirty years before Final Fantasy VII, while this game occurs about ten years after that—approximately **twenty years before the original game**. [Direct producer interview, Unreal Engine](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier-blends-rpg-sensibilities-with-battle-royale-action?lang=en-US). This distinction qualifies the widespread thirty-years-before shorthand. The separately released _Ever Crisis_ story also called _The First Soldier_ is not this closed battle royale service.

### A persistent creature raised for temporary matches

The launch **Chocobo Farm** incubated eggs and held up to seven chocobos. Feeding improved attributes including speed and HP; separate skins changed appearance. This made a persistent creature relevant to later combat without preserving the whole previous match's loadout. [Ateam feature reveal](https://www.ateam-entertainment.com/en/news/772/).

Justin Joy's release guide describes eggs found in special chests or bought from the **Moogle Emporium**, incubation, differently colored hatchlings, feeding vegetables and selecting which bird would be called in a match. Breeding was another farm action. Its golden-chocobo suggestion was speculation, so it is not evidence that the original FFVII's complete breeding quest existed here. [Pro Game Guides, November 18, 2021](https://progameguides.com/final-fantasy/how-to-use-the-chocobo-farm-in-final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier/).

**Constructed creature workflow:** obtain an egg and retain it for incubation, hatch it at the farm, obtain feed from match chests or the shop, improve the desired attributes and designate a mount for later play. The activity connects collection outside direct fighting to traversal in the next match. It is constrained selection and development inside an authored creature system, rather than importing a new species or writing its behavior. [Jamie Moorcroft-Sharp's launch farm guide](https://www.gamepur.com/guides/what-is-the-chocobo-farm-in-final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier).

The **Egg Hunter** event gave the creature another purpose. Between March 31 and April 26, 2022, riders searched the training field for event eggs, then returned them to the farm. Seasonal enemies, including a bunny-eared Cactuar, supplied special materia for challenges. This connected a familiar creature and seasonal visual theme to actual collection and combat tasks. [Square Enix, April 4, 2022](https://www.square-enix-games.com/en_GB/home/new-egg-hunter-event-under-way-final-fantasy-vii-first-soldier).

### Cosmetic expression and organized private matches

The **Collection** feature exchanged data from monsters and special boxes for themed cosmetics. Gil-bought weapon appearances included a **Nail Bat** sword skin and **Umbrella** staff skin. Those labels describe appearances, not newly programmed weapon classes. [Ateam feature reveal](https://www.ateam-entertainment.com/en/news/772/). Avatar editing and the final breadth of private-room controls remain under investigation in this checkpoint.

Actual private-room use is documented by Square Enix's **August 2022 North America championship rules**. Teams received a room name and password; organizers coordinated through Discord. The specified matches used Midgar Slums, omitted bots if the lobby was short, and disabled chocobos and their stops. Thus, at least organized events could assemble participants and impose a different competitive configuration. These rules establish neither universal player hosting nor an arbitrary map editor. They describe this tournament, not every normal queue. [Official championship rules](https://www.square-enix-games.com/en_US/documents/final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier-championship-north-america).

### Commercial outcome and metric boundary

### Announcement, purchase cutoff, closure and refunds

Square Enix announced the ending on **October 12, 2022**, saying it had not delivered the hoped-for player experience despite ongoing content updates. Its preserved statement specifies **January 11, 2023, 07:00 UTC** for shutdown. Shinra Credit purchases were removed on the announcement day; existing balances could still be spent until closure. The publisher's UTC wording is retained because Gematsu's introductory US-time conversion does not agree with it. The original game-site notice failed retrieval; its full statement is preserved in the linked report. [C02](https://www.gematsu.com/2022/10/final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier-to-end-service-on-january-11-2023)

The January 12 Pocket Gamer report reproduces the official account's **January 11 confirmation that service had ended**. Its unsupported characterization of the size of the audience is not used as financial evidence. [C05](https://www.pocketgamer.com/final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier/shut-down/)

Refunds require a territorial and product distinction. Square Enix's Japanese statutory notice provided a procedure for **unused paid Shinra Tokens**, with requests from **January 12 through September 15, 2023**. It explicitly excludes free-issued currency. This establishes that a blanket “no refunds anywhere” claim would be wrong; it does not promise repayment of previously consumed purchases or prove identical terms for every international store. The historical claim window is closed. [C03](https://www.jp.square-enix.com/company/ja/electronic/download/shikinnkessai_koukoku_20230112.pdf)

### Production and distribution

Ateam's **February 26, 2021** announcement identifies co-development with Square Enix, Tetsuya Nomura's creative direction and an official Compilation of Final Fantasy VII entry. It lists iOS/Android, free entry with purchases, **worldwide distribution excluding mainland China**, and seven interface languages. These define the announced product and release territory; sales of the wider Final Fantasy franchise cannot stand in for this game's receipts. [C04](https://www.a-tm.co.jp/en/news/service-20409/)

The commercial release was **November 17, 2021**, through the App Store and Google Play. [C02](https://www.gematsu.com/2022/10/final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier-to-end-service-on-january-11-2023)

Management's retrospective is more specific than the short closure statement. AUTOMATON reports Ateam's December 2022 investor Q&A identifying lack of PC availability, high launch crash rates and avatar-focused monetization that management considered insufficient for the intended audience. Those are management's explanations, not independent proof that any single factor caused the outcome. The original linked PDF is located, but currently exposes no extracted text; the reporting's translation is not presented as our direct reading of that PDF. [C06](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/20221215-17265/)

Producer Shoichi Ichikawa's March 2022 interview describes concrete mobile-production work: Unreal's texture streaming managed memory pressure, Mobile Preview approximated phone presentation inside the editor, and scalability settings helped adjust lighting. The team reduced effects cost through level-of-detail settings and mesh-based particles. Final Fantasy VII Remake assets supplied visual references and a shared quality target; that does not mean the entire game was simply copied. Level production moved from mock layouts to assets and detailing. These are documented development methods, not a disclosed budget, staffing count or measurement of how much each optimization saved. [C08](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier-blends-rpg-sensibilities-with-battle-royale-action?lang=en-US)

The October 3, 2021 publisher update connected June beta feedback to controller support, a practice mode and tutorial changes, and promoted the game through a Tokyo Game Show livestream. Its greater-than-90% satisfaction claim concerns respondents to the English-version beta survey, not a representative sample of all prospective players or evidence of eventual commercial retention. [C09](https://www.ateam-entertainment.com/en/news/700/)

### What players could buy, and why the products mattered

All amounts below describe historical offers in the closed service. They are not purchasable today, and regional storefront taxes and currency conversion should not be inferred from one launch report.

**Direct cosmetics and the season pass.** Siliconera's November 17, 2021 store account describes a 1,000-Shinra-Credit Season 1 pass, a 3,600-credit Cloud bundle and a 2,800-credit Tifa bundle. The bundles combined avatar presentation with related weapon or vehicle skins and other accessories; the pass required progression for later rewards, including its level-100 Rufus outfit. The report lists 1,300 credits at $9.99 and 2,800 at $19.99 in its observed dollar storefront. These are dated examples, not universal regional prices. A fan could buy a specific recognizable identity immediately or pay for a reward track that still required play. [C10](https://www.siliconera.com/first-ffvii-the-first-soldier-costumes-include-cloud-tifa-rufus-and-hojo/)

**Time-limited packs and earned access.** The March 17, 2022 Crisis Core campaign offered Premium Shinra Packs at **300 credits or five event tickets**. Login rewards and challenges supplied up to 30 tickets, alongside some directly earned accessories. Zack and Tifa appearances were the merchandise hook; the promotion ended later that month. Thus free participation could introduce the same pack system sold for premium currency. Thirty tickets covered six five-ticket packs, a calculation rather than a claim of six guaranteed featured costumes. The notice supplies neither complete item odds nor a universal pity rule, so expected acquisition cost is not reconstructed. [C11](https://www.ateam-entertainment.com/en/news/972/)

**Purchase incentives and regional reach.** The two-million-download celebration offered extra credits on the first purchase of each price tier during its stated window; it was not an unlimited bonus on every purchase. The same December 28 update added Thai and Latin American Spanish. These show active efforts to convert an existing audience and reduce language barriers. The evidence does not isolate how much spending either intervention produced. [C07](https://www.ateam-entertainment.com/en/news/872/)

The commercial distinction matters: acquiring a Cloud appearance is different from buying Cloud as a separate combat class. Avatar identity, collection and seasonal completion can support repeat spending without a new paid character kit. But the dossier should not turn that observation into a blanket claim that every purchase had no progression consequence. Exact class progression and native Chocobo Farm functions belong to the mechanics and creation sections; additional purchase effects require their own evidence.

## Reception, enjoyment and criticism

### Substantive written reviews

#### R1. Phil Hornshaw — GameSpot, February 16, 2022

Hornshaw played approximately eight hours on a Google Pixel 3a XL Android phone, using touch and a Bluetooth controller. He liked meaningful gun choices and combining magic with shooting: area spells could restrict escape or dislodge someone from cover. His central objection was execution. Fingers obscured action, remembering crowded commands was difficult, and turning the camera competed with attacking; close melee could carry an enemy out of view. A controller made the same combat substantially more enjoyable. He intended to keep playing, while doubting how easily he could improve on a phone.

[Full review and testing disclosure read](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/final-fantasy-7-the-first-soldier-review-straining-at-the-seams/1900-6417831/).

#### R2. Jack Brassell — Hardcore Droid, December 8, 2021

Brassell found the RPG additions appealing: familiar monsters made Midgar recognizable, while attacking monsters to strengthen the character also risked simultaneous attack by another player. Chocobo hatching, care and breeding excited the reviewer even more than the already enjoyable fighting. That is a concrete attachment loop beyond winning. Touch controls were difficult, but pairing a PS4 controller made opponents easier to defeat; the review still considered touch play viable. The favorable verdict therefore coexists with an important input qualification. The page concerns Android, but gives neither the phone model nor play hours. Its loose developer attribution and individual spell terminology are not used to establish mechanics.

[Full review body read](https://www.hardcoredroid.com/final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier-review/).

#### R3. Shaun Walton — Pocket Gamer Select, August 31, 2022

Walton described being immediately engaged by class choice, magic and in-match growth, rather than simply accepting a familiar license. His discussion includes the later Machinist, making it broader than a launch-only assessment. Automatic firing relieved the difficulty of moving, aiming and shooting simultaneously; he recommended manual firing for sniper use. Fighting roaming creatures and bosses supplied another route to improved combat capability. The review offers little sustained criticism beyond that input caveat, so it is useful as a clear positive viewpoint rather than evidence that later matchmaking or progression was trouble-free. Android and iOS are listed; device, exact build and playtime are absent. A shutdown notice was subsequently added to the page.

[Full written review read](https://ee.pocketgamer.com/articles/084153/final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier/).

#### R4. Alan Pomian — Cultura Geek, November 26, 2021

Pomian tested on a Moto G100 and found touch movement, attacks and automatic shooting manageable. He liked having magical and class-based alternatives in a firefight, with familiar locations and creatures supporting recognition. The written observations describe a landscape of buildings, desert terrain and drains, with lighting and textures especially noticeable on characters and monsters. Seasonal objectives and class mastery offered reasons to continue; the conclusion remained conditional on continued innovation. This is a launch-era Spanish-language review, summarized in English. The page is explicitly in a Motorola-branded mobile review section, which matters when weighing its especially positive hardware discussion. No numerical performance benchmark or playtime is disclosed.

[Full Spanish review body read](https://culturageek.com.ar/review-final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier/).

#### R5. Nicolò Bicego — SpazioGames, November 24, 2021

Bicego tested on a Galaxy S20 FE and found the game visually attractive, with occasional slowdown and acceptable audio, but considered its art direction bland outside recognizable locations. More seriously, awkward melee, casting and vehicle control prevented the class differences from becoming satisfying play. The reviewer wanted a stronger narrative identity and saw little reason to choose this over established alternatives. This is a strongly negative assessment of execution and franchise fit, not evidence that nobody wanted this genre combination. No playtime is disclosed. The Italian body was read and summarized in English; its four-class inventory and incorrect release-date metadata are excluded as mechanics evidence.

[Full Italian review body read](https://www.spaziogames.it/recensioni/final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier-recensione).

#### R6. Nicolas Dixmier, “TheXsable” — Jeuxvideo.com, November 25, 2021

Dixmier found an effective battle royale with extensive control options, recognizable combat animations and locations, and useful RPG additions. Simple buildings and textures accompanied smooth performance during his test. Native controller support made play more comfortable, but spell-button combinations remained awkward, and both an Xbox controller and DualShock 4 sometimes stopped behaving as expected. He could not determine whether the device caused those incidents. The conclusion was favorable while questioning which audience the combination would reach. The article covers iOS/Android, but does not identify its test device, hours or exact build.

[Full French review body read](https://www.jeuxvideo.com/test/1491214/final-fantasy-vii-the-first-soldier-que-vaut-le-battle-royale-dans-l-univers-de-ff7.htm).

### Original players: pleasure, persistence and leaving

These are selected firsthand accounts, not a representative sample or a measured explanation of shutdown. Reddit pages displayed **Best** ordering; hidden replies were not treated as read, and ranking was not interpreted as a survey. TapTap's listing displayed **Most Helpful**, but its retrieved sequence was largely chronological and included a misplaced review of a different Final Fantasy game. That entry and the aggregate rating are excluded. The selected original TapTap posts were each opened and read separately. Device and build are generally undisclosed.

#### P1. Enjoyment survives defects — Xerver, December 31, 2021

Xerver liked the challenge, detailed map and mixture of RPG and battle royale, while reporting unreliable climbing, difficulty clearing obstacles and crashes. The account matters because its complaint is about interrupted execution within an otherwise attractive game. The assertion that acquaintances quit is secondhand and supplies no retention count. The post does not establish that the author abandoned play. Its launch-period date makes it unsuitable as a blanket verdict on the following year's revisions.

[Complete original TapTap post](https://www.taptap.io/post/1097722).

#### P2. Anticipation becomes immediate abandonment — Alizesta, October 6, 2023

Looking back after closure, Alizesta described preregistering and downloading in advance, then leaving after only one or two rounds. Movement and fighting felt difficult; aiming options and perceived low gun damage disappointed them. This describes a conversion failure despite considerable initial interest. The device and actual play date are absent, and the remembered lack of gyroscope support is not used to establish a universal feature inventory. The dramatic complaint about wanting a refund is not evidence of an actual purchase or refund dispute.

[Complete original TapTap post](https://www.taptap.io/post/6395342).

#### P3. The hybrid itself is the attraction — Jae, September 6, 2022

Jae specifically enjoyed mixing magic and technology while anticipating which monsters an open area might contain. For this self-described franchise fan, the battle royale offered an appealing new way to inhabit Final Fantasy. The account supplies a positive motive tied to interaction and discovery, rather than merely a favorable score. It carries the hashtag **#TapTapExplorerS2**; any compensation is unverified. Its praise does not establish session frequency, balance or how many other fans agreed.

Blink reported enjoying play while finding aiming and shooting clumsy and the relationship between the minimap and player view unreliable. That final statement is a player's impression, not a verified bot proportion or matchmaking rule. The comments show that liking the concept and declining to keep engaging can coexist. ### Research questions for OpenLegend

These are hypotheses to investigate, not instructions to copy systems:

- **Can players execute the interesting combinations they understand?** R1 suggests testing whether expressive combat survives the available camera and input method.
- **Does optional care provide a valued reason to return?** R2's enthusiasm for chocobos suggests measuring attachment separately from competitive rank or victory.
- **What does a failure teach when movement is unreliable?** P1 distinguishes losing a difficult fight from losing confidence that an input will work.
- **Who is available to share the activity?** P6 suggests examining friends' access and the range of shared activities alongside individual enthusiasm.
- **Does early interest survive the first match?** P2 makes preregistration a poor substitute for observing the first few attempts.

These cases do not demonstrate that pressure to spend created retention, that rewards caused compulsion, or that changing platforms alone would have prevented closure. The commercial and mechanics sections should establish the actual offers and rule changes; reception establishes particular people's experiences.
