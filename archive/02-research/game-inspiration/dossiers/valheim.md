## Confirmed evidence

**Version 1.0 shipped September 9, 2026, including Deep North.** The resume ledger's provisional instruction to check future 1.0 plans must be corrected: it is not the current release state. The primary announcement lists new northern content, expanded equipment, building pieces, crafting stations, food, inventory pockets and achievements. Exact quantities are announcement claims, not this researcher's item census. [V1]

Five independently credited substantive written review bodies have been read through their conclusions: Christopher Livingston, PC Gamer (September 8, 2026); Oda Rygh, Gamer.no (September 8); Lyle Pendle, Loot Level Chill (September 8); Ken Talbot, Nintendo Life (September 18, Switch 2); and Raiden_Robin, Jeuxvideo.com (September 20). Norwegian and French articles are paraphrased in English. Talbot's platform-specific performance/network problems do not certify current PC behavior; his wording about world mods refers to settings, not unrestricted console software modding. [R1–R5]

The reviews agree on meaningful exploration and atmosphere more than on acceptable friction. Livingston values a difficult metal-hauling expedition while disliking repetitive mining; Rygh values a partner who does not spoil discovery; Pendle finds the first shelter's smoke a memorable lesson; Talbot reports port instability; Raiden_Robin objects to inventory/crafting inconvenience and weak pathfinding. Incidental claims that there are no portals or no construction snapping are not adopted as mechanics facts.
