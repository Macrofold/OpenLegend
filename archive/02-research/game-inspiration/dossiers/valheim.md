## Confirmed release and compatibility evidence

**Version 1.0 shipped September 9, 2026, including Deep North.** The resume ledger's provisional instruction to check future 1.0 plans must be corrected: it is not the current release state. The primary announcement lists new northern content, expanded equipment, building pieces, crafting stations, food, inventory pockets and achievements. Exact quantities are announcement claims, not this researcher's item census. This announcement was reopened in the continuation. [V1]

The dedicated 1.0 FAQ says old saves remain usable, while new biome generation needs unexplored areas. Its recommendation to restart is not a mandatory wipe. Mods are unofficial, compatibility can break, and the developer does not promise console mods or Steam Workshop. The FAQ promises continuing fixes/quality-of-life work without a definite support duration or guaranteed additional content. Its achievement/cheat description is a July prelaunch account and should not override later patch-specific rules. [V2]

The general FAQ is a mixed-age document: its reference to three boats and recently released Mistlands should not be used as an exhaustive 1.0 inventory. It remains useful for the stated visual approach, company origins, small-group cooperative intent and food that expands health/stamina rather than directly preventing starvation. [V3]

## Five written reviews

Five independently credited substantive written review bodies were read in the previous checkpoint and reopened here: Christopher Livingston, PC Gamer (September 8, 2026); Oda Rygh, Gamer.no (September 8); Lyle Pendle, Loot Level Chill (September 8); Ken Talbot, Nintendo Life (September 18, Switch 2); and Raiden_Robin, Jeuxvideo.com (September 20). Norwegian and French articles are paraphrased in English. [R1–R5]

- Livingston distinguishes a satisfying cooperative silver-hauling expedition from repetitive mining and chest handling. His Odin sighting is a personal interpretation of atmosphere, not evidence of a bespoke victory-response system. [R1]
- Rygh values slow discovery with a partner who agrees not to carry or spoil the novice's progression. Storage organization, awkward snapping and joining-server discoverability interrupt that pleasure. [R2]
- Pendle describes learning from falling timber and smoke in an over-sealed first home. He praises upgrades and building freedom but finds solo material collection slow. His claim that building lacks snapping is not adopted as a rules description. [R3]
- Talbot praises biome identity, atmospheric sound and adjustable world difficulty. His Switch 2 version experienced crossplay-version mismatch, disconnects and frame drops. These are platform/build observations, not current universal PC measurements; his phrase world mods refers to settings, not console code mods. [R4]
- Raiden_Robin values lighting, sailing and the initial progression/building loop but objects to late-biome friction, inventory/crafting usability and exploitable or awkward enemy navigation. The article's incidental no-fast-travel wording is not adopted: portals exist. [R5]

## Steam helpful-review retrieval now succeeds

The first attempts at parameter-heavy Steam community URLs and the appreviews JSON endpoint failed; a direct runtime request also failed DNS. The simpler community URLs below returned actual review bodies. Both surfaces were opened with `browsefilter=toprated`; this is a qualitative helpful-review sample, not random sampling, telemetry or a count of how common a complaint is. Review-page headings/dates must be preserved with the final sample. [S1–S2]

The accessible positive surface includes a detailed recommended account by Axiod appreciating construction, atmospheric sailing, environmental incidents and the return of discovery associated with earlier sandbox games. Another recommended review criticizes equipment occupying inventory, vertical combat and maintenance priorities despite considerable affection for the game. The negative surface includes addy's complaint about slow perceived development and usability, while explicitly praising the Meadows' atmosphere and exploration. These are not a binary split between people who see merit and people who do not. [S1–S2]

A historical negative review performs speculative arithmetic from sales to taxes, publisher share, staffing and profit. Do not reuse those invented business inputs as commercial evidence. Likewise, current displayed playtime is not playtime at publication. Earlier Early Access complaints remain dated testimony, not proof that every reported defect persists in 1.0. [S2]
