## Current retail is not the 2004 game with a larger number

Blizzard's Midnight launch notice establishes that the expansion is live, not a future release. Its new Devourer specialization changes the previously two-specialization Demon Hunter class, while housing creates another major play surface outside combat. A guide that simply repeats the old class count or describes player housing as perpetually promised would already be stale. [S01](#s01)

**Midnight Season 2 is live**: Blizzard's dated August schedule identifies the Venomous Abyss raid, a new dungeon rotation, Delves, Prey, PvP and the Tidebound Grotto Lair. It also distinguishes staged unlocks and changing Mythic 0 reset cadence. These are temporal service rules, not permanent universal properties of every dungeon in every WoW version. [S02](#s02)

The September 4 weekly update calls **12.1.5 a PTR update**, with a forthcoming single-boss raid, Labyrinths and outdoor event. This checkpoint does not relabel those previewed activities as shipped. Later September announcements and Classic/new-service boundaries still require the full completion pass. [S03](#s03)

## The player, the character and the account are different progression owners

Warbands expanded account-region sharing through Renown/reputation integration, a shared bank, achievements and collection handling. Crucially, **Warbound until Equipped** does not mean permanently tradable gear: it can move between eligible characters until equipping binds it to one. Collecting a plate appearance on another class does not let a Priest wear plate. Shared convenience is not unrestricted capability transfer. [S04](#s04)

**Interpretation:** these boundaries change the opportunity cost of playing another character. A player can make useful collection or reputation progress without feeling every session on an alternate character abandons the account's main goals. At the same time, leaving some gear progression character-specific preserves a reason to play that class. The full dossier must distinguish these deliberate ownership boundaries from a vague claim that everything is shared.

## A home connects old activities to new purposes

The February 2026 housing explanation describes homes and decor shared across a Warband, without a housing lottery or onerous upkeep. Neighborhoods supply a social scale between a solitary room and the entire server. Endeavors combine residents' tasks and change the neighborhood's visible presentation while unlocking themed decoration access; the required participation scales with neighborhood size. This is a structured collective activity, not an unrestricted political simulation. [S05](#s05)

August's Blueprints let a player save and share a room, interior, exterior or whole-home layout. Importing presents resource and budget requirements, owned versus missing decor and other information before applying a design. A shared layout is therefore a plan whose realization depends on the recipient's actual collection, not a magical transfer of every object in the author's home. [S06](#s06)

**Interpretation:** the same dungeon reward can now be interesting for power, appearance or display. A saved layout makes a creative act portable and revisable; a neighborhood gives it an audience. These are different retention mechanisms from continually increasing item level, and their long-term value should not be inferred solely from whether a player raids.

## Encounters can come to the traveler

Blizzard's original Prey explanation describes an opt-in hunt acquired from Astalor in Silvermoon. The hunter can continue other activity while the target may ambush them. It distinguishes Normal assistance from the more constrained and modified higher-difficulty encounters. The later Season 2 schedule confirms continuing Prey modes, but the original preview is not used as a current numeric reward table. [S07](#s07) [S02](#s02)

The updated February Delves article replaces The War Within's Brann companion with Valeera for Midnight and identifies staged availability for specific Delves. It describes outdoor instances and ground-mount use as well as new layouts. Companion progression, solo accessibility and activity rewards require further detailed treatment; the presence of an NPC ally is not evidence that a player has no meaningful decisions left. [S08](#s08)
