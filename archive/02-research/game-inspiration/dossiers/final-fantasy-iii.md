## Original reviews read

Five independent publications' complete review bodies have been read, not merely aggregator excerpts. Their different versions are preserved rather than averaged into a fictitious consensus.

- **J1, RPGFan — Zach Wilkerson, July 30, 2021, PC Pixel Remaster:** values experimentation and autosaving but finds repeated job-specific obstacles overused.
- **J2, Siliconera — Jenni Lada, July 28, 2021, PC Pixel Remaster:** values historical access and presentation while criticizing wasted interface space and inconsistent typography.
- **J3, WorthPlaying — Chris DeAngelus, August 4, 2021, PC Pixel Remaster:** considers free switching and revised pacing major improvements; some earlier jobs still become obsolete.
- **J4, GameSpot — Greg Kasavin, November 29, 2006, DS:** enjoys party variety but finds save restrictions punishing and the presentation slower than 2D entries.
- **J5, RPGamer — Bryan Boullette, November 14, 2006, DS:** appreciates the challenge and optional touch controls, with the final dungeon a notable frustration. The page's 2021 update is not a remaster reassessment.

The fifth review's enthusiastic assessment of combat speed contrasts with GameSpot's pacing complaint. Their conclusions are experiences of the same remake, not proof of a universal performance measurement. The DS's newly named protagonists and job-adjustment penalty must not be copied into the 2D Pixel Remaster description.

## Primary production evidence

The official 2025 Tanaka/Asano interview identifies Matrix Software's remake work and describes a ten-point direction document. It also explicitly says a favorability-based dialogue system was discarded. A proposed social mechanic is not a shipped feature. [J6]

## Direct Steam evidence

The English Most Helpful (All Time) surface for app 1173790 is readable. Strawblaze's July 28, 2021 recommendation values the first worldwide 2D version but finds HP-related job planning tiresome. bloopa64's January 28, 2023 negative review objects to critical-hit flashing. These are selected dated experiences, not population-wide sentiment or proof that later accessibility options did not change. [J7]
