## Short comparison routes

**Who defines a party member?** Compare [FFI](final-fantasy.md), [FFII](final-fantasy-ii.md), [FFIII](final-fantasy-iii.md) and [Expedition 33](clair-obscur-expedition-33.md): initial class commitment, practiced behavior, switchable job packages and individually distinct resource models. These are different forms of authorship, not successive steps toward one universally superior system.

**What makes an object more than a larger number?** Follow FFI's Healing Staff and access objects, FFII's spell tomes and Blood Sword, FFIII's item-enhancing Scholar and consumed Shurikens, and Expedition 33's distinction between equipped Pictos and learned Lumina. Each dossier supplies the edition and failure limits for its example.

**What changes when a familiar place is revisited?** Compare FFI's key/vehicle chains, FFII's Sunfire/tool dependency, FFIII's Nautilus and Invincible, and Expedition 33's relationship-linked diving. Notice whether the change is knowledge, possession, relationship progress or movement capability rather than calling them all an unlock.

**Does a convenience preserve the interesting decision?** Read the reviews and version histories beside the mechanics: saving, encounter controls, growth boosts, equipment information and timed-input support alter different costs. A launch complaint is not proof that every later version behaves the same way.
