# G92 — Final Fantasy XV

**Status: substantive research checkpoint, not a completed R01–R14 pass.** Research date: September 26, 2026. [Requirements](../research-requirements.md) · [Roster](../research-roster.md) · [Resume ledger](../research-progress.md).

## Edition boundaries established

The original game released November 29, 2016 on PS4 and Xbox One. Its launch reviews must not be treated as reviews of the later Royal/Windows content. Square Enix's current product description explicitly identifies the alternate Chapter 13 route, off-road Regalia and character switching as post-launch updates included in Royal. The March 6, 2018 release also adds the expanded Insomnia endgame area, a controllable boat, an enhanced Armiger mode and a first-person view. The original season-pass episodes and Comrades are included; the product's wording about all season-pass content does not establish that every later DLC or expired event is included. [S1][S2]

The Royal Edition Q&A is a primary interview with director Hajime Tabata. It explains why the team deliberately avoided calling this a Complete Edition while planning further service. The same interview distinguishes features that were difficult to implement, including first-person warp strikes, from underwater exploration, which remained an unrealized wish. A developer's wish is not a shipped mechanic. [S3]

## Reception findings captured

**Peter Brown, GameSpot, November 27, 2016:** praises the open world's appearance, fluid fighting, monsters and demanding optional endgame. He criticizes the predictable central plot, narrowly characterized companions and hard-to-rely-on Astral summons. The ordinary activities that make Eos inviting also weaken the urgency of reclaiming the kingdom. His account of only directly controlling Noctis belongs to launch, not later character switching. Its erroneous description of the invader's family relationship will not be used as a lore source. [S4]

**David Roberts, GamesRadar+, launch console review:** strongly values the four friends, shared meals, photos, traveling animations and coordinated combat. He finds the larger story poorly contextualized without outside media, the supporting cast underdeveloped, and camera/lock-on behavior occasionally obstructive. His praise for companionship directly contrasts with Brown's dissatisfaction about the companions' depth. Their disagreement concerns the value of everyday behavioral characterization, not just different numeric scores. [S5]

**Hannah Dwan, PC Gamer, March 13, 2018:** sees the Windows version as more complete and visually impressive, particularly its expanded finale and road-trip interactions. She nevertheless finds shallow sidequests, limited progression choices and repetition in longer fights. Her reported SSD/HDD and team hardware experiences are dated observations, not a current universal performance guarantee. [S6]

**Alex Donaldson, RPG Site, November 28, 2016:** stresses that this is a game with an open-world portion, not an entirely open campaign. He values the four friends' grounded behavior and deep optional dungeons, but criticizes fetch quests, unclear importance of sellable loot and poorly developed supporting characters. His inference that production changes may explain the muddled story remains a critic's hypothesis, not established internal production history. [S7]

**Mike Epstein, Digital Trends, page displayed August 23, 2019:** values the world, optional content and personable ensemble while finding its plot awkward and genre-changing set pieces less polished. Further body reading is still needed before its full review entry. The page contains an apparent XI/XIV mix-up and several misspelled names; those are not used as external facts. [S8]

## Mechanics evidence captured

Warp-striking consumes Noctis's MP and closes distance; point-warps use designated locations, restore MP and can provide recovery time. This makes spatial escape a resource-recovery action, not only movement. The combat guide also distinguishes phasing from prompted parries and the invulnerability window associated with commanded techniques. These are player-tested guide descriptions, not an audit of the game's AI or collision code. [S9]

The first useful OpenLegend hypothesis is that friendship becomes legible through repeated, functional actions: one companion cooks, another records the trip, and combat visibly links allies. That does not automatically solve thin dialogue, plot discontinuity or a player's desire for deeper relationship choice. This interpretation remains provisional until the broader systems and story analysis is complete.
