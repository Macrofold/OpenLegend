## Identity and dated commercial evidence

Square Enix and Acquire released the game for Switch, PS4, PS5 and PC on February 24, 2023. On June 7, 2023, their announcement placed worldwide shipments plus digital sales above one million. These are units, not revenue, profit, unique active players or a present-day lifetime total. Its new cast and setting do not require first-game completion. [S01](#s01) [S02](#s02)

## Five substantive written reviews

**Nintendo Life — Mitch Vogel, February 17, 2023, Switch.** Vogel praises strategic fights, portable job skills, distinct personal abilities and improved writing/presentation. He values more varied chapters and paired stories, while warning that the sequel remains close to a formula that some players already disliked. His description of a generally stronger sequel does not settle the ensemble-structure disagreement. [S03](#s03)

**RPGFan — Zach Wilkerson, February 17, 2023, PS5.** Wilkerson initially found the sequel too familiar, but came to value its personal mysteries, supporting characters, exploration and eventual connections. He welcomes faster battles and the tactical additions. He still criticizes reserve leveling, the initial protagonist lock, cryptic side quests and the scrolling equipment inventory. Those interface and pacing complaints are separate from his favorable final judgment. [S04](#s04)

**Destructoid — Eric Van Allen, March 22, 2023, PS5.** Van Allen finds emotional variety in the individual stories and a satisfying larger journey, especially after following every traveler. He praises NPC interactions, the battle resource puzzle and the world’s discoveries, while describing middle chapters that lose momentum and some underused companions needing attention. His detailed experiences belong to his review playthrough, not this researcher's firsthand testing. [S05](#s05)

**RPG Site — Cullen Black, February 21, 2023, Switch.** Black, who did not finish the first game, finds the sequel’s cast, exploration and pacing more compelling. He values an episodic structure rather than demanding one continuous party drama. He nevertheless criticizes high encounter frequency, level jumps for neglected travelers and some handheld visual softness. His claim that Crossed Paths are optional must be qualified against the requirements for the separate final chapter; it is not adopted as a universal completion rule. [S06](#s06)

**Push Square — Jade Sayers, February 17, 2023, PS5.** Sayers praises distinct character voices, environmental variety, Crossed Paths and tactical battles in short bursts. She finds later progression slower and long boss health pools repetitive even after a winning strategy is understood. This differs usefully from a claim that every fight stays interesting simply because the underlying rules are strong. Nintendo Life and Push Square have distinct reviews/authors but share a publisher; these are five review articles, not five independent corporate owners. [S07](#s07)

**Research interpretation:** the sequel can substantially improve character writing and party acknowledgment without abandoning the same modular structure that creates repeated openings, uneven levels and selective attachment. Praise for a battle system and criticism of how often or how long it is used are compatible.

## Direct player testimony and actual Steam limitation

The English top-rated Steam Community review URL and appreviews JSON endpoint failed through the research browser. Store aggregate information is not a substitute for individual review bodies. This dossier therefore does not claim to have inspected a ranked helpful-review sample.

An accessible April 1, 2023 Steam discussion supplies contrasting accounts. The opening post, currently under **Baron Slopbane** and quoted in replies under **JCD3nton**, reports that strong builds made later encounters trivial and eight openings damaged pacing, while still praising art and music. **saideron** reports similarly outleveling content but enjoying it because combat and the individual stories remained rewarding. **Grakor** connects an early power spike to seeking every secondary job, and says later chapters felt closer to recommended levels. These are situated player accounts, not measured prevalence or a controlled claim about the cause of overleveling. The opening post's assertion that there is no overarching plot is not treated as authoritative late-game documentation. [S08](#s08)
