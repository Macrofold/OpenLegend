## Current release boundary

The July 29, 2026 developer announcement released Build 42.20 to Stable, with incompatible older saves and legacy branch alternatives explicitly described. The September 23 **42.21 Unstable** announcement is a subsequent opt-in test, not the stable baseline. Its fixes address disappearing zombies, multiplayer duplication/desynchronization, vehicle/tree visibility and split-screen problems. Its body supersedes the site's stale header still listing the same version for both channels. No untested fix is described as personally verified. [P1] [P2]

The complete Build 42.20 feature overview was read: animals, wider crafting, weapon parts, new presets, art/lighting/map revisions, contextual music, mod manager and multiplayer improvements are delivered scope there, not merely the old roadmap. Planned human NPC work must remain separate. [P3]

## Five substantive written critical accounts now read

This corpus contains two explicitly titled reviews, two editorial hands-on retrospectives and one substantial player review. It is **not five scored professional reviews**. The original Spanish and Italian articles were read, with English paraphrases to follow.

1. **Ford Hollett, HollettGaming, date displayed 11/4/2024:** values building a defensible home, meaningful scavenging, configurable challenge and co-op; flags steep entry, simple graphics and limited authored narrative. The ambiguous numerical date is not silently converted to a month/day order. [R1]
2. **Ricardo Fernández, Meristation, March 4, 2022, 7.8/10:** praises interacting survival systems and modes while strongly criticizing control friction and presentation. His loose claims about pausing and character creation are not used as precise mechanics. [R2]
3. **Francesco Serino, Multiplayer.it, January 16, 2022:** his purchased-copy retrospective values Build 41's improved multiplayer, detailed object interactions and role-play communities. The closing reservations concern interface, accessibility and purpose after self-sufficiency. Its NPC forecast is historical, not a delivered-feature statement. [R3]
4. **Andrew Brown, GamesRadar:** the actual article credits Brown, despite a secondary reference crediting Iain Harris. Its full hands-on account follows teaching friends, a warehouse alarm and a failed retreat; teaching renews appreciation for accumulated practical knowledge. Its closing statement that Build 42 lacks multiplayer dates the experience, not today's product. A reliable publication date was not exposed in the retrieved text. [R4]
5. **AncientGammoner, Steam, December 1, 2015:** the complete long review values remembered survival lives while finding a fully self-sufficient safehouse boring. It offers a considered positive interpretation of bounded lives rather than a claim that no player can enjoy sustained settlement. [S1]

## Actual Steam contrast

The English **helpful-all-time positive** surface was read, freshly recovering **beben's January 2, 2022 deafness discovery** and **OrbitalMechanist's December 22, 2021 medicine-run disaster**.
