## Version and preservation baseline

Both earlier owners have been read and remain unchanged. Preserve the corpse's refusal and supported disguise workaround; disguise versus embodied access; drunk versus thrown potions; concentration/Haste transition; boxes, vents and structural supports; gaseous traversal; goblin-camp commitment; distinct companion motives; Karlach's quiet date; Wyll's developing appeal; the patientgamers disagreement; and the 2021 writing interview. None becomes a newly played example. The earlier viewing route remains unwatched.

Larian's April 15, 2025 Patch 8 announcement identifies the final **major** content update, adding twelve subclasses, cross-play, photo mode and Xbox Series S split screen. The official site's March 26, 2026 Hotfix 36 demonstrates that “final major patch” did not mean the immediate end of all fixes. Platform and launch-review limitations must retain dates. [B1][B2]

## Reception findings in progress

Andrew Thornton's August 16, 2023 Hey Poor Player review values alternative solutions and companions who can reject the player's course, but criticizes onboarding. Aimee Hart's September 8, 2023 Gayming review celebrates performance, customization and tactical improvisation while questioning inherited fantasy prejudice and the learning burden. Eric Van Allen's August 29, 2023 Destructoid review values linked character/quest continuity but reports later-act performance and false dialogue-state acknowledgements. These three substantive bodies have been read completely; Fraser Brown's review is still being finished and a fifth body remains to be acquired. Their reported technical experiences conflict and must not be flattened into one current bug verdict. [B3][B4][B5]
