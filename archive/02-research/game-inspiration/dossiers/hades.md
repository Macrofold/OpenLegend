## Scope and version boundaries

Hades is Supergiant's single-player action roguelite about Zagreus repeatedly attempting to escape the Underworld. A failed attempt removes the current run's build but does not erase the continuing household story. This pass covers the completed original game and its post-launch fixes, not Melinoë, the sequel's magic systems or Hades II ending disputes. [S1]

The developer's FAQ records Epic Early Access on December 6, 2018, Steam Early Access on December 10, 2019, and 1.0 on September 17, 2020 alongside Switch. Xbox and PlayStation followed August 13, 2021. The iOS Netflix edition launched March 19, 2024 and was removed from that service in July 2025. Do not describe that mobile subscription route as currently available. PC–Switch cross-saves do not imply PC cross-saves on Xbox/PlayStation. The developer explicitly designed the game for solo play; community strategy discussion is not native multiplayer. [S1]

## Initial reception findings

Jordan Devore's launch review describes fast, readable combat, six worthwhile weapons and short contextual conversations that give defeat another purpose. He also warns that several persistent currencies can initially overwhelm players preoccupied with the fastest route to winning. These are his judgments about the September 2020 version, not measurements of the average player. [S2]

Gaming Gideon's January 2021 review explains why apparently few buttons need not imply few decisions: knockback, reflected projectiles, chain lightning, weapon-specific handling and visible doorway rewards change the player's priorities. It is a firsthand review with declared play context, not developer telemetry. Its full criticism and the other four written reviews are still to be synthesized before completion. [S3]
