# Path of Exile — full research dossier

**G10 · Full pass completed September 25, 2026.** Original Path of Exile, not its sequel. The preserved [three-game comparison](../games/path-of-exile-path-of-exile-2-and-diablo-iv.md) retains earlier creator interviews and the league-cycle argument. This dossier supplies the missing independent systems, presentation and reception research. [Requirements](../research-requirements.md) · [Progress](../research-progress.md).

## 1. What the player does, and which version this describes

Grinding Gear Games presents an online action RPG in **Wraeclast**, built around fighting, finding valuable equipment and composing a character. Skills are socketed gems rather than only permanently assigned class abilities. The seven starting classes occupy different positions on a shared passive tree; traveling across it permits combinations outside a class's obvious specialty. [P01](#p01)

The immediate activity is moving through hostile areas, using attacks or spells, managing recovery, choosing what to collect and adapting equipment. Longer projects concern completing the campaign, developing an Ascendancy, constructing a build and choosing endgame content. A strong item matters when it makes a method viable, not merely because its label is rare.

**Current boundary:** the July 2026 **Curse of the Allflame / 3.29** patch changes sockets, brings back reworked Mercenaries and adds a seafloor-Voyage activity. Its retrieved patch reproduction is used where the official promotional page exposes no readable body. Older color-locking instructions are not current rules: gems can enter any equipment socket; matching a colored socket instead grants quality, and generated sockets default to white. [P02](#p02)

**Interpretation:** access to a composition and optimizing that composition are different problems. Removing an eligibility barrier does not automatically remove decisions about links, effects, resources and defense. It does change which earlier guides a newcomer can safely follow.

## 2. Skills, supports and passives form a compositional vocabulary

A skill gem supplies an action; compatible linked support gems alter its behavior. The official example changes **Fireball** through chaining or additional projectiles. Gems themselves develop and can be traded. [P01](#p01)

**Worked choice:** prepare an attack for groups or concentrate its usefulness on a harder individual target. Changing how an effect reaches enemies changes positioning and resource use, not only its displayed damage. A support that does not apply to the chosen skill cannot become useful through an attractive description. The fundamental test is whether the combination changes an actual action.

**Cluster Jewels** extend the passive tree from supported outer sockets. Their large, medium and small forms have different placement relationships and emphases; some supply additional sockets for further growth. [P03](#p03) **Interpretation:** the tree is not merely a huge static list. An acquired object can create another local set of choices. Its opportunity cost includes the points and route needed to use it, so installing the largest possible cluster is not automatically best.

**Chaos Inoculation** makes maximum life one while granting chaos-damage immunity, requiring the rest of the build to support survival through energy shield and other protection. [P04](#p04)

**Worked counterexample:** selecting the impressive immunity before obtaining suitable supporting defenses can make the character much less survivable. A powerful modifier can invalidate the old resource model. This is a useful reference for inventions that change a foundational rule: compatibility is a gameplay issue, not only a schema check.

## 3. Recovery and resources participate in combat

Flasks hold charges, spend them on use and recharge through supported sources, including kills and visiting town or a hideout. Flask type, modifiers and passive choices affect that behavior. They are reusable equipment, not an endless inventory of identical one-use potions. [P05](#p05)

**Worked situation:** ordinary enemies can replenish a resource that is harder to sustain during a long isolated encounter. A build that appears comfortable while clearing groups can therefore struggle against a boss. Adding damage does not necessarily solve a recovery failure; the player may need another source of charges, stronger prevention or a different method.

**Explosive Concoction** explicitly consumes charges from suitable elemental flasks to enhance an unarmed attack. Its weapon restrictions matter. [P06](#p06) **Interpretation:** the same object can protect the player and fuel an attack. This creates a real tradeoff between two uses rather than adding another unrelated mana bar. The exact tuning is version-specific; the compositional relationship is the useful reference.

Damage, defense and recovery must work together. A character optimized for one attractive interaction may still fail when its delivery is interrupted or an encounter suppresses its sustaining resource. Such failure can be informative when the relevant cause is visible, and opaque when all the player sees is a sudden death.

## 4. Currency is a tool as well as a medium of exchange

A **Chaos Orb** rerolls a rare item's modifiers. **Divine Orbs** alter numerical values of existing modifiers, while a **Mirror of Kalandra** makes a restricted mirrored copy. These are different operations, not interchangeable denominations of ordinary money. [P07](#p07)

**Worked decision:** spend an orb attempting to improve owned equipment, preserve it toward a known trade, or wait until there is a better base to modify. The crafting operation consumes something with an alternative use. A random result is therefore meaningful partly because the player chose to risk that alternative.

An **Eldritch Chaos Orb** rerolls prefixes or suffixes according to which supported influence is dominant. Equal-tier influences do not establish the same dominance condition. [P08](#p08) **Interpretation:** a more specialized operator can protect one portion of a construction while changing another, but only under explicit prerequisites. This is deeper than merely having more crafting currencies: the player chooses which uncertainty to accept.

Equipment bases, affixes, sockets, jewels and recipes interact. A desirable modifier can be worthless on an unusable base or incompatible with the intended defense. The library should preserve acquisition, modification and use as separate steps instead of reducing a build to a shopping list.

## 5. A helper can preserve equipment while still imposing a failure cost

**Animate Guardian** turns supported weapons and armor into an allied minion. The equipment's applicable properties affect it, but it does not inherit every player system—for example, ordinary notable passive allocations are not meaningful to a minion without that tree. Since **3.26**, its equipment is retained after death, while it normally cannot be summoned again in the area where it died. [P09](#p09)

**Worked choice:** equip the helper for a useful team effect and protect it through a dangerous encounter. Losing it now removes that assistance for the local attempt without necessarily deleting all the invested objects. An old guide warning of permanent item loss describes a different contract.

**Interpretation:** reducing permanent punishment need not make a companion irrelevant. The ongoing fight can still change materially when support disappears. This also separates being a useful autonomous combatant from being a person with private memory or goals; no source here establishes an LLM-driven Guardian.

The 3.29 Mercenary revision adds another boundary. Ordinary hired assistance is temporary, whereas the new **Luminary** specialization supports permanent recruitment and equipment customization. Mercenaries no longer count as human party members for the old associated scaling. [P02](#p02) A companion, party member and reusable equipment carrier are distinct roles even when they all fight nearby.

## 6. Campaign, Ascendancy and endgame provide different kinds of progress

**The Lord's Labyrinth** combines traps, puzzles and fights against **Izaro**, with a layout generated for the day. Completing its relevant versions grants Ascendancy choices and points; its Divine Font also supports gem-related rewards. [P10](#p10)

**Interpretation:** a specialized capability can be earned through another kind of challenge rather than only through more ordinary kills. However, forcing every player to repeat a preparatory activity can make an initially memorable place feel like a toll. The benefit must justify the form of repetition for the intended audience.

The **3.28 Atlas redesign** separates generic map-tier items from many specific destinations. Players choose an available area on the Atlas rather than needing that area's ordinary map item; special destinations still require their own access objects. The Atlas begins at the center and connects revealed content and quadrant-specific Memory Vault rewards. [P11](#p11)

**Worked choice:** use an appropriate map to pursue a location whose layout or reward fits the current goal. Access inventory and destination selection remain relevant but are no longer the same constraint. This preserves a targetable endgame while reducing one kind of item-management friction.

Allflame's **Charts** can be connected into a **Voyage**, revisiting areas as a larger seafloor excursion with modified rewards. [P02](#p02) **Interpretation:** previously completed pieces become inputs to another designed route. A reusable fragment can be worth keeping because of the larger activity it enables, not only because it raises a count.

## 7. Modes, death and trade change the meaning of a reward

Ordinary death can remove experience toward the next level, with penalties depending on the area, without reducing an already earned level. [P12](#p12) The Allflame patch explicitly parents its Hardcore variation to the corresponding Standard Allflame league, allowing continued play there after death. That current rule must not be replaced by an older generic migration description. [P02](#p02)

**Solo Self-Found** disables partying, trading and the Currency Exchange. It maintains separate progress and permits one-way migration into the corresponding trade environment. [P13](#p13)

**Worked comparison:** finding a useful base in SSF can be a new project in itself; in a trade league, it can instead become purchasing power for a different project. Neither interpretation is inherently superior. The same item economy supplies a different experience when access to other people's production changes.

The 2024 **Currency Exchange** introduction let players specify desired/offered resources and ratios through **Faustus**, paying a gold cost. Its original league-only rollout was historical. [P14](#p14) Later **Merchant's Tabs**, introduced in 3.27, support asynchronous item sales through a separate purchased-tab system, with buyer gold costs and item-locking rules. [P15](#p15)

**Interpretation:** direct negotiation, a bulk exchange and an asynchronous listing are different social and operational activities. Easier trading can remove repetitive messaging without removing decisions about what to build. It also makes access to specialized tab infrastructure part of the actual value proposition; the phrase free to play does not describe every convenience.

## 8. A authored world can make a build-enabling errand memorable

Wraeclast's dark-fantasy premise is reinforced by particular people rather than only hostile scenery. In **A Fixture of Fate**, the scholar **Siosa** asks for four Golden Pages beyond a hidden Library entrance. Completing the optional Act 3 quest opens his gem-vendor service. [P16](#p16)

**Worked story interaction:** investigate an unusual place, help someone unable to obtain the material himself, then gain access to options that support the player's own build. The narrative reward and the mechanical reward reinforce each other. The quest's significance is not simply that it awards another currency token.

**Interpretation:** an NPC can be memorable through a specific need, setting and useful consequence without being freely conversational. Conversely, repeatedly rushing an already-known errand can reduce that identity to a service unlock. The same authored content can support discovery on the first pass and friction on later ones.

The preserved creator accounts explain why campaign and repeated progression are central to the game's intended long-term structure. This is not evidence that every player wants periodic erasure of their main persistent world. A fresh league and an existing permanent collection are deliberately different contexts.

## 9. Art, audio and production are more than the size of the skill list

GGG's concept-artist account identifies work across monsters, weapons, bosses, characters, portals, divination cards and Atlas illustrations. **Shaun Brown's** responsibilities show a shared visual vocabulary spanning functional objects and interfaces, rather than treating every reward icon as disconnected decoration. [P17](#p17)

**Interpretation:** a dense item game needs recognizable categories and memorable particular objects. Too much similar-looking loot can move the real decision process into external filters. Visual richness and the ease of identifying the next useful action should therefore be evaluated separately.

Composer **Kamil Orman-Janowski** describes combining orchestral, electronic and other instrumental influences, and discusses layering/segmentation and the importance of thematic timing and narrative context. [P18](#p18) **Interpretation:** a continuous combat game still benefits from different emotional intensity across places and encounters. These are his production methods and intent, not this researcher's independent analysis of the entire recorded score.

GGG's published VFX-artist interview documents a distinct effects discipline; earlier experience with Unreal tools in the artist's biography is not evidence that Path of Exile itself uses Unreal. [P19](#p19) Avoid inferring proprietary architecture from a visual resemblance or an employee's previous work.

The earlier chapter retains **Chris Wilson's** 2016 interview and 2019 development-talk abstract/reporting: mastery-oriented design, procedural variation and a historical roughly thirteen-week league rhythm. That cadence is not asserted as the exact present schedule. Free expansions, revisions and two separately maintained games create production obligations, not an effortless perpetual-growth mechanism.

## 10. Distribution and reception: the return occasion needs a worthwhile activity

The official product offers free entry, while the inspected records establish paid storage/trading convenience and optional cosmetic products. A league supplies a shared starting point for discoveries, builds, races and creator discussion; a permanent environment preserves another kind of investment. [P01](#p01) [P15](#p15)

GGG's March 2026 post connects actual repair work with a launch campaign: it reports eleven hotfixes, identifies Atlas visibility and progression issues under investigation, and advertises Twitch Drops. [P20](#p20)

**Interpretation:** promotion, onboarding and operational reliability meet at release. A streamer can reveal an attractive possibility, but the incoming player still needs a functioning interface and a comprehensible first build. The documented promotion does not establish how many purchases or retained users it caused.

Fraser Brown's **May 25, 2018 PC Gamer re-review** praises the game's growing identity, experimentation and value, including how added systems change ordinary loot pursuit. It is a historical appraisal, not an assessment of 2026 socket or trade rules. [P21](#p21)

Direct responses to the 2026 Atlas change differ. **Sly_D_0_Double_G** likes the redesign but reports difficulty finding unique maps. **AgnosiousD** initially finds it unfamiliar and inefficient, wonders about repetitive map selection, and says they will keep trying. [P22](#p22)

**Interpretation:** a system can improve destination choice while increasing a particular player's interaction cost. The second response is an early impression, not a final verdict, and the first does not validate every acquisition rate. Preserve both rather than converting selected posts into a consensus score.

Public material inspected here does not establish audited title profit, a representative age distribution or feature-level retention. PoE2's Steam launch peak is not the original game's audience. The original comparison's distinction among free leagues, paid early access and premium expansions remains important.

## 11. Transferable inspiration and limits

The strongest reference patterns are **an action as an item**, **an upgrade that changes a rule**, **currency with an executable use**, and **a build that creates a new practical approach**. The most useful counterexamples are equally concrete: an immunity can break survival, an elaborate helper can become unavailable for the crucial encounter, and a new endgame interface can make a familiar repeated action slower.

For OpenLegend, a reusable invention should expose requirements, effects, incompatibilities and what its user gives up. A helper's local failure need not destroy all past investment. An authored person can connect a practical request with a new capability. None of these lessons requires the same periodic resets, immense item catalog or dependence on external build research.
