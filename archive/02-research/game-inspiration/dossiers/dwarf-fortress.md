Older drinking or social-frustration stories must therefore remain attributed to their original versions, not be repeated as verified current behavior. A description of something working in the developer's build is not by itself a shipped-feature announcement. [D1]

## Initial reception contrast

Chris McMullen's January 2023 GameSpew review describes narrowly averting a drinking shortage by brewing gathered fruit. He values the freedom to discover unexpected consequences and distinguishes broad material substitution from later specialized requirements. He also describes a substantial learning/time commitment and unresolved uncertainty about some events. His medical terminology about one dwarf is not adopted here as a verified diagnosis or clinical simulation claim. [D4]

Simone Tagliaferri's December 2022 Multiplayer.it review celebrates connected systems and stories but stresses the severe initial learning burden and the continuing need for experimentation/community help. Its broad geological claims are not treated as an independently verified model of the game's internal world generator. [D5]
