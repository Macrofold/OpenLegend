## What is directly evidenced

Tarn Adams's 2018 development log discusses retaining emotional experiences, later recollection, short- and longer-term memories, and personality-dependent consequences. It also addresses repeated similar experiences dominating remembered life. Later entries report released personality/value changes associated with the memory work. Those dated implementation reports support persistence and transformation, not a current exact numerical model of every emotion. [D1]

## Transfer: a memory must be able to change a later choice

A fluent diary that is never used is presentation, not operational memory. A useful memory may change whom a person approaches, which item they preserve, whether they trust a statement, what they teach or whether they continue an earlier activity. It need not generate a new public monologue every time it matters.

At least four records have different meanings: what happened, what this person could notice, what they believe about it, and how they currently interpret its significance. Keeping those distinctions allows misunderstanding, deception and later reinterpretation without rewriting the past.

A hypothetical borrowed tool can illustrate the chain. One person fails to return it; the owner notices its absence but may not know why. A later explanation changes the owner's belief or willingness to lend, not the original transfer record. If the tool is recovered, the material problem may be solved while the relationship remains unsettled. If the incident is forgotten, a durable outstanding obligation should not necessarily disappear with a conversational summary.

## Transfer: memory selection needs diversity and purpose

Repeated low-information events can crowd out a rare important exception. Simple recency is not enough, but neither is a universal importance score. Retrieve by the current person, object, place, commitment and question as well as relevance and time. Preserve provenance when compressing.

The countervailing burden is real: keeping every event forever, searching all of it on every decision and continuously generating reflection can cost more than acting. A factual event can be recorded cheaply; a subjective reinterpretation may wait until a relevant opportunity. This is a research hypothesis, not permission to change OpenLegend's existing retention or attention contracts.

## Transfer: institutions make individual behavior consequential

People need meaningful opportunities, not only richer personality text. A place to teach, a patient needing care, a shared workshop or an object with a disputed claim can turn preferences into visible action. Such affordances also let routine execution support continuing social life without requiring an LLM to narrate every work step.

Do not copy every institutional system. One complete interaction linking action, evidence, memory and a later decision is more informative than a hundred named institutions whose only effect is a score.

## Limits and evaluation

No claim of human-like psychological fidelity follows from an emotion system. Evaluate actual longitudinal behavior: does relevant evidence survive distraction, does a mistaken belief remain distinguishable from fact, can a character revise an interpretation, and can a achieved purpose remain achieved? The ordinary route is a small persistent record with good retrieval, not necessarily a separate cognitive architecture or extra paid reflection loop.
