# Cyberpunk 2077 — full research dossier

**G68 · Complete research pass, September 26, 2026.** This dossier uses the **post-2.0 / Patch 2.31 game** as the current base-game reference while preserving the materially different December 2020 launch state. **Phantom Liberty** is treated as a paid expansion layered on top of the free 2.0 systems overhaul, not as the source of every modern mechanic. Current platforms include PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2, and Mac; last-generation PS4/Xbox One remain on older feature/patch branches and do not receive the modern 2.x systems. [Requirements](../research-requirements.md) · [Progress](../research-progress.md).

Cyberpunk 2077 is one of the clearest examples in this corpus of a game whose **mechanical identity changed after launch**. For OpenLegend, its strongest ideas are:
- bodily modification as build composition;
- multiple playstyles sharing the same authored spaces;
- relationships/side quests carrying more emotional weight than the nominal open-world filler;
- environmental hacking as a reusable interaction vocabulary;
- and the importance of rebuilding systems around meaningful abilities rather than piles of incremental modifiers.

Its launch is also a major product warning: ambitious world promise, marketing expectations, and platform breadth can become liabilities when shipped implementation cannot support them reliably.

## 1. Identity, current scope, and version boundaries

**Developer/publisher:** CD PROJEKT RED / CD PROJEKT.  
**Original release:** December 10, 2020 on PC, PS4, Xbox One, Stadia.  
**Native PS5/Xbox Series release:** February 15, 2022.  
**Phantom Liberty:** September 26, 2023.  
**Nintendo Switch 2:** June 5, 2025.  
**Mac:** July 2025 alongside Update 2.3-era support. [CP01](#cp01) [CP02](#cp02)

The player is **V**, a customizable mercenary in Night City whose body contains an experimental Relic carrying the engram of **Johnny Silverhand**.

The modern game is:
- first-person;
- single-player;
- open-world;
- action RPG;
- quest/relationship driven;
- heavily build-customizable.

There is **no released multiplayer mode**. Earlier announced/planned multiplayer ambitions do not count as shipped functionality.

### Current patch

**Patch 2.31**, released September 11, 2025, remains the latest official patch located for this pass. It improves the 2.3 AutoDrive/Free Roam driving behavior and Photo Mode and fixes issues across PC, PS5, Xbox Series, Mac, and Switch 2. [CP03](#cp03)

## 2. V: body, identity, and Lifepath

Character creation configures:
- body/appearance;
- voice;
- selected bodily details;
- initial attributes;
- one of three **Lifepaths**:
  - Nomad;
  - Streetkid;
  - Corpo.

Lifepaths primarily alter:
- opening sequence;
- contextual dialogue options;
- occasional quest/social shortcuts.

They do **not** create three entirely separate campaigns. [CP04](#cp04)

This distinction matters because launch marketing created expectations of more structural divergence than the finished system supplies.

### OpenLegend lesson

Backstory is most valuable when it creates:
- knowledge;
- relationships;
- assumptions;
- access;
- liabilities.

A label alone is not enough.

## 3. Attributes and the post-2.0 perk architecture

Current attributes are:
- **Body**
- **Reflexes**
- **Technical Ability**
- **Intelligence**
- **Cool**

Update 2.0 completely rebuilt perk trees around fewer, more transformative abilities. CDPR explicitly said it wanted to replace repeated small percentage improvements with abilities that change how the player acts. [CP05](#cp05)

Examples:
- Body → Adrenaline Rush, blunt/shotgun/LMG play;
- Reflexes → dashes, air movement, blades/SMGs/ARs;
- Technical Ability → cyberware/grenades/Tech weapons;
- Intelligence → quickhacking/Overclock/smart weapons;
- Cool → stealth, pistols, precision rifles, throwing weapons.

Perk points can be refunded individually for free outside combat; attributes have a much more constrained reset boundary. [CP06](#cp06)

### OpenLegend lesson

A progression node earns its existence when it creates:
- a new verb;
- a new timing window;
- a meaningful new interaction.

“+2% damage” is low-information progression.

## 4. Skills: growth from style of play

Update 2.0 replaced earlier skill structure with five progression tracks:
- Headhunter;
- Netrunner;
- Shinobi;
- Solo;
- Engineer.

They grow through relevant activity and can reach level 60.

Every five levels grants a passive reward. [CP06](#cp06)

This is a useful hybrid:
- attributes/perks are deliberate build choices;
- skills record **what the player actually does**.

OpenLegend can distinguish:
- intention/education;
- embodied practice;
- current role.

## 5. Cyberware: body as equipment platform

Update 2.0 made cyberware central to build identity.

Cyberware occupies bodily systems/slots such as:
- operating system;
- frontal cortex;
- skeleton;
- nervous system;
- integumentary system;
- arms;
- legs;
- circulatory system;
- hands;
- face.

Cyberware now supplies most armor; clothing is primarily cosmetic. [CP07](#cp07)

### Cyberware Capacity

V has a capacity limit that increases with progression and shards.

The **Edgerunner** perk can exceed normal capacity at a health cost, explicitly echoing the setting's idea that too much chrome stresses the body. [CP07](#cp07)

### Operating systems

Major mutually exclusive build-defining operating systems include:
- **Cyberdeck** — quickhacks;
- **Sandevistan** — time dilation;
- **Berserk** — combat-survival/melee emphasis.

Equipping one closes off the others.

### OpenLegend relevance

This is strong compositional body design:
> a body has typed slots, resource limits, compatibility and consequences.

Generated augmentations should interact with:
- anatomy;
- energy;
- senses;
- medicine;
- social perception;
- maintenance.

Not just add stats.

## 6. Quickhacking: information access becomes action

With a cyberdeck, V can scan networked targets and use quickhacks.

Typical effect families include:
- distract devices;
- disable/overload;
- reveal/location control;
- weapon malfunction;
- movement impairment;
- memory/awareness effects;
- damage-over-time;
- contagion;
- system collapse.

Quickhacks consume **RAM** and can be queued/combined by advanced builds.

### Environmental composition

The same hacking framework applies to:
- cameras;
- turrets;
- doors/devices;
- vehicles after 2.0;
- people.

This is one of Cyberpunk's best transferable systems:
> one semantic capability—network intrusion—produces many context-specific interactions because objects expose compatible affordances.

OpenLegend should prefer that over bespoke “hack camera,” “hack door,” “hack car” features with unrelated implementations.

## 7. Weapons and combat families

Major firearm families include:
- **Power** — conventional projectiles/ricochet-oriented systems;
- **Tech** — charged shots that can penetrate cover under applicable rules;
- **Smart** — target-lock/homing behavior with compatible cyberware.

Other weapons include:
- blades;
- blunt weapons;
- throwing knives/axes;
- arm cyberware such as Mantis Blades/Gorilla Arms/Monowire/Projectile Launch System.

Combat composes:
- weapon family;
- attributes/perks;
- cyberware;
- quickhacks;
- grenades;
- movement;
- stealth;
- status effects.

### Iconic weapons

Named Iconics carry distinctive special effects and can often be upgraded across tiers, making them persistent authored objects rather than disposable loot.

## 8. Loot, tiers, crafting, and upgrades

Modern item tiers broadly progress:
- Tier 1 through Tier 5 and higher upgraded/iconic states.

Loot includes:
- weapons;
- clothing;
- components;
- cyberware-related items;
- consumables;
- crafting materials;
- money/shards.

Post-2.0 crafting is substantially streamlined:
- components upgrade/craft eligible items;
- Iconics are the primary weapon-upgrade focus;
- ordinary clothing no longer exists mainly as armor-stat churn.

### Why the redesign matters

Launch Cyberpunk often produced absurd fashion:
> wear mismatched clothing because it has better armor.

Update 2.0 moved defense to cyberware, restoring:
- visual authorship;
- semantic bodily protection.

That is a useful correction for OpenLegend:
> optimization should not force players to violate the fiction of an object's function.

## 9. Movement and traversal

V can:
- walk/run;
- slide;
- climb/mantle;
- swim;
- drive;
- ride motorcycles;
- dash/air dash with perks;
- double-jump/charged-jump through leg cyberware.

Night City supports:
- dense street movement;
- vertical approaches;
- alleys;
- rooftops;
- highway travel;
- Badlands open terrain.

The movement build can materially change mission approaches:
- double-jump to upper access;
- dash across gaps;
- stealth route;
- frontal breach.

### Limitation

Night City is visually vertical but only selected surfaces/interiors are interactable.

OpenLegend should avoid implying that every visible window/door is a simulation affordance unless the world supports it.

## 10. Stealth and detection

Stealth uses:
- line of sight;
- awareness meters;
- crouch/movement;
- cameras;
- cover;
- distractions;
- silencers/throwing weapons;
- quickhacks;
- takedowns/body disposal.

Cool perks deepen stealth.

Cyberpunk's best encounter spaces often support:
- scan first;
- mark enemies;
- disable cameras;
- open alternate route;
- distract one guard;
- isolate another;
- complete objective unseen.

### OpenLegend lesson

Stealth is strongest as:
> perception + sound + visibility + knowledge + environment.

Not a binary “stealth mode.”

## 11. Police, Heat, and MaxTac after Update 2.0

The original launch police system became infamous for shallow/teleporting response.

Update 2.0 explicitly rebuilt police around clear Heat states:
- criminal action triggers response;
- line-of-sight/search matters;
- roadblocks/chases emerge;
- five-star Heat can summon **MaxTac** as a high-tier encounter. [CP08](#cp08)

This transformation is important product evidence:
> a world reaction system can be structurally wrong even if individual missions are excellent.

OpenLegend should build law around reusable causal rules early rather than scripted emergency spawning.

## 12. Vehicle combat and current vehicle systems

Update 2.0 added:
- shooting pistols/SMGs from vehicles;
- motorcycle melee;
- mounted weapons on certain cars;
- vehicle quickhacks;
- more natural police chases. [CP09](#cp09)

Update 2.3 added:
- **AutoDrive**;
- Delamain taxi service;
- new vehicles/quests;
- expanded CrystalCoat customization;
- Photo Mode additions. [CP02](#cp02)

Patch 2.31 improved AutoDrive:
- overtaking;
- navigation;
- Free Roam traffic-rule behavior. [CP03](#cp03)

### OpenLegend lesson

Vehicles can participate in the same world affordances:
- attack;
- hack;
- ownership;
- navigation;
- autonomous control.

## 13. Quests, Gigs, and open-world activity

Activity families include:
- main Jobs;
- Side Jobs;
- fixer **Gigs**;
- NCPD Scanner encounters;
- Cyberpsycho Sightings;
- vehicle contracts/other later systems;
- hidden environmental stories.

The strongest content is frequently Side Jobs:
- relationship arcs;
- moral dilemmas;
- recurring people;
- investigations.

The weaker open-world activities can devolve into:
> travel to marker → kill/loot → read shard.

This tension appears in both launch and current player criticism. [CP10](#cp10) [CP11](#cp11)

### OpenLegend lesson

Generated opportunities should carry:
- people;
- consequences;
- changing state.

Markers without persistence are content volume, not world depth.

## 14. Dialogue, choices, and Lifepath knowledge

Dialogue can depend on:
- Lifepath;
- attributes;
- prior quest choices;
- relationships;
- quest state.

Some choices change:
- approach;
- reward;
- relationship;
- later availability;
- ending.

Others mostly change flavor.

### Important design distinction

Cyberpunk is often more reactive than its open-world surface suggests, but less radically branching than early hype implied.

OpenLegend should make **degrees of consequence legible**:
- conversational flavor;
- local state change;
- systemic world change.

## 15. Johnny Silverhand: involuntary companion

Johnny is an engram occupying V's mind.

He:
- appears in scenes;
- comments on places/people;
- argues;
- remembers;
- can gain/lose rapport;
- sometimes influences decision context.

This is a powerful relationship structure because he cannot simply leave.

### OpenLegend relevance

Persistent companions can be:
- internal;
- symbiotic;
- magical;
- technological.

A relationship need not occupy a second body to create dialogue/agency conflict.

## 16. Relationships and romance

Major romanceable characters include:
- Judy Alvarez;
- Panam Palmer;
- River Ward;
- Kerry Eurodyne,

with availability depending on V's configured body/voice criteria and relationship progression.

Relationships grow through:
- quest chains;
- dialogue;
- choices;
- helping the person's community/problem.

They can affect:
- ending options;
- epilogue;
- ongoing messages/interactions.

### Stronger than a meter

The strongest relationship arcs are attached to actual lives:
- Judy and the Mox/clouds context;
- Panam and the Aldecaldos;
- River and family/police investigation;
- Kerry and artistic/identity conflict.

OpenLegend should build relationships around shared problems and obligations rather than gifts alone.

## 17. Factions, gangs, corps, and reputation limits

Night City contains organizations including:
- Arasaka;
- Militech;
- NCPD;
- Trauma Team;
- Maelstrom;
- Tyger Claws;
- Valentinos;
- 6th Street;
- Voodoo Boys;
- Animals;
- Aldecaldos;
- fixers/netrunner communities.

They provide:
- visual language;
- equipment;
- territory;
- quests;
- lore.

### Limitation

V does not participate in a deep general-purpose faction reputation simulation.

Most faction consequences are:
- quest-authored;
- combat-hostility contextual.

OpenLegend can extend this into:
- memory;
- standing;
- debts;
- internal faction politics.

## 18. Night City: magnificent place, shallow ambient simulation

Night City is one of the game's strongest achievements:
- distinct districts;
- architecture;
- advertising;
- crowds;
- traffic;
- radio/music;
- environmental storytelling.

But launch-era PC Gamer criticism captured the core limitation:
> the city looks alive at scale, yet many pedestrians/services/interiors do not support deep systemic interaction. [CP12](#cp12)

Update 2.x improved:
- traffic;
- police;
- vehicle behavior;
- some NPC reactions.

It still is not an NPC-life simulation.

### OpenLegend opportunity

Preserve Cyberpunk's:
- density;
- style;
- geographic identity

while adding:
- persistent people;
- schedules;
- memory;
- ownership.

## 19. Death, retry, saves, and consequence

V's death:
- reloads a recent save/checkpoint;
- does not create persistent corpse/successor state.

Quests can have irreversible character/world outcomes, but combat failure itself is usually reversible.

This supports cinematic RPG flow but weakens embodied mortality.

OpenLegend can offer different world contracts:
- reload;
- injury;
- rescue;
- clone;
- successor;
- permanent death.

## 20. Phantom Liberty: scope and Dogtown

**Phantom Liberty** is Cyberpunk 2077's first and only major paid expansion.

It adds:
- **Dogtown**, a sealed district;
- spy-thriller campaign;
- Solomon Reed;
- Song So Mi / Songbird;
- Kurt Hansen/Barghest;
- new gigs/side content;
- vehicle contracts/airdrops;
- new equipment;
- Relic progression;
- additional ending possibilities for V. [CP13](#cp13)

PC Gamer called it CDPR operating at full strength as an RPG storyteller; GameSpot praised its tighter focus and consequential choices. [CP13](#cp13) [CP14](#cp14)

### Base-game versus expansion

**Free 2.0 includes:**
- perk redesign;
- cyberware redesign;
- police;
- vehicle combat;
- skill overhaul.

**Phantom Liberty owns:**
- Dogtown/story;
- Relic tree;
- expansion content/items/jobs.

Do not conflate them. [CP15](#cp15)

## 21. Relic tree: context-specific expansion progression

Phantom Liberty introduces a **Relic** skill tree powered by special points found through Militech data terminals / expansion progression.

It enhances cyberware-focused capabilities such as:
- optical weak-point analysis;
- arm cyberware;
- stealth/emergency effects.

The tree is orthogonal to the main attribute/perk system.

### OpenLegend lesson

An expansion can add a new progression axis only when it corresponds to a new world mechanism.

Avoid arbitrary “DLC levels” detached from fiction.

## 22. Production history and launch failure

Cyberpunk 2077 was announced years before release and drew enormous expectations through:
- CDPR's Witcher 3 reputation;
- cinematic reveals;
- long-form gameplay demos;
- Keanu Reeves marketing;
- repeated release-date delays.

Launch reviews were mostly based on PC builds.

The base PS4/Xbox One versions had:
- severe performance issues;
- crashes;
- bugs;
- visual degradation.

Game Informer later summarized that the technical failure overwhelmed discussion of the game's narrative/mechanical strengths. [CP16](#cp16)

Sony removed the PS4 version from the PlayStation Store for months and offered refunds—a rare, major platform response.

### Critical lesson

Do not expose a product to:
- many hardware targets;
- enormous promise surface;
- fixed launch date

unless the least-capable supported platform can deliver the intended experience.

## 23. Recovery: 1.5 → 2.0 → Phantom Liberty

The recovery was incremental.

### Patch 1.5 / native current-gen (2022)
Added/improved:
- PS5/Series native versions;
- systems/QoL;
- AI/world details;
- apartments/customization and other improvements.

Game Informer described it as the first state where the game felt much closer to a complete modern open-world RPG. [CP17](#cp17)

### Update 2.0 (2023)
Rebuilt foundational systems:
- perks;
- skills;
- cyberware;
- police;
- vehicle combat;
- loot/economy portions. [CP06](#cp06)

### Phantom Liberty
Added a concentrated expansion showing what the mature systems could support.

### 2.2 / 2.3 / 2.31
Continued adding:
- customization;
- vehicles;
- AutoDrive;
- Photo Mode;
- fixes.

This is not merely “bug fixing.” The shipped game was **redesigned**.

## 24. Art, audio, UI, and embodied perspective

Cyberpunk uses first-person almost universally for:
- exploration;
- dialogue;
- action.

That keeps body/cyberware interaction intimate:
- installing chrome;
- sitting;
- handing objects;
- riding in passenger seats.

Night City uses:
- aggressive advertising;
- corporate/gang visual language;
- dense neon;
- industrial Badlands contrast;
- diegetic screens;
- radio/music.

Reviews repeatedly praise:
- visual identity;
- soundtrack;
- voice performances.

The interface can become dense because:
- weapon stats;
- cyberware;
- perks;
- quickhacks;
- statuses

compete for attention.

Update 2.0's simplification improves semantic readability.

## 25. Distribution, current commercial context, and lifecycle

Cyberpunk remains a premium product:
- base game;
- paid Phantom Liberty;
- Ultimate Edition bundles.

No microtransaction economy defines the single-player game.

### Current sales

CD PROJEKT reported in November 2025 that **Cyberpunk 2077 surpassed 35 million units sold**. [CP18](#cp18)

In May 2025 it reported **Phantom Liberty surpassed 10 million copies**. [CP19](#cp19)

The company's 2025 annual summary repeated both milestones. [CP20](#cp20)

These are:
- unit sales,
not:
- active users;
- revenue per player;
- current monthly engagement.

### Franchise extension

Cyberpunk 2 entered preproduction in 2025. [CP19](#cp19)

The franchise also expanded through:
- Cyberpunk: Edgerunners;
- comics/merchandise;
- Switch 2/Mac ports.

## 26. Five substantive written review perspectives

### 1. PC Gamer — James Davenport, December 2020

**Praised:** setting, characters, smaller stories, visual ambition.

**Criticized:** relentless bugs and shallow/repetitive enemy/encounter behavior.

Verdict at launch: compelling game underneath instability; better to wait. [CP10](#cp10)

### 2. Game Informer — Andrew Reiner, December 2020 PC

**Praised:** scale, Night City, side content, character choice, stealth/cyberware possibilities.

**Criticized:** gunplay, enemy AI and glitches.

It explicitly warned console performance was far below PC. [CP21](#cp21)

### 3. RPG Site — Alex Donaldson, December 2020

**Praised:** extraordinary scope, broad RPG systems, world/quest craft.

**Criticized:** immaturity in tone/content and severe bugs; later addendum found console versions substantially worse. [CP22](#cp22) [CP23](#cp23)

### 4. Game Informer — last-gen review, December 2020

**Praised:** ensemble cast/world underneath the problems.

**Criticized:** base-console visuals, framerate, crashes/glitches and weak enemy AI; scored far below the PC review. [CP24](#cp24)

This is essential evidence that “the launch” was not one uniform experience.

### 5. PC Gamer — Phantom Liberty, September 2023

**Praised:** spy-thriller structure, difficult choices, characters, concentrated RPG design.

**Criticized/qualified:** expansion does not reinvent the whole base game; it succeeds by executing its existing RPG strengths extremely well. [CP13](#cp13)

### Additional modern check: GameSpot Phantom Liberty

GameSpot praised:
- Dogtown;
- Songbird/Reed;
- consequential endings;
- side content;
- mature 2.0 combat/build framework. [CP14](#cp14)

## 28. Worked interactions

### A. Hack a camera, then use it as remote perception

**Intent:** infiltrate without entering guarded sight lines.

**Actions:** scan camera → take control/hack → look through its view → mark enemies / hack connected devices.

**Result:** one networked object extends V's perception into another space.

**Next:** disable turret, distract guard, find route.

**OpenLegend lesson:** perception can be delegated through devices.

### B. Overclock trades bodily safety for cognition throughput

**Intent:** run more quickhacks than available RAM permits.

**Condition:** Intelligence/perk build.

**Action:** activate Overclock → spend health as hacking resource.

**Result:** body becomes computational budget.

**Next:** heal/kill/chain effects before vulnerability becomes fatal.

**Lesson:** resource substitution creates strong build identity when fiction supports it.

### C. Cyberware Capacity creates bodily opportunity cost

**Intent:** install another powerful implant.

**Constraint:** capacity limit.

**Options:** remove another implant, level/find capacity shard, take Edgerunner risk.

**Result:** body is a finite platform, not an infinite equipment bag.

### D. MaxTac turns crime escalation into a boss-like world response

**Intent:** keep fighting police at maximum Heat.

**Result:** ordinary response escalates to specialized MaxTac archetypes. [CP08](#cp08)

**Lesson:** institutions can possess escalation ladders.

### E. Relationship quest changes ending availability

**Intent:** help a major ally through their personal arc.

**Actions:** complete side jobs + relationship decisions.

**Result:** later ending options/epilogue state can change.

**Lesson:** “side” relationships can alter the highest-level story state.

### F. Use the same warehouse three different ways

**Build 1:** scan → quickhack cameras/enemies → avoid direct combat.  
**Build 2:** double-jump/dash → enter alternate vertical path → blade combat.  
**Build 3:** Body/Technical → force doors/tank damage → shotgun.

**Result:** authored space supports multiple capability grammars.

**Limit:** many activities still funnel into combat and not every visible route is systemic.

## 29. Transferable inspiration for OpenLegend

### A. The body should be a real system

Cyberware shows the power of:
- typed body slots;
- capacity;
- tradeoffs;
- visible fiction.

### B. Abilities should expose shared affordances

Quickhacking works because compatible entities participate in one network grammar.

### C. Let practice and deliberate builds coexist

Skills record behavior; perks express chosen specialization.

### D. Side characters can carry the emotional game

Many players remember:
- Judy;
- Panam;
- Johnny;
- Songbird;
- Reed

more than generic open-world events.

Invest simulation budget in persistent people.

### E. Police/law needs causal escalation

The 2.0 redesign is direct evidence that arbitrary spawning is insufficient.

### F. A redesign can restore fiction/mechanics alignment

Moving armor off clothing to cyberware let players dress for identity.

### G. Dense world art is not enough

Night City proves visual aliveness and social simulation are separate dimensions.

## 30. Limits / do not copy automatically

### Do not promise systemic depth that only exists in scripted quests

Players notice when cinematic interactivity exceeds ambient-world interactivity.

### Do not use item rarity churn as substitute for meaningful objects

Iconics are stronger references than hundreds of disposable guns.

### Do not make lifepath labels overpromise world divergence

Backstory should materially shape state if advertised as a major identity choice.

### Do not support platforms that cannot execute the product contract

The PS4/Xbox One launch is a major cautionary case.

### Do not assume post-launch redemption erases launch trust cost

Years of recovery can work commercially, but initial harm was real.
