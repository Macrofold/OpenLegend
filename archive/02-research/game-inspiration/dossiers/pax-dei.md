# Pax Dei — persistent places, social purpose and sustainable scope

**G189 · Full research pass in progress · Evidence cutoff: November 14, 2026.**

This case studies Mainframe Industries' PC social sandbox MMO, from June 18, 2024 Early Access through the October 16, 2025 commercial release and updates available at the cutoff. Historical descriptions and current implementation must remain separate. Research informs Open Legend; it does not authorize gameplay, architecture or business-model changes.

## Initial verified findings

- SteamDB records an all-time Steam concurrency peak of **11,531 on June 19, 2024**. Steam Charts' separate sampling records 11,483. The current scale is about one thousand simultaneous Steam players; this excludes other launchers and is not a subscriber, owner or monthly-active count. [SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/app/1995520/charts/) · [Steam Charts](https://steamcharts.com/app/1995520)
- The monthly history is not a single uninterrupted collapse: Steam Charts records roughly 200 average concurrent players in September 2025, 2,429 in October and 3,211 in November after 1.0, then 746 in October 2026. These are population observations, not measured cohort retention.
- Mainframe publicly acknowledges a smaller team, constrained scope and reliance on investment plus game receipts. Its February 2026 letter identifies a gap between attractive building and meaningful uses for buildings. These admissions support a financial/design warning; they do not disclose lifetime game profit. [Developer letter](https://playpaxdei.com/en-us/news/information/future-of-pax-dei-a-letter-from-the-team)
- The official current introduction describes player settlements, crafting, markets, clans, dungeons, Grace-based Miracles and a dangerous PvP region. The FAQ says towns, quests from conventional NPC quest-givers and NPC vendors are not supplied as in a traditional MMO. Autonomous social residents are therefore a material difference from Open Legend's intended experience. [Game introduction](https://playpaxdei.com/en-us/about-the-game) · [General FAQ](https://playpaxdei.com/en-us/faq/general-questions)
- Black Soup's firsthand campaign report documents substantial paid and organic creator promotion in Early Access and a smaller 1.0 response. Marketing exposure existed; channel conversion and spending remain unpublished. [Campaign report](https://www.blacksoup.gg/showroom-/pax-dei)
