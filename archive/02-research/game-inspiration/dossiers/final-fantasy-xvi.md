## Identity and initial mechanics findings

The publisher presents a single-protagonist action RPG about Clive Rosfield, the Eikons and Valisthea's dependence on Mothercrystals. Its claim to be the series' first fully fledged action RPG is positioning, not a neutral taxonomy that erases earlier games' real-time systems. The current site separates the base game, Echoes of the Fallen and The Rising Tide. [S1](#s1)

**Eikonic feat and slotted ability are different.** Clive equips three Eikon sets, each with a characteristic action and two ability slots. Mastering an ability permits assignment outside its original Eikon's slots: retaining Garuda's pull need not mean retaining its other attacks. Identity selection and action selection are related but not identical. The complete pass will verify named actions rather than treating this launch inventory as permanent. [S2](#s2)

The official battle page explicitly distinguishes **Torgal**, who accepts attack/healing commands and can participate in timed cooperation, from **human companions**, whose availability follows the story and who fight under AI control without direct player orders. This establishes an interface boundary, not a claim about freeform cognition, private goals or learned behavior. [S8](#s8)

## Current-version and edition evidence

The official update archive changes several launch-era conclusions. July 2023 added motion-blur and camera-follow controls; September added weapon-appearance changes. April 2024 altered Ramuh's Blind Justice input and permitted dodging during it. The same release added five saved Skill Sets, custom button assignments and Quick Complete quest turn-ins. December 2023's Inner Voice exposes story-dependent character feelings, while Arcade scoring changed to reward varied techniques. These are versioned features, not evidence that launch reviewers overlooked them. The archive labels the shipped Rising Tide update **1.31**, whereas the prior announcement had called its planned free update **1.30**. PC and console version numbers are separately listed. [S7](#s7) [S10](#s10)

**Echoes of the Fallen** follows suspicious black-market crystals into the Fallen Sagespire. It adds equipment and raises the ordinary/Final Fantasy mode caps to 55/105. Access requires Origin and the Where There's a Will/Priceless sidequest chain. **The Rising Tide** adds Mysidia and the rescue of Leviathan's Dominant, raising the corresponding caps to 60/110 and adding Kairos Gate. These are purchased, late-campaign expansions, not ordinary starting regions. [S9](#s9) [S11](#s11)

A first-party **June 8, 2025 Xbox Wire article by Square Enix's Duncan Heaney** confirms release that day on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox PC. Standard and Complete editions are distinct; Complete includes both expansions. Xbox Play Anywhere supplies entitlement and save continuity within those Xbox platforms, not unrestricted license transfer among Steam, PlayStation and Xbox. No current price or wider cross-save capability is inferred. [S12](#s12)

## Five substantive written reviews read

These are full substantive written-body readings, not scores, trailers or a consensus poll. Launch PS5 and 2024 PC experiences remain distinct.

**Cullen Black — RPG Site, June 21, 2023.** Values responsive action, expressive loadouts, spectacle and a community-against-oppression narrative. Finds gear/rewards weak, first-run difficulty low and repeated stagger sequences potentially monotonous. He appreciates sidequests' character writing despite errands. His prediction about future PC performance is not measured evidence. [S2](#s2)

**Daniella Lucas — PC Gamer, September 26, 2024.** Praises Clive, Cid, camaraderie, Eikon encounters and music. Finds exploration unrewarding and side activities thin, and criticizes the comparatively limited treatment of major women. Machine-specific performance and problematic hardware metadata are not universal minimum requirements; shorthand about other games is not used as taxonomy. [S3](#s3)

**Cat Bussell — TechRadar, June 21, 2023.** Values Clive's emotional growth, found family, narrative momentum and customizable action. Notes repeated background dialogue, occasional lip-sync problems and weak representation. The declared PS5 review includes the campaign and substantial optional content. Its launch accessibility limitations are separated from later changes rather than repeated as current facts. [S4](#s4)

**Jason Rodriguez — GameSkinny, June 27, 2023.** Values precise action, transformations, music and a strong opening, but dislikes repeated base errands and weak rewards between major encounters. He wants greater companion control. This contrasts with Black's tolerance of similar tasks for their writing. A nine-part loadout is not nine freely interchangeable abilities: feats and slots differ. [S5](#s5)

**Tony Polanco — Tom's Guide, June 30, 2023.** Welcomes the darker narrative, precise action, adjustable assistance and hunts, but criticizes linear dungeons and shallow equipment/crafting. Unlike several others, he finds the soundtrack competent but not memorable. His performance discussion is launch-specific; a sentence conflating experience with the ability-point currency is not used as a rules source. [S6](#s6)

## Knowledge, production, and commercial evidence captured

The April 2023 State of Play article presents the hideaway as a preparation and return point. **Harpocrates's Thousand Tomes** expands as Clive brings back knowledge; **Vivian** organizes historical relationships and changing world affairs. These are authored knowledge interfaces given in-world owners, not freely generated historical research by autonomous NPCs. The article documents a promotional showcase; its embedded 25-minute video was not watched in this pass. [S13](#s13)

In the **November 29, 2024 localization interview**, Koji Fox distinguishes concise spoken exchanges from the much larger body of unvoiced lore text. Eve Chauviré explains the separate labor of matching French lines to English-timed lip animation, including restructuring sentences. The interview is a primary production account, not evidence that all players found the resulting dialogue natural. Only the inspected opening section is used at this checkpoint; remaining sections still need reading. [S14](#s14)

The **June 29, 2023 publisher announcement** defines its milestone as **over three million worldwide packaged shipments plus digital sales** after the June 22 PS5 launch. Its player-oriented headline is not evidence of three million observed active users. It also describes the free demo's opening-prologue save transfer and separately unlocked battle demonstration. This documents an acquisition/onboarding route, not its measured conversion rate. [S15](#s15)

## Provisional OpenLegend question

Three forms of depth remain distinct: actions the player can compose, people the player cares about, and consequences the world can sustain. Drama is not automatically autonomous character behavior, and a broad combat list is not an economy simulation. The lore interfaces suggest a separate question: who helps the player recover context, and how is information limited to the current point in a story?
