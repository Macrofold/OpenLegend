## Identity and initial mechanics findings

The publisher presents a single-protagonist action RPG about Clive Rosfield, the Eikons and Valisthea's dependence on Mothercrystals. Its claim to be the series' first fully fledged action RPG is positioning, not a neutral taxonomy that erases earlier games' real-time systems. The current site separates the base game, Echoes of the Fallen and The Rising Tide. Detailed platform and patch boundaries are being verified before completion. [S1](#s1)

A useful distinction already established in the launch gameplay account is **Eikonic feat versus slotted ability**. Clive equips three Eikon sets, each with a characteristic action and two ability slots. Mastering an ability permits it to move away from its original Eikon's slots, so retaining Garuda's pull need not require retaining Garuda's other attacks. This makes identity selection and action selection related but not identical. The complete pass will verify specific action conditions and the DLC additions rather than treating this launch inventory as permanent. [S2](#s2)

## Four substantive written reviews read so far

These are written-body readings, not scores, trailers or a consensus poll. The launch PS5 experience and 2024 PC version remain distinct.

**Cullen Black — RPG Site, June 21, 2023.** Strongly values responsive action, expressive loadout combinations, spectacle and an earnest community-against-oppression narrative. He also finds reward/gear depth weak, first-run difficulty low and repeated stagger sequences potentially monotonous across a long RPG. He enjoys the character stories conveyed by sidequests despite their repetitive errands. His prediction that future PC performance would solve technical limits is not used as a measured result. [S2](#s2)

**Daniella Lucas — PC Gamer, September 26, 2024.** Praises Clive, Cid, the ensemble's camaraderie, large Eikon encounters and soundtrack. Finds exploration unrewarding and side activities thin, while particularly criticizing the comparatively limited treatment of major women. Her lower-setting performance account is machine-specific; the published hardware box is not used as a reliable universal minimum specification. Her shorthand characterization of other Final Fantasy battle systems is also not adopted as taxonomy. [S3](#s3)

**Cat Bussell — TechRadar, June 21, 2023.** Finds Clive's emotional growth, found family, narrative momentum and customizable action deeply effective. Notes repetitive background dialogue, occasional lip-sync problems and weak representation. The declared review covers the main campaign and substantial optional content on PS5. Its accessibility discussion distinguishes Timely Accessories, visual sound indications and haptics from missing launch camera/motion options. Launch absences will be checked against later updates rather than repeated as current facts. [S4](#s4)

**Jason Rodriguez — GameSkinny, June 27, 2023.** Values precise action, impressive transformations, music and a forceful opening. He is less forgiving of the cycle of returning to base, talking to several people and collecting weak rewards between dramatic encounters, and wants more control over companions. This contrasts with Black's willingness to accept similar task structure for its character writing. The review's nine-part loadout wording is not silently equated to nine freely interchangeable abilities; feats and ability slots must stay separate. [S5](#s5)

## Provisional OpenLegend question

The evidence suggests three independent forms of depth: the actions a player can compose, the people a player comes to care about, and the consequences a world can sustain. A game can excel at one without offering the others as freeform systems. The eventual interpretation should preserve that distinction rather than calling a dramatic companion automatically autonomous or a long action list an economy simulation.
