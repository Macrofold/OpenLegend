# Grow a Garden — full research dossier

**G05 · Full research pass completed September 25, 2026.** Major systems and R01–R14 categories are covered below. This is not an exhaustive current-price database, personal playthrough or proprietary source-code audit. Version uncertainty is localized to specific claims rather than hidden.

Read with the [preserved Grow a Garden subsection](../games/roblox.md), [Roblox platform dossier](roblox.md), [requirements](../research-requirements.md) and [resume ledger](../research-progress.md). This is **The Garden Game's place 126884695634066**, not Grow a Garden 2, a modded copy or a separately hosted trading lobby. The research is inspiration, not an OpenLegend implementation plan.

## 1. A small action loop acquires several purposes

The official experience identity is confirmed by its Roblox page. Its published description, also retained in an indexed directory copy, asks players to equip a seed, plant on their plot, wait, harvest and sell, while the garden continues growing during absence. [GG01](#gg01) The basic reward is a personally improving garden rather than combat mastery or following a large authored story.

**Interpretation:** the opening action is easy to recognize, but that alone does not explain repeat play. Later value can come from a scarce plant, a useful helper, a surprising crop, another player's gift or a shared event. Each is a different reason to return. The relevant research question is what ordinary activity contributes after novelty, not merely how large record concurrency became.

The official page's changing bracketed event title is not a history of every update. This pass excludes similarly named Roblox copies and generic pages that mix sequel systems, old events and contradictory current statistics. Offline plant growth is not proof that every pet, event and shared-server process runs identically during absence.

## 2. The plant and its produce are different objects

**Strawberry** is a multi-harvest crop: the plant remains a source of repeated produce. It belongs to categories—Sweet, Leafy, Summer and Berry—that can matter to tools or pets selecting particular types. The visible fruit participates in a more structured vocabulary than one sale-price number. [GG02](#gg02)

The GameSpot guide contrasts repeat-harvest plants with single-harvest cases such as **Bamboo** and **Mushroom**, whose value can depend on retaining them long enough to acquire useful changes. [GG03](#gg03)

**Worked choice:** favor recurring harvests, reserve part of the plot for a potentially valuable one-off crop, or keep something attractive despite a lower immediate return. Planting uses space; harvesting determines which developing object remains exposed to further opportunities. More seeds do not automatically resolve a limited plot's purpose.

**Interpretation:** a seed, a living plant and a harvested fruit have different lifetimes. A catalog that flattens them into one inventory identity misses decisions about arrangement, patience, replacement and continued yield. Increasing crop size can also change the visible character of the garden, not just its accounting.

## 3. Mutations compose, but not through unlimited multiplication

Crop mutations alter visual state and value and can come from weather, pets, gear or events. The calculation separates a growth variant such as Gold or Rainbow from other mutation contributions; it is not the product of every displayed multiplier. Some combinations replace their ingredients: **Wet or Drenched plus Chilled becomes Frozen**, replacing those contributing states; **Wet/Drenched plus Sandy becomes Clay**. [GG04](#gg04)

**Worked interaction:** leave wet produce available when a chilling opportunity occurs. The result can be a frozen state rather than three independent bonuses. A player expecting every extra label to persist predicts the wrong result. The value lies partly in understanding the transformation rather than collecting names.

A July 2025 player question about applying Wet spray to Frozen produce received a warning that the spray could be consumed without adding the incompatible state. [GG05](#gg05) This is a dated account, not a replayed current experiment. **Interpretation:** a valid-looking tool can fail to achieve an intended composition. Eligibility should be understandable before resource loss looks arbitrary.

Exact tables change, and community pages retain old maximum-combination lists. This dossier uses selected relationships, not a claim that a historical maximum or probability remains current.

## 4. Weather links a shared scene to a private crop

The weather reference distinguishes ordinary conditions, limited events and administrator-triggered spectacles. Rain, frost and thunderstorms change growth or mutation opportunities; a **Lightning Rod** attracts a limited number of nearby strikes. Visual and audio changes accompany conditions instead of every effect being an invisible probability modifier. [GG06](#gg06)

**Worked choice:** prepare a plot and tool before a storm, hoping to improve selected produce, or harvest a known result instead of waiting. A weather event does not guarantee that every plant gains the desired state.

**Interpretation:** one event supplies atmosphere, information, anticipation and an economic opportunity. Realistic agriculture is unnecessary for that connection. The tradeoff is attendance pressure: a shared occasion can become a demand to keep checking the clock when unique rewards depend on presence.

The community phrase **admin abuse** describes developer-run live events in this context, not unauthorized account compromise or permission for players to obtain administrative powers.

## 5. Useful pets: individuality, eligibility and another progression layer

**Dragonfly** periodically turns eligible fruit Gold. The reference excludes produce already carrying another growth variant, including Silver and Rainbow. Timing varies with condition and modifiers, so one old cooldown is not universal. [GG07](#gg07)

**Worked counterexample:** adding Dragonfly to an ineligible garden does not create another gold layer. **Crumplezone7**, in July 2025, initially thought their pet had stopped working, then discovered that the fruit was already Gold or Rainbow, sometimes obscured by other visual layers. [GG08](#gg08)

**Interpretation:** understandable eligibility is part of useful autonomy. A helper needs to communicate why no suitable work exists, not merely disappear into a timer. More notifications are not automatically better, but silence resembling malfunction turns assistance into supervision.

### Raccoon: copying and theft are different social mechanics

The current Raccoon reference describes **Rascal** copying fruit from another plot, with a full recipient inventory preventing the action. It records a 2026 period of actual removal followed by reversion. Another community page gives a different event boundary for that reversion; the exact transition date remains unresolved here. [GG09](#gg09) [GG10](#gg10)

**Interpretation:** duplication can make a prosperous neighbor useful without depriving them; removal creates conflict over a possession. Calling both stealing hides a major experiential difference. A change in that contract deserves more attention than a small numerical adjustment.

These are bounded timed capabilities. Recognizable animals and visible movement can support affection, but do not establish open-ended planning, social beliefs or language-model intelligence.

### Mutating a pet is not mutating a crop

The pet-mutation reference describes several routes, including a machine, shards and particular pet abilities. The machine accepts an eligible aged pet, charges currency and time, then resets its age; a replacement mutation overwrites rather than stacks with the existing one. Current documentation gives a lower age threshold than the level-100 proposal reported before introduction. [GG21](#gg21) [GG17](#gg17)

**Worked choice:** accept a temporary loss of developed capability for a different future effect, or keep the dependable current helper. Repeating the process risks replacing a useful outcome. That is a commitment about a particular companion, not an unconditional upgrade button. Exact thresholds, cooldowns and premium skips must be checked against the played version.

## 6. Gear, removal and the work of arranging a garden

Gear can accelerate growth, improve pet development, alter crop states or change placement. **Watering Can** helps growth; sprinklers affect nearby plants; a **Trowel** supports rearrangement. Gear comes from shops, crafting, certain pets and events rather than one universal acquisition route. [GG11](#gg11)

**Interpretation:** proximity-based tools make layout practical. Which plants share a beneficial area can matter as much as acquiring the tool. A spatial effect is a more specific design idea than a global efficiency bonus: it lets a player construct a local arrangement and inspect its consequences.

The **Shovel** removes plants, produce and supported placed objects, with confirmation for valuable cases and protection for favorited crops. Historical changes added warnings to more categories after some formerly disappeared immediately. [GG22](#gg22)

**Worked distinction:** moving a valued source plant and destroying it to free space are different intentions. A confirmation dialog protects an irreversible decision; it does not transform a destructive tool into a relocation tool. A helpful creator interface should expose that distinction rather than infer that every request to reorganize grants permission to delete.

The GameSpot guide describes achievements in a **Guide Book** that award useful objects, and paid-in-Sheckles garden expansion, including some timed extensions. [GG03](#gg03) **Interpretation:** goals can introduce systems and provide direction, but counting repetitive actions is not proof those actions remain interesting. A larger plot increases possibility and the amount to inspect.

## 7. Cooking and event requirements can redirect what is worth growing

The original **Cooking Event**, August 2–16, 2025, converted produce into food for **Chris P.** Better-quality or larger offerings affected rewards; matching his current craving upgraded the offered food's tier. After the event, cooking remained available through a Cooking Kit. [GG23](#gg23)

**Worked choice:** sell a valuable ingredient now or reserve it for a meal whose reward fits another goal. The desired dish can make a previously neglected crop useful. But an old event reward table is not proof that the same character is accepting the same offering today.

Chris P. is a pig chef with a chef's hat and a distinct physical design. [GG24](#gg24) **Interpretation:** a compact authored character makes an exchange recognizable. The player need not read a long quest introduction to understand a cooking request. That does not establish a relationship simulation; the personality may primarily frame a repeatable transaction.

A **Spaghetti Sloth**, introduced with the cooking content, supplies Pasta, Sauce or Meatball states that can combine into Spaghetti. [GG25](#gg25) This is an unusually concrete link among a themed helper, visible fantasy and compositional rules. Its whimsical name earns meaning through an actual transformation rather than being another label on the same damage statistic.

**Interpretation:** changing requested inputs can renew a garden's purpose. Repeating an identical donation-and-random-reward structure with new names can also expose the underlying repetition. The player feedback below distinguishes the pleasure of a shared event from the quality of the task it repeatedly asks people to perform.

## 8. Several transfer systems, several kinds of trust

### Gifts and paid removal

Pet gifting returned in July 2025 after a duplication exploit prompted a backend rewrite, according to Jandel's statement reported by PC Gamer. The same account presented trading as forthcoming; later documentation establishes its actual introduction. [GG17](#gg17)

**Take Player's Fruit** is a repeatable paid product that removes selected produce from another garden. It is not the same as a voluntary gift or the presently described Raccoon copy. [GG10](#gg10) **Interpretation:** generosity, exchange and imposed loss have different emotional costs. A friendly farming premise does not make every permitted interaction feel consensual.

### Bilateral exchange

A **Trading Ticket** opens an exchange with information about offered items, a delay before confirmation, and a reset when the offer changes. Rejecting or ignoring a request does not consume the corresponding ticket. The current reference says tickets are consistently stocked, unlike the older complaints. [GG12](#gg12)

**Worked choice:** inspect the actual offered pet and its properties rather than accept a verbal promise of a later gift. Confirmation helps protect the immediate transaction, but it does not prove that the price is fair, that every external promise will be kept or that an account is trustworthy. A displayed value is not an objective measure of what a particular collector should want.

### A market is not merely more bilateral trading

The **Farmers Market/Trade World**, introduced November 29, 2025, is a separate linked place. Players claim booths and list supported goods for **Trade Tokens**; an index helps find sellers and inspect price history. **Portal Pete** and **Portal Paul** frame travel between the two places. [GG14](#gg14)

**Interpretation:** searching a catalog, displaying stock and waiting for a buyer support specialization and discovery. They can also weaken the long pursuit of a desired pet by making it immediately obtainable. That may help one player and remove another's main reason to return. Improved access does not guarantee improved long-term purpose.

Community pages disagree on some current trade eligibility and currency details. This dossier deliberately does not turn their conflicting account-age thresholds or old tax rules into a universal current instruction.

## 9. Late progression, currencies and the value of abundance

**Sheckles** fund ordinary growth. **Garden Coins**, obtained through expensive Garden Ascension, buy capacity and fence-related rewards; their earlier use for Tier 2 seeds was later replaced. Costs rise across ascensions. [GG15](#gg15) The GameSpot guide connects those coins to additional save, egg and inventory capacity. [GG03](#gg03)

**Interpretation:** converting a large surplus into a new kind of capacity gives continued production another purpose. It is different from deleting the entire garden and all pets for a fresh start. Exact old ascension requirements and timers are version-specific; neither an old guide nor an abandoned quest should be treated as the current complete rule.

**Trade Tokens** are yet another currency, obtained through the market and purchasable with Robux. [GG26](#gg26) **Interpretation:** these currencies mediate different relationships: producing a crop, expanding one's capacity and exchanging with other players. Combining them in one comparison without naming the conversion and constraints would misstate the economy.

The monetization described in the sources includes direct purchases of seeds/gear, acceleration and transfer-related products; the platform separately supplies Robux infrastructure. A free-entry experience can still sell advantages or relief from waiting. This is a description of the model, not a recommendation to spend, a claim of universal unfairness, or an inference of game profit from platform activity.

## 10. Art, sound and the story people supply

AP's 2025 reporting describes simple block-like presentation and classical music including **Mozart's Rondo Alla Turca**. Its interviewees distinguish motivations: **Leah Ashe** enjoys rare-item completion and friends alerting one another to seed stock; **Betsy Bozdech** describes her son's appreciation of an activity without one compulsory overarching goal. [GG18](#gg18)

**Interpretation:** restrained scenery and familiar objects make small changes easy to notice. Mutation colors, unusually large fruit, themed pets and event weather can then supply spectacle without requiring a photorealistic world. The Dragonfly account also reveals the cost: overlapping visual states can hide the very information a player needs.

The garden is a record of choices, gifts and opportunities, not a conventional branching campaign. Sam's seed shop, Orson's ascension role, Chris P.'s food requests and the portal brothers supply a light authored frame for services. [GG03](#gg03) [GG14](#gg14) [GG24](#gg24) **Interpretation:** the more personal narrative may be that a stranger helped a new player, a long-awaited pet finally arrived or a valued crop was taken. The systems provide the event; people supply much of its meaning.

A player, **Idesignrealducks**, describes meeting friends and enjoying low-pressure evening conversation rather than focusing on rare stock or admin events. [GG20](#gg20) This is evidence of one social use, not verified demographics or proof that every visitor finds a welcoming group. Native game activity, platform communication and external voice calls should remain distinguishable.

## 11. Production and promotion: the original prototype was not the whole effort

In Dean Takahashi's September 2025 interview, **Janzen “Jandel” Madsen** describes partnering with the original creator, **BMWLux**, when the experience was still small. Splitting Point and Do Big participated in its expansion. Madsen brought experience from dozens of earlier projects and an established weekly-update practice; the original creator remained involved. He specifically emphasized offline growth and recognizable gardening. [GG16](#gg16)

**Interpretation:** this is not an account of an inexperienced person producing and supporting the entire final service in a few days. The prototype, the recognition of a promising activity, repeated content production and operation at scale are different contributions. An entertaining origin story should not erase the accumulated tools and collaborators.

The same interview reports a **22.3-million concurrent peak** and roughly **30 billion cumulative plays** at that historical moment. These are attributed creator figures, not current users, quarterly unique visitors or audited title revenue. Its uncertain elapsed-time arithmetic is not repeated. [GG16](#gg16)

### Feedback changed the production process

In July 2025, Jandel responded to criticism that recent events felt rushed by creating **two event teams**, giving each more than a single week to develop an event. Kara Phillips's report also describes the pet-gifting backend work and her own enjoyment of dinosaurs in the Prehistoric update. [GG17](#gg17)

**Interpretation:** more frequent releases and more enjoyable releases are not the same metric. Parallel preparation can preserve a public rhythm while increasing time for each piece. The source does not prove every subsequent event improved or reveal the full internal architecture. It identifies a specific process change rather than a universal staffing formula.

### Several sharing mechanisms coexist

The creator interview, stock-notification accounts and live-event documentation support distinct pathways: a familiar premise attracts a trial; a visible prized object motivates curiosity; a friend supplies a timely reason to join; and a developer event creates a shared occasion. [GG16](#gg16) [GG18](#gg18) [GG06](#gg06)

**Interpretation:** an enormous event peak cannot establish that ordinary gardening is equally compelling to all attendees. Nor does a return between events prove uninterrupted daily retention. A coherent business needs the service and content costs to fit its actual participation, not an assumption that each future release reproduces the highest peak. No private cost, profit, channel-attribution or retention dataset was established here.

## 13. Transferable patterns and limits

**Different lifetimes create different choices:** seed, source plant and produce; grown companion and newly mutated companion; installed gear and consumed spray. Record what persists when an action completes.

**A helper's eligibility must be legible:** the Dragonfly case turns a seeming reliability failure into an explanation about available work. In an AI world, a correct refusal or no-op should not masquerade as broken autonomy.

**Copying, giving, exchanging and taking are different verbs:** Raccoon, gifts, ticketed trade and paid removal cannot share one ambiguous transfer description. Their trust and loss contracts matter more than superficial animations.

**Shared occasions need ordinary value:** weather and events can coordinate a community, but a compelling clip does not establish an enjoyable routine between appearances. A world should provide a reason to care that is not only waiting for the next scarce drop.

**Abundance is not automatically purpose:** an economy, a collection and a friendship group can each sustain play, but one does not prove the others exist. Avoid making an optimized or wealthy player relevant only by inflating the next cost.

These are inspiration lenses, not instructions to copy rare-drop timers, premium relief, visual assets or names into OpenLegend.
