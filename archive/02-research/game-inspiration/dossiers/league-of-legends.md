# League of Legends — full research dossier

**G08 · Full research pass completed September 25, 2026.** This covers the major gameplay and product systems and all R01–R14 categories. It is not an exhaustive champion/item database, personal playthrough or representative player survey. Current primary documentation, historical descriptions and bounded community references are distinguished.

[Preserved League/Dota chapter](../games/league-of-legends-and-dota-2.md) · [Requirements](../research-requirements.md) · [Progress](../research-progress.md). This is PC League of Legends, not Wild Rift, Teamfight Tactics or Dota 2. League Classic is a distinct native mode combining historical elements, not proof that every old guide describes modern Summoner's Rift. Character/lore examples are references, not proposed OpenLegend assets.

## 1. One objective connects several activities

Riot's guide defines the principal mode as two teams of five trying to destroy the opposing **Nexus**. Turrets and inhibitors protect the approach. Gold purchases items; experience improves the champion's abilities and statistics during the match. Most kits have a passive, three basic abilities and an ultimate, alongside selected Summoner Spells such as **Flash, Teleport, Smite and Ignite**. [LL01](#ll01)

**Interpretation:** eliminating a rival matters because it changes resources, access and available time, not because the whole objective is a kill counter. A player contributes by protecting an ally, revealing a route, threatening another part of the map or converting an advantage into access to the base. Reusing a map does not repeat the same decision when its participants and capabilities change.

Riot distinguishes top, jungle, mid, bot and support. Junglers fight neutral creatures and intervene in lanes; support protects and enables others. **Baron Nashor** and **Drakes** provide contested team benefits. [LL01](#ll01) A neutral monster is therefore part of human competition: attempting it commits time and position while opponents can intervene.

**Worked objective choice:** the team can fight for a monster, threaten a structure elsewhere or avoid a contest it cannot support. A useful local action is not automatically the best team action. Several people need a compatible plan, enough evidence to judge it and a way to change it when an opponent appears.

## 2. Farming, shopping and preparation have different clocks

The last hit on an enemy minion supplies its ordinary gold reward, while nearby champions can receive experience without delivering that blow. Shared experience and supported gold-sharing exceptions complicate the simple solo case. A 2019 practical guide explains this distinction without requiring its old numerical tables to be current. [LL08](#ll08)

**Worked interaction:** a player wants the killing blow on a low-health minion but must move into an opponent's threatening range. Securing the resource, avoiding damage and preserving a route are competing demands. Merely watching the gold total misses the position and timing that produced it. An ally occupying the same area can affect resource distribution even while helping defend it.

**Interpretation:** slow accumulation can make a later power threshold meaningful, but the interesting part is not clicking an identical target indefinitely. The opposing player turns an otherwise routine action into a contested opportunity. A newcomer can experience this as invisible disadvantage before understanding why the opposing equipment improved faster.

Items support offense, protection, movement and utility rather than one universally superior stat. Riot's guide places purchasing in the team's base shop; the roster and pre-match choices exist on another layer. [LL01](#ll01) **Interpretation:** money available for an item, owning its components and actually returning to acquire its completed capability are different states. Shopping has an opportunity cost when activity elsewhere continues.

### Zhonya's Hourglass: temporary safety also suspends agency

The documented Hourglass active enters stasis: the user is protected and untargetable but cannot move, attack or use ordinary abilities during it. Its recipe combines components into a completed item with that active. [LL09](#ll09)

**Worked situation:** avoid the impact of a dangerous incoming sequence, but emerge in the same threatened location unless something else changes. An ally may create protection, or enemies may wait to attack afterward. The item changes timing; it does not guarantee an escape. The gain and the temporary inability to act belong to the same effect.

**Interpretation:** an equipment upgrade can add a new decision rather than merely improve damage. Its value depends on the surrounding team, cooldowns and location. A reference library should preserve those dependencies, not only the item's strongest possible use.

### Runes prepare a behavior, not a complete outcome

The modern rune structure combines a primary path's Keystone and smaller selections with a limited secondary path. **Domination**, for example, emphasizes particular offensive and utility behaviors; it does not permit equipping every available effect at once. [LL10](#ll10) **Electrocute** rewards a short sequence of distinct qualifying actions against one target, rather than treating every repeated damage tick as another independent activation. [LL11](#ll11)

**Worked choice:** select support for the kind of engagement the champion can realistically perform. A brief burst sequence and a prolonged exchange need not value the same modification. The existence of an impressive effect does not establish that the chosen kit can meet its conditions safely.

Riot's 2022 **Rune Recommender** supplied champion/position-specific suggestions and an overwriteable temporary page, with recommendations maintained across patches. [LL12](#ll12) **Interpretation:** a usable default can remove preparatory research without preventing later specialization. It does not prove the recommended setup understands a particular player's plan, and old lists of removed runes must not be mixed into current builds.

## 3. Role quests make assignment part of progression

The January 2026 release extended role quests beyond the earlier support/jungle systems. Assignment comes from champion select and must be exchanged there when players trade positions. Appropriate activity advances the quest, with location affecting progress. Swiftplay and rotating modes were excluded from the same scheme. Top's rewards include experience/level capacity and Teleport; bot moves boots to a separate slot; support receives a dedicated place for Control Wards. [LL02](#ll02)

**Worked choice:** leave a lane to help elsewhere or continue earning local resources and role progress. Roaming is not inherently wrong; its team benefit competes with pressure on the structure, missed opportunities and delayed rewards. A role supplies an incentive, not a command that always overrides context.

**Interpretation:** an assignment can do more than label a matchmaking slot. It changes which actions yield progress and which later contributions become possible. Clear handoff matters when changing the label changes the player's practical incentives.

September's patch 26.19 adjusted top-lane Teleport to support map influence and split-pushing. Its modern, Classic and Arena changes are separate. [LL03](#ll03) These updates do not authorize combining every historical version of a champion or item into one build.

## 4. Information is a resource with spatial limits

Riot's **Faelights** put identifiable rings in selected locations. A ward placed there reveals an additional area temporarily, with location-specific shape and a countdown. The design aims to make vision more approachable without granting continuous knowledge of the entire map. [LL02](#ll02)

**Worked situation:** invest a ward before pressuring a side lane, creating an interval in which an approaching threat is easier to detect. Once that interval expires, the same aggressive position has a different information cost. The ward made a plan more plausible; it did not make it safe forever.

**Interpretation:** a visible information tool can teach a subtle system. Knowing an area is currently clear is not knowing an opponent's intention or future route. A simulated inhabitant should likewise distinguish observed location, remembered location and a predicted next action.

This is also why another player's disappearance can matter. The team must reason about possible movement rather than require a freshly generated map to create uncertainty. A helpful communication interface should transmit relevant evidence, not imply omniscience.

## 5. Thresh: assistance is an opportunity the recipient completes

Thresh's **Death Sentence** catches and pulls an enemy, with another activation moving him toward the target. **Dark Passage** throws a protective lantern an ally can select to dash toward him. **Flay** displaces enemies in a chosen direction. Soul collection supplies improvement within the match. [LL04](#ll04)

**Worked rescue:** place the lantern where an endangered ally can reach it while Thresh stands somewhere safer. The ally's action completes the escape. Community rules also identify control states that prevent selecting it. [LL05](#ll05)

**Interpretation:** the caster chooses an invitation's position; the other person decides or manages to use it. Hostile pulling and friendly travel can share a visual language while having different targets, permissions and consequences. Merely displaying a helpful object does not prove someone benefited from it.

A lantern is not reliable assistance when its intended user cannot recognize or select it. That interface problem differs from whether the shield's numerical strength is balanced. It is a useful test for any proposed agent helper: did the assistance actually make the recipient's next action possible?

## 6. Orianna: origin and delivery can belong to different actors

Orianna commands a Ball that remains at a selected location or attaches to an ally. **Command: Dissonance** creates an allied-speed/enemy-slowing field around it. **Command: Protect** supplies protection during attachment; **Command: Shockwave** pulls nearby enemies toward the Ball after a delay. [LL06](#ll06)

**Worked combination:** attach the Ball to someone who can enter the desired part of an encounter, then use its area effect there. The ally's movement helps deliver a capability originating somewhere other than Orianna's body. Moving the Ball for protection also changes where the next offensive effect can happen.

**Interpretation:** origin, delivery, allegiance and timing are separate composition points. A small kit creates cooperation through a shared positional object. Another partner can improve a plan by changing access, not only by adding damage.

The published character premise—an increasingly mechanical body and a brass companion—gives those controls an authored identity. [LL07](#ll07) It is not evidence that the Ball has an autonomous social mind or that arbitrary fictional intent determines its effects.

## 7. Control changes can preserve the decision while changing its execution

Riot's April 2026 WASD account describes adding the movement option to Ranked after prior testing. Direct movement and point-and-click pathfinding had produced different expectations around walls and off-screen travel; the team changed input handling and related behavior. Its comparative performance claims are developer measurements, not independently audited results. [LL13](#ll13)

**Interpretation:** a player still chooses where to go and when to commit, while a different input method changes how that choice is expressed. A stuck character is not necessarily a strategic mistake. The same applies to an authoring assistant: reducing manipulation friction need not mean choosing the player's destination.

A moving target, cast timing and the player's attention remain part of execution. An accessible control option cannot by itself remove the learning required to interpret the scene. Nor does preserving a difficult input prove that the input itself is the valuable challenge.

## 8. Native alternatives deliberately rearrange the rules

| Activity | Documented difference | What to study |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Summoner's Rift | Lane/jungle objectives, team progression and modern pre-match choices. [LL01](#ll01) | Coordination and longer-term resource decisions inside a match. |
| ARAM / Howling Abyss | A distinct single-lane mode with random champion assignment; account access is separately documented. [LL15](#ll15) [LL31](#ll31) | Reusing a cast without asking players to reproduce the whole map-management activity. |
| ARAM: Mayhem | Adds tiered Augments and its own progression; ordinary rune/Exhaust rules differ. [LL15](#ll15) | A familiar character altered by a new modifier rather than a new biography. |
| Arena | Eight pairs alternate preparation and small-team combat; its items, Augments, environmental plants and recovery rules differ from the Rift. [LL14](#ll14) | Concentrated partner synergy and repeated local adaptation. |
| League Classic | A Season 3-anchored selection from multiple earlier periods, with old kits, items and preparations, not one unmodified archived patch. [LL16](#ll16) | Preserving a desired pace and identity while deliberately changing historical friction. |

### Arena makes the space part of the recovery plan

The official Arena guide describes **Power Flowers** that restore health/shields and reduce cooldowns. A surviving teammate can also revive the other under the round's stated limits, while an enclosing danger forces confrontation. Preparation includes different item and Augment opportunities, not a standard Rift rune page. [LL14](#ll14)

**Worked choice:** contest a useful plant, pursue a vulnerable opponent or create time to revive a partner. A positional object can be the next objective even when there is no turret or long farming lane. The limit matters: recovery is not endlessly reusable within a round.

Mayhem's example of modifying **Annie's Q** shows another route to variation: the Augment changes an established action's behavior. Its free progression also includes gameplay options, not exclusively cosmetics. [LL15](#ll15) **Interpretation:** a shared cast can support experimentation without pretending all mode-specific modifications belong to the permanent base kit.

### Classic has its own progression and governance

The Classic track begins separately from the ordinary account and unlocks rune/mastery access and **Summoner's Journey**. The latter measures competitive progression inside its existing queue rather than creating another Ranked queue. [LL17](#ll17)

Classic Runes use Marks, Seals, Glyphs and Quintessences. Empty slots receive fallback benefits; default Masteries protect someone who did not configure a page. Previously owned legacy runes are not simply restored into the new mode. [LL18](#ll18)

**Interpretation:** continuity of theme does not require continuity of every old inconvenience or entitlement. A fresh progression track can serve returning players but also recreate a barrier they thought they had already completed. Communicating exactly what carries across is part of the return experience.

**The Council** gives eligible Classic players votes about additions and changes, while Riot retains responsibility for game health and balance. More play can increase voting power. [LL16](#ll16) **Interpretation:** community influence is a governance design with tradeoffs, not proof that the resulting decisions represent every potential player equally.

## 9. Account history is not the current champion's combat level

Riot distinguishes purchased **RP**, earned **Blue Essence** for champion acquisition and **XP** for account levels. Current support documentation places much Blue Essence acquisition in Battle Pass progression. The collection and account level persist beyond one match; they are not the gold or experience currently earned on the map. [LL19](#ll19)

**Interpretation:** owning a wider roster expands choice rather than granting every chosen champion all its late-match items immediately. Learned skill, a preferred character, a cosmetic collection and a ladder position are different kinds of persistence. Treating all as one level obscures why someone returns.

Ranked documentation distinguishes hidden **MMR**, visible tier/division and **League Points**. Wins/losses influence progression, while their interaction can produce unequal visible gains and losses. [LL32](#ll32) This describes Riot's intended matching/ranking system, not an independent guarantee of a fair-feeling match or proof of commenters' theories about deliberately forced losses.

### Entitlements can be temporary and mode-specific

The experimental **ARAM Wardrobe** grants thirty-day access to a defined pool of eligible skins in ARAM/Mayhem, not permanent ownership. The inspected help page limits it to BR, OCE and KR shards and allows the eligible pool to change as the store changes. [LL20](#ll20)

**Worked distinction:** a player can enjoy expressing a different identity this month without retaining that skin afterward or using it everywhere. A familiar purchase button does not mean every transaction has the same persistence scope. The exact access promise matters more than simply counting purchasable cosmetics.

## 10. Cooperation, conduct and group access belong to the game experience

Riot's access guide starts newcomers with Co-op versus AI and custom matches, then unlocks other activities through progression and additional conditions. Ranked requires more than creating an account. [LL31](#ll31)

**Interpretation:** a practice environment can teach controls without reproducing a human team's intentions or frustration. Entering the ordinary competitive population is another step. Solo participation exists, but a single queued player is still dependent on teammates; it is not a solo campaign with fully controllable companions.

The **Honor** system connects conduct with rewards and communication privileges. It includes post-match recognition, with restrictions intended to prevent premade friends from simply boosting each other. Its current account says absence alone does not reduce Honor. [LL33](#ll33)

**Interpretation:** social systems can preserve a reputation beyond the current game without making it a complete model of character. A helpful player can receive recognition even in a loss. That does not prove every toxic interaction is prevented or every automated judgment is correct.

The September 22 patch explicitly **postponed broader Team Voice** and supplied no new target date. [LL03](#ll03) A plan to add a channel is not a delivered solution to coordination. More communication can help agreement and also increase exposure to unwanted behavior; the product must address both rather than assume voice alone creates teamwork.

## 11. Art and sound communicate the rules as well as the character

Riot's clarity article prioritizes recognizable silhouettes, effects that match their hit areas and greater prominence for more consequential abilities. Cosmetic novelty is bounded by the need to identify a champion and its action. The article includes responding to a skin's clarity criticism rather than claiming every released example already met the ideal. [LL21](#ll21)

**Interpretation:** a generated creature or spell should not require relearning the meaning of every warning. A larger visual effect is not necessarily a better effect; it can hide the information needed to respond. The precise relationship between appearance and action matters more than a generic promise of high production quality.

### A character's sound can embody its fiction

Riot's **Bel'Veth** account describes a decade-old Void-ray concept that previously lacked a suitable kit and supporting technology. Its eventual visual design used an apparent human head/cloak over a manta-like body. Sound designer **Brandon Reader** transformed sounds associated with Kai'Sa, Vel'Koz and Rek'Sai to express the character consuming and repurposing others. [LL23](#ll23)

**Interpretation:** the sound is not an unrelated atmospheric layer; its production method reinforces the fiction. A memorable concept still needed a workable action identity. An impressive illustration can remain unrealized until the controls, animation and effects support what playing it should feel like.

Riot's music team describes champion themes growing from design, narrative and art together. **Milio's** theme drew on family/cultural references and collaboration with percussionist **Alex Acuña** and woodwind player **Pedro Eustache**. The theme is a musical expression of identity, not proof that it plays throughout every match using that character. [LL22](#ll22)

**Interpretation:** recognizable music can deepen attachment outside immediate combat. Coherence across disciplines can matter without every layer having an explicit numerical gameplay effect. Removing everything not strictly necessary to win would remove part of the reason people care who they are playing.

## 12. Runeterra's authored world and the match are different kinds of story

Published character identities supply places, motives and relationships. Orianna's mechanical transformation and Bel'Veth's consuming Void identity give their abilities a context beyond a spell table. [LL07](#ll07) [LL23](#ll23)

**Interpretation:** a player can care about that context while the actual match follows competitive rather than biography-driven logic. A team composition is not evidence that every fictional alliance or defeat permanently changes the wider story. Narrative scope and simulation authority should remain explicit.

The January 2026 Demacia update combines petricite-styled structures and gold-winged accents with a separate **Demacia Rising** metagame about growing the kingdom. It also describes longer-term client/Rift work as plans, not already delivered features. [LL24](#ll24)

**Worked interpretation:** a themed season can offer several entry points into the same identity—visual changes on a familiar map, a separate activity and character material—without turning the normal match into a persistent kingdom-management campaign. A person attracted by the theme still needs to understand the rules of the chosen activity.

Human events form another narrative layer: a rescue, a coordinated setup, a mistaken commitment or a reversal after an apparent loss. These stories are meaningful because players participated in the causal decisions. A short action can be retellable without requiring a new generated plot for each match.

## 13. Production: a service and a cast evolved together

Riot's historical founder account places **Brandon Beck and Marc Merrill's** company origins in 2006 and describes their shared ambition for League and ongoing game support. It is not a current staffing or financial disclosure. [LL26](#ll26)

A July 14, 2009 Riot announcement established the free-entry and optional-currency model before release. Its then-projected September date is superseded by the actual October 27 launch record. That record describes free download, optional digital/retail Collector's Packs, a limited all-champion launch period and future season/community features. Those plans are not all claimed to have shipped on day one. [LL25](#ll25) [LL27](#ll27)

**Interpretation:** accessibility of purchase, availability of characters and a complete first-play experience are different production problems. Free entry lowers one barrier; it does not eliminate the cost of learning a large cast or coordinating with strangers. A service promise also commits the developer to maintenance and change after the initial product is usable.

The later primary accounts make specific production work visible: revising movement/pathfinding for another input style, connecting art and sound to character identity, updating recommendation defaults and separating an older-feeling mode from the modern game. [LL13](#ll13) [LL22](#ll22) [LL23](#ll23) [LL12](#ll12) [LL16](#ll16)

**Interpretation:** this is accumulated craft and infrastructure, not just continuously adding new nouns. New content also creates compatibility and learning obligations. A balance adjustment can preserve a fair contest while disrupting a favorite practiced sequence; treating that change as costless would ignore what the player learned.

This research does not establish proprietary engine internals, a comparable project budget or exact current staff allocation. It therefore does not claim that the game would be cheap to reproduce because the camera is overhead or the main map is reused.

## 14. Distribution, spectatorship and commercial limits

The launch materials document an initially free downloadable PC service with an optional retail route. Current product and support pages retain account-based access and purchases. Music, themes, returning modes and competitive seasons supply later occasions for attention. [LL25](#ll25) [LL27](#ll27) [LL19](#ll19)

The 2019 World Championship report, reproduced by **Tom Matthiesen**, records a **21.8-million average-minute audience** and **44-million peak concurrent viewers**, attributing the figures to Riot/Nielsen. These are historical spectator measures, not current players or attributed purchases. The linked original archive redirects to the homepage, so the figures remain labeled as reported rather than newly retrieved primary data. [LL28](#ll28)

**Interpretation:** spectators can learn a recognizable situation, follow a team and then discuss or attempt a technique. A visually legible rescue or combination can travel beyond a participant's own match. This is a plausible cultural loop, not a measured conversion rate from esports viewing to game spending.

A season or the return of an older kit can also reactivate an existing group through shared memory. That audience is an accumulated asset a new game cannot assume. Neither a large viewing peak nor a free-to-play model establishes that any particular update pays for its content, service, marketing and support costs.

The earlier chapter's refusal to invent a current League active-player total remains intact. No audited title-profit series, verified player-age distribution or channel-attribution dataset was established. Dota's Steam figures cannot fill those gaps for League, and an esports audience cannot be relabeled as active accounts.

## 15. Reception: depth can be the pleasure and the obstacle

The **2018 PC Gamer re-review by Steven Strom** praises mechanical mastery while objecting to roster-access friction, the burden of keeping up with the metagame and unpleasant team behavior. Its opening Fizz-versus-Caitlyn episode illustrates how positioning, concealment, timing and a character-specific move make a small encounter memorable. The recorded 82/100 assessment is historical, not a current balance score. [LL29](#ll29)

| Direct player account | What it values | Friction or boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lunatis, May 13, 2026 | Rewards learning and finds the five roles meaningfully different. | Also objects to community behavior and uneven balance. |
| brak201, April 4, 2026 | Likes the mechanics and regular updates. | Feels trapped in lengthy matches with uncooperative teammates. |
| Moogington, May 25, 2026 | Remembers enjoying the earlier game. | Finds continued additions increase the knowledge burden without restoring enjoyment. |

These are individually inspected posts from a self-selected listing, not a representative consensus. The last account's diagnostic remarks about other players were excluded. Assertions elsewhere that matchmaking secretly engineers losses were not adopted as facts. [LL30](#ll30)

**Interpretation:** a difficult but learnable interaction differs from time spent in a match where the participant no longer sees a useful decision. More champions may expand discovery for one person and raise the return barrier for another. Team dependence can supply cooperation and also reduce control over one's evening.

Riot's documented recommendation tools, input changes, Honor rules and mode alternatives address different parts of this problem. Their existence does not prove they solved every complaint. A numerical balance fix cannot by itself supply friends or explain the next useful action to a newcomer.

## 16. Transferable patterns and limits

**An ability can invite rather than commandeer.** Thresh's lantern makes assistance contingent on the recipient's action and circumstances. A helper should produce an actually usable opportunity, not merely declare that it helped.

**Compositions need explicit origin and delivery.** Orianna's Ball separates the place an effect originates from the body casting it. This supports real cooperation and counterplay without unlimited natural-language powers.

**Information should have an expiry and a scope.** A vision interval permits a plan, not certainty forever. A persistent world should preserve the distinction between observation and prediction.

**Reduce unnecessary setup without erasing specialization.** Recommendations and alternate controls can expose the core activity sooner. They are not the same as selecting every tactic or playing the match for the user.

**One shared cast can serve different contracts.** Arena, Mayhem, Classic and modern Rift differ in preparation, pacing and persistence. Their boundaries are part of the product, not inconvenient exceptions to hide.

**Do not import the competitive contract automatically.** Ranked ladders, repeated complete match resets and dependence on unfamiliar teammates are not prerequisites for a living world. The stronger reference is a finite, legible vocabulary creating consequential interaction among particular participants.
