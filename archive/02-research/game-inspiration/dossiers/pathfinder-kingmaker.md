# G58 — Pathfinder: Kingmaker

**Status: substantive research checkpoint; incomplete R01–R14 pass.** Evidence date September 26, 2026. [Roster](../research-roster.md) · [Progress](../research-progress.md) · [Requirements](../research-requirements.md). Reference research only, not an accepted OpenLegend design.

## Scope and preservation

This is the independent Kingmaker pass, not a substitute for Wrath of the Righteous. The inspected branch had no same-named dossier or original game chapter. Existing chapters, mechanics and packet-provenance records remain untouched. Original PC, Enhanced/Enhanced Plus and console Definitive editions must be distinguished. Launch criticism does not establish current bugs.

The [current developer/publisher Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/640820/Pathfinder_Kingmaker__Enhanced_Plus_Edition/) identifies Owlcat Games as developer and Knights Peak as current publisher, while the original reviews identify Deep Silver. It describes single-player exploration and kingdom building, character customization and companion involvement, rather than native cooperative play. Do not import Wrath's mythic paths or crusade mechanics.

## Three substantive reviews read so far

### Andy Kelly — PC Gamer, October 1, 2018

[Full review](https://www.pcgamer.com/pathfinder-kingmaker-review/). Kelly appreciates familiar party controls, conversations affected by changing alignment, illustrated skill-check interludes and the opportunity to run a barony. He finds the overall fantasy presentation less distinctive than contemporary competitors and describes sharp difficulty changes. His account values the management addition without treating it as a fully fledged strategy simulation. Release-era patching and difficulty observations are dated evidence.

### Bryan Vitale — RPG Site, October 8, 2018

[Full review](https://www.rpgsite.net/review/7850-pathfinder-kingmaker-review). Vitale finds the relationship between adventuring, advisers and artisans unusually promising, with substantial companion stories. He criticizes abstract settlement feedback and long adviser commitments. His reported late-game crisis became impossible to address because the eligible advisers were unavailable, and bugs ultimately obstructed completion. The result is not simply a preference against complexity: he enjoyed the underlying adventure enough to continue despite major technical failures. A later reassessment is worth researching before treating this as the final state of the game.

### Richard Barker — TheXboxHub, September 3, 2020

[Full review](https://www.thexboxhub.com/pathfinder-kingmaker-definitive-edition-review/). Barker values character-building breadth, mechanical combinations and consequential interaction. His Xbox One experience was undermined by slowdown and crashes. He distinguishes liking the game from recommending that particular port in its then-current condition. His informal frame-rate estimate is not a measured benchmark, and the research did not test the console version. Reader comments beneath the article disagree about encountered technical trouble; their small, self-selected sample cannot establish a failure rate.

## Defined commercial evidence

The [Kickstarter record](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/owlcatgames/pathfinder-kingmaker) records **18,351 backers and $909,057 pledged against a $500,000 goal**, with a funding period of **June 6–July 11, 2017**. These are gross campaign pledges, not sales revenue, net funding or the entire development budget. A secondary encyclopedia incorrectly shortened the funding period in its prose; the primary campaign dates take precedence.
