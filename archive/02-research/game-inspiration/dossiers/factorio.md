## Verified version and production evidence

Wube's May 29, 2026 update describes 2.1 as a quality-of-life, polish and modding release rather than new planets, enemies or research chains. Its June 26 post confirms experimental availability and warns that upgraded saves/blueprints cannot be downgraded to 2.0. These dated announcements do not by themselves prove the current stable channel. The developer envisages long-term maintenance after the last major update, not immediate abandonment. [V1][V2]

The November 1, 2024 soundtrack account distinguishes in-game music from an album whose selections were remixed and whose material includes sections cut from the game. Its production retrospective attributes a smoother launch partly to testing and development practices while acknowledging hundreds of open bug reports. That is the team's assessment, not a fresh performance certification. [V3]

## Reception bodies read

Five separately credited base-game reviews have been read through their conclusions: Rick Lane (PC Gamer, August 24, 2020), Erik-André Vik Mamen (Gamer.no, August 21, 2020), Trent Cannon (Nintendo Life, October 28, 2022), Mick Fraser (God is a Geek, November 7, 2022), and Aiden Crawford (Nintendo World Report, February 28, 2023). Lane's October 24, 2024 Space Age review is additional expansion evidence, not a sixth independent author. [R1–R6]

The interesting disagreement is not whether automation exists. Lane values how new systems become understandable but is less enthusiastic about direct fighting; Mamen wants more advanced instructional missions despite enjoying repeated fresh starts. Fraser finds unstructured play less directed, while the Switch accounts identify targeting/text/pop-up friction. The latter are historical port experiences and must not become current PC or Switch 2 verdicts. Steam's filtered helpful routes failed; the bare all-review page was accessible and is still being inspected. No negative review body has yet been acquired.
