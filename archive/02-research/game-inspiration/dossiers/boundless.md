# Boundless — a place worth making, visiting and maintaining

Research cutoff: **November 14, 2026, America/New_York**; retrieval continued into November 15 UTC. Scope: the released **Boundless** shared universe, including its PC/Mac and PS4 editions, live Sovereign/Creative world services and a separately bounded PC modification ecosystem. No firsthand play, installed modifications, footage inspection or audio audition was performed for this dossier.

Boundless demonstrates that building becomes more compelling when a desired appearance creates a reason to explore, trade and visit other people's work. It also exposes a difficult dependency: players supply much of the world's useful infrastructure and social meaning, while protecting that investment requires continuing service and upkeep. These are interpretations of the evidence below, not measured explanations of retention.

Its commercial record warrants caution. Durable affection and ongoing operation coexist with a severe decline in Steam activity and a dated operator statement of operating losses. That does not establish today's total population or profitability. For OpenLegend, the strongest competitive overlap is useful making in a recognizable shared place—not merely voxel construction.

Related: [comparative essay](../essays/shared-world-sandbox-mmos-and-openlegend.md) · [research ledger](../research-progress-shared-world-sandbox-mmos-2026-11-14.md) · [research requirements](../research-requirements.md).

## Identity, current service and release boundaries

Boundless released on **September 11, 2018**; Steam currently lists **Monumental, LLC** and Windows/macOS. [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/324510/Boundless/)

The PS4 edition participates in the shared universe. PS5 access is backward compatibility with that edition, not a separately verified native version. [PlayStation](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/product/UP3887-CUSA07244_00-BOUNDLESSUS00000)

There is recent primary evidence of operation: Jon-Eric announced and confirmed completion of an **October 13, 2026 event-schedule server update**. That proves maintenance occurred, not a major expansion. An October 2025 Steam crash-fix thread received a further particle-related build on November 2; its November 17 closure is not the patch date. [Maintenance](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/maintenance-today/69528) · [Crash fixes](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/steam-build-with-crash-fix/69008)

Version discipline matters. The official landing page explicitly mixes current features and future designs. Its unimplemented Protectors/Titans and stale future-feature language cannot establish shipped encounters. **Testing 249**, announced in December 2020, describes melee weapons, shields, local universes and changed skill budgets; it is a testing announcement. A May 2026 player discussion still requests those changes. This dossier does not count them as live features. [Site](https://playboundless.com/) · [Testing 249](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/testing-249-melee-weapons-shields-and-local-universes-continued/53908) · [2026 discussion](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/increase-max-skill-set/69395)

### Four different kinds of world

| World | What persistence and access mean |
| --- | --- |
| Public survival world | Shared progression, gathering, construction and trade; players claim protected space within the larger landscape. |
| Sovereign world | Rented survival world connected to the universe. Its owner controls access/build permissions and configurable colors; it is not a Creative item source. |
| Creative world | Unlimited building resources and configurable constraints such as flight; progression/coin restrictions and blocked item export protect the survival economy. |
| Exoworld | Temporary exploration/resource destination. The release rules disallow beacons and regeneration; structures disappear with the world. Some provide rare colors or lucent materials. |

The official Sovereign/Creative FAQ documents account-level ownership, initial 30-day service and fuel renewal. Expired worlds lock, with portal/beacon fuel paused; after a minimum two-week grace period they may be deleted. Renting is therefore an ongoing hosting arrangement. Exoworlds have a different, intentionally temporary contract. [World FAQ](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/faq-sovereign-and-creative-world/50628/1) · [Exoworld release](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/release-219-exoworlds-blink-rift-and-umbris/28550)

## What players actually do

### From a foothold to a chosen occupation

The beginner path starts in the **Sanctum**, choosing a destination and establishing a beacon. Gathering plants, wood and stone supplies food, tools and a workshop. Journal objectives provide direction and rewards. A crafting table and furnace lead to workbenches and specialized machines; coal or peat powers spark generators linked to machinery. Coils support advanced production. The historical community guide establishes these relationships, not a certified optimal 2026 build order. [Beginner guide](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/comprehensive-beginner-guide-release-198/16631)

Progression combines experience, attributes, ordinary skills and mutually exclusive Epic choices. **Coin**, used in trade, differs from **Cubits**, earned through progression and optionally purchased for plots, customization and related account utilities. Specialization makes a hunting, gathering, crafting or forging configuration useful. Contemporary players describe five skill pages and additional characters as ways to cover roles; that reduces dependence on a fixed human profession without eliminating the convenience of buying another player's work. [Progression release](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/release-195-centraforge-gear-and-epic-skills/14250) · [2026 skill discussion](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/increase-max-skill-set/69395)

The important loop is not simply gaining levels: choose a project, discover its material and skill dependencies, acquire capacity or trade for it, construct something usable, then decide what the result makes possible. That is an analytical description of the interacting systems, and it can produce either satisfying mastery or exhausting prerequisite work.

### Objects, transformation and invention boundaries

The vocabulary includes terrain blocks, timber, stone, ores, gems, food, fuels and intermediate ingredients; tools such as hammers, axes, shovels, chisels, grapples and spanners; weapons and brews; storage, workstations, generators, coils, lights, signs, portal conduits and trade fixtures. Tools differentiate actions rather than merely changing damage numbers: chiseling shapes a block, grappling changes traversal, and refining or compacting prepares materials for another recipe. Resource-focused Atlases help locate deposits. [Beginner guide](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/comprehensive-beginner-guide-release-198/16631) · [Forging guide](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/guide-the-methodology-to-forging-everything-v2-3-added-repair-spanner/26188/1)

**Centraforge** makes equipment customization a probabilistic production discipline. Gums influence trait categories, compounds add boon points, and other ingredients manage costs and unwanted quirks/defects. Setting resin completes a result; deconstruction offers a recovery route. Outcomes include area tools, healing or regeneration bombs, altered grapples and automatic-door spanners. Vansten's guide supplies concrete methods and tradeoffs, but its exact recipes were not reproduced against the current client. This is expressive composition within authored traits, not unrestricted invention of executable mechanics. [Forging guide](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/guide-the-methodology-to-forging-everything-v2-3-added-repair-spanner/26188/1)

The shipped **Harvest Era** added farming conditions involving substrate, surrounding blocks, light and fluids. Goo kernels from exoworlds can produce pigments influenced by growth conditions and gleam colors; pigments become sprays for tinting blocks. Forged liquid-breaking tools collect liquids, while solidifiers transform them. Thus a visual goal can create a farming and processing project, rather than remaining a menu-only cosmetic choice. [Harvest Era](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/the-harvest-era-comes-to-boundless-farming-is-here/32265)

### Ownership, upkeep and absence

Beacon plots protect authored places, with permissions distinguishing collaboration from unrestricted editing. Fuel is the continuity obligation; the 2018 release established four-week basic fuel. This is separate from rented-world fuel and from Oort shards consumed by portals. [Fuel rules](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/release-195-centraforge-gear-and-epic-skills/14250) · [World FAQ](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/faq-sovereign-and-creative-world/50628/1)

**Release 242's reclaim system** materially qualifies older stories of losing everything. Expired beacon contents—including placed blocks, stored items and coins—can be recovered through another beacon. It does **not** preserve the building's arrangement: reconstruction is manual. Deliberate deletion and reclaim have different consequences, and compactness rules constrain problematic claim shapes. Material recovery therefore protects economic investment better than it protects authored composition or a familiar location. [Reclaim release](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/release-242-beacon-reclaims-and-compactness/44553)

### Travel, shops and collective work

Portals turn player construction into transport infrastructure. Conduits, a destination token, suitable skills and continuing fuel establish a connection; warp travel instead uses coin. Oort stone obtained from meteor activity becomes portal fuel, connecting exploration and combat to everyone else's convenient travel. Public hubs reduce navigation effort, but their continued usefulness depends on somebody sustaining them. [Portal guide](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/a-guide-to-portals-warps/45762) · [Beginner guide](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/comprehensive-beginner-guide-release-198/16631)

Shop stands sell goods; request baskets buy them. The shipped **World Shop Scanner** surfaces local-world prices, stock and directions to the physical seller. It improves discovery without turning every transaction into automatic global delivery. That preserves the chance to see a shop, neighborhood or build while obtaining an item. [Scanner release](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/release-231-world-shop-scanners/35068)

The economy also has system mechanisms. **Chrysominter** converts items to coin, so calling all money purely player-generated would be misleading. Its 2019 introduction explicitly addressed surplus materials and cash-constrained purchasing, with conversion intended as a low-value fallback. That diagnosis is historical, not evidence of today's supply and demand. [Chrysominter release](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/release-221-chrysominter/29139/1)

Guild books, ranks and permissions organize joint projects. Beacon alignment connects individual property to guild settlements; guild activity generates Endeavour, and Helix buffs consume guild resources. Settlement prestige recognizes construction and material variety, but a ranking is not the same thing as human admiration. The rules produce spaces for cooperation and competition without requiring territorial warfare as the main loop. [Guild release](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/release-211-empires-guilds-and-messaging/24546) · [Prestige revision](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/release-219-exoworlds-blink-rift-and-umbris/28550)

### Danger, creatures and narrative

Meteor events spawn waves that groups defeat for rewards including Oort stone. Creature roles include charging Wildstock, ranged Spitters, aerial Cuttletrunks and explosive Hoppers. Positioning, equipment and support matter; atmospheric hazards also constrain exploration. The introduction established group-scaled events, but its numerical balance is not treated as current. Defeat uses an experience-debt/revival structure rather than the older equipment-damage penalty. [Meteor release](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/release-184-meteorites-and-combat/10745) · [Death revision](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/release-195-centraforge-gear-and-epic-skills/14250)

The vanished **Oort** frame ancient technology and interworld travel. This provides atmosphere and a reason for extraordinary artifacts; no branching authored campaign or population of independently developing civilian NPCs was established. The durable stories in the evidence concern identifiable human builders, traders, guides and companions. Likewise, old “conquest” marketing should not be mistaken for a live full-loot territorial-PvP endgame: an October 2026 discussion still requests broader PvP possibilities and recalls social claim disputes. [World framing](https://playboundless.com/) · [Current PvP discussion](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/early-pvp-language-and-shift-to-more-cooperative-gameplay/69547)

## Four situated examples

These are constructed illustrations of documented systems, not claimed play sessions or verbatim player anecdotes.

1. **A particular facade.** A builder wants a shade that nearby materials do not provide. They compare gathering on another world, purchasing blocks and cultivating pigment. Travel reveals a stranger's unusual roof, changing the plan. The result is a chosen appearance and a reason to visit again; the next decision is whether to build more production capacity or retain a trading relationship. Color is driving action. [Farming](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/the-harvest-era-comes-to-boundless-farming-is-here/32265) · [Shopping](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/release-231-world-shop-scanners/35068)

2. **Keeping a route useful.** A shopkeeper's portal needs fuel, while fighting is not their preferred activity. They buy shards from hunters, whose group uses equipment and support to finish meteor waves. Customers can still reach the shop. The next choice is whether its traffic justifies continued maintenance; a formally optional combat activity has become an economic service for someone else. [Portals](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/a-guide-to-portals-warps/45762) · [Meteors](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/release-184-meteorites-and-combat/10745)

3. **Returning after a long absence.** A beacon has expired. Reclaim returns its contents, but the workshop's arrangement must be rebuilt. The player can relocate and use the recovered resources differently; they can also feel that the personally meaningful place has gone. The next choice is about willingness to recreate, not merely ability to afford replacement materials. [Reclaim](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/release-242-beacon-reclaims-and-compactness/44553)

4. **Turning a hobby into a public service.** A farmer starts selling pies, buys missing ingredients and expands the shop after visitors arrive. Helping a newcomer connect a portal or joining a hunt creates relationships beyond the transaction. The next goal can be a better gathering place rather than another level. A 2026 bakery thread documents this kind of interconnected activity; it supplies an existence example, not audited commercial success. [Pastry Portal Bakery](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/pastry-portal-bakery-new-shop-yes-we-have-daily-free-pies/69215)

The current captured Steam score was **73% positive from 1,615 scored reviews**, a purchaser-filtered count rather than all submitted reviews. ## Graphics, sound and interface are part of the proposition

The evidence distinguishes **rendering detail, visual direction, responsiveness and readability**. Expressive block colors and shapes can make a destination worth reaching even when geometry is simple. The more transferable lesson is that players can choose and visibly realize an appearance; that choice recruits exploration, production and exchange into the same goal. This is an inference from the mechanics and attributed reception, not proof of why the overall audience grew or declined.

James Austin's 2017 theming explanation describes acoustic strings/piano alongside synthesizers, connecting primitive activity with unexplained advanced technology. That documents design intent. The contrasting console review above shows that intention does not guarantee a satisfying delivered mix. No soundtrack audition or modern platform comparison was performed. [Theming](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/q-what-is-the-theming-of-boundless/7494)

Interface criticism is also specific: some reviewers could see desirable possibilities but could not find the needed information, materials or operations. Hidden knowledge and repeated low-level actions can obstruct expression before a player reaches the rewarding part. A small project cannot assume that attractive long-term screenshots compensate for that early experience.

For OpenLegend, this supports deliberate color, readable materials and visibly consequential work within its accepted grounded stylized direction. It does not justify adopting blocky or toy-like forms that the project has rejected. [Current art direction](../../../../art-direction/decisions.md#current-direction-at-a-glance)

## Production, discovery and economic outcome

### Production history and operational evidence

James Austin's September 2022 account traces **Oort Online** to a one-person prototype in late 2013, followed by a team and Sony/Square Enix support. He said adoption disappointed and early-pandemic finances forced feature development into maintenance. Monumental's acquisition was announced September 11 on the forum and September 12 in its release. The promised revival was an intention. This history does not show that one person produced the shipped MMO; current dedicated headcount, original budget, investment and acquisition price remain unknown. [Original-team account](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/monumental-boundless-news/61759) · [Acquisition release](https://www.gamespress.com/en-US/Monumental-Acquires-Boundless-MMO)

On **June 20, 2025**, Jon-Eric said operating Boundless lost money, connected costs to worlds simulating continuously and described optimization toward break-even. Another acquired game's work had drawn attention away. He subsequently limited his knowledge to execution, not corporate planning. This is dated developer testimony without an audited amount, and it does not establish November 2026 profitability. A later technical discussion considered world update frequency and possible side effects; no verified saving or achieved break-even result was found. [Developer statement](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/please-opensource-the-game/68160?page=8) · [Update-frequency discussion](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/world-update-frequency/68352)

## Creation and modding — R15 investigation

### Applicability and scope

**Yes, narrowly for a modest PC client-mod scene, as evidenced in November 2026.** A multi-author curated manager/collection, weather variants, later independently authored interface/particle changes, user feedback and requests spanning 2019–2025 demonstrate sustained creation and use. This is not a claim of a large current overhaul market. Built-in construction and external APIs are important but do not themselves pass the gate. No comparable PS4 file-mod installation path or large gameplay overhaul was established. [Collection](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/mod-manager-and-mod-collection/28870) · [Weather variants](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/removing-decreasing-weather/44360) · [Particle modification](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/dust-removed-from-chiseling-breaking-blocks-and-plants/64177)

### Actual capabilities and a creation-to-play workflow

Pfiffel's Python-based collection uses local assets and JSON replacements, with contributors credited; Mayumichi later supplied a GUI manager. A representative workflow is to back up files, define the original/new values and asset-relative paths, place the packaged mod in the manager's expected directory, apply it and restart the client. The GUI merges JSON but overwrites other assets. Previewing means observing the relevant client behavior and checking unintended effects. Authors document restoration through backups or Steam verification; changed game defaults and missing error handling make blind reuse unsafe. This was documented, not installed or reproduced here. [Manager and workflow](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/mod-manager-and-mod-collection/28870) · [GUI instructions](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/mod-manager-and-mod-collection/28870/33)

Representative work illustrates different goals and limits:

- **Jiivita's weather changes**, with HABABAS variants, replace weather definitions to reduce obscuration. Discussion considers retaining elemental atmosphere; removing visual interference can also remove identity or danger cues. A PS4 participant cannot follow the PC file-edit method. [Weather](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/removing-decreasing-weather/44360)
- **Huntsman's menu camera change** edits a GUI layout setting to avoid the intrusive character view; it also affects clothing/tint preview. The author describes removing mods during troubleshooting and then reapplying. [Camera modification](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/opening-menu-storages-etc-skip-the-idle-animation-taking-up-3-5th-of-the-screen/61455)
- **SWProzee1's dust reduction** replaces definitions and optional textures for chiseling/breaking effects. A March 2025 user reports a dead download link: distribution continuity is a concrete failure even before engine compatibility is considered. [Particle modification](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/dust-removed-from-chiseling-breaking-blocks-and-plants/64177)

These changes share files; merge behavior, replacement order and version drift can conflict. They do not grant authority over the MMO's economy or server simulation. No save-integrity guarantee, multiplayer anti-cheat certification or comprehensive compatibility matrix was established. Support begins with the mod author and its documented dependencies, not an assumption that the operator owns every modified-client bug.

### Official tools and companion infrastructure

A July 2025 **World Builder** discussion documents georgegroeg experimenting with biomes and building prefabs with community help. Access was described through an eligible Steam upgrade and operator assignment; this is not proof of universal public access. Proposed exoworld prefabs require operator consideration, especially where reusable structures would introduce valuable resources. [World Builder discussion](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/world-builder/68742)

Official documentation describes sandboxed **Lua** server scripting and HTTP shopping access. Restricted prototype/Creative scripting is not unrestricted production-server authority or engine source. The shopping workflow discovers world endpoints, queries buy/sell item data using an approved key, decodes results and limits concurrent requests. Neither interface was executed here. [Lua documentation](https://docs.playboundless.com/modding/server-scripting.html) · [Shopping API](https://docs.playboundless.com/modding/http-shopping.html)

Separate companion projects include **Angellus's Boundlexx** API; **nidaren's BoundlessProxyUI** continuation, with upstream permission, for collecting client/server information; and **Kanjiro77's 2026 Boundless Info** color/world finder. The latter credits existing tools and reports stale-data fixes. ProxyUI documents an authenticated upload path and a temporarily disabled upload feature after parsing trouble. These are examples of useful community maintenance, not independently audited security or guaranteed data accuracy. [Boundlexx](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/boundlexx-api-an-api-to-get-all-things-related-to-boundless/49284) · [ProxyUI](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/scanning-planets-boundlessproxyui-updated/67333) · [Boundless Info](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/a-little-colour-world-tool-i-built-for-the-community/69428)

### Rights, incentives and transfer

The game-specific web EULA permits noncommercial mods, requests distribution notification and distinguishes original work from game assets. Its old header/updated ownership wording requires caution; a 2024 reminder also points to Monumental's general terms. Available files are not blanket permission for unfair advantages or third-party IP reuse. No paid-mod market or verified creator income was established. Boundlexx's original creator declined real-money donations while welcoming in-game support; that is dated individual testimony. [EULA](https://playboundless.com/terms-and-conditions/) · [Policy reminder](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/reminder-code-of-conduct-and-terms-of-service/66815) · [Creator account](https://forum.playboundless.com/t/boundlexx-api-an-api-to-get-all-things-related-to-boundless/49284)

The transferable principle is documented, bounded extension plus attribution and maintainable delivery. Volunteer tools can solve discovery problems but also introduce outages, stale data and support burdens. OpenLegend must separately define user expression, data access and authoritative world changes under its [current policies](../../../../AGENTS.md); another game's file-edit permissions or Lua surface are not accepted OpenLegend contracts.

## What OpenLegend should learn

These are proposals to test, not claims of an already demonstrated advantage.

| A desired appearance can generate an adventure. | Tie readable materials and visual choices to exploration, making and use. Test the satisfaction of the result within the accepted art style. [Art direction](../../../../art-direction/decisions.md#current-direction-at-a-glance) |

Boundless already provides ordinary work, expressive construction, specialization, travel, trade and public history. “Build a life in a shared world” therefore does not distinguish OpenLegend. The prospective distinction is whether particular independent people and usable invention combine into understandable consequences: someone changes their routine because of what the player made, remembers its meaning and responds differently later. It must earn preference against human-authored worlds, rather than merely displaying more inhabitants.
