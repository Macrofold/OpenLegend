## Scope and player promise

Warhorse's original game launched on 13 February 2018. Its native campaign is single-player. Henry is an authored protagonist, the son of a blacksmith, whose initial inadequacy makes learning part of the fiction. The core promise is a historically framed, first-person role-playing journey through rural Bohemia: earn competence, access, money, equipment, and trust through activities and relationships. The game is neither a kingdom-conquest strategy game nor a general household or settlement simulator. [Original Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/379430/Kingdom_Come_Deliverance/), [publisher overview](https://www.deepsilver.com/games/kingdom-come-deliverance).

Original-game content must be separated from Kingdom Come: Deliverance II. In particular, maintenance and alchemy here do not establish a general blacksmith weapon-forging system. Building is confined to the authored Pribyslavitz reconstruction in From the Ashes. Current console editions also differ from launch builds: the 2026 PS5/Series patch is designated 1.9.7 and describes up to 60 fps and cross-generation save transfer; that does not identify the PC build. [Maintenance guide](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/how-to-repair-weapon-or-a-piece-of-armor/z6aa2b), [reconstruction guide](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/reconstruction-of-pribyslawitz/z9b1cc), [official console patch](https://www.deepsilver.com/games/kingdom-come-deliverance/kingdom-come-deliverance-patch-197-playstation-5-xbox-series-x-s).

## Action and system inventory

| System | What the player does, what constrains it, and what it changes |
|---|---|
| Melee combat | Choose slash or stab, change attack zone, block, clinch, manage distance and stamina, switch targets, or disengage. Ordinary blocking covers frontal strikes; the original codex does **not** require matching the opponent's attack direction. Stamina pays for action and helps absorb punishment; exhaustion exposes health. Damage, armor and bleeding matter beyond the immediate exchange. Blunt weapons suit heavily armored opponents better than indiscriminate sword cuts. Weapons can be switched in inventory during combat; armor changes and eating are restricted. [Original basic-combat codex transcription](https://kingdom-come-deliverance.fandom.com/wiki/Codex_entry:_Basic_Combat). |
| Advanced fighting | Timed perfect blocks and earlier master strikes reward reading an attack; master strikes produce a counter, while clinches and openings can enable combinations. This combines player timing with Henry's capabilities rather than making every encounter a pure stat comparison. Guide advice about particular openings is tactical evidence, not a universal guarantee against every enemy. [Advanced combat guide](https://gameranx.com/features/id/139409/article/kingdom-come-deliverance-advanced-combat-guide-clinches-perfect-blocks-master-strikes-more/). |
| Weapons and protection | Longswords, shortswords, hunting swords, axes, maces, bows, arrows and shields occupy distinct roles. Daggers support stealth kills rather than ordinary sword fighting. Picked-up polearms have special handling and do not behave like ordinary stored inventory weapons. Equipment exposes damage types, durability, protection and appearance properties. Layering includes padded, mail and plate pieces plus clothing, head protection, gloves and footwear; jewelry and spurs also affect presentation. Gear has social and sensory costs as well as defensive value. [Weapons and armor guide](https://www.gamerguides.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/guide/introduction/useful-gameplay-tips/weapons-and-armour). |
| Archery, hunting and travel | Bow use is a practiced skill, and early aiming is difficult. Walking and horse travel make route and load decisions important; normal fast travel can be interrupted by encounters. Hunting connects weapon skill to meat, sale and provisioning. Its legality is contextual: the official 1.5 notes specifically make hunting legal after Henry becomes Master Huntsman. [Survival guide](https://gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2018/02/19/a-guide-to-surviving-kingdom-come-deliverance), [official patch archive](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=379430&enddate=1530107035&feed=steam_community_announcements). |
| Stealth and intrusion | Crouch, use concealment and distance, choose quiet unobtrusive clothes, and exploit darkness or sleeping residents. A torch solves Henry's visibility problem while revealing him. Stealth killing additionally needs the relevant perk and dagger. Nighttime is therefore a change in information and occupancy, not an unconditional invisibility switch. Lockpicking and pickpocketing are separate learned activities. [Stealth guide](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/how-to-sneak/z2a88a), [skills guide](https://www.powerpyx.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-how-to-level-up-all-stats-skills/). |
| Conversation and social access | Dialogue can use speech, charisma, intimidation or payment. Appearance and context affect the credibility of an approach: clean respectable clothing and a threatening martial appearance express different identities. Reputation is local and can differ between social groups, so fame is not one universal friendship meter. Quest allies exist, but a standing human companion party is not the normal campaign structure. [Exploration and NPC guide](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/world-exploration-npcs/z7a886). |
| Crime and justice | Theft and other offenses can prompt witnesses, guards and searches. Stolen property can be confiscated, and fines or imprisonment impose consequences. Traders have specialties; fences provide an outlet for goods ordinary merchants reject. These systems let profit, legality, reputation and access collide, but do not establish an unrestricted judicial simulation for every conceivable offense. [Exploration and NPC guide](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/world-exploration-npcs/z7a886). |
| Alchemy and literacy | Collect or buy herbs and manipulate a physical workbench: select ingredients and liquid, grind, heat, time and bottle. Recipes are initially difficult to read; literacy can be learned from the Uzhitz scribe. Knowing a procedure can still permit brewing before reading competence. Savior Schnapps connects brewing to saving, so a craft can support exploration rather than only producing sale goods. [Alchemy guide](https://gameranx.com/features/id/138821/article/kingdom-come-deliverance-how-to-learn-alchemy-brew-useful-potions-crafting-guide/). |
| Maintenance | Worn equipment loses effectiveness. Kits repair appropriate categories within Henry's competence; professionals provide a paid alternative. The grindstone handles suitable blades and asks the player to manage pressure and angle; poor technique can worsen condition. This is repair and preparation, not general manufacture from raw ore. [Maintenance guide](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/how-to-repair-weapon-or-a-piece-of-armor/z6aa2b). |
| Bodily needs and rest | Food and sleep sustain activity. Food spoils, rotten provisions can poison Henry, and overeating also has costs. Rest consumes world time, while beds and baths connect recovery, cleanliness and saving. These needs make a return to settlement useful, but ordinary play offers accessible food sources, including communal cooking pots; scarcity should not be described as uniformly severe. [Survival guide](https://gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2018/02/19/a-guide-to-surviving-kingdom-come-deliverance). |
| Gambling and optional routines | Dice wagers provide a compact press-your-luck activity: retain scoring dice, risk another roll, or bank the turn. A non-scoring roll forfeits that turn's accumulation, and acquired special dice can alter prospects. It is an economic and social diversion, not a second strategic campaign. [Dice guide](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/dice-minigame/zfaa7f). |

### Progression, money, time and failure

Captain Bernard teaches perfect blocking during Train Hard, Fight Easy. Master strikes require a separate lesson: after reporting to Rattay's bailiff, Henry can return and demonstrate sufficient fighting competence in a practice duel. Knowing the player's timing input does not itself unlock Henry's technique. This sequence is documented by the original-game community reference; its indexed substantive passage was readable, while direct page retrieval was blocked. [Bernard reference](https://kingdomcomedeliverance.wiki.gg/wiki/Bernard), [contemporary defense guide](https://www.magicgameworld.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-combo-and-defence-mechanics-guide/).

Practice develops strength, agility, vitality and speech alongside warfare, defense and weapon competencies. Noncombat disciplines include alchemy, drinking, herbalism, horsemanship, hunting, lockpicking, maintenance, pickpocketing, reading and stealth; Houndmaster belongs to the later A Woman's Lot content. Perks specialize behavior without imposing a permanent exclusive class. Under-qualified use of a demanding weapon can disappoint despite its price. The meaningful progression is therefore both numerical and practical: Henry improves, the player understands timing and procedures, and better equipment and social access expand the available plans. [Skills guide](https://www.powerpyx.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-how-to-level-up-all-stats-skills/), [character-progression guide](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/character-progression/z7a9f7).

The common currency is **Groschen**. Income includes quest/activity rewards, sales of recovered equipment, gathered herbs and hunted goods, theft/fencing, and successful wagers. Merchant funds are finite: a valuable haul may need multiple buyers. [Money guide](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/how-to-quickly-gain-money/z5a9d7). Brewing potions supplies another sale path. [Alchemy economy guide](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/alchemy/zcaa7c). Haggling can improve prices, but pressing a trader too far risks refusal and reputation; paying generously can improve the relationship. [Haggling guide](https://www.gamerguides.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/guide/introduction/useful-gameplay-tips/haggling). Money flows back into equipment and supplies, paid instruction, repairs, food, accommodation, bath services and save potions, with Pribyslavitz adding construction and running costs. These sinks connect the economy to the preparation, recovery and village systems above rather than creating a separate abstract score. [Training and purchases](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/how-to-quickly-gain-money/z5a9d7), [repair](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/how-to-repair-weapon-or-a-piece-of-armor/z6aa2b), [saving](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/why-cant-i-save-the-game/z2a9d4), [reconstruction](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/reconstruction-of-pribyslawitz/z9b1cc).

Inventory weight stops loot from being consequence-free income. Encumbrance impairs mobility; strength and appropriate perks improve carrying capacity, while a horse and owned storage support larger hauls. Selling, storing and discarding are decisions about the next journey, not simply menu cleanup. Early preparation favors useful food, repair capacity, affordable protection and a reserve for saving. Later wealth can reduce these pressures; From the Ashes adds a substantial investment sink and potentially a recurring income source. This early-to-late interpretation follows the documented systems and is not a measured balance curve. [Weight and storage guide](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/how-to-deal-with-the-weight-limit/z3a96c), [reconstruction guide](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/reconstruction-of-pribyslawitz/z9b1cc).

**Travel can change the plan — interpretation.** The [supplied commentary](#supplied-video-commentary--november-10-2026) connects mechanics already described here into an experiential pattern: a journey can acquire an optional purpose through hunting, investigation or avoiding danger. A detour changes the load, condition or time available for the original goal; returning then connects those consequences to recovery, repairs and social presentation. The value lies in understandable choices across activities, not constant encounters or a guaranteed procedural story. The narrator’s composite twenty-minute journey is not a verified original-game play session.

Ordinary saves are associated with quest checkpoints, suitable beds, bath services and consumable Savior Schnapps. Death commonly costs the progress since a usable save; it does not continue the campaign as an heir. [Saving guide](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/why-cant-i-save-the-game/z2a9d4). Save-and-Exit arrived in patch 1.3.1 on 9 March 2018, along with an indicator showing whether a bed saves; launch reviews therefore describe a different convenience baseline. [Official 1.3.1 notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=379430&enddate=1522366016&feed=steam_community_announcements). Some quests are time-sensitive, but this is not evidence that every journal entry shares a hidden deadline.

A voluntary fresh campaign restarts Henry’s journey and its saved progression; the player retains their knowledge of routes, tactics and procedures. The publisher specifically classifies old items carrying into a new game as a bug in its [platform patch notes](https://www.deepsilver.com/games/kingdom-come-deliverance/kingdom-come-deliverance-patch-notes). Hardcore expressly requires a new campaign. This is different from saving and exiting, which preserves a resumable state of the current campaign. Death returns to an available save; it is not a shared-server wipe, seasonal prestige or continuing life as an heir. The rules provide no reason to treat happily finishing the authored story as failed retention. [Official Hardcore announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=379430&enddate=1530107035&feed=steam_community_announcements) · [Save-and-Exit release notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=379430&enddate=1522366016&feed=steam_community_announcements).

### People, schedules and institutions

The publisher describes NPCs with needs, routines and reactions to crime, and appearances that change how Henry is received. These claims support a world in which waiting, arriving after closing, observing a household and returning in different clothes can matter. They do not reveal the proprietary AI architecture or prove that every actor runs a fully independent economic life. The central design effect is legible social friction: merchants, guards, clergy, nobles and refugees operate within different roles and access rules. [Official retrospective](https://www.deepsilver.com/games/kingdom-come-deliverance-ii/news/kingdom-come-deliverance-the-story-so-far).

Romance is chiefly authored interaction. Theresa's courtship uses separate meetings, conversation, a walk, tavern events and a later encounter; Stephanie's quest combines procurement for a wedding with an optional affair. These establish personal relationships and alternative quest solutions, not marriage, children and dynastic household management. A wedding wine objective, for example, can be resolved through archery success or negotiation with the winner. [Theresa walkthrough](https://www.powerpyx.com/kingdome-come-deliverance-theresa-romance-guide/), [Stephanie walkthrough](https://www.powerpyx.com/kingdome-come-deliverance-stephanie-romance-guide/).

## Historical setting, lore and story — full spoilers

The setting is Bohemia in 1403 during a struggle involving King Wenceslaus IV and his half-brother Sigismund. The creator's launch article places Henry's local disaster within this political crisis and describes research at surviving sites, including Talmberg, Pirkstein and Sasau monastery. Rivers, hills, villages and reconstructed buildings make travel a connection between ordinary rural work and elite power. A codex supplies historical and social context. These are documented historical-reconstruction intentions; a publisher's accuracy claim is not an independent audit of medieval history. [Creator's launch article](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2018/02/12/kingdom-come-deliverance-welcome-to-bohemia/).

Henry helps Martin forge a sword for Sir Radzig before Sigismund's forces destroy Skalitz and kill his parents. He escapes to Divish's Talmberg, returns to bury them, loses the sword to Runt, and survives through Theresa's intervention. Service around Rattay brings Hanush, Hans Capon and Radzig into his life. A Neuhof raid leads through bandit investigations to Pribyslavitz and Runt; counterfeit coinage then draws Henry into Sasau and the camp at Vranik. Istvan Toth emerges as the enemy, and Henry learns that Radzig is his biological father. Toth captures Talmberg with Radzig and Stephanie as hostages. A siege involving Konrad Kyeser's trebuchet ends in their release, but Toth escapes with the sword. Jobst outlines a larger political alliance; Henry and Hans depart toward Trosky. Thus the first game completes a local campaign while leaving the sword and revenge thread unresolved. [Original-game plot reference](https://kingdom-come-deliverance.fandom.com/wiki/Kingdom_Come:_Deliverance).

The important institutions are playable constraints. Noble patronage gives Henry work, protection and obligations. The Church controls confession, literacy and monastery discipline; millers connect commerce with illicit opportunities; the town and its guards regulate property and conduct. Religious belief can produce competing loyalties without establishing objectively real spellcasting. In Waldensians, following testimony and local knowledge identifies the Bauer household, and choices involve the vicar, Father Godwin and whether to persuade the family to flee. The conflict is personal conscience versus institutional coercion. [Waldensians walkthrough](https://www.rpgsite.net/feature/6850-kingdom-come-deliverance-waldensians-side-quest-guide-finding-the-waldensian-congregation-and-persuading-mistress-bauer-to-flee).

## Presentation, audio, feedback and feel

The visual approach makes a plausible lived landscape carry much of the identity: dense woodland, agricultural space, compact settlements and local architectural distinctions. Andy Kelly's launch PC review praised the setting's richness and described a reactive world while also reporting technical instability; those observations are one critic's 2018 PC evidence. They should not be relabeled current-platform performance. Clothing's protection, visibility, noise and charisma connect visual identity to practical consequences. [PC Gamer review](https://www.pcgamer.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-review/), [weapons and armor guide](https://www.gamerguides.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/guide/introduction/useful-gameplay-tips/weapons-and-armour).

Composers Jan Valta and Adam Sporka describe a score that evokes period character through modes, cadence, phrase shape and instrumental color, while remaining largely newly composed music. A six-note idea developed into the main theme. Their account distinguishes original adaptive scoring from the use of historical chant and describes implementing their own music system in the engine. This is evidence of intended musical construction, not a claim that the complete soundtrack is an untouched medieval archive. Exploration music, religious spaces and character-linked material can frame the same countryside differently. The analytical lesson is that historical coherence can come from a consistent musical vocabulary and responsive placement as well as literal quotation of old repertoire. [Composer interview, 23 December 2019](https://gamemusic.net/jan-valta-and-adam-sporka-we-decided-to-sit-down-and-implement-our-own-technology-for-adaptive-music/).

Interfaces make competence visible through equipment properties, status and the journal, while individual activities ask for different forms of attention: combat timing, a workbench procedure, dice risk or a village ledger. Hardcore's removal of guidance changes how confidently these cues can be interpreted. Analysis: the appealing friction is strongest when a visible cause explains a cost; the same friction becomes frustrating when a player cannot distinguish an intended failure from poor feedback or a defect. [Character-progression guide](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/character-progression/z7a9f7), [official Hardcore announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=379430&enddate=1530107035&feed=steam_community_announcements).

## Six concrete situations — constructed from documented rules

These are analytical reconstructions, not reported play sessions. Their results describe supported possibilities, not universal scripts for arbitrary NPCs.

1. **A plated opponent changes the loadout decision.** Henry wants to survive a duel. Repeated attacks and blocks consume stamina, and the opponent's armor reduces the usefulness of careless cutting. Choosing a suitable blunt weapon, preserving stamina and timing a defensive response can create a safer opening. A failed exchange can leave injury and repair costs that influence whether to continue or seek rest. Limit: this does not imply that every plate wearer is invulnerable to every sword or that one technique guarantees victory. [Basic-combat codex](https://kingdom-come-deliverance.fandom.com/wiki/Codex_entry:_Basic_Combat), [advanced guide](https://gameranx.com/features/id/139409/article/kingdom-come-deliverance-advanced-combat-guide-clinches-perfect-blocks-master-strikes-more/).

2. **An investigative drinking night can replace a failed conversation.** Henry needs information from Father Godwin in Mysterious Ways. The priest's refusal can lead to drinking together and helping with a sermon; a persuasive shortcut or a different investigation route can bypass that episode. If this approach fails or is declined, Henry can pursue the bailiff and Rattay records, including help from a scribe. Result: failure can redirect the investigation and reveal additional authored scenes. Next decision: spend effort repairing a social encounter or pursue documentary evidence. Limit: this is a carefully authored quest network, not proof that all failed checks generate equivalent new content. [Mysterious Ways walkthrough](https://www.powerpyx.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-mysterious-ways-main-quest-walkthrough/).

3. **Saving a prisoner requires saving a village.** During Pestilence, Henry seeks information while Merhojed's residents and captive are sick. Interviewing households establishes symptoms, and consulting Brother Nicodemus connects investigation to diagnosis and brewing. Correct treatment and distribution can save people, including the prisoner needed for testimony; delay or a wrong approach can allow deaths. Result: literacy, observation, medicine and the main investigation become interdependent. Next decision: prioritize urgent care over an unrelated excursion. Limit: the documented quest is time-sensitive; it does not establish a general autonomous epidemic engine or a deadline on every quest. [Pestilence walkthrough](https://www.powerpyx.com/kingdom-come-deliverance-pestilence-side-quest-walkthrough/).

4. **A monastery turns the timetable into terrain.** Henry enters Sasau as novice Gregor to find Pious. Prayer, meals, library copying and alchemy duties constrain when he can search or question people; circators enforce rules and forbidden spaces. Identifying Antonius as Pious opens lethal and nonlethal possibilities, including arranging an apparent death and escape. A player can exploit keys and routines, but punishment creates pressure to return to the expected role. Result: an institution becomes a temporary play structure. Next decision: preserve cover, accept punishment, or abandon the discreet approach. Limit: this is a bespoke infiltration arc; monastery employment is not an endlessly expandable vocation. [A Needle in a Haystack walkthrough](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/a-needle-in-a-haystack/z9aaa6).

5. **Earlier help lowers a later village cost.** As bailiff in From the Ashes, Henry needs construction supplies. Establishing road/bridge access and a trader enables contracts for stone, grain, charcoal and livestock. Stone is a one-time purchase while other supplies impose continuing charges. Prior help during Ginger's story can improve a charcoal deal; another supplier brings a refugee-related social obstacle. Result: the earlier adventure changes reconstruction economics. Next decision: travel for a better price, negotiate, or accept a costly convenient supply. Limit: these are predefined suppliers and contracts, not simulated caravans or a universal commodity exchange. [Supplies walkthrough](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/supplies/zbb1ce).

6. **Quiet clothes buy access at the cost of protection.** Henry intends a nighttime intrusion. Removing noisy conspicuous equipment and observing when occupants sleep can improve the approach, while carrying a lit torch compromises concealment. Getting through a lock or taking property also depends on the relevant skill and detection risk. Result: the same equipment decision affects stealth, survivability and how Henry appears in later public encounters. Next decision: flee with limited loot, risk another room, or return in different clothing. Limit: darkness and skill do not guarantee invisibility, and this example does not assign a particular invented reaction to an unnamed NPC. [Stealth guide](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/how-to-sneak/z2a88a), [character progression](https://www.gamepressure.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/character-progression/z7a9f7).

## R09 — How the particular game became deliverable

Warhorse began in 2011 around Daniel Vávra and Martin Klíma. Its historical RPG was substantially financed before crowdfunding: in the January 2014 announcement Vávra described about US$1.5 million already invested, mostly by one outside investor. The Kickstarter target would demonstrate demand and unlock continued investment; it was never presented there as the game's entire production budget. This distinction matters when studying the project as an independent-development example. [Warhorse announcement hosted by Crytek, January 23, 2014](https://www.cryengine.com/news/view/kickstarter-for-kingdom-come-deliverance).

The January 22–February 20, 2014 Kickstarter raised **£1,106,371 from 35,384 backers**, against £300,000. Its sharply identifiable promise was a realistic single-player medieval RPG, summarized by the campaign's “Dungeons & no Dragons” phrase. Pledged crowdfunding revenue is neither the cost of making the game nor a count of its later retail customers. [Original Kickstarter campaign](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/1294225970/kingdom-come-deliverance).

In a 2014 interview, Vávra said the team had spent roughly three months testing about ten engines before selecting CryEngine for the combination of large environments, lighting, tools and multiple platforms. He also described the tension between historical reconstruction and compelling composition: correcting the appearance of chickens and researching vegetation did not prevent deliberately creating an atmospheric dark wood. That is attributed design intent during development, not proof that every shipped detail is historically exact. [Digital Digging interview, May 1, 2014](https://www.digitaldigging.net/kingdom-come-deliverance-dan-vavra-interview-video/).

By December 2015, PR manager Tobias Stolz-Zwilling described growth from a roughly 25-person prototype team to 90 staff. The backer alpha exposed alchemy, combat, riding, branching conversations and bodily needs as technical showcases for feedback. Local visits and terrain reference supported the setting. This interview still targeted summer 2016; that was a plan, not the release date. [Gamesplanet developer interview, December 7, 2015](https://uk.gamesplanet.com/community/kingdom-come-deliverance-steam-key--3316-1/news_updates/2185-kingdom-come-deliverance-developer-interview).

The cinematic pipeline grew with the production. Cinematic director Martin Klekner described learning CryEngine techniques from *Ryse* while adopting a less spectacular visual treatment. Vávra directed character performance, Klekner handled staging/technical direction, and John Comer guided English speech. Motion capture, actor performance, animation assembly and camera placement were separate work; convincing historical drama was not generated merely by choosing an engine. [Original PCGamesN interview, April 11, 2017](https://www.pcgamesn.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/making-of-kingdom-come-deliverance-cutscenes).

**NPC detail had explicit production controls.** In an October 5, 2016 development Q&A, senior AI programmer Tomáš Plch described two layers of control over NPC detail: script-level rules can simplify behavior, while execution-level rules can skip animation, apply action effects and move distant characters more coarsely. Abilities need not disappear when their detailed execution is unnecessary. This is a pre-release developer account, not a current benchmark or proof of unrestricted cognition. It supports the transcript’s broad near/far distinction without validating its illustrative carpet-sweeping explanation. [Warhorse Weekly Torch, relevant Q&A read](https://forum.kingdomcomerpg.com/t/warhorse-studios-weekly-torch/29593/11).

## R10 — How the promise reached people

The campaign sold a recognizable experience: become a vulnerable person in a particular historical place, using physical skills and social position to advance. The 2017 Rick Lagnese interview gives a concrete route from interest to community work: an IGN clip attracted him, an E3 fan event brought him into contact with Warhorse, and subsequent GDC presentation led to his US community role. He describes direct fan feedback and the difficulty of respecting enthusiasm without promising every requested feature. This is one attributable account of community formation, not a representative conversion funnel. [MonsterVine interview, July 17, 2017](https://monstervine.com/2017/07/historical-accuracy-and-kingdom-come-deliverance-interviewing-rick-lagnese/).

Former marketing lead Jiří Rýdl's 2023 retrospective adds sustained work behind that visibility: advance journalist demonstrations, repeated E3/Gamescom and other event appearances, backer builds, developer communication, physical rewards and distribution costs. He attributes tolerance of the troubled launch partly to community trust. He also recalls refund concerns and expensive reward obligations. Treat his causal explanation as a participant's retrospective judgment; no channel attribution experiment or advertising return figures were disclosed. [VisionGame's published Czech interview transcript, July 28, 2023](https://visiongame.cz/fpi-jiri-rydl-2-2-i-warhorse-kingdom-come-deliverance-kickstarter-indie-marketing).

Later campaigns gave people new reasons to encounter the same game: complete-edition bundles and developer livestreams in 2019, a portable edition in 2024, and modern-console updates in 2026. The April 20–26, 2026 Steam Medieval Fest promotion connected purchases to preservation of the real Pirkštejn Castle, pledging US$1 per base-game sale. This is a specific example of a game's setting supporting an ongoing cultural relationship. The announcement does not prove that the fundraiser increased sales, and the donation must not be multiplied by lifetime unit sales. [Deep Silver's Pirkštejn announcement](https://www.deepsilver.com/au/games/kingdom-come-deliverance-ii/news/preserving-pirkstejn-castle-bringing-history-full-circle).

**Interpretation for OpenLegend:** a distinctive promise becomes easier to communicate when a small scene makes it visible. Training for a duel, trying to appear respectable after a disreputable evening, or returning to a place with a shared history can each explain the experience better than a list of simulation subsystems. That is a creative hypothesis, not measured attribution for this game's sales. Production disclosure works when it demonstrates the promised experience and clarifies what is actually ready.

## R15 — Popular PC modding ecosystem: yes, with platform and version limits

The original game's Nexus catalog showed **about 1.7k mods and 30 collections** at inspection. These are catalog counts, not active authors or players. Sustained use is additionally supported by independent community curation and projects maintained years after launch. [Nexus game catalog](https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance) · [PC Gamer's original-game mod survey](https://www.pcgamer.com/best-kingdom-come-deliverance-mods/). ### What the game provides, what creators supply

| Layer | Available capability | Work still owned by the creator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Game data and scene authoring | Warhorse's free 2019 package supplies an extended CryEngine Sandbox, raw level data, a database dump, animation resources, Skald and supporting tools. | Design, assets with appropriate rights, dialogue, behavior, testing and a coherent playable result. The database page's indexed documentation text was readable, while its direct page body rendered only a script shell; no installation was performed.

### A concrete creation-to-play path

**Constructed workflow from the original-game author's tutorial; not personally executed:** make a small combat-rule variant rather than attempting a total conversion first.

1. Inspect the original game's table definitions and select the particular combat parameter to change.
2. Create a named mod directory, manifest and `Data` folder. Record creator, revision and supported game version.
3. Make a mod-specific table patch containing the changed rows; keep the internal table paths expected by the game.
4. Package that structure as a PAK and place it beneath the mod's `Data` directory. Install with a known load order.
5. **Proposed verification:** compare a duel with and without the variant; exercise both the affected move and an unaffected move, and repeat in Hardcore if the author claims support. The original tutorial specifically warns that Hardcore overrides may require their own change.
6. Package the mod folder with installation, conflicts and removal notes; publish a versioned download. On a game update, inspect the relevant definitions and repeat that behavioral check before claiming compatibility.

The author documents last-loaded precedence and a worked master-strike change. The verification/publishing plan above is this study's proposed completion of that workflow. [EkErilaz's original-game modding instructions, March 8, 2020](https://forums.nexusmods.com/topic/8461008-link-to-moddingediting-instructions/).

An expert script project has more machinery. The public *Cheat* repository documents editing its Lua startup version, building, refreshing generated command/help documentation, creating release archives and uploading the two variants. Its user instructions explain checking the game's console for the loaded version. This separates source authoring, release packaging and player installation instead of treating a loose script as the delivered mod. [Maintainer repository](https://github.com/pryans/kcd-cheat) · [author's installation description](https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance/mods/106).

### Composition, rights, incentives and failure

Individual-row table changes were an explicit aim of official patch 1.9.3; it also added mod Lua loading while blocking unsafe Lua functions. This is a stronger composition approach than every small tweak replacing a whole shared table, but two changes to the same rule can still disagree. [Official announcement excerpts, October 29, 2019](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=379430&enddate=1575042722&feed=steam_community_announcements). Manifest version acceptance is not a proof of semantic compatibility. Save integrity, removal and platform support must be checked per creation; no campaign/save safety test was performed here.

Nexus supplies discovery, comments, author pages and collections; Vortex and manual installation are both used. Unlimited Saving's author documents manual or Vortex installation, deletion of its folder for removal, and directs support questions to maintained posts. Its specific permissions allow redistribution with credit but require permission for modification and prohibit certain uses. The official tool package has different restrictions, including prohibitions on reuploading and selling its assets. Neither a free download nor source visibility grants blanket rights to redistribute the game, sell third-party content or import it into OpenLegend. [Saving mod permissions/support](https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance/mods/1?tab=description) · [official tool permissions](https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance/mods/864?BH=0).

The creators' stated goals supply two concrete incentives: alter friction to suit one's preferred play, and deepen a personally valued interpretation of realism. Public credits, support exchanges and open development demonstrate collaboration; donation availability does not establish income or profitability. The sampled sources do not establish a unified paid-mod market or quantify mod-driven retention.

**Transfer hypothesis:** useful authoring starts with a small, inspectable change and a complete playable test. Names, dependencies, rights, ownership and support must remain visible when packages are composed. OpenLegend can learn from that author experience while keeping its own world-owned rules, trusted execution and current development compatibility policy. External mods do not alter OpenLegend’s [trusted execution boundary](../../../../AGENTS.md#boundaries) or [current development compatibility policy](../../../../AGENTS.md#development-save-policy).

### Why return, why stop, and what the disagreement means

**The positive experience is more specific than realism.** The recurring attraction in these accounts is inhabiting a coherent place while becoming more capable within it. Woopzilla values deciding how to live; Orderly values changing tactics after overconfidence; x 3 n describes learning alongside Henry. Mundane details can become meaningful because they affect a chosen plan, a social interaction or a remembered place. This is a research interpretation grounded in those accounts, not a measured causal explanation of retention. [Woopzilla](https://steamcommunity.com/id/NoArmsIrene/recommended/379430/) · [Orderly](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198081477001/recommended/379430/) · [x 3 n](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197995805783/recommended/379430/).

**Preparation can deepen commitment or suppress curiosity.** AKatarzynaK likes having to think before acting; Teagan_Lawson reports avoiding exploration and combat experiments because lost time felt too costly. Modular Power Supply still rejects the same risk combination in a recent surfaced review. A slow game therefore cannot justify every interruption as atmosphere. A useful OpenLegend question is whether the player can explain a setback, learn something actionable and reach another worthwhile decision without replaying chores. This is an evaluation hypothesis, not a proposed compulsory save rule. [AKatarzynaK](https://steamcommunity.com/id/akatarzynak/recommended/379430/) · [Teagan_Lawson](https://steamcommunity.com/id/teaganlawson/recommended/379430/) · [Modular Power Supply](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198930312491/recommended/379430/).

**Returning is not proof the original friction was necessary.** Kingslayer wanted to revisit a beloved setting before its sequel; x 3 n returned after both hardware and expectations changed; Mister Snizz waited for time to restart rather than resume a forgotten campaign. These are distinct motivations. They support accessible re-entry and memorable places, while giving no license to require fifteen unpleasant hours before the first satisfying experience. [Kingslayer](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197971198862/recommended/379430/) · [x 3 n](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197995805783/recommended/379430/) · [Mister Snizz](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197972258172/recommended/379430/).

**Repetition and progression change over a long campaign.** Early weakness can make training and new equipment feel consequential; established wealth, difficult-to-use advanced techniques and prolonged endings can flatten later choices. Mr. Milkshake, ValzRon and Mister Snizz describe different versions of that problem. The transferable opportunity is useful growth with changed situations and eventual closure, not endless scarcity or a larger checklist. [Mr. Milkshake](https://steamcommunity.com/id/daddyembiid/recommended/379430/) · [ValzRon](https://steamcommunity.com/id/valzron/recommended/379430/) · [Mister Snizz](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197972258172/recommended/379430/).

For this finite authored adventure, happily finishing and moving on is a valid success. The accounts of an abrupt ending or excessive final chores concern the quality of that closure; they do not establish that a completed campaign needs an endless endgame. No sampled hours or recommendation label can distinguish satisfied completion from abandonment by itself.

## OpenLegend: useful transfers and boundaries

These are research interpretations and candidate questions, not accepted feature changes. The [playable-game priorities](../../../../docs/repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md) remain the selection authority. The opportunity is to make a particular life worth inhabiting: useful learning, readable consequences, surprising routes through a problem, people with their own purposes, and a place that changes through what happens there.

| Pattern to investigate | Why it could help OpenLegend | Existing owner and limiting question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Practice changes a wanted later activity | A novice's improvement matters when the player recognizes a new possibility in an encounter, contribution or expedition. Equipment, help and preparation can provide alternative routes. | [Sling-practice competence profile](../../../../docs/worlds/base/practical-competence.md), [DG14](../../../../docs/maintainers/needs-design.md#dg14--useful-competence-and-practice). Does the complete practice-to-use episode repay its time, including after the player understands the efficient method? |
| Ordinary objects connect situations | Clothing, food, tools and consumables can matter across preparation, social access and danger. Their value need not be a larger damage number. | [Persistent objects](../../../../docs/maintainers/persistent-objects.md), [survival delivery](../../../../docs/maintainers/first-playable-survival.md). Which state creates a meaningful decision, and which adds recurring inventory work without another choice? |
| Particular people make information and help valuable | A teacher, witness, skilled worker or reluctant ally can open a different route while retaining interests beyond the player's request. | [First-playable cooperation](../../../../docs/projects/first-playable-cooperation-feature-spec.md), [events and perception](../../../../docs/events-perception-and-reactions.md). Can the player understand what this person knows, why they cooperate or refuse, and what actually changed? |
| One place accumulates meaning through use | Home improvement is more compelling when a building supports something wanted, a contribution is recognizable, and revisiting reveals a real consequence. Beauty and personal expression can also suffice. | [Editable shelters](../../../../docs/projects/editable-shelters-feature-spec.md), [DG13](../../../../docs/maintainers/needs-design.md#dg13--editable-shelter-and-useful-places). Keep authored building menus, physical construction and institutional management distinct; adding one does not require all three. |
| Demanding action preserves an accessible ordinary route | The reviews disagree about how much embodied friction is rewarding. Choice, readable feedback and optional mastery deserve separate treatment from bugs and unreliable input. | [Action execution and skill expression](../../../../docs/projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-feature-spec.md), [player-learning journeys](../../../../docs/projects/first-playable-playtest-plan.md). Would someone voluntarily repeat this execution after novelty and uncertainty fade? |

**Let a journey acquire another purpose.** Through the existing [survival](../../../../docs/maintainers/first-playable-survival.md) and [playtest](../../../../docs/projects/first-playable-playtest-plan.md) owners, ask whether ordinary travel offers an optional reason to reconsider a plan without padding distance or forcing detours. This is a research question, not a new encounter-system requirement.

**Do not turn historical particularity into universal engine law.** Henry's authored identity, medieval social institutions, a specific religious order and the game's chosen save economy serve this particular work. OpenLegend's reusable engine must also serve worlds with different bodies, institutions, laws and tones. Reuse the principle that a world feels particular through things people actually do; author its content through the existing world boundary.

**Do not mistake persistent simulation for narrative freedom.** KCD combines detailed local state with authored quests, fixed institutions and reloads. OpenLegend's independent people and continuing consequences require their own evidence. A rich schedule does not establish an inner life; an alternate quest solution does not establish that any intention can be fulfilled.

**Protect achievement and voluntary stopping.** Useful competence, a completed commission, a repaired relationship or a finished home can stay achieved. The case supports asking how future possibilities become attractive, not forcing endless maintenance or resetting success to manufacture more hours. The reception section records why some players value the journey while others reject its time costs.
