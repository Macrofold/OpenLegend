## R09 — How the particular game became deliverable

Warhorse began in 2011 around Daniel Vávra and Martin Klíma. Its historical RPG was substantially financed before crowdfunding: in the January 2014 announcement Vávra described about US$1.5 million already invested, mostly by one outside investor. The Kickstarter target would demonstrate demand and unlock continued investment; it was never presented there as the game's entire production budget. This distinction matters when studying the project as an independent-development example. [Warhorse announcement hosted by Crytek, January 23, 2014](https://www.cryengine.com/news/view/kickstarter-for-kingdom-come-deliverance).

The January 22–February 20, 2014 Kickstarter raised **£1,106,371 from 35,384 backers**, against £300,000. Its sharply identifiable promise was a realistic single-player medieval RPG, summarized by the campaign's “Dungeons & no Dragons” phrase. Pledged crowdfunding revenue is neither the cost of making the game nor a count of its later retail customers. [Original Kickstarter campaign](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/1294225970/kingdom-come-deliverance).

In a 2014 interview, Vávra said the team had spent roughly three months testing about ten engines before selecting CryEngine for the combination of large environments, lighting, tools and multiple platforms. He also described the tension between historical reconstruction and compelling composition: correcting the appearance of chickens and researching vegetation did not prevent deliberately creating an atmospheric dark wood. That is attributed design intent during development, not proof that every shipped detail is historically exact. [Digital Digging interview, May 1, 2014](https://www.digitaldigging.net/kingdom-come-deliverance-dan-vavra-interview-video/).

By December 2015, PR manager Tobias Stolz-Zwilling described growth from a roughly 25-person prototype team to 90 staff. The backer alpha exposed alchemy, combat, riding, branching conversations and bodily needs as technical showcases for feedback. Local visits and terrain reference supported the setting. This interview still targeted summer 2016; that was a plan, not the release date. [Gamesplanet developer interview, December 7, 2015](https://uk.gamesplanet.com/community/kingdom-come-deliverance-steam-key--3316-1/news_updates/2185-kingdom-come-deliverance-developer-interview).

The cinematic pipeline grew with the production. Cinematic director Martin Klekner described learning CryEngine techniques from *Ryse* while adopting a less spectacular visual treatment. Vávra directed character performance, Klekner handled staging/technical direction, and John Comer guided English speech. Motion capture, actor performance, animation assembly and camera placement were separate work; convincing historical drama was not generated merely by choosing an engine. [Original PCGamesN interview, April 11, 2017](https://www.pcgamesn.com/kingdom-come-deliverance/making-of-kingdom-come-deliverance-cutscenes).

## R10 — How the promise reached people

The campaign sold a recognizable experience: become a vulnerable person in a particular historical place, using physical skills and social position to advance. The 2017 Rick Lagnese interview gives a concrete route from interest to community work: an IGN clip attracted him, an E3 fan event brought him into contact with Warhorse, and subsequent GDC presentation led to his US community role. He describes direct fan feedback and the difficulty of respecting enthusiasm without promising every requested feature. This is one attributable account of community formation, not a representative conversion funnel. [MonsterVine interview, July 17, 2017](https://monstervine.com/2017/07/historical-accuracy-and-kingdom-come-deliverance-interviewing-rick-lagnese/).

Former marketing lead Jiří Rýdl's 2023 retrospective adds sustained work behind that visibility: advance journalist demonstrations, repeated E3/Gamescom and other event appearances, backer builds, developer communication, physical rewards and distribution costs. He attributes tolerance of the troubled launch partly to community trust. He also recalls refund concerns and expensive reward obligations. Treat his causal explanation as a participant's retrospective judgment; no channel attribution experiment or advertising return figures were disclosed. [VisionGame's published Czech interview transcript, July 28, 2023](https://visiongame.cz/fpi-jiri-rydl-2-2-i-warhorse-kingdom-come-deliverance-kickstarter-indie-marketing).

Later campaigns gave people new reasons to encounter the same game: complete-edition bundles and developer livestreams in 2019, a portable edition in 2024, and modern-console updates in 2026. The April 20–26, 2026 Steam Medieval Fest promotion connected purchases to preservation of the real Pirkštejn Castle, pledging US$1 per base-game sale. This is a specific example of a game's setting supporting an ongoing cultural relationship. The announcement does not prove that the fundraiser increased sales, and the donation must not be multiplied by lifetime unit sales. [Deep Silver's Pirkštejn announcement](https://www.deepsilver.com/au/games/kingdom-come-deliverance-ii/news/preserving-pirkstejn-castle-bringing-history-full-circle).

**Interpretation for OpenLegend:** a distinctive promise becomes easier to communicate when a small scene makes it visible. Training for a duel, trying to appear respectable after a disreputable evening, or returning to a place with a shared history can each explain the experience better than a list of simulation subsystems. That is a creative hypothesis, not measured attribution for this game's sales. Production disclosure works when it demonstrates the promised experience and clarifies what is actually ready.

## R15 — Popular PC modding ecosystem: yes, with platform and version limits

The original game's Nexus catalog showed **about 1.7k mods and 30 collections** at inspection. These are catalog counts, not active authors or players. Sustained use is additionally supported by independent community curation and projects maintained years after launch. [Nexus game catalog](https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance) · [PC Gamer's original-game mod survey](https://www.pcgamer.com/best-kingdom-come-deliverance-mods/). This is sufficient to trigger the full R15 investigation for **PC**. The 2026 console upgrade does not establish console support for these packages.

### What the game provides, what creators supply

| Layer | Available capability | Work still owned by the creator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Game data and scene authoring | Warhorse's free 2019 package supplies an extended CryEngine Sandbox, raw level data, a database dump, animation resources, Skald and supporting tools. | Design, assets with appropriate rights, dialogue, behavior, testing and a coherent playable result. Availability of tools is not release of all proprietary engine/game source. |

The documentation warns that PostgreSQL 12 can break a setup script. |

Sources: [Warhorse's original tools page](https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance/mods/864?BH=0); [original-game database documentation](https://modding.wiki/en/kingdomcomedeliverance/developers/setting-up-database). The database page's indexed documentation text was readable, while its direct page body rendered only a script shell; no installation was performed.

### A concrete creation-to-play path

**Constructed workflow from the original-game author's tutorial; not personally executed:** make a small combat-rule variant rather than attempting a total conversion first.

1. Inspect the original game's table definitions and select the particular combat parameter to change.
2. Create a named mod directory, manifest and `Data` folder. Record creator, revision and supported game version.
3. Make a mod-specific table patch containing the changed rows; keep the internal table paths expected by the game.
4. Package that structure as a PAK and place it beneath the mod's `Data` directory. Install with a known load order.
5. **Proposed verification:** compare a duel with and without the variant; exercise both the affected move and an unaffected move, and repeat in Hardcore if the author claims support. The original tutorial specifically warns that Hardcore overrides may require their own change.
6. Package the mod folder with installation, conflicts and removal notes; publish a versioned download. On a game update, inspect the relevant definitions and repeat that behavioral check before claiming compatibility.

The author documents last-loaded precedence and a worked master-strike change. The verification/publishing plan above is this study's proposed completion of that workflow. [EkErilaz's original-game modding instructions, March 8, 2020](https://forums.nexusmods.com/topic/8461008-link-to-moddingediting-instructions/).

An expert script project has more machinery. The public *Cheat* repository documents editing its Lua startup version, building, refreshing generated command/help documentation, creating release archives and uploading the two variants. Its user instructions explain checking the game's console for the loaded version. This separates source authoring, release packaging and player installation instead of treating a loose script as the delivered mod. [Maintainer repository](https://github.com/pryans/kcd-cheat) · [author's installation description](https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance/mods/106).

### Composition, rights, incentives and failure

Individual-row table changes were an explicit aim of official patch 1.9.3; it also added mod Lua loading while blocking unsafe Lua functions. This is a stronger composition approach than every small tweak replacing a whole shared table, but two changes to the same rule can still disagree. [Official announcement excerpts, October 29, 2019](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=379430&enddate=1575042722&feed=steam_community_announcements). Manifest version acceptance is not a proof of semantic compatibility. Save integrity, removal and platform support must be checked per creation; no campaign/save safety test was performed here.

Nexus supplies discovery, comments, author pages and collections; Vortex and manual installation are both used. Unlimited Saving's author documents manual or Vortex installation, deletion of its folder for removal, and directs support questions to maintained posts. Its specific permissions allow redistribution with credit but require permission for modification and prohibit certain uses. The official tool package has different restrictions, including prohibitions on reuploading and selling its assets. Neither a free download nor source visibility grants blanket rights to redistribute the game, sell third-party content or import it into OpenLegend. [Saving mod permissions/support](https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance/mods/1?tab=description) · [official tool permissions](https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdomcomedeliverance/mods/864?BH=0).

The creators' stated goals supply two concrete incentives: alter friction to suit one's preferred play, and deepen a personally valued interpretation of realism. Public credits, support exchanges and open development demonstrate collaboration; donation availability does not establish income or profitability. The sampled sources do not establish a unified paid-mod market or quantify mod-driven retention.

**Transfer hypothesis:** useful authoring starts with a small, inspectable change and a complete playable test. Names, dependencies, rights, ownership and support must remain visible when packages are composed. OpenLegend can learn from that author experience while keeping its own world-owned rules, trusted execution and current development compatibility policy. External mods are not permission to load arbitrary generated code, and maintaining this mature game's old saves is not a reason to introduce legacy support into OpenLegend.

### Why return, why stop, and what the disagreement means

**The positive experience is more specific than realism.** The recurring attraction in these accounts is inhabiting a coherent place while becoming more capable within it. Woopzilla values deciding how to live; Orderly values changing tactics after overconfidence; x 3 n describes learning alongside Henry. Mundane details can become meaningful because they affect a chosen plan, a social interaction or a remembered place. This is a research interpretation grounded in those accounts, not a measured causal explanation of retention. [Woopzilla](https://steamcommunity.com/id/NoArmsIrene/recommended/379430/) · [Orderly](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198081477001/recommended/379430/) · [x 3 n](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197995805783/recommended/379430/).

**Preparation can deepen commitment or suppress curiosity.** AKatarzynaK likes having to think before acting; Teagan_Lawson reports avoiding exploration and combat experiments because lost time felt too costly. Modular Power Supply still rejects the same risk combination in a recent surfaced review. A slow game therefore cannot justify every interruption as atmosphere. A useful OpenLegend question is whether the player can explain a setback, learn something actionable and reach another worthwhile decision without replaying chores. This is an evaluation hypothesis, not a proposed compulsory save rule. [AKatarzynaK](https://steamcommunity.com/id/akatarzynak/recommended/379430/) · [Teagan_Lawson](https://steamcommunity.com/id/teaganlawson/recommended/379430/) · [Modular Power Supply](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198930312491/recommended/379430/).

**Returning is not proof the original friction was necessary.** Kingslayer wanted to revisit a beloved setting before its sequel; x 3 n returned after both hardware and expectations changed; Mister Snizz waited for time to restart rather than resume a forgotten campaign. These are distinct motivations. They support accessible re-entry and memorable places, while giving no license to require fifteen unpleasant hours before the first satisfying experience. [Kingslayer](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197971198862/recommended/379430/) · [x 3 n](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197995805783/recommended/379430/) · [Mister Snizz](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197972258172/recommended/379430/).

**Repetition and progression change over a long campaign.** Early weakness can make training and new equipment feel consequential; established wealth, difficult-to-use advanced techniques and prolonged endings can flatten later choices. Mr. Milkshake, ValzRon and Mister Snizz describe different versions of that problem. The transferable opportunity is useful growth with changed situations and eventual closure, not endless scarcity or a larger checklist. [Mr. Milkshake](https://steamcommunity.com/id/daddyembiid/recommended/379430/) · [ValzRon](https://steamcommunity.com/id/valzron/recommended/379430/) · [Mister Snizz](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561197972258172/recommended/379430/).

For this finite authored adventure, happily finishing and moving on is a valid success. The accounts of an abrupt ending or excessive final chores concern the quality of that closure; they do not establish that a completed campaign needs an endless endgame. No sampled hours or recommendation label can distinguish satisfied completion from abandonment by itself.

### Reception source register and limits

The six linked critic rows identify author, date, platform, play-stage evidence and score; their source bodies, not aggregate score snippets, support the summaries. PC Gamer and GameSpot were read through web retrieval; Destructoid, Game Informer, RPGFan and PowerPyx likewise exposed substantive bodies. Eurogamer, Rock Paper Shotgun and IGN candidate links failed to open and are not counted. The Guardian body was accessible but is not needed for the six-review minimum or the analysis here.

Steam hub and individual-review HTML was read directly after using the public page's “View Community Hub” content-preference action; no account or credential was used. The web retriever alone initially returned the preference notice. Each table row is independently attributable through its permalink. The sample omits meme-only and duplicate-text entries and is neither random nor exhaustive. No inference is made from helpful votes to the number or share of all players with that opinion.

Launch reviews establish what those authors experienced in 2018. They do not assess the 2024 Switch port or 2026 console upgrade. Later Steam reviews are useful longitudinal testimony but lack controlled hardware, patch and mod conditions. No claims about cohort retention, completion percentages, current defect incidence or revenue causality follow from these sources. The patch-note source is Warhorse's own dated announcement, evidence of documented changes rather than an independently reproduced fix rate.

## OpenLegend: useful transfers and boundaries

These are research interpretations and candidate questions, not accepted feature changes. The [playable-game priorities](../../../../docs/repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md) remain the selection authority. The opportunity is to make a particular life worth inhabiting: useful learning, readable consequences, surprising routes through a problem, people with their own purposes, and a place that changes through what happens there.

| Pattern to investigate | Why it could help OpenLegend | Existing owner and limiting question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Practice changes a wanted later activity | A novice's improvement matters when the player recognizes a new possibility in an encounter, contribution or expedition. Equipment, help and preparation can provide alternative routes. | [Practical competence](../../../../docs/worlds/base/practical-competence.md), [DG14](../../../../docs/maintainers/needs-design.md#dg14--useful-competence-and-practice). Does the complete practice-to-use episode repay its time, including after the player understands the efficient method? |
| Ordinary objects connect situations | Clothing, food, tools and consumables can matter across preparation, social access and danger. Their value need not be a larger damage number. | [Persistent objects](../../../../docs/maintainers/persistent-objects.md), [survival delivery](../../../../docs/maintainers/first-playable-survival.md). Which state creates a meaningful decision, and which adds recurring inventory work without another choice? |
| Particular people make information and help valuable | A teacher, witness, skilled worker or reluctant ally can open a different route while retaining interests beyond the player's request. | [First-playable cooperation](../../../../docs/projects/first-playable-cooperation-feature-spec.md), [events and perception](../../../../docs/events-perception-and-reactions.md). Can the player understand what this person knows, why they cooperate or refuse, and what actually changed? |
| One place accumulates meaning through use | Home improvement is more compelling when a building supports something wanted, a contribution is recognizable, and revisiting reveals a real consequence. Beauty and personal expression can also suffice. | [Editable shelters](../../../../docs/projects/editable-shelters-feature-spec.md), [DG13](../../../../docs/maintainers/needs-design.md#dg13--editable-shelter-and-useful-places). Keep authored building menus, physical construction and institutional management distinct; adding one does not require all three. |
| Demanding action preserves an accessible ordinary route | The reviews disagree about how much embodied friction is rewarding. Choice, readable feedback and optional mastery deserve separate treatment from bugs and unreliable input. | [Action execution and skill expression](../../../../docs/projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-feature-spec.md), [player-learning journeys](../../../../docs/projects/first-playable-playtest-plan.md). Would someone voluntarily repeat this execution after novelty and uncertainty fade? |

**Do not turn historical particularity into universal engine law.** Henry's authored identity, medieval social institutions, a specific religious order and the game's chosen save economy serve this particular work. OpenLegend's reusable engine must also serve worlds with different bodies, institutions, laws and tones. Reuse the principle that a world feels particular through things people actually do; author its content through the existing world boundary.

**Do not mistake persistent simulation for narrative freedom.** KCD combines detailed local state with authored quests, fixed institutions and reloads. OpenLegend's independent people and continuing consequences require their own evidence. A rich schedule does not establish an inner life; an alternate quest solution does not establish that any intention can be fulfilled.

**Protect achievement and voluntary stopping.** Useful competence, a completed commission, a repaired relationship or a finished home can stay achieved. The case supports asking how future possibilities become attractive, not forcing endless maintenance or resetting success to manufacture more hours. The reception section records why some players value the journey while others reject its time costs.
