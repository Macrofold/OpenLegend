## Established scope and version boundary

A franchise-wide account cannot treat The Sims 4's launch as its current state, or transfer its emotion system and lot structure backward to The Sims 3. The September 22, 2026 Sims 4 update changes autonomy, including conversation facing, bathroom-sink selection, sleep priorities and behavior around disliked activities. It also fixes particular stuck interactions while explicitly leaving other cancellation and freezing problems under investigation. This is evidence of specific maintenance, not proof that all autonomy defects are resolved. [S1]

The original game's household administration, The Sims 3's roamable neighborhood and The Sims 4's more articulated multitasking offer different relationships between control and observation. Graham Smith's Sims 3 review was originally published in PC Gamer UK in July 2009 and republished online in May 2026; it is not an independent review of a new 2026 edition. [S2]
