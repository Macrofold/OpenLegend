# The Sims — households, authored lives, and negotiated autonomy

**G24 · Completed research pass · September 26, 2026.** A comparative franchise dossier centered on the original The Sims, The Sims 3 and The Sims 4, with explicitly identified expansion examples. Research inspiration, not an accepted OpenLegend specification. No gameplay, current source-code audit, benchmark or full video viewing was performed.

[Original chapter](../games/the-sims.md) · [Preserved granular study](../mechanics/the-sims-emotional-tools-shared-homes-and-chosen-autonomy.md) · [Requirements](../research-requirements.md) · [Progress](../research-progress.md)

## 1. What the game promises, and which game is being discussed

The common activity is creating people, arranging their surroundings and directing or observing domestic life. The three games do not offer identical rules. A player may be a builder, an optimizer, a family storyteller, a provocateur or a caregiver; Will Wright's early interview explicitly describes different intentions rather than one correct way to play. His account is a historical design reflection, not evidence that every autonomous Sim independently understands a player's story. [S7]

| Scope | Distinctive structure and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| **The Sims, original desktop game (2000)** | Household needs, possessions, work and relationships form a deliberately abstract domestic simulation. Build, Buy and Live are distinct ways to intervene. Console adaptations and The Sims Online are not this rule set. [S8] |
| **The Sims 3 (2009)** | A roamable neighborhood makes leaving the household part of a continuous geography. Traits, a lifetime wish and shorter wishes support personal goals. Some destinations are visible places, others are rabbit holes. [S3] |
| **The Sims 4 (2014 onward)** | Direct body/face editing, room-level construction, more articulated multitasking and dominant emotional states; travel between lots still differs from The Sims 3's open neighborhood. [S9] |
| **The Sims Legacy Collection (January 31, 2025)** | A Windows 10/11 rerelease of the original with Livin' Large, House Party, Hot Date, Vacation, Unleashed, Superstar and Makin' Magic. It is not a remake using Sims 4's systems. [S26] |

The Sims 4's free base game is distinct from optional packs and EA Play's additional offering. Its Steam product is single-player and requires EA account/client integration. Gallery sharing and paid creator content do not make household simulation native synchronous multiplayer. A storefront subscription panel is not evidence that a subscription is mandatory for the free base game. [S25]

**Current baseline:** the September 22, 2026 update changes specific autonomy decisions, conversation facing, sink selection and sleep behavior, while acknowledging other cancellation/freezing issues still under investigation. Launch reviews remain historical evidence: missing toddlers or pools in 2014 must not be asserted as current omissions. [S1][S5]

**Interpretation:** the newcomer may ask “can I keep this household functioning?” The established player may instead ask “what sort of life should this person have?” These questions overlap, but an efficient answer to the first can remove rather than create the second's interest.

## 2. Player actions and the resources they spend

The original game's cycle connects work income to furnishings, recurring needs, friendships and opportunities. Creating a more convenient house can free time, but maintaining a larger life also creates demands. The game represents selected domestic activities, not a scientifically complete model of either households or labor. [S8]

For ordinary Sims in The Sims 4, the familiar needs are **hunger, bladder, hygiene, social, fun and energy**. Its life stages are **newborn, infant, toddler, child, teen, young adult, adult and elder**. Those are current categories, not the original game's aging structure. Build mode supports moving or duplicating whole rooms; Willow Creek, Oasis Springs and the later-added blank-lot Newcrest provide different starting canvases. [S9]

| Verb family | Concrete operation | Cost, prerequisite, feedback or failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Create and revise** | Choose appearance, clothing, personality and an aspiration; construct a household rather than only a combat avatar. | A visual identity and a behavioral trait are separate choices. A better creator does not automatically produce better autonomous decisions. [S5][S9] |
| **Direct and observe** | Select a person, queue activities, cancel, pause, accelerate or let ordinary autonomy act. | The intervention can fail to produce the intended scene when routing or cancellation interferes. Specific bugs and later fixes need version labels. [S1][S4] |
| **Care and socialize** | Eat, sleep, clean, entertain, visit, joke, flirt, argue or host. | Time and context compete. A social interaction's availability and reception depend on more than standing in the same room. [S10] |
| **Build and furnish** | Draw rooms, move them, place doors, appliances, skill objects and decoration. | Layout expresses a style and enables activities. A technically placeable object is not necessarily convenient in daily use. [S4][S9] |
| **Learn and produce** | Practice skills, read appropriate books, make food, repair, fish, perform or manufacture. | Skill, equipment, materials and time can gate the result. Production may supply money, useful objects or another recipe. [S12][S14] |
| **Pursue an aspiration** | Complete milestones, earn Satisfaction and ultimately a reward trait. | Sims 4 aspirations can be changed; the initially granted bonus trait does not simply change with every switch. These rules differ from Sims 3's lifetime-wish framing. [S11] |
| **Work or operate a business** | Earn through ordinary careers, active career tasks or selling goods and services. | Active doctor, detective and scientist workplaces require Get to Work; its retail system also requires premises, goods, customers and management. [S27] |

| **Adopt or share a creation** | Save and place a room, lot or household through the Gallery. | A lot must fit its destination. Sharing an arrangement is distinct from possessing every underlying asset or buying a Maker Pack. [S18][S19] |

**Interpretation:** time is not merely a timer. It is the opportunity cost linking a job, another person's attention, a birthday, a meal and a private ambition. A coherent life simulation can create meaningful choices with familiar verbs, but can also bury them under repeated servicing.

## 3. Character differentiation, skills, relationships and possessions

### A personality is not the same thing as the current mood

The original game's personality axes include neatness, outgoingness, activity, playfulness and niceness. Frasca's contemporary criticism stresses the interpretive space around these abstractions: the player can see a recognizable person without the computer representing every attributed motive. [S2]

Sims 3 connects traits to activities: Green Thumb supports gardening and Virtuoso supports music. Its skill journal exposes levels, accomplishments and subsidiary challenges; practice, books and classes offer different learning routes. Supernatural's alchemy and Into the Future's advanced technology are additions, not base skills simply overlooked by a newcomer. [S13]

Sims 4's moodlets carry duration and influence, contributing to a dominant emotion such as Inspired, Focused, Energized, Flirty, Playful, Tense or Sad. Emotions can unlock interactions and alter activities; objects and surroundings can contribute. The guide is mixed-age community research, not a definitive current formula for range, ties or every added emotion. [S15]

**Interpretation:** a mood matters when it changes a useful possibility or the way an action unfolds. A colored portrait alone is presentation. Conversely, making the preferred mood easy to manufacture can turn emotional life into a preparation buff. Temporary feeling, disposition, relationship and remembered event should not be treated as interchangeable design concepts.

### A functional inventory, not only a list of meters

| Family | Named examples and the decisions they support |
| --- | --- |
| **Domestic and expressive skills** | Cooking and Gourmet Cooking produce meals and special recipes; Comedy supports performance; Charisma supports social opportunities; Fishing supplies catches. Practice and appropriate books develop skills, but not every skill can be mastered by indefinitely rereading one book. [S12] |
| **Expansion-dependent crafts** | Acting belongs to Get Famous, Archaeology to Jungle Adventure, Baking to Get to Work, Flower Arranging to Seasons, and Fabrication to Eco Lifestyle. Fabrication's materials and candles are a different activity from simply buying furniture. [S12] |
| **Maintenance and manufacture** | Handiness connects broken appliances, recovered scrap, upgrade parts and woodworking. Common, plumbing, kitchen and electronic parts serve different upgrades. Reliability, cleaning and comfort improvements are not all the same property. [S14] |
| **Milestone rewards** | Aspirations produce Satisfaction and reward traits; Bodybuilder's Long Lived changes an elder's available time. Switching aspirations allows different goals without turning every previously completed milestone into an endlessly repeatable payment. [S11] |
| **Social standing and context** | Friendship and romance have separate visible tracks. Conversation tone, traits, emotions and the relationship affect available interactions. Some choices are age- or relationship-restricted. A group chat's appearance is not proof that everyone gained the same relationship change. [S10] |
| **Household goods** | Beds, showers, fridges, seating, entertainment, skill equipment, appliances and decoration provide different functions. Some also require useful space and access. Their aesthetic value need not coincide with maximum efficiency. [S4][S9] |

**Resource horizons:** money and owned household objects persist across ordinary sessions; skills and relationships belong to particular people; consumable ingredients are spent to obtain a result. The exact transfer and persistence rules depend on household changes, life state and expansion. A single universal inventory model would conceal those distinctions. There is no general loot-armor tier or tactical-party combat progression in the core household loop. Magic and dueling belong to specific additional scopes, discussed below.

## 4. Expansion families change what a life can involve

This is a map of consequential subsystem families, not a claim to list every decorative item or all compatibility interactions in every pack. The older chapter's examples remain separately versioned. A purchase is an account-level entitlement; an in-game skill unlock does not acquire a missing paid pack.

**Work as an activity rather than an absence.** Get to Work lets the player follow doctor, detective and scientist work, or manage retail with staff, chosen goods and customers. This changes what the player does during a workday, not simply the size of a paycheck. Its launch description advertises Photography and Baking as additions; later migration of a feature to the base game must be checked separately rather than treating old promotional wording as permanent exclusivity. [S27]

**Work built from hobbies.** Businesses & Hobbies, released March 6, 2025, adds a small-business framework with home or dedicated space, employees, admission or time-based charges and reputation perks. Pottery uses a wheel and kiln; Tattooing supports created designs. Meetups, mentoring, classes and lectures make learning a social activity. Kintsugi offers a use for broken ceramics instead of requiring every damaged product to be discarded. These systems are not identical to Get to Work's earlier retail model. [S29]

**A recurring calendar.** Seasons adds weather, season-dependent activities and holidays whose traditions can be customized. Weather changes what is comfortable or appropriate; a celebration can gather people around a known date. Its gardening career branches into floral design or botany. Do not describe the existence of every later base-game calendar feature as requiring Seasons merely because the expansion initially emphasized holidays. [S30]

**Growing people and relationships.** Growing Together adds family dynamics, compatibility and milestones that the reviewed player found meaningful across ages. The infant life stage arrived in a free update; the pack's further development and family systems are not all included in that free addition. In the reviewed account, supportive or strained relationships change the texture of caregiving rather than simply awarding more money. [S6][S21]

**Death as a transition and an obligation.** Life & Death, released October 31, 2024, adds a Bucket List, ghostly Unfinished Business, rebirth options, reaping or mortuary careers, grief, wills and Ravenwood. Wills can designate guardians, money and heirlooms. Ghost activity can earn Fear or Goodwill essences. These are authored supernatural rules, not a simulation of real bereavement or an unlimited guarantee that any lost person can return under any conditions. [S28]

**Family as an institution.** Royalty & Legacy, released February 12, 2026, adds Dynasties with shared values, Prestige, Unity, heirs and outcasts. Noble advancement can involve public or noble favor, scandal and sword duels; the pack supplies Ondarion and its competing households. This is not a historical constitutional model or The Sims Medieval transplanted wholesale. Its relevance is that a household can possess a reputation and succession problem distinct from one individual's friendship score. [S31]

### Magic has its own acquisition and risk model

Realm of Magic is a paid game pack. Spellcasters acquire spells, recipes and ranked perks; the families are **Practical, Mischief, Untamed and Alchemy**. Glimmerbrook's portal leads to the Magic Realm, teachers and supplies. Wands, brooms, familiars and cauldrons occupy different roles; a cosmetic wand is not automatically a new damage tier. [S22]

Casting builds **Charge** rather than simply spending a conventional mana pool. Continued use can make overcharge dangerous; waiting and suitable perks alter the decision. Familiar assistance is limited, not proof of permanent immunity. A powerful solution to a household problem can therefore create a new immediate risk. This system is not the original Makin' Magic's ingredient-and-charm structure or Sims 3 Supernatural's alchemy under a different name. [S22][S32]

**Interpretation:** expansions are most interesting when they introduce a new relationship between existing activities. A craft can become a class, a house can become a workplace, a dead person's object can become an obligation. More catalogue entries alone do not establish that depth.

## 5. Progression, world activity and player authorship

The Sims 4's aspirations can support a sequence of chosen projects rather than one irreversible profession. A player can focus on a career, a craft, a relationship, a building or the next generation. **Early, middle and late play are therefore interpretive phases:** establish a viable routine; develop distinctive people and opportunities; then decide what continuity, change or challenge is worth pursuing. A longer lifespan is not automatically a more interesting life. [S11]

Neighborhood Stories applies to significant offscreen changes such as careers, moves, children and accidental death, with household and category controls. Its published defaults distinguish protected played households from other households. A mailbox offers a route to inspect recent developments. This is a particular overview feature, not evidence that every Sim witnesses or remembers every neighbor's change. [S16]

The preserved Myshunosun account uses ordinary autonomy while controlling aging and limiting major neighborhood changes. Named mods and player rules help produce that chosen experience. It demonstrates that “more autonomy” is not one scalar preference: moment-to-moment behavior and intergenerational authorship can reasonably have different policies. The earlier chapter retains the user's exact distinctions. [S17]

**Authored versus emergent story:** named worlds, objects and starting households supply a frame; simulation changes circumstances; the player supplies selection and interpretation. The earlier Alice and Kev homelessness narrative uses The Sims 3 to investigate a premise chosen by its author. It is neither an official campaign nor proof that the game's economic abstraction predicts real homelessness. [S33]

**Interpretation:** OpenLegend should not confuse an event happening offscreen with the player receiving value from it. A town can change without becoming more interesting, and a familiar family can disappear in a way that violates an implicit promise. Useful continuity requires explicit policies and a proportionate way to discover consequences.

## 6. Worked situations and counterexamples

Unless explicitly attributed, these are constructed explanations of documented mechanics, not playtests or guaranteed event sequences.

### A. Conversation need not suspend ordinary life

A Sim prepares a meal while speaking to another person. Compatible multitasking lets the conversation inhabit the kitchen rather than become a separate modal scene. Thursten's launch review also reports implausible positioning and distant conversation: supporting two actions does not prove that their combination is spatially convincing. [S4]

**Transfer hypothesis:** an agent could discuss a plan while preparing supplies, but the meaning of both activities must survive conflicts of hands, movement and attention. More simultaneous animations are not the same as a more believable companion.

### B. A damaged appliance changes from nuisance to learning material

A player wants a functioning bathroom and has a repair-capable Sim. Repair consumes time and yields an opportunity to salvage upgrade parts; further Handiness and appropriate components enable reliability or cleaning improvements. Immediately replacing the object solves the present failure but forgoes that path. Unsafe electrical work is not equivalent to a routine plumbing task. [S14]

**Transfer hypothesis:** maintenance can change future options rather than merely restore a meter. It becomes tedious when failures happen only to extract repetitive clicks, or when the interface cannot distinguish an unavailable part from a routing problem.

### C. A room becomes an emotional preparation ritual

A player arranges a space to encourage an emotion useful for the intended activity. Moodlets from the environment and recent actions influence the dominant feeling; the player waits or changes the situation before beginning. The setup can fail when a stronger competing state matters more. [S15]

**Transfer hypothesis:** surroundings can shape action without dictating personality. The risk is that a supposedly expressive emotional system becomes “drink the correct tea before work.” A character's values and history should not disappear whenever a more efficient colored mood becomes available.

### D. Bringing someone back makes several ordinary skills relevant

**Supernatural recipe spoiler:** a Sims 4 household pursuing Ambrosia must assemble Angelfish, Death Flower and Potion of Youth, and develop the necessary Cooking and Gourmet Cooking mastery. Fishing and horticultural preparation become part of a goal that is not primarily about either skill. The guide describes feeding the prepared dish to an eligible ghost in the household to restore life. Missing one ingredient or the relevant cooking ability blocks the result. [S20]

**Transfer hypothesis:** a personally meaningful intention can connect otherwise separate production systems. The recipe should remain a learnable rule; narration should not invent a resurrection because the player's request sounds emotionally compelling.

### E. An autonomous neighborhood can preserve the wrong things

The player wants neighbors to change jobs while protecting a particular family from an unseen accidental death or move. Neighborhood Stories permits category and household overrides. Leaving every category enabled is a policy choice, not the only authentic way to simulate life. Inspecting a recent change does not undo it or mean the embodied character knew it in advance. [S16]

**Transfer hypothesis:** distinguish ongoing activity from irreversible changes to the story's subject. Scope controls can increase usable autonomy, not merely weaken simulation. The failure case is a confusing override hierarchy that changes a protected life unexpectedly.

### F. A failed celebration gives the player a new intention

Graham Smith's historical Sims 3 review describes a wedding party that fails to achieve its intended marriage and includes a guest's death. A subsequent wish concerning a ghost redirects his attention toward a cemetery excursion. This is the critic's reported sequence, not an assertion that every failed wedding spawns that chain. [S3]

**Transfer hypothesis:** a consequence can suggest a new activity without erasing the original disappointment. Generated narration should not silently convert a failed objective into success merely to keep the tone positive.

### G. A broken pot can become a teaching business

With Businesses & Hobbies installed, a skilled potter can make and finish objects, improve damaged ceramics through Kintsugi, and teach others. Charging for attendance or time creates a different business from selling every object directly. The activity still needs the relevant skill, facilities and an operating business; a business label alone does not guarantee customers or profit. [S29]

**Transfer hypothesis:** reusable systems become expressive when outputs, audiences and payment models can be recombined. Keep the model's scope visible: the product description is not evidence that literally any imaginable commercial activity can be expressed without limits.

## 7. Presentation, accessibility and production craft

Frasca identifies ambiguous expressions, icons and nonliteral speech as room for player interpretation. That is not a claim that dialogue must remain unintelligible forever. It shows that literal language is only one route to meaningful social play. A generated conversation should add something the existing behavior and framing do not already supply. [S2]

Sims 4's creator and construction tools are major activities in their own right. Thursten's modernist-home example values fidelity to an imagined style, not only low walking time. Carter likewise finds the creator approachable while remaining dissatisfied with other parts of the launch simulation. Better editing and better autonomous life are separable product achievements. [S4][S5]

The relevant craft includes animation, expressive timing, readable mood and relationship feedback, item affordances, audio cues and the distinction between watching and intervening. These should be evaluated by whether the player can understand a scene, not by the count of simulated traits. Pausing and switching households provide a style of access very different from requiring continuous action-game reactions.

**Production evidence:** Wright's 2001 interview, published in 2002, describes players taking the game in directions beyond a single prescribed objective and warns that an enormous parameter space alone does not ensure quality. Current patch notes demonstrate continued behavioral maintenance. Neither source establishes the present team's architecture, staffing, development cost or the internals of a proprietary autonomy scheduler. [S7][S1]

**Interpretation:** familiar domestic behavior has a high expectation burden. A player notices an inappropriate sink or impossible conversation immediately. An agent system should not interpret this familiarity as permission to skip animation, pathing, interruption and task-completion quality.

## 8. Sharing, distribution and commercial boundaries

The Gallery supports households, rooms and lots, with saving, searching, placement and editing. Tattoo sharing is separately associated with Businesses & Hobbies. A person can adopt a small creation rather than become an expert world builder; the destination and asset requirements still matter. [S18]

The **March 3, 2026 Maker Program announcement** describes approved Create-a-Sim and Build/Buy packs sold in an in-game Marketplace using **Moola**, separate from household **Simoleons**. It schedules PC/Mac launch for March 17 and describes a later console rollout; this announcement alone does not prove the console completion date. It explicitly preserves free Gallery content and outside mod distribution under separate conditions. Its stated creator return is approximately 30%, expressed as US$0.30 per 100 Moola spent on the creator's pack—not 30% of every Sims product sale. [S19]

**Dated scale evidence:** EA reported **more than 70 million Sims 4 players worldwide on April 18, 2023**, and more than **16 million new downloads since the October 2022 free-base transition**. These are company-reported reach/download measures, not sold copies, concurrent users or profit. Its report of seven million Gallery downloads in one preceding week likewise does not mean seven million unique creators. No current lifetime revenue or project margin is established here. [S21]

The original Legacy Collection is sold as a package; Sims 4 uses a free base with paid additional content. The new Marketplace is another content-discovery and monetization route, not evidence that free household play was removed. Prices and sale totals visible during research are regional and transient, so no durable “buy everything” figure is presented. [S25][S26]

**Marketing interpretation:** identifiable people, before-and-after buildings, household diaries, challenges and reusable creations make participation visible to nonexperts. The preserved Alice and Kev example demonstrates a memorable story, not a measured acquisition channel. Large download totals do not isolate how much growth came from creators, discounts, free entry or any particular feature.

**Risk:** a player attracted by one visible activity may discover that it depends on several additional purchases or mods. That can make a technically honest “base game is free” message feel incomplete. An inspiration library should identify the smallest actual setup that produces the advertised experience.

## 10. Questions and patterns for OpenLegend

**Separate bodily maintenance from meaningful care.** Keeping someone fed can create a reason to cooperate or become an interruption that destroys the scene. Test whether the routine changes a choice or merely demands acknowledgment. Competent assistance should remove low-value supervision without making personalities interchangeable.

**Support simultaneous life, not only longer conversations.** Speech, work and movement can coexist when their physical requirements are compatible. Interruptions and resumption need to make sense. An eloquent exchange while everyone ignores a burning kitchen would weaken rather than deepen the world.

**Treat autonomy as several policies.** Immediate action choice, long-term ambition, offscreen progression and irreversible loss have different stakes. A player may want lively neighbors while reserving authorship over a particular relationship. Make those promises understandable rather than hiding them in inconsistent defaults.

**Keep expression distinct from optimization.** A room, outfit, family or craft can be valuable because it expresses a person's intention. An assistant should not automatically replace that value with the most efficient layout or highest-yield routine. Conversely, attractive decoration should not disguise a broken functional promise.

**Compose outcomes across ordinary systems.** Repair parts, ingredients, teaching and heirlooms show how a personally important project can make everyday actions newly relevant. The rules must remain stable enough for planning. Do not produce the desired narrative outcome while bypassing the material conditions that gave the goal meaning.

**Offer several sizes of authorship.** Adopting a room is different from designing a town; following one household is different from directing generations. Useful defaults and small reusable units can broaden participation. A creator economy adds moderation, compatibility, discovery and ownership questions rather than resolving them automatically.

These are hypotheses and design questions, not instructions to copy a particular psychological model, household structure, monetization system or autonomy implementation.
