## A compact game, not a miniature version of every trilogy system

Galaxy puts the player in control of Jacob Taylor in a short, authored mobile adventure. Tilt input moves Jacob through top-down encounters; the game shoots automatically, while tapping selects a target and right-side ability icons provide special attacks. Conversations use illustrated panels and selectable responses. The compact action-and-dialogue structure is not a full class, inventory, crafting, romance or party-management system. The dossier must explain the actual scope rather than inventing the absent RPG layers. [S1](#s1) [S2](#s2)

The powers include a biotic stasis effect, shield sabotage and an explosive missile attack. Target choice and avoidance therefore remain decisions even when ordinary firing is automatic. However, the critical question is whether those decisions sustain the repeated room-clearing activity; several contemporary writers thought they did not. An easier input is not automatically a more satisfying action. [S1](#s1) [S6](#s6)

Macworld's Chris Holt reports that pausing recalibrates the tilt reference, an important distinction between difficulty in the encounter and discomfort from the device's current resting angle. His review also criticizes loading and technical instability on his test hardware. Those observations are dated firsthand evidence, not proof that every surviving installation behaves identically. [S2](#s2)

## Dialogue has local consequences, but not unlimited branching

Pocket Gamer describes negotiations that can avert a fight, while early TouchArcade impressions found that several tested opening answers reconverged. These claims need not contradict each other: an early conversation, a later conflict and an entirely different ending are different scales of reactivity. The full dossier will preserve that distinction rather than declare either that every choice matters equally or that no choice matters. [S1](#s1) [S7](#s7)

**Story spoilers.** Illo Nazario's illness connects the investigation to the rescued scientist Batha and the element-zero shipment. Arriving with the required assistance changes whether Jacob can offer a cure immediately. It remains an authored dependency, not a free-form medical or crafting simulation. Nazario's disclosure also changes the apparent target of the planned attack. [S8](#s8)

## Cross-title reward boundary

The original completion/account-link message promised a Mass Effect 2 reward without explaining its content. The later mechanics reference identifies additional dialogue involving Ish, Jacob and Miranda, with both companions required for the full exchange. Legendary Edition includes that extra dialogue without requiring a present-day purchase of the retired phone game. This is a continuity bonus, not imported weapons, levels or a compulsory prerequisite to understand the sequel. No current activation service is claimed to work. [S9](#s9)
