# 06. Evidence method, coverage, and further reading

**Scope: G189 Pax Dei only. Evidence cutoff: November 14, 2026.** [Dossier index](../pax-dei.md)

## 1. What was researched

This is a desk-research dossier, not a hands-on review or source-code inspection. It combines primary developer publications, infrastructure and production partners, financial summaries, population trackers, first-person criticism, and direct player testimony. Detailed factual claims have adjacent links in the chapter that owns them.

Current implementation, historical implementation, announced intentions, personal testimony, vendor measurement, third-party estimates, and this dossier's interpretations are explicitly separated. No individual source proves the causal explanation for a population decline.

The review corpus contains **six substantive written articles representing five review works from four principal reviewers and four outlets**, including a two-part review and a longitudinal revisit. A separate enthusiast feature adds another firsthand perspective. The [reception chapter](04-reception.md) records the work, author, date, version, positive findings, criticisms, and access quality. Eight contrasting helpful Steam reviews are an intentionally selected qualitative sample, not a random survey; indexed recent community discussions add context with weaker access.

## 4. Material limitations

- Public concurrency excludes other clients and cannot isolate purchaser cohorts. Dynamic snapshots are dated; rolling periods are not substituted for calendar months.
- Original statutory accounts, project budgets, publisher/platform contracts, campaign invoices, subscriber counts, player-level retention, and current cash runway were not inspected.
- Financial databases and modeled sales totals are not interchangeable. Conflicting figures remain visible rather than being reconciled through an invented explanation.
- Steam and review samples have selection, language, version, playstyle, and survivorship limitations. Indexed forum snippets cannot replace a complete discussion or a representative survey.
- No live server behavior, crafting balance, frame-rate claim, exact network consistency guarantee, or private implementation was independently reproduced.
- Primary publications sometimes retain stale instructions. Later patch notes govern the specific changes identified in the dossier; absence of a later correction does not certify every old rule as current.
- The dossier does not exhaust every item, encounter, fictional text, fan project, or future patch. It covers the major systems and the requested commercial/design questions with the actual evidence available.

## 5. Preservation and scope

G189 is a new dossier. Earlier game dossiers and subjects remain in their existing locations; no prior material is replaced or recertified by this pass. The inventory adds Pax Dei once, and the research index and scoped ledger provide navigation.

Open Legend's existing gameplay, world, architecture, art and commercial owners remain authoritative. The research proposes questions and interpretations without modifying runtime contracts, creating implementation tasks, changing pricing, or authorizing spending.
