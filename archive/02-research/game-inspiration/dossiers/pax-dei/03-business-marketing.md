# Pax Dei: business, development, and marketing

Research checked November 14, 2026. This chapter distinguishes company disclosures, third-party estimates, and our interpretation. It does not establish a complete game profit-and-loss account.

## 1. What the commercial evidence supports

Pax Dei attracted substantial attention, launched commercially, and maintained a paying community. Its developer also reduced staff, narrowed its ambitions, and acknowledged financial constraints. The evidence supports **a commercially pressured, continuing game**, not a confident declaration of either profitability or imminent closure. In February 2026 Mainframe explicitly said development depended on both investment and game revenue, acknowledged reducing team size and scope, and committed to improving existing systems. [Official team letter, February 13, 2026](https://playpaxdei.com/en-us/news/information/future-of-pax-dei-a-letter-from-the-team)

This assessment does not rely solely on Steam concurrency. Mainframe reported more than 90,000 active players across platforms in the first week of its October 2025 release. That is a developer-reported period audience, not concurrent players, paying subscribers, sales, or retained customers. The original Discord announcement was quoted by reporting we could inspect; the underlying Discord post was not independently retrieved. [MassivelyOP, October 23, 2025](https://massivelyop.com/2025/10/23/pax-dei-says-its-seen-more-than-90000-active-players-on-all-platforms-since-launch-last-week/)

## 2. Studio scale and development chronology

Mainframe formed in April 2019 with thirteen founders. Its history describes talent from CCP, Remedy, Blizzard, Ubisoft, Next Games, and other studios, with offices in Helsinki, Reykjavík, and Paris. Its documented ambition was a persistent social world accessible across devices. These credentials explain access to funding and press; they do not guarantee that a particular new gameplay economy works. [Company history](https://themainframe.com/en/)

| Date | Evidence and commercial significance |
|---|---|

**Interpretation:** roughly five years from studio formation to EA and six-and-a-half to 1.0 demonstrates the integration burden of a persistent MMO. It does not identify how much time or money belonged specifically to graphics, networking, content, or gameplay redesign. An indie team should not assume AI-assisted production removes those integration costs.

## 3. Funding, revenue, and actual cost: separate quantities

### Disclosed financing

The company timeline lists €2 million seed financing in 2019, €7.6 million Series A in 2020, and approximately €20 million Series B in 2021: roughly €30 million in announced financing. Contemporary trade reporting gives the last package as €20.3 million, including a €3 million Business Finland loan. Therefore avoid describing the rounded total as exclusively equity, a precisely audited lifetime funding total, or the game's development budget. [Company timeline](https://themainframe.com/en/), [contemporary financing report](https://www.pcgamesinsider.biz/news/72692/mainframe-industries-lands-23m-investment/)

Financing is money available to a company. Development expenditure is money spent making the game. Customer spending, company-recognized revenue, operating profit, and cash balance are additional, different measures. We found no public project ledger separating development, marketing, hosting, support, platform deductions, and continuing investment.

### Financial indicators stronger than a Steam-sales model

Finnish company-information provider Asiakastieto reports the following for **Mainframe Industries Oy, business ID 2997728-8**:

| Fiscal year | Reported entity revenue | Reported operating loss |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2024 | €2.562 million | €12.047 million |
| 2025 | €4.017 million | €9.964 million |

These are entity-level database figures, not directly inspected audited statements or a Pax Dei lifetime revenue tally. The site's displayed 2025 margin does not reconcile arithmetically with its own revenue and operating-loss amounts; do not reproduce that percentage. [Asiakastieto financial summary](https://www.asiakastieto.fi/yritykset/fi/mainframe-industries-oy/29977288/taloustiedot)

Separately, Deloitte's 2025 Technology Fast 50 Finland report shows Mainframe's 2024 revenue as **€2.660 million** and EBITDA margin as **−247%**. Its company chart on printed page 19 was inspected visually. This is a different figure and profit measure; its reconciliation with the entity database is unavailable. Do not average the revenue numbers, add them together, or treat EBITDA and operating loss as interchangeable. Both independently indicate serious historical losses relative to revenue. Neither establishes present cash runway or the remaining investor commitment. [Deloitte report, p.19](https://www.epressi.com/media/userfiles/161771/1761740621/deloitte-technology-fast-50-finland-2025.pdf)

### Third-party game revenue estimates

The accessible Video Game Insights developer page labels lifetime Steam revenue at approximately **$4.2 million**. This is a modeled Steam figure, not publisher reporting; its freshness and treatment of founder upgrades are not established. [VGI/Sensor Tower developer listing](https://app.sensortower.com/vgi/developer/1515316/mainframe-industries)

Other public estimators produce substantially different numbers: GamesBerry showed about $9.3 million gross, while RageQuit showed a $6.9–18 million range derived from reviews and pricing. These are **competing models, not additional revenue**. They do not justify a single confidently stated lifetime-sales figure. [GamesBerry](https://gamesberry.app/game/pax-dei), [RageQuit methodology-labelled listing](https://ragequit.gg/steam-charts/pax-dei/)

Material missing inputs include direct sales, Epic purchases, Microsoft contract terms, refunds, discounts, founder-pack mix, plot-token sales, premium conversion, renewals, and publisher revenue allocation. Launcher migration also means a Steam account or review need not correspond to a new Steam purchase. The most defensible commercial conclusion uses financing, company-loss indicators, staffing cuts, developer admissions, and retention together.

## 4. How the monetization evolved

The 2024 Founder packs were priced at $39.99, $59.99, and $99.99. Higher tiers supplied more land access during EA and additional perks. Crucially, a June 2024 developer clarification already described a likely recurring fee after EA and stated that founders' permanent entitlements did not mean permanently free plot upkeep. The exact future prices were unresolved. Therefore, “subscriptions appeared with no warning” is inaccurate; uncertainty and changing details were real. [Contemporary pack coverage](https://www.mmorpg.com/features/pax-dei-will-enter-early-access-on-june-18th-aims-to-develop-mmo-alongside-its-players-2000131748), [developer clarification](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1995520/discussions/0/4331979689561092729/)

Current core pricing is **$29.99/€29.99 once**, including permanent game access, two character slots, and an initial premium month with one plot token. A subscription is optional. A player can maintain one plot using 400 Grace, the in-game currency, and can build cooperatively under another owner's permissions. [Official monetization FAQ](https://playpaxdei.com/en-us/faq/1-0-monetization)

| Optional monthly plan | Published USD/EUR price |
|---|---:|
| Premium, no plot tokens | 3.99 |
| Premium + one plot token | 6.99 |
| Premium + two plot tokens | 10.99 |
| Premium + four plot tokens | 18.99 |

The live shop additionally lists six- and eight-token plans, so the FAQ's shorter plan list is not exhaustive. Premium grants 50% bonuses to XP and Grace acquisition; each token maintains one plot for one month. Membership and tokens are distinct: canceling membership does not invalidate unused tokens. Plot expiry eventually frees land and stores recoverable contents; manually releasing land has different consequences. [Membership shop](https://playpaxdei.com/en-us/shop/subscriptions)

In October 2026 Mainframe removed the previous twelve-month expiration of **unused tokens**. That did not abolish monthly plot upkeep or guarantee a permanent location. The anniversary post explicitly addressed reducing friction for returning players. [Anniversary update](https://playpaxdei.com/en-us/news/information/pax-dei-turns-one-horses-and-a-special-anniversary-boost-are-coming)

**Interpretation:** land pricing can help fund persistence and release abandoned space. It also attaches recurring cost to players' emotional attachment and most visible creative achievement. A one-plot route through play softens that conflict but may replace monetary pressure with a maintenance obligation. Whether this causes churn requires player-level evidence; it cannot be measured from review complaints alone.

### Economics illustration — assumptions, not Pax Dei results

At the published $6.99 one-plot price, 10,000 renewing subscriptions produce about **$70,000 gross monthly**; 30,000 produce about **$210,000**. At an assumed blended $10 monthly payment, those totals become $100,000 and $300,000. These calculations exclude taxes, processing/platform shares, refunds, and all costs. Actual subscriber counts and blended spend are unknown.

This explains why a few thousand concurrent users can support a small operation yet fail to sustain a much larger studio's development pace. Concurrent users are neither monthly payers nor revenue. For Open Legend, model retained monthly customers, net receipts per customer, fixed team expense, and variable server/AI cost separately. A smaller payroll can lower break-even; expensive AI may raise the cost per active player. Neither conclusion follows automatically from Pax Dei's Steam chart.

## 5. Marketing: what actually happened

### Awareness and community recruitment

The reveal sold a distinctive fantasy: inhabit a medieval society and create its settlements and stories. The official media archive includes the announcement, PC Gaming Show 2023 appearance, world-making footage, building demonstrations, and EA trailer. Alpha signups, Steam wishlists, Discord, and public playtests formed the recruitment funnel. [Announcement](https://playpaxdei.com/en-us/news/information/announcing-pax-dei), [official video archive](https://playpaxdei.com/en-us/media/videos)

June 7–11, 2024's **Gallia's Grand Gathering** paired creators with their audiences, distributed invitations, and supplied exploration, gathering, combat, and building challenges. The published lineup included Asmongold, CohhCarnage, AnnieFuchsia, Danneh, Sacriel, and others in English, French, and German. A village-building contest and developer interview supplied shareable moments. Presence on the list establishes planned participation, not every individual's actual deliverables or paid status. [Official event announcement](https://playpaxdei.com/en-us/news/early-access/gallias-grand-gathering)

Agency Black Soup reports that its EA campaign used 22 paid creators and generated more than 392,000 watch-hours. Of 407 organic invitations, 71 creators participated, adding over 245,000 watch-hours. Its 1.0 paid campaign generated over 58,000 watch-hours; the agency reported weaker organic participation, less effective challenge incentives, lost novelty, and competition around TwitchCon and other releases. These are interested-party campaign reports, not audited acquisition or sales results. Aggregate audience metrics must not be read as unique viewers. Campaign scope and budgets differ, so the watch-hours alone do not prove worsening advertising efficiency. [Agency case study](https://www.blacksoup.gg/showroom-/pax-dei)

**Our inference:** the preview supplied a social group, leader, explicit goals, rewards, and a deadline outside the base game. That can make an open-ended sandbox compelling on stream while leaving an unaccompanied newcomer unsure what to do. Test the ordinary arrival experience separately from a staffed community event.

### Audience research and creator infrastructure

Solsten's vendor case study describes persona research, playtesting, and UX work emphasizing collaboration, building, and collection. It says over 60% of first-alpha participants matched the primary target persona and another 20% a second segment. This documents deliberate audience work, not an absence of research. Its inference of product-market fit is much stronger than the evidence supplied: persona match does not establish paid conversion, retention, or viable unit economics. [Solsten case study](https://solsten.io/cases/the-making-of-legends-in-pax-deis-expansive-sandbox-world)

The ongoing creator program offers keys, community rewards, media assets, potential featuring, and developer interview opportunities, using Lurkit. Public criteria include audience/view thresholds; no total creator budget or revenue attribution is published. [Creator program](https://creators.playpaxdei.com/)

### Distribution and reactivation

1.0 expanded access through Steam, Epic, PC Game Pass, and GeForce Now; the latter is a way to access an underlying game entitlement, not an independent population to automatically add to store users. [Launch schedule](https://playpaxdei.com/en-us/news/1-0/pax-dei-1-0-schedule-and-details)

January 19–26, 2026 brought a Steam free week, temporary premium/plot access, and a 40% base-game sale through February 2. [Official free-week offer](https://playpaxdei.com/en-us/news/information/steam-free-week-is-incoming-invite-your-friends-to-pax-dei)

An ongoing seven-day trial launched July 16, 2026. It includes the full game and a temporary plot; it can be repeated after approximately ninety days. Character progress survives, while expired land is released after a short grace period. This is a trial of a paid game, not conversion to unlimited free-to-play. [Trial announcement](https://playpaxdei.com/en-us/news/information/pax-dei-7-day-trial-is-now-live-on-steam), [dated Steam announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1995520/eventcomments/574921459914357623/)

### Recognition and limits

Pax Dei won Finnish Game of the Year 2024, announced in May 2025 following an industry-association member vote. That is real professional recognition alongside weak commercial signals; it is not a sales award or a global consumer poll. [Organizers' announcement](https://visionist.fi/lehdistotiedotteet/the-sandbox-game-was-named-the-best-finnish-game-of-the-year-at-the-finnish-game-awards-gala/)

We did not find campaign spend, acquisition cost, wishlist-to-sale conversion, publisher terms, or current subscriber counts. The record establishes substantial exposure and repeated reactivation attempts. It does not establish whether more advertising would have solved the problem.

## 6. Decisions Open Legend should take from this

These are proposed lessons, not changes to accepted Open Legend contracts:

- **Validate paid return behavior before pursuing mass exposure.** People liking a pitch, matching a persona, or joining a free test is weaker evidence than choosing to return and pay.
- **Make the marketed experience reproducible.** A compelling creator event should expose a loop ordinary players can enter without a celebrity organizer.
- **Protect the first major launch.** EA creates lasting expectations; 1.0 does not restore novelty automatically.
- **State persistence obligations early.** Explain upkeep, absence, recovery, relocation, and termination in terms players understand before they invest emotionally.
- **Budget for the audience actually retained.** A niche can work with appropriate costs; large financing and a prestige presentation impose a larger commercial burden.
- **Separate onboarding from world size.** Connect newcomers to meaningful communities and activity before acquiring enough players to fill a vast map.
- **Use a coherent art promise.** Pax Dei demonstrates that impressive visuals can attract attention without resolving progression, social, and economic problems. Open Legend still needs appealing presentation; it need not copy the same rendering target.
