# Dungeon Fighter Online — full research dossier

**G198 · October 28, 2026 — substantive draft, incomplete.** This pass studies the PC Dungeon & Fighter / Dungeon Fighter Online regional services, using **DFO Global** for the operational mechanics below. Korean DNF, Chinese DNF, Global and mobile/franchise results must remain separate. Economy/continuity, production/marketing, reception, external creation, connected lore, full interaction cases and independent review are still being integrated. No client, account, editor or payment was used.

[Requirements](../research-requirements.md) · [MMO research progress](../research-progress-mmos-2026-10-26.md)

The **Dungeon&Fighter franchise dates to 2005**. [E01](#e01) This study concerns the original PC action RPG, with its side-on 2D brawling, persistent characters and online social/party systems. **Neople** develops and publishes the Global Steam edition, a free-to-play Windows release listed from **August 9, 2016**; that is the Steam date, not the franchise’s beginning or the 2015 Global relaunch covered by the reviews. Current official Global guides describe level-115 play. The mobile game, DNF Duel and other franchise products are separate. [S25](#s25) [S01](#s01)

The OpenLegend question is how a persistent character can support satisfying short activities and an evolving repertoire. A lasting inventory or account does not by itself establish a continuously shared, mutable landscape. DFO’s individual dungeon entries, repeatable encounters and service timers require separate treatment from its social spaces.

## 1. Starting a character and choosing a role

The creation guide distinguishes **Cain** and **Sirocco**: characters are accessed through their selected server, while users from both can be seen. Choosing a character includes a unique permitted name. The role display distinguishes **Dealers**, oriented toward damage, from **Buffers**, who improve party capability through effects such as stat increases. Those labels describe practical contributions; they do not make every advancement within a family interchangeable. [S04](#s04)

A new character’s tutorial introduces controls and a class-specific background. **Act Quests** lead through scenario dungeons, grant experience and suitable magic-sealed equipment, and currently provide the route to level **115**. The Quest Book’s Location function directs the next destination. After one account character clears an applicable quest, another can use **Clear Quest** instead of repeating it. That separates another character’s progression from a compulsory replay of every scene. [S01](#s01)

## 2. Dungeon time and repeated entry

The Fatigue guide describes a daily activity allowance: ordinary rooms consume FP, the baseline is **156**, and the daily reset is **09:00 UTC**. A remaining point can permit entry even when the whole dungeon contains more rooms. Recovery potions have crafting, market, shop and event routes; the Neo: Premium Contract increases the allowance. Content-specific entry rules still need to be checked separately. [S02](#s02)

This is a resource spent on access, distinct from health lost during combat or character progress retained afterward. The design question is whether a chosen session ends with an attainable accomplishment, or whether eligibility, daily resets and preparation consume the time the player wanted to spend acting. Paid effects and actual player testimony are still under review.

## 3. Legibility, travel and accumulated complexity

The Sunken Depths system update introduced an **Adventure Navigator** that identifies equipment/character improvements behind a missing Fame requirement, shows content already completed for its entry/reward allowance, and recommends relevant activities. Its revised world map searches NPCs and dungeons, supplies location help, consolidates travel through warp points and saves favorites per account. The map changes with scenario knowledge. This is a navigation and access aid, not evidence that the underlying geography is permanently rebuilt by players. [S03](#s03)

The transferable hypothesis is modest: when a player cannot attempt a desired activity, explain the actual missing prerequisite and an attainable route to it. A large map or a numerical score is insufficient if the person cannot tell which action would help. The complete dossier will compare that accessibility with encounter skill, equipment acquisition and the burden of maintaining multiple characters.

## 4. Advancement and a practical home base

Advancement supplies subclass-specific skills. The current guide places the first Awakening at level 50, the second at 75 and Neo Awakening at 100; these unlock stronger skills and associated statistics. Side Story characters use Self-Awakening instead of an ordinary subclass tree. An established character therefore has both learned execution and an authored sequence of capability unlocks. [S05](#s05)

**Seria’s Room** is a login and logistics base in Elvenguard: portal, mailbox, safe and avatar closet. Mail can move eligible items between people or one person’s characters; the account safe becomes available at level 60 for account-bound transfers. Safe expansion increases storage. A guild application requires acceptance by an authorized member, and membership provides a teleport facility. These are recognizable reasons to visit a familiar place, but the documented room is not a player-built settlement. [S06](#s06)

## 5. Equipment composition, risk and remembered discoveries

The Set Point system combines exclusive points within one equipment set; common points support the set with the most exclusive points. Each set comprises twelve items. This makes composition matter beyond picking an isolated high-stat drop: replacing one item can change a threshold elsewhere in the loadout. [S07](#s07)

Reinforcement improves different statistics by slot. Percentage-damage attacks depend on physical/magical attack; fixed-damage attacks use Independent Attack. Refinement raises the latter and can fail without destroying the weapon or reducing its refinement. Reinforcement and Amplification can lose levels or destroy equipment at their higher thresholds. Amplification adds a dimensional base statistic; tainted equipment first needs purification. The guide also documents bounded safe-upgrade routes that preserve levels on failure and increase subsequent success chances. Consequently, an upgrade’s cost includes the risk to the existing item, not only the listed currency price. [S08](#s08)

The level-115 **Armory** records equipment per character when explicitly registered or destroyed through listed equipment operations. Equipment Conversion can exchange an equipped eligible item for a registered alternative while retaining enhancement properties. Its exact material requirements depend on rarity. This is a collection-backed route to another loadout: discovery can remain useful after the original object has gone. It does not establish freely tradable copies or account-wide access to every recorded item. [S09](#s09)

## 6. Skill changes and legible choice

Skills are learned with SP. **Skill Evolution**, available after Neo/Self-Awakening, supplies five VP for functional options; Skill Enhancement separately modifies damage/cooldown or eligible healing. This system replaced the old Rune/Talisman structure, so an old equipment guide can prescribe an obsolete route. The worked example below distinguishes a bigger number from a different usable technique. [S10](#s10)

The current normal-dungeon guide illustrates why entry rules need content names. **Revelation: Sky Embracing a Thousand Seas** has a limited daily version; after its two clears the slot changes to the normal version. The normal form randomly chooses the Grand Constellation Turtle Library or Castle of the Apostate, uses two FP per room and has five Fame-gated difficulty levels. These are repeatable entries, not proof of a single continuously shared landscape. [S11](#s11)

## 7. What the player actually controls

The current skill tables span male/female **Slayers, Fighters, Gunners, Mages and Priests**, plus **Thief, Knight, Demonic Lancer, Agent, Archer, Dark Knight and Creator**. A family contains materially different specializations: Fighters include Striker, Nen Master, Brawler and Grappler; Gunners include Ranger, Launcher and Mechanic; Mages include Summoner and Elementalist; support options include Crusader, Enchantress, Muse and Paramedic. A current table is more useful than a fixed class count copied from older marketing. [S20](#s20)

Sagoe’s 2015 Global assessment describes directional skill commands alongside movement, jumping and basic attacks; hotkeys and skill locking offer alternatives. Juggling and grappling extend combos. [R03](#r03) Official controller support subsequently supplied configurable keyboard-equivalent actions, combination keys, item/skill slots, mouse emulation, stick sensitivity and vibration settings. Analog tilt distinguishes walking from running; monster prompts can map to the appropriate controller action. The August 2023 implementation warned that some events would have incomplete support. Its dated support restrictions do not prove every later device configuration works. [S24](#s24)

Three examples show why a class list alone is insufficient:

- **Berserker** has attacks that require **Frenzy**. Neople explicitly added disabled icons, a message and a tooltip when that state is missing. The failure explanation identifies a missing condition instead of leaving the player to blame timing or a broken key. [S19](#s19)
- **Summoner** delegates attacks but still manages a composition. The 2022 renewal made **Loop of Domination** summon eligible minions together, excluding Kasijas and Lamos. **Hailom** periodically heals minions, protects Greater Spirits and grants status immunity; **Dark Lunacy** changes Louise’s behavior into an armored attacking state with its own restriction. This is authored combat-agent behavior, not simulated people with independent lives. [S18](#s18)
- **Muse** connects an Archer’s lyra bow and amplifier to group support. Note sequences activate scores; **Lovely Tempo** strengthens allies, while other songs heal, cleanse or shield. Her fiction of music moving magic becomes something the player performs during combat. [S12](#s12)

Arena provides a separate competitive activity. Neople’s September 2026 championship recruitment explicitly used ranked/free-battle experience and excluded specified advancements from that tournament. Its November championship was still scheduled, not an already-observed outcome on this research date. This establishes active PvP organization without assuming every class, equipment rule or regional mode is equivalent. [S26](#s26)

## 8. Encounters, failure and lasting progress

**Delezie’s raid** has solo, matching and normal modes; the published matching/normal formats use twelve people. Entry combines level, Fame and a story prerequisite. The modes share a weekly reward allowance, reset Tuesday at 09:00 UTC, rather than consuming FP. Failure restores normal-mode entry. Normal resurrection draws from a shared Life Token allowance; solo/matching use automatic revival. Recovery therefore depends on mode, not a universal corpse or permadeath rule. [S15](#s15)

The game layers personal progress, roster convenience, consumable/entry budgets and repeated encounters. Death inside an attempt, a failed risky upgrade, an expired event item and service-side account retention are distinct loss mechanisms. The continuity and payment sections below keep them separate. A boss cleared again for rewards is not necessarily a second permanent change to the fiction’s landscape.

## 9. Named motives and the worlds those actions serve

The player is an **Adventurer**, not an unnamed manager of a loot spreadsheet. The authored stories repeatedly put apparently divine power, vulnerable communities and other people’s motives behind the next confrontation. These are scenario and character narratives; they do not establish a freeform branching political simulation.

The **Pandemonium prequel, Chronicles of Luke**, makes missing adventurers the initial mystery: **Hilder and Kasijas** investigate, eventually encountering **Luke**. The **Red Witch** protects Central Park while concealing childhood sadness. **Domina Hailey** isolates herself because a curse gives her a nightly thirst for blood. Luke’s subordinate manipulates believers in Time Square. The story introduces threat through relationships, deception and an unsafe place to live, rather than only announcing a stronger monster. This account uses the published chapter text; its animations were not watched. [S17](#s17)

**Forged God, Nabel** connects scientific ambition to disaster. **Dr. Eldirh**, pursuing **Project Teana** on Terra, discovers a power she recognizes as dangerous and cannot contain, yet revives an artificial-god project to exploit it. **Zero**, an old colleague and Genesis Seeker, warns that personal ambition threatens everyone. **Nabel**, initially celebrated for controlling climate, becomes feared and cursed as human uses of that power devastate homes. **Anima’s** capacity to reconstruct matter extends into thought, and the institute’s account suspects hatred toward Eldirh. The playable confrontation with a “forged god” thus concerns the consequences of making power without accepting responsibility, not only supernatural spectacle. [S13](#s13)

**Delezie of Plague** resents both those who worship him and those who define him as a monster; his existence spreads suffering even when he did not seek their devotion. His synopsis longs for a death that perpetually eludes him. **Mahnarval** is unsettled when people choose to save a former enemy instead of taking revenge, undermining the harsh survival logic that sustained him. Unable to surrender that worldview, he defends its ruins. The associated Act quests open the Depths of Empty Sea and Azure Main, and the raid turns plague, threatened islands and coordinated defense into encounter rules. The character motives give the fight emotional stakes; they do not justify treating repeated raid clears as permanent global history. [S14](#s14)

The **Grand Constellation Turtle Library** stores Seon’s memories, carried through mist, inside a magically expanded tree associated with **Myre** and Record Keepers. The **Castle of the Apostate**, formerly a place of worship for **Lemidios**, now lacks its old hopeful prayers. Those distinct sites occupy a repeatable dungeon selection, so fictional history and encounter reuse coexist. [S11](#s11)

## 10. Crafting, useful objects and presentation

Equipment moves through competing uses: wear it, retain it for another configuration, sell eligible goods, or consume it through disassembly/extraction. The Sunken Depths material revision connects **rare/unique equipment extraction** with an Alchemist’s **Bottle of Wind**, **Golden Powder** and **Dragon Heart**; Abello also sells profession inputs for Clear Cube Fragments. Enchanters use **Mana Solvent**, while Animators have distinct spirit inputs. Level-115 Primeval registration/disassembly can yield **Prime Stella** for weapon acquisition, but converted equipment does not yield that material again. That exception prevents a described conversion route from being mistaken for unlimited resource creation. [S21](#s21)

The profession interfaces support recipes, enchanting and bead creation, show enchant success percentages, and can consume crafting materials from the vault. The same convenience update colors party members by guild, Explorer Club friendship or recent companionship. Storage access and social recognition reduce repeated administrative work without changing what a recipe or teammate can actually do. [S27](#s27)

**Dungeon Life Online’s** workshop connects its quieter activities to useful objects: an Enchanting Device adds effects to equipment, a Bead Fusion Device converts monster cards, and portable disassemblers recover materials. Foods restore HP/MP or temporarily change combat statistics; for example, **Gold Rice Cake** restores both resources, while **Lucid Cider** removes harmful status effects. Shared consumable cooldowns and content-specific use limits still constrain preparation. The village’s bespoke currency and storage are not interchangeable with the main economy. [S23](#s23)

DFO’s presentation makes the authored class itself an object of attachment: sprite action, large illustrated identity and avatar/dye choices. The store explicitly links its side-scrolling format to arcade brawlers. [S25](#s25) Sagoe heard orchestral travel music and rock/electronic battle music. Korean voices limited his judgment, but Danjin’s older, eccentric English delivery had more personality for him. [R03](#r03) The positive opportunity is expressive movement, sound and a recognizable role; the risk is that many spectacular effects obscure the condition or danger the person needs to see. That readability question should be tested in actual overlapping play, not inferred from a clean promotional image.

## 11. Seven worked situations

These are **constructed illustrations of documented rules**, not personal play sessions. Actual reported returns and departures appear separately in the reception section.

### A. Perform support while participating in the fight

A Muse wants to keep allies effective during an encounter. She uses **Lovely Tempo**, then activates the appropriate Effector and enters the required notes to play a score. **Adrenaline Rhapsody** enhances that buff; **Rubato of My Heart** instead supplies a protective shield. Choosing a defensive score addresses a different situation from amplifying offense. **Hot Debut** supplies a directional reposition, and **Street Performance** can hold nearby enemies. The next decision is which help is needed and whether there is a safe opportunity to perform it. Support has its own execution vocabulary; simply selecting the Buffer label does not execute the sequence. [S12](#s12)

### B. Turn a remembered drop into another useful loadout

An established character wants a different equipment set without discarding every prior upgrade. After discovering and registering the desired level-115 piece, the player uses **Equipment Conversion** on an eligible equipped item. The registered alternative becomes available while enhancement properties carry over; rarity determines material costs. The player then checks the resulting set contribution instead of assuming a higher-rarity single item produces the desired whole build. An unregistered alternative or ineligible item breaks this route. Collection can support experimentation, but the conversion is neither free nor an unrestricted duplication command. [S09](#s09) [S07](#s07)

### C. Repeat a short dungeon without mistaking continuity of movement for continuity of world

A level-115 character plans repeated **Endkeeper of Order** attempts. The published entry takes eight FP and forty-nine Doom Oracles and permits one to four players. After clearing, moving right begins a **new dungeon** while preserving buffs and minions; the Retry button remains an alternative. A bonus can waive the next entry’s materials, and a rift can lead to different rewards. The next choice is whether another attempt is worth the remaining access resources. Continuous movement lowers interruption, but it does not turn those newly loaded attempts into one shared persistent field or remove their costs. [S16](#s16)

### D. Help the raid by preventing a collective failure

In Delezie’s normal raid, a **Malefic Bomb Transporter** threatens an island while the group pursues larger targets. Ignoring it damages the island and raises the **Malefic Energy Gauge**, which fails the raid at full. Diverting a party to defeat it restores Plague Reversal and adds resurrection/consumable capacity. The group must weigh that recovery against lost boss time. One person’s death consumes a shared allowance; healing consumable counts are personal. A useful contributor therefore changes the group’s remaining possibilities, rather than merely raising an individual damage total. [S15](#s15)

### E. Build a place that another player can meaningfully visit

**Dungeon Life Online** begins with **Ei** seeking protection for the vulnerable **Ratties** in Pandemonium. Its editor places prescribed buildings on free grid cells. Raise the fountain, build Farm Storage and a Garden, buy seeds from **Goodman**, then spend AP and wait for crops. A Workshop permanently learns purchased manuals and consumes inputs to craft. **Happy Hearts** from requests improve the fountain, expanding possibilities. With a **Leave Permit**, advertise through an NPC in the Exchange Hub; another person visits that village and collects a fountain heart, also crediting its owner. The next choice can be production or making the visit appealing. AP renews daily, Sill stays local, and the design is a bounded village rather than a published programmable world. NPC gifts raise Likeness and unlock paid requests for assistance. [S22](#s22)

The 2025 system update moved Pioneer Ei to Elvenguard and added menu entry; the 2022 entry instructions therefore require that correction. These published native rules were inspected, without verifying a new account’s present access in the client. [S03](#s03)

### F. Choose a usable skill improvement

A player has reached Neo Awakening and wants a move to fit an encounter better. They allocate one of the five Evolution points to one of that skill’s two functional options, then compare its behavior. Separately, Enhancement can favor stronger individual casts or a smaller increase with a shorter cooldown. A shorter cooldown helps only if the next safe opportunity comes soon enough to use it. The limitation is encounter fit: the largest tooltip percentage is not automatically the most useful choice. [S10](#s10)

### G. Return for a different experience without treating a boost as competence

A returning player wants to try another class. Previously completed applicable Act Quests can be cleared through the account’s skip route, reducing repeated scenario work. [S01](#s01) If the desired activity is still inaccessible, the **Adventure Navigator** identifies the missing improvement or prerequisite. [S03](#s03) The productive next action is to learn the new repertoire and attempt a suitable activity, perhaps with someone willing to help. This is a design interpretation: faster eligibility alone cannot demonstrate understanding, social confidence or a satisfying reason to remain. The review accounts below show why those outcomes must be distinguished.
