# Dungeon Fighter Online — full research dossier

**G198 · October 28, 2026 — substantive draft, incomplete.** This pass studies the PC Dungeon & Fighter / Dungeon Fighter Online regional services, using **DFO Global** for the operational mechanics below. Korean DNF, Chinese DNF, Global and mobile/franchise results must remain separate. Economy/continuity, production/marketing, reception, external creation, connected lore, full interaction cases and independent review are still being integrated. No client, account, editor or payment was used.

[Requirements](../research-requirements.md) · [MMO research progress](../research-progress-mmos-2026-10-26.md)

The initial OpenLegend question is how a persistent character can support satisfying short activities and an evolving repertoire. A lasting inventory or account does not by itself establish a continuously shared, mutable landscape. DFO’s individual dungeon entries, repeatable encounters and service timers require separate treatment from its social spaces.

## 1. Starting a character and choosing a role

The creation guide distinguishes **Cain** and **Sirocco**: characters are accessed through their selected server, while users from both can be seen. Choosing a character includes a unique permitted name. The role display distinguishes **Dealers**, oriented toward damage, from **Buffers**, who improve party capability through effects such as stat increases. Those labels describe practical contributions; they do not make every advancement within a family interchangeable. [S04](#s04)

A new character’s tutorial introduces controls and a class-specific background. **Act Quests** lead through scenario dungeons, grant experience and suitable magic-sealed equipment, and currently provide the route to level **115**. The Quest Book’s Location function directs the next destination. After one account character clears an applicable quest, another can use **Clear Quest** instead of repeating it. That separates another character’s progression from a compulsory replay of every scene. [S01](#s01)

## 2. Dungeon time and repeated entry

The Fatigue guide describes a daily activity allowance: ordinary rooms consume FP, the baseline is **156**, and the daily reset is **09:00 UTC**. A remaining point can permit entry even when the whole dungeon contains more rooms. Recovery potions have crafting, market, shop and event routes; the Neo: Premium Contract increases the allowance. Content-specific entry rules still need to be checked separately. [S02](#s02)

This is a resource spent on access, distinct from health lost during combat or character progress retained afterward. The design question is whether a chosen session ends with an attainable accomplishment, or whether eligibility, daily resets and preparation consume the time the player wanted to spend acting. Paid effects and actual player testimony are still under review.

## 3. Legibility, travel and accumulated complexity

The Sunken Depths system update introduced an **Adventure Navigator** that identifies equipment/character improvements behind a missing Fame requirement, shows content already completed for its entry/reward allowance, and recommends relevant activities. Its revised world map searches NPCs and dungeons, supplies location help, consolidates travel through warp points and saves favorites per account. The map changes with scenario knowledge. This is a navigation and access aid, not evidence that the underlying geography is permanently rebuilt by players. [S03](#s03)

The transferable hypothesis is modest: when a player cannot attempt a desired activity, explain the actual missing prerequisite and an attainable route to it. A large map or a numerical score is insufficient if the person cannot tell which action would help. The complete dossier will compare that accessibility with encounter skill, equipment acquisition and the burden of maintaining multiple characters.

## 4. Advancement and a practical home base

Advancement supplies subclass-specific skills. The current guide places the first Awakening at level 50, the second at 75 and Neo Awakening at 100; these unlock stronger skills and associated statistics. Side Story characters use Self-Awakening instead of an ordinary subclass tree. An established character therefore has both learned execution and an authored sequence of capability unlocks. [S05](#s05)

**Seria’s Room** is a login and logistics base in Elvenguard: portal, mailbox, safe and avatar closet. Mail can move eligible items between people or one person’s characters; the account safe becomes available at level 60 for account-bound transfers. Safe expansion increases storage. A guild application requires acceptance by an authorized member, and membership provides a teleport facility. These are recognizable reasons to visit a familiar place, but the documented room is not a player-built settlement. [S06](#s06)

## 5. Equipment composition, risk and remembered discoveries

The Set Point system combines exclusive points within one equipment set; common points support the set with the most exclusive points. Each set comprises twelve items. This makes composition matter beyond picking an isolated high-stat drop: replacing one item can change a threshold elsewhere in the loadout. [S07](#s07)

Reinforcement improves different statistics by slot. Percentage-damage attacks depend on physical/magical attack; fixed-damage attacks use Independent Attack. Refinement raises the latter and can fail without destroying the weapon or reducing its refinement. Reinforcement and Amplification can lose levels or destroy equipment at their higher thresholds. Amplification adds a dimensional base statistic; tainted equipment first needs purification. The guide also documents bounded safe-upgrade routes that preserve levels on failure and increase subsequent success chances. Consequently, an upgrade’s cost includes the risk to the existing item, not only the listed currency price. [S08](#s08)

The level-115 **Armory** records equipment per character when explicitly registered or destroyed through listed equipment operations. Equipment Conversion can exchange an equipped eligible item for a registered alternative while retaining enhancement properties. Its exact material requirements depend on rarity. This is a collection-backed route to another loadout: discovery can remain useful after the original object has gone. It does not establish freely tradable copies or account-wide access to every recorded item. [S09](#s09)

## 6. Skill changes and legible choice

**Skill Evolution** unlocks after Neo/Self-Awakening. Five VP buy five eligible options, with one of two options selected per skill. Its stated purpose is a functional change rather than increasing cooldown-adjusted total attack. The separate Skill Enhancement selection trades higher per-use damage against a smaller damage increase with cooldown reduction, or makes corresponding healing choices for eligible support skills. These are different kinds of improvement: changing how a move behaves, changing how often it is available, and raising output. Exact encounter usefulness still depends on when the player can safely use it. [S10](#s10)

The current normal-dungeon guide illustrates why entry rules need content names. **Revelation: Sky Embracing a Thousand Seas** has a limited daily version; after its two clears the slot changes to the normal version. The normal form randomly chooses the Grand Constellation Turtle Library or Castle of the Apostate, uses two FP per room and has five Fame-gated difficulty levels. These are repeatable entries, not proof of a single continuously shared landscape. [S11](#s11)
