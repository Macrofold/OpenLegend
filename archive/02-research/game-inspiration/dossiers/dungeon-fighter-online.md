# Dungeon Fighter Online — full research dossier

**G198 · October 28, 2026 — researched and independently reviewed.** This pass studies the PC Dungeon & Fighter / Dungeon Fighter Online regional services, using **DFO Global** for the operational mechanics below. Korean DNF, Chinese DNF, Global and mobile/franchise results remain separate. R01–R14 and applicable R15 are covered below, with regional scope, dated mechanics and evidence limits retained. No client, account, editor or payment was used.

[Requirements](../research-requirements.md) · [MMO research progress](../research-progress-mmos-2026-10-26.md)

The **Dungeon&Fighter franchise dates to 2005**. [E01](#e01) This study concerns the original PC action RPG, with its side-on 2D brawling, persistent characters and online social/party systems. **Neople** develops and publishes the Global Steam edition, a free-to-play Windows release listed from **August 9, 2016**; that is the Steam date, not the franchise’s beginning or the 2015 Global relaunch covered by the reviews. Current official Global guides describe level-115 play. The mobile game, DNF Duel and other franchise products are separate. [S25](#s25) [S01](#s01)

Nexon’s former **North American service** announced a June 13, 2013 shutdown, citing insufficient regional interest to sustain service; this is the operator’s stated reason, not proof of one failed mechanic. [S28](#s28) Neople’s separate **Global relaunch entered open beta in March 2015**, the service Henn actually returned to. [R05](#r05) The older North American reviews and later Global reviews therefore concern different service periods.

The OpenLegend question is how a persistent character can support satisfying short activities and an evolving repertoire. A lasting inventory or account does not by itself establish a continuously shared, mutable landscape. DFO’s individual dungeon entries, repeatable encounters and service timers require separate treatment from its social spaces.

## 1. Starting a character and choosing a role

The creation guide distinguishes **Cain** and **Sirocco**: characters are accessed through their selected server, while users from both can be seen. Choosing a character includes a unique permitted name. The role display distinguishes **Dealers**, oriented toward damage, from **Buffers**, who improve party capability through effects such as stat increases. Those labels describe practical contributions; they do not make every advancement within a family interchangeable. [S04](#s04)

A new character’s tutorial introduces controls and a class-specific background. **Act Quests** lead through scenario dungeons, grant experience and suitable magic-sealed equipment, and currently provide the route to level **115**. The Quest Book’s Location function directs the next destination. After one account character clears an applicable quest, another can use **Clear Quest** instead of repeating it. That separates another character’s progression from a compulsory replay of every scene. [S01](#s01)

## 2. Dungeon time and repeated entry

The Fatigue guide describes a daily activity allowance: ordinary rooms consume FP, the baseline is **156**, and the daily reset is **09:00 UTC**. A remaining point can permit entry even when the whole dungeon contains more rooms. Recovery potions have crafting, market, shop and event routes; the Neo: Premium Contract increases the allowance. Content-specific entry rules still need to be checked separately. [S02](#s02)

This is a resource spent on access, distinct from health lost during combat or character progress retained afterward. The design question is whether a chosen session ends with an attainable accomplishment, or whether eligibility, daily resets and preparation consume the time the player wanted to spend acting. The payment and reception sections examine both the documented access changes and actual player reactions.

## 3. Legibility, travel and accumulated complexity

The Sunken Depths system update introduced an **Adventure Navigator** that identifies equipment/character improvements behind a missing Fame requirement, shows content already completed for its entry/reward allowance, and recommends relevant activities. Its revised world map searches NPCs and dungeons, supplies location help, consolidates travel through warp points and saves favorites per account. The map changes with scenario knowledge. This is a navigation and access aid, not evidence that the underlying geography is permanently rebuilt by players. [S03](#s03)

The transferable hypothesis is modest: when a player cannot attempt a desired activity, explain the actual missing prerequisite and an attainable route to it. A large map or a numerical score is insufficient if the person cannot tell which action would help. That accessibility must be assessed alongside encounter skill, equipment acquisition and the burden of maintaining multiple characters.

## 4. Advancement and a practical home base

Advancement supplies subclass-specific skills. The current guide places the first Awakening at level 50, the second at 75 and Neo Awakening at 100; these unlock stronger skills and associated statistics. Side Story characters use Self-Awakening instead of an ordinary subclass tree. An established character therefore has both learned execution and an authored sequence of capability unlocks. [S05](#s05)

**Seria’s Room** is a login and logistics base in Elvenguard: portal, mailbox, safe and avatar closet. Mail can move eligible items between people or one person’s characters; the account safe becomes available at level 60 for account-bound transfers. Safe expansion increases storage. A guild application requires acceptance by an authorized member, and membership provides a teleport facility. These are recognizable reasons to visit a familiar place, but the documented room is not a player-built settlement. [S06](#s06)

## 5. Equipment composition, risk and remembered discoveries

The Set Point system combines exclusive points within one equipment set; common points support the set with the most exclusive points. Each set comprises twelve items. This makes composition matter beyond picking an isolated high-stat drop: replacing one item can change a threshold elsewhere in the loadout. [S07](#s07)

Reinforcement improves different statistics by slot. Percentage-damage attacks depend on physical/magical attack; fixed-damage attacks use Independent Attack. Refinement raises the latter and can fail without destroying the weapon or reducing its refinement. Reinforcement and Amplification can lose levels or destroy equipment at their higher thresholds. Amplification adds a dimensional base statistic; tainted equipment first needs purification. The guide also documents bounded safe-upgrade routes that preserve levels on failure and increase subsequent success chances. Consequently, an upgrade’s cost includes the risk to the existing item, not only the listed currency price. [S08](#s08)

The level-115 **Armory** records equipment per character when explicitly registered or destroyed through listed equipment operations. Equipment Conversion can exchange an equipped eligible item for a registered alternative while retaining enhancement properties. Its exact material requirements depend on rarity. This is a collection-backed route to another loadout: discovery can remain useful after the original object has gone. It does not establish freely tradable copies or account-wide access to every recorded item. [S09](#s09)

## 6. Skill changes and legible choice

Skills are learned with SP. **Skill Evolution**, available after Neo/Self-Awakening, supplies five VP for functional options; Skill Enhancement separately modifies damage/cooldown or eligible healing. This system replaced the old Rune/Talisman structure, so an old equipment guide can prescribe an obsolete route. The worked example below distinguishes a bigger number from a different usable technique. [S10](#s10)

The current normal-dungeon guide illustrates why entry rules need content names. **Revelation: Sky Embracing a Thousand Seas** has a limited daily version; after its two clears the slot changes to the normal version. The normal form randomly chooses the Grand Constellation Turtle Library or Castle of the Apostate, uses two FP per room and has five Fame-gated difficulty levels. These are repeatable entries, not proof of a single continuously shared landscape. [S11](#s11)

## 7. What the player actually controls

The current skill tables span male/female **Slayers, Fighters, Gunners, Mages and Priests**, plus **Thief, Knight, Demonic Lancer, Agent, Archer, Dark Knight and Creator**. A family contains materially different specializations: Fighters include Striker, Nen Master, Brawler and Grappler; Gunners include Ranger, Launcher and Mechanic; Mages include Summoner and Elementalist; support options include Crusader, Enchantress, Muse and Paramedic. A current table is more useful than a fixed class count copied from older marketing. [S20](#s20)

Sagoe’s 2015 Global assessment describes directional skill commands alongside movement, jumping and basic attacks; hotkeys and skill locking offer alternatives. Juggling and grappling extend combos. [R03](#r03) Official controller support subsequently supplied configurable keyboard-equivalent actions, combination keys, item/skill slots, mouse emulation, stick sensitivity and vibration settings. Analog tilt distinguishes walking from running; monster prompts can map to the appropriate controller action. The August 2023 implementation warned that some events would have incomplete support. Its dated support restrictions do not prove every later device configuration works. [S24](#s24)

Three examples show why a class list alone is insufficient:

- **Berserker** has attacks that require **Frenzy**. Neople explicitly added disabled icons, a message and a tooltip when that state is missing. The failure explanation identifies a missing condition instead of leaving the player to blame timing or a broken key. [S19](#s19)
- **Summoner** delegates attacks but still manages a composition. The 2022 renewal made **Loop of Domination** summon eligible minions together, excluding Kasijas and Lamos. **Hailom** periodically heals minions, protects Greater Spirits and grants status immunity; **Dark Lunacy** changes Louise’s behavior into an armored attacking state with its own restriction. This is authored combat-agent behavior, not simulated people with independent lives. [S18](#s18)
- **Muse** connects an Archer’s lyra bow and amplifier to group support. Note sequences activate scores; **Lovely Tempo** strengthens allies, while other songs heal, cleanse or shield. Her fiction of music moving magic becomes something the player performs during combat. [S12](#s12)

Arena provides a separate competitive activity. Neople’s September 2026 championship recruitment explicitly used ranked/free-battle experience and excluded specified advancements from that tournament. Its November championship was still scheduled, not an already-observed outcome on this research date. This establishes active PvP organization without assuming every class, equipment rule or regional mode is equivalent. [S26](#s26)

## 8. Encounters, failure and lasting progress

**Delezie’s raid** has solo, matching and normal modes; the published matching/normal formats use twelve people. Entry combines level, Fame and a story prerequisite. The modes share a weekly reward allowance, reset Tuesday at 09:00 UTC, rather than consuming FP. Failure restores normal-mode entry. Normal mode shares a Life Token allowance for token-based resurrection; solo/matching use automatic revival. Recovery therefore depends on mode, not a universal corpse or permadeath rule. [S15](#s15)

The game layers personal progress, roster convenience, consumable/entry budgets and repeated encounters. Death inside an attempt, a failed risky upgrade, an expired event item and service-side account retention are distinct loss mechanisms. The continuity and payment sections below keep them separate. A boss cleared again for rewards is not necessarily a second permanent change to the fiction’s landscape.

## 9. Named motives and the worlds those actions serve

The player is an **Adventurer**, not an unnamed manager of a loot spreadsheet. The authored stories repeatedly put apparently divine power, vulnerable communities and other people’s motives behind the next confrontation. These are scenario and character narratives; they do not establish a freeform branching political simulation.

**Origins give classes a cultural history.** Pandemonium was a city of **Terra** that drifted into space after the planet's destruction. Its inhabitants learned combat magic to survive among arriving creatures; **Hilder** is credited with discovering it. When Pandemonium joined Arad, experimentation with local spirits produced the **Circle Mages/Summoners**, with **Kate** remembered as the first monster summoner. Witches study ancient Terran science, while **Niu** developed Chasers during conflict between Kashipa and the Guardians. These histories distinguish what different playable disciplines value and practice. [S29](#s29)

**Empyrean's institutions carry the aftermath of resistance.** The Ispins history describes **Bakal's five-century tyranny** and the allied **Machine Revolution**. Archpriestess **Irine** united people across social ranks, then established the Black Roses as an official force. **Flo** developed special projectiles against dragon armor, became the first Spitfire and was appointed General; later Spitfires inherited that leadership association. A playable gunner discipline thereby reflects a society's answer to oppression and a continuing institution, rather than being only a weapon menu. [S30](#s30)

The **Pandemonium prequel, Chronicles of Luke**, makes missing adventurers the initial mystery: **Hilder and Kasijas** investigate, eventually encountering **Luke**. The **Red Witch** protects Central Park while concealing childhood sadness. **Domina Hailey** isolates herself because a curse gives her a nightly thirst for blood. Luke’s subordinate manipulates believers in Time Square. The story introduces threat through relationships, deception and an unsafe place to live, rather than only announcing a stronger monster. This account uses the published chapter text; its animations were not watched. [S17](#s17)

**Forged God, Nabel** connects scientific ambition to disaster. **Dr. Eldirh**, pursuing **Project Teana** on Terra, discovers a power she recognizes as dangerous and cannot contain, yet revives an artificial-god project to exploit it. **Zero**, an old colleague and Genesis Seeker, warns that personal ambition threatens everyone. **Nabel**, initially celebrated for controlling climate, becomes feared and cursed as human uses of that power devastate homes. **Anima’s** capacity to reconstruct matter extends into thought, and the institute’s account suspects hatred toward Eldirh. The playable confrontation with a “forged god” thus concerns the consequences of making power without accepting responsibility, not only supernatural spectacle. [S13](#s13)

**Delezie of Plague** resents both those who worship him and those who define him as a monster; his existence spreads suffering even when he did not seek their devotion. His synopsis longs for a death that perpetually eludes him. **Mahnarval** is unsettled when people choose to save a former enemy instead of taking revenge, undermining the harsh survival logic that sustained him. Unable to surrender that worldview, he defends its ruins. The associated Act quests open the Depths of Empty Sea and Azure Main, and the raid turns plague, threatened islands and coordinated defense into encounter rules. The character motives give the fight emotional stakes; they do not justify treating repeated raid clears as permanent global history. [S14](#s14)

The **Grand Constellation Turtle Library** stores Seon’s memories, carried through mist, inside a magically expanded tree associated with **Myre** and Record Keepers. The **Castle of the Apostate**, formerly a place of worship for **Lemidios**, now lacks its old hopeful prayers. Those distinct sites occupy a repeatable dungeon selection, so fictional history and encounter reuse coexist. [S11](#s11)

## 10. Crafting, useful objects and presentation

Gold pays for equipment and profession operations; the commercial offers below use CERA. Equipment moves through competing uses: wear it, retain it for another configuration, sell eligible goods, or consume it through disassembly/extraction. The Sunken Depths material revision connects **rare/unique equipment extraction** with an Alchemist’s **Bottle of Wind**, **Golden Powder** and **Dragon Heart**; Abello also sells profession inputs for Clear Cube Fragments. Enchanters use **Mana Solvent**, while Animators have distinct spirit inputs. Level-115 Primeval registration/disassembly can yield **Prime Stella** for weapon acquisition, but converted equipment does not yield that material again. That exception prevents a described conversion route from being mistaken for unlimited resource creation. [S21](#s21)

The profession interfaces support recipes, enchanting and bead creation, show enchant success percentages, and can consume crafting materials from the vault. The same convenience update colors party members by guild, Explorer Club friendship or recent companionship. Storage access and social recognition reduce repeated administrative work without changing what a recipe or teammate can actually do. [S27](#s27)

**Dungeon Life Online’s** workshop connects its quieter activities to useful objects: an Enchanting Device adds effects to equipment, a Bead Fusion Device converts monster cards, and portable disassemblers recover materials. Foods restore HP/MP or temporarily change combat statistics; for example, **Gold Rice Cake** restores both resources, while **Lucid Cider** removes harmful status effects. Shared consumable cooldowns and content-specific use limits still constrain preparation. [S23](#s23)

DFO’s presentation makes the authored class itself an object of attachment: sprite action, large illustrated identity and avatar/dye choices. The store explicitly links its side-scrolling format to arcade brawlers. [S25](#s25) Sagoe heard orchestral travel music and rock/electronic battle music. Korean voices limited his judgment, but Danjin’s older, eccentric English delivery had more personality for him. [R03](#r03) The positive opportunity is expressive movement, sound and a recognizable role; the risk is that many spectacular effects obscure the condition or danger the person needs to see. That readability question should be tested in actual overlapping play, not inferred from a clean promotional image.

## 11. Seven worked situations

These are **constructed illustrations of documented rules**, not personal play sessions. Actual reported returns and departures appear separately in the reception section.

### A. Perform support while participating in the fight

A Muse wants to keep allies effective during an encounter. She uses **Lovely Tempo**, then activates the appropriate Effector and enters the required notes to play a score. **Adrenaline Rhapsody** enhances that buff; **Rubato of My Heart** instead supplies a protective shield. Choosing a defensive score addresses a different situation from amplifying offense. **Hot Debut** supplies a directional reposition, and **Street Performance** can hold nearby enemies. The next decision is which help is needed and whether there is a safe opportunity to perform it. Support has its own execution vocabulary; simply selecting the Buffer label does not execute the sequence. [S12](#s12)

### B. Turn a remembered drop into another useful loadout

A character wants to replace a **Little Fairy Top (Unique)** with a registered **Pack Beta Top (Epic)**. Equipment Conversion retains enhancement properties, requires an Epic Soul plus **40,000 Gold or eight Malefic Residue**, and refunds a Unique Soul. The next check is the resulting set contribution: rarity alone does not establish the desired whole build. An unregistered alternative or ineligible item breaks this route. Discovery supports another loadout without making conversion free or creating unrestricted duplicate equipment. [S09](#s09) [S07](#s07)

### C. Repeat a short dungeon without mistaking continuity of movement for continuity of world

A level-115 character plans repeated **Endkeeper of Order** attempts. The published entry takes eight FP and forty-nine Doom Oracles and permits one to four players. After clearing, moving right begins a **new dungeon** while preserving buffs and minions; the Retry button remains an alternative. A bonus can waive the next entry’s materials, and a rift can lead to different rewards. The next choice is whether another attempt is worth the remaining access resources. Continuous movement lowers interruption, but it does not turn those newly loaded attempts into one shared persistent field or remove their costs. [S16](#s16)

### D. Help the raid by preventing a collective failure

In Delezie’s normal raid, a **Malefic Bomb Transporter** threatens an island while the group pursues larger targets. Ignoring it damages the island and raises the **Malefic Energy Gauge**, which fails the raid at full. Diverting a party to defeat it restores Plague Reversal and adds resurrection/consumable capacity. The group must weigh that recovery against lost boss time. A token-based resurrection spends the shared allowance; healing consumable counts are personal. A useful contributor therefore changes the group’s remaining possibilities, rather than merely raising an individual damage total. [S15](#s15)

### E. Build a place that another player can meaningfully visit

**Dungeon Life Online** begins with **Ei** seeking protection for the vulnerable **Ratties** in Pandemonium. Its editor places prescribed buildings on free grid cells. Raise the fountain, build Farm Storage and a Garden, buy seeds from **Goodman**, then spend AP and wait for crops. A Workshop permanently learns purchased manuals and consumes inputs to craft. **Happy Hearts** from requests improve the fountain, expanding possibilities. With a **Leave Permit**, advertise through an NPC in the Exchange Hub; another person visits that village and collects a fountain heart, also crediting its owner. The next choice can be production or making the visit appealing. AP renews daily, Sill stays local, and the design is a bounded village rather than a published programmable world. NPC gifts raise Likeness and unlock requests for assistance paid in Sill. [S22](#s22)

The 2025 system update moved Pioneer Ei to Elvenguard and added menu entry; the 2022 entry instructions therefore require that correction. These published native rules were inspected, without verifying a new account’s present access in the client. [S03](#s03)

### F. Choose a usable skill improvement

A player has reached Neo Awakening and wants a move to fit an encounter better. They allocate one of the five Evolution points to one of that skill’s two functional options, then compare its behavior. Separately, Enhancement can favor stronger individual casts or a smaller increase with a shorter cooldown. A shorter cooldown helps only if the next safe opportunity comes soon enough to use it. The limitation is encounter fit: the largest tooltip percentage is not automatically the most useful choice. [S10](#s10)

### G. Return for a different experience without treating a boost as competence

A returning player wants to try another class. Previously completed applicable Act Quests can be cleared through the account’s skip route, reducing repeated scenario work. [S01](#s01) If the desired activity is still inaccessible, the **Adventure Navigator** identifies the missing improvement or prerequisite. [S03](#s03) The productive next action is to learn the new repertoire and attempt a suitable activity, perhaps with someone willing to help. This is a design interpretation: faster eligibility alone cannot demonstrate understanding, social confidence or a satisfying reason to remain. The review accounts below show why those outcomes must be distinguished.

## 12. Persistence, paid capability and commercial outcome

### Persistence, access and absence

Steam and direct Neople accounts share servers and gameplay, although payment/DLC arrangements differ. This does not connect Korean, Chinese and Global characters into one service. No general cross-region or cross-server character-transfer service was established in the inspected documentation. [E04](#e04)

Persistence is conditional even outside combat. Neople’s **March 11, 2025** notice scheduled an April 15 reset of names belonging to characters/accounts without qualifying logins since January 1, 2023. Entering Seria’s Room protected the relevant name; a returning affected character/account could choose a new name. This was a name-reclamation announcement, not a character-progress wipe or evidence of an automatic annual schedule. [E08](#e08) Separately, the **September 8, 2026 terms** allow an account dormant for more than a year to expire and be deleted. That is a reserved policy power, not proof that every such account is deleted. [E07](#e07)

Loss recovery is also limited. The February 2024 support update describes up to five recoveries per account/year, requests within three days for items or twenty-five days for character deletion, and case-specific restrictions. Probability-based improvements or spent materials may not return. A recoverable mistake is therefore different from guaranteed preservation. [E10](#e10)

### What payment changes

The inspected **Neo Premium Plus** offer lists **1,560 CERA for thirty days**. It increases daily Fatigue capacity from **156 to 273**, adds dungeon experience and rental equipment, and removes the usual stamina penalty for leaving a dungeon. Skill-level benefits have explicit content exclusions. [E09](#e09) The ordinary Fatigue guide also permits recovery potions through crafting, the market, shops and events. [S02](#s02) Payment changes the amount of activity and preparation available; it is not merely a visual purchase.

The **October 20, 2026–January 26, 2027 Arad Royal Pass** costs **2,980 CERA**, alongside a free reward track. Its stated benefits include bonus dungeon rewards, entry-material discounts, rental equipment and an unlimited Warlord’s Cry item supplying an automatic **12% damage increase** inside eligible dungeons when placed in a quick slot; Arena, town and Training Center Challenge mode are excluded. Additional character selections cost **590 CERA** each, up to nine. Benefits and rentals end at the stated deadline, while specified earned rewards can become permanent. Selected Royal Pass characters cannot simply be reselected. [E05](#e05)

That combat effect is not exclusive to payment: the **October 6 Romeo’s Adventure Log** awards finite Warlord’s Cry potions through Legion clears, each granting the same stated percentage for sixty seconds. Its missions reset weekly, while accumulated objectives do not; unused potions expire November 3. [E12](#e12) The useful comparison is automatic/unlimited availability versus the ordinary acquisition and consumption burden, not a universal twelve-percent gap between paying and nonpaying players.

The **Black Ink Remanence** offer also separates appearance from power: its title, aura and creature options include combat modifiers. Some containers can be traded once before becoming account-bound; obtained items have their own restrictions. The creature is permanent, while unused containers expire December 15, 2026. Its creature-transfer item applies only within the same account/server and excludes ineligible creatures. Neither “permanent” nor “tradable” describes the entire purchase uniformly. [E06](#e06)

**Interpretation:** these are concrete paid capability and preparation effects. They do not by themselves establish competitive necessity, unaffordability through ordinary play, or a causal explanation of player loss. CO01–03 can compare the desired activity, baseline route, purchased change and expiry consequence without reversing OpenLegend’s accepted finite free/paid authoring model.

## 13. Production, operation and actual promotion

**An expressive action format matched the team's capabilities.** In his July 2009 interview, director **Yunjong Kim** explains that 2D suited side-scrolling action, preserved the character illustrations' look and used Neople's existing strengths. He recalled initially allocating about five months to a small project, then expanding resources as the market response exceeded expectations. This is an intended initial scope, not a verified five-month full production history. During closed beta the team collected daytime feedback and revised overnight. Kim described Nexon's acquisition as bringing international operating infrastructure that Neople lacked. The useful hypothesis is to prove a distinctive playable activity with the team's strengths, then expand from evidence; the interview does not establish that continuous overnight work is sustainable. [P01](#p01)

**Success made delivery harder.** Former DNF technical director **ChangKyu Song's 2016 GDC slides** describe separate regional branches growing until a major integration could take four months. The eventual merge/test pipeline took two to three years to establish across seven teams. Build automation and data validation helped; indiscriminate warning cleanup offered less benefit than examining frequently changed files and their dependencies. Focused work roughly halved build time in the reported case. This is dated production evidence, not a benchmark for OpenLegend or an instruction to preserve development saves. It supports focusing validation and architectural boundaries on actual changes and operational costs. [P02](#p02)

Neople's **September 1–29, 2026 creator recruitment** explicitly sought streams, videos, guides and fan art, with selection announcements scheduled for October 6. Applicants needed a level-115 main character. Monthly output requirements and additional missions were tied to in-game rewards: a title, consumables, a Premium contract and the latest avatar package, with a higher reward tier for extra activity. This is a documented promotion program and a route for knowledgeable players to help others; no campaign spend, acquisition conversion or cash earnings were disclosed. It is not a market for playable mods. [P03](#p03)

**Communication can expose an operational mismatch.** In April 2023, director **Jong Min Park** described his own Bakal raid clear and reported that checking latency only after entering forced party rearrangement. Korean ping thresholds were unsuitable for many international groups. He also acknowledged complaints about restrictions and difficulties new/returning players had understanding current equipment, and described intended improvements. These are dated diagnoses and plans, not proof each remedy shipped or that personal communication independently caused retention. [P04](#p04)

## 14. Player creation and distribution

The native village workflow is in §11E.

### External creation: R15 applies

There is a documented **NPK image/UI modification ecosystem**, distinct from native avatar choices, promotional fan art, mobile releases and **DNF Duel**. Its practical scope is local presentation/resource editing, not authority to rewrite a live server's economy or quests. The inspected sources establish existence and workflows, not population size or current Global compatibility for every patch.

**Creators and motives.** **MagicSunz's Chinese-PC Muse patch** replaces note/buff icons with letters, numbers or readable words. The author explains that attention had been shifting between skill and UI bars, then revised the patch for Chinese automatic note playback; v1.1/v1.2 improved the presentation. The August 7, 2023 release supplies previews and an NPK installation instruction. Its site displays an attachment/points mechanism and tipping interface; actual receipts or income were not available. This is a concrete readability intervention with a regional rules dependency, not a universal approved accessibility feature. [M02](#m02) **LHCGreg's DFOToolBox**, archived in 2017, provides an NPK viewer and image-to-GIF utility under Apache 2.0. **DressUp_Fighter_Online's 2021 extraction tutorial** explains transparent cut-in extraction and stream-transition use. These demonstrate tools and creative expression without establishing permission to redistribute game art. [M03](#m03) [M04](#m04)

**Representative authored result:** a replacement class appearance. This is a reconstruction from tool-author documentation; it is not a claim MagicSunz used this tool or that the result was executed here.

1. **Author and preview.** `carlos999-hqsama/dnf-patch-studio` documents selecting an `ImagePacks2` directory, exporting class sprite frames as a grid, repainting externally and importing the result. The tool removes the background and aligns feet, then makes a percent-prefixed override package. It deliberately leaves animation files untouched. Its TypeScript/Vite/WebAssembly tooling needs Chrome/Edge file-system support; the WASM binary is not committed and requires a separate build. MIT applies to the tool, not automatically to the extracted artwork. [M05](#m05)
2. **Validate a meaningful result.** The architecture document separates animation preview from real client validation. It explicitly leaves Windows directory selection, actual in-game appearance/alignment, format acceptance and large-character memory use to real-machine verification. A useful proposed test is to inspect idle/movement/attacks with the selected outfit and verify the intended visual change without missing or displaced frames. That test was not performed. The documented data unit is one skin NPK; skeletal variants require expansion, so a superficially correct image is insufficient. [M06](#m06)
3. **Package and share.** MagicSunz’s release distributes a versioned NPK patch with visual previews and a recipient directory instruction. [M02](#m02) A reviewable release should specify region/client revision, affected class/resources, dependencies, original-art permissions and conflicts. This proposed checklist follows from the documented dependencies; it is not a claimed publisher standard. The artifact changes client presentation rather than supplying a server-authoritative gameplay script.
4. **Recipient use.** MagicSunz directs the recipient to copy the NPK into the game’s `ImagePacks2` folder. [M02](#m02) Separately, **Dogend’s DNFImagePacks2Manager** documents selecting the game path, importing an NPK by browse/drag-and-drop and enabling it. It can export configurations for backup/sharing. This documents installation/control, without certifying that these independent tools work together on current DFO Global. [M07](#m07)
5. **Revise and remove.** The author can repaint/repack a new override; recipients can disable or delete selected files with the manager. Conflicting replacements of the same resource and changes in regional base data remain compatibility questions; no universal precedence or automatic conflict resolution was verified. A local display replacement does not establish migration of account state. Removal should be followed by checking the original appearance; this is a proposed acceptance check, not a successful rollback test. [M05](#m05) [M07](#m07)

**Service rights and earnings.** DFO's current terms prohibit unauthorized software that modifies code or user experience and reserve the service's intellectual property. A repository license or a creator's historical download does not grant live-service permission or game-asset redistribution rights. [E07](#e07) The official creator program rewards media/helpful content with in-game goods; neither that program nor the inspected mod pages establishes these modders' cash earnings. OpenLegend's relevant route is supported, declared pack dependencies/rights and recipient use through existing PACK01–03, not adoption of unofficial client changes or a legacy-save compatibility promise.

## 16. Critique, opportunity and route into OpenLegend

**The useful positive proposition is an expressive repertoire that remains worth practicing.** DFO offers a different reason to care about a persistent character from owning territory: a particular rhythm, sound, movement style or supportive performance. Its cases also show how remembered discoveries, readable prerequisites and a useful place can support another experiment. These are hypotheses about mechanisms, not a claim that copying the class roster, grind or business model would reproduce its scale.

**The critical distinctions survive review.** Character persistence does not make every encounter a persistent open world. A free acquisition path does not make a paid shortcut irrelevant. A story completion, a later return, a stated intention and a reported installation-stage exit are different outcomes. Most importantly, the successful franchise, the closed North American operation, the current Global service and current regional PC trends do not have one interchangeable commercial history. Five older full reviews and a small Steam sample illuminate experiences; they cannot identify the proportion affected or isolate a single cause of decline.

The prospective opportunity is to make an activity enjoyable before stacking maintenance systems around it. The failure hypothesis is that prerequisites, repeated trivial work, unreadable equipment and access friction can consume the attention intended for expressive play. Those hypotheses need observed choices: what a person tries, understands, contributes, repeats voluntarily and stops doing. They should not become more mandatory daily routines merely because this successful comparison contains them.

**Engine/world boundary:** an engine may support actions and effects, scoped agents, item transformations, authored places, persistence boundaries and declared packages. DFO’s exact classes, stat formulas, FP, daily reset, raid timetable, currencies and fiction are authored game choices. None becomes a universal OpenLegend requirement. Likewise, live-service continuity expectations do not amend the repository’s [development-save policy](../../../../AGENTS.md#development-save-policy). The accepted finite free/paid authoring and creator model remains the project’s own commercial design.

**Organization decision:** keep this evidence in one dossier and route only the cross-game observations through existing owners. No new design document, needs-design ID or DFO-shaped task catalogue is warranted. The substantive core, worked examples and source limits remain reviewable here; implementation work should arise from the existing owner’s design decision and acceptance evidence.
