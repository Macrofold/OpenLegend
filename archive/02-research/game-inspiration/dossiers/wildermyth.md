## A persistence boundary worth getting right

The current wiki describes Legacy recruitment as selective: equipment is scaled to the receiving chapter, augments are not carried over, and retained abilities depend on legacy rank. Most themes and maimings persist, with explicit exceptions such as petrified limbs. Promoting a returning hero saves the new version; merely using that hero in another campaign does not automatically overwrite the old legacy. Thus recognition and unrestricted power retention are not the same promise. [W1]

The equipment page separately says heroes cannot freely pass gear among themselves. Different armor branches trade mobility and protection; Armors and Skins adds branches beyond the base game's choices. A missing hand or transformation can make a former loadout unavailable. This is a deliberately bounded equipment model, not an unrestricted survival inventory. [W2]
