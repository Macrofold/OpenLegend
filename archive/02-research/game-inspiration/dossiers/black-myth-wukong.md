# G76 — Black Myth: Wukong

[Library](../README.md) · [Roster](../research-roster.md) · [Progress](../research-progress.md) · [Requirements](../research-requirements.md)

**Full research pass, September 26, 2026.** Original campaign, post-completion Challenges and Journeyer's Charts are distinguished. Five substantive independent review bodies and contrasting helpful Steam accounts were read. The checkpoint's disagreements and update corrections are preserved below. This standalone expansion-roster dossier replaces no original packet or selective mechanics chapter. Global preservation/integration gates remain separate. Research only: no accepted OpenLegend design, runtime change or implementation backlog.

## 1. Identity and player promise

Game Science's single-player, third-person action RPG released on PC and PS5 on August 20, 2024; the Xbox Series version followed on August 20, 2025. The player is the **Destined One**, not a blank custom class and not simply interchangeable with Sun Wukong in the opening spectacle. The game's premise revisits the legacy of Journey to the West through encounters with powerful rivals and the histories behind them. Native co-op, faction diplomacy and a player-controlled party are not part of this promise. [W8](#w8) [W16](#w16) [W17](#w17)

A characteristic session moves from a Keeper's Shrine through an authored region, discovers a side route or adversary, then returns to preparation after a difficult attempt. The game has six chapters rather than Elden Ring's broadly open continental route structure. Early play introduces a small staff-and-dodge vocabulary; later play gives more ways to arrange magic, equipment and recovery around it. Whether this feels focused or repetitive is a central reception disagreement, not something the genre label settles. [W2](#w2) [W4](#w4)

The ordinary campaign's lack of a difficulty selector is a historical design boundary, not a correct description of every later mode. The December 2024 update added three difficulty levels to Return of Rivals rematches. Launch complaints about absent maps must likewise be separated from the later Journeyer's Charts. This study does not assume an old review tested those additions. [W7](#w7)

## 2. Actions, combat and capability families

Movement, sprinting, jumping, dodging, light and heavy attacks, stance changes, spell use, drinking and short-range interaction comprise the recurring vocabulary. Stamina limits physical actions. Focus rewards building an opening before spending it on a stronger response. Smash, Pillar and Thrust change charged/heavy actions and follow-ups rather than replacing the entire basic light-attack sequence. A player learns the enemy's timing while choosing where to allocate skill points. Free skill respecialization makes an unsuccessful approach revisable. [W4](#w4)

Magic is not one unrestricted pool of transformations. **Immobilize** holds an eligible enemy briefly; **Cloud Step** provides concealment and a decoy-oriented opening; **Rock Solid** is a spell-based defensive answer. **A Pluck of Many** creates duplicates. These operations support different intentions: gaining damage time, repositioning, surviving an attack or dividing attention. Mana and cooldowns prevent treating every option as indefinitely available. Wood's review values those decisions but finds their interesting variations insufficient to eliminate repetition in the core attack chain. [W1](#w1)

**Spells, transformations and Spirits require separate descriptions.** Red Tides is acquired through the optional Guangzhi encounter. Azure Dust follows the Man-in-Stone route. Ebon Flow comes from dueling Yin Tiger. A transformation changes the player's form and moveset for a bounded period; a Spirit skill is a brief acquired action, not a permanently traveling NPC or the same duration model. Ring of Fire and Spell Binder offer further alternatives to repeated disabling spells. Somersault Cloud belongs to Chapter 6 traversal, not unrestricted flight through all previous maps. [W9](#w9) [W3](#w3)

The old man's blessed-gourd service enables Spirit acquisition; the shrine can recover an already defeated enemy's uncollected Spirit. This is distinct from a boss becoming unavailable before being defeated. The former is collection recovery; the latter is a quest/world-state exclusion. Conflating them would incorrectly promise that every missed reward can always be reclaimed. [W12](#w12) [W15](#w15)

### Item and resource inventory

| Family | What it does and where the boundary matters |
| --- | --- |
| Staff equipment and armor | Crafted/acquired equipment changes statistics, bonuses and specialization around the staff-focused moveset. Armor enhancement can preserve a favored set. It is not a broad selection of unrelated weapon classes or an ordinary weapon-wear economy. [W1](#w1) [W4](#w4) |
| Gourd | The vessel carrying recovery uses. Luojia Fragrant Vines increase the starting gourd's capacity; later names can describe an upgraded version rather than an additional independent object. Special gourds trade recovery against other benefits. [W10](#w10) |
| Drink | Determines the recovery mixture. Starting Coconut Wine improves through Awaken Wine Worms; its age/name and healing change. Other drinks are found or become available through chapter-gated purchases. [W11](#w11) |
| Soaks | Modify the chosen drink. Shen Monkey's Brew interface teaches this with Gall Gem. This is an equipment configuration attached to a recurring action, not a second healing button for every ingredient. [W15](#w15) |
| Will and crafting materials | Currency and components support purchases, medicine and equipment. Skill allocation and money are different resources; possessing spare points does not pay a material bill. [W4](#w4) |
| Sparks and meditation | Experience and discoveries support skill development. Investment changes stance/spell behavior and basic capacities, while respecialization lets preparation respond to a particular encounter. [W1](#w1) |
| Mind Cores | Xu Dog converts them into Celestial Medicines affecting health, mana, stamina, defense, recovery or resistance. These allocations can be revised through his service; they are not simply another name for Sparks. [W14](#w14) |
| Vessels | Distinct equipped artifacts acquired through major or secret sequences: Fireproof Mantle, Wind Tamer, Weaver's Needle and Plantain Fan. They connect optional investigation with a new combat resource. [W13](#w13) |
| Curios and medicine | Passive equipment and consumable preparation support a build without changing the protagonist's identity. Later Challenge rewards add more examples; those are not all launch-campaign drops. [W3](#w3) [W7](#w7) |

**Interpretation:** the notable design pattern is the number of meanings attached to one familiar action. Drinking can be about immediate survival, a temporary benefit, clone support or resisting a hazard. Depth comes from comprehensible relationships between the pieces, not simply adding another inventory page.

## 3. Progression, recovery, economics and revisitation

A shrine is a preparation and travel node, not merely a death checkpoint. It restores health and mana and connects previously reached regions. The character's advancement includes skills, attributes, armor, recovery configuration and newly learned actions. None of these guarantees that an unfamiliar attack sequence can be ignored. Conversely, repeated failure need not demand repeating the exact same build. [W15](#w15)

Mind Cores arrive through bosses, purchases and some chests. Turning them into persistent attributes can reduce a particular vulnerability. Reforging involves the service's Will cost, so free skill-point reallocation should not be generalized into a claim that every form of revision has no cost. Resistances can be useful without replacing learning the attack itself. [W14](#w14)

Gourd differences make a concrete tradeoff. **Plaguebane Gourd** sacrifices some healing for an offensive benefit; **Jade Lotus/Jade Guanyin** can support nearby duplicates; **Stained Jade** speeds drinking; **Fiery Gourd** adds protection against chill and a stamina-related benefit. These are supported combinations with authored properties, not arbitrary chemistry. Some rewards are missable: Stained Jade depends on resolving Scorpionlord before the Duskveil encounter removes the opportunity. [W10](#w10)

After the ending, **Continue Journey** returns before the final boss and permits revisiting regions. **Enter a New Cycle** begins New Game Plus instead. Region access does not resurrect every missed NPC or encounter, and another cycle is not a reversible menu toggle. New cycles also supply further equipment/material opportunities. Recovery from a failed fight, recovery of an uncollected Spirit and recovery of a missed quest therefore have different rules. [W11](#w11) [W15](#w15)

The failure experience in the sampled accounts is primarily repeated attempts, relearning timing and deciding whether to revise preparation. This pass did not independently instrument every loss/consumable rule, so it does not import Elden Ring's dropped-rune retrieval system or invent a durability penalty. The direct player accounts demonstrate that a lenient or costly recovery loop cannot be judged from boss difficulty alone: some players enjoy many attempts, while others find the surrounding traversal the greater burden. [W6](#w6)

## 4. People, place, story and service relationships

**Chapter, quest and character spoilers in this section.** Black Wind Mountain, Yellow Wind Ridge, the New West, the Webbed Hollow, the Flaming Mountains and Mount Huaguo provide different local histories around the larger Wukong legacy. The official premise stresses the motives behind adversaries, rather than treating their mythological appearance as sufficient characterization. The player discovers these through authored encounters, dialogue, journal material and chapter presentation. There is no freeform conversation engine or general reputation meter. [W8](#w8) [W3](#w3)

Zhu Bajie offers a companion presence during parts of the journey, but is not a freely assembled tactical party slot. An encounter such as Apramana Bat can depend on his presence. The game also contains service relationships with more permanent practical effects: Shen Monkey improves recovery equipment, Xu Dog supports medicine and attributes, and Yin Tiger supplies armor work as well as a duel. These examples distinguish accompanying someone, unlocking a service and acquiring a combat ability. [W15](#w15) [W18](#w18)

**Zodiac Village is a particularly useful authored social project.** After the Chen Loong encounter, the requested medicine comes from choosing Xu Dog's conversation option, not purchasing an arbitrary healing item. Delivering it yields the Ruyi Scroll route to the village. Shen Monkey and Xu Dog relocate there; Chen Loong accepts seeds and later provides harvests; Yin Tiger adds armor enhancement and his challenge. This consolidates an earned network of services without becoming an autonomous settlement builder. [W18](#w18)

Secret regions make local curiosity relevant to combat progression. Three bells lead to the Ancient Guanyin Temple and the Fireproof Mantle sequence. The drunken-boar chain leads to Sahali and Wind Tamer; the repeated Venom Daoist encounters open Purple Cloud Mountain and the route to Weaver's Needle. Plantain Fan instead follows the main story. These acquisition paths differ intentionally: not every powerful tool is obtained by the same reward channel. [W13](#w13)

**Interpretation:** helping someone can matter even without a numerical friendship meter. A remembered relationship may become a place, a service or a new recurring routine. The limit is equally important: the village is an authored destination with specific residents, not evidence that any monster can be befriended and assigned any profession.

## 5. Worked interactions

These are **constructed rules-based illustrations**, not claimed firsthand play, captured incidents or tested balance recommendations.

**A. Revise a weakness without discarding the character.** Intention: survive a repeated elemental threat. Conditions: Xu Dog's service and available Mind Core allocation. Actions: revise attributes toward the relevant resistance, then retry. Interaction: persistent growth becomes encounter preparation. Result: a mistake may be more survivable. Next decision: retain that allocation or return to general-purpose attributes. Limit: the fee and service are separate from free Spark respec, and resistance does not eliminate every attack consequence. [W14](#w14)

**B. Make healing an offensive commitment.** Intention: improve a damage window. Conditions: a gourd whose recovery tradeoff grants an attack benefit. Actions: equip it knowingly and drink when the benefit can be used. Interaction: a recovery action also changes offense. Result: familiar timing gains a second purpose. Next decision: exploit the window or retreat because the smaller recovery was insufficient. Limit: this is not automatically better than the ordinary gourd; the lost healing matters. [W10](#w10)

**C. Recover a reward without repeating its fight.** Intention: obtain a Spirit defeated before collection was possible. Conditions: the acquisition service is now unlocked and the reward remains recoverable. Actions: use the shrine's Spirit-retrieval option. Interaction: delayed capability and past accomplishment are reconciled. Result: an earlier victory need not be repeated. Next decision: test the action against the existing loadout. Limit: this does not recover an enemy never defeated before its disappearance condition. [W12](#w12)

**D. Turn a favor into a support hub.** Intention: assist Chen Loong. Conditions: his request and access to Xu Dog. Actions: talk to obtain the specific medicine, return, then use the Ruyi Scroll. Interaction: a local request opens several useful services. Result: the journey's helpers become easier to revisit together. Next decision: supply seeds, improve armor or challenge Yin Tiger. Limit: buying a generic consumable is not equivalent to resolving the dialogue condition. [W18](#w18)

**E. Pursue an alternate capability through an optional route.** Intention: expand available tools before another demanding encounter. Conditions: knowledge of an unfinished secret-area lead. Actions: follow its authored sequence and obtain the associated vessel. Interaction: exploration changes the next combat attempt. Result: the detour yields a new operation, not merely more health. Next decision: learn its timing or continue the main route. Limit: this does not mean every side quest has an equally powerful reward or every vessel counters every opponent. [W13](#w13)

**F. Revisit mastery without replaying the entire campaign.** Intention: practice a defeated rival. Conditions: campaign completion and the December 2024 Challenge feature. Actions: select Return of Rivals and a difficulty, or choose a Gauntlet with additional constraints. Interaction: remembered encounters become configurable practice and mastery content. Result: the player can return for combat rather than a full narrative cycle. Next decision: adjust the challenge or seek a reward. Limit: unencountered opponents are not unlocked merely by opening the menu. [W7](#w7)

## 6. Presentation, controls and iteration

The sampled criticism repeatedly values monumental scenery, detailed material surfaces, creature designs and forceful staff animation. Musical and chapter-ending sequences contribute a presentation rhythm distinct from the fights. Yet visual detail can mislead when a plausible path ends at an invisible wall. This is not just a complaint about map size: it concerns whether the world reliably communicates an actionable route. [W2](#w2) [W3](#w3) [W6](#w6)

Camera behavior in confined spaces and collision boundaries can undermine otherwise responsive combat. The PS5 launch review raises performance and latency concerns; the PC reviews report different experiences. Those are tested-version observations, not a synchronized benchmark or proof that one platform was uniformly unaffected. The official product page supports 3D audio and lists Chinese/English voice options, but a listed feature is not a comprehensive accessibility evaluation. [W5](#w5) [W8](#w8)

Game Science's December 2024 Steam release notes add Journeyer's Charts, obtained first in Black Wind Mountain and through later shrine purchases. They also introduce post-completion rematches and gauntlets. Later October 2025 and January 2026 announcements describe technical compatibility, rendering and crash fixes. Announcing a fix is not independent evidence that every related problem disappeared. This history preserves the original criticism while showing that the shipped interface was not frozen at launch. [W7](#w7)

## 7. Production, adaptation and discoverability

Feng Ji's Xinhua interview, accessed through an explicitly translated and edited English republication, describes choosing Journey to the West from the team's attachment to it rather than merely following a theme trend. He emphasizes the stories of adversaries, not only the central hero. The original Xinhua link returned no readable body, so this pass does not claim to have verified the Chinese transcript word for word. [W19](#w19)

The same interview offers a concrete localization example: translating Tudigong as **Keeper** aims to communicate a local relationship and protective knowledge rather than imply an all-powerful lord. Some names retain their cultural specificity. **Interpretation:** localization can preserve the role a character plays in a player's mental model without insisting that every term be either literally translated or left unexplained. Cultural specificity and accessibility are not automatically opposites.

Feng Ji describes the 2020 demonstration as a recruitment effort whose response exceeded expectations. That gives the initial public artifact a production purpose as well as a marketing purpose. A later translated practitioner interview describes cut encounter ideas and revised chapter pacing, but its unofficial translation was not used here to certify engine architecture, staffing percentages or precise production costs. No audited budget, total labor count or source-code inspection was obtained. [W19](#w19) [W20](#w20)

The original Game Science announcement, reproduced by Gematsu on August 23, 2024, reports **more than ten million copies sold within three days** and **three million simultaneous players across all platforms**. Neither is a 2026 lifetime figure. Concurrent players, copies sold and revenue are different measures; the dossier does not multiply units by a US price and call that profit. [W16](#w16)

The commercial model is premium single-player software with edition extras and later updates, not a mandatory ongoing MMO subscription. The 2025 Xbox release extends distribution rather than constituting a different sequel. Steam community spaces provide routes for reviews, screenshots, guides and announcements. **Sharing hypothesis:** distinctive boss victories, chapter animations, cultural explanations and unusual combat configurations offer different reasons to share. The sources do not quantify their individual contributions to sales. [W17](#w17) [W6](#w6)

The positive account by **ʙʟxᴄᴋʙᴜꜱᴛᴀ**, showing 153.7 hours, values persistence culminating in successful timing. **Isupposeitsdan**, showing eight hours, liked bosses but disliked travel and wanted more satisfying build variety. **ɴᴀɢᴀᴅᴢᴜ** particularly valued chapter animations while acknowledging invisible walls. The useful disagreement is specific. A compact weapon vocabulary can produce mastery or feel repetitive. A mapless route can reward attention or waste it. Difficulty can be satisfying in the encounter yet frustrating in the trip between attempts. Calling the game a Soulslike or refusing that label does not answer any of those questions.

## 9. OpenLegend transfer hypotheses

**One action, several understandable configurations.** The gourd system suggests adding depth to a familiar action before proliferating buttons. The dependency is clear explanation of which layer changes capacity, recovery and secondary effects. A failure mode is making the user compare nearly identical descriptions across unrelated menus.

**Earn service relationships through concrete help.** A favor can change where someone lives and what recurring support becomes available. For OpenLegend, the interesting test is whether the player remembers the person as well as the benefit. Avoid claiming that an authored hub justifies a general migration/economy simulation.

**Separate acquiring a form from borrowing an action.** A full transformation and a brief Spirit skill impose different learning, animation, AI and balance requirements. A reusable mechanic should name its scope: whose body changes, which actions become available, how long it lasts and what ends it. “Can transform” is not a sufficient specification.

**Make failure revision cheap where experimentation is the point.** Free skill respec invites adaptation; scarce upgrade materials and service fees preserve other commitments. The balance can be more nuanced than making every decision permanent or every cost disappear. Test whether players actually revise a build or simply repeat the first attempt indefinitely.

**Distinguish visible scenery from usable space.** Rich scenery increases the cost of misleading affordances. A world need not be fully traversable, but its limits should be readable enough that players can form reliable expectations. More accurate collision feedback may matter more than another square kilometer.

**Recover accomplishments without erasing meaningful consequences.** Shrine retrieval of uncollected Spirits protects a recorded success. A missable quest remains a different decision. An OpenLegend persistence model should specify which state is protected and which expires, rather than use one generic recovery policy for everything.

**Let later modes serve a different return intention.** A rematch mode is for practice; a new cycle is for replay; a map is for navigation. Bundling them into one catch-all endgame system can obscure their distinct audiences. None requires pretending the original campaign offers unlimited procedural stories.
