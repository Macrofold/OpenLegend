# Fantasy Westward Journey — original PC dossier

**Game:** G199, 梦幻西游电脑版 (Fantasy Westward Journey, original PC).  
**Status:** Research in progress; expanded substantive checkpoint, not independently reviewed.  
**Research date:** October 28, 2026.

## 1. Identity, scope and the useful comparison

NetEase developed this Chinese PC MMORPG in-house: open beta began in December 2003 and commercial service in January 2004. [E05](#e05) Current reporting calls the 2D game **Fantasy Westward Journey Online**, previously **Fantasy Westward Journey II**, and lists its companion and standalone mobile products separately. [E04](#e04)

The scope covers the original PC lineage, including the time-charged service and the newer Changwan variant where explicitly identified. The mechanical examples describe the publisher's documented PC systems; they do not establish identical rules across every service. English names below are descriptive glosses of Chinese terms.

The useful OpenLegend comparison is a socially persistent world whose encounters use a bounded command interface. Its questions are concrete: what can another person contribute, what does a crafted object enable, and how does a place become useful to its occupants? Longevity alone does not establish that every retained system is enjoyable or that one payment model caused success.

## 2. Persistent worlds and continuity

Original PC realms retain character wealth and equipment between sessions. They are separate social/economic populations, not one universal world; the mechanics section distinguishes shared travel from separately initiated battles. [E05](#e05) Continuity also has explicit boundaries:

- **Absence and unpaid access:** the time-server guide describes a restricted experience state when points are owed, including trading/chat/group limits. Returning-player support restores eligible houses’ cleanliness and durability to 50 and offers limited recovery benefits. Neither source establishes an unconditional forever-retention or inactivity-deletion guarantee. [E06](#e06) [E14](#e14)
- **Housing custody:** a character can own several houses but occupy one; moving out enables a house deed to be traded, retaining its room configuration and feng shui information. This is a bounded game asset with explicit transfer procedures. The original transfer FAQ preserves access to an owned house after changing servers. [E10](#e10) [E11](#e11)
- **Time-server transfer:** the ordinary published price is 1,200 jade per character, with eligibility, destination and surcharge exceptions and a 360-hour cooldown. Some ongoing dungeon/artifact progress is cleared; certain relationships require dissolution or joint travel. Transfer locks also restrict later asset sales, subject to marketplace exceptions. [E10](#e10)
- **Changwan transfer:** the separate 2026 rules auction limited entry rights starting at 2,400 paid jade; the acquired right expires after 60 days, and another transfer requires 90 days plus earned experience. Particular server exceptions changed in September. A paid right therefore does not mean unrestricted mobility or preserved social context. [E12](#e12)
- **Merger and test scope:** a September 2025 announcement scheduled selected mergers after weighted voting, with ID/name collision handling and temporary retention of old entry names. This documents conditional consolidation, not a universal progress wipe or proof of decline. Separately published test-server rules permit irregular wipes and distinguish copied test characters from original-server state. [E13](#e13) [E15](#e15)

## 3. What the player actually does

The publisher's basic interface uses mouse-directed movement, clicking nearby NPCs to talk, picking up ground items, and using inventory objects. Destroying an item requires confirmation. Separate controls hide other characters or market stalls to make crowded locations navigable. [S14](#s14)

The general combat manual distinguishes visible encounters, hidden encounters triggered during travel, and explicitly initiated attacks. Players submit commands for themselves and a summoned creature during a timed round; speed influences action order. The documented default for a missed command is a physical attack, and disconnecting does not immediately remove the character from an ongoing fight. These are dated general rules, not verification of every modern event variant. [S03](#s03)

The build vocabulary includes constitution, magic, strength, endurance and agility. The allocation manual gives each level automatic increases plus freely assigned points; agility affects speed, while strength and endurance affect different sides of physical combat. Racial growth differences make identical point distributions produce different results. [S16](#s16)

The current school index distinguishes ordinary destiny schools from adventure schools. **Datang Government (大唐官府)** ties its military identity to Cheng Yaojin, weapon knowledge and physical attacks. Its documented **横扫千军** attack strikes three times, requires sufficient health and imposes a recovery round; **兵器谱** also supplies equipment identification. A class therefore offers useful services beyond its damage role. Current pages retain older publication dates while incorporating later additions, so the old twelve-school count is not a current roster claim. [S04](#s04)

### Travel and the party boundary

Named transport NPCs connect distant places: the East Sea Bay's Old Shrimp leads to Dragon Palace, while guides connect mainland and Putuo Mountain. Guild transport returns members to their guild scene. These documented connections make travel a network of places and services, rather than evidence for one physically seamless terrain. [S25](#s25)

A normal documented party holds up to five people. Its leader accepts applicants and moves the group; followers' movement commands do not independently move the party. Members can leave, and successful battle escape affects membership. This reduces travel coordination work while concentrating control. It is a distinct boundary from the optional tactical command permissions in case A. [S26](#s26)

## 4. Goals, progression and time

School duties offer an intelligible short-session loop: visit the master, obtain a letter, purchase or capture request, patrol, return and receive money, experience and school contribution. The manual varies assignments with progression, and gives the first twenty daily completions doubled rewards. Some assignments survive logout; others have a deadline. The same structure that teaches places and useful objects can become an obligation when its reward schedule dominates voluntary goals. [S21](#s21)

Longer development is more than character level: attributes, school techniques, auxiliary skills, summons, equipment and collective facilities provide separate investments. The documented **Ascension** quest illustrates a consequential transition: a sufficiently advanced character undertakes Wu Gang's trials, accepts a level reduction and obtains higher development limits and new abilities. Previously usable equipment can become temporarily ineligible. These are the dated ascension system's consequences, not a statement of today's universal level cap. [S27](#s27)

One returning player's earlier level-69 Datang main pursued equipment and local-arena strength; a stronger opponent exposed another equipment tier. This documents a personally meaningful intermediate build, not the formal rules of level-bracket competition. [R03](#r03)

The time-bonus manual gives weekly double-experience time that can be frozen, plus a separately accumulating refinement allowance for eligible activities. Additional refinement can be earned by remaining immobile at home, during which stamina and vitality stop regenerating. The design tradeoff is explicit: bankable opportunity can accommodate a schedule, while earning it through idle time competes with active play and resource recovery. These older rules should not be presumed identical across newer service variants. [S22](#s22)

## 5. Things, skills and continuing consequences

Life skills include cooking, medicine, forging, tailoring, alchemy and furniture work. The manual routes substantial learning through guilds, with more limited alternatives at named NPCs. This gives collective development a practical use beyond a shared chat channel. [S15](#s15)

Medicine provides another material chain. The official recipe table lets four second-tier medicines become a random third-tier result, with **金创药** as the listed failure output. Production consumes vitality and benefits from the medicine skill. Temporary school recipes can improve success and quality, so knowing when and where information is available can matter alongside stockpiling ingredients. [S23](#s23)

**Pet and summon are different roles.** The zodiac companion can follow the player and supports carrying and training combat summons. A summon with low loyalty may refuse battle; training restores loyalty by spending the companion's endurance, which food replenishes. The manual connects companion growth to time online. This is a care-and-resource loop, with a potential friction cost as well as attachment. [S06](#s06)

The dated death rules distinguish being downed from final defeat: resurrection medicine or magic can restore a fallen character before battle ends. Ordinary deaths can cost cash and experience; insufficient experience can reduce a skill. PK adds different equipment or inventory-loss exposure. Some special activities waive these losses. Exact old percentages are omitted because mode and service differences matter more than preserving a historical tariff. [S03](#s03)

Guild facilities also require decisions outside the map editor. Leaders commission construction through internal affairs, spending guild resources; cancelling or demolishing does not refund them. Automatic dues require a member vote, and changing the amount requires another vote. The documented reward rules can distribute guild funds for contributions. This turns a useful collective place into an institution with authority, resource constraints and accountability; the editor alone does not supply those functions. [S24](#s24)

## 6. Relationships and leisure

**Marriage** requires the game's eligible characters to be friends, reach its affinity and progression thresholds, have an occupied home and pay the selected ceremony cost. **Yue Lao** asks both to confirm. Weddings give guests ways to congratulate, exchange gifts and participate; later shared activities build a recorded intimacy level. The system also defines mutual and unilateral divorce routes, with confirmation delays, costs and changes to relationship abilities. The relationship combines shared activities with formal entry, upkeep and exit rules. [P06](#p06)

**Mentoring** gives an experienced player a formal teaching role. Both people confirm the relationship; learning records show completed teaching steps, and graduation rewards both. A mentor can make limited gifts that assist the novice, while two-person tasks provide reasons to play together. Ending a relationship can reduce rewards and impose a waiting period. The useful question is whether a newcomer receives understandable help; completion rewards and a formal title alone do not prove good teaching. [P07](#p07)

**The 2018 child-rearing system** lets married characters have a child and single characters adopt. Scheduled work earns training funds, study spends them on attributes, and leisure reduces fatigue; repeating one activity raises rebellion and lowers returns. Training advances through commanded actions rather than by absence alone. Attributes and encounters determine an eventual role and special skill: the **Great Poet** is one possible outcome. Mature children can contribute in battle, but affinity declines over time and affects willingness to appear. This is a prescribed development system, not evidence of open-ended autonomous childhood. [P09](#p09)

### A quieter activity still has decisions

**Fishing** can be solo or shared. Buy a time-limited rod, cast, and time the pull as a moving marker enters a target zone; harder fish narrow that opportunity. Consecutive success can improve the catch, while fishing tools change chances or respond to unusual encounters. Eligible catches exchange for points, supplies and recognition. Friends fishing together can gain affinity. It offers an execution-based leisure pursuit with social value, though daily rod limits and exchange rewards can also turn it into another scheduled task. [P08](#p08)

## 7. Foundational world, cultures and motives

**Story spoilers:** this section and case F discuss the pilgrimage premise and the resolution of Xuanzang’s family quest.

The publisher's setting begins with **Chiyou (蚩尤)** provoking conflict among humans, immortals and demons; heroes of all three eventually expose and seal him. Tang-era peace remains vulnerable to resentment. Four continents distinguish spiritual and institutional landscapes: Dongsheng's immortals, dangerous Beiju, Xiniu's Daoist retreats and Nanzhan's mixed mortal society. **Guanyin** seeks people able to restore compassion through the scriptures. Receiving a Golden Lotus gives the player a mission to awaken five destined pilgrims, including the exiled Golden Cicada, disgraced Tianpeng and Curtain-Lifting General, wronged Little White Dragon and Sun Wukong. Their grief, shame and forgotten histories make persuasion and reconciliation central motives. [S01](#s01)

## 8. Seven worked situations

These are **constructed walkthroughs of published rules**, not claims of our own client play. Each identifies a goal, action, consequence and useful design question.

### A. Share tactical responsibility

A party leader wants another member to coordinate recovery while the leader tracks targets. The command interface permits several appointed commanders. They can place text on a character or summon, using saved or newly written instructions. Presets are stored locally; an edit made during battle needs a refresh before it appears. The result is a shared reference attached to the relevant participant, while that participant still chooses an action. [S02](#s02)

**Inference:** the interesting contribution is attention and communication. OpenLegend can test whether a player can notice danger, indicate the relevant object or person, and help a teammate act without taking over their controls. The Datang recovery round above creates an understandable reason to coordinate timing.

### B. Make an identifiable, maintainable weapon

A prospective smith acquires a **manufacturing guide (制造指南书)** and **refined iron (百炼精铁)** of sufficient quality. The guide specifies equipment type; the relevant craft skill and proficiency gate production. At the appropriate workshop, crafting spends physical stamina and produces an item requiring identification before use. Same-level outputs can differ in attributes and special effects. Durability then creates a maintenance decision: personal skill or guild contribution can support repair, while the old manual limits repeatedly failed repairs. [S05](#s05)

**Inference:** this chain creates several useful roles—supplier, maker, identifier and maintainer—without requiring four mandatory players. For OpenLegend, the test is whether another person's contribution changes the outcome intelligibly; random rolls alone do not create an interesting profession.

### C. Improve a summon while risking its configuration

A player wants a different combination of summon abilities. The documented fusion procedure takes two eligible summons through the pet interface and creates a new one whose species, attributes and inherited skills are uncertain. A **魔兽要诀** skill book can grant an ability while replacing an existing one; **金柳露** resets eligible ordinary summons to level zero. Treasure hunting and some quests supply books. The player's goal is therefore a particular usable companion, not simply a larger inventory. [S07](#s07)

**Inference:** configuration makes the creature memorable, but replacement uncertainty can turn attachment into pressure to risk a valued companion. OpenLegend should expose what can change and what is protected before a consequential transformation.

### D. Author a guild place that members actually use

A member opens the guild map manager, edits a scene plan and saves it for the guild to inspect. Any member may submit a design; the leader chooses whether to adopt or delete it. The rules require roads to every building and prohibit disconnected ground. The adopted scene is walkable by guild members. Libraries support research, dormitories support membership capacity, and treasury and warehouse buildings support resources. Building counts grow through guild internal affairs; drawing a layout is a separate act. [S08](#s08)

**Inference:** this is a strong native creation example because authoring reaches a recipient: a member makes a proposal, others can review it, an authorized person publishes it, and the group inhabits the result. A useful-place prototype should measure both comprehensible editing and whether visitors can complete the activity the place supports.

### E. Choose a home around a useful routine

A house builder visits the construction official, selects a scale and location, allocates limited space among bedroom, kitchen, storage, pet and servant rooms, and completes construction tasks. Greater building skill supports more ambitious houses. The owner then moves in and uses the transport NPC to return. Allocating more room to one function constrains another; the design is a functional plan before it is decoration. [S09](#s09)

Furniture work adds a maker's route: learn the craft, create a design with Lu Cheng using selected materials and techniques, obtain the furniture, then place and rotate it at home. Invalid placements appear translucent. A removal charm returns ordinary placed furniture to inventory. Saved decoration arrangements record object types, locations and directions, but replacement skips missing furniture and moving can invalidate a plan. [S10](#s10)

**Inference:** the promising loop is plan → make → arrange → use → revise. OpenLegend can validate a modest useful shelter before adding a large furniture catalogue.

### F. Help Xuanzang resolve the reason he cannot leave

The **Xuanzang's Origins** quest connects a monk's family history to the pilgrimage. The jealous **Wine-and-Meat Monk** bullies and later poisons him. Helping requires information, medicine and intervention. One branch heals **Elder Faming** before investigating **White Glass**; learning her origin lets her recall her past and become a glass fragment, while another route first fights her to recover a relic. Later, evidence exposes **Liu Hong** and **Li Biao's** theft of Xuanzang's father's position and coercion of his mother, **Yin Wenjiao**. Resolving the family's danger lets Xuanzang commit to helping others through the scriptures. The quest awards story points alongside money and experience. [S12](#s12)

**Inference:** the emotional result is permission to move forward. A branching sequence can make healing, testimony and delivery matter even when combat remains frequent; it does not establish that every NPC has a general autonomous memory model.

### G. Turn knowledge of a person into a group challenge

In the documented **天下识君** biography mode, **Wei Zheng** compiles lives under **Emperor Li Shimin's** authority. A leader with the required relationship unlocks a biography node, then forms a five-person party; teammates need not duplicate that unlock. Normal completion opens a harder version with additional battle properties. First clears provide brushes; spending relationship affinity records memories as fragments used for rewards. Repeating a node reduces rewards, while the four-week cycle expires unused recording opportunities and temporarily stores settlement rewards. Failure revives the party without ordinary death losses. [S11](#s11)

**Inference:** one person's social preparation can open an experience for friends. The expiry rules also show how a meaningful relationship activity can acquire an appointment burden. OpenLegend can test the shared opportunity without importing the deadline machinery.

## 9. Presentation

The publisher describes rounded, exaggerated character silhouettes, Chinese architectural references and hand-painted scenes. Its examples range from Chang'an and frontier desert to the underworld and Dragon Palace. Treat the source's declarations of beauty and immersion as promotion; the useful design observation is that distinct places and recognizable silhouettes support orientation and attachment. The page's roster counts are inconsistent with newer school material and are not adopted. [S13](#s13)

**Music can be something a host arranges.** The publisher's May 2013 Cloud Music manual describes free in-game searching and playlists, with friends able to see and listen to the selected song. Its separate customization features charge for mood tags, scheduled scene music and optional weather effects. In the home workflow, buy a **Trumpet Flower (喇叭花)**, place it in a house or courtyard, choose a track and let visitors hear it; the documented music permission lasts thirty days and allows changes during that window. This makes sound part of a shared visit, with an explicit entitlement boundary. These are dated documented capabilities, not a newly reproduced claim about today's catalogue, licensing or audio quality. [P05](#p05)

### What these accounts let us infer

Affection can outlast departure; returning can mean lowering competitive ambitions; an inviter can prompt re-entry while new friends sustain it. Economically invested players may judge expansion through lost asset value. These accounts establish distinct personal goals, not their prevalence. [R01](#r01) [R02](#r02) [R04](#r04) [R05](#r05)

**OpenLegend hypothesis:** a compelling world should offer legible opportunities for expression, learning, competent cooperation and renewed acquaintance before asking players to maintain an expensive or exhausting progression routine. Evaluate whether people choose another enjoyable activity, change their goals, return later or simply stop. A player's continued affection does not itself establish continued play. Route those observations into existing **CE05 / DG14**, **CP10**, **FPV06–08**, and the included-versus-paid comparison in **CO01–03**; no additional feature or retention mandate follows from these essays.

**Steam/access limit:** no verified Steam distribution of the original PC game was established in this pass. Mobile-store reviews, modern unattributed reposts, AI summary wrappers and publisher return promotions were excluded from the five. These original player essays are the strongest fully accessible firsthand material recovered here; they do not substitute for a representative survey or current gameplay test.

### What payment changes

**Separate the services.** The time-server guide publishes **six points per hour at RMB0.10 per point**, or RMB0.60/hour, with free play below level 10. Its monthly card costs 600 points and supplies 100 hours plus 200 jade; an annual card costs 7,200 points for fourteen monthly periods. Unused monthly hours remain valid for 360 days but require an active monthly card to consume. Monthly benefits include additional experience and service discounts, so even this access model includes progression utility. These maintained guides retain old publication dates and contradictory debt thresholds; an exact current debt cutoff is not established. [E06](#e06) [E07](#e07)

The newer **Changwan / 再续前缘 service** advertises free basic play and connected PC/mobile clients; it is distinct from the standalone mobile game. Its published entitlement offer lists **900 points for 30 days or 5,400 for 180 days**, bound to the selected character. Advertised privileges include trading privileges, money production, unlimited automatic battle rounds, faster vitality/energy recovery and preferential login channels. That is a materially different access and convenience package, not evidence that every PC realm abandoned time charging. Prices and benefits were read from the public offer’s supplied interface text, without purchasing or testing the client. [E16](#e16)

Time-server players can sell prepaid points for in-game money or buy time with earned money. This offers an earned access route while connecting cash spending to the game economy; it does not establish that an ordinary newcomer can easily finance play. [E08](#e08) The publisher’s **藏宝阁 (CBG)** marketplace additionally supports real-money character, currency and equipment trading. The time-server standard character seller fee is **5%, RMB60–1,000**; Changwan’s is **8%, RMB60–20,000**. Exceptional early-sale and cross-server charges differ. Character purchase transfers the character between accounts while keeping its server; it is not account purchase or automatic world travel. [E09](#e09) [E17](#e17)

**Interpretation:** money can buy established economic resources and progression, not just appearance. Fairness analysis therefore needs the particular PvE/PvP rules, acquisition effort and social expectations. Paid advantage and commercial endurance coexist here; neither determines whether players find the terms fair or whether this economy fits OpenLegend.

## 12. Production and iteration

**Production built on an earlier online world.** In 2006, former designer Xu Bo (also Xu Youzhen) recalled maintaining the unsuccessful first Westward Journey, rewriting its client with the second game's engine, revising rules and replacing shared art with a cute visual identity. He credited an artist's proposal, separate gameplay/art production, and adding writing and project management to a small effort. This is participant recollection, without an audited schedule or proprietary-code verification. [P01](#p01)

**Credit and chronology are disputed.** The former designer's account presents himself as the principal systems author; NetEase's July 2006 statement emphasizes team development and Ding Yingfeng's design/project leadership, dating Xu Bo's lead-designer appointment to March 2005. [P01](#p01) [P02](#p02) Neither account justifies attributing decades of service to one person or treating a short formal production interval as the entire development cost. The useful lesson is narrower: previous operations, player feedback and existing production capabilities can be inputs to a new game, while authorship claims require attribution.

The 2011 core-team interview makes later iteration more concrete. Lin Yunfeng described decorative additions chosen after player research and a coming expansion intended to serve several progression bands, with more relaxed activities as well as competition. He also distinguished the standardized **All-People PK tournament** from server competition shaped by accumulated characters and equipment. Those were dated design intentions and event rules, not evidence that every subsequent mode was equally accessible. [P03](#p03)

## 13. Actual distribution and marketing

NetEase's December 2003 launch report describes a meeting with provincial distributors at which **Ding Yingfeng demonstrated the beginner experience** and the company explained its campaign and client-distribution arrangements. Regional internet-café promotion was an explicit channel. The report positioned the game through its Journey to the West setting, cute figures, humorous dialogue and social expression. This establishes an actual demonstration and distribution effort; its enthusiastic product judgments are publisher promotion. [P04](#p04)

By 2011, the brand and sales leads described tournament outreach through experienced players' QQ groups, partnerships with Wahaha, Li-Ning and a customized BBK phone, and face-to-face promotion through cafés, campuses, communities and retail venues. They emphasized maintaining relationships with café operators and gathering service complaints. This is a documented combination of social circulation, physical distribution and sponsored competition. It does not isolate the acquisition or retention contribution of any one channel. [P03](#p03)

For OpenLegend, the transferable hypothesis is that an understandable, enjoyable activity can become a unit people demonstrate, discuss and invite someone to try. Sponsorship, mass tournaments and an internet-café network depend on a different market and operating scale. They are not prerequisites for the existing first-playable validation work.

## 14. External creation and tools

**R15 — uncertain, original PC, October 28, 2026.** External authoring and technical experimentation exist, but the inspected evidence does not establish a popular, sustained modification ecosystem for the studied game:

- **mumuWorld's MHXYTool** is a separate pet-stat planning calculator. Its documented online and offline calculation modes help compare attributes; this does not establish that it edits the live world or supplies gameplay mods. [M01](#m01)
- **CQ-ChengQi's YZXY**, forked from oceancx's work, documents a C++/Lua reimplementation aimed at classic Fantasy Westward Journey. It reads resources from clients **before 2.0.186**, supplies no original game resources itself, and exposes character/pet and battle-command editors. Its author describes unfinished combat work and restricts the project to personal learning. It is not a NetEase SDK or a demonstrated extension of current official servers. [M02](#m02)
- **oceancx's CXEngine** documents Lua/ImGui tooling, a launcher and a separate client/server build; its notice points toward a Unity successor. The inspected repository showed 281 stars and 88 forks. Those are signs of developer interest, not counts of installed mods, active players or supported current-client compatibility. [M03](#m03)
- The **Xiaohuitu** catalog lists WDF archive and WAS palette tools, resource extraction and GGE-related tutorials. It also advertises paid membership and exclusive groups. The site says its resources are collected from the internet; listing volume and traffic displays do not establish original authorship, rights, actual customer outcomes or creator income. [M04](#m04)

This screen therefore records a real, unofficial classic-resource and recreation scene with unresolved adoption and provenance. It does not certify a complete, maintained author-to-recipient modification workflow. Native house furnishing and other in-game composition belong in the main mechanics/cases; calculators, fan art, commercial resale and reimplementations are different activities. For OpenLegend, the already-owned pack work should still require a usable artifact, dependencies, rights and actual recipient use; this game supplies no reason to assume access to another product's assets or to add a legacy-support obligation.

## 15. OpenLegend interpretation and routes into owned work

The strongest opportunity is **several useful ways to belong in the same world**. A participant can understand a school identity, configure a companion, help a teammate, resolve a character's problem, make an object or improve a shared place. These examples support hypotheses about enjoyment; they do not establish that OpenLegend needs this game's class roster, inventory volume or scheduled progression.

**Engine/world boundary.** Reusable permissions, references, persistence scopes, publication validation and clear feedback belong in the engine or authoring contracts. Schools, spiritual cosmology, summon inheritance, specific losses and calendar rules belong to a world's authored design. A branching biography is evidence of structured authored behavior; implementing general agents with memory and independent motives would require separate capability, quality and cost measurement.

**Review judgment.** Keep the seven varied situations and their positive goals. Retain the distinction between visual editing and actually funding guild infrastructure. Remove obsolete counts and unverified current-price assumptions, and keep historical reception attributed to its actual period. This title adds evidence to existing owners rather than a parallel roadmap. The live service's published custody and payment rules can inform product promises, while OpenLegend's [development save policy](../../../../AGENTS.md#development-save-policy) continues to govern experimental development.
