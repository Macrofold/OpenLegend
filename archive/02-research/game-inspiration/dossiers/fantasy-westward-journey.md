# Fantasy Westward Journey — original PC dossier

**Game:** G199, 梦幻西游电脑版 (Fantasy Westward Journey, original PC).
**Status:** Research in progress; substantive checkpoint, not a completed or reviewed dossier.
**Research date:** October 28, 2026.

## 1. Scope and research question

This dossier concerns the original PC game and its documented service variants. The mobile game, browser adaptations and franchise-wide business results need separate labels. The main OpenLegend question is how a socially persistent world can sustain useful identities, tactical cooperation, trade and authored places when encounters use a bounded command interface. Current service boundaries, payment rules and retention promises are still being checked; no general hourly price or single-world claim is adopted here.

The useful comparison will distinguish what a player does, what remains afterward, and what another person can meaningfully contribute. Evidence about longevity cannot by itself show that every retained system is enjoyable or that a particular payment model caused success.

## 2. Foundational world and motives

An archived setting account places humans, immortals and demons in a Tang-era world scarred by war. Zixia's forbidden love for Sun Wukong and death precede his revolt against Heaven; five centuries later, Guanyin sees desire and resentment threatening renewed peace. The player receives a Golden Lotus and helps awaken five prospective pilgrims' willingness to seek the scriptures. Their unresolved lives make persuasion and restoration part of the fantasy, alongside fighting. [S01](#s01)

**OpenLegend opportunity:** a world can make a player's usefulness legible through another character's changed intention. The eventual design comparison must identify how a quest expresses that change in actions and consequences; a large mythology alone does not establish an interactive social simulation.

## 3. A concrete cooperation interface

The documented command system lets a party leader appoint several commanders. A commander attaches written instructions to a character or summoned creature; this is a communication aid rather than evidence of direct control over another player's action. Preset commands can be added, edited or deleted and saved locally. Editing them during battle requires refreshing the command list before the new version appears. [S02](#s02)

**Constructed situation:** a player responsible for coordinating a fight marks an ally and communicates a recovery instruction. Delegating command permission lets another member contribute that coordination. The remaining design question is whether participants understand and act on the instruction: text on a target supplies shared reference, while player agency still matters. This is an interpretation of the documented interface, not a recorded play session. [S02](#s02)
