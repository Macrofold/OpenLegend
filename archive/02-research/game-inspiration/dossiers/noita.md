## Primary production and scope evidence

The developer press kit dates Early Access to September 24, 2019 and 1.0 to October 15, 2020. It identifies Liero and falling-sand games among the inspirations, the in-house Falling Everything Engine, founders Petri Purho, Olli Harjola and Arvi Teikari, and collaborators including Antti Tiihonen. Niilo Takalainen handles sound design; he and From Grotto collaborate on the music. Its prose still anticipates the 2020 launch, so it is historical production evidence, not a current patch ledger. [P1]

The official 2019 devlog documents community-requested particle kicking, Thunderstone electricity interacting with water/metal, screenshot/GIF posts, wishlist links and creator-key outreach. Those are documented activities, not measured marketing-channel attribution. Its displayed Tumblr app ratings are not Noita player counts. [P2]

## Five substantive written reviews read

- Luke Winkie, PC Gamer, October 14, 2020: embraces spectacular self-destructive combinations while acknowledging uneven difficulty and obscurity. Incidental alchemy/geographical statements are not adopted without rules verification. [R1]
- Andrew Duncan, GameGrin, 2020: loves destructibility, exploration and distinct material environments, but explicitly used a higher-health mod and historical save restoration. His enthusiasm is not evidence that default punishment suited him. [R2]
- Mike Suskie, GameCritics, November 18, 2020: admires material physics but rejects repetitive early play, opaque systems and the combat/narrative experience he encountered. His limited progress is part of the account, not a claim to have exhausted the world. [R3]
- Gabriele Malacasa, RPGamer, October 25, 2020, updated July 26, 2021: values synergistic spells, secrets and sound but finds access to interesting tools too dependent on luck and the difficulty escalation punishing. [R4]
- Alessandro Barbosa, GameSpot, October 20, 2020: praises environmental solutions and spell composition while finding the first hours abrasive and under-explained. His returning-bomb mistake is a useful attributed example rather than a universal wand recipe. [R5]

All five bodies, not merely score blurbs, were inspected through their conclusions. Current mechanics must not be inferred from every incidental statement in these launch-era reviews.

## Steam helpful bodies read

The English Most Helpful (All Time) surface and its Negative Only counterpart returned substantive bodies. Recommended reviewer Puli's February 8, 2021 entry includes a later five-year edit qualifying an original recommendation to avoid guides: deeper knowledge often comes through community exchange. The negative account dated December 31, 2022, with an obscured display name, respects the game's depth while rejecting its repetition and wiki dependence. Neither sample establishes how common those preferences are; current hours are not publication-time hours. User percentages are not adopted as population telemetry. [S1] [S2]
