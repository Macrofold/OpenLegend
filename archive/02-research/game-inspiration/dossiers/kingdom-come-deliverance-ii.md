# G114 — Kingdom Come: Deliverance II

**Full research pass — September 26, 2026; Steam-body access gap explicitly retained.** [Roster](../research-roster.md) · [Requirements](../research-requirements.md) · [Progress](../research-progress.md). Comparative reference, not an implementation specification. Five independent written reviews, primary publisher and creator accounts, and specific observed-rule guides were read. No gameplay execution, footage viewing or engine audit is claimed. **Quest and relationship spoilers follow.**

## Identity, editions and central promise

Warhorse's sequel launched February 4, 2025 for PC, PS5 and Xbox Series consoles. It continues Henry's story alongside Hans Capon, rather than offering an anonymous race/class creator. A mission to deliver a letter becomes entangled with the struggle between Wenceslas and Sigismund and the people who endure it. Trosky and Kuttenberg provide different regional settings. This is a first-person, single-player historical RPG—not a documentary, medieval MMO or spellcasting fantasy. [PC Gamer review](#r-pcg), [strategy index](#s-hub)

The Royal Edition includes the three delivered story expansions, additional equipment and The Lion's Crest. The Saga package additionally contains the first game and its own expansions; those earlier systems must not be silently assigned to the sequel. [Publisher](#s-publisher) A **Switch 2 Royal Edition was announced in September 2026 for 2027**. Its proposed mouse controls and enlarged handheld menus are future-port features, not evidence of a shipped version or independently measured performance. [Portable announcement](#s-port)

The central analytical distinction is between **permission to attempt an action**, **practical competence**, and **social acceptance**. A road may be open while Henry is hungry, poorly equipped or unwelcome at its destination. The first hours establish vulnerability; later equipment, habits and relationships make the same region easier to inhabit. This interpretation explains why the game can reward seemingly mundane preparation without assuming that every source of friction is beneficial.

## Character development and complete action families

### Skills are practices, not fixed classes

Four main attributes—**Strength, Agility, Vitality and Speech**—develop alongside six combat skills: **Warfare, Swords, Heavy Weapons, Polearms, Unarmed and Marksmanship**. Nine further skills are **Alchemy, Craftsmanship, Drinking, Houndmaster, Horsemanship, Scholarship, Stealth, Survival and Thievery**. Practice, books, teachers and quests contribute differently. Perk points unlock additional capabilities but do not automatically purchase every option. Health, Energy and Nourishment are state variables, not interchangeable experience levels. [Skills](#s-stats)

The design significance is that a socially adept Henry need not be a master fighter, and performing a trade can improve a life outside that trade. A skill system becomes meaningful through the occasions that recognize it: a book, a wound, a lock, an animal or a difficult conversation. Merely reproducing the same number of skill headings would not reproduce that relationship.

### Fighting and movement

Henry walks, runs, crouches, jumps, rides, draws weapons, aims, attacks, blocks, parries, dodges and uses trained combinations. Swords, maces, polearms, bows, crossbows and early firearms have different handling and upkeep. The publisher specifically emphasizes weapon-specific animations and specialized motion capture; its dramatic equipment-failure language is marketing, not an audited universal breakage formula. [Publisher](#s-publisher)

Directional fighting asks the player to read stance, timing, reach and stamina rather than only activate a hotbar attack. Heavy protection also affects mobility and ordinary work. Encounters against several people are not simply duels multiplied: keeping threats in view changes the challenge. Kobylanski finds those group fights particularly poor even while liking the clearer one-on-one feedback. No exact current-patch damage or counter window is claimed here. [RPGFan](#r-rpgfan)

Walking and riding connect exploration to the passage of time. Ordinary fast travel remains a route through the world rather than teleporting freely from combat. Hardcore later removes fast travel and much navigation assistance. The same terrain therefore supports different information and planning demands under different modes. [Hardcore](#s-hardcore)

### Access, ownership and social action

Talking, persuading, intimidating, haggling, reading, waiting, helping, drinking and playing dice provide noncombat activities. Clothing, cleanliness and present condition help determine how a meeting goes. A dialogue score is not a universal permission to persuade anybody of anything. A military scene can still impose an authored route. [VideoGamer](#r-vg), [wedding clothing](#s-clothes)

Theft includes picking locks, pickpocketing, taking owned goods and moving contraband. Stealth clothing and noise differ from ordinary protection. Violence, theft and trespass can provoke suspicion and punishment; repeated serious offending is not reduced to an endlessly harmless small fee. The practical tips discuss fines, pillory, flogging and branding. Their anecdotal crime advice is not proof of omniscient police or a fully simulated investigation engine. [Tips](#s-tips)

Our comparative model separates three questions: can the lock be opened, can the act go unnoticed, and will the resulting possession be accepted later? Conflating them makes stealth either implausibly easy or arbitrarily punitive. Different penalties also matter emotionally: lost money, lost time and damaged standing are not equivalent costs.

## Equipment, production and resources

### Clothing is functional context

Armor, ordinary garments, weapons, ammunition, tools, food, books, potions and valuable goods compete for weight and money. Three saved outfit arrangements help switch between combat, social presentation and quiet movement; switching during active fighting is restricted. An owned or rented bed also changes which nearby resources can be used legitimately. [Tips](#s-tips)

Wedding admission illustrates the specificity: a suitable visible outfit and sufficient Charisma are checked by the tailor, and blood or dirt can invalidate otherwise suitable clothes. The admission test is not simply an item's sale price. [Wedding clothing](#s-clothes) Our interpretation is that an equipment system gains expressive power when a valuable piece can be appropriate in one situation and conspicuously wrong in another.

### Blacksmithing is a performed procedure

Radovan's introductory job supplies metal and a hunting-sword pattern. The player heats the workpiece evenly, uses bellows, moves it on the anvil, distributes strikes, turns it, reheats when necessary and finishes it. Heat color and sparks provide feedback; overheating and repeatedly striking one cold area are not equivalent to competent work. Further patterns include horseshoes and an axe. These are bounded recipes and operations, not arbitrary metallurgy or a physics construction editor. [Blacksmith's Son](#s-smith)

Craftsmanship therefore contains player execution as well as numerical progression. The analytical benefit is recognizing the difference between **knowing a plan**, **having materials**, **performing the process**, and **owning the result**. The potential cost is repetition: an evocative first craft can become routine labor when repeated for income. Better automation or service use can address repetition without making the original learning experience pointless.

### Alchemy combines knowledge, ingredients and technique

Alchemy uses ordered heating, grinding, mixing and distilling operations. A player who knows a procedure can successfully prepare it without first buying its written recipe; doing so records the discovery. Quality varies with execution and relevant perks. **Aesop** supports animal-related needs, **Buck's Blood** stamina, **Cockerel** alertness, **Chamomile Brew** recovery, and **Saviour Schnapps** manual saving. These are distinct applications rather than one healing item with several names. [Alchemy](#s-alchemy)

The practical distinction between prevention and recovery is important. A potion that supports an exhausting journey is not a substitute for treating an already bleeding wound. A reagent's value also depends on what Henry can make from it; selling every plant immediately exchanges future options for present cash. These are analytical consequences of the documented recipe system, not a claim that this dossier tests every recipe permutation.

### Economy and maintenance

Groschen purchases food, clothing, lodging, training, equipment and services. Work, quests, trade, loot and crime supply different income routes with different risks. Armor and clothing deteriorate and get dirty; repair, washing and replacement connect outings to later preparation. Reviewers describe the transition from scarcity to comfortable provisioning rather than a perfectly stable scarcity curve. [Push Square](#r-push), [VideoGamer](#r-vg)

There is no player-run international market or automatically simulated feudal tax system in this reference. The later forge adds a particular home and business progression, not proof that every shop can be purchased and every settlement governed. The important research question is whether a resource still prompts decisions after Henry becomes prosperous, or merely becomes another counter to keep topped up.

## Time, failure and return

Early life is constrained by suitable meals, rest, clothing and access to services. Middle play broadens mobility, equipment, contacts and professional options; later play emphasizes consequential campaign events, specialized builds, side stories and expansions. Some side content becomes unavailable at main-story commitment points. Finishing the campaign permits further exploration, but it does not restore every previously failed or missed task. [Strategy index](#s-hub)

Saving has several routes: checkpoints, save-on-exit, appropriate beds and Saviour Schnapps. It is false that every save requires drinking. Dying normally returns to a save; a successfully completed scene can still carry a bad social or narrative result. A crash that destroys progress is a reliability defect, not an intended form of historical hardship. [Push Square](#r-push)

**Hardcore**, added April 15, 2025, removes health/stamina display and ordinary map-position assistance, disables fast travel and adds selectable disadvantages. Named examples include **Bad Back**, **Heavy-Footed**, **Numbskull** and **Somnambulant**, affecting carrying, noise, advancement or sleep-related risk. The guide's all-ten-perks route is an achievement challenge, not a statement that every ordinary Hardcore run mandates all ten. [Hardcore](#s-hardcore)

This is a finite authored campaign with continued optional activity, not a seasonal reset service. A fresh run can explore another temperament or difficulty, while existing-character DLC supplies a different return purpose. Those are separate retention mechanisms and should not be measured as if every returning player were participating in the same loop.

## People, institutions and narrative

### Henry remains a particular person

Henry's friendship with Hans Capon provides continuity and contrast as they move between noble ambitions, ordinary labor and danger. Radovan, miller Kreyzl, Father Godwin, the Dry Devil and healer Klara represent different relationships to work, violence, faith and care. Political authority is not one homogeneous faction: the opening letter and later loyalties connect local obligations to a wider conflict. [PC Gamer](#r-pcg), [strategy index](#s-hub)

The player can shape Henry's behavior without rewriting his family, birthplace or entire past. This supports strong authored callbacks but limits blank-slate identification. It also gives a trade a personal meaning: smithing is not simply the game's interchangeable crafting profession. The distinction between a fixed protagonist and flexible conduct should remain explicit in comparisons with fully custom-character RPGs.

NPCs eat, sleep, work and react to conduct; a worker may not be available at night, and an ordinary request may require returning during the day. Radovan's guide provides a concrete example. [Blacksmith's Son](#s-smith) These routines do not establish autonomous generation of every occupation, relationship or political event. A world can feel inhabited through carefully coordinated authored schedules without simulating its whole economy.

### Relationships and animals

The dog Mutt and horses connect companionship to practical travel, tracking and conflict. Houndmaster and Horsemanship improve those capabilities through relevant activity. Henry does not command a permanently customizable tactical party of all his friends; narrative allies and animal assistance are different systems. [Skills](#s-stats)

Romance exists through specific authored opportunities, including Klara. Her later encounter connects assistance with wounded soldiers to a personal interaction; simply choosing a flirt line before doing anything else is not equivalent. The guide's claim that this is her only encounter is contradicted by its own comment evidence, so it is not repeated as a universal rule. No general marriage, household dynasty or free social-network simulator is established. [Klara](#s-klara)

### Stories emerge from obligations as well as spectacle

Wedding Crashers uses employment and social appearance as entry problems. For Whom the Bells Tolls makes medical help part of saving a friend. The Finger of God places personal moments alongside care for injured people. Their stakes are authored, but practical systems determine portions of the route through them. [Clothing](#s-clothes), [Thomas](#s-thomas), [Klara](#s-klara)

The reviews disagree about the limits. Some celebrate believable alternatives in local episodes; others encounter a sensible plan that the main story refuses to acknowledge. A dossier should not generalize from one successful improvisation into “anything works,” nor generalize one rigid scene into “choices never matter.” The more useful comparison asks which kinds of intent receive recognition, and where the contract changes without warning.

## Eight concrete situations

The following are constructed explanations of documented rules except the explicitly attributed review incident. They are not gameplay tests. Each preserves conditions, interaction, outcome, next choice and a boundary.

### 1. Prepare for a wedding rather than merely reach its door

**Intent:** gain admission. **Conditions:** the invitation route and a suitable outfit. **Actions:** inspect visible clothes, meet the Charisma requirement, wash and ask the tailor to assess readiness. **Interaction:** money, appearance and cleanliness jointly determine access. **Result:** social preparation opens a narrative space. **Next:** choose what to do inside. **Limit:** expensive equipment that is dirty or inappropriate can still fail the test. Our interpretation: social gates work best when their criteria are learnable rather than arbitrary hidden approval. [Clothing](#s-clothes)

### 2. Learn a trade through an employer who needs proof

**Intent:** secure Radovan's support. **Conditions:** materials and his smithing tutorial. **Actions:** heat, inspect, strike evenly, turn and reheat the workpiece. **Interaction:** the mentor's feedback links visible changes to successful technique. **Result:** a usable item and entry into further work. **Next:** pursue the employer's chain or another wedding route. **Limit:** possessing steel is not sufficient; incorrect execution can reduce quality. The interpretive lesson is that professional recognition can be tied to demonstrated competence. [Blacksmith's Son](#s-smith)

### 3. Save a friend by treating somebody else

**Intent:** prevent Hans' execution. **Conditions:** the castle sequence and access to Captain Thomas. **Actions:** overcome access obstacles, read the physician's notes, obtain ingredients and prepare Fever Tonic. **Interaction:** social access, stealth and alchemy serve the same urgent objective. **Result:** curing the captain advances the rescue. **Next:** complete the remaining intervention before time expires. **Limit:** the recipe is a specific recognized treatment, not an invitation to invent arbitrary medicine. This is a game walkthrough, not real-world medical guidance. [Thomas](#s-thomas)

### 4. Let practical care precede intimacy

**Intent:** assist Klara and the wounded at Nebakov. **Conditions:** the relevant main-story interval before sleeping. **Actions:** inspect patients, obtain supplies and apply the recognized treatments, then return to her. **Interaction:** skills and resources affect a relationship opportunity. **Result:** competent help can support the later personal scene. **Next:** accept or decline the interaction and continue the story. **Limit:** the source's numerical observations are not proven universal minimums; its internally inconsistent drinking instruction is excluded. Our interpretation: competence can create a human connection without a generic gift-spam meter. [Klara](#s-klara)

### 5. Reconsider an enemy in an ordinary social setting

**Attributed play:** Mills reports confronting Cumans, hearing their account, then drinking with them. An incapacitated participant subsequently creates opportunities for further conduct. **Intent:** initially challenge people associated with Henry's enemies. **Interaction:** conversation changes context before ordinary looting or violence becomes possible. **Next:** decide what kind of person Henry will be. **Limit:** this is a particular reported scene, not proof that every enemy faction can be reconciled by alcohol. [Destructoid](#r-destructoid)

### 6. Enter a workshop looking like a worker, not an armored intruder

**Intent:** undertake quiet or delicate work after a journey. **Conditions:** several prepared outfit sets and no active combat. **Actions:** change from battle equipment to the appropriate saved arrangement before the task. **Interaction:** preparation reduces repeated inventory labor without erasing contextual tradeoffs. **Result:** less noise or more suitable presentation. **Next:** decide what to carry rather than wear. **Limit:** an outfit is not a universal disguise that overrides all recognition. This is an illustrative use of outfit rules, not a named scripted quest. [Tips](#s-tips)

### 7. Make a permanent home carry a family history

**Intent:** restore the Kuttenberg forge. **Conditions:** Legacy of the Forge and its progression. **Actions:** work through guild obligations, improve the premises and investigate Martin's unfinished ambitions. **Interaction:** income, customization and backstory reinforce one place. **Result:** a returning destination with both utility and personal meaning. **Next:** invest earnings in different improvements or continue other adventures. **Limit:** it is a specific authored property, not permission to own any building. Our interpretation: a home becomes more than a storage menu when work and memory change it together. [Forge](#s-forge)

### 8. Navigate without a self-position marker

**Intent:** reach an objective in Hardcore. **Conditions:** that mode's reduced interface and no fast travel. **Actions:** observe landmarks, compare geography with the map and plan rest and supplies. **Interaction:** previously optional geographic knowledge becomes operationally important. **Result:** arrival feels earned through orientation, or a mistaken route creates a new problem. **Next:** revise the route or seek help. **Limit:** removing information can produce disorientation rather than discovery, especially for newcomers. This is a constructed comparison, not a claim of a measured accessibility benefit. [Hardcore](#s-hardcore)

## Delivered expansions, not launch promises

### Brushes with Death

Master Voyta's story crosses both regions and mixes investigation, persuasion and difficult assistance. Shield designs provide an expressive reward distinct from a new combat class. The painter's skull and uncertain past motivate inquiry; this is not a general painting editor for arbitrary world assets. [Brushes publisher](#s-brushes) The guide separates its paid quest chain from free horse racing and mounted archery introduced around patch 1.3. Access depends on campaign progress; not every new activity belongs to the paid purchase. [Brushes guide](#s-brushes-guide)

### Legacy of the Forge

A burnt forge associated with Henry's father becomes a home and professional center. Customization covers the forge, surroundings and private space; daily work supports earning and improvement. The publisher's combinatorial customization count is not treated as a count of meaningfully different stories. [Forge](#s-forge) The delivered September 9, 2025 chain includes Adept, Martin's Dream and subsequent legacy quests, with additional guild investigations and activities. These titles establish a progression route, not independent verification of every branch. [Forge guide](#s-forge-guide)

### Mysteria Ecclesiae

The quarantined Sedletz Monastery introduces a constrained investigation involving disease, scarce resources and institutional access. New books, equipment and potions support the episode; the monastery remains accessible afterward. This is a specialized mystery, not a new global epidemic simulation. [Monastery publisher](#s-monastery) The guide identifies physician Albich, infirmary work, library interactions and missable optional tasks. Reading the overview does not equal independently reproducing all endings. [Monastery guide](#s-monastery-guide)

## Presentation and production

First-person embodiment makes hands, tools, cloth, faces and landscape continuously relevant. Long quiet journeys contrast with intimate conversation and violence. Kobylanski values sound and performances while objecting to certain humor and characterization. Visual detail therefore does not settle whether the story treats every character convincingly. [RPGFan](#r-rpgfan)

Wolens emphasizes small gestures and practical companionship: people eat, play dice, drink and spend time together rather than existing only to issue objectives. Bardwell instead finds portions of the late journey repetitive and the acting uneven. These are different judgments of the same authored presentation, not evidence that one critic failed to understand the game. [PC Gamer](#r-pcg), [VideoGamer](#r-vg)

In a December 2025 interview, executive producer and Warhorse co-founder **Martin Klima** describes deliberately trusting players and accepting that a demanding, distinctive RPG will not suit everyone. He connects design director **Viktor Bocan's** interests in Souls games to discovery and earned competence, and contrasts this with risk-averse attempts to please every audience. This is creator interpretation of the project's intent and reception, not proof that difficulty caused sales or that all players prefer less guidance. [Creator interview](#s-creator)

The sequel preserves a strong identity while revising onboarding and combat readability. Publisher accounts identify specialized motion capture; reviewers report notably improved launch reliability compared with the first game, but not perfection. No development-budget audit, staff census, source-code analysis, current performance benchmark or comprehensive accessibility test was conducted. Controls, visibility, load, long sessions and reduced Hardcore aids all remain legitimate accessibility questions rather than assumed advantages.

## Distribution, discovery and commercial evidence

The business model is a premium base game, paid story additions and collected editions, alongside free updates. It is not sustained by a required subscription or a player-trading commission. Publisher pages distinguish the two-game Saga from the sequel-only Royal package and an upcoming handheld port. [Publisher](#s-publisher), [Portable announcement](#s-port)

**Primary milestone:** Deep Silver announced **more than six million copies sold worldwide** in June 2026; the announcement was contemporaneously reported June 30. This is sold copies, not concurrent players, active subscribers, gross retail value or net profit. The parent group's earlier annual-report narrative separately says five million within the first year. No title-level profit or audited development/marketing cost is established here. [Publisher sales](#s-sales), [dated report](#s-sales-date), [annual-report text](#s-annual)

The publisher pairs the milestone with community events, concerts and gatherings. Our interpretation is that recognizable characters, unusual daily-life anecdotes and visible skill acquisition provide material for retelling and sharing. The sources do not quantify the acquisition effect of any streamer, controversy, award or social post. A successful game's memorable stories are not, by themselves, a causal marketing experiment.

## Transferable patterns and counterexamples — research interpretation

**Make routine preparation change a later encounter.** Washing or clothing selection is more meaningful when it changes admission or treatment. Without that connection, maintenance risks becoming a tax. The dependency is clear feedback about which state matters and how to alter it; hidden standards produce frustration rather than social depth.

**Represent a profession through perception and action.** Heating and striking can teach the player what competent work looks and sounds like. The failure mode is making every subsequent item demand the same tutorial-length labor. Skill, tools or trusted services can provide shortcuts while preserving the early experience of learning.

**Separate fixed identity from flexible conduct.** A known protagonist gives relationships and family history weight, while choices allow different behavior. The contract fails when the game advertises complete authorship but silently overrides the player's chosen temperament. A smaller set of strongly recognized approaches may be more convincing than unlimited-looking dialogue that converges identically.

**Use consequences that differ in kind.** Lost time, damaged standing, confiscated goods and bodily vulnerability create different dilemmas. More severe punishment is not automatically better. A consequence must be attributable to an understandable act and permit proportionate recovery, or players learn to reload rather than inhabit the situation.

**Optional difficulty can change knowledge requirements, not only damage.** Removing location information makes geography matter. It also changes the required patience, memory and sensory access. Such a mode should be described as a different experience, not the sole authentic way to play.

**A home is stronger when utility and biography meet.** Repeated work, visible improvements and a family connection make returning meaningful. Arbitrary decoration counts do not substitute for those relationships. A modest workshop with a few consequential upgrades can convey more attachment than a huge menu of cosmetic permutations.

**Preserve friction selectively.** Several critics value the demands but differ sharply on when they become repetitive. A useful evaluation would distinguish the first successful procedure, the tenth repetition and the late-game shortcut. Measuring only completion time or overall satisfaction would miss that change in value.

These hypotheses do not authorize implementation, copy the setting or names, or establish OpenLegend architecture requirements.
