## Evidence acquired

Five complete, independently credited 1.0 review bodies have been read: Adam Cook, God is a Geek (September 10, 2024); TJ Denzer, Shacknews (September 10, 2024); Francesco Serino, Multiplayer.it (September 19, 2024); Michal Krupička, Games.cz (the original article says September 18, 2024, not the aggregator's September 26 date); and Claudia Tjia, Gameliner (September 15, 2024). The Italian, Czech and Dutch articles were read in their original text and will be paraphrased in English, not passed off as English-language originals. [R1–R5]

They distinguish the satisfaction of improving an understandable process from manual placement, early repetition, directionlessness and the strain of another expanding dependency chain. Krupička's self-reported road-building example is particularly useful: the road did not accelerate transport, but he valued its appearance. This is an attributed play account, not a new research playtest.

Version 1.2 shipped June 2, 2026 simultaneously on PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series S/X. Its developer notes introduce build-gun vehicle paths, fluid trucks/stations, weather and new-game resource randomization/cost options. The older assertion that controller support is absent is no longer current. Platform parity must not be confused with universal cross-play: current documentation allows Steam/Epic together and consoles together, but not PC/console together. [V1][V2]
