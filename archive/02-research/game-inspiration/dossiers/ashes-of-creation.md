# Ashes of Creation

**Research date:** October 26, 2026. **Current pass:** in progress; [scoped ledger](../research-progress-mmos-2026-10-26.md). Mike's “Ashes of Cremation” is interpreted as this title. This dossier distinguishes the playable Alpha Two/Early Access build, the much larger intended product and the subsequent studio closure.

## Identity and current boundary

Intrepid's Windows MMORPG entered Steam Early Access on December 11, 2025. The developer's store description explicitly calls it Alpha Two, warns of incomplete systems, instability and wipes, and describes two future beta phases. It is not evidence of a finished 1.0. The continuing presence of that marketing text in the store's retrieved page is not evidence that development remains active. [A01]

Game Developer's February 10, 2026 update reports that a California WARN notice describes Intrepid's permanent closure and 123 job losses. The same article attributes the explanation of a board/leadership dispute to Steven Sharif; it does not independently establish that explanation. Separate the corroborated closure from disputed responsibility or allegations of financial wrongdoing. [A02]

The original attraction was consequential shared civilization: people contribute to settlements, exchange goods, choose leaders, contest transport and change a realm together. This remains a useful design proposition even though the commercial production failed. It does not demonstrate that a complete, sustainable version of that proposition was delivered.

## Tested systems versus the broader promise

A gameplay engineer's account of his own work provides unusually useful implementation evidence. Jacob Burdecki identifies settlement citizenship, elections/mayorship, warehouses, freeholds and item/decor placement as delivered responsibilities during 2024–2026. He describes settlements growing from wilderness to town, elected mayors directing buildings, account-bound citizenship and both globally accessible and location-restricted storage. Treat his portfolio's quality claims as self-description; it is not an independent capacity benchmark. [A03]

The design relationship matters: civic membership gives a player a place to care about; municipal construction creates demand for resources; local material storage makes geography consequential. It also produces dependency: a player's trade may rely on other people maintaining a settlement and transport route. That can create cooperation or exclusion. The desired social story must be tested alongside the cost of being late, absent or outside the dominant group.

The November 2025 studio announcement names Summoner as the final primary archetype and describes its essence resource and three vessel types. Its announcement of a December 11 Steam transition and Harbinger update is a dated production statement, not proof that every advertised future class combination shipped. [A04]

## Production and commercial evidence

| Measurement | Scope and date | Meaning and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US$3,271,809 pledged by 19,576 backers | Original Kickstarter, May 1–June 2, 2017 | Campaign pledges; not lifetime sales, operating revenue, cash remaining or profit. [A05] |
| Paid Alpha Two access, intended later subscription | Steam Early Access developer statement, December 2025 | Access funded testing and services; intended subscription never establishes an achieved subscription business. [A01] |
| Permanent studio closure, 123 affected jobs | California WARN as reported February 10, 2026 | Evidence of production failure; not an audited explanation of liabilities or total development cost. [A02] |

Lifetime product revenue and project profit cannot be calculated from Kickstarter pledges, review counts or a Steam launch price. A defensible estimate requires paid access/package receipts, refunds, other funding, fees and operating costs. Those inputs are not established by the sources above. Further commercial and reception research remains part of this pass.
