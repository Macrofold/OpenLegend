# G142 — Grounded

**Status: complete full-pass research dossier.** Research date: September 27, 2026. [Roster](../research-roster.md) · [Requirements](../research-requirements.md) · [Progress](../research-progress.md). Research only, not an OpenLegend implementation proposal.

Grounded is most useful as a study of **making scale itself a game system**. A suburban yard becomes a legible wilderness because the player already understands grass, soda cans, sprinklers, picnic tables, sandboxes and garden tools at human scale. Obsidian then layers survival, creature ecology, combat mastery, authored mystery and construction over that familiar topology. The result is a survival game whose world is dense because ordinary objects become mountains, roads, caves, landmarks, resources and hazards at once.

## R01 — identity, scope, versions and player promise

Obsidian Entertainment developed Grounded and Xbox Game Studios publishes it. It entered Xbox Game Preview and Steam Early Access on **July 28, 2020** with a thirteen-person team and reached **1.0 on September 27, 2022** after more than two years of public development. [Launch announcement](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/grounded/it-s-time-to-get-grounded) · [1.0 release](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/grounded/grounded-1-0-release).

The original game is now a cross-platform product:
- Windows/Steam, Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S;
- PlayStation 4/5 and Nintendo Switch from **April 16, 2024**;
- cross-play across supported platforms;
- single-player or up to four-player online co-op;
- cloud-backed **Shared Worlds** that trusted friends can host without the original owner online. [20-million/platform announcement](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/grounded/coming-soon-to-other-platforms) · [Shared Worlds](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/grounded/grounded-heads-into-the-home-stretch).

**Update 1.4, Fully Yoked**, launched with the additional platforms and was explicitly described by Obsidian as the **last planned content update**. It added New Game+, Ant Queens and related choices/items, new equipment, Playgrounds additions and other features while preserving future bug-fix/refinement support. Later maintenance does not turn Grounded into a live-content service with an open-ended first-game roadmap. [Fully Yoked](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/grounded/fully-yoked).

**Current original-game patch boundary:** the newest first-party original-Grounded patch located in this audit is **1.4.7.4815 (May 12, 2025)**, a hotfix after 1.4.7. It fixes pet/REMIX.D/playground issues and follows 1.4.7's pet, zipline, AFK-needs and Playgrounds changes. No later original-game 1.4.8+ patch was found on Obsidian's current news surface; Grounded 2 updates are separate. [1.4.7](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/grounded/grounded-patch-147) · [1.4.7.4815](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/grounded/grounded-patch-1471).

**Grounded 2 is a separate sequel.** It entered Game Preview / Steam Early Access on July 29, 2025 and remains explicitly in Early Access as of September 2026, with its own park, Buggies, archetypes, progression and content cadence. Its current systems do **not** establish original Grounded rules. [Grounded 2 current site](https://grounded2.obsidian.net/) · [September 2026 patch](https://grounded2.obsidian.net/news/grounded-2/patch-0505).

The original player's promise is: choose one of four shrunken teenagers, discover why you have been reduced to insect scale, survive a backyard ecology, craft gear from what lives there, build routes and homes into the landscape, and eventually become capable of confronting creatures and spaces that initially felt impossibly large.

First-session play is about water, food, shelter, analyzing materials and avoiding creatures. Established play is about deliberate loadouts, elemental matchups, upgraded gear, traversal infrastructure, labs/bosses, resource logistics, shared bases and story completion. Fully Yoked adds an explicit replay/endgame loop through the REMIX.R and New Game+.

## R02 — player actions and major mechanics

### Teen identity and progression baseline

The player chooses **Max, Willow, Pete or Hoops**. They are authored personalities with different voice lines but share the same underlying mechanical capability: there is no custom-stat character creator and no teen-specific combat class. Build identity emerges later from gear, mutations, Milk Molars, food, trinkets and player skill rather than from the selected teen.

### Gathering, analysis and recipe discovery

Players chop grass, break pebbles and acorns, harvest sap/fibers, hunt insects, dive for aquatic materials, dig buried resources and scavenge human-made remnants. A field station's Resource Analyzer converts newly found material examples into recipe knowledge and Raw Science, so **seeing a new material creates a concrete research reason to bring one home**.

BURG.L/ASL terminals use chips and Raw Science to unlock further recipes/upgrades. The progression pattern is therefore not one monolithic tech tree: environmental discovery, story-lab chips, shop-like science purchases, boss drops and special events each open different capability families.

### Survival needs and environmental hazards

Health, hunger, thirst and stamina are persistent constraints. Dew, juice/soda droplets, water storage and crafted infrastructure gradually make hydration reliable; hunting/gathering/cooking do the same for food.

The yard adds specialized environmental pressures:
- underwater oxygen;
- haze/toxic-gas exposure without appropriate protection;
- **sizzle** heat in the sandbox/BBQ region;
- darkness and dangerous cave/lab spaces.

This is useful because the game does not make one generic "survival resistance" solve every biome; environment-specific equipment changes what routes are viable.

### Combat: observation, blocking and perfect blocking

Grounded's melee combat has a stronger timing component than many survival-crafting games. Players attack in weapon-specific sequences, manage stamina, block with weapons/shields and can **perfect block/parry** by timing defense close to impact. Strong creatures telegraph attack chains; learning the rhythm can matter more than simply crafting the next tier.

The **PEEP.R** observation mode lets players identify creatures and reveal information including weaknesses/resistances. Equipment can exploit physical and elemental vulnerabilities. Current original-game elemental paths include **Fresh, Spicy, Salty and Sour**; smithing can commit eligible weapons to an augment route after their early upgrades. [Creature strengths/weaknesses](https://grounded.wiki.gg/wiki/Creature_Strengths_and_Weaknesses) · [Smithing Station](https://grounded.wiki.gg/wiki/Smithing_Station).

Ranged bows/crossbows, throwable weapons and later magical staves broaden combat. Status effects, armor perks, mutations, trinkets and consumables can compose into specialized builds.

### Armor, weapons and irreversible upgrade choices

Creature parts and world materials become tools, weapons and armor. Armor can be light/medium/heavy with different protection/stamina tradeoffs, piece effects and full-set bonuses. At the Smithing Station, weapons/armor gain damage/defense and durability. Higher weapon levels require a commitment to Mighty or an elemental route; armor eventually branches between **Bulky** (more raw durability/defense) and **Sleek** (lower raw improvement plus a distinctive piece effect). [Armor](https://grounded.wiki.gg/wiki/Armor_%28Grounded%29) · [Smithing Station](https://grounded.wiki.gg/wiki/Smithing_Station).

That branching is important: an insect drop is not merely a sword recipe; it can become the center of an equipment interaction set.

### Mutations are earned perks, not conventional levels

Grounded has no normal XP-to-character-level ladder. **Mutations** unlock through behavior, discoveries, bosses or purchases and can improve or qualitatively change combat/exploration. Many action-derived mutations have multiple phases. Players begin with limited active slots; Milk Molar progression can expand capacity, and mutation loadouts support rapid build changes. The current original game contains dozens of mutations rather than one fixed class tree. [Mutations](https://grounded.wiki.gg/wiki/Mutations_%28Grounded%29).

This creates a "what you repeatedly do teaches your character" feel without pretending every attribute grows organically.

### Milk Molars: explicit permanent-stat progression

Hidden **Milk Molars** are collectible upgrade currency for personal stats such as health/stamina and survival efficiency. **Mega Milk Molars** improve shared-world capacity categories that benefit all players. The original game's current world contains separate normal and Mega collections; New Game+ changes their repeatability. [Milk Molars](https://grounded.wiki.gg/wiki/Milk_Molars_%28Grounded%29).

This couples exploration/completionism directly to mechanical character improvement.

### Building and the authored map

Players place floors, walls, roofs, stairs, scaffolds, storage, production stations, lighting and decorative structures. Bases can become large forts, but building is also a **traversal language**: stairs/bridges can reach vertical landmarks and zipline towers can turn a long dangerous route into infrastructure.

Raw-material logistics matter. Grass planks and weed stems are carried as bulky physical stacks rather than ordinary inventory items; pallets and hauling effects make construction throughput a separate problem from personal inventory.

### Crafting, repair and production

Workbench/equipment recipes transform insect parts and plants into gear. Specialized stations produce food, jerky, smoothies, upgrade material and other intermediates. Durability creates repair/maintenance costs; higher upgraded equipment shifts toward Repair Glue rather than repeating exact rare components.

The game has no money-driven merchant economy as its core. Raw Science acts as a knowledge currency, while materials/time/creature risk are the real production budget.

### Traversal and player-built routes

Running/jumping/climbing improvised terrain are constant. Dandelion tufts historically function as fall-arrest/glide tools; swimming and underwater gear open the pond; ziplines connect constructed anchors across the yard. The ZIP.R later enables two-way zipline travel, turning bases and high points into a player-authored transit network.

Grounded rewards treating **space itself as something to engineer**.

### Pets and companion mechanics

Grounded has a real—but bounded—companion layer. Players can tame **aphids, weevils and gnats** with species-specific slurries, and Fully Yoked adds **baby Red, Black and Fire Ants** through friendly Ant Queen outcomes. A following pet provides a mechanical bonus and extra inventory; pets can be named, housed and dressed. Difficulty changes their risk: current references describe pets as invulnerable by default on Mild/Medium, while they can die on Whoa!, leaving a memorial. [Pets](https://grounded.wiki.gg/wiki/Pets).

Pets are not autonomous combat-party characters with dialogue, quests or affinity arcs. Their randomized "personalities" and happiness are cosmetic rather than deep agent goals, so the companion system should not be inflated into an RPG relationship simulation.

### Quests, activities and reward loops

The **ASL/BURG.L quest system** supplies both main-line Apprentice tasks and repeatable Daily Quests for **Raw Science**. Tasks deliberately sample the game's verbs—crafting, building, creature kills, cooking, perfect blocking, smoothies, exploration landmarks and other yard activities—so the quest layer teaches/rewards system breadth rather than adding a separate quest-only ruleset. [BURG.L Quests](https://grounded.wiki.gg/wiki/BURG.L_Quests).

Other recurring activities include:
- MIX.R/Super MIX.R defense challenges;
- creature-card discovery and gold-card hunting through PEEP.R;
- optional bosses;
- pet taming;
- cooking/smoothie preparation;
- base decoration/coziness;
- Playgrounds creation and browsing.

There is no casino-style minigame progression or separate sports economy; the activities reuse the same survival/combat/building resources.

### Creature raids and defensive events

Insects can react to player behavior and attack a base through the faction-reactivity/waft-emitter systems. MIX.R/Super MIX.R events invert exploration into defense: activate a machine and protect it against waves for a reward. PC Gamer's 1.0 review specifically notes that over-hunting creature populations can produce retaliation. [PC Gamer review](https://www.pcgamer.com/grounded-review/).

### Fully Yoked: Ant Queens and New Game+

Update 1.4 added queen-ant interactions with choices that affect rewards/creature relations and introduced the **REMIX.R** New Game+ loop. Entering New Game+ remixes the yard with stronger/infused creatures, new loot/progression and repeatable stat growth while resetting/recontextualizing selected progression. The REMIX.R itself requires major story/boss accomplishments before activation. [Fully Yoked](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/grounded/fully-yoked) · [REMIX.R](https://grounded.wiki.gg/wiki/REMIX.R).

### Playgrounds: creator mode boundary

Grounded later added **Playgrounds**, a separate authoring mode with logic/gizmos and build tools for custom experiences. It is relevant to the product's creativity but should not be mistaken for ordinary Survival's progression rules.

### Useful absences

There is no romance system, recruitable **humanoid** companion party or job/class selection in the original. The four teens have characterization and voice differences but are not different RPG classes. Grounded does have fantasy-like elemental staff weapons/mutations, but no separate mana/spellbook class system. Stealth exists behaviorally—crouching, avoiding patrols, using terrain—but there is no deep stealth-skill progression tree.

The faction boundary needs precision: there is no dialogue-driven political-faction campaign, but insect groups **do** track hostility strongly enough to trigger Payback raids, the Waft Emitter can deliberately provoke factional raids, and Ant Queen choices can change how a colony reacts. That creature-faction layer is gameplay, not merely flavor. [Waft Emitter](https://grounded.wiki.gg/wiki/Waft_Emitter) · [Fully Yoked](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/grounded/fully-yoked).

## R03 — items, entities and composition

### Resource ecology

Grounded's strongest item logic is **material identity**. Familiar backyard life becomes a manufacturing taxonomy:
- grass → planks/building;
- clover → early armor/roofs;
- sap/fiber → adhesives/rope-like inputs;
- acorns → shells/storage/armor;
- insect parts → tools, armor and weapons;
- berry/pupa-derived materials → leather-like processed goods;
- quartzite/marble → weapon/armor improvement;
- mints/spicy candy/salt/sour material → elemental equipment;
- pond materials → diving progression;
- upper-yard creatures/resources → top-tier equipment.

Because the world is hand-authored, material acquisition also teaches geography.

### Equipment as behavioral packages

Armor sets are meaningful because a set can support a way of playing: hauling/building, blocking, ranged damage, poison, stamina, etc. The heavy/light category imposes stamina/protection tradeoffs; piece effects and Sleek bonuses invite mixing. Trinkets add a separate modifier slot.

Weapons are similarly differentiated by damage type, speed, reach, stamina cost, shield compatibility, charged attacks and elemental augmentation. A "better" weapon can be wrong for a target whose weakness differs.

### Inventory versus carried construction materials

Ordinary items occupy slots and stack according to category/capacity progression. Grass planks/weed stems are visibly carried rather than disappearing into normal bags. This separation communicates construction load physically and makes a hauling build/player role possible.

### Loot, duplication and reward objects

Creature drops, boss parts, treasure/chests, trinkets and creature cards are the main loot/reward families rather than randomized Diablo-style weapon showers. Raw Science functions as an unlock/duplication currency in later progression, while smithing materials improve chosen equipment. Rare trinkets and special boss/event drops support build experimentation; the player's long-term inventory is therefore a mixture of deterministic crafted gear and discoverable/rare modifiers.

### Creatures as resource nodes and world actors

Ants, ladybugs, bombardier beetles, spiders, bees, mosquitoes, termites, roly-polies and many others are simultaneously:
- threats or neutral wildlife;
- sources of distinct crafting parts;
- behaviors to observe;
- territorial/ecological signals;
- later progression checks.

Killing an animal is therefore not generic "XP"; it changes what you can physically build and may influence local/faction hostility.

## R04 — progression, economy, time, failure and return loops

### Progression layers

Grounded stacks several parallel advancement systems:
1. **recipes/material tiers** through analysis and discoveries;
2. **Raw Science/chip purchases**;
3. **gear tiers and smithing upgrades**;
4. **mutations** earned through actions/events;
5. **Milk Molar permanent stats/capacity**;
6. **story/lab access** and boss gates;
7. **player-built infrastructure/routes**;
8. **knowledge mastery** of attack patterns, creature locations and environmental routes.

That breadth is why a character can feel dramatically stronger without ever gaining "level 27."

### Day/night ecology

Time changes creature routines and navigation risk. Some dangerous creatures sleep during parts of the day and roam at night; darkness reduces player visibility. Beds/sleep can skip dangerous or inconvenient hours. PC Gamer describes learning safe routes partly by understanding creature patterns rather than treating enemies as random spawns. [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/grounded-review/).

### Death and recovery

On ordinary survival settings, death creates a recoverable **backpack** containing carried items while permanent/world progression persists. Co-op players can revive downed allies before full death. Custom difficulty settings can change survival/death friction. This supports tense corpse runs without making a dead avatar erase the base/story.

### Economy

There is no conventional gold market. Major currencies/sinks are:
- materials and durability;
- Raw Science for recipes/upgrades/duplication;
- Milk Molars for persistent stats/capacity;
- time/risk spent on repeated creature drops;
- late-game/New Game+ resources for further equipment growth.

This keeps economy spatial: "wealth" means knowing and controlling the yard's resource routes.

### Endgame and return loop

The authored story has an actual ending. Optional bosses, collectibles, large builds and completion goals add play beyond it. Fully Yoked's REMIX.R made the endgame explicitly replayable: restart into a remixed, harder yard with continued meta-strength and new item/effect opportunities rather than simply reloading the same solved map.

## R05 — worked interactions and concrete situations

Unless attributed otherwise, these are rules-based illustrations from documented systems.

### Case 1 — a wolf spider changes the route before it becomes a combat target

**Intention:** reach oak-area resources early.  
**Conditions:** poor armor/weapons; wolf-spider den/patrol nearby.  
**Actions:** PEEP.R/observe, move during a safer window, crouch/use grass and roots as cover or build an alternate path.  
**Interaction:** the creature's power turns map knowledge and timing into progression before the player can win a direct fight.  
**Result:** "avoid" is a valid early solution.  
**Later change:** upgraded equipment + learned parry timings can turn the same spider into a farmable material source.

### Case 2 — perfect blocking converts knowledge into survivability

**Intention:** defeat a creature whose raw damage exceeds the player's comfortable healing budget.  
**Conditions:** known attack animation/sequence and a block-capable loadout.  
**Actions:** stop attacking, watch the tell and block inside the perfect-block window.  
**Interaction:** timing can negate/mitigate the dangerous hit and preserve stamina/health compared with merely face-tanking it.  
**Result:** combat mastery reduces resource consumption.  
**Limit:** latency in multiplayer and enemy/hitbox complexity can make the timing feel less reliable to some players; current Steam criticism still mentions combat/hitbox frustration.

### Case 3 — PEEP.R turns ecology into a crafting decision

**Intention:** kill a new upper-yard insect efficiently.  
**Conditions:** several weapon options with different damage/element types.  
**Actions:** peep the creature to expose resistances/weaknesses, then select/augment appropriate equipment.  
**Interaction:** observation changes the expected value of inventory choices.  
**Result:** a world-reading verb becomes part of the build system rather than lore-only scanning.

### Case 4 — research a material, then buy the capability

**Intention:** progress from improvised gear to a new recipe family.  
**Conditions:** player obtains unfamiliar material and reaches a field station/BURG.L progression.  
**Actions:** analyze it for knowledge/Science; recover the relevant chip when required; spend Raw Science on recipes/upgrades.  
**Interaction:** exploration yields both physical stock and informational currency.  
**Result:** the same expedition expands both today's inventory and tomorrow's design space.

### Case 5 — build a staircase because the world is not just scenery

**Intention:** reach a high landmark/resource without repeating a dangerous climbing route.  
**Conditions:** nearby construction materials and valid support/placement.  
**Actions:** erect stairs/scaffolding/bridge into the environment.  
**Interaction:** survival construction changes traversal, not only shelter quality.  
**Result:** the authored map acquires a persistent player-authored route.  
**Next decision:** eventually replace the slow route with ziplines.

### Case 6 — shared hauling creates a co-op builder role

**Intention:** build a distant multi-story base quickly.  
**Conditions:** grass/weed resources nearby; multiple players and pallets.  
**Actions:** one player cuts stems/planks, another uses hauling gear/effects to move bulk stacks, another places blueprints.  
**Interaction:** bulky build resources and carrying limits create real logistical specialization without character classes.  
**Result:** co-op accelerates construction through complementary actions, not a blanket multiplayer productivity bonus.

### Case 7 — provoke or avoid an insect raid

**Intention:** farm a creature population/material while protecting a home.  
**Conditions:** repeated aggression has increased faction irritation.  
**Actions:** continue hunting and prepare defenses, or change activity/location before retaliation.  
**Interaction:** the world can translate resource extraction into a later base-defense cost.  
**Result:** "farm everything near home" is not entirely consequence-free.  
**Attributed reception:** PC Gamer recounts ants effectively forcing abandonment of an early base after aggressive ant killing. [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/grounded-review/).

### Case 8 — REMIX.R turns completion into altered-world progression

**Intention:** continue after beating the major story/boss gates.  
**Conditions:** JavaMatic and required bosses completed; REMIX.R unlocked.  
**Actions:** activate the machine and enter New Game+.  
**Interaction:** story/world progression remixes while persistent advancement interacts with harder infused enemies, further Milk Molar growth and new equipment/effect systems.  
**Result:** mastery of the handcrafted yard becomes a foundation for a changed yard rather than an immediate content endpoint.  
**Boundary:** this is Fully Yoked 1.4 content, not the launch-1.0 endgame.

## R06 — characters, creature AI, social systems and multiplayer

### Four authored teen identities, one mechanics baseline

Max, Willow, Pete and Hoops provide different voices/personality reactions. They share the same underlying capability system; selecting a teen is not selecting fighter/mage/rogue stats. This supports co-op banter/identity without forcing friends into roles they did not choose.

### Pets are useful companions, not simulated friends

Tamed aphids/weevils/gnats and baby ants can follow a player, carry inventory and apply buffs. Their names, attire, cosmetic happiness/personality and difficulty-dependent mortality can create attachment, but they do not maintain dialogue, independent goals, relationship arcs or schedules comparable to human companions. This is the closest original Grounded comes to a persistent companion mechanic. [Pets](https://grounded.wiki.gg/wiki/Pets).

### Creature behavior is the social/ecological fabric

The backyard lacks towns full of humanoid NPC schedules, but its creatures have:
- neutral/aggressive/territorial relationships;
- patrol/sleep/den behavior;
- attacks against other species;
- faction retaliation;
- tameable pet roles;
- boss/event-specific behavior.

The player's "social model" is therefore partly ecological: learn what tolerates you, what patrols when, what can be provoked and which ecosystem relationship can be exploited.

Update 1.4 even added a custom option affecting whether natural enemies fight each other, making the ecological behavior explicit enough to expose as a creator/sandbox rule. [Fully Yoked](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/grounded/fully-yoked).

### Human co-op and Shared Worlds

Grounded supports solo play and four-player online co-op. Ordinary co-op lets players divide exploration/build/combat work. **Shared Worlds**, introduced before 1.0, solve an important persistence problem: a shared cloud world can be hosted by another authorized friend while the owner is absent, with one active host at a time and progress restored for the next host. [Shared Worlds](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/grounded/grounded-heads-into-the-home-stretch).

This is not the same as an always-running dedicated server. It is a handoff model that preserves group ownership without operating a continuously simulated shard.

Current Steam testimony still reports disconnections/session errors for some users; positive players conversely praise the cross-play/achievement continuity and family/friend co-op. These are qualitative accounts, not a measured network-failure rate. [Steam reviews](https://steamcommunity.com/app/962130/reviews/).

### Story relationships

BURG.L, Dr. Wendell Tully and Ominent provide the authored relationship/narrative axis. It is not a romance/reputation simulator: relationships mostly progress through story tasks, discoveries and dialogue rather than freeform social stats.

## R07 — art, audio, interface, accessibility and feel

Grounded's central art trick is **recognizable material at alien scale**. Blades of grass become tree trunks; a juice box is architecture; a picnic table becomes a major biome/landmark. Because the map is handcrafted, silhouettes of the oak, hedge, shed, sandbox, picnic table and household trash become navigational memory.

Creature animation/audio are functional telegraphs as well as atmosphere. Spiders are scary partly because leg motion, footsteps and scale communicate threat before a stat sheet does. PC Gamer praises how different insects' behavior makes learning the yard feel ecological rather than like clearing enemy camps. [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/grounded-review/).

The game supports first- and third-person play and extensive difficulty/custom settings. Most distinctively, its **Arachnophobia Safe Mode** progressively abstracts spider visuals for players who cannot tolerate realistic spiders. That is a domain-specific accessibility response to a threat central to the game's fantasy rather than simply removing the content.

The UI exposes creature weaknesses through PEEP.R, crafting dependencies through recipes, construction as translucent placement, status effects/mutations through build screens and location/story through the SCA.B interface. Some critics still find progression direction unclear despite those tools—an important distinction between **mechanical legibility** and **goal legibility**.

### Audio and music

Creature sound is gameplay information: footsteps, wing buzzes, attack calls and environmental cues can warn the player before a threat is visible in tall grass or darkness. The official soundtrack also deliberately maps music to places/states: the 34-track Year 1/Year 2 release includes biome/home/danger/story pieces such as **Grasslands, Home, The Dark, Haze, Koi, Upper Grasslands Day/Night** and boss/event cues. Steam credits **Justin E. Bell** as composer, with Year 2 music by Brian Trifon, Brian Lee White and Jay Wiltzen/Finishing Move. [Official soundtrack](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2161390/Grounded_Official_Soundtrack/).

That state-linked score reinforces geography: the yard's regions can sound distinct even though they are all physically one backyard.

## R08 — story, authored mystery and player-created narrative

**Story spoilers below at a structural level.**

The teenagers wake in the backyard with no explanation for their shrunken state. Investigation eventually connects the SPAC.R shrinking technology, Ominent, the abandoned labs, BURG.L and scientist **Wendell Tully**. The story asks players to restore lab systems, gather super chips, explore increasingly dangerous regions and eventually prepare the means to return to normal size.

Grounded's story matters to the survival loop because labs and narrative destinations are also capability gates. Entering the Hedge, Pond, Haze and Black Anthill regions is not a separate "quest corridor"; those trips teach new ecology, resources and traversal while delivering authored evidence. 1.0 explicitly completed/revised the quest flow and fully voiced story. [1.0 notes](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/grounded/grounded-1-0-release).

There are meaningful outcome differences at the end depending on whether the player completes optional late objectives tied to Wendell's condition, giving exploration an authored payoff beyond percentage completion.

Player-authored stories coexist with this plot: the first spider that chased the group home, the fortress that survived a raid, a zipline accidentally ending among enemies, or a friend's backpack rescue can be more memorable than a cutscene. This is a productive hybrid: **a fixed mystery gives direction while systemic ecology supplies anecdotes**.

## R09 — production and development

Grounded began inside Obsidian as a deliberately smaller project. Xbox reported that the team had only **13 people** when the Early Access/Game Preview release passed one million players in its first two days. By 1.0, Windows Central described a roughly **20-person** team. Those are dated snapshots, not evidence of an exact constant staffing curve. [Xbox one-million announcement](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2020/07/31/grounded-over-one-million-players/) · [Windows Central review](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/xbox/grounded-review).

Public development was a core production method, not just a distribution label:
- Xbox Game Preview + Steam Early Access;
- in-menu feedback/Discord;
- a feature board exposing community suggestions and planned/delivered items;
- Public Test builds for pre-release validation;
- cadence changes based on feedback and small-team capacity.

Obsidian explicitly said in November 2020 that community feedback led it to move from more frequent releases toward larger, more stable content releases. A May 2021 developer letter repeats the tradeoff: a small team could not ship monthly content while simultaneously doing the larger work required for 1.0. [Koi Pond developer letter](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/grounded/early-access-update-0-4-the-koi-pond-out-now) · [2021 developer letter](https://www.obsidian.net/news/grounded/letter-from-the-developers).

This development method had real risk. An October 2020 Xbox Flight was closed to new users after save-file wiping when moving between test/live builds. That is useful evidence that "test with the community" creates migration/save integrity obligations, not a free feedback channel. [Public Test incident](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/grounded/public-test-through-xbox-flight-closed-for-new-players).

At 1.0, Obsidian reset Early Access **story/quest progress** because the narrative flow had been reworked while preserving inventories, bases, recipes and other progression. That is a strong example of selective migration: preserve investment that still means the same thing; reset state whose semantics changed. [1.0](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/grounded/grounded-1-0-release).

Double Eleven later assisted with the 2024 additional-platform release. [Fully Yoked announcement](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/get-ready-to-flex-in-grounded-with-the-upcoming-fully-yoked-1-4-update).

## R10 — marketing, distribution, community and virality

Grounded was announced at X019 and used playable preview programs, PAX/streams, Xbox Game Pass, Steam Early Access and recurring themed content updates to maintain discovery. The premise is highly communicable in still images/video: **ordinary yard object + tiny human** communicates scale immediately.

Game Pass materially lowered the access barrier during Preview, while Steam provided a conventional Early Access storefront. More than one million players entered in the first two days, according to Xbox; the title had passed ten million Preview players before 1.0 and fifteen million shortly after full release. **Negative/mixed current accounts** include:

### Reception tensions worth preserving

**World density versus navigation uncertainty.** Critics consistently like the backyard as a place, yet Windows Central/Gamereactor note that knowing *what to do next* can be harder than reading the space itself.

**Creature danger versus annoyance.** Spiders produce some of the game's most memorable fear and route-planning, but both critics and players describe moments where they function more as progression obstruction than interesting choice.

**Skillful combat versus friction.** Timed blocking and weaknesses give combat mastery, but hitboxes, multiplayer latency and repeated farming can make the same system feel grindy.

**Co-op amplification versus dependency.** Sharing a base/world produces strong social play; if networking fails, the feature with the highest upside can become the dominant frustration.

## R13 — transferable inspiration and limits for OpenLegend

### 1. Use scale to make the familiar newly systemic

Grounded does not need procedural planet generation to create wonder. It changes the **relationship between the player and known objects**. A soda can is a landmark, shelter and resource context because size changed.

OpenLegend can learn from this more broadly: alter one law of relationship—scale, gravity, perception, social status, time—and ordinary objects can acquire new affordances without inventing an entirely unfamiliar ontology.

### 2. Let ecology teach the world

Creature species have recognizable territories, routines, relationships, weaknesses and materials. That makes them more than combat encounters.

For agent-driven worlds, NPC/creature behavior can do double duty:
- create local danger/opportunity;
- communicate place;
- generate resources;
- produce consequences;
- become something players learn rather than merely out-stat.

### 3. Multiple progression dimensions make exploration valuable for different reasons

A side cave might contain a Molar (stats), a new resource (recipes), a chip (tech), a boss/material (gear), or simply a shortcut. Exploration remains rewarding because not every discovery maps to the same XP bar.

**Failure mode:** too many currencies can become bookkeeping. Grounded works best when each is strongly associated with a world activity.

### 4. Knowledge can be a first-class combat stat

PEEP.R weaknesses and attack-pattern learning mean player observation changes outcomes. OpenLegend can reward **knowing what something is and how it behaves** instead of only acquiring a larger damage number.

### 5. Player-built traversal can make construction part of exploration

Stairs, bridges and ziplines permanently rewrite how the authored yard is navigated. This is more meaningful than a base that only stores inventory.

For OpenLegend, construction/invention is strongest when it changes future behavior: who can reach whom, how long journeys take, what hazards remain and what trade/relationship routes become practical.

### 6. Shared Worlds are a useful middle ground between host saves and servers

Grounded's cloud handoff lets a trusted group continue a world without running an always-on simulation. It preserves shared ownership while bounding infrastructure.

**Limit:** only one host at a time and network/account dependencies remain. Do not copy this if the world requires autonomous continuous simulation or stronger multi-writer semantics.

### 7. Selective migration is better than pretending changed semantics are compatible

At 1.0, Obsidian reset story/quest state while preserving bases, inventory and unlocks because the story flow had changed. That is a strong precedent for OpenLegend's development-world policy: **preserve identity/investment where meaning survives; migrate/reset only what cannot truthfully retain meaning**, and tell the player.

### 8. Community feedback works when the product exposes a clear learning loop

Feature boards, test branches and cadence changes made feedback visible. The lesson is not "crowdsource design"; it is to build a process where player evidence can safely influence priorities without turning every request into a promise.

### What not to copy automatically

- insect/spider designs, characters, names or backyard story;
- survival bars merely to signal genre;
- upgrade-currency proliferation without activity identity;
- repeated creature farming if it stops producing new decisions;
- progression gates that rely on unclear next objectives;
- client-host networking assumptions for worlds needing persistent autonomous simulation.
