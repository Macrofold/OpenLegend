# G59 — Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous

**In-progress research checkpoint — September 26, 2026. Not a completed R01–R14 pass.** [Roster](../research-roster.md) · [Progress](../research-progress.md) · [Requirements](../research-requirements.md). Reference research only; no gameplay or engine changes are authorized.

## Scope and preservation

This is the independent Wrath dossier, not an extension that treats [Kingmaker](pathfinder-kingmaker.md) as evidence for the sequel's rules. The target dossier and same-named legacy chapter were absent at the branch's recorded G59 resume point. Existing chapters, mechanics studies, packet sources and the Kingmaker dossier are retained unchanged. The canonical roster explicitly calls for mythic paths, crusade, companions, class/build breadth, native turn-based and real-time-with-pause combat, expansions and branching narrative.

The PC game released on September 2, 2021. Its store describes a single-player Pathfinder First Edition adaptation; Steam Cloud and remote-play features are not cooperative multiplayer. The opening disaster in Kenabres leads into command of a crusade against the Worldwound's demonic invasion. This is an authored party adventure with strategic management, not a continuously simulated population. [S01]

### Edition boundary already established

Owlcat's September 29, 2022 announcement identifies Enhanced Edition as a free PC update and the edition supplied with the console launch. The console announcement explicitly separates Nintendo Switch cloud streaming from the other platforms. This is not a new Enhanced Edition first released in 2026. Later expansion availability, PS5-specific releases, complete-bundle contents and final maintenance dates still need separate verification. [S02]

## Reception evidence already inspected

These notes preserve specific evidence read so far. They do not yet certify that all five required reviews have been read or that the finished reception comparison is complete.

### PC Gamer — Jody Macgregor, September 13, 2021

The full review's body was inspected. Macgregor values the long character-transformation arc, companion variety and ability to change combat presentation mid-fight. He also describes creation choices whose consequences become apparent only hours later, repetitive encounters, uneven mythic-path writing and late-game technical failures. His reported inability to see the ending because of a crash is evidence about his launch review build, not a present-day reproduction. The review finds crusade administration more legible than Kingmaker's while still criticizing the time spent resolving already-decided army battles. [C01]

**Interpretation:** complexity and agency are different properties. A large option list becomes expressive only when the world acknowledges the resulting identity and the player can understand the dependencies. The same reviewer can value customization and dislike the cost of discovering a nonfunctional combination.

### Gamesear — Ash, September 1, 2021

The inspected review explains an Azata playthrough in which NPCs acknowledge the protagonist's altered identity and dragon companion. This is a concrete account of reactivity rather than simply a count of dialogue branches. The reviewer preferred the early story's pacing to Kingmaker but explicitly had not finished the campaign after more than fifty hours. That limitation must remain attached to any conclusion about the story. [C02]

**Interpretation:** small recognition moments can make a dramatic transformation feel socially real. A mechanical aura alone would communicate power; other people noticing it communicates identity. The example does not prove uniform reactivity across every path or every scene.

### RPGFan — Abraham Kobylanski, February 22, 2022

The inspected body discusses consequential recruitment and dialogue, a morally complicated party and the usefulness of character interruptions. The review's Nenio example links characterization to a combat consequence: her experimental eating causes sickness before a later fight. This is an attributed incident, not a claimed playtest by this researcher. The article is later than the launch reviews and should not be flattened into a September 2021 consensus. Its remaining sections and production-related claims require review before final synthesis. [C03]

### Tom's Guide — Alex Wawro, September 13, 2021

Wawro explicitly reports being midway through the second chapter after more than thirty hours. His criticism of dull text and simplistic early evil choices is therefore an early-campaign judgment, not evidence that every late mythic branch lacks nuance. The inspected sections praise optional tutorials and adjustable difficulty, but find army battles lengthy and potentially frustrating when losses reduce future fighting resources. The existence of crusade automation is not evidence that automation preserves every reward and decision. [C04]

## Commercial evidence boundary

Owlcat's official announcement says international sales exceeded **one million units**. Its dated company-news version is January 23, 2023. This is a unit-sales milestone, not active players, lifetime revenue or profit. The January 2025 two-million-copy story encountered during research concerns **Kingmaker**, so it must not be transferred to Wrath. No new private financial estimates are justified by these sources. [S03]
