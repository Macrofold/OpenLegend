# Lineage — original and Remastered PC dossier

**Game:** G200, Lineage's original 1998 PC lineage and its Remastered continuation.  
**Status:** Research in progress; substantive first checkpoint, not completed or independently reviewed.  
**Research date:** October 28, 2026.

## 1. Scope and player promise

NC's official retrospective dates the original service to September 1998. This dossier studies that PC lineage, including its Remastered continuation; Lineage II, the mobile M/W/2M products and the separate 2026 Classic service require distinct labels. [S01](#s01)

The current Korean download page directs players through PURPLE to the Remastered client and lists Windows 10 or later, 64-bit, among its requirements. That verifies the documented regional PC access route; other regions' operation, payment and continuity are being researched separately. No client was installed or run. [S02](#s02)

The useful OpenLegend question is how familiar places, economic roles and collective authority can give a persistent world meaning beyond individual advancement. The eventual comparison must distinguish players' freely chosen cooperation from institutions that become obligatory, and shared geography from the different lifetimes of characters, possessions and encounters.

## 2. Foundational conflict and a concrete collective stake

The official artbook places **Deforge**, a survivor of the royal family's tragedy, on Whispering Isle; **Kurtz** and the Black Knights pursue him. **Ken Rauhel** is the opposing ruler. Elves originate around the Mother Tree and are drawn from relative isolation into surrounding conflict. Later, Ken Rauhel seeks **Dantes** and the Dark Elves after defeat; their desire to return to the surface helps drive another war. These motives connect political legitimacy, exclusion and territorial conflict. [S01](#s01)

Castle sieges give player alliances an analogous stake: taking a throne can grant authority to adjust and collect taxes. This is a documented relationship between military success and economic governance, not merely an achievement label. [S01](#s01)

**OpenLegend hypothesis:** a shared accomplishment is more meaningful when it changes a place or service people use. That does not establish a need for mandatory sieges, player taxation or winner-take-all politics. The full dossier will test this possibility against other roles and firsthand accounts of enjoyment, effort and departure.

## 3. Remaining work and evidence limits

The complete pass still requires current and historical mechanical boundaries, major classes and skills, production and item chains, death and recovery, everyday traversal and native composition, seven varied causal situations, presentation, five independent written assessments, production/marketing, an economic evidence table and the conditional external-creation investigation. Commercial, reception and production researchers are working in parallel on this same title.

The official artbook's complete written body and the download page were read. Some official beginner and Powerbook pages return a JavaScript shell through the web reader; full publicly supplied guide content is being recovered before adopting detailed claims. Promotional historical claims of being the world's first siege system are not adopted.
