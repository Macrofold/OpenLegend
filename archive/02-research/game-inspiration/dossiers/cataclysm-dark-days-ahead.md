## Version and distribution evidence

The [upstream README](https://github.com/CleverRaven/Cataclysm-DDA/blob/master/README.md), read through the GitHub connector, describes a persistent, procedurally generated, turn-based survival world and separates stable from experimental downloads. It credits contributions from over 1,000 volunteers and identifies the project's CC BY-SA 3.0 licensing, while noting that some bundled components have other notices. Terminal and graphical-tile builds and community packaging are separate presentation/distribution routes. This is not a Steam-exclusive game.

The [Steam publisher's August 24, 2026 announcement, mirrored with its source link](https://steamdb.info/patchnotes/24898632/), says 0.I (Ito) has reached Steam, restores a Linux build, and explicitly declines to distribute nightly experimental builds there. The announcement also acknowledges a delay between upstream stable release and Steam packaging. Do not use a dated 0.H review as evidence of the latest stable version or assume every channel updates simultaneously. The announcement's indexed substantive text has been read; the primary linked Steam body and upstream release metadata remain to be checked.

## R12 — Read reception evidence

### Shaun Musgrave — TouchArcade, January 15, 2016; historical iOS port

Musgrave praises the breadth of interactions and the ability to create an individual survival story. Crafting, disassembly, clothing and storage generate meaningful decisions rather than a decorative loot list. His central reservations concern two separate barriers: learning the dense game itself and operating a PC-oriented interface through a mobile software keyboard. The positive judgment is conditional on accepting both. This is criticism of a particular 2016 port, not an evaluation of today's Steam interface or mobile pricing. [Full review body read](https://toucharcade.com/2016/01/15/cataclysm-dark-days-ahead-review/).

### Eric Limer — Popular Mechanics, December 17, 2018; firsthand critical essay

Limer describes deep investment in self-directed survival, but also an intimidating learning burden and escalating chains of prerequisites. The essay follows his transition from player to contributor, making participation in the game's development part of its appeal and frustration. That involvement is disclosed rather than treated as independent outsider testing. His historical account of release cadence is not a statement about the current stable build. [Substantive essay read](https://www.popularmechanics.com/culture/gaming/a25399561/cataclysm-dark-days-ahead-review-open-source/).

### Cabinet de Chaologie — “Survivre en ASCII”; undated retrieved French review

The writer finds the presentation austere and keyboard interface difficult, yet values the depth hidden behind them. Clothing's protection, warmth and storage properties create competing needs; scavenging and constructing a workable base give the player goals without a central campaign plot. The criticism includes time demands, rough NPC behavior and a perceived pause in updates. These are historical observations with an unverified article date, not evidence that current development has stopped. The retrieved body does not establish a personal byline. [Full substantive review body read](https://cabinetdechaologie.wordpress.com/seances/onzieme-seance/cataclysm-dark-days-ahead-survivre-en-ascii/).

### Liam Squires-Hand — GamingOnLinux, March 19, 2019; update report with firsthand impressions

This article combines a release report with actual play impressions. Squires-Hand values being able to choose different graphical styles and describes memorable starts, including an isolated winter cabin and an already infected survivor. He found the tutorial useful but noticed that its explanations assumed ASCII presentation even when using tiles. That is a concrete discoverability mismatch rather than a criticism of tactical difficulty. This shorter account is labeled as impressions, not misrepresented as a long formal review. [Full article body read](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2019/03/cataclysm-dark-days-ahead-a-free-and-open-source-turn-based-survival-game-had-a-huge-update/).
