## What is actually evidenced

Cliff and Grand describe each Norn as having its own modular recurrent neural network, with lifetime Hebbian learning and modulation by a simulated biochemical system. Network and biochemical details are genetically specified; reproduction can mutate and duplicate genetic material. Norns interact with users, objects and one another. This establishes a learned, embodied controller rather than a purely fixed decision tree. It does not establish general reasoning or human-like understanding. [C1]

[C1: Dave Cliff and Stephen Grand, *The Creatures Global Digital Ecosystem*, Artificial Life 5(1), 77–93, 1999](https://doi.org/10.1162/106454699568683). Primary publisher abstract was available through indexed text; direct publisher access failed. [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10421678/) confirms identity. No network implementation, training run or released binary was inspected.

## Why it is a valuable counterexample

The choice is not limited to 'hardcoded rules or a large language model'. A compact learned controller can map sensory and bodily conditions to behavior, acquire associations, and vary between individuals. That is a different kind of intelligence from interpreting a promise or explaining a political disagreement.

For OpenLegend, the useful question is which responsibilities need language-like interpretation. Locomotion, reflexive orientation, familiar sensorimotor skills or an authored animal's behavior may have good nonverbal solutions. A person can also execute a learned skill without reconsidering its purpose at every movement.

This is not permission to put an uninspectable survival optimizer under every person. A low-level controller must operate within the scope of what the character has chosen and what its body can do. Learning how to approach a bowl should not grant the right to eat from it or decide that hunger outweighs a personal commitment.

## Learning has a different engineering cost

A model that learns online can improve through experience, but can also acquire undesirable, unstable or hard-to-explain behavior. Debugging now involves training history and internal state as well as the current inputs. A learned preference may need to change after the character has an important new experience; simply preserving old weights does not guarantee autobiographical continuity.

An explicit reusable method is a simpler alternative for reliable routine work. Native execution makes errors easier to reproduce, while cognition chooses when the method fits. Online learning should earn its complexity through a specific benefit, not merely through a stronger appearance of 'real AI'.

## Hypothetical comparison

Consider an authored small creature learning to approach a safe food source while avoiding a recurring nuisance. Compare a small adaptive controller with a simple species policy and an LLM decision. Evaluate sustained behavior, resource cost and understandable variation rather than verbal eloquence. Separately test a human character deciding whether the same food belongs to someone else. Success on the first task cannot justify bypassing judgment on the second.

## Unresearched dimensions

This case does not certify exact neural architecture, training dynamics, language acquisition, inherited versus learned traits in later editions, population scale, player reception or welfare-like subjective experience. It supplies a bounded alternative controller family for the synthesis, not a claim that contemporary OpenLegend should reproduce Creatures.
