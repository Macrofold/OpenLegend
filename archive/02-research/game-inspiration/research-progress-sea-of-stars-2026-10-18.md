## Evidence and verification

Six substantive independent written base-game reviews were read: Nintendo Life, PC Gamer, RPG Site, GamesRadar+, RPGamer and TheSixthAxis. The dossier records their actual praise and criticism, with launch-era context. Three substantive helpful recommended Steam accounts and three helpful negative accounts are summarized alongside recent testimony and a dated English-language aggregate. Those samples are qualitative, not estimates of complaint prevalence. Additional criticism supports the mechanics and expansion sections.

The review checked the separation of the original game, Dawn of Equinox, Watchmaker, mobile and Sunset; Sunball's hold/release and Moonerang's deflections; dated NG+ reports; current versus historical food/combo rules; spoiler labels; paid copies versus players; the hypothetical first-week economic sensitivity; and the R15 modding screen. R15 is **uncertain**: modifications exist, but sustained popular adoption was not established, so the conditional deep dive is not triggered.

All ten selected image files were visually inspected, and their byte counts, SHA-256 hashes and supplied dimensions were verified. Seven JPEGs are 1920 × 1080; three PNGs are 250 × 250. No exact game build, internal resolution, clock time or motion result is inferred. A separate download archive preserves the ten unaltered files and provenance outside Git: `Sea-of-Stars-reference-screenshots.zip`, 4,564,291 bytes, SHA-256 `f79b0dd925504f20ce0dfeb9f159da5833ad7cdeedeb1c25c20563ba40237b9d`. Public reference availability does not grant production reuse rights.

The full affected documentation diff received content review, including independent checks of art integration, mechanics/version boundaries and reception/economic claims. A malformed interview link was corrected and a repeated developer-source explanation consolidated. New relative paths and section anchors resolve. The new dossier, illustrated study, manifest and this ledger are formatted with the repository's pinned Prettier 3.6.2. No runtime code or image binaries are part of the change; no game stress, motion, performance or gameplay tests were run for this documentation task.
