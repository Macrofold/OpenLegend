## Evidence limits and open information

Completion means the requested research categories were substantively investigated and documented, not that every private number or individual interaction is known.

- **Business:** all-in cost, realized price/platform mix, the actual publishing/recoupment contract, developer receipts/profit, paid campaign spend and channel attribution remain unknown. The Steam estimate is a third-party model with generalized pricing, not disclosed accounts.
- **Players:** the purposive review/comment set is qualitative. It cannot establish population preference shares, current retention, churn causes or the proportion who eventually enjoy the game.
- **Rules:** community documentation frequently required indexed access because direct wiki retrieval was blocked. Specific unresolved automatic quest-trigger and exceptional transport descriptions remain labeled, rather than harmonized by invention.
- **Hands-on scope:** no gameplay, performance benchmarking, mod installation, creator workflow, package security or license-compliance audit was performed. Linked video descriptions and music credits do not imply watched footage or auditioned audio.
- **Scope:** this pass does not reproduce every item coefficient, animation or quest branch. It does not certify previous dossiers or close earlier packet-wide preservation/integration gates.

**Remaining work in this session:** none for the authorized Outward dossier after the completion commit. Future player testing, private commercial analysis or adoption of a design hypothesis would be separate work.
