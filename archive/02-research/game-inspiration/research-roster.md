## Dragon Age series scope

G51–G54 each require the complete R01–R14 pass. Major expansions and DLC need substantive, explicitly separated treatment inside their parent dossier: changed player activities, progression, companions, narrative consequences and reception, not just a list of titles. A series comparison must connect the four dossiers and examine the evolution of protagonist identity, party control and tactics, relationships, choice import, setting continuity, production and audience expectations.

The series comparison must also account for the franchise's distinct smaller games—**Journeys, Legends (including its Remix), The Last Court and Heroes of Dragon Age**—with their actual play, distribution, continuity and retirement boundaries. These are required supplemental series coverage, not silently omitted or passed off as mainline mechanics; they do not replace any G51–G54 research. Do not assume a browser service is still available because its original announcement survives. Dragon Age Keep is continuity infrastructure rather than another full campaign. Books, television, tabletop and other transmedia are context where they materially inform the games, not a new unrequested exhaustive media survey.
