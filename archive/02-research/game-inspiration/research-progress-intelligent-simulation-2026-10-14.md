## Scope and repository provenance

Mike authorized a research pass on intelligence, perceived intelligence, planning, interruptions, memory, world consequences and efficient large-scale simulation. The pass includes game implementations and academic/industrial research, with refreshed Simile material. It does not authorize runtime changes, a new psychological model, a vendor choice, paid experiments or an implementation project.

## Academic, corporate and general engineering scope

The language-model study covers Generative Agents, the revised 1,052-person study, Socrates, Simile's 2026 confidence work and Itaú case, AgentSociety, LMAgent, OASIS, Project Sid, Concordia and Voyager. The architecture study covers temporal abstraction/options, discrete-event scheduling, metareasoning, FLAME GPU 2, local steering and collision avoidance, parallel simulation and population archetypes. The counterevidence note adds a direct LLM-guided combat-policy comparison.

Each study records whether the inspected evidence was an abstract, specified sections, an official technical description or a first-party case report. No claim is made to have read every appendix or watched every presentation. Corporate workflow savings, mathematical benchmarks, survey prediction and interactive game responsiveness remain different evidence classes.

Two especially important refreshes are retained: the June 2026 revision of the 1,052-person paper changes the older headline comparison, and Simile's confidence work distinguishes estimated prediction error from concentrated choice probabilities. Large-population results are qualified by active subsets, actual time-step duration, hardware and what counts as an interaction. Exact figures and sources stay in their research owners rather than becoming a second register here.

## Review and remaining research boundaries

Review checks actual source-to-lesson contributions, contrary evidence, current-versus-target OpenLegend claims, stable keys, topic-only labels, matrix dimensions/codes and local navigation. This is document verification, not gameplay or a performance test. Final publication is established by repository commits; no self-referential future commit hash is invented in this record.

No paid model calls, simulations, model training, game playtests or OpenLegend runtime benchmarks were performed. The current Songs of Syx citizen controller remains unverified; the previously used RimWorld decompilation was unavailable. Several GDC sources are descriptions only. These limits constrain the strength of the findings rather than becoming invented implementation details.

The research does not settle a safe cognition cadence, acceptable approximation of a particular person, a calibrated decision gate or whole-life reliability. Those require separately authorized design and evaluation through existing owners. The broader NPC topic still includes unreviewed source coverage and deeper relationship domains; other learning topics and world dossiers are not marked complete. This focused pass supplies reusable evidence and hypotheses, not a claim to have exhausted every relevant game or paper.
