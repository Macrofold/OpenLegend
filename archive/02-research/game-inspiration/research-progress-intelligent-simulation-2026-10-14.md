## Early evidence — not completed synthesis

- The original [Generative Agents paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.03442v2) explicitly records expensive, slow simulation and behavior/retrieval failures. It is architectural evidence, not proof of economical real-time populations.
- [AgentSociety](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.08691v1) describes grouped asynchronous execution and connection reuse. A reported population or message rate must not be confused with per-character interactive decision latency.
- [Light Society](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.12078v1) uses semantic reuse and task-specific surrogate models for large, structured social experiments. Its billion-agent claim needs the experiment and approximation boundaries alongside it.
- Simile's [August 2026 confidence article](https://www.simile.com/blog/confidence) evaluates predicting simulation error separately from predicting behavior. A high action score is not itself calibrated evidence that a cheap decision should be trusted. The company's broader validation and case studies must remain attributed vendor evidence.
