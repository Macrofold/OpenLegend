## Scope and questions

What remains worth doing after immediate needs and obvious goals are solved?

- Compare finite endings, mastery goals, festivals, social rituals, world changes, expansions and player-created projects.
- How do live events and updates add variety without coercive attendance, power obsolescence or impossible support costs?
- How do long-lived worlds accommodate late arrivals, hiatuses and different desired levels of commitment?

## Ownership boundary

Owns mature participation and content cadence. Underlying motivation belongs to FUN, challenge tuning to PACING, and support economics to BUSINESS/PRODUCTION. This is research, not an approved OpenLegend specification.

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### A world continuing beyond the current session

How do independent lives, seasons, generations, ecological change and institutions continue while someone is absent? Compare attended and offscreen representations without inventing events or promising unmeasured scale.

### Long projects, cultural change and endings

What makes returning to people and places rewarding after a hiatus, and what lets a player leave satisfied? Link NPC, LORE, LOSS and BUSINESS; ongoing support and permanent attendance are not the same design goal.

### Additional boundaries from the broader source review

Distinguish canonical world events, forecasts, authored future possibilities and narrative retellings. Compare local participation in shared campaigns, time away, late arrivals, irreversible achievements and ongoing ordinary access. Background abstraction must not invent exact witnessing or duplicate a unique victory for every party.
