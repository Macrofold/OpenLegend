## Scope and questions

How do groups, institutions and conflicting interests create meaningful social choices?

- Compare reputation, allegiance, status, law, crime, governance, religion, diplomacy and organized conflict.
- How do individual decisions alter wider relationships, access, resources or authority?
- Which systems allow negotiation and change rather than reducing politics to fixed faction hostility or a universal score?

## Ownership boundary

Owns collective social rules. Individual bonds belong to NPC, material incentives to ECON, and moderation/player protection to TRUST. This is research, not an approved OpenLegend specification.

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### Institutions that inhabitants can change

How do groups form, select leaders, inherit responsibilities, change customs and resolve disputes? Examine individual agency within class, faith, law and governance rather than only fixed allegiance bonuses.

### Conflict, resistance and negotiated order

Compare diplomacy, crime, espionage, propaganda, coercion, war, resistance and peace. Link ECON, NPC, KNOW and LORE; fictional wrongdoing and real-participant abuse are not the same policy problem.
