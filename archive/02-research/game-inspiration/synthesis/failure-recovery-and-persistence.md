## Scope and questions

What worthwhile decision, lesson or continuing story comes after a setback?

- Compare death, injury, lost equipment, rescue, checkpoints, permadeath, voluntary resets and campaign continuity.
- What survives as knowledge, relationships, possessions or unlocks, and how quickly does rebuilding become interesting?
- When does loss renew discovery versus repeat chores, separate friends or break trust? How are technical failures kept distinct?

## Ownership boundary

Owns loss and continuity promises. Long-run motivation stays with FUN, advancement with GROWTH, and ordinary pacing with PACING. This is research, not an approved OpenLegend specification.

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### Defeat beyond a reload screen

Compare capture, flight, rescue, grief, broken relationships, inheritance and ruined places as consequences of conflict or ordinary life. What meaningful choices remain for the player and independently acting residents?

### Rebuilding, absence and trustworthy continuity

What persists through a setback or time away, and what must be recoverable when the software rather than the fiction fails? Link LIVE, NPC, SOCIETY and LORE without assuming either total loss or permanent accumulation is always better.
