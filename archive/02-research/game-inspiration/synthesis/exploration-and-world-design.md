## Scope and questions

What makes a place invite curiosity, orientation and a worthwhile journey?

- Compare landmarks, routes, secrets, biome identity, encounter placement, density and environmental opportunities.
- How do authored and procedural places support discovery without repetitive filler or unreadable randomness?
- What should be signposted, mapped, gated or left unknown, and how do discoveries change later choices?

## Ownership boundary

Owns the arrangement and discovery of places. Moving through them belongs to TRAVEL, interpreting clues to KNOW, and their history to LORE. This is research, not an approved OpenLegend specification.

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### Curiosity beyond quests and combat

What makes a peaceful outing, scientific observation, beautiful place or useful resource discovery worth pursuing? Compare self-directed exploration with authored invitations without requiring every destination to supply a fight or quest marker.

### Places changed by inhabitants and events

How do new homes, ruins, memorials, disputed access and altered habitats change a return visit? Link BUILD, LORE and LIVE; distinguish actual changes and discoverable evidence from decorative backstory.

### Additional boundaries from the broader source review

Compare recognizable local detail with a wider implied world, meaningful revisits, changing routes and self-contained destinations. Authored geography and procedural generation have different evidence needs: a large map or a rich novel setting is not proof of enjoyable exploration. ACTION owns methods of interacting with a discovered place.
