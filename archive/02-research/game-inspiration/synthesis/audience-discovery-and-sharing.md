## Research subtopics to keep explicit

### Communicating an actual experience

How do a demo, opening session, image, story or invitation show what people can really do? Consider players interested in adventure, peaceful life, independent characters, observation or creation without promising unsupported freedom.

### Discovery through people and shared artifacts

What helps someone find a suitable world, join friends or understand a reusable creation? Compare attention, successful entry, useful sharing and satisfied return rather than treating exposure as evidence of a good game.
