## Scope and questions

How does a world feel particular, coherent and larger than what the player sees?

- Compare cosmology, histories, cultures, faiths, customs, myths, named places and conflicting beliefs.
- How do architecture, objects, crafting, language and ordinary behavior communicate lore without exposition overload?
- Which mysteries reward inquiry, and how can authored identity coexist with player-created history?

## Ownership boundary

Owns worldbuilding and meaning. Quests belong to STORY, institutions to SOCIETY, and audiovisual execution to PRESENT. This is research, not an approved OpenLegend specification.

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### From events to remembered and contested history

How could deeds, disasters, inventions, migrations and ordinary lives become memories, oral accounts, traditions, artifacts, place names and legends? Trace actual events separately from witnesses' partial knowledge, rumor, deliberate fiction and canon.

### Culture and mystery that continue to change

How can faith, language, art, rituals and institutions evolve while preserving a world's recognizable identity? Link KNOW, STORY, SOCIETY, NPC and LIVE; generated exposition is not by itself emergent lore or lived cultural change.
