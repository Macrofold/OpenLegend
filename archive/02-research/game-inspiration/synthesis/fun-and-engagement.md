## Research subtopics to keep explicit

### Different forms of worthwhile play

Compare violent mastery, peaceful creation and care, discovery, social improvisation, quiet observation and self-chosen projects. Which audiences and stages of play benefit, and what does each activity ask of the participant?

### Enjoyment, repeated play and wanting to stop

What creates another wanted decision rather than obligation, repetitive recovery or attendance pressure? Distinguish voluntary engagement, a satisfying finite ending, measured retention and commercial incentives. Keep counterexamples and evidence limits with each conclusion.
