## Scope and questions

How do challenges and possibilities stay well matched as players learn?

- Compare tension/rest rhythms, escalating complexity, challenge settings, randomness, pacing and optional goals.
- How do authored variation, procedural variation, alternative builds and changing objectives support replay?
- Which balance changes preserve interesting decisions, and which flatten mastery or produce grind and runaway advantage?

## Ownership boundary

Owns challenge and temporal structure. Motivations/compulsion belong to FUN, reset consequences to LOSS, and update cadence to LIVE. This is research, not an approved OpenLegend specification.

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### Quiet life, wanted pressure and worthwhile stopping

How do self-chosen projects, ordinary autonomous life and periods of safety coexist with danger and urgent goals? Examine whether imposed incidents improve the selected experience rather than assuming escalation is always needed.

### Variety that preserves learning

How do changing situations, fair uncertainty, character initiative and alternative methods renew decisions without undoing ordinary competence? Link FUN, NPC and LIVE; procedural quantity and arbitrary randomness are not automatic depth.
