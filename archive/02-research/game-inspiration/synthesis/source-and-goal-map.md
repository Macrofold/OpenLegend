## Review scope and conclusion

The earlier map had good breadth, but still put several different design problems inside broad headings. General improvisation was scattered between combat, crafting and interface; communication between knowledge and characters; everyday pleasure between survival, presentation and multiplayer; bodily life between ecology and character relationships; technical systems between crafting, automation and magic. Giving each a primary owner makes those questions easier to research without creating a file for every individual mechanic.

The map therefore expands from 30 to **35 topics**, adding ACTION, SPEECH, LEISURE, BODY and TECH. Existing codes, filenames, useful questions and source records remain. The change is a research organization decision, not a selection of five implementation projects or a requirement to build every aspect before delivering a playable game.

The review inspected the existing topic framework; the six worldbuilding comparison essays below; relevant sections of the current README, repertoire foundation, whole-game coverage, source-atlas method, character specification, invention journeys, product-scalability specification and design-disposition register; and selected stored game material, including Don't Starve Together's cooperative framing and the merged creator-tooling essay. Earlier inspected game dossiers and the repertoire's source routes informed the comparison. This is **not** a fresh line-by-line audit of every game/world dossier, all repertoire entries, every current feature design, or their external citations. No game execution or new audience study was performed.

## Keep four kinds of evidence distinct

| Source family                                                                                                         | Useful contribution                                                                                    | What it cannot establish alone                                                                            |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Game dossiers and mechanics studies](../README.md)                                                                   | Documented rules, worked interactions, player criticism, development and commercial context            | Universal enjoyment, current runtime support in OpenLegend, or causal proof of commercial success         |
| [World dossiers and comparative essays](../../worldbuilding/README.md)                                                | Particular lives, institutions, language, culture, history, tone, causality and narrative presentation | That performing an analogous activity is fun, or that a literary event is an implemented simulation rule  |
| [OpenLegend goals and repertoire proposals](../../../../docs/repertoires/README.md)                                   | Intended experiences, optional world families, candidate mechanics and selection boundaries            | Implementation, acceptance, measured player benefit, or independent confirmation of their source research |
| Current contracts, project designs, [maintainer work](../../../../docs/maintainers/README.md) and linked verification | Reuse boundaries; distinctions between delivered, designed, proposed and unverified scope              | General audience preference or automatic permission to extend a feature                                   |

A game, its fictional-world dossier and a later atlas summarizing both are not three independent confirmations of one claim. Preserve the originating source and continuity. Read the actual primary or player evidence when resolving a consequential disputed claim. An old source's date is not refreshed merely by linking it in this review.

## Completeness checks across the research requirements

[Full research requirements](../research-requirements.md) and the [economics/enjoyment/creation amendment](../research-economics-engagement-and-creation.md) remain the owners of per-game completion. This framework supplies a synthesis destination for their dimensions:

- Identity and player promise: FUN, DISCOVERY and the intended-experience section in every guide.
- Actions, entities, combinations, progression, economy, time and worked cases: the relevant gameplay topic, ACTION and cross-topic situations; not only combat and crafting.
- Social/AI/multiplayer, presentation and narrative: NPC, MULTI, SPEECH, UX, PRESENT, STORY, LORE and KNOW, with BODY and LEISURE where relevant.
- Production, marketing, commercial results and reception: PRODUCTION, DISCOVERY, BUSINESS and FUN; player criticism also belongs beside the domain lesson it qualifies.
- Transfer, limits, source preservation and creation/modding: every guide's applicability/evidence sections, CREATE and its conditional modding sources. A tooling catalog is not a successful creator journey.

## What this review does not turn into a requirement

The first playable experience still needs a small complete combination of ordinary enjoyable play and OpenLegend's distinct useful invention, independent character purpose and persistent consequence, under [current priorities](../../../../docs/repertoires/coverage.md). Richer research is not a reason to delay all play until a general civilization, ecology, language or physics engine is finished.

The four proposed world families remain options, not simultaneous shipping commitments. Modern careers, intimacy and crime; medieval survival and power; planetary technology; and living fantasy each select different rules, emphasis and audience. The [world-tone policy](../../../../docs/repertoires/design-foundation.md#world-tone-and-scope) permits mature subject matter and PG alternatives without mandatory innocence, brutality or equal coverage quotas. Canonical engine integrity, permissions, real spending and private data are not fictional settings.

The useful test is a complete situation that combines only what it needs. A peaceful festival, a contested workshop, a transformed body learning to use an ordinary tool, an investigation revising a rumor, or a shared victory that changes later travel can reveal different gaps. These are **hypothetical comparison prompts**, not five newly selected designs or claims of current support. Let prosperity, a friendship, a functioning device or a victory remain achieved when that serves the experience.
