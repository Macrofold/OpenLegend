## Scope and questions

How do understandable local rules create a world worth experimenting with or observing?

- Compare creature ecologies, weather, materials, fire, fluids, physical interactions and resource regeneration.
- Which interactions produce useful invention and unexpected stories rather than noise or catastrophic chain reactions?
- What fidelity and observability earn their cost, and what can remain abstract without losing the experience?

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### Living populations rather than decorative creatures

How do competing purposes, predation, care, reproduction, aging, death and habitat change make a place worth observing or inhabiting? Distinguish useful emergent behavior from more agents or biological variables alone.

### Consequences across physical and social systems

How can material change alter livelihoods, relationships and later history? Link NPC, ECON, LIVE and LORE; compare detailed and abstract approaches without inventing offscreen events or uncontrolled simulation obligations.

### Additional boundaries from the broader source review

Focus on interactions between environments, materials and populations. BODY owns an organism's capabilities and life cycle, NPC its individual motives, TECH constructed systems, and ACTION what a participant can attempt. Examine selective depth rather than demanding a complete ecological simulation.
