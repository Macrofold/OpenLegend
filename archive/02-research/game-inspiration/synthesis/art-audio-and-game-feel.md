## Scope and questions

What makes ordinary moments expressive, legible and satisfying?

- Compare coherent visual identity, silhouettes, animation, camera, sound, music and tactile feedback.
- How do cues communicate action, material, danger, emotion and inhabited place without excessive noise?
- Which expressive choices earn production cost, and when do spectacle or fidelity undermine clarity and tone?

## Ownership boundary

Owns expressive execution and immediate feedback. Navigation/controls belong to TRAVEL, usability to UX, and setting meaning to LORE. This is research, not an approved OpenLegend specification.

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### Expressive everyday life and dangerous moments

How do faces, bodies, movement, sound and environmental detail make quiet care, humor, beauty and conflict legible? Compare restrained and spectacular approaches without equating fidelity with an alive world.

### Art and sound as activities and evidence

When are music, performance, decoration and making art valuable play rather than background polish? How do audible or visible clues respect observer perspective? Link MULTI, LORE and KNOW.

### Additional boundaries from the broader source review

Keep presentation, attention and sensory feedback here; playable music, fashion, performance and decoration are activities in LEISURE. Explain what a visual or sound effect communicates versus what the world physically or socially implements. Tone can be comic, beautiful, frightening or restrained without making fidelity the quality measure.
