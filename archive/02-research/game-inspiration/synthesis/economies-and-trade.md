## Scope and questions

How can earning, exchanging and spending create interesting choices rather than an ever-larger balance?

- Map sources, sinks, prices, scarcity, abundance, specialization and player/NPC trade. What gives resources lasting value?
- When do inflation, hoarding, dominant strategies, automation or market power remove decisions?
- Compare local livelihoods and large economies, resource substitutes and meaningful non-monetary rewards.

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### Livelihoods, reciprocity and independent exchange

How can gifts, barter, wages, commissions, debt and specialization express relationships and self-directed NPC purposes rather than only a shop interface? Compare peaceful prosperity with scarcity and unequal access.

### Conflict and economic consequences

How do theft, coercion, disrupted routes, war and recovery change economic choices? Connect SOCIETY, NPC and LOSS without making violence a universal requirement for wealth or progression.
