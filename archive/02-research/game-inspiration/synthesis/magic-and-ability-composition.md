## Scope and questions

How can unusual capabilities support surprising but understandable solutions?

- Compare targeting, delivery, costs, cooldowns, conditions, counters and interactions with ordinary objects and environments.
- How do combinations create new possibilities without runaway multiplication or opaque exceptions?
- When do utility, traversal, social or constructive powers matter as much as damage?

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### Extraordinary capabilities in ordinary life

Investigate healing, communication, perception, transformation, binding and constructive uses alongside destructive power. How can a power change a practical or relational decision rather than only a combat statistic?

### Coherent rules in different authored realities

How can experimentation reveal limits, interactions and counters without making every world's magic obey one fixed setting? Link KNOW, NPC and SYSTEMS; a fictional claim about a power is not evidence that the simulation implements it.

### Additional boundaries from the broader source review

Distinguish supernatural conditions, beliefs about them, religious institutions and lived practice. Compare material, bodily, informational, relational and moral costs, including costs borne by others. Ask whether an established power creates an obvious alternative the world must acknowledge; mystery is not permission for convenient inconsistency. TECH owns devices and infrastructure even where the fictional boundary overlaps.
