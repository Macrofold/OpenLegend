## Scope and questions

What makes making or improving an object feel like achievement, expertise or expression?

- Compare known recipes, experimentation, ingredient substitution, quality, repair and repurposing. What new actions do outputs enable?
- When are tools, stations, embodied steps, time and failure worthwhile constraints rather than repetitive chores?
- How do learning a technique, commissioning work and collaborating differ from simply unlocking a menu entry?

## Ownership boundary

Owns the making process. Output properties belong to GEAR, mass production to LOGISTICS, and editor/mod tools to CREATE. This is research, not an approved OpenLegend specification.

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### Useful invention versus renamed recipes

What makes an invented object or process enable a genuinely new activity? Compare experimentation, failed prototypes, material substitution and repurposing with dependable ordinary crafting; do not substitute a hidden canned recipe for claimed invention.

### Craftsmanship, teaching and shared making

How can cooking, art, repair, toolmaking and cooperative projects matter without serving only combat? Examine teaching, commissions and NPC initiative, linking CREATE, GEAR, GROWTH and NPC rather than duplicating their rules.

### Additional boundaries from the broader source review

Trace acquisition or learning of a method, experimentation, manufacture and actual use separately. Practical invention belongs here; general improvised action links to ACTION, devices to TECH, and art or cooking pursued for pleasure to LEISURE. Creation should be useful without requiring a new universal manufacturing system.
