## Scope and questions

How does learning something become a genuine capability or satisfying discovery?

- Compare observation, clues, deduction, experimentation, puzzle constraints and alternative solutions.
- How do character knowledge, player knowledge, rumors, deception and shared information differ?
- When is uncertainty intriguing, and when is it an unfair hidden rule, a spoiler dependency or missing interface feedback?

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### Situated perception, testimony and secrets

How do senses, attention, observation, messages, concealment and deception shape what each person can know? Compare investigation, stealth and ordinary learning while preserving the difference between facts, beliefs and claims.

### Knowledge moving through a living world

How can teaching, demonstration, rumor, records and lost information change practical capability and relationships? Link NPC, LORE, CRAFT and SOCIETY; neither a shared location nor a narrator's knowledge implies everyone knows the same thing.

### Additional boundaries from the broader source review

Separate sensing and acquired evidence from interpreting a claim and deciding what to do. SPEECH owns the exchange or medium; BODY the relevant senses; this guide owns learning, uncertainty and inquiry. Include disconfirming evidence, mistaken testimony and knowledge that changes an already familiar relationship or place.
