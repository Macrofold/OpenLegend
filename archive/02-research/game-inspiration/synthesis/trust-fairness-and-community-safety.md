## Scope and questions

How can meaningful freedom and risk coexist with reliable expectations and protection from abuse?

- Compare consent to PvP/loss, griefing prevention, ownership, shared resources, safe participation and moderation.
- How do games communicate consequences, resolve disputes and recover from cheating or technical failure?
- Which protections preserve valued tension and collaboration rather than silently removing agency or imposing surveillance?

## Ownership boundary

Owns player-facing trust and protection. Fictional law belongs to SOCIETY, creator code/tool permissions to CREATE, and purchase design to BUSINESS. This is research, not an approved OpenLegend specification.

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### Fictional conflict versus real participation boundaries

How can a world include danger, crime, betrayal or war while participants understand its rules, ownership and chosen stakes? Do not conflate depicting conflict with permitting harassment, or protecting players with requiring harmless fiction.

### Trust across observation, creation and recovery

How do private character knowledge, shared spaces, creator edits, moderation and recovery from technical loss preserve confidence? Link KNOW, CREATE, LOSS and SOCIETY without creating competing permission policies here.

### Additional boundaries from the broader source review

Separate private human/account data, permitted character knowledge and deliberately authored fictional secrets. Consider clear admission, reporting, exit, correction, moderation and recovery without giving an in-world ruler platform authority. Scope audience and mature subjects explicitly without equating fictional violence with real-participant abuse.
