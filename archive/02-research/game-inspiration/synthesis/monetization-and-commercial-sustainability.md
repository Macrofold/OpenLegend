## Scope and questions

Which product and commercial choices support a worthwhile game and a sustainable business?

- Compare pricing, demos, premium/free models, DLC, subscriptions, cosmetics and creator payouts with player value and trust.
- What do dated revenue, cost, scope and support evidence actually say about success, failure or uncertainty?
- Which causal explanations survive contrary cases? How do developer livelihood, publisher return and community goals differ?

## Ownership boundary

Owns real commercial outcomes and purchase design. Fictional currency belongs to ECON, acquisition to DISCOVERY, and production process to PRODUCTION. This is research, not an approved OpenLegend specification.

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### Funding the experience actually promised

How do pricing, creator livelihood and evidenced development, inference, hosting and support costs fit different forms of participation? Distinguish financial sustainability from player enjoyment and fictional wealth.

### Incentives, access and player trust

Which commercial incentives support useful creation and satisfying play, and which reward pressure or undermine clear ownership? Link FUN, CREATE, TRUST and PRODUCTION; an operating-policy lesson need not become a gameplay feature.
