# Cross-game and world learning guides

**Synthesis scope as of November 7, 2026.** [Game library](../README.md) · [World library](../../worldbuilding/README.md) · [Source and goal map](source-and-goal-map.md) · [Template](main-topic-template.md) · [Progress](progress.md) · [Game coverage](coverage.csv) · [World coverage](world-coverage.csv) · [Assessment evidence](assessment-records.md).

This is the bridge between **what the researched games and fictional worlds do** and **which design choices we can learn from them**. It is not another feature catalogue or an approved OpenLegend roadmap. [Repertoires](../../../../docs/repertoires/README.md) own ideas; current specifications, project designs and maintainer trackers own accepted behavior and delivery.

**Current state: 35 topic entry pages—25 intentionally unfilled synthesis shells, six substantive guides (NPC, COMBAT, EXPLORE, PRESENT, STORY and PACING), and four routes to existing research owners.** NPC retains its focused intelligence/simulation pass; COMBAT has six scoped sources, EXPLORE two and PRESENT a focused Sea of Stars pass. STORY and PACING each incorporate the focused Chrono Trigger / Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 comparison. Broader coverage of all six substantive guides remains open. Five topics were added after comparing the original framework with world research and current goals: ACTION, SPEECH, LEISURE, BODY and TECH. All original topic codes and paths are retained. The [broader review and coverage crosswalk](source-and-goal-map.md) explains the changes; the [earlier 30-topic critique](coverage-review.md) remains historical context.

**Intelligent individuals and efficient simulation:** start with the [NPC guide](characters-companions-and-relationships.md), then its [game implementation evidence](../references/game-ai-implementation-and-scale.md), [simulation architecture study](../references/efficient-individual-simulation.md), [language-model societies and Simile study](../references/language-model-societies-and-simile.md) and [counterevidence/verification notes](../references/intelligent-simulation-verification-notes.md). The [focused inventory](../research-progress-intelligent-simulation-2026-10-14.md) records 21 existing-dossier reassessments and nine topic-only additions without granting whole-game completion.

**Responsive combat and credible animal escape:** the [COMBAT guide](combat-and-encounters.md) compares native creature priorities, purposeful pursuit, targeting displays and attack defaults. Sea of Stars and Elden Ring extend the comparison to tactical variety, execution, equipment, preparation, assistance and readable commitment. It links existing OpenLegend design owners while leaving wider weapons, bosses, rescue, ecology and relationship research open. [Assessment evidence](assessment-records.md) preserves each source's inspected scope, versions and limits.

**Expressive places and action feel:** the [PRESENT guide](art-audio-and-game-feel.md) retains its focused Sea of Stars pass on organized visual richness, purposeful light, persistent physical identity and execution feedback. The [EXPLORE guide](exploration-and-world-design.md) compares Sea of Stars and Elden Ring on readable destinations, distinctive regions and discoveries that change another worthwhile activity. Alternative routes, layered geography and reward usefulness add depth without establishing that size, obscurity or content counts produce enjoyable exploration. Both guides retain their evidence limits and existing OpenLegend owners.

**Authored consequences, challenge and worthwhile stopping:** the [STORY guide](quests-narrative-and-consequences.md#story-l001) and [PACING guide](pacing-difficulty-and-replay-variety.md#pacing-l001) compare G187 Chrono Trigger and G77 Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 at a declared scope. Recognition, reframing and closure inform STORY-L001–L004; learning, the complete return trip, access costs and completion versus replay inform PACING-L001–L004. The guides preserve contrasting player responses and explicitly constructed OpenLegend situations. This is scoped incorporation into two topics, not completion of either topic or every possible lesson from either game. [Assessment evidence](assessment-records.md) owns exact revisions, inspected sections and review limits.

The following groups are reading routes, not implementation batches or a demand to build every system. Topic questions are not completed findings. A good guide compares different approaches, prerequisites, player reactions, counterexamples and the value of simpler alternatives or omission.

## How the boundaries fit together

A sword can inform several guides without duplicating one lesson: finding it is LOOT; its uses and carrying tradeoffs are GEAR; making it is CRAFT; learning a sword build is GROWTH; fighting is COMBAT; selling it is ECON; losing it is LOSS. ACTION asks how a situated person discovers and attempts a method. One conclusion has a primary owner and cross-links, not repeated copies.

A music gathering can similarly involve LEISURE's actual activity, SPEECH's communication, MULTI's human participation, NPC's particular motives, PRESENT's audible/visible feedback and LORE's continuing cultural meaning. Separating these avoids treating a background song, simulated friendship, human coordination and a playable performance as the same feature.

BODY owns an organism's capability and life cycle; SYSTEMS owns habitats and interacting populations; SURVIVE owns recurring pressure; NPC owns individual purposes. TECH owns working device systems, LOGISTICS coordinated flows, CRAFT making, and CREATE authoring their supported definitions. Shared subject matter does not require shared status or duplicate specifications.

## Cross-topic questions that must not disappear

The [source and goal map](source-and-goal-map.md) maps every repertoire category and the research requirements to an owner. Use it for questions that cross filenames: stealth and deception; religion's metaphysics, belief, institutions and practice; grief and legacy; adult intimacy and family; crime and power; humor, horror and wonder; technology-driven social change; and different authored realities.

Emergent lore needs a chain from an actual or explicitly authored event to evidence, partial observation, memory, accounts, institutions and later choices. More generated backstory is not automatically a living history. Likewise, violence and peaceful life both need substance: victory can be valuable, and care, work, art, companionship or solitude need not justify themselves as combat preparation. A prosperous place or resolved conflict may stay achieved.

The [four proposed world families and tone policy](../../../../docs/repertoires/design-foundation.md) remain optional scopes. No topic makes all worlds medieval, human-bodied, violent, harmless, scarce, multiplayer or permanently escalating. Protect the difference between fictional authority and real permissions, private data, billing and participant control.

## Shared guide sections and research-to-design handoff

The [adaptable template](main-topic-template.md) now covers desired experiences and contrasting approaches; typed evidence and counterexamples; current OpenLegend baseline and existing design owners; complete situations and cross-topic consequences; lessons, tradeoffs and omissions; and evidence that would change a recommendation. Each page has places for these functions, but authors may reorganize them to suit the topic.

Before proposing a feature, check the current contracts and [Future work needing design](../../../../docs/maintainers/needs-design.md). Use the existing ND ID where the scope matches. A genuinely new bounded proposal gets a register entry and a reciprocal topic reference; both eventual design documents retain that ID and link implementation modules in the existing focused trackers. Research incorporation, design completion, runtime delivery and player-experience evidence remain separate. No ND entries are created by this framework review or the focused NPC research pass.
