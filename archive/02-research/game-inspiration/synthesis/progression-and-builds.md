## Scope and questions

How does improvement change what the player can attempt, understand or express?

- Compare use-based skills, levels, unlocks, specializations, technology, character identity and horizontal versus vertical growth.
- When do synergy, meaningful commitments and respecialization support experimentation without erasing identity?
- How do catch-up, power gaps, mastery and endgame ceilings preserve worthwhile early and late decisions?

## Ownership boundary

Owns advancement and build choices. Immediate power delivery belongs to MAGIC/GEAR and timing/balance to PACING. This is research, not an approved OpenLegend specification.

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### Becoming capable without a mandatory combat ladder

How do practical, social, artistic and exploratory expertise develop alongside martial power? Compare reliable learned competence with grind, arbitrary failure and rewards unrelated to what was actually learned.

### Learning people and changing identities

How can mentorship, practice, discovery and NPC learning affect relationships and future choices? Link NPC and CRAFT; distinguish player knowledge, character expertise and long-term identity rather than treating all growth as experience points.
