## Scope and questions

When do bodily needs and environmental preparation create valued stakes rather than chores?

- Compare food, cooking, temperature, shelter, rest, illness, farming and seasonal preparation.
- How do safety, competence and comfortable periods reward earlier preparation without removing every future goal?
- Which needs create different strategies or cooperative roles, and which merely interrupt wanted activities?

## Ownership boundary

Owns living with needs. Ecosystems belong to SYSTEMS, making food/tools to CRAFT, and recovery after major loss to LOSS. This is research, not an approved OpenLegend specification.

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### Living well, not only avoiding deficits

How do pleasure, comfort, shared meals, farming, gardening, rest and caring for others become worthwhile activities rather than refilling meters? Preserve room for prosperity and voluntary challenge.

### Different bodies and useful care

Compare injury, illness, disability, healing, growth and aging where they add meaningful experience. Link NPC, SYSTEMS, CRAFT and LOSS; do not assume biological detail or constant hardship is necessary for a convincing life.
