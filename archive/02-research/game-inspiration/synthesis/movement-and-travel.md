## Scope and questions

When is getting somewhere an enjoyable activity instead of lost time?

- Compare climbing, jumping, vehicles, mounts, flight, route planning and fast travel.
- How do controls, terrain, transportable supplies and environmental hazards turn routes into choices?
- When should movement become dependable or convenient, and when does removing friction erase the adventure?

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### Journeys together and independently

How can group travel, accompaniment, accessibility, separation and regrouping preserve participation without forcing identical speeds or constant commands? What makes a quiet journey worthwhile in its own right?

### Tracking, pursuit and the cost of distance

Compare hiding, following, escaping and contested routes with ordinary transport. Examine effects on time, supplies and relationships through MULTI, COMBAT and SURVIVE without turning every trip into upkeep.

### Additional boundaries from the broader source review

Connect unusual movement to BODY, vehicles to TECH and communication during separation to SPEECH. Distinguish fictional travel time from technical waiting, and preserve the existing planetary-world scope rather than treating a vehicle example as approval for playable spaceflight.
