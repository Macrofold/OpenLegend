## Scope and questions

What makes a life worth enjoying when there is no enemy to defeat, shortage to fix or reward meter to fill?

### Pleasure, taste and self-chosen projects

Compare cooking, gardening, decorating, fashion, collections, music, art, dancing, humor, play and quiet observation. How does doing the activity become satisfying beyond its yield or bonus? A comfortable, prosperous life is a valid starting condition or achievement, not a defect that must be interrupted.

### Games, performance and occasions

Examine sports, minigames, contests, performances, feasts, festivals, hospitality and shared rituals. What are the actual actions, expressive choices, skill, audience reactions and opportunities to join or leave? Separate a participant's performance from a background animation or a scheduled reward claim.

### Individual and shared everyday lives

How do taste, memory, household routines, vocations, status, friendships and chosen solitude give an activity meaning? Compare intrinsic enjoyment with competition, commercial ambition and social obligation. Adult worlds and PG worlds can choose different subjects and presentation; neither is a universal template for all realities.

## Ownership boundary

Owns everyday activities pursued for pleasure, expression and ordinary fulfillment. SURVIVE owns resource pressure; CRAFT owns making processes; PRESENT owns audiovisual presentation; MULTI owns human coordination; NPC owns personal relationships. A song can be an activity here, an audible signal in SPEECH and a tradition in LORE without duplicating its rules.
