## Scope and questions

What makes a simulated person or creature someone the player cares about?

- Compare distinctive motives, personality, dialogue, memory, changing relationships and visible follow-through.
- How do friendship, rivalry, romance, teaching, care, animal bonds and party roles affect play rather than only a score?
- When does autonomy feel credible and helpful, and when does it become obstruction, scripted predictability or repetitive dialogue?

## Ownership boundary

Owns personal relationships and companion experience. Player-to-player coordination belongs to MULTI and society-wide relations to SOCIETY. This is research, not an approved OpenLegend specification.

## OpenLegend-specific research subtopics

### Independent lives, not only services for the player

How do needs, emotions, identity, limited knowledge, initiative, refusal and changing purposes produce coherent activity? Examine NPC-to-NPC cooperation and conflict, chosen solitude and continuing pursuits without equating an imposed schedule with independent life.

### Relationships, learning and lasting personal change

How can trust, promises, family, courtship, rivalry, grief, teaching and animal bonds change behavior over time? Link KNOW, SYSTEMS and LORE; a remembered claim, actual event and personality change need distinct evidence.
