## Research subtopics to keep explicit

### From playing to making to authoring rules

How do ordinary in-world building, useful live invention, configurable components, asset creation and trusted rule extensions differ? Compare what a platform supplies with what a creator must decide, test and explain.

### Reuse that preserves coherent worlds and trust

How do templates, dependencies, compatibility, permissions, testing, publishing, discovery and creator incentives support useful shared content? Examine varied violent and peaceful authored worlds without making any one world's laws universal or treating generated prose as executable authority.
