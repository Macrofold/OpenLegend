## Evidence boundaries that remain even after completion

“Dossier complete” means complete against this packet's stated comparative scope, not an exhaustive survey of every release or a fresh complete rereading of every novel, episode, comic run, or game. The primary-work anchors identify where to check literary arguments. Retrieved web sources support the indicated production and historical claims; interpretations of fictional scenes remain distinguishable from documented creator intentions.

Source access is uneven. Several early dossiers explicitly report reliance on a secondary orientation or a retrieved excerpt where an interview, archive, or primary source was inaccessible. The continuation will verify accessible sources and retain unresolved limitations rather than treating a bibliography as proof of inspection. No claim of empirically establishing why all audiences enjoy a world is made.
