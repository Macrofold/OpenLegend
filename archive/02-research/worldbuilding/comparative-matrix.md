# Comparative matrix: where each world's depth lives

Reconciled on 2026-09-27 against **29 dossiers**. This is a navigation and comparison aid, not a ranking or a good/bad-content ratio. Each row preserves the world's accessible foothold, developed relationships, contrasting cases, and a material limit. It is not a proportionate substitute for the full dossier. Versions, original passages, criticism, and precise reading records remain in the linked files.

## Cross-world contrasts worth reading together

| Question | Contrasting cases | What to keep distinct |
|---|---|---|
| Must a deep world be enormous? | [Foundation](worlds/16-foundation.md), [Austen](worlds/23-austens-social-worlds.md), [Spirited Away](worlds/25-spirited-away.md) | Centuries of institutions, a household's social field, and one workplace develop different forms of depth. |
| Does mastery always mean moral growth? | [Pokémon](worlds/08-pokemon.md), [Dune](worlds/09-dune.md), [Warhammer](worlds/26-warhammer-40000.md) | Winning, belonging, and formidable technical or martial performance have different ends and costs. |
| Must language be a complete conlang? | [Middle-earth](worlds/01-middle-earth.md), [Star Trek](worlds/04-star-trek.md), [The Wire](worlds/21-the-wire.md) | Invented history, performed construction, and familiar registers are different achievements. |
| Does wanted intimacy have one institutional form? | [Austen](worlds/23-austens-social-worlds.md), [The Culture](worlds/17-the-culture.md), [The Broken Earth](worlds/18-the-broken-earth.md) | Material options, bodily autonomy, imposed pairing, and actual preferences cannot be inferred from one label. |
| Is joy merely a pause before punishment? | [Middle-earth](worlds/01-middle-earth.md), [Narnia](worlds/19-narnia.md), [D&D](worlds/29-dungeons-and-dragons.md) | A party, liberation festival, and recurring gala can deliver real pleasures without a compulsory sinister reveal. |
| Can an institution work within an unjust world? | [Discworld](worlds/12-discworld.md), [One Piece](worlds/14-one-piece.md), [Spirited Away](worlds/25-spirited-away.md) | Effective service, distribution of benefits, legitimacy, and later injustice are separate assessments. |
| Does understanding produce reconciliation? | [Foundation](worlds/16-foundation.md), [Fallout](worlds/28-fallout.md), [Avatar](worlds/13-avatar-the-last-airbender.md) | Knowledge can be withheld, used to defeat a project, or accompany a decision not to forgive. |
| Can success endure beside unresolved loss? | [Middle-earth](worlds/01-middle-earth.md), [The Wire](worlds/21-the-wire.md), [The Broken Earth](worlds/18-the-broken-earth.md) | Household renewal, individual recovery, and planetary change need not restore everybody or become meaningless because they do not. |
| Does a reader's objection erase an element from the world? | [Macondo](worlds/24-macondo.md), [The Elder Scrolls](worlds/27-the-elder-scrolls.md), [The Culture](worlds/17-the-culture.md) | Presence, narrative treatment, available activity, and critical response are different kinds of evidence. |
