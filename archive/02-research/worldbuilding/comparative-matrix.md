# Comparative matrix: where each world's depth lives

This is a navigation and comparison aid, not a ranking. “Depth” identifies the dimensions the selected works develop particularly strongly; it does not claim equal completeness throughout a franchise. Each linked dossier identifies its primary corpus, worked examples, creator evidence, and limitations. Interpretive judgments below inherit those boundaries rather than silently combining adaptations.

## The eight requested anchors

| World and dossier | Audience's initial foothold | Particularly developed relationships | Worked integration | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [01. Middle-earth](worlds/01-middle-earth.md) | A home, an unwanted journey, familiar comforts worth protecting. | Language history, landscape, memory, mortality, inherited obligations, and different scales of courage. | The Scouring joins household life, political coercion, ecological damage, and altered responsibilities. | Ordinary economies and many peripheral peoples receive less interiority than the central mythic and linguistic histories. |
| [02. Wizarding World](worlds/02-wizarding-world.md) | Recognition, friendship, school routines, and the promise of belonging. | Family reputation, education, magical objects, inherited exclusion, and public credibility. | Umbridge's classroom policy connects state denial to learning, friendship, and collective resistance. | British school life is much more elaborated than global society; later supplemental rules are not all dramatized in the novels. |
| [03. Star Wars](worlds/03-star-wars.md) | Escape, adventure, family, friendship, and an immediately legible crisis. | Used objects, public myths, spiritual traditions, unequal protection, and competing commitments within coalitions. | Ferrix's funeral joins work, ritual, memory, public space, and political action. | Mythic family stories and local civic stories offer different depths; Legends and screen continuity must remain distinct. |
| [04. Star Trek](worlds/04-star-trek.md) | A familiar crew, workplace, and professional purpose confronting the unknown. | Institutional ideals, diplomacy, cultural difference, cultivated expertise, and the consequences of historical injury. | “Duet” joins an occupation's memory to identity, guilt, public justice, and revised judgment. | Planetary shorthand and convenient technology can undercut pluralism or consequence; different series and eras are not one unchanged society. |
| [05. Marvel](worlds/05-marvel.md) | Extraordinary ability colliding with money, school, work, family, and reputation. | Public and private identity, supporting relationships, bodies, responsibility, exclusion, and shared history. | Spider-Man's machinery sequence connects physical endurance to medicine and Aunt May's life. | Comic runs and screen adaptations differ; spectacular technologies do not consistently transform ordinary society. |
| [06. DC Universe](worlds/06-dc-universe.md) | Recognizable heroic ideals, symbolic places, mystery, and acts of care. | Legacy, civic identity, family, public responsibility, restraint, and competing forms of authority. | “No Man's Land” connects infrastructure failure, territorial power, policing, trust, and information. | Different continuities cannot be collapsed; the wider universe's capabilities can strain a local crisis's plausibility. |
| [07. The Witcher](worlds/07-the-witcher.md) | A skilled professional needs work, but the supposed job is morally unclear. | Precarious livelihood, contested history, dynastic politics, chosen kinship, and unreliable classifications of people. | Blaviken joins public safety, revenge, official authority, partial information, and lasting reputation. | Books, games, and television are separate authored layers; moral difficulty does not imply every choice is equally bad. |
| [08. Pokémon](worlds/08-pokemon.md) | A companion, curiosity, travel, and the possibility of becoming competent. | Classification and individuality, care infrastructure, regional variation, relationships with nonhuman beings. | Lavender Town joins grief, family, exploitation, local ritual, and investigation. | Creature ecology and companionship are much more developed than government or a complete account of ownership and consent. |

## Mythic, political, and animated worlds

| World and dossier | Audience's initial foothold | Particularly developed relationships | Worked integration | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [09. Dune](worlds/09-dune.md) | A family enters danger; a child inherits abilities, duties, and secrets. | Ecology, religious expectation, institutional dependency, dynastic planning, and the risks of heroic certainty. | The Arrakeen dinner turns water, etiquette, trade, rank, and hidden agendas into one encounter. | One tightly coupled resource system is not a complete imperial economy; later novels change the meaning of the first apparent victory. |
| [10. A Song of Ice and Fire](worlds/10-a-song-of-ice-and-fire.md) | A household, different children's aspirations, and an old friendship's demands. | Genealogy, succession, competing memory, restricted viewpoints, war's aftermath, and unequal options. | The Red Wedding connects marriage, military need, hospitality, grievance, and the destruction of trust. | The dossier covers named published books, not television's conclusion; historical texture is not a complete model of medieval society. |
| [11. Earthsea](worlds/11-earthsea.md) | A gifted young person learns that ability is not judgment. | Naming, trust, restraint, sea travel, care, and the revision of prestigious institutions' histories. | Leaving the Tombs connects religious office, local knowledge, architecture, mutual dependence, and political symbolism. | Later books examine limitations in earlier gender arrangements; sparse exposition is not a hidden complete economic account. |
| [12. Discworld](worlds/12-discworld.md) | A recognizable job or genre becomes comic and then socially consequential. | Work, public services, civic change, speech, institutional incentives, care, and self-restraint. | Restoring the post joins neglected labor, material repair, public trust, monopoly, and relationships implied by letters. | Satire is not uniformly free of caricature; ingenious reformers do not settle every question about collective power. |
| [13. Avatar: The Last Airbender](worlds/13-avatar-the-last-airbender.md) | A traveling group, a learnable elemental framework, humor, and friendship. | Embodied training, national memory, cultural loss, family expectations, and moral development. | Ba Sing Se makes walls, class, information control, policing, and denial mutually reinforcing. | Elemental nations are an entry shorthand, not complete or one-to-one portraits of real cultures. |
| [14. One Piece](worlds/14-one-piece.md) | A clear ambition and a crew whose members retain different dreams. | Chosen family, island livelihoods, public reputation, forbidden history, and the politics of freedom. | Enies Lobby makes rescuing a person inseparable from challenging the authority that criminalizes her knowledge. | The principal corpus ends at Dressrosa; later revelations are not assumed. Length and repeated rescue structures can impose costs. |
| [18. The Broken Earth](worlds/18-the-broken-earth.md) | A family's catastrophe and a mother's search. | Geological conditions, personhood, education, coercion, bodily control, and a life revealed across time. | The node station changes the ethical meaning of an apparently useful public infrastructure. | Essun's earlier identities are stages of one person, not separate independent character samples; suffering does not determine one response. |
| [19. Narnia](worlds/19-narnia.md) | A sensory threshold, hospitality, wonder, and a place one can miss. | Moral testing, changed returns, sacred authority, childhood, and the value of ordinary pleasures. | Tumnus's tea makes private hospitality a choice within a coercive public order. | Mythic depth exceeds institutional detail; hereditary legitimacy and portrayals of gender and Calormen remain critical tensions. |
| [20. His Dark Materials](worlds/20-his-dark-materials.md) | A missing friend and a visible, vulnerable bond with a dæmon. | Personhood, intimate boundaries, knowledge, competing kinds of care, institutional religion, and maturation. | The land of the dead joins cosmology, separation, truthful storytelling, attention, and loyalty. | The original trilogy is distinct from later extensions; its religious institutions are not representative evidence about all actual believers. |

## Spacefaring societies

| World and dossier | Audience's initial foothold | Particularly developed relationships | Worked integration | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [15. The Expanse](worlds/15-the-expanse.md) | A working crew, a disappearance, survival, and conflicting information. | Infrastructure, bodily environments, expertise, political plurality, and futures disrupted by discovery. | Ganymede connects damaged systems, food, displacement, military framing, and a parent's search. | Local causal seriousness does not make all propulsion and alien premises scientifically established; television changes some biographies. |
| [16. Foundation](worlds/16-foundation.md) | A historical crisis that seems impossible until its real dependencies become visible. | Institutional succession, legitimacy, technical knowledge, trade, historical prediction, and interpretive reversals. | Anacreon's apparent military advantage is altered by technological and religious dependencies already in place. | Institutional depth often exceeds individual and domestic depth; psychohistory is fictional, not established social science. |
| [17. The Culture](worlds/17-the-culture.md) | A compelling outsider, vocation, dissatisfaction, or encounter with another society. | Abundance, artificial personhood, voluntary pursuits, intervention, asymmetric knowledge, and bodily possibility. | Azad connects a game to hierarchy, language, prestige, covert influence, and political change. | Exceptional agents and crises are not a representative sample of ordinary citizens; benevolent purpose does not settle consent. |
| [26. Warhammer 40,000](worlds/26-warhammer-40000.md) | Monumental images made intimate through a soldier, investigator, or distinctive rival. | Religious institutions, inherited technology, local loyalties, bodily power, long memory, and black humor. | Vervunhive's siege and aftermath connect industry, class, command, civilians, displacement, and a regiment's changing identity. | Satire, horror, and heroic presentation can conflict; local depth does not verify galactic numerical consistency. |

## Realist, literary, and concentrated screen worlds

| World and dossier | Audience's initial foothold | Particularly developed relationships | Worked integration | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [21. The Wire](worlds/21-the-wire.md) | An investigation that gradually reveals more than a case. | Institutions, work, neighborhood knowledge, speech, unequal opportunity, and distributed consequences. | Randy's cooperation passes through adults and organizations unable to preserve the protection he expects. | Fictional Baltimore is an authored selection, not a current or exhaustive documentary account of the real city. |
| [22. The Sopranos](worlds/22-the-sopranos.md) | A household and therapy encounter intertwined with a criminal organization. | Family memory, role conflict, status, consumption, self-justification, and the limits of insight. | “College” places a daughter's future and an ordinary family schedule beside coercive obligations. | Empathy does not establish endorsement; the central families do not represent all Italian Americans or all victims. |
| [23. Austen's social worlds](worlds/23-austens-social-worlds.md) | Attraction, wit, household security, and the possibility of misjudging someone. | Social inference, property, patronage, language, unequal choices, and the moral meaning of small actions. | Box Hill connects a joke to age, status, dependency, humiliation, and changed judgment. | The novels are related social worlds, not necessarily one literal continuity; servants and colonial dependencies receive less interiority. |
| [24. Macondo](worlds/24-macondo.md) | A family, a house, extraordinary arrivals, and repeated ordinary desires. | Memory, language, temporal recurrence, domestic labor, political denial, and sensory abundance. | The massacre and denial change both public reality and a survivor's relation to household and speech. | Fictional historical transformation is not a source for exact real-world casualties or a theory of regional destiny. |
| [25. Spirited Away](worlds/25-spirited-away.md) | A frightened child must protect her parents and find a place to stand. | Spatial hierarchy, work, names, appetite, hospitality, and recognition across difference. | Cleaning the river spirit joins care, labor, pollution, misclassification, cooperation, and gratitude. | The film's concentrated social detail does not imply a complete ancient chronology or reform of the bathhouse's hierarchy. |

## Cross-world contrasts worth reading together

These pairings isolate differences of mechanism rather than declare a winner. Each points back to the dossiers that support the comparison.

| Question | Contrasting cases | What the comparison reveals |
|---|---|---|
| Must a world be enormous to be deep? | [Foundation](worlds/16-foundation.md), [Austen](worlds/23-austens-social-worlds.md), [Spirited Away](worlds/25-spirited-away.md) | Institutions across centuries, a household's social field, and one workplace can each generate depth at a different scale. |
| Must language be fully constructed? | [Middle-earth](worlds/01-middle-earth.md), [Star Trek](worlds/04-star-trek.md), [The Wire](worlds/21-the-wire.md) | Linguistic history, performed conlangs, and ordinary social registers can make different forms of belonging legible. |
| Does magic need a complete technical explanation? | [Earthsea](worlds/11-earthsea.md), [Wizarding World](worlds/02-wizarding-world.md), [Macondo](worlds/24-macondo.md) | Ethical clarity, locally explicit procedures, and narrative-symbolic coherence are distinct kinds of consistency. |
| How does public history become private? | [Dune](worlds/09-dune.md), [The Broken Earth](worlds/18-the-broken-earth.md), [The Sopranos](worlds/22-the-sopranos.md) | Institutions claim a person's future, enter habits of care, or become the stories a household uses to excuse itself. |
| Is a technologically advanced society automatically free? | [The Culture](worlds/17-the-culture.md), [Warhammer 40,000](worlds/26-warhammer-40000.md), [Fallout](worlds/28-fallout.md) | Capacity, access, authority, consent, and public recognition can vary independently. |
| What makes travel more than a sequence of scenery? | [Middle-earth](worlds/01-middle-earth.md), [One Piece](worlds/14-one-piece.md), [The Expanse](worlds/15-the-expanse.md) | Bodily duration, specialized companions, local obligations, and logistical dependence make places consequential. |
| What does a familiar ritual add? | [Star Wars](worlds/03-star-wars.md), [Wizarding World](worlds/02-wizarding-world.md), [Austen](worlds/23-austens-social-worlds.md) | Funerals, school calendars, and visits establish expectations through which altered authority or relationships become visible. |
| How can a welcoming world contain serious harm? | [Pokémon](worlds/08-pokemon.md), [Narnia](worlds/19-narnia.md), [Avatar](worlds/13-avatar-the-last-airbender.md) | Companionship, hospitality, humor, and beauty establish things worth protecting; they do not require the absence of grief or moral difficulty. |
