# Content safety, mature storytelling and AI companions

**Reviewed October 16, 2026.** This folder preserves the research behind Mike's decision to support mature, non-graphic storytelling while excluding graphic interactive sexual roleplay from the initial OpenLegend product. It separates law, voluntary ratings, commercial restrictions, product judgment and implementation requirements.

The current product remains adult-player, non-explicit and browser-first. Non-graphic romance, profanity and serious adult topics are not a license for explicit erotic chat. A shared trusted character prompt is necessary but does not replace application-level eligibility, disclosures, output controls, privacy or abuse response.

Sources reflect the review date, not permanent legal or vendor approval. In particular, the fetched Steam pages did not reproduce the categorical live-generated adult-sex restriction quoted in the earlier conversation; the distribution brief preserves that verification gap rather than silently treating it as current permission. The companion brief distinguishes the California rules applicable in 2026 from the staged 2027 changes.

Implementation and actual release evidence remain open under [production deployment PD07, PD08 and PD10](../../../docs/maintainers/production-deployment.md). No runtime prompts, model configuration, moderation software or age controls were changed by this documentation capture.
