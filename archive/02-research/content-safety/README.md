# Content safety, mature storytelling and AI companions

**Reviewed October 16, 2026.** This folder preserves the research behind Mike's decision to support mature, non-graphic storytelling while excluding graphic interactive sexual roleplay from the initial OpenLegend product. It separates law, voluntary ratings, commercial restrictions, product judgment and implementation requirements.

| Document | Purpose |
| --- | --- |

| [AI companions and player moderation](ai-companions-and-player-moderation.md) | California/New York companion definitions and operator duties, enacted future changes, player-chat versus NPC risks, Section 230 uncertainty, abuse reporting and intimate-image removal. |
| [Agent content and companion safety](../../../docs/agent-content-and-companion-safety.md) | The canonical accepted policy and every-character system-instruction requirements. This is the rule owner; the research does not create a competing policy. |

The current product remains adult-player, non-explicit and browser-first. The character policy includes [reference shared system instructions](../../../docs/agent-content-and-companion-safety.md#reference-shared-system-instructions) and paired allowed/refused examples; that wording is not installed or behaviorally qualified. Non-graphic romance, profanity and serious adult topics are not a license for explicit erotic chat. A shared trusted character prompt is necessary but does not replace application-level eligibility, disclosures, output controls, privacy or abuse response.

Sources reflect the review date, not permanent legal or vendor approval. The critique corrected the initial Steam retrieval error: its Content Survey FAQ explicitly excludes live-generated AI Adult Only Sexual Content. Twitch restrictions were also verified through its official Community Guidelines rather than the inaccessible help page. The distribution brief records those sources and the distinct OpenAI/Anthropic policies; the companion brief separates California's 2026 rules from staged 2027 changes.

Implementation and actual release evidence remain open under [production deployment PD07, PD08 and PD10](../../../docs/maintainers/production-deployment.md). No runtime prompts, model configuration, moderation software or age controls were changed by this documentation capture.
