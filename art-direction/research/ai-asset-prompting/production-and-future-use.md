# Production techniques and future applications

This original synthesis preserves useful mechanisms and opportunities from the seven [inspected packages and four prompt packs](README.md). It complements the outcome recipes; it is not an implementation plan. The techniques below are available without rereading the purchased library. Source builds, performance claims and gameplay quality were not independently reproduced.

## Preserve a reusable result, not only a prompt

A recipe has inputs, operations, outputs and consumers. Identify the exact source geometry/reference, rights, required parts, bind/rig data, clips, texture channels, fitting information and export conventions that explain the result. Distinguish a packed output from an editable source and a working rebuild. Check that the documented helper actually produces what the current consumer reads. Missing raw models, stale texture rebakers and absent dependencies in the library show why an attractive demo does not establish reproducibility.

Reuse established project systems and compatible art instead of rebuilding the renderer, interaction framework or wardrobe for each scene. Keep the selected world's content, vocabulary and art choices outside generic mechanisms. A reused system should expose the parameters it genuinely needs; preserving dummy off-map plan entries to stop old code crashing is not an acceptable OpenLegend integration strategy. Source concatenation order, global monkey-patches, compatibility wrappers and single-file delivery are implementation details to leave behind.

When a task is large enough to divide, define spatial/asset boundaries and the small shared contract before independent work: units and axes, site bounds, door/floor/seat meanings, pivots, reserved clearances and the outputs each consumer needs. Use existing coordination guidance; the source's fixed team sizes, model choices, polish rounds and time budgets are not requirements. A shared plan is useful when it prevents incompatible joins, not as a new planning bureaucracy.

## Inspect the interfaces between parts

Many source failures occurred at joins rather than inside an individual model. Retain these concrete cases for the relevant task:

| Interface                          | Failure to look for                                                                                            | Transferable method                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Building, ground and movement      | Visible steps disagree with collision/support; a bridge's channel is filled by the terrain pass                | Derive related geometry from the same intended dimensions and check the real approach/entry/traversal                      |
| Seat and character                 | One producer supplies floor height while another expects seat-top height                                       | Name the reference surface and fit the actor's actual hip/support offset                                                   |
| Body, prop and clip                | Correct sockets with bad finger contact, stale fit or transition pops                                          | Preserve dependency identity and inspect the assembled moving result                                                       |
| Vehicle and camera                 | Entry/exit places the camera inside moving geometry; the first capture catches an unfinished camera transition | Inspect the whole entry/use/exit sequence and the actual view, not only a mode flag                                        |
| Culling and restoration            | A removed object never returns, or only the wheels return without the body                                     | Exercise both leaving and re-entering visibility with the complete object; one component's successful cull is insufficient |
| Material and renderer              | Reversed faces, duplicate light, wrong channel interpretation or unexpected tint                               | Isolate shape, normals, colour, material and illumination before replacing assets                                          |
| Action and subsequent action       | Transient flags or attachments from one operation alter the next                                               | Verify the complete reset/transition at the owning boundary, including a second ordinary operation                         |
| Scene evidence and claimed feature | A nonblank capture misses the named subject, contact or state                                                  | Inspect the actual image/motion and state what it demonstrates                                                             |

Do not copy the source traffic's timeout-based ghosting through people, camera-dependent collision behavior or hidden test-only bypasses. They can make a showcase finish while violating a persistent world's semantics. Reuse OpenLegend's existing spatial, lifecycle and verification owners when implementing any related feature.

Spatial queries and captures must use the current transforms, including those changed outside the render callback. Derive facing from the declared local basis rather than copying a source engine's yaw signs. Keep a doorway's visual opening, obstruction and permitted traversal consistent; a large round door does not necessarily seal a rectangular passage. In embedded previews, measure the actual drawing container rather than assuming the browser window matches it. These are concrete consumer checks, not justification for another geometry or validation framework.

Retain semantic identity through ordering changes: an item's state belongs to that item or its declared slot, not its incidental pickup/list position. Follow helper contracts for coordinates, channel types and parameters; source anecdotes about a colour clone, sky orientation or missing noise amplitude do not establish universal API behavior. For offline inspection, use explicit readiness and a controlled study clock where supported: a background tab may suspend frame callbacks, so waiting for one is not a reliable completion condition.

## Performance ideas worth keeping available

These are candidates for a demonstrated bottleneck, not new defaults or a scale claim:

- **Repeated art:** share compatible geometry and material definitions; use instancing or spatial/material grouping where the existing renderer supports it. Preserve separate moving parts and per-instance appearance. Count draw calls and skinning work as well as triangles; many simple body parts can cost more than expected.
- **Static versus changing detail:** build stable geometry once, rebuild only affected regions when content changes, and avoid repeating expensive authoring work per frame. Region size trades culling/rebuild granularity against overhead; measure the actual scene before choosing it.
- **Distance-dependent presentation:** simplify or omit fine rendering detail at distance and restore it correctly on return. Add hysteresis or staged preparation only for a real pop/hitch problem. Rendering less must not stop, invent or falsify independent lives in the world.
- **Lights and effects:** pool or select relevant lights if light preparation is the bottleneck; fade transitions where useful. A light with zero visible intensity may still incur shadow work. Measure and use the renderer's actual enable/remove semantics. Transparent layers, particles and overdraw can dominate despite low mesh counts.
- **Repeated surfaces:** shared material families and coherent texture scale reduce both inconsistency and accidental material proliferation. Do not force every asset into one atlas or material when it damages editing, motion or appearance.
- **First-use stalls:** identify shader/material/asset preparation that causes an actual hitch. A bounded warm-up may help; loading every possible asset at startup is not automatically better.
- **Offline route preparation:** arc-length tables, reusable fitted contacts or cached authored assemblies can move stable work out of a recurring path when their dependencies are explicit. Recompute when those dependencies change; do not create another writable authority.
- **Measurement:** attribute cost by disabling or replacing one relevant component in the same representative workload. Record device, view, population, shadows/effects, warm-up and build. A busy headless run, a tutorial's counts or a reduced-resolution sheet does not establish game performance.

If recurring rendering work is spread across frames, inspect its combined schedule: independently throttled shadows and reflections may still coincide and produce a periodic hitch. Measure frame-time variation as well as average throughput. The source's particular frame cadence and effect-removal counts are not OpenLegend budgets. Cosmetic cloth/effect simplification must preserve coherent return to view; it must not delete authoritative objects or skip world consequences.

For a future task that actually selects a grid representation, the voxel scaffold retains another useful method: emit exposed faces, merge compatible coplanar faces, consult neighbouring cells across region borders, and keep opaque/transparent output distinct. Material or ambient-occlusion differences can limit merging. Rebuild affected boundary neighbours when edits change exposure. This is a conditional meshing technique, not a proposal to convert OpenLegend's art to voxels.

The [performance core](../../../.agents/skills/openlegend-performance/SKILL.md) and [world presentation](../../../docs/world-presentation.md) own implementation decisions. The source's voxel mesher, palette lookup, fixed population/triangle/light limits and retro framebuffer solve a different representation; retain the general questions above without importing those systems.

## Feedback and demonstrations

Use actual project items, output revisions and captures in review. Attach feedback to the exact candidate and view or moment; show enough adjacent context to understand the defect. A contact sheet can compare a family cheaply, but inspect an ambiguous detail at adequate resolution and motion in motion. Label representative samples and missing views. Do not call a sample exhaustive or fill missing measurements with invented numbers.

Record the concrete next correction and stop once the requested outcome is met. Numerical ratings can summarize a person's preference; they are not a substitute for observable criteria or permission for an automated endless polishing loop. Bots or scripted scenarios can exercise control and state, but an infallible aiming bot does not establish human balance. A run stuck on scenery is evidence to diagnose, not a failed sample to discard until the result looks favourable.

For an authorized demonstration, show the actual distinctive experience: a recognizable resident doing something independently, a useful creation being used, a place before and after a real contribution, or an ordinary enjoyable moment there. Establish the place, show the action and let its consequence register. Capture an informative approach/interior/return view and relevant light contrast rather than only a picturesque aerial. Keep a clean presentation capture distinct from evidence with interface and interaction context. Check every chosen frame for occlusion, clipping and misleading staging. No forced golden-hour preset, trailer, marketing site or publication is required.

The [first-playable playtest plan](../../../docs/projects/first-playable-playtest-plan.md) and [FPV tracker](../../../docs/maintainers/first-playable-validation.md) own real player evidence and demonstrations. The source workbench is useful as a feedback pattern, not a new dashboard requirement. Its password-gated page with public backing blobs does not establish private storage, and its deployment scripts do not authorize publishing or removing deployments. Use existing asset/storage/permission owners.

## Useful future questions and their existing homes

The following opportunities survive the source review without making every source mechanic desirable. They are design inputs to use when their existing owner selects the corresponding work. Preserving an idea here does not authorize its implementation, and a well-written source prompt does not establish that it is fun.

| Opportunity                                                      | Retained method and decision still needed                                                                                                                                                                                | Existing route                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Recognizable residents with varied bodies and removable clothing | Complete bodies and fitted interchangeable parts; decide coverage, head joins, common rig and fit/visibility across garment layers                                                                                       | [PPL01–PPL03](../../../docs/maintainers/art/people.md), [character recipe](characters-and-clothing.md)                                                                                                                                                            |
| Inventions whose form explains their use                         | Visible joins, working ends, pivots and the same identity in held/ground/icon views; select supported part/animation families before promising them                                                                      | [INV-12](../../../docs/maintainers/inventions-and-world-evolution.md#inv-12--progressive-in-game-art-and-compatible-publication), [prop recipe](props-and-materials.md)                                                                                           |
| Homes worth building and revisiting                              | Coherent thresholds, useful circulation, activity-specific furnishings and actual changed states; decide the smallest additional visual capability a selected shelter feature needs                                      | [Editable shelters](../../../docs/maintainers/editable-shelters.md), [architecture recipe](architecture-and-worlds.md)                                                                                                                                            |
| Satisfying practical work and skill                              | Readable preparation/contact/result, tool-specific handling and enough control to express skill; decide where interaction improves the repeated activity rather than adding routine busywork                             | [Action execution and skill expression](../../../docs/maintainers/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md), [motion recipe](motion-and-visible-work.md)                                                                                                          |

| Different authored realities                                     | Change construction, materials, ecology, clothing and place use coherently while reusing generic systems; select one world's actual laws and art direction                                                               | DG12 in [needs design](../../../docs/maintainers/needs-design.md#dg12--create-a-world-and-reuse-an-invention), [world-style method](architecture-and-worlds.md#develop-a-different-setting-without-merely-recolouring-it)                                         |

| Transport, mounted work or complex moving devices                | Distance-based travel, actual support/contact, linked motion and coherent entry/exit; select a worthwhile player activity and its physical/authority contract first                                                      | Existing spatial/feature design when selected; [mechanism reference](motion-and-visible-work.md#mechanisms-and-travel)                                                                                                                                            |

Source genre mechanics—loot rarity, respawning checkpoints, fixed quests, resource rewards for kills, invulnerable finishers, physics powers, traffic schedules or a potion catalogue—remain examples, not accepted gameplay. Their useful underlying questions are retained above: do players understand an opportunity, have meaningful choices, see honest consequences and enjoy returning? OpenLegend should use its independent people, practical invention and persistent places to answer those questions, rather than accumulate a checklist of comparable games' features.

For a selected group activity, distinguish participants by useful roles, positions and intentions before adding more bodies. Everyone pursuing the same nearest target or work position can collapse into an unreadable clump. Stable responsibilities and clear transfers of work can make cooperation understandable; their selection belongs to the existing character/action design, not a presentation-only schedule. Likewise, protect the player's view of the consequence: competing labels, particles and camera motion can obscure the very work or reaction meant to be rewarding. Add feedback for information the player needs, then remove redundant emphasis. Neither fewer notifications nor more spectacle is an automatic rule.
