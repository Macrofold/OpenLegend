# Grounded 3D asset source survey for Open Legend

Researched **October 17, 2026**, against `origin/codex/starting-scene-pixel-art` at [`dc20b4c97c6f171f8a45a4938d34e671672048cf`](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/commit/dc20b4c97c6f171f8a45a4938d34e671672048cf). This expands the [October 16 shortlist](grounded-asset-creators.md).

## Recommendation

Build a curated source library around **Poly Haven, NatureManufacture, polygoniq/botaniq, Meshingun, Dekogon, GiM Animalia, and one affordable animated wildlife supplier**. Evaluate **Bartosz Woźny, BMS and Reallusion** separately for people and clothing; they solve different parts of the civilian/warrior problem. For later worlds, prioritize **GameAssetFactory and Leartes for inhabited fantasy**, then **Sierra Division and Hivemind3D for inhabited planetary sci-fi**.

The strongest sources supply believable forms that can survive deliberate material and pixel interpretation. None supplies the accepted Open Legend finish as a turnkey style. Beautiful source renders, dense scans, large pack counts and a “game ready” label do not establish a successful moving-camera pixel result.

This survey ranks **59 concrete collection entries from 55 distinct providers**: 15 shared foundations, 20 medieval-survival entries, nine fantasy entries and 15 sci-fi entries. Meshingun, Dekogon, BMS and KitBash3D each appear in two world groups but count once as providers. Marketplace storefronts, different editions of one pack, colorways, poses and prefabs are not counted as additional suppliers or unique creatures. Additional screened-out or conditional leads are recorded separately.

Public preview artwork was actually inspected for **39 ranked entries**. Some were small lineups that support only silhouette-level observations; the tiny Junnichi thumbnail does not count as close inspection. No new source model was purchased, acquired, imported, retopologized or tested through the Open Legend renderer. This is a source comparison and proposed audition order, not production acceptance.

## Art direction and world requirements used

The controlling references are the [current visual decisions](../../decisions.md#current-visual-requirements--october-16-2026), [creative brief](../../final-board/creative-brief.md), the refinement-round analyses, [house-style guidance](../../midjourney/07-open-legend-house-style.md), [adaptation research](openlegend-adaptation.md), [natural-replacement study](../../assets/source-study/natural-replacements.md), and existing source/provenance records. The [appearance-family project](../../../docs/projects/3d-pixel-art-appearance-families.md), [asset pipeline](../../../docs/projects/3d-pixel-art-asset-pipeline.md), and [validation rubric](../../../docs/projects/3d-pixel-art-validation.md#3-art-acceptance-rubric) continue to own implementation and acceptance.

The ranking follows these requirements:

- **Believable proportions and construction.** Natural head/body ratios, convincing animal anatomy, continuous clothing coverage, sensible armor, functioning-looking tools, real tree branching and irregular natural silhouettes matter before texture detail.
- **Deliberate pixel craft.** Source detail should resolve into readable color and material groups. The latest preference for larger groups rules out treating finer textures as an automatic improvement. Preserve the face, garment boundaries, hands, weapon silhouette and important joints as detail is simplified.
- **Organic vegetation with space inside it.** Favor trunks, boughs and canopy gaps that remain visible. Dense foliage may need thinning; reduce tiny leaf and twig noise without turning a tree into a faceted ball. Keep varied greens and diffuse undersides rather than opaque planes, black self-shadow or random bright painted patches.
- **Warmth and danger together.** Ordinary daylight, welcoming inhabited places and attractive natural landscapes are needed alongside harsh survival and threatening ruins. A permanently desaturated horror kit or uniformly cheerful toy village would each narrow the world too far.
- **Free camera and motion.** Faces, thin leaves, roof edges, fur and scan backsides must hold up during ordinary play, rotation and zoom. A fixed-angle preview does not prove this.

Bitgem, KayKit, Polytope Studio and Synty remain excluded as art directions. Quaternius is recognized as capable in its own style, but its visible low-poly look remains outside this brief. A technical “low-poly” tag alone is not disqualifying: efficient geometry can still have natural silhouettes and smooth, believable forms. The original mercenary remains the identity and character-quality control; a purchased person does not automatically replace it.

## How to read the rankings

The order is **best overall fit for the stated role first**, balancing visible form, useful content coverage, ability to preserve the form through adaptation, portable source access, rights clarity and total acquisition effort. It is not a contest in photographic realism. A specialist lower in one group may be the best answer to a narrow need. For example, Reallusion is a strong civilian-art lead whose creator-use conditions reduce immediate readiness; VFX Grace is a strong anatomical reference with an expensive, labor-intensive runtime path.

Prices are observations on October 17, 2026, in **USD unless another currency is shown**. Sale, regular, seat, revenue, engine and export tiers are kept distinct. A hidden or failed checkout is marked unverified, never recorded as free. The numeric workbook price is an observed edition anchor, not a promise that it covers Open Legend's company, every dependency, or unrestricted distribution. No exchange-rate conversion or tax estimate is applied.

The sweep covered creator stores and the relevant parts of Fab, Unity Asset Store, ArtStation Marketplace, CGTrader, Superhive, Gumroad, Cubebrush, TurboSquid, RenderHub, Sketchfab, itch.io and OpenGameArt, plus Daz, Reallusion, botanical/scan specialists, Poly Haven, MakeHuman, NASA and Smithsonian. The goal was broad source coverage with concrete collections and primary evidence. It cannot certify that every upload on every marketplace has been inspected. Failed pages, texture-only suppliers, repeated storefronts and unverified names do not pad the ranked count.

## Close comparisons and what they imply

### 1. Open canopy: botaniq versus Maxtree

The inspected botaniq oak forms are useful starting points for the branch's requirement to see through and between boughs. Its branch/leaf controls make it a practical first canopy comparison. The inspected Maxtree ash variants have convincing height, weight, bifurcations and irregular outlines, but several crowns remain too solid for the desired image. That is a source-selection and thinning problem, not a reason to prefer artificial tree anatomy.

I would audition one relatively open oak from [botaniq](https://superhivemarket.com/products/botaniq-trees) against a selected alder or ash from [Maxtree Vol.184](https://superhivemarket.com/products/plant-models-vol-184-blender). Compare the visible branch hierarchy before material work, then judge canopy gaps, diffuse underside color and moving-camera leaf noise after adaptation. The cheapest number on Maxtree's multi-edition page is not the verified Blender price.

### 2. Forest composition: NatureManufacture versus Evermotion and BProduction

The inspected [NatureManufacture forest](https://naturemanufacture.com/forest-environment-set/) does a good job connecting exposed roots, rocks and trunks to a slope. That spatial relationship is more valuable here than inheriting its dark foreground or dense carpet of small plants. [Evermotion's Forest Biomes](https://superhivemarket.com/products/archmodels-vol-308-forest-biomes-collection) is a useful broader composition source, but a fully dressed forest floor can become busy after pixel treatment. BProduction's trees and ground vegetation offer variety, yet volume alone does not ensure a coherent riverbank.

Use a few well-chosen trees, understory clumps and rock/root pieces to establish depth. Preserve negative space around paths, gathering objects and animals. Rebuild the material and lighting treatment for Open Legend rather than porting an entire engine scene. NatureManufacture's wind-related notices include separately licensed code; a paid vegetation license does not automatically make every included shader or script transferable.

### 3. Free control: Poly Haven's chest and rocks

The inspected [Treasure Chest](https://polyhaven.com/a/treasure_chest) has readable plank curvature, credible iron strap thickness and clear wood/metal boundaries. Fine scratches, rivets and small ornament can be quieted without destroying its construction. It is a strong material-separation control, although an ornate curved chest should not stand in for every mundane crate. No opening hinge or lid animation was tested.

[Coast Rocks 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/coast_rocks_01) adds genuine geological structure for a later tidal shore. Select useful masses at an appropriate scale rather than treating a landscape scan as a camp prop. Existing adapted Poly Haven assets are prior controls, not newly acquired work in this survey. Their [CC0 asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license) is unusually helpful for an open repository; the site's promotional artwork has separate rights.

### 4. Bear and deer: GiM versus the affordable wildlife packs

The inspected GiM bear has the strongest combination of shoulder mass, small ears, restrained face and believable weight. This matters because the current bear remains a weak comparison point. GiM is a good priority for a single hero-animal audition once the exact Commercial/PRO FBX edition and price are confirmed. The deer page's displayed price is not a quote for the bear or for every source tier.

Protofactor and RedDeer are the more economical broad-wildlife comparisons. Protofactor's set covers immediate forest species; RedDeer adds useful adult/young families and variation. Their small inspected lineup images establish silhouette potential, not approved faces, skeletons or gait. [Malbers' Realistic line](https://marketplace.unity.com/packages/3d/characters/animals/realistic-animal-forest-set-317683) has extensive motion, but several inspected faces retain expressive brows, bright eyes or cute mouth shapes. Smooth meshes alone do not resolve that mismatch.

### 5. Ordinary people: Bartosz, Reallusion, BMS and Dary

The strongest civilian image is a covered, adult-proportioned person with a readable face, ordinary footwear, useful layers and clothing that survives walking and tool use. Bartosz's older medieval man is a focused inexpensive comparison. Reallusion's working clothes and mature bodies are particularly relevant. Its current commercial-game grant is broader than old Extended-license advice suggests, while character-generation and protected-format requirements remain directly relevant to this project.

BMS is strongest for garment construction, armor and convincing mounted/armored human form. Repeated posed civilians do not supply a varied living population. **Dated correction:** the [current Fab medieval character edition](https://www.fab.com/listings/a7537685-1502-4c43-b294-92ff3cdb632f) now includes rigged knights. Its villagers remain posed, and its horse and animation scope must not be inferred from the [different direct collection](https://www.bigmediumsmall.com/medieval-update). Poses are not locomotion/action clips.

Dary's face and rig controls offer useful customization, but several inspected designs need less glamorous facial treatment and more cloth coverage. MakeHuman/MPFB provides a free core foundation for authored body variety, with substantial clothing, hair and face work still required. These are complementary options, not interchangeable “NPC packs.”

### 6. Survival tools: Dekogon, Red Blue Pixel and Dryzhak

The inspected Dekogon tools have convincing practical construction and material boundaries. Red Blue Pixel's carpentry collection supports recognizable hands-on work without requiring a whole village purchase. Dryzhak's Survivalist Forge has useful functional coverage and a concrete FBX route, but the inspected image shows more painted wood grain, bright outlined stones and a chunky hearth treatment. I rank it below Dekogon and Red Blue for this source-art target.

This does not mean Dryzhak must be discarded: a selected tool may remain useful after material replacement. Do not assume the staged walls and floor are part of its object manifest. In every collection, prioritize readable handles, working edges, supports and human scale over decorative microdetail.

### 7. Camp and settlement: Graphic District, Meshingun and Freshcan

Graphic District's rough pole-and-cover camp is closer to the starting situation than a finished monastery or elaborate military encampment. Meshingun's village is the broadest inspected supplier for the next stage: ordinary timber/stone buildings, furnishings, food, tools and lived-in clutter. Its large count includes assemblies, and the included female NPC's variations do not amount to five independent civilians.

Freshcan has appealing natural building proportions and established-town architecture, but its monastery scope and dense supplied trees make it a later donor. PolyPixel's docks have a narrower, valuable river-crossing role despite the package's age. For the first settlement, favor practical thresholds, doors, work surfaces and believable building materials over an elaborate skyline.

### 8. Birds: mechanics before feathers

Junnichi is a useful middle-cost source lead because the documented wing-folding mechanics relate to real anatomy, but close artwork inspection remains outstanding. VFX Grace's inspected collage has convincing necks, beaks, feet and wings; its dense feathers and high-end authoring workflow make it a reference ceiling or selected hero donor. NoneCG supplies another native-DCC route, with action breadth and project licensing to check.

Start with a crow, robin, chicken or water bird relevant to the scene. Flight alone does not establish useful landing, perching, feeding or ground movement. Avoid purchasing exotic breadth before one ordinary bird survives wing folding and camera motion at gameplay distance.

### 9. Inhabited fantasy: GameAssetFactory and Leartes before skyline kits

[GameAssetFactory's forge](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/medieval-props-vol-2-forge-196820) supports work, repair, kilns and the warmth economy. [Leartes' interior](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/fantasy/fantasy-interior-376828) offers useful furniture, study and archive dressing; select pieces with suitable period cues. These support actual social places in the proposal. Neither automatically supplies a whole teaching hall or functional household, but both are more directly useful than another distant castle.

Meshingun Gothic is better reserved for the Unfinished Cloister/Hollow Bell Vault. Scans Factory supplies organic ruin surroundings, with an explicit free-camera issue: scan backs, undersides and buried surfaces may be missing. Lordenfel is a strong adventure-architecture lead whose publisher explicitly excludes inhabited town buildings. Dark Fantasy supplies later architectural accents; dense spires should not establish every town's identity.

### 10. Fantasy creatures: Naked Singularity versus 3D Disco

The inspected Naked Singularity wyvern feels organized around weight-bearing wing forelimbs, a heavy body, an elongated jaw and restrained head scale. Those forms are promising for a threatening animal that belongs in the world. The inspected 3D Disco dragon has useful membrane wings and head proportions, but its rounded belly and large bright claws/horns read more storybook. Its many variants are not evidence of many independent anatomical designs.

Keep dense scales, spines, membranes and effects separate in the adaptation decision. A creature may have good underlying anatomy while still requiring aggressive detail grouping. Infinity PBR's High Fidelity line remains a product-scope lead; its separate stylized lines and exposed barbarian wardrobe should not be generalized into approval for the current people brief.

### 11. Working sci-fi: Sierra, Hivemind and Jonathon Frederick

The inspected [Sierra infrastructure](https://www.sierradivision.com/store/industrial-infrastructure-pack) has broad, functional industrial shapes that leave room for Open Legend's culture and palette. Explicit FBX/USD delivery makes a source comparison more concrete. [Hivemind's outpost](https://www.cgtrader.com/3d-models/exterior/other/modular-sci-fi-outpost) combines buildings with furnished-room scope; only its exterior cover was visually assessed here, so usable interiors remain a model inspection item.

[Jonathon Frederick's free bundle](https://www.fab.com/listings/cb3c4494-4060-4a80-b079-e46936cb8dd0) is a useful mechanical-room comparison, with separate material regions that could become calmer pixel groups. Its exact current grant and UE export route need qualification. These sources can support cargo, workshops and service spaces. Hinge Port also needs homes, kitchens, music/social places, shared facilities and a clinic; corridor sets do not supply the complete social world.

### 12. Sci-fi humans, kitbash and real engineering

[BMS AstroNova](https://www.bigmediumsmall.com/astronova) has convincing adult bodies and layered suits, with heavy source geometry and a pose/animation distinction. [Mission to Minerva](https://kitbash3d.com/products/mission-to-minerva) is a useful free habitation/vehicle control, but its total source geometry is substantial. Free purchase price does not imply low adaptation cost.

NASA's rover is a valuable check on practical suspension, instruments and serviceable mechanical structure. Smithsonian's explicitly CC0 objects can inform cramped spacecraft ergonomics and mundane object construction; object-specific files and rights still need checking. Averkin and Ushenok supply construction details for original devices. They do not solve a species' anatomy, a culture's visual language or a working ship's domestic spaces.

## Recommended audition order and remaining gaps

| Comparison | Smallest useful scope | What it should resolve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing free control | Original mercenary plus existing Poly Haven tree/rock/chest controls | Establish the actual current art-quality baseline and retain existing provenance. |
| Natural canopy | One open oak and one alder/ash from botaniq or Maxtree | Branch visibility, canopy gaps, diffuse undersides, readable leaf groups and motion stability. |
| Forest ground | One root/rock transition and a few undergrowth clumps from NatureManufacture or Evermotion | Natural integration without a noisy carpet or black foreground. |
| Wildlife | One GiM bear and one affordable deer/wolf from Protofactor or RedDeer | Face, weight, gait, fur grouping and worthwhile quality/cost difference. |
| Civilian | One covered older villager, plus a wardrobe/body comparison | Face identity, cloth coverage, hand/tool use, body variety and legally usable export. |
| Camp/craft | One shelter, workbench, axe/hammer and container from Graphic District/Dekogon/Red Blue | Practical scale and construction at normal gameplay distance. |
| Inhabited fantasy | One furnished craft/study room plus a small ruin threshold | Everyday life and magical-world identity without blanket gothic styling. |
| Planetary sci-fi | One workshop/cargo corner and one living compartment | Functional forms, calm material groups and room for authored culture. |

For every acquired candidate, compare **source, adapted artwork and final pixel render** at matched world scale, camera and lighting. Include ordinary gameplay distance, rotation, zoom and motion. The [existing acceptance rubric](../../../docs/projects/3d-pixel-art-validation.md#3-art-acceptance-rubric) owns the decision; this report introduces no universal polygon cap, fixed texture density or replacement approval test.

Specific gaps remain: a visually coherent and varied population of ordinary working adults; domestic livestock selected for restrained faces; actual botanical reeds/cattails rather than recolored grass; convincing fruiting/harvest stages for forage plants; deforming clothing and usable animal ground actions; and sci-fi galley/clinic/home furnishings. The proposed nonhuman citizens require original art direction. The current pale tree is not established as a real birch, and current tall-grass substitutes are not evidence that true reeds have been sourced.

The next acquisition decision should select a few complementary comparison assets with an exact edition, price and permitted workflow. It should not treat all 59 entries as a shopping list or a claim of blanket supplier approval.
