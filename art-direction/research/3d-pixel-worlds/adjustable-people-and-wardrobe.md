# How adjustable bodies, faces and clothes work

**Research, November 5, 2026.** An artist can sculpt a tunic once and put it on many people. That works when the pipeline knows where the collar, sleeves and hem belong as the wearer changes shape, and how those parts move once the wearer starts walking. These are two separate jobs. This comparison covers both, from dragging a body control to reviewing the dressed, moving result. The [people-from-parts tracker](../../../docs/maintainers/art/people.md) leads to the project design and acceptance work.

## From a slider to a body

Imagine dragging a nose-width control. An artist has sculpted a narrow and a broad nose on copies of the same neutral face. On every copy, point 412 still belongs to the same spot on the nostril. The file can therefore record how far each point moves from neutral. Halfway from neutral to broad, the computer adds half of each displacement. This is a **morph target**, also called a blend shape or shape key. Most points on the head need no movement for this control. Moving the slider changes a number. An artist can save that number alongside cheek, brow and chin settings as a preset, then continue editing it. [MakeHuman's illustrated target authoring](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/creatingassets/maketarget/targets.html) and [Khronos's worked morph example](https://github.khronos.org/glTF-Tutorials/gltfTutorial/gltfTutorial_017_SimpleMorphTarget.html).

**What to drag.** The Sims 4 gives players direct push-and-pull sculpting. MetaHuman documents both numeric measurement controls and draggable markers for parts of the face. Its body measurements can influence one another, so the editor lets an artist pin a measurement. Rare's lead character artist shows another use of the same design space in Sea of Thieves, where a generator combines bodies, facial features and accessories to produce varied pirate and NPC designs. This is a useful precedent for population authoring. These examples demonstrate the interaction or production result. Their mesh formats and fitting algorithms differ. [EA's player's guide](https://cdn-assets-ts4.pulse.ea.com/Guide/TheSims4_Players_Guide.pdf), [Epic's body controls](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-creator-body-params-tool-in-unreal-engine), [Epic's facial markers](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-creator-head-sculpt-tool-in-unreal-engine) and [Rare artist Sam Chester's production examples](https://samchester.co.uk/projects/Y1xzq).

For OpenLegend, a front-view shoulder handle can change breadth while a profile-view chin handle changes projection. Both can write the same named values as sliders and numeric entry. A description such as “height 75%” selects a point three quarters of the way along the family's declared height range. Show the resulting standing height in metres. An illustrated character sheet drawn without a size reference can show long legs relative to the torso, but it cannot establish absolute height. Establish the person's intended height separately. An authored build preset can set several controls together, including shoulder breadth, waist, soft tissue and muscle volume. The artist can continue from that result with individual controls.

**Where the knees bend.** Lengthening a shin moves the ankle as well as the skin. The motion system needs joint locations that match the body when it stands still. MakeHuman's rig implementation, for example, can place a joint from the average positions of selected body points. The SMPL research separates identity shape, the locations of joints on that body and corrections for what skin does in particular poses. OpenLegend can author a small set of such landmarks and corrections on its own art. A longer neck should carry the head joint, ears and collar reference up with it. A heavy, muscular shoulder may need an extra corrective sculpt when the arm lifts. Independent endpoint sculpts alone give little information about how those two changes interact. [MPFB's rig authoring method](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb/developer/rigging_and_posing.html) and [the SMPL authors' paper](https://is.mpg.de/ps/publications/smpl-2015).

## Getting from concept art to reusable geometry

People can look markedly different and still share the same arrangement of surface points. A **wrap** aligns a template with a detailed sculpt, then deforms the template to match. The artist identifies corresponding places such as the corners of the eyes, the tip of the nose and the ends of the mouth. Misplacing a loop of points on an eyelid can leave a convincing still face whose eye fails to close. Faceform documents pre-alignment, landmark pairs, progressively finer fitting and exclusion of recessed sockets. Epic also calls out the anatomical meaning of loops around folds, eyes and ears. This gives the artist a way to preserve a selected face while retaining the base layout for future controls. Judge the result in bare clay, in profile, at the neck seam and in any supported facial poses. [Faceform's wrapping method](https://www.faceform.com/docs/Wrap/Nodes/Wrapping/Wrapping.html) and [Epic's template-fitting guidance](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/metahuman/from-template-mesh).

## Fitting a shirt, a breastplate and an earring

**A shirt follows a surface.** In MakeClothes, each clothing point can reference three points on the underlying body or on a simplified helper surface. Three weights specify the location between them, and an offset keeps the cloth away from the skin. After broadening the chest, the computer finds that location on the broader chest and reconstructs the shirt there. An artist can limit each sleeve to its own arm so a cuff never chooses the nearby hip. A skirt can use a helper spanning both legs, giving the front panel one coherent surface to follow. The garment and the body can have entirely different numbers of points. [MakeClothes authoring and attachment data](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/creatingassets/makeclothes/clothes.html).

**An outfit needs room between layers.** Roblox authors fit surfaces on both sides of layered garments. Reallusion distinguishes preserving the outer silhouette while tucking an inner layer from expanding the outer layer to keep the inner garment's shape. Epic's parametric outfits can choose among artist-supplied source sizes. Its own test procedure calls for adding a size where resizing fails. For OpenLegend, these are practical shortcuts. A coat can have an authored wearing-over-mail fit. A tunic can hide covered torso faces, provided the wearer can remove it and get those faces back. An open collar needs visible neck skin, and a slit skirt needs visible legs through the slit. Test the open areas while the person moves. [Roblox layered clothing](https://create.roblox.com/docs/avatar/layered-accessories), [Reallusion cloth layers](https://manual.reallusion.com/Character-Creator-5/Content/ENU/5.2/04-Introducing-the-User-Interface/Cloth-Layer-Settings.htm) and [Epic's outfit testing](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/testing-and-configuring-your-parametric-outfit-asset).

**Each material needs an appropriate attachment.** An earring can sit at an authored point on the shaped earlobe. A helmet can use a head attachment and a reviewed shell size. A breastplate should keep its metal panels rigid while straps and articulated plates accommodate the torso and shoulder. A necklace's pendant may hang from one point, but the chain needs a path around the neck and room above the collar. For loose garments, a later production route could grade a sewing pattern against actual measurements and drape it offline. GarmentCode demonstrates separating the design of a garment from the target body measurements. This is an option when a fitted surface loses the folds or sweep that make the garment recognizable. [Roblox rigid accessory authoring](https://create.roblox.com/docs/avatar/rigid-accessories) and [GarmentCode research](https://igl.ethz.ch/projects/garmentcode/).

**A shortcut from Baldur’s Gate 3.** Larian gives artists a common boundary at the wrist and ankle. Shirt sleeves and trouser ends stay inside it, while gloves and boots fit around it and overlap far enough to close the join. A new pair of boots can consequently be designed against one interface instead of every pair of trousers. Its armour guide also shows how a tall collar suited to one person collides with the head of a shorter-necked wearer. For OpenLegend, publish shaped wrist and ankle guides with the family and qualify a tucked variant for intentionally bulky garments. A smooth height slider can coexist with these simple, artist-authored construction rules. [Larian’s armour construction guide](https://docs.baldursgate3.game/Creating_Armour) and [equipment and helmet-hair assignments](https://docs.baldursgate3.game/Adding_Armour).

## Fitting motion and delivering it to the game

A good standing fit answers the first question. To make the shirt move, each point also needs **skin weights**, which set how much it follows each moving bone. Weights transferred from the shoulder often make a useful start for a sleeve. A hanging cape is more difficult. The nearest body surface might be a leg on one side of the hem and the opposite leg on the other. When the wearer runs, the cape splits. OpenLegend has seen this in its own motion study. Research on weight transfer also documents how distance, layers and loose geometry make naive transfers fail. Author a separate chain of bones or an offline motion pass where the garment needs it, and inspect the final hem in a stride. [OpenLegend motion study](../../studies/2026-10-31-character-motion/README.md) and [Epic researchers' weight-transfer method](https://www.dgp.toronto.edu/~rinat/projects/RobustSkinWeightsTransfer/).

The binary glTF model delivered to the game can carry mesh deformations, a skeleton, skin weights and animation tracks. glTF applies vertex shape changes before the skeleton bends the resulting surface. Each skin's stored transforms must correspond to its joints in the correct order. The file format does not perform a garment fit or decide where a longer forearm puts its elbow. PlayCanvas has an interface for setting a model instance's morph weights. That makes a live preview possible, while a first production export can bake identity and clothing fit and retain the skeleton for motion. Keep the editable fitted pieces upstream. Compare the number of draws, decoded assets and frame time in the intended scene before choosing a runtime assembly strategy. Sharing a pose can reduce animation work while separate material and mesh sections still cost draws. [Khronos glTF geometry and skin specification](https://registry.khronos.org/glTF/specs/2.0/glTF-2.0.html#geometry), [PlayCanvas morph-instance API](https://api.playcanvas.com/engine/classes/MorphInstance.html) and [Epic's modular-character cost comparison](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/working-with-modular-characters-in-unreal-engine).

Put the dressed person into the actual clip on the actual fitted skeleton. A long arm changes where a tool lands and a short leg changes floor contact. A narrow hip can uncover a seam between shirt and trousers when sitting. With feet planted, compare drift, ground penetration and stride length on the short and tall bodies. Check cuffs under a raised arm, the jaw against a gorget, gloves on a tool and a braid against the cloak. Review the default camera, a close orbit and the lighting used in play. The proposed NVIDIA route supplies offline human-motion candidates. It leaves proportion-aware retargeting, contact correction, garment motion and reviewing the exported dressed person with the project. [NVIDIA research](nvidia-animation-2026-11-04.md), [motion authoring route](../../../docs/production-workflows/art-flows/animation.md) and [OpenLegend's prior production research](character-production-2026-10-31.md).

The project's local evidence covers individual generated people, a chosen face, a library lineup and retargeted clips. The continuous control ranges, likeness after wrapping, fitting of the same removable garment across both starting shapes, and browser cost of a dressed crowd still require project measurements. The decisive comparison is the same approved garment on distinct bodies through the same demanding poses, followed by removing it and reusing it on someone else.
