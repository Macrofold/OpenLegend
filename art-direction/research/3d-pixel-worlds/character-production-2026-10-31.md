**Research and repertoire review: 2026-10-31.** This bounded study asks which lessons from the supplied character-production conversation and professional practice should improve Open Legend's existing art workflow. Its inspected repository base is `Macrofold/OpenLegend@7e95b415f18cc58828eacbddc88da8a86693c3d0`. Current requirements remain in the [visual decisions](../../decisions.md#current-direction-at-a-glance), [creative brief](../../final-board/creative-brief.md) and linked design owners below.

**Bounded follow-through: 2026-11-05**, against `Macrofold/OpenLegend@6277b516e8cc10613e37bc289723d710d22f731a`: the current look is grounded stylized plain 3D. Future people require canonical bodies, adjustable faces/hair and removable clothing under [PPL01/PPL02](../../../docs/maintainers/art/people.md); their source, fit and construction choices remain unresolved. Selective generation remains a production technique, not permission to fuse clothes into a new person's body or reuse the rejected MakeHuman appearance. The [prototype](../../../docs/production-workflows/art-flows/README.md#prototype-path) and [polish](../../../docs/production-workflows/art-flows/production.md#polish-or-finalize-a-selected-model) paths remain the execution owners.

## Evidence and method

The review combined the supplied conversation, primary studio/practitioner guidance, current vendor documentation and an art-focused review across all 42 files in `docs/repertoires/`. Repertoire files were retrieved in full; reviewers inspected their complete heading structures, file-wide searches and relevant surrounding passages concerning appearance, identity, materials, fit, motion, contact and useful player situations. This is not a line-by-line editorial review of every catalogue entry, nor fresh verification of every historical source.

No character, animation or provider comparison was produced. No runtime, artistic-quality, crowd-capacity or cost result is claimed. Vendor capabilities describe documented offerings; production readiness still requires the project's actual qualification. The original source notes record the pages consulted on October 31, 2026; the dated follow-through below identifies additional primary evidence checked November 5. It does not repeat the earlier repertoire audit. Older craft articles remain relevant process evidence, while changing service capabilities and rights require checking at use.

The main conclusion is selective: the existing system already has the right owners for reusable bodies, rigs, equipment, manual repair, generated meshes, export and gameplay review. Add concrete decision rules and representative acceptance cases there. A new character factory, art style, general animation framework or second provider-comparison project would not follow from this evidence.

## Supplied conversation: useful points retained

The conversation references [Stefan 3D AI's video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VeOU0FnTjjc). Direct retrieval was throttled during the October 31 review. The supplied transcript was available for the November 5 follow-through; the video itself was not independently watched. The creator reports approximately three minutes for a basic model, just over an hour for an animated example and roughly six hours for the fuller treatment. The transcript includes manual assembly, geometry repair, UV work, texturing, rigging and Unreal setup, and some reuse of the earlier assembly. These are **creator-reported examples**, not matched trials, verified Open Legend timings or a production forecast. Do not manufacture timestamps.

## Professional practice and its transfer

### Read the character in context

[Valve's Character Art Guide](https://help.steampowered.com/en/faqs/view/0688-7692-4D5A-1935) emphasizes identifiable silhouette and facing, useful value separation, restrained detail and repeated checks from gameplay view under differing light. Transfer the review discipline: test the normal camera before polishing a portrait, and make important features readable without uniform noise. Dota's exaggerated anatomy, class vocabulary and particular top-to-bottom value gradient are not Open Legend requirements.

[Riot's Clarity in League](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/clarity-in-league/) (March 12, 2021) explains how silhouette, movement and effects compete for recognition and attention. Apply that to the same inhabitant remaining recognizable through activities and clothing changes. Decorative motion must not imply a different collision body, successful action or active condition. This is an interpretation for Open Legend, not a demand to encode people's danger, morality or hidden motives into appearance.

[Riot's portfolio guidance](https://www.riotgames.com/en/portfolio-and-reel-suggestions) asks for exploratory process, references, multiple views, interaction reasoning, material/anatomy fundamentals and expressive poses. A production brief should expose these useful decisions. It need not become a portfolio exercise or a mandatory sheet of every possible view for every small prop.

### Establish action and weight before polish

[Disney Animation's process page](https://disneyanimation.com/process/animation/) links performance to timing, staging, anticipation, follow-through, secondary action, anatomy and weight. Those principles make ordinary work, hesitation, humor and quiet company compelling as well as danger. They do not override the project's natural proportions.

[Drew Adams's blocking guidance](https://www.animationmentor.com/blog/stepped-vs-spline-blocking-in-animation/) (April 4, 2018) treats rough key poses and breakdowns as a test of the idea before smoothing motion. For a junior contributor: establish the intended beat and contact, review timed poses, refine movement and transitions, then add secondary detail. Stepped blocking is an authoring choice; it does not select a runtime frame cadence.

[Animation Mentor's game-animation course outline](https://www.animationmentor.com/workshops/game-animation-fundamentals/) places characters in-engine early and addresses constraints and transitions before final polish. This is teaching/process evidence, not a measured productivity result or a recommendation to buy the course. The useful transfer is to review the complete controlled action rather than an isolated looping clip.

### Make geometry, materials and export serve the result

[Autodesk's rig-preparation guidance](https://app.learn-one.autodesk.com/learn/ondemand/curated/realtime-rigging-prepare-to-create-the-rig) and [topology tutorial](https://www.autodesk.com/learn/ondemand/tutorial/checking-the-model-topology) put model scale, transformations, pose and deformation suitability before rigging. A mesh's face arrangement matters where the required joints bend. Review representative shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees and relevant contacts before investing in finished clips; fix the failed area rather than impose a reconstruction ritual. The accessible course descriptions informed this finding; their embedded videos were not independently reviewed.

[Adobe's PBR Guide, Part 2](https://www.adobe.com/learn/substance-3d-designer/web/the-pbr-guide-part-2) distinguishes base color from lighting and allows art-directed materials. Transfer that separation into generated-texture cleanup and moving-light checks. Do not copy its numerical material ranges into a universal palette rule or demand photorealism. A directional highlight painted into skin can look plausible under one lamp and contradictory under another.

[PlayCanvas's supported formats](https://developer.playcanvas.com/user-manual/assets/supported-formats/), [model construction](https://developer.playcanvas.com/user-manual/assets/models/building/) and [export guidance](https://developer.playcanvas.com/user-manual/assets/models/exporting/) recommend GLB and distinguish engine loading from Editor conversion. Export only required content with supported materials, textures, hierarchy and animation. The project must qualify its pinned consumer and exported bytes; generic format support does not prove that every authoring constraint, procedural material or physics setup transfers.

The [Khronos glTF specification](https://registry.khronos.org/glTF/specs/2.0/glTF-2.0.html) makes joints, weights and inverse bind matrices explicit. Consequently, matching bone labels alone cannot prove compatible binding or movement. Keep the editable source and inspect the exported skeleton and actual non-root deformation. Direct Blender manual retrieval failed during the October 31 study; the November 5 follow-through below uses the maintained exporter documentation without inferring a guarantee for the project's pinned version.

[Epic's modular-character documentation](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/working-with-modular-characters-in-unreal-engine) exposes tradeoffs among shared poses, independent parts and merged meshes. Shared animation does not eliminate draw cost; merging also has setup and feature consequences. Apply the cost question, not Unreal's implementation: distinguish editable production parts, exported render units and actual world equipment. Combine fixed pieces when appropriate, retaining the ability to depict real equipment changes correctly.

### Treat generation as one production stage

The [official Tripo P2 reference](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/models/p2) documents `P2-20260801`, optional quad output and unsupported `generate_parts`. Selective part generation therefore does not mean sending an assumed P2 part-generation switch. Use separate planned inputs or a separately documented operation. Record geometry/texture versions and seeds, orientation and scale settings; check outcomes rather than assuming automatic sizing or de-lighting succeeded. Native-quad and riggability claims remain vendor claims until deformation and export pass.

A separate [Tripo-hosted creator workflow](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/tripo-to-ue5-character-walk) describes component assembly, manual skinning, garment-weight transfer, game-skeleton preparation and export corrections. It illustrates finishing work still needed around generation. It is not the supplied video, a controlled comparison or a reason to copy its polygon budgets, custom bridge plugin or character style.

Tripo rights still require care in the existing provenance owner. [Terms dated July 11, 2025](https://www.tripo3d.ai/terms), [commercial-use help](https://www.tripo3d.ai/help/privacy-policy/how-to-use-tripo-models-commercially) and [October 2026 pricing guidance](https://www.tripo3d.ai/product-doc/getting-started/pricing-credits) disagree about free-user commercial permission; the pricing page also conditions paid ownership on private models. Preserve exact applicable terms, plan, generation date, privacy setting and input rights. Do not infer retroactive rights from upgrading. This does not block design or require selecting a provider now; uncertain outputs remain unqualified for production.
