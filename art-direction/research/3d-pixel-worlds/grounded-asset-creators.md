# Grounded 3D source creators for dimensional pixel art

Researched October 16, 2026. This is a source-art shortlist under Mike's [current visual requirements](../../decisions.md#current-visual-requirements--october-16-2026): beautiful dimensional pixel art with believable real-world proportions, explicitly excluding visibly low-poly, blocky, cutesy and big-head/small-body designs. These are candidates for adaptation, not selected suppliers or a promise that conversion will produce the desired finish.

## What changed

The previous shortlist overemphasized coherent stylized low-poly libraries. Mike explicitly rejected Bitgem, KayKit, Polytope Studio and Synty as art directions. He recognizes Quaternius as solid for its own style; its visibly low-poly look is still outside this target. Preserve earlier assets, acquisition records and comparisons without recommending those styles again.

Search for convincing anatomy, garment construction, branching structure, natural silhouettes and practical proportions first. Pixel craft and expressive lighting will interpret those forms. A realistic source is useful without requiring a photorealistic final game. “Low poly” sometimes describes an efficient mesh rather than a faceted visual style; inspect the actual artwork and its construction instead of accepting or rejecting it solely by that technical tag.

## Evidence and recommendation

Inspected the public product text and browser previews for Big Medium Small's medieval character gallery, Poly Haven's chest, Maxtree's shade trees and NatureManufacture's forest. Their more natural proportions and forms are a visual assessment from those previews, not a head-to-head test in OpenLegend. Dekogon's listing establishes the relevant product scope; its imported geometry and pixel result remain untested.

Start the next source comparison with **Poly Haven for a free prop/rock control**, **Big Medium Small for character/clothing form**, and **Maxtree or NatureManufacture for vegetation**. Keep the original mercenary as the identity and character-quality control. This is a research recommendation; no supplier has earned blanket approval and no paid pack was purchased or downloaded in this task.

Before acquisition, verify the exact product and edition: the [separate BMS Fab character listing](https://www.fab.com/listings/a7537685-1502-4c43-b294-92ff3cdb632f) says its current characters lack rigs and animation, while the direct updated collection lists particular rigged subjects. Do not transfer the direct bundle's features to another storefront edition. Similarly, “Unity/Unreal compatible” does not establish a portable PlayCanvas asset or transferable shader.

For each acquired candidate, compare the source, the adapted artwork and its final pixel render at the same world scale, camera and lighting. Preserve face structure, cloth coverage, useful material boundaries and organic shapes while removing noisy microdetail. Review ordinary gameplay distance plus rotation, zoom and motion: conversion must not produce faceted toy forms, stretched coarse texture, lost faces or shimmer. Production acceptance remains in [the existing qualification owner](../../../docs/projects/3d-pixel-art-validation.md#3-art-acceptance-rubric).

Check commercial-game use, adaptation, public-repository redistribution and any intended AI-service upload separately under the [existing rights guidance](tool-and-license-notes.md). Store research screenshots and marketing claims are not model inspections, asset licenses, download provenance or proof of zero shimmer. No game assets, renderer, save, provider integration or production acceptance changed in this research pass.
