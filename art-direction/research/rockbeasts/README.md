# ROCKBEASTS: color, light, expressive motion and menus

Mike likes ROCKBEASTS' colors, lighting, animation and menus that belong to its world. Although it is not pixel art, it is a useful reference for how those qualities work together. OpenLegend can carry that coherence into its **beautiful dimensional pixel art and believable real-world proportions**. The endorsed qualities do not adopt ROCKBEASTS' cartoon anatomy, music-industry setting or rendering style.

This study develops the [current visual requirements](../../decisions.md#current-visual-requirements--october-16-2026) and [creative brief](../../final-board/creative-brief.md). The companion [narrative case study](../../../docs/narrative/case-studies/rockbeasts.md) considers its characters and storytelling.

## 1. Give color a clear job

![S03: A red-lit nightclub sits against a purple evening city; the selected building has a bright yellow interface outline.](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/4182460/405741e2beda8a3b334a7ea4a4bb7de10343b25b/ss_405741e2beda8a3b334a7ea4a4bb7de10343b25b.1920x1080.jpg?t=1792585119)

_S03 — Official Steam screenshot: Rose Night Club, evening city and selected-location outline; exact build unknown._

Purple connects sky, distant buildings and shadow. Warmer windows, red signage and the club frontage create local destinations within that common atmosphere. The broad road and dark architectural areas give the luminous details room to register. The yellow building outline is selection feedback, distinct from the depicted light sources.

R01, T1 **00:19.2**, supplies a related comparison: warm windows and a tall green sign organize a denser street. Colorful does not mean every surface has equal intensity.

**OpenLegend application:** establish a scene's ambient family, then choose what deserves a stronger accent. A firelit doorway, a wet river crossing or a distinctive piece of clothing can provide a focal point. Preserve deliberate pixel groups and quieter surroundings so richness supports navigation and recognition. Apply this hierarchy to OpenLegend's own wilderness and settlements; neon is specific to this reference's setting.

## 2. Let lighting describe the place and its people

![S01: A pink-haired character stands at a bar framed by cyan bulbs and pink signage, above dark dialogue panels.](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/4182460/2c6508a1c8215d8651db189b2caf29047268e796/ss_2c6508a1c8215d8651db189b2caf29047268e796.1920x1080.jpg?t=1792585119)

_S01 — Official Steam screenshot: saturated venue lighting frames a conversation while solid dark panels support the text._

The cyan bulbs and pink surroundings give this conversation a recognizable venue atmosphere. The face remains a coherent shape against that busy background. A different mood appears in R02, T1 **00:21.5**: warm light gathers around the tavern's people and wood, while surrounding tables and corners retain shadow. R06, T2 **00:28.5**, uses warm edge highlights to separate the drummer, jacket and cymbals from a dark backdrop.

**OpenLegend application:** use identifiable illumination to create intimacy, danger, warmth or exposure. Let neighboring surfaces participate in a shared light while retaining their material character. Keep faces and important actions readable in shade. These images suggest useful relationships between value, hue and silhouette; they do not establish a shader recipe or justify painting unexplained bright patches onto assets.

## 3. Personality lives in poses and changes between them

T2 **00:30–00:37** shows a character on the phone changing expression and bringing the free hand into an open conversational gesture; R07 selects **00:34.5**. The occupied hand, sideways glance and changing gesture make this a particular interaction rather than a neutral portrait beside dialogue.

M01 samples the drummer at T2 **00:28.0, 00:28.4 and 00:28.8**. The sticks move between crossed, lowered and raised arrangements while the face stays concentrated. The action has readable stages. M02 samples the small-stage ending at **01:14.5, 01:17.5 and 01:20.0**: the ensemble remains largely in place while expressions and the bassist's instrument angle change. A held group composition leaves room for individual reactions. Trailer editing and sparse samples limit conclusions about exact timing, smoothness or looping.

**OpenLegend application:** give characters intentions that shape posture, gaze, hands and pauses. A wary greeting, someone protecting a wounded arm or a companion reacting late can convey character without extra exposition. Judge the sequence at actual gameplay scale, with natural anatomy and stable pixel treatment. Close cinematic shots are a reference for acting, not evidence that their detail will remain legible on a distant character.

## 4. Make menus feel like objects and actions in this world

![S04: A ring-bound calendar notebook holds clipped opportunities, resource requirements and a highlighted date.](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/4182460/3b99951bb93414c1c95dd0349bcc2d409451c9ad/ss_3b99951bb93414c1c95dd0349bcc2d409451c9ad.1920x1080.jpg?t=1792585119)

_S04 — Official Steam screenshot: paper, tabs, clippings and calendar structure turn planning into an activity with a recognizable material identity._

The notebook gives opportunities and dates a shared physical container. Repeated headings and aligned numbers organize the information inside its torn-paper styling. T1 **00:42–00:43** presents a recording contract with a pen; R05 at **00:44.5** presents an instrument with customization controls. Different tasks use appropriate objects while retaining related graphic language.

![S00: A gig-planning interface arranges song cards beneath the band, stage lighting and a neon cat emblem.](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/4182460/2186ee8b4e40e26fc14d166122f485d60481068a/ss_2186ee8b4e40e26fc14d166122f485d60481068a.1920x1080.jpg?t=1792585119)

_S00 — Official Steam screenshot: the planning interface retains the stage, band and music imagery behind its structured choices._

![S09: A bartender and illustrated group snapshot accompany two choices with clearly separated costs and effects.](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/4182460/9405a9ff4130c50f6e78bae98ad56eb1f25b79c9/ss_9405a9ff4130c50f6e78bae98ad56eb1f25b79c9.1920x1080.jpg?t=1792585119)

_S09 — Official Steam screenshot: a social moment and its resource choices share one screen; a yellow border marks the selected option._

The stage plan keeps the musicians visible; the social choice keeps a person and an illustrated moment beside its numbers. Both connect abstract management to what the player is doing. These are dense screens, so thematic consistency alone does not prove usability.

**OpenLegend application:** explore materials and objects appropriate to each world: a field journal, annotated map, equipment spread or keepsake display. Let their arrangement communicate the task. Preserve clear labels, stable controls, legible text and recognizable selection states. Texture, ornament and animation should help the player understand and enjoy the action. They should earn their place at normal viewing size.

## Applying the reference

For a future OpenLegend comparison, ask whether the palette directs attention, lighting situates the people, motion communicates intention, and the interface belongs to the same world while remaining easy to use. Evaluate those qualities in the existing dimensional pixel-art treatment with believable proportions.
