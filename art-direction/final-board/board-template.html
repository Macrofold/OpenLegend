A world with detail.
People with life.

Rich environments, expressive characters and light that gives everything a place.

Eleven confirmed favorites. Pixels and painted forms share the same ambition: a beautiful, dimensional world that rewards looking closer. This board consolidates your preferences; it does not lock a production technique.

Detail that describes

Clothing, equipment, bark and small possessions should tell us what something is. More detail should reveal more life.

People at two scales

Recognizable bodies and outfits in the world; richer faces, gestures and materials in profiles and close views.

Light with purpose

Warm fire against cool shade. Reflections, directional shadows and atmosphere that connect people to their surroundings.

Richness with clarity

Layered nature and lived-in spaces, balanced by clean silhouettes, quiet areas and deliberate focal points.
