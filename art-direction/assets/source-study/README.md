# Source-based starting-world study

Work in progress under the [approved replacement strategy](../../../docs/projects/3d-pixel-art-first-scene-plan.md#proposed-replacement-study--october-16-2026). These are review candidates, not accepted artwork or completed game integration.

The original concept-derived mercenary, three Quaternius tree structures, three fitted Ada designs and three source chest constructions are being compared in the existing `/art-study.html` inspector. The earlier rejected study remains preserved and explicitly labeled. Each new catalog beside the runtime GLBs records sources, exact hashes, measurements and the editable Blender file here.

- Mercenary: owner-supplied Meshy body and rig with original OpenLegend cape; imported source production rights remain unqualified. The restored control recovers the earlier viewer's 29-color preparation. Tailored and embroidered alternatives retain the original face while changing clothing/material organization.
- Broadleaf sources: Quaternius Stylized Nature MegaKit Standard, exact acquired CC0 archive and licenses recorded in [environment provenance](../../../apps/client/src/props/library-environment/provenance.json). Actual library topology and leaf cards, normalized for the study. Olive coloration and canopy-normal edits are under review; the initial vertex-color-only recolor missed the source's colored image and was corrected in the recipe.
- Ada: real MakeHuman anatomy, eyes and hair with Rehman Polanski's fitted Viking garments, all CC0. [Exact acquisition records](human-sources.json) and [core asset license](CC0-MakeHuman.txt) are retained. MPFB runs offline with temporary preferences; its code is not installed in the user's profile or bundled into the game. The field tunic, work vest and travel coat are provisional visual proposals. The visible skin mask belongs to this complete outfit; it is not a body or collision rule.
- Chest: the retained attributed generated chest and Kenney Survival Kit chest/box sources. The generated derivative retains its [CC BY 4.0 credit and source caveat](../../../docs/verification/generated-prop-coherence.md#source-and-permitted-reuse). The open lid on the initial Kenney comparison still needs correction before it can represent a closed supply crate.

`scripts/prepare-source-study.py` reads those retained sources without changing them and reuses the existing static export helper. Static GLBs demonstrate source/material choices; they do not establish animation, cape coverage or in-game qualification. The original animated files and cloth owner remain available for that work.

The inspector compares approximately 40/60/80 adult rows, an earlier fine-detail control and smooth 3D. Zoom retains the selected density until limited by available display pixels, which is reported. The ordinary adult is 1.85 m and mercenary 2.035 m in the calibration view. These are presentation calibration measurements, not changed physical world rules. Enlarged images and source textures do not by themselves prove rotation stability. Actual animation, scene composition, remaining families and runtime integration are still being implemented.
