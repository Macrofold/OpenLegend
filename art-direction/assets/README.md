# Art assets

None of the eight linked reference-board packs (A01–A08) has been adopted as a production asset library. Creators, source URLs and brief licensing notes remain in `../references.json` and `../references.md`. The separate [mercenary study](../../docs/maintainers/art/mercenary.md) has a prepared game-scene pilot; its editable source lives in OpenLegend-art, and final style and production-rights acceptance remain open.

Keep acquired packs or commissions with their source URL, creator, acquisition date, license/readme, modifications and intended use. This directory can hold an adopted pack; active editable studies follow the stable OpenLegend-art location in the [art progress owner](../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md), and deliberately prepared runtime files belong with their game consumer. Keep browser preference exports separately from artwork.
