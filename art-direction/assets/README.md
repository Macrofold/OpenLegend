# Art assets

Select sources against the [current visual requirements](../decisions.md#current-visual-requirements--october-16-2026): believable real-world proportions and forms for beautiful dimensional pixel art; reject visibly low-poly, blocky, cutesy and big-head/small-body designs. Existing acquired packs are preserved studies, not approved style suppliers. The [grounded creator shortlist](../research/3d-pixel-worlds/grounded-asset-creators.md) distinguishes new candidates from acquired or tested assets.

The [current natural-source replacements](source-study/natural-replacements.md) use Poly Haven for most vegetation/props and credited other sources for animals, archery and the leather pack. That record owns exact licenses, preparation and current choices; [the dated archive](../studies/2026-10-17-natural-replacements/README.md) preserves all meaningful tested iterations.

Kenney/Quaternius environment sources and a CC0 animated rabbit were acquired on October 15, 2026. Exact sources, licenses, retained files and provisional use are recorded in the [library acquisition report](../../docs/verification/library-art-variants.md#actually-downloaded-sources), [environment provenance](../../apps/client/src/props/library-environment/provenance.json) and [animal attribution](../../apps/client/src/props/library-animals/ATTRIBUTION.md). Their acquisition does not establish final pixel-art acceptance. The original [representative Blender study](representative-study/README.md) is also retained, with its art now [rejected](../decisions.md#corrected-study-requirements--october-16-2026).

Eight linked pack previews remain in the reference board (A01–A08), with creators, source URLs and brief licensing notes in `references.json` and `references.md`.

Keep editable source studies beneath this directory; runtime-ready bundled resources and their provenance may live with their existing client asset owner. Keep each pack or commission grouped with its source URL, creator, acquisition date, license/readme, modifications, and intended use. Link the owning provenance instead of creating a second writable acquisition catalogue. Keep browser preference exports separately from artwork.

The [October 14 pixel-world research library](../research/3d-pixel-worlds/gallery.md) contains locally saved screenshots, product previews and artist/tool illustrations with a source/rights manifest. These are reference media, not acquired production assets; saving a preview does not license the underlying artwork or pack for the game or a generation service.

The screenshot previews on the board remain external references. They are not an acquired asset library. A selected asset pack's actual download and license should be reviewed together before importing it into the game.
