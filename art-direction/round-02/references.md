## R01 — Kathy Rain 2: Soothsayer

**Game · People and clothing**

**Look at:** Readable outfits, adult proportions, atmospheric streets

Following your praise for Tails Noir: inspect the people standing in the scene, their clothing layers, and colored light on wet surfaces.

**Watch for:** A detailed dialogue portrait is visible too. Judge the smaller world figures separately; the city and mystery setting are not proposals.

**Feedback question:** Do the world figures have enough face, clothing, and body detail—or does only the large portrait meet your target?

## R02 — Unavowed

**Game · People and clothing**

**Look at:** Distinct people, clothing, and restrained material shading

The cast has different builds, hairstyles, layers, and recognizable clothing. This tests the identity you want beyond Eastward’s simplified bodies.

**Watch for:** Its adventure-game camera gives people much more screen space than an overhead simulation would. The large portrait is a separate artwork.

**Feedback question:** Is this a useful target for ordinary people in the world, or does the finish still feel too old-fashioned?
