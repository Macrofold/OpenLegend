## R01 — Kathy Rain 2: Soothsayer

**Game · People and clothing**

**Look at:** Readable outfits, adult proportions, atmospheric streets

Following your praise for Tails Noir: inspect the people standing in the scene, their clothing layers, and colored light on wet surfaces.

**Watch for:** A detailed dialogue portrait is visible too. Judge the smaller world figures separately; the city and mystery setting are not proposals.

**Feedback question:** Do the world figures have enough face, clothing, and body detail—or does only the large portrait meet your target?

## R02 — Unavowed

**Game · People and clothing**

**Look at:** Distinct people, clothing, and restrained material shading

The cast has different builds, hairstyles, layers, and recognizable clothing. This tests the identity you want beyond Eastward’s simplified bodies.

**Watch for:** Its adventure-game camera gives people much more screen space than an overhead simulation would. The large portrait is a separate artwork.

**Feedback question:** Is this a useful target for ordinary people in the world, or does the finish still feel too old-fashioned?

## R03 — HUNTDOWN

**Game · People and clothing**

**Look at:** Costume detail, dense materials, sharp local light

Compare readable clothing and bodies against carefully drawn urban surfaces. This tests how much detail matters independently of the setting.

**Watch for:** Exaggerated action poses and a deliberate arcade flavor may conflict with your preference for a contemporary finish.

**Feedback question:** Do the sprites meet your detail threshold, and can you separate their craft from the retro action styling?

## R04 — Gestalt: Steam & Cinder

**Game · Nature and fantasy**

**Look at:** Layered foliage, textured machinery, expressive color

A bridge from Owlboy’s environmental richness to taller action sprites. Look at light through foliage, depth layers, and material texture.

**Watch for:** The hero remains small in gameplay; large anime dialogue portraits are shown only as a comparison, not evidence of world-sprite detail.

**Feedback question:** Does the forest have the right richness? Are the actual world sprites detailed enough, independent of the portraits?

## R05 — The Excavation of Hob’s Barrow

**Game · Nature and fantasy**

**Look at:** Organic woodland, layered branches, ordinary clothing

Moves the comparison out of neon cities: inspect irregular trees, undergrowth, atmospheric distance, and the clothing of people in a landscape.

**Watch for:** Its folk-horror mood and visible pixel grain may be too gloomy or coarse. This is a wilderness/material study, not a horror recommendation.

**Feedback question:** Would you want this kind of organic landscape with warmer daylight and finer characters, or is the pixel treatment itself unsuitable?

## R06 — The Last Faith

**Game · Nature and fantasy**

**Look at:** Long silhouettes, coats, gear, and intricate architecture

Follows your positive reaction to Blasphemous 2’s legible gear. Study equipment and clothing shapes alongside detailed surfaces.

**Watch for:** Gothic gloom and combat are not the target. Rate the character and material definition separately from the oppressive atmosphere.

**Feedback question:** Is this closer to the clothing and equipment fidelity you want, if placed in a more inviting world?

## R07 — Chrono Sword

**Game · Angled views**

**Look at:** Oblique view, depth, textured ground, local light

Tests the hardest translation: bringing atmospheric pixel scenery into an angled view while keeping individual agents legible.

**Watch for:** The small cloaked figure has less facial and clothing information than a side-view adventure cast. Do not treat scene detail as proof of character detail.

**Feedback question:** Does this camera and ground treatment appeal? How much larger or more finely drawn would the people need to be?

## R08 — NORCO

**Game · Light and landscape**

**Look at:** Wetland atmosphere, reflective water, layered vegetation

An environment-only study following your interest in Ferrari’s water and Rocha’s wilderness composition.

**Watch for:** Scenes and close-up artwork are not ordinary simulation sprites. The industrial melancholy may not be the emotional target.

**Feedback question:** Would you borrow its atmosphere and landscape texture, with more warmth and visible life?
