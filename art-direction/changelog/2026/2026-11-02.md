# Week of November 2, 2026

## November 4, 2026 — An asset library of whole free packs

**Study.** Mike asked for a large survey of the assets the repertoires call for, free and paid, with every downloadable free asset added to the study. The [asset library](../../studies/2026-11-04-asset-library/README.md) adds 2,989 pieces from 85 CC0 and CC BY packs to the survey page: the rest of the Quaternius anchor line with its people, outfits and animation libraries, the rest of _Fantasy Creatures_, Gruginski's village and dungeon kit, a mine and cave kit, Poly Haven models and textures, ambientCG materials and Sketchfab models. When Mike asked for more weapons in the stylized textured look, a [weapons round](../../studies/2026-11-04-asset-library/README.md#weapons-round--november-4-2026) added 202 weapon, shield and armour pieces, 84 of them painted, including 20 hand-painted OpenGameArt weapons converted from Blender files and Gruginski's _Medieval Weapons & Items_ (converted from FBX, with 101 ground items such as potions, food and jewellery). 72 paid packs are listed with prices; nothing was bought.

**Marks.** A piece is a sure fit only when it comes from a set Mike has already liked or approved: the Quaternius line, _Fantasy Creatures_ and Poly Haven's scanned conifers, deadwood, shrubs and ferns. Everything else is on the fence, with its reason on its card.

**Tradeoffs.** Whole packs are shown at their makers' sizes and filed by name rules, so a few pieces may read the wrong size or sit under a near subject. Paid packs from Fab, Unity and CGTrader carry clauses against players extracting files, which an open-source browser game may not meet.

**Consequences.** Publishing the library to S3 needs the asset authority on Mike's other Mac, and 48 Sketchfab models remain queued ([AD09](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad09--follow-through-on-the-grounded-stylized-direction)).

## November 3, 2026 — Free animal sets ranked; WildMesh 3D animals enter the game

**Decision.** Mike ranked the free animal sets in his world asset survey: "Wildmesh 3D wins, Fantasy Creatures second", the current game picks third, and "All the low poly ones are 100% no go." Earlier the same day he had ruled out classic low-poly in general. Each species now uses WildMesh 3D's model where it has one, then _Fantasy Creatures_', then the current pick. The game uses WildMesh 3D's painted doe, stag and brown bear and the _Fantasy Creatures_ grey wolf; the hare and woodland bird keep their models. The [current visual requirements](../../decisions.md#current-visual-requirements--october-31-2026) state it.

**Why.** WildMesh 3D's animals have natural anatomy and painted coats without shine, the closest free match to the grounded stylized direction. _Fantasy Creatures_ fills the wolf, which WildMesh 3D lacks.

**Tradeoffs.** WildMesh 3D's models are CC BY 4.0, so the game must credit them; Settings → Credits now lists every credit the game's artwork licenses require ([AD06](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad06--show-artwork-credits-in-the-game)). Their creator calls these free versions older, with known rigging problems, and the coats show flat patches up close. Their 1024 px textures and three or four materials each cost more than the earlier animals' single 256 px texture. Fox, boar, hare and most birds still have no source in the direction.

**Consequences.** The game versions and the remaining publication on Mike's other Mac are tracked in [AD09](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad09--follow-through-on-the-grounded-stylized-direction); the [survey record](../../studies/2026-10-31-world-asset-survey/README.md#mikes-animal-verdict--november-3-2026) has the details and checks.
