# Week of November 2, 2026

## November 3, 2026 — Free animal sets ranked; WildMesh 3D animals enter the game

**Decision.** Mike ranked the free animal sets in his world asset survey: "Wildmesh 3D wins, Fantasy Creatures second", the current game picks third, and "All the low poly ones are 100% no go." Earlier the same day he had ruled out classic low-poly in general. Each species now uses WildMesh 3D's model where it has one, then _Fantasy Creatures_', then the current pick. The game uses WildMesh 3D's painted doe, stag and brown bear and the _Fantasy Creatures_ grey wolf; the hare and woodland bird keep their models. The [current visual requirements](../../decisions.md#current-visual-requirements--october-31-2026) state it.

**Why.** WildMesh 3D's animals have natural anatomy and painted coats without shine, the closest free match to the grounded stylized direction. _Fantasy Creatures_ fills the wolf, which WildMesh 3D lacks.

**Tradeoffs.** WildMesh 3D's models are CC BY 4.0, so the game must credit them; Settings → Credits now lists every credit the game's artwork licenses require ([AD06](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad06--show-artwork-credits-in-the-game)). Their creator calls these free versions older, with known rigging problems, and the coats show flat patches up close. Their 1024 px textures and three or four materials each cost more than the earlier animals' single 256 px texture. Fox, boar, hare and most birds still have no source in the direction.

**Consequences.** The game versions and the remaining publication on Mike's other Mac are tracked in [AD09](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad09--follow-through-on-the-grounded-stylized-direction); the [survey record](../../studies/2026-10-31-world-asset-survey/README.md#mikes-animal-verdict--november-3-2026) has the details and checks.
