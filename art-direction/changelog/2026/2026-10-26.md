# Week of October 26, 2026

## October 31, 2026 — Plain 3D models replace the pixel-art direction

**Decision.** Mike changed the world's art direction from dimensional pixel art to **plain 3D models and textures**, shown at full display resolution with no pixel treatment. The [current visual requirements](../../decisions.md#current-visual-requirements--october-31-2026) and [creative brief](../../final-board/creative-brief.md) now state the plain-3D direction. The October 16 pixel-art requirements remain as history.

**Why.**

- **Scale.** The game aims to generate many characters, items and environments procedurally. Pixel art seemed the cheaper route, but in a 3D game with a rotating camera every subject still needs a 3D model; the pixel look is extra work on top. Generation tools produce textured 3D models directly, so plain 3D adds no per-asset step.
- **Stability.** The running game's pixel look came mainly from a grid applied to the whole finished image, plus a separate low-resolution capture of the mercenary. Because those pixels belong to the screen rather than the models, rotating the camera made the mercenary shimmer heavily, a failure the art direction already treated as critical. Ordinary textured 3D with mipmaps and antialiasing is the easiest of the options to keep stable.
- **The alternatives cost more.** Stable pixel art in the style of The Last Night would need every model to carry a small pixel-painted texture at a consistent size and palette. That adds a conversion step per asset, plus repaired texture layouts and hand-designed low-resolution faces. A stable screen-space grid would need camera snapping and restricted rotation. Both are more work than plain 3D at the intended scale.
- **History of the grid.** The October 15 experiments had rejected whole-scene downsampling because it removed useful detail. The October 16 autonomous study brought a single screen grid back as an agent working choice, and October 17 coarsened it. Mike never accepted it as the art direction.

**Tradeoffs.** The game gives up the pixel-art identity that the references, studies and October 14–18 decisions were building toward. Style consistency now depends on shared generation and sourcing rules (shape, palette, materials, texture detail, lighting) rather than a pixel filter. Without them, assets from different sources can look mismatched. The rejection of low-poly, blocky, cute and big-head styles is unchanged, so "plain" does not permit a low-poly look.

**What stays.** Believable proportions, the rejected creator styles, the survival mood, foliage and detail preferences, Poly Haven as a source, and the non-pixel qualities of each reference carry forward. Every pixel-art study, comparison, research note and verification record stays preserved as evidence.

**Consequences and open work.**

- **The running game still looks pixelated** (screen grid, mercenary capture, held poses) until [AD01](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad01--switch-the-runtime-presentation-to-plain-3d) switches the presentation.
- **Pixel-specific tasks are retired** in the [3D pixel-art tracker](../../../docs/maintainers/3d-pixel-art.md). Its reusable pipeline and appearance-family scope awaits re-scoping under [AD02](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad02--re-scope-the-asset-pipeline-and-appearance-families-for-plain-3d).
- **Still open:** the shading approach, texture budgets and automated consistency rules for generated content ([AD03](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad03--choose-the-plain-3d-shading-and-consistency-rules)).

## October 31, 2026 — The running game switches to plain 3D

Later the same day, [AD01](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad01--switch-the-runtime-presentation-to-plain-3d) made plain 3D the game's default presentation. The screen-space pixel grid, finer small-subject grid, painted stone/timber cells, hard-edged texture enlargement and held 12-poses-per-second walking are off; the mercenary keeps a full-detail capture that is no longer pixelated. The pixel settings remain as developer comparisons until Mike decides whether to delete them. Low-resolution 2D pixel content such as sprite people and animals, the campfire, target and signposts still needs plain-3D replacements ([AD05](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad05--replace-the-remaining-2d-pixel-content-with-plain-3d-art)).

## October 31, 2026 — Pixel presentation code deleted

Mike asked for the pixel code to be deleted, provided a record of the look stays in S3. The study archive already holds it, including the final 30/60 pixel default as stills and a moving camp video. The screen grid, small-subject grid, painted surface cells, hard-edged texture filtering, held poses, effects buffer, the mercenary's character capture and the old block-mercenary renderer are gone from the game and its study pages. The mercenary now renders directly as a 3D model, like the other people. Details and remaining gaps are in [AD01](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad01--switch-the-runtime-presentation-to-plain-3d).

## October 31, 2026 — Canopy trees and carved crate become the defaults

Reviewing the plain-3D scene study, Mike preferred the "Open fir / varied greens" trees (adapted Quaternius Stylized Nature MegaKit vegetation plus Poly Haven's fir), the carved crate and diffuse leaf light. The first two are now the game's defaults; diffuse leaf light already was. The preference covers these adapted models and does not reverse the rejection of Quaternius's visibly low-poly look as the overall style. The carved crate is CC BY 4.0, so in-game artwork credits are tracked as [AD06](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad06--show-artwork-credits-in-the-game). His wind question is [AD07](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad07--wind-for-trees-bushes-and-grass). The [current visual requirements](../../decisions.md#current-visual-requirements--october-31-2026) record the preferences.

## October 31, 2026 — Quaternius's stylized MegaKit line is accepted

Asked whether liking the canopy trees conflicts with the rejection of Quaternius's low-poly look, Mike confirmed he likes these trees, which are adapted from Quaternius's textured *Stylized Nature MegaKit*. The [creator feedback](../../decisions.md#current-visual-requirements--october-31-2026) now rejects only Quaternius's flat, visibly low-poly packs. The general rejection of the visible low-poly aesthetic, blocky, cute and big-head styles is unchanged.

## November 1, 2026 — Light wind and the presentation defaults confirmed

Mike chose **light** as the default wind for trees, bushes and grass, and kept the presentation settings recommended after his October 31 review: four-sample edge smoothing, the steady soft-shadow sample pattern and the natural animal and prop sources. All were already the game's defaults, so nothing on screen changes. In a matched scene-study measurement, wind added no measurable GPU time ([AD07](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad07--wind-for-trees-bushes-and-grass)).

## November 1, 2026 — Grounded stylized direction adopted

**Decision.** From his picks in the world asset survey, Mike adopted a **grounded stylized** direction. Quaternius's stylized textured CC0 kits are the visual anchor for every category: props, nature, modular village buildings, base characters with outfits, and humanoid animation libraries. Nature is nudged toward natural growth. The [current visual requirements](../../decisions.md#current-visual-requirements--october-31-2026) and [creative brief](../../final-board/creative-brief.md) now state it.

**Why.** 55 of his 84 likes were stylized textured, including every one of the 36 stylized objects, and the stylized family was his best fit for ground plants. His notes asked for slightly less cartoony trees and bushes and rejected the kit's pines as "too cartoony like zelda"; he liked the scanned fir, dead trunks and shrubs. The kits are free, consistent and light: stylized objects average about 1,000 triangles against about 7,200 for the scanned ones. They cover objects, nature, buildings and people.

**Tradeoffs.** Poly Haven scans move from the default natural source to a supporting role. The kit's trees need tuning toward natural forms. No stylized animals exist, so animals need generation or commissioned models, and generating assets in volume is unproven until a measured pilot. Shading and environment light stay open ([AD03](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad03--choose-the-plain-3d-shading-and-consistency-rules)).

**Consequences.** The follow-through studies and the generation pilot are tracked as [AD09](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad09--follow-through-on-the-grounded-stylized-direction). The picks and the approved proposal are in the [survey record](../../studies/2026-10-31-world-asset-survey/README.md#unified-direction--approved-november-1-2026).

## November 1, 2026 — Thinner woods, and firelight shadows only as the sun goes down

Mike found the wooded areas a little dense, so the forest ring around the map now leaves about three in ten tree positions empty. Main has 526 trees instead of 719 ([landscape](../../../docs/worlds/base/landscape.md)). He also chose firelight shadows only while the sun is weak: they fade in through dusk and out after dawn, so they never switch on at once. By day the camp fire still glows and lights its surroundings, but people no longer cast a second shadow away from it ([lighting](../../../docs/world-presentation.md#sprite-lighting)). Both changes, with a closer zoom-out limit, make wide views cheaper to draw ([decision history](../../../docs/changelog/2026/2026-10-26.md)).

## November 1, 2026 — Whole buildings from the village kit approved

Mike approved the first whole-building study: a plaster cottage, a timber-framed house, a townhouse with a balcony and a stone tower, each assembled from the free _Medieval Village MegaKit_'s grid pieces ("The buildings look good"). Buildings in the grounded stylized direction are built from the kit's modular parts. The kit's stone corner posts and plank doors are heavy, up to about 50,000 triangles for the tower, so the game needs lighter versions of them before it uses these buildings ([AD09](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad09--follow-through-on-the-grounded-stylized-direction), [study](../../studies/2026-10-31-world-asset-survey/README.md#whole-buildings-from-the-village-kit--november-1-2026)).
