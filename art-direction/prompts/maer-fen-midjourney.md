# Maer Fen: Midjourney concept-art prompts

[Midjourney guide](../midjourney/README.md) · Written 2026-09-27 for Midjourney V8.2. Painted concept art, not pixel art, following the [creative brief](../final-board/creative-brief.md). Made with the same method as the [Halven Rusk prompts](halven-rusk-midjourney.md).

## 1. Canon

Two sources. **Mike's expanded description (2026-09-27)** is newer than the repository's [Threewater March proposal](../../docs/repertoires/worlds/medieval-survival.md#maer-fen-38--the-raider-who-calls-every-payment-a-debt) and wins where they differ.

**From Mike's description:**

- 38, **master of the Reed Knives**.
- He began moving untaxed cloth after a levy dispute destroyed his family's landing. He "learned customers, channels and the price of frightened silence," then killed the gang leader who tried to take his route.
- Over seven years the Reed Knives have **burned rival boats**, **sold captured travelers back to their kin** and **collected false tolls from three stretches of river**. He personally killed a surrendered informer in front of the gang.
- His grievance against the Compact is true. So is his decision to profit from other people's fear.
- He **keeps accounts**, **pays informants promptly**, and has twice survived betrayal by his lieutenants.
- His **younger cousin** believes the stories of his generosity tell the whole man, and Maer encourages that.
- He **races shallow boats** for pleasure, **dresses splendidly on feast days**, and once paid for the funeral of a washerwoman who had hidden him. Sela's remembered kindness may protect her boat.
- Players might meet him at a **fishing supper while stolen convoy goods are priced in the next room**.
- He wants the **winter storehouses**, the **cargo's silver**, and recognition as a power that must be bargained with.

**From the repository proposal (not contradicted):** he fights from prepared positions with a **bow and hooked blade**; the stolen convoy's **toll seal** lets the thieves pose as lawful collectors; his refuge is the ruined storehouses on the Old Reed Road's raised causeways.

**Visual hook:** a gracious, generous host whose hospitality sits one doorway away from the price of fear. The images should read charming first and dangerous second. He has two faces: plain river-working kit, and splendid feast-day dress.

## 2. Invented for these prompts (not canon)

His face, skin, hair, beard and earring; every colour and garment except the bow and hooked blade; the feast-day coat; the bronze seal; all six scenes. Showing the hooked blade as a reed-cutter's tool is an interpretation of his origin, not canon.

**Identity anchor (repeated in every prompt):** "Compact and wiry, weathered olive-brown skin, shaved head, neat black mustache and short pointed chin beard flecked with grey, heavy-lidded patient eyes, a small gold ring in his left ear."

He's deliberately distinct from Halven (lean and rangy, close-cropped hair, short beard) so the two read differently side by side.

**Working kit (repeated):** "dark oiled-leather jerkin over an indigo-grey wool tunic, reed-green hooded wool cloak, a reed-cutter's hooked blade at his hip, a short hunting bow."

**Feast-day dress (prompt 5):** "an oxblood-red wool coat trimmed with otter fur, a silver-studded belt, rings on his fingers."

## 3. Five images, five jobs, plus one for 3D

| # | Shot | Medium | Question it answers | Parameters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Design sheet | Gouache and graphite on toned paper | What exactly does he wear and carry? | `--ar 3:2 --raw --s 75` |
| 2 | Portrait | Oil paint | How does he come across when you meet him? | `--ar 4:5 --s 250` |
| 3 | Story keyframe | Painterly digital | How does he keep his power? | `--ar 21:9 --s 300 --chaos 15` |
| 4 | Environment + character | Painterly digital | How does the gang make its money, and where? | `--ar 16:9 --s 250 --chaos 10` |
| 5 | Full-body hero | Stylized painterly | The public face his cousin believes in | `--ar 2:3 --s 400 --exp 15` |
| 6 | 3D reference (optional) | Gouache, plain background | A front T-pose view for cleanup and image-to-3D | `--ar 3:2 --raw --s 50` |

### 1 · Character design sheet: the reference

```
Character design sheet for an original medieval adventure game: a river smuggler chief in his late thirties shown in front, three-quarter and back views, relaxed neutral stance, on warm toned paper. Compact and wiry, weathered olive-brown skin, shaved head, neat black mustache and short pointed chin beard flecked with grey, heavy-lidded patient eyes, a small gold ring in his left ear. Dark oiled-leather jerkin over an indigo-grey wool tunic, reed-green hooded wool cloak, wide belt with a leather purse and a wax-tablet ledger case, a reed-cutter's hooked blade at his hip, a short hunting bow across his back, soft boots stained with marsh mud. Small side studies of the hooked blade, the ledger case and a stolen bronze toll seal. Gouache and graphite, natural proportions, earthy palette of reed green, peat brown, river grey and oiled leather --ar 3:2 --raw --s 75 --v 8.2 --no text, watermark, signature
```

**Why:** the standard sheet recipe ([guide 06](../midjourney/06-game-concept-art.md#character-design-sheet--turnaround)). The side studies pin down his three story objects: the blade (violence), the ledger (accounts) and the seal (false tolls). **Watch for:** the hooked blade coming out as a fantasy sickle or scythe; the three views not matching; the ledger case sprouting written text.

### 2 · The fishing supper: meeting him

```
Painted half-length portrait of a river smuggler chief in his late thirties hosting a fishing supper in a reed-thatched boathouse, leaning back at a plank table and pouring ale for the viewer with a warm, unhurried smile that stops short of his eyes. Compact and wiry, weathered olive-brown skin, shaved head, neat black mustache and short pointed chin beard flecked with grey, heavy-lidded patient eyes, a small gold ring in his left ear. Oiled-leather jerkin, rings on two fingers, a reed-cutter's hooked blade laid casually beside a platter of grilled eels and flatbread. Through a doorway behind him, in lamplight, two of his men weigh stolen silver and iron bars on a merchant's scale beside open crates. Warm firelight on his face, cool blue dusk at the reed-screen walls. Rich visible oil-paint brushwork, character concept art for a medieval game --ar 4:5 --s 250 --v 8.2
```

**Why:** this is Mike's suggested first meeting. It puts the player at his table, with hospitality in front and the stolen convoy goods being priced through the doorway behind. The blade next to the food carries the threat without violence on screen. Firelight on his face against cool dusk is the brief's warm/cool light. **Watch for:** the back room getting lost or competing with him (reroll, or strengthen it with the Editor); a cartoon-villain sneer instead of a warm smile. If the smile looks menacing, change "stops short of his eyes" to "genuinely warm".

### 3 · Burning the rival boats: how he keeps power

```
Cinematic story keyframe for a medieval survival game: a smuggler chief watches a rival's boats burn. At night on a wide reed marsh, three flat-bottomed boats blaze at a wooden landing, their flames doubled in the black water, sparks lifting into the smoke. In the foreground he sits calmly in the stern of a narrow shallow boat while two of his crew pole it away through the reeds. He is compact and wiry, shaved head, neat black mustache and short pointed chin beard, weathered olive-brown face lit orange from one side, hooded reed-green cloak, a hunting bow across his knees. Painterly digital concept art, deep blue-black night against roaring orange fire, a mood of cold, practiced control --ar 21:9 --s 300 --chaos 15 --v 8.2
```

**Why:** a canon act ("burned rival boats") that shows method and temperament without depicting killing. Fire reflected on water is the brief's light-and-material quality. His stillness against the blaze says "practiced". **Watch for:** the fire washing out his face; the figures merging in the dark (reroll); an over-dramatic "evil lord" pose. Keep him seated and calm.

### 4 · The false toll: the business

```
Foreground: at a narrow reed channel at dawn, a smuggler chief stands on a plank landing beside a chain stretched across the water, checking a wax-tablet ledger while a stopped trader's barge waits, its owner holding out a purse with both hands. Two of his crew stand ready: one with a drawn hunting bow, one pressing a stolen bronze toll seal into wax. He wears an oiled-leather jerkin and reed-green hooded cloak, a reed-cutter's hooked blade at his hip; compact and wiry, shaved head, neat black mustache and short pointed chin beard. Background: reed marsh, a flooded willow grove, cranes lifting through low mist, a ruined stone storehouse on a raised causeway fading into haze. Pale gold dawn light and cool mist. Painterly environment-and-character concept art for a medieval adventure game --ar 16:9 --s 250 --chaos 10 --v 8.2
```

**Why:** it shows the everyday racket: false tolls, the stolen seal, the accounts. The ruined storehouse he wants sits in the background. The crew is kept to two, and the Foreground/Background labels help with the several-figure layout ([guide 02](../midjourney/02-writing-prompts.md#several-people-in-one-image)). **Watch for:** the chain vanishing or turning into a bridge; readable text on the ledger; too many extra crew. `--no text` helps if the wax tablet sprouts words.

### 5 · Feast-day race: the man his cousin sees

```
Full-body hero illustration of a river smuggler chief standing in the prow of a narrow racing boat on a festival day, one foot up on the gunwale, arms spread, laughing as his crew drive the paddles past cheering crowds on the bank. He is dressed splendidly: an oxblood-red wool coat trimmed with otter fur, a silver-studded belt, rings flashing on his fingers, the reed-cutter's hooked blade still at his hip. Compact and wiry, weathered olive-brown skin, shaved head, neat black mustache and short pointed chin beard flecked with grey, a small gold ring in his left ear. On the bank, a young cousin waves at him in open admiration beneath strings of bunting. Golden afternoon light glittering on the river, reed-thatched roofs beyond. Stylized painterly game art, bold clean silhouette, strong color grouping, visible brush texture, natural proportions --ar 2:3 --s 400 --exp 15 --v 8.2
```

**Why:** three canon pleasures in one image (boat racing, splendid feast-day dress, the adoring cousin), in the stylized finish the brief welcomes. The blade still at his hip is the one reminder. **Watch for:** the cousin getting lost in the crowd (acceptable, or cut that sentence); the coat turning into fantasy royalty (add `--no crown, filigree`); exaggerated proportions.

### 6 · Optional: front T-pose reference for 3D

For the workflow of cleaning a concept into a T-pose and building the model in an image-to-3D tool:

```
Full-body character reference of a river smuggler chief in his late thirties, front view, standing in a T-pose with arms straight out to the sides and feet shoulder-width apart, facing the viewer, on a plain light grey background. Compact and wiry, weathered olive-brown skin, shaved head, neat black mustache and short pointed chin beard flecked with grey, a small gold ring in his left ear. Dark oiled-leather jerkin over an indigo-grey wool tunic, reed-green hooded cloak thrown back over his shoulders, wide belt with a leather purse, a reed-cutter's hooked blade at his hip, soft leather boots. Even soft lighting without cast shadows, clean readable shapes and flat colour regions, gouache concept art, natural proportions --ar 3:2 --raw --s 50 --v 8.2 --no text, watermark, shadows
```

**Why:** a plain background, even light, a front view and a symmetrical pose make cleanup and 3D reconstruction easier ([guide 06](../midjourney/06-game-concept-art.md#concepts-for-the-3d-pixel-art-pipeline)). The bow is left off because it complicates the silhouette; add it as a separate prop. **Watch for:** Midjourney drifting into an A-pose or a hero stance (use the Edit model with an instruction to straighten the arms); the cloak hiding the arms.

## 4. After Mike picks

1. **Choose the defining image** (usually 1 or 2) and record its prompt, seed and link ([prompt log](../midjourney/05-workflow-and-troubleshooting.md#keep-a-prompt-log)).
2. **Rebuild the sheet around it** with the Edit model: the chosen image as a reference, prompt 1 as the instruction ("the same man as in the reference, shown in front, three-quarter and back views …").
3. **Crop references:** face, full-body front, hooked blade, feast-day coat.
4. **Re-run the scenes** with the Edit model and those references, adding `--sref` from the chosen painting (`--sw 100–200`) so the set matches Halven's series ([references and consistency](../midjourney/04-references-and-consistency.md#the-edit-model-v8x)).
5. **Update the anchor** in this file if the chosen face differs, and note the decision in [`art-direction/decisions.md`](../decisions.md).

## 5. Other ways to explore

- **Change the look:** swap the anchor in every prompt (for example "tall and heavy-shouldered, ruddy freckled skin, long rust-red hair tied back, clean-shaven, a crooked broken nose").
- **The ransom exchange** (canon): "at a reed-marsh landing at dusk, a family hands a purse to a smuggler chief while two bound travelers wait between his men" as a keyframe (`--ar 16:9`).
- **The washerwoman's funeral** (canon): him standing apart at a riverside burial, paying the shrine keeper. A quiet portrait of the generosity his cousin believes in.
- **Sela's boat** (canon): him waving her ferry through a toll chain while stopping the passenger behind her.
- **Test the medium:** `{gouache and graphite, oil painting, ink and wash}` as a permutation on prompt 2.

## If results go wrong

See the guide's [troubleshooting table](../midjourney/05-workflow-and-troubleshooting.md#troubleshooting). The likely fixes here:

- Fantasy villain costume → name the period cloth and leather again; `--no pauldrons, filigree, runes, crown`.
- Night scenes too dark → "his face lit orange from one side"; raise `--s` slightly.
- Too stylized → `--raw` or a lower `--s`.
- Face drifting between images → the Edit model with reference crops.
