# Open Legend — a first visual pass

Twelve contrasting references from the [full 48-reference board](index.html). React to individual qualities: lighting, trees, palette, character proportions, texture, mood. These selections are a viewing route, not confirmed design choices. Images require an internet connection.

## G01 — Octopath Traveler II

Warm fire, cool night, miniature people. Campfire light makes a small group in a huge wilderness feel intimate.

![Octopath Traveler II](https://gh.cdn.sewest.net/assets/ident/games/8bd47aa2/3-OPII_partyofheightaroundafirecamp-upoy5vqh9.jpg?quality=65)

How much glow and background blur feels right?

[Image source](https://www.square-enix-games.com/en_GB/games/octopath-traveler-ii)

## G07 — Songs of Conquest

Dense forests and readable world objects. Study the relationship between little people, large trees, and landmarks.

![Songs of Conquest](https://www.pcgames.de/screenshots/1280x1024/2022/05/SongsOfConquest-04-pc-games.jpg)

Rich enough to explore, or too visually busy?

[Image source](https://www.pcgames.de/Songs-of-Conquest-Spiel-67705/Specials/Early-Access-Check-Strategie-Rollenspiel-1394819/)

## G08 — Drova – Forsaken Kin

Earthy vegetation and less exaggerated people. Study rough terrain, belongings, and a survival setting.

![Drova – Forsaken Kin](https://cdn.releases.com/img/image/b76bd1dd-b57c-4a9f-9c3d-14d73ba978b6.jpg/800)

Do these human proportions feel closer to Open Legend?

[Image source](https://www.releases.com/p/drova-forsaken-kin)

## G09 — Stoneshard

Crisp objects and fine material detail. Study how plants, rocks, clothing, and tools stay distinguishable.

![Stoneshard](https://cdn.cloudflare.steamstatic.com/steam/apps/625960/ss_564bcfdeb7509223009a669b5264cbf077880067.1920x1080.jpg?t=1644319159)

Do you like this pixel density and object scale?

[Image source](https://store.steampowered.com/app/625960/Stoneshard/)

## G12 — Eastward

Lush clutter and a world with history. Props, overgrowth, and worn surfaces imply lives beyond the camera.

![Eastward](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/FjCGBKPmgyXH9rxphyywqQ.jpeg)

How much everyday clutter should our world contain?

[Image source](https://www.gamesradar.com/eastward-review/)

## G13 — Sea of Stars

Luminous water, lush greenery, clear paths. Study separation between traversable ground and decorative detail.

![Sea of Stars](https://cdn.staticneo.com/ew/9/94/Ss_227.jpg)

Is this saturation inviting or too polished for survival?

[Image source](https://www.neoseeker.com/sea-of-stars/walkthrough/Jungle_Path)

## G17 — Hyper Light Drifter

Turquoise, magenta, and large graphic shapes. World identity can come from palette and silhouette.
