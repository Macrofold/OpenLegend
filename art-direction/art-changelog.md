# Art direction changelog

Changes to the game's art direction: what changed, why, the tradeoffs and where the current guidance lives. The [current visual requirements](decisions.md#current-visual-requirements--october-31-2026) and [creative brief](final-board/creative-brief.md) own today's direction; this changelog is history, not a specification.

Browse [2026](changelog/2026/). New files use the Monday starting each week, `YYYY-MM-DD.md`, matching the [documentation changelog](../docs/documentation-changelog.md); create only weeks with meaningful entries. Record direction changes, accepted or rejected styles and consequential art decisions, not every study round or asset revision.

Art decisions before this changelog existed are recorded in [decisions.md](decisions.md), the [documentation changelog](../docs/documentation-changelog.md) and the [study archive](studies/README.md). They are not copied here.
