## Construction evidence and next decision

The body used `MHP_OrlandoFit02` with only seven active controls: height 203.5 cm, chest 127 cm, waist 97 cm, neck 46 cm, across shoulders 43 cm, muscularity 1.4 and fat −0.6. Muscularity/fat are native unitless controls. The leaner targets were chest 119, waist 93, neck 44, shoulders 41.5 cm and muscularity 0.85. These are requested constraints, not concept-derived or achieved circumference measurements. Other anatomy inherited the preset; no source-specific torso-depth, taper, limb-profile or head/body-ratio targets were recorded.

`native/face01/plan.json` in the retained working study contains one call's 32 indexed landmark displacement vectors, not 32 measured source correspondences. The controls affect brows, nose, cheeks, jaw/chin and ears; several jaw-point offsets request 2.4–3.1 mm outward per side. The plan has no anatomical labels or target image locations. Front/profile/quarter comparison renders and projected handle locations existed, but no source-mesh conform or iterative anatomical likeness solve is recorded. Later passes mainly change pigment, hair and materials; face-specific elapsed effort is unknown.

The inner coat, mail, trousers, helmet and brows were constructed procedurally. Cape/boot source geometry and artwork were substantially retained. Stock MetaHuman hair/beard cards were fitted, then altered with a synthetic fibre atlas and authored beard additions. No new Meshy/Higgsfield generation occurred. Detailed scripts and successful fitting did not preserve the concept's visible design. The displayed original is itself a prepared derivative; diagnose its geometry, normals and UV/texture history before attributing every facial defect to generation.
