## Rejected trials retained

01/03/04 show the failed legacy deer material conversions; 05 restores the albedo. 02 is the sparse first berry candidate. 06 is the rejected wispy crown before fuller whole-leaf selection. 07 retains the source's four-plant row, rejected as a game bush. 08/09 show pale leaf color in pixel/smooth rendering before woodland greens. 13 is the rejected rigid box pack. 14 records the invisible pack caused by an invalid UV binding; 32 shows its corrected textured export. None of these files was overwritten to hide an unsuccessful iteration.

All original models, their editable alternatives and older capture archives remain available. The shared tree control preserves original high-detail geometry; it is intentionally unsuitable for ordinary gameplay cost. The selected vegetation uses real gaps and prepared source geometry. Fine distant edges can still change coverage during movement; these images are not a universal stability guarantee.
