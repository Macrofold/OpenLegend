Natural forms, dimensional pixels

The remaining current Quaternius selections now use more natural source models. Poly Haven supplies most vegetation and props; credited sources fill the animal, archery and pack gaps. The 30/60 pixel density, original mercenary and world dimensions remain.

Three finishes and original controls are preserved for each new family. These are working selections for feedback. The earlier bear replacement and broader production qualification remain open.
