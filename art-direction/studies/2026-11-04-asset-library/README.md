# Asset library — November 4, 2026

## Brief

- **Purpose:** Mike asked for a large survey of the game assets the repertoires call for, free and paid, with every free asset that can be downloaded added to the study, each marked as a sure fit for his taste or on the fence, and the library kept in S3. He is willing to pay, so paid packs that fit are listed with prices. Later the same day he asked for more weapons in the stylized textured look ([Weapons round](#weapons-round--november-4-2026)), then to finish the queued downloads ([Queue completed](#queue-completed--november-4-2026)), to replace the game's low-poly stand-ins with library pieces, and for the comparisons the library can supply ([Comparisons](#comparisons--november-4-2026)).
- **Flow and finish:** Standard flow, general-style profile, finishing at **study**. Presence here is not approval for the game.
- **Viewing conditions:** the same page and renderer as the [world asset survey](../2026-10-31-world-asset-survey/README.md): every piece at real size under the same light on a neutral metre-scale ground.
- **Selection authority:** Mike. The marks are the agent's judgement of his taste, from the decisions he has recorded, not a decision.
- **Budget:** no paid work. Nothing was bought; research used no provider account.
- **State:** prepared and reviewed in the local study viewer. **Not yet archived:** the files exist only in the external workspace below ([Resume](#resume)). The queue is fetched; two free finds state no licence ([Pending downloads](#pending-downloads)). All nine of the game's stand-ins have replacements chosen from Mike's comparison picks ([Comparisons](#comparisons--november-4-2026)); five need publishing before the running game shows them. Visibility: public.

- **On the fence (64):** the strongest leads per area are WildMesh 3D's _Ultimate 3D Animal Pack_ (100+ animals, $39.99, realistic coats), Silver Delivery's Arcane-painted spruce and birch forests ($8.99 each), Cratial 3D's species-accurate biome and village kits, Holotna's cabins, fort and camp, Nico Hartl's painted village and tavern kits, LapaModels' 400-piece castle ($14.99), Lore-3D's armory ($249.99), Kevin Iglesias's animation packs and, for weapons, Daniel Gigena's _Stylized Medieval Weapon Set_ ($2 or more) and MaxHit Games' _Stylized Medieval Weapon Pack_ ($10 or more). The Quaternius _Fantasy Props MegaKit_ Pro tier, already a sure fit, adds the anchor's own extra weapons and shields.
- **Licence decision (Mike, November 5, 2026): open licences only.** The game uses CC0 and CC BY art. Store licences (Fab, Unity Asset Store, CGTrader) and most creators' own terms forbid sharing the files, and the stores also require stopping players from extracting them; a browser game hands every model to the player as a file, and OpenLegend's game art sits at public addresses, so those packs stay out of the game. Sketchfab's free models each carry their creator's licence: the library takes only CC0 and CC BY ones. CC BY-SA would also be open (changed copies must keep the same licence); non-commercial, no-derivatives and Sketchfab's own Standard licences are not. Epic is folding Sketchfab into its Fab store, which keeps CC BY models but not CC0 ones, and Sketchfab is expected to stop offering downloads, so this record's copies of its downloads are worth archiving.
- Prices are as listed on November 4, 2026; several itch.io prices are a Halloween sale (the spruce and birch forests were back to $8.99 that evening), and Fab prices could not be read automatically.

## Construction and sources

- **Downloads (November 4, 2026):** the Quaternius free tiers, loafbrr's kit and Gruginski's village kit through their itch.io pages' free downloads; 57 models through a signed-in Sketchfab session as auto-converted GLBs with textures up to 1024 px; 274 models and 471 texture sets through Poly Haven's public API (with a User-Agent naming OpenLegend, as its terms ask, four requests at a time); 433 ambientCG 1K JPG archives one at a time; 17 OpenGameArt archives from their pages' file lists; Gruginski's _Medieval Weapons & Items_ and _Medieval Humans, Orcs & Goblins_ (FBX versions) and Syntaxes of Play's _Medieval Café_ from their itch.io pages' free downloads; the 48 queued Sketchfab models ([Queue completed](#queue-completed--november-4-2026)). The survey's own downloads supply the anchor MegaKits and _Fantasy Creatures_. About 9.5 GB in all.
- **Recipe:** [scripts/prepare-asset-library.ts](../../../scripts/prepare-asset-library.ts) prepares whole packs from the plan. Name rules, tried first against the start of a file's name, then anywhere in it, then its folders, file each piece under a subject. Pieces keep their makers' size unless the survey chose sizes for the same kit and subject (then its median scale), the plan sets a pack's unit (24 Sketchfab weapons, authored at sizes from a 7 cm knife to an 11 m spear, are set to real lengths), or their largest dimension is more than five times outside the subject's range (then a power of ten). Files holding a whole set (Sketchfab packs, the mine kit) are split into one piece per object. Textures are embedded and reduced to 1024 px as in the survey; the mine kit's separately shipped textures and the village kit's Godot materials are attached by material name. The shared model helpers live in [scripts/model-files.ts](../../../scripts/model-files.ts). Packs that ship only Blender or FBX files (the `oga-*` weapons, Gruginski's weapons and items and his goblins, and the café) are first converted to GLB in each pack's `converted` folder by [scripts/convert-to-gltf.py](../../../scripts/convert-to-gltf.py) in Blender 5.2, with the files' embedded scripts disabled and no image read from outside the pack; naming a folder inside a pack's `extracted` copy converts only that part. A plan texture set can be limited to some files of a pack (`file`), for kits that reuse one material name over several atlases; a plan `tints` list gives a material a pack colours per item at run time its own colour; and `excludePieces` also leaves parts out of a model shown whole, such as the ground it stands on.
- **Left out:** pieces the survey already shows, Poly Haven's modern and industrial items, the village kit's terrain chunks, test pieces and hundreds of connectivity-coded ground and cave tiles, lower levels of detail, the ooze (a custom jelly shader) and one Poly Haven model over 150,000 triangles. The catalog's `skipped` list records each with its reason.
