# Asset library — November 4, 2026

## Brief

- **Purpose:** Mike asked for a large survey of the game assets the repertoires call for, free and paid, with every free asset that can be downloaded added to the study, each marked as a sure fit for his taste or on the fence, and the library kept in S3. He is willing to pay, so paid packs that fit are listed with prices. Later the same day he asked for more weapons in the stylized textured look ([Weapons round](#weapons-round--november-4-2026)).
- **Flow and finish:** Standard flow, general-style profile, finishing at **study**. Presence here is not approval for the game.
- **Viewing conditions:** the same page and renderer as the [world asset survey](../2026-10-31-world-asset-survey/README.md): every piece at real size under the same light on a neutral metre-scale ground.
- **Selection authority:** Mike. The marks are the agent's judgement of his taste, from the decisions he has recorded, not a decision.
- **Budget:** no paid work. Nothing was bought; research used no provider account.
- **State:** prepared and reviewed in the local study viewer. **Not yet archived:** the files exist only in the external workspace below ([Resume](#resume)). 48 Sketchfab models remain queued, and two free finds state no licence ([Pending downloads](#pending-downloads)).

## The marks

- **Sure fit:** only sets Mike has already liked or approved: the Quaternius anchor line (he liked every stylized object shown, approved the village-kit buildings and liked the nature kit's broadleaf trees, shrubs and ground plants), the people, outfits and animation libraries made for it, the rest of _Fantasy Creatures_ (his second choice for animals) and Poly Haven's scanned conifers, deadwood, shrubs and ferns, which he liked.
- **On the fence:** everything else, each with a one-line reason on its card: the nature kit's pines ("too cartoony like zelda"), twisted and dead trees and rocks (no rock has a like yet), the free Superhero body build (the Regular build with natural proportions is paid), every photographic piece and material, and every maker Mike has not seen. Spot checks in the page: the village kit's forge and props are chunky painted stone, nearer a toy look than the anchor; the hand-painted workshop tools have natural proportions and painted wood and metal.

## Construction and sources

- **Downloads (November 4, 2026):** the Quaternius free tiers, loafbrr's kit and Gruginski's village kit through their itch.io pages' free downloads; 57 models through a signed-in Sketchfab session as auto-converted GLBs with textures up to 1024 px; 274 models and 471 texture sets through Poly Haven's public API (with a User-Agent naming OpenLegend, as its terms ask, four requests at a time); 433 ambientCG 1K JPG archives one at a time; 17 OpenGameArt archives from their pages' file lists; Gruginski's _Medieval Weapons & Items_ (FBX version) from its itch.io page's free download. The survey's own downloads supply the anchor MegaKits and _Fantasy Creatures_. About 8.4 GB in all.
- **Recipe:** [scripts/prepare-asset-library.ts](../../../scripts/prepare-asset-library.ts) prepares whole packs from the plan. Name rules, tried first against the start of a file's name, then anywhere in it, then its folders, file each piece under a subject. Pieces keep their makers' size unless the survey chose sizes for the same kit and subject (then its median scale), the plan sets a pack's unit (24 Sketchfab weapons, authored at sizes from a 7 cm knife to an 11 m spear, are set to real lengths), or their largest dimension is more than five times outside the subject's range (then a power of ten). Files holding a whole set (Sketchfab packs, the mine kit) are split into one piece per object. Textures are embedded and reduced to 1024 px as in the survey; the mine kit's separately shipped textures and the village kit's Godot materials are attached by material name. The shared model helpers live in [scripts/model-files.ts](../../../scripts/model-files.ts). Packs that ship only Blender or FBX files (the `oga-*` weapons and Gruginski's weapons and items) are first converted to GLB in each pack's `converted` folder by [scripts/convert-to-gltf.py](../../../scripts/convert-to-gltf.py) in Blender 5.2, with the files' embedded scripts disabled and no image read from outside the pack. A plan texture set can be limited to some files of a pack (`file`), for kits that reuse one material name over several atlases.
- **Left out:** pieces the survey already shows, Poly Haven's modern and industrial items, the village kit's terrain chunks, test pieces and hundreds of connectivity-coded ground and cave tiles, lower levels of detail, the ooze (a custom jelly shader) and one Poly Haven model over 150,000 triangles. The catalog's `skipped` list records each with its reason.

## Weapons round — November 4, 2026

Mike asked for more weapons in the stylized textured look. Sketchfab's public search, run for 72 weapon and armour terms over downloadable CC BY and CC0 models, returned 1,541 models; leaving out low-poly, cartoon, sci-fi and firearm titles and keeping names that are weapons left 442, whose pictures were compared by eye in two passes (all 442, then 92 close-ups). itch.io and OpenGameArt were searched for free packs with clear licences, and the stores for paid ones.

- **Kept (38 painted, 4 realistic):** painted wood and iron with natural proportions, matte enough to hold up without sky light: 18 shields (round, kite and heater shields, a crusader shield, painted heraldry), axes and hatchets, maces, a morning star, a greatsword and war hammer, swords, spears, a javelin, a self bow, a horned helmet, a weapon rack, and a weapon seller's cart and stall. Jack Bronswijk's _Rusty Bandit Weapon Kit_ shares one texture across eight weapons, as the anchor's props share texture sets. Gintoki1234's five ten-piece sets (maces and hammers, polearms, swords, shields, axes) came from the queue and are realistic.
- **Left out:** glowing, crystal and demonic fantasy blades; toy-like stylized hammers and shields; WoW-style and Zelda or Marvel shields; low-poly, voxel and pixel packs (binbun3d's _RPG Weapons_, Cethiel's 3D weapons and others); sci-fi; and scenes such as weapons on a table.
- **Marks:** all on the fence, since Mike has seen none of these makers. The closest to the anchor are CGArtem's and Kirhl's round shields, patroo's kite shield and sword, kozachoks' and -wMw-'s round shields, rudolfs' hand-painted _Weapon set_ and Miguel Fonseca's bearded axe.
- **OpenGameArt, converted from Blender (November 4, 2026, after Mike approved installing Blender):** Price's twelve CC0 hand-painted weapons (axes, maces, swords and a cleaver, one axe in four paints), Wolfgang Wozniak's poleaxe and round shield and Ulf's bearded axe in new, used and old finishes (both CC BY 3.0): 20 pieces, among the closest free weapons to the anchor's painted look. Saved by Blender 2.69–2.79, their materials kept textures in a form current Blender drops, so [scripts/convert-to-gltf.py](../../../scripts/convert-to-gltf.py) reconnects the colour map (and normal map) shipped beside each file.
- **Gruginski's _Medieval Weapons & Items_ (CC0, converted from FBX after Mike asked for it):** 40 weapons, 6 shields, 15 pieces of armour laid on the ground and 101 items (potions, herbs, food, jewellery, tomes and scrolls, gold, crafting materials and creature parts): 162 pieces in metres, made for a top-down camera. Like _Fantasy Creatures_, the maker built them with AI-written Blender scripts. The 24 glowing "rune" copies are left out, since the conversion keeps no glow; the weapon and shield atlases and ten shared kit textures, which the FBX files do not name, are attached from the plan, following the pack's own material list. Potions, seals and gems show the pack's red placeholder tint; their own colours are in its `items.json`.
- **Fixes in the plan:** sets whose objects are parts or unnamed shapes (an axe in a stump, a sword and shield, three heraldic shields, a knife and sheath) are shown whole; the hatchet leaves out the disc of ice it stands on; 24 packs set a unit for real size; sheaths and scabbards file with blades. jellystuff's _Stylized Axe_ shows in flat colour because Sketchfab's converted file has no texture.

## Review and limits

**Checked in the local viewer:** the weapon, shield and armour subjects list the new pieces at real size (shields about 0.6–1 m, one-handed axes 0.5–0.9 m, spears 1.4–2.2 m), with cards, marks and credits; close views of the painted shields, the bearded axe and Price's converted swords show their painted textures; each converted file carries the one colour map shipped beside it (the Viking axe also its normal map); Gruginski's swords and kite shield show their atlases at real size (arming sword 0.88 m, halberd 2.09 m, kite shield 0.94 m). The new categories and subjects load; library pieces render textured and stand on the ground at their recorded sizes, including split pieces, the palm set at its corrected size, the mine kit's attached textures, the village kit's Godot materials, Quaternius outfits and the Fantasy Creatures coats; marks, reasons, paging and the paid panel work. Not checked: every one of the 2,807 pieces individually, keyboard-only and screen-reader use beyond native controls, phone width, and memory across a full page of 36 photo-material swatches.

**Limits:** sizes are the makers' except where calibrated or corrected, so some pieces may still read too large or small; split sets take their pieces' names from the objects in the file, which are sometimes generic ("Tool 11"), and pieces that share a pack and a name are numbered ("Lean to 1", "Lean to 2"); translucent wings render opaque; animated pieces stand in the first frame of a standing clip; style marks for unseen makers come from listings and spot checks, not a review of every piece.
