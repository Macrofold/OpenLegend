# World asset survey — October 31, 2026

## Brief

- **Purpose:** Mike wants to start filling the world with more things. He asked for a comprehensive study panel of objects (weapons, items, crates), environment (trees, grass, rocks, terrain, water), animals (deer, bears and others) and buildings (walls, floors and their parts), with varied styles, textures and models. He will mark what he likes; a unified art direction will then be derived from his picks.
- **Flow and finish:** Standard flow, general-style profile, finishing at **study**. The survey's own files are for study only; the game uses separately prepared versions of the animals Mike chose on November 3 ([verdict](#mikes-animal-verdict--november-3-2026)).
- **Viewing conditions:** every piece at real size, under the same light, on a neutral metre-scale ground, in the existing plain-3D renderer (the same material preparation, foliage light and optional soft light bands as the game).
- **Selection authority:** Mike. The page's family labels describe style, not a recommendation.
- **Budget:** no paid work. Every piece is a free CC0 or CC BY model or texture; no generation service was used.
- **State:** created and reviewed in the local study viewer. **Not yet archived:** the prepared files exist only in the external workspace below (see [Resume](#resume)). Mike's picks are recorded below, and he approved the resulting [unified direction](#unified-direction--approved-november-1-2026) on November 1, 2026. Four [whole buildings assembled from the village kit](#whole-buildings-from-the-village-kit--november-1-2026) were added the same day, and he approved them. The 12 animals of Quaternius's [animated animals pack](#animated-animals-pack--november-2-2026), which Mike downloaded, were added on November 2. A November 3 [search for free animals in the direction](#free-animal-sources--november-3-2026) found two candidate sets. Ten animals from the CC0 set, [_Fantasy Creatures_](#fantasy-creatures-in-the-survey--november-3-2026), and the seven painted animals of the CC BY set, [WildMesh 3D's](#wildmesh-3d-in-the-survey--november-3-2026), were added the same day. Mike then [ranked them](#mikes-animal-verdict--november-3-2026): WildMesh 3D first, _Fantasy Creatures_ second and the current game picks third, with every low-poly family ruled out for animals. The game now shows WildMesh 3D's doe, stag and brown bear and the _Fantasy Creatures_ wolf, once their files are published.

## Using the page

Choose a category and subject, or switch **Group** to one family across a whole category to judge a family's coherence. Drag to orbit, shift-drag to pan, scroll to zoom, click a piece to select its card and double-click (or **Focus**) to frame it. **Light**, **Shading** (natural or the game's soft bands) and **Reflections** apply to everything. Each card has **Like** and a note; each family block has **Best fit** for the subject. Picks stay in the browser (`localStorage`); **Copy picks as text** produces the summary to paste back into the conversation.

## Construction and sources

- **Downloads (October 31, 2026):** 34 archives (Kenney and Quaternius packs from their OpenGameArt pages, KayKit from its GitHub repositories at pinned commits, two community animal packs) and 95 Poly Haven assets (69 models as 1k glTF, 26 textures as 1k JPG). [sources.json](sources.json) records every archive and file URL, size and SHA-256, so the survey can be rebuilt from upstream. Kenney's own site refused scripted downloads; the OpenGameArt copies are Kenney's published CC0 archives. A 35th archive, Quaternius's _Ultimate Animated Animals_, was not on OpenGameArt or itch.io, and its Google Drive folder refused scripted downloads with "Quota exceeded", so Mike downloaded it on November 1. Drive builds a folder archive on request, so a new download can differ byte for byte from the recorded one. A 36th, the Godot version of algumacoisaqq's _Fantasy Creatures_, came from its itch.io page on November 3, and WildMesh 3D's seven animals came from Sketchfab the same day, downloaded while signed in to Mike's account because Sketchfab allows downloads only to signed-in accounts.
- **Recipe:** [scripts/prepare-asset-survey.ts](../../../scripts/prepare-asset-survey.ts) holds the plan (families, subjects, chosen models and real sizes) and writes one GLB per model into the external workspace, plus the catalog. Geometry, materials and textures are copied unchanged except that external buffers and images are embedded; images over 1024 px are reduced to 1024 px with macOS `sips`, so a lineup of MegaKit trim-sheet models fits in GPU memory; Kenney Nature Kit's unlit flag is removed so every family is lit alike; OBJ-only packs are converted with their material colours (and, for Quaternius's textured trees, the textures their materials name only by convention); _Fantasy Creatures_' untextured models get the coat material their Godot scenes apply ([below](#fantasy-creatures-in-the-survey--november-3-2026)); and WildMesh 3D's animals are shown in the first frame of their standing idle clip ([below](#wildmesh-3d-in-the-survey--november-3-2026)). Sizes are measured in the pose the page draws, so a skinned model is measured through its skeleton. Embedded images are named by content, so the page shares one texture among models that embed the same trim sheet: 36 MegaKit objects use 94 MB of texture memory instead of 1,033 MB. Its assembly plan also builds whole buildings from the village kit's parts ([below](#whole-buildings-from-the-village-kit--november-1-2026)).
- **Scale:** each piece is scaled to a stated real size along its subject's axis (height, ground length or longest extent). Existing OpenLegend study exports are already in metres and keep their own scale.
- **Exclusions:** Poly Haven's trees (2–17 million triangles, up to about 1 GB each) are represented by OpenLegend's existing simplified versions of `tree_small_02` and `fir_sapling_medium`. KayKit's forest and resource packs are not on GitHub. Poly Pizza refused scripted access.

## Review and remaining gaps

**Checked in the local viewer:** all 28 subjects and the family views load with no failed pieces, using 22–266 MB of texture memory per subject and about 480 MB for the whole-category photo-scanned environment view; sizes are consistent; scanned metals, textured trees and trim-sheet textures render; selecting by click, focusing, likes, notes, best-fit choices and the picks summary work and persist; the page has no horizontal overflow at phone width. Not checked: keyboard-only and screen-reader use beyond native controls, and every one of the 512 pieces individually up close.

**Finding for the art direction:** physically based models, metals especially, are authored for surrounding light to reflect. Without it their metal reads almost black, and the game currently has no environment lighting. The survey's **Reflections** control compares a simple sky light with none; this belongs to the shading decision in [AD03](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad03--choose-the-plain-3d-shading-and-consistency-rules).

**Limits:** models are shown in a static pose, without animation: their stored pose, or for WildMesh 3D's animals the first frame of their standing idle. Ground textures tile at an approximate 2 m per repeat. The water treatments are simple first candidates (scrolling ripple normals and a sky reflection, or a painted gradient with soft bands). Modular building kits are shown as parts, apart from the four village-kit buildings assembled on November 1. The animal subjects have no photo-scanned or stylized-textured options: those families do not publish animals, so animals in the direction need another source, such as CC BY community models or generated models. Two sets of [free candidates](#free-animal-sources--november-3-2026), _Fantasy Creatures_ and WildMesh 3D's painted animals, are shown beside them.

## Unified direction — approved November 1, 2026

**Grounded stylized:** Quaternius's stylized textured line is the anchor for every category, nudged toward natural forms where Mike found it cartoony. Mike approved this proposal on November 1, 2026 ("Adopt it"). The [current visual requirements](../../decisions.md#current-visual-requirements--october-31-2026) now own the direction and [AD09](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad09--follow-through-on-the-grounded-stylized-direction) tracks its follow-through; this section keeps the proposal as approved. It does not settle the shading questions in [AD03](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad03--choose-the-plain-3d-shading-and-consistency-rules).

**The anchor.** These free CC0 kits share one look and cover most of the world: the _Fantasy Props MegaKit_ (over 200 props on four shared texture sets), _Stylized Nature MegaKit_ (116 models), _Medieval Village MegaKit_ (over 300 modular building pieces on a grid, walls with both outside and inside faces), _Universal Base Characters_ (six humanoid bodies) with _Modular Character Outfits – Fantasy_ (12 outfits from 62 parts), and the two _Universal Animation Libraries_ (over 250 humanoid clips). The survey showed only 36 stylized objects, 28 nature pieces and 22 building parts from these kits. New sources and generated assets are judged against this line.

**Look rules.**

- **Shape:** real proportions and readable silhouettes, slightly simplified: softened bevels and small parts (handles, blades, straps) a little thicker so they read at play distance, never toy proportions. Nature stays closer to real growth than the kit: irregular crowns with gaps and visible branching, no cloud-ball canopies or stacked-cone pines.
- **Surface:** painted-looking colour textures with broad value gradients and simplified material grain (wood grain, chipped edges, stone facets); no photographic noise and little baked lighting. Objects share texture sets per family instead of each carrying its own.
- **Materials:** mostly matte. Metal reads as painted metal with moderate shine, so it holds up without environment light; adding sky light remains an AD03 choice.
- **Palette:** natural earth tones and varied, slightly muted greens, as the current canopy adaptation does; saturated colour stays in small accents such as potions, cloth and flowers.
- **Budgets,** measured from the survey's stylized pieces: objects median about 1,000 triangles (most 300–2,500), nature about 1,650 (most under 6,300), building parts about 220. The photo-scanned pieces measured about 7,200 for objects and 27,000 for nature.

**By category.**

- **Objects:** adopt the _Fantasy Props MegaKit_ broadly. Where the survey's stylized set had nothing for a subject (bows, spears, arrows) or no equivalent piece (shovel, scythe, fish, meat, some blades), keep the classic low-poly pieces Mike liked as placeholders until stylized replacements are generated or found; the full kits may already contain some.
- **Environment:** stylized grass, flowers, ferns and mushrooms. Trees and bushes from the kit, tuned toward natural (more varied, less saturated foliage; prefer the kit's least stylized models), with the photo-scanned fir and dead trunks where the kit is too cartoony. Rocks, ground and water had no likes and need their own short study.
- **Buildings:** the _Medieval Village MegaKit_, assembled into whole buildings from its grid pieces. The survey showed only loose parts, so a whole-building study comes first.
- **People:** the base characters at regular proportions with the fantasy outfits and animation libraries could replace today's mixed people sources and give everyone one shared skeleton and clip set; the mercenary would need a matching retexture study.
- **Animals:** no stylized source exists. Animals need generation (concept art, then image-to-3D, cleanup, rigging and walk, run, idle and death clips) or commissioned models, and are the hardest category.

**Sourcing and generation.** Kits first, because they are free, consistent, light and immediately available. Generation fills gaps (animals, setting-specific objects) and later the items players invent. Its route: a style sheet rendered from liked kit pieces; concept images made against it; image-to-3D; an automated conformance recipe for scale, pivot, triangle and texture budgets, palette and roughness, like the existing [generated-chest recipe](../../../scripts/prepare-generated-chest.py); and side-by-side review against kit pieces in this survey page. Volume waits for a pilot of about eight props and two animals that records cost, cleanup time, rejection rate and how closely each matches the kit. Evidence so far is thin: the game's only generated model is a third-party Meshy chest (40 credits, about $0.80 at the recorded Pro-plan rate) that needed scripted cleanup and still shows rough ornament up close ([evidence](../../../docs/verification/generated-prop-coherence.md)), and the [generation research](../../research/3d-pixel-worlds/generative-pipeline.md) rates single rigid props promising and finished rigged characters difficult.

**What approval changed.** Stylized textured became the primary family. Poly Haven moved to a supporting role (conifers, deadwood and possibly ground and rock textures) instead of the endorsed default for natural objects. The rejection of the visible low-poly aesthetic stays; the classic low-poly placeholders are temporary.

- **Files:** the four GLBs are in the survey workspace beside the other prepared files and, like them, are not yet archived.
- **Verdict:** Mike approved them on November 1, 2026: "The buildings look good." This closes the AD09 item. The heavy corners and doors above still need lighter versions before the game uses them.

## Animated animals pack — November 2, 2026

Quaternius's _Ultimate Animated Animals_ (July 2021 release, CC0) adds 12 animals to the classic low-poly family, each labelled "(animated pack)": a doe and a stag under **Deer**; a wolf, fox, husky and Shiba Inu under **Wolves, bears and foxes**; and a cow, bull, brown and white horses, donkey and alpaca under **Farm and work animals**.

- **Look:** flat colours with no textures on faceted forms, 1,848–3,670 triangles each (median about 2,100).
- **Rigs and clips:** every animal is rigged (38–51 bones) and carries 12 or 13 clips. All have walk, gallop, gallop jump, jump landing, two idles and a head-low idle, eating, two hit reactions and death; the hoofed animals add headbutt and kick attacks, and the wolf, fox and dogs one attack. The page shows them in their rest pose; the pack's `Preview.mp4`, in its source folder, shows them moving.
- **Sizes:** set from typical shoulder heights. The doe (1.45 m), stag (1.95 m at the antlers) and wolf (0.96 m) match the game's earlier repainted versions; the horses are 2.0 m to the ears, about 1.5 m at the shoulder, so they stand taller than the classic farm pack's 1.7 m horse.
- **Same release as the game's earlier animals:** the pack's doe, stag and wolf `.blend` files have the SHA-256s recorded for the game's earlier repainted deer, stag and wolf (kept as comparisons; see [attribution](../../../apps/client/src/props/ATTRIBUTION.md#source-based-animals)). The survey's **Doe (repainted)** and **Stag (repainted)** now stand beside their originals.
- **Checked in the local viewer:** the three subjects load with no failed pieces, and all 12 stand on the ground at their stated sizes. Not checked: the clips in the page or the game, and the animals in the starting world.
- **For the direction:** this is still the visibly low-poly look the direction rejects, so the pack does not fill the animal gap. Mike declined repainting its animals and asked for other free sources instead ([below](#free-animal-sources--november-3-2026)). Its rigs and clips, which the game has used before, remain a base that generated bodies could be fitted to in the [generation pilot](../../../docs/maintainers/art-direction.md#ad09--follow-through-on-the-grounded-stylized-direction).
- **Files:** the 12 GLBs (0.8–2.2 MB each, mostly animation data) are in the survey workspace beside the other prepared files and, like them, are not yet archived.

## Free animal sources — November 3, 2026

Offered a repaint of the pack above or a wider search, Mike answered "we don't want classic low poly in general" and asked for free animal packs that fit the direction. Quaternius cannot supply them: its site marks all four of its animal packs (2018–2021) untextured, and its newest textured kit, _Bestiary_ (August 2026), holds humanoid dungeon monsters under a license marked QAL rather than CC0.

- **Searched:** on Sketchfab, the top 24 public search results for each of 33 animal names, alone and with "stylized" or "hand painted", gave 1,286 different downloadable animal models, 1,111 of them CC BY, CC BY-SA or CC0; 15 more searches looked for multi-animal packs. Also itch.io's free 3D asset listings by species and style tags, OpenGameArt, Poly Pizza, BlendSwap, BlenderKit's CC0 models and the Blender Studio open-movie characters. Most free animals are flat-colour low-poly, cartoon or photo-textured, and no source matches the kits across every species.
- **Closest painted look:** [WildMesh 3D](https://sketchfab.com/WildMesh_3D)'s free Sketchfab models, CC BY 4.0: a doe, stag, elk, moose, bighorn sheep, pronghorn and brown bear in painted versions, plus realistic versions of the doe, elk and bear and a realistic donkey. Natural anatomy and smooth forms carry hard-edged painted colour patches; 4,700–7,900 triangles and 53–81 clips each (the donkey 117). The creator describes these free versions as older, with known rigging problems. The same creator's wolf, fox, boar, hare, horse and farm animals are paid, or free only for non-commercial use. Sketchfab downloads need a signed-in account.
- **Widest free set:** algumacoisaqq's [_Fantasy Creatures_](https://algumacoisaqq.itch.io/fantasy-creatures-wolf-giant-rat-spider-wasp-centipede), CC0 1.0, with its generator scripts in [Beasts](https://github.com/danielgruginski/Beasts): a grey wolf, cave bear, red deer stag, golden eagle, horse, cow, goat, sheep, pig and hen among 26 creatures (the rest are fantasy monsters and giant vermin). Natural proportions with painted coats; 4,564–5,884 triangles (the hen 2,118), 1024 px colour textures, most with two to four coats, and 10–13 clips each keyed by code. Its author states that an AI coding assistant wrote the Blender scripts that build, paint, rig and animate every creature, and marks the pack a prototype. Downloads are 288–303 MB archives of all 26 (Godot, FBX or Unity).
- **Weaker options:** igor-lir's CC BY 4.0 Sketchfab set (wolf, fox, stag, sheep, chipmunk and others) has natural forms but photographic fur that would need repainting, and one clip each. Single openly licensed models come from different artists in different styles: Lyndon Daniels's painted cow and horse (CC0, unrigged), bluekiwi.dog's animated hare (CC BY 4.0) and a rigged BlendSwap red deer labelled CC0 although its mesh comes from a CC BY-SA model.
- **Excluded for license:** free packs whose terms forbid or do not grant redistribution of their files (Literock Studios' warhorse and dire wolf, RetroStyle Games' cow) or forbid commercial use (WildMesh 3D's other demos), because published study files are redistributed. The game's current wolf already comes from 0 A.D.'s CC BY-SA art and is in the survey.
- **Still without a fitting free source:** fox, boar, hare, squirrel, badger, wild birds other than the eagle, a painted donkey and dogs.
- **Not checked:** apart from the two sets now in the survey ([_Fantasy Creatures_](#fantasy-creatures-in-the-survey--november-3-2026) and [WildMesh 3D's painted animals](#wildmesh-3d-in-the-survey--november-3-2026)), nothing has been downloaded. These judgments come from the creators' own preview images, not the survey's light, scale or close inspection.

## Fantasy Creatures in the survey — November 3, 2026

Mike asked for both candidate sets to be added. Ten animals from the Godot version of _Fantasy Creatures_ (CC0 1.0, by Daniel Gruginski, whose itch.io name is algumacoisaqq) form their own **Fantasy Creatures** family, marked as a candidate so it can be shown alone or beside the other families: a red deer stag under **Deer**; a grey wolf and a cave bear under **Wolves, bears and foxes**; a golden eagle under **Hares, birds and small animals**; and a horse, cow, goat, sheep, pig and hen under **Farm and work animals**. WildMesh 3D's painted animals followed the same day ([below](#wildmesh-3d-in-the-survey--november-3-2026)).

- **Coats:** the pack's models carry no textures; its Godot scenes paint each one at runtime with one of two to four coat materials. The survey shows each creature in the first coat its scene lists (a bay horse, brown bear, golden eagle and grey wolf, and the base coat of the others) and writes that material into the model as the creator's shader draws it: the coat texture for colour, the horse's normal map, no metal and one-sided faces. The shader also takes gloss from the texture's alpha channel, which glTF cannot. In nine of the coats, 87–98% of the texture has the same low gloss (the eagle's feathers are a little glossier), so the survey uses it as a constant roughness of 0.9: the eagle comes out slightly more matte than its shader draws it, and the few glossier spots, such as eyes, noses and hooves, look matte.
- **Look:** natural proportions with painted fur; 2,118–5,884 triangles (median about 4,800) and one 1024 px coat texture each (the hen's is 512 px). Files are 1.1–4.6 MB.
- **Rigs and clips:** 23–88 bones and 10–13 clips each, keyed by code, with root motion on a "Motion" bone. The page shows the rest pose, which is within 12° of each creature's first idle frame except the eagle's: its rest pose spreads the wings that its idle clip folds, so it is sized by a 2 m wingspan and lies flat beside the small animals.
- **Sizes:** set like the other versions of each species: the stag 1.95 m at the antlers, wolf 0.96 m, bear 1.25 m like the game's bear, horse 2.0 m, cow 1.5 m, sheep 1.0 m and pig 0.9 m, with the goat at 0.95 m and the hen at 0.4 m. Some of the creator's own sizes differ, such as a 2.36 m stag and a 1.54 m cave bear.
- **Flaws seen:** the pig has a thin crest of bristles along its spine that reads as a stick lying on its back, and the stag's antlers are thin and uneven.
- **Checked in the local viewer:** the four animal subjects load with no failed pieces, and all ten stand on the ground in their coats at their stated sizes. Not checked: the clips, the other coats, the creatures beside people at the game camera, and their cost in the game.
- **Files:** the ten GLBs are in the survey workspace beside the other prepared files and, like them, are not yet archived.
- **Verdict:** Mike's second choice on November 3, 2026 ([verdict](#mikes-animal-verdict--november-3-2026)). Its grey wolf is in the game, since WildMesh 3D has no wolf.

## WildMesh 3D in the survey — November 3, 2026

Once Mike had signed in to Sketchfab in the session's browser, he asked for WildMesh 3D's seven painted animals (CC BY 4.0) to be downloaded. They form their own **WildMesh 3D** family, also marked as a candidate: a doe, stag, elk, moose, bighorn ram and pronghorn under **Deer**, and a brown bear under **Wolves, bears and foxes**. Bighorn sheep and pronghorn have no subject of their own; the deer are the nearest hoofed wild animals.

- **Downloads:** each model is its own Sketchfab download, taken as Sketchfab's autoconverted GLB with textures up to 2048 px: 6.7–12.4 MB each, 54 MB in all. Each has its own source folder recording its page, size, SHA-256 and download time, and [sources.json](sources.json) lists all seven. Sketchfab serves files through short-lived signed links, so the model pages are the lasting addresses. Sketchfab dates the models to May 12–13, 2025, last updated on July 23, 2026.
- **Materials:** kept as exported: a painted colour texture and no shine (a specular–glossiness material with zero specular, which the page's renderer reads directly). The paint is laid in flat patches with hard edges, which is what "cel shading" in their titles refers to; no shading effect is added.
- **Look:** natural anatomy and smooth forms; 4,688–7,948 triangles and three or four textures each, at 512–1024 px after reduction. Files are 7.4–13.4 MB: mostly animation data, plus about 1 MB of original 2048 px textures that the recipe leaves unused beside their reduced copies.
- **Rigs and clips:** 37–38 bones and 53–81 clips each (walks, trots, runs, idles, eating, lying, sitting, hits, deaths and turns). The stored poses are frames of other clips: the bighorn's folds its legs under it and the pronghorn's is mid-stride, while the others stand within 30° of their standing idle. The survey therefore shows all seven in the first frame of their `Stand_Breathing_01` idle, standing as the creator's previews show them; the clips are kept. The creator describes these free versions as older, with known rigging problems.
- **Sizes:** the doe 1.45 m and the stag 1.95 m at the antlers like the other deer, and the bear 1.25 m like the other bears. The creator's own sizes are less consistent: its pronghorn is 1.9 m tall, taller than its doe.
- **Measurement fix:** measured from their mesh coordinates alone, as the recipe used to measure every model, four of these animals came out about a hundredth of their real size and the three antlered ones lost most of their body length, because a renderer draws a skinned mesh through its skeleton. The recipe now measures skinned models through their skeletons, in the pose the page draws. This also remeasured ten _Ultimate Animated Animals_ entries whose rest pose differs slightly from their stored mesh: the alpaca's scale changed by 1.7% and four others' by 0.3–0.4%, and the wolf's recorded length fell by 10 cm at the tail. Their files are unchanged, and they still stand on the ground at their stated sizes.
- **Flaws seen:** up close, the coats' flat patches are visible; the elk has a ragged pale edge along the top of its shoulders; the moose's antlers are pinkish grey.
- **Credit:** CC BY 4.0 requires credit. Each card links its model's Sketchfab page as "WildMesh 3D · CC BY 4.0", and the page's source line names WildMesh 3D. The survey's changes are the reduced textures and the standing pose.
- **Checked in the local viewer:** the deer and predator subjects load with no failed pieces; all seven stand on the ground at their stated sizes, with coats matching the creator's previews. Not checked: the clips and their rigging problems, the animals beside people at the game camera, and their cost in the game.
