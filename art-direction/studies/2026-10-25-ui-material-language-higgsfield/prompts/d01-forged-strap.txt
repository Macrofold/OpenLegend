NAME: Forgewright
Direction: hand-forged blackened iron strap-work framing deep green panels. Panel faces are dark green smoked glass with a faint hammered texture, slightly translucent. Frames are flat iron straps with visible hammer marks, square-headed rivets at the corners, and a hair-thin inlaid line of champagne gold running along the inside of each strap. Corners are riveted L-plates with a small fleur-cut finial. Panels are joined by short forged iron brackets and flat strap bridges bolted at both ends — like a blacksmith's hinge work — reading as scaffolding that physically holds panels together. Title rails are a flat iron bar with a recessed gold-inlaid nameplate. Dividers are thin iron bars with tiny rivet ends.
