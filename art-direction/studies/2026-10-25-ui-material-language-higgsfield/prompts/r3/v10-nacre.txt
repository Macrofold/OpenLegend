NAME: River Nacre
The portrait sits in a round setting faced with iridescent mother-of-pearl from river mussel shells, in natural irregular pieces with a soft silvery-green sheen, set into a dark grey river-stone mount anchored in the screen corner. A few small natural freshwater pearls sit at structural points, and a hairline of worn champagne gold separates the nacre from the stone. The sheen is subtle and natural, never glowing.
