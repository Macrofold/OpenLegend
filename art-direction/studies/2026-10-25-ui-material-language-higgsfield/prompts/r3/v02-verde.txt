NAME: Verde Antico
The portrait is set into a carved slab of verde antico marble (very dark green serpentine marble with fine white veining), carved as a classical oval cameo frame. A laurel wreath of carved stone leaves surrounds the portrait opening, with traces of worn gilding in the grooves of the wreath. The slab's outline follows the carving rather than a rectangle and anchors into the screen corner, like a fragment of an ancient statue's plinth.
