NAME: Stag Crown
The portrait sits in a ring of dark polished stone lined with a hairline of worn champagne gold. Behind it rises a pair of real, weathered stag antlers, natural in shape and colour (pale bone shading to grey-brown), spreading above the ring like a crown. It should feel like a noble hunter's heirloom from a great hall, not tribal. The antlers stay compact and never overlap the bars.
