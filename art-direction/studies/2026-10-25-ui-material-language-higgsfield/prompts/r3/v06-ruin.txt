NAME: Mossbound Ruin
The portrait is framed by a fragment of an ancient carved granite ring, like a piece of a ruined monument at a river's edge: weathered and chipped, with worn carved mouldings that still hold flakes of old gilding. Small cushions of real green moss, a tiny fern and pale lichen grow in its crevices and along its top. Restrained: the moss is small and crisp-edged and never covers the portrait or the bars.
