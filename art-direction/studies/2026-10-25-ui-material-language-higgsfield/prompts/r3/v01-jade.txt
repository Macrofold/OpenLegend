NAME: Jade Medallion
The portrait sits in a round opening carved into a thick disc of dark green nephrite jade with natural translucent veining and cloudy inclusions, polished smooth. A shallow low-relief carving of slender river reeds and leaves wraps around the opening, and a hairline bezel of worn champagne gold lines it. The jade is set into the screen's top-left corner; its outer edge is naturally irregular, like a carved river boulder rather than a perfect circle. The bar panel's left end tucks behind the jade.
