NAME: Kintsugi Stone
A heavy, smooth, water-worn dark river stone (near-black green basalt) holds the portrait in a polished round recess. The stone was once cracked and has been mended: fine natural fractures filled with pale champagne gold (kintsugi) radiate from the recess, and one gold seam runs out of the stone and becomes the thin top edge of the bar panel, as if the gold mending holds the whole HUD together.
