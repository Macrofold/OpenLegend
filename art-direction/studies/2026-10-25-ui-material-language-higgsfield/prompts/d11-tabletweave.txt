NAME: Brooch & Band
Direction: early-medieval textile and brooch craft. Panel faces are smooth dark green felted wool. Borders are narrow tablet-woven bands with an original crisp geometric pattern (diamonds, chevrons) in dark green, muted gold and ivory threads, flat and precisely woven with clean edges. Corners are cast bronze brooch plates with fine interlace-free geometric engraving. Panels are joined by bronze penannular cloak-brooches whose pins pass through the bands of two neighbouring panels, holding them together like a cloak is fastened. Title rails are a wider woven band with the title woven into it. Dividers are a single narrow woven stripe. No rope, no cord, no knots.
