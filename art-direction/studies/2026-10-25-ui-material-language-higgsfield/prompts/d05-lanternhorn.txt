NAME: Lanternhorn
Direction: a medieval horn lantern. Panel faces are thin translucent panes of pressed horn, dark green-tinted and slightly mottled, through which a little warm light passes. Frames are slim wrought-iron cames and bars with small pierced vent holes, like the cage of a lantern. Corners are small cast-iron caps with a pierced quatrefoil. Panels are joined by the lantern's iron frame itself: vertical iron posts and cross-bars that continue from one panel to the next, forming a visible scaffold. Title rails are a pierced iron crown-band with the title cut out as a stencil, showing the pane behind. Dividers are a thin iron bar. By day the light is subdued and nothing glows; the panes simply read as rich translucent material.
