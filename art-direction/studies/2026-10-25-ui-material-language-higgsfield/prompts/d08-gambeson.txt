NAME: Gambeson
Direction: the craft of armour and padded garments. Panel faces are dark green quilted linen, tightly stitched in a diamond quilting pattern that is subtle and low so text stays readable on a smooth centre. Edges are bound with narrow blackened leather piping, and the outer frame is a band of fine riveted mail rings in dark steel with a thin champagne-gold edge. Corners are small overlapping lamellar steel plates with a brass rivet. Panels are joined by short riveted leather straps with small blackened steel buckles, like armour points fastening plates together. Title rails are a single steel lamellar plate with the title stamped and gilded. Dividers are a single row of tight stitches.
