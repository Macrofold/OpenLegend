NAME: Cartographer
Direction: a surveyor's engraved chart. Panel faces are a smooth deep green surface with a very faint engraved grid. Borders are fine copperplate-style engraved lines in champagne gold: a double rule with delicate cross-hatched shading along one side, like the border of an old hand-engraved map. Corners are small engraved compass-rose roundels and scale-bar ticks. Panels are joined by engraved measuring lines and dividers' arcs that run from one panel to the next, with a small brass pivot pin at each junction, like a navigator's tools. Title rails are a cartouche drawn in engraved line. Dividers are a scale-bar with tiny ticks. Precise, elegant, all crisp line work; no sketchiness, no paper texture.
