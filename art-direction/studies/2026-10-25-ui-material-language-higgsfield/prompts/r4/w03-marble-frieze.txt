The dark green, white-veined verde antico marble now fills the whole screen corner as part of an ancient temple's carved cornice and frieze, running off the top and left edges of the image, with a finely carved classical moulding along its lower edge. The oval cameo is set into the frieze, and its laurel wreath is now ornate champagne gold rather than gilded stone. The eye button sits in a small round carved marble boss to the right of the bars.
