NAME: Overgrown Relic

A blend of the first two attached designs (the mossbound granite ruin and the root-gripped medallion). The corner is the weathered remnant of an ancient granite ruin, gripped by the grey roots of an old tree, with small crisp cushions of moss and pale lichen; it fills the corner and runs off the top and left edges of the image. Inside the ruin, an ornate champagne-gold ring of engraved leaf scrollwork, worn but still bright in places, frames the portrait, as if a royal relic survived within the ruin. The roots continue a short way along the top edge. The eye button sits in a small root-wrapped stone to the right of the bars.
