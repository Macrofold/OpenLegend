The screen's top-left corner is a mass of dark green nephrite jade rock, like a mountain outcrop of jade ore, that fills the corner and runs off the top and left edges of the image, so the HUD appears set into a larger rock face beyond the screen. Natural veins of gold thread through the jade. The portrait is set deep into the rock inside an ornate, finely engraved champagne-gold bezel with leaf-and-reed scrollwork, as if a goldsmith had set it into the living stone. The bar panel and status tray emerge from the rock with their left ends embedded in it. The eye button sits in a small jade outcrop to the right of the bars.
