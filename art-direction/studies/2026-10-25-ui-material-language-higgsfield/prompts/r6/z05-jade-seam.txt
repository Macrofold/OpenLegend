NAME: Jade Seam
REFS: liked=r4-w01-jade-lode-clean-detail.jpg
The jade becomes a broad seam of polished jade running through a large mass of dark grey-green bedrock, as if the HUD were set into a cliff where a vein of jade breaks the surface: the matte bedrock has big, calm, weathered forms, and the smooth, glossy jade band holds the portrait bezel and sweeps across the corner. Thin champagne-gold ore lines mark where the jade meets the rock. The eye button sits in a small knob of the same jade.
