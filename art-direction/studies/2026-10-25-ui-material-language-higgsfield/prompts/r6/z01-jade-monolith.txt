NAME: Jade Monolith
REFS: liked=r4-w01-jade-lode-clean-detail.jpg
The jade becomes the corner of one enormous single block of dark nephrite jade, a monolith that continues far beyond the screen. Its surface is calm and smooth: a few broad, gently curved planes meet at softly rounded edges, with two or three large natural fracture faces. The colour shifts slowly from deep forest green in shadow to a lighter sea green where the light catches the rounded edges, with faint translucency at the thinnest edges. Two or three long, thin gold veins cross the stone in sweeping lines. The eye button sits in a small, smooth jade knuckle of the same block.
