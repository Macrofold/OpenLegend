NAME: Carved Jade Plinth
REFS: liked=r4-w01-jade-lode-clean-detail.jpg
The jade becomes a fragment of an ancient monument carved from dark nephrite jade, part of a much larger structure beyond the screen: two or three massive, softly worn carved blocks with smooth chiselled faces and rounded, weathered edges, separated by one fine seam. Around the portrait bezel the stone carries a shallow, eroded relief band of leaves. The surfaces are large and quiet, polished smooth by age, with a few chips and one or two long gold veins. It stays a compact corner fragment, never a wall, column or cornice running along the screen edges. The eye button sits in a small carved jade boss.
