NAME: River Jade
REFS: liked=r4-w01-jade-lode-clean-detail.jpg
The jade becomes a huge water-worn jade boulder, smoothed by a river for centuries: soft, polished, undulating surfaces with long gentle highlights, and deep green translucency where the stone thins at its edges. The stone shows only slow, cloudy variations deep inside it, never a surface pattern, and one or two thin gold veins. The eye button sits in a small rounded, water-smoothed jade lobe.
