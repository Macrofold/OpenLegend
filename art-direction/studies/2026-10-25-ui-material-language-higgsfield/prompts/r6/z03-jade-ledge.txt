NAME: Jade Ledge
REFS: liked=r4-w01-jade-lode-clean-detail.jpg
The jade becomes the end of a great ledge of layered jade bedrock, like the edge of a cliff that continues off-screen: a few broad strata of dark green jade run diagonally down from the top-left corner, each with a clean natural fracture face and a softly weathered, rounded lip. Between the strata the stone is smooth and quiet with subtle depth; one thin gold vein follows a stratum. The eye button sits on a small ledge stepping out from the same strata.
