NAME: Riverstone
Direction: carved dark slate and smooth river stone, like a stonemason's work at a river ford. Panel faces are honed dark green-black slate with faint natural mineral veining, matte and calm. Frames are bevelled carved stone mouldings with a narrow inlaid channel of aged bronze running inside them. Corners are squared keystone blocks with a small chiselled chevron. Panels are joined by carved stone lintels and stepped corbels set into each frame, as if panels were built into one dry-stone structure. Title rails are a carved stone lintel with the title chiselled and gilded into it. Dividers are a single incised line ending in small drilled holes.
