NAME: Gildmoss Crest
Direction: the most restrained option, refined and modern while still crafted. Panel faces are slightly translucent dark green glass with a soft blur of the world behind and a gentle warm light falling from the top edge. There is no heavy border: just a single crisp hairline of pale champagne metal rim on the outer edge. Ornament appears only at structural joints: small engraved champagne-gold roundels and a short tapered metal spine where panels connect, with a gleam of light on the metal. Title rails are a slim engraved metal bar with a tiny diamond at each end and the title set above it. Dividers are a hairline that tapers at both ends. Elegant, quiet, premium.
