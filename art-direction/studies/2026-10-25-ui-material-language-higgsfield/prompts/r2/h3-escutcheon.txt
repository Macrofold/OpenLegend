NAME: Escutcheon
The portrait sits inside a heater-shield-shaped frame (straight top, sides curving to a point at the bottom), rimmed in champagne gilded metal, with a small cast crest ornament on top. To the right of the shield, a slender rigid glass plate shaped like a horizontal pennant (long, tapering, ending in a swallowtail notch) carries the four need gauges; a second, shorter pennant below it carries the lasting effect and the two timed statuses. Both pennants are glass and metal, fixed to the shield by small gilded clasps. A slim gilded rail along the top edge of the screen anchors the shield in the corner. The lock toggle is a small round boss on the shield's upper right.
