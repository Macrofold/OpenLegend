NAME: Gildmoss Bare
Remove the continuous champagne hairline rim from every panel, frame and button. Panels are only translucent dark green glass whose edge is a crisp, slightly lighter polished glass bevel that catches the light (no metal on the edges). Champagne metal appears only as small engraved corner caps on each panel's four corners and as a slim engraved clasp where two panels join. Title rails keep the title with a short champagne hairline beneath it that has a tiny diamond at each end.
