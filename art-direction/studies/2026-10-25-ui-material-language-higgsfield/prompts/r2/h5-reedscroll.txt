NAME: Reedscroll
A single continuous cast champagne housing: the portrait's round ring flows to the right into a gently curving cradle that holds the four need gauges, and the metal ends in a calligraphic scroll. Fine gilded flourishes shaped like slender river-reed stems and leaves curl out from the portrait ring along the top and left screen edges, framing the corner like the flourishes around a crest. Beneath the gauges, a slim curved rail holds the lasting effect and the two timed statuses. The lock toggle sits at the tip of the upper flourish.
