NAME: Corner Mount
The HUD is built into the screen corner like the corner of an ornate picture frame: a sculpted champagne corner piece fills the very top-left corner, and two slim gilded rails run a short way along the top edge and down the left edge of the screen, each tapering into a small reed-leaf finial. A round portrait medallion with a thick engraved champagne ring is set into that corner piece. Extending right from the medallion along the top edge is a tapered glass plate with a pointed end that holds the four need gauges; directly beneath it, a shorter tapered plate holds the lasting effect and the two timed statuses. The lock toggle sits in a small round socket docked onto the lower right of the portrait ring.
