NAME: Gildmoss Etched
Keep the single champagne hairline rim, and add a narrow decorative band etched (frosted) into the glass just inside the rim of every panel: a delicate repeating pattern of slender river reeds and leaves, frosted pale green-white, very low contrast so it reads as texture in the glass rather than ornament. No extra metal. Joints between panels stay small engraved champagne roundels.
