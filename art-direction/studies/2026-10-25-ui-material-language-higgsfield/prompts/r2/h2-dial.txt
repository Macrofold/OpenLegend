NAME: Ringed Dial
A large round portrait medallion is the heart of the HUD. Instead of straight bars, the four need gauges are thin concentric arcs that sweep around the lower right of the portrait ring like the rings of an astrolabe, each arc ending in its small icon and number. The lasting effect and the two timed statuses sit in small round sockets docked along the outer edge of the outermost arc, like stones set in a ring. The portrait ring is finely engraved with a river-reed motif. A small gilded quarter-circle bracket anchors the whole piece into the screen's top-left corner. The lock toggle is a small round socket at the end of the arcs.
