NAME: Gildmoss Lantern
At the centre of each panel's top edge sits a small champagne crest ornament shaped like a lantern bracket: a slim arch with a tiny finial and a small hanging ring. The warm lantern light visibly spreads down over the glass from that point. The hairline rim is slightly thicker along the top edge and thins along the sides and bottom. Joints between panels stay small engraved roundels.
