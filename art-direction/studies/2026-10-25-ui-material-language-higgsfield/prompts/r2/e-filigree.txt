NAME: Gildmoss Filigree
The most decorative of the variations, still restrained: each panel corner carries delicate pierced champagne filigree made of fine leaf-and-reed scrolls (thin, open, airy metalwork, about twice the size of the reference's corner roundels); the rim is a thin double hairline; the joint between connected panels is a small filigree clasp. Calm, smooth glass behind all text.
