NAME: Lantern Window
The portrait is set inside a tall pointed-arch window, like one pane of a lantern, framed by slender champagne metal tracery with a small domed lantern cap and ring on top. The arch hangs from a short ornate gilded bracket fixed to the top edge of the screen near the corner, and warm lantern light falls from the top of the arch over the portrait. To the right, a slim horizontal glass plate hangs from the same bracket on two short rigid gilded rods and holds the four need gauges; beneath it, a smaller plate holds the lasting effect and the two timed statuses. The lock toggle hangs as a small round medallion from the end of the bracket.
