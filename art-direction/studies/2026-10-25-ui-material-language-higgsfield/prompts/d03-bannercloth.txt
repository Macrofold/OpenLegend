NAME: Bannercloth
Direction: heraldic embroidered textile. Panel faces are dense dark forest-green felted wool, smooth and tightly woven. Borders are bands of couched champagne-gold metal-thread embroidery in an original flowing vine-and-feather pattern (like a noble's embroidered cloak), stitched precisely with crisp clean edges. Corners carry a small embroidered heraldic roundel. Title rails are a short hanging pennant of the same embroidered cloth suspended from a slim blackened iron rod with ball finials. Panels are joined by those iron rods and iron rings, as if banners hang from a frame. Dividers are a single line of gold chain-stitch. No fraying, no rope, clean cut edges bound with narrow silk tape.
