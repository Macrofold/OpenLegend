NAME: Antlerwork
Direction: a hunter-crafter's carved bone and antler inlay. Panel faces are smooth dark green stained hardwood with no visible grain, almost like dark leather. Frames are slim strips of polished bone and antler, ivory-coloured with natural warm tones, carved with a fine repeating notch pattern and pinned with small iron pins. Corners are carved antler tines curving gently around the corner. Panels are joined by short carved antler crosspieces and bone toggles that slot through iron loops, like a hunter's gear fastenings. Title rails are a flat carved bone plaque with incised, ink-filled lettering. Dividers are a thin bone strip with tiny drilled dots.
