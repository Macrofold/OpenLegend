NAME: Reliquary
Direction: medieval reliquary goldsmith work. Panel faces are deep green champlevé enamel, glassy and smooth, set flush in a gilt champagne-gold metal ground. Frames are narrow gilt bands with fine engraved hatching and a beaded edge. Corners are small square bosses each holding a polished cabochon stone (deep garnet or dark green agate) in a claw setting. Panels are joined by short gilt hinge-straps and pinned barrel hinges, like the panels of a portable altar. Title rails are a gilt band with engraved lettering filled with dark enamel. Dividers are a fine beaded gold wire. Rich but restrained: ornament only at corners and joints, calm enamel behind text.
