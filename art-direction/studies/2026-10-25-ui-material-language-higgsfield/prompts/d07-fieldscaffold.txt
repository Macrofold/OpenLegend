NAME: Fieldworks
Direction: a well-made survival camp's timber-and-iron framework. Panel faces are waxed dark green oilcloth stretched taut and flat, with subtle canvas weave. Frames are square-cut, smoothed dark ash poles (oiled, near-black, no prominent grain) joined at the corners by forged iron clamps with square wedges and pegs. Panels hang from and connect to each other by these poles and iron clamp-brackets, forming visible scaffolding like a camp shelter frame. Oilcloth is fixed to the frame with iron rings and grommets (no rope, no cords). Title rails are a short iron-banded crossbeam with a riveted iron nameplate. Dividers are thin iron straps.
