NAME: Verdigris
Direction: aged copper and bronze with a natural green patina. Panel faces are a deep green-black burnished metal surface, softly mottled, calm and smooth. Frames are hammered copper repoussé bands with a low raised relief of an original reed-and-river-wave motif, the raised parts polished to warm copper-gold and the recesses filled with soft blue-green verdigris patina. Corners are domed bosses with a small raised leaf. Panels are joined by hammered copper bands and pinned clasps that physically bridge one frame to the next. Title rails are a riveted copper plate with the title in raised polished letters. Dividers are a thin hammered copper wire. Gold accents stay pale champagne; patina is subtle, never bright teal.
