## Results

- **Definition.** Regional torso and limb registration transfers useful Meshy geometry onto the canonical mesh. Five matched neutral clay settings show a smooth transition at the chest, abdomen and upper back. The midpoint needs no corrective in these views. Stature, lower ribs, waist, pelvis and seat hold at the measured sections. Chest depth grows 5.78 mm and shoulder width falls 0.94 mm. Pectoral relief gains 4.87 mm, upper and lower abs 1.84 and 1.17 mm, and upper back 1.82 mm. Obliques gain only 0.10 mm and the deltoid probe recedes 1.88 mm. The toned end remains softer than the reference overall. The existing normal atlas carries the fine skin detail.
- **Muscle mass and fullness.** At fixed 50% definition, shoulder width spans 54.19, 56.02 and 59.06 cm across runner, Peacock and lifter, with the same 28.04 cm waist at the mass probe. The separate fuller control moves the waist from 27.73 to 30.51 cm at its measured section and leaves shoulder width and stature fixed. Definition stays on the same anatomical regions at both mass extremes. The shared evaluator agrees exactly at stored precision across 20 cold and incremental cases applied in both orders, with all rest matrices preserved. The fuller proof covers one added build.
- **Shirt.** The retained premade shirt uses its existing region-aware surface binding. Build changes reuse that binding, and the fit stays fixed across definition within a build. On Peacock, exterior points more than 2 mm inside skin rise from 18 to 61 at rest and 54 to 100 with the arm raised. The broad toned wearer reaches 113 such points raised and the broad fit stretches the most affected 5% of edges by at least 24.5% at rest. Visible chest, abdomen and sleeve breakthroughs survive in the actual game actor. One fixed fit per build fails visual acceptance without a clearance or pose correction.
- **Native MetaHuman.** Built-in Muscularity changes the broad fit with little added definition at fixed abdominal probes. Native paired import retains 98.16% of intended relief projection and keeps the custom offsets through one common adjustment. The import changes the selected underlying arm pose and fit, and the visible relief moves at fixed anatomy probes. OpenLegend blends the exported custom field on the frozen family.
