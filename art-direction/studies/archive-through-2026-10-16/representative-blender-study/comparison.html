Principles I recommend

Use shaped silhouettes and broad quiet material fields. Place deliberate stepped paint where it explains leaves, grain, woven cloth, seams, eyes or blade structure. Keep faces clearer than surrounding texture. Build real closed coverage for clothes and foliage. Let metal, wood and wool differ without adding noise everywhere.

For feedback, the useful decisions are how rounded trees should be, how strong stone/wood paint should be, how much armor detail survives game distance, and whether the painted faces feel expressive enough up close. At the real default game camera these figures are too small for reliable facial detail; they remain close-study proposals rather than accepted gameplay appearances. The current rounded crowns, repeated leaf paint and static character posing remain limits.
