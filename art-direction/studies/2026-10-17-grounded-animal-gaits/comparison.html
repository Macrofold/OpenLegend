Doe · footfall and articulation

The revised walk uses a consistent four-step order, fixed leg lengths and different bend directions for the front joints and rear hocks. The foot path continues smoothly through contact.

Stag · the same corrected stride

The larger stag uses its own original segment lengths. Antlers, head attachment and scale are preserved.

Wolf · paws that hold their shape

The source walk stretched the wrist-to-toe distance from about 9 to 28 cm. It now remains about 11 cm. Overall timing is retained; the foreleg joints rotate without lengthening and the toes keep their neutral shape.
