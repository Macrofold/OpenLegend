## Preserve future iterations

Before changing a selected asset or rendering profile, preserve its matched baseline. Retain meaningful rejected candidates, selected stills, gameplay-scale views and moving comparisons as immutable external study revisions; never overwrite an earlier capture. Follow the [art-flow preservation procedure](../../docs/production-workflows/art-flows/README.md#preserve-and-resume) and the current asset tooling: all new media/source payloads go to S3, regardless of size. Keep original pixels, source commit, settings, exact asset/source identity, selection and known defects together through the study record and verified manifest references. Git holds that README, small catalogs and permanent references, not the image files. Existing historical assets stay preserved until their own verified migration permits removal.
