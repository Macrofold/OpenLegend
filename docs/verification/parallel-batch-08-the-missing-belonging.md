## 1. Mechanical validity, privacy and persistence continuity

Verified through existing domain/server/UI suites (`packages/domain/src/boundaries.test.ts`, `packages/domain/src/kernel.test.ts`, `packages/domain/src/mind.test.ts`, `packages/domain/src/perception.test.ts`, `apps/server/src/context.test.ts`, `apps/server/src/http.test.ts`, `apps/client/src/api.test.ts`) and an end-to-end disposable PostgreSQL scenario covering MY01–MY05:

- **Authored incident and physical reach (MY01):**
  - `createStarterWorld` installs `Mara Reed` (`mara` at `(9.5, 13.5)`), `Tavin Vale` (`tavin` at `(9.5, 18)`), `Mending Clearing` (`mending-clearing` at `(11, 18)` on `terrain`), `Reed-side Store` (`reed-side-store` at `(11, 22.5)` on `terrain`), `Mending Clearing marker` (`mending-clearing-marker` at `(11, 16.5)`), `Mending Clearing work spot` (`mending-clearing-spot` at `(11, 18)` containing `torn_red_fastening` × 1 and `tavins_sorting_note` × 1), and `Reed-side Store` pile (`reed-side-store-pile` at `(11, 22.5)` containing `red_stitched_satchel` × 1 with 2 nested `cord` units and 1 co-located `prepared_fiber` unit).
  - Existing starter characters (`entity-0001`, `ada`), expedition sites and the first-threat `territorial-stag` home `(32, 28)` and refuge `(38, 31)` remain intact and unobstructed.
  - Step-by-step pathing from camp `(8, 8)` to `Mending Clearing` `(11, 18)` and onward to `Reed-side Store` `(11, 22.5)` stays on dry `terrain` without water crossings, climbing, combat or construction; `canReachEntity` passes for all markers, ground piles and residents at arrival.
  - Current-format PostgreSQL save/load after removing `torn_red_fastening` and unpacking `red_stitched_satchel` preserves the modified world without reseeding removed clues or resetting actor knowledge.
- **Exact personal evidence and two-observer privacy (MY02):**
  - `containerPage` (`inventoryItemView`) omits `ItemDefinition.inscription` before deliberate `inspect-inventory` admission, preventing unopened ground-pile browsing from leaking `tavins_sorting_note`'s text.
  - Deliberate `inspect-inventory` records `EncounterExposure` (`kind: 'inventory-item'`) with optional `inscription` (`1..600` chars) and `observedLocation` (`{ kind: 'world', point, label, containerId }` for accessible world piles/nested containers or `{ kind: 'possession', label: 'Carried possession' }` for held items).
  - `POST /api/world-events/sources` (`readPerceivedEventSources`) validates opaque `WorldEventSourceReference` tokens bound to `[scope, family, sourceId, revision]` using `JSON.stringify` array encoding so separator-containing IDs (`world:a|b` vs `world:a` + `b|evt:1`) never collide.
  - Inspecting `tavins_sorting_note` yields `status: 'available'`, `acquisition: 'inspected-document'`, `acquisitionLabel: 'Inspected a written account'`, `observedLocation.label: 'Mending Clearing work spot'`, and the exact note inscription as attributed document text.
  - A second observer (`tavin`) who never inspected the note is rejected on foreign tokens (`expired`) and receives `{ status: 'unavailable', reason: 'This source is no longer available in retained personal history.' }` for forged observer-scoped references, with no fallback to `history_events.payload`.
  - Moving `red_stitched_satchel` into player inventory leaves the earlier inspection record's `observedLocation` fixed at `Reed-side Store (11, 22.5)`; re-inspecting while carried records a new encounter with `observedLocation: { kind: 'possession', label: 'Carried possession' }`.
  - A non-mystery ordinary inspection case (inspecting `berries` in `camp-chest`, taking them, and comparing that encounter with heard speech) uses the identical `EncounterExposure` and `readPerceivedEventSources` path without incident-specific branches.
- **Correction, forgetting, custody and delayed-response fencing (MY03 & MY05):**
  - Keeping `red_stitched_satchel` allows ordinary container storage (packing and unpacking 2 `berries` inside the satchel while preserving its 2 `cord` units).
  - Offering `red_stitched_satchel` via `handover` (`operation: 'offer'`) creates a pending `itemOffers` entry that survives PostgreSQL save/load without transferring custody; `decline` leaves the satchel with the player; removing 1 `cord` from inside the satchel while an offer is pending causes subsequent `accept` to fail (`staleAcceptRes.outcome.ok === false`), protecting whole-container consent; `accept` transfers the satchel and its 2 nested `cord` units atomically to `mara`, and duplicate `accept` is rejected.
  - When `tavin` is nearby at `(9.5, 18)`, he witnesses the handover acceptance (`witness-observation`); when `tavin` is out of range at `(45, 13.5)` on `terrain-3-1`, he receives no awareness of the transfer.
  - `correctMemory(player, noteEventId, satchelEventId)` adds `qualification` to `readPerceivedEventSources` while keeping the source available; `forgetMemory(player, noteEventId)` revokes both `memories` and `history_perspectives` (`invalidateExperience` now includes `memory.eventId` alongside `memory.id`), turning the source into `{ status: 'unavailable' }` with no retained text.
  - Rewinding the world timeline via `service.replaceWorld` while a delayed cognition response is in flight causes the late response to be fenced and discarded without mutating the restored timeline (`transcriptEpoch` bumped, old job absent).
