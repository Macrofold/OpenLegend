## Fitting one garment

In Blender 5.2.3, a synthetic torso had 96 points around each of 48 levels from 0.88 to 1.56 m. One shape key widened and deepened the torso by different amounts along its height. The 9,024 garment samples sat 8 mm off their corresponding triangle centres along the surface normal. | Eight fits sharing the same endpoints          |                                  1.377 ms |                                    5.316 ms |

Endpoint preparation is included once per batch. Both methods agreed at the ends within 0.000063 mm. Their posed midpoint outputs differed by at most 0.123 mm. For a first fixed export the direct path avoids preparing unused targets. For a batch or live creator, compare reusable endpoints against the direct reference on an actual garment, including time to correct its shape.
