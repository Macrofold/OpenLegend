## Verification failures and limits

Early disposable specimens used an unsupported ground surface or skipped the world's collision initialization; these were fixture failures and supplied no gameplay evidence. A sparse-edit specimen attempted to replace a newly appended unsaved page without a physical predecessor; saving before the protected edit corrected its lifecycle. Early native-only validator calls omitted their explicit temporal owner argument and therefore did not validate that component; actual full PostgreSQL validation did. The final native scenarios pass the correct owner and validate it directly. Final connected runs include the affected paths. No failed attempt is counted as a speed sample.
