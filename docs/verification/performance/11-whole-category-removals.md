## Frozen animals and frozen noticing: completed

Two separate baseline-derived private copies each change only the native kernel:

- **Frozen animals:** filter animals out of the native participant list. All forty animal objects remain in the world and can still be seen, but their movement, physiology and native reactions stop. This removes their downstream influence on simulation scheduling as well as direct animal work. It is not a test of movement alone or acceptable gameplay.
- **Frozen noticing:** replace the incremental encounter-update generator with an empty generator. The original body stays uncalled for source comparison. New sightings, physical-contact episodes and recognition updates no longer reconcile. Direct command checks and event emission outside that generator remain active; this does not remove every perception-related calculation.

The actual product delta is one filtering expression for animals and one empty-generator redirection for noticing. Pinned formatting leaves the animal owner unchanged and only wraps the new noticing signature. Current source index `/private/tmp/ol-pf125-ablation-source-preparation-20261015-b.json` records both complete 442-file roots. The exact existing counterless driver and fresh complete opening remain unchanged.
