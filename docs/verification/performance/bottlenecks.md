Thirteen walks are blocked by ordinary gameplay; none fails busy or remains unconfirmed. The player sleeps for about 120 real seconds, reducing offered walking during that period. Both page and console error counts are zero, but four network reads are aborted. These are separate facts, not an error-free claim.

**The worst walk has a measured cause.** It waits3.204 seconds for an earlier background save, then spends about 408 msin its own command handler. That earlier save waits for previously accepted changes to reach the ordinary tables and for preparation of searchable memory text occupying the database writer; it then performs its own required SQL. Network/command identity now connects those steps. This is stronger evidence than guessing from similarly long unrelated saves. SQL durations also include delayed callbacks and overlapping simulation, so they are not database CPU alone. The [focused report](save-and-publication-followthrough.md#exact-saving-delay-found-in-the-seventh-observation) preserves exact timestamps and limits.

The previous sixth observation's Orin death and four busy walks remain historical failures, preserved in their [full report](overhaul-compromises.md#sixth-combined-compromise-result--near-target-average-failures-remain). The narrow driver repair only reschedules requests proved never to have entered execution and retains failed-measurement accounting; it does not retry uncertain effects. The seventh run has no resident admission error and therefore does not establish that repair caused the better survival result.

**The constrained saving worker is now implemented, with runtime and cost unverified:** one background worker applies a complete eligible batch of already accepted world facts, history and searchable accounts through the same writers, intended to avoid repeated game-thread wakeups between database replies. The code reuses existing writers, preserves confirmation/recovery order and sends changed rows rather than copying the entire world. ## Decisions and unresolved gaps

The retained ordinary changes preserve intended gameplay. Search preparation may finish after exact facts are saved; actual search waits or reports that preparation is unavailable. Meter and work reads include earned arithmetic in order. Persistent entity storage trades cheaper local editing for tree lookups and cold construction; an unfrozen-builder comparison regressed and remains documented.

Small-model cloth tiers are retained; the busy-witness memory-loss rule is removed. Grouping routine gathering records while retaining their original evidence is rejected for the measured workload without AI calls. Remote stationary crafting and offscreen decorative suspension have focused correctness/lifecycle evidence, but the latest complete observation does not establish their individual speed benefit. General ordered saving and retained calculations through saves are included in the new combined observation, but that observation cannot isolate their individual benefits. Further deferred-saving families were removed after complete-cost comparisons failed. A [whole-architecture comparison](overhaul-storage-implementation.md#whole-saving-architecture-control--october-17-2026-1452-edt) also finds extra journal work in its finite scenario; its real-time and archived-source coverage limits remain explicit. [Saving contract and focused results](../../projects/performance-overhaul/saved-native-calculations.md).

Smooth sustained 3×, prompt walking, the required long-play input count, natural food/rest choices and absence of drawing freezes remain open. All 16 residents survive and complete work/sleep/wake in the seventh scripted observation; that is not independent decision quality. Five ordinary and seven compromise observations are complete. Orin's hunger death is confirmed; the demonstrated driver scheduling hole and missing exact failed-request attribution remain explicit. The guarded diagnostic recovery passes its focused actual-owner checks, TypeScript and review; full workload qualification after it remains open. The original faster-work/one-metre workload has not passed. Checked save/load sighting identities are repaired, with a remaining tiny saved-countdown difference explicitly tracked. **This branch is not qualified as merge-ready for the requested performance outcome.**

## Changes after the latest game measurement

A correct prepared-information handoff and four-interval grouping are tested together and removed: both changed runs consume more CPU than both controls, despite fewer world publications. Optional search priority passes real database ordering/privacy/cold-reopening checks. Its four-sample comparison lowers mean wake-to-all-command acknowledgement by 58.1%, while increasing complete catch-up elapsed time by 26.5% and process CPU by 8.0%; host variance and the controlled burst limit causal/general-game claims. This is a retained responsiveness tradeoff, not net work removed. The final-specimen tool passes an actual waiting manual-save/export proof. The complete accepted-batch worker, selective participant preparation, retained false numeric-condition checks, matching planned crafting and CSS-viewport visibility correction are installed. Their new runtime/performance qualification is deliberately unrun under Mike’s implementation-only direction. None changes the latest measured 2.995× result or closes walking/drawing acceptance.
