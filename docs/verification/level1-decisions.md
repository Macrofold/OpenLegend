# Level-1 decisions verification

Evidence for the [level-1 decisions plan](../projects/level1-decisions.md): [CR02](../maintainers/cognition-redesign.md#cr02--semantic-levels-and-immediate-speech) level-1 selection and verification items, the CR02 Intelligence rows, [CR01](../maintainers/cognition-redesign.md#cr01--separate-model-text-from-execution-metadata) per-level limits and payload inspection, and the [CR12](../maintainers/cognition-redesign.md#cr12--acceptance-and-tokenlatency-evidence) level-1 fixture cases.

**Scope of evidence:** fixture and native only. Every provider exchange was a fake `AiClient` or a fake transport behind the real direct adapters. The new fixture files and `ai-director.test.ts` install a global network guard that asserts zero outbound requests; `client.test.ts` and `cognition.test.ts` rely on injected or stubbed transports. No live Jev, LLM or embedding request was made; additional Jev cost for this task is **$0** (no provider was reachable, and fixture receipts are simulated accounting). Nothing here establishes model quality, calibration, latency or real spending. Live provider verification remains a separate gate that needs an approved spending cap.

What the fixtures establish:

- **Act:** hungry Ada with owned berries receives one combined Jev request (route plus independent ratings); the eat binding rated 0.9 is admitted through `commitActorResponse`, the berry lot decreases and fullness rises on the next native step, with zero generation and no thought, goal, speech or reflection.
- **Continue:** a 0.9 rating for the offered continue binding records `continued`, an empty admitted response and unchanged action, plan and goals; an unchanged later opportunity admits no job and makes no call.
- **Defer:** missing and invalid ratings (a missing key, 1.5, a Choice answer) defer as `missing-answers`/`invalid-answers` without escalation even when another action is rated 0.9; uncertain selections defer with `escalationBlocked: generation-unavailable` in Jev-only mode or without an LLM key.
- **Escalate:** uncertain (0.6) and no-fit (0.1) selections escalate once to level 2, or to level 3 when the route answer ranks it highest, offering generation only actions rated at least 0.5; the root route records `level1→levelN`. A refused escalation reservation defers with `budget-exhausted` and no generation.
- **Routing modes:** non-speech triggers send one combined request; speech sends route/reflection/action-gate questions first and only the ratings second, never batching them; an oversized combined request falls back to the dependent path before dispatch; addressed speech with a closed action gate escalates as `no-candidates`.
- **Safety:** unoffered answers are recorded and never executed; a background decision paused mid-request is cancelled and a late answer changes nothing; an exhausted budget dispatches nothing; refused, unavailable and invalid rating requests fail the job with that outcome and never escalate; an uncertain combined request fails as `uncertain` and keeps its reservation charged.
- **CR02:** a greeting uses one level-2 mini request with low effort and a 1,024-token ceiling (1,800 when actor invention is enabled), an all-optional schema and no harness; an exceptional event queues reflection while the decision completes and simulation time advances; two consecutive decisions through the real direct adapters send fresh bodies (`store:false`, no continuation fields, current instructions); levels 2/3/4 forward mini/complex models, low/low/high effort and 1,024/4,096/8,192 tokens; Jev-only mode offers no generative route and substitutes nothing; refused, unavailable, invalid, uncertain and cancelled outcomes stay distinct in jobs and trace roots; fixture traces cover native, level 1 act/escalate, levels 2–4, speech and each failure outcome. Level-5 harness dispatch with fresh sessions is covered by the existing `cognition.test.ts`.

## Payload inspection

Sizes are UTF-8 bytes for both providers (Jev's own request limit is checked separately in serialized characters); token counts are bytes/4–bytes/3 estimates, not tokenizer counts.

Every request stayed within its level limits and no model-facing text contained `undefined`, `NaN` or `[object Object]`. Meaningful empty facts survived: empty commitments render “None supplied.”, no plan renders “No remaining chosen work.”, and a missing food supply renders “I have no food.” in both the Jev state and `## Me`. A real spoken promise created an active commitment that the next decision listed under `## My commitments`. Oversized output (beyond the 40,000-byte advertised envelope), a response with two operation kinds and an unoffered action handle were each rejected as `invalid` after exactly one generation request, with no world effect and no paid repair. An earlier limit of 16,384 visible bytes for level 2 was withdrawn because it contradicted the envelope advertised to the model.

Null/empty scaffolding does **not** yet disappear: `## General knowledge notepad` is empty JSON, `## Me` keeps trailing spaces from empty values, the Jev rating state carries empty values, and every generation context ends with an empty `privateInventionResults` array. These are recorded as findings for their owners. The greeting request (19,173 bytes plus a 7,598-byte schema with only 1,616 bytes of accepted text) far exceeds the 2,000-byte greeting target, and a near-quota accepted snapshot (79,708 bytes) is refused before dispatch; see [CG09](../limits/cognition.md#cg09).

## Native scenario and Intelligence panel

An ad-hoc script (not committed) started the real game server with god mode, a disposable database it created and later dropped, a scripted fixture Jev/LLM client and a network guard, then drove six decisions through real routing, admission and native simulation: autonomous hungry Ada **acted** (combined request; a berry was eaten on the next native step, 3 → 2), addressed speech **continued** (dependent request; `continued`), a greeting **escalated** to level 2 and its spoken reply was admitted, missing ratings **deferred**, a routing request was **refused**, and a generation with unknown completion ended **uncertain**. The escalation trace showed the dependent routing and level-1 selection requests, `Escalate · No suitable action`, the escalated LM request (`gpt-5-mini · fast · low effort · ≤ 1024 output tokens`) and response, admission, and per-level cost. The narrow-screen sheet rendered the same rows without overflow. Inspection found and fixed two readability defects before this record: action-rating questions were not grouped (each embedded its own option label) and their targets lacked descriptions.

## Review

A five-dimension review of the full branch diff (routing, ledger, outcomes, panel, documentation and tests), each followed by an adversarial verifier, confirmed 25 low/medium findings and refuted 13. Fixed in the final commit: a refused escalation now restores the level-1 route and records a blocked selection stage; escalations record the actions they offered, so responses show descriptions rather than raw handles; a failed escalation keeps its level-1 outcome in the job result; schema-size refusals report their real cause; ledger sizes use one unit; the level-2 limit table uses the real 1,024-token ceiling; `actor-unavailable` shows as canceled and coalesced opportunities render; the outcome filter matches status as well as disposition; the routing stage labels the routed level; `ai-director.test.ts` gained a network guard; and documentation overclaims were corrected (unoffered answers, ledger coverage of embeddings, relevance reuse for non-speech level-2–4 routes, escalation per resolution, adapter-fixture scope, this report's counts and units). Embedding reservations remain outside the ledger ([CR01](../maintainers/cognition-redesign.md#cr01--separate-model-text-from-execution-metadata)).

## Live check attempt (September 29)

Mike approved a live check under the standing $10 task ceiling. A script ran the real server code on a throwaway database with the configured Macrofold/OpenRouter BYOK route, a per-account cap of `AI_BUDGET_USD=2`, embeddings and the reflection harness disabled, and network access limited to the configured Macrofold host. It covered four decisions: hungry Ada with berries, “carry on” while busy, hungry Ada without food, and a greeting.

**Result: blocked before any model call.** The configured `MACROFOLD_BASE_URL` is a local service (`http://localhost:3210`) that was not running, so each decision's first Jev routing request was refused locally within 3–12 ms (`fetch failed`) and the job failed as `uncertain` with no world effect and no retry. No request reached a provider; actual spend is **$0** (additional and cumulative Jev cost $0). The throwaway ledger conservatively charged each refused attempt its $0.005 reservation as uncertain ($0.02 simulated). That confirms the recorded Macrofold mapping gap: a refused connection is certain non-dispatch and should be `unavailable` with nothing charged. Direct Jev is not configured (no Typesafe key), so no alternative route existed. Live verification remains open.

## Findings

- The existing director test harness reached `https://api.openai.com/v1/embeddings` with its non-credential fixture key through background recall indexing; this happened in two baseline runs before the harness was fixed (`OPENAI_EMBEDDING_API_KEY: ''`). The request could not authenticate and spent nothing. Other harnesses are listed for audit in [TODO](../maintainers/TODO.md#level-1-decisions-follow-up).
- Admission queues a known eat whose food left the inventory during the decision and blocks it only at execution (`not-edible`): no effect occurs, but the job reports `responded`. Tracked in [TODO](../maintainers/TODO.md#level-1-decisions-follow-up).
- Direct-provider level-2–4 models are unpriced, so successful replies settle as uncertain at the full $0.25 reservation (fixture: three replies totalled 0.750126 in simulated accounting). Tracked in [TODO](../maintainers/TODO.md#level-1-decisions-follow-up).
