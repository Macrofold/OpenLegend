**Question semantics, experimentally supported:** “Would I choose this now?” asks for a prediction about one exact act. The world rubric supplied once in the same request describes whether an alternative serves this person's reasons. Those predicates can differ: several actions may fit while no exact action is very predictable. This is a demonstrated wording sensitivity, not access to the model's internal cause.

TypeSafe defines a Noul as the probability of a yes answer; optional true/false criteria define the boundary. It is not an action utility or a calibrated probability of good game conduct. Questions evaluate shared state independently; hundreds of alternative ratings are not a single normalized distribution. Request size may add noise, but no experiment here establishes count-based probability dilution. ### Retained experiment disposition

**Keep the original choice wording and unchanged 0.70 threshold.** E is rejected for default adoption: increased probabilities and one extra damaging outcome do not outweigh explicit blocked-action selection and needless replacement of useful work. F and repeated full criteria remain unadopted request-only diagnostics. The canonical evidence-address and durable-export repairs remain general fixes. No aggression controller, mandatory speech/action pair, paid critic, forced anger, new escalation, model change or world tuning is installed.

The defensible game conclusion is narrower than “Brann would not defend himself.” The original hurt/taunt/withdrawal scene deferred its physical judgments; renewed genuine pressure produces an actual successful dodge with the default. The player hears a recognizable mercenary and sees a consequence. One finite retaliation may also fit, but maximum damage is not the objective. Human enjoyment, repeated reliability and post-contact cessation remain unqualified.

The experiment-checkpoint recommendations below retain their original scope; the source-audit designs linked above now specify readiness, pre-admission durability and encounter continuity through their current owners:

- **CTR07, exact decision meaning:** compare the selected world's existing action-selection question directly with exact-act prediction on impact, withdrawal, active useful work and an explicit temporary blocker. Define whether yes means worthwhile next execution, future plausibility or expected exclusive choice; do not silently substitute one for another. Keep all alternatives and native admission. Reject a wording that merely raises the whole distribution.
- **CTR07/CTR08, readiness and continuity:** present readiness/deadline and current-work identity compactly through native structured fields, with clear continuation versus replacement. Temporarily blocked methods can remain visible without being confused with immediate execution. Before changing admission or filtering, qualify preserving reconsideration after recovery and distinct purposes sharing a physical method. A purpose change must not accidentally restart the same useful movement or strike. The current experiment demonstrates a quality problem, not permission to remove the accepted complete-set contract.
- **CTR07, semantic menu factoring:** test purpose-first then method-conditional judgment against the complete reference map, preserving all relevant/uncertain exact bindings. Measure complete calls, latency, cost and lost useful alternatives; do not equate fewer questions with better cognition or add a second relevance pass by default.
- **CTR08/DE06, clock-aware reaction timing:** isolate cue acquisition, queue admission, preparation, dispatch and commit under the actual selected clock before changing them. A proposed matched slower-clock control can preserve injury, options, model deadlines and dispositions while measuring whether both people have an understandable opportunity to prepare before contact. Use the existing native clock owner; any product pacing change retains PS05/world-clock acceptance. Do not infer component latency from compressed game time or make larger deadlines the treatment.
- **CE05/CTR10, player value:** score readable boundary, successful self-protection, coherent stopping and individual voice separately from retaliation/contact. Use real sustained pressure, real withdrawal, a changed opportunity and a nonviolent disagreement. Reuse retained failures, keep expected outcomes out of prompts and distinguish justified silence from unavailable cognition. Additional funded post-contact observation is needed before claiming cessation quality.
