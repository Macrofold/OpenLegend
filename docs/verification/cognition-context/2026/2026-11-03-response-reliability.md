## Conclusions and next boundary

Contributor's prompted-JSON format is selected; its subsequent default adoption is recorded below. Frozen same-provider inputs show that adding schema text alone is insufficient; removing strict remote response-format enforcement changes useful action selection here. Fresh native pairs confirm actual contributions, without proving the provider's internal cause or general reliability. Open does not qualify: its useful JSON-object proposals retain unsupported properties, the provider changes across arms, and the generic reminder worsens usable output.

Do not manufacture physical follow-through from prose or force preparation to improve its count. Detail access is native-verified, but the watch clarifications and outing alternatives do not establish a repeated failure to execute a clearly chosen, fully specified undertaking. More stages or a reference-wire redesign remain conditional. The narrower worthwhile next quality comparisons stay with CE05: optional voice-example copying, present-versus-completed attribution and voluntary detailed activities when genuinely needed. Original/changed demanded recall works in this bounded sample; spontaneous psychological influence and human enjoyment remain unproven.
