## Conclusions and existing next work

The implementation preserves choices while reducing what must be expanded up front. A character can still decline help, keep an intimate memory private, choose silence or suggest company after work. Those are valuable independent behaviors, not errors to repair into obedient quest-giving. Source-counterfactual answers are stronger recall evidence than expecting a memory to appear in an unrelated invitation.

Contributor is promising for inexpensive ordinary dialogue and explicit recall: it follows changed facts and expresses uncertainty without the source. Its unsolicited filler and pretend handoff prevent a broad quality recommendation. Open also follows changed facts and the completed-work state, but privacy responses and confident improvisation complicate absent-memory grading. This small, partly serial sample establishes neither a universal winner nor stable personality reliability. Real-provider pricing, context and surrounding judgment/embedding work must be considered together.

General improvements retain their existing owners:

- [CE05](../../../maintainers/character-experience.md#ce05--integrated-character-quality-and-complete-cost-qualification): compare response composition on retained placeholder/filler and speech-versus-action failures; prefer better task/operation descriptions and bounded matched prompting/model comparisons before another paid checking layer. Never synthesize a transfer from speech or require every response to include an action.
- [CTR05/CTR07](../../../maintainers/combat-and-threat-responses.md#ctr05--complete-choices-while-working): measure complete threat-catalogue construction/scoring and repeated physical preflight; share real common work and preserve every consequential alternative. World-owned voluntary-action thresholds are a separate tuning question, not an engine cutoff to lower until an NPC attacks.
- [VW09](../../../maintainers/verification-workers.md): qualify usable concurrent character-generation throughput separately from successful isolated execution, original-result recovery and generic fleet capacity. Macrofold admission failures need platform evidence before attributing them to a game preparation step.
- [SC12](../../../maintainers/cognition-context.md#sc12--bind-derivable-metadata-optionally-factor-local-schemas): current write revisions/work-mode projection remains deferred; it was not silently removed to make inputs smaller. Richer memory services and persistent-intention pacing retain their separate design owners.

There is no new world law, forced emotion/action, string blacklist, background inventory reconciler or automatic paid retry in this delivery. Broader enjoyment, population-scale performance, cold/warm retrieval variance and long-term personality reliability remain unqualified.
