# Bounded live playtest protocol

Use this procedure to run a focused check in the real game. Mike plans any separate sessions with human participants under the [human playtest boundary](../../AGENTS.md#completion-and-handoff--every-task).

Use a backed-up or isolated save. Choose the player action and the outcome the check must demonstrate. A controlled provider response can verify the request, reply handling and resulting gameplay. To judge what a live model actually chooses or says, use an authorized live call with an explicit spending cap. Record the receipt and any uncertain charge. A timeout must not repeat a paid request or consume gameplay resources for an action that did not execute. See [verification and spending](../../.agents/rules/verification.md#spending).

## Live invention and survival journey

When qualifying this particular journey, verify a real conversation and later permitted recall, one NPC decision, a generated sling that is crafted and used, finite harvest/cooking/eating, reuse without regeneration, and a separately compatible bow/arrow path. Exercise pause, restart, idempotent resubmission, stale work and uncertain accounting. Keep the relevant acceptance criterion open when a model misroutes, leaks, refuses or produces an invalid definition.

## Continuing an episode

When an episode will be continued, reserve shutdown time to pause and complete an ordinary manual checkpoint after its last committed change. Retain its complete current-format package and metadata outside Git, and verify it through the ordinary saved-world read or load path before dropping the owned database. World dumps and choice exports are diagnostics, not continuation checkpoints: they omit associated persisted personal evidence. If capture cannot finish within the authorized bounds, record the unrecoverable segment and last usable checkpoint; do not claim its new state can resume or combine it with older personal evidence. [The post-meal continuation limit](rivals-and-contested-ground.md#normal-continuation-and-the-npc-to-player-action-path--october-14-2026) demonstrates this distinction.
