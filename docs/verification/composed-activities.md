## Workload and interpretation

Scenarios drive the real `WorldService` and `AiDirector` on a fresh seed-73 world: player text goes through the ordinary action submission (typed commands commit synchronously), NPC decisions are injected structured responses through the ordinary response admission, and time advances through the ordinary navigation-aware world update. Setup edits use service transitions only to move the NPC, refill fullness/energy during multi-hour waits (so starvation does not end them) and occlude a deer. These are native, no-provider runs. They say nothing about how well a live model interprets free wording, and no AX/NAV/REL/AUT acceptance matrix was run.

The earlier stage runs were repeated after the review fixes; the results below are from that final rerun, on the working tree that became `371af491` except for the last cook-form fix, which the authored-description probe checked afterwards. Where the rerun differed from the first run, the difference is noted.

This report preserves the observed results and limitations. The generated probe output files were removed from the current tree at Mike's request on September 30, 2026; the scenarios were not rerun for that documentation change.

## Exact request details

A drop of two stones with `slots {item: stone, quantity: 2, exact}` was queued. A binding that dropped a different amount or item was refused as `slot-mismatch`; a tool reference outside the decision (`campfire` not permitted) was refused as `unpermitted-target`; a stone given as the recipient was refused as `invalid-slot`. An "exact" fulfillment claim that ignored a method slot ("quietly") was refused as `slot-mismatch`; the grounding path instead turns such an unkept detail into a confirmation (see "slot method" under typed commands). The same wording with different amounts created separate pending requests, while an identical repeat reused the existing one without repeated paid work. The world validated afterwards.

## Typed commands

Without any provider: "go to 9, 12" arrived at (9, 12); "drop 2 stones" left 4 carried; "pick up stones" took the whole stack back; "pick up 1 stone" took part of a stack; "drop 99 stones" was refused with "You carry only 4 Small stone in that stack."; "gather wood" gathered; "follow the person" followed and "stop" cancelled it. "Eat 2 berries" with none carried was refused plainly. Negation ("don't pick up the stone"), hypothetical ("I might follow the deer tomorrow") and speech (`say "hello"`) were refused as not actions. "Tend the fire until dawn" became a held revision ("Walk near Banked campfire, then wait there until dawn. Not fulfilled: keep the fire fuelled"). A request id replay returned `duplicate` without a second effect.

After the review fixes, words the parser does not know ("follow the dragon", "dance wildly under the moon") go to interpretation, which is unavailable in this configuration, instead of receiving a false "not in view" refusal. Authored action descriptions such as "Cook one raw meat over the nearby lit campfire before eating it." are no longer refused by the typed parser; interpretation binds them through the exact-description match first. "Cook the meat over the dragon forge" goes to interpretation, while "cook the meat over the fire" with no meat carried is refused as "You are not carrying any meat."

## Navigation

- **Offsets:** at heading 0, "move 3 m right" moved from x = 11 toward −x (the right-hand side in the right-handed, Y-up frame), ending at x = 8.8 within native arrival tolerance. "Move 80 m forward" and "move left" (no distance) were refused with the reason.
- **Approach:** "go to the campfire" stopped 1.41 m from it.
- **Stances:** following 2 m behind settled 2.72 m behind while the NPC travelled +x, then 2.75 m behind after it turned to −z; "on the left" settled on the NPC's left (+x while it travelled +z). Before any observed travel the follower held near.
- **Last-seen pursuit:** with the deer occluded behind real geometry, the follower walked to the recorded point (21, 19), stopped with "reached where the target was last seen; it is not in view", and never moved toward the deer's live position (2.5, 18.4). "Go to where I last saw the deer" later returned to (21, 19). The remembered place stores the label "a deer" and its encounter.
- **Identity:** with a remembered sighting from an old encounter and the same hidden entity visible in a new encounter, typed "go to where I last saw the person" walked to the record's own point (18, 12), not toward the visible person at (13, 12). An interpreted invocation naming the visible person is a fresh snapshot of what is seen now and approaches them; neither path ties a new sighting to an older record by hidden entity ID.
- **Stopping time:** "follow the person until dusk" completed at 18:00. The probe then advanced in 600-second updates that straddled the deadline; the follow's recorded end time was exactly 36,000 s (dusk), confirming the exact-boundary fix. "Follow the person then go to the campfire" was refused: "Following has no natural end, so nothing can come after it; add "until dawn" or put it last."

## Requested activities

## Interrupt then resume

"Gather wood until I have 10" started at 2 wood and fullness 40. "Cook meat at the fire then eat it" with interrupt mode paused the gathering: the running gather step was recorded as "Paused for other work; restarted later as a new step." A second interrupt while cooking was refused ("Other paused work is already waiting to resume"). A same-version save/restore while paused and cooking matched the saved state. After the meal (fullness 77), gathering resumed automatically with a private memory ("I resumed my paused work…"), and finished at exactly 10 wood. In a second run, Stop during the interrupting cook cancelled it and discarded the paused gathering; wood already gathered (12) stayed. The world validated.

## Review

A five-dimension adversarial review (authority/privacy, navigation, composition, parser, performance/lifecycle) of the committed stages produced 40 findings; each was independently verified. Confirmed findings were fixed in the review-fix commit and exercised above: stored refusal left on an accepted revision (world failed to load), slot permission checked after kind (hidden-ID probing), composed targets missing from encounter pins, last-seen identity by hidden entity ID (typed parser, binder and pursuit), labels from live entities, stances that never settled on unwalkable ground, handedness of relative directions, participation check leaking an unseen target leaving, held-activity acceptance committing on failure, enqueue refusing behind a direct action, archived output producers, pile items in compositions, clock-deadline lateness, parser qualifiers/amount/ground-suffix/recipient/source-choice/stop-word issues, authored descriptions refused before interpretation, slot control hiding steps or bypassing the indefinite-follow guard, and job records written as completed on failure.

Refuted by verification: job rows accumulating per typed request (rows are needed for durable retry identity, like other requests). Accepted with a narrower fix: planning and review rejections no longer depend on everything in view, but "interpretation named nothing permitted in view" still reopens when the visible set changes, because that reason does depend on it.

## Second review

A second five-dimension adversarial review covered AG03, BW05, AC11.1 and the first fixes (`829f3b07..371af491`); its 40 findings (some reported by two dimensions) were independently verified, and the confirmed ones were fixed and checked with a targeted probe (the probe gave the bare starting berry bush 12 berries so gathering could run):

- A two-step plan (gather berries, then eat what was gathered) paused mid-gather: the restarted gather became `…:r5` and the eat step's item reference followed it; the world validated while paused, and after resuming both steps completed. Plan revisions went 5 → 6 (paused) → 7 (interrupting plan) → 14 (resumed and finished), never repeating.
- Interrupting while a gather started directly (outside any plan) was refused with "This work is not a chosen plan that can be paused; replace it or let it finish."; the gather kept running and the queued "wait until dusk" card said "Starts after the action already under way ends."
- With the stop time set to dusk, "go to the campfire then gather wood" became a walk followed by a dusk-bounded gather repeat; the walk no longer waited until dusk.
- "Gather berries until I have 5 wood" went to interpretation instead of counting berries; "drop 2 of them" with an item chosen dropped that item; "pick up the items" took the whole pile; "stay by it until dawn" with the campfire selected was admitted.

Other confirmed fixes are exercised only by code reading and the unchanged scenario reruns: paused plans keep their goal from pruning, a paused goal defers rather than blocks, spoken-text bindings are not checked as entities, NPC plan cancellation and leaving the world discard paused work, a refused learned-activity interrupt rolls back its pause, exact pickup amounts equal to a stack stay exact, the interpreter's pile list reports coverage, and the Character panel keys Stop retries to the shown work, keeps unavailable exact details visible and resettable, omits a finished repeat's extra row and refreshes labels when names change. ## World-supplied wording and named times

After Mike made the engine/world boundary a hard rule, a probe exercised differently configured worlds through the same server paths (a setup edit replaced the saved world's `namedTimes`):

- **Bundled world:** the view offered dawn and dusk and the bundled example wording; the no-AI help text listed "until dawn|dusk".
- **A world naming only noon:** the saved world validated; the view, the help text, the follow AI instructions ("until noon to end it") and the follow capability description all said noon. "Wait until noon" was admitted with its deadline at exactly 04:00 of game time from the 08:00 start; "wait until dawn" was not recognized and went to interpretation; a request whose chosen stop time was "dawn" was refused with "A chosen detail is not valid here, such as a stopping time this world does not name."; "follow the person until noon" was admitted.
- **A world naming no times:** the view offered none and the help text had no "until" forms; "wait" answered "Say until when, for example "wait 30 minutes"."; "wait 30 minutes" was admitted.
- **Load validation:** a saved clock policy without `namedTimes` (an older save) was refused, as were a name with punctuation, an hour of 30 and nine names.

Every stage scenario was rerun on the bundled world: results were unchanged except the no-AI help text, which now lists "wait N minutes", "cook ITEM [at NAME]" and the world's own named times.

A three-dimension adversarial review of that change (boundary completeness, correctness, saves and lifecycle) confirmed nine further items, fixed and checked with a probe:

- The AI's structured stopping-time choices are the world's names (dawn or dusk here; forced to null when a world names none), and a proposal naming another time is refused before any paid interpretation.
- AI plans may take a later step's item only from families that record a single item. A plain reference to a harvest step is refused up front ("invalid-plan"); one with a named output port is accepted.
- A stored request whose stopping time the world no longer names still loads, and is refused only when used.
- Clock displays read the world's offset from the view.
- "Cook X" without naming a fire uses the selected target when one is chosen.
- The grounding policy no longer says following has "no sunset stop".

Daylight hours for the renderer and the HUD icon remain their own constants and are tracked under BW06. Two dry-run reviews of those fixes found two more problems, both fixed and checked through the full commit path:

- **HUD clock:** the minute could read one minute early.
- **Cooking with a selection:** "cook the raw meat" with a person selected was rejected as a target mismatch. It now answers "A person is not something to cook over; select what to cook over, or clear the selection." With the campfire selected, or nothing selected, it cooks at the fire. "Pick up the raw meat then cook the raw meat" with the pile selected is admitted, because the pickup uses the selection.

Six more dry-run reviews found problems with how a selection applies, all fixed and checked by probes:

- **Two parses disagreed (`57a004d8`):** the parse that decides which step names a chosen item did not treat steps as part of a sequence, while the real parse did, so they could disagree about which step owns the item. Both now build each step's scope with one helper. With the meat chosen, "gather fiber then cook the meat then drop the item" goes to interpretation whether the reeds, the fire or nothing is selected.
- **A selection limited steps it did not match (`a65f5938`):** with the reeds selected, "gather fiber then gather wood" was refused ("No wood to gather is in view"), because the selection limited every gather step. A selection now limits a step only when that step's words match it (for a pile, when it holds the named item); some step must still use it (see the next item). That request now gathers fiber from the reeds and then wood from the branches; "go to the reeds then go to the fire" with the fire selected walks to both. A lone request whose selection does not match now says so instead of claiming nothing is in view: "gather wood" with the reeds selected answers "River reeds does not match "wood"; select a match, or clear the selection." Lone requests that match their selection, and requests with nothing selected, are unchanged.
- **A sequence could ignore the selection (`8f662a5f`):** a walk step records only a position, and the thing walked to is listed as what the request is about. The check that a request uses the player's selection skipped requests whose steps named no thing, so with the deer selected "go to the fire then wait until dusk" was admitted and walked to the fire. The check now also applies when the request lists what it is about; the same activity built without a selection and committed with the deer selected is refused ("target-mismatch"), and with the fire selected it is admitted. The typed parser says it first: "A deer is not used by any step; select something a step names, or clear the selection." A request of only places and waits ("wait 5 minutes then move 3 m right") still ignores a selection, as before.
- **A contradictory refusal (`47d9ee0b`):** with a dead hare selected and a living one in view, "follow the hare" answered "A hare does not match "hare"". A selection that fits the words but cannot take the action now gets "A hare cannot be followed; select something else, or clear the selection."
- **Remaining gaps in the same rule (`518e50b2`):** "follow it" with the dead hare or the fire selected went to interpretation instead of that refusal; in "go to the hare then follow the hare" with the dead hare selected, the follow step quietly used the living hare; and a lone "go to where I last saw the deer" with the fire selected and the deer still in view ignored the selection. Each is now refused plainly ("A hare cannot be followed…", "Step 2 (follow the hare): A hare cannot be followed…", "Banked campfire is not the one you last saw (a deer); select that one, or clear the selection."). A walk to a sighting no longer in view names only a place, so it ignores a selection, as it does inside a sequence. With the living hare selected, or the deer itself, or nothing selected, these requests run as before; "gather stones then pick up the stones" with the stone outcrop selected still gathers there and then picks up from the pile. A final review corrected two wordings (`bfe7a8da`): "take it" with the wrong pile selected now says "Items on the ground does not hold Raw meat; select the pile it lies in, or clear the selection." instead of claiming the pile cannot be picked up from, and the last-seen refusal names the sighting as recorded rather than lowercasing a known name.

Every earlier scenario and probe was rerun after these fixes with no differences in results.
