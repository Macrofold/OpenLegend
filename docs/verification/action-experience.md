## Workload and interpretation

The native scenario starts from seed 42 with Ada's authored knife and a full-health hare at a known nearby position. Actual native commands produce an equip, one miss, three contributing hits, harvest of two raw meat and two bone fragments, cooking and eating. Discovery proposes four connected methods; native retention/replay then exercises their real downstream execution. These are synthetic controlled experiences, not evidence that a fresh character spontaneously discovered the method.

The live scenario supplies those actual synthetic **own** experiences without acquisitions, admits an idle learning assessment, then puts hungry Ada beside a fresh full-health hare. Her biography and empty starting goals remain; no hunting goal or forced method choice is installed. Only Ada's cognition is enabled to isolate accounting. The automatic director processes real action-result/perception/body triggers as native time advances in five-second increments. This qualifies acquisition and use in that environment; it does not establish universal survival, optimal weapon choice or discovery from no prior experience.

Early fixtures used the obsolete support name `ground`. They exercised native actions and model context but were not valid whole-world save fixtures. The active checkpoint test exposed this. Corrected fixtures use `terrain` and full world validation; final native/checkpoint/automatic qualification uses the correction. Earlier receipts and outcomes remain historical evidence rather than being silently reclassified.

## Persistence, capacity and preparation

PostgreSQL persisted 510 scoped occurrences, paged them in five reads, and reopened with 128 resident records and retained permitted methods. A one-byte SQL hydration allowance returned no decoded record. Concurrent publication and duplicate structural-key rollback were checked separately. An active native Cook → Eat frontier survived a real saved checkpoint and completed with the already consumed raw item still debited only once. Restoring a pre-revocation checkpoint did not resurrect revoked method access.

The native 100,000 admitted-record boundary explicitly refused a new recorded action. Recursive node/expansion, renderer byte and invalid frontier admission checks refused unsupported work. Optional retention uses native admission in a discarded draft before paid dispatch; a later failed retain aborts publication rather than committing a partial catalogue/acquisition. Coalesced pending IDs do not replace durable row positions or canonical history.

The catalogue preparation fixture contained 256 definitions, 64 private acquisitions and four offered methods, measured over 25 passes. Median preparation was below one millisecond in these runs, with 2,397 serialized offer bytes; exact median/max measurements remain in the native log. These are local CPU measurements, not PostgreSQL p95, multi-agent throughput or maximum-retention qualification. Broader population/server/browser performance remains under existing PF/AG/CR trackers.
