# Playing from one character’s point of view

[Unreal player experience](README.md) · [Desktop access and qualification](desktop-access.md) · [Landscapes and building](landscapes-and-building.md)

An Open Legend player stands on a ridge, sees the road to a village, hears someone calling from below and chooses whether to investigate. They can look down the valley, read what they heard, approach, speak and return later to someone who remembers the encounter differently. The player needs to understand what their character can sense, what they can try, and what actually happened. The same rules apply to a shared room, a crowded marketplace or a world whose inhabitants have an entirely different set of senses.

## Entering and controlling a character

The inputs to ordinary play are the selected world and timeline, the signed-in player's current viewing and control rights, the character's permitted information and the activities offered by that world. The outputs are a personal view, opportunities to act and an understandable account of each submitted intention. Choosing a character, receiving a view and possessing control are distinct. A signed-in player without an assigned character can understand their next permitted step. An observer can read the view their role grants. Only the current controller can act for a character. A creator opening a tool or a second copy of the game does not take over an existing character.

Before accepting gameplay input, show an understandable entry or loading state until the player is entitled to the selected view and a complete playable scene is visible. Losing focus releases held input. Returning to a competing session follows the world's explicit resume or takeover process. A quiet pause keeps permitted views readable and disables stale actions. If rights change, immediately remove the affected facts and actions even while paused, including from focus descriptions, previously opened panels and pending results. Restoring control starts from the current eligible view. [Identity and control](../../identity-and-references.md) · [Access and return](desktop-access.md)

## Looking, moving and choosing an action

- **Ordinary framing.** Start a new character view centred on the character with perspective lock following the character’s physical gaze. Preserve an existing camera preference on a same-character, same-timeline return when it remains applicable. Provide a discoverable horizon-facing view for exploring a valley and an equally accessible way back to the character and the ordinary framing. A player can orbit, tilt, pan, zoom, change projection or recenter without turning a character or issuing a movement order. Deliberate physical Look, when supported by the world and accepted, can change what that character sees. Panning above a roof cannot reveal an occupant the character cannot sense. Held see-through reveal can make covering scenery transparent for an already visible person or object. It gives no evidence of a hidden person.
- **Travel and targeting.** Preserve click-to-move, continuous world-focused steering, object inspection and context-sensitive action discovery. Pointing at a visible surface in a multistorey building identifies the surface actually under the pointer. Permitted objects and people support the same inspection and action choices from the scene or the interface. On slopes, through cutaway roofs and in a crowd, the highlight, label and action refer to the same visible subject. A click that started on a trader must never become an attack on a passerby who occupied the same pixels on release. If the original target, offered action or owning input mode changes during a gesture, cancel that gesture or report why the original intention can no longer proceed. A lower-detail depiction preserves the same target and usable surface.
- **One gesture, one result.** A camera drag ends as a drag. Closing a picker with a world click does not also walk. A wheel over a panel scrolls its contents. Escape closes the active child control before its parent and returns focus to a useful place. Typing or confirming a composed character in chat cannot send a message, swing a weapon or start walking as a side effect. Losing pointer capture or focus ends the held gesture. Reopening a panel never restarts a released movement key.
- **One deliberate commitment.** Show available actions on request, with the selected subject, held item, known cost or blocker and any consequential choice before confirmation. Preview, accepted intention, work underway, interruption, refusal and completed effect each have their own meaning. A refusal names only a reason this player can know. When someone moves the chest, the former target's reach can expire while its panel is open. No substitution of another chest or transfer from the remembered contents is allowed. A combat shortcut uses the action actually offered for the chosen subject. It must never silently replace a failed conversation, observation or noncombat choice with an attack.

## Scenes, speech and what two people can know

Present each fact through its admitted sense, precision, observation time and identity. A body, a recognized face, carried equipment, spoken words and the location of a sound can have different evidence. Seeing a stranger by a bridge does not reveal their name, inventory or destination. If a character loses sight, show only the retained description and time the character can still recall. A remembered position may be useful on a map. It cannot track, attack or inspect a person's present condition. Withdrawal of a fact removes it from highlights, descriptions, portraits, captions, names, reflections, shadows, sounds, markers and accessible descriptions wherever applicable. Other still-permitted facts remain. Avoid revealing why a private fact is missing when the viewer has no evidence of the reason.

A player can address a particular person or speak to the scene. Volume, physical reception, recognition and words understood determine the separate result for each listener. A visible associated speaker has an overhead caption. For an off-screen or unassociated sound, supply only its permitted direction or region. With no bearing, a neutral caption identifies that a sound was heard. Partial speech shows recognizable fragments and explicit gaps. A detected voice with no intelligible words gives no quotations. When several people speak at once, captions queue and heard entries remain findable in the character's Events. Muting playback, panning the camera, using captions or switching audio devices changes none of the evidence. Old lines read from history or after reconnecting never play as new speech. [Hearing and captions](../../hearing-and-speech.md) · [Perceived Events](../../perceived-world-events.md)

For example, a player outside a workshop may hear part of a remark while a companion inside sees the speaker and recognizes their face. The first player gets no name by clicking the subtitle. A guard across the road might notice a gesture, stay on duty and later describe it. Another passerby may have no account to offer. Camera distance, graphics settings, the number of people drawn and opening a view of the scene do not decide whether either NPC observed, understood, chose to act or remembered. A visible reaction must have a basis in that person's behavior. Access to another character's private thoughts requires its own permission and context. A dark-adapted creature or an inhabitant who senses vibration uses its world's admitted cues without importing ordinary human vision or language. ## The interface that makes the world playable

| Player's task | Required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Orient, act quickly and investigate | The Gildmoss interface theme keeps the world foremost while exposing known condition and time, minimap with its own zoom, item hotbar, narrator feedback and Events. Preserve the selected visual themes and character-panel style choices. Sense guides and markers obey knowledge. Looking closer in the scene, the In view list or Events opens the appropriate permitted inspection. Independent person and object panels preserve their targets. |
| Understand a character or place | Character exposes the actual body's equipment, attributes and permitted mind records. Skills, Journal, Map and Crafting remain discoverable. Journal carries personal records. Events presents what was perceived in chronological context, including speech alongside actions. Search, filters, unknown names, missed time intervals and history links respect the same information rights as the scene. |
| Work with possessions and other people | Open two eligible inventories together, inspect a specific object, move an exact eligible quantity, equip or use the bound item, and negotiate a trade. Identical names do not establish interchangeability or ownership. Changed custody, capacity or reach can invalidate an offer. Show the committed result to each participant at their permitted detail. Returning to a visible stack does not convert a pending trade into a second offer. |
| Use an activity the world supports | Contextual Say and Act, work, investigation, survival, care, conflict, cooperation, crafting, invention and construction use the installed world's available options, consent, prerequisites, time and consequences. Expose start, progress when known, interruption and result as appropriate. Attaching finite attention to a walk retains its actual deadline. Stopping that watch need not stop compatible travel. Finding a workshop or an appealing tool does not itself teach a recipe, grant materials or create the item. |
| Read, navigate and change preferences | Support scalable legible text, captions, contrast, reduced motion, visible focus, labeled controls, keyboard navigation, non-drag alternatives and platform text composition. Essential costs, outcomes and blockers must be available without hover, color or sound alone. Enlarging text, narrowing a window, receiving an update or opening a second inspection preserves the readable task, selected subject, draft and scroll position while they remain eligible. |

Speech remains possible where the world admits it even if a generated reply is unavailable. Silence supplies no evidence that the NPC deliberately snubbed the speaker. A private draft, suggested action or narration cannot establish that words were said, that a player consented or that a physical action succeeded. Creator editing, operations and privileged investigation stay in their explicitly authorized contexts. Leaving one clears its protected details before ordinary play resumes. ## Interrupted and shared play

| Scenario | Observable outcome |
| --- | --- |
| A response is late, duplicated, or connection drops immediately after Send, Trade or Build | Stop issuing stale commands. Keep the original request distinguishable from a draft or a new attempt. Recover its outcome under current permission. A confirmed result appears once. An unresolved result stays uncertain and offers the applicable original-outcome check. Reopening play, editing text or checking status never repeats the transfer, speech, build or paid generation. |
| Someone locks a door during the player's approach, or another player removes a supporting wall during placement | The visible intention can be corrected or refused. The player sees the current accessible result and can choose again. The player cannot pass through the locked door or stand on a floor whose supporting structure is gone unless the world actually permits it. Each observer receives the consequences they could perceive. |
| The app sleeps, quits, changes character or rejoins after a host restart | Present the host's current clock, control, actions and latest durable state. Resume a supported same-character timeline with applicable preferences. Refresh changing facts, clear transient captions and invalidate lost targets. A different character, world, restored timeline or access role cannot inherit out-of-scope private panels, drafts, hidden objects or pending orders. A delayed result from it cannot repopulate the screen. |
| One player opens a menu, leaves the valley or closes the game while someone else is playing | Apply the world's activity, pause and time-control rules. An individual menu or loading screen cannot arbitrarily stop another character’s time. If the host pauses, visual time and action progress hold at the last admitted point. After resuming, show actual consequences. A remote valley retains its people, places and accepted changes under the world's progression policy. |

A complete acceptance journey follows two embodied characters with different knowledge through an encounter, a conversation, loss of sight, an interrupted action and return on the same timeline. Repeat the critical step after authority changes. Check the scene, map, audio, Events, caption, interaction and accessible description together. A correct subtitle cannot compensate for a hidden speaker exposed by a shadow, menu option or positional sound.
