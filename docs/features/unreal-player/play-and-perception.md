# Playing from one character’s point of view

[Unreal player experience](README.md) · [Desktop access and qualification](desktop-access.md) · [Landscapes and building](landscapes-and-building.md)

A player can look down a street, hear someone calling and decide whether to approach. The scene, controls and account of what happened reflect that character’s senses and opportunities. The same rules apply to a crowded marketplace and to a world whose inhabitants sense their surroundings differently.

## Entering and controlling a character

The inputs to ordinary play are the selected world and timeline, the signed-in player's current viewing and control rights, the character's permitted information and the activities offered by that world. The outputs are a personal view, opportunities to act and an understandable account of each submitted intention. Choosing a character, receiving a view and possessing control are distinct. A signed-in player without an assigned character can understand their next permitted step. An observer can read the view their role grants. Only the current controller can act for a character. A creator opening a tool or a second copy of the game does not take over an existing character.

Show the loading state until the player has permission and the first complete playable frame is ready. Losing focus releases held input. A sole eligible play session enters or resumes automatically, including on reload or return to the game. If another session holds control of the same character, switching requires an explicit takeover. During a manual world pause the player can read, inspect and cancel eligible work at frozen time. Movement and new work wait for resume. Remove withdrawn facts and actions even from open panels, focus descriptions and pending results while paused. Restore control against the current permitted view. [Identity and control](../../identity-and-references.md) · [Access and return](desktop-access.md)

## Looking, moving and choosing an action

- **Ordinary framing.** Start a new character view centred on the character with perspective lock following the character’s physical gaze. Preserve an existing camera preference on a same-character, same-timeline return when it remains applicable. Provide a discoverable horizon-facing view for exploring a valley and an equally accessible way back to the character and the ordinary framing. A player can orbit, tilt, pan, zoom, change projection or recenter without turning a character or issuing a movement order. Deliberate physical Look, when supported by the world and accepted, can change what that character sees. Panning above a roof cannot reveal an occupant the character cannot sense. Holding see-through reveal shows only the silhouettes of eligible, already perceived subjects over covering scenery. The roof or tree stays visible, and a hidden occupant stays hidden. Releasing the control or losing focus ends the reveal.
- **Travel and targeting.** Preserve click-to-move, continuous world-focused steering, object inspection and context-sensitive action discovery. Pointing at a visible surface in a multistorey building identifies the surface actually under the pointer. Permitted objects and people support the same inspection and action choices from the scene or the interface. On slopes, through cutaway roofs and in a crowd, the highlight, label and action refer to the same visible subject. A click that started on a trader must never become an attack on a passerby who occupied the same pixels on release. Named actions stay bound to the subject and action chosen on press and are rechecked on release. In deliberate physical aim, release picks the current eligible body or point on visible scenery or ground. Aiming into empty sky gives no valid point. A target stepping aside can expose the ground behind it. If readiness or confirmation is pending, freeze that release choice. Losing input ownership cancels the gesture. A lower-detail depiction preserves the same target and usable surface.
- **One gesture, one result.** A camera drag ends as a drag. Closing a picker with a world click does not also walk. A wheel over a panel scrolls its contents. Escape closes the active child control before its parent and returns focus to a useful place. Typing or confirming a composed character in chat cannot send a message, swing a weapon or start walking as a side effect. Losing pointer capture or focus ends the held gesture. Reopening a panel never restarts a released movement key.
- **One deliberate commitment.** Show the selected subject, held item and known cost or blocker when offering an action. Safe single actions such as Move or Equip stay direct. Show a review when the action calls for a consequential choice. Preview, accepted intention, work underway, interruption, refusal and completed effect each have their own meaning. A refusal names only a reason this player can know. When someone moves the chest, the former target's reach can expire while its panel is open. No substitution of another chest or transfer from the remembered contents is allowed. A combat shortcut uses the action actually offered for the chosen subject. It must never silently replace a failed conversation, observation or noncombat choice with an attack.

## Scenes, speech and what two people can know

Present each fact through its admitted sense, precision, observation time and identity. A body, a recognized face, carried equipment, spoken words and the location of a sound can have different evidence. Seeing a stranger by a bridge does not reveal their name, inventory or destination. When sight is lost, distinguish remembered visual details from current information the character can still hear or otherwise sense. A remembered position may be useful on a map. It cannot track, attack or inspect a person's present condition. Withdrawal of a fact removes it from highlights, descriptions, portraits, captions, names, reflections, shadows, sounds, markers and accessible descriptions wherever applicable. Other still-permitted facts remain. Avoid revealing why a private fact is missing when the viewer has no evidence of the reason.

A player can address a particular person or speak to the scene. Volume, physical reception, recognition and words understood determine the separate result for each listener. A visible associated speaker has an overhead caption. For an off-screen or unassociated sound, a permitted approximate bearing applies only while valid. When it no longer applies, or none was received, use a neutral caption. Moving the camera cannot triangulate an undisclosed speaker. Partial speech shows recognizable fragments and explicit gaps. A detected voice with no intelligible words gives no quotations. When several people speak at once, captions follow the bounded display rules for committed speech. Overflow or suspension produces a missed-caption notice leading to heard history. Muting audio keeps enabled captions available. Sound settings and camera movement never change the evidence. Old lines read from history or after reconnecting never play as new speech. [Hearing and captions](../../hearing-and-speech.md) · [Perceived Events](../../perceived-world-events.md)

For example, a player outside a workshop may hear part of a remark while a companion inside sees the speaker and recognizes their face. If the outside listener cannot recognize the voice, opening the subtitle supplies no name. A familiar voice can be recognized from sufficient sound evidence even when some words are unclear. Use a name only if the listener also knows it. A guard across the road might notice a gesture, stay on duty and later describe it. Another passerby may have no account to offer. Camera distance, graphics settings, the number of people drawn and opening a view of the scene do not decide whether either NPC observed, understood, chose to act or remembered. A visible reaction must have a basis in that person's behavior. Access to another character's private thoughts requires its own permission and context. A dark-adapted creature or an inhabitant who senses vibration uses its world's admitted cues without importing ordinary human vision or language. Wind, water and ambient music can establish a place. A footstep, a voice that sounds like someone familiar, a reflection or a moving shadow can also give a person away. Every such cue must carry only the permitted event, apparent direction, identity and precision. Playing a permitted utterance must not drop words the character heard merely because the viewing camera is farther away. Offer volume, mute and captions independently. Speculative feedback can indicate a request in progress. It must clear or correct if the world refuses it. An uncommitted impact must never read as confirmed damage. Live player microphones and additional voiced production require separate scope. [Bundled physical sound](../../worlds/base/physical-sound.md)

Speech remains possible where the world admits it even if a generated reply is unavailable. Silence supplies no evidence that the NPC deliberately snubbed the speaker. A private draft, suggested action or narration cannot establish that words were said, that a player consented or that a physical action succeeded. Creator editing, operations and privileged investigation stay in their explicitly authorized contexts. Leaving one clears its protected details before ordinary play resumes. ## Interrupted and shared play

| Scenario                                                                                                        | Observable outcome                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A reply is late, duplicated or lost after Send, Trade or Build                                                  | Check the original request under current permissions. A confirmed result appears once. While a result is unknown, say so and offer a status check when the original request can be identified. Reopening play, editing a draft, changing devices or checking status never resends the action or incurs a second charge. A new intention requires a new player choice.                                                                          |
| Someone locks a door during the player's approach, or another player removes a supporting wall during placement | The visible intention can be corrected or refused. The player sees the current accessible result and can choose again. The player cannot pass through the locked door or stand on a floor whose supporting structure is gone unless the world actually permits it. Each observer receives the consequences they could perceive.                                                                                                                |
| The app sleeps, quits, changes character or rejoins after a host restart                                        | Present the host's current clock, control, actions and latest durable state. Resume a supported same-character timeline with applicable preferences. Refresh changing facts, clear transient captions and invalidate lost targets. A different character, world, restored timeline or access role cannot inherit out-of-scope private panels, drafts, hidden objects or pending orders. A delayed result from it cannot repopulate the screen. |
| One player opens a menu, leaves the valley or closes the game while someone else is playing                     | Apply the world's activity, pause and time-control rules. An individual menu or loading screen cannot arbitrarily stop another character’s time. If the host pauses, visual time and action progress hold at the last admitted point. After resuming, show actual consequences. A remote valley retains its people, places and accepted changes under the world's progression policy.                                                          |

If updates arrive out of order, a recent return or old result must never rewind the character to an obsolete position, re-enable a revoked action or reveal a previously hidden fact. When continuity is unknown, visibly resynchronize from the current permitted view before allowing affected actions. A tentative movement may ease into the confirmed position. Its animation cannot outrun physical reach, become a teleport past a barrier or rewrite what a witness received. A restart recovers the latest complete durable world or explicit restoration, with the appropriate time and permissions. An on-screen preview is never a saved change.

A complete acceptance journey follows two embodied characters with different knowledge through an encounter, a conversation, loss of sight, an interrupted action and return on the same timeline. Repeat the critical step after authority changes. Check the scene, map, audio, Events, caption, interaction and accessible description together. A correct subtitle cannot compensate for a hidden speaker exposed by a shadow, menu option or positional sound.
