# Art, animation and agent-led production

World creators can request or select art within their permissions. Access to private sources, paid generation and publication each requires its own grant. Authorized production agents can prepare and deliver the selected work without routine manual editor steps. The person directing the art retains the choices reserved to them.

## From a creative brief to a playable release

| Input or event | Required outcome |
| --- | --- |
| A creator requests a study, an improvement or a playable release | Resolve the world/style, subject, player viewpoint, intended uses and visible states, locked design choices, permitted references, degree of variation, spending authority, intended audience, selection authority and requested stopping point. A study ends with candidates and evidence. A request to refine a selected design preserves its identity and identifies what changed. |
| A permitted source already fits | Reuse it exactly or combine approved parts. The intended use determines which checks a generated, sourced or hand-authored piece needs. Qualification applies to the declared family, states, viewing range and platforms. |
| New work is needed | Preserve permitted source files and how the candidate was derived. For a view-only reference, record only what its terms allow. A concept may be painted. Judge the finished piece in the world, with other assets, at walking and inspection distance, moving through representative light. If a provider restricts automation or use of an input, choose a permitted path or report the blocked step. |
| A candidate is returned | Record separately whether it exists, passed named checks, was preserved, was selected by a person or authorized rule, and was integrated into an exact release. A successful export, good still or agent recommendation establishes only that result. Missing required materials, rights or evidence block the affected use. Other candidates retain their results. |
| A family is revised or two agents publish alternatives | Each change identifies its starting selection and affected dependents. Present conflicting selections for resolution and preserve both candidates. Players receive a complete compatible release or retain the prior permitted one. An unfinished replacement never silently changes an existing selection. |

A release identifies its exact inputs and outputs, permitted audiences and uses, attribution, platforms and limitations. An authorized worker can reproduce the selected job on a clean machine from permitted inputs. Use matched moving captures and a packaged game to qualify new families, changed behavior and combined game releases. Routine selection and checks can be delegated within the creator’s stated scope.

## What makes an asset ready for play

| Asset family | Observable checks |
| --- | --- |
| Trees, vegetation, rocks and terrain dressing | Silhouette, scale, grounding, response to wind/light where appropriate, species variation and near-to-far continuity hold on a moving route. A whole pine stand can share a small set of designs. A tree counted by the world as cover or timber has a consistent outline, location and relevant state across supported views. Decorative lookalikes must not advertise a use the world forbids. |
| Buildings and construction pieces | Compatible walls, corners, floors, roofs, stairs, doors and bridge ends join at their declared sizes. Demonstrate a traversable assembly and an allowed edit in play, including its interior, clearance, thresholds, surface contact and how it reads from afar. An intact building image is insufficient evidence for editable pieces. |
| Portable objects, tools, armor and furniture | Material, scale, front/up direction, grip, resting point, mounting points and supported open/closed, worn, carried, placed or damaged states read correctly for each declared use. A tool must fit the hand during use as well as on an inventory card. Its artwork cannot confer abilities or ownership. |
| People, clothing and creatures | Test representative wearers or species, including the limits of the claimed fit, across the supported motions, equipment and viewing distances. Preserve recognizable silhouette, face, coloration, identity marks, side-specific features and accessories in close and ordinary views. A humanoid walk qualifies no bird, four-legged animal or unsupported body. |

For the bundled world, honor the accepted grounded, painted-looking 3D direction without a pixel filter. Objects retain believable construction and scale. Use recognizably different forms and local material variation before adding fine texture. Moving sunlight must change the visible lighting of the model. A highlight permanently painted on its shaded side fails. Inspect seams, missing surfaces or textures, flickering detail, repeated silhouettes and material response in context. Qualification sets no universal minimum polygon count. Give detail to what players can actually see and recognize.

Count distinct designs, qualified variations and placed instances separately. Ten bridge components can make many crossings. Combinations within a tested family’s fit, motion, materials and playable uses inherit its evidence. Check each variation against those bounds. Recheck the affected uses when a change can invalidate them, such as a longer sleeve catching a tool. Placing another approved pine requires no new art approval or game build. Measure accepted yield, cost, repair time, download size, memory and scene performance before promising production volume.

## Bodies, wardrobe and motion

- A world chooses its appearance families, controls, compatible parts and visible states. Keep each person's appearance recipe independent of the currently worn items. For bundled human families preserve real stature and proportions, sculpted facial form, hair, independent muscle mass and fullness, and the resulting muscle relief. Only expose supported combinations. Identify an unsupported request and retain the last valid selection. Another world can use a creature family with entirely different controls.
- Select the actual equipped item for a compatible wearer, under the world's equipment rules. Fitted coverage stays independent of removable outer clothing. A shirt can pass between two compatible people. The first wearer loses that shirt, the second gains the same physical item, and eligible viewers and a returning player see the correct outfits. Check underlayers, seams, elbows, shoulders, neck, hands and soles during bends, raised arms and transitions. Cosmetics, hair volume and garment bulk cannot silently increase reach, protection or body size.
- Map approved motion to the admitted action and its progress. Test standing, starting, stopping, turning, pace changes and transitions on actual slopes. Check hand/tool contact and foot planting, slipping, clipping and loop seams at the permitted closest view. If the world interrupts work or rejects movement, the depiction must reflect the resulting state. A swing, sound or clip ending cannot independently cause a hit, movement or completed job.
- Reuse compatible motion across wearers. A new generated motion is a candidate until mapped onto the target and verified in play, including different equipment where claimed. A truthful neutral pose with an allowed progress cue may cover an unavailable work motion. If identifying the active state is essential for safe interaction, the fallback must convey that state or the affected use stays unavailable. Qualify facial, hair, cloth and creature movement individually when a release claims them.

## Art created during play

An invention, its appearance, a player's knowledge of it and an instance owned by the player have separate outcomes. The world's invention and equipment rules decide what exists and what can be made, used, transferred or learned. Art changes only its permitted depiction. [Invention readiness, review and live use](../../invention-foundation.md#6-readiness-review-and-live-use) · [Knowledge](../../knowledge.md)

| Situation | What a player or creator can rely on |
| --- | --- |
| Place approved art or combine an approved family | The accepted arrangement appears using permitted parts at the correct scale, with its true functional state. Ordinary players need no editor, generation account or paid call to use it. A second client receives only the appearance and state its viewer is entitled to perceive. |
| A new tool has active mechanics and sufficient basic art | An eligible player can use it as allowed while optional refinement is pending, denied or fails. A basic icon or family depiction must not impersonate a unique part or reveal a hidden property. An approved later skin preserves quantities, custody, identity and prior events. |
| The visual distinction is essential | A dangerous active trap requires its authored, perceptible state cues for observers entitled to them. An operable doorway must show a usable opening of the correct dimensions. Before activation, if no approved fallback conveys the required information, follow invention readiness and keep the proposed use unavailable. A non-executable draft may remain. If an already active release loses an essential depiction on one device, explain that the affected interaction cannot safely be presented there and offer recovery. The existing doorway, trap, owners and others’ permitted actions still exist in the world. Hidden traps retain their discovery rules. |
| An artist wants a change with gameplay consequences | Correcting how an existing basket’s weave appears in light can use a compatible visual revision. Physically dyeing one basket, widening a doorway, changing capacity or granting armor resistance goes through the world's ordinary definition or modification rules. The same family label grants no automatic permission to edit other instances or worlds. |
| Bespoke art is pending, late, withdrawn or inaccessible | Use a currently permitted, adequate family depiction where available and preserve the real object's state. Report any affected use that lacks one. No promised turnaround for arbitrary new geometry or motion follows from immediate support for approved parts. If cancellation, replacement, restore or a permission change invalidates the request, its late result cannot be published into live play. |

## Money, publication and recovery

An agent sees the budget remaining and the permissions relevant to its task. Approval of art does not approve spending, new mechanics, executable behavior or redistribution. A provider may accept a request and time out before reporting the result. Preserve the attempt, known or uncertain cost, prior successful work and the selected inputs. Reconcile the uncertain result before considering another authorized paid attempt. Cancelled or failed work never automatically incurs a repeat charge. An incomplete batch can resume at the affected items without paying to regenerate successful outputs.

Track permission to **view a reference, submit it to a provider, preserve editable sources, distribute a packaged game, and redistribute raw art** independently. The project’s sourcing policy requires CC0 or CC BY for sourced third-party game art. Record attribution as required and verify rights separately for generated outputs. A private character study, attractive concept or entitled marketplace purchase has only its actual permissions. A public package containing an unauthorized dependency fails publication. Material granted to one audience stays within its permitted audience, including previews and diagnostic captures. Provider output may supply artwork and permitted values within a supported family. Embedded executable content gains no right to run.

Withdrawal stops further authorized distribution and records the affected release and reason. An old backup or worker cache cannot reinstate a withdrawn selection. Users may retain files already delivered to them. Keep independently permitted uses and existing world progress intact. For an interrupted release or missing dependency, establish which usable, permitted release actually reached each audience before reporting completion.
