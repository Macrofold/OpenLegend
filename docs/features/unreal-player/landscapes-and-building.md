# Landscapes you can travel through and build in

On a wooded ridge, the player sees a winding stream, a sunlit crossing and roofs beyond it. They can pick the crossing as a destination, find it again beneath the canopy, meet someone there, choose a physical site near the water and return later to the shelter they helped build. From the opposite bank they can recognize where they built. A resident who later visits can have their own experience of that place. This is the bundled world's target in ordinary play.

## From an image to a place

The bundled world uses grounded 3D without a deliberate pixel-art treatment. People, trees, tools and buildings have believable proportions. Forms, color and texture can be painterly. The textured Quaternius line anchors the material and detail treatment, with plants closer to natural growth. Reserve dense detail for forms a player can approach or use. A distant roof needs a recognizable outline. Close to the same house, a person should be able to find its door and judge whether they can enter. The reference paintings and illustrations guide composition. Their use for review grants no distribution or reproduction rights. [Selected art requirements](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/d8e39b132611a10641704c3fc87b0f9409901532/art-direction/decisions.md) · [Creative brief](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/d8e39b132611a10641704c3fc87b0f9409901532/art-direction/final-board/creative-brief.md)

| Reference scene | What a player should experience |
| --- | --- |
| A break in the clouds above a mountain pass | A nearby rock, wooded shoulder and distant peaks overlap. As the player walks sideways, the rock passes quickly, the shoulder shifts slowly and the peaks hold their place. A river or gap suggests a direction to explore. A distant landmark remains recognizable once the player descends. |
| A settlement along a stream | Different roof heights, paths, fences, bridge approaches and clear space by a door explain how people use the place. From ground level, a player can find the crossing they saw from above. From the far bank, the new shelter has a recognizable relationship to it. |
| Tall forest, wet marsh and open valley | Clumps of trees, gaps, low growth, exposed rock and patches of wet ground vary with terrain. A canopy can enclose the walk, then open onto a view. Even in deep shade the player can read the immediate route and distinguish a visible figure from a trunk at the detail their character actually has. |
| A castle on a ridge or a party above a lake | Human-scale figures, walls and paths give the landscape scale. Distinctive places get a silhouette and an approach. The composition should work when the player turns away from the original viewpoint and walks back. |

A region can deliver these qualities with different landmarks, inhabitants and routes. Preserve the skyline in the easily reached horizon view, the usable ground and nearby activity in the ordinary tactical view, and the object's identity in close inspection. A cinematic still alone cannot qualify a region. The player's view and the character's senses remain distinct. [Play and perception](play-and-perception.md)

## Ground, routes and repeated placement

A world's placement inputs include the shape and extent of its ground, supported surfaces, water, climate or biome where authored, landmarks, routes, settled uses, areas reserved for play, and existing edits. The output is a place that multiple people can recognize and navigate. An author can reserve a view through the trees, a bridge approach or a working yard. Rules can distribute many trees or reeds throughout the remaining terrain. Vary size, spacing and grouping within a coherent family. A change to decorative density must preserve the usable opening, the landmark and the physical objects there. An empty authored clearing is a legitimate result. Authors can vary placement by altitude, slope, water, terrain, allowed species and local exceptions. For example, keep the pass clear even where an otherwise valid pine cluster would grow. Running the same approved arrangement again must reproduce its gameplay-relevant sites and identities. Another world can select different rules and get a different landscape. A revised art or placement recipe can supply candidates for review. Adopting it in an inhabited world cannot reroll bridges, reassign belongings, erase a path people built or silently relocate an interactive tree.

Distinguish three kinds of scenery by what it promises to the player.

| What appears in the landscape | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| Reachable ground, structure or usable object | Its visible location, extent, support, openings and allowed interactions agree with the world's physical facts. A ramp leads to its real landing. Two people at the same map coordinates can stand on the bridge and beneath it. Picking, movement and proposed construction identify the intended surface. A drawn reflection supplies no second selectable object. |
| Decoration within a playable place | Grass, leaf litter, decorative trunks and reeds can add depth and cast scenic shadows. They grant no harvest, solid support, concealment or other action of their own. Dress work areas and approaches so decoration does not imitate or hide an essential interaction. Give a harvestable tree its actual authorized actions. Distinct materials or readable inspection can explain a difference that shape alone cannot. |
| Distant scenery beyond supported ground | A skyline can imply a larger world. A far peak can be decorative. Do not offer travel, discovery, placement or a route marker there. A boundary on a plausible walking route must be legible before a player invests a long walk in an apparent destination. Where travel stops, explain the supported limit without inventing lore or unseen obstacles. |

Following a river to a village creates opportunities for discovery. Recognizing a roofline, arriving at a named site, learning its name, remembering a visit and finding out who is inside remain distinct. A public ridgeline can be visible to everyone. A private camp, its name, smoke, light, reflection, sound or people can appear only to the extent the viewer has evidence of that individual fact. A distant version of the scene follows the same rule. ## Light, air and time

A world with an authored sun has a visible, directional source shared by the sky, lit faces of slopes and buildings, clouds where present, and cast shadows. Morning light can pick out one side of a valley and evening light another. The sun and shadows keep their compass direction when the camera rotates. The bundled daily cycle has dawn, midday, dusk and night. Direct sun disappears below the horizon. At night the sky and permitted local lights determine what the scene looks like. A roof shields the ground beneath it and an opening admits light. A fire illuminates nearby material within its permitted effect. A player crossing an interior threshold should retain orientation through the change of brightness. [Bundled daylight](../../worlds/base/time.md#daylight-presentation) · [Light and sight](../../worlds/base/vision-and-gaze.md#world-illumination-and-optical-materials)

World time selects daylight. A paused world holds its phase. On loss of updates the display settles at the last permitted time. Resuming or rejoining adopts the actual world time, including an intentional jump. Device clocks, graphics settings and opening an inspection panel cannot fast-forward a sunset or grant new visual evidence. In the bundled world, moving branches, mist, cloud shadows, water reflections and shafts of sun should appear where the scene and time support them. A misty hollow and a clear ridge can coexist. Preserve the layered composition when a device needs a simpler treatment. Where darkness, smoke or weather changes a character's ability to witness an event, the world's sensing rules define the change for players and NPCs. Decorative fog cannot invent a witness or suppress an accepted action. Reducing glare, motion, foliage density or shadow cost must leave traversal cues, physical cover and each observer's permitted detail intact. [Desktop qualification](desktop-access.md)

Other worlds choose their own geography and sky. For example, in an authored underground world without a sun preset the player gets steady generic illumination when no other presentation is specified. A fork between two supported galleries can serve the role of a mountain pass. Removing a player-built obstruction there reopens only the passage allowed by its actual geometry. Surface astronomy, seasons, storms, a weather economy and magical light sources are separate world choices.

## Building changes the place

1. **Choose and inspect.** A player acting under the selected world's construction rules picks an available arrangement, actual inputs, an orientation and a supporting site. Show usable dimensions and position against the ground at the chosen level. In a world where water cannot support construction, a river surface must refuse the foundation. Selecting the bridge deck cannot choose a site below it. Offer a non-drag route. A moving or edited preview remains a draft.
2. **Check this choice.** Distinguish unchecked, awaiting a check, blocked, checked for these inputs and temporarily unavailable. A changed footprint, resource, permission or support calls for a fresh check. Where disclosure allows, help the player correct the issue. Preserve useful draft choices when a request is refused. A hidden occupant can prevent placement without being named, outlined or located by a special diagnostic.
3. **Start and observe the work.** Confirmation requests the world's work. Report its actual admission and committed phases. A preview, a completed foundation and a finished roof read differently. A player can see where a permitted part ended up, which materials were actually used and what work remains. If two players compete for the same site or materials, each receives their own actual result. Reopening the screen or recovering the result after a disconnect never builds or charges twice.
4. **Use, change and return.** A qualifying shelter can be entered and left. Where the world permits, rest or put a belonging there. Revision, reclaim, construction in stages and loss of support follow the installed arrangement and physical rules. A finished roof does not establish household membership, a safe zone or automatic knowledge by every local NPC. A retained memory of an older shelter can disagree with what a returning visitor encounters today. [Building, correction and reclaim](../../projects/item-and-building-workflows-feature-spec.md#6-place-understand-a-blocker-build-and-revise)

The newest committed edit takes precedence over automatic dressing across loads, viewing distances and quality settings. If a world supports felling an interactive tree, the removed tree stays removed until that world admits a real replacement or regrowth. Surface decoration must cease clipping through the resulting occupied floor. Replacing a roof preserves unaffected parts and belongings alongside any actual changes the work caused. Removing a roof exposes precisely the space and facts now available to that observer. Unknown contents stay private even if another player can see them. Rejoining or revisiting a region in the same world and timeline preserves accepted changes. A remembered image, stale distant outline or missing optional art cannot silently restore the old arrangement. On first arrival or a fast return, use a truthful simpler representation for known ground or structures while art loads. If the ground or placement facts are unavailable, keep the affected choice unavailable until it can be checked. Missing art supplies neither a new obstacle nor an opening through an existing wall.

## Acceptance

- **Required in ordinary motion.** Walk the ridge–woodland–valley route, turn across foreground trunks, follow a visible path, enter the settlement and look back. Repeat an inhabited water crossing and a roof/interior transition. Inspect dawn, midday, dusk and night on the same route. Look for shimmering foliage, flickering or detached shadows, sudden changes of landmark shape, unreadable exposure and openings that close as art detail changes. A defect that changes what can be reached, selected or learned fails the playable route.
- **Required with two observers.** One sees construction while another is absent or lacks the relevant evidence. Both encounter the correct structure when entitled. Leave, change it, rejoin and compare their permitted descriptions, memories and views. A result that reveals a hidden builder, duplicates materials, restores a removed object or changes support with graphics quality fails.
- **Human review.** From normal game controls, an uncoached player identifies somewhere they want to go, reaches it, chooses somewhere they would build within the world's rules, and recognizes a change on return. Record whether the player actually wanted to explore separately from task completion. Mike reviews composition and atmosphere in motion. An attractive screenshot, a scripted traversal and a hardware performance measurement answer different questions.
