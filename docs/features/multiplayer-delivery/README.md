# Multiplayer updates and recovery

A player follows a companion through a doorway while another player speaks nearby. The companion's movement remains understandable, the sentence keeps its meaning and audience, and a slow connection on another player's device does not hold up the encounter. Returning after a connection failure restores the player's permitted view without repeating an action or revealing what happened out of sight.

This packet specifies the **desired behavior** of delivering already authoritative, already permitted information to players and other authorized viewers. It defines a shared behavioral foundation for a small playable scene and later multiplayer growth. It makes no claim that every requirement or experiment has been implemented or qualified. Delivery and evidence status live in the [focused work tracker](../../maintainers/multiplayer.md#multiplayer-delivery-and-recovery).

## Purpose and boundaries

The purpose is to protect responsive, understandable play and the integrity of independent characters, personal knowledge and persistent consequences. A player should spend attention on the encounter rather than reconnecting, deciphering contradictory views, or wondering whether a delayed action happened twice. Efficiency matters because avoidable delivery work competes with that experience; a smaller packet alone is not a successful feature.

Delivery does not decide what happens, what a character senses, what is remembered, who may control a body, whether an action succeeds, or when an accepted effect becomes durable. It consumes those decisions and preserves their meaning. A camera movement, delivery subscription, nearby player, shared party, reusable representation or faster connection grants no additional fictional knowledge or application permission.

The feature applies to ordinary character views, authorized noncontrolling views, supported inspection/history surfaces and operational views under each surface's own permission and disclosure contract. It does not turn a spectator into a character, enrich an operational overview, enable PvP, or alter presence, departure, pause or unattended-world policy.

## Inputs and outputs

These are behavioral inputs and outcomes, not a message schema or implementation architecture.

| Input                                              | Required meaning                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Authorized recipient and requested surface         | Current account/session rights, world and character context, viewing versus control, and the purpose for which information is requested.                                                          |
| Accepted state and personally admitted evidence    | The state or records selected by their owners, including their identity, applicable time, order, absence/removal and current disclosure restrictions.                                             |
| Previous valid delivered view, if any              | What this recipient can actually use as a basis for further updates; another viewer's view does not substitute for it.                                                                            |
| Lifecycle and permission changes                   | Entry, exit, forgetting/correction, reference expiry, control/access changes, restart and world restoration that affect this surface.                                                             |
| Delivery eligibility and requirements              | Which information is current replaceable state, a distinct outcome/evidence record, required lifecycle information or optional presentation, and any applicable freshness/retention requirements. |
| Connection condition and admitted operating limits | Readiness, interruption, pressure and the selected supported workload; these do not change the world's laws.                                                                                      |
| A qualified delivery policy                        | The established behavior, or an explicitly admitted experimental policy with stated scope, bounds and fallback. No unselected experiment changes ordinary play.                                   |

Outputs are a complete permitted starting/replacement view, a valid change to that viewer's existing view, an eligible distinct outcome or record, an explicit current-state removal/restriction, or an honest deferred/unavailable/resynchronizing outcome. There may be no gameplay update when the selected output is unchanged. These outcomes do not themselves prove a new observation, successful command, committed save, or permanent retention.
