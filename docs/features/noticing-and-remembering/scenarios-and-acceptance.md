# Scenarios and acceptance

Use these journeys to judge whether a player can surprise people, read a situation, ask a useful witness or exploit a real blind spot. A good outcome does not require a particular line of dialogue or emotion. The character's reason, available evidence and action must fit what happened.

## The jester and fifty witnesses

A costumed performer emerges, flips and raises a middle finger. Fifty capable NPCs receive the gesture in the principal example. Give it a shared importance high enough to attract notice. No one has a separate urgent need or unused discovery opportunity in this example. The following groups are disjoint so their counts add up.

| Witnesses | What they can establish                                                                                                                       | Expected outcome                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------: | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|         1 | The intended recipient can tell the gesture was directed at them.                                                                             | Offer a personal decision. A joke, offense, a question or choosing silence are all possible.                                                                                                                                            |
|         1 | A guard undertaking a watch recognizes the performer they were asked to find. Their instruction requires choosing what to do on finding them. | Offer one guard decision. Changing the instruction to “record the time I see them” can satisfy it with a record and no new deliberate choice.                                                                                           |
|         1 | Someone with a structured fear of clown attire recognizes the appearance.                                                                     | Apply the supported fear on first recognition, which can precede the flip. If the person can continue their chosen course, no immediate decision is due. If the fear presents an unresolved choice about that course, offer one.        |
|        47 | Each witnesses the gesture without an individual reason for an immediate decision.                                                            | Update evidence and applicable attention. No immediate paid interpretation solely for this event. Actual signs of noticing can be visible to other characters within their own senses. Nobody is assigned a private opinion by default. |

Thus this setup has **two immediate decision opportunities, or three with the unresolved fear choice**. Count from each person's own first qualifying fact, not three times for appearance, flip and gesture. Decisions, actual provider requests, applied actions and visible responses are different counts. An NPC can have several reasons at once without duplicating a response. A personalized decision may use several paid stages; refusal before dispatch may use none.

Vary the scene:

- Put a fence between the guard and the performer's face, or a tree between the recipient and the gesture. Seeing the costume does not prove identity or who was insulted. The dependent decision disappears unless other permitted evidence independently establishes it.
- Bring back the same jester after a brief occlusion, and repeat the same flip during one continuing performance. No artificial clearance, new discovery allowance or series of paid reactions. A genuinely new address, newly supported identity, worsened danger or actual new choice can still matter.
- Have all fifty deliberately watch the show. More can later describe the flip accurately. Watching does not give each person a new decision at each move. Give all fifty a real, individually received request for a choice and all fifty may qualify. If resources only serve some in time, the others were unserved; they did not agree, refuse or deliberately remain silent.
- Let one witness have only a free-form childhood memory of clowns. A later independently warranted turn may connect the memory and the costume. An immediate visceral response is not promised without a structured trigger. Conversely, a costumed stranger can trigger the structured fear; a performer known privately to be a jester in ordinary clothes cannot trigger it solely through that hidden fact.
- Put observers at different distances and angles. Some see the hand; others see only a person tumble. Others may hear a crowd react without seeing why. Hearing real laughter can become new evidence. No second wave of paid thoughts or laughter is owed simply because other witnesses reacted. A transmitted account remains testimony.

## Other playable cases

| Approach a cautious guard in an open field, then circle outside the gate               | Rooms confer no special omniscience. The bundled world can detect a measurably closing body within 4 m, requiring at least 0.25 m of supported closing; separation beyond 6 m is one supported way to clear the cue. Clearance needs sustained, adequate evidence. Small oscillations or a tree hiding the player cannot rearm it. The world owns those distances. Caution alone adds no new sense or automatic hostility. A guard might warn, retreat, investigate or hold position. [Full bundled-world cue and clearance conditions](../../worlds/base/attention.md#native-relation-cues) |
| A person continues approaching after an actual warning                                 | A specifically chosen, still-applicable step back can execute without reinterpretation. A blocked step creates an actual problem. A distinct intention to call for help or go somewhere safe is not presumed. A bystander outside normal sight can still receive a supported sound or actual bodily effect.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Mara walks a repeating pasture patrol behind trees                                     | Two people identically named Mara stay distinct when identified. An incidental witness may remember that one passed, and a scout may retain detailed portions and gaps. Neither gets hundreds of permanent prose entries, a hidden destination, or a named barn from anonymous obstructions. Saving while Mara is out of view does not change what either can say after return.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| An NPC delivers pie to the town, meets a stranger and runs out                         | The arbitrary objective is meaningful as text. Accepted transfers, a refused offer or a depleted supply can change a specific outstanding step. An unrelated exchange across town does nothing to the giver. Unknown residents, an unavailable action or ambiguous goals can prompt asking, adapting the plan, or abandoning it when the NPC next has a reason to choose. No bespoke “pie delivery” behavior is required.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Someone shouts; a building begins burning; a colleague leaves for a break              | Hearing noise does not imply hearing exact words. Received direct speech can warrant a reply; a sustained visible fire can produce native danger and a first or escalated choice, without paid thought per tick. The colleague leaving can matter to the person whose relief arrangement depends on them. Seeing an unassigned passerby leave does not establish an abandoned post.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| A drummer misses a beat, or a witness notices an unfamiliar portrait                   | A supported condition about an expected beat can qualify when due if the listener could actually monitor it. Silence during missing coverage proves nothing. An unusual portrait can reach a free discovery turn or a later conversation; the game need not interpret every painting against every viewer's biography.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| “Mara told me she likes cats and intends to pick up the violin from the shop tomorrow” | Read it back verbatim while exact wording remains. Resolve both occurrences of the identified person; permit zero object or location bindings if unknown. Recall it by subject **or** meaning. A later summary must not claim that Mara collected the violin. Correcting or withdrawing the only source removes the unsupported derivative under the privacy rules.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| A character is asleep, incapacitated, disconnected, or waiting on a decision           | Apply actual body/effect rules. No retrospective sight or hearing. A player returning sees their permitted present view; a completed personal action or reply cannot happen twice. If evidence is revoked during deliberation, dependent stale results cannot take effect. Unrelated activity need not cancel a still-valid reply.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| A deer hears a qualifying sound while two people see it move                           | The deer can forage, flee or settle under its installed animal rules without a human-style thought, autobiography or model call. Each human witness has their own permitted evidence. More observed movement can cost attention and history even when the animal's own choice was cheap.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

## Evidence needed for acceptance

The [56 medieval witness decisions](medieval-examples.md) are behavioral examples wherever the underlying activity is supported. They do not require adding every medieval activity to qualify the witness rules. Exercise these paired controls through the same sensing and decision paths, counting opportunities separately from requests actually dispatched:

- **Open need and feasible help.** Compare the passenger who can see the struggle and reach a rope with a passenger who hears only the splash. Repeat for holding a gate for someone with a cart. Check two helpers who can each contribute, then let only one observe that the need was met.
- **Scope of a public request.** Ask the same assembly to vote, then to supply one cart. Every eligible, informed NPC with an outstanding ballot gets their own opportunity. For the cart, independently capable, available suppliers can offer; other listeners owe no refusal. An unclear shout supplies no missing details. Count unserved opportunities separately from voluntary nonresponse.
- **Recognizable personal reference.** Let Oren hear just “money,” just his name, then the intelligible accusation about him in a conversation addressed to someone else. Only the last supplies enough personal content for an immediate decision about the accusation. With two listeners called Oren, check that both can orient without either learning the intended identity from the speaker's private knowledge. A personally significant subject mentioned with no recognized reference or active condition still requires no general biography search.

| **Required quality assessment**         | In a separate, privately scoped evaluation, give all witnesses in a small frozen scene, or a documented sample of skipped witnesses in larger scenes, a chance to interpret the evidence each actually had. Have reviewers judge whether a reaction was plausible, its absence mattered, and a player could perceive the distinction. Include cases where no thought or no visible display was appropriate. A model's different answer is a lead for investigation, not ground truth.                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Diagnose a miss or unnecessary cost** | Distinguish never sensed, sensed but unidentified, observed without a personal reason, known response handled locally, decision offered but unserved, stale/refused, model chose quiet, and action occurred but the player couldn't see it. Count misses and unnecessary jobs by reason, using sampling weights when estimating whole-crowd rates. An empty-looking audience could need better visible attention, better eligibility or better choices; an invisible private thought alone does not fix the display.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

Delivery can be judged in order: the ordinary witness and direct recipient first, then a watch and a structured personal reaction, then evidence-preserving recall through consolidation and return, then the crowded and subtle cases. Mechanical checks establish fidelity and counts. Human play establishes whether NPCs feel observant, surprising and worth interacting with.

For scale, fifty witnesses receiving twenty different observable action sequences per minute would create a thousand witness/sequence pairs per minute. This is arithmetic, not a prediction of latency or model demand. Count once-per-world occurrences, actual per-witness sensing, matched personal conditions, actual funded decisions and downstream requests separately. Check that sharing a public performance never shares a private interpretation. During play review, let people see the performance and question the witnesses without first telling them who should react. Test whether they can distinguish a missed cue, a working guard, a frightened character trying to carry on, and a crowd that merely looks inert. Use only outward signs the player really could perceive.
