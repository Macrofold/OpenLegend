# Who gets a decision

## What reaches a witness

1. Establish the change and its order in the world. A gesture, an impact, someone's words, a fire starting, a private bodily change and a blocked action can each matter. Not every motion sample is an event and not every thought has an outside witness.
2. Establish the witness's own evidence. A body can be visible while its hand is hidden. A yell can be audible without intelligible words or a recognized speaker. Someone across a field may see a person stumble without seeing the stone. Being in the same room, party, crowd or map region establishes none of these facts. Location, lighting, obstructions, distance, noise, facing and capabilities matter where the world's senses support them.
3. Distinguish **physical strength** (how noticeable here), **novelty** (new or materially changed for me), **importance** (how much this event can warrant attention or retention), **urgency** (how promptly a supported change needs handling) and **personal relevance** (what it changes for me). A bright familiar signal can be urgent; a quiet correction to a promise can be important. Neither requires a rarity score. Narrative importance to an audience is a separate question.
4. Update warranted perception, attention, evidence and physical effects. Look for an individual reason to make a decision. An event's world-authored default importance, including an illustrative 6 on the bundled world's scale, can make it worth noticing. For an ordinary witness it is insufficient by itself to buy a thought. Neither a raw position change nor a new appearance description is a personal decision.

Attention can make more precise evidence available. Deliberate focus, direct involvement, current work, urgent cues and opportunities to notice overlooked subjects can compete. Ten subjects at the bundled world's default Focus is an individual's capacity for detailed observation. It is neither a quota of crowd members who respond nor ten guaranteed choices. Under the Base rule, actors using the `npc` controller can track at most one subject in detail solely because of an ordinary cue. Within the same overall capacity, one subject whose current description has yet to be presented can receive detailed attention for discovery. Direct involvement and other priorities can select additional subjects. Selecting a subject for detailed observation does not initiate a decision about it. A hit to one's body or a spoken address can matter outside detailed visual focus. [Bundled-world selection and cadence](../../worlds/base/attention.md)

## When a witness has a decision

Several conditions can apply to one witness. The world resolves supported physical effects and previously chosen steps directly. A guard can turn to follow a performer while a baker keeps kneading and hears the exchange. A player who sees the baker carry on has no evidence from that alone of what the baker thought.

| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A physical effect or supported involuntary reaction applies                                         | Apply it under the world's rules at its actual time. It can affect subsequent choices. Deliberation, admission pressure and an unavailable provider cannot postpone the effect.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| An existing chosen response still covers this situation                                             | Continue it if the same purpose, subject, evidence, conditions and means still hold. Recording a sighting under a watch may finish the job. A meaningful change, failure or choice outside that instruction can require thought.                                                                                                                  |
| The NPC receives communication directed at them                                                     | An NPC who hears that they were directly addressed can ask for repetition even if every word was masked. If they heard nothing, the intended address grants no reply opportunity. A visible gesture needs evidence it was directed at them. Preserve the interactive route for a direct player conversation.                                      |
| A supported change alters an active undertaking or affects the character personally                 | Offer a decision when an outcome, interruption, newly blocked step, relevant contradiction, required choice, or condition crossing leaves a choice open. Test the actual dependency: a delivered item settles my outstanding request; an unrelated delivery does not. A change already handled by the undertaking needs no new choice.            |
| An observed alarm, personal sensitivity or deliberately selected watch condition becomes true       | Execute its defined notice, feeling, report or previously chosen response once as applicable. Offer deliberation if the condition calls for a choice or leaves the existing course inadequate. Recognition alone gives no universal order to attack, flee or speak.                                                                               |
| A witness recognizes an opportunity to help, a public request or an overheard reference to themself | Apply the personal evidence and choice rules below. None requires a prior assignment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Ordinary bystander evidence only                                                                    | Update the permitted scene and any warranted history. No immediate paid job from importance alone. A subsequent justified turn may consider it while still available. A quiet witness may truthfully remember it.                                                                                                                                 |
| A genuine free interval offers a chance to notice something unconsidered                            | The NPC can use the world's finite discovery opportunity for an eligible description. Newly unoccupied time or an independently warranted leisure opportunity can qualify. Repeated polling, remaining idle, finishing a model call or receiving another animation frame cannot replenish it. There is no guarantee of choosing this observation. |

Offer one opportunity for the unresolved choice. Hearing words can give an NPC something to answer without deciding in advance what the speaker meant, whether they were truthful or how the NPC feels. During a warranted turn, any personally available sight, account or free-form goal can take on unexpected significance. Rethinking unchanged evidence cannot repeatedly buy another turn.

### A stranger has a practical opportunity to help

A duty, friendship or cry for help is unnecessary. A witness qualifies when they personally recognize **both a need that still appears open and something feasible they could do about it now**, and their existing activity or prepared response doesn't already settle the choice. Someone who sees a passenger struggling beside the ferry and has a rope can choose whether to offer it, call the ferrymaster or keep hold of a child. Someone walking through a gate can choose to hold it for a struggling carter. Ordinary courtesy qualifies when the opportunity would otherwise pass. Hearing a splash alone can draw attention; it does not establish that someone is drowning.

Use what the witness can already recognize about the need and their means. A known helper within reach can be enough. Whether to help, and how best to do it, can be decided during the turn. If an accepted task already covers it, execute the permitted step. Otherwise offer deliberation without prescribing generosity, courage, consent or success. Several people can independently start to help. Only a witness who perceives adequate help, completion or another relevant change can treat the need as covered. Seeing a rescue begin does not establish success. A helper may arrive late believing the need is still open.

### A public request asks for a particular kind of answer

| What the listener can establish                                                                               | Who has a decision                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Each eligible person is asked for a formal vote (deferred)                                                    | Each eligible participant who receives and understands the request and still has an individual choice to make. One person's vote cannot settle another's.                                                                       |
| One or some people with the relevant means are invited to volunteer, as in a request for a cart or a ferryman | Each listener who understands the invitation, knows they can plausibly supply the requested help and has a fresh choice about offering it. A bystander with no means owes no refusal. Two plausible volunteers can both answer. |
| A general appeal signals a need, as in “Somebody help!”                                                       | Use the need and practical contribution each witness can personally establish. Hearing urgency can draw attention. It does not supply a missing location, means of helping, or the nature of the emergency.                     |

Direct conversation and an explicit public request for help are first-delivery requirements. A player can tell Mara “I need a way to haul this grain” in their own words and give her a chance to answer. In the square, the player can explicitly ask for volunteers to supply transport. A listener must receive the invitation and understand what help is needed to have that choice. If the player simply remarks on the grain to nobody in particular, inferring the same request is lower priority. An already involved listener with a decision at stake can ask for clarification. Others can consider the words they retain at a later decision. An ambiguous remark alone triggers no per-listener model call to classify it.

A public request uses ordinary speech with no individual addressee. Volunteers learn of acceptance, withdrawal or fulfillment through their own evidence. Formal votes are deferred until a playable council or dispute needs a separately recorded answer from each eligible participant. People can still ask each other what they think. [Speech and addressing](../../hearing-and-speech.md#received-direct-addressing)

### A listener hears a recognizable reference to themself

A personally recognized name or established form of address can draw attention even when the speaker addresses somebody else. Hearing only “Oren” can prompt a turn toward the voice or closer listening where supported. It grants no missing words and does not by itself buy a decision. If the received words and circumstances also support that **the listener is the subject of something they can respond to**, the listener may get a decision immediately. Hearing “Oren stole the toll money” in full can give the clerk concerned something to answer. The game need not decide whether it was an accusation, a joke or a quotation before offering that choice. Hearing only “money” across the forge gives the clerk no such evidence. Even the full words establish only what was said.

If two listeners know the same name as their own, both may orient. Neither gains certain identification from the shared name. Further context might identify one, leave both unsure, or give someone a genuine need to clarify. An alias matters only to someone who knows it. This exception does not test each conversation against every listener's biography, family, commercial interests or secrets. Overhearing “the mill floods at thaw” can matter through an already chosen supported watch or during a later justified thought. A private plan alone buys no search or call about that line.

The [56 medieval examples](medieval-examples.md) apply the same rules to routine work, surprises, politics and emergencies. Their model-call column identifies the first unresolved choice. Actual dispatch still requires current evidence, permission, capacity and funding.

## General behavior without a catalogue of lives

For a task to cause immediate reconsideration, there must be a supported link: this is the person I was waiting for, this offer answers my request, this outcome stopped my current step, or this observed change meets a condition I chose. A task can express several conditions or alternatives in ordinary language. A condition the world cannot recognize must remain uncovered. The NPC can still pursue the goal, ask someone to help, or use what it has actually noticed when it next thinks. The feature has no list of every event that could ever be consequential.

A task, value or personality statement can consist entirely of ordinary language. “I am cautious around strangers” can influence interpretation during a decision without defining a new sense, distance ring or defensive action. “Deliver slices of pie to the residents” can use supported asking, giving, carrying and moving. Who counts as a resident might be unknown; the NPC can ask. A recipient's refusal can change the task without deciding whether the giver gives up. A goal that matches no existing condition remains meaningful at the next warranted turn. Writing a sentence grants neither continuous semantic monitoring nor permission to execute a new physical mechanic.

A **chosen watch** belongs to the person undertaking the activity. It specifies the purpose, observable subject or area, one supported event or ongoing condition, what to do on a match, and when to stop. “Tell me when you see Mara at the gate” requires the watcher to see and recognize Mara there. “Only tell me if the gate is also closed” adds a second, independently changing condition. Compound watches are deferred. Keep the whole instruction as a goal the NPC can consider when they next have reason to think. Starting the simpler watch instead requires explicit acceptance.

A watch for a change reports a witnessed event or entry into a condition. A request to report what is true now can report an already visible person without claiming they just arrived. An ongoing condition can report meaningful escalation and rearms only on supported clearance. Two separately heard calls for help can count as two events. Sleep, an obstruction or absence can leave the answer unknown. “I couldn't see the gate” and “I checked and found nobody” are different reports. Ending, replacing or revoking the activity ends its watch.

A **prepared response** is a particular choice already made for a supported condition, with its actual target, means and purpose. If the NPC chose to step back upon a supported continued approach, it can execute when the condition and physical step are valid. If the way is blocked, it cannot step through a wall. The NPC need not select a safe point, hiding place, alarm bell and weapon before anybody appears. Other answers remain available to a later decision. An arbitrary instruction that cannot be expressed or executed by the world remains a goal, a request for interpretation, or an explicitly unavailable action.

A **structured personal trigger** links an identified character, a recognizable cue or category supplied by their world, and a supported response. An authored fear of clown attire can produce a bodily reaction on personally recognizing that attire. It can affect a player-character's supported bodily state too; it does not choose their voluntary movement. A performer in ordinary clothes supplies no match merely by being a clown. A non-performer wearing recognizable attire can match. Partial colors or a faint resemblance that do not meet recognition requirements have no deterministic effect. A free-form childhood account can still evoke an association during later thought. Neither cause reveals someone's private fear to onlookers. They may witness an actual outward sign. If multiple supported causes produce the same fear, retain their respective sources and apply the world's emotion-composition rule once. Their resolution can be independent. The [emotion contract](../../projects/emotional-physiology-feature-spec.md) owns strength, duration, recovery and available bodily effects.

Supported positive or practical interests can use the same distinction: recognizing the emblem one is looking for can complete a watch; hearing an expected reply can allow work to resume. Adding a new authored trigger must identify what the witness can actually perceive and what change it can cause. Only conditions applicable to this change, character or active work should require attention. Enlarging the world's catalogue with thousands of unrelated rules must not make each witness assess them all. There is no arbitrary count of actions, interests, conditions or witnesses justified by these examples. Resource bounds and their consequences follow the [limits policy](../../limits/README.md#entry-structure).

## Timing, crowds and failure

For a structured sensitivity, onset is the first supported match for an inactive condition. A sustained match can continue to affect the person under the installed response law. Repeated samples, a momentary loss of support or a delayed thought cannot renew the onset. A fresh independent exposure can qualify after the world's supported clearing and recovery conditions have been met. An ambiguous recollection alone cannot trigger a physical response specified to require present recognition. Changing or withdrawing a trigger ends its future authority; it cannot undo a committed physical action. Any continuing emotion or derived interpretation uses the applicable evidence, recovery and correction rules.

Keep “appeared,” “flipped” and “gestured” as ordered observations. One pending decision can consider compatible evidence already received by the same NPC. Do not invent a single intention or require the world to name an encounter. Do not hold urgent communication or bodily effects for a universal quiet period. A later observation cannot be supplied to a decision as if the NPC knew it earlier. It can inform the next warranted decision, or the existing justified urgent refresh. A witness who missed the flip gets no flip in either case.

People with the same evidence and circumstances receive the same eligibility rules. Different personal histories can produce different decisions. Fifty legitimate individual choices can justify fifty opportunities; fifty incidental witnesses can justify zero immediate jobs. No first-few audience quota, proximity lottery, random paid turn for low-scoring evidence, semantic search through every witness's memories, or compulsory vector score per observation decides who thinks. Cheap personal matching requires actual supported evidence. General semantic recall belongs within an already justified decision. A subtle unsignaled association may be missed. This is a deliberate quality/cost tradeoff.

Eligibility is an opportunity, not a guaranteed decision, spoken reply or provider request. Combine compatible reasons per person; keep distinct occurrences and independently required evidence. A pending or active job has one owner and may have several provider stages. Give direct communication and genuine urgency their priority, with fair service for waiting eligible people. Keep the world progressing during waits. Capacity, permission, privacy, lack of funds, cancellation and stale sources have distinct outcomes; none implies a private decision to ignore an event. No blind provider retry, forced synchronous crowd response or release of private evidence to another NPC. After restart or reconnect, previously accepted effects, replies, attempts and satisfied watches cannot fire again solely because the scene was loaded. [Provider admission](../../ai-providers.md#admission-and-cancellation) · [Save and return](../../save-and-load.md)

In the bundled world allow at most one optional discovery attempt per genuine free interval. A refusal before provider dispatch releases its reservation; a dispatched attempt spends the allowance even if the provider fails. Only a genuinely new free interval renews it. This is a limit on optional exploration. A guard in the middle of a task can still receive a decision because the task actually changed. Fair service among eligible NPCs does not create opportunities for an arbitrary fraction of bystanders. [Bundled-world semantic opportunities](../../worlds/base/attention.md#semantic-opportunities)
