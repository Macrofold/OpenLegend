# What a witness can remember

An NPC may notice a traveler, turn toward them, and later remember who passed without remembering how the traveler went around each tree. A scout actually following the traveler can have a much better account. The player should be able to discover that difference by asking them.

## From current evidence to lasting recollection

| Lifetime                  | What enters                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | What survives and what ends it                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Current perception        | Actually received sights, sounds or other supported signals, at the precision of this character's senses.                                                                                                                                  | The latest supported fact can guide an immediate response. Compatible successive measurements can support motion. Loss of sight ends current visual support immediately. Last seen is historical, even if still recent.                               |
| Recent detailed history   | Entry into detailed attention establishes what the witness could observe then. Subsequent **changed, received** facts, real occurrences and meaningful coverage boundaries remain in order. Other required evidence has its own admission. | Within the world's admitted recent coverage the witness can recover those details, including gaps. Equal confirmations need no duplicate narrative. Pressure or age can shorten optional coverage; absence of a record never proves nothing happened. |
| Lasting personal account  | A supported consequence, independently needed evidence, a report the character actually made, a deliberately chosen recollection, or another warranted impression. An unremarkable sighting can leave none.                                | The account keeps its original perspective, time, identity and uncertainty. A chosen task needs the minimum evidence to complete it. Keeping an appointment or recognizing a friend need not preserve every footstep.                                 |
| Consolidated recollection | Eligible, still-retained personal accounts selected for memory maintenance.                                                                                                                                                                | A summary can express common meaning in new words and preserve exceptional details, distinct people and unresolved matters. Its wording and source coverage are its own. Exact evidence remains available where separately protected.                 |

**There is no mandatory “encounter” to open and close.** Current sight, recent history and lasting recollection have different entry conditions. A one-second background save may happen mid-patrol. Return or restart restores the same coverage and accepted work without announcing a new arrival or granting access to unseen travel. An ended observation supports “I last saw Mara there.” It supports “Mara crossed the pasture” only when the witness actually has evidence for that movement and knows the relevant area. “She disappeared behind the barn” requires separate evidence for the occlusion, its cause and the witness's recognition of the barn. A row of walls does not name itself.

Trees on a repeated patrol produce alternating periods of genuine visibility. Incidental broad sight doesn't write a lifelong arrival/departure diary or pay to summarize every gap. Detailed attention can keep **many** actual changes and coverage breaks while it applies. A deliberate instruction to follow the route preserves ordered detail to the extent the character actually received and retained it. An ordinarily selected detailed subject also keeps its admitted recent detail without a formal watch action. A missing interval remains missing even if someone walks this patrol every day. A character may _infer_ repetition or describe a familiar pattern as such. Neither inference establishes exact positions for an unseen lap.

The bundled world illustrates the cost. At its normal clock, detailed sight can sample changed facts about twice per real second. Fifty witnesses following a single moving subject for three real minutes could therefore have about 18,000 witness/sample opportunities. These are not 18,000 distinct events, memories or database writes. The world prefers six game hours of recent detail within its stated capacity. Keeping every irregular turn indefinitely is incompatible with that promise. Ordinary perception sufficient to react to someone approaching does not require granting every bystander detailed tracking. Any experiment that removes already warranted detail must compare what the bystander **and the person actually following** can later recount. ## Exact words and references

A **personal memory** belongs to one character and can contain arbitrary text, including observation, testimony, imagination, judgment and doubt together. There is no compulsory speaker or single label such as heard, thought or summary that determines what every part of the text means. The speech occurrence and the listener's evidence of what they heard have separate identities. A memory may cite that personal evidence or other permitted sources. It must remain possible to tell “I heard words,” “the speaker said this happened” and “I concluded this happened” apart when those differences matter. Free text can express all three without a separately parsed claim for every clause.

The memory preserves the exact text of each retained version and an optional **reference map**. Reading a memory verbatim reproduces the witness’s account. Quoting a speaker verbatim requires support for the speaker’s exact words. Specific passages can point to entries in that map. The word itself remains present, including punctuation, inflection, pronouns and relative dates. Binding an ID cannot replace “tomorrow” with a date or “her” with a name. A passage can have several applicable references, one reference can apply at several occurrences, and one cited passage may contain another. A reference to the whole source belongs to its actual scope. It does not attach every entity in a sentence to the entire sentence.

For the exact memory **“I heard Mara say ‘I like cats’, and I judged her for it.”**, the reference map and its uses mean the following. The quotation marks delimit language this witness actually heard.

| Exact passage in the memory    | Reference                                   | What this establishes                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| “Mara” and, separately, “her”  | The same identified person's ID             | Two mentions refer to one person. Another person also called Mara is distinct.                                                             |
| “I like cats”                  | This listener's evidence for this utterance | The witness heard Mara speak these words. It need not establish her true preference.                                                       |
| “cats” within the quoted words | The known category, if resolved             | This word refers to the category. With no category reference the word still exists and is searchable.                                      |
| “I judged her for it”          | No separate thought record required         | This is the witness's own judgment. The “her” within it still refers to Mara. The utterance supplies no evidence that Mara judged herself. |

A second example is **“Mara told me she likes cats and intends to pick up the violin from the shop tomorrow.”** Keep the identified person at “Mara” and “she.” Link “violin” to a particular object, “shop” to a particular known location and “tomorrow” to a game-time interval **only when the witness has that information**. Relative time is anchored to the supported conversation time and relevant calendar, not to the later day of recall. If which violin is unknown, asking about a violin can retrieve the memory through its wording or meaning; asking “which one?” may get “I don't know.” An intention remains an intention on the following day. A completed errand requires separate evidence. Free text with zero references remains valid when there are no established referents.

As a reading aid, the second example with all five references established could be shown as “Mara〈1〉 told me she〈1〉 likes cats〈2〉 and intends to pick up the violin〈3〉 from the shop〈4〉 tomorrow〈5〉.” The key identifies 1 as the person, 2 as the category, 3 as the particular instrument, 4 as the known shop and 5 as the relevant day. The markers are annotations; removing them recovers the literal sentence. If two identical words in one account refer to different objects, each occurrence has its own binding. A separate source reference can cover the whole reported passage without changing any of those bindings.

### Identity and location

- A retained fact about a **physically identified person** always keeps the reference to that individual through recall, retelling as the witness's own account, consolidation, save and return. A display name, a pronoun or “the stranger” can all refer to the same person. Losing current sight, losing an optional route, or knowing no name cannot turn two identified people into one. Basic face continuity can be represented once identity was established; this feature requires no simulation of individual facial features. Seeing an unidentifiable distant figure, hearing an unfamiliar voice or hearing someone claim a name does not establish an identification. A memory does not identify a disguised person currently in view without current support.
- A reference is private evidence of identity, not a globally revealing label or a means to target someone now. A listener hearing a secondhand account gets the identity the _listener_ can legitimately associate. Different witnesses can be certain, uncertain or mistaken about different parts of the same occurrence. An explicit correction, withdrawal or authorized forgetting can retire a fact or correct a false association; normal aging must not silently strip the person's ID while keeping an apparently specific fact.
- An animal, object or location may be recalled generically. A recognized scarred bear, a particular violin or a learned shop can retain an individual reference. A description alone cannot turn all deer into the same deer or assign a hidden violin to unbound words. The game need not construct a complete taxonomy to admit useful language.
- No character needs a personal directory of every coordinate or room they ever entered. A recollection can use a supported description, approximate bearing, observed landmark or learned place. A later name can be attached to legitimately associated evidence without refreshing its original time or precision. An unrecognized hillside remains describable. Knowing a shop's identity never discloses where its former contents are now.

## How compaction and consolidation differ

**During movement** the game applies the world's recording and retention rules without a summarization model. Repeated equal evidence, current position changes, sight loss and ordinary expiry need no prose. An NPC can receive a lasting impression through an already warranted turn, or a consequence can preserve the evidence it needs. There is no model request per observation to decide whether it was uneventful. “Uneventful” here means no independently supported result, obligation, deliberate recollection or other retention reason was established before optional detail expired. It is not a claim that the moment meant nothing to the person.

**During scheduled semantic consolidation** a separately funded summarization model can group related retained accounts and write new language. In the bundled policy, routine hourly review considers eligible personal memories and awareness accounts older than six game hours that still have a reason to exist. It does not review every position sample, promise to summarize before expiry, or buy an hourly thought for each witness. Protected incidents separate otherwise similar sources. Later daily review can consolidate eligible older material again. No weekly loss of identity, fixed date for erasing a face or universal decay schedule follows from this feature. [Consolidation and exact speech](../../memory-architecture.md#6-hourly-consolidation-and-six-hour-raw-recall)

Bind references to the passages they describe in the **new wording**. Old word positions do not transfer. Each accepted summary identifies which permitted source revisions support it and distinguishes an exact quote from a gist. It may drop a detail when the applicable retention rule allows it, combine references that support the same fact, or retain alternatives. It cannot invent an identity, turn a promise into fulfillment, transfer a private judgment to the speaker, or change who saw what. A reference to a summary does not entitle anyone to inaccessible originals. Exact promised terms and other protected evidence survive for their required lifetime. Source withdrawal or correction reaches dependent summaries and later retrieval. An unsupported summary cannot replace good evidence. Failure, cancellation or lack of funds leaves no partial replacement; ordinary independent expiry still applies.

An approved account reading “I often saw Mara on patrol” is useful because the guard can answer **who was seen**, offer a lead about a customary route or recognize an exception on a later warranted turn. The guard cannot certify an alibi for an unobserved hour. If nobody chose to retain the pattern and the evidence expired, “I don't remember” is a valid answer. A person asked to find Mara can retain a supported report while forgetting the turns behind the trees. The player gains witnesses worth questioning without acquiring a perfect tracking system.
