# Editable actor knowledge

This specification owns the reusable document, mutation, context and persistence contract. [Base-world knowledge](worlds/base/knowledge.md) owns the bundled cognition and observer-name policies. Event memories remain owned by [Memory architecture](memory-architecture.md); executable techniques remain separate learned capabilities. Delivery is tracked in [CR13](maintainers/cognition-redesign.md#cr13--editable-knowledge-documents).

## Documents and authority

An actor owns one optional general document and optional subject documents keyed by canonical subject identity and kind. An omitted subject kind identifies an entity; `place` and `map-marker` identify the map's distinct subject namespaces, so equal string IDs cannot expose or overwrite a person's note. Each document has text, a revision, explicit origin and evidence references. Origin identifies the current complete text as trusted authored starting understanding (`authored-starting`) or a later ordinary publication (`actor-edit`). A subject can be any supported entity, including an actor or object, a retained known place, or the actor's own map marker. Documents describe current understanding, including uncertainty and false beliefs; they never modify the subject or grant capabilities. About me describes self-understanding. Event memories describe experiences over time. Neither duplicates the external-understanding canvas as a second writable authority.

Storage keys and revisions are engine metadata. A given name, social interpretation or recognition rule is not a mandatory engine document field. The base world's identity policy owns its structured observer labels separately. Applications may project those labels alongside documents without parsing prose for mechanical identifiers.

## Mutation and limits

All accepted edits use `editKnowledge`: validate owner, admitted subject binding, expected document revision, configured character limit and forgotten-evidence restrictions before replacing the complete text. Every changed publication records `actor-edit`, including the first edit and edits replacing an authored seed; an unchanged publication retains its existing origin. Origin describes how the text was supplied, not whether every claim in it is true. Referenced memories still distinguish actual observation, heard testimony, inference and imagination. Missing documents have revision zero. Empty text clears a document while retaining a revisioned tombstone, preventing stale creates from restoring cleared content. Concurrent or oversized edits preserve the prior text and return explicit feedback. Ordinary responses admit note operations independently from speech; an invalid note cannot reject independently valid speech. Reflection publication remains atomic.

The mutation preflights both the document and actor-wide revision successors before replacing text, origin or evidence. An exhausted document refuses a changed publication atomically; an unchanged publication remains permitted without advancing either counter.

Count Unicode code points, including whitespace and Markdown. The admitted world policy chooses document limits; [base-world limits](worlds/base/knowledge.md#notepads) are authored configuration. Request byte/output budgets also apply. Show current count, limit and revision. At capacity, the author must rewrite, summarize or shorten within the existing admitted execution; never silently truncate or purchase an automatic repair call. Evidence metadata is server-supplied from bounded admitted context; prior provenance remains in committed revision history rather than an indefinitely growing source list on each pad.

## Subject binding

Canonical subject IDs identify storage and mutation targets, not evidence of
recognition. The selected world's installed social-perception policy determines
which actual exposures establish an individual association. The bundled policy
reuses the existing observer identity owner for stable human sight/voice
recognition, independently of names. Saved continuous-exposure episodes still
bind current physical observation and record its brief linger; they do not erase
a stable association when sight ends. Loss of perception/participation retires
active exposure bindings while retaining permitted knowledge.

Model references are opaque observer-scoped tokens, normally four hexadecimal
characters and extended on collisions. Recognized individuals use the existing
complete observer/subject key independently of encounter tokens; unrecognized
exposures retain episode/snapshot scope. Only offered references resolve in
responses. Delayed work rechecks its actual recognition, current body and native
prerequisites. A recognized voice can be remembered without revealing a hidden
position or granting a physical target. Names and duplicate labels never resolve
identities.

Selected existing pads also receive document-only references, accepted only by note edits. These let an actor revise or summarize remembered knowledge after losing recognition, including knowledge about a retired subject. Their server binding resolves to that actor's existing document and still checks its revision and evidence. They cannot address speech, actions, naming or a visible entity, and editing through them does not establish a new recognition association.

## Context selection

Include the bounded general pad directly in cognition and relevance context. Explicit `authored-starting` origin produces the label **Authored starting understanding** for nonempty text; a revision number or absence of evidence never establishes that source. Trusted creation supplies the origin for initial background and assumptions; first-person starting beliefs use `# Beliefs` within that pad ([MY01/MY03](maintainers/parallel-batch-08-the-missing-belonging.md)); subsequent changed `editKnowledge` publications replace that seed without reapplying it. Subject context and permitted owner inspection also retain explicit origin. Load notes for directly involved recognized subjects as required context; optional subject notes use the existing actor-scoped retrieval, vector and relevance path. Required documents survive optional relevance failure and remain subject to the complete request budget. Semantic revisions include document revision and the stored preferred name, excluding changing exposure tokens and current location. Never expose another actor's pads through an unrestricted subject join.

Map notes use the same owner-private retrieval limits. They are read-only annotations in cognition context, with no physical entity reference or model note-edit token. Their labels come from the actor's marker or retained place knowledge, never hidden current place facts. Player edits use the existing knowledge owner; place edits require the exact retained observation and marker edits require the current actor's marker.

Unbound remembered subjects can contribute subjective note text to recall without claiming that they match a currently visible individual. Do not expose a hidden document's revision, contents or name through a newly observed stranger's reference. New observations alone create no pads; deliberate editing or naming establishes a sparse personal record.

## Persistence and evolution

Dynamic documents are canonical `mind_knowledge_documents` records, owned by the existing `editKnowledge` transition. Actor/subject lookup is indexed; unchanged documents keep their record and revision. The in-memory world is the active working set. Atomic world commits and same-format save/restore preserve document origin and recognition identity without a second writable world-JSON copy. Current-format validation requires explicit supported origin; older prose is not converted into documents or inferred authored background. Fresh creation installs the selected world's knowledge defaults. Startup reads only the current canonical records. The obsolete `knowledge_documents` projection and its extraction reader were removed on October 14, 2026; the former conversion procedure is historical, not a supported load path.

The [production target](../archive/07-technical-architecture/production-data-model.md#8-minds-evidence-knowledge-and-conversation) still includes broader query interfaces and hosted scope. Revisioned deletion tombstones already exist in the current document owner; they are not an unimplemented capability. Current cognition uses the active working set for its small bounded pads; this does not claim a shipped D3 query compiler or regional eviction. A storage reorganization must not turn a derived relationship summary into a second editable document; the [root compatibility policy](../AGENTS.md#development-save-policy) still governs unsupported prior layouts.

Fresh creation supplies admitted knowledge documents directly. Compatible restoration preserves their accepted text, origin and subject IDs without moving unrelated About me files, inferring subject associations from filenames or reseeding authored background. Incompatible development data follows the [development policy](../AGENTS.md#development-save-policy); no old-file conversion is implemented.

Per-document limits do not impose a count limit. Existing request and save-size admission still applies; bounded hot retrieval and aggregate actor-storage quotas require measured scale work before large long-running worlds. No automatic eviction or invented ten-subject limit is part of this contract.

## Privacy and correction

Fictional NPC pads and observer identities are private to their owner and authorized god inspection/editing. They never enter the public actor DTO. The private-mind subject search matches and orders only the inspected character's own labels (a learned name or a species description), never global names, and never marks a notes-only subject as the person currently in view ([QU11](limits/interface.md#qu11)). The production [human-private boundary](../archive/07-technical-architecture/data-queries-and-mcp.md#human-private-content-boundary) excludes human-private messages and private character notes from creator powers; they require their own participant/owner permissions and cannot be exposed through an NPC projection. Owner-scoped character-note reading/editing and denial of another human’s private mind are already implemented through the shared authority boundary, with scoped authenticated-browser and native evidence under [MP01](maintainers/multiplayer.md#priority-2-implementation-slices). This does not imply a separate private player-to-player messaging channel or complete hosted security qualification. Restore generation and document revisions fence owner edits. Forgetting/correction must invalidate accepted knowledge and derived interests as well as memory. The current conservative invalidation clears knowledge text alongside the existing accepted-mind reset; forgetting also clears observer identity associations. More selective dependency invalidation requires evidence before replacing this safe boundary.

## Learned methods

Reusable action methods have world-scoped structural definitions and actor-owned acquisitions, separate from recipe invention and prose notepads. Independent permitted reproduction can reuse a definition; catalogue existence alone grants no knowledge. Private support, revocation, compatible-definition checks and tentative evidence follow [action records and learned activities](action-experience.md).

## Physical production-method records

An ordinary item definition may disclose one exact installed production method through a typed recipe-record capability. Reading requires current access to the real item and its container. Inventory selection commits deliberate inspection before method facts appear; the selected page exposes the actual output, ingredients, held quantities, work and permitted limitations. Neither visible scenery, a container exterior nor prose about a method grants knowledge.

**Learn this method** is an explicit instantaneous native transaction. It rechecks current item/placement revisions, exact record meaning, disclosure eligibility, deliberate inspection and the installed method's native compiled meaning before calling the existing recipe-knowledge grant. It creates no item and consumes no record. Repeating it returns **already known** without another acquisition; read-only preview changes no stock, knowledge, history or saved randomness. An unavailable or changed record refuses before granting anything. A portable record can move through ordinary custody and teach another permitted visitor, without an invisible site copy.

The acquired method is actor-private, distinct from prose notepads and reusable action activities. Authored methods carry world provenance rather than invented-by attribution. Installing them teaches nobody, and record learning never completes the bundled live-invention goal. Manufacture and later use retain their normal knowledge, material, work, equipment and target checks. Same-format persistence preserves actual stock, source-bearing acquisitions and taken items; no restored world is reseeded. ## Selected stealth evidence and familiarity consumer

**Accepted shared target, reconciled 2026-10-24; implementation remains with the existing knowledge/perception owners.** [Stealth-awareness contracts](projects/stealth-awareness-tech-design.md#shared-contracts) distinguish world fact, physical signal, perceived evidence, inferred belief, remembered fact and generated language. An observer can remember a person or suspect a familiar voice without acquiring that person's current body location, equipment or activity. Name knowledge, voice recognition and current physical target permission remain independent; the [bundled recognition policy](worlds/base/knowledge.md) selects familiarity, actual evidence quality and Perception rather than guaranteed identity from an unfamiliar first hearing.

Retained facts preserve their own observation channel, time, precision, support and correction/forgetting eligibility through current and cold records, summaries and context. A later introduction or recognized voice does not refresh a previously seen object or unseen attribute. Subject documents and canonical storage keys do not bypass this boundary; an interpretation stays attributed even if its prose sounds certain. Existing source overlays and asynchronous authority checks invalidate dependent interpretations and disclosure without undoing already committed physical consequences. [A/G and related delivery](maintainers/stealth-awareness.md) extend these owners rather than adding a second voice or knowledge registry.

The [observer-specific perception design](projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md) supplies the detailed supported association and fact contract, with implementation in [PP03–PP05](maintainers/perspective-perception.md). An anonymous contact is not automatically a subject document or canonical person. A tentative candidate can be discussed as a hypothesis without opening that candidate's notes as verified information about the current source. Existing independently authorized remembered-document access remains available for its own purpose; it never asserts that the contact is the remembered person. Free-text notes may contain contradictory testimony or a mistaken inference, but cannot manufacture a recognition edge, current location or executable target. Explicit evidence correction and forgetting use their existing mutation owner rather than treating a new statement as an administrative rewrite.

The selected target extends the existing subject reference with an observer-local learned-individual alternative. Sufficient personally acquired evidence can establish a distinguishable unnamed voice or face without revealing a canonical entity. A deliberately written note about that learned individual uses this same knowledge-document owner, validation, revisions and privacy; it does not require a fake person entity or a second note store. The canonical-subject form described in the delivered binding above remains a distinct supported purpose. A later evidence-supported entity association follows PP03's binding and document-reconciliation rules, preserving provenance and refusing silent overwrite of conflicting notes. Detection alone grants neither a durable learned individual nor a note, and private source equality cannot create the association.

Current knowledge projections preserve A’s original measured value/time and actual acquisition separately from provider-held availability. B’s canonical sampled exposure and C’s held acoustic support never authorize a new hidden coordinate, pose or equipment value merely because context is refreshed. A supported two-sample motion or observed axis retains its original received geometry/uncertainty, and a private action target cannot complete it. [Exact family revisions](projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md#exact-shared-source-revisions) identify the real supporting source, including personal-body string digests and summary storage kind; the knowledge document’s own revision is a different authority.

The [native ordinary-warning adapter](projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md#native-ordinary-warning-testimony) grounds an understood place label only in complete prepared current/retained Known-place evidence. Those minimal source-owned place capsules and their disposable exact-label index remain with the existing place/experience owner, not knowledge-note prose. Correction and forgetting invalidate affected entries before native use; incomplete preparation holds instead of declaring the place unknown. No global names, hidden current geometry, prose parser over subject pads or full-history SQL in hearing/cuts is a fallback. The attributed danger claim retains original utterance time and actual admission, so recall or a copied note cannot issue a new current warning.
