Mike confirmed that OpenLegend will switch its game client to **Unreal Engine 5 (UE5)** and recorded the planned client-side character detail and normal-shading targets in [character detail and shading](../../features/graphics/character-detail-and-shading.md) (indexed from [feature owners](../../features/README.md)):

- **Client-side pose drivers as a scalable graphics setting:** live joint-angle pose correctives (UE5 Animation Blueprint **Pose Driver (RBF)** nodes) stay 100% client-side with zero server/gameplay impact, fall back cleanly to 4-bone GPU skinning when disabled, and target a **High / Ultra Character Detail** preset (or dedicated toggle) combined with **camera-distance gating** (active in the character creator and nearby, skipped on distant NPCs).
- **Required skin normal maps with exposed-body-only cost control:** normal maps remain required rather than omitted; under UE5, hardware **BC5 compression** and **Virtual Texture Streaming** support blending `Muscle`, `Fullness` and `Age` normal maps routinely while avoiding VRAM streaming for covered body tiles unless clothing is removed (alongside coverage-mask hidden-surface culling, which already avoids GPU shading on covered body triangles, and a region-split or single `muscle × (1 - fullness)` detail-map bridge while PlayCanvas remains active).
- **Scope boundary:** this records accepted target direction only; current client code remains PlayCanvas 2.22.2, and full UE5 client migration design, hardware/VRAM budgets and disposition of the browser renderer comparison ([RCP](../../maintainers/renderer-comparison.md)) remain open.
