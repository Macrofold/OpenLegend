# Week of October 26, 2026

## October 26, 2026 — Gildmoss becomes the accepted interface design system

After eight rounds of review, Mike made **Gildmoss** — row Q of the direct method's Claude Design canvas — the final design of the in-game interface. Borderless olive glass panels carry lantern light. Pale champagne gold is reflective metal, used only on chosen things and rims, with silver for everything unselected. Gelasio sets names and titles and Figtree everything else. Need bars use rich Cobalt-pigment tones. A day-and-night dial with a sun and moon sits beside a corner minimap, and a soft firelight glow appears only at night. It is saved as the accepted target in the [Gildmoss design system](../../../apps/client/src/design-system/gildmoss/README.md), with his look-and-feel preferences in the [art-direction decisions](../../../art-direction/decisions.md#interface-look-and-feel--accepted-october-26-2026) and his layout and interaction preferences in the [UI brief](../../ui-design-brief.md).

The production package is archived privately on S3 with verified retrieval, along with every round's study material and the shared comparison inputs. It contains tokens, the reference stylesheet, a 117-key icon catalog in the client's existing format, HUD glyphs, status frames, self-hosted fonts, raster art, sources, renders and recipes. A final design round completed the icon family, and a merge plan says what happens to each icon the game uses today: redrawn in Gildmoss, drawn by an existing Gildmoss glyph, retired, or kept as world artwork until the world's own sprites replace it. The [study record](../../../art-direction/studies/2026-10-18-interface-direct-method/README.md) holds the exact references.

Nothing is integrated yet, and the running game keeps its [current design system](../../../apps/client/src/design-system/README.md). Mike asked to stop before integration. Integration must map Gildmoss onto the existing semantic tokens and components rather than add a parallel set. Its open decisions — who can pause, "Try", game-written observations, unbuilt gameplay drawn in the designs, and the day number at midnight — are tracked in the [project plan](../../projects/interface-art-direction-overhaul-plan.md).

## October 27, 2026 — Interface decisions settled; character and map additions designed

Mike settled the Gildmoss interface's open questions ([plan decision 20](../../projects/interface-art-direction-overhaul-plan.md#decisions)). Nothing is implemented yet.
