# Week of October 26, 2026

## October 26 — Emergency reactions and equipment loadouts

**Selected design; runtime implementation and qualification remain open.** [ECL00–ECL08](../../maintainers/emergency-combat-and-loadouts.md), the [feature specification](../../projects/emergency-combat-and-loadouts-feature-spec.md), [technical design](../../projects/emergency-combat-and-loadouts-tech-design.md) and [world policy](../../worlds/base/combat-readiness.md) define a top-center Defend/Retaliate alert, a focused view of the existing world, one opening-attack reaction allowance and rapid exact equipment sets.

The opening adds physical preparation instead of slowing the shared world or granting menu immunity. It is shared across an encounter's attackers, can consume authored stat/effect contributions, and never rewinds released projectiles or completed harm. An anonymous player-assistance cue grants no attacker identity or character preparedness. This deliberately favors an initial player reaction over wholly unannounced targeted ambushes, while preserving ordinary sensing and collateral consequences.

Defend/Retaliate use current gear and ordinary native actions, interruption and recovery. An opt-in combat set responds once to actual mode entry: explicit entry can replace work, automatic assault/offense notices cannot, and emergency reactions take priority. Canceled/refused admitted set changes are not automatically retried. Exiting combat never restores another set. Named sets refer to real carried objects and commit together; missing members refuse the whole change. A simple body brace and one craftable padded outfit make the ordinary working-gear-to-protection journey useful without selecting a full armor system.

Inventory/trade drafts and actual transaction results survive the deliberate emergency transition. Returning restores the last nonempty task workspace without replaying a transaction, restarting work or changing gear. The [repertoire selection](../../projects/emergency-combat-and-loadouts-repertoire-review.md) connects ordinary livelihood, a worthwhile encounter and a quiet return across all catalogue areas; it is not a line-by-line reranking of every entry. Scoped ND11/ND13/ND38 design coverage is recorded in ECL; broader unresolved design and existing runtime/player acceptance remain with their original owners. [Limits](../../limits/emergency-combat-and-loadouts.md) distinguish selected initial tuning from unmeasured balance and capacity.
