# Week of October 26, 2026

## October 26, 2026 — Gildmoss becomes the accepted interface design system

After eight rounds of review, Mike made **Gildmoss** — row Q of the direct method's Claude Design canvas — the final design of the in-game interface. Borderless olive glass panels carry lantern light. Pale champagne gold is reflective metal, used only on chosen things and rims, with silver for everything unselected. Gelasio sets names and titles and Figtree everything else. Need bars use rich Cobalt-pigment tones. A day-and-night dial with a sun and moon sits beside a corner minimap, and a soft firelight glow appears only at night. It is saved as the accepted target in the [Gildmoss design system](../../../apps/client/src/design-system/gildmoss/README.md), with his look-and-feel preferences in the [art-direction decisions](../../../art-direction/decisions.md#interface-look-and-feel--accepted-october-26-2026) and his layout and interaction preferences in the [UI brief](../../ui-design-brief.md).

The production package is archived privately on S3 with verified retrieval, along with every round's study material and the shared comparison inputs. It contains tokens, the reference stylesheet, a 117-key icon catalog in the client's existing format, HUD glyphs, status frames, self-hosted fonts, raster art, sources, renders and recipes. A final design round completed the icon family, and a merge plan says what happens to each icon the game uses today: redrawn in Gildmoss, drawn by an existing Gildmoss glyph, retired, or kept as world artwork until the world's own sprites replace it. The [study record](../../../art-direction/studies/2026-10-18-interface-direct-method/README.md) holds the exact references.

Nothing is integrated yet, and the running game keeps its [current design system](../../../apps/client/src/design-system/README.md). Mike asked to stop before integration. Integration must map Gildmoss onto the existing semantic tokens and components rather than add a parallel set. Its open decisions — who can pause, "Try", game-written observations, unbuilt gameplay drawn in the designs, and the day number at midnight — are tracked in the [project plan](../../projects/interface-art-direction-overhaul-plan.md).

## October 26, 2026 — Gildmoss panel materials and selectable character-panel styles

In the [UI material study](../../../art-direction/studies/2026-10-25-ui-material-language-higgsfield/README.md),
Mike chose Gildmoss's panel materials: **Bare** (glass with a polished bevel and small corner
caps) for panels, **Crest** for buttons and **Filigree** for special panels. For the top-left
character panel he chose elaborate one-off settings in realistic natural materials, set into the
screen corner, and asked for a player setting that switches among the 14 styles he liked.

The [UI brief](../../ui-design-brief.md#visual-style-exploration) owns the direction and
[UIUX22](../../maintainers/ui-ux.md#uiux22) the integration. The production package is
exported, technically checked and privately archived; it is not integrated or selected for
runtime.

## October 27, 2026 — Interface decisions settled; character and map additions designed

Mike settled the Gildmoss interface's open questions ([plan decision 20](../../projects/interface-art-direction-overhaul-plan.md#decisions)). Only the world owner and the admins they authorize may pause a shared world; today any invited player can, which [MP19](../../maintainers/multiplayer.md) tracks. The composer keeps Say and Act and drops "Try" (describing an action for the AI to carry out). A person's window shows observations written by the game. The day number will change at midnight instead of at 08:00 ([BW27](../../maintainers/base-world.md#bw27--day-number-changes-at-midnight)). Gildmoss becomes a theme of its own that players can switch to, and its top-left frame will be the corner piece from the Higgsfield material study. Later that day he chose the study's kit for every panel too ([plan decision 21](../../projects/interface-art-direction-overhaul-plan.md#decisions)): Bare frames and textured glass replace the borderless glass, Crest becomes the secondary button while the primary stays solid gold, and Filigree frames the narrator's caption and special notifications.

He also asked for the gameplay the designs draw but the game lacks to be designed now and built later. [Character and map additions](../../projects/character-and-map-additions-feature-spec.md) specifies Spirit, ten character stats with calculated values, ten equipment slots, a map and minimap with markers and place notes, and nicknames, as six playable stages (CMA01–CMA06). Each builds on existing owners: Spirit is derived from a character's feelings rather than stored; stats change existing movement, injury, energy, crafting, accuracy and practice rules; and scars become stat modifiers at their current strength. Nothing is implemented yet.

## October 27, 2026 — Gildmoss implementation and scope correction

The delivery scope is Gildmoss stage 6, CMA01/CMA02 nicknames/maps and BW27 midnight day numbering. The [interface integration](../../projects/interface-art-direction-overhaul-plan.md) installs Gildmoss beside the other themes, exact external runtime art and the accepted screen composition while showing the installed world's actual attributes and equipment places. Equipment expansion, stats, carrying, Spirit and critical hits are separate future work; their accepted [designs](../../projects/character-and-map-additions-feature-spec.md) remain preserved.

A separate mechanics prototype explored equipment, stat consumers, carrying and outward-expression interpretation. It is preserved at `codex/character-mechanics-prototype`, commit `1ad0bbf6cdcbb0d079e4407bd0938d8a4e2ba05b`, with [bounded evidence](../../verification/character-and-map-additions.md); those results are not current UI-branch delivery or blockers for Gildmoss qualification. No paid game-model calls were dispatched for that prototype.

Fresh Main requests installed artwork through its world-owned opening preparation, with exact-release permission and retention bound atomically on first startup or reviewed Create activation. The independent artwork catalog/permissions remain intact when disposable gameplay is replaced. Shared UI review corrections cover narrow HUD reservations, keyboard explanations, Person/Journal layout and editor Escape/focus ownership. Current interface results and remaining checks belong to the [Gildmoss evidence](../../verification/gildmoss-interface.md); prototype garment/stat/carrying observations retain their separate historical scope.

## October 28, 2026 — Nineteen character-panel styles, Jade Monolith by default

Mike kept all five calmer-jade versions of Jade Lode from the
[material study's round 6](../../../art-direction/studies/2026-10-25-ui-material-language-higgsfield/README.md#round-6--calmer-larger-scale-jade)

## October 28, 2026 — Gildmoss material kit and character styles integrated

[UIUX22](../../maintainers/ui-ux.md#uiux22) delivers the revision-3 Bare/Crest/Filigree kit through Gildmoss's existing component owners and all 14 selectable corner-panel styles. The development runtime preserves its previous artwork and adds all 154 exports; payloads stay outside Git. Settings persists the selected style on this device, defaulting to Jade Lode. Mike approved the installed world's three rows in the first three artwork positions, with the fourth unused; the artwork adds no world mechanic. Primary gold and quiet buttons retain their roles, and no special-notification feature was invented.

Complete decoded style replacement and frame-outset reservations address the loading and viewport defects found in the game. The [bounded evidence](../../verification/gildmoss-interface.md#uiux22-material-kit-and-character-panels--october-28-2026) records actual style/material, viewport/scale, native keyboard, reduced-motion, day/night and theme checks, matched-scale comparisons and local static checks. The exported package's known differences remain preserved. UIUX21's separate fidelity corrections and live World Agent presentation gate remain open.

Mike clarified that the new artwork supplies texture, corners and bezels while Gildmoss keeps its effects ([decision 22](../../projects/interface-art-direction-overhaul-plan.md#decisions)). Runtime glass now retains the original opacity/blur, panel-sized amber night lantern and shadows; chosen controls keep their semantic fill and glow without lighting ordinary secondary buttons. The minimap uses the existing metallic rim gradient. The exported files and layout metadata remain unchanged.

Interface sizing now defaults to Auto, following the available game window from the authored 100% at 1920×1080 down to the existing 90% minimum, with compact layouts and scrolling below that floor. Explicit fixed sizes, including 115%/130% enlargement, remain available and persist independently of game saves. [UXL14](../../limits/ui-ux.md#uxl14) records the tuning and browser-zoom tradeoff.

The existing **Arriving here** opening guidance now shares narration's ornate Filigree frame, as Mike requested, with text inset clear of the corner leaves. Its world-authored content, untimed disclosure and scrolling remain with their existing owners.

Ordinary panels, windows, menus and tooltips now use a continuous rounded glass bezel instead of the visibly repeated Bare border image and gold corner diamonds ([decision 25](../../projects/interface-art-direction-overhaul-plan.md#decisions)). Gildmoss's glass, lighting, blur and shadows remain; narration and opening guidance retain all Filigree borders and ornaments. The source artwork is preserved unchanged.

Icon-search rows now reuse one shared control. Native field defaults no longer draw a second border, fill or padding inside search shells, searchable selects or conversation composers. The shell owns the frame and focus treatment, while existing filtering, submission, clear buttons and multiline drafts retain their native behavior. [Focused verification](../../verification/gildmoss-interface.md#shared-composite-field-correction--october-28-2026) records the right-click regression and affected callers.

Character status trays now disappear when empty and size to their visible groups, with lasting effects first and a small divider before timed statuses. The selected artwork preserves its portrait collar and frame caps while its lower sides adapt; lower lock controls stay beside the need bars. The exact original art release is retained.

## October 30, 2026 — Jade Cloud glass and the revised character-panel catalog

Gildmoss replaces repeated glass tiles with the approved round-16 Clear Slab/Jade Cloud material, including its natural grain, stationary lantern, two mouse-driven light pools, continuous glass bevel and soft top glow. Ordinary and raised panes share the same material. The existing blur, shadows, amber night treatment and semantic control states remain, while Filigree narration keeps its complete ornaments. The [runtime specification](../../../apps/client/src/design-system/gildmoss/README.md#runtime-integration) and [UXL15](../../limits/ui-ux.md#uxl15) own assembly, motion preferences, preparation and resource tradeoffs.

The new exact development release preserves all prior paths and integrates the five added jade styles. The catalog supplies Jade Monolith as the default; valid device choices remain. Selected plate/layout replacement stays atomic, and native glass replaces unused glass-PNG preparation. Content scrolling is separated from the material across popovers, dialogs and notices. The [UIUX22 evidence](../../verification/gildmoss-interface.md#jade-cloud-glass-and-nineteen-styles--october-30-2026) records actual verification and limitations.
