# Week of October 26, 2026

## October 26, 2026 — Gildmoss becomes the accepted interface design system

After eight rounds of review, Mike made **Gildmoss** — row Q of the direct method's Claude Design canvas — the final design of the in-game interface. Borderless olive glass panels carry lantern light. Pale champagne gold is reflective metal, used only on chosen things and rims, with silver for everything unselected. Gelasio sets names and titles and Figtree everything else. Need bars use rich Cobalt-pigment tones. A day-and-night dial with a sun and moon sits beside a corner minimap, and a soft firelight glow appears only at night. It is saved as the accepted target in the [Gildmoss design system](../../../apps/client/src/design-system/gildmoss/README.md), with his look-and-feel preferences in the [art-direction decisions](../../../art-direction/decisions.md#interface-look-and-feel--accepted-october-26-2026) and his layout and interaction preferences in the [UI brief](../../ui-design-brief.md).

The production package is archived privately on S3 with verified retrieval, along with every round's study material and the shared comparison inputs. It contains tokens, the reference stylesheet, a 117-key icon catalog in the client's existing format, HUD glyphs, status frames, self-hosted fonts, raster art, sources, renders and recipes. A final design round completed the icon family, and a merge plan says what happens to each icon the game uses today: redrawn in Gildmoss, drawn by an existing Gildmoss glyph, retired, or kept as world artwork until the world's own sprites replace it. The [study record](../../../art-direction/studies/2026-10-18-interface-direct-method/README.md) holds the exact references.

Nothing is integrated yet, and the running game keeps its [current design system](../../../apps/client/src/design-system/README.md). Mike asked to stop before integration. Integration must map Gildmoss onto the existing semantic tokens and components rather than add a parallel set. Its open decisions — who can pause, "Try", game-written observations, unbuilt gameplay drawn in the designs, and the day number at midnight — are tracked in the [project plan](../../projects/interface-art-direction-overhaul-plan.md).

## October 26, 2026 — Gildmoss panel materials and selectable character-panel styles

In the [UI material study](../../../art-direction/studies/2026-10-25-ui-material-language-higgsfield/README.md),
Mike chose Gildmoss's panel materials: **Bare** (glass with a polished bevel and small corner
caps) for panels, **Crest** for buttons and **Filigree** for special panels. For the top-left
character panel he chose elaborate one-off settings in realistic natural materials, set into the
screen corner, and asked for a player setting that switches among the 14 styles he liked.

The [UI brief](../../ui-design-brief.md#visual-style-exploration) owns the direction and
[UIUX22](../../maintainers/ui-ux.md#uiux22) the integration. The production package is
exported, technically checked and privately archived; it is not integrated or selected for
runtime.

## October 27, 2026 — Interface decisions settled; character and map additions designed

Mike settled the Gildmoss interface's open questions ([plan decision 20](../../projects/interface-art-direction-overhaul-plan.md#decisions)). Only the world owner and the admins they authorize may pause a shared world; today any invited player can, which [MP19](../../maintainers/multiplayer.md) tracks. The composer keeps Say and Act and drops "Try" (describing an action for the AI to carry out). A person's window shows observations written by the game. The day number will change at midnight instead of at 08:00 ([BW27](../../maintainers/base-world.md#bw27--day-number-changes-at-midnight)). Gildmoss becomes a theme of its own that players can switch to, and its top-left frame will be the corner piece from the Higgsfield material study. Later that day he chose the study's kit for every panel too ([plan decision 21](../../projects/interface-art-direction-overhaul-plan.md#decisions)): Bare frames and textured glass replace the borderless glass, Crest becomes the secondary button while the primary stays solid gold, and Filigree frames the narrator's caption and special notifications.

He also asked for the gameplay the designs draw but the game lacks to be designed now and built later. [Character and map additions](../../projects/character-and-map-additions-feature-spec.md) specifies Spirit, ten character stats with calculated values, ten equipment slots, a map and minimap with markers and place notes, and nicknames, as six playable stages (CMA01–CMA06). Each builds on existing owners: Spirit is derived from a character's feelings rather than stored; stats change existing movement, injury, energy, crafting, accuracy and practice rules; and scars become stat modifiers at their current strength. Nothing is implemented yet.

## October 25–27, 2026 — Personal evidence, attention and selective memory

The [received-evidence foundation](../../verification/personal-perception-foundation.md) and [attention/memory core](../../verification/attention-and-memory-core.md) connect existing senses to broad surroundings, configurable detailed attention, exact recent chronology, shared familiarity and selective lasting memories. Local identity, names, current detail and remembered places have separate authority. Immutable source values share storage while each character retains exact permitted receipts; correction, forgetting and expiry remove dependent sources/search data. Player history and ordinary character decisions consume these records. Future physical producers, full PP07/SAI-A and wider speech/crowd acceptance remain separate.

The [selected design](../../projects/attention-and-scenes-tech-design.md#5-perception-attention-interpretation-and-memory) replaces deliberate-watch recording, endpoint-only movement summaries and copied background sampling with automatic attended chronology and observer-specific receipts over common source fragments. Ten configurable detail slots coexist with broad/static surroundings and fair discovery; the [research comparison](../../projects/attention-and-scenes-research.md) preserves alternatives and rationale. Physical relations and one optional discovery attempt per genuine free interval justify thinking without polling or precompiled personality/object associations. A few-real-second interpretation objective adds no recurring model call or new hard cutoff. One read-only query batch can precede one separately funded answer. Finite AI working copies do not extend optional retention: expired sources can invalidate delayed replies, avoiding a second retention-lease owner. World defaults, models, output ceilings and automatic-retry policy remain unchanged; integrated semantic and capacity qualification stays explicit in [PS04-A](../../maintainers/product-scalability.md#ps04-a--core-attention-and-recording).

Existing guidance now routes personal evidence, public identity references and game-time deadlines to their contracts. Copied performance comparisons verify pinned contents and actual local package resolution. These extend existing owners without another policy layer; [routing observations](../../verification/agent-guidance/2026/2026-10-24-workflow-adoption.md#personal-evidence-routing--october-25-2026) retain their limits.

## October 28, 2026 — Exact received data share one storage owner

Current observations and recent chronology use the same exact-value pool, including repeated receiver headers and observation frames. Personal originals, permission, timing and full logical retention charges remain distinct; complete memories and captured cuts own self-contained proofs. Last-owner cleanup releases individual values without reusing their addresses. The current-format codec remains independent of the rest of the world, and incompatible development data is replaced under the existing policy. This removes repeated data rather than reducing what characters receive or changing attention/world defaults; [the implementation contract](../../projects/attention-and-scenes-tech-design.md#53-recent-chronology-without-narrative-boundaries) and [measured qualification](../../verification/attention-and-memory-core.md#shared-value-completion--october-28-2026) distinguish correctness and storage results from still-open whole-game speed and capacity acceptance.
