# Week of November 2, 2026

## November 5, 2026 — All nine stand-ins replaced in the art selection

The art selection now names a library or existing game model for each of the nine low-poly stand-ins the game's worlds show, chosen from Mike's survey comparisons. [scripts/prepare-game-props.ts](../../../scripts/prepare-game-props.ts) makes five of them, with a size multiplier for a pile whose size its role sets and textures reduced to 512 px by the new `reduceImages` helper in [scripts/model-files.ts](../../../scripts/model-files.ts); three are existing published art named directly. The game uses openly licensed art only (CC0 and CC BY), and the asset library will be published publicly ([AD09](../../maintainers/art-direction.md#ad09--follow-through-on-the-grounded-stylized-direction)).

## November 4, 2026 — Survey comparisons and the first library props in the game

The survey page opens on a **Comparisons** category built from [comparisons.json](../../../apps/client/src/props/art-library/comparisons.json): each subject shows the game's own piece (survey, library or art-study art) beside chosen candidates, so a pick is one like. [scripts/prepare-game-props.ts](../../../scripts/prepare-game-props.ts) turns a picked survey or library piece into an art-study group the game's appearance selection names, at the page's size on its footprint; the selection now replaces the supply box, workbench, tied bundle and supply chest with anchor-kit pieces Mike liked. The asset library recipe gained a `tints` plan option for materials a pack colours per item, and `excludePieces` now also drops parts from a model shown whole; [scripts/convert-to-gltf.py](../../../scripts/convert-to-gltf.py) can convert one folder of a pack. The library holds 3,362 pieces from 135 packs. Publishing the props and library remains ([AD09](../../maintainers/art-direction.md#ad09--follow-through-on-the-grounded-stylized-direction)).

## November 4, 2026 — The asset library in the survey page

The world asset survey page now also shows the [asset library](../../../art-direction/studies/2026-11-04-asset-library/README.md): 2,989 pieces from 85 free packs, each marked a sure fit or on the fence with its reason, 36 pieces to a page with **Previous** and **Next**, a **Marks** filter and a panel of 72 paid candidates. [scripts/prepare-asset-library.ts](../../../scripts/prepare-asset-library.ts) prepares whole packs from an authored plan: it files pieces under subjects by name rules, keeps makers' sizes (calibrated from the survey for the same kits, set by a pack's unit in the plan, or corrected by a power of ten when far outside a subject's range), splits files that hold a whole set into one piece per object, and attaches separately shipped textures and Godot materials by material name. [scripts/convert-to-gltf.py](../../../scripts/convert-to-gltf.py) first converts packs that ship only Blender or FBX files, keeping every image inside the pack and reconnecting the textures that Blender 2.7x files keep in a form current Blender drops; a plan texture set can be limited to some of a pack's files. The survey and game-animal recipes now share those model helpers in [scripts/model-files.ts](../../../scripts/model-files.ts), and the macOS `sips` image-reduction allowance in the [network-boundary inventory](../../../scripts/network-boundary-inventory.ts) moved with them. Publication to S3 remains ([AD09](../../maintainers/art-direction.md#ad09--follow-through-on-the-grounded-stylized-direction)).

## November 3, 2026 — Free-source animals and artwork credits in the game

Mike ranked the free animal sets from his world asset survey: WildMesh 3D first, _Fantasy Creatures_ second and the current game picks third, with every low-poly family ruled out for animals. The game's doe, stag and brown bear now come from WildMesh 3D and its wolf from _Fantasy Creatures_; the hare and woodland bird are unchanged. The [art changelog entry](../../../art-direction/changelog/2026/2026-11-02.md#november-3-2026--free-animal-sets-ranked-wildmesh-3d-animals-enter-the-game) records the reasons and [AD09](../../maintainers/art-direction.md#ad09--follow-through-on-the-grounded-stylized-direction) the remaining publication.

Because WildMesh 3D's models are CC BY 4.0, Settings → Credits gained an **Artwork credits** list built from the credits that art-study catalogs record, covering every attribution-required model the game ships ([AD06](../../maintainers/art-direction.md#ad06--show-artwork-credits-in-the-game)). The network-boundary check now admits a reviewed local tool other than Git or Node by exact name; the asset survey's macOS `sips` image reduction is the only one ([design](../../projects/completed/bounded-network-requests-tech-design.md#10-source-boundary-enforcement)).
