## Bounded live playtest

1. Back up a stopped save or set a fresh `OPEN_LEGEND_DATA_DIR`. Follow [live setup](live-ai-setup.md) for the selected backend; current full NPC cognition requires Macrofold plus explicit model/compute allowance. Verify model/rate configuration and any recorded credit blocker before dispatch. Keep provider-side account limits as appropriate. Start the local game and inspect **AI & allowance**.
2. Talk to Ada about a distinctive, harmless detail. Observe a real Jev call and LLM response, move through ordinary play, and later ask about that detail. Confirm permitted memory contributes to recall; do not expect access to unseen facts.
3. Observe a real resident decision and a meaningful reconsideration. Confirm ordinary walking, gathering, eating and animal behavior do not increase model-call counts each step. Record Jev direct-action and deeper-reflection routes separately.
4. With an empty recipe registry, freely describe a physical sling made from available cord and prepared fibers. Inspect the generated recipe's roles, material/work costs and provenance. Craft it, equip it, carry stones and hunt. A miss is a valid physical outcome; no service timeout should consume ammunition.
5. Harvest an animal's finite remains, cook meat at the fire and eat it. Repeated harvest must not yield more food. Ask for the same invention in different wording and confirm reuse without a second generation call. Check distinct feedback for unsupported, forbidden and materially incompatible requests.
6. Invent and craft a bow and a compatible arrow separately using suitable branch/fiber/bone roles. The bow cannot shoot sling stones; an arrow alone cannot fire. Confirm this uses the shared ranged family.
7. Pause during pending work, switch away/return, reload, restart and repeat an idempotent request. Confirm no duplicate recipe, ammo, harvest or paid dispatch. A paused completion must not apply; explicit chat/invention holds successful results until resume, while background thought cancels. Verify no next paid stage while paused, stale checks after resume and shutdown cleanup; held-result restart recovery remains pending. Uncertainty retains its spending reservation. Resume retains the exact saved world, without offline catch-up.
8. Record the actual receipts, model identifiers, routes, call/token counts, configured-price estimates, observed latency, refusals and failures. Keep keys/private player data out of shared reports. Acceptance requires successful live cases, not merely configured status. If the chosen model misroutes or produces invalid definitions, keep that gate open and adjust/evaluate the policy.

## Current limits

One map, one NPC, one local process and one owner are intentional. The G1 envelope admits at most 64 recipes across three construction families. Workflows allow one concurrent AI job; ambiguity/failure does not trigger automatic paid retries. Recent events and ordinary memory are bounded; receipts remain durable without compaction. The world has a finite starting ecosystem and a long-lived starting fire, not replenishment or a full ecosystem. Player recovery exists for the personal playtest; NPC death is permanent in the current world.

The original procedural artwork is an initial playable treatment, not a final art-production pipeline. Voice, multiplayer, world creation, deep anatomy, thermal propagation, structures, arbitrary G2 scripts and commerce are outside this slice. The architecture keeps those future responsibilities separate; it does not claim they are already implemented.
