## Ownership and purpose

The [feature specification](../projects/participants-and-world-travel-feature-spec.md) refines [DG29](needs-design.md#dg29--more-participants-and-travel-between-worlds) beneath [PS05–PS08](product-scalability.md#ps05--transitions-protection-logout-and-clocks). The existing [product-scalability inventory](../limits/product-scalability.md) remains the single owner for these proposed limits. [Multiplayer](multiplayer.md), current [simulation time](../simulation-time.md), [save/load](../save-and-load.md), data/transfer and work/accounting retain their native responsibilities. This is a focused delivery consumer, not another identity, clock, world writer or payment system.

The first useful outcome is a wanted visit to a real place, with a clear invitation, appropriate participation and a dependable next step home. Broader capacity is valuable when it lets more people enjoy that experience without weakening particular relationships, actions or history. A small invited game does not need unattended communities, a crowd scene, a marketplace, a seamless federation or a campaign before it is worth playing.

- [ ] **PT01 — Establish the useful visit and participation scope.** Select actual supported origins, destinations, bodies, permissions, native activities and return conditions. Distinguish account access, membership, current control, bodily presence, place and history. **Exit:** a visitor can understand the invitation, enter the intended existing place, do something worthwhile with another participant and leave without a copied companion or a mandatory commerce step. Technical design and evidence remain explicit.
- [ ] **PT02 — Complete preparation, arrival and return.** Reuse current authority, readiness, safe placement and exact outcomes before changing bodily presence. Include cancellation, changed permission, expired capacity, reconnect, lost acknowledgment, restart and current-format recovery. **Exit:** one continuous identity and its actual possessions are in one known state; unavailable return remains a truthful safe pending outcome with a resolution path, never an invented destination or destructive retry.
- [ ] **PT03 — Reconcile property, imports, clocks and connected effects.** Keep entry, building, item access, harm, inspection and campaign grants separate. Define supported carried bodies, items, learned abilities, definitions, autonomous companions and information under actual provenance and rights. Preserve current clocks until a selected extension is adopted and qualified. **Exit:** visiting neither imports privileged resources nor leaks private history; refusals occur before irreversible travel, and connected-world outcomes cannot be duplicated by restore or clock changes.
- [ ] **PT04 — Provide understandable capacity and fair waiting.** Qualify the actual interaction envelope, including admitted effect growth after entry. Define visible waits, complete arrival admission, resident/party/reconnect treatment, inactivity, cancellation and abuse handling before expansion. **Exit:** overload preserves accepted consequences and permitted evidence; a queue is neither fabricated capacity nor an excuse to strand participants in a hazard. Costs and current authority remain visible without secret purchase priority.
- [ ] **PT05 — Preserve belonging through absence and mature history.** Reuse current MP04 exit/protected inactivity and source-world retention; introduce dangerous logout or independent calendar only through their separate adopted consumers. Explain actual return condition, current facts, memories, testimony and public news without inventing a biography or replaying every missed thought. **Exit:** changed geometry, absence, world closure and a long history preserve legitimate continuity under disclosed funded terms; quiet occupancy alone grants no deletion or forced timeline merge.
- [ ] **PT06 — Qualify useful growth and complete economics.** Compare concentrated/dispersed encounters, one populated/many small worlds, return bursts, cold preparation, long histories, changing permissions and all relevant storage/network/inference/presentation costs. Retain D5/D6/PF and existing operational gates. **Exit:** publish only the actual supported workload with measured tails, sustainable funding and worthwhile play; narrow or stop an expansion whose complexity or costs exceed its player value. Native fixtures, stored identities and queued-away arrivals do not establish live capacity.

## Verification boundary

All PT delivery items remain open. The specification makes proposed product decisions reviewable; it does not authorize a public service, paid experiment, larger advertised capacity, destructive retention action or new world rule. Existing checked authority, inactive participation and regional-time work remains checked only for its original evidenced scope. The design assignment performs documentation review and validation, without runtime or player-load qualification.
