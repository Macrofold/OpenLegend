## Ownership and purpose

The [feature specification](../projects/creator-fund-and-contributor-governance-feature-spec.md) refines conditional [DG30/ND25](needs-design.md#dg30--a-creator-fund-and-contributor-governance) and [D37](../../archive/05-project/open-decisions.md). It consumes the existing financial, rights and platform-authority owners. [Customer offers](commercial-offers.md) and [published packs and creator revenue](published-packs-and-creator-revenue.md) retain their distinct customer and creator obligations; a grant does not recreate those flows or give a fund reviewer world-administration authority.

Fund a small wanted contribution only when a real affordable program is selected. The first proposed operating round should help a creator make an enjoyable or useful improvement that the game can actually sustain. A concise manual process can complete independently of a grant portal, marketplace, election system, token or new corporate structure. The full operating budget and the time to finish accepted obligations matter as much as the award amounts.

- [ ] **FUND01 — Adopt a complete funded round.** Name the accountable payer and budget owner, actual available funds after existing liabilities, award envelope, administration/recovery reserve, operator/reviewer capacity, calendar, eligible scope and shutdown disposition. Distinguish unrestricted money, dedicated sponsorship and customer or creator liabilities. **Exit:** the real round can honor every accepted commitment without future sales, automatic customer allocations, unpaid recurring work or an implied second round. An illustrative budget is not authorization to launch.
- [ ] **FUND02 — Make application and selection fair and manageable.** Publish understandable eligibility, brief application inputs, capacity, selection criteria, conflicts, decision reasons and correction/reconsideration. Require no purchase, popularity campaign or unpaid bespoke implementation. **Exit:** an applicant can propose worthwhile work, decline a changed offer or understand a rejection; bounded reviewer work does not silently exclude late or inconvenient applicants, reward manufactured activity or require everyone to have prior fame.
- [ ] **FUND03 — Complete the creator's work and payment journey.** Agree scope, usable milestones, exact acceptance evidence, licensed outputs, amounts, timing, payment readiness, partial delivery, corrections, extensions, cancellation and dispute remedies before work. Reconcile failed or uncertain payments without duplicate release. **Exit:** earned work is paid under the agreed terms, evidence of an unsuccessful experiment can be accepted when that was the funded scope, and neither changed popularity nor operator withdrawal silently rewrites the deal. All real contracting and payout qualification remains open.
- [ ] **FUND04 — Preserve rights, privacy and truthful public proof.** Use target-component and inherited licenses, actual contributor consent and publishing authority; separate funding, attribution, copyright, use rights, private history and maintenance. Keep payment identity and confidential evidence private while publishing a useful permitted result and reconciled financial summary. **Exit:** no grant, badge or vote appropriates another person's work, leaks a world, overrides current privacy or claims unperformed delivery; the receiving maintainer can accept, decline or support the exact contribution through ordinary authority.
- [ ] **FUND05 — Give contributors and supporters a bounded voice.** Define useful contribution, financial support, overlapping membership, independent review, recusal, advisory recommendations and actual binding budget/technical authority. Explain how feedback affected decisions and how errors or conflicts can be challenged. **Exit:** a larger purchase or more commits buys no controlling vote; applicants do not judge their own awards; absent or conflicted reviewers have a complete replacement/deferral path; community participation grants no company equity, automatic relicensing, privileged gameplay or control of others' worlds.
- [ ] **FUND06 — Review value, cost and closure before expansion.** Measure actual useful output, creator experience, player benefit, maintainability, full cash cost and operator effort; finish reporting, remaining payment/retrieval obligations and finite closure. **Exit:** continuing the fund needs fresh sustainable authority and a reason grounded in wanted results. Stop or simplify a round whose selection machinery or operating burden costs more than the contribution is worth. No governance software or recurring program is a prerequisite for completing the first manual round.

## Verification boundary

All FUND delivery items remain open. This documentation assignment proposes the operating program; it does not create a fund, authorize a payment, recruit an applicant, adopt a creator standard, change corporate or platform authority, or establish a live result. Preserve the existing rights and customer-service promises while making actual program adoption and any necessary technical work explicit.
