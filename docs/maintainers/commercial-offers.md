## Ownership and first useful delivery

The [customer and supporter offers feature specification](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md) refines ND22, ND24 and the paid-offer portion of ND26. It consumes [PD10](production-deployment.md#conditional-launch-and-expansion), [INV-13](inventions-and-world-evolution.md#inv-13--episode-budgets-and-installed-cost-enforcement) and the [billing and entitlement owner](../../archive/07-technical-architecture/billing-and-usage-reporting.md). This is one commercial product consumer, not a second wallet, payment platform or runtime task system. [CO-L01–CO-L08](../limits/commercial-offers.md) owns its proposed scope and allowance policies; shared billing, production, invention and continuation inventories retain their purposes.

Start with a demonstrated enjoyable attended world and one understandable owner-paid offer. A guest does not need a second hosting subscription for access already funded by the host. The accepted free recurring invention tier, larger paid allowances, platform membership with a fixed creator allocation and standalone premium-world entry remain product directions. They need not all launch together. Optional supporter recognition can follow independently. Creator distribution and payouts belong to DG28; a separately funded grant/governance program belongs to DG30.

- [ ] **CO01 — Adopt one deliverable offer.** Select an actual audience, operator, funding/time ceiling, review point, supported play, prices, units, real renewal period, service scope and retention/retrieval terms. Distinguish adopted values from historic examples. Qualify PD01/PD10 and applicable age, territory, rights and remedy requirements for the actual offer. **Exit:** a customer can explain what they buy, who can participate, what consumes each allowance and what happens at the boundary; the operator can actually deliver it. No unattended-life, capacity or margin claim is inferred from a subscription.
- [ ] **CO02 — Consume one authoritative account and cost history.** Implement the specification's qualifying-invention classification, pre-dispatch reservation, successful-admission consumption, explicit revisions/bundles and real-period treatment under the existing billing/INV owners. Retain origin across worlds and player-directed NPC work; distinguish allowance from provider spend and world permissions. **Exit:** two worlds competing for the final unit, late admission, uncertain execution, failure and restore neither double-spend nor invent free execution; the person sees a useful recovery path and their actual remaining rights.
- [ ] **CO03 — Complete the customer lifecycle.** Deliver checkout recovery, paid-but-unfulfilled state, renewal, plan changes, optional prepaid funding, exhaustion, cancellation, service expiry, retained-work retrieval and traceable refunds/disputes. Current commercial authority survives fictional restore. **Exit:** no refresh or duplicate event repeats a charge/benefit; cancellation preserves the selected paid-through service; loss of optional funding has its proper scoped effect; unresolved refunds stay visibly unresolved. Technical/provider integration and end-to-end payment evidence remain required.
- [ ] **CO04 — Deliver one optional historical dedication.** Adopt the exact official-world or public-chronicle venue, review and public-name consent, one redemption, correction/privacy, retirement remedy and any representation's ordinary fictional fate. Separate original attribution from current possession and earned contributor credit. **Exit:** rejection, retry, transfer, destruction, refund and restore cannot silently substitute a name or sell the same naming claim twice. No special control, perpetual computation or binding governance is implied.
- [ ] **CO05 — Run the bounded renewal test.** After permission to sell and the actual operating envelope exist, evaluate the business objective of ten renewing customers, positive cash flow and sustainable operator time. Separate genuine game renewal from support donations; include heavy usage, failures, refunds, liabilities and founder hours. **Exit:** record observed play, voluntary renewals/cancellations, complete costs and remaining exposure; improve or stop an unsustainable offer before its approved envelope is exhausted. A spreadsheet example or first successful charge is not this evidence.
- [ ] **CO06 — Expand only a wanted, qualified offer.** Preserve standalone premium entry, explain overlapping platform/world benefits, and consume DG28's adopted allocation/rights/payout behavior before enabling it. Add broader patron packages or DG30 participation only with their own complete terms and capacity. **Exit:** added offers solve observed customer/creator needs without duplicate entitlement fees, unsupported promises or an administration burden larger than the game.

## Verification boundary

All items remain open until their named product adoption, technical, implementation and observed acceptance work occurs. This design assignment runs no payment, infrastructure, private-customer study or paid-provider experiment. Existing checked INV accounting work stays checked for its recorded scope; it does not certify the full commercial lifecycle.
