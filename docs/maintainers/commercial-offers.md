## Ownership and first useful delivery

The [customer and supporter offers feature specification](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md) refines ND22, ND24 and the paid-offer portion of ND26. It consumes [PD10](production-deployment.md#conditional-launch-and-expansion), [INV-13](inventions-and-world-evolution.md#inv-13--episode-budgets-and-installed-cost-enforcement) and the [billing and entitlement owner](../../archive/07-technical-architecture/billing-and-usage-reporting.md). This is one commercial product consumer, not a second wallet, payment platform or runtime task system. [CO-L01–CO-L08](../limits/commercial-offers.md) owns its proposed scope and allowance policies; shared billing, production, invention and continuation inventories retain their purposes.

Use the [initial-offer decision brief](../projects/initial-commercial-offer-decision-brief.md) to compare the managed-world candidate with its strongest feasible browser alternative for the actual [first-playable experience](../worlds/base/playable-openings.md#first-playable-game-target). The existing owner-paid lifecycle design is reusable preparation, not evidence that subscriptions have won the comparison. Actual offer selection does not require building several billing systems. [Player validation](first-playable-validation.md) supplies game/audience evidence without substituting for commercial readiness.

For an adopted owner-paid offer, a guest does not need a second hosting subscription for access already funded by the host. The accepted free recurring invention tier, larger paid allowances, platform membership with a fixed creator allocation and standalone premium-world entry remain product directions. They need not all launch together. Optional supporter recognition can follow independently. Creator distribution and payouts belong to DG28; a separately funded grant/governance program belongs to DG30.

- [ ] **CO01 — Compare and adopt one deliverable offer.** Follow the linked decision brief: compare realistic player preferences, the same current game promise, access/service terms, costs, support and downside exposure for the managed-world candidate and a feasible browser alternative. Distinguish survey interest, game demand and patronage. Select an actual audience, operator, funding/time ceiling, review point, desired business outcome, supported play, prices, units, any real renewal period, service scope and retention/retrieval terms before dependent implementation or public promises. Historical examples are not adopted values. Qualify PD01/PD10 and applicable age, territory, rights and remedy requirements for the actual offer; the current adult/browser choices are not unresolved. **Exit:** an adopted comparison result and deliverable release profile; a customer can explain what they buy, who can participate, what consumes each allowance and what happens at the boundary. No unattended-life, capacity or margin claim follows merely from payment.
- [ ] **CO02 — Consume one authoritative account and cost history.** Implement the selected offer's qualifying-invention classification, pre-dispatch reservation, successful-admission consumption, explicit revisions/bundles and applicable real-period treatment under the existing billing/INV owners. Retain origin across worlds and player-directed NPC work; distinguish allowance from provider spend and world permissions. **Exit:** two worlds competing for the final unit, late admission, uncertain execution, failure and restore neither double-spend nor invent free execution; the person sees a useful recovery path and their actual remaining rights. Do not enable unselected tiers merely because their product specification exists.
- [ ] **CO03 — Complete the customer lifecycle.** Deliver the adopted offer's checkout recovery, paid-but-unfulfilled state, applicable renewal/plan changes, optional prepaid funding, exhaustion, cancellation, service expiry, retained-work retrieval and traceable refunds/disputes. Current commercial authority survives fictional restore. **Exit:** no refresh or duplicate event repeats a charge/benefit; cancellation preserves the selected paid-through service; loss of optional funding has its proper scoped effect; unresolved refunds stay visibly unresolved. Technical/provider integration and end-to-end payment evidence remain required.
- [ ] **CO04 — Deliver one optional historical dedication.** Adopt the exact official-world or public-chronicle venue, review and public-name consent, one redemption, correction/privacy, retirement remedy and any representation's ordinary fictional fate. Separate original attribution from current possession and earned contributor credit. **Exit:** rejection, retry, transfer, destruction, refund and restore cannot silently substitute a name or sell the same naming claim twice. No special control, perpetual computation or binding governance is implied.
- [ ] **CO05 — Run the bounded paid-offer test.** After permission to sell and the operating envelope exist, evaluate the adopted offer against its actual financial/time objective. For a recurring managed-world offer, retain the proposed ten-renewing-customer milestone with positive operating cash flow and sustainable operator time; it is an early operating test, not proof of livelihood. For a different offer use corresponding purchase, satisfaction, continuing-service and cost measures rather than imposing renewals. Separate genuine game purchases/renewal from support donations; include heavy usage, failures, refunds, liabilities and founder hours. **Exit:** observed play, eligible renewals/cancellations or appropriate purchase evidence, complete costs and remaining exposure; improve or stop an unsustainable offer before the approved envelope is exhausted. A spreadsheet or first successful charge is not this evidence.
- [ ] **CO06 — Expand only a wanted, qualified offer.** Preserve standalone premium entry, explain overlapping platform/world benefits, and consume DG28's adopted allocation/rights/payout behavior before enabling it. Add broader patron packages or DG30 participation only with their own complete terms and capacity. **Exit:** added offers solve observed customer/creator needs without duplicate entitlement fees, unsupported promises or an administration burden larger than the game.

## Verification boundary

All items remain open until their named product adoption, technical, implementation and observed acceptance work occurs. Documentation completion runs no payment, infrastructure, private-customer study or paid-provider experiment. Existing checked INV accounting work stays checked for its recorded scope; it does not certify the full commercial lifecycle. The first-playable plan's actual game tests are independent of a decision to charge; a commercial test cannot advertise missing scenario requirements as delivered.
