## What the research does not reopen

[CTR](combat-and-threat-responses.md), [SPK](speech-and-social-perception.md), [NST](npc-speech-streaming.md) and [IS](intelligent-simulation.md) already have selected designs. Their unchecked runtime/qualification work is not a missing full design merely because Kevin or Mantella uses a different mechanism. Existing [CE](character-experience.md), [AG](agent-agency.md), [CR](cognition-redesign.md), perception, native work and memory owners remain responsible for their current contracts.

Ordinary continuation, required recent outcomes, independent character choices, complete permitted evidence and fresh native admission are existing requirements. First diagnose failures through those owners. Do not create another nervous-system service, scheduler, plan store, memory truth, dialogue controller or per-frame model loop to implement the same responsibility twice.

The [accepted content/companion policy](../agent-content-and-companion-safety.md) remains unchanged. Low unnecessary refusal is a core model-quality concern within permitted fiction, not permission to weaken the service boundary. A resident's authentic disagreement is not a failed model refusal.

## 1. A bounded local decision-provider comparison

**Potential benefit:** more responsive or economical judgment without changing what the person can know or choose. [The model catalogue](../../archive/02-research/npc-cognition/04-models-local-hosted-openrouter.md) establishes Kev as a concrete candidate, not the only candidate. [The architecture synthesis](../../archive/02-research/npc-cognition/10-architecture-composition-and-serving.md#3-separate-understanding-a-command-from-choosing-to-follow-it) adds compact Qwen, FunctionGemma, LFM2.5 and fixed-label alternatives for appropriate distinct jobs.

**Needs before adoption:** exact typed API and score semantics, supported batching/dependencies, context limits, error/abstention handling, version pinning, cost admission, CPU/GPU ownership and equivalent evidence across direct/platform/local routes. A local process is not free or automatically trusted. Preserve current provider bindings until a separately approved change. Command extraction must not silently become forced obedience or replace the character's voluntary choice.

**Next evidence:** E1/E2 with current providers as baselines, existing synthetic tooling as developmental evidence and new natural held-out cases. Include negative/quoted/hypothetical requests and dynamic action lists. **Owners:** R03, IS03, CR12 and existing provider/execution/accounting owners. The existing conditional routing design is not replaced; only a proven new adapter/deployment requirement needs a scoped technical extension.

## 2. Finite motor activity versus repeated independent choices

**Potential benefit:** a resident can execute a chosen task fluidly while speech and higher-level consideration proceed. Kevin's native controller illustrates this well, but retains combat orders with different authority from OpenLegend's selected policy.

**Question requiring an explicit future choice:** should a resident be able to choose a finite combat activity that authorizes more than one strike? The current CTR contract gives one offensive option one strike. This research grants no attack loop, forced retaliation, implicit retargeting or infinite pursuit.

A future selected extension must specify chosen purpose, admitted duration/termination conditions, eligible targets, loss of sight, withdrawal, surrender/changed evidence, resource limits, interruption and saved continuation. Separate motor adjustment within an accepted act from a new consequential choice. Reuse current native navigation/actions rather than asking a model for every movement delta.

**Next evidence:** E1 plus actual CTR qualification to identify whether repeated choice is the material blocker. **Owners:** AG/CTR and their authored-world policies; DG02/ND11 only for genuinely new scope. Existing noncombat continuation needs no new product decision merely to be used correctly.

## 3. Actor-grounded references and optional visual evidence

**Potential benefit:** people can discuss and act on real nearby places/objects without inventing coordinates. Kevin's numbered target selection is a useful grounding pattern; his player-camera input is not an acceptable implicit observer model for OpenLegend.

First compare complete actor-permitted structured projection with current input. A selected image extension needs a viewpoint/capture owner, source time, UI/label exclusion, permitted subjects, recognition rules, uncertainty, exact handle binding and stale-result validation. A player pointing or describing a location does not grant hidden camera evidence. Native reachability and resource checks remain authoritative.

**Next evidence:** E4 with missing, stale, ambiguous and actually absent evidence separately identified. Include hidden subjects and changed destinations. **Owners:** EPR/SW, SPK and CR context/knowledge; DG02 and existing discovery owners where consumed. Continuous screenshot processing is not a selected requirement or prerequisite.

## 4. Character coherence, own-action truth and belief revision

**Potential benefit:** distinct residents can disagree or change their minds without denying their actual conduct or accepting a player's unsupported assertion as observation. Mantella's impressive dialogue and visible grounding failures motivate this combined test.

No new psychology schema is yet justified. Preserve actual own work and outcomes, attributed testimony, accepted biography and independent interpretation through the existing experience path. Compare stock, creative and explicitly low-refusal models on text-only whole episodes before adding expressive voice. Do not equate more profanity, compliance or dramatic self-report with character depth.

A future prompt/context refinement must state what evidence remains required, what can be selected, how uncertainty is represented and which whole episodes should improve. Existing complete About me inclusion cannot be silently dropped to imitate Mantella's overflow policy. Keep an issued request distinct from a completed or denied action in later dialogue and memory.

**Next evidence:** E3/E4 and the existing CE flows, extended by the [original-character and longitudinal protocol](../../archive/02-research/npc-cognition/11-evaluation-and-research-gates.md). **Owners:** DG02/CE05, CR12/AG12, R10/R24; DG23/ND19 only for lasting outlook reinterpretation, not ordinary initial fidelity. Shared service instructions and output admission remain unchanged.

## 5. Optional expression and truly interactive voice

**Potential benefit:** a useful reply can be heard earlier and delivered in a recognizable, context-sensitive voice. This is separate from better reasoning. The [voice dossier](../../archive/02-research/npc-cognition/05-realtime-voice-and-serving.md) and [additional direct-audio candidates](../../archive/02-research/npc-cognition/10-architecture-composition-and-serving.md#6-speech-component-quality-and-semantic-timing-are-different-questions) record deployment and rights considerations.

**Reuse:** NST owns growing committed public text, not physical speech duration. HE07 owns optional speech media and microphone composition. Its current private editable unsent draft is not Kevin's direct speech-to-action behavior.

**Questions for a selected extension:** exact committed words versus delivery directions; generated, played and actually heard audio; voice identity and unknown speakers; permitted partial fragments; interruption and late chunks; output checks before audio exposure; cold/warm/concurrent serving; lawful reference voices; text-only equivalence; and whether direct voice submission is actually wanted. Timed hearing and direct/full-duplex voice require their own perception/authority decisions rather than being hidden inside a TTS adapter.

**Next evidence:** E6, including extended interaction and separate semantic/vocal emotion scoring. Compare a cascade before investing in a direct-audio design. **Owners:** DG26/ND16, HE07, applicable NST/NC/PS and release/content/privacy owners. No commercial self-hosting of Breeze is assumed from its code license. No always-listening microphone or audio model is enabled here.

## 6. Useful recall and shared computation without shared private minds

**Potential benefit:** better later choices at lower preparation/maintenance cost. The paper panel suggests temporal, lexical and associative retrieval; Mantella demonstrates computation sharing for identical heard histories and also shows why a shared scene prompt differs from independent actors.

**Reuse first:** DG04's demonstrated-omission gate and existing R11/R19 comparisons. Preserve canonical source records, required evidence, correction/forgetting, restore generation and actor-private accepted understanding. An optional candidate service remains replaceable; a generated summary is not a new world fact.

**Conditional questions:** can exact common preparation or prefix computation be reused safely, and is a richer candidate method worth its ingestion/operational cost? Do not merge private biographies/memories into one multi-person generation and call it a performance-only change. A shared-scene writer is a separate product mode, not an unnoticed implementation of independent minds.

**Next evidence:** E5/E7, including divergent observers, late listeners, corrections and retained older evidence. **Owners:** DG04/ND20, DG18, CR memory/knowledge, IS05/IS06/IS08 and PF/PS. No additional memory database or social-director authority is selected.

## 7. Experience-derived routines and training only after a concrete gap

**Potential benefit:** a person can reuse a settled chosen method without repeatedly planning it. Existing AE/AG already own learned activity and continued intention; inspect those before inventing an autonomous habit engine.

A future routine extension must distinguish an opportunity to reconsider from permission to execute, explain source provenance and applicability, preserve revocation/expiry/changed circumstances and avoid automatic personality instructions. Journal-derived text is not weight training. A creator-installed “when X, do Y” sentence cannot qualify spontaneous character motivation.

Before selecting fine-tuning or distillation, identify the repeatable failure, baseline prompt/evidence fixes, sufficient representative examples, dataset rights and private-data permissions. Separate training, development and held-out character/situation families. A successful short fine-tune demo does not establish general tools, reasoning or permitted-content fidelity. No private player history is automatically a training corpus.

**Next evidence:** E8 only after a demonstrated current-path limitation, or a specifically justified training study after the model screen. **Owners:** AE/AG/CR and CE, with DG23 where actual outlook change is involved and release/privacy owners for data use. Model training, reinforcement learning and generated executable policies remain exploratory, not initial prerequisites.

## 8. Low-refusal characterization without loss of agency

**Potential benefit:** permitted antagonism, deception, mature non-explicit expression and morally complicated motives survive model alignment and player pressure. The [low-refusal dossier](../../archive/02-research/npc-cognition/09-low-refusal-character-simulation.md) records exact candidates and contrary evidence. The popularity survey alone cannot establish this capability.

**Needs evaluation:** distinguish explicit assistant refusal, quiet sanitization, player-pleasing, true character refusal, engine rejection, provider filtering and appropriate service withholding. Assess dialogue and action selection independently. Contrast warm, principled, fearful and hostile people; do not define success as always attacking, swearing or obeying. Evaluate both allowed and correctly withheld content with the same applicable trusted instructions in every arm.

**Needs criteria before adoption:** which permitted-fiction failures are unacceptable, how subjective sanitization is reviewed, what longitudinal consistency is sufficient and how much latency/cost is acceptable. The owner-approved content boundary is not reopened by those questions. A model's own refusal benchmark cannot supply our release threshold.

**Next evidence:** E2/E3, with HauhauCS, Hermes 4.3 and hosted Cydonia/Venice alongside contemporary stock, popular hosted and creative-model controls as applicable. **Owners:** DG02/CE05, CR12/AG12, R10/R24 and PD07. A new shared prompt or provider is selected only after evidence and its existing qualification; no refusal-triggered automatic fallback or policy stripping follows.

## 9. Exact provider capabilities, deployment and whole cost

**Potential benefit:** model comparisons and later gameplay use the intended weights and features rather than a similarly named endpoint or a silent parameter downgrade. The [architecture/serving synthesis](../../archive/02-research/npc-cognition/10-architecture-composition-and-serving.md#7-serving-choices-and-their-tradeoffs) and low-refusal endpoint findings supply concrete questions.

**Needs verification before a selected comparison:** exact model/provider/version and terms; JSON-mode versus strict-schema versus function-calling support; reasoning/tokenizer/template compatibility; latency under short replies; failure classification; streaming/cancellation; billing/provenance; and configured provider fallbacks. The Cydonia/Venice parameter and tool-choice metadata is not fully consistent, so qualify the real request rather than choose the most convenient field.

**Conditional design:** a useful new local adapter or operator-hosted deployment must reuse existing admission, currentness, spending and privacy owners. Player-local inference is a different trust/deployment question; browser delivery does not select a large GPU requirement. A shared loaded model is not shared private history. Mixture-of-experts active size and quantized download size do not establish complete memory capacity.

**Next evidence:** E1/E2/E3/E7 on identified cold/warm/concurrent workloads, with all preprocessing, reasoning, screening, voice and maintenance costs. **Owners:** AI providers/MW/CR, PF/PS and release/rights owners. No routing defaults, retry behavior, service account or hosting contract changes here.

## 10. A fair longitudinal model screen before a production choice

**Potential benefit:** select models that remain recognizable, grounded and useful beyond one entertaining clip. [RoleBreak, CHARM and anonymous-character research](../../archive/02-research/npc-cognition/11-evaluation-and-research-gates.md#4-recent-papers-that-sharpen-the-evaluation) motivate tests of long interaction, actual knowledge restraint and unfamiliar original people.

**Needs preparation:** freeze the scoped panel, exact configuration manifest, original-character episodes, allowed alternative outcomes, held-out family splits, assessment rubric, human-review process, sample exposure and task budget. Reuse the existing [benchmark fairness contract](../../scripts/decision-benchmark/README.md#grading-and-fairness); old synthetic results are not independent natural-game validation. Keep failed/unattempted coverage, provider refusals, severe incidents and subjective scores visible separately.

**Needs execution later:** voice-free input replay, supported native whole episodes and later recall; then selected audio/resource comparisons. Report time to first meaningful drift with exposure length, useful action and expression latency separately, and every attempt/uncertain charge. Model judges cannot substitute for human assessment of character authenticity or turn model agreement into psychological truth.

**Exit from preparation:** a reviewable, runnable, budgeted experiment through existing tooling and owners, with known capability blockers and unresolved thresholds explicit. That closes preparation only; actual quality/capacity requires execution evidence. **Owners:** CE05/CR12/AG12, R24 and the existing benchmark/verification owners, with PD07/HE/NST for their respective release and media gates.

## Design-session handoff

For any selected question, read its canonical pair/tracker and linked dossier, identify the smallest missing contract, then update the existing pair or create the required focused pair only where there is no owner. Record unresolved product choices before dependent implementation. Carry over actual evidence and failed cases rather than a demo's implied success.

**No runtime acceptance is closed. No model or policy is adopted. No paid comparison was run.** This task completes the research and records the outstanding preparation/testing needs; it leaves full designs and execution to separately selected work.
