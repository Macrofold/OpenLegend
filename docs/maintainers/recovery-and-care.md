# Injuries, recovery and care — DG21 / ND05 delivery

**Status: selected detailed-injury and care design complete, October 25, 2026; runtime implementation and gameplay qualification remain open.** The [feature specification](../projects/recovery-and-care-feature-spec.md) and [technical design](../projects/recovery-and-care-tech-design.md) own the selected behavior and implementation contracts. The [base-world profile](../worlds/base/recovery-and-care.md) owns fictional injuries, treatments and tuning, and the [CARE limit inventory](../limits/recovery-and-care.md) records their scope, reasons and expansion triggers. This is the focused delivery owner for [DG21 / ND05](needs-design.md#dg21--injury-illness-and-useful-care), coordinating ACT04, BW14, actions, time, perception and persistence without replacing them.

Current runtime has health damage/healing, wetness, burning, death and separately treatable death scars. It has no aggregate injury meter, persistent individual injuries or ordinary health-recovery activity. Sleep restores energy and eating restores fullness. This project adds selected injury consequences and care; its design is not evidence that those activities run.

## Selection and delivery applicability

The complete selected journey runs from a real harmful contact through a particular persistent injury, readable functional consequences, a useful response and a worthwhile next activity. Injuries are individual effects, not another needs bar or mandatory body-slot graph. A temporary injury persists until its specified recovery transition; a lasting injury persists until an effective supported treatment changes it. There is no character-level injury-count limit. Ordinary health recovery remains useful separately from curing an injury. Illness and detailed anatomical hit detection retain their own conditional scope.

## CARE01 — Recover after surviving an encounter

- [ ] Use the paired technical contract against the current body, action, time and participation owners. Preserve the selected death/Continue/scar law and the future world-owned-body seam under [EWF03](extensible-world-foundation.md#ewf03--extract-default-body-and-need-policies-through-real-consumers); this consumer does not complete optional-health worlds or a general disease framework.
- [ ] Deliver the selected material-free **Recover at camp** activity for a living, awake human with lost ordinary health. Apply the authored reach, duration, food/energy eligibility and interruption rules in the world profile. A lit flame, bed, shelter, helper, new skill or supply item is not required.
- [ ] At valid completion, restore ordinary health through its existing owner with any applicable injury effect. Do not cure an injury, remove scars, refill food/energy, extinguish burning, cure an illness, revive a dead body, relocate the person or alter someone else's body by implication. Mere progress toward completion grants no healing; actual disqualifying damage at the completion boundary interrupts before healing.
- [ ] Preserve actual bodily losses and prior world consequences when work stops. Actual movement, leaving reach, admitted incompatible physical work, incoming harm, zero food/energy, departure or death cancels recovery; an invalid command does not cancel it. There is no automatic restart or immunity while recovering. Talking and inspection remain available.

**Exit:** someone who survived a real encounter can choose one finite recovery activity and use the restored function afterward. The first result is not gated by CARE03's supplies/helpers or CARE04's illness. Existing scar treatment keeps its separate cost and effect.

## CARE02 — Readable care, life continuity and interruption

- [ ] Expose the actual action and current reason for refusal through ordinary camp/character interaction, keyboard use and the relevant actor choices. Explain the effect and time before starting, show progress and a clear Stop, and distinguish health recovery from injury treatment, scar treatment and energy-restoring Sleep. For ordinary camp health recovery, use current injury-modified duration and expenditure to refuse a predictably exhausted need; later changes still require interruption checks. Injury treatment follows its separately authored prerequisites rather than inheriting that food/energy gate. Routine self-care needs no extra confirmation dialog, invention or model reply.
- [ ] Preserve current observation boundaries: visible health is public in the inspected base world, while food/energy and private thoughts remain owner-scoped. A helper learns only the care facts the patient actually shares or the world permits; an offer, refusal or availability indicator cannot reveal a hidden diagnosis or another person's private meter.
- [ ] Keep exactly one active physical care activity per participant under ordinary action ownership. Choosing care while doing something else must use the existing interruption/replacement behavior. Reading or speaking alone does not cancel it. The current control holder, selected physical life, chosen camp where required, reach and bodily eligibility must still be valid when the effect completes.
- [ ] Reconcile Pause, disconnect, hidden-tab release, death, Continue, creator revival, current-format save/reopen and restored timelines for both injury state and treatment. Pausing preserves uncompleted work; player departure cancels active treatment under the selected departure law. Downtime grants no elapsed healing, and stale work for an old physical life cannot affect its successor. Preserve active work only where current participation and the actual save contract permit it. Validate the current format without legacy readers or conversion paths.
- [ ] Give a specific outcome for injury, hunger, exhaustion, movement, lost access, changed definition or unavailable service. Busy execution does not become fictional refusal, complete the treatment, fabricate a disease or authorize a paid retry. No hidden nearby-danger scan blocks care; physical danger remains capable of interrupting it.

**Exit:** the real person and body receive the correct consequence once, and the player can understand both success and stopping without consulting a separate administration interface.

## CARE03 — A useful field dressing and willing help

**Selected injury-care delivery, dependent on CARE06 and its authored treatment definitions.** A treatment binds an actual supported injury and a defined result. Compare its usefulness with walking back to a camp and with a self-sufficient outing; preserve a credible solo route and independently chosen help.

- [ ] Deliver the selected treatments through one shared exact-injury preparation/execution path, with the world profile's actual method, supplies, duration, eligibility and result. Distinguish immediate relief, stopping an ongoing effect, starting recovery and resolving an injury. A dressing does not clear a scalar or turn arbitrary health loss into a dressable wound. Show the selected injury, supply and patient before starting; a treatment without a useful supported result cannot consume material for fictitious progress.
- [ ] For assistance, obtain both participants' agreement to that patient, exact injury cohort, care activity and offered supply. Require both patient and helper to remain living, awake, present and capable within the authored reach; apply the selected method's actual need/interruption rules. Either can stop. A helper receives only the condition facts the patient explicitly shares for that treatment, never private meter values or a private reason for unavailability. Acceptance grants no inventory access, future touching permission, promise of repayment or authority to choose other work for the patient.
- [ ] Extend existing cooperation terms with patient-initiated care, intentional sharing of the selected condition facts and the exact method/cohort/maximum resource requirements. Helper-first invitations remain ordinary speech until the patient chooses that request. Bind the helper's own exact supplies at explicit treatment start; agreement alone cannot interrupt or reserve the patient. Add one patient participation marker linked to the performer's actual action, with no separate timer; start, stop, withdrawal, life change and restore validate/clean both directions through existing action/cooperation owners.
- [ ] Preserve the exact material owner, selected lot, custody and competing claims through existing inventory/action owners. Commit the bound material and injury transition together at the selected method's effect boundary. Cancellation, new harm, withdrawal, stale completion or simultaneous claims cannot spend twice, cure twice or substitute another injury/supply. A prior handover remains a real handover if subsequent care is declined.
- [ ] Apply treatment-specific interruption rules. Continuing symptoms that a method is explicitly intended to treat cannot accidentally make its own completion impossible; new disqualifying harm still has the authored consequence. Repeated or concurrent treatment cannot restart expiry, stack relief or accelerate recovery beyond the selected rules.
- [ ] Give assistance no automatic speed, healing, relationship or gratitude bonus. The helper contributes an actual item or the effort to apply it, and can independently decline or pursue another purpose. Qualify the interrupted and refused interaction as well as a successful shared recovery.

**Exit:** each selected injury has its promised useful response, and real self-care and willing assistance work through ordinary controls. A medical title or spoken claim grants no cure. Rescue/carrying, forced treatment and long-term patient administration remain separate capabilities.
