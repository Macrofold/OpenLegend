## GEN01 — A worthwhile adult-family episode

- [ ] Deliver a deliberately authored optional scenario with the two named adult relatives, a human acquaintance, consistent starting age/background and actual reachable activity. Preserve existing cast and worlds; do not infer ages from placeholder timestamps or fictionalize unperformed history.
- [ ] Give each relative a concrete independent purpose and permitted knowledge of their shared past. Complete a useful shared or separate activity, real accepted item use/transfer and actual remembered consequence. Cooperation and praise are possible outcomes, never scripted evidence of independent choice.
- [ ] Use ordinary Talk, movement, inventory, crafting/invention and teaching where actually supported. Keep a known solution, solitude and refusal viable. Do not require a roof, family menu, score, formal agreement, new care mechanic or unattended service.

**Exit:** the relationship makes the encounter recognizably different and worth returning to without coaching, mandatory attendance or invented feelings. If it does not, improve character content and agency before adding biology.

## GEN02 — Truthful adult age and selected bodily consequences

- [ ] Preserve deliberately authored starting ages separately in meaning from events actually lived. Advance any enabled age record only with supported elapsed life, with readable calendar-versus-bodily meaning and correct save, pause, absence, age-at-death and physical-life boundaries. Qualify one-world chronology first; later compatible travel must preserve origins and count each actual interval once without age reset or invented held life.
- [ ] Keep this proposal's lack of automatic birthday decline, forced retirement, fertility rules and control succession explicit. If one later adult bodily consequence is selected, first identify a worthwhile activity it changes and specify its actual cause, visibility, progression, interruption and useful response through the existing body/care owners.
- [ ] Resolve any required new chronological/biological clock assignment through PS-D03 before using it. Do not infer mature behavior, learning, food, money or remembered years from a calendar label or a restored timestamp.

**Exit:** age and any deliberately selected consequence have a truthful, comprehensible meaning; no absent human acquires hidden deterioration or a forced replacement character. Adding a displayed age alone does not establish a worthwhile gameplay expansion.

## GEN03 — Complete independence, continuity and cost

- [ ] Qualify distinct biological facts, each person's learned claims, private feelings, ordinary custody, permission, current control and physical life. Include unknown ancestry, mistaken reports, disagreement, refusal, interrupted help, separation, death, Continue and current-format restoration without duplicated events, goods or grants.
- [ ] Use actual food/access and selected ordinary care. A handover is not feeding; kinship supplies no automatic care or privacy exception. Departure cannot transform an absent human into a caregiver, protect detached household goods or extend a previously qualified unattended population.
- [ ] Qualify the full three-person scene with independent resident decisions and ordinary human controls, accessible reading and editable drafts. Record actual script, browser, uncoached-play and provider evidence separately. Pending work/service failure cannot masquerade as a person's refusal or successful care.
- [ ] Account for the full resident workflows, movement, resources, observation, private history, retained dead identities, save/load and operator effort. Bound or qualify growing discovery, pending work and retained collections through their existing owners; a two-NPC example does not bound mature worlds or ancestry traversal. Preserve complete permitted evidence and accepted capacity ambitions.

**Exit:** the chosen attended experience works and its full cost is understood. Continued population, dependent care or long-lived-world support is claimed only for the actually qualified scope.

- [ ] After that answer, complete the affected reproductive/dependent-care product design and world profile, including the selected lifecycle, population admission, real food/care/access, time, independent agency, absence, recovery and whole operating cost. Update this tracker and inventory with only the actual selected staged scope.
