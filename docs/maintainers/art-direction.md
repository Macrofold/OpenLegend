# Art direction and study progress

**Latest preference:** follow the [current visual requirements](../../art-direction/decisions.md#current-visual-requirements--october-31-2026): plain 3D models and textures with believable proportions and no pixel treatment; reject visibly low-poly, blocky, cutesy and big-head/small-body art. On October 31, 2026 Mike replaced the dimensional pixel-art direction; the [art changelog](../../art-direction/art-changelog.md) records why. The running game's default presentation has been plain 3D since [AD01](#ad01--switch-the-runtime-presentation-to-plain-3d); remaining 2D pixel content is [AD05](#ad05--replace-the-remaining-2d-pixel-content-with-plain-3d-art).

**People:** Mike rejected the stock MakeHuman bodies and requires new people to use canonical bodies, adjustable face/hair and removable clothes. [PPL01–PPL03](art/people.md) own the accepted parts/family design and its still-unqualified routine production procedure; [OD3](../projects/art-workflow-feature-spec.md#od3--the-humanoid-body-family-and-rig-for-the-cast) records the selected SAM/MHR pilot foundation and source restrictions. Hidden MPFB wrapping remains unqualified and needs a separate explicit choice. Existing shipped exceptions remain limited to Mike's recorded choices in AF14.

This tracker is the entry point for visual work, individual studies and Mike's art-review decisions. The [creative brief](../../art-direction/final-board/creative-brief.md), [reference boards](../../art-direction/README.md) and [recorded decisions](../../art-direction/decisions.md) remain the aesthetic owners. A working asset or passing renderer check does not establish final style approval.

**Sea of Stars environmental preference:** Mike endorses environmental novelty, varied color and purposeful light within the existing believable-proportion target; its pixel rendering is no longer a target. The [illustrated study](../../art-direction/research/sea-of-stars/README.md) owns screenshot analysis and scene-review criteria; the [game dossier](../../archive/02-research/game-inspiration/dossiers/sea-of-stars.md) owns mechanics, lore, production and reception. [Research completion](../../archive/02-research/game-inspiration/research-progress-sea-of-stars-2026-10-18.md) is tracked separately from the art, motion and performance acceptance work below.

The absolute source links are intentionally specific to Mike's computer and work across Git worktrees. On another machine, establish the local art location and update these links. A Git checkout does not supply these large editable study files. Only deliberately prepared runtime artifacts enter the game asset directories.

Reference repository documentation by repository-relative path, including from the local art workspace READMEs. Active file locations must use the stable art workspace, never a temporary Codex worktree or visualization directory. Old visualization aliases only preserve earlier links; they are not the source locations to record for future work.

## Reusable production methods

For any art request, such as “turn this character sheet into a rigged model” or “use the Higgsfield flow for a UI style study,” follow the [art workflow](../production-workflows/art-flows/README.md): its settings and defaults, the matching [profile](../production-workflows/art-flows/profiles.md), [tools](../production-workflows/art-flows/tools.md) and, for props, characters and creatures, [3D models](../production-workflows/art-flows/3d-models.md). New study records live at `art-direction/studies/<study-id>/README.md`; all image and source payloads remain external in S3, with verified exact references in Git. Existing progress records remain in their current owners. The [UI surface-language brief](../../art-direction/studies/ui-surface-language/brief.md) is prepared; generation and artistic selection have not been performed for that brief.

The [production handbook](../production-workflows/README.md) owns reusable development methods: [objects, bodies and animation](../production-workflows/assets-and-animation.md), [scenes, material recipes, lighting and interfaces](../production-workflows/scenes-and-interfaces.md), and [sound, motion and effects](../production-workflows/audio-and-feedback.md). Use a route for a particular study without treating every optional stage as required. Early visual exploration can discover a stronger idea while a rough playable version tests what the player can actually do.

Keep the brief, chosen references, editable source location, relevant tool/model versions, useful export settings and reviewed in-game result together in the existing study record. Record the visual or player-experience reason for the selection and the remaining repair work. For generated or third-party inputs, retain the source and applicable use terms. Large source assets continue to live in the established art workspace; a new asset database is not required by this guidance.

When a body, material, camera treatment or animation changes, identify the exports and views that need checking again. Develop a useful variation before treating a recipe as a production family. A coherent identity and an expressive action can justify the work even when the route does not reduce production time. [V3D](3d-pixel-art.md) owns technical family qualification and progressive appearances until [AD02](#ad02--re-scope-the-asset-pipeline-and-appearance-families-for-plain-3d) re-scopes them.
