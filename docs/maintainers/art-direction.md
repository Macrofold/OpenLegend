# Art direction and study progress

**Latest preference:** follow the [current visual requirements](../../art-direction/decisions.md#current-visual-requirements--october-31-2026): plain 3D models and textures with believable proportions and no pixel treatment; reject visibly low-poly, blocky, cutesy and big-head/small-body art. On October 31, 2026 Mike replaced the dimensional pixel-art direction; the [art changelog](../../art-direction/art-changelog.md) records why. The running game's default presentation has been plain 3D since [AD01](#ad01--switch-the-runtime-presentation-to-plain-3d); remaining 2D pixel content is [AD05](#ad05--replace-the-remaining-2d-pixel-content-with-plain-3d-art).

This tracker is the entry point for visual work, individual studies and Mike's art-review decisions. The [creative brief](../../art-direction/final-board/creative-brief.md), [reference boards](../../art-direction/README.md) and [recorded decisions](../../art-direction/decisions.md) remain the aesthetic owners. A working asset or passing renderer check does not establish final style approval.

**Sea of Stars environmental preference:** Mike endorses environmental novelty, varied color and purposeful light within the existing believable-proportion target; its pixel rendering is no longer a target. The [illustrated study](../../art-direction/research/sea-of-stars/README.md) owns screenshot analysis and scene-review criteria; the [game dossier](../../archive/02-research/game-inspiration/dossiers/sea-of-stars.md) owns mechanics, lore, production and reception. ## Reusable production methods

For short requests such as “Use the Higgsfield flow for a UI style study,” follow the [shared art procedure](../production-workflows/art-flows/README.md), its chosen tool route and matching deliverable profile. New flow records live at `art-direction/studies/<study-id>/README.md`; all image/source payloads remain external in S3, with verified exact references in Git. Existing progress records remain in their current owners. The [UI surface-language brief](../../art-direction/studies/ui-surface-language/brief.md) is prepared; generation and artistic selection have not been performed by this documentation work.

The [production handbook](../production-workflows/README.md) owns reusable development methods: [objects, bodies and animation](../production-workflows/assets-and-animation.md), [scenes, material recipes, lighting and interfaces](../production-workflows/scenes-and-interfaces.md), and [sound, motion and effects](../production-workflows/audio-and-feedback.md). Use a route for a particular study without treating every optional stage as required. Early visual exploration can discover a stronger idea while a rough playable version tests what the player can actually do.

Keep the brief, chosen references, editable source location, relevant tool/model versions, useful export settings and reviewed in-game result together in the existing study record. Record the visual or player-experience reason for the selection and the remaining repair work. For generated or third-party inputs, retain the source and applicable use terms. Large source assets continue to live in the established art workspace; a new asset database is not required by this guidance.

When a body, material, camera treatment or animation changes, identify the exports and views that need checking again. Develop a useful variation before treating a recipe as a production family. A coherent identity and an expressive action can justify the work even when the route does not reduce production time. [V3D](3d-pixel-art.md) owns technical family qualification and progressive appearances until [AD02](#ad02--re-scope-the-asset-pipeline-and-appearance-families-for-plain-3d) re-scopes them.

### AD07 — Wind for trees, bushes and grass

- [x] Mike asked whether trees and grass can sway slightly and rustle occasionally. [Wind](../world-presentation.md#wind) is implemented: trees, bushes, grass and plant props sway with travelling gusts and a slight leaf rustle, shadows included, with light / breezy / gusty / still strengths in the art comparisons panel and the scene study's Wind control. **Mike chose light as the default on November 1, 2026**; it already was. GPU cost, measured the same day in the scene study's camp view (1147×885 canvas, economy shadows, 10-second runs on this Mac): GPU pass median 13.69 and 13.71 ms with wind still, 13.74 and 13.68 ms light and 13.66 ms gusty, each at a 16.7 ms displayed-frame median. The first run after a page load read lower in both sessions (still: 9.38 and 10.44 ms) than every later run, with or without wind, likely because the GPU lowers its clock once the load settles; those runs are excluded. A repeat after Mike noticed slight lag with gusty wind found no frame drops at the close view (slowest frame 17.7 ms, still and gusty alike) and about 0.1 ms of wind cost at the wider overview framing (22.3 against 22.4 ms), which runs at 30 fps either way ([SW18.18](spatial-world.md#sw18--world-presentation-delivery)). In the scene study any wind other than still also starts the world clock, adding about 1 ms of CPU animation work per frame. Wind's own cost is within run-to-run noise; these runs on one Apple M4 machine are not a device qualification.
