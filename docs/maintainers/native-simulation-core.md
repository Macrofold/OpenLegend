## Current scope

**Design complete; implementation not started.** Rust is the selected proof candidate. Full supported-game adoption depends on a complete pipeline comparison; distributed and adaptive execution have later independent gates. No runtime, dependency, benchmark or resource change accompanies the design. Existing BK/PF/RU/SR implementation and evidence remain intact.

This tracker owns native-core conversion and its experiments. [PF10/PF11](performance.md#pf10--workers-and-movement-prediction) retains worker/operating and population qualification; [D6/PD12](production-deployment.md#conditional-launch-and-expansion) retains production distributed delivery. Do not duplicate their task bodies or claim their completion from a proof. [BK29](production-data.md#bk29--pressure-and-preparation-work-removal-proposed) remains the owner of current-runtime semantic/retention improvements; take its accepted source as common behavior when comparing.

The [MMORPG foundations research](../../research/mmorpg-foundations/README.md) and [focused needs-design register](mmorpg-scalability-needs-design.md) identify additional preparation for large coordinates, sparse physical residency, independent seed-world lifecycles and cross-owner capacity contracts. Reconcile these against the actual implementation branch before proposing new work. They do not replace NSC's design, duplicate its implementation queue, change its stage order or establish capacity.

## Delivery stages

- [x] **NSC00 — Architecture and staged decision design.** Specify compact resident authority, full smaller pipeline, host/native boundaries, exact personal evidence and restore, work pooling, cells, machine handoff, adaptive placement, isolation experiments and removal targets. Source inspection, primary research and affected documentation review establish design completion only. The [design pair](../projects/native-simulation-core-feature-spec.md) and E1–E7 are the deliverables.

- [ ] **NSC01 — Comparison contract and minimal native host.** After implementation authorization, pin the latest integrated source and selected family/input inventory; reconcile BK29 and all relevant existing acceptance. Establish the [caller/field coverage manifest](../projects/native-simulation-core-tech-design.md#94-full-adoption-inventory-and-removal-targets) before converting each owner, including indirect readers and finalization duties. Build the two-crate workspace, exact toolchain/lockfile, generated boundary types, bounded native lifecycle and current-format installation. Prepare a narrow equivalent compact-state TS control for the replaced hot path, not a second full engine. **Exit:** explicit supported/refused installation, one authority per proof world, ready/accept/reject lifecycle with host-current input and capacity fences, bounded cancellation/rollback and shared operation checkpoints, generated NAPI declarations with real runtime conversion, no raw world crossing, per-field FFI or hidden live TS fallback. Pin comparison criteria before runs. Correctness/setup only does not establish performance.

- [ ] **NSC02 — Complete one-worker gameplay proof.** Depends on NSC01. Port the entire declared movement/action/rate → sensing/local identity → attention/history → player/NPC capture → actual PostgreSQL save/cold-restore pipeline. Include real native animal reaction/continuation, speech, independent stale detail, unknown identity, correction/forgetting and pending-result fences. Preserve current numerical policies and the same semantic work in both arms. **Exit:** all declared [proof journeys](../projects/native-simulation-core-tech-design.md#91-freeze-the-comparison-contract) and applicable conversion cases pass through real consumers, including complete context/reference/trigger equivalence; no empty save/recording stubs. Rejection restores state/RNG/history without debt subtraction; partial refusal and previews preserve their established outcomes. Exercise private-candidate read isolation, capacity refusal before acceptance, partial/cold history and unsaved query overlays, semantic rather than global invalidation, protected pre-effect facts, failed saves and current privacy/identity fences on restore. Run E1/E3 only after the implemented stage is complete.

- [ ] **NSC03 — Bounded parallel proof and adoption decision.** Depends on complete NSC02. Add one shared native compute pool, existing phase barriers, typed disjoint ranges and deterministic effects/RNG; no per-interval generic dependency graph or repeated shared input copies. Compare identical one/multiple-worker execution with bridge, Node consumers, database, allocation and waiting included. Exercise tiny, crowded and independent workloads. **Exit:** E1–E3 evidence establishes complete-path benefit and isolated variant results under preselected existing criteria; a narrow TS control does not isolate the whole pipeline's language benefit. Record **adopt**, **reject** or **inconclusive**. Reject/inconclusive stops broad migration; remove unsuccessful experiments and report the remaining bottleneck. A fast isolated kernel cannot pass this gate.

- [ ] **NSC04 — Complete supported-game conversion.** Conditional on NSC03 adoption. Complete and reconcile NSC01's caller/field/command coverage manifest against the latest integrated source. Port the installed world-family closure and convert action/menu/inspection, body/status/inventory/assembly, authoring, grant, native response, cognition, memory and current-format storage callers together. Retire replaced Immer/draft/certification/diff and dual-authority paths after assigning every meaningful finalization duty. **Exit:** real standard opening and ordinary browser/NPC/save/restore journeys pass; no supported field, operation or indirect reader is unmapped, silently defaulted or served by a hidden TS world. Future unimplemented feature families retain their own trackers. Preserve useful starting content; no legacy reader or permanent backend switch.

- [ ] **NSC05 — Same-machine cells and interaction ownership.** Depends on NSC04 for full-game adoption; a scoped E4 can run earlier on the proof without claiming full delivery. Separate geometry membership from cell ownership and core scheduling. Implement conservative read overlap, exact conflict ownership and border-stable routing; no network stack is required. **Exit:** equal interior/edge/corner outcomes, large bodies/attachments, converging/dispersing groups, real heard/received history and scoped privacy; no duplicated simulation per player, full-cell copy per tick or unconditional global scan. Include E4's added metadata/copy costs.

- [ ] **NSC06 — Controlled distributed execution and custody.** Conditional on measured single-host capacity needs and NSC05. Coordinate with D6/PD12; retain one existing writer, same-operator/same-build world and exact affected-scope timing. Implement declared influence scopes, bounded prepared exchanges and prepare/quiesce/stage/fence/activate handoff. Preserve current provider jobs across routing changes. Use coherent world cuts and whole-world failure recovery initially; independently advancing scopes and live precopy are separately measured E5 variants. Keep unsupported/global scopes colocated. **Exit:** E5 establishes useful capacity after network/synchronization costs; commands, prepared responses and save batches occur once through failure before/after fencing, restart, stale replies and cross-boundary action/receipt/save paths; transfer pauses and stalled global cuts are included. No global per-action RPC barrier, speculative rollback, full past-memory transfer or independently sharded storage. No production approval follows from a two-machine experiment.

- [ ] **NSC07 — Adaptive grouping and hysteresis.** Depends on NSC06; prototype placement decisions against captured counters without executing migration first. Add bounded actual-work/communication counters, neighboring-cell candidates, destination capacity checks and amortized transfer decisions. Reuse existing boundary data and immutable resources; retain no full former-owner replica. **Exit:** isolated E6 beats fixed assignments on complete stationary/moving/cycling/dispersal workloads without churn, enlarged overlap debt, gameplay barriers or starvation. If adaptive work costs more, remove it and retain static assignments. Exact operating values remain measured tuning, not world rules.

- [ ] **NSC08 — Adoption cleanup and existing qualification integration.** At each adopted boundary, update current contracts, manifests, limits, removal evidence and the scope of completed tasks. Remove rejected experiments and replace unsupported performance claims with source/workload-specific results. Coordinate dispersed-player, crowded-scene, boundary, mature-memory and failure acceptance through PF/PS/PD rather than a new suite. **Exit:** applicable stage obligations pass or are explicitly failed; no unfinished agreed conversion is relabeled a future improvement. Final production capacity and enjoyment remain their actual evidence owners.
