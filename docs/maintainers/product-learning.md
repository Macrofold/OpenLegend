# Product learning — demonstration and audience experiment

**Status: DG03 product preparation completed October 4, 2026; execution remains open.** The [feature specification](../projects/demo-and-audience-learning-feature-spec.md) owns the audience, demo, participant behavior, measures, critique and research. [ND26](needs-design.md#nd26--a-bounded-commercial-test-and-repeatable-product-demonstration) retains overall coverage; later paid-offer/renewal questions belong to DG27. These records do not authorize outreach, publication, paid execution or a commercial offer.

## DL01 — Exact supported scope and execution brief

- [ ] Select the real build, local/hosted/recorded mode, supported invention family, initial world, permissions and enabled services. Establish absence of the exact completed live-invention composition, not merely lack of character knowledge.
- [ ] Name facilitator/operator and record actual cash/time ceilings, rehearsal budget, access dates, review date, participant data/deletion dates and explicitly authorized contact. Preserve current development-save and absence policies.
- [ ] Link applicable INV/PW/CE and PD/MP readiness; do not duplicate their work or infer a public-service pass.

**Exit:** one concrete bounded execution brief supports the exact proposed claim. The product design is ready; these real-world inputs and later authorization remain outstanding.

## DL02 — Repeatable honest demonstration

- [ ] Qualify live creation, real crafting/use, ordinary Modify existing, checked derived result and reuse through the selected browser route. Preserve native costs, original recipe/items and scoped learning.
- [ ] Retain a real complete run, actual latency/cost and any interventions. Produce a clearly labeled short edit and recorded fallback only from available evidence.
- [ ] Keep any owner-workshop or resident segment scoped to its separately qualified behavior. A narrower clip does not close first-playable acceptance.

**Exit:** another authorized operator can present the same kind of actual activity without hidden world repair, prewritten invention or false speed/capability claims.

## DL03 — First formative round

- [ ] Under the authorized brief, observe up to three fresh participants from the selected audience with consistent demo exposure and personally chosen supported variations.
- [ ] Record comprehension, real outcomes, assistance, waits, failures, self-chosen continuation and full cost/time. Preserve declined and incomplete observations.
- [ ] Identify a concrete obstacle or contrary evidence; do not force every person through the whole storyboard.

**Exit:** a small inspectable set of individual observations supports a specific next change or a decision not to change. It establishes no representative market estimate.

## DL04 — Revision, fresh comparison and optional return

- [ ] Make the chosen product/message revision through its existing owner, if warranted. Identify what changed.
- [ ] Observe up to three fresh participants in the second round; retain version/exposure differences instead of pooling incompatible conditions.
- [ ] Offer an optional return only when the same world/build can remain available through the disclosed window. Separate scheduled, invited and voluntary visits; no automatic reminders or fabricated absent life.

**Exit:** evidence addresses the chosen first-use improvement and any actually observed continued value, with missing/declined returns visible.

## DL05 — Complete economics and support

- [ ] Reconcile all paid paths, preparation, failed/uncertain attempts, retained-service costs and operator time against the selected ceilings.
- [ ] Distinguish pleasant creative time from queueing, troubleshooting and moderator rescue.
- [ ] Stop or narrow scope when the offered experience exceeds its budget. Do not finance a weak loop with hidden subsidies or infer margins from free participation.

**Exit:** the team can explain the complete observed cost and support burden without claiming steady-state economics from a tiny sample.

## DL06 — Decision, evidence retention and next scope

- [ ] Review on the selected date with contrary evidence, individual outcomes, costs and uncertainty.
- [ ] Choose stop, revise, a different defined audience, or one bounded expansion. Paid willingness/renewal testing requires its own DG27 offer and gates.
- [ ] Honor participant access/contact and study-material deletion dates; retain only the agreed concise de-identified decision report.

**Exit:** an explicit product decision closes the cycle rather than an indefinite demand for more data or an automatic marketing campaign.
