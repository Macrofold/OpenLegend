## Runtime boundaries and dependencies

- IS01/IS02/IS04/IS05 first use current attended play, exact speech and native actions. A useful small personal episode need not wait for unattended service, new clocks, crowds or lower-detail work.
- IS06 profiling can run without changing character decisions; any optimization enters its existing owner and is evaluated through IS09. IS03 training/cache/calibration remains conditional on representative permitted traces and a concrete failure/cost question.
- IS07 consumes IS01's admitted work and existing time/geometry/observer boundaries, plus the selected fidelity decision; it does not grant planning, knowledge or NPC consent. IS08 preserves current fairness and host/account ceilings before any wider service.
- Broader approximation, gist, unattended funding, protection changes, separate calendars and cross-world effects may not borrow approval from a passed smaller case. Their actual decision, budget and recovery owners remain required.
