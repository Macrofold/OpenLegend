## Runtime boundaries and dependencies

- IS01/IS02/IS04 use current native work and personal evidence plus the selected numerical-reaction extension. IS05 has independently complete exact attended and compact background contributions. No slice enables an unattended hosting offer, new clock or crowd requirement.
- IS06 profiling can run without changing character decisions; any optimization enters its existing owner and is evaluated through IS09. IS03 training/cache/calibration remains conditional on representative permitted traces and a concrete failure/cost question.
- IS07 consumes IS01's admitted work and existing time/geometry/observer boundaries, under the selected exact fidelity contract; it does not grant planning, knowledge or NPC consent. IS08 preserves current fairness and host/account ceilings before any wider service.
- Broader approximation, aggregate crowd sound, unattended funding, protection changes, separate calendars and cross-world effects may not borrow approval from a passed smaller case. Their actual decision, budget and recovery owners remain required.
