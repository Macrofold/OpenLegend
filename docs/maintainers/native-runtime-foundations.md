## Ordering and parallel work

NRF01 selects rather than blindly queues all four candidates. J and S are independent and measured separately. P must not race another rewrite of player publication. R may be designed while the one-worker proof continues, but its implementation/measurement follows NSC03's prerequisite. Assign one writer per shared owner; candidate work may proceed separately only when files and evidence controls genuinely do not overlap. Do not add a new development branch or change the active proof's files as part of this design-only record.

No product question blocks these four recipes. Benefit and eligibility remain empirical. General physical-region unloading, new pushing/crowd rules, independently advancing clocks and fleet/storage distribution remain outside this tracker, with residual design in the [foundation handoff](decisions/mmorpg-engine-foundations.md).
