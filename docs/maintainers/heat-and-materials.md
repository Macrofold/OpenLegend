## HM02 — One material, source and elapsed-time history

- [ ] Introduce only the selected heating, ignition, burning, cooling and fuel behavior through the existing material/state owners. Distinguish a source's actual strength and duration from a target's material, section and current condition.
- [ ] Carry actual moisture, thermal condition, consumed fuel and damage through every supported transfer, split, conversion, placement, reclaim, save and restoration. A name, new container, repeated attempt or new object identity cannot replenish fuel or erase a consequence.
- [ ] Combine actual admitted local contributions without multiplying one source's output by the number of targets or counting the same remaining material as both an item and banked fuel. Unknown material behavior is distinct from noncombustibility or immunity.

**Exit:** the same reviewed circumstances lead to the same supported outcome regardless of observation, frame subdivision or retry. An anticipated influence has no effect before its own admission.

## HM03 — Deliberate work, interruption and extinguishing
