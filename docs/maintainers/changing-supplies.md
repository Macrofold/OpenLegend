# Changing supplies — BW25 / ND33 and selected ND06 delivery

**Status: product design in progress; no technical design or runtime change authorized by this assignment.** [DG19](needs-design.md#dg19--supplies-that-change-over-time) develops one useful food-preservation and resource-renewal loop beneath [BW25](base-world.md#bw25--supplies-that-change-over-time) and INV-6. The [feature specification](../projects/changing-supplies-feature-spec.md) owns product behavior; the [authored wilderness profile](../worlds/base/changing-supplies.md) owns materials, rates, work and the selected workload. [CS limits](../limits/changing-supplies.md) inventory the proposed restrictions and their rationale.

This tracker does not replace PO's single object/lot owner, AC/native work, ST contributions, SL persistence, installed simulation time or independent character agency. Current food does not spoil, resource patches do not regrow, and woven containers provide organization rather than preservation. Corpse rot and safe physical cleanup are already delivered under BW23. Existing completed tasks and their narrower evidence remain intact.

## CS01 — A worthwhile preparation and return loop

- [ ] Complete the researched product proposal, concrete authored profile, player/resident journeys and game-first critique. Distinguish current mechanics from the selected extension and record every discretionary restriction in CS limits.
- [ ] Before runtime implementation, prepare the scoped technical design through existing owners, including the actual lot-state, work, renewal, disclosure and lifecycle changes. The product-only assignment does not supply or approve that design.
- [ ] Qualify a full ordinary journey from acquired food through deliberate preservation, packing, an independently worthwhile outing, return and useful consumption. Compare against the simpler current food loop; reject a feature whose main result is compulsory inspection and repeated chores.

**Exit:** preservation offers an understandable benefit in actual play without a required new technology tree, a food-management quota or free manufactured supplies. A short outing remains possible without selecting preservation.

## CS02 — Food condition follows the real material

- [ ] Introduce the selected condition and aging behavior through the shared material/lot owner. Preserve authoritative elapsed time, source age, quantity, identity, custody, lineage and the installed definition meaning.
- [ ] Extend the current stateless-only split/merge/consumption boundary for this particular supported condition. A transfer, split, merge, rename, nested move or repeated transformation cannot rejuvenate food, erase worse condition or spend a referenced ingredient twice.
- [ ] Preserve active work, pending offers and claims under actual dependency rules. Revalidate changed food and access before a consequence; stale controls do not consume a replacement lot.
- [ ] Establish one coherent current-format save/restart/restore result across threshold crossings and interrupted work. Reads, cold materialization and retries cannot age twice, reset a timer or reroll a previous result.

**Dependencies:** PO03/PO06/PO08/PO10, existing state/resource accounting, current time and SL. **Exit:** the stateful family is useful through actual ordinary inventory actions; it is not a per-unit object explosion or a UI-only freshness label. Wider arbitrary stateful stacking remains separate.

## CS03 — Finite preservation work and honest consumption

- [ ] Admit the actual selected ingredients, equipment, work, heat or other supported consumer and output yield. Keep the new process's consumption boundary explicit and preserve current cooking/fire behavior outside its selected changes.
- [ ] Deliver review, Start, progress, Stop, access loss, resource loss, interrupted heat, completion, collection and stale-result behavior. No food or fuel is credited twice; an unfinished process cannot silently become a completed ration.
- [ ] Expose supported edibility, condition, uncertainty and remaining-use implications through ordinary inventory/inspection. Hidden poisoning, illness, detailed nutrition and unsupported preservation claims are not inferred from a food's name.
- [ ] Keep a known ordinary method available in the selected profile. Invention may vary only actually supported materials and consequences; a generated description cannot supply preservation, immunity or free yield.

**Exit:** a player and a resident can each choose and complete the same native preparation, or choose a different activity. A model/provider failure does not become a fictional cooking mistake, refusal or poisoned meal.

## CS04 — Depletion and renewal with a finite yield

- [ ] Install only the selected patch family, initial quantities, capacity, growth eligibility and time rules. Existing finite resources and wildlife remain finite unless their own family is deliberately extended.
- [ ] Reconcile harvesting with actual stock, maturation, saturation, competing actors, work completion and changes to the source. A full patch does not bank an unlimited invisible harvest, and inspection does not trigger growth.
- [ ] Make depleted, renewing, ripe and unavailable conditions understandable from permitted evidence. Remembered quantities remain remembered; a character must observe or receive a real report to learn a remote change.
- [ ] Qualify chronological advancement and pause/return without duplicating growth, hiding elapsed debt or replaying every visual frame. Quantify sustainable food flow against actual consumers, loss and work; finite initial stock is distinct from ongoing production.

**Exit:** waiting for or revisiting a known place is one choice among useful activities, with credible scarcity and replenishment. Neither a regenerating label nor a harvest animation establishes an ecosystem, agriculture or stable population.

## CS05 — People and the supply interface

- [ ] Extend current exact inventory quantity drafts, permitted contents, food actions, source inspection and process progress. Preserve keyboard/narrow-layout use, explicit partial results and useful reasons for unavailable choices.
- [ ] Keep inaccessible containers, another person's possessions and unobserved patch changes private. A shared supply total must disclose its actual known scope rather than claiming to inventory the entire camp.
- [ ] Let residents judge current permitted food, their own needs, plans and preferences through existing attention/agency. No compulsory stocking goal, automatic spoiled-food avoidance persona or extra model call for every aging decrement.
- [ ] Distinguish held-world time, individual absence, real-time authorization and supply viability. The selected personal profile does not silently extend DG17's unattended offer or invent shared-world property protection.

**Exit:** a human can understand what they have, what needs attention, why preservation helps and what they can do next without maintaining a spreadsheet or inspecting every identical unit.
