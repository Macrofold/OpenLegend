**Status: product proposal prepared, 5 October 2026.** [DG11/ND29](needs-design.md) has a detailed [optional connection-study specification](../projects/wellbeing-evidence-feature-spec.md). No study operation, human-outcome collection, technical design, recruitment or benefit evidence is delivered. The [well-being archive](../../archive/08-wellbeing-vision/README.md) remains open ideation.

This tracker owns only the proposed human-outcome study. Existing performance profiling, ordinary game playtesting and R24/CE05 fictional-character evaluation retain their owners. The proposal can be postponed without blocking a playable game.

## Work and acceptance

- [x] **WBE01 — Product question, participant behavior and evidence interpretation.** Specify a finite invited-adult feasibility comparison between an offered shared outing with a known companion and separate worthwhile solo outings. Cover independent consent, optional response, early ending, pair-level assignment, missingness, corrections, retention and withdrawal. Explain custom measurement and restricted inference; cite primary research. This checkbox records the product document, not adoption of a health objective or study execution.

- [ ] **WBE02 — Worthwhile activity in both formats.** Demonstrate the selected route through current ordinary controls, per-person resource opportunity, a real decision, useful outcome and comfortable ending. Existing gameplay owners deliver any missing activity behavior. **Exit:** a solo person and a known pair can enjoy the activity without research, scripted conversation or an exclusive study reward. Record world and activity revision, preparation and operator cost. Qualify the [selected outing clock, disclosed body/meals and ordinary rest/recovery](../worlds/base/connection-study-outing.md), actual per-person gathering opportunity and readable form boundaries outside advancing participant play; report coarse game time and the four bounded interruption flags. This is independent of a survey implementation.

- [ ] **WBE03 — Select the actual study and responsible operator.** Before recruiting or collecting responses, choose the audience, territory, operator, dates, operating budget, exact purpose and applicable review; confirm the proposed private-data and deletion commitments. **Owners:** product/research with PD01/PD08/PD10. **Exit:** one concrete, reviewable study profile and notice. No inferred company health mission, minors, public recruitment, payment, academic partnership, public dataset or marketing claim. If requested scope requires an unanswered developer choice, follow the root question rule before dependent work.

- [ ] **WBE04 — Private participant journey and technical design.** Under separate authorization, design and implement enrollment, assignment, private questions, truthful save state, early ending, correction, withdrawal and finite export. Reuse current account/access and data owners; research answers never enter speech, memories or AI processing. **Dependencies:** selected behavior and the applicable WBE03 decisions. **Exit:** complete journey, clear error/recovery, scoped roles, finite work and retained bytes, and no automatic prompt or enrollment renewal. The current product-only assignment intentionally leaves this open.

- [ ] **WBE05 — Precollection qualification.** Use nonparticipant sample records to demonstrate independent consent, all skips, incomplete pairs, late/corrected answers, false-success prevention, prohibited access, interrupted deletion and old-backup restore with current withdrawal restrictions. Review accessibility and report disclosure risk. **Owners:** WBE04 with D2/MH/PD06/PD10 as consumed. **Exit:** the actual system can honor the notice, including active deletion and backup expiry, before any real answer is accepted. No existing memory-forgetting command substitutes for this.

- [ ] **WBE06 — Finite pilot and honest decision report.** Only after explicit operation authorization, run the chosen cohort/window with the fixed protocol; report assignment flow, delivery, stops, missingness, burden, costs, negative experiences and limited exploratory responses. **Dependencies:** WBE02–WBE05. **Exit:** a feasible next decision or a justified stop, all removal/retention duties fulfilled, and no unsupported benefit or no-effect claim. A favorable mean does not automatically authorize expansion.
