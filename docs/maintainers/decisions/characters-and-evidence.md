## SA-D01 — Psychological fear for controlled characters

**Open; scope clarified October 24, 2026.** The [stealth feature specification](../../projects/stealth-awareness-feature-spec.md#player-fear-decision), [shared technical contract](../../projects/stealth-awareness-tech-design.md#emotions-and-reconsideration) and [SA tracker](../stealth-awareness.md#open-product-decision-and-bounded-blockers) retain one bounded product choice: how a directly controlled fictional character acquires longer-term psychological fear, whether retained interpreted appraisals remain mechanically applicable during direct control, and the corresponding disclosure, revocation and control-transfer rules. Native bodily startle is already a selected design requirement and needs no AI. The player policy is not selected merely because a shared effect can influence accuracy/evasion. Disabling new acquisition alone does not prevent previously acquired appraisals from affecting a newly controlled body. This decision record owns selection of the policy. The [emotion feature](../../projects/emotional-physiology-feature-spec.md#directly-controlled-characters-sa-d01) details its player-facing consequences and [EP](../emotional-physiology.md#controlled-character-dependency) tracks the bounded implementation gate.

| Alternative                                 | Consequence and tradeoff                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A. Native body only during direct control   | Disable autonomous interpreted-emotion creation/review and mask appraised-emotion mechanics during control. Retained appraisals preserve identity, sources and native lifetime; transfer feedback explains masking/unmasking. This preserves subjective roleplay but supplies no persistent interpreted player fear. |
| B. Deterministic authored interpretation    | Explicit native rules use permitted character evidence to create/reassess selected families through the appraisal owner. Predictable, inspectable consequences require declared causal rules and retained-record applicability; interpretation is less expressive than autonomous cognition.                         |
| C. Explicitly opted-in model interpretation | The active controller grants bounded interpretation for named families. Consent, revocation, private context, cost, uncertainty and post-revocation projection must be selected; permission does not transfer automatically between human controllers.                                                               |

**Recommendation, not adopted:** start with A. If persistent interpreted fear is selected for ordinary player mechanics, prefer B. C is a separate opt-in alternative, never a fallback. Selecting fear does not authorize joy, grief, anger or broader needs by implication.

The decision must settle producer and human-authoring authority, selected families, mechanical application, disclosure, revocation and transfer together. Until selection, keep the unresolved retained-appraisal/direct-control composition disabled before profile/feature admission. This is not transient required-work preparation and must not become an indefinite native-combat hold; independent complete native-body paths remain available without silently treating retained appraisals as absent. No choice grants forced movement/speech/goals, running/fleeing slowdown, mind reading or an inference about the human's psychology. Control changes cannot renew lifetimes or review allowances; missing policy adopts no option.

**Bounded dependency:** only controlled-character interpretation/application and its acceptance are blocked. Native bodily effects, NPC review, native animals, mechanical queries, time/save and other specialist contracts proceed independently. The [technical contract](../../projects/emotional-physiology-tech-design.md#ep-c10--capabilities-current-context-and-permitted-presentation) and [SA tracker](../stealth-awareness.md#open-product-decision-and-bounded-blockers) retain that boundary.
