## SA-D01 — Psychological fear for controlled characters

**B selected by Mike, October 25, 2026 — authored rules.** Events can affect the fictional character's lasting psychology while a human controls them. Mike selected **fear, anger, joy and grief** for the initial authored profile. Explicit installed world rules use that character's admitted evidence to create or reassess source-linked emotions through the existing appraisal owner. This is an accepted design target; runtime delivery and qualification remain [EP06–EP09](../emotional-physiology.md). The [emotion feature](../../projects/emotional-physiology-feature-spec.md#directly-controlled-characters-sa-d01) owns the complete player policy; [base-world rules](../../worlds/base/psychological-reactions.md#directly-controlled-characters) own selected families and event mappings. Engine code must not hard-code the bundled world's psychology.

Authored emotions have their installed consequences, including fear's accuracy/evasion effects. Compatible retained NPC appraisals remain applicable during direct control at their naturally progressed values; taking control neither masks them nor reinterprets their cause. Identity, exact definition pins, provenance, lifetimes and finite review allowances survive transfer and restore. Pending operations with stale control authority refuse publication. Autonomous model creation/review stops during direct control; native event rules take over without replaying old events or granting a new review allowance. Returning to autonomy restores only currently authorized NPC interpretation, with existing record progress intact.

The controller receives understandable own-character emotion/consequence feedback and permitted causal explanation. Other people receive only admitted outward cues; another controller's private thoughts or ungranted records are not disclosed. Fictional emotion never asserts the human user's actual feelings or chooses their movement, speech, goals, attack or consent. Running/fleeing speed is unchanged. Human narration is not an authoritative appraisal writer; privileged authoring retains its existing grants.

These are ordinary world rules, with no per-player model opt-in or revocation workflow. Ending control fences that controller's pending operations and access; it does not revoke the character's emotions or erase effects. Rule changes retain existing live-definition/pin integrity, rather than retroactively relabelling retained records. An absent rule produces no new emotion and never falls back to AI, a guessed label or hidden whole-world knowledge.

**Alternatives considered:** A, bodily effects alone with masked appraisals during direct control, was the initial recommendation and is not selected. C, explicitly opted-in model interpretation, is not selected. B trades autonomous interpretive expressiveness for predictable, inspectable event consequences without paid player interpretation. Native bodily reactions, NPC interpretation, native animals and the other sensing/combat contracts retain their separate owners.
