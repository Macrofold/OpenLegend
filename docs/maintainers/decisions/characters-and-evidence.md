## Knowledge storage scale

Aggregate actor storage allowance and admission for very many subject pads remain a scale question. Per-document limits do not establish aggregate capacity. Reuse measured storage admission; do not silently evict knowledge or restore an arbitrary ten-document cap. [Knowledge](../../knowledge.md) owns current semantics.

## D66 — Re-authoring committed speech

Before administrative re-authoring, choose attributed correction, dependent-evidence invalidation or explicit whole-timeline god authority; define handling of perceived fragments and identities. Never reconstruct unavailable historical acoustics from current positions or add old-event migration. Ordinary corrections and accepted append-only open speech do not select administrative rewriting. [Hearing](../../hearing-and-speech.md#editing-committed-speech), HE06 and NST retain their separate authority.
