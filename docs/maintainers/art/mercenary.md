## Direction and feedback retained

The [creative brief](../../../art-direction/final-board/creative-brief.md) remains the overall target. For the earlier study, Mike rejected a painterly model merely displayed at low resolution, rigid cape movement, a visible front-cloth gap and distracting shimmer during rotation. The study explores purposeful pixel detail and material colors, readable adult proportions, fitted cloth and dimensional lighting. These requests are recorded in its design/refinement files; they do not establish that every visual issue is resolved.

The isolated viewer's target is about 192 vertical pixels with integer display scaling. The earlier game pilot samples the model into a fixed 128×192 color/depth image while the world renders at full screen resolution. Those are historical implementation choices in their respective contexts, not an approved universal resolution for all artwork.

On October 14, Mike reported that the game mercenary looked too small, gained apparent detail when zoomed in, lost its eyes and face, and showed moving pixels or underlying body parts during rotation. He requested a new design whose colored blocks belong permanently to the body and clothing, preserving the old model. That feedback authorizes this iteration; it is not final aesthetic acceptance. [Current rendering behavior](../../world-presentation.md#animated-character-pixels) and the [iteration plan](../../projects/completed/mercenary-voxel-iteration.md) own the resulting contract and delivery scope.

## Rejected first block figure and new sculpted iteration

Mike explicitly rejected MER08's figure as ugly and asked for art closer to the original 3D model. Its technical checks stand, but its box-like anatomy, squared face, rigid horizontal cape throw and oversized details are not accepted art direction. MER09 delivered source files, game integration and its own scoped source/browser qualification, but Mike subsequently rejected its face and Lego-like appearance and preferred the original model. During authoring, the evaluated original body, outfit and cape guide the finer cell sculpture; deliberately painted skin, hair/beard, facial features, mail, leather/wraps and cape regions replace the texture colors. The clothed body volume is retained; the cape is fitted outward around the bounded walking poses instead of removing body cells that happen to be covered while standing. There is no separate bare-body layer under the outfit, and the cape can naturally cover mail. Garment thickness and clearance are authored into the geometry. [The replacement’s own evidence](../../verification/mercenary-default.md#sculpted-source-guided-iteration--october-14-2026) records the inspected front/profile/back/face and normal/close game views, including both walking extremes without observed seam openings or body protrusions. Fixed mesh/color data survived all six camera cases, and scoped lighting, selection/reveal/fade, resize, fallback/late-load and replacement/teardown checks passed. These bounded checks do not establish arbitrary-pose fit, creator style acceptance or crowd performance. The [completed revision plan](../../projects/completed/mercenary-voxel-iteration.md#revised-art-direction-after-explicit-rejection) owns this revision.

## Original-model refinement

Mike prefers the original model from before the block experiments, requests cleanup and wants the mercenary to read as a tall person in the world. **MER10** supersedes MER09 as the active game candidate while preserving all source assets and prior evidence. The [refinement plan](../../projects/completed/mercenary-original-refinement.md) owns the agreed scope.

The original textured face, outfit, Idle/Walk clips and separate moving cape return. Runtime texture reduction/recoloring and the extra 128×192 character image are removed from the default, so the game draws the authored surfaces directly. Imported emission is disabled to let world lighting illuminate the outfit. A fixed 1.6 display scale gives a 2.96 m presentation height; this compensates for upright geometry beside camera-facing people and does not enlarge the native gameplay body. Camera zoom/orbit never change that scale. Cape lengths, tethers, clearance, ground allowance and gravity follow the enlargement; chest contact remains in source coordinates through the animated bone transforms. Original dark lining and embroidered outer cloth share the same simulated panels.

A normal pitched-camera opaque-silhouette comparison reads about 12% taller than a nearby person; lower pitch enlarges the apparent difference. Actual front/profile/back and walking views preserve the face, shoulder coverage and lining; pause/visibility/load/replacement checks are recorded separately from final creator art approval. Source/model bytes remain unchanged. The historical sampled rendering and both block trials stay preserved; MER10 does not claim a new hand-painted pixel asset, literal cube anatomy, arbitrary-pose cloth, interchangeable equipment or production rights. Actual game evidence is recorded under [MER10 verification](../../verification/mercenary-default.md#original-model-refinement--october-14-2026).

## Integration and next iteration

The earlier [preparation script](../../../scripts/prepare-mercenary.py) takes the external study directory as its argument and creates the original trusted bundled GLB plus cloth data. Its [runtime provenance](../../../apps/client/src/characters/mercenary-assets/provenance.json) records those source/output digests. The source study and prepared game output are different artifacts; editing Blender or a texture does not automatically update the game.

MER08’s earlier authoring script constructs colored blocks directly without sampling the imported mesh; its 0.025 m source cells and box-like figure remain preserved as the rejected prototype. The preserved MER09 [sculpture script](../../../scripts/sculpt-voxel-mercenary.py) instead uses the evaluated original shape as an offline scaffold, sculpts permanent 0.0125 m cells and deliberately paints the face and coherent material regions. Its source height is 2.1 m. The same fixed 1.4 display scale gives 0.0175 m displayed cells, 2.94 m height and 1.435 m width. It compensates for an upright model appearing shorter than camera-facing people at the ordinary pitched camera; lower pitch can make the mercenary look larger. Zoom and orbit never change the cells or scale. These are art dimensions, not a change to the character’s native body.

The preserved MER09 iteration uses the rigid-part walking driver, with leg clearance prepared from the new exported part dimensions, and freezes the walking pose while paused. Loading, failure and unsupported actions/conditions retain sprite fallback. Its cape is permanent thick geometry with authored clearance rather than simulated thin cloth; the tradeoff remains that folds do not flow freely. [World presentation](../../world-presentation.md#animated-character-pixels) owns lighting, nearest-surface protection, selection and fallback. [MER08 evidence](../../verification/mercenary-default.md#authored-voxel-iteration--october-14-2026) records the rejected prototype’s successful renderer, pause, hidden-character/load and lifecycle checks; [MER09’s own evidence](../../verification/mercenary-default.md#sculpted-source-guided-iteration--october-14-2026) records successful scoped visual/contact/gait, fallback/load and replacement checks against the fitted export.
