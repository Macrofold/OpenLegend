## Direction and feedback retained

The [creative brief](../../../art-direction/final-board/creative-brief.md) remains the overall target. For the earlier study, Mike rejected a painterly model merely displayed at low resolution, rigid cape movement, a visible front-cloth gap and distracting shimmer during rotation. The study explores purposeful pixel detail and material colors, readable adult proportions, fitted cloth and dimensional lighting. These requests are recorded in its design/refinement files; they do not establish that every visual issue is resolved.

The isolated viewer's target is about 192 vertical pixels with integer display scaling. The earlier game pilot samples the model into a fixed 128×192 color/depth image while the world renders at full screen resolution. Those are historical implementation choices in their respective contexts, not an approved universal resolution for all artwork.

On October 14, Mike reported that the game mercenary looked too small, gained apparent detail when zoomed in, lost its eyes and face, and showed moving pixels or underlying body parts during rotation. He requested a new design whose colored blocks belong permanently to the body and clothing, preserving the old model. That feedback authorizes this iteration; it is not final aesthetic acceptance. [Current rendering behavior](../../world-presentation.md#animated-character-pixels) and the [iteration plan](../../projects/mercenary-voxel-iteration.md) own the resulting contract and delivery scope.

## Rejected first block figure and new sculpted iteration

Mike explicitly rejected MER08's figure as ugly and asked for art closer to the original 3D model. Its technical checks stand, but its box-like anatomy, squared face, rigid horizontal cape throw and oversized details are not accepted art direction. MER09 now has source files and game integration. During authoring, the evaluated original body, outfit and cape guide the finer cell sculpture; deliberately painted skin, hair/beard, facial features, mail, leather/wraps and cape regions replace the texture colors. The clothed body volume is retained; the cape is fitted outward around the bounded walking poses instead of removing body cells that happen to be covered while standing. There is no separate bare-body layer under the outfit, and the cape can naturally cover mail. Garment thickness and clearance are authored into the geometry. Its current contact, walking and browser qualification remain pending; earlier renderer checks do not prove the replacement’s fit or appearance. The [reopened plan](../../projects/mercenary-voxel-iteration.md#revised-art-direction-after-explicit-rejection) owns this revision.

## Integration and next iteration

The earlier [preparation script](../../../scripts/prepare-mercenary.py) takes the external study directory as its argument and creates the original trusted bundled GLB plus cloth data. Its [runtime provenance](../../../apps/client/src/characters/mercenary-assets/provenance.json) records those source/output digests. The source study and prepared game output are different artifacts; editing Blender or a texture does not automatically update the game.

MER08’s earlier authoring script constructs colored blocks directly without sampling the imported mesh; its 0.025 m source cells and box-like figure remain preserved as the rejected prototype. The current [sculpture script](../../../scripts/sculpt-voxel-mercenary.py) instead uses the evaluated original shape as an offline scaffold, sculpts permanent 0.0125 m cells and deliberately paints the face and coherent material regions. Its source height is 2.1 m. The same fixed 1.4 display scale gives 0.0175 m displayed cells, 2.94 m height and 1.435 m width. It compensates for an upright model appearing shorter than camera-facing people at the ordinary pitched camera; lower pitch can make the mercenary look larger. Zoom and orbit never change the cells or scale. These are art dimensions, not a change to the character’s native body.

The current iteration uses the same rigid-part walking driver, with leg clearance prepared from the new exported part dimensions, and freezes the walking pose while paused. Loading, failure and unsupported actions/conditions retain sprite fallback. Its cape is permanent thick geometry with authored clearance rather than simulated thin cloth; the tradeoff remains that folds do not flow freely. [World presentation](../../world-presentation.md#animated-character-pixels) owns lighting, nearest-surface protection, selection and fallback. [MER08 evidence](../../verification/mercenary-default.md#authored-voxel-iteration--october-14-2026) records the rejected prototype’s successful renderer, pause, hidden-character/load and lifecycle checks; MER09 requires fresh visual/contact/gait and replacement checks.
