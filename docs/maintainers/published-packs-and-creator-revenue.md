## Ownership and useful first delivery

The [feature specification](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md) refines ND23 and the broader publication/distribution part of ND12. It consumes [INV-8](inventions-and-world-evolution.md#inv-8--portable-inventions-and-later-algorithm-extensions), [EWF11](extensible-world-foundation.md#ewf11--reusable-construct-integration-and-local-portability-proof), [PD10](production-deployment.md#conditional-launch-and-expansion), [commercial offers](commercial-offers.md) and the established [governance and ownership contract](../../archive/03-design-proposals/invention-governance-and-ownership.md). The [PACK inventory](../limits/published-packs-and-creator-revenue.md) owns new catalog/commerce/participation scope; existing [WC-L06–WC-L12](../limits/world-creation.md#wc-l06--first-library-and-publication-scope) retains the narrow free-transfer profile.

Prove free reuse before paid marketplace work. One useful invention should travel with its complete authorized requirements and lead to an actual action. A curated catalog helps somebody find that result; it does not become a compulsory shopping trip before entering a world. Free libraries and self-hosting remain independent of payout onboarding, platform membership and a creator fund. The existing account library automatically retains eligible attributed contribution history; an independently retained complete reusable copy is a distinct promise.

- [ ] **PACK01 — Complete the useful free round trip and small catalog.** Consume DG12's known-authority, supported-recipe profile and prove source play, retained contribution, full eligible closure, destination review, explicit installation, separate knowledge/material acquisition and useful play. Publish only actually reviewed supported entries, with honest compatibility and operation cost. **Exit:** an ordinary creator reaches useful reuse with less effort than reinvention; missing dependencies or permissions preserve the original and give a concrete remedy. No arbitrary template, remote federation or general live migration is claimed.
- [ ] **PACK02 — Resolve rights and exact publication.** Adopt contribution, hosting, publication, modification, commercial-operation, export and redistribution terms for the selected content. Preserve D43 account ownership, contributor credit, private-origin visibility, inherited/component licenses and sufficient existing grants. Obtain a publisher/payee mandate and complete agreed revenue split before any sale; ownership supplies no automatic split. **Exit:** a release contains its exact eligible closure and truthful complete/partial description, with no private history or invented license exception; missing consent blocks only the dependent release. Old granted versions remain governed by their actual terms.
- [ ] **PACK03 — Deliver acquisition, updates and retained access.** Consume CO's fulfillment/reconciliation for one-time account acquisition of the exact selected release. Explain license versus download, copy versus reference, destination authority, operating grant, installation, update and future re-download scope. Distinguish ordinary unpublication from exceptional rights/privacy/security restrictions. **Exit:** payment/disconnect, source closure, buyer departure, incompatible destination, current revocation, refund and retirement preserve truthful status and the appropriate previous rights; no automatic world update, character teaching or duplicated purchase follows.
- [ ] **PACK04 — Adopt the fixed subscriber allocation.** Select the real per-subscriber amount, qualifying human participation, supported recipient worlds, choice/default policy, premium overlap and disposition of unused allocation. Consume the specification's concrete proposal before implementing collection. **Exit:** one subscriber creates only one bounded pool; NPCs, alternate bodies, simulated speed, idle connections and fabricated participation cannot multiply it; private choices and exact monetary totals remain appropriately scoped. No live pool is funded by a design example.
- [ ] **PACK05 — Complete creator accounting and payment recovery.** Qualify eligible payees, agreed collaborator shares, actual fees, reserves, pending/settled/withheld/payable/paid states, refund and dispute adjustments, review/appeal, failed payout and creator exit. Keep pack purchases, premium-world proceeds, subscriber allocations, hosting expense and DG30 grants distinct. **Exit:** one actual receipt is allocated once; no estimate is advertised as cash paid; current authorization and external accounting survive fictional restore; a missing collaborator or failed transfer has a funded resolution path rather than silently forfeited earnings.
- [ ] **PACK06 — Expand for demonstrated player and creator value.** Measure useful import/use, repeat voluntary play, publisher maintenance/support, failed compatibility, full preparation/storage/serving cost and reconciliation burden. Add richer packs/media, broader discovery, more operators or account synchronization only for a demonstrated need under their existing owners. **Exit:** the expanded scope improves a wanted game and can honor its complete rights, resource and service obligations. Catalog size, published variations, session time and gross receipts alone do not establish success.
