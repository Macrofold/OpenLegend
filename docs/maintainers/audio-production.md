## AUD03 — Produce and qualify a representative soundscape

- [ ] Prepare a small musical family and coherent effect/ambience families around one real 10–15-minute sequence: movement/materials, handling, camp work, a supported threat, ambience, restrained interface feedback and recovery. This is a listening workload, not a quota of composition.
- [ ] After required AUD04 playback, review repetition, overlaps, conversation, quiet, source interruption/removal and audio-off understanding. Record appeal, fit and fatigue separately.
- **Dependencies:** actual events, preliminary assets, AUD02 rights and budget. Preparation precedes playback where useful; final in-play acceptance cannot. Effects work does not wait for a music winner.
- **Outcome/evidence:** a useful integrated soundscape with meaningful action/material distinctions and no invented threats. A capture mix is preparatory only. Expand for demonstrated coverage or repetition needs rather than volume alone.

## Design needs deliberately left separate

- **Musician career/song learning:** [Arts](../repertoires/arts-leisure.md#fixed-song-performance-and-a-personal-jukebox) preserves Mike's opportunity; [DG34](needs-design.md#dg34--shared-campaigns-with-local-opportunities) retains grounded bard/history work. Learning/unlocks, skill/instrument admission, bands, audience choice, tips/gigs and interruptions still need a playable design. Fixed audio and role notes do not close it.
- **Richer semantic scoring:** disclosed backstories, fight motives and subtle emotion need a separately selected evidence-scoped intent producer. Basic known-context selection is designed; arbitrary prose interpretation remains undesigned. No hidden facts or keyword shortcut is accepted.
- **Voice:** [HE07](hearing-and-speech.md#he07--optional-speech-media-and-selected-hearing-extensions) retains TTS/media scope. No speech provider is selected here.
- **Advanced distribution/music:** OST/creator/self-host packages, synchronized parts, live generation and playable instruments are optional future work, not prerequisites.

Native routing observations remain with [CG05](agent-guidance.md#cg05--read-only-routing-cases). This tracker enables no automated suites; follow current [verification policy](../../.agents/rules/verification.md).
