# Compelling character experience — integration tracker

**Status: product direction accepted and documented; implementation and integrated qualification remain open.** The [feature specification](../projects/compelling-characters-feature-spec.md) defines the intended experience. This tracker owns completeness across existing character, cognition, agency and world-content owners. It does not replace their tasks, reset delivered foundations or authorize implementation, paid trials or new permissions. Previous approvals retain their original scope.

The first useful scope is one resident whose body experience, desire for contact/belonging, enjoyment, interests, relationships and chosen intentions form an intelligible life. These dimensions must influence meaningful choices, including solitude and stopping after satisfaction. They need neither mandatory meters nor a giant psychological simulation. Deliver within the [existing first-playable agreement](../../archive/05-project/first-playable-mvp.md), preserving real live invention and its consequences; a scripted personality demonstration cannot substitute for it. Follow the [delivery sequence](../projects/compelling-characters-feature-spec.md#delivery-sequence) without waiting for large populations or unattended-world infrastructure.

## CE01 — A coherent authored person in a supported world

[PG02](parallel-batch-03-personal-game.md#pg02--coherent-attended-resident-behavior) proposes the next bounded attended-world integration: current concerns, actual outcomes, reconsideration and stopping through existing character owners. It excludes new psychological meters, family disclosure and unattended communities; the complete CE acceptance below remains open.

**Owners:** [base-world character content](base-world.md), [actor identity and relationships](actor-model.md) and [accepted personal knowledge](cognition-redesign.md). Coordinate ND04's later personality development without making that whole program a prerequisite.

- [ ] Define the initial resident through coherent background, values, interests, relationship history, practical knowledge and current circumstances. Give them plausible competing motives rather than unrelated adjectives, an exhaustive biography or a prewritten sequence of actions. Distinguish creator-authored starting information from later lived experience.
- [ ] Make bodily experience, contact/belonging and enjoyment meaningful in this small world. Identify the actual people, places, objects and supported activities through which each can matter. Ordinary conversation, observation, movement, food, rest and work can supply first examples; missing pastime or social-effect families remain explicit work with their existing owners.
- [ ] Preserve authored-world variation and human control. Definitions grant no capabilities, private knowledge, automatic relationship facts or permission to write a player's speech or goals. Reuse current authoring/admission rather than introducing freeform executable character rules.

**Exit:** an inspectable character/world proposal supplies genuinely available opportunities and distinct reasons to choose among them, without requiring a new universal trait taxonomy or canned successful outcome.

## CE02 — Meaningful psychological experience and its lifetime

**Owners:** [feeling and relationship foundations, ACT07/ACT08](actor-model.md#advanced-emotional-state-foundation), and [accepted mind/knowledge behavior](cognition-redesign.md); existing body and world-definition owners retain their consequences.

- [ ] Specify how each initial experience becomes relevant, persists, changes, competes with other concerns and can subside or end. Hunger is experienced bodily pressure grounded in actual physiology. Wanting company, belonging and enjoyment must have intelligible causes and possible satisfaction, disappointment or reprioritization; proximity or an activity label alone does not guarantee fulfillment.
- [ ] Keep stable tendencies, temporary feelings, desires, beliefs, relationships and operational goals distinct. Use existing supported appraisals and accepted character-owned understanding. Add a missing mechanical effect only through its proper owner; descriptive text does not secretly become a new need bar or authority.
- [ ] Demonstrate enough food changing food-seeking priority, sufficient or unwelcome contact ending an approach, and enjoyment giving way to another concern. Permit mixed feelings, refusal, risk-taking and chosen solitude. Repetition can remain worthwhile when its purpose remains; an obsolete motive must not compel endless hunting, cooking, fire tending or greetings.

**Exit:** the initial experiences have understandable beginnings, changes and endings through actual supported behavior, with existing feeling persistence, correction, privacy and authored-world boundaries preserved.

## CE03 — The world and inner experience enter attention coherently

**Owners:** [cognition context, attention and reflection](cognition-redesign.md), [agency context and reconsideration, AG06/AG07](agent-agency.md), and existing perception/intake owners; [conversation work](narration-and-conversations.md) retains evidence and expression.

- [ ] Integrate a compact, current window containing relevant perceived surroundings, bodily pressure, feelings, personal knowledge, relationships and unfinished intentions. Show why circumstances matter to this actor without copying their whole history into every decision or prescribing the desired action. Preserve the complete accepted About me text and required evidence under the current memory contract; optional salience does not permit truncating identity.
- [ ] Allow meaningful inner changes and remembered concerns to attract attention alongside external events. Preserve permitted scope, important contrary evidence, ongoing conversation and real danger. Do not add constant paid introspection, a second scheduler or unlimited hidden interests merely to make the character appear active. Qualify the CE-F03 quiet-interval control through an ordinary supported internal opportunity; all-external-prompt demonstrations cannot close this requirement.
- [ ] Keep prompts, supplied context and admitted output consistent. Fictional thoughts remain optional; hidden model reasoning is not required character evidence. A hypothesis, remembered report or feeling cannot become an observed fact, physical effect or another person's consent.

**Exit:** the same integrated decision path can notice a bodily or psychological concern, relate it to current circumstances and choose coherently; later satisfaction or changed evidence also reaches that path. Apply [CG12](../limits/cognition.md#cg12) and [FL19](../limits/feelings.md#fl19).

## CE04 — A complete life episode with continued activity

**Owners:** existing [goals, plans and continuation](agent-agency.md), [conversation](narration-and-conversations.md), [recall](cognition-redesign.md) and [playable content](base-world.md). Reuse actor foundations and current action families.

- [ ] Deliver the [complete behavioral flows](../projects/compelling-characters-feature-spec.md#complete-behavioral-flows) through ordinary player and NPC surfaces. Include a whole afternoon, social approach/refusal, solitary enjoyment, bodily reprioritization, purposeful repetition and interruption/resumption. These are open situations with legitimate alternatives, not a mandatory itinerary.
- [ ] Connect intention to actual admitted movement, speech, work and consequences, then later recollection and changed priorities. Preserve partial work and retained purposes where supported. The player can pursue their own activity without directing every NPC step; the NPC can end an activity without receiving another instruction.
- [ ] Preserve current pause, absence, outage, recovery and human-control policies. Distinguish unavailable cognition from fictional indifference; neither narration nor a hidden fallback may fabricate completed life. Track missing activity-family support explicitly rather than claiming it from prose.

**Exit:** one recognizable resident sustains meaningful, revisable pursuits across an observed episode and later return. Interaction is worthwhile without requiring constant emergencies, maximum action variety or compulsory player caretaking.

## CE05 — Integrated character quality and complete-cost qualification

**Owners:** [behavioral evaluation, AG12](agent-agency.md#ag12--behavioral-value-and-cost-separately-authorized), and [cognition qualification, CR12](cognition-redesign.md), remain the comparison and evidence owners. This tracker records whether their combined findings satisfy the feature; it creates no independent test program.

- [ ] Add these flows to the existing matched evaluation, including different characters interpreting the same event, changed needs/history, prompt/context variations, useful repetition versus perseveration, delayed recall and outage/absence controls. Qualification accompanies each delivered slice; preserve failures and uncertainty.
- [ ] Qualify ordinary attended pacing with actual human reading/composition, AI preparation/reply delays and the offered clock while food, sleep, work and conversation coexist. The player must have room for their own worthwhile activity. If maintenance or waiting dominates, identify and resolve the relevant authored starting conditions, need/work pacing or interaction burden through its existing owner; sufficient food stock or manually advanced actions alone do not establish a playable pace. Any selected change to clock or absence semantics consumes PS05, and this criterion adopts no automatic feeding, new pause behavior or universal rate.
- [ ] Judge coherent motivation, appropriate stopping, adaptation, independent interests, social understanding and enjoyable interaction separately from mechanical validity, verbosity, obedient task completion and optimal resource use. Compare complete work and cost, including context, attention, interpretation and memory; fixtures alone do not establish live character quality.
- [ ] Record evidence and remaining gaps in the existing verification owners. If behavior is monotonous, brittle or costly, improve the relevant authored opportunities, context or continuation before adding personality fields, mandatory thoughts or population. Preserve privacy and real consequences while simplifying.

**Exit:** AG12/CR12 provide evidence for the integrated experience, its limitations and its operating cost. Close CE items only when their stated outcomes are demonstrated; documenting the design or completing one subsystem does not close this tracker.
