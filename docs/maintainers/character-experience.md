# Compelling character experience — integration tracker

**Status: product direction accepted and documented; implementation and integrated qualification remain open.** The [feature specification](../projects/compelling-characters-feature-spec.md) defines the intended experience. This tracker owns completeness across existing character, cognition, agency and world-content owners. It does not replace their tasks, reset delivered foundations or authorize implementation, paid trials or new permissions. Previous approvals retain their original scope.

The first useful scope is one resident whose body experience, desire for contact/belonging, enjoyment, interests, relationships and chosen intentions form an intelligible life. These dimensions must influence meaningful choices, including solitude and stopping after satisfaction. They need neither mandatory meters nor a giant psychological simulation. Deliver within the [existing first-playable agreement](../../archive/05-project/first-playable-mvp.md), preserving real live invention and its consequences; a scripted personality demonstration cannot substitute for it. Follow the [delivery sequence](../projects/compelling-characters-feature-spec.md#delivery-sequence) without waiting for large populations or unattended-world infrastructure.
