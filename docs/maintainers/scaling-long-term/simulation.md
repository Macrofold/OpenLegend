# LT-S — Advanced simulation, spatial execution and fidelity

[Deferred backlog](README.md) · **G4/LATER, conditional evaluations.** Parents: PF/SW/EWF/INV and the relevant world mechanic. Existing PF03/PF09/PF10 and SW work remain owners of immediate measured optimizations. **Massive-scale research for every item:** [simulation/time](../../../archive/02-research/massive-scale/domains/simulation-time.md), [physics/navigation](../../../archive/02-research/massive-scale/domains/physics-navigation.md), [environment systems](../../../archive/02-research/massive-scale/domains/environment-systems.md), [architectural options](../../../archive/02-research/massive-scale/architectural-options.md). Audit: SCA23/SCA28–SCA31/SCA44/SCA45.

<a id="lt-s01"></a>
- [ ] **LT-S01 — Exact scheduled-process experiment.** Trigger: repeated simple per-second updates remain a material cost after applicable/active indexing. Choose one supported analytic process and implement an experimental due-event/lazy representation. **Exit:** differential comparison preserves every consequential threshold/intermediate exposure, pause/speed/restore/cancellation, phase order and RNG outcome. Adopt only after equivalence; this is not approval for coarse offscreen simulation.

<a id="lt-s02"></a>
- [ ] **LT-S02 — Data-oriented/native-kernel comparison.** Trigger: a measured numerical kernel or allocation profile dominates. Through PF10 compare current code against compact arrays/batching or a worker/WASM/native boundary with compact versioned messages. **Exit:** report end-to-end copying, cold setup, GC, p99 and recovery costs plus exact results. Keep the ordinary PF10 implementation task there; this item owns only the comparative evidence for a deeper representation change, not an ECS rewrite mandate.

<a id="lt-s03"></a>
- [ ] **LT-S03 — Large mutable navigation experiment.** Trigger: measured geometry edits or long routes exceed the qualified SW provider. Compare tiled/hierarchical/incremental search, selective rebuilds and asynchronous requests against the existing implementation. **Exit:** stacked surfaces, body-specific clearance, door churn, no-route/partial/coverage outcomes and stale-result rejection remain correct; preparation and queue costs are included. The current finite route/status fixes stay in SW05/SW06.

<a id="lt-s04"></a>
- [ ] **LT-S04 — Dense movement and connected-physics policy.** Trigger: a concrete crowd, vehicle, rope or rigid-body mechanic is approved. Define local avoidance/reservations and collision/constraint-island ownership; prototype only the required fidelity. **Exit:** chokepoints do not oscillate indefinitely, collision authority remains native and connected structures are co-located or explicitly coordinated. A 2D/rigid-body library benchmark is not proof for the game's 3D workload; no general physics sandbox is implicitly required.

<a id="lt-s05"></a>
- [ ] **LT-S05 — Environmental solver refinement.** Trigger: a coarse owned mechanic blocks demonstrated gameplay. Specify one richer fire/heat/moisture/support/ecology model, spatial/dependency footprint, numerical stability and migration from its predecessor. **Exit:** conservation, step-size sensitivity, old/new representation conversion and intervention-at-boundary tests pass under its declared approximation; no two writable physical truths or per-frame model adjudication.

<a id="lt-s06"></a>
- [ ] **LT-S06 — Explicit fidelity-tier feasibility.** Trigger: the owner approves abstraction for a named domain and exact simulation is too costly. Evaluate detailed/aggregate state and promotion/demotion mappings without assuming approval for named-character approximation. **Exit:** repeated re-entry preserves protected identity, owned resources, witnessed history and obligations; measure error and exploits from observer-dependent outcomes. A rejected approximation can close this evaluation; no silent LOD enters production.

<a id="lt-s07"></a>
- [ ] **LT-S07 — Narrow behavioral surrogate evaluation.** Trigger: a repeated, constrained background decision dominates inference and its owner approves a surrogate experiment. Compare native rules, smaller classifiers or distilled/cached behavior against the original task distribution. **Exit:** individual evidence/continuity and aggregate social/economic drift are evaluated, including adversarial and out-of-distribution inputs; unsupported cases defer/escalate under existing spend rules. Billion-agent opinion simulations are inspiration, not proof of open-ended NPC quality.

<a id="lt-s08"></a>
- [ ] **LT-S08 — Dense-event product alternatives.** Trigger: a specific gathering exceeds tested required interaction/output density. Resolve D61 with prototypes of any proposed capacity admission, distributed stages, explicit instances, remote viewers or clock policy. **Exit:** the owner chooses the experience explicitly; human versus character knowledge, interaction rights, legitimate witnesses and participant messaging are tested. Do not silently turn excess participants into spectators or impose time dilation. Implementation belongs to the selected feature/protocol owners after this decision.

G2 execution isolation remains in INV/EWF's existing conditional work. The [feature-readiness tasks](../scaling-feature-readiness.md) add its research-derived evaluation criteria without creating another sandbox implementation owner.
