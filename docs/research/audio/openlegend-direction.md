# Music and sound direction for Open Legend

The selected [base-world audio direction](../../worlds/base/audio-direction.md) now owns musical range, editorial vocabulary and initial playback policy. The [audio production specification](../../projects/audio-production-feature-spec.md), [technical design](../../projects/audio-production-tech-design.md) and [operator procedure](../../production-workflows/audio-production.md) own production and optional integration. These are designed targets; provider qualification, asset production and runtime delivery remain open in [AUD](../../maintainers/audio-production.md).

Mike selected broad genres, automation in service of quality and fixed recordings with intelligent selection/transitions. No composer-first purchase, narrow folk/chamber palette, eight-minute package or synchronized stems is required. A provider interface, licensed recording, ordinary editing or specialist remains available when it improves the actual result. Initial delivery targets official gameplay and an internal jukebox; wider distribution is optional.

The [original November 4 direction proposal](archive/2026/2026-11-04-direction-proposal.md) is preserved in full. Its home/belonging, wilderness/possibility and history/consequence themes remain useful optional candidates. Its production examples and commissioning approach remain available without overriding the selected broader exploration.

The [sourcing research](README.md) and [player-music study](../player-music-appreciation/README.md) supply evidence and limits. They support attention to recognizable relationships, scene fit, repetition, personal preference and meaningful quiet; they do not establish an OpenLegend quality winner or a universal musical formula.
