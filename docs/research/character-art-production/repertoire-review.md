# Character-art production — repertoire application review

**Reviewed November 5, 2026 against `Macrofold/OpenLegend` at `fc67caf8eae3abe7168edd25746124671a18e291`.** This is a production-coverage review of the introductions, ownership rules, domain maps and directly relevant examples across all 28 catalogues and the four proposed world families. It is not a line-by-line balance audit or reprioritization of every catalogue entry. No repertoire mechanic, character performance, generated asset or player response was executed by this review.

The [feature specification](../../projects/character-art-production-feature-spec.md) and [execution guide](../../projects/character-art-production-tech-design.md) own the resulting production decisions. [Gameplay priorities](../../repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md) remains the selection owner: a complete enjoyable survival-adventure must include practical invention, independently motivated people and consequential return, rather than postpone those qualities behind generic combat parity or exhaustive art infrastructure. [The catalogue map](../../repertoires/README.md#catalogue-map) owns the repertoire inventory and its counts.

## Findings that change the workflow

The existing library already contains ordinary pleasure, ambition, mastery, cruelty, hospitality, rivalry, useful objects, unusual bodies and changing worlds. More biographies, equipment variants or animation verbs would not repair the production gap. The useful addition is a way to translate selected content into an inspectable identity, supported action, usable export and economical revision.

- **A person needs continuity, not a single occupational pose.** Inspect the same identity through the selected movement, useful activity and relevant quiet or social context. This is coverage of the artwork, not a script forcing that person to demonstrate a personality.
- **An object needs coherent construction across uses.** A practical invention, inherited tool or changed outfit should retain its recognizable design across the views actually needed. A local change should not regenerate an unrelated person or their whole history.
- **Readability must respect uncertainty.** An intentionally concealed mark, disguise, transformed body or unfamiliar signal must not be made perfectly recognizable by an art-review rule. Preserve the intended visible information, not an omniscient viewer's knowledge of the underlying identity.
- **Expression is not an emotional truth detector.** Distinguish an authored manner, a supported chosen gesture, a bodily reaction and private interpretation. Do not turn traits into permanent idle loops, anatomical differences into moral labels, or a smile into guaranteed affection or consent.
- **Variation should answer a production question.** A second needed outfit tests fit and revision; a genuinely different anatomy tests a different family. Neither requires generating the entire catalogue. Shared rigs and material recipes must leave room for particular people and worlds.

These are operational applications of existing repertoire boundaries, not new cognitive, physical, relationship or recognition mechanics. The professional sources in the [research study](README.md) support early playable work and readable design; the translation into these OpenLegend cases is a project judgment.

## Small, useful production cases

Use these as examples for selecting coverage, not as a required new scene or animation set. The task owner chooses an existing suitable subject and supported activity; named catalogue people remain proposals until adopted. Each case separates the visual evidence from the game behavior that would need its own implementation.

### The same person at work and in wanted company

[CB-012, Halden Pike](../../repertoires/characters-backstories.md#ordinary-livelihoods-and-local-company), combines beekeeping, poor singing and hospitality. The production lesson is not to add singing to every character. For an adopted comparable person, preserve the same face, build and distinctive clothing through the required work, travel and quiet/social poses. Evaluate weight, posture and deliberate pauses rather than a permanently cheerful idle. A manually staged acting sample does not demonstrate that the resident independently sought company.

### A useful object with a recognizable past

The [surveyor's folding frame](../../repertoires/objects.md#surveyors-folding-frame) has practical geometry and particular markings. A selected supported object can test consistent world, held and inventory depictions, a local material or handle revision, and preservation of intentional asymmetry. Its visible markings may invite interpretation; they do not reveal every fact about an expedition. Do not invent folding, load-bearing or new grip mechanics merely to complete a cosmetic proof.

### An interrupted activity without a false success

The [work catalogue](../../repertoires/work-crafting.md#base-coverage) requires a busy person to retain the relevant ways of stopping or responding to danger. Choose an actually supported activity, inspect its moving body and equipment, interrupt through the existing owner, and examine the resulting pose. The visual must not complete an abandoned transfer, strike or output. This tests the art/action interface; it neither scripts a threat response nor revives the withdrawn craft-timing proof.

### Recognition without an omniscient costume rule

For a selected compatible outfit or equipment revision, preserve the person's intended visible identity while checking garment removal, coverage and required motion. Separately review any deliberately concealed feature: do not expose it through an atlas, thumbnail, missing layer or forced facial reveal. The [knowledge catalogue](../../repertoires/languages-knowledge.md#selected-recognition-and-evidence-boundary) retains observer-specific recognition. A disguise or genuine transformation may intentionally change recognition; no new disguise mechanic is implemented by this workflow.

### Expression without exposing a private mind

[TR-005, demonstrative delight](../../repertoires/traits.md#temperament-and-emotional-style), already distinguishes private enjoyment from a chosen cheer. A selected supported expression can be broad or restrained while a neutral body remains equally valid for someone who does not express it. The proposed Nemei example provides a genuinely different future test: a visible mantle pattern may have several meanings. Neither case requires an animation for every emotional value or a universal emotion-color table.

## Disposition and limits of the review

**No new catalogue entries, numerical priorities, counts, world rules or character biographies are necessary for this production task.** Preserve the existing breadth instead of adding another familiar tool, another sad backstory, a generic emotion loop or a compulsory visual scorecard. The concrete improvements belong in the production guide: purposeful part boundaries, direct-mesh repair, early motion proof, prototype-to-polish continuation, truthful expression, final-export checks and a useful revision.

[CAP00](../../maintainers/art-direction.md#character-production-follow-through) owns completion of this selected design and its reconciliation; CAP01 owns the unrun offline production proof. Existing V3D, character-experience and first-playable owners retain runtime, family, artistic and player acceptance. The broad needs-design register already describes the general appearance pipeline as designed; this review does not close its wider qualification or promote an entire ND/DG umbrella to complete.

A later production task should deepen the specific selected catalogue example and actual consumer, not reread or implement the whole library. If that execution exposes an unsupported action, body, sensory disclosure or object capability, route the concrete gap to its existing owner before promising it in art.
