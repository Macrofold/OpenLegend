# Character-production strategy research

OpenLegend character-production strategy: convincing characters, controllable bodies.

Strategic research and proposed validation plan | November 11, 2026

**Status:** Research recommendation and proposed validation plan. No candidate has been adopted through this research, and no proof has been executed.

## Research recommendation

**Use an established anatomical character system under Mike’s or his delegate’s art review. Evaluate native controls and appropriate existing assets first, preserve the approved artistic identity, and export a bounded, validated shape family into OpenLegend. MetaHuman 5.8 is the strongest first experiment; it is not yet a proven long-term winner.**

The important change is the source of anatomical knowledge. A canonical MetaHuman mesh used as the recipient of invented expansion fields does not test MetaHuman's native body model. Similarly, a Character Creator or scan-library body should first be evaluated with the correspondence, rig and garment tools that make that foundation valuable.

Use Meshy where it already contributes: visual invention, reference fidelity, distinctive face/body forms and costume detail. A good generated surface can be a selective source for fitting and sculpting. It is not, by itself, an anatomical variation model.

**Make the next step a small native-body and browser-export proof, reusing applicable existing evidence.** Obtain targeted specialist help only for an observed material gap when repairing it is preferable to changing the source. Do not extend the current heuristic fullness generator, commit to a wardrobe, train a body model, or port a complete proprietary authoring environment before this proof resolves the foundational question.

## What would change this recommendation?

Character Creator 5.2 should win if its native results, compatible garments and export path reduce total work substantially and its Enterprise rights are acceptable. An artist-owned family should win if the native generators cannot preserve the Peacock's identity and believable anatomy across the intended range. MHR deserves a short native evaluation when control over the model and redistribution rights are decisive. Retaining today's mesh is justified only by demonstrated benefits that exceed the transfer and maintenance cost.

## 1. Why this production method is the best first investment

The brief describes a missing source of good shapes, rather than a missing interpolation mechanism. OpenLegend already has recipes, targets, corrections, fitting and rendering. More elaborate arithmetic cannot establish where a fuller abdomen should meet the pelvis, how upper-arm tissue changes around the shoulder, or which facial structures make the Peacock recognizable.

Three kinds of knowledge need to work together:

- **Anatomical variation:** measured/statistical shape spaces, coherent authored morph families, and appropriate reference anatomy.
- **Artistic identity:** the selected face, build, proportions, silhouette and costume design, interpreted consistently across views.
- **Deformation knowledge:** a useful rig, skin weights, shape-dependent skeleton adjustments, garment fitting and corrections for moving poses.

A native ecosystem often already connects these pieces. Moving its surface onto another mesh too early can discard those connections. The aim is to preserve useful knowledge and art quality, rather than preserve one particular vertex layout.

The recommended division of work is practical: the established system supplies credible starting variations; Mike or his delegate approves the art; AI automates repeatable operations and comparative inspection; OpenLegend evaluates only the approved portable data at runtime. Use native controls and appropriate existing assets before commissioning corrections. If targeted specialist work is preferable to changing the source, it may involve major fat or muscle forms, not merely pores and definition; record the actual effort.

### Decision comparison

The ratings below are judgments about suitability and risk, not measured scores.

| Strategy | Main advantage | Main burden | Decision for OpenLegend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Native established generator plus art review | Existing anatomy controls, correspondence, rigging and compatible garments | Export gaps, license conditions, identity changes at extremes | Preferred production method; MetaHuman first, CC a serious competitor |
| Native scan/morph library plus art review | Convincing connected surfaces and already corresponding shapes | Sparse trait combinations, identity reconciliation, rigging and generator rights | Strong endpoint source when rights and coverage are suitable |
| Generated geometry fitted to a template | Often preserves concept-specific forms better than stock presets | Cleanup, pose reconciliation, semantic correspondence and detail recovery | Use when artistic advantage outweighs transfer work |
| Original artist-authored family | Precise art direction, explicit control semantics and negotiable ownership | Highest initial skilled labor; rig and wardrobe still needed | Reliable fallback and likely source of missing shapes |
| Statistical model such as MHR | Inspectable shape/rig system and reproducible fitting | Semantic mapping, art finish, complete face and export work | Bounded alternative, especially for an open foundation |
| New coordinate-field generator | Cheap to produce technically valid displacement data | No validated anatomical prior; every interaction becomes custom research | Stop investing as the principal shape source |
| New learned model or volumetric tissue simulator | Potential long-term control and realism | Dataset, licensing, training/simulation, tuning and runtime work | Defer until a specific proven limitation justifies it |

## 2. Candidate foundations and their actual anatomical knowledge

### 2.1 MetaHuman 5.8: first native-system experiment

Epic documents a statistical body space with Muscularity, Fat, height and regional constraints. Muscle and fat are correlated unless appropriately constrained; pin order matters. A Body Delta facility exists, but there is no documented guarantee that a custom sculpt residual remains appropriate through every native variation. The reviewed interface does not establish an independent Definition axis or the required whole-body age progression. [S02](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-creator-body-params-tool-in-unreal-engine)

Current From Custom Mesh accepts custom humanoid topology and pose, including generated meshes, and outputs MetaHuman topology. Its documentation explicitly warns about extreme proportions, defective sources and treating clothes as the body surface. This is a fitter, not hidden-anatomy reconstruction. [S03](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-creator-from-custom-mesh-tool-in-unreal-engine)

**Native adoption:** retain the native head/body correspondence, authoring rig and useful clothing tools during the evaluation. Shape the Peacock using native controls, selective fitting and sculpting; then vary the native body parameters. Test the stock anatomical model before adding the Peacock identity, so a poor shape prior cannot be disguised by a beautiful one-off sculpt.

**Transfer onto today's mesh:** worthwhile only if today's mesh has a verified advantage in runtime geometry, facial deformation or existing production assets. If the new candidate already uses suitable MetaHuman topology, replace poor target data rather than creating a second fitting step merely to preserve old vertex positions.

Import modes matter. Epic's From DNA tool can update mesh or joints while retaining editability, whereas Import Whole Rig creates a fixed body. Removing that rig and performing another parametric fit may lose imported information. Record the actual mode; “DNA imported” does not say whether the body remains parametrically editable. [S04](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-creator-from-dna-tool-in-unreal-engine)

Use a complete assembled character or DCC export for the runtime proof. From Custom Mesh's **Save Pose** exports an intermediate DNA with the combined head/body on the body skeleton, **without the facial rig**. Epic documents opening that file through Pose Editor, not Character Assembler. It is not a substitute for the complete character export. [S03](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-creator-from-custom-mesh-tool-in-unreal-engine)

The current Python API covers editing, body constraints, conforming, assembly and exports. Face auto-rigging and texture requests can use cloud authoring services. That is compatible with provider-free gameplay, provided all supported runtime data is exported beforehand. Exact installed examples must govern execution. [S05](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-creator-python-scripting-in-unreal-engine?lang=en-US)

**What does not export automatically:** the full Creator statistical body solver, native outfit-fitting behavior and all application-specific drivers. OpenRigLogic now provides MIT-licensed RigLogic/DNA libraries, but that is rig evaluation, not the Creator body generator or a demonstrated browser implementation. Initially sample and export only the behavior OpenLegend needs. [S06](https://www.metahuman.com/devkit)

### 2.2 Character Creator 5.2: the strongest integrated commercial competitor

CC combines an authored character/morph ecosystem with general proportion controls and HD surface forms. Its HD Ultimate Morphs explicitly preserve skeletal frame while supplying fat, muscle definition, folds and age detail. This is relevant anatomical knowledge, but individual slider examples do not establish every desired combination or identity-preserving ageing. [S07](https://www.reallusion.com/ContentStore/Character-Creator/3d-character/hd-ultimate-morphs/default.html)

The October 29, 2026 release adds CC Wrap for converting custom/AI humanoids to native CC topology, plus improved garment layering, conforming and skin-weight tools. That makes a complete native CC trial competitive. The release discussion also contains staff confirmation of baked appearance deformations and practitioner reports of morph-export problems; qualify the exact export route rather than assuming every in-app slider survives. [S08](https://discussions.reallusion.com/t/character-creator-5-2-release-note-ai-3d-generator-cc-wrap-enhanced-outfit-workflows-and-bug-fixes/18782)

**Native adoption:** fit/sculpt the Peacock into CC, retain its body topology and skeleton, compose existing morphs into the intended controls, and test a compatible shirt. This preserves the most valuable ecosystem advantages.

**Transfer to MetaHuman:** creates another correspondence, rigging and garment problem. It may still be justified by a demonstrated facial-rig or runtime advantage, but is not the default. Transfer does not remove restrictions on derivative content.

CC5 has Python scripting, including a vendor-provided shaping-morph example. Installed API definitions are important because documentation coverage is incomplete. This supports offline sampling; it is not evidence of a fully unattended, headless production pipeline. [S09](https://discussions.reallusion.com/t/slider-morph-settings-in-python/15814)

**Adoption gate:** Reallusion's current Content EULA explicitly requires Enterprise licensing for runtime anatomical morph sliders, bone manipulation and parametric character generation. The Software EULA also restricts character-generation uses of CC-derived characters. The required price is unknown, not presumed unaffordable. If an acceptable agreement saves weeks of artist work, CC could be the better economic choice. [S10](https://www.reallusion.com/Content/EULA/EULA.htm), [S11](https://www.reallusion.com/Content/EULA/AP/EULA_AP.htm)

### 2.3 Daz Genesis 9: rich authored anatomy with real morph export

Genesis 9's common base and morph ecosystem offer authored muscularity, regional shape, body and facial ageing targets. The muscularity product names individual muscle groups; the age product includes geometry as well as detail. That is a better source of anatomical hypotheses than radial inflation, while still requiring coherent art direction and intermediate review. [S12](https://www.daz3d.com/muscularity-morphs-for-genesis-9), [S13](https://docs.daz3d.com/public/read_me/index/93000_93999/93950/start)

The official Blender bridge supports selected morph export. Morph keys, controller relationships, pose corrections and skeleton changes must be distinguished: exporting the keys does not reproduce every native driver. [S14](https://github.com/daz3d/DazToBlender/blob/master/README.md)

**Native adoption:** retain Genesis correspondence, rig and clothing conventions. **Transfer:** use only for a specific demonstrated benefit; reconstructing its morph family on another topology increases labor and leaves donor rights intact.

Daz requires product-specific Interactive licenses for assets used directly in games, and not every product offers one. Its EULA contains extraction, separate-sale, cloud-delivery and AI-use conditions. These deserve exact-use review for a browser creator and reusable body library. Do not claim Daz has Reallusion's identical blanket rule against in-game morph sliders: the terms differ. [S15](https://www.daz3d.com/blog/daz-3d-interactive-license), [S16](https://www.daz3d.com/eula)

**Verdict:** credible anatomical and garment ecosystem, below MetaHuman/CC for this first proof because the selected product set, rights and export behavior must all be qualified.

### 2.4 MakeHuman, MPFB, MB-Lab, CharMorph and ANNY

MPFB already implements combinations of macro targets, including weight, muscle and age. Its target system is inspectable and its core meshes, rigs and targets are CC0; community additions can have different terms. These are authored anatomical priors, not the same technique as inventing an expansion field for each region. [S17](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/blob/master/LICENSE.md), [S18](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/blob/master/docs/services/targetservice.md)

However, the project has rejected the previous visible MPFB bodies. Native MPFB is useful as hidden rig/layout infrastructure or as an implementation reference. Reapplying its stock targets to an attractive new body can reintroduce the rejected anatomy. That possibility must be tested, not concealed behind the new mesh.

MB-Lab is retired. CharMorph is a relevant successor with several databases: its documentation identifies CC0, CC BY and AGPL assets separately. “Open-source add-on” does not mean every included character is unrestricted or equally suitable. [S19](https://github.com/animate1978/MB-Lab/discussions/410), [S20](https://blendercharacterproject.org/docs/html/Introduction.html)

ANNY is particularly instructive. It exposes explicit age, muscle, weight, height and regional controls, with batch evaluation. Its shape data comes from MakeHuman/MPFB, and its paper describes interpolation of authored prototype combinations. Apache code and CC0 core data do not cover its optional separately licensed SMPL-X topology. ANNY offers transparent control logic, not an independent escape from the already rejected source anatomy. [S21](https://github.com/naver/anny), [S22](https://naver.github.io/anny/build/shape_parameterization.html)

**Native versus transfer:** native operation retains established target interactions; converting topology creates no new anatomical knowledge. Use these systems only where the actual resulting art passes, or as hidden scaffolding for a new art-approved family. None supplies verified coverage of this brief merely by having similarly named controls.

### 2.5 MHR: the strongest additional open rig/model candidate

Meta's Momentum Human Rig supplies separate skeletal and surface parameters, body/head/hand identity coordinates, facial expressions, rigged FBX assets and multiple LODs. The published body identity space is learned from registered scans rather than named physiological tissue compartments. [S23](https://github.com/facebookresearch/MHR)

The paper reports 7,110 adult body scans and separate skeleton parameters. It also identifies limits: pose and expression corrections are independent of body shape in the described model, and complete tissue/clothing integration remains future work. The described face lacks some internal anatomy. Its fitting results are not a qualification of the Peacock's shape range. [S24](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.15586v1)

**Native adoption:** preserve the rig, skeletal parameterization and identity model; add the art layer and only the semantic controls actually needed. **Transfer:** likely loses important advantages, leaving MetaHuman to reconstruct behavior that MHR already owns.

The model assets are explicitly confirmed Apache-2.0 by a maintainer, not just inferred from a code badge. Apache asset licensing falls outside the default CC0/CC BY sourcing rule, but the [existing SAM/MHR source permission](../../../../projects/people-from-parts-authoring-contract.md) retains its scoped validity. That permission does not automatically cover different native model assets or public redistribution; qualify the actual proposed use without reopening unchanged permissions. [S25](https://github.com/facebookresearch/MHR/issues/62)

Its browser viewer is useful for inspection and export, but relies on a local Flask/TorchScript evaluator. A browser UI is not proof that the complete model runs in the browser without a backend. [S26](https://github.com/facebookresearch/MHR/blob/main/web-viewer/README.md)

**Verdict:** a serious fallback or competing proof when open control matters. Reject it for this stage if attaining the first convincing body requires a large optimizer, custom neural runtime or extensive new anatomy authoring.

### 2.6 SMPL, SMPL-X, STAR, GHUM and SOMA-X

SMPL-family systems provide coherent body fitting and animation priors. Their latent shape coordinates generally describe correlated variations; they are not automatically independent muscle, fat distribution and definition axes. STAR's local, shape-dependent pose corrections are relevant to fuller-body deformation, but still do not establish complete contact or the requested creator semantics. [S27](https://star.is.tue.mpg.de/)

The licensing distinction is consequential. Fixed SMPL and SMPL-X bodies can be CC BY 4.0, including rig and pose/dynamic shapes, while the identity-generating shape model has separate terms and commercial licensing. Do not assume that sampling model outputs into editable shape sliders bypasses those model rights. [S28](https://smpl.is.tue.mpg.de/license.html), [S29](https://smpl-x.is.tue.mpg.de/bodylicense.html), [S30](https://smpl.is.tue.mpg.de/modellicense.html)

GHUM supplies expressive learned body models and fitting machinery, but the model access and commercial redistribution terms were not fully established here. The generic repository license is not enough to qualify separately distributed data. [S31](https://github.com/google-research/google-research/blob/master/ghum/README.md?plain=1)

SOMA-X provides a shared topology/rig framework over several body-model backends. That could help a future multi-source pipeline, but importing several models before choosing a visual foundation adds a representation to maintain. Backend licenses remain relevant. [S32](https://nvlabs.github.io/SOMA-X/latest/model_card.html)

**Verdict:** useful research and fitting infrastructure; not the shortest route to an attractive, fully dressed game-character family. If one wins a native visual/export test, preserve its native model first. Surface transfer alone loses much of what makes the representation coherent.

### 2.7 Scan-derived libraries and artist-authored foundations

3D Scan Store's morphable male product supplies skinny, athletic/average and overweight shapes on shared topology with ZBrush layers and exported meshes. Some related products also supply Blender shape-key scenes. This is a concrete source of corresponding reference-grounded geometry. It does not prove independent high-muscle/high-fat forms, invariant facial identity or continuous age behavior. [S33](https://www.3dscanstore.com/base-mesh-3d-models-and-textures/morphable-male-base-mesh)

**Native adoption:** retain the library's actual correspondence and inspect its existing blends first. Add a rig suited to the approved family. **Transfer:** justified where a tested target rig or face ecosystem provides greater value than the fitting burden.

An artist-created family is an option when native controls and appropriate existing shapes still fall short. If targeted specialist work is preferable to changing the source, commission coherent endpoints and problematic combinations on one topology, with references and explicit ownership/delivery terms. A purchased base mesh or scan can accelerate this only within its license. An independent sculpt based on anatomical observation and a derived scan-based asset are different provenance cases.

Scan Store's standard terms distinguish internal R&D from commercial projects and restrict exportable character makers, derivative asset collections and open-source release. Obtain specific permission before building the planned editable library from these products. A game-use license alone does not settle the case. [S34](https://www.3dscanstore.com/terms-and-conditions-licensing)

Human Generator is another plausible Blender authoring ecosystem, with APIs for body, face, age, clothes and export. Its proprietary asset rules and the unproven full trait range make it a secondary candidate. Native use preserves more value than stripping it down to a shape donor. [S35](https://humgen3d.com/docs/api), [S36](https://humgen3d.com/docs/faq/license-faq)

### 2.8 Measurement datasets and muscle simulation

ANSUR II's public release contains measurements and demographics; its scans are not public. CAESAR genuinely contains scans, landmarks and measurements, and HumanShape now offers commercially licensable template-fitted variants. Neither is already a complete muscle/fat/definition system. [S37](https://ph.health.mil/topics/workplacehealth/ergo/Pages/Anthropometric-Database.aspx), [S38](https://humanshape.org/CAESAR/)

**Why measurements cannot supply the missing anatomy:** the same waist circumference can surround a wide/shallow or narrow/deep cross-section. The same height and mass can belong to different skeletal frames and tissue distributions. A measurement fit therefore needs an anatomical prior and visual review. It cannot derive muscle boundaries from a circumference table.

Muscle/tissue systems address moving deformation after the anatomy is supplied. Houdini's transfer instructions explicitly provide a way to remove fat-related bulges from the transfer proxy, so regions such as a fuller belly or seat are not incorrectly filled with muscle. This is a treatment for relevant regions, not a universal deflation requirement. Ziva's discontinuation makes older demonstrations a poor basis for selecting a currently purchasable tool. [S39](https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/muscles/muscletransfer.html), [S40](https://unity.com/blog/news/update-about-ziva)

**Verdict:** use measurements for bounds and sanity checks, scans for surfaces, and tissue tools for selected later pose problems. Do not start by building a population model or tissue simulator.

## 3. Rights, costs and maintainability

### Treat each permission separately

A usable foundation must permit the actual operation: viewing references; fitting to geometry; deriving target shapes; using AI tools with the input; distributing meshes and morphs to a browser; and any future character export, sharing or asset-library reuse. Permission for an ordinary compiled game is not automatically permission for all of these.

Topology changes and normal-map baking do not automatically remove donor rights. Software, code, model weights, data, exported assets and bundled animations may have different licenses. Keep original licenses with the exact selected release and resolve material ambiguity before production use.

Epic's current licensing permits MetaHumans outside Unreal. Its October 30, 2026 EULA update removed the former UE/MetaHuman AI-input/training restrictions. Existing agreements and separately licensed assets still need checking; the change does not make every asset CC0 or authorize redistribution of proprietary authoring technology. [S41](https://www.metahuman.com/license), [S42](https://www.unrealengine.com/eula-change-log/unreal)

Meshy's current terms distinguish Customer Output from supplied Service Assets. Paid-plan customers own their outputs to the extent permitted by law; free-plan outputs are provided under CC BY 4.0 with attribution to Meshy. Supplied rigs, animations and other Service Assets have an incorporated-use license rather than the same ownership grant. The input artwork must also be licensed for the intended use. Record the actual plan and generation provenance for each chosen source. [S79](https://www.meshy.ai/terms-of-use)

A proposed asset foundation must fit the default sourcing rule or an applicable scoped permission. Existing SAM/MHR permissions and the [MetaHuman proof’s private-evaluation permission](../../../../maintainers/art/metahuman-poc.md) remain valid within their scope; public browser delivery and a redistributable customization kit remain open adoption questions. Evaluating alternatives does not expand those permissions. Any commissioned work should specify the desired game, runtime morph, modification, contributor and asset-sharing rights in the contract.

### Planning labor, explicitly estimated

If paid assistance is selected after native controls and appropriate existing assets reveal a material gap, one hypothetical scenario is **4-8 technical-artist hours for preparation and native sampling, then 8-24 artist hours and 4-12 technical-artist hours for the first identity pair and browser witness**. These are planning assumptions for usable inputs and existing export machinery, not benchmarks or a minimum work package. Reuse valid earlier work and estimate only the remaining gap.

At an illustrative rate of USD 60-120 per skilled hour, that hypothetical 16-44-hour paid-assistance scenario costs roughly USD 960-5,280 before licenses and revisions. The rate is an assumption, not a surveyed market rate. This is not a baseline budget, admission requirement or spending authorization. If paid help is chosen, bound it by the observed gap, deliverables and a cap; replace these estimates with actual effort before expanding.

The broader approved adult range, corrections, feminine foundation and age extension are separate authoring work. A custom fantasy outfit can require substantial topology, hidden-surface, materials, weight and deformation work even when the generated image or mesh is attractive. No defensible fixed price for the full wardrobe or age system can be derived from the accessible evidence.

Evaluate **cost per accepted reusable family**, including rework and rights, rather than subscription cost or number of generated models. CC's unknown Enterprise quote may be economically good; a free model that demands weeks of reconstruction may not be.

## 4. The recommended end-to-end workflow

The following is a proposed production procedure synthesized from the evidence. It is deliberately bounded and reuses OpenLegend's current recipe machinery.

### Step 1: make the reference package coherent

Retain the approved clothed Peacock concept, the separate body study and the bald clean-shaven head sheet as distinct authorities. Label which image governs face identity, underlying build, hair and costume. Armor width is not body width.

Add front, side, back and three-quarter anatomy references where needed. Generated views can help articulate an intended design, but are not guaranteed consistent measurements of one person. Reconcile contradictions in pose, perspective and proportions before fitting. Record uncertain concealed areas as artistic reconstruction.

The package should define stature, body frame, silhouette and identity landmarks, plus a small number of relevant lean, fuller, low-muscle and muscular references. It should show tissue transitions and posterior/side views, not only bodybuilder front views. References are not automatically licensed geometry inputs.

### Step 2: inspect native anatomy before custom fitting

In the leading foundation, create a small native grid: low/high muscularity crossed with low/high fullness, at one fixed stature and intended frame. Use native controls and their documented interactions, then render neutral clay. Do not immediately add corrective sculpts to every bad output.

This answers whether the tool supplies the missing anatomical knowledge. If all extremes need major reconstruction, its rig may remain useful, but its shape generator has failed the main economic test.

Keep the native topology and skeleton for this comparison. Record exact control values, constraint/pin state and tool/model version. The user-facing 0-100 values are a calibrated recipe range; they are not the provider's raw coefficients or medical measurements.

### Step 3: create one art-approved identity

Use native face and body controls first. If they cannot reach the Peacock, selectively conform a cleaned generated/scanned source or sculpt the missing structure. Preserve distinctive face geometry and the lean body's major muscle forms. Inspect eyes, eyelids, mouth, ears, hands, feet and head-to-body scale separately.

Give the person doing the fitting the original reference and the best generated source, not just the latest fitted mesh. Otherwise each repair inherits earlier information loss. Compare both a native-shaped identity and a source-fitted identity when the difference could determine the foundation.

Where sources are defective, rebuild those regions from sound anatomy. Do not transfer fused shorts, armor bulges or painted facial shadows as flesh. Preserve a body approved independently of its clothes and textures.

### Step 4: obtain convincing variation from the native source

Vary existing anatomical controls on the approved identity. Compare this result with the same changes on the untouched native base. The difference reveals whether the identity sculpt, fitting residual or constraint choices introduce the failure.

Use existing native shapes wherever they pass. If a fuller arm, high-muscle/full torso, elderly neck or another combination fails, first check native controls and appropriate existing alternatives. Commission a targeted correction on the shared topology only for a remaining material gap when that is preferable to changing the source. The target is a coherent whole person; fixing a local region requires rechecking its neighboring forms.

Do not add the same world-space identity displacement blindly to every physique. Reapply or revise identity details per approved support shape as necessary. This trial establishes whether specialist sculpting is needed; native controls may supply the required result, but their coverage must be judged rather than assumed.

### Step 5: prove export immediately

After the first accepted lean/fuller pair, export both and the necessary rig data. Have OpenLegend evaluate the pair, sweep intermediate values, and compare its result against a held-out native midpoint. A separately exported midpoint is a reference for this test, not a substitute for demonstrating runtime interpolation. If an approved sampled basis is necessary to reproduce the interval, document that change and test further held-out values. Reproduce a few poses before polishing a larger library.

glTF supports morph deltas, joint hierarchies and skinning. It does not standardize the Creator body solver, arbitrary garment-fitting graphs or DCC pose drivers. Export those effects as supported data or implement only the small required runtime behavior. An FBX/DNA/GLB file's existence is not proof of equivalent deformation. [S47](https://registry.khronos.org/glTF/specs/2.0/glTF-2.0.html)

The initial runtime should evaluate approved recipes using OpenLegend's existing mechanisms. Do not introduce a full neural body model or new tissue simulation merely to make the first proof run.

### Step 6: complete the bounded adult family

Choose support shapes at failures and meaningful control combinations. Author a correction only when it fixes a demonstrated gap. Inspect intermediate sweeps and held-out combinations not used to build the targets.

Keep body geometry, joint/rest data, useful pose corrections, texture channels and garment fits coordinated. Named people such as Peacock, runner and lifter remain editable recipe values within the family.

### Step 7: fit representative clothing and motion

Use a suitable native compatible garment as the first control. Reuse valid earlier fit evidence; adapt or retest the old experimental shirt only where it resolves a migration or fit question. Inspect complete anatomy and an unmasked body/garment diagnostic separately, then qualify the intended package with legitimate coverage masks. Check idle, walk, shoulder lift, elbow bend and a crouch/reach before broad wardrobe work.

Adapt the selected art style at the material stage: calm color groups, coherent roughness and appropriate detail density. Do not rely on dramatic lighting, pores or dark painted muscle grooves to rescue weak geometry.

### Step 8: extend foundations and age only after the adult proof

Treat the feminine foundation as its own anatomical art task, even if it shares topology and rig conventions. Establish its native controls and interactions independently. Reusing machinery is valuable; pretending one set of masculine displacement vectors is a complete feminine body is not.

Then evaluate older athletic and older fuller endpoints while retaining identity and the comparison's fixed stature. Inspect apparent age around the whole person, including torso, limbs, hands, neck and face. Existing age morphs and scans are inputs, not qualification.

Scans of different younger and older people are cross-sectional references, not evidence of how the same identity ages. Qualify identity retention and the whole-person appearance independently.

The age midpoint must receive its own art review. Linear interpolation between two acceptable endpoints can still be implausible. If a coherent middle cannot be obtained from the native system, author an intermediate support shape. Posture, physical condition, hair greying and wrinkles should remain distinguishable from the chosen apparent-age control.

## 5. Designing the controls without inventing the anatomy

### First ask what the existing system already supplies

The desired interface can be simpler than the internal model. A Muscle slider may map to several native parameters; Fullness may use a family-specific combination of global and regional shapes; Definition may use both geometry and materials. This is acceptable if the visible result has the intended meaning.

Before adding anything, list each intended behavior against the native controls and inspect it. Classify it as supplied, supplied with coupling, supplied only at some builds, or missing. Test whether native parameter mapping and constraints give acceptable behavior first. Add geometry only for missing behavior, failed intervals, or unwanted coupling that cannot be resolved through the native controls.

| Intended control | Preserve during the first comparison | Desired change | Common false solution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Muscle mass | Identity, selected frame, height, fullness | Muscle-group volumes with connected insertions and transitions | Inflating the whole limb or ribcage |
| Fullness | Identity, intended frame, muscle selection | Plausible family-specific tissue distribution and changed visibility of underlying forms | Uniform outward offset or muscle-shaped arm enlargement |
| Definition | Mass and fullness to the degree visually intended | Separation and relief visible through the selected tissue coverage | Blurring all vertices, or painting permanent abdominal grooves |
| Proportions/frame | Identity and explicit independent controls | Coherent limb lengths, ribcage/pelvis relations and joint locations | Scaling skin without updating the rest skeleton |
| Apparent age | Identity, comparison stature and selected athletic build | A coherent older or younger person | Automatic stoop, muscle removal or a wrinkle-only shader |

### Definition needs conditional behavior

At high fullness, maximum Definition should mean the clearest plausible structure for that physique, not the same exposed abdominal grooves as the lean endpoint. Conversely, reducing Definition should not erase knees, shoulder blades or underlying skeletal structure.

Test available native detail controls and suitable existing morphs first. For a remaining material gap, obtain low/high-definition versions of the same approved build through targeted specialist work only when that is preferable to changing the source. Use true geometry for forms affecting silhouette, volume or garment clearance; use normal/material detail for sufficiently fine surface changes.

Do not invent a universal “definition attenuation versus fullness” formula as a substitute for those references. A simple formula may eventually interpolate approved data, but the approved shapes establish its behavior.

### When simple blending works, and when it does not

Small, localized changes with aligned anatomical features can often add acceptably. Large body-composition changes and coupled extremes need direct examples or corrections. A high-muscle/high-fullness body should be sampled from the native model or independently approved; it should not be assumed equal to adding a muscle extreme and a fat extreme.

A small piecewise blend over approved support shapes is a sensible first representation. Add a mid-shape or combination correction only where the interval fails. More sophisticated radial-basis or learned interpolation is justified only if the simpler representation cannot reproduce the approved range efficiently.

Same vertex count is insufficient: a vertex near a pectoral edge should not drift into an abdominal region across samples. Maintain anatomical landmark/boundary alignment and one intended frame/identity. Avoid extrapolating beyond the qualified range.

**Art acceptance:** Mike or his delegate reviews the reference and difficult endpoints. Some native samples may need no manual change. Definition pairs, combined extremes, elderly bodies and face/body transitions may expose gaps that justify targeted specialist work; paid review or sculpting is not a prerequisite. There is no evidence that a fixed handful of handmade shapes covers every body foundation and age. The proposed eight-state proof below is a diagnostic set, not that promise.

## 6. When generated-geometry transfer is competitive

Transfer is preferable when a generated or sculpted source captures important art qualities that a native generator cannot reach economically, and a chosen template supplies useful animation or correspondence. It is less attractive when a native generator already reaches the target and supplies a good rig and wardrobe.

Meshy's current API supports multiple reference images, remeshing and rigging, but the reviewed interfaces do not document a shared muscle/fat/definition shape family. Its rigging API has specific subject/geometry requirements; successful rigging of one generated human does not prove reusable character-system suitability. [S48](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/multi-image-to-3d), [S49](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/rigging)

Other generators can be compared as source producers. Rodin Gen-2.5 documents pose conditioning and topology/detail options; CC5.2 integrates several generation providers. These features may improve the initial mesh, but clean quad topology alone does not establish shared anatomical correspondence across independently generated bodies. [S50](https://docs.hyper3d.ai/en/api-specification/rodin-gen2-5), [S08](https://discussions.reallusion.com/t/character-creator-5-2-release-note-ai-3d-generator-cc-wrap-enhanced-outfit-workflows-and-bug-fixes/18782)

### Use different tools for different jobs

| Method | Useful contribution | Important limit |
|---|---|---|
| Landmark-assisted non-rigid Wrap fitting | Retains a chosen template while fitting broad shape; supports excluded regions [S51](https://docs.faceform.com/Wrap/Nodes/Wrapping/Wrapping.html) | Must start from suitable anatomy and rough pose/alignment |
| Anatomical curves and landmarks | Align lips, muscle borders and joint regions, not just overall outline [S52](https://docs.faceform.com/Wrap/Nodes/SelectPointsWithSplines/SelectPointsWithSplines.html) | Meaningful anatomical boundaries require visual review |
| Wrap StickToSurface | Transfers an existing source deformation family to fitted recipient topology [S53](https://docs.faceform.com/Wrap/Nodes/StickToSurface/StickToSurface.html) | Wrong triangle correspondence, intersections or absent sockets can corrupt results |
| Surface/cage deformation | Carries broad changes to garments, proxies and accessory regions [S54](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/deform/surface_deform.html) | Supplies deformation mechanics, not new anatomy or cloth ease |
| Multiresolution sculpt and projection | Recovers selected medium/fine detail after broad fitting [S55](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/generate/multiresolution.html), [S56](https://ten_24.artstation.com/blog/category/6) | Can project donor defects and needs cleanup |
| Maya attribute transfer | Transfers positions, UVs or colors where correspondence is suitable [S57](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/MayaCRE-Modeling/files/GUID-76D5F064-C5A1-4310-B72A-788AB5C9A25B.htm) | Does not transfer the complete rig, skin weights or anatomical variation model |
| Normal/displacement baking | Preserves suitable fine appearance on a lighter runtime mesh | Normals cannot recover volume, silhouette or clearance |

### A reliable transfer sequence

1. **Normalize source and template.** Match units, height and orientation. Repose to a common neutral stance with clear limbs. Do not bake arm elevation or scapular movement into a body-build target.
2. **Repair or exclude unreliable source regions.** Fused clothing, holes, distorted hands, hidden skin and broken facial interiors need reconstruction or a better source.
3. **Fit large volumes.** Match stature, frame, head/body scale and joint centers before chasing surface detail. Preserve art-approved waist/seat changes as authoritative.
4. **Align anatomical features.** Use landmarks and boundary curves at relevant muscle, joint and facial regions. Inspect front, side, rear and three-quarter clay views.
5. **Recover relief separately.** Project only trustworthy detail at appropriate subdivisions; retain editable recovery states. Do not reproject the entire original narrow-waisted donor after approving a wider waist.
6. **Repair the whole character.** Review the jaw/neck/clavicle transition, armpits, abdomen/flanks/pelvis, seat, knees and hands/feet. Seam averaging alone is not a neck construction method.
7. **Adjust the rig and weights.** Update joint/rest/bind data coherently and correct observed pose failures. Geometry transfer alone does not transfer all animation behavior.
8. **Derive LODs consistently.** Use one reduction/mapping per LOD across the family. Independent decimation of every shape can destroy correspondence; verify dimensions and major relief after reduction.

The TEN24 practitioner walkthrough is useful because it explicitly separates low-resolution wrapping, later detail projection and manual repair, retaining better template anatomy where the source is defective. That is evidence for the method, not an estimate that every character can be completed in one pass. [S56](https://ten_24.artstation.com/blog/category/6)

### Several generated bodies: useful donors, risky direct endpoints

Generating lean, muscular and fuller references can supply more convincing starting forms than expanding one lean mesh mathematically. However, the generations may change bone structure, pose, face identity or muscle location as well as the requested trait.

Fit each candidate to a shared template only after deciding which differences belong in the control. Reconcile them into one character family and inspect blends. It may be cheaper for an artist to correct a good native family than to make many independent generations semantically consistent. The proof should measure that tradeoff instead of assuming generation is cheaper.

## 7. Clothing and animation across the candidate ecosystems

### Native garments are a material advantage

MetaHuman's parametric outfit workflow associates garments with source bodies and warps toward target bodies. Epic advises additional source sizes when the variation causes distortion. CC documents conforming, layering and weight workflows. These tools can reduce production work, but their engine/editor behavior does not automatically travel with exported meshes. [S58](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/getting-started-for-creating-parametric-clothing-in-metahuman?lang=en-US), [S59](https://www.reallusion.com/character-creator/custom-outfit.html)

Start with a genuinely compatible native garment and reuse valid prior comparisons. The old experimental shirt is an optional migration or fit diagnostic, not a prerequisite. If that comparison is needed and only the old shirt fails, that is migration cost, not necessarily a bad body foundation. If both fail in the same region, inspect the body or rig before adding clearance.

For large proportions, different foundations or elderly shapes, choose a closer source size or author a correction where needed. A single neutral shirt plus one uniform outward offset is not a credible universal fitting system.

### What transfers automatically, and what remains authored

| Usually a useful automated starting point | Usually needs inspection or authored decisions |
|---|---|
| Body deformation carried through a suitable surface/cage map | Ease, collar/cuff/hem position and garment design |
| Initial skin weights copied from nearby body regions | Shoulder, elbow, groin, breast/chest and layered-clothing behavior |
| Native-source garment selection and shape sampling | Distortion at combined extremes and changed proportions |
| Baking normals and transferring appropriate UV-dependent detail | Correct bake cage, seams, texture stretch and tangent convention |
| Skeleton retarget setup from a known mapping | Rest axes, limb bends, foot/hand contact and fuller-body arm clearance |
| Exporting existing corrective shapes | Driver meaning and matching deformation in the actual renderer |

Marvelous Designer 2026 can import blend-shape avatars, which is useful for offline fitting over an approved body family. Its topology-preserving fitting option has conditions; otherwise remeshing can occur. Simulation output is not automatically a compact runtime morph system. [S60](https://support.marvelousdesigner.com/hc/en-us/articles/55654243482649-Blend-Shape-Avatar), [S61](https://support.marvelousdesigner.com/hc/en-us/articles/47358335130649-Auto-Fitting)

Pixar's garment-refitting work combines body correspondence with tightness and seam/layer constraints. Its value here is the production principle: preserve garment structure and ease while mapping bodies, rather than treating the garment as inflated skin. It does not justify building a new research cloth solver for the initial proof. [S62](https://graphics.pixar.com/library//GarmentRefit/paper.pdf)

### Generated fantasy costumes

Preserve the generated costume as a high-detail visual source where it is good. Separate soft cloth, padded layers, rigid plates, straps, buckles, hair and beard. Build missing inner/back surfaces and real garment openings. Choose low-resolution topology appropriate to movement; bake ornamental detail where suitable.

A breastplate should retain rigid or piecewise rigid construction and adjust placement/size. A cape needs its own attachment and motion treatment. Boots, gloves, helmets and layered armor introduce different fitting problems. Native ordinary garments can reduce the baseline workload but do not make an arbitrary fused fantasy costume modular.

For the first fantasy outfit, compare estimated adaptation effort and available examples for the generated costume and suitable existing/native garments before building either route. Test an alternative only where the result could change the choice; do not require two completed prototypes. Consider custom construction only for a specific unmet need within the selected scope. Richer generated texture does not establish a finished wearable.

### Shape and motion must be coherent

Backward elbows are a rig/rest/retarget problem to investigate before widening the gait. Fuller bodies may need modest shape-aware arm trajectories and pose corrections, but conspicuously wide arm swing is a failed workaround.

Changing limb proportions can move joint centers, change rest transforms and alter contact. Existing animations may remain useful as motion intent while needing retargeting and contact adjustments. Rig corrections should be added after joint orientation and binding are correct. Maya's joint and pose-editor tools illustrate the established distinction between modifying the rig and authoring a corrective deformation. [S63](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2026/ENU/Maya-CharacterAnimation/files/GUID-A65407D4-25FA-4C20-B660-C981208D4DE9.htm), [S64](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2026/ENU/Maya-CharacterAnimation/files/GUID-2AB6C4C3-75AC-4094-A65C-C232739AFB30.htm)

Hair, beard and brows should remain separate assets following their attachments and expression needs. Teeth, eyes and eyelids need their own deformation check. A full-body silhouette test can miss a broken blink or jaw.

### When the body is stable enough for wardrobe production

Require an approved topology/UV/rig contract, a bounded range of good unclothed shapes, acceptable intermediate sweeps, a representative garment and an actual browser motion witness. This need not wait for every possible future NPC or age. It must clearly state which shapes and proportions a new garment is being built to support.

## 8. Diagnosing the reported recurring failures

These are hypotheses from the supplied case study, not findings from inspected meshes.

| Reported symptom | Plausible cause class | Prevention in the recommended process |
|---|---|---|
| Rectangular torso, abdominal/back shelves | Regional deformation boundaries and unvalidated tissue distribution | Native/reference-grounded full-body shapes; inspect side/rear transitions and intermediates |
| Fuller arms look muscular | Fat and muscle conflated by radial expansion | Separate anatomical priors; compare fuller and muscular references at fixed frame |
| Pecs, abs and obliques disappear after fit | Regularization, coarse geometry, recipient blending or missing relief recovery | Save broad-fit/detail stages and compare matched clay views |
| Corrected waist becomes narrow again | Original donor reintroduced as whole-surface authority | Preserve approved broad shape; transfer only appropriate residual detail |
| Blurred torso with sharp limbs | Definition reduced by inconsistent smoothing | Conditioned, reference-grounded relief changes rather than global blur |
| Neck bulges, extreme seat or awkward back curve | Source defects, pose mismatch, poor correspondence or incompatible head/body scales | Source repair and whole-person review before fitting clothes |
| LOD changes physique or relief | Inconsistent reduction, insufficient geometry, bake/normal differences | Shared LOD mapping and comparison at each actual runtime level |
| Shirt buckles, inflates or penetrates | Faulty body, cross-region mapping, excess clearance, poor weights or fit | Separate body approval, garment ease, skinning and pose corrections |
| Backward elbow or excessive arm swing | Rest axes/bind/retarget error, then compensation for collision | Inspect skeleton first; validate natural motion and contact separately |

A useful investigation saves original source, cleaned source, broad fit, detail pass and runtime export with the same camera and pose. Compare geometry-only and textured lighting conditions. A smooth technical diagnostic is not anatomical acceptance; an attractive shadow can conceal a missing form.

The likely problem is a combination of weak shape priors, inadequately constrained fitting and insufficient visual gates. That does not establish that procedural modeling is unreliable. Validated native statistical systems and coherent authored target interpolation are precisely the procedural knowledge worth reusing.

## 9. AI-assisted production: what the evidence actually supports

### Useful demonstrated processes

| Source / status | What was documented or demonstrated | What remains unproven for this task |
|---|---|---|
| Epic Vampire tutorial, UE 5.8; experienced-artist workflow [S65](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-stylized-character-tutorial?lang=en-US) | Custom stylized mesh fitting followed by Maya geometry/rig refinement and a downloadable rigged result | Autonomous sculpting, mass/fullness/definition range, whole-body age and browser parity |
| Darko Mitev's CC5 fantasy workflow; artist-authored case study [S66](https://magazine.reallusion.com/2026/04/27/mastering-maya-characters-ultimate-character-creator-5-workflow/) | Native wrapping/sculpting, restoration of lost facial identity, facial corrections and custom costume work | A one-click output or validated continuous body family |
| TEN24 Wrap/ZBrush walkthrough; full written tutorial [S56](https://ten_24.artstation.com/blog/category/6) | Coarse fitting, selective projection, manual artifact repair and preservation of better source regions | A reusable animated shape space |
| BlenderAlchemy, ECCV 2024; paper and project [S67](https://ianhuang0630.github.io/BlenderAlchemyWeb/) | Visual-feedback editing of materials, procedural geometry, lighting and existing facial blend coefficients | Creation of anatomically excellent full-body targets from nothing |
| Blender MCP tools and production-workflow repository; documented interfaces [S68](https://www.mcp-for-blender.com/docs/tools/look), [S69](https://github.com/DiegoArias32/blender-production-mcp) | Scene inspection, Python operations, multi-view captures, animation strips and candidate review/checkpoints | Reliable expert anatomical judgment or qualification of this exact toolchain |
| TADA, 3DV 2024; research results and code [S70](https://tada.is.tuebingen.mpg.de/) | Generated animatable avatars using an existing expressive body model and learned image priors | Required commercial rights, independent body controls, modular wardrobe and exact concept fidelity |

This review inspected documentation, papers, project pages and written practitioner accounts. It did not independently run these systems or watch every linked video frame by frame. Vendor renders and author demonstrations establish narrower capabilities than a reproduced production proof.

I found useful AI-assisted modeling evidence, but no reliable published demonstration that qualifies the entire requested custom identity, three-control body range, both bases, ageing, reusable clothes and browser animation autonomously. That is an evidence gap, not a claim that the task is impossible.

BlenderWorld's current multi-view benchmark also reports persistent difficulty with fine spatial and structural reproduction. Its tasks and metrics are not human-anatomy acceptance tests; its practical relevance is the need for direct visual evidence and bounded failure handling. [S71](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23986)

### Where AI should do the work

AI is well suited to preparing consistent reference packages; querying native parameters; generating and recording controlled samples; running repeatable wrap/bake/export operations; building matched-camera contact sheets; checking topology and rig data; locating collisions; and organizing rejected versus accepted evidence.

It can propose shape corrections and identify obvious defects, while art acceptance remains with Mike or his delegate. Seek targeted specialist help for unresolved anatomical gaps when that is preferable to changing the source. Automated measurements and model-generated critique should support review of tissue transitions, identity, interactions and motion, not overrule visible failures.

An MCP connection supplies access to tools. It does not supply anatomy. A prompt becomes useful when the process also has the actual references, a good body prior, suitable sculpt/fitting tools, stored checkpoints and authority to reject its own result.

### Why the earlier AI approach likely failed

The task was represented as coordinate editing while the objective was anatomical art. Sparse numerical constraints left fat distribution and muscle relationships underdetermined. Smoothing and masks made outputs easier to compute without providing the missing shapes. Limited or inconsistent visual checks allowed local fixes to damage another view. Repeated downstream clothing fixes then rewarded hiding symptoms.

The corrective action is to improve the source data and execution process first. A longer prompt asking the same agent to choose better expansion coefficients is unlikely to change the underlying limitation.

## 10. The decisive initial proof

This is a staged proposal. The original 30-recipe matrix and later 24 age/base samples remain intended coverage, not the first work package.

### Gate A: native anatomy and feasibility

**Inputs:** approved reference package; current baseline renders when accessible; one exact MetaHuman installation; documented license/export route; a suitable target device/browser; known simple poses.

Create a stock-native four-build muscle/fullness grid at fixed stature and intended frame. Review unclothed clay, including back and side. Reuse existing export/runtime evidence for an unchanged path; confirm one exported body and basic pose can reach OpenLegend only for a new source or materially changed route. Gate B still proves the new shape-family interval in the browser. Record native parameters and exact versions.

**Pass:** the source already supplies believable connected forms and the export can carry the essential shape/rig data. **Stop:** the stock prior needs broad anatomical rebuilding, rights are incompatible, or browser equivalence would require speculative infrastructure.

This is the earliest decision boundary. If it fails, compare CC natively once evaluation rights are settled, or move to a rights-cleared artist-owned foundation. Do not commission eight polished targets first.

### Gate B: the Peacock and one fuller version

Produce two approved bodies: the Peacock reference at mass 50/fullness 0/definition 100, and the same identity at mass 50/fullness 100/definition 100. Definition 100 means the maximum plausible definition at each fullness, not identical surface grooves.

Use native body variation and appropriate existing assets, adding targeted correction only for observed material gaps. Paid specialist work is conditional on repair being preferable to changing the source. Render both endpoints and intermediate fullness values. Immediately have the browser interpolate the exported pair or approved sampled basis, sweep the interval, and compare with a held-out native midpoint. Loading three separately baked bodies does not establish continuous runtime control. Include shoulder lift, elbow bend, locomotion and a minimal face/neck check. Inspect bare geometry before clothing.

**Deliverables:** exact editable authoring sources; exported targets and rig data; control/constraint records; front/back/side/three-quarter clay comparisons; face close-ups; one turntable; one native-versus-browser motion comparison; a record of actual authoring/technical effort, any paid assistance and reasons for corrections.

**Pass:** the lean reference retains identity and anatomy; the fuller body has convincing connected tissue distribution; intermediate shapes do not acquire shelves, drifting landmarks or disconnected forms; exported motion retains correct bends and acceptable volume.

**Stop:** two directed correction cycles fail to improve the same foundational problem, obtaining the family requires rebuilding every native variation, or critical deformation remains unavailable after export. The two-cycle limit is a proposed budget discipline, not a scientific quality threshold.

### Gate C: a diagnostic set of eight states

Only after Gate B passes, inspect the following states. They are sampled outputs and targeted corrections where needed, not a requirement for eight independent hand-sculpted bodies.

| State | Mass | Fullness | Definition | Diagnostic purpose |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| A | 0 | 0 | 50 | Low muscle, lean anatomy |
| B | 100 | 0 | 50 | High muscle without added fullness |
| C | 0 | 100 | 50 | Fuller body without muscular-arm substitution |
| D | 100 | 100 | 50 | Combined extreme |
| E | 50 | 0 | 100 | Approved Peacock anchor |
| F | 50 | 0 | 0 | Definition change at reference muscle/fullness |
| G | 50 | 100 | 0 | Fuller, soft-definition counterpart |
| H | 50 | 100 | 100 | Maximum plausible definition under fullness |

Include midpoints and at least one held-out interior recipe. Use the native compatible garment first, reusing valid prior fit evidence. Compare the old shirt only for an unresolved migration or fit question. Inspect the unmasked diagnostic separately from the intended masked package and use the same short motion set. If a native system already covers an interval convincingly, use it; add support only where the evidence calls for it.

Before expanding, also inspect an unpolished low/high-Definition pair at mass 100/fullness 100. These need not become two polished targets. They test the hardest combined interaction before committing to the full matrix.

### Gate D: intended coverage and later expansion

Run the original 30 recipes: three mass settings, two fullness settings and five definition settings. Preserve all requested continuous sweeps, including interior fullness values omitted by the endpoint matrix. This qualifies a bounded adult family, not every proportion, age or body base.

Then independently approve the feminine adult base and the older endpoints/midpoint. The requested two bases by three ages by four build combinations gives 24 samples, with eight age sweeps. Keep the athletic older person athletic and the comparison stature fixed. Where age and build interact badly, add relevant native support or authored geometry; do not declare the age system done because the endpoints look old.

### Acceptance criteria

- **Art:** recognizable identity and approved silhouette, with plausible front/side/back anatomy in clay and the game's material style.
- **Controls:** each control reads as intended without unintended frame or identity changes; combined extremes and intermediates remain coherent.
- **Structure:** stable correspondence and usable topology, with no unintended source-costume imprint or severe mesh defects.
- **Motion:** correct joint bends, natural limb clearance, credible shoulders/hips and intact face/neck behavior.
- **Clothing:** complete anatomy and the unmasked fit are inspected separately; the intended package, including legitimate masks, has no exposed breakthrough, malformed openings, failed restoration, persistent visible buckling or inflated waist. Reject invented masking repairs or exaggerated gait used to conceal conversion or deformation defects.
- **Runtime:** approved geometry and motion survive export; supported creation and variation execute without authoring-provider calls.
- **Production:** time, correction count and licensing make the next character cheaper and more predictable.

Do not collapse these into one average score. A beautiful face cannot compensate for unusable cloth, and a technically clean mesh cannot compensate for visibly implausible anatomy.

## 11. Adoption and invalidation plan

### Reuse what is already valuable

Retain appearance-recipe semantics, ordinary named presets, the generic interpolation/correction machinery, the browser viewer, input handling, rendering and useful animation source clips where their actual contracts fit. Preserve approved concepts and good Meshy results as versioned references/sources.

Treat the current body targets, arbitrary field coefficients, fitted anatomy and shirt binding as experimental data. Replace poor data rather than building more compensation around it. The report does not assert that current executable implementations are correct; they were not audited in this research.

The engine should own generic recipe evaluation, shape composition and rendering mechanisms. Anatomical ranges, character identities and the bundled world's art choices belong in the selected body family/world content. Do not make one provider's slider names or one world's human morphology universal engine laws.

### What later changes invalidate

| Change | Often reusable | Recheck or rebuild |
|---|---|---|
| Color/eye appearance with same UV/material contract | Geometry, rig, bindings | Material appearance and contrast |
| Fine normal detail only | Most shape and garment data | Bake, tangents and temporal stability |
| Small same-topology sculpt | Vertex identity, UV coordinates, much skinning | Local clearance, normals, pose corrections and LOD transfer |
| Wider build range | Recipe mechanisms and basic family assets | Garment sources, contact envelope, corrections and motion |
| Changed joint centers or limb lengths | Skeleton names and source motion intent | Rest/bind transforms, retargeting, attachments and contacts |
| Changed topology or vertex order | Concepts, control semantics, source textures and clips | Morphs, skin weights, bindings, masks and LOD maps; bakes if UVs change |
| New foundation or ageing range | Tools, capture/review procedure, some compatible garments | Actual anatomy, identity, fit and shape/pose interaction |

### Adoption sequence

Approve a production method now; qualify the actual source through Gates A and B. Select the winning native foundation only after its art, export and rights pass. Then finish the adult bounded range and representative clothing, freeze the assumptions for wardrobe production, and extend age/body coverage deliberately.

A failure should change the source or method at its responsible stage. It should not trigger a new general architecture, an indefinitely growing correction library, or a return to the same unsupported inflation formulas.

## 12. Reusable instructions for execution and review

These are task briefs for a capable tool-enabled process, not claims that a prompt replaces modeling data or skill.

### Instruction A: establish the native source
