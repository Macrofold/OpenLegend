## R25 / conditional — optional human connection-study feasibility

[DG11’s product proposal](../../projects/wellbeing-evidence-feature-spec.md) asks whether a useful outing and a minimal optional measurement can work for invited adults who already know one another. The finite proposal assigns whole friend pairs to a shared offer or separate worthwhile solo offers; custom immediate-connection answers are exploratory, not a validated health endpoint. [WBE01–WBE06](../../maintainers/wellbeing-evidence.md) owns the actual-study decision, delivery and unperformed evidence.

Before any authorized collection, fix the question, exact activity/version, audience, assignment, finite cohort/window, response timing, treatment of refusals/missingness/corrections, disclosure and removal policy. Report playability, stopping freedom, burden, delivery failures, operator cost and uncertainty. Preserve pair assignment and independent privacy. A favorable mean cannot close a health-benefit question or authorize expansion. This does not replace ordinary gameplay/cost evaluation or R24/CE05 fictional-character quality, and it does not adopt the well-being archive’s whole programme.
