## Selection decisions

No full expanded economic arrangement is a universal Core prerequisite. The first rewarding adventure can use found equipment, direct rewards and existing item transfer without a closed economy. Truthful stock, authorized transfers and payment integrity are mandatory whenever commerce is selected; they are not optional merely because a particular market arrangement is Complete or later.

Ordinary buying, selling, barter, understandable prices, equipment commissions and alternative suppliers are Complete. Their purpose is useful choice and access, not simulating every distant transaction. Trade routes, contested deliveries, equipment salvage, escorts, recruitment and explicit rewards for recovering cargo or people also earn Complete: ECON-246/255/273/282/283 connect money to an actual adventure. ECON-297 makes a fair prize a reason to visit another place without making every pastime compulsory provisioning.

Small businesses, fairs, creative commissions, teaching and gifts can be substantial enjoyable activities. Peaceful trade is not demoted simply because it is not combat. Conversely, reserves (ECON-041/042), repair fees and warranties (ECON-002/085), freshness rotation (ECON-045), delivery appointments (ECON-062), shift substitution (ECON-112), shared-pack fee division (ECON-262) and recovery retainers (ECON-272) are Detail as their described administrative additions. Their Small or Moderate implementation scope remains independent of product importance. Actual basic recovery and carrying remain earlier capabilities.

ECON-163 is a proposed outfitting-budget arrangement, not evidence that inventory and bags lack a generic prepare-supplies action. Reuse visible purchasing, equipment and capacity choices before inventing another preparation workflow. Keeping a tool available during an upgrade (ECON-241) can be Complete because it avoids blocking play; manufacturing frequent tool breakdowns to justify repair administration cannot.

Deeper competition, price response, debt stories and bounded magical services follow as Depth. Dedicated industrial networks, alternative-world commerce and complex licensing or service settings are Specialist for this survival-adventure, not real-world financial recommendations or a prohibition on future worlds. Existing fictional ownership, agency, source qualifications and implementation boundaries remain preserved.
