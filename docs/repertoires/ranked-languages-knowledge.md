## Selection decisions

Useful communication, interpretable warnings, ordinary reference resolution, clear next actions and a first learnable method are Core. A full constructed language, perfect translation or omniscient party knowledge is not required. LKN225 marks a creature's **last observed** position, not a tracker that secretly knows where it is now. LKN230 makes a failed attempt understandable without revealing every solution.

Knowledge is a useful reward: an enemy's technique (LKN177), a weapon's limitation (LKN189), recoverable diagrams, a deciphering key and a method retained after a borrowed manual is returned (LKN253). Fair clues, a word-responsive door (LKN242), a bounded dead witness (LKN143/245), the exception that permits escape from a curse (LKN247), and a portal's return conditions (LKN278) are Complete when their actual supporting capabilities are selected. A difficult dependency or a magical premise does not automatically make an enjoyable discovery Specialist.

Useful guides, rival teachers, forgery investigations, warning signs, animal communication and substantial creative learning also earn Complete. LKN228 preserves surprise while still supplying essential information. LKN229's disappointing maintenance accounts must lead somewhere useful; they do not justify repeatedly replacing promised discoveries with administrative homework. Not every Complete alternative is needed in the opening game.

Repair-language interpretation (LKN004), hinge sketches (LKN049), repeated repair diagnosis (LKN098/108/199), receipts (LKN130), shift handoffs (LKN193), assigning people to observe and report (LKN194), contribution credit (LKN204) and reading-stop administration (LKN232) are Detail as their full additions. They may remain Small. Basic truthful communication, interrupted work and party interaction must still work; correctness is not a reason to prioritize every related workflow.

Nuanced registers, private meanings and richer knowledge transmission follow as Depth. Copied libraries, temporal speech, industrial habitat lessons and unfamiliar collective senses are Specialist for this survival-adventure. These are fictional selections, not prescriptions about real languages or people. Access, private knowledge, consent and accurate uncertainty remain constraints wherever relevant, irrespective of an optional example's priority. Preserve the exact unhyphenated IDs LKN001–LKN280.
