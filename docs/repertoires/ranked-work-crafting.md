## Selection decisions

Making a usable bow and ammunition (WORK-082/083), fitting actual protection (WORK-084), functional doors, walls and containers (WORK-043/044/047), and one short food-production route (WORK-001) are Core representatives. They do not require every trade, weapon variant, realistic assembly detail or occupational simulation. Missing basic melee manufacture must be supplied by the selected equipment/crafting design; a repair bench is not a substitute for making a usable weapon.

Complete includes worthwhile farming, equipment conversion, useful discoveries and learning, construction, hunting and partial field harvests. Recovering a usable pattern (WORK-245), a scarce forge choice (WORK-251), defending an equipment upgrade (WORK-285) and adapting equipment to a known opponent (WORK-286) connect work to an interesting objective or reward. Batch production (WORK-196) and replenishing a learned formula (WORK-273) can remove repetition once a real crafting activity exists; they do not justify building production administration before that activity.

Peaceful activities also earn Complete: pottery, music, portraits, costume-making, attractive commissions and a self-chosen masterpiece. A profession should let the player perform its enjoyable defining activity rather than only complete supporting errands. The full detailed occupations and social tradeoffs can then deepen that play.

The general repair bench (WORK-081), sharpening service (WORK-085), shield refacing (WORK-086), strap replacement (WORK-087), laundry (WORK-141), packing (WORK-161), custody-handoff elaboration (WORK-163), contribution reviews (WORK-185), maintenance contracts (WORK-197) and injured-specialist handoffs (WORK-250) are Detail. WORK-085 and WORK-087 already describe Small bounded operations; keep that scope rather than equating low priority with complexity. Basic item transfer, truthful results, interruption integrity and participation remain required where used. Their correctness does not elevate a new administrative profession into the opening game.

Distinctive commissions and bounded magical work remain Depth where they fit this experience. Industrial infrastructure, separate adult-service settings, unfamiliar reality laws and dedicated experiments are Specialist here. The original source descriptions, safety caveats, build gaps and fictional-world boundaries remain unchanged.
