## Selection decisions

Readable opportunities, control over commitment, useful feedback, an attainable objective, retained learning and progress, and an actual ending are Core. Resource costs and occupied hands should make choices understandable, not manufacture a second preparation interface. MEC-139 explicitly permits a defeated enemy to remain an enemy; defeating opposition does not require completing a friendship or moral-conversion simulation.

Useful loadouts, tactics, alternate approaches, discoveries, teaching, upgrades and recovery are Complete. This includes a recurring enemy with a discoverable anchor (MEC-217), shared cooldown choices (MEC-252), a vulnerability-building combination (MEC-278) and improving a favorite rather than replacing it (MEC-273). A magical or Define label does not automatically make a concrete enjoyable mechanic late. Select representative interactions, not every modifier or combo before the first fight.

MEC-243 is Complete only as an explicitly selected checkpoint challenge that restores required props while preserving reward accounting. It is not permission to reset unrelated saves or bypass the repository's current save policy. MEC-272 separately allows a selected failure-safety option without taking decisions away from the player. Neither proposal installs itself or changes current runtime behavior.

Protected stock (MEC-022), promised-work reservations (MEC-121), successor handoffs (MEC-124), maintenance credit (MEC-108), detailed lifetime wear (MEC-214) and restoring a reduced reservoir ceiling (MEC-281) are Detail. Their bounded form may be Small. Usable recovery, item transfers and truthful outcomes are still required earlier; a new administrative mechanic is not required merely because those operations need to be correct.

Industrial flow elaboration, nuanced relationships, causal composition and creator tooling follow where they add worthwhile Depth. Alternative constitutions, cross-world imports and special collection/reset modes are Specialist. **MEC-197's compatibility proposal remains blocked by the [root development policy](../../AGENTS.md#development-save-policy); its inclusion is not authorization to preserve legacy code or saves.** The source is a proposal library, not an override of accepted contracts.

Peaceful creation and performance retain real value: appearance can be the reward, calm can be a successful outcome, and players may perform a solved routine personally for pleasure. The priority change removes compulsory busyness, not those activities. Privacy, authority, independent participation, cancellation and accounting remain requirements whenever a selected feature relies on them, regardless of the score of a particular optional elaboration.
