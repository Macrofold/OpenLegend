## Selection decisions

Hunting-bow, spear, shield and defensive-footwork learning (AP-049–AP-052), a first-aid method (AP-070), attainable advancement (AP-197), retained competence (AP-200) and a usable manual-backed recipe (AP-226) are Core. A real first progression loop should grant or improve a useful method and provide another challenge where it matters.

Coherent knight, scout, medic, artisan, duelist, investigator and troubadour paths are Complete choices, not prerequisites for a basic attack, recovery or crafting action. Reversible loadout rehearsal (AP-203), mastery that removes solved repetition (AP-205), material substitutions, cooperative moves, disarming and meaningful cast-time or defense/offense tradeoffs rank above maintenance detail. Playing music, games, painting and learning other substantial creative methods can also be Complete without combat bonuses.

Kitchen precision, load-packing judgment, darning, household scheduling, tool-edge judgment (AP-086), emergency reporting (AP-071), incident handover (AP-080) and focus upkeep (AP-168) are Detail. The minimum edge-judgment version can be Small when an observable condition already exists; missing condition support is a separate dependency, not a reason to build elaborate wear simulation. These are not foundations simply because the original catalogue called them Basics.

Later social fluency, adaptive practice, specialized teaching and detailed careers remain Depth. No refusal, boundary or control right depends on a character having learned a social skill. Other bodies, dream curricula, transferred skills and new magical schools remain Specialist; Level D is not itself the reason for a low gameplay priority. The ranking does not adopt any particular class system, spell tradition, biological law or skill-decay rule as current runtime behavior.
