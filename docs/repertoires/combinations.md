# Sixteen situations to build from the library

[Library](README.md) · [Labels](README.md#reading-the-labels) · [Selection and scale](selection-and-scale.md)

**Original, untested proposal scenes.** These combine specific cards into playable predicaments. The listed methods are examples, not exhaustive solutions or outcomes the narrator may guarantee. Participants act with their own knowledge and priorities. Implementation estimates cover a bounded scene, not a whole world. Stop when the scene's meaningful outcome is reached; do not generate endless emergencies to keep it running.

## The wedding bridge

**MD FA · Play · High · 2/Extend.** Gap: temporary supports, cooperative work and commitments.

A bridge fails before a wedding joining two river communities. The host has promised both safety and a ceremony at the ancestral orchard. Move the feast, build a limited crossing, arrange boats or split the ceremony between banks. A rival carpenter offers help but wants credit for an earlier unrecognized repair. The player can satisfy the practical need without settling every grievance. A failed crossing attempt should expose load or anchoring problems before anyone is forced into danger. The successful workaround may become next year's tradition.

Combine [expandable shelter](settlements-architecture.md#the-expandable-first-shelter), [shared credit](work-crafting.md#work-worth-sharing-credit-for), [promises](mechanics.md#promises-that-occupy-tomorrow) and [festival cloth](objects.md#festival-cloth).

## Soup before the summit

**MD SC FA · Play · High · 2/Extend.** Gap: food preparation, shared loads and return plans.

An expedition needs a warm meal at a cold lookout, but a companion's knee limits the load they can carry. Use warmstones, build a sheltered stove, change the menu, recruit a porter or choose a nearer view. The companion can plan the route and refuses to be treated as cargo. Wind may spoil one method while leaving others viable. Success is a good shared outing; no rare loot is needed. On returning, the group can teach the preparation as a reusable routine.

Combine [warmstone](materials-resources.md#warmstone), [accommodation](bodies-species.md#accommodation-as-invention), [way back](mechanics.md#leave-a-way-back) and [particular cooking](arts-leisure.md#cooking-for-a-particular-person).

## The wolf at the school gate

**MD FA · Blend · Try · 2/Extend.** Gap: animal evidence, ecological goals and bounded remedies.

A wolf keeps approaching a school garden. Tracks suggest a blocked route to water, while a frightened parent suspects deliberate attraction by a neighbor. Observe from safety, move attractants, reopen a path or relocate school activities. Hunting remains a world-selected option, with its own costs. The evidence may support several hypotheses until tested. A good outcome changes the layout and restores confidence without requiring everyone to agree about wildlife forever.

Combine [wildlife elsewhere](ecology-weather.md#wildlife-with-an-elsewhere), [useful weed](ecology-weather.md#a-useful-weed), [rumor routes](languages-knowledge.md#rumor-with-a-route) and [useful error](adventure-discovery.md#the-mystery-that-permits-useful-error).

## The harvest that tastes wrong

**MD MO FA · Play · High · 2/Extend.** Gap: ingredient properties, experiments and contextual preferences.

A hardy grain survives a wet year but makes the traditional festival bread dense. A miller wants to blend it, a cook wants a new dish and an elder wants to preserve the old recipe. Try small batches, borrow grain, adapt the oven or celebrate both dishes. The first bad loaf can feed animals if the world's food rules allow it. The meaningful result is a community finding a new practice, not proving tradition or innovation universally superior.

Combine [second-season seed](materials-resources.md#second-season-seed), [prototype club](work-crafting.md#the-prototype-club), [inherited method](work-crafting.md#an-inherited-method-questioned) and [amendable calendar](faith-ritual.md#a-calendar-people-can-amend).

## The midnight rehearsal

**MO · Play · High · 2/Extend.** Gap: schedules, local noise and negotiated shared space.

A band finally has all its members free at midnight, when a neighbor needs sleep before a hospital shift. Use a different room, change instruments, add insulation, rehearse quietly or negotiate a daytime shift swap. The neighbor also loves their music and wants to attend. A failed soundproofing attempt gives usable evidence about leakage. Once solved, the agreement should persist; repeat play comes from the music and relationships, not an endlessly reset noise complaint.

Combine [quiet hours](materials-resources.md#quiet-hours), [household agreements](relationships.md#household-agreements), [neighborhood sound](arts-leisure.md#the-neighborhood-sound) and [diagnostic work](work-crafting.md#the-diagnostic-profession).

## The library's hottest day

**MO SC · Play · High · 3/New.** Gap: coarse heat, shared services and accessible relocation.

A heatwave threatens a library's public cooling room just as its energy contract expires. Repair ventilation, borrow a portable unit, move activities or negotiate with a nearby workshop whose power schedule has slack. A rare manuscript needs special care, but people need shelter now. The librarian can choose to postpone an exhibit. Resolve one district-scale resource problem with clear limits; do not require a city-wide power-grid simulator to let players make a humane practical decision.

Combine [heat loom](technology.md#the-district-heat-loom), [two publics](institutions-politics.md#the-institution-with-two-publics), [dignified threshold](settlements-architecture.md#a-dignified-threshold) and [repair budget](institutions-politics.md#the-council-has-a-repair-budget).

## The missing parcel club

**MO · Play · High · 2/Extend.** Gap: parcel identity, scoped testimony and delivery histories.

Several packages disappear from a building lobby. Residents propose a locked cabinet, a shared receiving rota or accusing the newest tenant. Investigating may reveal mislabeling, an overhelpful neighbor or theft, depending on the authored starting facts. Players can improve delivery even before identifying a culprit. A wrong suspicion has a repair path through public correction. Neither a security camera nor a model may invent evidence from an unobserved interval.

Combine [empty box](objects.md#the-honest-empty-box), [handoff improvement](institutions-politics.md#bureaucracy-that-can-improve), [local reputation](relationships.md#reputation-has-an-address) and [traveling correction](languages-knowledge.md#a-public-correction-that-travels).

## A good job becomes smaller

**MO · Play · High · 2/Extend.** Gap: learned routines, changing service demand and career goals.

A tutor succeeds so well that their adult learners no longer need basic lessons. The group proposes a budgeting club, the tutor considers instrument making and one student offers to teach them. Continue the service, train a successor or celebrate its completion. The income change matters but need not trigger eviction. A useful system permits successful work to end and relationships to continue, instead of quietly making students forget so the quest can repeat.

Combine [profession changing clients](work-crafting.md#a-profession-that-changes-its-clients), [late beginner](characters-backstories.md#the-late-beginner), [mastery](needs.md#mastery-with-a-stopping-point) and [holiday](arts-leisure.md#a-holiday-after-completion).

## The alien welcome meal

**SC · Play · High · 3/New.** Gap: environmental compatibility, translation and safe shared interfaces.

Two delegations cannot breathe the same air or eat the same food. A chef, engineer and translator design adjacent dining rooms with a shared visual table, compatible scents or synchronized music. One guest prefers a simple conversation to ceremonial complexity. The challenge is creating a pleasant meeting, not forcing biological sameness. A prototype can fail through condensation or poor sound while preserving safety and offering another method.

Combine [different air](bodies-species.md#a-guest-with-different-air), [translation](languages-knowledge.md#translation-leaves-a-question), [neutral meal](diplomacy-conflict.md#a-neutral-meal-with-real-stakes) and [commission](work-crafting.md#the-commission-with-room-for-taste).

## The grounded rescue ship

**SC · Play · High · 3/New.** Gap: inhabited interiors, station roles and discrete external-action resolution.

A ship waiting at a surface port receives a distress call from another vessel on an abstract route. Crew can prepare medical space, negotiate a rendezvous, coordinate a remote tow service or support the other crew through communications. An optional encounter phase asks people to manage shields and damaged systems inside the ship. There are no flight controls or spatial pursuit. Success is arrival and rescue through bounded route-state changes, followed by care on the ground.

Combine [route room](technology.md#the-route-room), [crew stations](technology.md#external-action-crew-stations), [get everyone home](combat-rescue.md#win-by-getting-everyone-home) and [debrief](combat-rescue.md#the-debrief-at-the-kitchen-table).

## The colony's unwanted miracle

**SC · Blend · Try · 3/New.** Gap: living material growth, ventilation and bounded shared decisions.

Living sealant repairs a settlement's leaks so effectively that it begins closing vents used by another resident species. Prune it, change feeding, install separate passages or stop using it in shared spaces. The inventor is proud and initially defensive; the affected residents know a useful growth cue. A small test can reveal a safe arrangement. Innovation remains exciting while benefits and burdens become explicit rather than treated as unfortunate flavor text.

Combine [living sealant](materials-resources.md#living-sealant), [sensory refuge](needs.md#sensory-refuge), [useful refusal](mechanics.md#a-useful-refusal) and [symbiotic neighbors](bodies-species.md#symbiotic-neighbors).

## The mineral nobody needs more of

**SC · Play · Try · 3/New.** Gap: gate calibration, repairable components and local procurement.

A mining settlement depends on wayseed exports, but better repair techniques reduce demand. Its people can become calibrators, reclaim old components, develop another industry or oppose the change. The player helps explore futures rather than restarting resource depletion to preserve the old loop. Ground expeditions find technical knowledge and trade partners. Route travel remains abstract, and neither the mineral nor the engine is obliged to support a newly described hyperdrive mechanic.

Combine [wayseed](materials-resources.md#wayseed-lattice), [phase kiln](technology.md#phase-kiln), [substitute supplier](economy-logistics.md#the-substitute-supplier) and [continuity after change](needs.md#continuity-after-change).

## The dragon and the laundry

**FA · Play · Try · 3/New.** Gap: one bounded weather-transfer rule and negotiated schedules.

A dragon's fog-clearing bargain makes the orchard flourish but leaves the town's laundry perpetually damp. A washer proposes a rotating schedule, a mage suggests a warmstone drying room and the dragon wants a quiet nesting ledge. A modest domestic complaint becomes diplomacy among unequal capabilities. Everyone can benefit without producing free heat or forcing the dragon into servitude. The agreement should settle into routine after the interesting decision.

Combine [weather debt](magic.md#a-dragons-weather-debt), [borrowed warmth](magic.md#borrowed-warmth), [small promise](diplomacy-conflict.md#the-credible-small-promise) and [retired solution](mechanics.md#retire-a-successful-solution).

## The borrowed doorway

**FA · Play · High · 3/New.** Gap: temporary geometry, evidence and occupancy-safe expiry.

An echo carpenter reveals an old doorway between a public archive and someone's present kitchen. It offers access to a blocked collection but violates the resident's privacy. Negotiate hours, build a new route, move the collection or abandon the shortcut. The echo's fading edge gives a deadline for one visit, not a requirement to force entry. Historical access does not override current inhabitants or grant ownership of their home.

Combine [echo carpentry](magic.md#echo-carpentry), [privacy](needs.md#privacy-with-a-door), [ruin tenants](settlements-architecture.md#ruins-with-present-tenants) and [public archive](institutions-politics.md#the-public-archive-under-pressure).

## The werewolf's first commission

**FA · Play · High · 3/New.** Gap: transformation, compatible carrying and contextual client requirements.

A night-form courier accepts a fragile pottery delivery. They can design a pack, travel in human form, recruit a partner or stage supplies along the route. The potter's worry is breakage, not species, while a bystander invents a frightening rumor. Delivering well creates professional recognition without proving the courier represents everyone like them. A broken pot can be repaired or compensated; the story need not end in public panic.

Combine [two forms](bodies-species.md#two-forms-one-social-life), [commission](work-crafting.md#the-commission-with-room-for-taste), [borrower's roll](objects.md#borrowers-tool-roll) and [repair without erasure](relationships.md#repair-without-erasure).

## The spell everyone stops casting

**FA · Play · High · 3/New.** Gap: finite charm composition and transition to dependable infrastructure.

Apprentices spend mornings keeping market cloth rigid against rain. One invents a wet-triggered charm with a safe release, freeing them for lessons and play. Teachers disagree over losing a traditional exercise; shopkeepers worry about failure. Test, inspect, adopt selectively or keep manual alternatives. A good outcome removes repetitive labor and creates a new problem worth choosing, rather than inventing another compulsory spellcasting chore.

Combine [useful charms](magic.md#the-grammar-of-useful-charms), [deliberate limits](magic.md#the-school-of-deliberate-limits), [apprenticeship](relationships.md#an-apprenticeship-with-an-exit) and [rehearsal room](automation-creators.md#the-rehearsal-room).
