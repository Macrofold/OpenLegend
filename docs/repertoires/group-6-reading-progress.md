**G97 — World of Warcraft:** complete through S50 and final review; S45 intentionally unused. Housing, collecting, making and a modest completed session are purposes rather than lesser stages before raiding. A room blueprint shares an arrangement, not missing decor or an unlimited placement budget. Warbands connect one person's characters; cross-faction grouping connects different players; neither erases each activity's restrictions. A set interaction, an appearance and an equipment upgrade provide different value. Great Vault activities create additional choices, not ownership of every displayed item. Seasons can change numerical standing without deleting homes, friendships or memories of places. Newly useful decor can give an old location another purpose; it must not become mandatory attendance for unrelated play. Preserve retail/Classic/Hardcore and announced Forever boundaries, current Concentration rather than old Inspiration, current flight charges rather than old Vigor, and PTR rather than live Labyrinths. Official guide gaps, migrated review dates, self-selected testimony, limited endgame assessment and historical subscribers remain qualified.
