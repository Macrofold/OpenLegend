**G131 — Rust:** private stores, public infrastructure, shared performances and recurring strangers create different kinds of collective life. Fishing can support a livelihood; poker spends the same material wanted for practical progress; music and painting need not improve power. A base's evolving geometry can show remembered fear or a past attack, but identical efficient geometry is not proof of identical histories. Actual trust rests in participants' conduct, not the truth of a spoken friendly greeting. Map destruction and recipe-memory resets differ; predictable temporary ownership can suit one world without justifying automatic loss in OpenLegend's durable homes. Keep PC/console/mobile, stable/staging/roadmap, vanilla/community/Softcore/Hardcore and item-specific paid permissions distinct. The audit's added workbench upgrades, island-wide Power Trip services and leisure systems are included. Harassment is not a successful social mechanic, and solo disadvantage/time pressure are retained criticisms, not assumed evidence every loss creates attachment. No SC piloting.

**G132 — DayZ:** signs, skyline and known routes create practical geographical literacy without a leveled navigation skill. Small shared fishing or bandaging episodes can acquire meaning, while an empty walk need not secretly be tense or worthwhile. A camp can outlive its inhabitants; a maintained flag refreshes nearby object lifetime rather than establishing invulnerable sovereign territory. Familiarity with an item does not supply compatible parts or permission to take it. The new survivor's learned player knowledge differs from inherited character attributes; other players' recollections are not a universal reputation ledger. Preserve mod/standalone, official/community, map-specific conditions, historical/current audio, stable 1.29 versus experimental 1.30 and future Badlands terrain despite existing teaser assets. Bodily diagnostic examples are game rules, not real medical instructions. Dynamic player accounts do not measure cheating prevalence or the share of sessions ending in a memorable story.
