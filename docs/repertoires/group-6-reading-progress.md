## Latest source-specific cautions

All earlier detailed notes remain in the immutable checkpoints above. Preserve their version/access limits and distinctions between attributed accounts, constructed examples and new synthesis.

**G63 — Fates:** complete through FA31, including the broader DLC and surviving-local-communication corrections. Birthright, Conquest and Revelation are materially different campaigns; branch choice changes people, opportunities and resource assumptions, not merely an ending. Knowing a friend or spouse opens another discipline, but the seal abstracts rather than depicts teaching. Offspring/Deeprealm rules should not become a universal excuse for accelerated family life. A+ friendship and the two same-gender S-support exceptions have distinct outcomes; an unavailable child does not invalidate a relationship. Faction identity crosses buildings, tools, resources and musical motifs. Attack and Guard stances deliberately separate forms of cooperation. Capture is not proof of free consent or full social parity. Ordinary Conquest scarcity differs from historically available paid grinding; Heirs of Fate uses scenario-specific children. Offline home, local StreetPass, retired Internet visits, redownload rights and new-purchase closure differ. Preserve all three routes' conflicting reception rather than calling one audience correct by default.

**G64 — Three Houses:** complete through TH24, including restored core combat/equipment/loss details. Aptitude, competence, motivation, interest, eligibility and a teacher's authority differ; a student is not a build owned by the instructor. Former students' changed appearance, role and allegiance give elapsed history emotional meaning. Route-local political knowledge should not become an omniscient master summary. There are four main routes, not three; Cindered Shadows is a separately saved side story with main-game unlocks, not a fifth route. Personal spell knowledge differs even within one profession. A relic's history, inheritance claim and actual compatibility interact without making pedigree moral authority. Meals, tea, choir, fishing and gardening can create ordinary attachment but become chores when repeated only for progress. Paired endings are not Awakening's universal midgame marriage/child system. Classic/Casual and Divine Pulse are separate contracts. Preserve personal/class growth, no universal old weapon triangle, finite weapon life versus replenished spells, shared onboarding fatigue, and the difference between major co-development and mere asset outsourcing.

**G65 — Heroes:** complete through HE15 and the September 27 service correction. Familiar portraits, alternate outfits and remembered music can make collecting pleasurable without proving every summoned copy has a full independent biography. A Combat Manual or consumed donor is a collection abstraction, not a default treatment of people in OpenLegend. Revitalizing a favorite can support attachment, but escalating upgrade requirements can undermine it. Community voting guides commissioned variants without transferring lawmaking authority to popularity. Asynchronous defenses differ from synchronous Summoner Duels; saved setups are not live co-presence. Determinism does not guarantee understandable outcomes when conditional effects proliferate. Divine Games' five-battle temporary build, disabled normal enhancements and two-of-six Arts choices deliberately differ from taking one's accumulated best kit into another event. Preserve launch versus later reviews, historical stamina versus later complexity criticism, September 2026 written-update evidence versus unviewed video, and third-party 2022 spending estimates versus current revenue. Continued attendance is not proof of delight, and no paid random acquisition is made a necessary OpenLegend expression route.
