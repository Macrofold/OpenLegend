All four dossiers were read continuously through their complete registers, requirement maps and final evidence limits.

**G143 — The Long Dark:** learned geography, a journal collection and practiced skill provide different knowledge. An Aurora can be beautiful, dangerous and a temporary channel to records without making every appearance an obligatory event. Personalizing an existing shelter creates a home without a full construction tree. Fixed routes support mastery while eventually reducing discovery; an arbitrary reset is not the only way to renew interest. Optional Cheat Death materially qualifies the old absolute permadeath description: selected world history survives at a cost, while accepting a one-life ending remains possible. WINTERMUTE, Survival, Challenges and TALES do not share identical narrative or save contracts. The distant optional Trader does not turn solitude into a populated faction society. Preserve completed story versus continuing support, original versus revised wildlife knowledge, sound-production credits versus newly heard music, and 2017 criticism versus post-completion testimony. Physiological examples remain fictional mechanics rather than medical advice.

**G144 — Green Hell:** an observed symptom can justify investigation without revealing its cause. The notebook externalizes discovered names, effects and recipes rather than becoming an omniscient answer key. Identifying a plant, knowing its use and improving practical technique are separate achievements; co-op can distribute those activities without formal classes. Story radio conversations preserve a relationship amid physical isolation. Spirits of Amazonia's Trust, legends and ritual gates are a particular authored campaign, not ordinary Survival's universal diplomacy or evidence about actual Indigenous cultures. A final Decorations update gives completed homes expressive scope without requiring endless new threats. Preserve criticism of opaque cascades and awkward embodied interfaces, distinct flat-screen/VR and console generations, content completion versus technical support, and gross copies/IP revenue/company profit as different evidence categories. No plant or treatment description is imported as real-world survival instruction.

**G145 — Enshrouded:** restoring a ruin layers a new home onto earlier architecture rather than requiring erased history. Skills can travel with a character while local recipe access depends on rescued people, stations and quests; shared places do not automatically share every participant's discovery. The revised multiplayer record explicitly responds to newcomers missing advanced world quests. Named rescued artisans and animals can inhabit a base without a general personality, romance or political-faction simulation. Water, flame protection and the Shroud reshape known routes through different systems. Configurable threat can support either expedition pressure or more leisurely construction. Preserve live Early Access versus the announced October 15 1.0 changes, authored terrain versus player-editable matter, expandable entry tools versus predetermined narrative and aesthetic building versus maximum workshop efficiency. SC does not inherit glider piloting.

**G146 — V Rising:** consuming a named opponent's knowledge makes capability acquisition a consequential encounter, not a universal scholarly currency. The victim's blood type is a temporary build resource, not a transferable autobiography. A captive's functional usefulness does not establish willing employment; the villainous vampire premise should retain domination and predation rather than disguising them as generic cooperation. Human Form provides limited infiltration and vendor access, not earned trust or political allegiance. A gothic home can be proud display, industrial infrastructure and a visible rival's target. Regional music tells a different kind of story from text lore. The completed boss ladder can be satisfying while making later replay too linear for some players. Preserve actual servant outcomes, mode-specific death and raid contracts, content completion versus maintenance, rejected official-modding plans versus external mods, and a separate new project versus an unannounced 1.2. Source-specific historical save wipes are not OpenLegend's required development policy.
