## Reading rules

Checked means the complete manuscript was read, including corrections, worked examples, reception, embedded studies and annotated sources. Summaries and indexes do not qualify. Reading and incorporation are separate: incorporation identifies actual catalogue entries or substantive revisions. Partial reads record the next line. Repository reading does not claim fresh gameplay, code inspection, listening, video playback or reopening every external reference.

Preserve branch history, stable IDs and unrelated work. Save meaningful checkpoints at least every five minutes while changes are outstanding. Keep source-established behavior separate from original synthesis. An entry ID means a particular source-informed idea is committed, not that every possible lesson or every Group 6 domain is exhausted. A source can inform one appropriate catalogue without being forced into all four.
