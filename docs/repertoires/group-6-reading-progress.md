## Source-specific incorporation cautions

**G41 — Palworld:** dossier `4ffc9106f98ee44d01942fa008c0cb885b5bf78c`, complete through PAL-AC, including recovered source-register truncation. Work suitability differs from actual assignment. Preserve the Kindling versus imaginary consumable-fuel correction, manual fishing versus pond delegation, and early missions versus a generally simulated polity. The historical stressed-fire-creature anecdote is not a current guarantee. GDC burden statements are Buckley's, not the earlier Mizobe interview; source-date discrepancy remains attributed. **G42 — Balatro:** dossier `bb3a49c3a3ce2e8db477ea2397db3d46e421f655`, complete through BA-U and audit. Familiar cards support an original single-player scoring game, not poker against a human opponent. Holding, playing, discarding and pruning have different positive uses. Brainstorm selects the leftmost Joker and then checks compatibility; it does not skip to a later compatible one. Copying a capability is not copying a complete identity. Preserve peak power, consistency and counter-resilience as distinct; a negative player's strategy-convergence argument is not an optimality theorem. A pleasurable repeated experiment need not become mandatory completion. Community competition is not native multiplayer. Native-platform chronology, cosmetics versus rules expansion, and the absence of a verified shipped major rules sequel remain qualified.

**G43 — Slay the Spire:** dossier `3a2ea702e97a63e559d357767e25684bec40f845`, complete through STS-AB and audit. A visible imminent intention is not the whole future; experienced knowledge changes the price of hiding it. Immediate survival, future deck consistency and route access differ. Three keys spend different things: a recovery opportunity, a particular chest item, or risk/route flexibility. Sparse named resurrection framing, a costly tome and personal memory fragments are authored history, not a universal simulated cosmology. Orb Channel/Evoke/Focus and stance entry/current/exit remain distinct; Divinity exits at the next turn's start. A consumed card's replacement is not guaranteed useful, free or permanent. Preserve original/sequel/board-game/mod boundaries and do not import a roguelike's reset into persistent lives.

**G44 — Vampire Survivors:** dossier `509082ec529f00e26ef774bbe9610e78c4849436`, complete through VS-Z and audit. Automatic execution can preserve agency through position and purpose. Enjoying an earned spectacle for longer and requesting a harder challenge are different preferences. Ordinary equipment limits depend on acquisition channel; later DLC exceptions qualify older rules. Adventure renewal, in-run Limit Break and lasting collection progress are not one reset. Conflicting Adventure coefficients remain unresolved. Local shared-screen and online independent-roaming modes have different ownership, admission and interruption rules. The helpful-positive accessibility accounts are individual experiences; the negative alternative is a recent cache, not a recovered all-time ranking, and missing displayed years are not invented. Music/sound and parody can carry identity without a full history simulation or companion cognition.
