**G128 — Ultima VII:** the returning Avatar's earlier life and the player's remembered password do not give the current character every local fact. Watching a resident's work, meals and visits supplies knowledge through ordinary life; unclear schedules can also become wasted waiting. Small practical transformations and movable furnishings make a world usable without establishing universal crafting or town founding. A religious organization can meet genuine needs while its leaders enable domination; those are connected facts, not proof all its members know the whole design. Companions' interjections, physical needs and tactical autonomy have different effects on attachment and control. Preserve two centuries of changed society, original versus expansion reward curves, diegetic death recovery, modern preservation engines versus original behavior, and contemporary conflicting criticism rather than uniform retrospective acclaim. No Steam audience or unsupported sales is invented.

**G129 — Oxygen Not Included:** an expressive worker can acquire a memorable history without a deep interpersonal simulation. Research, practical construction and a collectively understandable design are different achievements. Overlays make invisible processes inspectable, but expert contraptions can become compulsory external homework when primitives are poorly explained. Recreation, meals, decor and a workable schedule support life alongside advanced skill; their meaning need not be reduced to production yield in an original synthesis. Yesterday's successful infrastructure can cause tomorrow's crisis, but critics also identify endless new problems as punishment for progress. Keep early-access/current disease and task behavior separate, gameplay versus cosmetic packs, base versus Spaced Out geography, and folklore groups versus actual playable factions. Fictional fluids, heat and germs are not real engineering or sanitation instructions; SC does not inherit rocketry controls.

**G130 — S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2:** a sensor reading is not knowing a safe path, and acquiring a valuable artifact is not yet a profitable return. Campfire music, speech and useful hub services provide human life without a general companion or romance system. An inherited place-name, visible ruin and current faction boundary can tell different parts of the Zone's story. The documented implementation history distinguishes promised, impaired, repaired and expanded world persistence; marketing alone does not prove every unseen event. Later improvements do not erase launch experience or current technical failures, and progression bugs are not meaningful survival difficulty. Preserve authored politics versus roaming territorial behavior, DLC start presets versus NG+, toolkits versus released multiplayer, production hardship versus immunity to criticism, and reach through subscription access versus purchased units.
