**W23 — Austen:** the six novels are related authored social environments, not one shared continuity. An invitation matters because a particular person wants it; Catherine disputes Henry while enjoying their dance, not accepting a binding proposal through an analogy. The Crofts' companionship has practiced habits and real pleasure without proving all naval households share their conditions. Emma's penitent visit is not an explicit apology and conclusive forgiveness scene. Brandon's mediated accounts of the two Elizas distinguish coerced marriage, later abandonment, immediate relief and a duel providing no restitution. Wrongdoers can prosper while others achieve genuine homes; no universal compensating moral economy. Preserve servants'/colonized people's limited viewpoints rather than inventing them, primary passage versus critical inference, and depicted historical arrangements versus current law.

**W24 — Macondo:** repeated names and rooms carry changed lives, not reset identities. Practical teaching and fairer settlement layout remain achievements alongside unreliable imaginative explanations. Naming a thing differs from knowing its use; a manuscript's existence differs from timely access. Petra/Aureliano's earlier exuberance and later modest companionship both matter, while initial identity deception is not retroactively consensual. Meme's musical ability is not liking imposed practice; her own parties, reading and chosen attachment differ. Fernanda deliberately recruits public authority through a false accusation; Mauricio survives the shooting with lasting injury. Narrative erotic framing does not erase refusal and force. The final destruction is not an in-world rescue through readers remembering it. Preserve translation/transcription limits, secondary criticism and fiction's relation to history without real casualty counts or regional stereotypes.

**W25 — Spirited Away:** useful bathing, offered food and a quiet journey can be intrinsically wanted. Yubaba's competent direction of the river cleanup, workers' effort, unequal rewards and actual celebration coexist. No-Face's appetite and harmed workers remain real; recognizing loneliness does not require accepting exclusive attachment. Zeniba's welcome does not erase earlier threats, nor is the later household another compulsory trap. Haku's apprenticeship joins ambition and displacement; recovering a name does not show the river restored or a later reunion. Chihiro's rejected false choices end her contract without reforming the whole institution. Preserve unofficial translated dialogue versus animation/music inspection, secondary sexual-industry interpretation versus no depicted child sexual work, limited history versus invented cosmology, and no generalization to actual religious doctrine.

**W26 — Warhammer 40,000:** visual inheritance, martial excitement, dangerous scholarship, collecting and articulate rivalry have distinct appeals. Satirical publisher framing is not proof of every audience response; continuity revisions cannot all be excused as unreliable testimony. A siege can be won without a city or dead restored, and recruitment changes an existing regiment's culture. The Necroteuch is actually dangerous within this fiction, but its duplicate means destroying one copy does not erase distributed knowledge. Crezia's professional judgment and lover's aims differ; affection is not obedience. Trazyn's preservation can violate people made into exhibits, while Orikan's different ambition sustains a feud across immense time. Preserve permanent injury despite technical repair, named novels/eras versus current franchise totals, real supernatural dangers versus justified institutional cruelty, and secondary scene evidence where primary samples failed.
