**G123 — Rain World:** a pearl can supply passage, exchange or interpreted history; its meaning depends on the recipient and situation. A body-language exchange and remembered treatment establish local social behavior without a universal human dialogue model. High reputation can produce help that still causes dangerous congestion or crossfire. Learning a route or creature's signals changes competence without a level increase; obscurity of controls is a different kind of obstacle from a world mystery. Industrial ruins are crossed before their cosmology becomes known. Campaign bodies and chronological world states are not one freely swapped class tree. Preserve base/Downpour/Remix/Watcher and current local co-op scope, Gourmand's actual crafting versus a universal profession, unreconciled dated creature counts, versioned accessibility options, and the possibility of genuinely unwinnable autonomous encounters rather than a promise every setback is fair.

**G124 — Persona 5 Royal:** darts, coffee, study, work and listening can compete for the same evening while serving different motives. A relationship established in ordinary life can become central much later, but hiding major story access behind its deadline can penalize a blind playthrough. Preserve the corrected Maruki-only semester gate versus Akechi/Kasumi's separate content requirements, Royal versus original ammunition/pacing, selected NG+ carryover versus persistent relationship history, port/spin-off boundaries, and combined franchise sales versus Royal-only measures.
