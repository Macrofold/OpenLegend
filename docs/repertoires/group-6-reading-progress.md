Instruments identify people; silence and selected musical cues can preserve belonging and solitude. A correct theory can fail in execution without becoming false. Preserve finite completion, fictional quantum rules, base/DLC/mod boundaries, the negative-rated Steam access gap and the distinction between no major updates planned and no possible maintenance. **G35 — Garry's Mod:** dossier blob `a516b96791f6b2867029a3f17e7239b63ff3fc8b`, complete through S3 and definitions. Making, adapting and joining are different depths of participation. Retain the content-dependency exclusions, historical community-loss accounts, good-faith versus excessive-rule tensions and the distinction between blog/player reviews and five professional reviews.
