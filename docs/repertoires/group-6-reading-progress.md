A writ's specific legal force is not moral innocence or immunity for unrelated acts. Political recognition can require a rescue and lethal duel; it is not simultaneous membership in all Houses. Ahnassi's specific relationship remains distinct from a universal romance system. Great geography and rich history do not automatically deliver individual recognition after promotion. Keep GOTY manual versus original launch, daily powers versus learned spells, physiological equipment exclusions, transformation/cure exceptions and Tribunal's journal/levitation changes. The manual's atmospheric absence of a known cure is not proof the cure quest is absent. A partial sixth review is not five-source evidence enlarged to six.

**G114 — Kingdom Come II:** appropriate dress can open a wedding while money, invitation and conduct remain different conditions. Knowing a recipe, performing a trade and earning another person's confidence are distinct. Keeping a familiar horse can reveal a later benefit rather than require replacement; the comparison does not establish every rival mount's ranking. Dice preserve banked points while risking the current turn, and collectible dice/badges alter the game rather than generic combat power. An artist's shield designs are expressive rewards, not a freeform painting editor. A working forge can embody family history and new personal ambition. Keep authored Henry versus self-authored conduct, consensual intimacy versus a reward owed for care, disagreement about repeated chores, indexed Steam evidence versus direct-page gaps, and a 2027 portable announcement versus shipped editions. Early firearms do not transfer to the MD seed.

**G115 — Bannerlord:** local board games make cultures encounterable through recreation, but their six documented forms do not prove a seventh Nord game or identical real-world rules. A town's successive captures, service and eventual award can become personal history; recurrence can also become exhausting rather than meaningful. Voluntary retirement, succession and complete closure are alternatives to forced death or endless conquest. Clan, party, army, kingdom, renown, influence and personal relations differ. Preserve stable/beta changes to governors and visible battle sites, later dual roles versus remote-job exclusivity, historical Festival and Games advice versus removed behavior, and launch criticism versus delivered diplomacy/economy changes. Mod tools and competitions are not source-code openness or proven sales attribution. War Sails adds a separate delivered layer; no SC piloting.
