**W15 — The Expanse:** infrastructure makes technical register, habitat and belonging practical, but expertise does not exhaust desired lives. Retirement with a partner is a substantive future, not cowardice; later interruption does not erase the intervening decades. Licensed adult sex work is distinguished from earlier exploitation, and criticism of its representation remains attributed rather than a general verdict. Eros has deliberate human organizers, not only alien uncertainty or incentives. Bobbie's achieved victory and irreversible loss coexist. Closing gates changes material dependence and separates families; later contact does not prove every colony survived. Keep novels/television/novella boundaries, book registers versus television conlang, fictional engineering versus science, and limited primary-excerpt access.

**W16 — Foundation:** institutions can outlast protagonists without a complete domestic encyclopedia. First arrival combines real scientific ambition, working ordinary services and wonder with surveillance. Musical pleasure is not consent to emotional control. Seldon's recorded authority can organize ceremonies while failing to describe present events. Riose's genuine loyalty/competence does not determine how imperial authority reads it; protagonists' failed intervention is distinct from their eventual safety. Bayta prevents disclosure by killing Mis; affection does not itself defeat the empire. Pritcher's induced loyalty and the Mule's altered disposition are not chosen agreement. Preserve original trilogy/later books/television boundaries, sparse religion/intimacy without invented detail, and psychohistory as fiction rather than proven sociology.

**W17 — The Culture:** abundance permits serious games, chosen bodily pleasure and artistic labor without subsistence compulsion. A recital can genuinely delight while its aftermath introduces institutional pressure; assistance need not be secretly coercive, nor does politeness prove freedom to refuse. Gurgeh's wanted excellence, cheating trap, winning, rescue and eventual understanding differ. A performance recording and a person have different continuity. Restoring Lededje's body does not supply every outcome she seeks; her abuse is not consensual experimentation. Deliberate atrocity and refused absolution remain in the operative's history. The saved population, Hub/Quilan's departure and Huyler's continuing life have separate outcomes; suspected conspirators remain suspected. Keep author essay/interview/excerpt versus secondary narrative and critical disagreement; fictional glands, Minds and permissions establish no real medicine or platform authority.

**W18 — The Broken Earth:** Damaya/Syenite/Essun are stages of one life, not independent people. Useful stonelore and preparation coexist with manufactured classifications and deliberately hidden captive labor; the harm is not an inevitable technical cost. Meov's pleasure and nonconventional household remain genuine despite later loss. Syenite's chosen competence at sea also harms the raided crew; command aboard ship differs from intimacy ashore. Storytelling and translation can attract and entertain, not only classify or conceal. Castrima's population, building, political experiment and individual health do not all survive together. Ending Seasons permits genuine improvement without restoring the dead or settling all injustice. Preserve compelled versus chosen intimacy, agency alongside institutional explanation, divergent pacing criticism and limited primary access rather than filling missing cultural detail.
