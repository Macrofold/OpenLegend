**G125 — XCOM 2:** a memorial can preserve how particular people died while their absence alters later work. Names, voices, chosen appearance and accumulated service jointly support recognition; sparse dialogue does not mean no attachment. A rescue can preserve a veteran despite a failed objective, and a tactical victory can leave the organization worse prepared. Formal bonds belong to War of the Chosen, not every base-game relationship. Preserve injury, Will fatigue and negative traits as distinct conditions, deliberate tactical challenge versus an unrecoverable campaign spiral, and historical bugs versus intended loss. Legacy content, individual DLC, Long War variants and the base campaign remain separate. Retired Steam modes and the removed launcher do not establish other platforms' current state. The early commentary's photograph example was broader than the fetched passage; this source specifically establishes customization, screenshots and the memorial, not a separately verified photograph-authoring mechanic.

**G126 — Crusader Kings III:** a title, a family resemblance, a remembered act and personal trust have different inheritance. A successor can receive institutions without the previous ruler's skills, personality or friendships. Displayed artifacts and public legends can attach meaning to authority, while coronation, feast and tournament supply distinct occasions rather than generic resource clicks. A marriage's immediate purpose and its descendants' later claims can diverge. Traits constrain choices without proving a scientific model of personality; critics can find them too rigid. Keep mobile landless lives, sedentary domains, administrative offices and nomadic dependence distinct, and historical flavor separate from a claim that every modeled arrangement accurately describes real societies. Announced By God Alone and Silk & Silver remain future in the source snapshot. Subscription access is not ownership; PC and console content are not synchronized by assumption.

**G127 — Dragon's Dogma 2:** translating, recalling a route and performing a combat role are independent reasons to keep a companion. A borrowed Pawn can bring another journey's knowledge without importing a human co-op player, identical quest state or guaranteed correct guidance. Replacing a lower-level helper can conflict with attachment; staying with it remains a meaningful preference rather than ignorance of efficiency. A gifted object, interpreted affection and an authored romance outcome are not one guarantee. Remembered expeditions can be stronger than the scripted political plot, but that does not make the latter's weaknesses irrelevant. Preserve reported title-update changes versus launch reviews, future expansion versus delivered base game, save slots versus save states, Main Pawn versus support-Pawn advancement, and available resurrection versus universal reversal. Performance improvement remains hardware-dependent and no fresh service or gameplay test is claimed.
