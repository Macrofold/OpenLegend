## Latest source-specific cautions

**G71 — Skyrim:** blob `d0754e460b66a26eb663f44fb6a45d562664e3e8`, complete through SK14 and preservation audit. Finding a Word of Power, unlocking it and combining words are distinct steps; a related creative language idea does not make ordinary literacy supernatural. An ingredient experiment can create persistent knowledge, while knowing a craft does not supply materials or tool access. Books, journals, ruins and dragon-language music give exploration cultural texture. Simultaneously holding many elite roles is a permissive game design, not proof institutions generally ignore conflicts. Named schedules and Radiant targets are not arbitrary autobiographical memory or causally generated need. Keep original, expansions, Special/Anniversary, optional Survival Mode and third-party mods distinct. The documented 2026 compatibility backlash is not a verdict on the 2011 design. Repeated skill grinding, repetition in ruins and shallow post-marriage reactivity remain criticisms, not lessons to erase. No fresh external release check or software test is claimed.

**G72 — The Witcher 3:** blob `40e5e06101518c5c39f02199eb9f9b3bfb3f960b`, complete through W3-18 and preservation audit. Geralt's established past permits choices about response without becoming a blank slate. Learned ability versus equipped emphasis, diagram versus materials/craftsperson, and professional observations versus deductions are different boundaries. Preserve criticism that highlighted clues often let Geralt infer answers automatically. Gwent connects a hobby to particular merchants, tournaments and revisits; Corvo Bianco gives life beyond contracts a place. Family harm and political cruelty need not become a compulsory reconciliation story, and strong writing does not establish best-in-class combat. Ciri's authored perspective sections are not an unrestricted second campaign. New Game+ retains capabilities while restarting authored decisions, not a continuous social history or recursive infinite replay. Keep Hearts of Stone/Blood and Wine, 2022 changes, REDkit/mods and the dossier's explicitly future Remastered/Songs of the Past claims separate; those release and sales claims were read as repository research, not independently reverified here.
