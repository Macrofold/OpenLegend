**G147 — Once Human:** character identity, social ties, learned blueprints and physical buildings have distinct continuities across scenarios. Eternaland, Seasonal Scenarios, Eternal Scenarios and Visional Wheels are not one universal reset or transfer rule. Expertise can become a service offered to others when a machine inherits a particular builder's specialist bonus, without transferring the builder's whole history. Deviations are specific companions, workers or resource sources, not an unrestricted personhood simulation. Domesticated animals differ from supernatural captures. **G148 — Abiotic Factor:** a profession supplies a starting history rather than a permanent prohibition on learning. Current late respecialization resets skills while retaining recipes and the journal; changing a role need not erase everything learned or lived. Institutional signs, departments and familiar office objects make a ruined workplace readable before its deeper history is known. The same cafeteria can acquire new uses and a shared past. Renewable portal expeditions preserve a stable home's physical history without requiring every extracted space to be permanent. Current pets persist, grow and act beyond immediate commands; that can help or interrupt a different plan. Horror, comic bodily needs and practical scientific invention coexist without each joke needing a plot justification. Preserve delivered community updates versus future mod support and expansion, publisher title-specific versus combined sales, and the criticism that conventional late combat and an unsatisfying finale can betray earlier inventive play.
