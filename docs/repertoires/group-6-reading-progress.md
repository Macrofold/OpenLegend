## Latest incorporation cautions

All earlier detailed notes remain in the immutable checkpoints above. Do not discard their version/source-access qualifications or turn attributed play accounts into newly reproduced events.

**G60 — The Blazing Blade:** complete through the final audit and every source annotation. Meeting a trained ally again can reward earlier participation without another stat increase. Named recruitment makes biography actionable; recognizing a mask or uniform is not the same as knowing who can persuade its wearer. Adjacency-based support can reward waiting rather than meaningful shared experience; established Pent/Louise is not courtship from zero, and A support is not universally romance. A ranking's asset conservation may conflict with saving a cherished person without making either goal incoherent. The recruitment theme gives joining social meaning beyond roster count. Keep regional effectiveness/localization, required-lord failure, Lyn-story returns, story-only withdrawal and Merlinus recovery separate. Native suspend, external rewind and supernatural time travel are not interchangeable. The support calculator's inconsistency and per-chapter disagreement remain unresolved; no inaccessible interview, PDF or full player review is newly claimed read.

**G61 — Path of Radiance:** complete through PR31, including biorhythm, Chapter 10 stealth and map-object correction material. Shared deployments create later camp-conversation eligibility, not proof of every imagined private interaction. The Base moves reflection away from urgent battle; it is not a mandatory architecture for continuous worlds. A commoner protagonist, social prejudice and differing bodies contribute to Tellius's history without treating every person's beliefs as species-wide. A named forged tool can acquire an individual history while retaining ordinary rules. Team-earned BEXP and personally earned experience have different interpretations of deservingness. Biorhythm is visible but may have too little useful influence, not hidden. Chapter-specific stealth is not a campaign stealth subsystem. Preserve Japanese/international difficulty and later emulation versus original rules/hardware links. Motion capture did not make literal realism the objective; scenery was altered for legibility. Staff testimony comes through a fan translation. Contemporary and rerelease reviewers disagree about challenge and pacing; neither period erases the other.

**G62 — Awakening:** complete through AW25 and the correction material. Inherited classes, techniques and social history can shape a descendant without defining their whole person; the ruined-future child is not an ordinary instant-aging simulation. The source's skill-inheritance moment and exceptions remain distinct from a universal hereditary-memory rule. A former class leaves learned capabilities after changing occupation; current role is not total biography. Pair Up bundles transport, defense, offense and relationship growth, so its dominance is a counter-lesson as well as an attachment mechanism. Small Barracks exchanges need not be major quests. A shared roster can be an asynchronous artifact rather than a second live person. StreetPass and Double Duel are local; SpotPass and new eShop purchases have different retirement boundaries; historically purchased DLC is not universally obtainable. Casual versus Classic changes loss policy separately from tactical difficulty. Preserve base/DLC, original/sequel and class/gender substitution boundaries rather than importing Fates' redesign. Developer enthusiasm for character love and generational play is not proof that one feature caused commercial revival.
