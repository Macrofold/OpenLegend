## Latest source-specific cautions

**G90 — Final Fantasy XIII:** complete through FFXIII42, including XIII-2 and Lightning Returns contrasts. Imposed Focus and a person's chosen purpose differ. Paradigms change whole-party policy within actual learned roles; they cannot supply missing abilities. Ordinary ATB magic, inventory items and TP techniques have different costs. Restored postbattle HP is not a reset of every resource; Retry restores prebattle state rather than preserving damage to an enemy. Higher stars improve some rewards, while lower ratings favor shrouds, so a score is not universally better for every goal. Libra informs choice rather than creating a weakness. Identical final weapon names can preserve different upgrade lineages. Waystone missions create particular infrastructure, not universal travel. Lightning Returns spends EP, not XIII's TP; ordinary menus/battles pause its clock but the Ultimate Lair is an exception. The pre-release experienced-day estimate is not the running-clock ratio. Mina's fictitious-language performance and Hamauzu's piano adaptation concern expressive function, not a new real-world language or a listening session. Preserve the primary chart's dated unit definition, source-author visual inspection versus this manuscript reading, and qualitative port criticism.

**G91 — Final Fantasy XIV:** complete through S59, with truncated activity and production sections recovered. Original 2010, ARR, expansion launches and later corrections remain distinct; future Evercold/Keybound Brawler descriptions are not shipped features at this snapshot. One character's changing jobs preserve identity, not universal readiness. A saved appearance, materia extraction and an item instance preserve different properties; dresser conversion can lose materia/spiritbond despite later item restoration. Limited jobs and regional abilities retain their activity boundaries. Hunt-train etiquette does not grant exclusive ownership of a public spawn. Matching roles differs from matching discovery, farming, teaching or relaxed intentions. Duty Support, Trust avatars, a temporary party, a chat circle and a Free Company are different structures. A reserved player wedding is not general NPC romance, and a housing lottery is not guaranteed access to a social fantasy. Shared construction is authored staged progress, not arbitrary collective world creation. Preserve temporary/regional/persistent progress distinctions, unavailable active-population metrics and genuinely divided reactions to an established protagonist becoming a supporting figure. Emotional continuity is not proof of emergent narrative or unrestricted memory.

**G92 — Final Fantasy XV:** complete through S49. Photography separates agent capture from the player's later selection, and a chosen journey photo can personalize a fixed farewell without branching its outcome. Cooking discovery, purchased ingredients, edible catches and collected trophies differ. Camping and lodging need not perform identical EXP/food functions. An injury changes a particular companion's capabilities in an authored arc, not a general autonomous life simulation. Hunts' later simultaneous-contract rule qualifies the old guide; repeat reward and drop objectives differ. The horn break is a drop opportunity, not certainty. Comrades' avatars/infrastructure and stat-before-level remodeling are separate from Noctis's campaign. Preserve original/Royal/Episode boundaries, cancelled episodes versus published alternate novel, partial PC/Origin feature retirement rather than universal shutdown, and the unavailable negative-only Steam sample.
