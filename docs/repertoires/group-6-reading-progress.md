## Reading and incorporation rules

Checked means the complete manuscript was read, including corrections, cases, reception, embedded studies and annotated sources. Indexes, summaries and discovery do not qualify. Incorporation separately identifies actual committed catalogue IDs or substantive revisions; it does not claim every possible lesson is exhausted. Partial reads record the next line. Repository reading does not claim new gameplay, source-code inspection, listening, video viewing or reopening every external citation.

Preserve branch history, stable IDs and unrelated work. Save meaningful checkpoints at least every five minutes while changes are outstanding. Keep source-established behavior separate from original synthesis. A source may inform the appropriate domain without being forced into all four catalogues.

## New incorporation cautions

All G01–G55 and Dragon Age series cautions remain in the immutable records above. In particular, retain personal interpretation versus public history, scene-specific information versus omniscience, public sacred status versus personal belief, performance versus lived event, and service preservation versus retained text or a new adaptation. Ship research never authorizes playable SC navigation.

**G56 — Original Sin:** complete through P2 and preservation gate. Two Source Hunters can express separate intentions even under one controller; winning a disagreement minigame is not universal authority over another person's commitments. The original, Bear and the Burglar, and Enhanced editions differ; two-player cooperation is not the sequel's four-player campaign. Pet Pal opens situated informants, not omniscient hints. Nails prevent a particular slipping failure, not all physical danger. A paired pyramid requires destination placement. Zombie care is not DOSII's ancestry system. VanOrd's grave-looting incident gives a trivial bone a grave social cost; his decision not to reload is attributed experience, not a universal mourning simulation. A reported ethically blocked investigation is not an exhaustive proof that no alternate route exists. Preserve repeated-bark and dynamic-camera criticism, older versus revised balance, learned/qualified/prepared/affordable skills, and prototype/editor/mod versus native rules.

**G57 — Original Sin II:** complete through all definitions and its build/authoring supplement. A shared quest state does not guarantee that every participant heard or participated in its conclusion. New perception can renew a place but should not imply compulsory rescanning. Brown's later conversation with a proud mother changes the understood meaning of an earlier killing, not the event itself. Origin identity survives a build revision; collecting all possible lives in one campaign is not the promise. Corpse Eater supplies particular memories/skills, not every dead person's entire biography. Undead fingers replace a consumable rather than lock expertise. Rune placement is reversible while framing changes later upgrade eligibility. GM preparation, played saves, vignettes, editor scripting and live human rulings have separate owners; powerful author tools can still create an attention bottleneck. Unfinished documentation is not proof an actual feature was unshipped. Arena and campaign stakes differ. Gift Bag 4's optional rules and Four Relics' integrated new-game content remain distinct, as do platform ports, remake claims and current-network assumptions.

**G58 — Kingmaker:** complete through all definitions and party/kingdom supplement. Linzi's chronicling voice makes a reign a particular account rather than an omniscient record. A parallel protagonist's actions can supply a place's history without transferring player knowledge into the later character. Valerie's rejection of a prescribed religious destiny and Nok-Nok's ambition need not become one generic heroic identity. A procured book can progress Bokken's research without immediately producing a masterpiece; maker, premises, knowledge, time and delivery remain distinct. Advisers' administrative availability is not physical absence from the adventuring party. Values versus ranks, deterministic projects versus uncertain events, final deadlines versus earlier crisis onset, and Stability versus unrest differ. Camping roles reduce different costs; a safe-house recovery option is not every field rest. Preserve PF1 computer adaptation versus tabletop, sequel mythic systems, native turn-based mode versus older mod advice, original versus revised publisher and edition dates, and the confounding of bug fixes with a returning reviewer's greater expertise.

**G59 — Wrath:** complete through the audit, source definitions and entire path/army supplement. Mythic identity changes social recognition and can cost relationships; it is not a free respec or ten equally long campaigns. Aivu grows on story-linked mythic ranks rather than ordinary XP; late Devil/Swarm/Legend choices have different losses. Trickster identification changes a qualifying acquisition, not every existing object's history. Finnean can retain a personal story across useful forms without being an arbitrary enchantment editor. Ulbrig supplies a situated witness to a lost civilization. Dance of Masks' celebration and mature-build arena serve different desires; Through the Ashes preserves vulnerable civilian stakes. Nenio's experimental eating is an attributed incident, not a claim of general autonomous consumption. Epic status and earned credibility remain separate. General Energy and strategic Energy Points, troop count and stack capacity, battlefield recovery and infirmary recovery, and campaign versus combat morale differ.
