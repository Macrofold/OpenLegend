**G88 — Final Fantasy XI:** complete through FFXI57. Linkshell tokens grant a particular community channel, not unrestricted shared knowledge. A predefined translation dictionary supports some coordination without eliminating language barriers; original reviewers disagree about its adequacy. Trusts reduce unavailable-human bottlenecks but also change opportunities to meet people; authored engagement rules are not general autonomy. Preserve old/current social contracts, regional economic differences, personal garden versus house, creature rearing versus combat pets, and spectator wagering versus steering a race. Ballista's combat opportunity is not its completed deposit; Brenner has a different objective. Ambuscade monthly balances, Odyssey expedition izzat, persistent Segments and recharging Sortie entry items have distinct persistence. Prishe's return to mortality is an authored hopeful resolution, not an every-player branch. Five reviews came from four publications; unavailable Steam bodies, qualitative community testimony and absent global population totals remain explicit. Retail and private-server rules are not merged.

Fishing execution, entry, a later rod and external quest prerequisites are distinct; a relaxing activity need not be conscripted into unrelated combat completion.
