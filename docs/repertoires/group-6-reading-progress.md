**Language:** sound, vocabulary, grammar, script, social usage and historical variation are different contributions, not six required quotas. A constructed language's fictional history differs from its creator's revisions and later adaptation. Translation is not cultural understanding, status or agreement; names and titles act through relationships. Labels without remembered use differ from public denial of a remembered event. Wit, song, rivalry and courtship can be worthwhile without a hidden puzzle. Fluency or harsh sounds do not establish morality; generic perfect translation and permanent opacity can each flatten difference.

**Magic, technology and ecology:** audience preparation, inhabitant expertise, reliability and social integration differ. Constraints need not be fuel; access, knowledge, permission, practical dependence and chosen restraint can matter separately. Abundance and useful provision need not conceal an invented victim.
