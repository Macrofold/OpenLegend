**W19 — Narnia:** sensory welcome, feasts, clothing, impressive fighting and responsible office have independent attractions. Bacchic renewal has military/material consequences without an invented sexual scene; the narrator leaves the boys' supposed transformation uncertain. Bern's local knowledge and a bluff help free captives, but a ruler's effective intervention is not a complete institutional model. Jadis chooses annihilation and provides self-justifying testimony; her stated unknown magical price remains unknown. Caspian's duty and Reepicheep's onward desire legitimately diverge. Preserve Susan alive with an open eventual fate, disputed narrative framing, books/adaptations and publication/fictional chronology, and the ending's actual fulfillment rather than an invented sinister reversal.

**W20 — His Dark Materials:** possession of a truth instrument differs from earned interpretation; curiosity can precede its eventual rescue use. Mary's manufacture draws on another people's practical knowledge, a relationship, observation and imperfect experiments; their world existed before helping her. Dæmon appearance is not independent animal ecology or a rigid personality chart. Adult pleasure and changing belief need not invalidate earlier sincerity; the young protagonists' unrepresented conduct is not invented. Iorek wins through lethal judgment and bodily knowledge, not compulsory reconciliation. Roger's death is Asriel's deliberate act, distinct from Coulter's later changed conduct; Authority and Metatron have different roles/ends. Preserve edition differences, original/later/adaptation boundaries, conflicting criticism, and the ending's separation, surviving opening and new vocation without an invented loophole.

**W21 — The Wire:** a public basketball game offers genuine rivalry, skill and shared spectatorship without reforming its sponsors; the creator does not certify it as a historical event. Cutty's gym needs patronage, permits and useful teaching and benefits some without saving everyone. Occupational vocabulary and code-switching differ from institutional access; intercepted intimate conversation is not automatic permission. Proxy manipulation is specific, not proof all measurement is fraud. Institutional handoff failures and deliberate arson have distinct authors; knowing a bond enables targeted cruelty. Omar's abrupt death, chosen prior retirement and later legend differ. Bubbles's recovery is real alongside Dukie's loss; Freamon's craft is not failed policing. Retain staged fiction versus contemporary Baltimore, selective episode accounts versus full viewing, and attributed criticism of repetitive endings and newspaper framing.

**W22 — The Sopranos:** a wanted birthday, attraction and good cooking remain actual pleasures without exonerating other conduct. A recipe book is usable inheritance, not only nostalgia; renewed attention to craft does not instantly restore customers or finances. Malapropisms need not imply general stupidity, and fluent therapeutic or religious language need not produce change. Family affection and a deliberate killing share one college trip. Melfi's refusal preserves a boundary without providing punishment or reconciliation. Tony's later enjoyment and felt revelation do not establish innocence or insight; adult pleasure elsewhere is not thereby condemned. Tracee's work, bread and pregnancy concerns are her own life, not only evidence of another person's compassion. Preserve series/prequel distinctions, concentrated secondary criticism, limited creator testimony, no clinical/real-world legal generalization and the unshown finale's unresolved future.
