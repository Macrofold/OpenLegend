**W07:** paid musical work, an unperformed private ballad and a public legend are distinct artistic lives. A bounded intimate encounter need not repair unequal affection; later loss does not make prior pleasure fraudulent. Geralt's adopted credential becomes an actual knighthood through a costly battle without restoring Milva's loss. Blaviken leaves counterfactuals unresolved while threats and killing remain real; visible outcomes travel farther than the actor's information. The Baron's attempt to seek treatment is not demonstrated cure/accountability or his family's consent to reunion. A genuine wedding can coexist with an unwilling-to-leave possessing guest; Shani's later intimacy is optional, not earned entitlement. Retain Ciri/Mistle's coerced beginning despite later attachment, planned versus completed reproductive violations, original saga/game/Netflix boundaries, translation and quest-writing collaboration, transcript reading rather than audio, and secondary/branch access limits.

**W08:** a genuinely capable rival can want and lose a championship; affection is not a substitute for competitive mastery. Athletic courses let familiar companions express different competences without inventing a canonical winning team. Pokédex assertions are not uniformly measured science, and the League is not automatically the whole state. Named legends do not imply complete clerical institutions. A frozen collection, useful storage and a continuing living bond differ. N's preferences survive criticism of his ethical arc; Ghetsis's liberation rhetoric conceals a stated power monopoly. Lillie's protective action and search for treatment do not establish recovery or an obligation to forgive. Preserve original/Ultra/remake/anime boundaries, newly earned champion versus Kukui's challenge, Lavender's lasting bereavement, and localized wordplay versus complete conlangs.

**W09:** environmental expertise becomes manners, sacred practice and imperial leverage, but the central dependency is not an exhaustive economy. Odrade's painting, Dortujla's coins and Gurney's returned instrument preserve pleasurable private life beyond strategic function. Ritual intoxication and affectionate hospitality differ from enslavement or engineered dependency. The maker ride is earned competence alongside mentor correction, not proof of a right to govern. Feyd's spectacular victory is rigged and coercive even when he has genuine skill; admired spectacle does not make a victim consenting. Paul's foresight and affection do not supply Chani control over concealed reproductive decisions, and the ghola offer exploits bereavement rather than certifying her return. Preserve original six-book/adaptation/continuation boundaries, cultural borrowing versus real anthropology, later institutional divergence and attributed criticism rather than universal popularity claims.

**W10:** broken hospitality remains a socially powerful norm precisely because ordinary feasts can really provide comfort, food and belonging. Brienne's achievement and wanted recognition are not canceled by her later circumstances. Consensual role-play is specifically narrated as such and cannot be generalized into treating refusals as performative; imposed marriage is a different relation. The Boltons use Jeyne's false identity and Theon's coerced testimony, not a merely misunderstood ceremony. Escape into another faction's control is not finished recovery or absolution for prior wrongdoing. Songs can support sincerely pursued ideals as well as mislead, and enforced naming can attack an identity. Preserve book Jeyne versus television Sansa, multiple historical witnesses, screen conlangs versus novel evocation, effective magic versus justified theology, and unresolved novels without release forecasts or invented endings.
