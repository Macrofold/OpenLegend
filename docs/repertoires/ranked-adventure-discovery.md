## Selection decisions

A threatened grain escort (AD-016), a hunt and predator problem (AD-046–AD-047), a small cellar or bandit tower (AD-061–AD-062), a finite mystery (AD-076), accessible treasure (AD-106), a raider roadblock (AD-181) and a dangerous mine (AD-196) provide representative Core premises. Spending the earned pay (AD-226) and leaving a useful crossing open (AD-228) close the loop. A first implementation should connect a small selection of these, not build their every associated institution or ecological system.

More dangerous quarries, varied dungeons, heists, rescues, useful discoveries, tournaments, creative competitions and optional challenge variants are Complete. A monster puzzle with two incompatible observation responses (AD-294) can be a valuable tactical addition even though its missing engineering is substantial. Scarcity or danger is not mandatory for every pleasure: music, games, a good tavern and an enjoyable contest can also be Complete activities.

The found weapon worth restoring (AD-107) is Complete because the quest delivers a useful new equipment option. That does not promote routine sharpening or repair to a foundational loop. Similarly, a workshop bought with treasure (AD-232) is a real progression reward, whereas incidental lantern cleanup and keepsake returns (AD-159) are Detail.

Personal obligations, nuanced memories, detailed institutional consequences, evolving ecology and wider campaign settlements follow the playable baseline. Shared-dream journeys, changing laws of space or time, alien body-sharing and specialist modern or adult-life scenarios remain available as Specialist proposals. Their source-world recommendations and descriptions are preserved; a priority is not a claim that the base world has adopted their lore or mechanics.

## Verification and limits

Coverage: 336 distinct source IDs, each assigned once, with no missing or duplicate assignment in the reviewed ID set. Priority values and within-tier ordering were checked. This is a document selection review, not a new implementation audit, runtime test, playtest or empirical measurement of fun. Low priority never relaxes the correctness, authority, privacy, participation or persistence requirements of a feature that is actually selected.
