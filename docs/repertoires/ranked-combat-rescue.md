## Selection decisions

Readable enemy roles (CR-121–CR-124), cues (CR-078), combat feedback (CR-118), a useful objective (CR-212–CR-213) and lasting victory (CR-225) are Core. New enemy roles, tactical answers, rescue objectives and a usable tournament can be Complete even when their engineering is substantial.

Basic protection does not require regional armor simulation (CR-106); basic recovery does not require localized disability, care handoffs or casualty-allocation systems. Weapon-fault clearing (CR-025), reserving a shot (CR-026), repair history (CR-201), spoils administration (CR-203) and debrief protocols (CR-210) are Detail. A request for help should use existing communication rather than become a new protocol. Respect for a participant's choices still applies whenever assistance exists.

The Core projectile entry permits a bounded coarse travel model. It does not demand continuous physics, ricochets or advanced aiming before ranged combat is playable. Core shield, evasion and escape entries provide representative defensive choices; they are not a requirement to ship every defense together. Different-time arenas, distributed-body rescue and explicitly different technological or supernatural settings remain Specialist.
