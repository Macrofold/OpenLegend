# Work, craft, professions and invention

[Library](README.md) · [Reading the labels](README.md#reading-the-labels) · [Selection and scale](selection-and-scale.md) · [Design foundation](design-foundation.md)

**Proposal catalogue · September 27, 2026.** Work can be satisfying mastery, a route to wealth or fame, preparation for danger, service to a loved person, or participation in a harmful enterprise. Professions connect individual skills to households, workshops and communities. Repetition deserves a purpose and a credible path to delegation when its decisions have become routine.

**Scope:** Own professions and the processes for making, modifying and repairing things here. Bowmaking and armor repair reference finished items in [Objects](objects.md), inputs in [Materials](materials-resources.md), and the performance affected by the work in [Combat](combat-rescue.md). Follow [category ownership](README.md#category-ownership-and-cross-references) instead of duplicating a weapon inventory inside recipes.

## The commission with room for taste

**ALL · Play · High · 2/Extend.** Gap: requirements, negotiable preferences and inspectable acceptance.

A client wants a rainproof market sign readable by an elderly parent, leaving material and style open. The maker can prototype, ask questions or propose an unexpected form. Craft becomes interpretation, not exact recipe guessing. Distinguish mandatory function from taste; a client may dislike a color without falsely declaring the sign mechanically unusable.

Seeds: [Assassin's Creed III](source-atlas.md#g99), [Kingdom Come: Deliverance II](source-atlas.md#g114), [Ultima VII: The Black Gate](source-atlas.md#g128).

## Teach the workshop one good routine

**ALL · Play · High · 2/Extend.** Gap: demonstrated plans with bounded inputs, stops and explanation.

The player shows a helper how to sort usable offcuts and reserve enough for repairs. Once understood, the routine runs until a meaningful exception occurs. Experts gain relief from repetitive work. Show what will be consumed and why work stopped; delegation must not invent missing materials, spend beyond agreement or require a new model call for each scrap.

Seeds: [Factorio](source-atlas.md#g27), [Final Fantasy XII](source-atlas.md#g89), [Kingdom Come: Deliverance II](source-atlas.md#g114).

## Make do beautifully

**ALL · Play · High · 2/Extend.** Gap: material substitution and expressive construction.

After a shipment fails, a town builds festival lanterns from tins and colored cloth. The resulting style may become a tradition. Players solve constraints while creating something visually distinctive. A substitute should have real differences and acceptable compromises; do not punish every deviation from the original blueprint with an inferior-quality label.

Seeds: [Minecraft](source-atlas.md#g20), [Against the Storm](source-atlas.md#g45), [The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom](source-atlas.md#g111).

## The diagnostic profession

**ALL · Play · High · 2/Extend.** Gap: observable faults, tests and bounded causal explanations.

A repairer listens, measures and asks about recent changes before deciding whether a pump needs cleaning or replacement. Different evidence supports different methods. Skilled play is learning what matters, with tools that improve inspection. Avoid fake investigation where every clue merely increments a hidden counter before the only permitted repair button unlocks.

Seeds: [Project Zomboid](source-atlas.md#g37), [The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt](source-atlas.md#g72), [Prey (2017)](source-atlas.md#g118).

## Craft as performance

**MD MO FA · Play · Try · 2/Extend.** Gap: optional timing/precision controls with equivalent accessible routes.

A potter can shape clay directly, describe a style, or commission a skilled partner. The hands-on mode offers expressive satisfaction without becoming a mandatory reflex gate for every household bowl. Material choices and design remain meaningful across control modes. Do not equate motor speed with the character's expertise or make accessibility settings erase economic viability.

Seeds: [Clair Obscur: Expedition 33](source-atlas.md#g77), [Kingdom Come: Deliverance II](source-atlas.md#g114), [Persona 5 Royal](source-atlas.md#g124).

## A profession that changes its clients

**ALL · Play · High · 2/Extend.** Gap: recurring service relationships and persistent improvements.

An itinerant tutor teaches enough arithmetic that shopkeepers eventually need fewer lessons and request new ones. Success changes the work instead of endlessly regenerating the same ignorance. Players can establish a school, train a successor or move on. The service should leave observable benefits, while former clients remain people with their own lives and disagreements.

Seeds: [Old School RuneScape](source-atlas.md#g49), [Assassin's Creed III](source-atlas.md#g99), [Fallout: New Vegas](source-atlas.md#g112).

## The prototype club

**ALL · Play · High · 2/Extend.** Gap: bounded trials, recorded outcomes and supported definition revision.

Makers meet to test one new device against ordinary problems: carry soup, open a high window, protect a seedling. Failures produce specific feedback: a friendly workshop can favor recoverable mishaps, while a dangerous prototype can cause real damage under the selected rules. Inventors get an audience and collaborators. Distinguish a simulated test from descriptive speculation, and never treat a successful demonstration as permission to deploy an unsafe or unsupported capability everywhere.

Seeds: [Dreams](source-atlas.md#g13), [Noita](source-atlas.md#g32), [The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom](source-atlas.md#g111).

## Work worth sharing credit for

**ALL · Blend · Try · 2/Extend.** Gap: contribution records and negotiated attribution.

A bridge needs surveying, cooking, hauling and persuasion as well as construction. A celebration can recognize different contributions without ranking everyone by damage or output. Creators can build cooperative projects with several useful roles. Keep attribution coarse and contestable where evidence is incomplete; measuring every minute can turn generosity into a competition for points.

Seeds: [Roblox](source-atlas.md#g04), [Dreams](source-atlas.md#g13), [Assassin's Creed III](source-atlas.md#g99).

## An inherited method questioned

**ALL · Play · Try · 2/Extend.** Gap: comparable outcomes and social knowledge transmission.

A bakery's customary proofing ritual may affect temperature, mark time, or simply bring people together. A curious apprentice tests the practical claim while respecting the social meaning. Players can refine a method without proving every tradition foolish or every old rule secretly magical. Separate observed effects, beliefs and chosen ceremonies so disagreement remains interesting.

Seeds: [Final Fantasy II](source-atlas.md#g79), [The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind](source-atlas.md#g113), [Earthsea](source-atlas.md#w11).

## A master who still learns

**ALL · Play · High · 1/Compose.** Gap: authored reciprocal teaching; capability learning requires supported paths.

An expert carpenter learns a folding joint from a traveling novice who grew up with portable homes. The expert contributes tools and structural judgment; the novice contributes a different problem history. Expertise becomes a network rather than a ladder with one top. Avoid universal masters who instantly know every variation, and let a lesson change later work visibly.

Seeds: [Fire Emblem: Three Houses](source-atlas.md#g64), [Final Fantasy IX](source-atlas.md#g86), [Star Trek](source-atlas.md#w04).
