# Traits, habits and changeable dispositions

**Canonical catalogue.** Descriptions and current priorities live together here. Each domain table is ordered by priority, then stable ID; implementation Level and build effort are not priority tie-breakers. These are proposals, not delivered features.

[Library](README.md) · [Shared classifications](README.md#reading-the-labels) · [Category ownership](README.md#category-ownership-and-cross-references) · [Selection and scale](selection-and-scale.md) · [Design foundation](design-foundation.md)

**Proposal catalogue · Group 3 expansion · 300 entries.** Traits are recognizable tendencies, tastes, standards and recurring ways of choosing. They are not diagnoses, moral scores, complete personalities or claims that a species, culture or childhood determines conduct. Select a few; let context, competing commitments and actual experience matter. A generous person can refuse, a cruel person can enjoy music, and a settled happy disposition need not conceal a wound.

**Ownership:** this file defines the continuing tendency. [Psychology](psychology-behavior.md) owns situational interpretation and response; [Needs](needs.md) owns desired conditions; [Characters](characters-backstories.md) owns particular biographies; [Relationships](relationships.md) owns bonds and agreements. Learned techniques belong in [Abilities](abilities-progression.md), bodily capacities in [Bodies](bodies-species.md), and supernatural effects in [Magic](magic.md). A temperament is neither a free capability grant nor authority over another person.

## Practical generosity

**Priority: 3 Depth.**


TR-021 retains the person who helps repair a rival's pump but declines a ceremonial banquet donation. Offer several useful forms of help and respect finite time; this is not a favor-farming mechanic. Inherited research links: [The Sims](source-atlas.md#g24), [Palworld](source-atlas.md#g41), [Discworld](source-atlas.md#w12).

Someone offers tools and labor readily but dislikes ceremonial gifts. During a shortage they may help repair a rival's pump while refusing an expensive banquet. Their generosity has a recognizable form and a real limit. This supports distinct companions without scoring “kindness”; circumstances and commitments can change their choice, and the player cannot farm identical favors forever.

Selection: Distinct help preferences enrich useful companions.

## Proud of the repair

**Priority: 4 Detail.**


TR-042 keeps visible repair as an individual aesthetic standard, with room for a customer who wants a hidden patch and a novice who admires the work. Criticism need not produce a feud. Inherited links: [Dwarf Fortress](source-atlas.md#g23), [Assassin's Creed III](source-atlas.md#g99), [Spirited Away](source-atlas.md#w25). G99 identifies Assassin's Creed III in the research roster; the earlier “Animal Crossing III” label was erroneous. These are thematic provenance links, not a claim that each source implements this exact trait.

A craftsperson keeps old patchwork visible because repairs record competence. They may argue with a customer who wants everything disguised as new, yet admire an ingenious novice. Let pride generate teaching, humor and occasional friction. Do not make every criticism a feud or every compliment a persuasion key; a good demonstration can matter more than flattery.

Selection: Repair-linked pride is optional character texture.

## Risk has a shape

**Priority: 3 Depth.**


TR-143 preserves the ridge-loving courier who fears family debt. Compare the actual risks and desired outcomes instead of reducing courage to one number. Inherited links: [Kenshi](source-atlas.md#g36), [Battle Brothers](source-atlas.md#g121), [Crusader Kings III](source-atlas.md#g126).

A courier calmly crosses exposed ridges but avoids borrowing money after a family business failure. One “bravery” value would erase the distinction. Players learn whom to trust with which task and can help revise a fear through evidence. Limit modeled domains to those that affect play; do not infer real psychological diagnoses from fictional habits.

Selection: Contextual risk differences enrich opponents and companions after they work.

## Keeps a little in reserve

**Priority: 4 Detail.**


TR-022 keeps guest supplies, festival use and emergency reserves in tension. Security can allow the habit to relax; no mandatory escalating stockpile. Inherited links: [RimWorld](source-atlas.md#g21), [Slay the Spire](source-atlas.md#g43), [Oxygen Not Included](source-atlas.md#g129).

A cook saves ingredients against unexpected guests, sometimes frustrating a workshop that needs the storage space. A reserve can become the thing that saves a festival. Show the person's reason and allow compromise, rather than making thrift a hidden efficiency modifier. A secure period may gradually relax the habit without erasing the character's history.

Selection: Personal reserve habits are not a core resource capability.

## Fluent in one room

**Priority: 3 Depth.**


TR-081 retains the mechanic who is witty in the workshop and awkward at a formal meeting. Demonstrations and familiar company can reveal competence without a universal charisma upgrade. Inherited links: [Fire Emblem: Three Houses](source-atlas.md#g64), [Disco Elysium](source-atlas.md#g66), [Austen](source-atlas.md#w23).

A mechanic who jokes easily with apprentices becomes formal at council meetings. Invite them to demonstrate a machine rather than force a speech; another character can translate institutional language. This opens multiple social methods. Confidence changes participation, not truth or worth, and fluency in public speaking must not become a universal measure of competence.

Selection: Contextual social confidence adds alternate social methods.

## Loves the unfinished

**Priority: 4 Detail.**


TR-045 keeps the inventor and maintainer's different pleasures, with actual handoff, credit and a bounded visible project set. The trait need not be cured into loving every stage of work. Inherited links: [G13](source-atlas.md#g13), [G16](source-atlas.md#g16), [The Sims](source-atlas.md#g24).

A builder starts exciting experiments, then loses interest once the solution is routine. Pairing with a patient maintainer can create a thriving workshop, or an unfair division of credit. Players can negotiate responsibilities instead of curing a flaw. Keep unfinished projects visible and bounded so the personality creates stories rather than an unmanageable mess of background jobs.

Selection: Project-handoff personality detail follows enjoyable projects.

## Ritual makes room

**Priority: 4 Detail.**


TR-183 retains boot cleaning as a transition into conversation and company. Compress familiar repetition when nothing changes; do not turn a harmless ritual into a diagnosis or mandatory buff. Inherited links: [Red Dead Redemption 2](source-atlas.md#g70), [Final Fantasy XV](source-atlas.md#g92), [The Sopranos](source-atlas.md#w22).

A traveler cleans their boots before a difficult conversation; the routine offers time to think and an opening for company. It need not grant a statistical bonus or indicate pathology. A friend can join, wait or gently interrupt when urgency matters. Repeated scenes should compress naturally while retaining exceptional moments, avoiding compulsory animation tax.

Selection: Boot-cleaning and similar routines are optional, never a play gate.

## Changes their mind carefully

**Priority: 3 Depth.**


TR-062 preserves an archivist's preference for independent witnesses after a damaging rumor. Useful caution can still delay action. Corrections reach actual recipients rather than every mind at once. Inherited links: [Among Us](source-atlas.md#g02), [Disco Elysium](source-atlas.md#g66), [Morrowind](source-atlas.md#g113).

An archivist resists rumors but revises a cherished story after two independent witnesses and a material clue. Players can persuade by investigating, not merely raising charisma. A cautious standard may also delay urgent help. The character can explain uncertainty; avoid absolute immunity to persuasion or instant updates from facts they never perceived.

Selection: Evidence-sensitive belief changes enrich investigation.

## A taste that connects strangers

**Priority: 3 Depth.**


TR-164 retains the inspector, drummer and badly painted bird cups. Shared taste can support an ordinary encounter without forcing friendship, romance, a quest or agreement on politics. Inherited links: [Stardew Valley](source-atlas.md#g30), [Persona 5 Royal](source-atlas.md#g124), [Star Wars](source-atlas.md#w03).

A stern harbor inspector and a young drummer both collect badly painted bird cups. Discovering the overlap can start a club or soften an awkward introduction without resolving a political dispute. Small pleasures make people more than functions. Interests should sometimes remain purely expressive; never require every hobby to unlock a quest, romance or numerical advantage.

Selection: Shared interests add memorable company without mandatory statistics.

## Contradiction under pressure

**Priority: 3 Depth.**


TR-203 preserves a fairness-minded organizer considering an exception for a sibling. Make the beneficiaries, costs and chosen response particular; contradiction is not an automatic villain arc. Inherited links: [Dragon Age II](source-atlas.md#g52), [Crusader Kings III](source-atlas.md#g126), [The Sopranos](source-atlas.md#w22).

A scrupulously fair organizer makes an exception for a sibling, then must answer the people affected. The contradiction emerges from loyalties and circumstances, not a random hypocrisy event. Players can defend, expose or help repair the decision. Keep the person capable of change and ordinary goodness; a trait combination must not predetermine betrayal or a villain arc.

Selection: Competing motives deepen established consequential choices.

## Selection guidance

Select a few useful dispositions, not a universal personality simulator. A person who finishes a working result (TR-041) and a representative opponent with an actual conflicting aim (TR-118/120/139/159/215) are Core. These are alternatives for filling real roles, not six mandatory villain systems. Native combat, goals and capabilities still determine what each person can actually do.

Challenge-seeking rivals, competent companions, practical teachers, interesting guides, merchants and hosts are Complete. TR-107 seeks worthy opponents; TR-157 returns to terrain that previously defeated them; TR-248 rejects empty practice; TR-298 lets a distinctive weapon encourage a learner. They support encounters, discovery, useful rewards and progress. A sophisticated appearance or difficult dependency does not lower a valuable role's product importance.

Music, comedy, art, food, conversation, playful courtship and peaceful enterprise retain legitimate early value. Traits that make those activities enjoyable can be Complete without granting a combat bonus. Mixed motives, nuanced loyalties, different tastes and most personal contradictions are Depth, to enrich a cast already doing things worth participating in.

Reserve keeping (TR-022), exact loan returns (TR-024), pride in patches (TR-042), fixing every wobble (TR-048), repairing rather than replacing (TR-054), cleaning boots (TR-183), rearranging chairs (TR-192), household checklists (TR-193) and handoff rehearsals (TR-241) are Detail. Their authored expression may be Small. Do not build upkeep or administrative machinery to give these tendencies something to react to before the first adventure works.

Most unfamiliar tastes are still ordinary Depth, even in a magical creature. Specialist is reserved here for separate synthetic/copying or reality-law premises and focused adult experiences outside the current game's foundation. Scores are not moral judgments, diagnoses or real-world political rankings. No disposition grants another person's participation, reveals private thoughts or bypasses a clear refusal; these remain constraints on every selected feature, not optional personality bonuses.
