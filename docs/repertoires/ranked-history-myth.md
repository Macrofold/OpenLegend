## Selection decisions

No complete historical vignette is a universal Core prerequisite. The opening game needs understandable places, goals, opponents and retained results, but not a fully simulated past or a history of every object. Select a few meaningful traces and tell the player enough to participate. The source IDs deliberately have no hyphen; HMY001–HMY276 are preserved exactly.

Inhabited ruins, material clues, useful old designs, route information and bounded witnesses are Complete. The lost prototype with a present use (HMY044), a ruin with a discoverable departure (HMY111), an exposed shoreline (HMY153), a lying dragon witness (HMY192), a borrowed-mask identification puzzle (HMY229) and imprisoned people inside a victory monument (HMY241) connect history to something worth exploring or resolving. The corpse interview (HMY230) and detached-shadow witness (HMY274) can support concrete mysteries when their admitted magical prerequisites exist; neither grants omniscience.

The defense that really succeeded (HMY053), a tyrant's genuine defeat (HMY088), a victory feast without a new emergency (HMY118), a joyful homecoming (HMY158) and a cure that ends a heroic institution's necessity (HMY257) are Complete. Progress can resolve a problem; history need not reveal every achievement as fraudulent or every enemy as misunderstood. Music, games, recovered recipes and creative reconstruction remain enjoyable reasons to engage with the past.

Signed repairs (HMY015), maintenance-credit reconstruction (HMY043), the return ledger (HMY217), summiter contribution records (HMY219), homecomings dated by repairs (HMY225) and inherited fuel tallies (HMY254) are Detail. Their small authored form need not be complex. Deeply layered family and civic interpretations follow as Depth; alternate timelines, copied towns and setting-specific industrial or alien histories remain Specialist. Historical authored rulesets are not permission to add legacy runtime compatibility. Source attribution, disclosure boundaries and the distinction between observed events and later interpretation remain intact.
