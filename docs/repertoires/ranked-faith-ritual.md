## Selection decisions

A militant chapter with an actual desire to dominate (FTH165) is a Core representative source of opposition. Ordinary enemies do not need a complete religious institution or theological simulation first. Choosing this particular faction still requires actual capabilities, a place, readable conduct and an attainable result; it does not impose a universal religion on the bundled world.

Interesting pilgrimages, occupied sanctuaries, recoverable relics, a guardian whose defeat grants instruction (FTH251), bounded curses and pacts, and a cult ritual that can actually be stopped (FTH205) are Complete. A war patron can really provide power and still give a cruel order (FTH238). Opposing it does not require exposing every miracle as a trick or discovering a benevolent hidden motive. Useful spells and protection remain owned by Magic, not supplied merely by ceremonial words.

The pilgrim's homecoming (FTH082), completion feast (FTH017), empty ward after a real cure (FTH258) and release from a defeated watcher's surveillance (FTH271) allow success to end a burden. Music, games, chosen observance, romance and beauty remain worthwhile activities. A prayer wall (FTH034) earns its place through concrete useful invitations, not a new task-agreement and reporting interface. Complete entries are alternatives, not a requirement to implement every rite.

Blessing a repair (FTH006), blankets taken for seasonal cleaning (FTH024), repair-counting beads (FTH043), road-mending observance (FTH092), shrine expense reports (FTH116), sacred-hat fit (FTH229), flame-relay attribution (FTH252), blessing-allocation administration (FTH257) and the gods' laundry celebration (FTH267) are Detail as their full additions. Their modest form can be Small; low importance does not imply high complexity. Actual healing, usable shelter, reliable transfers and clear promises still work wherever selected.

Richer traditions, disputed interpretation and divine relationships follow as Depth. Cross-time congregations, separate industrial or alien bodies, cosmic endings and dedicated unfamiliar metaphysics are Specialist for this survival-adventure. A real deity or supernatural afterlife remains an explicitly selected fictional premise, never a fact established by ranking a rite. Source meanings, historical evidence, privacy, valid participation and actual authority remain distinct; none of these product scores judges real faiths or their adherents.
