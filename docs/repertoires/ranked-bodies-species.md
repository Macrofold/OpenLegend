## Selection decisions

A usable human body, occupied hands, ordinary exertion/recovery and representative deer, wolf and boar profiles are Core. Select enough bodies for actual play; a full zoology or anatomy simulator is not a prerequisite. Creature anatomy does not grant an attack implementation, trained skill, hostility, cultural identity or allegiance. Combat and the encounter owners must supply a readable, rewarding situation.

Mounts, useful animal companions, predators with different approaches, natural protection and a coherent choice of fantasy bodies are Complete. A regeneration rule (BSP-158), a sun-constrained night body (BSP-159), a skeleton (BSP-160) or a bounded mimic (BSP-255) can support recognizable enemy variety without waiting for domestic simulation. The heat-storage dragon (BSP-157) is Depth as the full reservoir-based profile; this must not delay a simpler formidable dragon encounter.

Alternative movement, climbing, gliding and a bridge-spinning animal (BSP-253) can open rewarding destinations. The listed accommodations and differing body dimensions are Complete alternatives for the cast selected, not an optional waiver of access: whenever a body is playable, its controls, routes, equipment and communication must actually work. Do not add unsupported bodies merely to fill a diversity checklist.

Localized bruising (BSP-021), calluses (BSP-011), grooming/coat changes (BSP-082/161), detailed blood depletion (BSP-244), regional cold (BSP-247), lingering body heat (BSP-249) and nutrient accounting beyond a full stomach (BSP-250) are Detail. Basic harm, survival, rest and recovery remain earlier requirements. These detailed states must not turn each expedition into obligatory treatment or maintenance. Small surface or fit changes need not be complex systems.

Life-cycle care, synthetic citizens, radically different atmospheres and distributed personhood are Specialist in this survival-adventure selection, not claims about the value of real people or permanent exclusions from the library. Distinctive anatomy and transformation can be Depth without every impossible body being Frontier. Current source limitations, privacy, participation and identity continuity remain intact.
