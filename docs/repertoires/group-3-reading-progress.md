## Findings and evidence boundaries

[Earlier findings](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/a96a30ad564758233ec033f8a3f350fe8a0bf782/docs/repertoires/group-3-reading-progress.md#findings-and-evidence-boundaries) preserve Conan and both Forest games and link preceding synthesis.

**Subnautica:** curiosity can compete with self-preservation without making either motive insincere. Familiar landmarks, sounds and safe interiors can transform fear into competence. A distant stranger's gift can make solitude feel inhabited without an actual meeting. An expedition's seed or specimen can become a cherished part of home. Reliable infrastructure can genuinely retire a need and leave attention for wonder. All fifteen cases, oxygen pipes, filtration/recharging, Time Capsules and approval limits, vehicle distinctions, original/sequel boundaries and complete source register were read. An authored ecology is not an audited population simulation; fiction's cure is not medical evidence.

**Don't Starve Together:** different appetites, comforts and strengths create cooperation through difference rather than uniformity. Rescue can cost the rescuer without purchasing future obedience. An expert can welcome a once-frightening condition while a newcomer reasonably fears it. A repeated favor can be affectionate, strategic or both; rewards can displace the original motive. Mistakes can become funny shared history or serious resentment. All fifteen cases, Insight persistence, Woby's embodied courier work, fishing, Pearl's friendship, regrowth, mode-specific death and complete source register were read. Sanity/Nightmare Fuel is an authored supernatural rule, not a human clinical claim; beta and stable content remain distinct.

**Raft:** the same place can be home, livelihood and travel, keeping memories close while circumstances change. Improving a layout can feel like losing an earlier chapter even when resources survive. Roles can grow from preferred tasks instead of permanent class labels. Solving survival can reveal that someone wants decoration, leisure or continued company rather than another grand objective. All eight cases, research versus blueprint acquisition, six cosmetic identities, difficulty-specific rescue, current-versus-early-access reviews and complete source/evidence limits were read. Human co-op behavior is not evidence of autonomous NPC biographies; dated financial testimony and platform claims were not freshly reverified.

Extrapolations are **creative synthesis**, not franchise occurrences or implemented capabilities. Competence, passion, trust, affection, consent and allegiance differ. Satisfied goals and ordinary pleasures need no compulsory replacement craving or sinister twist. No gameplay, clinical validation, representative sentiment study, exhaustive external-bibliography reread or fresh verification of dated external claims is asserted.
