## Findings and evidence boundaries

The [previous findings](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/b3bb7c50078d7478f3baee5a0ad4828caa318ea9/docs/repertoires/group-3-reading-progress.md#findings-and-evidence-boundaries) retain Dragon's Dogma 2, Ultima VII and Oxygen Not Included and link earlier synthesis.

**S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2:** practical confidence can grow without a level increase. An anticipated profitable expedition can become a need to get home, and an expensive favorite can complicate that choice. Familiar services, sounds and company make refuge specific. A former ally's hostility can remove a mundane convenience before the next dramatic confrontation. The complete eight cases, gear/knowledge progression, versioned A-Life repairs, source limitations, expansion-start versus NG+ boundary and source register were read. Technical failure is not meaningful hardship; the dossier's dated update, production and release claims were not freshly verified here.

**Rust:** temporary ownership can support pride, attachment and shared history without a promise of permanence. A known ending can encourage celebration, experimentation or reckless spending instead of further accumulation. A farmer, trader or electrician can derive identity from contribution without a locked class. Helping maintain a public resource can benefit rivals; distrust can coexist with genuine generosity. Full current workbench/public-infrastructure/fishing/gambling additions, twelve cases, server/mode boundaries, five reviews and source register were read. Loss can also cause disengagement, and actual players' harassment is not a desirable design requirement.

**DayZ:** preparedness can become a burden when it prevents escape. A quiet place can evoke comfort, boredom or fear depending on what someone carries and remembers. A small act of assistance can acquire disproportionate personal meaning; scarce supplies can test whether a promise was merely cheap speech. A group's home can outlive a person without replacing that person's history. Complete survival/compatibility systems, eleven cases, expanded flag/persistence rules, stable/experimental/map boundaries and all sources were read. Fictional diagnosis and treatment are not medical advice; hostile interactions are not assumed to end in either betrayal or reconciliation.

All proposed catalogue extrapolations are **creative synthesis**, not franchise occurrences or implemented OpenLegend capabilities. Competence, passion, trust, affection, consent and allegiance remain distinct. Satisfied goals and ordinary pleasures need no compulsory replacement craving or sinister twist. No gameplay, live-model experiment, clinical validation, representative sentiment survey, exhaustive external-bibliography reread or fresh verification of dated external claims is asserted.
