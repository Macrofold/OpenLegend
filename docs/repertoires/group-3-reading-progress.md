## Findings and evidence boundaries

[Earlier findings](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/f3592f7747a1480e409663da3a79b15b87b78d2f/docs/repertoires/group-3-reading-progress.md#findings-and-evidence-boundaries) preserve both ARK editions and 7 Days to Die and link preceding synthesis.

**Conan Exiles:** power can change what a person is willing to give up without erasing an earlier attachment. Prosperity can bring anxiety as well as comfort. An inhabited workshop is not simply a stationary production modifier. Coercion, dependence, obedience, loyalty and affection must remain distinct: the source's enslavement is not recast as voluntary friendship. The complete thirteen cases, Journey objectives, travel, Siptah's deliberately loosened dependency chain, living settlements, sorcery and full source register were read. Archive guidance against a default slavery mechanic does not exclude fictional coercion from this adult idea library. Enhanced/Legacy, future content, commercial and technical claims remain dated repository evidence, not independently reverified facts.

**The Forest:** watching, circling, bluffing, hesitation and retreat can create an encounter before attack. Familiar company can turn frightening work into shared humor without changing the danger itself. A home can feel safer even while its light makes it more visible. A desired rescue can collide with responsibility for others. All twelve cases, original/sequel/VR boundaries, final source register and correction to use-shaped Strength/Athleticism were read. The narrow Sanity-to-effigy relationship and conflicting thresholds are not a general theory of mental health; the corrected closure qualifies older shorthand denying attributes.

**Sons of the Forest:** task delegation and voluntary assistance create different expectations. Useful help can be imperfect, and repeated treatment can alter later availability without a visible approval score. Removing routine scarcity can permit a wanted life rather than destroy meaning. An accomplished builder may care little about the story that motivated arrival. The complete fifteen cases, final source register, Strength correction, companion-sentiment limits, electrical system, Solafite plating/storm, seasons, Artifact and patch/security distinction were read. A task interface does not make a person an object, and an exposed AI variable is not proof of every proposed behavioral effect.

Catalogue extrapolations are **creative synthesis**, not franchise occurrences or implemented OpenLegend capabilities. Competence, passion, trust, affection, consent and allegiance differ. Ordinary pleasures and satisfied goals need no compulsory replacement craving or sinister twist. No gameplay, clinical validation, representative sentiment study, exhaustive external-bibliography reread or fresh verification of dated external claims is asserted.
