## Findings carried into the pending expansion

**Everyday individuality:** Animal Crossing distinguishes hobby, personality and remembered conduct. The Sims separates maintenance, meaningful care, emotion and productivity; daily autonomy is not permission for every irreversible life change. Rooms, gifts, food and routines can matter without optimization bonuses or disaster foreshadowing.

**Work and consequence:** RimWorld distinguishes skill, incapability, refusal and passion; an attacker can retain identity through custody and recruitment without recruitment being obligatory. Dwarf Fortress separates event, memory, interpretation, practical object value, prestige and attachment. Both warn that more variables need not make people emotionally convincing. Their famous stories are attributed accounts, not our sessions or proof of animal gratitude. Care after victory is actual activity; stability can be desirable, and boredom remains a legitimate response.

**Power and agency:** CKIII makes succession, secrets, obligations and incompatible loyalties consequential without proving traits should force actions. D&D separates willingness, authority and probability; routine competence from risky attempts; independent allies from employees, coerced servants and sentient equipment. A created person is not necessarily perpetual property. A new body does not automatically replace a personality. Successful abundance should change aims rather than invite invented scarcity to cancel it.

**Lives beyond one ending:** The formation essay and revised world studies distinguish achievement, safety, recognition, love, justice and reconciliation. The Shire's recovery can coexist with Frodo's wounds. Wizarding education, comic enterprise and competition can work alongside deliberate cruelty. Star Wars separates a child's victory from his mother's unachieved freedom, and a prison revolt from every participant's escape. Star Trek permits working recreation, private desire, enjoyment within a defeat, separation despite love, and strategically successful deception that remains lethal and coercive.

**Extraordinary ability need not choose a vocation:** Marvel's performer can decline a fighting career, and a capable investigator has work and desire beyond victimization. DC's escape artistry is also show business; its marriages, gifts and professional successes need not be destroyed to be interesting. Identity concealment, inability to become anonymous, inherited public roles and private bodily enjoyment have different consequences. A successful rescue need not restore family acceptance; a character's own account of love is not proof of the other's consent.

**Contrasting desires, not a tone quota:** The Witcher retains paid performance, bounded intimacy, work, friendship, real military success and unremedied loss; book events, game branches and intended future acts remain separate. Pokémon permits accomplished competitors and public noncombat mastery without making every collection a hidden dystopia. Dune links environmental expertise, public acclaim, personal art, collective ritual and unequal outcomes; foresight is not permission to control another person's body. Ice and Fire preserves a feast that provides, a contest genuinely won, consensual role-play distinct from imposed marriage, and a partial escape without inventing the novels' unresolved ending. No source requires every pleasure to become a trap or every antagonist to accept reconciliation.

**Local magic, particular people:** D&D distinguishes professional expertise from adventuring rank, hospitality from ownership of guests, translation from belonging, and inherited regional magic from its creators' motives. Its gala is a recurring institution; Holga's visit is a particular film encounter; Death House has conditional adventure outcomes. The lore supplement's eight indexed wiki articles are not an entire-wiki read. Beauty can be genuinely desirable while departure has real costs. A scholar, farmer, courier and ruler can understand the same phenomenon and want different changes.

New situations must be labeled **creative synthesis**, not represented as source-established events or mechanics. Clinical validity, commercial causation, exhaustive primary-source reading and playtesting are not claimed.
