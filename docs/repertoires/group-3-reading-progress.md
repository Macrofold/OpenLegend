## Findings and evidence boundaries

[Earlier findings](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/ac4b0576efa51b5ec00ceadbb97866ccda9043d8/docs/repertoires/group-3-reading-progress.md#findings-and-evidence-boundaries) preserve Grounded, The Long Dark and Green Hell and link preceding synthesis.

**Enshrouded:** home can be valuable through comfort, company and useful work without perpetual attack. Repairing an old place can preserve its history rather than demand a clean replacement. A shared world does not guarantee shared experience: someone may miss what mattered to companions. A visitor's mastery can be welcomed help or an unwanted shortcut. Full eight cases, revised starvation settings and NPC schedules, water, sharing, character/world ownership, source register and live-versus-future boundaries were read. Simple roaming is not autonomous labor, and dated 1.0 announcements were not independently reverified.

**V Rising:** appetite, taste, bodily vulnerability and personal ambition can interact without being the same trait. Protecting someone because they are useful is not valuing their freedom. A grand house can express pride while exposing its owner to risk. Clear objectives can relieve uncertainty and still become tedious on repetition. Full eight cases, Human Form, vendors, revised Hunt outcomes, configurable loss, current-support boundaries and all sources were read. Domination, misery management and extraction retain their coercive meaning; source worker abstractions are not universal claims about personhood.

**Once Human:** preserving possessions can fail to preserve the community that gave them meaning. One person can prefer continuity while another wants a fresh beginning; neither preference needs a moral verdict. A profession can change how someone attends to food, plants or animals without fixing their combat role. A strange being's appearance should suggest meaningful behavior, not only a renamed ordinary task. Full eight cases, Tech/blueprint/calibration changes, mode-specific stealth and loss, permanent-server changes, thirty-four-source register and future/test boundaries were read. September 28 announcements remain dated source claims, not freshly verified delivery; fictional contamination and body-type rules are not clinical descriptions.

Extrapolations are **creative synthesis**, not franchise occurrences or implemented capabilities. Competence, passion, trust, affection, consent and allegiance differ. Satisfied goals and ordinary pleasures need no compulsory replacement craving or sinister twist. No gameplay, clinical validation, representative sentiment study, exhaustive external-bibliography reread or fresh verification of dated external claims is asserted.
