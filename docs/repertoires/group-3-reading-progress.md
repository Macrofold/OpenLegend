## Findings carried into the pending expansion

**Everyday individuality:** Animal Crossing distinguishes hobby, personality and remembered conduct. The Sims separates maintenance, meaningful care, emotion and productivity; daily autonomy is not permission for every irreversible life change. Rooms, gifts, food and chosen routines can matter without becoming optimization bonuses or disaster foreshadowing.

**Work and consequence:** RimWorld distinguishes skill, incapability, refusal and passion; an attacker can retain identity through custody and recruitment without recruitment being obligatory. Dwarf Fortress separates an event, its memory, interpretation, practical object value, prestige and attachment. Both warn that more variables need not create emotionally convincing people. Their famous colony stories are attributed accounts, not our sessions or proof of animal gratitude. Care after victory is actual activity; a stable life can be desirable, and boredom remains a legitimate response.

**Power and agency:** CKIII makes succession, secrets, obligations and incompatible loyalties consequential without proving that traits should force actions. D&D distinguishes willingness, authority and probability; routine competence from risky attempts; independent allies from employees, coerced servants and sentient equipment. A created person is not necessarily perpetual property. A new body does not automatically replace a personality. Successful abundance should change aims rather than invite invented scarcity to cancel it.

**Lives beyond one ending:** The revised formation essay and world dossiers keep achievement, safety, recognition, love, justice and reconciliation distinct. Bilbo's celebration, the Shire's genuine recovery and Frodo's continuing wounds can coexist. The Wizarding World preserves useful teaching, entrepreneurial comedy and chosen competition alongside deliberate cruelty; Merope's potion history remains an in-world reconstruction, not a newly witnessed event. Star Wars distinguishes a child's thrilling victory from his mother's unachieved freedom, and a prison revolt from every participant's escape. Star Trek permits working recreation, private desire, defeat enjoyed differently, separation despite love, and a successful deception that remains coercive and lethal. No one outcome becomes compulsory for every world.

**Local magic, particular people:** The D&D studies distinguish professional expertise from adventuring rank, hospitality from ownership of guests, translation from belonging, and inherited regional magic from its creators' motives. The gala is a recurring institution; Holga's visit is a particular film encounter; Death House supplies conditional adventure outcomes. The lore supplement's eight indexed wiki articles are not an entire-wiki read. Beauty in a strange region can be genuinely desirable while departure has real costs. A scholar, farmer, courier and ruler can understand the same local phenomenon yet want different changes.

New catalogue situations must be labeled **creative synthesis**, not represented as events or mechanics established by these sources. Clinical validity, commercial causation and playtesting are not claimed.
