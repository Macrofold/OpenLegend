## Findings and evidence boundaries

The [preceding findings register](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/c324cdf1cf2b46887e60ed5dd31f5ec2aa2410fc/docs/repertoires/group-3-reading-progress.md#findings-and-evidence-boundaries) preserves Mass Effect Galaxy/Infiltrator and both Octopath findings and links all preceding checkpoints. Previous synthesis remains accessible at immutable revisions.

**Knights of the Old Republic:** taking a companion for their company rather than maximum efficiency can be a coherent choice. A shared ship acquires small consequences of travel: missing supplies, an unexpected passenger, a practical nuisance. A useful specialist can conserve supplies without owning the only possible solution. Starting expertise can survive a later identity change; a decisive change of allegiance need not erase competence. Favorable judgment, truth, reward and personal standards differ. The complete nine-companion inventory, individual versus party skills, bounded supply services, original/port/Yavin distinctions, six examples, repaired all-time player evidence and 36-source register were read. The ship is not a fully simulated household; cinematic duels do not establish action-game controls.

**Knights of the Old Republic II:** reliance on a mentor need not imply agreement with that mentor. Witnessed conduct, conversation and outside testimony can have different effects on a particular relationship. Knowing a person's history can open a new possibility without turning every conversation into a reward dispenser. A group can depend too heavily on its leader and discover that during separation. Repeated preparation can be satisfying judgment or empty administration once its answer is known. The complete base/port/restoration/canceled-DLC boundaries, corrected Force Chain and Security rules, influence caveats, six constructed situations, contemporary postmortem, later source-access limits and 43-source register were read. Historical instructions to stop after G110 describe the source's old assignment, not this task. The mentor's arguments are a character's position, not a compulsory moral conclusion.

Catalogue extrapolations are **creative synthesis**, not copied lore, clinical prediction, causal popularity proof or current implementation claims. Competence is not passion; support is not consent; victory, survival, freedom, restitution, forgiveness and reconciliation differ. Ordinary pleasures, deliberate harm and completed goals need no compulsory redemption or replacement craving. Human play, authored characters, simulated rules and canceled-game intentions remain distinct evidence. Historical source text never overrides current AGENTS instructions.
