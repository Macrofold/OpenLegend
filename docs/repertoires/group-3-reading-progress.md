## Findings and evidence boundaries

[Earlier findings](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/396dc0f4ed55844305f6143ecb87ab39973b939d/docs/repertoires/group-3-reading-progress.md#findings-and-evidence-boundaries) preserve Subnautica, Don't Starve Together and Raft and link preceding synthesis.

**Grounded:** familiar objects can become intimidating at a new scale, then familiar again through repeated use. Different voices and preferences can individualize people with equivalent capabilities. Attachment to a pet need not depend on its mortality, and a cosmetic personality label does not establish autonomous goals. Knowing a place can differ from knowing what one wants to do next. All eight cases, original/sequel distinctions, pets, mutations, Molars, Shared Worlds, Playgrounds, dated patch boundaries and complete source register were read. The authored teen cast is not a template for adult sexual entries; new biographies remain original. A source's old story-reset practice does not override OpenLegend's current save policy.

**The Long Dark:** mechanical success can conceal a bad strategic choice, such as using a scarce resource at the wrong moment. An item can embody a remembered expedition as well as future options. Solitude can be contemplative or lonely without imposing one response. Preserving a meaningful history can motivate revising a self-imposed rule. Waiting can be deliberate action rather than indecision. Full clothing, time, skills, eight cases, optional Cheat Death correction, Survival/WINTERMUTE/TALES distinctions and all sources were read. Old absolute permadeath language is qualified by the source's later correction; fictional injury/treatment is not medical advice.

**Green Hell:** noticing distress, identifying its cause and selecting a response are distinct steps. A familiar cue can change from inexplicable danger into useful information. A competent group can still contain individual vulnerabilities; shared resources do not make everyone's needs identical. Care can be temporary work, an enduring vocation or an unwelcome responsibility. All eight cases, thirteen use-based skills, limb armor, pottery, notebook knowledge, mode-specific Trust, husbandry, version boundaries and full source register were read. Fictional bodily and Sanity mechanics are not clinical evidence, and particular fictional cultures are not treated as generic enemies.

Extrapolations are **creative synthesis**, not franchise occurrences or implemented capabilities. Competence, passion, trust, affection, consent and allegiance differ. Satisfied goals and ordinary pleasures need no compulsory replacement craving or sinister twist. No gameplay, clinical validation, representative sentiment study, exhaustive external-bibliography reread or fresh verification of dated external claims is asserted.
