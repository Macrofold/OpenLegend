## Findings and evidence boundaries

The [preceding findings](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/f34704a8fc561b76cebe2ad34b797699763de4e5/docs/repertoires/group-3-reading-progress.md#findings-and-evidence-boundaries) preserve Rain World, Persona 5 Royal and XCOM 2 and link earlier synthesis.

**Dragon's Dogma 2:** keeping a familiar companion can conflict with replacing them for greater effectiveness. Another person's learned route can feel like companionship rather than an impersonal marker. Inclination changes the kind of assistance someone offers, but help can still miss the recipient's intent. A sequence of victories can leave a party needing rest. The full vocation/Pawn/travel inventory, eight cases, dated save/skill/plague changes, future-expansion boundary, six reviews and source register were read. These dated claims were not freshly verified; asynchronous Pawn exchange is not human co-op, and ordinary affinity is not a complete simulated relationship.

**Ultima VII:** individuality can emerge through a particular home, occupation, companions and ordinary daily routines. Watching someone can provide evidence that differs from what they say. A heroic title need not prevent selfish acts when possessions remain physically accessible. Hunger makes an ordinary meal matter during an extraordinary task. Assistance from a troubling institution can answer a genuine need without establishing agreement with its doctrine. Full creation, schedules, object interactions, eight cases, recovery, original/expansion/port/reimplementation boundaries and historical criticism were read. Source knowledge gates are not claims that players or characters should know unseen facts.

**Oxygen Not Included:** skill, expectations and satisfaction can change separately. A useful new colleague adds needs as well as productive capacity. Delayed consequences can change how someone interprets an earlier proud achievement. A clever fix may create another problem, but perpetual replacement problems can make achievement feel futile. Detailed physiology and task scheduling do not automatically create interpersonal depth. Full physical/labor systems, eight cases, expansion and cosmetic boundaries, five version-labeled reviews and sources were read. Fictional morale rules are not human psychological laws; no spaceflight is imported into Veyra Reach.

Extrapolations are **creative synthesis**, not copied lore, clinical prediction, popularity proof or implemented capability. Competence is not passion; support is not consent; survival, victory, freedom, restitution, forgiveness and reconciliation differ. Ordinary pleasures, deliberate harm and satisfied goals need no compulsory redemption or replacement craving. Human play, authored characters, simulated rules and canceled-game intentions remain distinct. Historical source instructions do not govern this assignment. No personal gameplay, live-model experiment, proprietary-code inspection, representative sentiment survey, exhaustive external-bibliography reread or fresh verification of dated release/commercial claims is asserted.
