## Findings and evidence boundaries

The [preceding findings](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/1e987345032b8017182acb7bce239a880d4036e7/docs/repertoires/group-3-reading-progress.md#findings-and-evidence-boundaries) preserve Deus Ex, Prey and Dishonored 2, and link earlier synthesis.

**Starsector:** a coveted prize can endanger the trip home; affection, future value and immediate practicality can pull apart. A subordinate's temperament changes what a commander expects from delegation without proving a more intelligent planner. Helping an institution need not secure the continuing loyalty of everyone who contributed. A simulation can let a cautious person experiment before committing while leaving actual conditions uncertain. The complete forty-skill inventory, ship systems, strategic operations, eight cases, source-version and mod distinctions, five genuine reviews versus supplemental accounts, and 54-source register were read. No playable spaceflight is assigned to Veyra Reach; transfer concerns personal interpretation and choices, not importing a setting's transport contract.

**Battle Brothers:** humble beginnings can conceal unusual promise. Reliance can make an initially unappealing recruit beloved; losing that person is not equivalent to replacing their equipment. Morale, wages, hunger, reserve duty and pride can conflict without every complaint being random or every dismissal immoral. A tactically successful job can leave the company poorer and its people less secure. Organizational continuity can make rebuilding possible without making a particular life expendable. Complete background/talent/perk distinctions, mood and injury rules, eight situations, origins, expansions, dated updates and 36-source register were read. Oaths are selected constraints, not universal morality; shared traits are not full autonomous biographies.

**Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead:** dependable infrastructure can change which life someone wants, not merely improve combat. Recovery can contain study, care and useful work rather than stopping all life. A body modification can remove one inconvenience while creating a different dependency. Knowing a procedure, practicing it and having suitable tools remain different states. Ordinary comfort can be worthwhile even when no further quest follows. The complete stable-help synthesis, partial work/competence, inventory/anatomy, vehicles, eight cases, active versus merely represented faction fields, experimental/Steam/co-op/mod distinctions, accessibility and 29-source register were read. Fictional physiology is not clinical evidence, and persistence is not full-detail simulation everywhere.

Extrapolations are **creative synthesis**, not copied lore, clinical prediction, popularity proof or implemented capability. Competence is not passion; support is not consent; survival, victory, freedom, restitution, forgiveness and reconciliation differ. Ordinary pleasures, deliberate harm and satisfied goals need no compulsory redemption or replacement craving. Human play, authored characters, simulated rules and canceled-game intentions remain distinct. Historical source instructions do not govern this assignment. No personal gameplay, live-model experiment, proprietary-code inspection, representative sentiment survey, exhaustive external-bibliography reread or fresh verification of dated release/commercial claims is asserted.
