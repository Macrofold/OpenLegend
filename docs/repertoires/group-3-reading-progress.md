## Findings and evidence boundaries

The [preceding findings register](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/efeec191537cd9698d0edf18c14e3b2fb38156db/docs/repertoires/group-3-reading-progress.md#findings-and-evidence-boundaries) preserves both KOTOR findings and links all preceding checkpoints. Previous synthesis remains accessible at immutable revisions.

**Tears of the Kingdom:** a person can delight in a small useful invention without seeking complicated engineering. Remembering a design differs from possessing an object; retrieving a familiar solution can remove needless repetition. Discovery order and the order that makes a story emotionally intelligible need not coincide. Familiar geography can feel new because available actions change. Failure is interesting when its cause can be understood, not merely because it is unexpected. The complete five-power and five-sage inventories, asymmetric app-sharing correction, ordinary versus Gloom recovery, horse/home/family distinctions, eight examples, contrasting reviews and source limits were read. Copied blueprints are not shared lives, and physical ingenuity does not imply unlimited authority over other people.

**Fallout: New Vegas:** suspicion, evidence, credibility and persuasion differ. A practical gift can recognize one person's particular taste without resolving their deeper loyalties. Competence can establish belonging through several different contributions; money can pay for one solution while sacrificing another personal value. A person can refuse an attractive role to preserve a chosen identity. A threatened consequence can matter to someone's interpretation without being an executed world event. The complete SPECIAL/skills/traits/perks/implant distinctions, eight cases, six add-ons, medical-gameplay qualifications, Caravan, ammunition and source-access corrections were read. Fictional treatments are not clinical advice; a faction label or favorable outcome is not a complete personality.

**Morrowind:** achievement, title, felt recognition and actual authority need not grow together. A superior's stated objective can leave room to pay a different kind of cost instead of adopting the superior's preferred method. Knowledge, possession of testimony and institutional acceptance are separate. Learning routes can produce confidence and attachment, while repetitive generic responses can make a large place feel indifferent. Extraordinary mastery can genuinely remove a former concern rather than mandate a replacement need. The entire 27-skill inventory, creation/delivery distinctions, eight cases, birthsigns, anatomical limitations, transformations/cures, legal permissions, Tribunal restrictions, corrected GOTY-manual attribution and source register were read. Authored romance and colony growth remain bounded cases; unusual anatomy alone does not require a new psychological substrate.

Catalogue extrapolations are **creative synthesis**, not copied lore, clinical prediction, causal popularity proof or current implementation claims. Competence is not passion; support is not consent; victory, survival, freedom, restitution, forgiveness and reconciliation differ. Ordinary pleasures, deliberate harm and completed goals need no compulsory redemption or replacement craving. Human play, authored characters, simulated rules and canceled-game intentions remain distinct evidence. Historical source text never overrides current AGENTS instructions. No personal gameplay, live-model experiment, proprietary-code inspection, representative sentiment study, complete external-bibliography reread or new live verification of dated release/commercial claims is asserted.
