## Findings and evidence boundaries

The [preceding findings](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/88941a4a91671ac223182e35b02e83e3c6bf8947/docs/repertoires/group-3-reading-progress.md#findings-and-evidence-boundaries) preserve Starsector, Battle Brothers and Cataclysm and link earlier synthesis.

**Rain World:** another creature's hunger, fear, curiosity or search for shelter can matter more to it than the player's presence. Familiarity can turn apparent chaos into recognizable opportunity without increasing a body's statistics. An ally can protect someone and still accidentally obstruct or endanger them. Carrying a prized object can compete with immediate safety, and curiosity can compete with getting home. The complete object/creature/movement inventory, eight cases, Karma versus knowledge progression, authored campaign bodies, Gourmand crafting, revised local-co-op and expansion boundaries, reviews and source limitations were read. Brutality is not a universal recipe for attachment, and creature trust is not automatically human-style consent or dialogue.

**Persona 5 Royal:** relationships, livelihood, pleasure, learning and a larger mission can compete for the same evening. A meeting can be sincerely desired and strategically useful at once; rewards can also displace the original reason for caring. Ordinary routines can acquire meaning through later shared action. A promise of happiness can conflict with a person's wish to choose an imperfect life. The entire calendar/combat/fusion/relationship inventory, eight cases, corrected Maruki-only semester gate versus other scene deadlines, network and no-building boundaries, review disagreement and source register were read. School-age characters are not templates for adult sexual catalogue entries; new adult-life examples are original synthesis, not imported biographies.

**XCOM 2:** reliance can turn a generated name into a particular person with remembered successes and failures. Protecting a veteran can conflict with a mission, while injuries redistribute responsibility across a group. Affection and practical dependence overlap without being identical. A trusted pair can enable a joint act unavailable to either alone; fatigue makes continued competence distinct from readiness to serve again. Complete class/equipment/Avenger systems, eight cases, base versus WotC bonds/fatigue, mods, dated service/launcher changes, criticism and sources were read. Fictional panic/negative traits are not clinical diagnoses; loss need not be copied as a compulsory source of meaning.

Extrapolations are **creative synthesis**, not copied lore, clinical prediction, popularity proof or implemented capability. Competence is not passion; support is not consent; survival, victory, freedom, restitution, forgiveness and reconciliation differ. Ordinary pleasures, deliberate harm and satisfied goals need no compulsory redemption or replacement craving. Human play, authored characters, simulated rules and canceled-game intentions remain distinct. Historical source instructions do not govern this assignment. No personal gameplay, live-model experiment, proprietary-code inspection, representative sentiment survey, exhaustive external-bibliography reread or fresh verification of dated release/commercial claims is asserted.
