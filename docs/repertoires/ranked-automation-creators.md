## Selection decisions

The first-session boundary, a complete visitor route, an actual rival and a finite opposing installation are Core. ACR-101/103/192 provide goals, methods and results rather than a camp-maintenance checklist. ACR-135/230 check the newcomer's real starting position and equipment instead of assuming an expert demonstration proves the beginning playable. Their selection is a design target, not a claim that these authoring tools or playtests already exist.

Known work must really execute, completed actions must stop, failures must be interpretable, and the game must continue honestly when novel AI judgment is unavailable. These are Core foundations. They do not require a general-purpose creator platform, every background citizen's psychology or a city-scale supply simulation. Sharing rooms, sophisticated cast generation, aggregate populations and editor remixes can follow as Depth. ACR-200 is Complete as its full packaged creation: its former Frontier label must not hide the value of an actually finished playable experience.

Useful companions, bounded missions, learning kits, meaningful equipment comparisons, readable recovery choices and repeatable challenges are Complete. So are real games, creative exhibitions, performances, gardening and places worth enjoying. ACR-078 preserves the manual part a person likes; automation should remove unwanted repetition, not the enjoyable activity. ACR-177 makes completed construction useful rather than immediately generating another demand.

Tomorrow's meal reserves (ACR-001/011), opening shutters (ACR-002), laundry (ACR-003), tool returns (ACR-004), maintenance spares (ACR-031), repair routing (ACR-033), workstation appointments (ACR-034), watch handoffs (ACR-051), completion checklists (ACR-058), project handovers (ACR-171), service-maintenance ownership (ACR-172) and restoration-price planning (ACR-251) are Detail. Their Small or Moderate scope remains separate. An inventory, a bag, a useful companion and a basic recovery action do not need these workflows to become usable.

The first-matching-rule inspector (ACR-257) is Complete, not a requirement to build a policy programming environment before ordinary actions work. Its explanation of a hidden rule is useful once such routines exist. ACR-168's historical-version proposal remains subject to the [root development policy](../../AGENTS.md#development-save-policy): no legacy runtime, save or compatibility support is authorized. Authored definition revisions and explicit transitions do not override that rule. Offline editions and alternative world continuities remain Specialist.

Privacy, authority, independent participation, actual resource accounting and isolated rehearsals remain mandatory whenever the corresponding capability is selected. An optional platform feature's later score never authorizes incorrect behavior. These ranks choose fictional gameplay and authoring possibilities, not real-world autonomy, spending or access permissions.
