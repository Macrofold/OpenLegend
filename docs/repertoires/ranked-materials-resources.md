## Selection decisions

A short, useful resource chain comes first: timber, shaft and bow wood, bindings, hide, feathers, common building stone, metal, fuel, usable water and basic recovery cloth. These Core rows are representative alternatives for actual outputs, not a requirement to implement every material or simulate microscopic properties. Water access is Core only when the selected consumption/cooking rules need it. Carried ration cakes (MAT-249) have a concrete use on a dangerous route; they do not require an additional expedition-planning interface.

Useful food, cultivation, protection, tools, construction, art supplies and attainable upgrades are Complete. Material-dependent exploration and counters can earn that tier too: revealing ink (MAT-257), countertone salt (MAT-259), relic consolidation (MAT-262) and a consumable learning seed (MAT-299). These do not adopt a magic system automatically; they are later compatible choices with an identifiable payoff. A returned reward claim (MAT-296) connects exploration to a real result rather than another administrative activity for its own sake.

Sharpening stone (MAT-065), patch offcuts (MAT-055), bedding straw (MAT-025), spare seals (MAT-174), specialist repair bundles (MAT-289), commemorative repair enamel (MAT-283) and staffed recovery capacity (MAT-260) are Detail as their full described maintenance or management additions. Ordinary sharpening and patching can be Small with supported conditions and recipes. Do not build wear, detailed staffing or per-part provenance to manufacture a need for these supplies. Basic healing and basic construction remain earlier capabilities.

Depth retains interesting substitutions, cultivation tradeoffs and bounded magical properties. Specialist covers resources whose defining use requires a different industrial, space-habitat or reality-law premise, not merely a difficult implementation. Substantial peaceful crafting and artistic activity can be Complete; narrowly differentiated decoration and material history need not be. Source recommendations, real-world safety disclaimers and ownership boundaries remain intact.
