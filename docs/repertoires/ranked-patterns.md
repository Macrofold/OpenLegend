# Ranked pattern repertoire

[Library](README.md) · [Priority policy](gameplay-priorities.md) · [Coverage](ranked-coverage.md) · [Actions](ranked-actions.md)

**Current-game selection register · October 3, 2026.** All **270 retained pattern cards in 27 catalogues** receive new product priorities here. This replaces their former High/Try/Niche recommendations for the current survival-adventure. The linked cards retain their descriptions, research, applicability and provisional build estimates; those estimates do not determine priority.

Read this with the coverage register: most cards describe an elaboration, not the smallest usable version of a capability. A storied kettle is not basic cooking; custody is not basic combat; a dragon's weather treaty is not a basic formidable enemy. **Only four cards are Core, but many underlying coverage capabilities are Core.** Do not mistake the distribution of old illustrative cards for the distribution of what the game needs.

Each section assigns its score to every row. Within each score, workstream numbers follow the [whole-game build order](gameplay-priorities.md#whole-game-build-order), then source headings provide stable tie order. Ties do not mean every alternative must be built. Choose representative content that closes an actual player-experience gap. The judgments below are design decisions, not empirical fun measurements or claims of implementation.

**Coverage check:** 270 unique source-file/heading pairs, ten per catalogue. Core: 4; Complete: 40; Depth: 126; Detail: 45; Specialist: 55. Bibliography entries, correctness contracts and individual proper names inside examples are not additional feature commitments.
