## Selection decisions

Basic rest and recovery, usable warning/choice information, stopping before collapse, usable exits, storage and equipment access lead. ND-002 is Core **only where hydration is enabled**; it does not justify adding thirst as an isolated draining meter. ND-123 and ND-125 concern tools and feedback that let people actually act and improve, not extra training bureaucracy. These minimum requirements do not mandate a separate needs subsystem for every row.

Attainable mastery (ND-122), worthy challenges (ND-127), a rival worth defeating (ND-145), a concrete revenge objective (ND-155), fair puzzles (ND-165), voluntary adventure (ND-177) and actual occasions to use a profession (ND-251) move ahead of domestic detail. Peaceful performance, games, creation and company also earn Complete when they provide a substantial enjoyable activity. A dependable food supply and somewhere to return are Complete as the **full ongoing arrangements described**, rather than new prerequisites for obtaining one meal or resting once.

Toileting (ND-007), clothing cleanliness (ND-012), reserve bookkeeping (ND-027), garment texture (ND-070), task handoffs (ND-202), promise sizing (ND-203), return-date commitments (ND-206), household renegotiation (ND-207) and repair credit (ND-259) are Detail. Their bounded version may be Small; neither a low score nor Level C means high engineering complexity. They must not become gates for enemies, exploration, rewards or useful equipment.

Most nuanced social and personal wants are Depth, not universal needs meters. Unusual physiologies, copied identity, reality-dependent nourishment and dedicated erotic or dependency-care experiences are Specialist in this survival-adventure context, not judgments against those subjects or permanent bans. User control, privacy and participation remain constraints whenever a selected feature uses them; a low score for a fictional desire does not make those protections optional.
