# Character formation and backstory seeds

**Canonical catalogue.** Descriptions and current priorities live together here. Each domain table is ordered by priority, then stable ID; implementation Level and build effort are not priority tie-breakers. These are proposals, not delivered features.

[Library](README.md) · [Shared classifications](README.md#reading-the-labels) · [Category ownership](README.md#category-ownership-and-cross-references) · [Selection and scale](selection-and-scale.md) · [Design foundation](design-foundation.md)

**Proposal catalogue · Group 3 expansion · 300 character/backstory entries.** These are original people and small ensembles to cast, adapt or encounter, not copies of franchise characters. Each has a role, something already lived or accomplished, and a present orientation that suggests actual play. Their proposed pasts become authored setting facts only when adopted; they are not claims about events already running in OpenLegend. A household, vocation or happy afternoon can be enough. Not everyone is a quest giver, secret heir, trauma case or prospective companion.

**Ownership:** this file owns particular lives. [Traits](traits.md) owns recurring dispositions, [Needs](needs.md) desired conditions, [Psychology](psychology-behavior.md) interpretation, [Relationships](relationships.md) bonds, [Institutions](institutions-politics.md) offices and organizations, and [History](history-myth.md) shared historical structures. A biography can name an achievement without defining its combat resolution, magical law or production process here. Build gaps identify the owner needed to make the person usable, not new runtime commitments. These biographies compose histories, motives and circumstances; an unusual person's missing bodily or magical premise remains a dependency in its own catalogue rather than automatically making their biography a new rule.

People are adults unless a younger age is explicitly stated. Child entries belong in age-appropriate scenes and are not participants in the adult intimacy entries. Consensual sex, pleasure, ambition, cruelty, coercion and survival are distinct elements, not interchangeable explanations for every life.

The twelve original domains retain 20 entries each; six further domains add 10 each. Within every domain, entries are ordered by Priority, then stable ID. **Synthesis** and **creative synthesis** mark original invention informed by a linked source's themes; the invented person and events are not asserted to appear in that source. Seed labels are recommended homes, not inspiration credits. **—** keeps a person whose premise belongs outside the four proposals. The ten original formation-pattern headings remain after the inventory.

### Casting an already inhabited world

The four seed proposals already supply 15-person starting casts, including established important figures. This expansion does not replace those rosters or add every new person to every world. A selected cast should contain people with finished deeds, practical resources, ordinary routines and relationships not waiting for the player to begin.

For an established figure, distinguish an actual achievement from a boast; identify who knows it, what remains materially available and what limits present authority. A crown does not command every soldier, a famous victory does not make a fighter unbeatable, and a beloved healer cannot serve everyone at once. Supporting people need their own purposes too. The entries below offer encounter routes through work, leisure, public obligations, conflict or particular interests rather than one repeated fetch-request template.

As an adult they translate practical expectations, sometimes enjoying their freedom and sometimes exhausting themselves as everyone's mediator. Let them choose when to explain. Mixed heritage must not automatically confer perfect diplomacy or make the character an encyclopedia for the player.

Selection: Cultural scheduling biography is optional detail.

## The former beneficiary

**Priority: 3 Depth.**


CB-139 preserves the surveyor whose education and pension connect to an unfair water concession. Reform has real costs; the affected community does not owe instant absolution or protection from every consequence. Inherited anchors: [Pillars of Eternity II](source-atlas.md#g55), [Fallout: New Vegas](source-atlas.md#g112), [Fallout](source-atlas.md#w28).

A surveyor discovers their comfortable education was funded by an unfair water concession. They want reform but still depend on the institution's pension and friendships. Players can help inspect records, improve service or organize negotiations. The interesting choice is what to change now, not a binary confession that instantly purifies them or makes them irredeemable.

Selection: Material entanglements add a later social dilemma.

## An ordinary happy childhood

**Priority: 3 Depth.**


CB-001 preserves supportive upbringing as a real source of confidence, curiosity and useful skills. Happiness is not a secret deficiency requiring a later trauma reveal. Inherited anchors: [Animal Crossing](source-atlas.md#g03), [The Sims](source-atlas.md#g24), [Pokémon](source-atlas.md#w08).

A cook grew up with patient caregivers and expects conflicts to be discussable. That confidence helps strangers feel welcome but sometimes underestimates real hostility. They leave home because they want to taste unfamiliar food, not because home was destroyed. Stable love can generate courage, humor and obligations every bit as useful for stories as suffering.

Selection: A positive biography adds tone without requiring simulated childhood.

## The competent understudy

**Priority: 3 Depth.**


CB-031 retains years of actual logistical achievement behind someone else's public credit. The next choice can involve leadership, negotiated conditions or ruthless ambition rather than a compulsory humble-helper identity. Inherited anchors: [G60](source-atlas.md#g60), [Final Fantasy VI](source-atlas.md#g83), [Mass Effect 2](source-atlas.md#g102).

An assistant has quietly run the expedition's logistics for years while its charismatic leader receives credit. When leadership becomes vacant, they must decide whether they want the public role at all. Players can recognize expertise without forcing promotion. Their competence should have visible evidence; ambition may mean better working conditions, rightful command or a ruthless bid to surpass the old leader.

Selection: Succession and recognition deepen an existing expedition group.

## A return to a changed home

**Priority: 3 Depth.**


CB-152 preserves the returning traveler whose friend actually completed their old shared plan. The traveler returns as a guest with choices, not to an NPC whose life froze in their absence. Inherited anchors: [Dragon Age II](source-atlas.md#g52), [Final Fantasy VI](source-atlas.md#g83), [Narnia](source-atlas.md#w19).

A traveler returns with money and stories to find their old friend has built the life they once planned together. They can become a guest, contribute to new work or leave again. Nostalgia becomes a negotiable relationship with the present. The world must not freeze absent characters or punish them for growing beyond the player's remembered version.

Selection: Persistent relationships add meaning to later return trips.

## The maker of an inconvenient truth

**Priority: 3 Depth.**


CB-027 keeps the restorer discovering older names under a painting, with commitments to both beauty and accuracy. The evidence can remain incomplete; no single reveal must settle the whole history. Inherited anchors: [Outer Wilds](source-atlas.md#g34), [Morrowind](source-atlas.md#g113), [Macondo](source-atlas.md#w24).

A young restorer uncovers names beneath a celebrated mural and cannot tell whether they record an injustice or an old repair. Their first goal is accuracy; public pressure demands a verdict. Players investigate, preserve, explain or postpone. Give the character reasons to care about the mural's beauty too, and let incomplete evidence remain incomplete.

Selection: A history investigation adds an optional substantive quest.

## Selection guidance

Nel’s inventor-and-finisher partnership (CB-025) is Complete: collaborating on a useful object can produce both a tangible result and an independently motivated relationship. A starting cast can contain one such person without adopting every biography or a labor-negotiation system.

Torren's occupied road post (CB-112) is a Core representative antagonist. The old Advanced label must not place an actual bandit behind a cook's domestic routine. Select an authored enemy with a place, resources, readable capabilities and a goal; no complete life simulation or universal faction engine is needed to cast them. Their specific biography remains an option, not mandatory canon.

A dragon defending a pass (CB-099), a finite conquering lord (CB-185/200), honest commercial competition, mercenaries, recruits and reachable opponents are Complete alternatives that add real activity. Trainers, a practical hunter, route guides, useful merchants, a conversion shop (CB-275), contest organizers and bounded recovery providers also earn Complete. The duel sponsor who improves opponents' equipment (CB-251) connects challenge directly to progression. Finished deeds can establish a capable cast without forcing the player to recreate every deed first.

Peaceful cooks, musicians, games, gardening, art and convivial venues remain worthwhile when they offer a usable activity. Other lives supply Depth: affection, mixed loyalties, grief, ordinary satisfaction and comic contradictions can enrich a working world. No score requires every person to be a quest dispenser, prospective recruit or tragic secret. Complete entries are alternatives for meaningful roles, not 83 additional compulsory NPCs.

Laundry standards (CB-009), instrument-repair biography (CB-021), the repair purist (CB-026), rotating-supper administration (CB-048), maintenance-focused specialists (CB-183/196), acoustic restoration (CB-277) and the paid last guest (CB-285) are Detail. Their simple authored version can remain Small; importance is not cost. A finished carpenter, useful store or healer can be selected without adding all their possible household and service administration.

Specialist marks biographies that require a separate modern/industrial, unfamiliar embodiment, time/identity or focused adult-service setting. It does not ban magical enemies or judge the worth of people. The cast's existing independence, bounded knowledge, privacy, age-appropriate participation and authority constraints remain required whenever a person is used. Scores concern optional fictional casting priorities, not real-world political, psychological or moral rankings.
