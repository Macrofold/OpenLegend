# Selecting ideas and paying for their complexity

[Library and labels](README.md) · [Worked combinations](combinations.md) · [Source atlas](source-atlas.md) · [Design foundation](design-foundation.md)

**Design judgment, September 27, 2026.** The library's breadth is for selection. Choose a coherent promise with desired rewards, meaningful resistance, distinctive people and consequences. Use [category ownership](README.md#category-ownership-and-cross-references) to locate the specific equipment, rules, skills and situations that deliver it; a selected experience can draw from several catalogues. The [foundation](design-foundation.md) covers both constructive and destructive possibilities; a world need not enable every catalogue at once. Ratings below summarize qualitative patterns in the research; they are neither a player survey nor a validated product forecast.

## The strongest candidates to explore first

These candidates offer different starting experiences. Select those that serve the chosen world: a combat opening needs an engaging fight, a domestic opening needs a satisfying life to build, and an exploratory opening needs a discovery worth pursuing.

| Candidate                                                                                                            | Why it has high potential                                                        | Research tension to resolve                                                                                                                                                              | A small proposed proof                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Borrowed purpose](mechanics.md#borrowed-purpose)                                                                    | Cleverness emerges from a small set of consistent affordances                    | Minecraft, immersive sims and Zelda reward reuse; Scribblenauts warns against one universal answer                                                                                       | A rack can sieve grain, brace cloth or fail visibly under excess load                                                  |
| A duel with distinct weapons                                                                                         | Timing, reach, commitment and reading an opponent make competence enjoyable      | [Hades](source-atlas.md#g22), [Elden Ring](source-atlas.md#g75) and [Kingdom Come II](source-atlas.md#g114) distinguish learning from unreliable feedback or stat inflation              | Defeat a named rival using spear distance or shield-and-sword pressure; the loser and spectators remember how it ended |
| [A dependable meal](needs.md#a-dependable-meal) and [public kitchen](settlements-architecture.md#the-public-kitchen) | Care, work and home intersect; stability frees attention                         | Sims/Stardew attachment can be undermined by chores and schedule anxiety                                                                                                                 | Three people arrange supper, cope with one missing ingredient and then run the routine                                 |

| [Reliable disagreement](relationships.md#the-reliable-disagreement)                                                  | A relationship can deepen without becoming unanimous                             | DAII's distinctions and Austen's social asymmetries challenge a single affection score                                                                                                   | Two trusted people disagree about a road while still cooperating on a repair                                           |
| A competition with a real loser                                                                                      | Recognition, ambition and excellence give familiar activities stakes             | [Austen](source-atlas.md#w23), [The Sims](source-atlas.md#g24), [The Culture](source-atlas.md#w17) support status, desire and skill; a universal win removes the contest                 | Compete for a festival commission, a sporting title or a prestigious apprenticeship and retain the resulting rival     |

| A threat defeated for good                                                                                           | Earned relief and changed circumstances make victory worth remembering           | [Middle-earth](source-atlas.md#w01), [Avatar](source-atlas.md#w13) connect resistance and things worth saving; endlessly restored threats empty success                                  | Defeat a raider leader or lift a curse, then actually inhabit the changed route, relationships and opportunities       |

The **High** tag also covers gardens, celebrations, amateur clubs and familiar places. Pleasure, beauty and belonging can be the desired payoff. A prototype should test the audience it serves and the experience it promises.

## Different people want different returns

| Perspective                                | What may be worth their time                                                                                              | Cost to avoid                                                                                                        | Good evidence of value                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| New player with a short evening            | One understandable problem, a person with a reason to care, a satisfying stopping point                                   | Terminology, inventory sorting, obligatory onboarding lectures                                                       | Finds and completes a self-chosen small goal without being told the answer                   |
| Domestic-life player                       | Decorating, rituals, jobs, evolving households, familiar faces                                                            | Ever-declining meters and punishment for absence                                                                     | Returns voluntarily to a settled place and chooses what to change                            |
| Explorer or investigator                   | Useful clues, different senses, alternate routes, consequential knowledge                                                 | Map-marker sweeps, exact phrase gates, pointless backtracking                                                        | Can explain a discovery that changed their next action                                       |
| Tactical or survival player                | Challenging opponents, martial skill, powerful builds, preparation, dangerous rewards and consequential victory or defeat | Arbitrary death, invisible collateral, mandatory repetitive training                                                 | Can explain a won or lost encounter and wants another meaningful challenge                   |
| Competitive player                         | Outsmart or outperform a capable opponent, gain rank, take a prize and meet a worthy rival                                | Opponents cannot respond, outcomes are predetermined, or an advantage removes all future contest                     | Can explain a win or loss and wants a rematch or a stronger opponent                         |
| Heroic adventurer                          | Explore, gain powers, defeat a genuine evil and protect particular people or places                                       | Every villain becomes a misunderstanding, victory changes nothing, or heroism requires a compulsory tragic backstory | Uses a learned ability to overcome an adversary and sees the victory persist                 |
| Roleplayer or story creator                | Particular motives, loyalties, romance, rivalry, ambition, betrayal, moral choice and historical specificity              | Predetermined emotional outcomes and generic assistant-like inhabitants                                              | Different plausible choices produce distinct, coherent continuations                         |
| Romance and social-drama player            | Attraction, wit, intimacy, reputation, conflicting commitments and change                                                 | Everyone is available and agreeable, or relationships reduce to gifts and one affection number                       | Recalls a particular shared experience and chooses how to develop or change the relationship |
| Builder and decorator                      | Reusable parts, functional layouts, beauty, adaptable blueprints                                                          | Constant repairs and one numerical optimum that makes taste irrelevant                                               | Another person understands and enjoys the place                                              |
| Systems power user                         | Small grammars, meaningful operators, diagnostics, routines, reusable methods                                             | Hidden order, unsupported combinations silently ignored, fragile dependencies                                        | Can explain, modify and transfer a creation to a different situation                         |
| Conqueror, ruler or ambitious operator     | Acquire power, choose policy, command others and deal with opposition or the costs of an exploitative path                | Power is cosmetic, subjects always agree, or harmful choices receive automatic narrative absolution                  | Can identify whose lives or opportunities changed through a decision they made               |
| Horror and harsh-survival player           | Apprehension, uncertain safety, vulnerability, resource judgment and earned survival                                      | Repetitive jump scares, unknowable rule changes or constant interruption replace tension                             | Anticipates danger, makes a consequential choice and recognizes when safety has been earned  |
| Community host                             | Stable shared projects, manageable exceptions, reliable native activity, clear participation terms                        | Always-on model spending, grief-driven churn and duties that require constant presence                               | A group enjoys a session within an explicit attention and operating budget                   |
| Educator or researcher                     | Reproducible assumptions, instrumentation, counterfactuals, disclosed approximations                                      | Confident dialogue mistaken for empirical validity                                                                   | Results are attributable to a stated model and limitations can be demonstrated               |
| Archivist, spectator or occasional visitor | Chronicles, performances, exhibits and visible change                                                                     | Having to become a full-time operator to appreciate the world                                                        | Can understand an interesting event and choose whether to participate                        |

Use [world tone and scope](design-foundation.md#world-tone-and-scope) to choose the subjects and presentation that serve that experience. Criticality measures an idea's role in the selected game; pleasant, disturbing or explicit subject matter alone does not determine its priority.

These perspectives overlap. A builder may want a severe expedition on Saturday and a quiet garden on Sunday. Use explicit world choices and activity options rather than assuming one permanent player type.

## Complexity budgets that protect the experience

The figures here are **illustrative prototype bounds**, not measured engine capacities or accepted product limits. A first scene might use 3–8 active named people, 3–5 interacting mechanic families, one locality and a finite project. Expand only when the additional detail changes an observable decision.

| Kind of complexity         | Pay for it when                                                | Contain it by                                                               | A warning sign                                                                         |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Material properties        | A property supports multiple uses or a meaningful tradeoff     | Few named properties, bounded local processes, coarse distant state         | Hundreds of substances differ only in price and color                                  |
| Relationship detail        | It changes an actual choice, interpretation or commitment      | Selected events and relationships; no all-to-all appraisal every tick       | Every encounter generates another mood update or lengthy monologue                     |
| Autonomous actors          | People pursue observable purposes with useful continuity       | Due work, stable plans, native execution, inference at meaningful decisions | The world's cost grows with simulated seconds even when nothing interesting happens    |
| Environmental simulation   | Spatial or temporal interactions are the point of the activity | Region graphs, activity windows, declared approximations and conservation   | Weather, fire or fluids recursively wake the whole world                               |
| Institutions and economics | Services, rights or exchange create interesting alternatives   | Named stakeholders and bounded aggregate flows                              | Most play is reconciling accounts that affect no meaningful choice                     |
| Creator combinations       | Operators can be inspected and reused in distinct situations   | Finite trusted families, composition caps and local failure domains         | A description can install arbitrary behavior or a small loop creates unbounded effects |
| Historical persistence     | Someone can revisit, recognize or use the consequence          | Identity for meaningful things, selected records and attributed summaries   | Every incidental object requires a generated biography and permanent detail            |
| Lab accuracy               | The experiment has a clear question and an explicit model      | Controlled parameters, seeds, units, run limits and sensitivity checks      | “It looks lifelike” substitutes for validation                                         |

A distant village can be an aggregate producer until someone visits; an encountered resident's identity and established commitments must remain consistent. A later detailed scene should not retroactively fabricate witnesses to an earlier event. The selected implementation must explain how aggregate quantities and named entities reconcile. These are design constraints on a future slice, not a proposal to build a universal simulation framework now.

Model calls are appropriate for interpreting a novel situation or expressing a consequential conversation within permitted context. Movement, known production, resource accounting, repeated conditions and scheduled effects should not depend on generating fresh prose. No claim about dollar costs, concurrency or real-time performance has been measured here.

## Combinations worth limiting initially

- **Full ecology + detailed metabolism + individual population cognition:** begin with a garden and a few species; add precision only for a specific play or lab question.
- **Every institution + dynamic markets + city-wide politics:** begin with one neighborhood service, one budget and several named stakeholders.
- **Freeform spell grammar + mutable bodies + arbitrary topology:** choose one new magical law and compose it with well-understood ordinary actions.
- **Persistent wars + lethal needs + severe injury:** choose whether the intended game centers survival, recovery, succession or final loss. Implement that coherent contract first; combining every severe system accidentally can eliminate interesting decisions before the player understands them.
- **Infinite generation + public marketplace + versioned packs:** prove a creation useful to a second person before treating distribution as a solved product.

## What would change our prioritization

For a selected idea, observe an uncoached encounter, a repeat encounter and an intentional variation. Ask what the player wanted, what they believed would happen, what actually happened and what they want to do next. Check a meaningful failure and an absent capability. A technically unsupported attempt should remain an honest limitation, never a fictional success.

Promotion is justified when the payoff survives ordinary use: a fight rewards learned judgment, a victory changes access or power, a rival reacts coherently, a relationship develops or breaks, a discovery enables a new method, a quiet activity is chosen for pleasure, a routine frees attention, or an experiment yields interpretable evidence about its declared model. If only the author can make it interesting, improve the scene or narrow the mechanic before expanding scope. This document records no completed playtests and no engineering acceptance.
