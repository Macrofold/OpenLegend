# Psychology, interpretation and behavioral depth

[Library](README.md) · [Shared classifications](README.md#reading-the-labels) · [Category ownership](README.md#category-ownership-and-cross-references) · [Selection and scale](selection-and-scale.md) · [Design foundation](design-foundation.md)

**Proposal catalogue · Group 3 expansion.** These 336 entries describe fictional patterns of attention, interpretation, feeling and choice. They are not diagnoses, clinical predictions, universal human rules or implemented capabilities. Select a few compatible patterns for a person and situation; do not run the whole catalogue as a hidden personality machine. A feeling can matter without compelling an action, and a changed interpretation need not erase a remembered event.

This catalogue owns **how an experience becomes meaningful and changes conduct**. Enduring tendencies belong in [Traits](traits.md), desired conditions in [Needs](needs.md), particular lives in [Characters](characters-backstories.md), and bonds between people in [Relationships](relationships.md). Use [Actions](actions.md#mnd-private-cognition-and-intentions) for concrete attempts. Combat execution, bodily effects, institutions and supernatural laws retain their respective owners. Build gaps below are broad dependencies, not an implementation audit.

Per-game checkmarks, source revisions, discovery corrections and findings are preserved in its immutable checkpoint chain. No repository document in those two research directories remains unread at that pin. Linked videos, external bibliographies, original uploaded packets and the research library's separate P01–P05 integration gates are not certified by this reading.

PB-001–PB-240 retain the earlier checkpoint's ideas and PB-241–PB-276 retain the Disco Elysium/Culture continuation. Their prior reading history is preserved in the [pre-expansion catalogue](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/a5cca30aa795c18d43aa3ee62eeec3893c43d40f/docs/repertoires/psychology-behavior.md#research-coverage). PB-277–PB-336 are sixty new creative syntheses across invention, attachment, appetite, power, embodied continuity and unusual inner laws. Research links identify the source of a question, not evidence that the invented scene occurs in that work.

The editorial pass preserves every prior ID and pattern heading, separates seed recommendations from Inspiration, and classifies ordinary appraisals under admitted unusual bodies as C rather than D. Removed setting-only citations are not silently reassigned to an unrelated franchise; rows without a specific research attribution remain original synthesis. D is retained where the entry actually proposes a reusable subjective rule.

Integration handoff: shared source-atlas navigation and counts still need reconciliation with the D&D additions, revised 29-world corpus and expanded catalogues. Those shared files remain unchanged. Attention, memory provenance, body/identity continuity, communication, institutions and supernatural transfers remain broad dependencies in their owners, not accepted implementation tasks.
