# Objects worth keeping and repurposing

A catalogue of finished possessions: useful, beautiful, dangerous, ridiculous, intimate, and occasionally impossible. These 262 selectable entries expand the original patterns. An object can be wanted for its taste, familiarity, style, or sheer fun; it need not conceal a quest, penalty, or moral lesson.

Use the [shared classifications and catalogue rules](README.md) and [category ownership](README.md#category-ownership-and-cross-references). Objects owns the item. [Work](work-crafting.md) owns making and repair, [Materials](materials-resources.md) its inputs, [Economy](economy-logistics.md) its exchange, [Combat and rescue](combat-rescue.md) fighting and rescue behavior, [Technology](technology.md) device principles, and [Magic](magic.md) extraordinary effects. Build scope names missing capabilities, not approved implementations. Dependencies without existing entries are named rather than assigned invented IDs.

Seed recommendations follow the [four proposals](worlds/). FA includes optional later developments, not a claim that every enchantment belongs in Borrowed Dawn's opening heat-transfer campaign. MD does not gain firearms or supernatural powers by association. SC equipment supports inhabited places and abstract passage, not flight gameplay. A dash retains ideas for other settings.

Every row is a proposal. Research links in Inspiration identify a motif or contrast used in creative synthesis, not evidence that the exact invented item exists in that franchise. Unlinked rows are original synthesis or retained seed-design patterns. Difficult subjects remain fictional design descriptions, not real-world procedures.

## Base coverage

Keep food, containers, furniture, clothes, lamps, hand tools, weapons, armor, mementos, documents, toys, and instruments alongside rarer inventions. Named possessions can retain ownership, repair, alteration, and loss histories; ordinary stock can remain fungible when that distinction adds nothing. Reuse follows actual properties and supported interactions, not a universal promise that any combination works.

### Equipment families to cover

The inventory includes hunting bows, crossbows, guns, ammunition, blades, polearms, shields, protective clothing, restraints, workshop tools, exploration gear, assistive equipment, and enchanted possessions. Owning a weapon does not grant training or decide attack resolution. Specialized ammunition changes delivery or effect, not merely a damage number.

## The mended kettle

A kettle becomes worth keeping through repairs, routines, and remembered owners without accumulating supernatural bonuses. ITEM-001 preserves this anchor; destruction may end its use without erasing its history.

## Surveyor's folding frame

ITEM-043 measures, braces, or displays something through the same joints and dimensions. Improvisation exposes fit and load rather than making an evocative name sufficient to do anything.

## The honest empty box

ITEM-061 makes containment useful without pretending its contents exist. Storage can protect, organize, conceal, or transport actual things; labels and wish lists are not inventories.

## Festival cloth

ITEM-164 becomes an awning, banner, table covering, or costume, preserving material and occasion across uses. Changing a communal decoration need not settle ownership or what its history means.

## Witness lamp

ITEM-150 preserves a bounded exposure trace and its provenance, not an omniscient scene recording. Saturation, missing coverage, and alteration remain different questions.

## Borrower's tool roll

ITEM-044 keeps custody of specific tools distinct from expendable supplies. Returning a familiar repaired hammer can matter differently from replacing it with an identical-looking one.

## A chair with a place

ITEM-006 anchors a routine and a relationship to a street. Furniture can move for welcome, access, work, or celebration without requiring a numerical social bonus.

## Map of unfinished things

ITEM-149 records selected work still worth doing. Marking an intention does not complete it; an owner can revise or abandon a project rather than acquire an endless compulsory checklist.

## Companion vessel

ITEM-197 is an inhabited, maintained possession with a bounded caretaker and usable interior. Its character does not eliminate fuel, cargo, promises, ownership questions, or the possibility of switching it off.

## Celebration kit

ITEM-163 gathers supplies for a real occasion: food, light, music, games, and decoration selected by its hosts. A successful celebration can remain simply a good evening.
