# Characters, decisions and AI services

[Glossary index](README.md) · For the out-of-world assistant, see [World Agent](worlds-and-creation.md#creating-or-changing-a-world). For residence and participation, see [the character in the world](access-and-participation.md#the-character-in-the-world).

## Who is acting?

| Canonical term                   | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Source                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Player                           | The real person playing the game. A player signs in to an account to play a character.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | [Account and character binding](access-and-participation.md#who-can-do-what)                                               |
| Player character                 | The character a player controls in the world. The account can remain bound to that character while the player is signed out.                                                                                                                                                                                           | [Account and character binding](access-and-participation.md#who-can-do-what)                                               |
| Entity                           | An individually identified thing in the simulated world. A gate and the gatekeeper beside it each have their own entity ID.                                                                                                                                                                                            | [Entity definition](../../packages/domain/src/types.ts), [identity contract](../identity-and-references.md#contract)       |
| Actor                            | An entity with an `actor` component, which the simulation uses to track its capabilities and actions. The gatekeeper and a hare both have one. An ordinary gate does not. The gatekeeper remains an actor while unconscious.                                                                                           | [TypeScript actor component](../../packages/domain/src/types.ts), [Rust actor state](../../native/simulation/src/state.rs) |
| Character                        | An individual in the fiction, such as the gatekeeper a player speaks to. Use **character** when describing someone’s role, choices or experiences in the story. Both an NPC and a player character are characters.                                                                                                     | [Character decisions](../agent-agency.md#2-a-decision-may-produce-nothing-one-thing-or-several-things)                     |
| Controller                       | The role that determines how an actor receives decisions. The saved `actor.controller` values are `player`, `npc` and `native`. Inviting a player can change this field while the entity keeps its original `kind`. Species, capabilities and the account's permission to take control are checked separately.         | [Control and projection](../identity-and-references.md#control-and-projection)                                             |
| NPC (non-player character)       | In OpenLegend prose, use **NPC** for a human character played by the game, such as a gatekeeper. An eligible NPC can become a player character when someone accepts an invitation and binds their account. The same person remains in the story.                                                                       | [Invitation and enrollment](../projects/completed/multiplayer-entry-maintenance.md#decisions)                              |
| Rule-controlled animal           | An animal whose choices come from the installed world's rules. Base hares, for example, can flee through the `native` controller without asking a model. In this usage, **native** names how behavior is chosen. It applies in both the TypeScript and Rust implementations.                                           | [Base animal responses](../worlds/base/animal-responses.md#playable-purpose)                                               |
| Animal with cognition and speech | A creator can grant those capabilities to a compatible animal. In the TypeScript implementation this can change its controller from `native` to `npc` while preserving the animal’s species and physical identity. When describing a code check, say **actor with the `npc` controller** if it includes these animals. | [God-mode capability grant](../../packages/domain/src/god-tools.ts)                                                        |
| Narrator                         | Renders permitted events or experiences as prose for a player. A description of an injury cannot cause the injury or give another character knowledge of it.                                                                                                                                                           | [World events, private experience and narration](../narration-and-conversations.md#2-three-kinds-of-truth)                 |

## How characters decide and respond

| Canonical term       | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Source                                                                                                 |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Agency               | A character’s ability to choose their own goals and actions. Continuing a plan they chose earlier is one way they exercise that agency.                                                                                                                                           | [Character decisions](../agent-agency.md#2-a-decision-may-produce-nothing-one-thing-or-several-things) |
| Cognition            | The work of considering a situation. A character may speak, think, attempt an action, change plans or decide to do nothing. Continuing an existing plan can require no new model call.                                                                                            | [Character decisions](../agent-agency.md#2-a-decision-may-produce-nothing-one-thing-or-several-things) |
| Autonomous decision  | A character considers what to do on the game's initiative, for example after hearing someone nearby. A direct player conversation begins with the player's request. Either can lead to a model request. A hare fleeing under its world rules uses a different decision mechanism. | [Reaction intake](../events-perception-and-reactions.md#one-intake-several-executors)                  |
| Reaction opportunity | A reason for this actor to reconsider, such as hearing a warning or finding a chosen path blocked. The game can continue existing work, use a world rule or admit a model request, depending on the opportunity.                                                                  | [Events, stimuli and reactions](../events-perception-and-reactions.md#2-the-six-distinct-concepts)     |
| Decision context     | What the server can supply for this actor's particular decision, including permitted evidence, relevant prior experience and offered choices. A returned reference must still resolve to an allowed target.                                                                       | [Actor context](../identity-and-references.md#actor-context)                                           |
| Mind document        | A character’s private record of how they understand themselves or their world, including beliefs, values and relationships. “They stole my berries” can be a mistaken belief even when “I saw them take berries” accurately records the event.                                    | [What belongs in a character’s mind](../memory-architecture.md#what-belongs-in-a-characters-mind)      |
| Private thought      | An in-character idea. It can be mistaken and other characters cannot hear it. It records what the character thinks, independently of a model’s internal reasoning.                                                                                                                | [Mental operations](../agent-agency.md#mental-and-external-operations)                                 |
| Reflection           | A character’s reconsideration of earlier experience. It can change what the character writes in their private account of their life.                                                                                                                                              | [Reflection](../memory-architecture.md#8-reflection-workspaces-and-publication)                        |
| Utterance            | Words a character speaks in the world, for example through **Speak**. Whether another character hears or understands them depends on that listener’s circumstances.                                                                                                               | [Speech and descriptive actions](../narration-and-conversations.md#player-speak-and-descriptive-act)   |
| Expression           | An observable gesture or descriptive attempt. A player can use **Act** to describe shaking a fist at a gate. Damaging the gate requires a separate action with that physical effect.                                                                                              | [Speech and descriptive actions](../narration-and-conversations.md#player-speak-and-descriptive-act)   |
| Addressee            | The individual a speaker intends to address. A quiet remark can be intended for the gatekeeper even if the gatekeeper misses it.                                                                                                                                                  | [Speech intent and audience](../narration-and-conversations.md#speech-intent-and-audience)             |
| Listener             | Someone who actually hears some part of an utterance. A bystander can hear more of a remark than its intended addressee.                                                                                                                                                          | [Speech intent and audience](../narration-and-conversations.md#speech-intent-and-audience)             |
| Witness              | Someone who personally perceived an event. A witness who saw the gatekeeper speaking might have heard none of the words.                                                                                                                                                          | [Speech intent and audience](../narration-and-conversations.md#speech-intent-and-audience)             |
| Provider stream      | A connection that delivers pieces of a model response to the server while the provider generates it. Receiving a piece alone gives nobody in the game anything to hear.                                                                                                           | [Public NPC speech streaming](../ai-providers.md#accepted-public-npc-speech-streaming)                 |
| Accepted speech      | Words the simulation has committed as an in-world utterance. Committing approved passages before a whole model reply finishes is an accepted design with implementation and qualification pending.                                                                                | [Public NPC speech streaming](../ai-providers.md#accepted-public-npc-speech-streaming)                 |

## What an AI request does

| Canonical term               | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                             | Source                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Artificial intelligence (AI) | In OpenLegend provider and billing discussions, AI refers to model services the game can call. Name the operation when discussing its cost because many NPC actions use only the world’s rules.   | [AI provider operations](../ai-providers.md)                                                                          |
| Large language model (LLM)   | A model that works with language. For example, it can propose an NPC’s next words using the context the server supplies for that NPC.                                                             | [AI provider operations](../ai-providers.md)                                                                          |
| Judgment                     | A model operation that answers supplied questions or rates offered choices using the permitted inputs. An answer can guide a choice. Executing the choice still requires the game’s checks.       | [AI provider operations](../ai-providers.md)                                                                          |
| Generation                   | A model operation that produces new material from the supplied inputs, such as dialogue or a proposed action. The simulation decides what can take effect.                                        | [AI provider operations](../ai-providers.md)                                                                          |
| Jev                          | TypeSafe’s judgment model. It can answer questions about supplied conditions, choose among offered options or assign a score. OpenLegend’s `judge` operation can also use other supported models. | [Provider behavior](../ai-providers.md#provider-behavior-and-limits)                                                  |
| TypeSafe                     | The service that provides direct API access to Jev. Direct access uses a TypeSafe credential.                                                                                                     | [Direct Jev setup](../live-ai-setup.md#1-get-a-jev-key)                                                               |
| Noul question                | A judgment question asking how likely a stated condition is to hold. The answer estimates that likelihood from zero to one.                                                                       | [Judgment question types](../../packages/ai/src/types.ts), [judgment requests](../ai-providers.md#jev-only-execution) |
| Macrofold                    | A service through which OpenLegend can send requests to configured models or run a hosted agent.                                                                                                  | [Macrofold routes](../ai-providers.md#current-macrofold-routes)                                                       |
| Model                        | The particular AI system that produces an answer from the inputs it receives. A single provider may offer several models.                                                                         | [AI provider operations](../ai-providers.md)                                                                          |
| AI provider                  | A service that executes model requests. A provider or an intermediary such as Macrofold may report its own request ID, timing and charge.                                                         | [Provider behavior](../ai-providers.md#provider-behavior-and-limits)                                                  |
| Macrofold Worker             | The compute resource on Macrofold that hosts an agent and its tools. Direct model inference through Macrofold needs no Worker.                                                                    | [Worker setup](../ai-providers.md#shared-worker-setup-and-cutover)                                                    |
| AI job                       | An application task such as preparing and applying an NPC decision. It can end before contacting a provider or involve several provider requests.                                                 | [AI admission](../ai-providers.md#admission-and-cancellation)                                                         |
| Provider request             | One call the application submits to a model service. Billing can apply once it has been dispatched, even if the job is later cancelled.                                                           | [AI admission](../ai-providers.md#admission-and-cancellation)                                                         |
| Provider receipt             | The record of what happened to one provider request, including known or uncertain usage. The game separately records whether it applied any proposed effects.                                     | [Receipts](../ai-providers.md#receipts-outcomes-and-accounting)                                                       |
| AI spending reservation      | The amount of the authorized AI allowance held before the application sends a request. An unresolved charge can keep that amount reserved after cancellation.                                     | [Provider accounting](../ai-providers.md#receipts-outcomes-and-accounting)                                            |
| Settled AI usage             | Usage recorded against the allowance once the application can attribute it to the request. Cancellation alone does not settle a charge for work already dispatched.                               | [Provider accounting](../ai-providers.md#receipts-outcomes-and-accounting)                                            |
