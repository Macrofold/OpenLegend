## How people understand and help one another

| Canonical term                | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Source                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Relationship description      | What one character thinks about another, recorded in the first character’s private notes. Editing that description changes only the writer’s account. The other character retains their own feelings and rights.                                                                                                                                                         | [Understanding another person](../worlds/base/social.md#understanding-another-person)                                                                           |
| Acquaintance                  | Someone a character knows. An authored starting acquaintance can supply [familiarity](perception-and-memory.md#what-the-character-knows-remembers-or-may-use) without inventing a shared encounter or making either character trust the other.                                                                                                                           | [Starting acquaintances](../worlds/base/first-playable-people.md#knowledge-stock-and-existing-history)                                                          |
| Trust                         | One character’s judgment of what they can rely on another person to do. Ada can trust a person who distrusts her. Knowing someone does not establish trust.                                                                                                                                                                                                              | [Understanding another person](../worlds/base/social.md#understanding-another-person)                                                                           |
| Appraisal                     | A character’s interpretation of what an event, memory or circumstance means to them. They can reconsider that interpretation when they receive new evidence.                                                                                                                                                                                                             | [Current feelings](../worlds/base/social.md#feelings)                                                                                                           |
| Felt emotion                  | What the character experiences emotionally, such as fear after a frightening near miss. A character can feel fear without suffering an injury. Another observer needs evidence of any outward expression.                                                                                                                                                                | [Current feelings](../worlds/base/social.md#feelings), [interpreted emotion](../projects/emotional-physiology-feature-spec.md#three-meanings-with-three-owners) |
| Bodily reaction               | A physical response, such as a startle. A startle alone does not establish fear or an intention to flee. In the proposed emotional physiology rules, supported actions can be affected by bodily reactions and separately interpreted feelings. For an outward gesture such as a frown, see [expression](characters-and-cognition.md#how-characters-decide-and-respond). | [Three meanings and their owners](../projects/emotional-physiology-feature-spec.md#three-meanings-with-three-owners)                                            |
| Reappraisal                   | Reconsidering what earlier evidence meant. A refused invitation may seem less personal after hearing why someone could not attend. The refusal still happened. The broader experience-shaped character design proposes carrying that revised outlook into later choices.                                                                                                 | [Experience-shaped characters](../worlds/base/experience-shaped-characters.md#a-life-can-contain-contrary-evidence)                                             |
| Cooperation episode           | A finite undertaking people have agreed to, such as guiding someone to a known place, carrying one load or hunting particular quarry. Each participant still chooses and performs their own actions. Agreeing to help conveys no right to take supplies or direct another body.                                                                                          | [What can be agreed](../projects/first-playable-cooperation-feature-spec.md#what-can-be-agreed)                                                                 |
| Alliance                      | Two characters’ mutual agreement to treat one another as allies. Each still decides whether to help. Shared property requires separate agreement.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | [Mutual alliances and finite ceasefires](../projects/first-playable-cooperation-tech-design.md#mutual-alliances-and-finite-ceasefires)                          |
| Ceasefire                     | Two characters’ agreement to stop initiating harm against one another for a defined period. They can agree to this while continuing to distrust each other.                                                                                                                                                                                                              | [Mutual alliances and finite ceasefires](../projects/first-playable-cooperation-tech-design.md#mutual-alliances-and-finite-ceasefires)                          |
| Commitment                    | A recorded undertaking by a character, such as a [spoken promise](gameplay-and-space.md#social-actions) to gather something. Missing the deadline can leave the obligation overdue.                                                                                                                                                                                      | [Recorded promises](../projects/readable-promises-feature-spec.md#2-supported-behavior) and [Base promise rules](../worlds/base/social.md#spoken-promises)      |
| Fulfillment (commitment)      | Completion of what was promised under the applicable rule. In Base, a later matching gather by the character can fulfill a supported gathering promise.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | [Base promise rules](../worlds/base/social.md#spoken-promises)                                                                                                  |
| Reliability (social judgment) | A character’s judgment of whether someone can be depended on. Completing or missing a promise can inform that judgment only for a character with the relevant evidence.                                                                                                                                                                                                  | [Recorded promises](../projects/readable-promises-feature-spec.md#2-supported-behavior)                                                                         |
| Commission (proposed)         | An agreement to provide a particular result later, such as making a pouch. Accepting the commission does not produce the pouch or reserve the materials to make it.                                                                                                                                                                                                      | [Proposed commission and advance](../worlds/base/durable-agreements.md#advances-and-remaining-claims)                                                           |
| Advance (proposed commission) | An actual transfer at the start of a commission that counts toward its agreed payment. Delivering the result and paying any remaining balance are separate acts.                                                                                                                                                                                                         | [Proposed commission and advance](../worlds/base/durable-agreements.md#advances-and-remaining-claims)                                                           |
| Association (proposed)        | A group people voluntarily join to coordinate repeated work. Membership records a common purpose. Access to its tools, custody of goods and any obligation to another member each need their own agreement.                                                                                                                                                              | [Proposed small association](../worlds/base/durable-agreements.md#small-association)                                                                            |

For biological kinship, an item offer or the terms of a shared outing, see [social actions](gameplay-and-space.md#social-actions). In the creator's inspector, a **relationship** can also mean a recorded link between things, such as a recipe and its ingredients. [Inspection relationships](../invention-graph.md#3-relationship-vocabulary)

## Danger, care and death

| Canonical term                   | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sign of danger                   | Something the character can actually perceive, such as hoofprints, disturbed foliage or a stag's threatening posture. How the character interprets it can differ from the danger the simulation actually presents. Missing a sign gives no guarantee of safety.                                 | [Player danger](../worlds/base/player-danger.md#observation-earns-safety)                                                                                                                                                         |
| Injury (proposed care system)    | A particular wound with a cause, effects and a course over time. Current health loss and death scars each have their own rules.                                                                                                                                                                 | [Proposed injury and care](../projects/recovery-and-care-feature-spec.md#3-individual-injuries-and-their-causes) and [current death scars](../worlds/base/player-death.md#death-scars)                                            |
| Treatment (proposed care system) | An action to care for a particular injury, such as stabilizing bleeding. A helper needs the patient’s consent and separate access to any supplies.                                                                                                                                              | [Field treatment and a willing helper](../projects/recovery-and-care-feature-spec.md#6-field-treatment-and-a-willing-helper)                                                                                                      |
| Remains                          | The physical body left after death. In the Base player-death rules, others can loot possessions left with the body.                                                                                                                                                                             | [Death and possessions](../worlds/base/player-death.md#death-and-possessions)                                                                                                                                                     |
| Continue (after player death)    | The Base player’s choice to begin a new physical life under the same character identity. Knowledge and relationships persist. Opening a new connection alone does not start a new life.                                                                                                         | [Continue near a rest spot](../worlds/base/player-death.md#continue-near-a-rest-spot)                                                                                                                                             |
| Ghost (Base afterlife design)    | The same NPC retained after death, with their identity and history. The dead NPC is normally outside active play. Speaking with them depends on a supported way to summon them.                                                                                                                 | [NPC death and ghosts](../worlds/base/lifecycle-and-protection.md#npc-death-ghosts-and-revival)                                                                                                                                   |
| Manifestation (proposed)         | A temporary presence through which a willing, deceased NPC could speak with someone at the cairn in the proposed Base scenario. Summoning grants no right to revive or control them.                                                                                                            | [A conversation at the cairn](../worlds/base/extraordinary-minds-and-afterlife.md#a-conversation-at-the-cairn)                                                                                                                    |
| Revival                          | Restoring a dead character to living participation. Authorized creators already have a revival tool. The proposed Base afterlife scenario would separately allow ordinary players to seek an NPC’s return.                                                                                      | [Creator and proposed ordinary revival](../worlds/base/lifecycle-and-protection.md#npc-death-ghosts-and-revival), [proposed extraordinary means](../worlds/base/extraordinary-minds-and-afterlife.md#a-conversation-at-the-cairn) |
| Unattended community (proposed)  | A community selected for continued operation while players are away. The proposed camp must have the actual food, equipment and work to sustain its inhabitants during a specified operating period. A returning player can discover what happened through permitted observations and accounts. | [Proposed community and operating horizon](../worlds/base/continuing-communities.md#selected-scope-and-operating-horizon)                                                                                                         |

## What a story can tell the player

| Canonical term                      | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Source                                                                                                |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Personal story                      | Permitted material presented to a player as prose. Reading it adds no world event or character memory. See [player history](perception-and-memory.md#what-the-character-knows-remembers-or-may-use) for what the player can review about their own character.                                        | [Story selection](../narration-and-conversations.md#replaceable-story-selection)                      |
| Story selection                     | Choosing which eligible source material to include in a personal story. A world event can be recorded and available in history without being selected for the story.                                                                                                                                 | [Story selection](../narration-and-conversations.md#replaceable-story-selection)                      |
| Story perspective (proposed)        | A separately authorized view of an event for the player as a reader. In the proposed private camp scene, the player could read that a familiar NPC finished making cord after the player character had left. The character neither witnessed the completion nor learns it from the player's reading. | [Proposed scene and audience](../worlds/base/story-perspectives.md#authored-scene-and-audience)       |
| Personal Journal edition (proposed) | A keepsake a player could prepare from selected eligible entries and download. The **Journal** already lets the player read personal records. An edition would be a separate, deliberately selected copy with its own permission checks.                                                             | [Proposed Journal edition](../projects/personal-journal-feature-spec.md#31-first-complete-capability) |
| Correspondence (proposed)           | Private written communication between characters who exchanged contacts in the proposed message-slate world. Sending a message creates no sound for bystanders.                                                                                                                                      | [Proposed private correspondence](../worlds/base/text-messages.md#a-note-across-separate-activities)  |
| Message delivery (proposed)         | A submitted message reaches the recipient’s private inbox when their policy allows it. **Submitted** confirms only the sender’s own submission. The sender receives no delivery receipt.                                                                                                             | [Proposed private correspondence](../worlds/base/text-messages.md#a-note-across-separate-activities)  |
| Message reading (proposed)          | The recipient opens the thread and sees its permitted text. The game would record that character’s reading experience without claiming they understood the message or sending the writer a read receipt.                                                                                             | [Proposed private correspondence](../worlds/base/text-messages.md#a-note-across-separate-activities)  |

## What a character experiment establishes

The Cognition Lab is a proposed workspace for examining character decisions. The existing decision benchmark gives models supplied questions and scores their answers.

| Canonical term                                  | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Source                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Scenario (Cognition Lab design)                 | A reusable starting situation for examining character decisions, including the people, surroundings and applicable rules. Several questions can be investigated in the same scenario.                                          | [Lab records](../projects/cognition-lab-tech-design.md#42-lab-records)                                                                                                 |
| Case (decision benchmark)                       | A benchmark item with a question and scoring criteria. One scene can supply several separately scored cases, such as questions about what different witnesses know.                                                            | [Benchmark cases](../../scripts/decision-benchmark/types.ts)                                                                                                           |
| Run (character experiment)                      | One execution from a stated starting situation, with the inputs, interventions and outcomes of that execution. The proposed Lab records a run separately from the reusable scenario.                                           | [Lab run records](../projects/cognition-lab-tech-design.md#42-lab-records)                                                                                             |
| Sample (character experiment)                   | One observation in a stated comparison. For a model benchmark, identify whether one sample is a response to one question or an independently played scene. Twenty questions about one scene are not twenty independent scenes. | [Decision benchmark evidence limits](../verification/decision-benchmark-audit.md)                                                                                      |
| Held (proposed Lab state)                       | The current run is still open after a step or requested hold. The operator can send the next stimulus or continue the same episode.                                                                                            | [Experiment controls](../projects/cognition-lab-feature-spec.md#9-experiment-controls-and-saved-work)                                                                  |
| Stop (proposed Lab control)                     | Ends an active run. The Lab stops starting new work, finishes or cancels outstanding work, and records how the run ended.                                                                                                      | [Experiment controls](../projects/cognition-lab-feature-spec.md#9-experiment-controls-and-saved-work)                                                                  |
| End run (proposed Lab control)                  | Closes an already held episode when the operator has finished with it.                                                                                                                                                         | [Experiment controls](../projects/cognition-lab-feature-spec.md#9-experiment-controls-and-saved-work)                                                                  |
| Held live call (Cognition Lab design)           | A call prepared for the current run and held before dispatch. **Submit this input once** sends an edited input for that one call.                                                                                              | [Prepared-input editing](../projects/cognition-lab-feature-spec.md#8-prepared-input-editing)                                                                           |
| Recorded request (Cognition Lab design)         | The saved input for a model call in an earlier run. Inspecting it sends nothing. A new request must be explicitly submitted.                                                                                                   | [Prepared-input editing](../projects/cognition-lab-feature-spec.md#8-prepared-input-editing)                                                                           |
| Replay recorded run (proposed Lab control)      | Displays the original events, requests and results. It makes no model call or change to the scenario.                                                                                                                          | [Replay and rerun](../projects/cognition-lab-tech-design.md#65-replay-and-rerun)                                                                                       |
| Rerun request (proposed Lab control)            | Explicitly requests a fresh model answer to reproducible saved input. The request comparison alone applies no answer to the simulated world.                                                                                   | [Replay and rerun](../projects/cognition-lab-tech-design.md#65-replay-and-rerun)                                                                                       |
| Continue from checkpoint (proposed Lab control) | Restores a compatible saved state from the chosen decision boundary, rechecks the necessary inputs and runs the new result through normal gameplay admission. It leaves the earlier run intact.                                | [Replay and rerun](../projects/cognition-lab-tech-design.md#65-replay-and-rerun)                                                                                       |
| Run scenario again (proposed Lab control)       | Restores the scenario origin and executes the sequence again, including perception and decision preparation. It is available as an alternative when continuing from a particular decision checkpoint is unsupported.           | [Replay and rerun](../projects/cognition-lab-tech-design.md#65-replay-and-rerun)                                                                                       |
| Variant (Cognition Lab design)                  | A comparison run from the same starting situation with a declared change, so the operator can examine what difference the change makes.                                                                                        | [Lab comparisons](../projects/cognition-lab-feature-spec.md#10-comparison-and-character-quality-workflow)                                                              |
| Ablation (character research)                   | An experiment that removes one input or mechanism, such as omitting a memory from the decision context. Its result describes the case that ran. Establishing later gameplay effects requires observing them.                   | [Character research comparisons](../projects/compelling-characters-feature-spec.md#ce-f08--prompt-history-and-needs-comparisons)                                       |
| Evaluated model                                 | A model whose own responses are assessed against stated criteria for a test input, such as whether its chosen action fits the supplied evidence.                                                                               | [Model answer quality](../verification/decision-api-benchmark.md#answer-quality)                                                                                       |
| Controlled response                             | An answer supplied by the experiment to exercise a known application path, such as verifying that eligible speech is applied and saved once. Evaluating a model’s choices requires collecting that model’s own answers.        | [Real callers and fixtures](measurement-and-delivery.md#what-does-a-check-establish), [model answer quality](../verification/decision-api-benchmark.md#answer-quality) |
