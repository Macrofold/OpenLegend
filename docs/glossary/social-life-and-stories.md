## How people understand and help one another

| Canonical term                       | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Source                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Relationship description             | What one character thinks about another, recorded in the first character's private notes. Ada can trust someone who distrusts her. Updating either account changes neither the other person's feelings nor their rights.                                                                            | [Understanding another person](../worlds/base/social.md#understanding-another-person)                                                                      |
| Acquaintance, trust                  | An acquaintance is someone a character knows. Familiarity can help them recognize a voice. Trust is their judgment about what to expect from the other person. A starting acquaintance grants no invented shared history or automatic trust.                                                        | [Starting acquaintances](../worlds/base/first-playable-people.md#knowledge-stock-and-existing-history)                                                     |
| Appraisal, felt emotion              | An appraisal records what an event, memory or circumstance means to a particular character. A frightening miss can support fear even when no injury occurred. The record belongs to the character and can change as they reconsider the evidence.                                                   | [Current feelings](../worlds/base/social.md#feelings)                                                                                                      |
| Bodily reaction, expression          | A startle is a bodily reaction. A frown is an outward expression an observer may see. Neither proves how the person feels or what they intend to do. The proposed emotional physiology rules combine short bodily reactions and separately interpreted feelings for specific game effects.          | [Three meanings and their owners](../projects/emotional-physiology-feature-spec.md#three-meanings-with-three-owners)                                       |
| Reappraisal                          | Reconsidering what earlier evidence meant. A refused invitation may seem less personal after hearing why someone could not attend. The refusal still happened. The broader experience-shaped character design proposes carrying that revised outlook into later choices.                            | [Experience-shaped characters](../worlds/base/experience-shaped-characters.md#a-life-can-contain-contrary-evidence)                                        |
| Cooperation episode                  | A finite undertaking people have agreed to, such as guiding someone to a known place, carrying one load or hunting particular quarry. Each participant still chooses and performs their own actions. Agreeing to help conveys no right to take supplies or direct another body.                     | [What can be agreed](../projects/first-playable-cooperation-feature-spec.md#what-can-be-agreed)                                                            |
| Alliance, ceasefire                  | An alliance records two people's agreement to treat one another as allies. Each still decides whether to help. A ceasefire is their agreement to stop initiating harm against one another for a defined period. Neither guarantees forgiveness, future help or shared property.                     | [Mutual alliances and finite ceasefires](../projects/first-playable-cooperation-tech-design.md#mutual-alliances-and-finite-ceasefires)                     |
| Commitment, fulfillment, reliability | A commitment records an undertaking. Base can mark a supported gathering promise fulfilled when the promised gathering actually occurs. A missed deadline can leave an obligation overdue. Whether those events make someone dependable is a judgment each person can make from evidence they have. | [Recorded promises](../projects/readable-promises-feature-spec.md#2-supported-behavior) and [Base promise rules](../worlds/base/social.md#spoken-promises) |
| Commission, advance (proposed)       | A commission is an agreement to provide something later. An advance is an actual transfer now against the accepted terms. Agreeing to make a pouch neither produces it nor reserves anyone's materials. Delivery and any remaining payment are separate acts.                                       | [Proposed commission and advance](../worlds/base/durable-agreements.md#advances-and-remaining-claims)                                                      |
| Association (proposed)               | A group people voluntarily join to coordinate repeated work. Membership records a common purpose. Access to its tools, custody of goods and any obligation to another member each need their own agreement.                                                                                         | [Proposed small association](../worlds/base/durable-agreements.md#small-association)                                                                       |

For biological kinship, an item offer or the terms of a shared outing, see [social actions](gameplay-and-space.md#social-actions). In the creator's inspector, a **relationship** can also mean a recorded link between things, such as a recipe and its ingredients. [Inspection relationships](../invention-graph.md#3-relationship-vocabulary)

## Danger, care and death

| Canonical term                           | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sign of danger                           | Something the character can actually perceive, such as hoofprints, disturbed foliage or a stag's threatening posture. How the character interprets it can differ from the danger the simulation actually presents. Missing a sign gives no guarantee of safety.                                                                   | [Player danger](../worlds/base/player-danger.md#observation-earns-safety)                                                                                                                                                       |
| Injury, treatment (proposed care system) | In the care design, an injury is a particular wound with a cause, effects and a course over time. Treatment is an action directed at that wound, such as stabilizing bleeding. The helper needs the patient's consent and separate access to any supplies. Current health loss and treatment of death scars have their own rules. | [Proposed injury and care](../projects/recovery-and-care-feature-spec.md#3-individual-injuries-and-their-causes) and [current death scars](../worlds/base/player-death.md#death-scars)                                          |
| Remains, Continue                        | In Base, death leaves a physical body. Possessions left with the body can be looted. **Continue** gives a dead player character a new physical life under the same identity. Remembered people and events can survive death. Opening a new connection cannot revive the body.                                                     | [Death and possessions](../worlds/base/player-death.md#death-and-possessions) and [Continue](../worlds/base/player-death.md#continue-near-a-rest-spot)                                                                          |
| Ghost, manifestation, revival            | The game retains a dead NPC's identity and history. In the proposed afterlife rules, a **manifestation** would let that person appear temporarily. **Revival** would let them live and act in the world again. Summoning the person for a conversation would grant no right to revive or control them.                            | [Current NPC death and proposed revival](../worlds/base/lifecycle-and-protection.md#npc-death-ghosts-and-revival) and [proposed manifestation](../worlds/base/extraordinary-minds-and-afterlife.md#a-conversation-at-the-cairn) |
| Unattended community (proposed)          | A community selected for continued operation while players are away. The proposed camp must have the actual food, equipment and work to sustain its inhabitants during a specified operating period. A returning player can discover what happened through permitted observations and accounts.                                   | [Proposed community and operating horizon](../worlds/base/continuing-communities.md#selected-scope-and-operating-horizon)                                                                                                       |

## What a story can tell the player

| Canonical term                               | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Source                                                                                                |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Personal story, story selection              | The player's story is a selection of permitted material, presented as prose. An event can be recorded without being selected for the story. Reading the prose adds no new world event or character memory. See [perception and memory](perception-and-memory.md#what-the-character-knows-remembers-or-may-use) for the difference between player history and character recall. | [Story selection](../narration-and-conversations.md#replaceable-story-selection)                      |
| Story perspective (proposed)                 | A separately authorized view of an event for the player as a reader. In the proposed private camp scene, the player could read that a familiar NPC finished making cord after the player character had left. The character neither witnessed the completion nor learns it from the player's reading.                                                                           | [Proposed scene and audience](../worlds/base/story-perspectives.md#authored-scene-and-audience)       |
| Personal Journal edition (proposed)          | A keepsake a player could prepare from selected eligible entries and download. The **Journal** already lets the player read personal records. An edition would be a separate, deliberately selected copy with its own permission checks.                                                                                                                                       | [Proposed Journal edition](../projects/personal-journal-feature-spec.md#31-first-complete-capability) |
| Correspondence, delivery, reading (proposed) | In the proposed message-slate world, two people who exchange contacts can send private written messages. The sender sees **Submitted**. If the message reaches the recipient, opening it records that character's reading experience. Bystanders hear nothing, and the sender receives no delivery or read receipt.                                                            | [Proposed private correspondence](../worlds/base/text-messages.md#a-note-across-separate-activities)  |

## What a character experiment establishes

The Cognition Lab is a proposed workspace for examining character decisions. The existing decision benchmark gives models supplied questions and scores their answers.

| Canonical term                                                          | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Scenario, case                                                          | A scenario sets up the people, situation and rules to investigate. A case asks a specific question about them. For example, one benchmark scene can contain several separately scored questions about what its witnesses know. The Lab design calls a reusable starting situation a **Scenario**.                                                                                              | [Lab records](../projects/cognition-lab-tech-design.md#42-lab-records) and [benchmark cases](../../scripts/decision-benchmark/types.ts)                                                                                            |
| Run, sample                                                             | A run records one execution from a particular starting situation with its inputs, interventions and outcomes. A sample is one observation in the stated comparison, such as one model's response to one question. Name what was counted. Twenty questions about one scene are different from twenty independently played scenes.                                                               | [Run records](../projects/cognition-lab-tech-design.md#42-lab-records) and [benchmark evidence limits](../verification/decision-benchmark-audit.md)                                                                                |
| Held, Stop (proposed Lab controls)                                      | **Held** keeps a run open after a step or requested hold. The operator can send the next stimulus or continue the same episode. **Stop** ends an active run after cancellation and settlement of its work. **End run** closes an already held episode.                                                                                                                                         | [Experiment controls](../projects/cognition-lab-feature-spec.md#9-experiment-controls-and-saved-work)                                                                                                                              |
| Held live call, recorded request (proposed Lab controls)                | A held live call has been prepared for the current run and has not been sent. **Submit this input once** sends the edited input for that one call. A recorded request belongs to an earlier run. Inspecting it sends nothing.                                                                                                                                                                  | [Prepared-input editing](../projects/cognition-lab-feature-spec.md#8-prepared-input-editing)                                                                                                                                       |
| Replay, Rerun request, Continue from checkpoint (proposed Lab controls) | **Replay recorded run** shows the original results without a model call. **Rerun request** sends the reproducible recorded input for a fresh answer without applying it to the world. Applying that answer to the simulated world requires **Continue from checkpoint** and a compatible saved state from that point. Without it, **Run scenario again** starts over from the scenario origin. | [Replay and rerun](../projects/cognition-lab-tech-design.md#65-replay-and-rerun)                                                                                                                                                   |
| Variant, ablation                                                       | The Lab design calls a comparison from the same starting situation with one declared difference a **variant**. In character research an **ablation** removes an input or mechanism, such as omitting one memory from a decision. It can show how the particular case changes. Later gameplay needs its own observation.                                                                        | [Lab comparisons](../projects/cognition-lab-feature-spec.md#10-comparison-and-character-quality-workflow) and [character research](../projects/compelling-characters-feature-spec.md#ce-f08--prompt-history-and-needs-comparisons) |
| Evaluated model, controlled response                                    | An evaluated model supplies its own answer to the test input. A controlled response is supplied by the experiment so the application can exercise known input, transport or effects. It can show whether allowed speech is saved once, for example. Assessing the model's choices requires its actual answers and a stated basis for evaluating them.                                          | [Lab reruns](../projects/cognition-lab-tech-design.md#65-replay-and-rerun) and [model answer quality](../verification/decision-api-benchmark.md#answer-quality)                                                                    |
