# Shared worlds, publishing and offers

[Glossary index](README.md) · [Accounts and participation](access-and-participation.md) · [World creation and invention](worlds-and-creation.md)

The shared-world entries name product designs. The commercial entries name proposed offers and accounting rules. Use the linked owner to check delivery or adoption before describing a feature as available.

## Shared-world design

| Canonical term                | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Source                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Federation                    | A design in which distinct worlds or homelands can participate in agreed connections. Each retains its own people and history. Specify **region** for an area within one authoritative world.                                                                                                                 | [Worlds and belonging](../product-scalability/worlds-and-belonging.md#2-preferred-topology-distinct-homelands-in-a-federation)             |
| Protected domain              | A space, such as a home or guild holding, with explicit permissions governing activities there. Admission, building, taking items, damage and administration can have different permissions.                                                                                                                  | [Personal and guild-controlled spaces](../product-scalability/worlds-and-belonging.md#3-personal-and-guild-controlled-spaces)              |
| Unattended progression        | Simulation continuing under an authorized world policy while players are away. Specify whether a particular character is still physically participating and what activity or funding the policy covers.                                                                                                       | [Participation and protection](../product-scalability/participation-and-protection.md#1-always-available-play-bounded-unattended-activity) |
| Low-power mode                | A world policy that reduces optional activity, such as how often a character starts a new conversation. The selected first design still requires equivalent physical effects and observations for the mechanics it advances.                                                                                  | [Background progression](../product-scalability/background-progression.md#1-what-low-power-progression-promises)                           |
| Promotion (simulation detail) | Switching an ongoing activity to more detailed execution, for example when a player arrives and can interact. The detailed simulation starts with the progress and effects already established.                                                                                                               | [Changes of detail](../projects/product-scalability-tech-design.md#5-promotion-demotion-and-arrival)                                       |
| Demotion (simulation detail)  | Switching an ongoing activity to less detailed execution under the world’s policy. Its progress and earlier consequences carry over.                                                                                                                                                                          | [Changes of detail](../projects/product-scalability-tech-design.md#5-promotion-demotion-and-arrival)                                       |
| Background protection         | A world rule limiting which harms background progression may introduce, including danger already set up by the time detailed play resumes. A world promising safety from background attacks cannot leave a character about to be killed by newly placed wolves. Existing conflicts retain their consequences. | [Background protection](../product-scalability/participation-and-protection.md#3-background-protection-includes-the-inherited-state)       |
| Arrival offer                 | In the travel proposal, notice that the destination can admit the waiting visitor under the reviewed conditions. The visitor still chooses whether to depart.                                                                                                                                                 | [Travel admission](../projects/participants-and-world-travel-feature-spec.md#51-request-wait-offer-depart)                                 |
| Shared campaign               | An in-world undertaking in which actions at different places can affect one continuing outcome. An AWS **verification campaign** is a group of development jobs.                                                                                                                                              | [Shared campaigns](../product-scalability/shared-campaigns.md), [verification](measurement-and-delivery.md)                                |
| Campaign forecast             | Information about a proposed, announced or ongoing campaign and the conditions that could change it. A scheduled opportunity to start is given in real time. An estimate of an in-world arrival depends on the route, world clock and what actually happens.                                                  | [Forecasts](../projects/shared-campaigns-feature-spec.md#5-forecasts-that-can-change-without-lying)                                        |
| Campaign contribution         | A participant’s evidenced action that contributed to the shared undertaking. Recognition, a promised reward and ownership of physical goods are separate consequences.                                                                                                                                        | [Contributions](../projects/shared-campaigns-feature-spec.md#8-contribution-recognition-and-actual-property)                               |

## Publishing and commercial design

| Canonical term           | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                        | Source                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Published pack           | Reusable world content offered for acquisition.                                                                                                                                              | [Pack scope](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#2-the-smallest-useful-catalog)                               |
| Pack release             | A particular published version of a pack, with specified contents, dependencies and rights. It can use a separately identified [asset release](assets-and-releases.md#files-and-identities). | [Pack scope](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#2-the-smallest-useful-catalog)                               |
| Inventor                 | The in-world character credited with the original invention. Preserve the inventor when another character learns the method, a pack is imported, or identical mechanics are reused.          | [Attribution](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#31-keep-attribution-grants-and-payees-distinct)             |
| Publisher                | The person or organization authorized to offer a particular release.                                                                                                                         | [Publishing roles](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#31-keep-attribution-grants-and-payees-distinct)        |
| Rights holder            | The party whose permission is needed for a particular use of contributed material.                                                                                                           | [Publishing roles](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#31-keep-attribution-grants-and-payees-distinct)        |
| Payee                    | The party to whom money is owed under an agreement. Publishing a release does not by itself determine who receives its proceeds.                                                             | [Publishing roles](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#31-keep-attribution-grants-and-payees-distinct)        |
| Contribution grant       | Permission to use contributed material within stated terms. Distinguish this permission from a **world grant** to access a game and from a **creator grant** funding work.                   | [Contributions and free-use worlds](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#33-contributions-and-free-use-worlds) |
| Free-use world           | In the publishing proposal, a world whose covered content and dependency permissions support the advertised reuse. Free admission to play establishes only the price of entry.               | [Free-use worlds](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#33-contributions-and-free-use-worlds)                   |
| Hosted world             | In the offer design, a world operated under specified service, guest and funding conditions.                                                                                                 | [Offer matrix](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#2-smallest-offer-matrix)                                         |
| World host               | The party providing or arranging the service that runs a hosted world.                                                                                                                       | [Offer matrix](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#2-smallest-offer-matrix)                                         |
| Platform membership      | The proposed account-level subscription with its published invention allowance and creator allocation.                                                                                       | [Offer matrix](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#2-smallest-offer-matrix)                                         |
| Premium-world membership | A proposed subscription for the stated benefits of one named world.                                                                                                                          | [Offer matrix](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#2-smallest-offer-matrix)                                         |
| Invention unit           | In the proposed account allowance, one qualifying playable addition admitted under the counting rules. Drafting or calling a provider can incur cost before an invention qualifies.          | [What counts](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#3-what-one-invention-means)                                       |
| World AI funding         | Money or service credit designated for eligible AI work in a world.                                                                                                                          | [Units, money and permission](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#32-units-money-permission-and-uncertainty)        |
| Invention lock           | A permission setting that can prevent invention even when funding is available.                                                                                                              | [Units, money and permission](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#32-units-money-permission-and-uncertainty)        |
| Invention allowance      | The number of qualifying inventions covered under the stated offer. A paid model call can occur before any invention qualifies.                                                              | [Units, money and permission](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#32-units-money-permission-and-uncertainty)        |
| Subscriber allocation    | The bounded portion of a subscriber’s payment assigned under the proposed creator participation rules. Sales earnings and project grants have different sources.                             | [Subscriber allocation](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#8-one-fixed-subscriber-allocation)                |
| Creator grant            | A proposed award of funding for agreed work or exploration.                                                                                                                                  | [Creator funding](../projects/creator-fund-and-contributor-governance-feature-spec.md#2-what-is-accepted-proposed-and-separate)           |
| Supporter dedication     | A proposed reviewed acknowledgment purchased for a named chronicle. Purchasing it grants no right to control characters.                                                                     | [Dedications](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#8-one-optional-supporter-dedication)                              |
| Payout                   | A transfer of creator earnings. Use **earned**, **held**, **payable** and **paid** for the distinct accounting states in the proposal. Only the last records payment.                        | [Payout states](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#92-earned-held-payable-and-paid)                          |
