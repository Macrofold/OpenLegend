# Shared worlds, publishing and offers

[Glossary index](README.md) · [Accounts and participation](access-and-participation.md) · [World creation and invention](worlds-and-creation.md)

The shared-world entries name product designs. The commercial entries name proposed offers and accounting rules. Use the linked owner to check delivery or adoption before describing a feature as available.

## Shared-world design

| Canonical term                | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Source                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Homeland                      | A persistent place and community within the proposed federation, with its own inhabitants, institutions, rules and recorded past. A town can be one homeland even when its simulation runs on several machines.                                                                                               | [Homelands and computing boundaries](../product-scalability/worlds-and-belonging.md#2-preferred-topology-distinct-homelands-in-a-federation) |
| Federation                    | A proposed way to connect distinct homelands under agreed rules while retaining each community’s identity and history. The connection could be a road between settlements or travel between worlds. The design leaves machine and process boundaries to the implementation.                                   | [Worlds and belonging](../product-scalability/worlds-and-belonging.md#2-preferred-topology-distinct-homelands-in-a-federation)               |
| Protected domain              | A space, such as a home or guild holding, with explicit permissions governing activities there. Admission, building, taking items, damage and administration can have different permissions.                                                                                                                  | [Personal and guild-controlled spaces](../product-scalability/worlds-and-belonging.md#3-personal-and-guild-controlled-spaces)                |
| Unattended progression        | The simulation continuing while players are away. The world’s policy specifies which activities can proceed, whether absent players’ characters still participate physically and what funding the work can use.                                                                                               | [Participation and protection](../product-scalability/participation-and-protection.md#1-always-available-play-bounded-unattended-activity)   |
| Low-power mode                | A proposed way to run an unattended world with fewer optional activities, such as new conversations. For the first physical activities selected, it must preserve the effects and relevant observations ordinary play would have produced.                                                                    | [Background progression](../product-scalability/background-progression.md#1-what-low-power-progression-promises)                             |
| Promotion (simulation detail) | Switching an ongoing activity to more detailed execution, for example when a player arrives and can interact. The detailed simulation starts with the progress and effects already established.                                                                                                               | [Changes of detail](../projects/product-scalability-tech-design.md#5-promotion-demotion-and-arrival)                                         |
| Demotion (simulation detail)  | Switching an ongoing activity to less detailed execution once no remaining observer or interaction needs the detail. Earlier progress, outcomes and ongoing conflicts carry forward.                                                                                                                          | [Changes of detail](../projects/product-scalability-tech-design.md#5-promotion-demotion-and-arrival)                                         |
| Background protection         | A world rule limiting which harms background progression may introduce, including danger already set up by the time detailed play resumes. A world promising safety from background attacks cannot leave a character about to be killed by newly placed wolves. Existing conflicts retain their consequences. | [Background protection](../product-scalability/participation-and-protection.md#3-background-protection-includes-the-inherited-state)         |
| Arrival offer                 | In the travel proposal, a time-limited reservation for an eligible visitor to enter the destination. The visitor must still choose to depart while the offer and its terms are valid.                                                                                                                         | [Travel admission](../projects/participants-and-world-travel-feature-spec.md#51-request-wait-offer-depart)                                   |
| Shared campaign               | An in-world undertaking in which actions at different places can affect one continuing outcome. An AWS **verification campaign** is a group of development jobs.                                                                                                                                              | [Shared campaigns](../product-scalability/shared-campaigns.md), [verification](measurement-and-delivery.md)                                  |
| Campaign forecast             | Information about a proposed, announced or ongoing campaign and the conditions that could change it. A scheduled opportunity to start is given in real time. An estimate of an in-world arrival depends on the route, world clock and what actually happens.                                                  | [Forecasts](../projects/shared-campaigns-feature-spec.md#5-forecasts-that-can-change-without-lying)                                          |
| Campaign contribution         | A completed effect on a shared campaign or a useful report with an attributable source. Disconnecting a relay that powers a gate qualifies. Simply being present does not. Publicly naming the participant requires separate permission.                                                                      | [Contributions](../projects/shared-campaigns-feature-spec.md#8-contribution-recognition-and-actual-property)                                 |

## Publishing and commercial design

| Canonical term             | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Published pack             | Reusable world content offered for acquisition.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | [Pack scope](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#2-the-smallest-useful-catalog)                                                                                                                       |
| Pack release               | A particular published version of a pack, with specified contents, dependencies and rights. It can use a separately identified [asset release](assets-and-releases.md#files-and-identities).                                                                                                                                  | [Pack scope](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#2-the-smallest-useful-catalog)                                                                                                                       |
| Inventor                   | The in-world character credited with the original invention. Preserve the inventor when another character learns the method, a pack is imported, or identical mechanics are reused.                                                                                                                                           | [Attribution](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#31-keep-attribution-grants-and-payees-distinct)                                                                                                     |
| Publisher                  | The person or organization authorized to offer a particular release.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | [Publishing roles](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#31-keep-attribution-grants-and-payees-distinct)                                                                                                |
| Rights holder              | The party whose permission is needed for a particular use of contributed material.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | [Publishing roles](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#31-keep-attribution-grants-and-payees-distinct)                                                                                                |
| Payee                      | The party to whom money is owed under an agreement. Publishing a release does not by itself determine who receives its proceeds.                                                                                                                                                                                              | [Publishing roles](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#31-keep-attribution-grants-and-payees-distinct)                                                                                                |
| Contribution grant         | Permission to use contributed material within stated terms. Distinguish this permission from a **world grant** to access a game and from a **creator grant** funding work.                                                                                                                                                    | [Contributions and free-use worlds](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#33-contributions-and-free-use-worlds)                                                                                         |
| Free-use world             | In the publishing proposal, a world whose covered content and dependency permissions support the advertised reuse. Free admission to play establishes only the price of entry.                                                                                                                                                | [Free-use worlds](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#33-contributions-and-free-use-worlds)                                                                                                           |
| Hosted world               | In the offer design, a world operated under specified service, guest and funding conditions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | [Offer matrix](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#2-smallest-offer-matrix)                                                                                                                                 |
| World host                 | In the proposed hosted-world offer, the customer who pays for the world’s operation and covered guest play. The service operator supplies and runs the hosting service.                                                                                                                                                       | [Offer matrix](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#2-smallest-offer-matrix)                                                                                                                                 |
| Platform membership        | The proposed account-level subscription with its published invention allowance and creator allocation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | [Offer matrix](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#2-smallest-offer-matrix)                                                                                                                                 |
| Premium-world membership   | A proposed subscription for the stated benefits of one named world.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | [Offer matrix](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#2-smallest-offer-matrix)                                                                                                                                 |
| Invention unit             | In the proposed account allowance, one independently useful new or materially changed mechanic or recipe the world admits. A waterproofing recipe and its required helper definitions count together. Drafts, rejected attempts and crafting another item use no completed unit. Paid model work is accounted for separately. | [What counts](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#3-what-one-invention-means)                                                                                                                               |
| World AI funding           | Money or service credit authorized to pay for eligible AI requests for a particular world. Funding a request gives the payer no additional authority over characters or their private information.                                                                                                                            | [Units, money and permission](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#32-units-money-permission-and-uncertainty)                                                                                                |
| Invention lock             | A world setting that restricts admission of new or revised definitions according to who initiated the invention. Player-directed and NPC-initiated inventions have separate settings. A player sending an NPC to do the work still counts as the player initiating it.                                                        | [Origin invention lock](../world-constitution.md#4-stability-is-several-independent-controls), [units, money and permission](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#32-units-money-permission-and-uncertainty) |
| Invention allowance        | The number of qualifying inventions an account’s offer covers during the specified period. An attempt may reserve units before work starts. Each qualifying invention uses its unit only when the world admits it.                                                                                                            | [Units, money and permission](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#32-units-money-permission-and-uncertainty)                                                                                                |
| Subscriber allocation      | In the proposed offer, a fixed amount from a paid platform membership that can go to creators of eligible worlds the subscriber visited. The amount follows the paid term. More time spent playing does not increase it.                                                                                                      | [Subscriber allocation](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#8-one-fixed-subscriber-allocation)                                                                                                        |
| Creator grant              | A proposed award of funding for agreed work or exploration.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | [Creator funding](../projects/creator-fund-and-contributor-governance-feature-spec.md#2-what-is-accepted-proposed-and-separate)                                                                                                   |
| Supporter dedication       | In the proposed offer, a reviewed personal acknowledgment published in a named external public chronicle. The purchase creates no item, event or right to control a character in the game.                                                                                                                                    | [Dedications](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#8-one-optional-supporter-dedication)                                                                                                                      |
| Payout                     | A transfer of creator earnings to the payee under the approved agreement. A pending transfer remains in processing until payment is confirmed.                                                                                                                                                                                | [Payout states](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#92-earned-held-payable-and-paid)                                                                                                                  |
| Earned (creator proceeds)  | Money credited to a payee under the revenue agreement for the relevant sale or membership period. It can still be held or adjusted.                                                                                                                                                                                           | [Creator earnings and payout states](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#92-earned-held-payable-and-paid)                                                                                             |
| Held (creator proceeds)    | Money withheld from payout during the ordinary waiting period or while a specific dispute is being resolved.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | [Creator earnings and payout states](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#92-earned-held-payable-and-paid)                                                                                             |
| Payable (creator proceeds) | Creator earnings eligible for transfer under the payout terms. The balance can still be awaiting a scheduled payout.                                                                                                                                                                                                          | [Creator earnings and payout states](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#92-earned-held-payable-and-paid)                                                                                             |
| Paid (creator proceeds)    | Money whose transfer to the payee has been confirmed. Starting the transfer alone leaves it in processing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | [Creator earnings and payout states](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#92-earned-held-payable-and-paid)                                                                                             |
