# Perception, attention and memory

[Glossary index](README.md)

## What happened and what reached the observer

| Canonical term                           | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Source                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| World event, occurrence                  | Something the simulation accepted as having happened at a particular time, such as someone speaking. A request to speak, the stored event and a listener's account are three different things.                                                                                                                       | [Events and reactions](../events-perception-and-reactions.md#2-the-six-distinct-concepts)                                                                     |
| Subject (perception)                     | The person, animal, object or other thing the observer is sensing or tracking. In “the guard watches a hare,” the guard is the observer and the hare is the subject. An observer can perceive an unidentified subject.                                                                                               | [Observer and subject](../technical/cognition-walkthrough.md#start-here-how-the-pieces-fit-together)                                                          |
| Observer, observer perspective           | An observer is the actor whose senses or experience are in question. Their perspective contains what they personally received or learned. It can differ from another witness's account of the same event.                                                                                                            | [Personal perspective and acquisition](../memory-architecture.md#personal-perspective-and-acquisition)                                                        |
| Physical signal, received evidence       | A physical signal can reach a sense, for example sound from someone's voice. Received evidence is what the observer actually acquired through that sense. A wall can leave words audible but the speaker unseen. The server's private knowledge of the sound's origin does not identify the speaker to the listener. | [Perception evidence and references](../projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md#three-representations-one-observation)                                 |
| Detection, localization, recognition     | Detection establishes that something was sensed. Localization establishes where, with whatever precision the evidence supports. Recognition associates it with an individual the observer knows. Hearing a familiar voice from the left can support all three with no exact position or visible body.                | [Shared perception contracts](../projects/stealth-awareness-tech-design.md#shared-contracts)                                                                  |
| Source association                       | Evidence linking two observations to the same source. A listener can associate two sufficiently distinctive voice samples with the same speaker without knowing their name. The server knowing who spoke does not establish that link for the observer.                                                              | [Matching and association](../worlds/base/perception-recognition.md#matching-and-association)                                                                 |
| Not detected, not checked                | “I detected nothing” requires a completed check within a particular time, area and sense. An unfinished search or an unobserved interval supplies no such result. Neither establishes that the whole area was empty.                                                                                                 | [Incomplete and missed observation](../projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md#incomplete-and-missed-observation)                                      |
| Exposure, current body reference         | An exposure is an observer's encounter with a particular sensory source. A current body reference can be used only for a physical target the observer may act on now. After losing sight, a character can remember the encounter while the permission to act on that visible body has already ended.                 | [Four reference roles](../projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md#one-identity-owner-four-distinct-reference-roles)                                    |
| Speech intelligibility                   | How much of an utterance the listener understood. The wire values are `none`, `partial` and `clear`. Hearing that somebody spoke can yield no words. A seen-only observation supplies neither words nor evidence about whom the speaker was addressing.                                                              | [Listener-specific speech evidence](../hearing-and-speech.md#5-one-occurrence-listener-specific-evidence)                                                     |
| Received order and viewing frame         | Received order distinguishes observations even when they share a game timestamp. A viewing frame records the observer’s viewpoint for a particular sample. A later view cannot move the earlier observation to the new viewpoint.                                                                                    | [Observation records](../technical/cognition-walkthrough.md), [shared temporal references](../identity-and-references.md#selected-shared-temporal-references) |
| Awareness record, personal account       | An awareness record stores one observer's account of a particular event. Two nearby listeners can retain different words from the same utterance. A **personal account** here means a record of experience, distinct from the account a player uses to sign in.                                                      | [Events and awareness](../memory-architecture.md#5-events-awareness-and-memory-storage)                                                                       |
| Direct observation, testimony, inference | “I heard Ada say the gate fell” records a direct observation of speech. “The gate fell” is Ada's testimony for this listener. “It probably broke in the storm” is an inference. Recall and retelling must preserve who supplied each claim and what was actually observed.                                           | [Personal perspective](../memory-architecture.md#personal-perspective-and-acquisition)                                                                        |

## What receives closer attention

| Canonical term                        | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Source                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Broad perception, detailed attention  | Broad perception can register several people passing. Detailed attention selects particular subjects for closer observation and can record intermediate movement for those subjects. Falling outside detailed attention does not mean a visible person disappeared.                                                                                                                                          | [Base attention capacity](../worlds/base/attention.md#foreground-capacity)                                                                                         |
| Detailed attention capacity           | The maximum number of subjects one observer can track in detail at the same time. The Base rules call this **foreground capacity**. A limit of one incidental subject restricts that reason for selection. It leaves other priorities, such as a person addressing the observer, subject to the overall limit.                                                                                               | [Base attention rules](../worlds/base/attention.md#foreground-capacity)                                                                                            |
| Perception, Focus (Base attributes)   | **Perception** is an authored ability used when recognizing individuals. **Focus** helps set how many subjects an observer can follow in detail. More Focus does not reveal a hidden object or identify a stranger. Lowercase **perception** also names the general process of sensing.                                                                                                                      | [Recognition ability](../worlds/base/perception-recognition.md#installation-and-ability) and [attention capacity](../worlds/base/attention.md#foreground-capacity) |
| Cue, incidental subject               | A cue is something the observer received that can draw attention. In Base, passing movement can make one additional subject worth following closely for an NPC. Urgent cues, being addressed, deliberate focus and a current work target have their own priorities. The stored reason `cue` covers both urgent and incidental selections.                                                                    | [Incidental subjects](../worlds/base/attention.md#how-many-incidental-subjects-an-npc-tracks)                                                                      |
| Discovery in attention selection      | The observer can follow one additional subject whose current description has yet to be shown in their decision context. The game can keep the same subject briefly. When selecting again, it takes eligible subjects in the order the observer encountered them, returning to the beginning when needed. A familiar person whose appearance has changed can qualify. This selection starts no model request. | [Discovery and detailed observation](../worlds/base/attention.md#how-many-incidental-subjects-an-npc-tracks)                                                       |
| Optional discovery decision           | A separate chance for an NPC that has free time to consider something it has not yet considered. The scheduler checks the character's actual circumstances and current evidence before admitting work. Seeing a new object does not replenish this opportunity.                                                                                                                                              | [Base decision opportunities](../worlds/base/attention.md#semantic-opportunities)                                                                                  |
| Attention during decision preparation | A relevance judgment about which already-permitted items to include for a particular decision. The items can include a recipe or an earlier conversation as well as a currently visible object. This is distinct from choosing which moving subjects to track with the senses.                                                                                                                               | [Jev attention and context](../memory-architecture.md#4-jev-attention-before-context-inclusion)                                                                    |
| Urgency, importance                   | Urgency describes how quickly a recent event warrants attention or reconsideration. Importance describes its lasting significance. A warning to duck can be urgent. Learning the name of a relative can matter much later without requiring an immediate reaction.                                                                                                                                           | [Importance and urgency](../memory-architecture.md#importance-urgency-and-in-flight-response-refresh)                                                              |

## What the character knows, remembers or may use

| Canonical term                         | Meaning in OpenLegend                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Source                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Familiarity, recognition, known name   | Familiarity reflects prior supported exposure to an individual. Recognition uses the evidence available now to make a match, which can be mistaken. A known name is a separately learned or authored label. Someone can recognize a voice without knowing its owner's name.                                                                                                   | [Base recognition](../worlds/base/perception-recognition.md#matching-and-association) and [names](../worlds/base/knowledge.md#spoken-introductions)                            |
| Observer-local individual              | A person or distinguishable source remembered under one observer's own identity record, such as “the stranger who helped me.” A private label or note can refer to that individual even when the observer cannot connect them to a particular identified person or current body.                                                                                              | [Learned individuals](../worlds/base/knowledge.md#learned-individuals-without-a-canonical-association)                                                                         |
| Recent history, lasting memory         | Recent history retains particular observations and their original times within the installed world's limits. Lasting memory keeps selected experiences or interpretations with their surviving support. Repeatedly displaying a memory supplies no new observation.                                                                                                           | [Base history and memory](../worlds/base/attention.md#recent-history-and-lasting-memory)                                                                                       |
| Recall, consolidation                  | Recall selects what a character can retrieve for a present purpose. Consolidation processes eligible earlier accounts into retained memories or summaries. A summary of one witness's words still has one witness, and cannot recover words that witness never heard.                                                                                                         | [Recall](../memory-architecture.md#retrieval-service-and-selective-recall-tools) and [consolidation](../memory-architecture.md#6-hourly-consolidation-and-six-hour-raw-recall) |
| Knowledge document, learned method     | A knowledge document is an editable personal note or authored starting text. A learned method is an actor's recorded acquisition of a capability they can try to use. Writing “I know how to make a bow” in a note cannot grant that capability.                                                                                                                              | [Actor knowledge](../knowledge.md#documents-and-authority) and [learned methods](../knowledge.md#learned-methods)                                                              |
| Player history, Events                 | **Events** is the read-only player view of this character's permitted recorded occurrences. It can show an old event outside the character's current recall window. Creator inspection has separate permissions, and cached player history must honor correction and forgetting.                                                                                              | [Perceived World Events](../perceived-world-events.md#1-navigation-and-scope)                                                                                                  |
| Source, evidence reference             | A source is the particular observation, event account or other record that supports a claim. A reference identifies it for a named use. The server checks the reader's permission and the current state of the source before revealing or using it. A remembered name, for example, cannot locate a hidden person.                                                            | [Evidence and permitted references](../projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md#evidence-records-and-permitted-references)                                               |
| Permitted evidence                     | The information this recipient is entitled to receive for this use, down to the individual fact or spoken fragment. The server can retain the original utterance while supplying a partial quotation to a listener. Signing into another tab does not broaden those rights.                                                                                                   | [Current authority](../identity-and-references.md#control-and-projection) and [partial speech](../hearing-and-speech.md#6-partial-text-and-privacy)                            |
| Correction, forgetting, natural expiry | An authorized correction changes what the affected source supports. Explicit forgetting removes the affected character's access and invalidates uses that rely on it. Ordinary expiry ends the retention or use of a record under its normal lifetime. A contradictory report by a second witness is a new claim and leaves the first witness's account attributable to them. | [Source changes and forgetting](../projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md#correction-contradiction-revocation-and-deletion)                                            |
