# Authored worlds

World specifications describe chosen realities and their mechanics. They do not define universal engine behavior. The [engine/world boundary](../engine-and-world-boundaries.md) owns that distinction.

- [Open Legend base world](base/README.md): the bundled first-party reality, its mechanics and content.

Give each additional authored world its own directory. Engine execution interfaces, permissions, transactions, perception privacy and transport remain in their existing subsystem specifications. A world may use a native executor without making its rules universal.
