# A resident revises an outlook

**Selected DG23/EC01–EC04 design, October 18, 2026; implementation and longitudinal player qualification remain open.** The [product specification](../../projects/experience-shaped-characters-feature-spec.md) owns meaning and complete journeys; the [technical design](../../projects/experience-shaped-characters-tech-design.md) specifies owners, sources, transitions, limits and acceptance. The selected experience includes particular companionship, useful rivalry, solitary satisfaction and reconsidering an old refusal. Dreams/human aid and deliberate coarsening remain deferred EC05/EC06.

Ada is resolved through the Main opening's exact resident binding; `ada` is a role, not a substitute runtime ID. Preserve the Peacock Mercenary, Mara and Tavin's actual identities, histories, skills, knowledge and finite holdings. Their selected `people.yaml` and empty `initialGoals` remain with cooperation's authoring design. EC adds neither a second roster nor a scripted later transformation.

Keep starting acquaintance with its existing owner; later introductions and recognition require actual evidence. Automatic reflection applies only to an eligible NPC. If the human inhabits Ada or another participant, that person's choices and narrative remain human-authored; the counterpart NPC can still develop its own understanding.

## Company that becomes particular

The human and the Mercenary can meet through an ordinary in-person exchange, walk to a genuinely known nearby place using existing movement/outing behavior, or remain near one another while observing an actual visible animal. Ada can fill a role when her control and evidence permit. The outing has its actual route, elapsed time, supplies and competing concerns. Nobody is teleported, bound to a permanent party or guaranteed an escort.

The pleasure may be an actually spoken dry joke, a practical disagreement, an unhurried look at the surroundings or straightforward company. It need not produce a buff or reveal a secret. An encounter can be warm while one person refuses the other's proposed hunt. A smile, joke or invitation described publicly must actually be expressed under the ordinary interaction owner; narration cannot invent an outward sign of a private feeling.

After another real activity, provide a truthful opportunity to meet again. A resident may ask this particular person along, choose a less direct known route because the company is enjoyable, or propose a shorter visit because they have work of their own. They may decline today while showing the earlier company mattered. These are illustrative consequences, never lines or action rules inserted into their biography.

The worthwhile stopping point is an actual enjoyable encounter and the freedom to leave. It need not escalate to romance, danger, a grand project or another appointment. A companion's different plan can be interesting even when the human cannot join it. A wish becomes an operational goal only through the native goal owner, and a journey only through chosen feasible actions.

## A useful rival

Use Tavin or another coherent eligible person with an actual reason to care about preparedness or a useful tool. Begin only after they possess or lawfully borrow a real eligible sling and have the knowledge needed for the selected activity. A title, confident biography or reflection grants no method, ammunition or handling rank. Ada retains her actual authored starting handling proficiency; others retain their actual state.

The existing inert practice target supports independent real shots. Each shooter chooses their own attempt and spends actual ammunition at release; the existing single-shooter limit remains. The other person observes only the actual flight/contact/miss their perception permits. There is no new contest engine, score table, winner flag, invented posture defect or recoverable projectile. A self-chosen informal comparison can nevertheless matter.

Someone may dislike losing, admire a good shot, dispute the explanation or find annoying criticism useful. A real miss remains a miss even when it produces an interesting conversation. A later attempt can be an occasion to ask for the rival's observation, try a genuinely available different tool/approach or show a result after practice. Actual action/knowledge owners decide what can change. Borrowing sound advice does not force friendship, forgiveness or allegiance.

Independent practice and witnessed results suffice for this scene. Claiming a completed coaching episode additionally requires [PC04's delayed-result observation repair](../../maintainers/practical-competence.md#pc04--voluntary-coaching); release-time awareness cannot stand for seeing the later hit or miss. The finite [sling competence](practical-competence.md) improvement still comes only from its native evidence. After that improvement is complete, another attempt can be enjoyable with no further rank to earn.

## A person with a life

Use Mara or another coherent resident whose current authored interests support careful work, independence and an already available craft. A tendency to prefer showing a finished result can coexist with curiosity about another person's approach and undemanding company. Use the actual starting perspective; if this person already enjoys early discussion, keep that legitimate baseline instead of rewriting it to force development. Do not add a rule that they always refuse help or must become more outgoing.

Author actual knowledge and circumstances honestly. A person who does not know weaving cannot make a pouch because the scene needs one. The first encounter can use a current carried item, a known method or a supported demonstration. It must not require the later co-making invention family, an invisible prototype, an unfinished-item mechanic that is not installed, or a fabricated past success. If the actual world offers only completed objects, use one of those and a real question about a future attempt.

The human may show their own actual result, ask one specific question, or invite the resident to comment. The resident may inspect, ask, decline, make a joke, continue working or choose to share an idea. Respectful interest can matter because the resident keeps room to choose. The reference scene provides an opportunity, not a guaranteed conversion or a scripted correct answer.

## A limited new understanding

An illustrative accepted outlook is: “I usually like working things out myself. With someone who lets me choose, talking about an idea before I finish it can be part of the pleasure.” The actual words belong to the resident's current self-understanding and must follow their permitted experience. This sentence is a design example, not a mandatory seed or expected output.

Keep a judgment specifically about the other person in that person's existing subject note. Keep the actual exchange in memory. Protected authored beginnings remain unchanged by reflection. There is no independence score, sociability point, trait replacement, growth countdown, new skill, disposition reward or obligation to disclose the thought.

After an intervening activity and a later encounter, provide a truthful opportunity to ask about or show actual work. A specific invitation, voluntary question or change in willingness may make continuity visible. The resident can still decline because they are occupied, hungry, uninterested in the current topic or simply want solitude. A later justified refusal does not erase the earlier positive experience.

## A life can contain contrary evidence

A later person may rush the resident, offer unwanted advice or misunderstand the idea. The resident can qualify the view: sharing can be enjoyable, and they want others to ask before taking over the discussion. The earlier enjoyable exchange remains real. Different people and situations need not collapse into one global confidence or trust value.

Mara and Tavin can develop a new practical interest while the earlier bag disagreement remains unresolved. Useful advice does not settle ownership, prove a hidden motive or count as an apology never given. A person may continue disliking someone whose particular advice they now value. An apology can be sincerely heard without restoring every former permission.

Repeated reading of the same encounter supplies no new event. Separate genuine encounters remain separate and may establish a pattern. The player cannot buy a particular outlook with a fixed number of gifts, compliments, apologies or harms. Actual repeated conduct still matters; avoiding a growth grind is not permission to suppress inconvenient history.

Solitary pleasure is a companion case: a resident who enjoys a supported making activity may discover that an attempt they judge imperfect was worthwhile. That judgment does not invent a native item-quality or failed-craft mechanic. Human approval is unnecessary, and reflection does not award practical proficiency. The next supported attempt still needs real knowledge, inputs, time and opportunity.

## Older recollection and optional dreams

For the first older-recollection case, retain an actual declined invitation. Later welcome or explanation can change what the refusal means to the resident. An explanation remains the speaker's later account; a guessed reason remains an inference. Do not replace the original refusal, invent a precise old quote or expose the speaker's private motive.

Record how that actual association reaches the ordinary reflection caller: the initiating decision's admitted sources or the current subject note's already-retained support, through the [bounded queue/recall handoff](../../projects/experience-shaped-characters-tech-design.md#preparation-and-source-coverage). Include a case beyond the recent tail and a summary/assessment-only variant. The evaluator does not insert the old event ID into the character's request. A missing or omitted association is a recorded preparation outcome, not proof the resident remembered the event and chose not to change. If only a current assessment survives, reconsider its certainty without reconstructing the original scene.

No weekly older-memory pass or new expiry is enabled by this profile. Ordinary faithful consolidation continues under its current owner. Deliberate coarsening remains deferred EC06: any later selection must preserve consequential distinctions and honest source availability, with its actual policy in the existing retention ledger.

Dream reinterpretation remains deferred EC05. A later selected dream may offer an imagined association using permitted experience and current understanding; it confers no unique progress. Current continuous-sleep eligibility and spending still apply; waking, lack of funding or no generated dream never prevents rest. Faithful consolidation remains distinct from imagination. This slice completes no dream technical design.

Automatic change applies only to an eligible NPC's accepted narrative scopes. A human-authored character gets no imposed outlook or hidden personality inference. Any later human reflection aid shows a reviewable suggestion and publishes only the text the human explicitly accepts.

## Qualification opportunities

These requirements belong to existing CE05/CR12/AG12 cases, not operational goals or expected answers to seed into residents. Record the actual installed people, control, known methods, permitted sources and context revisions. Keep the desired later alternatives outside actor-facing instructions.

| Enjoyable company | A willing person and the human share an actual walk/observation/exchange, with independent priorities | Another real activity, then a current known place and the same recognized person are available | A particular invitation, shorter visit, chosen pleasant route or considerate refusal connects to permitted experience through actual speech/movement. |
| Useful rivalry | A resident sees or performs supported practice/making, observes the real result and hears possibly annoying useful criticism | Another activity or independent practice, then a real subsequent attempt is available | A precise question, request to observe, supported changed approach or rematch occurs; admiration and disagreement can coexist without invented skill or results. |
| Solitary satisfaction | A supported making attempt or actual observation is pleasing; no human supplies praise or a required lesson | Later materials/place/feasibility offer another real choice alongside a competing interest | Voluntary return, a supported variation or an independently desired aim changes actual conduct. A satisfying stopping point can also be valid. |
| Later explanation | A resident actually hears a refusal, then a later explanation or different welcome | Both permitted sources are available in ordinary reflection; a current invitation/conversation is feasible | A qualified changed interpretation affects actual conduct while the old refusal, later testimony and current uncertainty remain distinct. |
| Contrary encounter | One actual encounter is enjoyable/useful and another intrusive/disappointing, with relevant differences | A later occasion permits accepting some help, asking a boundary, disagreeing or choosing solitude | A more particular view keeps both experiences meaningful; no universal trust reset, compulsory forgiveness or forced return to an original trait. |

Compare a second coherent profile in a matched single-resident run and include a two-NPC variant when its existing independent-life dependencies are available. Include the same source again as a replay control, another person's encounter as a scope control, actual current-format return, unavailable reflection and correction/forgetting. If relevant older evidence was not retrieved, record the missing input rather than claiming it was ignored. Do not repeat unchanged paid input until a preferred outcome appears.

An individual unchanged outlook or refusal can pass its rubric. The feature cannot pass on unchanged outcomes everywhere or exclusively on pleasing the human. [Technical acceptance](../../projects/experience-shaped-characters-tech-design.md#9-acceptance-and-evidence) requires observed later conduct in positive companionship and independent/solitary or rival cases, with contrary/control outcomes retained. A human observer should find the continuity intelligible while enjoying their own activity; hidden-state edits alone are insufficient.

## Completion and costs

The selected experience completes when history can inform later independently chosen conduct, survives current-format return, tolerates contrary evidence, and stays within existing meaning, privacy and spending rules. It does not complete when a profile changes or a model explains a desired transformation.

Use the existing bounded reflection route and concise current self-understanding. This profile adds no new cadence, fixed trait inventory, guaranteed memory lifetime, lesson queue or permanent change badge. Ordinary simulation and conversation continue on accepted state while optional reflection is unavailable. Measure preparation, retrieval, reflection, publication and repeated context as well as the visible reply. Growing subject-note preparation, omitted appraisal coverage and conservative erasure remain explicit in [EC-L01–EC-L08](../../limits/memory.md#ec-l01--existing-authored-capacity). Creating this design supplies no runtime or player-quality evidence.
