# A resident revises an outlook

**Proposed DG23 world profile, October 6, 2026.** The [product specification](../../projects/experience-shaped-characters-feature-spec.md) owns meaning and complete journeys. This profile supplies the reference experience, without installing new traits, methods, possessions, residents or cognition policy into current starts.

## A person with a life

Use a resident who values careful work and independence, enjoys an already supported craft, and generally prefers showing a result once satisfied with it. They may also be curious about another person's approach and enjoy undemanding company. Their starting tendency does not say that they always refuse help or must become more outgoing.

Author actual knowledge and circumstances honestly. A person who does not know weaving cannot make a pouch because the scene needs one. The first encounter can use a current carried item, a known method or a supported demonstration. It must not require the later co-making invention family, an invisible prototype, an unfinished-item mechanic that is not installed, or a fabricated past success. If the actual world offers only completed objects, use one of those and a real question about a future attempt.

The human may show their own actual result, ask one specific question, or invite the resident to comment. The resident may inspect, ask, decline, make a joke, continue working or choose to share an idea. Respectful interest can matter because the resident keeps room to choose. The reference scene provides an opportunity, not a guaranteed conversion or a scripted correct answer.

## A limited new understanding

An illustrative accepted outlook is: “I usually like working things out myself. With someone who lets me choose, talking about an idea before I finish it can be part of the pleasure.” The actual words belong to the resident's current self-understanding and must follow their permitted experience. This sentence is a design example, not a mandatory seed or expected output.

Keep a judgment specifically about the other person in that person's existing subject note. Keep the actual exchange in memory. Protected authored beginnings remain unchanged by reflection. There is no independence score, sociability point, trait replacement, growth countdown, new skill, disposition reward or obligation to disclose the thought.

After an intervening activity and a later encounter, provide a truthful opportunity to ask about or show actual work. A specific invitation, voluntary question or change in willingness may make continuity visible. The resident can still decline because they are occupied, hungry, uninterested in the current topic or simply want solitude. A later justified refusal does not erase the earlier positive experience.

## A life can contain contrary evidence

A later person may rush the resident, offer unwanted advice or misunderstand the idea. The resident can qualify the view: sharing can be enjoyable, and they want others to ask before taking over the discussion. The earlier enjoyable exchange remains real. Different people and situations need not collapse into one global confidence or trust value.

Repeated reading of the same encounter supplies no new event. Separate genuine encounters remain separate and may establish a pattern. The player cannot buy a particular outlook with a fixed number of gifts, compliments, apologies or harms. Actual repeated conduct still matters; avoiding a growth grind is not permission to suppress inconvenient history.

Solitary pleasure is a companion case: a resident who enjoys a supported making activity may discover that an attempt they judge imperfect was worthwhile. That judgment does not invent a native item-quality or failed-craft mechanic. Human approval is unnecessary, and reflection does not award practical proficiency. The next supported attempt still needs real knowledge, inputs, time and opportunity.

## Older recollection and optional dreams

For the first older-recollection case, retain an actual declined invitation. Later welcome or explanation can change what the refusal means to the resident. An explanation remains the speaker's later account; a guessed reason remains an inference. Do not replace the original refusal, invent a precise old quote or expose the speaker's private motive.

No weekly older-memory pass or new expiry is enabled by this profile. Ordinary faithful consolidation continues under its current owner. Later deliberate coarsening may lose incidental detail while preserving consequential distinctions and honest source availability; its actual policy belongs in the existing retention ledger.

The optional dream can offer an imagined association using permitted experience and current understanding. It confers no unique progress and is not required for change. The current continuous-sleep eligibility and spending policy still apply; waking, lack of funding or no generated dream never prevents rest. Daily faithful consolidation remains distinct from imaginative reinterpretation.

Automatic change applies only to an eligible NPC's accepted narrative scopes. A human-authored character gets no imposed outlook or hidden personality inference. Any later human reflection aid shows a reviewable suggestion and publishes only the text the human explicitly accepts.

## Completion and costs

The selected experience completes when history can inform later independently chosen conduct, survives current-format return, tolerates contrary evidence, and stays within existing meaning, privacy and spending rules. It does not complete when a profile changes or a model explains a desired transformation.

Use the existing bounded reflection route and concise current self-understanding. This profile adds no new cadence, fixed trait inventory or lifetime-memory allowance. Ordinary simulation and conversation continue on accepted state while optional reflection is unavailable. Measure preparation, retrieval, reflection and repeated context as well as the visible reply.
