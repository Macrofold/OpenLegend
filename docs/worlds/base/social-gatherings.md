# Something worth showing — wilderness gathering profile

**Proposed authored-world profile, October 6, 2026; not implemented or adopted.** This page owns DG18's initial gathering content, attendance and later qualification workload. The [attention and scenes feature specification](../../projects/attention-and-scenes-feature-spec.md#17-dg18--something-worth-showing-and-a-gathering-worth-joining) owns the shared behavior, independent choices, listener evidence, interface and service boundaries. Existing hearing, conversations, physical actions, objects and human participation remain authoritative.

## An ordinary encounter with a point

Begin at the existing camp with one human, Ada and the Peacock Mercenary. They are three actual people, not a population target or a required roster for every conversation. Retain the residents' existing identities, knowledge and current concerns. The encounter follows the player's own exploration, invention or making: they have a real object or experience to discuss, and can decide whether the company is interesting.

The first proposed subject is a useful object the human actually made and can show through normal perception. A carrying container can hold and organize real supplies; a genuinely invented sling can be discussed after a real attempt to use it. The offer does not grant an invented object merely to ensure that every run has the same prop. If the player has no made object, they can ask about something another resident actually has, share a real observation or simply talk; ordinary conversation is never locked behind a crafting milestone.

A possible exchange concerns whether the object solves the maker's problem. One resident might care about a practical limitation while the other enjoys an unusual design or doubts the maker's account. These are opportunities compatible with actual identities, not assigned lines or compulsory personality tests. Ada need not always be helpful. The Mercenary need not turn every conversation toward combat. Humor, curiosity, aesthetic preference, uncertainty, pride, embarrassment and quiet company are all legitimate.

The human can explain, ask, demonstrate a supported use, offer the object, retain it, propose another invention or leave. The player can also listen without speaking. Nobody must receive a reward, friendship increase or new chore for attending. Existing possessions and activity results carry the consequences.

| Place | Existing reachable camp space, with actual sight and acoustic access | No hall, stage, seating system, invitation board or new construction is required. Placement does not override perception. |
| Matter | A known real made object, its actual use, or the participants' current related interest | The player brings their own achievement or curiosity; no hidden-answer presentation or mandatory agenda. Subjects may change naturally. |

| Time | Current world clock and ordinary controls; no fixed meeting timer, daily reset or attendance reward | The gathering ends through real participation and decisions. Reading preferences do not stop bodily needs or lengthen sound. |
| Physical effects | Existing inspection, holding, dropping, work, native item offers/acceptance and supported uses | A fluent account cannot create a test result, grant a method or move an object. |
| Social direction | Independent resident choice with ordinary ability to refuse, remain silent, change subject or leave | Neither a host role nor the human's interest scripts other people. |
| Hosting | Attended play under existing authorization and service settings | This does not require funded empty-world service or extend DG17's unattended offer. |

The intended interaction does not require choosing Listen to separately from ordinary Talk. The shared conversation reading extension belongs to the feature spec; the next addressed person stays explicit. Other people hear only through current rules and may act on that evidence independently.

A participant can become hungry, tired or occupied during a long conversation. Do not freeze needs, reset their body or force them to ignore another concern for the demonstration. Prepare a suitable ordinary play situation and permit normal eating/rest choices. A person needing to leave can do so. A sequence that mainly makes humans race speech against preventable starvation is failed pacing evidence, not a reason to add a new conversation clock silently.

Current conversation inactivity and maximum hearing continuity remain their own rules. They are not a selected duration for this entire social occasion. Expiration of a conversation identity must not erase the human's permitted visible history or draft; a later spoken engagement can form its actual current conversation without pretending the old membership never ended.

## Physical demonstrations and custody

Show an object through actual permitted presence, inspection or an existing action. A visible model proves its appearance and presence, not every hidden property. A person's description of how it performed is testimony unless the listener witnessed the use or has another supported source.

The current woven container helps organize and share actual supplies. It does not increase carrying strength, preserve freshness or create waterproof storage. The gathering can reveal a desired improvement without pretending it already exists. The human can submit that idea through the ordinary invention owner and continue other play while it is considered.

Dropping an object creates its normal grounded custody/access situation; it is not a protected display case. If retaining control matters, show it while held or use the native offer operation. An offer moves and reserves nothing, and only the named recipient's actual acceptance moves custody after current checks. Refusal, withdrawal and expiry leave the object where the actual owner rules put it. An accepted handover does not invent a formal loan or guaranteed return.

The social consequence can be real even without a new numerical reward. A resident may later recall the maker's care, the failed attempt or a disagreement, through their permitted experience and current interpretation. The design does not assign an automatic positive relationship change or compel a future compliment.

## Gist and vocal activity selection

The first new gist family is a two-NPC informal exchange about a known object or current activity. Either current resident can initiate if it fits their actual concerns; the other independently answers or does nothing new. It is not an automatic camp routine and does not author a human's words.

For this new family, **clear eligible hearing receives the committed meaning; partial or merely detected sound receives only an indistinct-activity cue**. This is an intentionally conservative proposed world rule because no exact wording exists from which to preserve a safe partial fragment. It is not a change to current exact partial speech. Names, recipient identity and direction still require their own permitted evidence. A clearly hearing bystander may learn the meaning without becoming a participant.

Gist can communicate a question, opinion, observation, preference or intention. It cannot complete a trade, grant knowledge of a recipe or make a mechanically binding promise. Use actual exact/native requirements when the exchange reaches those actions. Keep source claims separate from physical facts, and preserve uncertainty and conditions when retelling.

The later nonverbatim vocal activity is a separately admitted chosen continuation. Each qualifying pair has **at most one currently vocal member** at an instant; another member can listen, respond later or leave. This is the selected first turn-taking activity, not a universal ban on overlapping speech. The other participant's willingness to remain involved is independently established, never inferred from a shared label.

Each such chosen continuation lasts **at most 30 game minutes**, which is **30 real seconds at the current normal 1× rate** if uninterrupted. This proposed ceiling bounds one period of genuinely ongoing conversational sound without making it an endless ambient emitter. It is unmeasured authoring for qualification, not a derived human conversation duration. It stops earlier if its actual conditions end. A later continuation requires a real existing chosen finite plan or a new admitted choice and complete-work allowance; expiration never schedules an automatic paid restart. Ending this activity creates no new semantic contribution or mandatory cognition opportunity. A further gist occurs only when a resident actually chooses and successfully communicates another contribution. The pair does not alternate speakers on a timer; silence remains possible.

Use the vocal member's installed ordinary speech source profile and actual position. The hearing owner combines real competing source energy and excludes the evaluated foreground speaker. Do not add sound from captions, old dialogue, pending provider work, a silent listener or the same speaker twice. A delayed decision cannot keep a supposedly lively discussion sounding forever. A gist contribution only creates listener knowledge when that contribution actually commits; detecting activity in the meantime does not reveal its topic.

The first selected focus policy stabilizes presentation and current-interest priority without an acoustic clarity bonus. Choosing observational focus does not cancel this activity. Actual interaction may change what participants choose to do, and subsequent exact speech uses its proper source boundary. The selected vocal continuation has no effect on sleep, injury or objects beyond separately supported rules; loud-sound waking and damage remain unsupported here.

## Later busy-camp workload

After the smaller encounter and gist family qualify, propose an attended camp with **seven actual bodies**: the human, Ada, the Peacock Mercenary and four additional independently authored residents. Its baseline arrangement has the human with the current residents and two additional NPC pairs with their own current matters. The additional residents need genuine individual identities, permitted histories and independent choices; duplicated audience fillers do not establish this workload.

One pair might compare a personally made container with a known alternative. Another might discuss something they actually encountered near the lookout, with room for a disagreement or an aesthetic interest. These are candidate matters for authored people, not programmed repeated exchanges. An empty subject does not justify generating gossip. The shared location permits the human to notice another useful possibility and approach it, while their original conversation remains understandable.

Admit **at most two** of the selected background vocal activities simultaneously in this qualification profile. Place the groups at actual reachable positions where the intended foreground interaction can be understood under the installed acoustic rules. Do not claim every layout works. A deliberately adverse arrangement where another group moves close is useful qualification of lost clarity, movement and choice; it is not the default that forces constant shouting.

The initial arrangement does not cap the foreground at three people. The workload must include a real group merge, direct engagement with a background participant and an ordinary exact conversation involving the available group. All eligible listeners retain exact evidence. If those transitions cannot be supported, the seven-body offer is not qualified merely because its quiet initial frame looks good.

The larger group is outside DG17's two-resident unattended supply and funding envelope. It cannot inherit that profile's horizon, food allowance or whole-community hosting promise. First qualify its actual attended cost and play; any later unattended version needs a fresh viable community and authorized service selection.

## What to observe before expansion

Players should be able to explain why they approached, what someone else wanted, who the next message addresses, what they know and what they can choose next. A quiet or refused exchange can pass if it expresses a believable person and leaves the human free to enjoy their own game.

Compare the initial exact gathering with the later gist and busy versions. Keep complete costs, repeated admission attempts, actual histories and failures. Examine experienced play as well as a novelty visit: no optimal reward loop should demand repetitive attendance, constant object passing or pressure on slower participants. Increasing population is justified only when the added people provide worthwhile encounters at a supported cost.
