# Something worth showing — wilderness gathering profile

**Selected design profile, updated October 17, 2026; not implemented.** This page owns DG18's exact attended gathering, selected compact two-NPC background exchange and the separately conditional vocal/crowd workload. The [attention and scenes feature specification](../../projects/attention-and-scenes-feature-spec.md#17-dg18--something-worth-showing-and-a-gathering-worth-joining) owns shared behavior, independent choices, listener evidence, interface and service boundaries. The selected [speech and social perception design](../../projects/speech-and-social-perception-feature-spec.md) (SPK) owns ordinary speech, directed address, recognition and presentation; this profile consumes that design without conversation membership. Current runtime hearing, speech, physical actions, objects and human participation remain authoritative until their selected changes ship.

## An ordinary encounter with a point

Begin with a useful ordinary exact conversation at the existing camp. The selected next group-qualification arrangement uses one human, Ada and the Peacock Mercenary when the other resident actually participates. They are three actual people, not a population target or a roster the player must assemble before talking. Retain the residents' existing identities, knowledge and current concerns. The encounter follows the player's own exploration, invention or making: they have a real object or experience to discuss, and can decide whether the company is interesting. The one-to-one improvement and this small-group encounter remain independently deliverable. The selected compact background family has its own runtime work; the busy-camp variant remains conditional.

The first proposed subject is a useful object the human actually made and can show through normal perception. A carrying container can hold and organize real supplies; a genuinely invented sling can be discussed after a real attempt to use it. The offer does not grant an invented object merely to ensure that every run has the same prop. If the player has no made object, they can ask about something another resident actually has, share a real observation or simply talk; ordinary conversation is never locked behind a crafting milestone.

A possible exchange concerns whether the object solves the maker's problem. One resident might care about a practical limitation while the other enjoys an unusual design or doubts the maker's account. These are opportunities compatible with actual identities, not assigned lines or compulsory personality tests. Ada need not always be helpful. The Mercenary need not turn every conversation toward combat. Humor, curiosity, aesthetic preference, uncertainty, pride, embarrassment and quiet company are all legitimate.

The human can explain, ask, demonstrate a supported use, offer the object, retain it, propose another invention or leave. The player can also listen without speaking. Nobody must receive a reward, friendship increase or new chore for attending. Existing possessions and activity results carry the consequences.

The intended interaction uses ordinary ambient speech or Speak to with one selected addressee; it requires no separate Listen to mode or social enrollment. The current WorldComposer, captions and Events provide composition and permitted multi-person reading. Extend that reading only if an actual additional person's contributions expose a demonstrated gap; do not restore a separate Talk transcript or make a new group view a prerequisite. The next addressed person stays explicit. Other people hear through the hearing rules and may act on that evidence independently.

A participant can become hungry, tired or occupied during a long conversation. Do not freeze needs, reset their body or force them to ignore another concern for the demonstration. Prepare a suitable ordinary play situation and permit normal eating/rest choices. Include actual generation delays, human reading and composition under the offered running clock; the [current food/rest arithmetic](continuing-communities.md#food-and-rest-viability) explains the short real-time margins. A person needing to leave can do so, with other useful play still available to the human. A sequence that mainly makes humans race speech against preventable starvation is failed pacing evidence. Review the authored starting conditions, bodily/work pacing and avoidable interaction burden through their owners before expanding the offered gathering; a new conversation clock, compulsory wakeup or hidden meal is not selected here.

Ordinary speech has no conversation membership, inactivity expiry or conversation identity that determines this social occasion's duration. Actual presence, movement, hearing and independent choices determine exposure and participation. Ending an exchange or changing a view must not erase the human's permitted history or drafts; later speech uses its own source record and explicit optional addressee. Independently owned outings, cooperative work and finite vocal activities retain their actual terms and lifetimes.

## Physical demonstrations and custody

Show an object through actual permitted presence, inspection or an existing action. A visible model proves its appearance and presence, not every hidden property. A person's description of how it performed is testimony unless the listener witnessed the use or has another supported source.

The implemented [woven-container family](camp-containers.md#purpose-and-supported-effect) supports organizing and sharing actual supplies; combined voluntary use remains subject to its recorded qualification. It does not increase carrying strength, preserve freshness or create waterproof storage. The gathering can reveal a desired improvement without pretending it already exists. The human can submit that idea through the ordinary invention owner and continue other play while it is considered.

Dropping an object creates its normal grounded custody/access situation; it is not a protected display case. For direct visual inspection, the supported baseline is a visible ground pile and its permitted contents. Merely carrying or equipping an item does not grant every bystander an inspectable view of it or its hidden properties. If retaining custody matters, the human can discuss their experience as testimony, or deliberately use the native directed offer with its actual recipient disclosure and acceptance behavior. That is a real offer, not a harmless display action that forbids acceptance. An offer moves and reserves nothing, and only the named recipient's actual acceptance moves custody after current checks. Refusal, withdrawal and expiry leave the object where the actual owner rules put it. An accepted handover does not invent a formal loan or guaranteed return.

The social consequence can be real even without a new numerical reward. A resident may later recall the maker's care, the failed attempt or a disagreement, through their permitted experience and current interpretation. The design does not assign an automatic positive relationship change or compel a future compliment.

## Gist and vocal activity selection

The selected first compact family is a two-NPC informal exchange about a known object or current activity, including personal meaning, humor, doubt or disagreement. Either existing resident may initiate when it fits their actual concerns. They directly communicate compact expressive English rather than generating full speech and summarizing it; optional structured references and a short explicit exact phrase preserve relevant bindings or wording. The other resident independently interprets the contribution, may change their own supported feeling, may answer and may remain silent. No small fixed intent menu or shared narrator supplies both sides. This replaces the earlier unselected-gist gate, preserving the need to compare complete cost and expressive quality with fewer exact exchanges. It is not an automatic camp routine and is not required to finish the exact attended gathering.

For this new family, **clear eligible NPC hearing receives the committed meaning and any explicit exact phrase; partial or merely detected sound receives only an indistinct-activity cue**. This is a conservative selected design rule because no complete exact word sequence exists to divide safely. It leaves current exact partial speech unchanged. Indistinct content does not suppress SPK's directed-address facts or reliable ordinary speaker association when the observer qualifies for them. Names and a bystander's knowledge of the recipient still follow SPK's permitted-evidence rules. A clearly hearing NPC bystander may learn the meaning without enrollment or a new model call. An unheard contribution teaches nothing.

**An eligible human-controlled character who would clearly hear a future contribution requires exact speech for that contribution.** Select source form during preparation and recheck the actual audience before commitment. If a compact contribution is still unsent when the human arrives, it becomes stale/needs-exact; preserve its opportunity for normal fresh admission without automatically generating words or retrying paid work. UI focus, camera movement, pane opening and private administrative inspection do not supply character observation. Completed meaning never becomes a recovered transcript. When the human later leaves, another freshly admitted background contribution may be compact; existing exact words remain exact.

Meaning communicates only what the speaker expresses. Warm thanks can be sincere, strategic or misunderstood; another person is not given hidden sincerity or motives. Claims about an object's performance remain testimony. The recipient's optional numerical change without required prose uses the [selected psychological profile](psychological-reactions.md#selected-first-profile) through the [shared personal-reaction owner](../../projects/continuing-lives-tech-design.md#55-numerical-personal-reactions-without-required-prose). This page defines no automatic gratitude, happiness, trust or friendship points and no new social-need meter. A recipient can make no change or refuse without explaining.

Compact speech may accompany an independently chosen current native outing proposal, item offer or acceptance using its exact supported terms. Native admission, real consent and receipts determine those effects; arbitrary agreement prose creates no enforceable promise, trade or recipe knowledge. Mechanics requiring exact language use their actual supported wording before effect. Retelling preserves source attribution, conditions, uncertainty and the absence of other exact words. Changing speech representation never changes bodies, elapsed time, action rates, custody or physical consequences.

The later nonverbatim vocal activity is a separately selected and admitted chosen continuation, justified only when actual competing activity improves a desired gathering. Gist communication does not by itself require this new ongoing sound, and a useful exact gathering needs neither. Each qualifying pair has **at most one currently vocal member** at an instant; another member can listen, respond later or leave. This is the selected first turn-taking activity for that conditional variant, not a universal ban on overlapping speech. The other participant's willingness to remain involved is independently established, never inferred from a shared label.

Each such chosen continuation lasts **at most 30 game minutes**, which is **30 real seconds at the current normal 1× rate** if uninterrupted. This proposed ceiling bounds one period of genuinely ongoing conversational sound without making it an endless ambient emitter. It is unmeasured authoring for qualification, not a derived human conversation duration. It stops earlier if its actual conditions end. A later continuation requires a real existing chosen finite plan or a new admitted choice and complete-work allowance; expiration never schedules an automatic paid restart. Ending this activity creates no new semantic contribution or mandatory cognition opportunity. A further gist occurs only when a resident actually chooses and successfully communicates another contribution. The pair does not alternate speakers on a timer; silence remains possible.

Use the vocal member's installed ordinary speech source profile and actual position. The hearing owner combines real competing source energy and excludes the evaluated foreground speaker. Do not add sound from captions, old dialogue, pending provider work, a silent listener or the same speaker twice. A delayed decision cannot keep a supposedly lively discussion sounding forever. A gist contribution only creates listener knowledge when that contribution actually commits; detecting activity in the meantime does not reveal its topic.

The first selected focus policy stabilizes presentation and current-interest priority without an acoustic clarity bonus. Choosing observational focus does not cancel this activity. Actual interaction may change what participants choose to do, and subsequent exact speech uses its proper source boundary. The selected vocal continuation has no effect on sleep, injury or objects beyond separately supported rules; loud-sound waking and damage remain unsupported here.

## Later busy-camp workload

After the smaller encounter is worthwhile, consider this larger qualification only if the additional people supply encounters or atmosphere players value: an attended camp with **seven actual bodies**, the human, Ada, the Peacock Mercenary and four additional independently authored residents. It is not the default next milestone or a minimum release population. Its baseline arrangement has the human with the current residents and two additional NPC pairs with their own current matters. Ordinary exact exchanges remain a valid simpler variant; the gist and vocal families must separately qualify before this profile uses them. The additional residents need genuine individual identities, permitted histories and independent choices; duplicated audience fillers do not establish this workload.

One pair might compare a personally made container with a known alternative. Another might discuss something they actually encountered near the lookout, with room for a disagreement or an aesthetic interest. These are candidate matters for authored people, not programmed repeated exchanges. An empty subject does not justify generating gossip. The shared location permits the human to notice another useful possibility and approach it, while their original conversation remains understandable.

Admit **at most two** of the selected background vocal activities simultaneously in this qualification profile. Place the groups at actual reachable positions where the intended foreground interaction can be understood under the installed acoustic rules. Do not claim every layout works. A deliberately adverse arrangement where another group moves close is useful qualification of lost clarity, movement and choice; it is not the default that forces constant shouting.

The initial arrangement does not cap the foreground at three people. The workload must include people interjecting across initially separate exchanges, direct engagement with a background speaker and ordinary exact speech involving the available people. Changing the selected addressee requires no group merge or participant transfer. All eligible listeners retain their permitted exact evidence. If those transitions cannot be supported, the seven-body offer is not qualified merely because its quiet initial frame looks good.

The larger group is outside DG17's two-resident unattended supply and funding envelope. It cannot inherit that profile's horizon, food allowance or whole-community hosting promise. First qualify its actual attended cost and play; any later unattended version needs a fresh viable community and authorized service selection.

## What to observe before expansion

Players should be able to explain why they approached, what someone else wanted, who the next message addresses, what they know and what they can choose next. A quiet or refused exchange can pass if it expresses a believable person and leaves the human free to enjoy their own game.

The exact gathering and selected compact background family have separate completion. Compare compact meaning's complete source, independent reaction/reply, memory and recovery cost and expressive quality with the ordinary exact route; do not claim a saving from word count alone. The busy variant still needs a wanted encounter and its own selection/qualification. No deliberately poor focus baseline is required to finish the smaller useful delivery. Keep complete costs, repeated admission attempts, actual histories and failures. Examine experienced play as well as a novelty visit: no optimal reward loop should demand repetitive attendance, constant object passing or pressure on slower participants. Increasing population is justified only when the added people provide worthwhile encounters at a supported cost.
