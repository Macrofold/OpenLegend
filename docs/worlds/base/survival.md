# Body, senses and survival

## Bodily conditions and chosen responses

Fullness below 40/30/20/10 is described as hungry/very hungry/famished/starving. At zero, starvation damages health. Health describes bodily integrity and the world's actual death/incapacity consequences; NPC death ends living activity with no assured return. The existing ghost/difficult-revival policy is unchanged, and this slice does not implement that future lifecycle work.

The engine derives labels from installed numeric definitions and records private initial, worsening and critical-review opportunities. Saved episode state prevents duplicate notifications; sleep or unavailable cognition capability retains pending state without manufacturing conscious awareness. Recovery uses the authored margin, while the displayed condition always follows the current value. [BW07](../../limits/base-world.md#bw07) owns tuning, and [Architecture](../../architecture.md#embodied-conditions-and-inventory) owns integration.

Automatic person-controller berry seeking/eating is removed. Metabolism, starvation, sleep and already chosen native actions continue without providers. A person can remain unfed even while carrying food when cognition is unavailable or declines to act. Live Jev has chosen eating, gathering, an unprompted knife hunt and retries after actual misses. [AG13](../../maintainers/agent-agency.md#ag13--embodied-survival-demonstration) distinguishes that demonstrated behavior from remaining qualification.

`bodies.ts` owns initial anatomy, health maxima, harvest yields and native animal capabilities; `spawn.ts` owns the God creation templates. Generic lifecycle and admission remain outside the world directory.

The base world uses bounded health, fullness and energy attributes, initial values and concern descriptions from `packages/domain/src/worlds/base/attributes.ts`. Sight in `senses.ts` defaults to 28 world units. Ordinary speech is clear to roughly 10 metres in the authored unobstructed reference conditions; [hearing](../../hearing-and-speech.md) applies continuous source levels, attenuation and receiver thresholds. Their detectors apply geometry and receiver scope; naming a sense never grants an actor all world knowledge.

`needs.ts` owns the base physiology: fullness drains 0.003 percentage points per simulation second; zero fullness costs 0.009 health points per second and zero energy costs 0.003. These rates and the presence of these needs are not universal engine laws. [Sleep and waking](sleep.md) owns authored recovery and expenditure of energy. Condition thresholds describe bodily state and invite consideration; they never select a meal, target or weapon.

## Embodied survival and authored start

Initial item definitions and the known fiber/cord preparations live in `items.ts`. World creation, basic possessions, people, resource sources and finite initial supplies live in `world.ts`; the starting clearing, lookout and bird routes live in `spatial.ts`. New starts give Mike, Ada and the Peacock Mercenary one knife each, remove their carried berries and deplete the two nearby berry patches. Ada starts healthy, with energy 85 and fullness 35, an authored biography/practical knowledge in `characters.ts`, and no operational goal. The Peacock Mercenary is a separate ordinary NPC at the camp with the bundled animated-model appearance, fullness 76, no initial operational goal and an authored travelling-mercenary identity. Ada and Mike keep their existing roles. Appearance grants no mechanics or extra observation; existing worlds are never reseeded. These authored definitions are the single code source for the bundled reality. The generic simulation still hosts finite adapters for gathering, preparation, cooking, hunting and body needs; directory separation does not claim full runtime replacement of these adapters.

The biography retains general practical knowledge, self-preservation and dislike of needless cruelty, without the earlier explicit hunting acceptance or food-preparation paragraph. `hunting.ts:BASE_HUNTING` owns the bundled world's hunt label, food purpose, recognized prey species (hare/deer/bird) and allowed melee, launcher and unarmed methods. It generates concise descriptions from the target and compatible carried tool, for example "Hunt a deer with Knife for meat. One attack." No actor or weapon name selects policy. Offers read observed species, never hidden individual health or yield. Another world's food ecology or learned species knowledge would replace this localized adapter; species being huntable is not a universal engine law.

Each compatible carried weapon produces a separately bound offer per observed target. Selecting an unequipped melee weapon or launcher through Jev queues its exact equip step and attack together; the offer ends with `(auto-equip)`, and no separate equipment decision or model call is needed. This combined offer requires a single-unit lot so equipment splitting cannot invalidate the bound identity. A stacked tool can first be equipped through the separate equip choice. Ranged offers also bind compatible ammunition and reuse native shot admission; unarmed offers use existing punch mechanics without an equip step. Hunt labels describe one finite attack, never an automatic chase/retry or harvest/cook chain. Unsupported hunting methods retain ordinary attack descriptions where the native capability exists.

The approved weapon-comparison follow-up supplies each attack's tool description and a compact definition-derived injury/time/reach/base-hit-chance summary. Time includes wind-up/recovery but excludes approach; actual range, target motion and damage susceptibility remain native decisions. Visible animals also expose current/maximum health and the meaning of zero through `hunting.ts:observedAnimalHealth`, shared by surroundings and target descriptions. This is bundled-world permission to judge visible animal condition, not universal access to another actor's private body: unseen animals and people's private health/needs are excluded. These facts are implemented; [live comparisons and complete meals are recorded](../../verification/embodied-survival.md#complete-jev-only-meals).

`actions.ts` owns movement/interaction defaults, gather yield and initial harvest/cook/shot durations consumed by those adapters. Simulation step size and bounded advancement remain engine execution concerns. World-configurable replacement of the remaining finite adapters follows their existing extension owners.

## Routine visual discovery

Current discovery follows the engine's [elapsed-time sensing and displacement boundaries](../../simulation-time.md), with unchanged-exposure reuse only when its dependencies remain valid. The older hearing branch's four-second descriptor setting is superseded and removed. Current inspection, targeting and every emitted event use current geometry. Speech reception, fragments and identity are instantaneous event-time decisions, independent of routine discovery or browser frame cadence. ## Choosing a complete meal

Harvest offers name the finite yields and quantities from the observed remains and their item definitions, rather than only saying “harvest.” This exposes the supported consequence without prescribing a meal or identifying raw meat as edible. Cooking remains offered only with carried raw meat and a perceived, reachable lit fire with sufficient expected fuel; eating consumes a supported nutritional item. Each newly completed plan supplies outcome evidence, and Jev can select the next action from current state. The code contains no automatic hunt → harvest → cook → eat sequence.

[Complete-meal evidence](../../verification/embodied-survival.md#complete-jev-only-meals) covers hare/deer, knife/spear/bow choices, misses, cooking and eating, plus food and missing-fire alternatives. Empty initial goals, actual native costs and the 0.7 decision threshold remain. This demonstrates the starting camp's chain, not reliable long-term survival or a learned activity library. Fire care is described below; ongoing tending and richer activity composition remain in the [action tracker](../../maintainers/action-capabilities.md#starting-scene-action-priorities).

## Tending the campfire

A campfire's saved heat state, lit or cold plus its remaining fuel seconds, is the only record of its fuel. `fire.ts:BASE_FIRE_CARE` owns this world's fire-care rules; the kernel admits, times and commits them like other manual work. A person can:

- **Add fuel.** One carried unit of a plain portable material with the `fuel` property (currently the Supple branch) is used up when the 20-second work finishes, adding **one game hour** of burning. Laying fuel works on a cold fire too. A fire holds at most **48 hours** of fuel, the banked camp fire's starting amount; a fuller fire refuses more. Eligibility comes from the item definition's property, never from an item name. Tools, weapons, containers and food are never burned.
- **Light a cold fire.** It must already hold fuel. Lighting takes **150 game seconds** and needs one carried tinder bundle (a plain fiber material such as reed or prepared fibers), which is used up, and a carried rigid shaft used as a fire drill, which is kept. Lighting always succeeds in this version. There is no flame transfer from another fire and no flint item yet.
- **Put the fire out.** This takes 30 game seconds. Unburnt fuel stays for relighting. It is refused while someone else is already cooking on that fire, because their meat was spent when cooking began; a cook still walking over has spent nothing and does not block it.

Materials are used and the fire changes together when the work finishes, so cancelling costs nothing and fuel is never gained without spending a unit. Each step rechecks the fire, reach and carried materials at admission, work start and completion. A lit fire burns down on its own whether or not anyone tends it and goes out when its fuel ends. Burn time counts only while it was lit: lighting or putting it out at the end of a simulation slice neither gains nor loses burn for that slice. Heat's only mechanical effect in this world is cooking; there is no warmth, spreading or burning of nearby objects.

People see whether a fire is burning and roughly how much fuel it holds (“about 3 hours of fuel”), never exact seconds. Lighting, fuelling and putting out a fire are visible events. Lighting and putting out are notable enough to invite nearby characters to reconsider. The player uses the selected fire's menu or quick actions or typed text. Characters choose the same options, and persistent plans and learned activities can include them, for example gathering branches and then fuelling the fire. Values and their rationale are in [BW10](../../limits/base-world.md#bw10); delivery status is in [tracker BW19](../../maintainers/base-world.md#bw19--camp-fire-care).
