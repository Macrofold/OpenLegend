# Body, senses and survival

## Bodily conditions and chosen responses

Fullness below 40/30/20/10 is described as hungry/very hungry/famished/starving. At zero, starvation damages health. Health describes bodily integrity and the world's actual death/incapacity consequences; NPC death ends living activity with no assured return. The existing ghost/difficult-revival policy is unchanged, and this slice does not implement that future lifecycle work.

The engine derives labels from installed numeric definitions and records private initial, worsening and critical-review opportunities. Saved episode state prevents duplicate notifications; sleep or unavailable cognition capability retains pending state without manufacturing conscious awareness. Recovery uses the authored margin, while the displayed condition always follows the current value. [BW07](../../limits/base-world.md#bw07) owns tuning, and [Architecture](../../architecture.md#embodied-conditions-and-inventory) owns integration.

Automatic person-controller berry seeking/eating is removed. Metabolism, starvation, sleep and already chosen native actions continue without providers. A person can remain unfed even while carrying food when cognition is unavailable or declines to act. Live Jev has chosen eating, gathering, an unprompted knife hunt and retries after actual misses. [AG13](../../maintainers/agent-agency.md#ag13--embodied-survival-demonstration) distinguishes that demonstrated behavior from remaining qualification.

`bodies.ts` owns initial anatomy, health maxima, harvest yields and native animal capabilities; `spawn.ts` owns the God creation templates. Generic lifecycle and admission remain outside the world directory.

The base world uses bounded health, fullness and energy attributes, initial values and concern descriptions from `packages/domain/src/worlds/base/attributes.ts`. Sight in `senses.ts` defaults to 28 world units. Ordinary speech is clear to roughly 10 metres in the authored unobstructed reference conditions; [hearing](../../hearing-and-speech.md) applies continuous source levels, attenuation and receiver thresholds. Their detectors apply geometry and receiver scope; naming a sense never grants an actor all world knowledge.

`config/status-effects.yaml` owns metabolism and quiet body damage: food drains 0.003 percentage points per game second; at zero food starvation costs 0.009 health percentage points per second; at zero energy while awake exhaustion costs 0.003. These three rules require an applicable food meter, so native animals retain energy but take neither kind of need damage. `needs.ts` exposes a derived summary of those authored defaults; it is not a second writable policy. Current saved rules are authoritative. Simultaneous quiet health rates reconcile the body once per materialized interval and never create injury narration or wake a sleeper. [Sleep and waking](sleep.md) owns authored recovery and expenditure of energy. Condition thresholds describe bodily state and invite consideration; they never select a meal, target or weapon.

**Integrated character target:** [Compelling characters](../../projects/compelling-characters-feature-spec.md#needs-experience-and-choice) distinguishes the native bodily condition, experienced hunger, personal interpretation and chosen response. Its complete flows require later behavior to reflect actual feeding and other concerns, including company, belonging and enjoyment. They preserve the current meters, admitted actions and unavailable-cognition behavior above; no automatic feeding or psychological depletion rule is introduced by the documentation.

## Installed meters and body services

People are created with food and energy values; native animals receive only energy. These are sparse passive numeric meters, with one authoritative value each. Installing a definition alone does not attach its value to a body. Missing optional meters remain absent, including on creator spawn; reads never replace absence with zero or the bundled defaults. Health remains raw bodily points: 100 for a person, 36 for a deer or wolf, 72 for a bear and 18 for a hare or bird. Its meter projects those points to the installed range; edits, configured rates and floors convert back to raw points. The creator revival action restores raw maximum health and only the configured applicable meters. An ordinary health edit leaves a dead body dead.

`body-policy.ts` owns this world's zero-health outcomes, recovery, consumption, food-carrying concern and separate background-thinking gates. All installed base-world controllers die at zero health. [Player Death](player-death.md) supplies a separate corpse, exact half-type retention, new-life Continue near a campfire and removable scars; the former collapse/65%-health camp service is no longer installed here. Native/NPC creator revival remains a separate action, and pending player/old successor corpse revival is refused. Eating uses one accessible nutritional item, adds its nutrition to this world's food meter within the configured range, and refuses raw meat using the cooking family's authored input reference.

Health and energy concerns are instant descriptions without additional notification events. Food retains its saved condition episodes and review opportunities. The compact bars are critical when the rounded displayed value is at most 20; the creator editor has separate strict thresholds below 40 health, 30 food and 25 energy. These distinctions are authored metadata, not universal display rules. Icon and color names refer to trusted client symbols.

The clockwork demonstration installs only integrity, charge (0–240) and disposition across the entire world. It has no food, energy, hunger/rest rules, consumption, food-carrying concern, camp recovery or dreaming. Its charge still drains, and real replenishment transfers from a finite compatible supply. This is new-world composition, not conversion of an existing save. Both compositions and the exact comparison limits are recorded in [current verification](../../verification/world-configured-survival.md).

## Injury feedback

Currently visible bodies show a health bar while injured and briefly after a permitted health change, including healing. The player sees changes to every known installed stat overhead; numeric changes aggregate into four-second notices and categorical changes name the new value. Returning to sight or restoring a timeline establishes a fresh baseline rather than inventing a gain/loss. Private needs remain owner-only. Inventory’s item context menu includes the existing Eat action, with its normal admission and one-item cost.

Withdrawing a meter's disclosure removes its retained feedback. A changed meter definition starts a fresh baseline; opposite numeric changes that cancel within the same notice window remove that notice.

## Death and remains

Every actual death leaves a saved body, including people and native animals. Player death separates the old body from the retained identity and possessions under [Player Death](player-death.md). Bodies stop living work and sensing, lie fallen, and start rotting **three game days after death**. Rotting has a muted green appearance and removes fresh harvest yields. At **seven game days after death**, the remains disappear. Carried items, including nested containers, move intact to a ground pile with declared ownership retained; unsupported placement delays cleanup until possessions can be released safely. Observers receive ordinary scoped rotting/removal events. Unobserved changes do not reveal hidden facts.

Cleanup removes physical presence, preserving the actor’s identity, memories and relationships. Native/NPC creator revival restores that identity in a new physical life at its retained supported location without recovering already dropped items or duplicating harvested materials; a pending player uses Continue instead. Deadlines use saved simulation time and pause with the world. Other worlds may omit decay through their installed body policy. Physical remains and one-attempt human lethal review are implemented; ghost experience, difficult ordinary NPC revival and broader PvP/indirect-harm protection remain [BW14/BW15](../../maintainers/base-world.md).

## Animal escape

An attack gives a native animal a remembered threat direction. It tracks the attacker’s position only while it sees that attacker, otherwise using the last perceived position. Shared authored motives score reachable directions by separation, open travel and continuity. Sixteen directions probe up to four metres, with four clearance samples each; blocked directions are excluded. This replaces a fixed 110-second escape and first-walkable straight/sideways pattern. No species script or model call chooses the route. Fear eases faster when farther from the threat; seeing a close threat renews it, so escape ends with safety rather than an unconditional timer.

Escape and ordinary wandering follow joined terrain patches, including slopes, through the shared straight-walk geometry checks. Internal patch edges are not fences. Gaps, unsupported drops and body collisions still refuse movement; prediction follows those same supported segments.

This is bounded local steering, not a complete predator ecology or long-range route planner. A completely trapped animal can remain in place; future scent, refuge selection and social/predator motives need their own supported evidence and actions. The saved direction is also used to predict sight/hearing crossings. [Limits](../../limits/base-world.md#bw12--remains-and-native-escape) and [evidence](../../verification/embodied-feedback.md) own tuning and measured scope.

## Embodied survival and authored start

Initial item definitions and the known fiber/cord preparations live in `items.ts`. World creation, basic possessions, people, resource sources and finite initial supplies live in `world.ts`; the starting clearing, lookout and bird routes live in `spatial.ts`. New starts give Mike, Ada and the Peacock Mercenary one knife each, remove their carried berries and deplete the two nearby berry patches. Ada starts healthy, with energy 85 and fullness 35, an authored biography/practical knowledge in `characters.ts`, and no operational goal. The Peacock Mercenary is a separate ordinary NPC at the camp with the bundled animated-model appearance, fullness 76, no initial operational goal and an authored travelling-mercenary identity. Ada and Mike keep their existing roles. Appearance grants no mechanics or extra observation; existing worlds are never reseeded. These authored definitions are the single code source for the bundled reality. The generic simulation still hosts finite adapters for gathering, preparation, cooking, hunting and configured body services; directory separation does not claim full runtime replacement of these adapters.

The biography retains general practical knowledge, self-preservation and dislike of needless cruelty, without the earlier explicit hunting acceptance or food-preparation paragraph. `hunting.ts:BASE_HUNTING` owns the bundled world's hunt label, food purpose, recognized prey species (hare/deer/bird) and allowed melee, launcher and unarmed methods. It generates concise descriptions from the target and compatible carried tool, for example "Hunt a deer with Knife for meat. One attack." No actor or weapon name selects policy. Offers read observed species, never hidden individual health or yield. Another world's food ecology or learned species knowledge would replace this localized adapter; species being huntable is not a universal engine law.

Each compatible carried weapon produces a separately bound offer per observed target. Selecting an unequipped melee weapon or launcher through Jev queues its exact equip step and attack together; the offer ends with `(auto-equip)`, and no separate equipment decision or model call is needed. This combined offer requires a single-unit lot so equipment splitting cannot invalidate the bound identity. A stacked tool can first be equipped through the separate equip choice. Ranged offers also bind compatible ammunition and reuse native shot admission; unarmed offers use existing punch mechanics without an equip step. Hunt labels describe one finite attack, never an automatic chase/retry or harvest/cook chain. Unsupported hunting methods retain ordinary attack descriptions where the native capability exists.

The approved weapon-comparison follow-up supplies each attack's tool description and a compact definition-derived injury/time/reach/base-hit-chance summary. Time includes wind-up/recovery but excludes approach; actual range, target motion and damage susceptibility remain native decisions. Visible animals also expose current/maximum health and the meaning of zero through `hunting.ts:observedAnimalHealth`, shared by surroundings and target descriptions. This is bundled-world permission to judge visible animal condition, not universal access to another actor's private body: unseen bodies and private food/energy values are excluded. This update makes visible bodily health public for every actor, replacing owner-only person health, so physical injury feedback uses one permitted meter contract. These facts are implemented; [live comparisons and complete meals are recorded](../../verification/embodied-survival.md#complete-jev-only-meals).

`actions.ts` owns movement/interaction defaults, gather yield and initial harvest/cook/shot durations consumed by those adapters. Simulation step size and bounded advancement remain engine execution concerns. World-configurable replacement of the remaining finite adapters follows their existing extension owners.

## Choosing a complete meal

Harvest offers name the finite yields and quantities from the observed remains and their item definitions, rather than only saying “harvest.” This exposes the supported consequence without prescribing a meal or identifying raw meat as edible. Cooking remains offered only with carried raw meat and a perceived, reachable lit fire with sufficient expected fuel; eating consumes a supported nutritional item. Each newly completed plan supplies outcome evidence, and Jev can select the next action from current state. The code contains no automatic hunt → harvest → cook → eat sequence.

[Complete-meal evidence](../../verification/embodied-survival.md#complete-jev-only-meals) covers hare/deer, knife/spear/bow choices, misses, cooking and eating, plus food and missing-fire alternatives. Empty initial goals, actual native costs and the 0.7 decision threshold remain. This demonstrates the starting camp's chain, not reliable long-term survival or a learned activity library. Fire care is described below; ongoing tending and richer activity composition remain in the [action tracker](../../maintainers/action-capabilities.md#starting-scene-action-priorities).

Ada’s biography now describes enjoying animal observation without a provisioning condition. Her current camp/possessions and unfamiliarity with the present location are no longer permanently asserted in biography; generic reasoning/narration instructions are also removed. Actual world state and permitted knowledge supply those facts. This implements the owner’s [authorship boundary](../../projects/compelling-characters-feature-spec.md#character-authorship-and-changing-personality), retaining her remaining history, traits, empty starter goals, supplies and bodily policy. The earlier successful solitary/stopping trial used conditional wording that was removed; [historical evidence and correction](../../verification/cognition-context.md#october-4-authorship-correction) do not qualify the revised content. Existing saved identities are not automatically rewritten. No enjoyment meter, automatic fulfillment, timetable or new native behavior was added.

## Tending the campfire

A campfire's saved heat state, lit or cold plus its remaining fuel seconds, is the only record of its fuel. `fire.ts:BASE_FIRE_CARE` owns this world's fire-care rules; the kernel admits, times and commits them like other manual work. A person can:

- **Add fuel.** One carried unit of a plain portable material with the `fuel` property (currently the Supple branch) is used up when the 20-second work finishes, adding **one game hour** of burning. Laying fuel works on a cold fire too. A fire holds at most **48 hours** of fuel, the banked camp fire's starting amount; a fuller fire refuses more. Eligibility comes from the item definition's property, never from an item name. Tools, weapons, containers and food are never burned.
- **Light a cold fire.** It must already hold fuel. Lighting takes **150 game seconds** and needs one carried tinder bundle (a plain fiber material such as reed or prepared fibers), which is used up, and a carried rigid shaft used as a fire drill, which is kept. Lighting always succeeds in this version. There is no flame transfer from another fire and no flint item yet.
- **Put the fire out.** This takes 30 game seconds. Unburnt fuel stays for relighting. It is refused while someone else is already cooking on that fire, because their meat was spent when cooking began; a cook still walking over has spent nothing and does not block it.

Materials are used and the fire changes together when the work finishes, so cancelling costs nothing and fuel is never gained without spending a unit. Each step rechecks the fire, reach and carried materials at admission, work start and completion. A lit fire burns down on its own whether or not anyone tends it and goes out when its fuel ends. Burn time counts only while it was lit: lighting or putting it out at the end of a simulation slice neither gains nor loses burn for that slice. Heat's only mechanical effect in this world is cooking; there is no warmth, spreading or burning of nearby objects.

People see whether a fire is burning and roughly how much fuel it holds (“about 3 hours of fuel”), never exact seconds. Lighting, fuelling and putting out a fire are visible events. Lighting and putting out are notable enough to invite nearby characters to reconsider. The player uses the selected fire's menu or quick actions or typed text. Characters choose the same options, and persistent plans and learned activities can include them, for example gathering branches and then fuelling the fire. Values and their rationale are in [BW10](../../limits/base-world.md#bw10); delivery status is in [tracker BW19](../../maintainers/base-world.md#bw19--camp-fire-care).

For another installed world using the supported floor-recovery service, recovery requires every configured floor meter to have a current value and any necessary write to remain admissible. A missing meter or exhausted required revision refuses the entire recovery, preserving position, health and life status; it never initializes a meter implicitly. A meter already above its floor needs no write. The shared body owner likewise refuses revision exhaustion rather than publishing an invalid current-format body.
