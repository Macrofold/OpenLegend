# Body, senses and survival

## Bodily conditions and chosen responses

Fullness below 40/30/20/10 is described as hungry/very hungry/famished/starving. At zero, starvation damages health. Health describes bodily integrity and the world's actual death/incapacity consequences; NPC death ends living activity with no assured return. The existing ghost/difficult-revival policy is unchanged, and this slice does not implement that future lifecycle work.

The engine derives labels from installed numeric definitions and records private initial, worsening and critical-review opportunities. Saved episode state prevents duplicate notifications; sleep or unavailable cognition capability retains pending state without manufacturing conscious awareness. Recovery uses the authored margin, while the displayed condition always follows the current value. [BW07](../../limits/base-world.md#bw07) owns tuning, and [Architecture](../../architecture.md#embodied-conditions-and-inventory) owns integration.

Automatic person-controller berry seeking/eating is removed. Metabolism, starvation, sleep and already chosen native actions continue without providers. A person can remain unfed even while carrying food when cognition is unavailable or declines to act. Live Jev has chosen eating and gathering, but the unprompted hunting demonstration remains incomplete under [AG13](../../maintainers/agent-agency.md#ag13--embodied-survival-demonstration).

`bodies.ts` owns initial anatomy, health maxima, harvest yields and native animal capabilities; `spawn.ts` owns the God creation templates. Generic lifecycle and admission remain outside the world directory.

The base world uses bounded health, fullness and energy attributes, initial values and concern descriptions from `packages/domain/src/worlds/base/attributes.ts`. Sight in `senses.ts` defaults to 28 world units. Ordinary speech is clear to roughly 10 metres in the authored unobstructed reference conditions; [hearing](../../hearing-and-speech.md) applies continuous source levels, attenuation and receiver thresholds. Their detectors apply geometry and receiver scope; naming a sense never grants an actor all world knowledge.

`needs.ts` owns the base physiology: fullness drains 0.003 percentage points per simulation second; zero fullness costs 0.009 health points per second and zero energy costs 0.003. These rates and the presence of these needs are not universal engine laws. [Sleep and waking](sleep.md) owns authored recovery and expenditure of energy. Condition thresholds describe bodily state and invite consideration; they never select a meal, target or weapon.

Initial item definitions and the known fiber/cord preparations live in `items.ts`. World creation, basic possessions, people, resource sources and finite initial supplies live in `world.ts`; the starting clearing, lookout and bird routes live in `spatial.ts`. New starts give only Mike and Ada one knife each, remove their carried berries and deplete the two nearby berry patches. Ada starts healthy, with energy 85 and fullness 35, an authored biography/practical knowledge in `characters.ts`, and no operational goal. Other actor defaults are unchanged; existing worlds are never reseeded. These authored definitions are the single code source for the bundled reality. The generic simulation still hosts finite adapters for gathering, preparation, cooking, hunting and body needs; directory separation does not claim full runtime replacement of these adapters.

`actions.ts` owns movement/interaction defaults, gather yield and initial harvest/cook/shot durations consumed by those adapters. Simulation step size and bounded advancement remain engine execution concerns. World-configurable replacement of the remaining finite adapters follows their existing extension owners.

## Routine visual discovery

Current discovery follows the engine's [elapsed-time sensing and displacement boundaries](../../simulation-time.md), with unchanged-exposure reuse only when its dependencies remain valid. The older hearing branch's four-second descriptor setting is superseded and removed. Current inspection, targeting and every emitted event use current geometry. Speech reception, fragments and identity are instantaneous event-time decisions, independent of routine discovery or browser frame cadence. Physical contact retains main's body-overlap and crossing/fidelity contract; no mandatory one-second inner loop is restored.
