# Proposed first wilderness threat

**Product proposal, 2026-10-04; unimplemented and subject to D07/PS-D01 adoption.** [The feature spec](../../projects/first-threat-encounter-feature-spec.md) owns the player journey, rationale, research and qualification. This file owns the selected bundled-world encounter rules. Current [combat](combat.md), [body/recovery tuning](../../limits/base-world.md#bw07) and [absence](lifecycle-and-protection.md) remain unchanged until delivery.

## The offered site

One territorial boar occupies a small woodland hollow containing one real equipment cache with one usable longer-reach spear. An observable trail approach, a bypass and a nearby recovery area make entering, avoiding and leaving understandable. The finite reward does not regenerate for another account, death, retreat or reconnect. The spear's use is supported by an installed weapon profile; a name or picture does not supply its mechanics.

The initial offered trial is explicitly selected at the existing 1× mechanical speed with ordinary pause. [TE01](../../limits/base-world.md#te01--proposed-first-threat-offering) owns participant, site and effect-family limits. A host cannot covertly change the risk or speed of an ongoing encounter. Before an incompatible change, stop new entry and reconcile current participants through the disclosed ending; creator authority is not an endless participant lock on world settings.

## Participation and boundaries

Entering the marked hollow for this visit requires accepting its wildlife-risk and recovery terms. The player may inspect, remain on the trail or choose another worthwhile activity. Crossing by another movement or offered action cannot bypass this participation boundary. The first site admits only the movement/effect families whose boundary behavior has been qualified; no invisible marker alone authorizes harm.

The review explains recoverable injury, preserved possessions, nearby retreat, a continuing shared world and PvP off. Participation does not authorize another human's attacks, control, inventory access or claims on future help. A rescuer accepts the relevant wildlife risk before entering but can leave under the same rule.

A nonparticipant is not an eligible victim or involuntary shield. The boar cannot be lured into the recovery area or beyond its authored territory to attack other people. A protected player cannot attack outward while retaining safety; starting that action requires actually leaving the protected approach and joining the encounter. Player-to-player harm remains refused in this offering, including supported delayed effects.

## The boar's behavior

The animal uses its actual supported senses. It warns before a first attack, chooses one eligible perceived target, winds up, commits to a direction, charges and recovers. The committed charge cannot turn to follow later movement. Its first physical body impact or an obstruction stops it, and an individual charge cannot repeatedly damage the same body. A failed or interrupted attack has an actual miss/cancellation result and the appropriate existing recovery.

Warning posture/direction and sound have equivalent visible meaning. The interface cannot identify an unseen animal or disclose its private current target. Lost sight allows only a bounded move toward the last observed position within the territory, not tracking unseen current coordinates. An unavailable route ends or limits the attempt; no teleport or world-wide search is permitted. [TE02](../../limits/base-world.md#te02--proposed-readable-threat-and-weapon-tuning) owns initial tuning and pursuit/search bounds.

The animal can be driven away by injury, and reaching the cache without killing is valid success. It retains actual injury, location and identity after retreat. Neither player departure nor absence resets the animal or reward. This one native animal policy does not replace a humanlike resident's need for its own cognition when choosing new actions.

## Defeat and intentional killing

Humans retain current zero-health incapacitation and possession-preserving recovery. In the selected encounter, ordinary human-initiated supported attacks can drive off or incapacitate the boar; they cannot cause unconfirmed death. This outcome must cover each admitted knife, unarmed, launcher and stronger profile route, including queued/committed effects. It is a proposed body-policy extension, not current nonhuman behavior.

A human can deliberately select an exact-target final lethal action against the incapacitated animal. Its review states the persistent death consequence and difficult ordinary revival, using only permitted target/context information. It applies to that current target/action only. Cancelled or stale confirmation does not kill. There is no confirmation interruption in every normal strike, and killing earns no extra reward in this site.

The proposed boar incapacity is temporary when nobody deliberately kills it: after the authored recovery interval it regains the specified partial health and attempts to withdraw to its resting point. It does not attack during that withdrawal or teleport through a blocked route. A blocked withdrawal leaves it nonaggressive at its actual place until an ordinary valid route is available; it creates no repeated expensive search loop. Its later health follows supported body processes, without a full-health reset. [TE03](../../limits/base-world.md#te03--proposed-retreat-recovery-and-help) owns the proposed interval and health floor.

Other NPC/environmental deaths are not converted into player modals. Unqualified damaging fire spread, traps, falling structures and arbitrary area harm remain outside this site's offered effects. Broader lethal, ghost, resurrection and indirect-hazard policies remain with D07/D15/BW14/BW15.

## Retreat, human recovery and help

An active participant may choose Retreat. New offense stops; the known physical exit is attempted within the same finite ending bound as disconnected retreat. Actual committed effects and spent supplies remain. At the bound, unresolved participation becomes abandonment at the nearby qualified return point. This forgiving world rule does not claim an unperformed physical rescue.

Retreat preserves current bodily values unless existing recovery eligibility is independently met. It never grants a healthy person the recovery floors. Eligible recovery uses the current body owner/floors with the qualified local destination; it preserves possessions and does not rewind the encounter. No new fee, skill loss, repair sink, corpse run or permanent human loss is added.

A collapsed participant is not a continued attack target, loses no further survival resources while waiting, and cannot attack, loot or obstruct the escape route. Recover remains available without another person's help; there is no forced bleed-out deadline. Normal absence still bounds disconnected participation.

A willing nearby person may offer Help up. The active recipient accepts that helper and standing at the current location. One short native assistance action requires actual reach and capability, stops on injury/withdrawal/lost conditions, and uses the existing body owner on success. No inventory access, debt or forced relationship change follows. It cannot finish into an already admitted unavoidable hit; otherwise self-recovery remains available. Nobody can reactivate an absent human into danger by approving help for them. Resident willingness remains an ordinary paid/admitted decision, never a requirement for human recovery.

## Safe placement and finite absence

Use the trail-side return point only if current standing geometry, the supported hazard checks and an ordinary route away are valid. The boar cannot pursue into it, and an admitted hit cannot immediately strike the returning person there. If invalid, use the existing authored fallback only under the same supported checks; if both fail, retain protected inactivity and expose an honest unavailable return. No arbitrary nearby search, hidden-threat disclosure or materialization into danger is permitted.

An engaged encounter requires an actually admitted hostile interaction or ongoing participation in it; hidden possibilities and proximity alone are insufficient. After departure is detected, retain the original real-time deadline. New attackers, retries and observer changes cannot renew it. [TE04](../../limits/base-world.md#te04--proposed-bounded-encounter-exit) proposes the existing 15-second default for this trial.

The disclosed standing response can attempt retreat and preserve previously committed consequences, but cannot initiate offense, speech, purchases, promises, new goals or discretionary model work for the human. Returning during exit restores the same person and current result without cancelling the requested retreat or restarting the deadline.

At the ending bound, apply the same abandonment/eligible recovery as connected Retreat, then ordinary protected inactivity. If mechanics paused when the last player left, do not secretly advance them; only actual prior effects exist. If the server stopped, restart reconciles the original deadline and real saved effects without retrospective invented damage. Warnings and the return account use permitted evidence; browser/tab controls remain their existing owner.
