# Threat responses — wilderness purpose and action catalogue

| Status      | Current progress                                                                                              | Last updated |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| In progress | The selected threat-response catalogue is designed; installation and independent behavior checks remain open. | 2026-10-17   |

**Selected target, not installed behavior.** This profile supplies the wilderness world's plausible purposes, bounded commitments, context language and candidate actions for an autonomous person's response to danger. It does not decide that a particular person is angry, create an intention for them, or execute an attack. The [feature spec](../../projects/combat-and-threat-responses-feature-spec.md), [technical design](../../projects/combat-and-threat-responses-tech-design.md) and [implementation tracker](../../maintainers/combat-and-threat-responses.md) own the complete delivery. Native animal escape is a different, model-free world policy.

## 1. What a response means

A response has a personally chosen purpose, an exact first action, and a finite commitment that says when the person must consider the situation again. The same punch can mean stopping an immediate assault, covering another person's retreat, enforcing a personal boundary, or seeking revenge. Those purposes are alternatives with different stopping conditions, not different damage multipliers. A choice may also be to withdraw, hold position, speak, continue existing work, or deliberately do nothing. No purpose label grants a capability that the world has not installed.

The initial supported candidate set combines current native strike, guard, evade, movement, follow, conversation and plan-control actions. General grappling, disarming, restraining, surrender agreements, group tactics and negotiated guarantees remain future capabilities; they must never be offered as executable merely because their prose is plausible. “Call for help” means ordinary permitted speech, with its actual audible recipients and no promise that anyone helps. “Check on someone” means an available observation or conversation, not an invented medical treatment.

A selected strike remains one strike. A selected finite pursuit or defensive commitment can continue only the exact actions and stop conditions that the person selected and native admission still permits. A purpose alone must never turn on infinite auto-attacking, chase a hidden opponent, queue attacks during recovery, or recruit another person.

## 2. Two decision stages

The pipeline has three boundaries:

1. **Native preparation:** collect the person's permitted evidence, current body, current/paused work, real action grants and exact feasible alternatives. Reject impossible bindings before spending. A protected-person option needs a real personally known person with a currently supported protective action; a guard option needs usable equipment. Failed or unfinished preparation is not evidence that an option is irrelevant.
2. **Cheap relevance decision:** the Decision API receives eligible grounded purpose instances and the actor-scoped situation. Each instance already binds its intended opponent/protected person/place and role, or explicitly has no identified subject. It classifies each supplied instance as relevant, irrelevant, or uncertain. In the same call, independent source-bound appraisal questions may propose supported feelings or no change. This is a mandatory semantic step for this selected human threat-response route; native predicates do not replace it. It creates no physical commitment.
3. **Actual choice:** after source and state revalidation, a second Decision API call receives the current situation, admitted appraisal results, the relevant/uncertain purposes, and their fully grounded feasible first actions. It chooses one complete response, explicit continuation/no action, or uncertainty. The model is choosing what this person wants to do, not rating what a generic optimal fighter should do. Publish only through normal native admission.

Each grounded purpose receives one existing **Choice** question with these three separately named meanings. A binary yes/no relevance rating cannot preserve this selected distinction.

| Relevance meaning | Meaning in this person's permitted situation                        | Treatment in the second stage                                       |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Uncertain         | The evidence leaves whether this purpose fits unresolved.           | Retain the exact instance and show that its relevance is uncertain. |
| Relevant          | This purpose plausibly fits this person and the supplied situation. | Retain the exact instance as relevant.                              |
| Irrelevant        | This supplied purpose does not fit this person and situation.       | Exclude this instance only.                                         |

The [technical stage-one binding](../../projects/combat-and-threat-responses-tech-design.md#stage-one-situation-relevance-and-independent-appraisals) maps these meanings to stable offered handles and the existing shared Choice decoder. Its conservative exact-tie order retains a plausible purpose; no new numerical confidence bands or probability calibration are introduced. Missing or malformed required answers remain failed judgments with their actual invalid-result outcome; actual service unavailability remains unavailable. Neither is semantic uncertainty or an irrelevant purpose. Ordinary noncombat relevance keeps its existing policy.

Uncertain relevance is retained for the person's choice, visibly marked uncertain. An empty shortlist still permits explicit continuation/no action and reports that no substantive response was judged relevant. It must not fabricate an attack, silently add a preferred answer, or describe a provider failure as the person's decision. Review a lost plausible purpose as a relevance defect, not by coercing the final selector.

The response handle identifies the whole grounded instance, not only `TR10` or a person's display name. “Protect this known person from this observed assailant” and the same purpose concerning another person are separate relevance questions. Stage two can choose only actions compatible with that same admitted instance; a relevant protectee must not become every nearby person during expansion. A new subject, source role or meaning after an await requires new relevance consideration. An unchanged binding may reuse exact native preparation while final execution still checks current authority and physical feasibility.

When stage one publishes a feeling, stage two binds the resulting admitted revision and current numerical meaning. That intentional update must not make its own choice permanently stale or schedule another pipeline from the same appraisal receipt. Preserve the original cause identity and evidence cut, retain independently newer cues, and recheck external changes and source privacy. Required relevant prior feelings are acquired completely through the appraisal owner, not inferred absent from a bounded first-page prefix; [psychological reactions](psychological-reactions.md#immediate-context-and-continuity) defines this continuity.

Do not pre-generate thoughts or manufacture memories to fill the catalogue. Author short reusable purpose descriptions and native plan templates. Ordinary richer cognition remains available through its existing authorization when actual language or a novel proposal is needed; this combat route neither requires generation nor starts an automatic paid fallback.

### 2.1 Complete choices while busy

An ongoing action is relevant context, not a reason to return before preparing alternatives. Every cognition opportunity receives the current action, progress, reservations, spent materials, recovery and exact consequences of continuing, stopping, replacing or temporarily interrupting it. Supply currently feasible strike/guard/evade/escape and relevant social choices even while work is active. Where an interruption is impossible, explain that blocker and the next meaningful native boundary; do not pretend the action is immediately executable. Use the existing plan-control and action-availability owners, including receipt and resumption rules.

“Continue the meal” and “stop the meal, then strike this attacker with this hand” are different choices. The latter must bind an explicit permitted control operation and exact strike. Validation, stock capacity and context preparation that can refuse precede interruption. If the action is physically committed and cannot stop, retain its real remaining work/recovery and reconsider at the supported boundary. This is not a new permanent combat mode.

Relevance is personal. A nearby stranger does not automatically become someone Ada wants to protect; native preparation establishes that protection is possible, while stage one decides whether that purpose fits her actual context. Conversely, a known endangered friend must not be omitted just because self-defense has the highest urgency. No numerical relationship model is introduced here.

### 3.1 Executable finite speech choices

The two-call route can select complete authored speech as its one response without buying a generator to fill in the words. The option holds the exact utterance, ordinary speech volume, actual permitted addressee or supported addressed audience, current source/role binding and native speech admission. Stage two sees the complete words before selection. The following initial utterances are alternatives, not required lines or assertions about what a character already said.

| Speech ID | Exact offered words                    | Eligible purpose/condition and delivery                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| TS01      | “Help!”                                | TR13 when ordinary speech and at least a possible actual audible recipient are available. Offer the native normal or shout volume explicitly; it does not find or alert an unseen helper outside actual hearing.                      |
| TS02      | “Help! I'm hurt.”                      | TR12/13/21 only with personally experienced current bodily hurt. Same actual volume/audience binding; unknown assailant stays unknown.                                                                                                |
| TS03      | “Back away.”                           | TR02/03/14/17 to an actually addressable identified or continuously observed person. This expresses a boundary, not proof of their motive or a compelled retreat.                                                                     |
| TS04      | “Leave me alone.”                      | TR06/14/17 to the actual perceived person involved. It asserts the speaker's present wish and no invented prior agreement.                                                                                                            |
| TS05      | “Was that an accident?”                | TR15 with an actual personally experienced contact and a valid addressee associated with it. Do not address a guessed shooter or convert the eventual answer into verified fact.                                                      |
| TS06      | “Stop. I don't want to keep fighting.” | TR06/16 during an actual exchange. This is unilateral speech, not a completed ceasefire or forced physical cancellation. Any stop of the speaker's native work must be separately selected and supported.                             |
| TS07      | “Stay back. I'm leaving.”              | TR05/06/14 only as an expressed present intention. Speaking does not itself move the body, end current work or guarantee safe passage.                                                                                                |
| TS08      | “Are you hurt?”                        | TR10/21 to an actual perceived/known addressable person. No diagnosis or automatic aid follows.                                                                                                                                       |
| TS09      | “Do you want my help?”                 | TR10/11/12/21 with an actual addressee and a plausible supported form of help. A reply is testimony and an independent response; silence is not consent.                                                                              |
| TS10      | “I'll stay here.”                      | TR03/09/16 when selecting a present commitment that can actually remain at this place. The line alone does not install hold behavior, a guard, a deadline or a promise to another person beyond the ordinary speech/commitment rules. |

No line names an assailant, promises that a helper will arrive, reports unseen harm, or claims completed movement/force. Exact names or other variants may later be authored through the shared observer-reference rendering owner; no runtime placeholder is filled from hidden identity or free generation. A purpose can offer more than one applicable utterance, but does not require speech. An alternative can choose no speech and a real counterattack.

In first delivery, a finite speech option is **one ordinary speech operation**, not an invented speech-and-strike action. A combined response is offerable only when its exact independent operations, lane compatibility, ordering/`requiresAccepted` meaning, costs and partial-outcome semantics are already supported and shown. No such combined template is required to deliver this initial catalogue. Richer conversation can be separately selected through the existing generated-dialogue route with its own admission and accounting. A listener's later independently triggered response is a separate real decision, not an undisclosed third generation stage to produce these authored words.

## 4. Initial Ada disposition and required behavior

Keep Ada's practical, curious, considerate, candid identity, woodland background, reluctance to need help, fear of pain and wish to have a future. Add ordinary assertiveness and self-respect as selected initial dispositions: **she values her bodily autonomy, is willing to use proportionate physical force, and sees fear and decisive action as compatible**. These traits apply to disagreement, risky work, protecting something valued, accepting help and physical conflict. They are not an installed goal, a story about an attack that never happened, or a sentence of the form “when punched, counterattack.” Current body capabilities and actual attack evidence belong in native context, not invented combat experience in biography.

In the ordinary playable baseline—Ada is able to act, personally identifies an unprovoked attacker, and has a feasible opportunity to strike—**Ada must independently choose and actually begin a physical counterattack**. Speech-only, flee-only, continued crafting, an aggressive sentence, a seeded goal and an injected command do not pass this baseline. The strike must enter its native preparation/wind-up or contact; approach alone does not pass. A missed or properly blocked native strike can satisfy “fought back”; merely selecting an impossible attack cannot. This acceptance requirement is for the actual authored character and actual choice pipeline. No engine branch, action filter, private forced objective, hidden candidate preference or fake receipt may special-case Ada to obtain it.

After that counterattack, further conduct remains a real contextual choice: she may drive the assailant away, stop at a boundary, pursue for a finite reason, withdraw as danger changes, or let the assailant leave. A baseline failure is a design/implementation failure to diagnose through delivered evidence, complete options, the authored disposition and the selector. Do not silently weaken the requirement to “any plausible reaction.” Counterfactual cases with an unseen source, incapacitation, overwhelming injury, impossible reach or unavailable decisions must remain truthful; the engine cannot execute an impossible response to satisfy a personality expectation.

Model choice is stochastic. A finite evaluation can establish a release acceptance result, not guarantee all future completions. Retain all predeclared baseline runs and failed comparisons; do not reroll until a counterattack appears or treat fixture selection as autonomous behavior. The technical design defines the ordinary-path evidence and contrasting characters required for qualification.

The initial technical acceptance window is 900 game seconds from the personally experienced hit (15 normal-speed seconds at the current 60:1 clock), with a legal, visible, reachable attacker and an able Ada. This is the product's declared qualification bound, not a provider-speed guarantee or permission to pause the world or redirect Ada. Her ongoing movement and interrupted work remain actual choices with real consequences; separate punch-and-run cases test whether a response remains feasible when the assailant leaves.

## 5. First-delivery commitments and reconsideration tuning

The following are selected, adjustable world defaults. They are not engine constants or observed performance results. Use the existing finite activity/plan and installed observed-condition owners. A purpose ID is descriptive selection metadata on that admitted work, not a parallel psychological goal store. A larger personal objective, if the person chooses one, remains an ordinary `ActorGoal`.

| Selected term                    | Default and exact meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| A counterattack                  | One admitted strike with its exact target life and method, including disclosed automatic approach where supported. After the actual result, reconsider before another strike. No auto-repeat or hidden attack queue is added in this delivery.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Visible pursuit                  | An explicitly selected approach/follow or approach-and-one-strike composition, ending no later than 600 game seconds after selection or at the boundary 30 m from its selected starting anchor, whichever occurs first. Keep current native movement, reach and recovery rules. The parameter and boundary are shown before choice. Another chase needs another actual choice.                                                                                                       |
| Lost sight                       | Native sight-dependent following/approach stops immediately under its owning rule. The person may separately choose the exact last-seen destination. The deadline/anchor does not reset when following is interrupted or restored.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Last-seen search                 | One movement to one personally recorded point. A continuation retains the original selected pursuit deadline/anchor. A newly and independently selected search with no continuing commitment uses the same 600-game-second/30 m defaults anchored at this new choice. Arrival without reacquisition ends it. A restoration, replan or stale-response retry cannot masquerade as a fresh selection. No radius search, hidden-coordinate update or automatic return route is supplied. |
| Hold a place                     | A selected reachable point and a 600-game-second maximum commitment. A guard is still its actual single-contact/time-limited action. Holding supplies no repeated attacks or guards. Actual selected action results and material changes remain decision boundaries.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Deliberately assess/wait         | A chosen review deadline no later than 120 game seconds after selection, or an earlier relevant cue. It does not poll all threats every tick or call the model periodically after the commitment ends.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Range-band hysteresis            | Enter the strike-relevant band at the actual selected method's admitted reach. Report leaving that band after distance exceeds that reach by 0.75 m continuously for 12 game seconds. At selection with no method, use the shortest currently available direct strike's actual reach; if none exists, use current feasible displacement/observation facts and do not invent an attack range.                                                                                         |
| Movement/occlusion stabilization | For an already known opponent's departure, lost sight or ceased preparation, coalesce a paid reconsideration until the changed observation has remained true for 12 game seconds. The native action and preview become invalid immediately when their prerequisites fail. A new hit, newly perceived attack or other immediately urgent danger bypasses this stabilization.                                                                                                          |
| Repeated identical cue           | Emit once per meaningful condition transition and source revision. Another footstep or another scheduler poll is not a new opportunity. A genuinely new blow/attempt remains new evidence even before the earlier decision finishes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Review admission                 | No universal combat cooldown is added. A genuinely new urgent opportunity enters existing scheduling immediately; pending ordinary changes for that actor coalesce. The already selected action can continue while an unrelated decision waits only while its native prerequisites and chosen conditions remain valid.                                                                                                                                                               |

At the current 60:1 clock, 600 game seconds is 10 normal-speed real seconds, 120 is 2 seconds, and 12 is 0.2 seconds. Pausing stops these deadlines; speed changes follow game time; restoration resumes remaining time without real-time catch-up. The independent Command-click recent-hostility preference happens to use the same initial 600-game-second duration, but it is a different owner and must not extend a pursuit, force an appraisal, authorize attack or erase a feeling when it expires.

The one-strike default deliberately leaves renewed aggression as a character choice. A future finite exchange of several strikes may be supplied as an explicitly chosen bounded composition if measurement shows that reconsidering after every strike is too slow or costly. It must preserve the chosen purpose, maximum attempts/deadline, observed stop conditions and current native validation. It is not installed by this first catalogue or silently substituted when the provider is slow.

## 6. Future repertoire, deliberately not first delivery

Retain motives such as disarming without killing, restraining, protecting offspring, defending a herd/pack, negotiated surrender, intimidation that succeeds, vengeance over long periods, social reputation, witness reports, institutional arrest, coordinated rescue and learned combat habits as repertoire material. Each needs its own installed capabilities, evidence and authority before it is an executable option. Long-term relationship dynamics are explicitly deferred. They should reuse existing personal evidence and interpretation, not be implied by the short threat episode or by a universal hostility meter.
