# Useful discoveries beyond the clearing

**Implemented base-world content, October 6, 2026.** Native journey and production-browser evidence are recorded [here](../../verification/rewarding-expeditions.md). The current single-equipped-tool system supplies weapon use; AV02 still owns the final two-hand integration. [AV01](../../maintainers/parallel-batch-05-adventure-defense-and-home.md#av01--rewarding-expeditions) owns delivery. This is an ordinary authored adventure, not generated invention or a universal quest system.

## Two places and a reason to go

Extend the existing lookout as the first destination; add one abandoned, open riverside workshop on supported ground. Use the current landscape and navigation, not a new vertical building or a crossing over unmodeled water. Keep both outside the starter clearing and essential food access. The workshop must have a longer walkable approach that avoids the incoming stag's aggression area. Neither site requires killing or changes that threat's behavior.

A physical camp marker points toward the visible lookout with truthful terrain/direction wording. An inspectable note at the lookout describes the workshop's river-bank location. These are authored messages with sources; they are not words newly spoken by Ada or guaranteed complete knowledge. PX03 owns retaining an actually learned place and the distinction between focus and walking. Exact coordinates belong in the base-world spatial/content source and are selected against real reachable surfaces; no engine or prompt duplicates a second coordinate table.

The lookout's existing wood source remains. Its visible ground bundle holds a portable equipment case containing one ordinary spear and the workshop note. The workshop ground bundle holds a portable supply satchel; its exterior identifies the site without disclosing the nested stock. The workshop contains finite supplies for its displayed method and a readable physical recipe record. Inventory contents are only shown through permitted inspection. A visitor who takes an item removes that same item for everyone; opening or revisiting does not restock it. Another person can still inspect a record left at the site. A player may take the portable record through normal custody, leaving no invisible copy behind.

## Rewards worth using

The spear is an authored contact weapon: 10 injury, 0.8 base accuracy, 2.2-metre reach, 1.8-metre approach distance, 18-game-second preparation and 36-game-second recovery. It has greater reach than the starting knife and a slower cycle, rather than being better in every respect. It occupies both hands under AV02; before that contract is integrated, native single-tool execution remains the available proof, not completion of equipment comparison. Keep its actual geometry/clearance and target rules with ordinary contact attacks.

The workshop record teaches one **field sling** recipe through exact current recipe knowledge. It uses the existing swing-launcher family and lawful material roles: two cord units for binding and one prepared-fiber unit for its flexible pouch. Admitted output: one sling with 14 injury, 6-metre range and 0.75 authored accuracy; manufacture takes 180 game seconds. New-world creation compiles these values and role bindings through the installed family. The saved method has authored-world provenance, teaches nobody on installation and creates no item; actors learn only through an admitted disclosure. Native checks exercised the actual compiled output, material debit and use.

The site supplies two cord, one prepared fiber and four stones as ordinary finite lots. They are not a supply guarantee after another visitor takes them. Existing gathering/preparation remains the alternative. The method is a reliable known choice, not a unique best recipe; the player can invent another supported method and benefit from its actual differences.

Reading the physical record is a new supported knowledge action. A successful in-reach inspection identifies its exact pinned method, explains what it makes and what is required, then offers **Learn this method**. Native admission grants only that method to that actor. It manufactures nothing, consumes no recipe record and does not reveal source-author private history. Repeated learning returns an already-known outcome. Merely seeing the outside of a bag holding the record or hearing another visitor's result supplies no grant.

An authored record is not an arbitrary executable document: it can refer only to an installed, validated method eligible for this disclosure. Missing, changed or withdrawn definitions make the relevant learning action unavailable with a permitted explanation. Known characters retain their actual acquired knowledge under current knowledge/revision rules, not through a second per-site completion flag.

Inventory selection first commits permitted inspection, then refreshes the exact method facts and learning action. Crafting identifies learned records as **Authored method** and keeps **Invent a tool** available. The bundled first-invention goal explicitly says **Invent a sling with live AI**: record learning and fixture proposals never complete it. An independently admitted live proposal can reuse the same mechanics without replacing the original record-learning evidence.

## Persistence and continuation

Install the sites, stock and records only in new authored starts. Do not inject rewards into existing saves, refill after reincarnation, or rewrite a restored world's history. World-authorized editing remains separate. The player can use the spear on another ordinary hunt, manufacture the sling and try it, share a carried reward through actual consent, or simply keep the discovered route. No automatic goal, victory banner, XP or completed-quest ledger is required.
