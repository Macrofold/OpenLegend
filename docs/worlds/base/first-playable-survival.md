# First-playable food, materials and making

**Selected design, October 17, 2026; implementation and play qualification remain open.** This is the base world's exact content owner for the survival-first [Main opening](playable-openings.md#first-playable-game-target). The [feature specification](../../projects/first-playable-survival-feature-spec.md) explains the experience; the [technical design](../../projects/first-playable-survival-tech-design.md) defines execution. Existing Main currently grants a kit and has empty nearby berry patches; neither establishes this target.

## Arrival and ordinary bodily rules

The ordinary player starts with 100 Food, 100 Energy and the body's full Health, no carried item, equipment, player-owned camp, prepared stock or construction permission tied to an old kit. Ordinary clothing is appearance only; it cannot be converted into free cord or a canopy. Known methods below are authored competence, with no fabricated earlier manufacture or invention attribution. The four friendly residents retain their individually authored stock and knowledge; their help is optional.

Retain the installed [survival](survival.md), [sleep](sleep.md), [time](time.md) and [death](player-death.md) laws. Food drains 0.003 points per game second; normal speed is 60 game seconds per real second. From 100 Food, zero occurs after 33,333.33 game seconds, approximately 9 minutes 15.6 seconds of continuously running normal-speed play. The first starvation injury follows the existing full 300-game-second interval. This is a paper budget, not a measured newcomer's available attention. Reading while running and real provider waits still cost actual world time; existing Pause/slow controls remain available without adding an automatic survival pause.

Wild berries restore 18 Food, cooked river fish 30 and cooked meat 38, clamped to the actual maximum. At the selected drain those amounts replace 100, 166.67 and 211.11 real seconds respectively at normal speed. Eating at full Food wastes the excess. [A deliberate meal](#a-deliberate-meal) lets the player choose several actual units in one ordinary interaction: five berries restore up to 90 Food, replacing 500 real seconds, or 8 minutes 20 seconds. This reduces repetitive input without changing metabolism, nutrition or future eating decisions. People cannot eat raw meat or fish under their selected diet; animals' explicitly authored diets and finite feeding belong to [roaming](first-playable-roaming.md). No thirst meter, poison roulette, spoilage, new sleep prerequisite, thermal damage or obligatory maintenance is selected. Fresh water is a geographical reason for fishing and material access, not an advertised drinking mechanic.

Near flint, stone, wood and fiber must be reachable without entering the territorial stag's initial aggression area or the rivals' starting ground. The initializer preserves the union of existing river reaches, quarry, rewards, practice target, missing-belonging scene, residents, rival stock and optional building materials. Existing player-granted equipment is reconciled by [the integrated opening design](../../projects/first-playable-world-tech-design.md); it is never silently left in the new player's inventory. No optional cache contains a complete free camp beside arrival. Existing stocked locations are additional physical content, not counted twice as new resource patches.

The table's totals are **subtotals for its named sources**, not the complete world's inventory. The twelve retained `woodland-patch-0` through `woodland-patch-11` sources in the existing landscape owner add 92 berries, 106 wood, 78 stone and 108 raw fibers. Preserve those identities, positions and quantities through the opening manifest rather than authoring duplicate rows here. The combined named-source plus retained-landscape totals are therefore **332 berries, 238 wood, 218 stone and 408 raw fibers**. Flint, pole and loose-bone table totals remain unchanged. Separate lookout resources, carried holdings, caches and future actual corpse yields remain separate categories; they are not free player stock. The candidate checker must derive the complete finite-stock report from the manifest and initialized world, including each origin once, rather than treating either table as the whole world.

All listed material units have packing load 1 except a straight pole, which has load 3. These are packing units, not kilograms or a new carrying-capacity law. Poles and finished structural components retain actual authored dimensions/condition where the construction consumer requires them. Stacking, bag eligibility and fitted parts follow [items](items.md) and [shelters](editable-shelters.md), not an adjective in an item name. Clay, resin, hide, sinew, fat, charcoal and metalwork are unselected for this first chain; their absence creates no hidden recipe prerequisite.

## Known manufacturing catalogue

`config/first-playable/recipes.yaml` is the single new content source for elementary conversion recipes and authored candidates passed to existing recipe-family compilers. Existing fiber, cord, food, shield, container and fishing laws are referenced or moved to their one applicable YAML owner, never copied into another executor. The table distinguishes retained tools from spent inputs; every quantity is a whole physical unit. All work uses game seconds. Successful outputs enter the maker's ordinary custody through existing object creation. Cancelling after manufacturing begins keeps the already-spent inputs spent; no unfinished complete product appears.

The player and four initially friendly residents know the elementary rows on arrival. This grants method knowledge, never stock. Existing distinct methods, private acquisitions and source-bearing physical records remain distinct. New unfamiliar people do not inherit the opening's competence merely because the engine supports it.

“Cutting tool” means a currently admitted `stone_tool` or `knife`, selected from the world's explicit tool-definition list and held for that batch. No consumable stone, spear name, arbitrary `point` tag or model-generated description grants this role. Another authored world may select a different exact list. This first scope adds no durability. Transferring or dropping a tool used by active work is refused until that work ends.

The wooden club uses the existing main-hand contact-weapon implementation: packing load 2, 6 injury, 0.75 base accuracy, 1.4 m reach, 1.0 m approach, 12-second preparation and 24-second recovery. It can share the hands with the existing shield. The club gives a dependable locally made weapon; the lookout spear retains longer reach and a slower cycle. Stone tools keep their existing cutting/harvesting purpose; any structural striking use is an explicit [construction rule](editable-shelters.md), not new creature-attack authority.

Reed comb, pole hook and branch hook are exact world-authored `base:gathering-tool` candidates, with the table's materials, work and quantity. They use the existing compatible-tool consumer and do not stack. No extra gather/craft effectiveness, carrying capacity or combat ability is inferred from their material tags. Known pouches and baskets are organization and shared placement choices; they do not increase the actor's unmodeled overall carrying capacity, waterproof their contents or preserve food. The camp basket explicitly selects the installed `base:open-woven-contents` profile, authored revision 1, through the woven-container family's `parameters.contentsExposureProfile` pin. The pouch does not. This capability allows actual prepared fiber/eligible cover material as direct children of one open basket, itself directly carried or on a world item pile, while transmitting rain fully (1). The material's real moisture persists and continues through the basket's ground/carried exposure; a roof can protect that actual basket. Other containers and deeper nesting keep their existing active-moisture refusal. [Construction's open-container exposure contract](editable-shelters.md) owns the precise shape, lifecycle and consumers; the recipe never infers shelter from a basket's name.

The structural-output dimensions, condition, moisture, attachment roles, packing eligibility and construction use are authored once in `construction.yaml` under [editable shelters](editable-shelters.md). The recipe compiler resolves those exact output pins. A shortened post cannot be turned back into a longer pole. The 0.60 m offcut from a short camp post is one usable ordinary branch. The 0.10 m trimming from a canopy or 2.30 m gate post is not an additional usable branch; it yields no retained item. This is a deliberate finite material rule, not a general reversible material algebra. Do not produce an offcut from a gate post, canopy post, full-length rail or tall post.

## A personally built fire

**Lay a hearth** is a known place-targeted native operation: choose a ground point, preview a ring of radius 0.45 m and height 0.15 m, confirm, approach a valid working stance and spend 120 game seconds. It requires four carried, available `stone` units. Reserve the exact stones at work start; consume them only when all completion checks pass and the one new hearth is created. Cancellation or a blocked completion releases the stones. This differs deliberately from manufacturing a carried item: there is no staged partial hearth to recover or completed ring to place twice.

The full disk must have the selected terrain support, a flatness difference at most 0.05 m across its footprint, no collision and clear space to 2 m above its support. A normal admitted canopy above that height can cover it; this supplies no smoke, warmth, carbon-monoxide or building-ignition simulation. The worker must reach a clear point on the ring without reaching through a wall. There is no placement over water, unsupported elevation, inside another body or on top of an existing fire. Admission also checks ordinary permission for that place. Construction permission is not granted merely by choosing a UI point.

Completion makes one ordinary **cold, unfueled campfire**, with the same fire behavior, light when lit and replacement-life eligibility as other campfires. Add one `wood` using existing fuel care: 20 seconds of work, adding 3,600 seconds of fuel. Lighting requires one ordinary fiber bundle as tinder (raw or prepared under the existing rule) and one kept `wood` drill, takes 150 seconds and spends tinder only at completion. Thus one fire from nothing needs four stones, two branches and one raw-fiber bundle; no cutting tool or preparation is required. If the player first cleans two bundles instead, lighting uses one prepared bundle and leaves one. The fuel branch is consumed; the drill remains. No player receives a free lit fire or seven days of fuel.

Existing fuel maximum, extinguishing, interruptions and cooking claims remain in [fire care](survival.md#tending-the-campfire). No automatic refuel, relight, portable torch, spreading fire or predator fear effect is selected. The permanent stone ring remains after fuel is exhausted or the player relocates. Ordinary dismantling/reclaim of hearth stone is not offered in this slice; four stones are a small intentional sunk cost, recorded in [SVL04](../../limits/first-playable-survival.md#svl04--hearth-construction-and-permanence). Shelter parts retain their separate actual reclaim behavior. An unlit hearth remains a usable rest spot under existing death rules, subject to actual arrival clearance.

## A complete bare-hand route

This is a dependency proof and possible player route, never a tutorial script or NPC instruction:

1. Inspect a nearby familiar berry patch, gather a berry bare-handed and eat it when useful. No knife, fire, craft, ally or model is needed.
2. Gather one loose stone and one flint. Use the retained stone to knap the flint into a cutting stone. No later recipe is needed to make the first cutting tool.
3. Gather two raw fibers and one branch. Clean the two fibers, twist the two prepared units into one cord and make a reed comb. The first cord requires neither a cutting tool nor a comb; subsequent reed collections can take three units.
4. Collect more actual fiber, branches, poles and stones. Make a pole hook if building; make a club/shield, basket or river line if useful. A known method's ingredients are visible before commitment.
5. Lay and fuel a hearth, light it using prepared tinder and a retained branch, then cook an actual fish or harvested meat. Berries remain an alternative if casts miss, quarry escapes or the fire is interrupted.
6. Make canopy/wall/gate components, construct a chosen site and bring actual goods home. Structure recipes do not require a prebuilt workstation or a machine that can only be made inside that structure.

Every consumable tool input is distinct from a kept tool. If one stone lot contains five units, the selected hammer reserves one and four can be spent on a hearth only through a supported split/claim; displaying five available when one is reserved is wrong. A lost cutting stone can be remade with another flint and stone, or an accessible existing knife can satisfy the explicit cutting role. Loss of every tool does not remove bare-hand food, wood, fibers, stones, flint or fallen-pole collection.

### Construction budget and value of improved tools

The [selected 4 m × 4 m enclosure](editable-shelters.md) plus one flat canopy uses five short camp posts, three tall gate/parking posts, six ordinary wall panels, one gate access panel, one sliding gate, two gate rails, four canopy posts, one roof mat and its actual installed ties. The tall supports keep the gate runner above a standing person's body. The gate needs six ties; seven wall panels need fourteen; the canopy needs four. The mat itself consumes two additional cords. The total remains **30 straight poles and 94 raw fibers**, yielding **26 cords** within that fiber total. Five usable wood offcuts result from the short camp posts; those can later become real fuel. The smaller 2 m × 4 m arrangement has three short posts, three tall gate posts, four ordinary wall panels and one gate access panel, producing three usable offcuts. The access panel's real hand window lets the parked gate be reached from outside; its exact opening and consequences belong to the construction profile. This count excludes utility tools, food, hearth stones and optional replacements; it does not count cord twice.

The two local pole patches together contain 36 poles; the initial existing reeds plus bank reeds contain 174 raw fiber. That is enough for the camp and some mistakes if available, without depending on the distant reward cache. The whole world adds genuine alternatives, with 96 poles and 300 raw fibers across the listed sources. A competing character may reduce local availability; those stocks are not reserved inventory. Full construction is optional before an expedition; a fire, one canopy, food and a weapon are useful stopping points.

With a reed comb and pole hook, 94 fibers need at least 32 collections and 30 poles need 10, instead of 124 one-unit collections. Preparation still needs 47 fiber batches and 26 cord batches. Those cleaning/twisting batches take 3,816 game seconds, or 63.6 real seconds at normal speed. Adding 32 reed collections and ten pole collections gives 5,568 game seconds, or 92.8 real seconds, before making/installing parts, travel or player deliberation. Thirty-two full three-unit reed collections produce 96 units, leaving two beyond the 94-unit build requirement; those real leftovers remain available. This is intentionally visible work rather than instant menus, but repeated identical clicks add no value; use the explicit finite batches below. Exact total journey time is unmeasured and must include controls, movement, delays and food, not only this arithmetic.

## Deliberate batches

An ordinary known action offers **Once** or **Repeat**, with an explicit integer of 1–16 batches and default one. Show the meaning before Start: for example, “Clean fibers 8 times: use 16 reed bundles, make 16 prepared bundles; 384 game seconds before interruptions.” A collection repeat names the actual source and a maximum potential yield, never promises unseen future stock. The existing bounded activity executor starts each real action in turn, checks prerequisites again and stops on the first refusal, interruption, death, loss of control or explicit Stop. Completed goods and spent inputs remain; queued future batches spend nothing. Finishing sixteen batches never silently starts another sixteen.

A repeat is an actor's finite requested work, not an installed personality habit, automatic hunger response or evidence that an NPC wants to repeat it. No automatic alternate-source, alternate-tool, shopping, gift request or invention step is inserted. A person can select another legitimate plan after the stop. Ingredient totals are derived from the same definitions; future batches have no blanket reservation over everyone else's stock. Reused tools can remain held only for the currently executing batch.

### A deliberate meal

Selecting an accessible edible food offers ordinary **Eat one** and **Eat a meal**. The meal form selects one exact food definition and its permitted accessible stock scope, with a count from 1 to 16 units. Suggest `min(16, available units, ceil((maximum Food − current Food) / nutrition))` from current permitted facts; the player can change the count before Start. Show the actual unit count, current/projected Food and any clipped nutrition in the final unit. For example, at Food 10, five berries project Food 100 with no waste; at Food 20, five project 100 with 10 nutrition unused. Unknown/ineligible food, no available units and already full are distinct explanations; already full does not offer a meal to start. Existing deliberate single-unit eating keeps its current semantics.

Start is one finite chosen meal through existing native activity execution, not a standing food rule. Each unit uses the ordinary Eat admission/debit/effect, freshly resolving unheld units of the same selected definition inside the selected accessible scope. Before each unit, stop successfully if the selected consumption meter is already full. Stop on lost control/life, changed definition, inaccessible/depleted food, interruption or explicit Stop, retaining only the actual consumed units and Food effects. If another physical activity is running, show its ordinary replacement/interruption consequence before commitment; opening the meal form does not cancel it. Do not silently replace berries with fish, take another person's stock, resume when hunger returns or call a model. A player may choose another meal later; NPCs may choose this same finite operation independently.

## Discovery, invention and worthwhile return

The known-crafting catalogue is available through the ordinary Craft/known-actions interface even when empty-handed. Group by the world's purposes—Food and fire, Tools and carrying, Camp components—and retain searchable unavailable known methods with their exact missing inputs. Selecting a resource or carried material also exposes its actual known uses. The table is not a world map, list of hidden possessions or a grant to use another person's stock. No prompt phrase is required for elementary work.

Existing Lookout and Riverside workshop discoveries retain their physical equipment, directions, finite stock and actual record. The lookout spear adds reach with a slower cycle. The workshop's field sling record remains unknown until permitted deliberate inspection and **Learn this method**; its existing 2-cord/1-prepared-fiber, 180-second, 14-injury/6-metre/0.75-accuracy rule is unchanged. Starting elementary competence does not grant this field sling. Learning the record creates neither sling nor invention credit. Every item taken is absent for the next visitor.

The player can instead ask for a new supported method through existing invention. A concrete design hypothesis is **hunting at a distance before acquiring the workshop's ranged method**: the player has a short club and locally made bindings, sees quarry that escapes close approach, and wants a supported ranged alternative. The existing sling-like family accepts binding and pouch roles using obtainable cord/prepared fiber, with range 3–7 m, injury 10–20, accuracy 0.6–0.9 and manufacture 48–480 game seconds. It consumes actual stones at release. It needs no newly invented family, granted record or preselected generated recipe. The actual proposal must select its own admitted materials/parameters; the ordinary club, discovered spear and reachable workshop record remain honest alternatives. This is an available problem and qualification hypothesis, never a forced hunt or guaranteed generated answer.

The actual provider proposal, normal admission, manufacture, use and a later meaningful second use must demonstrate that route. Compare it with the best actually known/accessible alternative, including gathering, making, travel, generation/reading delay, material costs and the remaining job. A longer reach can enable a real otherwise-unavailable shot; better supported accuracy/injury or a genuinely needed gathering improvement can change another actual choice. Do not stage hidden targets, force a lucky hit or count naming alone as value. The former two-branch-to-four-branch example saves only 42 game seconds—0.7 real seconds—per four branches before tool construction and generation; repeated token use of that marginal improvement does not establish the defining promise. A different supported tool remains eligible when its actual payoff is meaningful for the player's remaining need. There is no universal numerical return-on-investment threshold: mechanical differences, comparative burden and the player's stated reason to use it are recorded separately from enjoyment.

Satisfied stopping points include a meal and a remade cutting stone, a weather-covering place to return to, a closed gate and visible food stock, a recovered lost item, a newly learned method, or a useful trade with a willing person. No XP multiplier, building level, compulsory quest sequence, achievement-granted materials or relationship score is introduced. A second outing can use the spear's reach, a manufactured sling, a personally invented tool, a known safer route or an independently willing companion. Actual changes to options are the reward; repeating an identical gather tax is not the qualification target.

## Loss, depletion and continuation

Keep the existing exact half-type retention, separate corpse, stable person and new physical life, scars and campfire-based Continue. Opening construction supplies an existing **unowned cold Wayside hearth**, with no tool, food or building kit, plus the other retained public rest locations in the integrated map. It is a real object before the player arrives, never spawned on death. Known starting wayfinding identifies its location as authored information; it grants neither remote vision nor invented visit history. The player can therefore die before making any camp and still use ordinary Continue if a lawful arrival point exists.

After returning, bare-hand sources permit another meal and replacement cutting tool. Prepared fiber for one scar treatment is obtainable without that tool. Taking the old corpse's stock, accepting help, making replacements or leaving it behind remain choices. A corpse can be looted; returning does not promise recovery of lost goods or respawn killed wildlife. Treatments, new-life filled meters and world consequences stay with [player death](player-death.md).

All sources can eventually be depleted. Another accessible source, permitted trade, actual abandoned goods, ordinary cooking/fishing or a different project is an honest continuation. There is no silent respawn, hourly refill, invisible emergency bag or infinite barter source. The profile's finite budget must support the named first-playable qualification workload; it is not indefinite-ecology acceptance. Renewal/preservation remains broader [DG19/ND06](../../maintainers/needs-design.md#dg19--supplies-that-change-over-time).

If every usable recovery hearth is obstructed/unavailable, retain pending death and supplies and explain why Continue cannot place the player. The integrated **Start a fresh world** route is available only with current creation authority and an operator-prepared empty destination. A play-only guest, or a player without an available destination, receives the truthful host-assistance option instead; death grants no new powers. The draft previews a new current-format opening with the retained creation seed and this selected profile, a new world and actor identity, and no transfer of belongings, relationships or personal memory. It states the loss of continuity and requires deliberate confirmation. The original run, pending death and its consequences remain intact. Exact creation-result recovery resolves an uncertain acknowledgement before another attempt. An authorized creator may instead clear a genuine obstruction. The same permission-gated route can serve a deliberate decision to leave an exhausted world; it never promises that a destination exists, overwrites the old run, emits a resurrection there or rewinds real spending/account access. [Explicit fresh-world recovery](../../projects/first-playable-world-tech-design.md#explicit-fresh-world-recovery) owns the complete cross-world authority, preparation, confirmation, receipt and entry contract.
