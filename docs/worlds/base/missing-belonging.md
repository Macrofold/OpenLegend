**Implemented base-world content, October 13, 2026.** [Batch 08](../../projects/completed/parallel-batch-08-the-missing-belonging-feature-spec.md) delivers one complete noncombat investigation in `packages/domain/src/worlds/base/missing-belonging.ts`. These are finite authored circumstances, not a universal mystery or crime system.

## Incident and separate perspectives

Before the new world's opening, Mara left her satchel at a shared work spot beside a rough stone. Tavin moved it to the nearby Reed-side Store while clearing room for his own work, assuming it was shared equipment. Its fastening caught on the stone's rough edge as he lifted it. He left a sorting note. Mara returned, found the bag missing and connected it with a bundle she had seen Tavin carrying. That connection is a suspicion, not her witnessed knowledge of the relocation.

The fixed past creates the opportunity to investigate; it does not prewrite what either person will say or do. Seed each person's permitted understanding through existing trusted character creation, clearly identified as authored starting understanding. Do not emit a fake past world event, mark the player as a witness, expose the complete incident to both NPCs or reapply seed understanding after it changes. The player's later observations and conversations are actual recorded experiences.

At these coordinates:

- `Mara Reed` (`mara`) starts at `(x=9.5, z=13.5)`, `Mending Clearing marker` (`mending-clearing-marker`) stands at `(x=11, z=16.5)`, and `Mending Clearing work spot` (`mending-clearing-spot`) sits at `(x=11, z=18)`.
- `Tavin Vale` (`tavin`) starts at `(x=9.5, z=18)`, and the `Reed-side Store` ground pile (`reed-side-store-pile`) sits at `(x=11, z=22.5)`.

Both sites have a direct dry-ground route from the starter camp `(8, 8)` and between each other (`4.5m` apart) that requires no combat, water crossing, jumping, climbing, new door or shelter, and stays well clear of the territorial stag's home `(32, 28)` and refuge `(38, 31)`. A marker identifies the clearing and points toward the store. Place descriptions explain landmarks and possible use without promising current stock. The work spot/store are ordinary ground-pile access, not new elevated furniture or enclosed rooms. ## Return, refusal and consequence

Actual custody is authoritative. The bag may be left, picked up, kept and used as ordinary storage, or offered whole to Mara. [Ordinary gift consent](social.md#offering-and-accepting-possessions) governs exact bag/contents, reach, access, capacity, participant validity and acceptance. A special Return command, automatic reclaim or ownership exception is unnecessary. An offer is not a transfer; refusal leaves custody with the current holder.

The recipient can later remember an actual acceptance; an absent person learns only through later permitted observation or testimony. Either person may retain disagreement or uncertainty. There is no reward grant, friendship/forgiveness score, verdict, arrest, scripted confession or compulsory reconciliation. New psychological laws under D69, combat assistance and library/world-creation work are not prerequisites.
