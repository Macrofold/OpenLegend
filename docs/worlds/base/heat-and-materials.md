# Base-world heat and materials

**Proposed authored profile, October 6, 2026 — not enabled or measured.** This owns DG20's initial game values. [The feature specification](../../projects/heat-and-materials-feature-spec.md) explains product behavior and research; [HM-L01–HM-L10](../../limits/heat-and-materials.md) records proposed restrictions. All quantities below are game tuning, not real fire or pottery guidance.

## 1. A small place to make something

Select a separate isolated, attended personal world with the ordinary human, Ada and the Peacock Mercenary. It uses the current clock. The human can make and use a bowl without involving either resident. Participation, assistance, accepting an offered object and later use remain independent choices.

The supplied hearth begins cold on level ground, with clear ordinary approaches. Its nearest wooden panel and loose experimental target begin at least 2 metres away. Its two blank positions and cooling surface are real places for actual material. The cooling surface begins 0.75 metre from the hearth's exposed edge, outside its 0.5-metre heat radius and within ordinary handling reach. A changed destination must be rechecked; another source reaching it prevents the promised cooling. The first wall consists of two adjoining, independently grounded panels; each is 1 metre wide, 1.5 metres high and 0.1 metre thick. They occupy real upright geometry and form a useful divider. There is no upper floor or supported roof.

The initial kit is supplied **once when this prepared profile is created**:

| Actual material | Quantity and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Unfired clay bowl blanks | 2; each contributes 2 packing-load units. |
| Open stone firing hearth | 1, with a fitted reusable cover and adjacent stone cooling surface; firing-only. |
| Substantial wooden panels | 2, already installed in the grounded assembly; each is one recoverable material part, with 6 packing units before damage. |
| Selected small dry twigs | 2 experimental targets; each has 1 packing unit before damage. These are not automatically a full ordinary banked-fuel unit. |
| Finite brief igniters | 4 distinct small source items, each 1 packing unit before use; one reusable striker supplies their supported starting method. |
| Ordinary eligible fuel | 6 current full fuel units, actually transferable and consumed by Add fuel. |
| Ordinary dry prepared fiber | 4 units: lighting supply, two optional wet/dry targets and a spare. Actual use may exhaust a different allocation. |
| Rigid shaft | 1 actual retained tool for current friction lighting; burning it as eligible fuel would sacrifice that tool. |
| Mineral lifting/extinguishing tool | 1 reusable tool for the selected hot withdrawal and extinguishing actions. |

These are new prepared-scene supplies, not a claim about current default inventory. They begin in the human's actual custody or installed under that human's explicit authored use authority. That initial grant includes the listed thermal uses and altering the named two-panel assembly. Restart, return, failed work or resetting an uncommitted layout grants nothing. Visiting the site or offering advice grants no right to consume the kit.

The first resident-making route is the proposed **Allow this firing** control: the human selects one resident, one exact blank and exact fuel/tinder quantities. The grant allows that blank's placement, firing/reheating, hot withdrawal and final collection at this installed hearth using its actual tools. It does not move items, establish title, grant panel damage or transfer automatically with custody. Actual item offers remain independently accepted; the NPC need not use the grant. It ends on cooled-bowl collection, explicit revocation or recipient decline, persists through valid Pause/restart, and has no cooling-time deadline. Revocation stops new spending/unfinished consequential handling but cannot reverse completed physical effects; the current custodian may still extinguish or withdraw the active piece safely. More supplies or another blank require another explicit allocation. General property delegation is not added.

No new carrying-strength limit is introduced. A fired bowl has **8 packing units of capacity and 2 empty packing units**, using current solid-item containment and nesting rules. Its contents contribute their actual loads. It provides no liquids, serving action, fractional meal, preservation, insulation, waterproofing or automatic public contents display. An unfinished blank has no container capacity. Current woven containers remain available alternatives.

## 2. Supported forms and finite burning

The table gives original undamaged allowances. Each object retains its remaining fraction after use. While burning, its own retained warmth stays at its ignition threshold; its stated outward budget is already the transferable part after unmodeled retention and loss.

| Form | Ignition / useful change | Original burning allowance | Transferable output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brief igniter | Starts through the selected striker action; its own warming threshold is 1 unit | 5 game minutes | 1 heat unit/game minute |
| Dry small twig | Ignites at 4 accumulated warming units with an admitted live source | 20 game minutes | 0.5 unit/minute |
| Dry prepared-fiber unit | Ignites at 4 warming units; supported moisture is removed first | 10 game minutes | 0.25 unit/minute |
| Substantial panel | Ignites at 30 warming units with an admitted live source | 60 game minutes | 2 units/minute |
| Unfired / fired bowl | 40 warming units, then 200 irreversible firing units; does not ignite in this family | None | None |
| Supported stone hearth, cover and cooling surface | Noncombustible; the cover/solid obstruction has the expressly supported blocking form | None | None |

A burning twig or fiber gradually loses its real combustible material. Extinguished partial material is retained with that condition; it cannot satisfy a recipe requiring a pristine full plain unit. Exhaustion retires the combustible item through its actual material disposition and leaves one inert ash remnant at its location, with no fuel, container function or supported recipe value. The remnant retains residual warmth until ordinary cooling, has 1 packing unit when collected, and is not immediately made cold by conversion. Multiple residues may only combine through qualified condition-equivalent object operations. They are not new resource rewards.

At **50% original material remaining**, a panel ceases to stand. Its occupied upright section disappears and its remaining material becomes one nonblocking charred pile, 0.05 metre high over the original 1-metre by 0.1-metre ground footprint. Its thermal contact and exposure are recomputed from that low actual material, not inherited from the former upright face; it does not obstruct movement or close an exit. It keeps the remaining 30-minute fuel allowance and current warmth. A neighboring independently grounded panel remains. The pile can continue burning or be extinguished; exhaustion leaves inert ash. This is the small panel family's declared failure, not a heavy-collapse or body-safety model.

A reclaimed damaged part keeps its actual remaining material and condition. Its packing contribution is the original contribution scaled by remaining material, rounded up while a recoverable piece remains. It does not regain strength or pristine ingredient eligibility. Cooling does not repair it. No first method converts charred panels, partially used igniters or small twigs into a fresh one-hour banked-fuel unit. The new heat integration must make consuming actions reject those damaged/part-used instances even if their definition has an eligible property; this is a proposed condition check, not a claim that current plain-material eligibility already validates thermal remainder. Existing unmodified full fuel keeps its current credit.

Cloth ignition, arbitrary wooden posts, decorated trees, food heating, general containers, bodies and animal injury are not admitted thermal targets here. Selected cloth may use the moisture/drying owner without acquiring a combustion rule. Unsupported material response is stated as unsupported; it is not advertised as fireproofing or a valid heat barrier. Ordinary bodies, clothing, carried tools, background terrain and intentionally excluded item classes may approach or pass through this scene without pausing it or becoming heat recipients. Their omitted thermal interactions do not provide a supported shield, cooling sink or heat-storage method. A deliberate unsupported heat experiment is unavailable with a clear reason; ordinary movement is not that experiment. Hold the isolated world only when an already admitted thermal source or material cannot advance coherently, not when an intentionally excluded form happens to be nearby. Wider-world use must qualify the material coverage its promised interactions require.

## 3. One source cannot heat every target at full strength

The open hearth burns actual banked fuel and releases **4 transferable heat units per game minute**. It accepts the current one-game-hour credit per eligible full fuel unit, with a selected maximum bank of **3 game hours**. The existing contained cooking fire retains its own 48-hour cap and no-spread behavior. Its visual flame does not contribute this new outward heat.

For each admitted source, direct contact contributes exposure weight **4**. An exposed nearby material portion within **0.5 metre**, with no supported obstruction, contributes weight **1**. Greater distance contributes none. Divide the source's output across recipients using their weights and a divisor of at least 4; unused output dissipates. Thus one contacting blank receives all the hearth's output; one merely nearby panel receives one quarter if nothing else competes. Two contacting blanks share equally.

Weights describe actual exposed material portions. Splitting a portion divides its original weight; merging does not manufacture more area. Opposite faces do not duplicate the same material or fuel. A large wall uses its actual admitted sections; whole-building membership is not an exposure. Contributions from several sources are combined before changes at the same time are resolved. Actual geometry changes alter exposure when they occur.

A supported solid stone obstruction blocks the relevant direct path. Noncombustibility alone does not establish that result for an unknown form. Contact between adjoining panels can propagate; a gap greater than the declared radius cannot receive this source's heat. Candidate discovery and a connected chain can still involve many pieces. The radius is an authored physical approximation, not a way to ignore expensive eligible targets or a measured workload guarantee.

Ordinary material loses **1 warming unit per game minute only when it is no longer burning and receives no admitted external heat**. Warming values never fall below zero. Active quoted heating is retained arrival; there is no simultaneous one-unit loss cancelling the pilot. Several closely spaced exposures can therefore accumulate. Cooling itself emits no ignition-capable contribution in this family.

## 4. Firing, interruption and collection

One contacting blank first receives 40 warming units and then 200 firing units. Its warming stays at 40 during firing; the additional delivered heat advances that irreversible transformation. At full unshared output this takes **60 game minutes**, including warming. Two equal contacting blanks receive 2 units/minute each and finish after **120 game minutes**. Both initially cold blanks must already be placed before ignition for the exact two-unit fuel result.

A fully warmed clay object loses **2 warming units/minute when no admitted heat remains**, becoming cool after 20 game minutes. Partly warmed clay cools proportionally sooner. Firing progress survives cooling; reheating spends the actual missing warmth before further firing. A finished bowl retains its identity and custody through one actual transformation, with no blank left beside a duplicate output.

The first supported sources cannot overfire this selected clay. Once fired, further heat keeps it hot and unused incoming heat is lost, while the actual source still spends fuel. There is no secret cracking timer. Hot/unfinished, cold/unfinished, hot/fired and cold/fired must be distinguishable. Other ceramics, hotter processes and deliberate breakage are unsupported, not probabilistic outcomes of this method.

| Action | Work and actual result |
| --- | --- |
| Place a cold blank | 30 game seconds; completes the exact transfer into a free admitted position. Cancellation leaves it at source. |
| Withdraw onto the cooling surface | 30 seconds with the mineral tool and an actual clear destination within ordinary reach; condition is retained and heating changes only on completed movement. |
| Move the hearth | 120 seconds; requires it and its contents cool, no active source and a permitted clear destination. The same bank and actual pieces move; no refill. |
| Install or reclaim one panel | 120 seconds under construction permission, cool/nonburning condition and actual valid space; surviving damage remains. |
| Strike/place one brief igniter | 30 seconds with the striker, exact permitted target/contact and actual igniter; cancellation before completion does not start or consume it. Its own finite burning begins at completion. |
| Extinguish the selected material | 30 seconds with the mineral tool and ordinary reach; only the named source/material is affected. |
| Cover the firing hearth | 30 seconds using its actual cover; stops its source and retains unburnt fuel. |

Current Add fuel and Light retain their existing work/consumption behavior on the admitted hearth: 20 and 150 game seconds respectively, with actual supplies spent on valid completion. A cold blank may be positioned first; no banked fuel burns until lighting completes. A lit hearth already spends fuel during approach and placement. Completion exactly when the final sufficient heat exhausts the bank **earns the bowl**; checking “still lit” only after that boundary must not erase completed firing.

Collection uses ordinary actual pickup once cool. Hot bowls cannot be packed, filled or handed over; burning material cannot be dismantled into an inventory escape. Tool withdrawal uses the cooling surface directly. Ending an actor's action does not pause a placed object's thermal history. An unfinished cooled blank can be carried and later resumed with its real progress.

## 5. Stopping combustion and moisture variation

Extinguishing a combustible target ends its combustion, preserves its remaining fuel/material and lowers warming to **one quarter of its ignition threshold**. This reduction is an explicit game loss to the surroundings. Ordinary cooling follows when no external heat remains. A smothered wall therefore has 7.5 warming units to lose, while a twig has 1. A live neighboring source can reheat and reignite them; this is not permanent immunity. The tool is reusable and does not generate water or consumed mineral supplies.

The existing contained campfire and the new firing hearth are plainly different supported places. The first new hearth does not accept Cook or Preserve. It consequently does not override another person's food-work protection. The feature's later combined-fire emergency rule is a future combination requirement, including the exact source/threat check and honest interrupted-food outcome.

The optional moisture comparison uses DG13's single selected moisture amount, from 0 to 1. Full incident rain adds **1/30 per game minute**; zero incident rain allows **1/90 per minute** ambient drying. Its selected shower lasts **20 game minutes**. No ambient drying is simultaneously charged during incident rain. These are the existing selected rules, including actual vertical cover.

New delivered heat spends **30 units per full moisture amount removed** before warming that moist ignition target. With no rain, thermal and ambient contributions reduce the same amount once, stopping at dry; any remaining actual heat then warms the material. A fiber target starting at 2/3 moisture after the full shower receives a five-minute pilot's heat and remains wet: the pilot cannot ignite it. A dry matched fiber reaches its ignition threshold. The required dry twig versus substantial wall comparison is still performed separately.

This adds no current campfire wet-tinder failure, crafting penalty, wet-wood law, dangerous body condition or usable-water output. First-shelter cover and ordinary crafting retain their own useful behavior.

## 6. Practical arithmetic and operating scope

At current 1×, one game minute is one real second. The pilot lasts 5 real seconds and the dry twig reaches its threshold after 4. At 8× those intervals are 0.625 and 0.5 real seconds. Neither is a reading or rescue allowance. The experiment is chosen while cold; the useful bowl has finite fuel and no overfiring deadline. All materially equal outcomes still need accelerated-clock qualification.

An ideal cold-start single bowl needs one real minute of heating plus 20 seconds of cooling. Placement, fuel addition and lighting add 30 + 20 + 150 game seconds, or about 3.33 real seconds at 1×, excluding travel and inspection. Existing hunger over those 83.33 game minutes costs about **15 fullness** per normally progressing person at 259.2/day. Two cold blanks need two fuel units and two minutes of heating before cooling, plus actual setup. These are useful short activities, not free time or an unattended production promise.

With one contacting blank and one nearby panel, weights 4 and 1 share the hearth: the blank receives 3.2 units/minute and the panel 0.8. The panel can reach ignition after 37.5 game minutes if its actual circumstances stay unchanged. Further burning then changes the arrangement and heat contributions; the initial calculation is not a fixed 75-minute completion guarantee for the entire incident. Review actual remaining fuel and material conditions, including those changes.

First qualification exercises the finite supplied material, current three people, repeated positioning, several simultaneous brief sources, both wall sections, partial firing, extinction, altered geometry, ash aftermath and cold reopen. It measures discovery, all actual targets, native changes, perception, independent decision opportunities and storage together. No numeric throughput or population limit is inferred from this inventory.

Offscreen connected play advances normally. Whole-world Pause holds all simulation-time effects. Sole-human departure first reconciles that human's work, then the selected isolated world holds coherently. Restart follows current participation rules without imaginary downtime burning. Mechanical inability to advance the complete scene visibly holds that world; an unavailable optional model does not manufacture a caretaker. MP04 protected carried possessions, public-property policy and later continuing-community qualification remain distinct.
