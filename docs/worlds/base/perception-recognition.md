# Perception, recognition and familiarity — selected world policy

**Selected design; not installed or qualified.** This is the bundled world's policy owner for [module A](../../projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md). [Knowledge](knowledge.md) retains naming, notes and identity ownership; [hearing](../../hearing-and-speech.md) and specialists B/C retain physical sight and acoustics. These values are initial gameplay choices, not measured human psychology, biometric accuracy or a universal engine law. [PP03](../../maintainers/perspective-perception.md#pp03--familiarity-local-individuals-recognition-and-names) owns implementation; the [inventory](../../limits/perspective-perception.md) owns restrictiveness and qualification.

## Installation and ability

Install the recognition policy only for an actor with the supported individual-recognition capability. Physical detection, autobiographical memory, speech, controller type and recognition remain separately declared capabilities. A memoryless creature can receive sound without this policy; a future native familiarity policy can use the same permitted-feature input without human notes or language.

The inspected base at `a3d40b4d71e29d6504e9f3fcc21e76344a0a284e` has no installed Perception attribute and no learned voice/appearance signature. Add an authored numeric **Perception** attribute with range **0–100** and starting value **50** for the selected recognition-capable people, unless their authored profile supplies another admitted value. Its ID, initialization, range, description and applicability belong to the existing base attribute/world creation definitions. It changes no other check, accuracy, hearing threshold or movement rule merely by being installed. Initial value 50 provides a neutral baseline for character variation without making ordinary acquaintances difficult to recognize.

Read the current value through `world-modules.ts:readAttribute`, including `native-values.ts:currentAttributeState`; do not read a potentially unsettled stored scalar. Let `P = value / 100`. Missing required Perception is invalid installation/preparation, not a secret zero or a generic engine fallback. An omitted recognition capability is a supported different outcome. Committed changes to Perception enter once through that attribute owner. Receiver incapacity, blocked senses, masks, low light and acoustic degradation constrain the actual B/C sample. This slice adds no separate condition multiplier, temporary-stat stack, biography-driven bonus or automatic skill progression.

Authored existing acquaintances must provide the appropriate recognition modality and its legitimate starting familiarity through the identity owner, not only a friend's name in biography. Knowing a face does not imply having heard its voice. A world author can explicitly supply both. Such seeding is authored starting knowledge, not a fabricated past event.

## Perceivable signatures

Use small, authored physical recognizability profiles, not names, entity IDs, species IDs, icon URLs, asset filenames, mesh identity or globally unique fingerprints. The profile may be instantiated using the existing saved randomness during authorized character construction, or supplied explicitly by world content. Never derive it from a canonical ID. Profile values are permitted to collide. Record the instantiated values with their owning body/voice definition; rendering an asset cannot create them. Save and restore those exact instantiated values. Construction or an explicit body replacement may instantiate a new profile under its owner; restore, model inference and recollection never rerun construction randomness.

The selected voice family has four equally weighted normalized features: **pitch range, resonance, texture and speech rhythm**, each an integer from 0 through 1000. These are fictional recognition descriptors; they do not promise raw-audio analysis. An emitted sound's physical descriptor reflects its actual delivery and source body. C determines which portions survive propagation and masking, returning an interval for each available feature and acoustic identity fidelity in `[0,1]`. A receives neither unavailable profile dimensions nor the original clean vector as a convenience. Recognizing a recording's voice, if a recording family is later installed, still does not establish the speaker's current presence.

B supplies the corresponding supported appearance family, including actually available distinguishing features and masks. The same matching operation accepts its declared finite dimensions and weights. B must establish what remains observable with a face obscured, at a distance, or in another pose; A never treats clothing, equipment or a scene-specific asset as permanent individual identity. This is a B input dependency, not permission for A to invent visible facial measurements. Until a physical appearance family supplies discriminating evidence, current body tracking may work while cross-episode recognition remains unavailable.

For voice, a feature is **usable** when its received interval width is at most 400 and C supplies it at the original observation. Require at least two usable dimensions to offer a voice candidate and three to recognize one reliably. A completely absent feature never becomes a midpoint guess. For a learned prototype and current sample, compare only mutually usable dimensions. The dimension distance is the absolute difference between their interval midpoints divided by 1000; `D` is the weighted mean, in `[0,1]`. The width of uncertain observations additionally limits quality below, so a broad interval overlapping many voices does not become a perfect identification.

For a current voice sample, define:

- `audibility = clamp((clarityMarginDb - 3) / 12, 0, 1)` from the actual received signal and background/floor, not source loudness.
- `resolution = mean(1 - receivedFeatureWidth / 1000)` over its usable dimensions.
- `Q = min(audibility, resolution, C's identityFidelity)`.
- For matching one remembered exemplar, `Q_pair = min(Q_current, Q_exemplar, currentResolutionOnSharedFeatures, exemplarResolutionOnSharedFeatures)`. `Q_exemplar` is its original acquisition quality, not a recomputation from today's body or hearing. Use `Q_pair` for candidate and recognition admission; use the current sample's own `Q` for learning admission. Keep both original feature intervals; do not narrow or average them.

The 3/15 dB endpoints are a recognition-quality mapping, not changes to the existing detect/partial/clear word thresholds. Speech can be audible or intelligible while voice recognition is unavailable. C must supply a defensible identity-fidelity value from its supported physical family; copying `1` after timbre has been removed is invalid. The current direct-path model can declare preserved timbre for its supported cases, while unsupported transformations remain unavailable rather than guessed. Future C changes reconcile this mapping through this owner; they must not duplicate it in a renderer or speech formatter.

## Learning and familiarity

A first unfamiliar voice creates an anonymous auditory exposure. It does not select a hidden person's existing identity record. A stable learned-individual record can subsequently describe **this distinguishable voice**, even without a visible body or known name. It remains an observer-local subject through the existing recognition and knowledge owners, not an entity spawned into the world.

A qualifying learning sample needs `Q >= 0.55`, at least three usable dimensions and eligible original physical support. A discrete utterance contributes at most one sample. A genuinely continuous physical exposure may contribute one sample per non-overlapping **two normal-speed seconds** of supplied usable signal, anchored at that emission's first qualifying reception and reset on a disqualifying gap. Token streaming, string length, rendering and server polling do not invent voiced duration. The current instantaneous speech owner therefore contributes discrete samples, not an estimated speaking interval.

Distinguish four identities. The **learning root** is the original physical utterance or recorded exemplar; it deduplicates retransmission and playback without revealing its producer. The **sample key** is that root plus the original discrete sample or continuous interval ordinal. The **credit key** is the sample key within this observer's modality. The **exposure encounter** is the observer's acquired episode under the existing exposure owner. Two qualifying disjoint intervals from one genuine continuous source may establish a learned voice, but the encounter still contributes at most two credits. A recording retains its original root and interval ordinals across playbacks. A server/client reading of stored history supplies no physical sample at all.

To establish an unbound learned voice, compare the second qualifying sample with eligible anonymous exemplars from the preceding **30 normal-speed seconds**. This seed window is separate from live exposure grace. Compare only received features, using at least three shared dimensions, `Q_pair >= 0.55` and `D <= 0.10`; require the next distinct compatible seed cluster to be at least `0.08` farther away. Reduce samples belonging to an already supported perceptual cluster before the competitor check. Two genuinely continuous samples can supply that continuity directly; separated utterances use this conservative feature match. Neither path consults hidden emitter equality. A tie, a concurrently competing indistinguishable producer or incomplete enumeration leaves the new sample anonymous. No sample outside the seed window is resurrected automatically by a history query.

The resulting learned subject means **a distinguishable voice pattern this observer associates across those samples**. It does not prove one physical producer, unique personhood or a canonical identity. An unseen imitator or identical-sounding unknown stranger may be misassociated; preserve that fallibility and support so later evidence can split the association. This selected short-history matching lets ordinary conversational pauses support familiarity without requiring two instantaneous utterances within the live exposure grace. If an independent, unambiguous sight-and-voice association already establishes the individual, one qualifying voice sample may be stored against it, but later voice-only recognition still needs two samples or explicitly authored voice familiarity.

Each eligible sample contributes one credit, at most **two credits per exposure encounter** and **12 retained credits per modality/individual**. A learned voice has at least two credits; six credits from at least three eligible encounters reach the familiar plateau. Let `F = min(credits / 6, 1)` after the minimum sample rule. Credit and prototype supports are the same bounded set, not separate unbounded lists. Additional capacity preserves support diversity rather than raising the plateau indefinitely.

Later voice-only recognition may add a qualifying sample after the pre-learning match succeeds, retaining both that original observation and the prior association/exemplar references as support. This is fallible learning, not independent corroboration of who physically spoke. A tentative match cannot add familiarity or enlarge a prototype. Independently supported cross-sense or continuous-source association can also admit a sample. Store original interpretation inputs, including Perception, definition pin and the selected prior exemplar; later ability, profile or policy changes cannot replay the old sample into a stronger original result.

For credit retention, sort eligible samples by descending original `Q`, then descending usable dimension count, then ascending original acquisition time, occurrence/interval order and observer-local sample key. First greedily select the highest-ranked sample from each distinct encounter until the 12 slots fill or encounters run out. Then fill remaining slots in that same order, skipping any encounter already represented twice. A deterministic tie never uses a private producer identity. Apply this operation to the current retained set plus new admitted samples; an evicted sample is not silently reloaded later to improve a score. This bounds native sufficient support without deleting the observer's historical account.

Retain at most **four exact exemplars per modality/individual**, selected from those same retained credit samples. Do not average, intersect, union or otherwise synthesize their feature intervals. Select greedily by largest count of usable dimensions not yet covered by selected exemplars, then highest original `Q`, then largest usable dimension count, then the original acquisition/order/key tie-break above. Continue after all dimensions are covered using those remaining tie-breaks. Each exemplar references one retained sample; deleting its only support retires it. This deliberately favors high-quality supported exemplars over an unbounded catalogue of costumes or voice changes.

Episode identity is not sufficient proof of independence. Original sample/root receipts, observed continuity and acquisition lineage prevent duplicates, correction publication, retellings, recall and summaries from adding credits. Deliberately leaving and returning cannot replenish the allowance of a still-continuing exposure. A repeated physical recording can be heard again but cannot re-earn its already acquired learning samples. No source gets extra credit from being both a raw observation and a summary leaf.

Correction/forgetting invalidates affected credits and every derived association that depends on them, then recomputes the selected retained exemplars and familiarity from eligible support. Source compaction preserves sufficient typed support; it does not fake a new learning event. The first implementation may conservatively clear more affected learned recognition through the existing invalidation owner while finer dependency work proceeds. It cannot fill gaps from a source's current body. Native records retain bounded exact support references, not recursively embedded histories; existing personal-source lineage and invalidation own the reachable dependency history.

No passive calendar decay or fixed lifetime expiry is selected for learned individual recognition. The short exposure window, raw-recall window and an animal's sensory retention are not acquaintance expiry. Explicit forgetting/correction and supported loss of the learned profile still matter. Broader natural memory decay remains ND19 work.

## Matching and association

Match only against this observer's eligible learned prototypes of the supplied modality, selected by the conservative permitted-feature index. Do not use the physical source ID, global name or hidden life to choose the candidate. Evaluate against the pre-learning record set; the current sample cannot create its own prior familiarity and then prove its own identification in the same transition.

1. Evaluate every eligible exemplar against the current received sample. Candidate admission requires `Q_pair >= 0.20`, at least two mutually usable features and `D <= 0.30`. Reduce candidate results to each distinct learned individual's smallest eligible distance.
2. Evaluate recognition eligibility separately. Require a learned voice, at least three mutually usable features, `Q_pair >= 0.70 - 0.20P - 0.15F`, and `D <= 0.08 + 0.04P + 0.04F`. Reduce to each individual's smallest distance among exemplars satisfying these recognition-stage criteria. A two-feature exemplar cannot suppress that same individual's valid three-feature exemplar. Rank recognition-eligible individuals by that distance, then higher pair quality, then observer-local key.
3. For the best recognition-eligible individual, every **other learned individual's** best candidate-stage distance must satisfy `otherDistance - winnerDistance >= 0.08`. No competing candidate satisfies this condition by absence only after complete enumeration. An individual's other exemplars are never competitors against itself. An exact tie remains ambiguous even with maximum Perception and familiarity. A truncated page cannot prove uniqueness. Use inclusive quality/feature-width/distance thresholds and the inclusive minimum competitor gap throughout.
4. If reliable criteria fail but candidates exist, retain a tentative association, including up to the two best tied/near candidates for ordinary presentation and an explicit more-candidates indicator when appropriate. No tentative candidate authorizes canonical subject binding, body targeting or automatic merging of private notes.
5. If no candidate qualifies, preserve the detected sound and other permitted detail without an individual association. Weak/absent recognition is not failed hearing.

At `P = 0.5`, a two-credit learned voice requires `Q_pair >= 0.55` and `D <= 0.1133…`; a familiar voice at six credits requires `Q_pair >= 0.45` and `D <= 0.14`. Keep the exact algebra in execution; these displayed values are explanatory, not rounded admission thresholds. Familiarity and perceptiveness can recover a noisy or changed voice. The competitor margin prevents a forced winner among competing learned individuals; it does not establish uniqueness among all world beings. Successful recognition is this observer's supported but fallible association, not proof that the actual emitter is that individual and not a current-body capability. These are authored scores and thresholds, not calibrated probabilities.

An already recognized visual person can be matched in a different place through the same supported feature policy. Place, clothing, activity, companions and carried items may support an explicitly tentative contextual inference but cannot replace missing individual evidence. A recognized person's new voice observation updates that voice fact and its supported association only. It does not refresh equipment, location or unseen activity.

## Exposure and weak evidence

Use B/C's completed physical evaluation. An immediate detection opportunity is acquired at its actual native boundary with no deliberate delay. A's weak-evidence integrator consumes a supplied nonnegative detection-support rate or impulse under the pinned detector policy. B/C owns the physical mapping into that support, including receiver capability, material, light and signal strength. Existing current sight and ordinary detected speech remain immediate until their respective specialist laws deliberately select a weak path.

For the selected accumulating family, threshold and maximum are **one support unit**. A continuous full-strength rate supplies one unit per **0.25 normal-speed seconds**; a weaker rate scales this linearly. A discrete weak impulse contributes its supplied fraction once. During an unsupported gap, support decays linearly to zero over **0.5 normal-speed seconds** from a full unit. Integrate disjoint supported intervals and solve the first crossing exactly within the supplied interval; never apply a whole future chunk before its threshold time. Store progress, through-time, detector pin and consumed original-source identity through the episode owner. B/C may split a sample at a physical discontinuity; publishing it more often does not change the integral.

Weak cues combine only with an observed continuity justification: intersecting permitted direction cones/regions anchored in the same world frame, a supported continuous interval or successive impulses no farther apart than the **0.5 normal-speed second** decay interval, and no contradictory available feature or independently observed competing producer. A zero-rate interval is an unsupported gap and decays; its mere presence cannot hold support steady. Missing identity features allow an anonymous regional pattern to accumulate, not an inference that a single person produced it. A hidden shared emitter ID is insufficient. If several candidate patterns are equally compatible, acquire a broad regional pattern without fusing their individual claims; never copy the same physical contribution into multiple sums used to cross the threshold. Losing the correlation justification ends that accumulated hypothesis. Subthreshold state is not player or model knowledge.

An episode may reconnect for **0.5 normal-speed seconds** after the actual last supported observation; this is 30 simulation seconds under the current 60:1 clock. Anchor the grace to actual sensory loss, not to whether a throttled arrival/departure story row happened to be written. Any return during the supported grace preserves the episode but authorizes only newly supported current facets. This replaces the current recorded-departure-dependent grace for the selected episode path while retaining its initial effective duration. Narrative encounter quotas remain separately owned and do not control recognition.

Actual loss immediately removes live body/detail authorization. The grace preserves correlation only, and remembered identity has its separate lifetime. A heard impulse is historical after its physical reception ends even if G keeps its permitted cue readable briefly. User display duration cannot prolong hearing or current source activity.

## Names, source kinds and danger

An observer may give a learned individual a private label under existing name-edit limits. A heard self-introduction records the actually understood name as a **name claim** associated only as far as the voice/body evidence supports. Knowing or choosing a name does not improve recognition, prove a claimed identity, rename the subject globally, or attach a second stranger to that person's notes. Duplicate names remain distinct.

The ordinary native classifier can label an acoustic pattern as footstep-like or rustling using the installed physical family. Such labels are inferred source kinds, not proof that a person exists. A direct observed person, an uncertain footstep classification and a reported danger are different records. The selected world does not require a model request for ordinary sensory classification.

A received warning can support a belief that an area is dangerous without identifying the danger. Whether to trust it, investigate or prepare belongs to existing knowledge, attention and encounter owners. A does not assign fear, hostility or surprise. False testimony remains attributed even when several people repeat it; absent a supported transmission link, the listener does not magically know that two accounts share an origin.
