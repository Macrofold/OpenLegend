# Perception, recognition and familiarity — selected world policy

**Selected design; not installed or qualified.** This is the bundled world's policy owner for [module A](../../projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md). [Knowledge](knowledge.md) retains naming, notes and identity ownership; [hearing](../../hearing-and-speech.md) and specialists B/C retain physical sight and acoustics. These values are initial gameplay choices, not measured human psychology, biometric accuracy or a universal engine law. [PP03](../../maintainers/perspective-perception.md#pp03--familiarity-local-individuals-recognition-and-names) owns implementation; the [inventory](../../limits/perspective-perception.md) owns restrictiveness and qualification.

## Installation and ability

Install the recognition policy only for an actor with the supported individual-recognition capability. Physical detection, autobiographical memory, speech, controller type and recognition remain separately declared capabilities. A memoryless creature can receive sound without this policy; a future native familiarity policy can use the same permitted-feature input without human notes or language.

The inspected base at `a3d40b4d71e29d6504e9f3fcc21e76344a0a284e` has no installed Perception attribute and no learned voice/appearance signature. The integrated policy adds one authored numeric **Perception** attribute with range **0–100** and starting value **50**, unless the authored profile supplies another admitted value. Install it for selected people whose visual or individual-recognition policy requires it, independently of autobiographical memory. Its ID, initialization, range and applicability belong to the existing base attribute/world creation definitions. [Vision](vision-and-gaze.md#ownership-and-units) uses it for detection and detail; this policy uses it for recognition. Hearing thresholds, movement and combat accuracy do not acquire an additional Perception modifier merely because it exists. Nonhuman and machine visual fixtures explicitly select gain 1 without this attribute.

Read the settled `perceptionValue` through `world-modules.ts:readAttribute`, including `native-values.ts:currentAttributeState`; do not read an unsettled stored scalar. Let `p = perceptionValue / 100`, in `[0,1]`. All recognition formulas below use `p`; B's visual gain is `0.75 + 0.5p`. Missing required Perception is invalid installation/preparation, not a secret zero or generic fallback. Omitting recognition is supported and does not remove an otherwise installed physical sense or its required attribute. Committed changes enter once through the existing attribute/condition owner. Receiver incapacity, blocked senses, masks, low light and acoustic degradation constrain B/C's actual sample. This design adds no separate condition stack, biography bonus or automatic progression.

Authored existing acquaintances must provide the appropriate recognition modality and its legitimate starting familiarity through the identity owner, not only a friend's name in biography. Knowing a face does not imply having heard its voice. A world author can explicitly supply both. PP03 must audit and author the current starting acquaintances' intended modalities; an old acquaintance/name entry alone does not install a voice profile. Such seeding is authored starting knowledge, not a fabricated past event.

## Perceivable signatures

Use small, authored physical recognizability profiles, not names, entity IDs, species IDs, icon URLs, asset filenames, mesh identity or globally unique fingerprints. The profile may be instantiated using the existing saved randomness during authorized character construction, or supplied explicitly by world content. Never derive it from a canonical ID. Profile values are permitted to collide. Record the instantiated values with their owning body/voice definition; rendering an asset cannot create them. Save and restore those exact instantiated values. Construction or an explicit body replacement may instantiate a new profile under its owner; restore, model inference and recollection never rerun construction randomness.

The selected voice family has four equally weighted normalized features: **pitch range, resonance, texture and speech rhythm**, each an integer from 0 through 1000. These are fictional recognition descriptors; they do not promise raw-audio analysis. An emitted sound's physical descriptor reflects its actual delivery and source body. C determines which portions survive propagation and masking, returning an interval for each available feature and acoustic identity fidelity in `[0,1]`. A receives neither unavailable profile dimensions nor the original clean vector as a convenience. Recognizing a recording's voice, if a recording family is later installed, still does not establish the speaker's current presence.

B supplies the supported appearance family's finite distinguishing features, physical ranges, normalization, positive weights, masks and received identity fidelity in `[0,1]`. Its admitted intervals map to the same normalized 0–1000 comparison scale; the family needs at least three identity dimensions to support reliable recognition. B establishes what remains observable with a face obscured, at a distance, or in another pose; A applies the learning and matching rules below. Clothing, equipment and scene-specific assets are not permanent individual identity. Until B supplies discriminating physical evidence, current body tracking may work while cross-episode recognition remains unavailable.

For either modality, a feature is **usable** when its normalized interval width is at most 400 and it was supplied by B/C at the original observation or explicitly admitted in the authored modality seed below. Current samples require actual received features. Require at least two mutually usable dimensions to offer a candidate and three to recognize reliably. A completely absent feature never becomes a midpoint guess. Compare only mutually usable dimensions; each distance is the absolute difference between interval midpoints divided by 1000, and `D` is their weighted mean in `[0,1]`. Uncertain interval width also limits quality, so a broad interval does not become a perfect identification.

For a current sample, define:

- `audibility = clamp((clarityMarginDb - 3) / 12, 0, 1)` from the actual received signal and background/floor, not source loudness.
- `resolution = mean(1 - receivedFeatureWidth / 1000)` over its usable dimensions.
- `Q_voice = min(audibility, resolution, C's identityFidelity)`; `Q_sight = min(resolution, B's identityFidelity)`. Use the sample modality's value as `Q`; sight has no acoustic term.
- For matching one remembered exemplar, `Q_pair = min(Q_current, Q_exemplar, currentResolutionOnSharedFeatures, exemplarResolutionOnSharedFeatures)`. `Q_exemplar` is its original acquisition quality or explicitly authored seed quality, not a recomputation from today's body or hearing. Use `Q_pair` for candidate and recognition admission; use the current sample's own `Q` for learning admission. Keep both original feature intervals; do not narrow or average them.

The 3/15 dB endpoints are a recognition-quality mapping, not changes to the existing detect/partial/clear word thresholds. Speech can be audible or intelligible while voice recognition is unavailable. Conversely, the selected acceptance route for a recognized voice without understood words uses a preserved-timbre sample with unavailable lexical comprehension, such as an unfamiliar language or a supported nonverbal call. Under the current margin-only speech bands, margins below 6 dB give this quality mapping less than 0.25 and do not pass reliable recognition, even at maximum ability/familiarity. Do not claim that low-margin path already supports the converse. C/SPK can later supply a supported transformation that preserves identity features while independently losing words; adopting it requires its actual physical/lexical evidence, not changing A's result after seeing a speaker ID.

C must supply a defensible identity-fidelity value from its supported physical family; copying `1` after timbre has been removed is invalid. The current direct-path model can declare preserved timbre for its supported cases, while unsupported transformations remain unavailable rather than guessed. Future C changes reconcile this mapping through this owner; they must not duplicate it in a renderer or speech formatter.

## Learning and familiarity

A first unfamiliar voice creates an anonymous auditory exposure. It does not select a hidden person's existing identity record. A stable learned-individual record can subsequently describe **this distinguishable voice**, even without a visible body or known name. It remains an observer-local subject through the existing recognition and knowledge owners, not an entity spawned into the world.

A qualifying learning sample of either modality needs `Q >= 0.55`, at least three usable dimensions and eligible original physical support. For voice, a discrete utterance contributes at most one sample. A genuinely continuous acoustic exposure may contribute one sample per non-overlapping **two normal-speed seconds** of supplied usable signal, anchored at that emission's first qualifying reception and reset on a disqualifying gap. Token streaming, string length, rendering and server polling do not invent voiced duration. The current instantaneous speech owner therefore contributes discrete samples, not an estimated speaking interval.

For sight, one qualifying sample unambiguously associated with a currently perceived body establishes a learned visual pattern after the pre-learning recognition result is fixed. Reuse an individual only through eligible recognition, perceptual continuity or cross-sense proof; otherwise create a fresh observer-local individual. The body's private canonical ID cannot select an older identity after failed or ambiguous recognition. Publish a separate supported `visual-learning` association citing the direct body detection, the actual visual sample, their same observer/life and supported correspondence, and the installed policy. This grants one actual credit, not two, and does not rerun the current match or grant a name, canonical association or continuing body target. A useful brief glimpse can therefore be remembered without a required stare. A sample without unambiguous body correspondence remains detection/features or a contextual hypothesis; this slice selects no anonymous visual seed-clustering route.

Subsequent visual learning uses the eligible individual association and the same two-normal-second credit deadline. A dynamic exposure waits for the next actual canonical B measurement at or after that deadline, within B’s declared cell period; a dependency-proved constant-feature interval can cover it directly. No new sensing timer is introduced. Qualifying features/quality remain required. Held samples, renderer polls, elapsed time and replay produce no credit. A failed/ambiguous match cannot train a guessed prior individual, while current physical tracking remains separate.

Distinguish four identities. The **learning root** is the original physical utterance/recorded exemplar or observer-local visual exposure contribution; it deduplicates retransmission and playback without identifying a producer. The **sample key** is that root plus the original discrete measurement or actually supported continuous interval ordinal, never a polling counter. Visual intervals use the existing exposure's consumed prefix and pinned credit boundaries. The **credit key** is the sample key within this observer's modality. The **exposure encounter** supplies support diversity, not a compulsory meeting count. Two qualifying disjoint acoustic intervals may establish a learned voice. A recording retains its original root and interval ordinals across playbacks. Reading stored history supplies no physical sample.

To establish a learned voice, compare the new qualifying sample with eligible seed exemplars from the preceding **30 normal-speed seconds**. A seed belongs either to an anonymous group or to an independently admitted individual whose voice has only one qualifying sample. This seed window is separate from live exposure grace. Seed samples group only through an already supported perceptual continuity proof; feature similarity alone does not transitively fuse seed groups. Compare only received features, using at least three shared dimensions and `Q_pair >= 0.55`. Enumerate potential competitors within `D <= 0.30`; the best seed group must additionally satisfy `D <= 0.10`, and every other seed group or eligible learned-individual candidate must be at least `0.08` farther away. Reduce each group's exemplars to its best eligible distance before comparing distinct groups or individuals. A subject-bound seed and a candidate already supported as that same individual are one competitor, not competitors against themselves. The two credited samples must be at least **two normal-speed seconds** apart. Two genuinely continuous samples can supply that continuity directly; separated utterances use this conservative feature match. Neither path consults hidden emitter equality. A tie, an observed competing indistinguishable producer or incomplete enumeration leaves the new sample without a new individual association. No sample outside the seed window is resurrected automatically by a history query.

If the current sample has a unique conservative seed match but arrives less than two normal-speed seconds after its earliest eligible seed, retain that earliest seed and the current heard fact with only a tentative correlation. Do not create a competing seed group solely because this equivalent sample is too early, and do not bank it for later credit. With otherwise equivalent qualifying utterances at `t = 0`, `1` and `2` normal-speed seconds, the third can promote from the first; the middle utterance neither blocks promotion nor earns credit. Independently observed distinct producers remain distinct competitors, and a tentative correlation never transitively joins their groups.

After the pre-learning recognition result is fixed, a successful two-sample promotion publishes a **separate supported learned-voice association**, citing both original samples and the seed-match inputs. Allocate one observer-local individual for an unbound winning group; when the first sample was independently associated through sight or another admitted proof, attach the learned modality to that existing permitted subject and retain that proof. A second visible meeting is unnecessary. The association is immediately usable for permitted local-subject recollection and notes, without rerunning the current sample against its newly added credits. It grants no new canonical identity, name or physical body permission. Remove the consumed seeds from anonymous candidacy; other groups remain separate. When pre-learning recognition already succeeded for a learned voice, use the later-learning route below instead of duplicate promotion.

The resulting learned subject means **a distinguishable voice pattern this observer associates across those samples**. It does not prove one physical producer, unique personhood or a canonical identity. An unseen imitator or identical-sounding unknown stranger may be misassociated; preserve that fallibility and support so later evidence can split the association. This selected short-history matching lets ordinary conversational pauses support familiarity without requiring two instantaneous utterances within the live exposure grace. If an independent, unambiguous sight-and-voice association already establishes the individual, one qualifying voice sample may be stored against it. Later voice-only input may complete the same two-sample seed route while its original seed remains eligible; reliable pre-learning voice recognition still requires an already learned voice or explicit authored voice familiarity.

Each eligible actual sample contributes one credit, at most **one new credit per two normal-speed seconds per perceived individual/modality**, with **12 retained actual credits per modality/individual**. The first qualifying visual sample earns its credit immediately; voice promotion retains its two original credited times. A learned profile needs at least **one** eligible actual sample for sight or **two** for voice, or an eligible authored modality seed below; six actual credits reach the familiar plateau. Let `authoredEquivalent` be the eligible authored value (`0`, the modality's learned minimum, or `6`) and `actualCredits` the count of retained eligible actual credits. Use `F = min(1, max(authoredEquivalent, actualCredits) / 6)` after that modality's learned minimum; authored equivalence is never added to actual credits. Later admission requires a new eligible original sample at least two normal-speed seconds after the last actually credited sample. No unused allowance accumulates, an early sample is not banked for later credit, and leaving/returning does not reset this per-individual deadline. Ordinary conversation or shared activity can reach familiarity without artificial repeated visits. Actual credits and observed prototype supports use the same bounded set; additional capacity preserves support diversity rather than raising the plateau indefinitely.

Later voice-only recognition may add a qualifying sample after the pre-learning match succeeds, retaining both that original observation and the prior association/exemplar references as support. This is fallible learning, not independent corroboration of who physically spoke. Outside the explicit two-sample seed-promotion rule above, a tentative match cannot add familiarity or enlarge a prototype. Independently supported cross-sense or continuous-source association can also admit a sample. Store original interpretation inputs, including Perception, definition pin and the selected prior exemplar; later ability, profile or policy changes cannot replay the old sample into a stronger original result.

For actual-credit retention, sort eligible samples by descending original `Q`, then descending usable dimension count, then ascending original acquisition time, occurrence/interval order and observer-local sample key. First greedily select the highest-ranked sample from each distinct encounter until the 12 slots fill or encounters run out. Then fill remaining slots in that same order without an encounter quota. A deterministic tie never uses a private producer identity. Apply this operation to the current retained set plus new admitted samples; an evicted sample is not silently reloaded later to improve a score. Persist the admitted last-credit time and next eligible deadline separately from the selected support list, including through exemplar/support eviction and restoration, so replacement cannot bypass the learning rate. This bounds native sufficient support without deleting the observer's historical account.

An **authored modality seed** contains its exact installation source/definition pin, permitted subject, modality, `installedAt`, stable installation order, declared baseline `learned` (equivalent **1** for sight or **2** for voice) or `familiar` (equivalent **6**), and at most **four** explicitly supplied exact exemplar feature-interval records with declared quality `Q` and stable exemplar ordinals. Either modality requires at least one exemplar with three usable dimensions and `Q >= 0.55`; every supplied `Q` is finite in `[0,1]` and no greater than its interval resolution. Its features must be admitted by that modality's pinned family. These are authorized starting-memory descriptors, not observations generated from a current hidden body. Do not invent sensory samples, encounters, acquisition times, learning roots or historical physical measurements to justify them. `installedAt` is their installation time only. An authored equivalent affects familiarity while its installation/knowledge authority remains eligible; it is not a bag of synthetic learning credits. Missing or revoked modality state supplies `authoredEquivalent = 0`, and a name or unrelated authored acquaintance cannot replace it. An actual sample learned through authored recognition retains that association as dependency and follows the ordinary two-second actual-credit rule; installation creates no fictitious last-credit deadline.

Select at most **four matching exact exemplars per modality/individual** from the union of eligible authored candidates and the **12** retained actual credit samples. There are at most four authored candidates in addition to those actual credit records, not four extra selected matching exemplars. Do not average, intersect, union or otherwise synthesize feature intervals. Select greedily by largest count of usable dimensions not yet covered by selected exemplars, then highest original observed or declared authored `Q`, then largest usable dimension count. Break remaining ties by ascending actual acquisition time or authored `installedAt`; at an equal time use authored before observed, then that origin's stable installation/exemplar order or actual occurrence/interval order, and finally its observer-local record key. This source-kind tie order is deterministic selection, not evidence priority or a fictional sensory time. Continue after all dimensions are covered using those remaining tie-breaks. Each exemplar refers to one retained actual sample or authored installation record; deleting its only eligible support retires it. This deliberately favors high-quality supported exemplars over an unbounded catalogue of costumes or voice changes.

Episode identity is not sufficient proof of independence. Original sample/root receipts, observed continuity and acquisition lineage prevent duplicates, correction publication, retellings, recall and summaries from adding credits. A repeated physical recording can be heard again but cannot re-earn its already acquired learning samples. No source gets extra credit from being both a raw observation and a summary leaf. These receipts remain valid even when their corresponding exemplar is evicted; otherwise playing the same recording after replacement could replenish familiarity. Resolve them through the existing personal/native source lineage and its prepared eligibility index, not a second unbounded global or per-observer seen-root store. When a future recording adapter permits arbitrarily old roots, it must prepare that exact eligibility before required learning; missing preparation does not mean unseen.

Correction/forgetting invalidates affected credits and every derived association that depends on them, then recomputes the selected retained exemplars and familiarity from eligible support. Authored installation records obey the same current forgetting/revocation overlay under their explicit owner; preserve those revocations through save/restore, and do not reinstall forgotten starting knowledge merely because a default acquaintance remains in world content. Source compaction preserves sufficient typed support; it does not fake a new learning event. The first implementation may conservatively clear more affected learned recognition through the existing invalidation owner while finer dependency work proceeds. It cannot fill gaps from a source's current body. Native records retain bounded exact support references, not recursively embedded histories; existing personal-source lineage and invalidation own the reachable dependency history.

No passive calendar decay or fixed lifetime expiry is selected for learned individual recognition. The short exposure window, raw-recall window and an animal's sensory retention are not acquaintance expiry. Explicit forgetting/correction and supported loss of the learned profile still matter. Broader natural memory decay remains ND19 work.

## Matching and association

Match only against this observer's eligible learned prototypes of the supplied modality, selected by the conservative permitted-feature index. Do not use the physical source ID, global name or hidden life to choose the candidate. Evaluate against the pre-learning record set; the current sample cannot create its own prior familiarity and then prove its own identification in the same transition.

1. Evaluate every eligible exemplar against the current received sample. Candidate admission requires `Q_pair >= 0.20`, at least two mutually usable features and `D <= 0.30`. Reduce candidate results to each distinct learned individual's smallest eligible distance.
2. Evaluate recognition eligibility separately. Require a learned profile of this modality, at least three mutually usable features, `Q_pair >= 0.70 - 0.20p - 0.15F`, and `D <= 0.08 + 0.04p + 0.04F`. Reduce to each individual's smallest distance among exemplars satisfying these recognition-stage criteria. A two-feature exemplar cannot suppress that same individual's valid three-feature exemplar. Rank recognition-eligible individuals by that distance, then higher pair quality, then observer-local key.
3. For the best recognition-eligible individual, every **other learned individual's** best candidate-stage distance must satisfy `otherDistance - winnerDistance >= 0.08`. The condition is satisfied without a competing candidate only after enumeration is complete. An individual's other exemplars are never competitors against itself. An exact tie remains ambiguous even with maximum Perception and familiarity. A truncated page cannot prove uniqueness. Use inclusive quality/feature-width/distance thresholds and the inclusive minimum competitor gap throughout.
4. If reliable criteria fail but candidates exist, retain a tentative association, including up to the two best tied/near candidates for ordinary presentation and an explicit more-candidates indicator when appropriate. No tentative candidate authorizes canonical subject binding, body targeting or automatic merging of private notes.
5. If no candidate qualifies, preserve detection and other permitted detail without a prior-individual association. Weak/absent recognition is not failed sensing; the separate learning rules may establish a new local pattern after this result is fixed.

At `p = 0.5`, a two-credit learned voice requires `Q_pair >= 0.55` and `D <= 0.1133…`; a familiar voice at six credits requires `Q_pair >= 0.45` and `D <= 0.14`. Keep the exact algebra in execution; these displayed values are explanatory, not rounded admission thresholds. Familiarity and perceptiveness can recover a noisy or changed voice. The competitor margin prevents a forced winner among competing learned individuals; it does not establish uniqueness among all world beings. Successful recognition is this observer's supported but fallible association, not proof that the actual emitter is that individual and not a current-body capability. These are authored scores and thresholds, not calibrated probabilities.

An already recognized visual person can be matched in a different place through the same supported feature policy. Place, clothing, activity, companions and carried items may support an explicitly tentative contextual inference but cannot replace missing individual evidence. A recognized person's new voice observation updates that voice fact and its supported association only. It does not refresh equipment, location or unseen activity.

## Exposure and weak evidence

Use B/C’s completed physical evaluation at its declared fidelity. B holds canonical visual samples and C holds its sampled acoustic anchors; these provider-defined spans are not proof of continuously measured geometry. Keep value measurement, actual acquisition and held availability distinct. An immediate detection opportunity is acquired at its actual native boundary with no deliberate delay. A's weak-evidence integrator consumes a supplied nonnegative detection-support rate or impulse under the pinned detector policy. B/C owns the physical mapping into that support, including receiver capability, material, light and signal strength. Existing current sight and ordinary detected speech remain immediate until their respective specialist laws deliberately select a weak path.

For the selected accumulating family, threshold and maximum are **one support unit**. A continuous full-strength rate supplies one unit per **0.25 normal-speed seconds**; a weaker rate scales this linearly. A discrete weak impulse contributes its supplied fraction once. During an unsupported gap, support decays linearly to zero over **0.5 normal-speed seconds** from a full unit. Integrate disjoint supported intervals and solve the first crossing exactly within the supplied interval; never apply a whole future chunk before its threshold time. Store progress, through-time, detector pin and consumed original-source identity through the episode owner. B/C may split a sample at a physical discontinuity; publishing it more often does not change the integral.

Weak cues combine only with an observed continuity justification: intersecting permitted direction cones/regions anchored in the same world frame, a supported continuous interval or successive impulses no farther apart than the **0.5 normal-speed second** decay interval, and no contradictory available feature or independently observed competing producer. A zero-rate interval is an unsupported gap and decays; its mere presence cannot hold support steady. Missing identity features allow an anonymous regional pattern to accumulate, not an inference that a single person produced it. A hidden shared emitter ID is insufficient. If several candidate patterns are equally compatible, acquire a broad regional pattern without fusing their individual claims; never copy the same physical contribution into multiple sums used to cross the threshold. Losing the correlation justification ends that accumulated hypothesis. Subthreshold state is not player or model knowledge.

An episode may reconnect for **0.5 normal-speed seconds** after the actual last supported observation; this is 30 simulation seconds under the current 60:1 clock. Anchor the grace to actual sensory loss, not to whether a throttled arrival/departure story row happened to be written. Any return during the supported grace preserves the episode but authorizes only newly supported current facets. This replaces the current recorded-departure-dependent grace for the selected episode path while retaining its initial effective duration. Narrative encounter quotas remain separately owned and do not control recognition.

Actual loss immediately removes live body/detail authorization. The grace preserves correlation only, and remembered identity has its separate lifetime. A heard impulse is historical after its physical reception ends even if G keeps its permitted cue readable briefly. User display duration cannot prolong hearing or current source activity.

## Names, source kinds and danger

An observer may give a learned individual a private label under existing name-edit limits. A heard self-introduction records the actually understood name as a **name claim** associated only as far as the voice/body evidence supports. Knowing or choosing a name does not improve recognition, prove a claimed identity, rename the subject globally, or attach a second stranger to that person's notes. Duplicate names remain distinct.

The ordinary native classifier can label an acoustic pattern as footstep-like or rustling using the installed physical family. Such labels are inferred source kinds, not proof that a person exists. A direct observed person, an uncertain footstep classification and a reported danger are different records. The selected world does not require a model request for ordinary sensory classification.

A received warning can support a belief that an area is dangerous without identifying the danger. Whether to trust it, investigate or prepare belongs to existing knowledge, attention and encounter owners. A does not assign fear, hostility or surprise. False testimony remains attributed even when several people repeat it; absent a supported transmission link, the listener does not magically know that two accounts share an origin.

## Ordinary spoken warning policy

The selected native language family recognizes a complete understood clause `look out at <place> now`, `watch out at <place> now` or `danger at <place> now`, ignoring case and clause punctuation. `<place>` must exactly and uniquely match a label in the listener’s permitted current place evidence or eligible retained Known-place snapshots. “The doorway” is an ordinary authored/discovered place label. No alias registry, global place-name search or current hidden geometry lookup is selected. Missing words, unsupported wording, unknown labels and genuinely ambiguous labels do not produce the native current danger claim; they retain their ordinary speech meaning. Required incomplete place preparation holds before dependent mutation and cannot be reported as an unknown place.

Resolve `now` to the approximate half-open interval `[utteranceAt, utteranceAt + 2 normal-speed seconds)`, using the original direct utterance time and the shared clock conversion. Require `from <= acquiredAt < through` for the actual completed claim admission. Preserve the place snapshot’s original bounded location/precision; point-only support stays point-only. The native result is attributed testimony with danger `presence: possible` and outer `strength: possible`, actual permitted speaker, and exact heard-span/place support. F owns subsequent warning retention and relevance. A/SPK owns the [source-complete native adapter](../../projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md#native-ordinary-warning-testimony); no model call or hand-built warning record is necessary. A later history/audio replay neither reinterprets `now` nor supplies a fresh warning.
