# Visits and arrival

**Status: proposed authored profile, October 7, 2026.** This is the first bundled-world consumer of [Visits and wider participation](../../projects/visits-and-wider-participation-feature-spec.md), not an implemented portal, general sanctuary law, trade route or measured capacity. PS05–PS08 retain delivery. [PS limits](../../limits/product-scalability.md) records its selected restrictions.

## A friend at another camp

Prepare two distinct worlds under the same actual operating authority. The departure world is the visitor's home. The destination has a friend, an independently authored resident and a camp/workyard that differs meaningfully from home. The visitor can meet them, share an actual meal or help with an ordinary supported task, and return with an experience worth discussing. The resident's interests and response remain independent.

The first qualification uses two humans and one resident together at the destination: the visitor, their friend and that resident. This is an authored scenario, not the shared-world first-release population target. The resident is not a copy of the source world's person or private biography. No additional resident is required merely to animate a welcome.

Use existing supported movement, Talk, local tool/container handling, campfire cooking and eating. Choose actual finite raw meat/fuel and permitted local tools at preparation; record their quantities in the qualification setup. The exact task can be declined or changed through ordinary play. A visitor need not complete a recipe, buy an item, gain friendship or earn a travel badge for the visit to count as useful.

The first route is an authored waygate between the two prepared reception places. It has an ordinary inspectable name and departure action. It introduces no toll, per-trip reward, universal portal-building ability or faster exit in combat. A waygate's mechanics and visual vocabulary remain this world's choices.

## Scope and compatible state

Each world may have one outstanding outbound visitor in this profile, so the pair has at most two such travelers and two funded inactive recovery obligations. There is no third destination or onward chain before the person returns. The actual operator qualifies active human/scene capacity, waiting/preparation capacity, retained bytes and support for these obligations before enabling travel.

The worlds use the same qualified current base body and mechanical-time semantics. They may have different current times and pause separately. Preserve current condition and remaining durations through a crossing. Ordinary food, rest and real treatment can change the person; preserve those legitimate changes on return. Body appearance can continue, but excluded physical equipment supplies no mechanical benefit.

No physical item, animal, companion, title, competitive receipt or currency crosses. A live body's current supported needs, injuries, scars and personal experience do. Newly acquired mechanical capabilities still require actual destination support and rights. Do not reinitialize a visitor with starting food, tools, health or knowledge. The complete post-Continue person, if real death/recovery occurred, supersedes a stale pre-trip body snapshot.

An unsupported intrinsic power/effect or conflicting destination human binding prevents a new ordinary trip. Keep the affected person intact. Once a trip is admitted, world/body changes that would invalidate the promised return are deferred; unexpected changes use actual protected recovery without silently stripping or replacing the person.

## Actual storage and local goods

Provide one real protected permitted source container and a destination deposit place with actual native capacity. These are prepared world objects/custody, not an unlimited account inventory. Their continued availability is part of the route's qualification. Review owned, equipped, nested and borrowed goods before departure; ordinary placement/loan-return actions remain visible.

The visitor stores eligible home belongings before leaving. At the destination, named tools and finite inputs are offered under clear local ownership and loan terms. A visitor can use them, consume actual food or produce actual output. A return never recreates consumed inputs or silently transfers the output home.

Reserve destination deposit space for the allowed carried load, including nested/equipped items, gifts, directly carried output and supported later changes in required space. Refuse additional property or unsupported growth before its consequential admission exceeds this custody; an ordinary task may instead use a permitted actual local output place. Normal return uses ordinary placement. The visit terms authorize exact safe deposit of that same local load into the named protected custody if service closes or access is revoked. Record what was placed and retain ownership; do not narrate a fictional completed walk, gift or contract fulfillment. Detached items, corpse goods and ordinary abandoned property keep their actual local rules.

Protect these specifically admitted storage objects from unauthorized access and unsupported destruction while a visitor relies on them. This does not make every owned building or abandoned possession safe because its owner is offline.

## Supported reception protection

Prepare valid standing space, room for the actual bodies/tools and an unblocked ordinary return interaction. Protect the named reception/workyard against eligible animal strikes and human harm. Current direct PvP remains denied. A boundary check must prevent a creature or delegated effect from harming inward or using the protected space to attack outward.

Ordinary current campfire cooking and its supported local consequences may operate with qualified placement. No new spreading fire, explosives, traps, collateral projectile, push or area-attack family is admitted to this profile. General indirect harm and siege remain separate work. A protection label cannot make an unsupported effect safe.

An initially injured or hungry visitor keeps that condition. Existing supported body effects retain their real consequences, and ordinary survival needs continue during active play. Protection against new incoming attacks is not healing or a guarantee that someone who never eats cannot die.

Qualify every campfire/standing result that the unchanged current Continue evaluator can select from any reachable first-profile death location. Current base chooses among usable campfires within 396 units, otherwise the nearest; one safe fire among unsafe alternatives is insufficient. The prepared first yard admits only suitable selectable fires and actual positions. Defer a new fire, relocation, obstruction or permission change that would introduce an unsafe or inaccessible candidate while a visitor relies on this recovery. The operator may not remove the last qualified facility while an admitted visitor's life or pending death depends on it. If qualification cannot make the offered arrival/death-recovery claim true, keep travel unavailable and fix the authored place. A floor-only return check is insufficient.

The first invitation grants entry, ordinary perception/Talk and named work/tool use. It grants no private storage inspection, mind reading, world edits, land ownership or administrative power. The owner can stop new entry and revoke ordinary local work; admitted exit, property recovery and private-human protections persist.

## Departure, return and service hold

Use the [current-main base departure rule](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/0a3ab79b7a698a7f1941dc23722f89220d1ba425/docs/worlds/base/player-danger.md): 300 actually simulated game seconds, normally five real seconds. Human unfinished work stops at departure start. Committed effects and any eligible damage remain, and attacks cannot extend the interval. Pause, preparation, downtime and merely waiting for capacity spend none of this exposure.

The product owner selects a 60-real-second ready offer and reconnect reservation, and a 120-real-second precommit reservation after accepting departure. Those operating clocks never replace the mechanical exit clock. A definitely uncommitted timeout leaves actual source custody and ordinary control/absence intact. An uncertain committed move requires reconciliation, not reactivation at both gates.

Hold one real home-return admission for each admitted outbound visitor and count it against the home world's offered capacity. The person's stored history does not need active simulation. If both humans depart from their own homes at once, the profile still counts both return obligations; the operator cannot sell those places again.

After arrival, current destination participation and death rules apply. Real death leaves its actual corpse and retained/lost possessions there. Explicit Continue uses that world's actual rest-spot evaluation, new physical life and scars; return never substitutes for it. If service cannot currently evaluate recovery, keep the pending person protected and explain the service wait. No remote corpse collection or invented resurrection occurs.

A normal return carries the resulting actual living person, with fresh home-local identity and explicit continuity. A full home uses its held admission. A paused/expired home can receive the person into its coherent held state without starting unpaid simulation. An unavailable/blocked home uses the funded protected inactive custody described by the product owner, with no survival debt or duplicate embodiment.

For the first no-sale development proof, use current-format authority and preserve exact trip state until recovery completes. An ongoing customer offer additionally requires the actual adopted DG27 continuity/recovery terms; the prototype does not make that commercial promise.

No new funded unattended period begins on departure. The current home/destination policies determine whether other residents continue or the world pauses. The first profile has no play-duration timer or compulsory automatic return after a short session; actual hosting, retained custody and planned closure terms remain visible. New trips stop before their accepted return/recovery obligations can no longer be supported.

## Qualification that demonstrates a visit

The complete experience includes storing home possessions, requesting and accepting a place, consuming actual departure, arriving safely, meeting the actual friend, choosing ordinary useful activity, handling destination goods and returning with retained experience. Check a subsequent actual meeting: a resident may remember what they truly experienced, with no automatic biography upload.

Qualify disconnect, ambiguous arrival, source pause, invalid placement, entry revocation, world closure, current death/Continue, nested forbidden load, finite food/tool consumption and attempted pre-crossing restore. The [restoration owner](../../projects/corrections-and-shared-restoration-feature-spec.md) refuses unsupported independent rewind across the connected history.

Record actual source/destination state, human control, preserved privacy and full operating/support costs. This small consumer leaves the original 100-human/100-agent/100-animal/1,000-object and later 10,000-human targets intact. It proves neither those capacities nor an arbitrary connected economy.
