# Optional speech and private slate conversations

**Proposed DG26 world profile, October 8, 2026.** [Voice and calls](../../projects/voice-and-calls-feature-spec.md) owns the product proposal and stages. This profile introduces no runtime behavior or primitive starting equipment. Current speech/hearing and [personal message slates](text-messages.md) retain their exact existing or proposed status.

## Ordinary words before richer acoustics

The bundled world's present speech emits its full textual utterance at one admitted moment. A listener receives only the words and source identity permitted then. Optional audio may read that already acquired evidence; its playback length is not a new world duration. The world does not stop moving because a player chose audio, and Stop playback does not undo what a character said or another character learned.

World hearing still uses authored whisper/normal/shout and actual geometry. Human headset loudness and microphone level do not change the person's fictional voice range. Unrecognized speakers remain unrecognized in media; no recognizable voice is attached merely because the engine knows their identity. Recorded emotional evidence is distinct from private thoughts.

The selected later timed consumer is a conversation across a real doorway: a person can hear an initial phrase, step beyond the opening or close the door, and miss a later phrase; another person entering late hears only what is actually emitted during their eligible presence. Its exact emission and speed behavior remains with DG26 and PS04, not a caption animation. First delivery does not require propagation around corners, sleep interruption, a voice-familiarity score or language mechanics. A different world may use supported vibrations or private transmitted symbols instead of air speech.

## A specifically enabled call edition

A creator can later choose a slate edition that explicitly supports live private conversation between two humans in the same world. It uses the actual slates, individual bindings and finite spare already specified in the message-slate profile. Enabling the call capability does not create additional items, refill the spare, retarget an old binding, expose stored text or automatically upgrade every text-only world.

The purpose is an ordinary wanted moment: two companions on separate parts of an outing briefly compare what happened and decide where to meet. They may type, dictate and review a draft, or hear permitted synthesized words. The first live slate exchange is an active text conversation with optional sound presentation; it is not a claim that unrestricted personal microphone audio is already supported.

Calling requires the same actual living, awake, participating, capable person and carried usable bound device as new text use. The contact must already be established through the legitimate local mutual exchange. A contact grants private text only. Each recipient can privately allow or silence incoming call invitations by contact. A request is an invitation to this conversation, never blanket permission to record or transmit the human's microphone.

## Ask, join and leave without revealing private absence

The sender chooses the known contact and Request call. The control explains that successful joining will leave their currently active local conversation. The request stays valid only for that disclosed conversation context; choosing another local conversation withdraws it so a late acceptance cannot unexpectedly replace the player's new activity.

The recipient receives one quiet reachable invitation without focus theft or message-body preview. Accepting deliberately reveals their participation and leaves their own active local conversation if present. Declining, silencing, blocking or being unable to answer produces the same generic no-answer result at the caller's deadline; no busy, asleep, dead, blocked or online label is exposed. Call-history metadata stays private to its legitimate participants and the appropriate exact request party; a world creator gains no call roster.

A person already in a call must hang up before requesting or accepting another call; an incoming attempt is handled privately as unavailable. Only one outgoing request per person and one request per pair can be active, with three new requests per sender across contacts in a rolling sixty real seconds and a thirty-real-second request expiry. Duplicate requests preserve the original outcome. If both people independently request the same pair, there remains one invitation; the reciprocal explicit request can count as acceptance only after both have confirmed the same displayed pair and active-conversation consequences. Otherwise the existing invitation stays pending. This is no automatic acceptance based on earlier text.

An accepted call occupies the first active conversation place. It never reserves attention or consent indefinitely: Hang up is always available, and starting another local conversation requires an explicit choice to leave. Ordinary nearby sound is still heard under normal hearing; joining a call does not create deafness, grant remote ambient sound or automatically answer a nearby person.

## What the slate transmits

A submitted slate turn conveys its exact private words to the other participating person through the installed magical affordance. It creates no local spoken utterance. Dictation is a real human input method for composing those words, not proof that the fictional person spoke aloud. Incoming synthesis is private presentation, not a world loudspeaker. To initiate ordinary local speech or another active local conversation, the person explicitly hangs up first; the new local utterance then has its normal audience and consequences. Passive nearby hearing continues during the call, and the two histories are never merged.

Submitted text and reviewed dictation share the same call history and the existing per-contact/total slate allowance. Each turn retains the NC22 text limits; a call is not a rate-limit bypass. Exact per-turn admission and read/evidence results remain separate from playback. Text is visible without waiting for synthesis and a synthetic-voice failure never invents unheard words, repeats an accepted turn or converts it to nearby speech.

A newly accepted call grants no access to unrelated local conversations, older unread private notes, private thought or account metadata. Existing D48/NC22 current permissions and erasure apply. First calls have no NPC participant, conference invitation, personal raw-voice stream, recording archive, voicemail, speakerphone or external phone network. Each later extension must preserve the same meaning through its actual supported behavior.

## Interruption, device loss and return

Hang-up, person blocking or revoked call/contact permission, disabled call capability, world pause, loss of controlling participation or access, an unusable/missing bound device, incapacity/death or a replaced world history ends the call. The departing person receives their own relevant actionable explanation. The other person sees that the call ended without disclosure of the private reason. A pending recipient invitation stops internally when its own eligibility fails, but the caller's original generic no-answer deadline does not shorten for that private change. Caller-local withdrawal or loss of the caller's own eligibility can give that caller an immediate own-action result; a visible whole-world shutdown is also explicit. Neither behavior reveals the remote person's private condition.

No new call starts and no microphone resumes automatically after a pause, reconnect, restored save, recovered slate or human takeover. Already committed permitted text remains subject to the proper retained-history/private-cut policy; unsubmitted drafts stay private and require a fresh valid destination before sending. A lost acknowledgement is resolved through its original outcome rather than an automatic duplicate turn.

A human's return can preserve their person and known history while their physical slate remains elsewhere; private content never becomes loot. Current blocks, revoked bindings, account grants and real anti-abuse/spending allowances do not rewind with fictional history. A future NPC call additionally needs the existing private-derivative and controller-transition qualification; this profile does not assume it.

There is no arbitrary active-call duration maximum while the people and underlying service remain valid. This does not promise free media or unattended world time. Existing funded service and optional-media budgets determine availability. Audio failure can leave a valid native text conversation usable; a stopped world does not acquire a new communication service through that fallback.
