# Prepared wilderness community — first unattended profile

**Proposed authored-world profile, October 6, 2026; not implemented or adopted.** This is the world-content owner for DG17's first finite unattended community. The [continuing-lives feature specification](../../projects/continuing-lives-feature-spec.md#15-dg17--a-first-funded-unattended-community) owns the common service, choice, privacy, funding and return contract. Current [survival](survival.md), [sleep](sleep.md), [time](time.md), [possessions](camp-containers.md) and [human protection](lifecycle-and-protection.md) remain authoritative until an implementation is explicitly authorized and qualified.

## What makes this camp worth revisiting

Use the current wilderness map and its two existing NPC residents, Ada and the Peacock Mercenary, after their independently chosen attended behavior is qualified. Preserve their actual biographies and relationships. The additional human is a visitor/player with their own exploration, invention or adventure; they are not assigned to supervise camp logistics. This is a later optional prepared-world choice, not a replacement for the accepted one-live-resident opening creative loop or a requirement to add more people to it.

A resident can continue a finite making project, inspect a result, walk to a known pleasant place, talk, decline company, eat or rest. An ordinary visit to the existing lookout is a useful possible interest because it offers a place and view that actually exist; it is not a compulsory sightseeing schedule or a new skill/joy reward. A made cord or container can have use in another activity, but nobody must produce one to justify their existence. The resident's own purpose supplies the choice and stopping condition.

The selected profile contains no new predator behavior, weather, crop growth, births, food spoilage or fire spread. Those are not current consequences of merely leaving these people alone. Existing finite resources, actual action costs and permitted bodily risk remain real. For the first offer, do not admit an unresolved fight, a resident already needing rescue, or a new unsupported activity coupling. A known quiet starting situation reduces unrelated uncertainty; it does not impose permanent invulnerability.

This is a finite qualification offer, not an overnight service. At the current rate its entire three-day period fits within 72 uninterrupted real minutes; leaving for a real night or workday does not promise that much real time of independent living. Longer service must earn its own supply, self-care and cost evidence, or an explicitly chosen later clock design.

An existing world can enter this profile only through its actual current state. Selecting the service does not grant the table's provisions, repair wounds, reset a resident's hunger or equip a missing tool. A prepared new-world option must disclose that it starts stocked; those items have normal identities, ownership and consumption afterward. The player may choose the lean adventure start instead. Do not turn gathering these exact amounts into a compulsory quest or hide them behind the claim that all current worlds already contain this stock.

The first qualification uses the normal rate from the start of the reviewed period. A manual pause stops progression as usual. Changing rate, relevant body rules, admitted activities or the population withdraws the earlier readiness claim; the creator must review the changed offer before unattended running can continue. This does not reinterpret ordinary attended speed controls or automatically restart the period when a player returns.

## Food and rest viability

The current world drains **0.003 fullness points per game second**. Cooked meat restores **38 points**, within the body's current maximum. Therefore one continuously participating person uses **259.2 fullness per game day**, or **777.6 points over the proposed three-day period**. Their 24 meat units contain **912 points** before waste, leaving **134.4 points of food-supply margin** without counting starting bodily fullness. For two residents, that is 1,824 supplied points against 1,555.2 points of ongoing drain. These calculations come from the current [bodily rules](survival.md#bodily-conditions-and-chosen-responses) and [item definitions](items.md); they are arithmetic checks, not measured reliable survival.

The margin accommodates some actual food loss or poorly timed eating; it cannot guarantee survival under arbitrary consumption, inaccessible food or a resident refusing to eat. Admission uses current available quantities, not nominal initial stock or promised future hunting. Review current injuries, energy, fullness, known food and what a chosen method can actually do. Required actions still take their real time and can fail for a supported reason. A resident who has adequate food can deliberately prepare future travel supplies, but an old meal concern cannot become an endless cooking order.

Rest is the stronger behavioral test. Current restorative sleep recovers empty-to-full energy over **eight game hours**, during which fullness still declines by **86.4 points**. Entering that maximum rest at full food leaves only 13.6 fullness, while one meat unit added to Ada's current starting 35 supplies 73 and does not cover that whole interval. Actual sleep duration depends on current energy, so this example is a warning about the longest supported rest, not a prediction that newly created Ada sleeps eight hours immediately. Sleep does not stop hunger and quiet starvation does not automatically wake the sleeper. The current [sleep rules](sleep.md) own those behaviors.

Qualification must therefore show the resident uses real knowledge of food and rest to prepare appropriately, rather than merely reacting to hunger after becoming unable to decide. Meaningful continuation can include an already chosen preparation, a timely additional meal, avoiding needless exhaustion or another actually supported method. The design does not prescribe which choice the resident must make, add an automatic meal controller, change sleep's native threshold or introduce a rescue wakeup. If current choice/continuation cannot handle the ordinary case reliably, repair that selected experience before offering unattended care. Repeated failures remain visible evidence; the prepared food allocation is not a license to label them eccentricity.

Food in the human's carried possessions is excluded from readiness after departure. A dropped public cache can be consumed only through its actual access, known contents and transfer rules; an unaccepted offer or a vague instruction to share is not a meal. The initial own-stock choice avoids depending on an unimplemented standing food entitlement or a communal-pantry planner. It does not prohibit a later real gift and independent acceptance, and it does not stop a resident choosing to share their own food. Such changes affect subsequent viability honestly.

## Current supplies are finite

The expanded wilderness currently has four outer berry patches containing 24, 18, 28 and 22 berries, **92 total**, in addition to the depleted patches near camp. At the current berry nutrition of 18, those outer berries supply **1,656 fullness points**, about **6.39 continuously active consumer-days** before waste and travel/access limits. See the actual authored [landscape sources](../../../packages/domain/src/worlds/base/landscape.ts) and [food definitions](../../../packages/domain/src/worlds/base/items.ts). These berries are a genuine optional resource, not assumed renewed stock or a hidden reserve required to make the prepared profile pass.

Current animals wander and can supply finite hunted remains. They do not reproduce or operate a complete food web; their energy meter is not a hunger-driven ecology. A hunt is a real attack, not a guaranteed meal chain. Food from a successful hunt requires actual harvest and cooking where applicable. Dead remains also lose harvest availability under their existing decay deadlines. Do not count every visible animal as a guaranteed provision or assign a future kill to a resident who has not chosen it.

The starting fire currently holds at most 48 game hours of fuel, shorter than the proposed period if continuously lit. The prepared meals make continued survival independent of keeping it lit. A resident may let it go out, or choose actual fuel gathering/tending/relighting for a real purpose. The existing one-session watch is bounded and is not an automatic duty rotation. Fire currently supplies cooking, not warmth; its extinction does not secretly chill the camp. Current [fire care](survival.md#tending-the-campfire) and [camp activities](camp-routines.md) retain their own costs and limits.

Renewing the period requires actual usable supply for its new horizon. An extra allowance does not create meat or regrow a patch. DG19 can later supply a qualified renewal/preservation family, and real exchange can later supply trade, but this profile promises neither. It deliberately permits comfortable initial provisions instead of manufacturing starvation so a new ecology system appears necessary.

## Return situations

**A useful object, a changed priority.** A resident genuinely chooses to make something within a known supported family. It may be finished, partly worked or abandoned when the human returns. If finished, its real owner can use it or offer it; it is not automatically a gift. The resident may now prefer the lookout or quiet company. The human can inquire, ask to see it, propose their own invention or continue elsewhere.

**Company without a duty.** One resident approaches the other with an actual thought to share. The second can welcome them, be busy or decline. Later they may report the exchange in their own words, omit it or interpret it differently. A return summary cannot report a friendship improvement as fact, fill in exact unrecorded speech or tell the human about either private mind. No compulsory reconciliation or social reward is scheduled.

**A quiet camp and an extinguished fire.** The human sees the actual cold fire, available people and permitted possessions. Nobody is blamed for neglect merely because the prepared meals made more burning unnecessary. If someone chose to keep it lit for a supported task, their actual work and any failure explain that result. Useful preparation can remove a chore instead of repeatedly recreating it.

**A period that ended before the human returned.** The service says when progression stopped and why; the fiction shows only the state actually reached. A resident can remember their own experiences during that period, but cannot truthfully describe living through the longer real-world absence. The human owes no retroactive greetings, meals or missed optional social scenes. A new funded period starts from the same people and actual remaining supplies.

The human can also resume ordinary attended play without authorizing another unattended period, subject to its existing service and spending rules. That session leaves the finite pantry unchanged until real actions use or replenish it. Leaving afterward pauses under current absence rules unless a new unattended period was separately authorized. The optional prepared profile must not become a gate on the player's ordinary adventure.
