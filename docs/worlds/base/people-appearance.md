# Human appearance in the bundled world

**Designed adult art profile, November 6, 2026; assets and ranges remain experimental until qualified.** This is the human content for the [people-from-parts design](../../projects/people-from-parts-feature-spec.md). It describes the first adult kit to build and judge. The [current art direction](../../../art-direction/decisions.md#current-direction-at-a-glance) owns the look, and the [existing cast](first-playable-people.md) owns who these people are.

The executable authoring owner is [people-appearance.ts](../../../packages/domain/src/worlds/base/people-appearance.ts): `BASE_APPEARANCE_PROFILE` supplies controls/choices/presets and `BASE_WARDROBE` supplies actual item/part mappings and creation-default flags. Export these into the art package rather than maintaining a second JSON list. Main's Wayfarer installation selects the public descriptor identity `world/base/wayfarer-adult-people` through its trusted opening initializer, with the exact revision and compiled file supplied by the installed presentation release. Assets and browser acceptance remain required before that family is published for use.

## An adult body to start from

Start with two sculpted adult frames, feminine and masculine, on the same mesh layout. Offer them as starting buttons for the two gendered looks Mike requested. The frame control can travel between them. Both expose the same body, face, hair and clothing choices. Personal gender, pronouns, age, attributes and skills have their own authoring. Changing shoulder breadth has no effect on those facts.

For the qualified family, author four build recipes on each frame after the three-body proof. Eight starting buttons give distinct silhouettes that an artist can compare at the play camera. Height is independently editable on all eight. Working recipes below use proportions of each **art control's** travel. The endpoints are sculpted visual examples. The numbers do not measure kilos, body-fat percentage, strength or ability. Publish actual measured dimensions with the approved kit.

Start all four at the same reference height for the comparison, then show short and tall examples. A maker can create a broad, lightly muscled person or a narrow, soft-bodied person between the examples. Preserve their actual values when saving a named preset. Grandparenthood, a profession or a gender never selects a mandatory build.

**Height** runs from a proposed **1.50 to 2.10 metres** for this proof. A height slider at 75% gives 1.50 + .75 × .60 = **1.95 m** from bare soles to crown in the upright reference pose. Hair, hats, footwear and a crouching animation are excluded. The span includes the study's 1.58 m Mara Reed and the 2.035 m Peacock Mercenary. A stated height wins over an estimate from an illustration. People outside the interval deserve another supported range or family through the no-match decision. Shrinking this adult kit cannot produce a child. Actual play currently fits people to a common 1.75 m body. Use the [heights study](../../../art-direction/studies/2026-11-03-character-heights/README.md) for the individual proposed heights and the [technical handoff](../../projects/people-from-parts-tech-design.md#height-and-gameplay) for the integration gate.

Each local axis gets a neutral and measured authored endpoints on the common anatomy; its recipe names landmarks, affected pieces, dependencies and tested combinations. Qualify at least two distinct head presets and two hair constructions (a short style and a tied or braided style) before claiming face or hair variation.

### Control definitions

These are the selected initial sculpt briefs, not measured human-population limits or claims of completed assets. The family manifest carries the resulting approved endpoint coordinates and measurements. Every normalized control uses `[0,1]`, with neutral `.5` unless listed otherwise. The two frame anchors are sculpted at the same **1.75 m**, **35-year adult appearance**, neutral local controls and the same intended fitness level. Frame interpolation must not secretly add belly/thigh fullness or select a head. Ranges below are measured against the applicable neutral frame/head before other local edits; their combined geometry is qualified by [the authoring contract](../../projects/people-from-parts-authoring-contract.md). Do not silently clamp an unsupported combination or mark an unbuilt axis available.

| Recipe key                      | Minimum → neutral → maximum target                                                 | Measurement / protected meaning                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `frame`                         | Feminine anchor → interpolated frame → masculine anchor                            | Two adult structural starting shapes; default `.5`. Same other controls, identity and coverage                                  |
| `statureMetres`                 | 1.50 → 1.75 → 2.10 m                                                               | Soles to anatomical scalp crown, hair excluded; linear percentage mapping uses the full interval, so neutral stature is not 50% |
| `breadth`                       | .90 → 1 → 1.10 times neutral ribcage/pelvis breadth                                | Whole skeletal build; no muscle/fat change; head size and stature remain fixed                                                  |
| `shoulderBreadth`, `hipBreadth` | .90 → 1 → 1.10 times neutral local breadth                                         | Shoulder joint span / pelvis landmark span; additional local refinement, without scaling the head                               |
| `muscle`                        | Low visible development → natural fit definition → pronounced athletic musculature | Deltoids, chest/back, arms, abdomen, thighs and calves; do not move joint centres or remove selected fullness                   |
| `fullness`                      | Lean soft-tissue layer → moderate layer → full abdomen, hips and limbs             | No forced change to muscle, skeletal widths or stature; a full muscular person must remain possible                             |
| `belly`                         | Reduced → neutral → increased abdominal projection                                 | Local residual over fullness; no negative volume, pregnancy state or internal physiology claim                                  |
| `chestVolume`                   | Reduced → neutral → increased chest soft-tissue volume                             | Local tissue shape over the same ribcage; separate from pectoral muscle and frame                                               |
| `legLength`                     | .94 → 1 → 1.06 times neutral hip-to-ankle length                                   | Preserve total stature by compensating trunk length; maintain knee fraction of the segment                                      |
| `armLength`                     | .92 → 1 → 1.08 times neutral shoulder-to-wrist length                              | Preserve elbow fraction; hand dimensions stay fixed                                                                             |
| `neckLength`                    | .85 → 1 → 1.15 times neutral shoulder-to-head interval                             | Preserve stature by compensating trunk length; head size and leg length stay fixed                                              |
| `neckGirth`                     | .85 → 1 → 1.15 times neutral neck circumference                                    | Follow collar, necklace and nape; keep airway/neck mesh uncollapsed                                                             |
| `adultAgeYears`                 | 18 → 35 → 80 apparent years                                                        | Piecewise sculpt/material knots at 18, 35, 60, 80; independent of chronological age, muscle, fullness and posture               |

Length compensation acts on the trunk between pelvis and the shoulder/neck base, with the head and limb lengths protected. If the combined requested dimensions leave an invalid trunk, the recipe fails against the family envelope; do not scale every limb to conceal it. Belly and chest volume endpoints require front/profile sculpt approval because a single millimetre rule would disguise different anatomy. Their manifest publishes the measured excursion at the navel/chest landmarks. Proposed percentage-based dimensional ranges are subject to the same approval and can be narrowed explicitly after a failed experiment.

Face presets share head topology, scalp, UVs, neck ring and anatomical landmarks. Provide two concept-led adult identities first; both support every qualified local control. The default local value is `.5`. Surface motion tapers to zero at protected features and the neck ring. Symmetric edits are the starting mechanism; selected asymmetric identity details belong to the preset rather than a second full set of left/right sliders.

| `headWidth`, `headLength`                 | .92–1.08 times cranial width / crown-to-chin distance; retain stature by trunk compensation for head length      |
| `jawWidth`, `chinWidth`, `cheekboneWidth` | .85–1.15 times their measured neutral spans; maintain jaw/neck clearance                                         |
| `chinProjection`                          | Neutral chin-tip depth ±8 mm; lower lip remains attached                                                         |
| `browHeight`                              | Neutral brow landmark height ±5 mm; do not move the eyeball                                                      |
| `eyeAperture`, `eyeSpacing`, `eyeTilt`    | Lid opening .80–1.20 times neutral; eye-centre spacing .90–1.10 times neutral; canthal tilt neutral ±5°          |
| `noseBridge`, `noseTip`, `noseWidth`      | Bridge/tip projection neutral ±5 mm; alar width .80–1.20 times neutral; preserve nostril openings                |

Age uses shared anatomical residuals on the selected face and body skin, plus specific face corrections only where needed. The 18-year target remains an adult; 35 is the neutral sculpt; 60 and 80 add progressively changed cheek/jaw/neck volumes, folds and hand detail. Age does not mandate grey hair, baldness, stooping, weakness or a different gait. Keep birth/biography facts in their existing authoring owner. `adultAgeYears` is a fixed appearance choice until explicitly edited; it does not tick with the world clock.

Hair starts with a removable short swept style and a tied/braided style; provide a scalp underneath both. Each style defines its neutral length in metres and a `.75–1.25` multiplier range around that length, with roots fixed. A style with no meaningful length travel exposes no length slider. Hair pigment, optional greying, brow/beard choice, iris colour, skin palette and optional marks are independent. Facial presets do not replace skin pigment or attach hair permanently. The skin palette is world art content; its colours do not identify an ancestry, nationality, personality or capability.

### Playable scope and content authority

One selected baked trio, one shared Wayfarer outfit and compatible motion are the first asset delivery. The full family adds the above controls and age samples without blocking use of an individually accepted baked person. The eight preset recipes are evaluated examples, never separate meshes with different topology. First-cast recipes use explicit resolved values; no repeated randomisation occurs on load. Body control names and limits belong to this selected family, not engine-wide enums. The bundled world module selects its exact family release, permitted parts and preset recipes; the asset manifest owns the shape/fit data and control mappings once. The engine consumes this data without restating human ages, gendered frames or this world's garment slots.

The separate private [MHP07 Main-world pilot](../../maintainers/art/metahuman-poc.md#current-progress) uses one provisional Vivian family, Bald and linen bra/briefs, with one removable compound shirt/shorts item occupying torso and legs. [modular-pilot.ts](../../../packages/domain/src/worlds/base/modular-pilot.ts) owns its person brief and item mapping. It uses ordinary creation, equipment and stature admission; it does not replace this broader Wayfarer design or convert the existing cast. Actual-game qualification remains with MHP07.

## Height in play

For the first individual-stature experiment, start from the [bundled ordinary human spatial profile](../../../packages/domain/src/worlds/base/spatial-definitions.ts). At creation multiply its eye, ear and interaction heights by the ratio of requested to reference stature, and set standing height to the requested metres. Keep the authored radius, allowable slope and foot position. This rule concerns the physical height of a new person. Arm, neck, shoulder and face controls remain cosmetic. Use the same prospective profile to check placement and to initialize the saved body. Test open ground and low overhead clearance, then confirm actual sight/hearing anchors and posture-dependent display before adopting individual stature in play. ## What a person wears

### Baseline coverage

Show adult men in a small opaque medieval-style linen loincloth and adult women in an opaque linen bra and briefs. Fit these as soft, close-fitting garments with real waist openings, fabric thickness and finished edges. Keep the abdomen, waist, hips and limbs readable for body review. The first masculine proof uses the loincloth, the feminine proof uses the bra and briefs, and the intermediate proof explicitly uses the bra and briefs. Each resolved appearance recipe selects its coverage set independently of frame or personal gender, and either set can fit either frame.

Preserve the complete body beneath these separate pieces in the editable master. The covered review must show the actual shaped torso and pelvis carrying the undergarments. Inspect the body and coverage pieces separately, then together from front, profile, back and below in motion. Derive hidden-surface masks only after the fitted complete body passes review. A selected outfit can replace fully covered sections while retaining appropriate coverage at openings. This presentation baseline carries no price, protection, weight or inventory identity. Removable world undergarments follow the ordinary item contract.

An outfit recipe can request empty positions. A long shirt covers more of the body without occupying the legs port. An armoured coat made of joined plates and straps can be one item with several render pieces. A head item cannot silently delete a worn earring. Use a compatible visual variant if it is covered.

The pilot's creation defaults select the Wayfarer shirt, trousers, paired boots, necklace and ear studs. Gloves, padding, plate and hat remain selectable. The creator reads these world-owned flags for its selected family; changing family resets that draft's starting outfit to the new family's defaults. These are editable creation intentions. Only the authorized spawn creates/equips actual item lots; the body recipe never becomes a second worn-item list. The offline art procedure derives the same default part request from these flags, while an explicit empty outfit still produces the covered baseline.

The base's [equipment definitions](../../../packages/domain/src/worlds/base/equipment.ts) own main hand, off hand, back, head, neck, hands, torso clothing, inner torso, outer torso, legs, feet and paired ears. A travel cloak already uses the back port. Each removable garment follows custody, equip admission, placement, condition and supported material properties through the [ordinary equipment contract](../../projects/character-and-map-additions-tech-design.md#equipment-slots). Its armour or thermal effects require the appropriate authored rules. A picture of a breastplate by itself supplies neither. Other people can perceive visible clothing through the normal world rules. Changing an outfit grants no social reaction, identification or modesty penalty by default.
