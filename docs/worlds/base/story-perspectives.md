# Base-world story perspective: after you left at camp

This is the proposed world-content profile for [DG15's optional story perspective](../../projects/story-perspectives-feature-spec.md). It is not current gameplay. The [NC20 consumer](../../maintainers/narration-and-conversations.md#nc20--optional-after-you-left-perspective) must qualify the new external-source permission, capture, generation and history behavior before the profile can be offered. Ordinary narration and private-thought restrictions remain under [NC](../../narration-and-conversations.md). The [SP inventory](../../limits/narration.md#sp01--selected-external-perspective) records candidate bounds and their rationale.

## Authored scene and audience

Use one private world with one human account in its entire participation history, that account's one character and one familiar authored resident. The world author must select this external craft-completion projection for that reader; a public-looking place does not grant disclosure by itself. The player's story preference is separately off by default. Shared-world copies and worlds that have ever admitted another ordinary human participant or spectator are outside this profile. Account enrollment or invite redemption granting access counts before body control; a characterless spectator also counts. Separately authorized operational inspection creates no ordinary story-recipient grant.

The scene is the existing starting campfire and an authored ground area extending eight metres from its starting centre. This radius supplies a recognizable small scene, not perception, property, a building footprint or a simulation boundary. It does not make the campfire's current state or fuel visible remotely. The initial campfire is not relocatable for this profile; if it or the scene binding is removed or materially changed, invalidate unrequested anchors/candidates rather than following an object to an unseen place. Published historical facts remain governed by source validity.

The character must actually have perceived this campfire and the resident, with legitimate identity binding, and have witnessed the resident complete the native cord-making activity within the scene. Use the name already legitimately recognized; never resolve an unknown person from private authoring data. If current knowledge support cannot retain the required identity and familiarity evidence, qualify that narrow source binding before offering the profile. An invented bookmark or broad place-knowledge feature is not a shortcut.

## What can follow the encounter

After the character crosses outside the scene, the first later native completion of the same cord-making activity by that resident within the scene may qualify, provided it occurs within one game hour of departure and is outside the character's actual awareness. Returning inside the camp closes an unused departure window; an already captured historical scene can remain available. Crossing eight metres is not proof of being unable to see or hear; the awareness owner still decides. A completed occurrence the character perceived belongs to ordinary narration and supplies no cutaway for that occurrence.

This is the current native cord activity, not a new generated recipe, mastery bonus, mandatory chore or simulation of productive hours. The resident chooses and performs it through actual agency and action admission, with real materials and elapsed work. The profile adds no free cord, bulk production, absent-time catch-up or special reason for the resident to keep crafting. A halted or failed attempt is not a completion. A known crafted item in current inventory is not evidence that this qualifying episode happened.

The minimum scene fact is the familiar resident's historical completion of that familiar activity at the familiar camp. The prior witnessed completion can justify a word such as “another”; otherwise use simpler wording. Do not add where the new cord was stored, how many items the resident has, who requested it, why they acted, whether they are proud, a later rest or a gesture. No speech, private goals, plans, thoughts, commitments, new recipe details or hidden resource information can enter through supporting context.

One witnessed anchor can produce at most one captured completion. Reentering and leaving without a new witnessed eligible completion cannot create another anchor. Capturing is sparse and prospective while the preference and world grant are enabled. Turning it off clears the anchor and candidate; enabling it again requires a fresh witnessed eligible activity. The profile does not search all past NPC activity or create an event to satisfy a reading request.

## Clock, presentation and continuation

The source window is one hour of the world's actual game time; pause and absence follow existing [base-world time](time.md) and participation behavior. No clock change is introduced. At the current sixty game seconds per real second, an uninterrupted source window is about one real minute. This is a candidate relevance window, not a deadline the player must watch. The completed unrequested scene can remain available for seven real days through the bounded buffer in the feature spec.

Opening the historical paragraph leaves the character at the real location and does not pause needs or danger. It gives no map route, new action target, item claim, work reward, competence, relationship score or in-character awareness. The player may return through ordinary movement and discover what is now there, or simply continue the adventure. The resident receives no notification that the human viewed or declined the entry.

The first qualified prose can be as small as one grounded sentence. Its appeal must come from a real continuation that the reader cares about. Do not lengthen native work, script a follow-up rest, introduce rain or add hidden emotional narration to make the mode appear successful. A low useful-scene rate is a result to review honestly.

## Expansion boundary

Only the selected cord activity and this camp are admitted initially. Another craft, public construction completion, animal event or natural change needs its own explicit source projection and useful reason to be shown. The first selection does not authorize story access to every activity that happens inside the circle. Private internal perspectives, another human's events and current distant tactical information need separate product decisions. DG13's shelter and DG14's practice are not prerequisites.
