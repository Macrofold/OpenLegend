# Player danger and departure

Current bundled-world behavior, implemented October 4, 2026. Authored mortality, final-blow wording and exposure are selected by the bundled body/participation policies. [Lifecycle and protection](lifecycle-and-protection.md) owns the wider cooperative-play and NPC-continuity requirements.

## Observation earns safety

Active players face dangerous animals without an encounter consent prompt or guaranteed advance warning. The first [stag encounter](first-threat-encounter.md) supplies inspectable tracks, disturbed foliage and actual aggressive posture. Players can miss them and die; movement, sight-breaking cover, preparation and useful equipment provide responses. Signs do not report hidden live intent or absence. Add warnings only if subsequent play demonstrates that learning from the physical evidence is too difficult. No raids, factions, deep ecology or universal danger score is added.

The stag independently chooses seen, eligible players. Another active player inside its territory can become a later target after fresh preparation. Direct PvP remains denied at admission and continuation. This permits incidental danger without implementing deliberate-luring moderation or a general attributed indirect-harm law; broader D07 policy remains open. No new traps, spread fire, area attacks, push, explosions or collateral projectile mechanics are admitted by this release. Existing native environmental/status consequences retain their own owners; creator effects are not ordinary human combat permissions.

## Five simulated seconds to leave

Logout, hidden-tab control release or losing the last controlling presence starts a visible fade and **300 game seconds of full vulnerability**: five seconds at the installed normal 60:1 rate. The player should reach somewhere safe enough to survive it. Existing injury/effects and new animal attacks can kill the fading life. Fade opacity does not reduce damage, collision or targeting. There is no automatic retreat, defense, healing, new absent-player attack or paid model work. Unfinished human movement, attacks, plans and rest stop at departure start; committed effects/costs remain, unused ammunition stays.

Only normally advanced mechanics spend exposure. Pause, maintenance, navigation preparation and downtime do not. The server continues ordinary simulation during a lone player's fade instead of treating absence as an immediate pause. Faster authorized playback consumes the same game-time exposure faster. A fully simulated interval is required; an expired wall-clock attempt cannot grant early immunity. There is no catch-up combat for server downtime.

At the exact simulation deadline, continuous effects for the preceding exposed interval settle, then participation becomes inactive before discrete attacks at that instant. Only impacts strictly before expiry can injure that life. Repeated attacks cannot extend exposure. An inactive human is protected from bodily harm and survival depletion; world time can continue for other players. A fatal pre-expiry result uses [Player Death](player-death.md); account absence protects neither the corpse nor abandoned loot.

Returning control before expiry cancels that fade and retains the actual body/location/injuries. A later loss after this explicit return starts a fresh exposure; repeated notifications for the same absence attempt do not renew it. This favors promptly playable reconnection over retaining an old penalty after a genuine return. Multiple tabs/control generations still share one body. After expiry, current checked return placement restores the surviving body; a dead character receives Continue. Return does not rewind supplies, injuries or stag aftermath. A general logout notice explains the rule without detecting a hidden animal.

## Final-blow confirmation

A human submits one ordinary strike or ranged hunt. If conservative maximum supported injury could kill the current target, the world requires review headed **“Attempt a final blow?”**. The dialog shows the observed name and chosen attack/tool, range, preparation/recovery and ammunition facts, and explains persistent death and unavailable ordinary animal/NPC revival. **Cancel** initially has focus; Escape dismisses without movement or firing; confirmation is **Attempt lethal blow**. Choosing does not pause the world.

Permission covers one exact attack, source/target physical life, tool lot, ammunition lot and current definition/effect commitment. It is not permanent permission to kill a person, a replacement with the same name or another target. A miss spends normal committed costs and another lethal attempt needs another review. A previously survivable attempt that becomes lethal due to concurrent injury stops before further damage/ammunition consumption. A valid already-confirmed possible-death consequence covers health-only changes; changed bodies, tool/profile/scar effect, control, world or timeline invalidate it. Pending reviews expire after 60 real seconds and are not restored from saves; accepted actions retain their permission and ordinary completion checks.

The first implementation uses existing attacks and death, without clamping health at one, universal NPC unconsciousness or a distinct execution-only state. Animals do not confirm before killing humans. Base observed health is public; introducing private target health requires reviewing every-damaging-attempt disclosure before reusing this conditional UI, rather than leaking it through a prompt. Broader indirect harm and NPC/background conflict remain separate choices.
