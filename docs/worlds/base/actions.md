# Base-world action family facts

The bundled world's action families (gather, prepare, craft, cook, fish, harvest, eat, equip, strike, hunt) run through existing native adapters. Facts about them that other engine code needs are declared once in `BASE_FAMILY_FACTS` in [`worlds/base/actions.ts`](../../../packages/domain/src/worlds/base/actions.ts), beside the family balance values, and read from there:

- **Item outputs:** gather, prepare, craft, cook, fish and harvest can leave actual items a later step may use. Gather, prepare, craft and a successful fish attempt supply a single item receipt; cooking does so when its selected preparation has one output. Multiple preparation/harvest outputs need named output ports. An empty cast supplies no item, so a dependent step cannot treat its attempt as a catch. Picking up is the engine's own single-receipt family. Native plan validation and output resolution retain actual identities and quantities.
- **Tool fields:** a request's chosen tool is kept when a strike uses it as its weapon, a hunt as its weapon or ammunition, a fish attempt as its reusable work tool, or an equip as the item equipped.
- **Units per command:** each eat command handles one unit, so "eat 3" is three commands and keeps an exact amount of 3.
- **No pause once working:** strikes and hunts under way cannot pause for other work; they finish or are replaced.
- **Cooking:** reads installed [food preparation definitions](../../food-preparation.md), with exact ingredient roles/quantities, products, work and heat. Meat and river fish are authored definitions in the same owner, not a universal input/output pair.

Balance values stay with their owners ([survival](survival.md), [combat](combat.md)). Older native adapters still contain some wording and item literals of their own; those are listed as remaining work in the [base-world tracker](../../maintainers/base-world.md).

**Selected native animal change, runtime pending — CTR03–CTR04:** the [animal response contract](animal-responses.md) replaces the current shared flee-speed/steering adapter with installed native response and species locomotion profiles. Escape and ordinary wandering use one actual locomotion owner; movement prediction, energy work, sensory crossings and projectile contact read the same trajectory and speed. Airborne birds remain with the flight owner, including finite escape landings; territorial stag withdrawal delegates to the shared response without changing its antler timings or pursuit pace. This is coded world policy with no animal Decision API calls. It is not implemented by adding competing family facts or writing positions from a second controller.

Gathering choices use this world's authored description and the same best compatible carried-tool yield as native gathering, capped by the observed remaining supply. A learned technique or an uncarried tool does not increase the advertised yield. When a large inventory is shown as a page, choices identify the yield supported by the inspected possessions and disclose that omitted carried tools may change the final result; preparing choices does not rescan all possessions.

## Player-facing weapon commitments

The bundled player catalogue's supplemental wording is authored beside `nativeActivityView` in `worlds/base/action-views.ts`. Melee and ranged choices bind an exact accessible compatible tool and its real equipment use; an explicit auto-equip choice atomically puts away conflicting items. Ranged preparation holds one compatible projectile without spending it. **Aim** remains prepared until **Shoot** or cancellation; **Shoot once** and ranged hunting release once automatically after preparation. The release rechecks tool, ammunition, sight, range, permission and exact lethal review, then spends one projectile. Its committed straight flight checks moving bodies and solids rather than rechecking the shooter's later range/sight or causing immediate injury. Neither a shot nor a melee attempt promises a hit or kill. [Ranged counterplay](ranged-counterplay.md), [targeted execution](../../targeted-actions.md) and [player action discovery](../../action-experience.md#ordinary-player-action-discovery) own the mechanics and presentation journey.

Complete player discovery exposes each permitted accessible offer/fuel lot, including contents; selected-item discovery binds that exact lot. Compact social/fuel builders retain their existing defaults for other consumers. This changes discovery coverage, not the authored transfer/fire rules or native admission. [Review evidence](../../verification/player-clarity-ui.md#requested-review-follow-up--october-4-2026) records exact later/nested lot discovery and actual fuel consumption.

Player cooking choices bind the selected preparation, each exact ingredient lot and a perceived fire; inventory enters that choice through **Explore uses and targets**. The actual fire panel exposes the same choices. A fire going out causes native refusal or interruption rather than substitution. Fiber/cord preparation remains supply-based: it can consume across carried lots, which the player catalogue explains. Food preparation instead binds one sufficient lot per named role under [AV-L03](../../limits/food-preparation.md#av-l03--finite-river-casts-and-preparation). The [river profile](river-fishing.md) owns the tool, two reaches and food rules.

## Contextual default actions

Command-click selects the same world-ranked exact offer shown in the object's action catalogue. Resource gathering, collecting loose items, harvesting, fishing and practice lead their relevant object choices; living speech-capable characters normally offer **Speak to**. Generic movement, inspection and stopping work are not object defaults. These priorities live in `worlds/base/context-actions.ts`, reached through the existing selected-world composition owner; they do not install an unsupported action or change native prerequisites.

A currently visible living body switches that shortcut to an attack when either person has a committed attack against the other's current physical life. Preparation, approach and strike recovery retain that relationship. Private queued plans, old attacks and remembered hostility do not. An active projectile preserves the relationship while it remains in flight, but an incoming projectile identifies an aggressor only when the character actually witnessed that source firing at them and can still associate that evidence with the visible source. Missing or forgotten evidence does not infer a shooter from hidden flight data. Cancellation, completion, loss of sight, departure or a replaced life ends the relevant relationship; no persistent universal “in combat” flag is saved.

The exact attack already chosen by the player takes precedence, then an equipped weapon, then other offered attacks. A prepared manual aim instead selects **Shoot** for that exact aim and target; it never releases at another clicked object. A temporarily unavailable preferred attack shows its native reason rather than changing to speech or selecting another weapon. A shortcut begins one chosen attempt, never an automatic repeated-attack loop, and lethal review remains required when native admission requests it.

Menu preparation checks the two selected bodies and, only when needed, scans current projectiles once. Incoming-shot attribution reuses the bounded resident awareness tail with its existing recognition guard; it does not load historical memories or build menus every frame. [Verification](../../verification/player-experience-overhaul.md) distinguishes the small native scene from population-scale qualification.

**Selected continuation, runtime pending — CTR02/CTR09:** [Combat and threat responses](../../projects/combat-and-threat-responses-feature-spec.md) extends this current shortcut with exact observer-scoped, per-opponent recent danger and own offensive preference. The selected grace preserves a relevant attack response after current targeting ends; ordinary observations and hover do not renew it. Complete permitted evidence replaces the current whole-projectile menu scan. Exact displayed-choice validation, one finite attempt, real admission and explicit social choice remain required. The paired technical design owns the selected deadlines and refresh rules; the paragraphs above describe the current baseline.

## Installed action meaning

The bundled `native-activities.ts` descriptors install gathering, plant preparation, manufacture, hunting, harvest, cooking, fishing, teaching and practice separately; fire care retains its existing descriptor. Their exact definitions include authored descriptions, fallback explanations, tool fields, meaningful output behavior and safe-pause meaning. Shared readers consume those selected descriptions rather than reconstructing a bundled action list. Camp compositions additionally declare the native operations they require, so a world omitting gathering or fire care offers only compositions it can support. Material/equipment shortages remain temporary prerequisites for installed actions.

The trusted composition links these implementations without a runtime code loader. Native operation types/argument validation remain closed and protected; descriptor prose grants no authority. Expanding the shipped family set requires a trusted implementation serving a real consumer under EWF/AC, not a new string in saved data. [Actual omission and continuation evidence](../../verification/world-engine-boundaries.md#installation-action-meaning-and-current-bundled-gameplay) covers this scope.
