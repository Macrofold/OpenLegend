## Foreground capacity

Use **ten detailed subjects at Focus 50**. Resolve an explicit authored actor override first; otherwise `round(5 + effective Focus / 10)` on the world's 0–100 scale, giving 5–15. Missing Focus uses the authored ordinary value 50. An override must fit the installed bounded actor profile and its qualified service envelope, not an arbitrary client integer. At default capacity reserve five soft ongoing places, four competing-cue places and one discovery place. Otherwise reserve one discovery place and split the remainder, with the extra place favoring ongoing work. The selected world profile owns this split and the score ceiling below. Lend unused places. A group cannot occupy one place while concealing unlimited individual detail.

These allocations govern detailed temporal retention, **not visibility**. Broad current received surroundings and static geometry remain available beyond them. Automatic selection retains any supported received temporal changes without a follow/watch action. Focus changes neither Perception, gaze, exact truth nor required bodily/effect evidence. Old historical occupancy never blocks selecting a new subject.

Direct involvement and established explicit/current-work focus have priority classes. Within optional competition, use `min(10, physicalCue + 3 * familiarity / 100)`. Read the one legitimately associated valence-neutral [familiarity score](perception-recognition.md) from its owner; do not duplicate it or infer affection/hostility. Unrecognized sources cannot borrow a known person's score through private identity. This modest bonus favors familiar companions and rivals without making either a guaranteed focus or reducing real new warnings.

Set `physicalCue` to the maximum of received event urgency (0–10), 2 for newly received qualitative evidence, and `min(4, floor(received displacement / 2 metres))` since the last presented change. Use the uncertainty-safe lower displacement; absent/unsupported terms are zero. An installed sense may supply another declared normalized intensity term. Do not sum samples or read private intent/importance. An unchanged description is not novel because its position changed. Future semantic preferences may request offered subjects during ordinary thought; no reflection-generated profession/object whitelist is selected.

Keep optional incumbents for 120 game seconds (two normal-play seconds) unless a higher interruption class arrives. Afterwards a challenger needs a two-point margin; break ties by earlier selection then stable local exposure order. Ongoing reservations are soft and empty lanes lend capacity. One waiting discovery subject keeps its place even when urgent subjects fill every other place; required causes and physical effects remain independent. A new cue about an existing subject updates that entry.

Actually received event urgency and directed involvement last at most that same stability window before the next selection drops their priority. A weaker later event cannot renew an earlier stronger urgency. Correcting its exact source removes the derived priority. Showing a body alone does not acknowledge a later heard/seen event; description and movement acknowledgments retain their separate exact revisions. Routine reinforcement and these deadlines alone do not buy cognition. An unseen voice cannot attach priority to a current body through a hidden identity match.

For a justified ordinary turn, use the existing one-pending-ticket coalescing and aim for useful publication in roughly two to five real seconds under admitted load. Pending cause families combine without losing newer generations; durability, active-work and dispatch capacity bound admission. This introduces no quiet-delay timer or changed clock cadence. This is an operating objective, not a measured guarantee or a separate five-second cancellation deadline. Use existing director/per-actor exclusion/provider/world spending admission. Its current two-pipeline/one-ordinary limit is not a population result. Configure finite shared throughput within actual provider, spending and host capacity; overload leaves one coalesced current opportunity per actor. A few hundred active people need not all be interpreting at once. Actual meaningful-event/choice rate determines model demand. Existing-thinking-only mode can disable the optional free-interval call without changing received evidence or memory.

The clock is 60 game seconds per real second. At nominal walking speed 0.11 metres/game-second, three real seconds allows 19.8 metres of uninterrupted travel. Interpretation cannot guarantee prevention of an imminent consequence. ## Recent history and lasting memory

Equal received values share source fragments at the append boundary; saved current-source references reuse unchanged retained values across later confirmations and restoration. Personal receipt times and observer-specific variants remain independent. There is **no separate background-history sampler**. Broad current evidence/current motion remains available, but a longer route begins only with actual detailed attention or an independently required personal record. Ordinary heard speech retains its separate finite exact-source tail even without a detail entry; it is not a temporal route or an automatic thinking request. Another person's surviving source history never grants retrospective witnessing. The [shared timeline contract](../../projects/attention-and-scenes-tech-design.md#53-recent-chronology-without-narrative-boundaries) owns exactness, compression and cold reads.

While detail is active, newly received terrain is recorded once with the attended subjects it accompanied. A later attention entry can point to still-current terrain at its original measurement time. When a view or attention scope changes, or before preparing actual cognition, a compact confirmation preserves only the static interval the native receiver actually certified; repeated preparation at the same supported time adds nothing. Ordinary unchanged sensing adds no copied terrain. If old recorded support retires while the view remains current, the next native receiver phase records a charged current baseline without resensing or renewing its original acquisition time. Retained historical measurements use these original facts and intervals, never the later map.

## Busy-worker attention experiment — not retained

**Tested and rejected for adoption, updated 2026-10-16 23:33 EDT.** The initial PF17.5 trial let autonomous people busy gathering, preparing or crafting miss unimportant neighboring routine work. Their own outcomes, direct involvement, speech and important events stayed. This would reduce incidental personal chronology; missed facts were never fabricated later.

The controlled scenario reduced new personal observations from 2,012 to 1,244, preserving all 51 own preparation results, but did not reduce the counted physical/status/sight calculations. The combined game observation failed at 0.432× under different host load and unequal activity. It provides no acceptable full-game gain to justify adopting lost recollection. The runtime rule and its unused engine hook were removed; ordinary sensory-eligible event recording remains current.
