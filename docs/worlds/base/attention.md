# Attention and recording in the base world

**Core recommendation; runtime pending.** The [feature specification](../../projects/attention-and-scenes-feature-spec.md), [technical design](../../projects/attention-and-scenes-tech-design.md) and [research comparison](../../projects/attention-and-scenes-research.md) distinguish this proposal from delivered behavior. Focus is not currently installed among the base-world attributes. [PS-L02](../../limits/product-scalability.md#ps-l02--attention-and-sensory-grouping) and [PS-L21](../../limits/product-scalability.md#ps-l21--initial-attention-and-scene-coverage) retain limits and qualification.

## Foreground capacity

Recommend **ten total tracks at Focus 50** on the world's 0–100 scale. One world-owned resolver applies an explicit authored capacity override or **round(5 + effective Focus / 10)**, yielding 5–15 tracks. This replaces the earlier six-track proposal; it is reversible gameplay tuning, not measured human or engine capacity. The engine consumes the resolved value without a second writable derived capacity.

At the default, give **five soft reservations to ongoing subjects**, **four to competing salient subjects**, and **one to discovery**. For other capacities, reserve one discovery place and divide the remainder with the extra place favoring ongoing subjects. Lend unused reservations. Urgency can preempt optional attention; equal salience should not churn it. Expired, deliberately replaced or no-longer-relevant subjects release their places. A new event about an already followed subject updates that thread rather than claiming another place.

A track covers one subject or bounded interaction with exact received references, not an unlimited crowd or transcript. Admitted watch capture consumes this finite allocation even if a particular prompt omits its detail. Previous watched histories continue consuming the aggregate recent-history byte budget after their live track is released; rapid switching cannot evade retention cost or erase prior promises. A real watch interruption must be explicit through its owner; a prompt layout change does not interrupt the watch or erase its past. Focus does not change Perception, sensory delivery, physical gaze, required bodily/native effects or evidence obligations. Those paths still consume resources under their own bounds.

## Unfamiliar surroundings

Reserve one total place for an eligible unconsidered current exposure; lend it when empty. Preserve a stable oldest-eligible frontier so repeated loud arrivals do not restart the queue. Routine coordinate updates do not renew novelty. Scanning alone does not count as consideration: acknowledge only the exact meaning actually supplied to admitted cognition, preserving newer meaningful changes. An unavailable attempt does not consume the turn or authorize a retry.

Advance at eligible ordinary/quiet thinking opportunities; optional background interpretation follows the technical design's bounded admission. This promises eventual opportunity in a finite stable scene with continuing service, not instant comprehension of every object or a deadline during unending emergencies. Departed unrecorded cues create no lifetime backlog. Personal associations use actual descriptions and knowledge, without compiled object-category lists. A relevant portrait can be recognized only if the received detail and personal knowledge support that connection.

## Recent history and lasting memory

Recommend a **six-game-hour recent-detail window**, matching the existing routine-context horizon as a starting hypothesis: **six minutes of normal play at the current 60:1 clock**. Pause freezes its game age; acceleration spends it faster. This is an age promise, not a byte limit or a new sampling cadence. Qualify actual source rates, bytes, density and speed before enabling a watch reservation. A future wider window needs corresponding resources; storage failure must not masquerade as fictional forgetting inside a promised window.

Keep the received ordered sequence for admitted watches, attended interactions and required personal consequences. Encode repeated data and deltas losslessly; do not collapse a witnessed detour into endpoints or infer stillness between equal samples. Contact loss remains a coverage transition with last observed location, not an automatic lasting episode. Recent history does not require naming an activity or deciding narrative boundaries.

| Information                                                              | Recommended lasting treatment                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Routine scenery, passive coordinates and ordinary meter drift            | Current support and admitted recent coverage only; no lasting entry merely for exposure, resampling or loss.                                                    |
| Watched movement and interaction                                         | Reliable recent received sequence; promote a supported meaningful consequence or an explicit choice to remember, with needed evidence.                          |
| Inspection, learning and recognition                                     | Preserve the acquired result and minimum support required by the knowledge/recognition owner, without pinning unrelated history.                                |
| Personal outcomes, bodily consequences, accepted work and exact language | Preserve each owner's required account and protection lifetime. Hearing or encountering someone does not pin an entire surrounding journal forever.             |
| Optional lasting accounts                                                | Finite record/byte retention with world-owned importance, age and reinforcement; older detail may fade. Interpretation remains distinct from exact observation. |

Use one experience admission policy for every automatic writer, including batch events, final facets and private results. Producers cannot confer persistence merely by supplying importance. Recent optional chunks and unneeded handles, versions and indexes actually expire. Long-term budgets include their source closure; consolidating prose while keeping every source is not a bound. Required lifetimes and optional quota values must be reconciled and sized through the existing memory/speech/knowledge owners during implementation; current protection is unchanged by this proposal. No fixed total lifetime bound is claimed while required records or creator audit remain unlimited.

Native attention, faithful recent history and expiry require no AI. Reuse existing thinking for semantic connections first; permit funded background batches only where their measured noticing benefit justifies preparation, retrieval, generation and retained-source costs. No per-coordinate calls, per-object reflection whitelist or unpaid backlog is selected.

## Busy-worker attention experiment — not retained

**Tested and rejected for adoption, updated 2026-10-16 23:33 EDT.** The initial PF17.5 trial let autonomous people busy gathering, preparing or crafting miss unimportant neighboring routine work. Their own outcomes, direct involvement, speech and important events stayed. This would reduce incidental personal chronology; missed facts were never fabricated later.

The controlled scenario reduced new personal observations from 2,012 to 1,244, preserving all 51 own preparation results, but did not reduce the counted physical/status/sight calculations. The combined game observation failed at 0.432× under different host load and unequal activity. It provides no acceptable full-game gain to justify adopting lost recollection. The runtime rule and its unused engine hook were removed; ordinary sensory-eligible event recording remains current.
