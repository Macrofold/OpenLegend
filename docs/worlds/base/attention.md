# Attention and recording in the base world

**Redesign recommendation, October 25, 2026; runtime pending.** The [feature specification](../../projects/attention-and-scenes-feature-spec.md), [technical design](../../projects/attention-and-scenes-tech-design.md#5-perception-attention-interpretation-and-memory) and [research comparison](../../projects/attention-and-scenes-research.md) own behavior and mechanism. Mike selected a few-real-second interpretation target with possible misses, reliable recent attended sequences, and useful lasting accounts only when warranted. Defaults below are reversible hypotheses for implementation qualification, not measured capacity. Focus is not currently installed among base-world attributes. [PS-L02](../../limits/product-scalability.md#ps-l02--attention-and-sensory-grouping) retains limits/status.

## Foreground capacity

Recommend **ten detailed subjects at Focus 50**. Resolve an explicit authored actor override first, otherwise `round(5 + effective Focus / 10)` on the world's 0–100 scale, giving 5–15. Use five soft ongoing reservations, four competing physical-cue places and one discovery place at the default; for other capacities reserve one discovery place and split the remainder with the extra place favoring ongoing work. Lend unused places. One subject's entry cannot conceal an unbounded group of individually tracked people.

These are detailed tracking allocations, **not a ten-object visibility limit**. A broad received scene separately represents additional bodies and static surroundings. Selection automatically captures that subject's received movement and interactions; no follow/watch action is required. Historical pool occupancy never refuses normal attention. Focus does not change Perception, physical gaze, required bodily evidence, native effects or exact source validity.

Start with a 120-game-second optional stability interval (two normal-play seconds) and a two-point replacement margin on the selected 0–10 physical-cue scale. Higher interruption classes may preempt it. Direct involvement/current work/explicit focus and fair discovery have stated lanes; a physical cue score is not semantic knowledge of danger or personal relevance. The initial physical-cue mapping is explicit: take the maximum of received event urgency (already 0–10), 2 for a newly received exposure/qualitative description, and `min(4, floor(received displacement / 2 metres))` since that change was last presented. Use the uncertainty-safe lower displacement; unsupported/absent terms contribute zero. Only a sense family with its own declared received-intensity mapping may add another normalized term. No summing repeated samples or scoring private importance/intent. Refresh these cheap terms on affected changes and clear presented transient causes at exact acknowledgment. This is deliberately coarse physical salience; personal meaning comes from cognition and can request focus. The engine executes the selected policy and stores its references, without hardcoded professions, family roles or destinations.

## Unfamiliar surroundings

Reserve discovery both in detail selection and in scene expansion. Supply up to eight oldest eligible received descriptions per cognition opportunity within the packet allowance; only one needs to retain a long detailed track. Reserve at least one quarter of optional scene-expansion work for waiting descriptions/regions, lending it when empty. The rest favors current anchors and measurable changes. A broad count does not acknowledge its hidden members' descriptions. Repeated coordinate samples do not renew novelty; qualitative new evidence remains eligible.

Initial optional scene-packet allowances are **12 KiB**, at most **48 broad rows**, and **128 hierarchy-node visits**. Reserve 3 KiB/eight rows for static geometry and 2 KiB/eight rows for discovery, lending unused space without permanently consuming either reservation. Expanded body rows may include up to two neighboring bodies and two observed physical boundaries. A scene row can be a coarse spatial aggregate with explicit unexpanded detail; it is not an unlimited transcript or evidence of shared intent. Required trigger/body/identity/obligation context is additional and retains existing complete-context admission.

Native geometry preparation allows at most 64 candidate visits per expanded anchor and 512 across the packet. Allocate initial shares across expanded anchors with a rotating remainder, reserving 128 of the 512 visits for later queries. Lend unused initial shares among anchors. A partial answer stays partial; it cannot establish absence or safety. Additional model-requested expansion uses at most four bounded queries and one further provider round trip within that same total allowance and admitted turn. These limits trade more uncertain detail for bounded preparation; changing them requires complete-caller qualification, not merely a larger prompt allowance.

## Semantic opportunities

Aim for useful unfamiliar-situation interpretation in **roughly two to five real seconds under admitted load**, with a three-second target. Use a 250 ms real-time coalescing opportunity and a three-second minimum interval between optional semantic dispatches for the same actor while meaningful unconsidered change remains. Existing required/urgent decision policy can take priority. A five-second optional request deadline covers preparation, provider work and publication after dispatch; queued age is measured separately and counts against the end-to-end target. Do not cancel already funded ordinary work merely to start optional noticing.

A new source/description, entry/loss, changed work or a cumulative received displacement of 2 metres or heading change of 30 degrees from the last eligible cut can mark a situation changed. Thresholds apply only where that measurement is received and uncertainty permits it. Count cumulative change from the retained anchor so tiny steps cannot evade the threshold. Pure freshness renewals do not wake thought. Quiet discovery becomes eligible after three real seconds when an eligible unconsidered current description remains. A failed/uncertain dispatched cause becomes dormant at that exact revision and discovery advances to other candidates. A timer or a new name for the same opportunity cannot renew its attempt; genuinely new evidence or an independently justified ordinary decision is required. Failure never counts as consideration.

Use the existing director, per-actor exclusion, provider/world admission and accounting. Its current two-pipeline/one-ordinary limit is not a population solution. Configure shared throughput only within actual provider, spending and host capacity; benchmark the permitted cohort before promising the target. With 300 independently novel perspectives every three seconds, demand is 100 decisions/second. Queue fairness cannot make an undersized service meet that demand. Continuing known native work and unchanged surroundings need no such rate.

The base clock is 60 game seconds per real second. At the current nominal walking speed of 0.11 metres/game-second, three real seconds permits 19.8 metres of uninterrupted walking, more under acceleration. Optional interpretation cannot guarantee prevention of an imminent physical consequence. Native supported reactions remain immediate under their separate owners. Pausing stops game-age expiry; real provider deadlines continue. Acceleration does not multiply funded semantic throughput or create an owed catch-up queue.

## Recent history and lasting memory

The following are initial **logical payload** allowances. Physical database/index overhead, replicas/checkpoints and resident copies require separate accounting. Shared bytes are charged once to each owning class's complete support, without duplicate payload storage.

| Class                                                                                    | Proposed bound                                                                    | Consequence                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sampled broad sensory buffer                                                             | 15 game minutes preferred age; 128 KiB / 2,048 samples per actor                  | Supplies sparse preceding context; neither unattended continuity nor every source is guaranteed.                                |
| Attended recent detail                                                                   | Six game hours preferred age; 8 MiB / 131,072 changes per actor                   | Six normal-play minutes, reliable at actual received resolution within the qualified aggregate envelope.                        |
| Chunk / resident recent tail                                                             | 64 KiB target; two active/tail chunks per actor, plus a bounded shared read cache | Select time/subject ranges before decoding cold chunks; no whole-window hydration.                                              |
| Optional lasting impressions, ordinary person/place knowledge and their complete support | 4 MiB / 2,048 records per actor                                                   | Replace or forget optional records by significance, capped reinforcement and age.                                               |
| Active required accounts/support                                                         | 4 MiB / 512 records as the initial admitted-work envelope                         | Existing obligations cannot be evicted to fit; admission and required-storage backpressure must preserve them.                  |
| Exact ordinary speech tail                                                               | Up to 512 eligible sources and 8 MiB, whichever is reached first                  | Whole older optional sources leave exact protection; active exact-language obligations retain their necessary terms separately. |
| Derived search artifacts                                                                 | 1 MiB per actor                                                                   | Evict replaceable vectors first; exact required lookup remains available.                                                       |

Sample changed background sources at most once per 15 game seconds at an existing receipt/commit boundary, allowing at most 16 visited sources/samples and 1 KiB of complete encoded payload per actor per opportunity. Unused allowance expires; missed opportunities produce no catch-up work. At the normal clock these are a quarter-second cadence and 4 KiB/second; acceleration increases real-time demand. Use a rotating dirty-source cursor, one latest pointer per source and metadata-first admission before allocating historical payloads; oversized or unavailable entries consume a visit and yield. Store exact sampled values with explicit sparse coverage. Do not write every intervening background update or later present these samples as a continuous route. Current receipt and exact attended/required capture remain independent of sampling. A stable 300-source dirty set takes roughly five normal-play seconds for one fair pass at the maximum visit rate; the current broad scene, not this sparse preview, supplies immediate context.

Both current and former detailed subjects share the same recent pool. Lossless encoding preserves intermediate measurements and received discrete events; chunk boundaries need no story label. Unchanged source values share references, but repeated endpoints do not prove an unobserved interval. Contact loss retains last received location and the loss boundary without automatically creating autobiography. Technical overflow reports the actual shortened coverage, not fictional loss of sight.

An optional impression is at most 4 KiB with 16 claims and eight provenance spans; its complete support still counts against the combined allowance. Unsupported or oversized optional proposals are declined, not silently clipped. Semantic prose is a fallible claim; native weakening may discard it whole and render surviving structured facts. These limits do not truncate a necessary exact knowledge/obligation capsule.

On recent expiry, preserve minimum exact support for active work, learned capabilities and still-retained recognition/names/places. Personal outcomes remain exact through bounded preparation/in-flight consumption; a merely queued optional opportunity owns no retention pin. Expired real-time consumption leases release their pins without marking evidence considered. One game day is a preferred ordinary aftermath horizon once those consumers release them: pressure may then retain a smaller warranted account or expire optional aftermath. Reserve active-obligation support before new voluntary acceptance and leave supported native-batch headroom; expiring real-time job pins and optional cleanup cannot depend on advancing a held game clock. Completed obligations then release unnecessary detail. The finite speech-tail proposal changes today's count-only protection; it does not change what was heard or authorize clipping a quote. If an unavoidable native batch still cannot be durably recorded, existing journal admission holds it before installation and reports technical capacity failure; it never claims an unrecorded effect completed. Required overflow is not fictional forgetting and has no unlimited emergency archive.

Optional lasting records start with native recording reasons, actual consequences or a supported choice to remember; passive sighting/resampling/loss supplies none. For optional eviction, rank explicit choices to remember above source-backed personal consequences/learned associations, then other warranted interpretations; within a class prefer more meaningful reinforcement, then newer experience, with source order breaking ties. Explicit retention is a preference, not permanent immunity. Active required support is outside this optional ranking. Ordinary impressions become due for weakening after seven game days and expire after thirty without meaningful recall/reinforcement; existing important character/knowledge owners can retain a useful selected account within their quota. Recall reinforces at most once per game day, not once per index/read/UI refresh. Native weakening removes optional detail and regenerates corresponding text; meaningful semantic reinterpretation needs admitted cognition.

These allowances do not promise fixed total storage for unlimited learned capabilities, unresolved obligations, creator audits, world-event archives or save copies. Bound those through their existing owners, and report their separate growth. Routine character memory must not grow with every lifetime encounter. Optional retention includes exact source closure, versions, identities, provenance and index entries; a short summary with an eternal raw archive fails this policy.

No mandatory gist or AI call is created for each sequence. When no useful interpretation completes, native required facts survive and trivial optional experience may disappear. A character can infer a kitchen from observed appearance/activity; an inferred category is separate from an acquired proper name or observed room extent. Remembered impressions cannot later supply expired coordinates, quotations or unknown identities as exact evidence.

## Busy-worker attention experiment — not retained

**Tested and rejected for adoption, updated 2026-10-16 23:33 EDT.** The initial PF17.5 trial let autonomous people busy gathering, preparing or crafting miss unimportant neighboring routine work. Their own outcomes, direct involvement, speech and important events stayed. This would reduce incidental personal chronology; missed facts were never fabricated later.

The controlled scenario reduced new personal observations from 2,012 to 1,244, preserving all 51 own preparation results, but did not reduce the counted physical/status/sight calculations. The combined game observation failed at 0.432× under different host load and unequal activity. It provides no acceptable full-game gain to justify adopting lost recollection. The runtime rule and its unused engine hook were removed; ordinary sensory-eligible event recording remains current.
