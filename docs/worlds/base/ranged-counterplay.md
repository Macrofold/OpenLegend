# Ranged counterplay and evasion

**Implemented October 9, 2026, integrated in current main as checked October 14.** These are bundled-world choices, with [native and database evidence](../../verification/rivals-and-contested-ground.md); independent-character and combined player qualification remain open. [NPC combat participation](lifecycle-and-protection.md#npc-combat-participation) supplies common permission rather than ranged geometry deciding eligibility.

## Ranged tools and preparation

Use existing compatible sling/bow definitions, finite ammunition, injury, range and accuracy values. The existing 18-game-second shot preparation remains the default for those current families; execution and ordinary choice facts use the installed handling preparation when one applies. Add authored flight speed: **0.5 metres per game second** for stone and **0.75 metres per game second** for arrows. At the installed normal 60:1 time rate, these are respectively 30 and 45 metres per real second. This is tuning for a coarse straight-flight game, not a physical claim about historical weapons. Range stays the actual installed launcher range; flight ends at contact or maximum range.

Holding prepared aim is allowed until canceled, interrupted or its prerequisites cease to be usable. It has no aim-duration accuracy bonus or automatic release deadline. It occupies the actor's existing physical-action lane and holds one projectile through normal claims. Movement cancels aim. No new aim-fatigue meter is added merely to justify a duration limit.

At release, clamp the existing base accuracy plus the applicable competence contribution to the supported probability range. One saved draw selects steady versus deviated aim. Steady aim uses the observed target point. Deviated aim uses a uniformly selected direction in the plane perpendicular to the shot and an offset of **two observed target capsule radii** at that point. The resulting direction is then fixed. These are two draws only for a deviated release, not per frame. A target moving into or out of the resulting ray can still change contact, so the accuracy field is described as **base aim steadiness**, not an eventual hit guarantee. No extra fleeing multiplier is applied after geometry already accounts for target movement; this replaces the previous 0.85 hunting modifier; AV03 competence now contributes to release steadiness.

The compiled base-world source owns this error law and its outcome wording; native release still owns permission, the ammunition debit and the resulting flight. Current launcher families bind to this one law. An installed alternative error distribution is not supported yet: add a qualified family/profile binding when a selected world needs one, preserving the common collision executor and saved random sequence. Moving the rule to its world source does not establish arbitrary ballistic extensibility. [Boundary review evidence](../../verification/rivals-and-contested-ground.md#world-and-engine-boundary-review--october-14-2026) preserves exact prior directions and draw counts.

The first flight family has point projectiles, straight travel and body/solid interception. It does not model arc drop, thickness-based penetration, ricochet or retained spent ammunition. **Current runtime:** a non-target body intercepts without injury; no collateral attack permission is inferred. **Selected October 17 revision:** [actual contact consequences](#selected-contact-consequences) replace that target-only immunity under CTR01. CF02 extends AV02's original contact-only guard to the supported stone/arrow projectile impacts through that same resolver: use the incoming trajectory direction at contact, the shield's existing sector/reduction and one-interception limit. The selected revision also applies that guard to contact on an unintended interceptor's own body; it does not add an area shield. Tuning a different supported launcher must change authored definitions rather than branch on an enemy's identity.

## Selected contact consequences

**Selected October 17, 2026; CTR01 implementation remains open.** A projectile is a physical committed effect. It damages what it actually contacts according to the installed world/material law, regardless of which body was selected at release. This world allows direct human PvP and collateral injury to vulnerable humans, NPCs and animals. Friendship, the intended target, an attacker's change of mind, and the word **practice** grant no projectile immunity.

The engine supplies ordered swept collision and typed contact-effect execution. The bundled world selects **damage on actual contact**, using existing captured launcher/ammunition injury and the contacted body's current susceptibility and defenses. A different installed world can supply a supported harmless training-contact profile or different participant policy. Do not hardcode the wilderness's damage rule in collision detection, client targeting or generic actor classification.

### First contact and material meaning

Keep the current finite straight-flight family: choose the earliest exact swept contact in the interval, preserve the existing stable contact tie order, apply one impact, and stop that projectile. “Anything touched” does not mean passing through the first person to damage everyone behind them. Multiple damaging contacts require a separately designed penetration, ricochet or fragment family.

Every real contacted collider is offered the selected contact response. Living bodies use the existing contact/body-effect owner, including full and partial shield prevention. Damage means the actual effect after that physical defense; a shield may reduce injury to zero without making a target immune by identity. The source body's launch-overlap exclusion is limited to exiting its own launch capsule; do not exclude that actor forever. If the same physical source body later intersects its own flight, it receives the ordinary contact consequence. An invalid muzzle/launch geometry refuses before ammunition is spent.

Nonliving colliders use their installed material/contact response. A practice target can record a scored impact; a supported destructible object uses its existing durability/material-effect owner; ground, rock and current rigid cover stop a projectile through material resistance. Do not manufacture a health bar, arbitrary destruction or a silent target-based exception for an object without a supported damage operation. The first implementation's mandatory expansion is bodily collateral; generalized structural breakage, penetration depth and visible surface wounds remain an explicitly separate material capability. The engine must still emit the true material interception, not report an intended-body hit. This is a limit of the supported material model, not a claim that arrows can never damage wood.

### Release is the commitment boundary

Before release, revalidate the human's exact source/target life, control, tool/ammunition, physical feasibility and any required [final-blow review](player-danger.md#final-blow-confirmation). The review explains that a shot can also hit other bodies. It authorizes this one physical release under the current world's contact law; it is not a guarantee of hitting only the reviewed person. Do not scan hidden nearby bodies to decide whether to show a warning or disclose them in a modal.

After release, trajectory, maximum range, injury inputs, source attribution, issuing-controller provenance and contact-policy pin are fixed. The original target remains historical aiming intent. Actual collision binds the current physical life that is struck, including an unintended body or a new life physically crossing the ray. There is no retargeted command or magical tracking. The original target's later injury, control change, death, disappearance or successor does not suppress damage to another real intersecting body. Stop the old contact-time exact-target/final-review check from turning released ammunition harmless. Ordinary source death, departure, tool removal or target loss also cannot recall a shot. Do not require a live source entity to apply an already committed effect; retain exact historical attribution without giving the source new personal observations.

Current participation remains authoritative at contact. An exposed fading player is vulnerable. A fully inactive player is absent from physical projectile-body participation and protected under the existing departure rule; do not render them as a tangible harmless interceptor. At the departure deadline use the existing expiry-before-tied-impact ordering. Account authority and unused action permission still cannot be transferred through a shot.

The world cannot silently remove/change the meaning of a contact profile used by active flight. Preserve the admitted immutable pin until termination, or refuse an incompatible definition removal through the existing in-use dependency owner. A new profile applies to later releases. Explicit authorized world editing of existing effects remains a separate operation with its own truthful history.

### Practice, defense and evidence

Practice provenance selects scoring, not physical immunity. A shot released at a practice target carries its actual installed projectile injury. An intervening living body can be hurt; do not award a practice hit when the practice target was not struck. A separately authored harmless projectile must select its supported zero-injury/contact profile through the same release path. No universal `practice` branch may skip bodily effects.

An unintended body's correctly prepared shield can intercept contact on that body using the actual incoming direction, ordinary coverage and one-contact consumption. Moving in front of someone may therefore protect them through actual geometry, at risk to the interposing person. This supplies no group-protection aura, remote guard, compulsory rescue or automatic damage transfer.

Record attempted target/life separately from actual contact body/life/material, committed source/life and issuer, exact contact time/position, incoming direction, pre-defense injury, prevention, susceptibility, actual loss and terminal reason. Commit effects once before distributing observations. The contacted person's evidence includes their actual injury and only a source identity/direction they could know. A nearby animal can become alarmed from its permitted impact/attack evidence without learning an unseen archer's coordinates. The shooter learns a hit, miss or collateral result only when the ordinary sight/evidence rules establish it; firing does not grant remote telemetry. Personal appraisals and encounter continuity consume the actual observed contact, including meaningful blocked contact, rather than assigning fear to the original uninjured target.

CTR01 must exercise selected target hit; incidental human/NPC/animal hit; shielded incidental hit; intended target moving aside; several potential contacts with one earliest hit; current target-life replacement physically crossing versus not crossing the ray; source death/removal after release; practice interception; newly lethal injury after release; source re-entry after muzzle exit; active/fading/inactive participation and the exact deadline tie; blocked scenery; replay and current-format save/load. Ordinary ballistic range, injury and clock tuning remain unchanged by this permission revision. ## Evasion

The starting biped action permits a chosen destination up to **2 metres** away, traversed in **12 game seconds**, with **120 game seconds** between evasion commitments. At normal speed this is 0.2 seconds of movement and a 2-second repeat interval. Shorter selected distances use the same duration; no zero-distance action is admitted. Directional examples project onto the current supporting ground and stay within the same three-dimensional distance limit, including slopes. Collision and support remain authoritative throughout. Another body is an obstacle, not something to push through.

Player and character descriptions report the selected supported distance and installed duration/repeat interval from the same rule used by execution. The policy's qualitative wording carries no duplicate default numbers. Current-format continuation checks the captured profile, deadlines, speed and remaining direct path together; a changed waypoint cannot substitute another evasive destination. [Native caller/restoration evidence](../../verification/rivals-and-contested-ground.md#continued-native-collision-and-continuation-review--october-14-2026) includes a different 3.3-metre/seven-second/91-second profile without changing the starting world's tuning.

There are **no invulnerability frames**, no new stamina cost and no attack-damage bonus. A valid evasion can fail to escape an attack's actual reach. Normal walking stays available during the evasion repeat interval. Existing attack recovery survives switching to evasion, preventing it from becoming a way to remove weapon recovery. A canceled/interrupted committed evasion retains its repeat deadline; refusal before commitment spends nothing.

This world's actual health loss interrupts unreleased aim and evasive movement. Its compiled body-policy adapter selects that reaction; the existing body-effect owner cancels work, releases an unspent aim claim and retains committed evasion recovery. Different interruption laws need a supported binding under [EWF03](../../maintainers/extensible-world-foundation.md#ewf03--extract-default-body-and-need-policies-through-real-consumers); these native families do not yet select them from installed definitions.

These initial values should be assessed for useful choice, especially at the current accelerated clock and with keyboard/pointer input. Adjust through this same world owner when evidence shows a poor result; report the change and tradeoff. A small successful dodge does not establish optimal balance or universal reflex accessibility.

## Discovery and visible information

Use plain labels: **Aim at [target] with [tool]**, **Shoot**, **Cancel aim**, **Shoot once at [target] with [tool]**, and **Evade to [destination]**. Hunting can retain its world-authored purpose label while resolving to that same physical shot. Include relevant known distance, preparation, cost, observed health, actual tool and blocker, with optional detail for the other stats.

Release text says a shot was fired; impact text distinguishes observed target injury, shield prevention, scenery interception and a miss. Do not reveal an unseen interception location or body. A character knows their own evasion result and cooldown; an observer sees only what current perception permits. Current final-blow/PvP behavior remains installed until CTR01 delivers the selected release/contact revision; inactive protection and observer privacy remain controlling in both versions.

Visible body condition reports the installed meter's name, projected value and bounds only when its disclosure and current perception permit it. It does not infer death from a displayed zero: an installed meter can use a different range, and its body policy can incapacitate rather than kill. Base-world Health retains its own authored death/return explanation. Evasion readiness uses the installed action label, and client controls use the supplied label rather than copying the world's action name.
