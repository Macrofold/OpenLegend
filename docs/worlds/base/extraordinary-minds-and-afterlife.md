# Extraordinary minds and afterlife — proposed optional families

This profile accompanies [DG33's product specification](../../projects/extraordinary-minds-and-afterlife-feature-spec.md). It selects a voluntary conversation with a known deceased person, a separately earned ordinary return to life, and an independent deliberate telepathy family. These are proposed authored-world capabilities, not installed content, completed BW15 delivery or a mandatory magic progression. [BW15.1–BW15.6](../../maintainers/base-world.md#dg33--voluntary-spirits-and-ordinary-revival) owns the lifecycle consumer; [EWF10T.1–EWF10T.4](../../maintainers/extensible-world-foundation.md#dg33--deliberate-telepathic-messages) coordinates the selected message consumer through existing owners. [MIND-L01–MIND-L08](../../limits/extraordinary-minds-and-afterlife.md) records scope and remaining growth qualification.

## A conversation at the cairn

Eren is an adult speaking NPC whom the human already knows through actual acquaintance and prior play. The optional episode provides a real reachable Stillwater Cairn and a reason to remember Eren: perhaps the player finished the useful bag they had once discussed. The player may call, show what actually exists, talk, listen or leave. Eren may be interested, angry, uncertain, affectionate or unwilling. No mandatory secret, forgiveness, therapy outcome or quest completion is owed. The first selected body has no harvest output; that is an explicit authored qualification choice, not a statement about every current actor.

The creator supplies the real character history, supported body and speaking capability, actual acquaintance, anchor and invitation affordance. Merely writing a person's name, acquiring a global identifier or placing a story about them in a note does not establish recognition or permission to address them. The caller's choices contain only legitimately known, presently addressable candidates. There is no world-wide directory of dead people or omniscient indication of a stranger's death.

An awake, present caller at the real cairn deliberately chooses the known person and offers a short purpose. Preparation lasts 60 game seconds, one real second at the normal 60:1 clock. The caller must remain within 2.2 world units of the cairn. One caller may have one pending invitation or one active spirit encounter, and one retained person may have only one pending invitation or active manifestation. No scarce offering is consumed merely to ask; duplicate input cannot generate another invitation or another body. Contact exchange, telepathy-link and spirit invitations share TX-L02’s one outstanding outgoing request and three new offers per sender per rolling sixty real seconds. Dismissal suppresses another cue in the same attended encounter; reconnect or a new label does not bypass actual admission. Existing native and complete AI budgets still apply, and no identical failed or uncertain paid attempt repeats automatically.

The invitation permits one bounded consent decision with the caller, actual place and offered purpose. This temporary decision is distinct from ongoing afterlife cognition. A dormant person receives no ordinary senses, bodily depletion, dreams, unpaid work, hidden social life or lonely countdown. They can decline or give no response without a retrying paid scheduler. Provider failure is an operational failure, not evidence that the character refused. Before an accepted manifestation appears, current identity, death state, willingness, caller presence, anchor, family availability and authority must still permit it.

The manifestation is the same person. It can perceive the currently permitted local scene and speak through ordinary local sight/hearing rules. It has no physical cargo, harvesting, labor, weapon use, locomotion outside the selected anchor encounter, remote senses or authority over the living. Ordinary nearby people may hear actual speech if eligible; no private conversation or new knowledge is automatically granted to everyone in the world. Actual new experience can join the retained person's history, with its source and disclosure restrictions intact. Memory is neither omniscience nor guaranteed truth.

There is no fixed conversation countdown. The encounter continues while Eren is willing, the actual caller remains awake and present within the anchor range, and the supporting world service remains available. Either participant may end it. Caller departure, loss of participation, explicit world pause, supported family removal or successful revival also ends the manifestation. Returning later requires a new deliberate invitation. An interrupted preparation produces no spirit, resource loss or automatic repeat. Returning to dormancy preserves legitimate new experience without inventing activity between visits.

The normal world keeps its actual time and needs while the caller talks; the spirit has no survival meter. Finite service limits still apply. A long conversation does not buy unlimited hosting or an independent inference allowance. Explain a real service interruption through existing feedback without making Eren plead for attendance or blaming a fictional character for provider capacity.

## A separate wanted return to life

The ordinary revival option starts with the player's wish to have this willing person alive again. It does not require exhausting dialogue, earning a relationship score or performing a mandatory farewell. One actual finite return ember is available at the authored Windward Ledge. Reaching and bringing it back is a worthwhile outing using real supported travel, obstacles and possible avoidance, cooperation or invention. The creator must qualify the actual route and resource; a location name cannot supply danger, an item or a guaranteed solution. The proposed layout reuses the separately inspected [newer-main territorial-stag family](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/0a3ab79b7a698a7f1941dc23722f89220d1ba425/docs/worlds/base/first-threat-encounter.md): actual hoof/foliage signs, a short approach through its four-unit entry footprint, an ordinary longer ground approach outside that footprint, and real sight-blocking stone for retreat. The outer approach can pass beyond the eight-unit pursuit bound; the collection stance and its final approach must be placed and checked explicitly. A prior active pursuit is not made safe merely by stepping outside the entry footprint. The player may read the signs, observe, take the outer path, retreat or use an actually supported ranged tool with real ammunition. Current ordinary residents are excluded by that specific threat law, so asking a willing NPC for help can be a legitimate different solution. This newer-main source also carries its actual human death and five-second vulnerable-exit rules; the encounter package is a named dependency, not silently installed on this branch’s older collapse baseline. No obligatory combat, repeated random drop or ingredient checklist is added solely to lengthen the return.

The first profile supplies one nonrenewing ember. Its finite availability is explicit. This proves one useful return and its failures; it does not establish a world-wide one-revival law or sustainable supply for every future death. If someone else uses it or it is genuinely lost, the game reports the actual situation. A broader promise of repeated ordinary revival needs a supported resource source, opportunity cost and admitted workload. It cannot depend on an operator secretly replenishing stock.

The ritual occurs at the actual cairn with the exact available ember and the retained person's current willingness. Its 600 game seconds take ten real seconds at normal speed. Material is committed only with the successful once-only return. Interruption, stale consent, a competing successful revival, unavailable space or an invalid body transition leaves the attempt incomplete without consuming the ember as a retry fee. Previously admitted real movement or other actions remain real; cancelling the ritual is not a world rewind.

The person returns in one supported living body with full health and normal full needs. Age, identity, actual experience, relationships, permanent authored traits and existing scars remain. A new body is not youth, a debt reset or a second copy of the mind. Prior physical-life actions cannot strike or control the successor by stale binding. The old manifestation ends and any remaining old body matter is reconciled so there is no second active physical life. Actual separated equipment and belongings remain where their custody says they are; nothing is copied into the new hands or backpack.

No fresh corpse is required. The current three-game-day rot and seven-game-day removal of remains cannot impose a deadline on a beloved NPC's return. Their retained identity is the target, while actual surviving property and body matter follow their real owners. The first non-harvestable person needs no new generic harvesting subsystem. A later harvestable body must conserve already-used and remaining original yield, reconcile old remnants, and grant no additional output merely because of revival. Already distributed goods cannot be recalled or duplicated. Existing creator revival has its own administrative resource exception and is not an ordinary-gameplay precedent.

The full physiological restoration has real economic value, but the first ember is finite and already consumed for the return. Do not add a second hunger or exhaustion penalty at the reunion. A restored NPC chooses their next activity from current circumstances; they do not resume an obsolete plan, owe labor to the caller or become a controllable companion. A refusal to resume an old role is possible. Human collapse, Human Continue where separately delivered, PvP consent and final-blow policy retain their own owners and never require this ember or ghost encounter.

## Deliberate telepathic messages

This independent optional family helps a willing known living pair coordinate across an actual hearing obstruction. It is selected only where that is useful; ordinary local talk and DG24's separate slate family remain valid alternatives. Both parties must be capable, awake, present and in actual sight and reach when they consent. Acceptance creates one current pair per participant, with chosen messages supported within 30 world units measured by actual straight-line separation. Ordinary walls do not block this family. It supplies no image, sound stream, remote inventory, raw thought reading or automatic mental context.

A human writes and explicitly sends their chosen words. An NPC independently chooses whether to send or respond through its existing agency; awareness is not agreement or a new goal imposed by the sender. The message does not enroll a second local conversation, make words audible to bystanders or generate dialogue on behalf of a human. One-to-one text uses the same proposed content and shared sender allowance as [TX-L03–TX-L04](../../limits/narration.md#tx-l03--message-content-envelope): 2,000 Unicode code points and 8,000 UTF-8 bytes, six submissions per contact and twenty-four overall per rolling sixty real seconds. Text, calls and telepathy share that allowance; changing channel supplies no extra quota. Qualify a natural useful exchange before claiming these rates support rapid conversation.

Submitted confirms the sender's exact admitted submission only. There is no delivery, read, typing, presence, hidden death, range or blocked receipt. A recipient gains the chosen mental-message experience only if the link, range, current participation and privacy still permit delivery at the actual commit. Ineligible messages are not held for future reception or replayed when a person returns. No new inbox, passive thought feed, automatic ping or paid retry loop is selected.

Either participant can leave their own link immediately and stop future reception. This retires only their own pair place; it does not alter the other person's known count or announce a private reason. A peer may therefore retain a clearly described last-agreed link without knowing whether communication is currently possible. They can leave it at any time, or explicitly replace it through a new mutually consented local exchange. The interface must make this ordinary choice easy rather than presenting a secret remote status. A stale agreement never identifies a stranger or enrolls a returning human automatically. Loss of active participation/control, death, controller rebinding, explicit world pause or family removal ends the affected active selection; Resume does not rejoin it. Temporary range loss or sleep prevents new reception without queuing messages. Future messages may arrive after range or wakefulness returns only while an otherwise valid grant still remains; an ended grant needs a fresh mutually consented local exchange.

Human-private source restrictions follow the content into NPC experience, notes, reasons, memories, narration, inspection, exports and later ghost or living states. Becoming a ghost, joining another group, gaining a new controller, restoring a world or being viewed through God tools does not publish those derivatives. End removes the family's own permission and future effect; it does not erase legitimately learned public events or restore an old whole-mind snapshot. Compulsion, passive thought reading, shared memory, colony consciousness and multi-body control are separately scoped future families, not promised by this message channel.

## Continuity and qualification

Supported current-format save/restart must preserve one actual result: retained identity, physical-life succession, actual item custody, current permission, legitimate experience and once-only ember consumption. Restart does not replay a call, a thought or a return. Reconcile loss of the attended caller before representing a manifestation. Fictional restoration consumes DG25's protected coherent cut, preserving current privacy, real spending and external effects; an old save cannot create a second ember or undo another world's actual participation.

First qualify a complete optional local encounter and return, and the independent chosen-message use. Mature dead-person history, crowded anchors, repeated invitations, many active worlds and privacy derivatives require their existing memory, admission and service owners. Dormancy avoids recurring ordinary simulation but not durable storage or cold recall. A small displayed list or one active pair is not an aggregate workload bound. Larger qualification must preserve the ambition without blocking these smaller complete experiences.
