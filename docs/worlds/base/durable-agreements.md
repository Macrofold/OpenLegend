# A small wilderness commission

**Proposed DG22 world profile, October 6, 2026.** This is selected product behavior for [Agreements worth coming back to](../../projects/durable-agreements-feature-spec.md), not an installed economy or a change to D11/D18/D64's adoption status. Current context was inspected on main [0a3ab79b](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/commit/0a3ab79b7a698a7f1941dc23722f89220d1ba425).

## A reason to cooperate

The reference activity is an optional commission between two known people: one would like an understood small pouch for an outing; the other wants an offered food payment and is willing to make it. An illustrative bargain is **one empty small woven pouch for two cooked-meat items**, with no deadline and a planned meeting at the known camp. This is an example proposed by a participant, not a fixed fair price, compulsory request, standing order, automatic NPC goal or starting-world endowment.

The output definition must already be installed, known and supported. A maker needs the actual learned method to produce it and real supplies to work. Merely agreeing adds none of those. The customer can decline, make their own pouch, give a gift instead, change the outing or do nothing. The item provides its actual current organizational use; it grants no carrying strength, food preservation, waterproofing or group storage rights.

A useful scene can let the participants pursue separate activities and later return with what they chose. That needs real desire, distribution, knowledge or travel. The game must not slow ordinary weaving to justify a commission or require the human to leave the session while the maker performs a few seconds of work.

## First agreement rules

The family has **two parties**, **one item requirement on each side**, and a positive supported quantity for each. Future output is identified by its known installed definition and the accepted condition requirements. A later actual exchange selects **one exact lot on each side**, including whole individual objects and current bag/content rules. Empty is an actual requirement for the reference pouch.

There is no automatic equivalent-material substitution, mixed-goods bundle, unique-item loan, unspecified invention result, subjective labor assessment or automatic partial delivery. If the maker offers a different item, both review new exact terms. Actual receipt evidence is credited to the explicitly selected agreement once. Neither gathering nor crafting proves delivery.

No goods are held by the commission. Acceptance creates accepted intent and protected participant evidence; materials and payment remain their owners' current possessions and may be used elsewhere. Each later custody change needs the ordinary exact selected transfer and consent. Current reciprocal barter remains separately unimplemented PX02 work required for the final two-sided exchange.

Commission discussion, proposal communication, acceptance of terms, amendments and actual trade initially use local interaction with the named party. The normal item-handling reach is **1.6 world units** in the inspected world; current visibility, life, participation and authority remain necessary. A remembered deal does not grant remote stock inspection, communication or transfer. Durable status seen by an actor derives from the accepted terms and events that actor actually knows.

## Timing and admission

The default commission and its communicated proposal have **no automatic time expiry**. The proposer can withdraw an unaccepted proposal, and either participant can end an accepted undertaking unfulfilled. An optional reply-by time and an optional delivery due time are separately explicit game-clock choices. A delivery at the accepted due instant counts on time; only a later actual time is late. A pending proposal is no longer acceptable at or after its explicit expiry.

Display the actual game-time date and the offered clock's meaning. At current normal speed a game day is twenty-four real minutes while the world advances. There is no promise that an absent participant gets another real day or that the world continues without a separately offered service. Pause and attendance policy determine what time actually passes.

Do not extend an ordinary item offer into a durable commission. Its current **1,800 game-second** expiry equals thirty real seconds at normal speed and changes with acceleration. That remains the short final handover/exchange opportunity; the remembered agreement has a different purpose and lifetime.

A person can have **three pending durable proposals**, including their pending amendment proposals, separately from current short gift offers. An accepted commission consumes **one active undertaking place for each participant within the current sixteen unresolved-commitment allowance**. It does not grant sixteen additional protected promises. Admission with no place available refuses rather than evicting an older commitment. At most **one amendment** is pending on a given agreement.

End unfulfilled retires only the ending participant's active undertaking/scheduling entry and frees their place. The unaware counterpart keeps their known terms, count and independently chosen work until they receive permitted evidence or choose to end their own participation; no remote status/count change reveals a hidden decision. It preserves original terms, actual transfers, ending evidence and any remaining claim. That claim is shown as unresolved historical business, never paid or forgiven. Later actual payment or the entitled party's waiver can still resolve it. The complete claim history has no new total cap; it must not be loaded into every ordinary decision or silently discarded.

These modest limits keep the initial coordination intelligible, not economically balanced by decree. Repeated endings do not entitle a person to another advance: every new proposal and transfer requires independently renewed participation.

## Advances and remaining claims

The optional advance is **one exact present transfer** associated with an accepted revision. Its review separates what leaves now from the promised future result and any remaining payment. The maker explicitly accepts the actual goods. The goods are theirs to use under ordinary custody; no escrow copy, automatic refund, interest, penalty or collection power exists.

For example, a customer may separately agree to pay one of the two meat items now and one on delivery. The later exchange is the real pouch for the remaining one meat, not a second charge of two. If the advance was the whole payment, actual accepted delivery of the pouch can fulfill the remaining obligation without another payment.

No delivered output means no unconditional price debt or cancellation damages in payable-on-delivery terms. The first advance can be resolved by actual delivery of the remaining output, or by the claimant accepting return of the complete advance in the same definitions/quantities together with release of the prospective commission. A substitute requires a mutually accepted amended remainder. A dropped item or rejected offer settles nothing.

Failure does not reconstruct spent inputs. End unfulfilled retains the advance and claim; a later agreed repayment or waiver is separately recorded. A relative, association or new player inherits no liability by implication. Human Continue retains the stable person and their known agreement history while actual lost possessions remain lost. An NPC's death leaves unfulfilled evidence; it does not create revival or automatic inheritance. Private out-of-sight death is not disclosed through the agreement list.

## Small association

Select the association only for a repeated wanted cooperative purpose. A new record identifies its chosen name/purpose, invitation history, independently joined members, departure history and a named consenting custodian for any referenced kit. It grants no automatic access, physical custody, collective title, recurring contribution, treasury, wage, membership fee, leadership control, tax or platform permission.

The founding inviter maintains the name, invites/removes members and may close future coordination. A changed founding purpose requires a new independently joined association. Transfer of record stewardship needs the named successor’s assent. If the steward leaves with no successor, new invitations and coordination close; history, real objects and individual agreements remain. Ordinary absence alone changes no membership. These powers concern the record only and grant no inventory, labor or platform authority. Referenced custody is last legitimately known, not a remote live location.

A member may leave immediately. Contributions retain their actual gift/possession/agreement meaning. Separately permitted object access follows its own explicit controls and revocation; items already taken are not teleported back. A self-appointed steward cannot edit other people's bag permissions. Ordinary member-operated sharing would require a separately selected PO capability before the UI claims it works.

Dissolution retires invitations and future group coordination while preserving former membership, actual items and outstanding agreements. Two people can simply keep cooperating without any association record. No association is seeded into current starts.

[Current preparation rules](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/0a3ab79b7a698a7f1941dc23722f89220d1ba425/packages/domain/src/worlds/base/items.ts), [gathering/source rules](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/0a3ab79b7a698a7f1941dc23722f89220d1ba425/packages/domain/src/worlds/base/world.ts), [woven family](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/0a3ab79b7a698a7f1941dc23722f89220d1ba425/packages/domain/src/worlds/base/camp-container-family.ts). Inputs are actually consumed by the current work; interruption does not provide free refunds. Sources are finite and recipe knowledge supplies no ingredients.

At current food depletion, 300 game seconds of work costs 0.9 fullness. A minute of ordinary real-time negotiation at normal speed costs 10.8 fullness while that body's metabolism advances. These are arithmetic comparisons, not a recommended talk limit. They make human reading, menus and actual decision latency central to the whole experience.

**No currency, price table, infinite buyer or automated repeat contract is selected.** A later currency needs a real multi-party exchange problem plus complete issuance, acceptance, loss and circulation choices under D11/R09. Ordinary barter remains available without money.
