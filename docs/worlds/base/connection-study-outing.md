# Proposed connection-study outing

This is the selected fictional scene and preparation for [DG11's optional feasibility pilot](../../projects/wellbeing-evidence-feature-spec.md). It is not a change to ordinary new worlds and does not authorize a study. [WBE02/WBE05](../../maintainers/wellbeing-evidence.md) must qualify both formats through current controls before recruitment. [WE03](../../limits/wellbeing-evidence.md#we03--activity-and-response-time) inventories the proposed timing and preparation.

## A small ordinary outing

Use one inspected route from the familiar starting camp to the existing lookout, with a choice between ordinary berry gathering and reed fibers usable in current fiber/cord preparation. The return route, gathering opportunities and per-person available quantities must be physically usable in the selected world revision. Shared and separate solo offers use the same brief, difficulty, visible opportunities and per-person supplies. A pair does not receive twice the useful opportunity for only one participant; qualify simultaneous depletion/access behavior before operation.

These are separately prepared study worlds and disclosed study characters. New supplies are authored preparation, not an earned research reward, hidden refill or transfer from another community. The invitation explains that possessions and knowledge remain in those study worlds under the chosen access/retention terms. A player can use gathered berries to preserve carried meals or continue ordinary play later; choosing fibers offers a different useful result. Nothing requires a prescribed conversation, trade, gathering count or companion task.

## Clock, starting body and finite supplies

Select normal base speed: one real second equals sixty game seconds, as owned by [Time](time.md). Twenty real minutes therefore spans twenty game hours if uninterrupted. Keep this speed and the same starting game time in both formats. Record pauses, material speed deviations and elapsed game time; do not pretend that twenty minutes is a brief fictional walk. Changing the qualified speed or scenario creates a new protocol revision rather than an unreported convenience.

Each newly authored study participant starts at the normal 08:00 game time with health, food and energy at 100, and **six carried units of current cooked meat**, each restoring 38 food points under the [item definition](../../../packages/domain/src/worlds/base/items.ts). This full-energy preparation is explicitly different from the ordinary base character's default energy; it is not a reset of an existing character. Every participant receives the same preparation, including those offered separate solo worlds. Use current legitimate world-authoring/initialization authority.

Current [survival](survival.md) drains food at 0.003 points per game second: twenty game hours consume 216 points. Six meat units contain 228 points before the food meter's ceiling, in addition to the initial 100. This is a finite provisioning check, not automatic feeding or a promise that wasteful consumption prevents hunger. The ordinary brief shows how to inspect Food and choose Eat when needed; eating near full can waste nutrition under current rules. No timed study command chooses a meal, prevents depletion, replenishes used portions or rewards a questionnaire answer.

Provide an inspected ordinary location suitable for Sleep/Wake Up near the start and return route, and an actual usable existing recovery/exit path. [Sleep](sleep.md) governs expenditure, recovery and interruption. Players retain the choice to rest, continue or end early; a partner does not have to wait for a forced sleep to finish. The route excludes deliberately imposed combat/high-stakes loss, but does not acquire invulnerability or immunity from ordinary mistakes. Inventory, navigation, body clearance and rest must work through the real controls.

Keep any ordinary resident population, its disclosed preparation and operating settings consistent across formats. Residents retain their actual needs and choices; no silent feeding or scripted helpfulness repairs a study session. Any consequential resident or survival disruption is part of delivered-experience reporting, not a reason to remove an inconvenient participant after assignment.

## Reading forms without advancing a participant's life

Consent, instructions and baseline questions occur before the participant takes control in an advancing study world. The prepared world remains stopped through its actual supported pause/entry behavior until the relevant participant or pair is ready. Do not start someone's hunger clock while their partner reads the invitation.

At measured play end, the optional end form remains separate from active world controls. Offer its reading surface only after the participant uses an ordinary departure/protection route or a legitimately paused world. Do not presume that hiding a panel, being asleep or closing a browser instantly pauses needs. Qualify the actual body/pending-action behavior; if the route cannot honor this boundary, do not open the study yet.

A person may keep playing after the research segment. They can leave the form unopened while continuing; it retains the feature's original thirty-minute/collection-close deadline. The study never pauses a friend's world without authority, forces departure, cancels real work merely to obtain answers, or resets a delayed baseline. At an urgent moment, ordinary play takes priority and a missing answer is acceptable.

## Minimal operational evidence

The research record contains coarse real and game duration, the declared speed/profile revision and four per-segment interruption flags: food-related interruption, rest-related interruption, recovery used, and technical/pause deviation. Each flag is at most one yes/no occurrence indicator; use unknown when unavailable. No frequency series, meter history, body diagnosis, location trail or list of actions is collected. The flags explain substantial differences in the delivered offer and follow the same research withdrawal and retention rules as other operational records.

Equal supplies do not imply equal experiences or remove the consequences of coordinating with a partner. Report those differences rather than controlling every action or deleting them from the comparison. A food/rest-heavy outing may show that this chosen scene is unsuitable for the selected research question; improve the ordinary activity or stop the study rather than hiding needs.
