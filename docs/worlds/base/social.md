# Relationships, feelings and promises

Character authorship follows [evolving history and disposition](../../projects/compelling-characters-feature-spec.md#character-authorship-and-changing-personality): starter Ada enjoys quiet animal observation, without a supply condition or standing camp instruction. Ordinary spoken welcome/refusal reaches cognition; it is not a special social mechanic. [PG02 comparisons](../../verification/cognition-context.md#october-5--unified-experience-and-unprescribed-choices) retain actual choices, failures and limits.

This page owns the bundled world's current social rules and their visible limits. It does not prescribe universal kinship, emotion or obligation laws for the engine. General ownership and privacy remain in [Architecture](../../architecture.md#actor-means-any-living-being), [knowledge](../../knowledge.md) and the [boundary principles](../../engine-and-world-boundaries.md#design-principles-for-every-feature).

## Understanding another person

An actor's directional relationship description is its subject knowledge document. **My thoughts and relationships** displays that understanding and edits the same text through Knowledge notepads. One person's revision does not revise the other's view, establish kinship, cancel an obligation or grant possession access. Known/remembered subjects do not automatically become currently visible or targetable. Human-private content stays private even from an in-game creator.

For example, Ada can write that she distrusts Bo while Bo still considers Ada a friend. These are independent interpretations, not friendship scores. The [appraisal/social project](../../projects/appraisal-social-continuity-feature-spec.md) defines the delivered continuity scope; it does not promise that arbitrary prose changes physical behavior.

## Objective family facts

The [family owner](../../../packages/domain/src/worlds/base/family.ts) records biological parent links. A character has zero, one or two recorded parents; missing ancestry is unknown. Self-parentage, duplicates, missing endpoints, different/unknown species, construct ancestry and cycles at any depth are rejected both when editing and when loading current saves. Related parents and shared ancestors are permitted; chronology, reproduction, adoption, partnership and inheritance are not inferred.

**Family** in a character's God-mode controls searches existing characters, previews parent direction and records or deletes a link. The paged inspection computes parents, children, siblings and ancestors/descendants. Full siblings share two parents. Half-siblings share one and both have distinct second parents recorded; otherwise the description explicitly leaves other ancestry unknown. More distant generation counts use the shortest recorded ancestry path. Cousins and social-family roles are not described by this first vocabulary; their omission does not establish unrelatedness.

There are no saved sibling facts. Deleting a link updates computed descriptions but never rewrites memories or personal notes. Writes use current-tree revision checks and permanent command receipts; an old creation retry cannot restore a deleted link. No mistaken status or dedicated family audit is introduced. Death retains the actor and ancestry; future hard actor deletion must handle its explicit link dependencies.

Objective inspection is creator-only. NPCs and ordinary players learn through their existing permitted observations, memories and personal knowledge. Hearing a claim does not certify current parentage. Neither NPC input nor public views automatically receive the objective tree. [General creator-edit policy](../../projects/creator-edit-propagation-feature-spec.md) distinguishes optional memory repair from explicit dependency integrity.

The fixed parent meaning, two-parent rule and relationship wording belong to this compiled bundled-world owner, not generic engine law. [BW16](../../maintainers/base-world.md#bw16--family-authoring-and-inspection) tracks delivery and evidence; [BW05](../../limits/base-world.md#bw05) records limits and growing-query costs.

## Feelings

[Bundled appraisal definitions](../../../packages/domain/src/worlds/base/appraisals.ts) supply fear/discomfort, persistent grief, condition-sustained restlessness and recurring calm examples. Only the compatibility damage reactions run by default: positive shot/strike damage maps to fear; supported negative body-health effects map to discomfort. Numeric pacing belongs to the [world defaults inventory](../../limits/base-world.md#bw02) and [decay entry](../../limits/feelings.md#la081).

Installed definitions are not automatic actor enrollment. Kinship and personality prose do not cause grief or enroll restlessness/calm processes by themselves. A valid admitted cause/process is required. Feelings can persist, expire, decay or follow a supported enduring condition through the native owner; they do not force a human's choices.

The mind panel displays permitted current feelings. God authoring exposes qualitative NPC creation/resolution; numeric authoring, reframing and process configuration lack a general editor. [FL13–FL16](../../limits/feelings.md#fl13) distinguish native lifecycle limits from missing UI. [ACT09](../../maintainers/actor-model.md#act09--internal-feeling-process-authoring) records a conditional future authoring slice, without enabling any new default emotions.

## Proposed psychological needs and lived motives

The [compelling-character product target](../../projects/compelling-characters-feature-spec.md) requires contact, belonging, enjoyment and chosen solitude to matter alongside bodily needs. Their meaning, causes, satisfaction/recovery and competing priorities must be specified through [CE01–CE03](../../maintainers/character-experience.md), then demonstrated in complete episodes. Existing qualitative understanding and admitted appraisals can supply first consumers. A greeting does not automatically grant belonging, and proximity does not prove affection.

This is accepted direction with open world-content/integration work. It adds no current social meter, automatic feeling enrollment, friendship score or new native effect. [FL19](../../limits/feelings.md#fl19) and [D69](../../../archive/05-project/open-decisions.md#d69--multidimensional-character-experience) retain the proposed scope and choices.

## Spoken promises

The [current native parser](../../../packages/domain/src/commitments.ts) recognizes committed, self-attributed speech beginning with `I promise to` followed by content, case-insensitively. It records an obligation as protected memory. The exact gather form can bind completion when the remaining item name resolves uniquely; a later matching gather by that actor fulfills it. Other recognized promises have no automatically inferred completion predicate. Ordinary paraphrases are not a general promise-understanding feature.

For example, “I promise to gather berries” can bind the installed berries definition. It does not imply delivering a meal, transferring ownership, rewarding the speaker or creating a reciprocal agreement. The current admission ceiling and matching restrictions are in [BW04](../../limits/base-world.md#bw04).

`POST /api/commitment` can amend the current actor's existing obligation using its expected revision, including cancellation, a future/current deadline or a supported completion binding. Invalid/stale requests reject. Permitted God mind diagnostics publish commitment summaries. The player's Journal has a read-only **Promises** list of their own obligations: exact words, recipient as they know them, status, a plain statement of what this world checks automatically (only the gathering form) and fulfillment evidence. It offers no amendment or cancellation; that waits for [D64](../../../archive/05-project/open-decisions.md#social-exposure-decisions). Native fulfillment/overdue/cancellation state is distinct from an actor merely claiming success.

The English parser and gathering interpretation currently live beside reusable obligation state in the general domain module. Their semantic home is this bundled-world contract. [BW17](../../maintainers/base-world.md#bw17--readable-promises-and-commitment-management) calls for extracting policy when that slice needs it, reusing the existing mutation owner rather than adding another promise store. A world with ritual vows or negotiated contracts can differ without changing engine evidence and revision integrity.

## Offering and accepting possessions

A person may offer a gift or propose one exact item quantity for another exact item quantity. Submission moves and reserves nothing. Only the named recipient can accept, through their own command; acceptance transfers both agreed sides together or neither. The current owner is `packages/domain/src/handover.ts`, with authored choices and wording in `worlds/base/handover.ts`.

- **Exact agreement:** a proposal identifies the two people, exact lots/individuals and quantities, definition and item/placement revisions, bag contents and game-clock deadline. The proposer's explicit submission agrees to that revision. The recipient must accept the same revision. A **Counteroffer** replaces the terms, advances the revision and makes its author the proposer; earlier consent cannot accept the new terms.
- **Gift:** omitting the requested side retains a one-way gift. Enough free units must remain, but using a different unit from a sufficiently large offered stack does not alone invalidate the gift. Reciprocal terms additionally require unchanged item and placement revisions on both sides.
- **Final checks:** offering needs sight and arm's reach. Accepting needs mutual sight/reach, living active participants, accessible custody, free quantities and no conflicting work or claims. Bags and individual objects move whole. Bag contents, including nested changes, must still match; access grants must be cleared before offering a bag. The object owner validates final combined packing and placement before changing either side. Reciprocal moves keep distinct lots rather than merging one promised source into the other; ordinary gifts retain their existing merge behavior.
- **No-transfer endings:** the recipient can decline and the proposer can withdraw. Both require current terms. Unanswered offers expire; changed/unavailable goods or participants lapse at the next simulation moment. Reach loss blocks acceptance without itself transferring or reserving goods. Native failure or storage rollback commits neither side nor a success receipt.

Gifts still work in the familiar way: Mike can hold out two Wild berries to Ada; only her acceptance moves them. Ignoring, declining or withdrawing moves nothing. If fewer than two free berries remain, acceptance says “The offered items are no longer available.” The native check also verified that eating one different berry from a six-unit stack leaves that two-unit gift valid.

Only explicitly disclosed exact items can be requested from another person. An offer discloses its source lot to its recipient through ordinary perception and memory; it grants no inventory inspection. Remembered terms show last-disclosed quantities, not unseen live availability. The current open proposal also permits a counteroffer using its exact disclosed lot, even after newer disclosures push that reference out of the compact remembered list. This grants no larger quantity or other inventory reference. Guessed IDs, inaccessible goods, changed private stock and conflicts with another private proposal get the same generic unavailability refusal. Neither a bag's interior nor somebody else's remembered disclosures enter the player's or a resident's public observation.

Exact reciprocal terms and actual outcomes are private observations for the two participants. Nearby witnesses perceive only an item exchange. Existing gift event wording remains visible as before. Offer creation/counteroffers invite the recipient's ordinary decision; accept/decline do not force a choice or interrupt unrelated work. A resident receives permitted exact terms, own possessions, needs and goals, with acceptance, refusal and other ordinary choices. There is no automatic barter preference or acceptance rule.

The person and inventory panels show **You give / You receive**, whole-number quantities, waiting/incoming status and reachable Offer, Counteroffer, Accept, Decline and Withdraw controls. Changed terms block Accept until explicitly reviewed. Refusal/staleness preserves draft quantities; **Review current items** refreshes selected revisions without silently changing those quantities. Inventory and person panels share one draft, reviewed revision and submission guard; opening the other panel cannot overwrite an edit. Drafts and reviewed revisions survive reconnect/reopening within the same authorized account, character and save timeline; access/logout cleanup clears private drafts. An unconfirmed submission requires review, never an automatic repeat. Existing typed requests and ordinary action discovery use the same descriptions and prerequisite checks. The compact panel explains its candidate bounds; another owned lot can be selected through Inventory, and an older other-person item needs a fresh disclosure unless it is still part of the open proposal being countered.

[Current acceptance evidence](../../verification/reciprocal-barter.md) includes two berries for an individual invented sling, refusal, stale terms, contents/quantity/work/claim/reach changes, withdrawal, duplicate delivery, PostgreSQL rollback/reopening and actual client controls. Controlled live resident decisions accepted food for a spare sling and declined stones to keep a needed sling; this does not establish general preference reliability. Lifetime, pending conflicts, candidate bounds, rationale and expansion triggers are owned by [BW11](../../limits/base-world.md#bw11).

Direct deposit into another person's carried inventory is refused ([items](items.md#shared-containers-and-active-work)). Currency, pricing scripts, credit, deferred delivery, generalized negotiation, institutions and commercial promises remain outside this first barter family. Feeding and contested ownership are separate consumers. [BW21](../../maintainers/base-world.md#bw21--reserve-a-portion-for-a-named-person) remains open: offers do not protect stock from later use, and standing reservations need their own claim-lifetime owner.

## Voluntary outings

A nearby person can be invited to one specific destination through **Invite to travel** in their action menu or the installed activity request. The picker offers currently visible stationary things; an explicit ordinary request can name a permitted map point. No undiscovered place list or moving-person destination is implied. The invitation shows the companion as the inviter knows them, destination, distance, optional stated purpose and how the inviter's present work may be handled on acceptance. Purpose is a statement, not permission or a promise.

This social family requires the existing personal-memory capability, so ordinary native wildlife cannot receive or supply consent. The restriction is capability-based, not a species or body-shape rule. The pair has one live invitation or outing between them, and each person can join only one accepted outing. The recipient freely chooses **Accept** or **Decline** through their own ordinary decision or player controls. Neither moves before acceptance. Acceptance binds the exact invitation revision and both people's unchanged chosen work. Starting while idle, explicitly replacing existing work, or pausing supported work are distinct choices; changed work requires a fresh invitation. The acceptance control explicitly names replacement when either current or paused work remains. Both ordinary travel activities are admitted together or neither is changed.

Each participant then walks to the agreed fixed point through their own ordinary movement, with their own route and completion. There is no leader who drives the other person's body, formation controller or following of an unseen companion. Losing sight records that separation once and leaves the companion's location unknown; the agreed destination remains available. Ordinary decisions may continue there or leave. Arrival records the arriving person's actual result, and grants no knowledge of an unseen companion's progress. Both arrivals close the outing. Gathering, conversation and other work afterward must be chosen separately.

**Leave outing** is available alongside current work in Inventory and Character. Pending invitations can be withdrawn; recipients can decline them. Leaving, changed destination placement, blocked/cancelled/replaced travel, incapacity or departure ends consent and cancels only the still-owned trip activities. New unrelated work is preserved. Previously paused work retains the ordinary activity owner's resumption rules. Threats supply ordinary perceived evidence; the invitation enlists no combat assistance. Ending the outing tells each participant only that the agreement ended, never the other person's private reason, condition or unseen location.

Unanswered invitations expire on the existing social-offer game-clock lifetime. Active work survives a current-format save with the actual positions and native navigation revalidation; an ended outing never resumes on return/reload. Stable command receipts refuse duplicate effects. Each outing notice is one private piece of personal evidence; a linked plan refers to that same evidence instead of writing a duplicate account. Completed consent remains private event/memory evidence for the participants, not a retained party record. There is no automatic friendship, item transfer, psychological satisfaction or spoken-promise creation.

Authored vocabulary, scope and tuning live in `packages/domain/src/worlds/base/outing-policy.ts` and the installed `outing.ts` request family. The shared consent owner in `outings.ts` links existing activity and motion owners. [BW14](../../limits/base-world.md#bw14--paired-outings) owns the pair/destination limits; [PX04](../../maintainers/parallel-batch-04-expeditions-and-exchange.md#px04--voluntary-shared-outings) and [verification](../../verification/voluntary-outings.md) distinguish delivery from broader social qualification.
