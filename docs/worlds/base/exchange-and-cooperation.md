# Proposed exchange and small-cooperation rules

**Proposal, 2026-10-04; not implemented or approved for runtime delivery.** These are the selected bundled-world rules for [DG06's product design](../../projects/exchange-and-cooperation-feature-spec.md). Current [gifts and personal promises](social.md) remain unchanged. Exact assent, authoritative custody, actual evidence and account permissions retain their existing owners; another authored world can choose different commercial and institutional rules.

## Direct barter

The first barter family exchanges present portable possessions between two nearby people. Both offer something; a one-sided transfer remains a gift. Each participant selects their own actual carried items and quantities. An item type requested in conversation is not an exact offered object, and neither participant can inspect or select the other's undisclosed inventory through the trade surface.

Supported bundles, pending interactions and bag restrictions are owned by [EX01](../../limits/base-world.md#ex01--proposed-direct-barter-envelope). Individual objects move whole. Empty portable bags are eligible under existing handling/access rules; a nonempty bag is refused intact with an explanation to trade its contents separately or empty it deliberately. Nothing automatically unpacks, discloses or drops the contents.

The selected family uses no currency, fees, remote delivery, services, credit, escrow or institution. A completed barter transfers the exact accepted possessions and creates no new duty to return them. It does not change the creator-recorded declared owner, determine rightful title, erase another claim or cancel a separate promise. Visible wording says what each person gives and receives, not that legal ownership was adjudicated.

## Review and settlement

Each person sees the current complete proposal, with their own and the other side clearly identified. Each independently accepts that proposal once. The second acceptance settles both sides together if the proposal and physical conditions remain current. A changed item, quantity or relevant supported condition clears both acceptances and identifies the change. Casual conversational assent cannot sell possessions: human text and resident actions must intentionally accept the complete current proposal, and ambiguous assent leads to review. Acceptance of earlier terms cannot authorize later terms.

A draft moves and reserves nothing. Current work claims and ordinary access continue to apply. If offered items change, disappear or become unavailable, the old proposal cannot settle. No silent substitution, quantity reduction, partial settlement or later automatic retry is permitted. An invalid bundle changes neither person's possessions.

At settlement, both people must be alive, active, capable, mutually visible and within the world's existing item-handling reach. Each must still carry their selected free items. Incoming items enter each recipient’s own carried inventory; no destination picker or global carrying cap is introduced. Later packing uses ordinary actions. A future finite-container destination needs explicit access, selection and complete-result capacity rules. The existing custody owner determines actual changes and receipts.

## Reading, interruption and return

Barter does not inherit the accelerated-clock timeout of one-way handover. Reading alone does not expire an otherwise current eligible exchange; [EX02](../../limits/base-world.md#ex02--proposed-reading-and-presence-policy) records this choice. The world continues, ordinary needs and danger remain visible, and either person may withdraw. A real loss of eligibility ends the nearby exchange. An NPC reading-delay penalty is not added.

Opening or closing the detailed panel is a viewing action. A pending exchange remains visibly reachable until explicitly withdrawn, completed or ended by its conditions. Replies use immediate social choices compatible with ongoing work; they are not queued consent or permission to interrupt unrelated work.

A connection failure requires finding the actual result before another transfer can be proposed. A cancellation that loses a race with completed settlement cannot undo it. After departure or restoration, an old nearby proposal cannot execute against a different world, controller or current set of possessions. A completed exchange can be reversed only through a new willing transfer of actual available items; no automatic refund or duplication is promised.

## Residents and observed knowledge

A resident's acceptance, refusal or counteroffer is its own ordinary admitted decision. It can value present usefulness, identity, relationships and other interests without a universal price score. Private plans, stock minima and undisclosed possessions are not exposed to explain a refusal. Unavailable cognition produces an unavailable decision, never fictional consent, refusal or an automatic charged retry.

An actual offer can invite a decision; private drafting, reading, settlement, cancellation and history do not require one. Public events and personal knowledge follow current visibility, hearing and privacy. Inspecting a controller's receipt does not automatically teach the embodied character an unobserved fact. Claims about objects remain attributed claims rather than guaranteed properties.

## Remembering a small understanding

Two people may mutually accept an optional remembered understanding after useful shared play. It contains their exact identities, a readable shared purpose, optional mutually known meeting place/cache, the actual sharing convention and the statement that joining each later occasion is a fresh choice. Its practical actions prepare a new invitation or a new similar trade; neither repeats old consent or starts work.

An arrangement name creates no organization, legal person, shared title, platform membership or billing authority. The selected world does not install a government, leader or compulsory common wallet. Active-arrangement and retained-history scope is recorded in [EX03](../../limits/base-world.md#ex03--proposed-arrangement-and-history-growth).

Preferred roles are suggestions for future play, not standing duties. This first family creates no automatic quotas, deadlines, wages, daily promises, attendance records or periodic cognition. Its proof uses present contributions, gifts and barter. Separately accepted personal obligations retain their own terms and evidence; a current gathering promise is not proof that supplies were delivered. General deferred-delivery and commercial obligations remain future consumers.

## Shared supplies

A selected chest retains its actual access rules. Absence of a restriction means others may use it; a restriction still requires an existing grant. The arrangement never grants access or allows a participant to change permissions merely by naming themselves a member.

An explicit deposit into shared supplies contributes those actual items under those access rules. It creates no repayment balance, protected portion, fractional ownership or loan. No automatic clawback follows consumption or departure. A personal object merely nearby is not a contribution. Someone wanting simultaneous reciprocity can use barter instead.

There is no compulsory contribution scoreboard or omniscient group ledger. Each participant sees their own actual receipts and permitted current contents. Other characters learn contributions, shortages or withdrawal through supported observation and communication. An unobserved withdrawal is not automatically known, and an allowed withdrawal is not mechanically classified as theft without an authored entitlement rule.

## Changes and departure

Changing mutually accepted purpose, place or sharing terms requires a new proposal and each person's assent. It cannot rewrite the previous accepted terms or another person's memory. A personal note remains a personal note.

Either person may stop future participation immediately. No approval, paid acknowledgement or reputation penalty is required. The ended arrangement remains distinguishable from its past effects in permitted history. Ending the arrangement does not undo trades, reclaim consumed contributions, cancel separate obligations, delete memories or revoke unrelated physical access.

Absence creates no automatic debt or off-screen contribution. A remembered understanding may remain idle without scheduling work or model calls. The other person may continue ordinary life. Returning permits a fresh invitation, not execution of an old queued choice.
