# Proposed first resident's lived motives

**Status: DG02 product proposal, October 4, 2026; not installed world behavior.** This page owns the proposed bundled-world choices for the first integrated character episode. [Compelling characters](../../projects/compelling-characters-feature-spec.md) owns the product experience and full behavioral flows. [CE01–CE05](../../maintainers/character-experience.md) owns delivery; [social rules](social.md), [survival](survival.md), [camp activities](camp-routines.md) and current cognition/agency owners retain their existing behavior.

These are humanlike wilderness choices, not mandatory psychology for all Open Legend worlds. No new social, fun or happiness meter is selected. No automatic grief, calm, restlessness or other feeling enrollment follows from this page.

## 1. A concrete first resident and a world worth attending to

Use one disposable authored version of Rin, the patient maker from the feature specification, for the first controlled product prototype. This is not a replacement of canonical Ada or a claim that Rin already exists. Use the same starting Rin in baseline/candidate comparisons unless changing the profile is the explicit comparison.

Rin values careful useful work and having a choice in how to spend time. They enjoy looking closely at made things, dry humor, unhurried company and occasionally being alone. They can take pride in their work without wanting every moment to be productive. Asking for help can feel awkward; that is a tendency they may overcome, not a refusal rule.

The seed supplies an explicitly authored background and a present interest, not a scripted afternoon. It does not include remembered quotations or supposedly witnessed events that never happened. Practical knowledge must name actually installed, learned methods. A story about having been a maker does not grant an unsupported repair or construction capability.

CE-F01's initial familiarity comes from an actual earlier exchange in the prototype, or explicitly labeled authored initial relationship history under the existing knowledge rules. Record which basis was used. An authored acquaintance is not evidence of a runtime conversation; it supplies no fabricated quotation.

The starting scene supplies manageable bodily conditions, some accessible known food, the current means to rest, one real material or object worth examining, and an accessible place with actual visible detail. A human can pursue their own current creative activity nearby. Do not create a resource emergency to force Rin's first interesting choice.

Rin may be curious about making one useful piece of cord or a supported carrying object, depending on the world's actual known techniques. The seed may state that interest; it must not automatically install a queue of gather/craft/eat/talk steps. If a comparison starts with a previously chosen unfinished activity, label that initial intention and real partial work explicitly.

The early available alternatives must be meaningfully different:

- Continue or initiate a finite known practical activity.
- Eat known food or rest for an actual bodily reason.
- Approach the human and share a particular observation, question or interest.
- Ask about or inspect something the human actually made and disclosed.
- Offer a carried item through the current recipient-accepted handover.
- Go to an actually known pleasant or interesting spot and remain to observe.
- Decline contact, wait, change a method or stop.

The first episode does not require a music system, minigame, building simulation or second resident. An unsupported pastime cannot be narrated into existence. If the current actor cannot choose a quiet observation/waiting activity through ordinary supported actions, CE01/CE04 must deliver that small missing opportunity before claiming solitary enjoyment works.

## 2. The chosen representation

Use current bodily condition, actor-owned self/subject understanding, existing supported appraisals and explicitly chosen intentions. Each has its existing owner. A concern can be described in ordinary language without becoming a continuously depleting resource.

A current concern needs a specific meaning: what matters, why now, which person/object/situation it refers to, and what changed since the last relevant decision. It need not become a separate record if the existing current intention, note or appraisal already expresses it. Avoid copies that later disagree.

The resident's interpretation can be mistaken or mixed. It remains an interpretation. “I feel welcome here” does not install membership, access, another person's affection or a human player's obligation to respond. “I enjoyed that” does not award a hidden happiness point.

Current automatic feeling enrollment remains damage-derived fear/discomfort. Installed grief, restlessness and calm definitions do not enroll from this profile. A welcome or enjoyable visit can inform an immediate choice and later accepted understanding without waiting for a note rewrite; it does not automatically create a feeling process. Richer condition/disposition enrollment, compound psychological conditions and general process editing remain separate ACT09 work.

The design deliberately selects no generic duration after which someone becomes lonely, no fixed social quota and no compulsory rotation among needs. A remembered absence or unfinished wish can matter at an ordinary supported reconsideration opportunity; the mere passage of a browser minute does not order a social action.

## 3. Bodily pressure and ordinary competence

**What makes it relevant.** Actual authored bodily changes become meaningful at their existing observation/opportunity boundaries. The resident receives the condition and its permitted experiential interpretation. A current task, food in possession, known sources and present danger determine the practical choice.

**What the resident may do.** Eat available food, finish a short safe step first, obtain food through a known route, ask, rest or knowingly accept a supported risk for a stronger reason. The world does not assign hunting because hunger exists. In particular, adequate carried food makes hunting unnecessary for that immediate concern.

**What changes the concern.** Only real native effects change fullness, energy or bodily harm. Saying “I ate” has no effect. When eating supplies enough relief, the prior food-seeking reason is reconsidered. A separately chosen provisioning purpose may remain.

**How it ends or returns.** The immediate concern can subside after actual relief, be superseded by a more urgent condition, or return after subsequent genuine bodily change. Recalling hunger does not drain fullness again. A completed food goal does not reappear solely because the same sentence remains in a biography.

**Important current limitation.** New person feeding choices currently use the actual cognition path; the old automatic native feeding fallback was removed. During unavailable cognition, native physiology and already chosen work continue only as current policy permits, and an NPC can remain unfed. This proposal neither secretly feeds nor selectively freezes that actor. Any new operational protection belongs to an explicitly adopted service/time policy.

This limitation does not remove current [automatic sleep and waking rules](sleep.md). Sleep has real movement, work, speech and perception consequences. A sleeping resident's silence is not an authored refusal or provider failure; ordinary speech does not automatically wake them. Use the existing Wake Up interaction where permitted.

## 4. Wanting contact

**What makes it relevant.** A familiar person becomes genuinely available, a new shared interest appears, a remembered exchange prompts a wish to talk, or Rin notices that they would enjoy company. A visible idle body is an opportunity, not proof that the human wants conversation. The resident cannot read whether a human is busy outside the game.

**What contact means here.** A particular exchange or shared presence can be worthwhile: asking about an object, offering a thought, making a joke or sitting nearby where that activity is supported. Contact is not a required greeting every time someone enters view.

**Starting and continuing.** Approach the chosen person through actual movement; speak within the supported channel; then respond to what was actually heard. A pending invitation is one pending approach, not a fresh invitation on every perception refresh. A short exchange can be enough. Continuing nearby work while talking is allowed only where the actual activity permits it.

**What changes the concern.** An engaging exchange can satisfy the immediate wish; an awkward answer can change the topic or make solitude preferable. The resident can enjoy brief contact without becoming a friend. No model must manufacture a positive feeling because the conversation had a certain length.

**How it ends or returns.** A clear decline, a completed exchange, the person's departure, unanswered contact after a reasonable contextual opportunity, a changed motive or another activity can end the attempt. The first authored policy uses the actual encounter and response, not an extra fixed invitation timer. A new meaningful opening can justify another approach; unchanged silence cannot.

An unanswered bid need not freeze Rin. They can continue compatible work or turn to another activity while a reply remains possible. Human reading delay, accelerated game time, a closed panel and technical latency are not evidence of personal rejection. A later reply uses current hearing/conversation rules and does not undo activity already completed. Ending a pending bid does not itself justify another invitation.

A human's “I want to work alone” remains relevant across panel closing and reopening. Rin may be disappointed or irritated in character, but should not restart the same bid because another decision opportunity arrived. A later actual invitation, agreed later occasion or materially new circumstance can reopen it. This is contextual respect, not a permanent universal no-contact flag.

## 5. Wanting belonging

**What makes it relevant.** Rin considers whether they are welcome, understood or included by a particular person or in an actual shared situation. A remembered welcome, exclusion, contribution, disagreement or uncertain invitation may matter. An authored insecurity can shape interpretation, but it cannot fabricate an excluding event.

**What can address it.** Being listened to about a real interest, being invited to participate, choosing a meaningful contribution or learning that a disagreement did not end the relationship can influence Rin's own understanding. A brief hello is not automatically equivalent to belonging. Giving away supplies cannot buy a guaranteed attachment.

**What changes.** An accepted self/subject-note update or supported appraisal can record what Rin now thinks and feels, citing legitimate causes where required. The other person's understanding does not update automatically. Objective group facts, kinship, access and promises retain their own rules.

**Mixed results.** Rin can feel appreciated for making something while still uncertain about friendship. They can enjoy inclusion and dislike being treated as useful labor. They can decline help because independence matters, and later reconsider. The aim is a coherent tension, not a perfect social strategy.

**How it settles or returns.** A reassuring experience can reduce the urgency to seek reassurance without permanently solving identity. An ambiguous rejection may remain unresolved but need not demand action. Later independent evidence can change the interpretation; repeatedly recalling the same evidence is not a new injury or social reward.

The resident should have other ways to spend a worthwhile afternoon while a relationship is uncertain. The design does not make the human responsible for filling an attachment deficit, nor portray leaving the application as abandonment.

## 6. Enjoyment and curiosity

**What makes it relevant.** A real object, view, pattern, remembered pleasure or unanswered practical question connects with Rin's interests. Enjoyment can be anticipated, occur during an activity and be recalled afterward. There is no productivity prerequisite.

**What can happen now.** Inspect an actual made object, look at a known landscape or bird, talk about a disclosed discovery, quietly continue an interesting known method, or revisit a pleasant spot. The character must actually move, inspect or remain where the relevant experience can occur. Prose cannot supply unseen wildlife or an unsupported game/music performance.

**The smallest quiet activity.** Rin selects a particular known object or place and a reason to spend time there, approaches if necessary, then remains available to relevant perception. Observation reveals only actual permitted detail and does not require generated prose. The choice includes an understandable completion or reconsideration condition: inspection has finished, the selected short visit ends, the view is lost, an interruption matters or Rin chooses to leave. Continuing the selected interval uses ordinary native activity; it does not request a model decision every moment. If choosing such an interval is unsupported, deliver that small CE01/CE03/CE04 capability before treating idle standing as successful enjoyment.

A destination must actually be available to the resident. Current remembered-place support is limited to eight places recorded through following/acting on a subject; passive scenic sightings do not populate it. A biography saying Rin likes a lookout supplies neither a target nor a route. Use a genuinely known place in the first prototype, or record the missing destination support under CE03/AG06.

**What changes the concern.** A new detail, an absorbing interval, a satisfying result, disappointment, boredom, physical discomfort or another concern can influence the next choice. Saying the activity was enjoyable is permitted as a character self-report, but the full episode must show at least one real choice affected by the interest.

**How it ends.** Rin can decide they have seen enough, finish the chosen small activity, turn toward a new purpose, or stay because they still enjoy it. A familiar pleasure may remain rewarding; repeated visits are not forbidden. The stopping question is whether the current pursuit still makes sense, not whether a novelty counter has expired.

**How it returns.** A genuinely changed scene, a remembered positive experience or another opportunity can renew interest. Do not require a new discovery each time. Equally, do not replay the same scenic monologue or treat each glance as a new reward.

If the game offers only gather/hunt/cook and idle standing, personality text cannot compensate. Add the smallest actual missing enjoyable option before adding more adjectives, motives or generated interior monologue.

## 7. Competence, purpose and chosen solitude

A finite practical purpose can be chosen for usefulness, pride, curiosity or generosity. The intended result should be inspectable in ordinary terms: make one useful object, finish a chosen amount or examine a problem. The actor may revise or abandon the purpose; already spent materials and an actual promise do not vanish with that change.

Completing a purpose changes the next decision. Preparing a small supply may be sensible; perpetually producing the same item without use, demand, pleasure or a retained intention is a failure to notice completion. An unchanged blocked method should lead to a different method, investigation, asking or waiting for a meaningful change. It should not produce repeated requests or a new excuse at every opportunity.

Solitude is an affirmative choice, not necessarily loneliness or hostility. Rin may prefer to finish a delicate task without interruption, enjoy a quiet visit or recover after a difficult conversation. A player can later be welcomed without “repairing” a solitude penalty.

A refusal can be short and particular: the resident wants to finish, is tired, disagrees, does not trust this use of their belongings or simply does not want to join. It need not sound like a service error or a generic counseling script. The player is allowed to dislike the refusal; the product must still make the available choices understandable.

## 8. Competing concerns and interruption

The actor evaluates relevant concerns together. There is no fixed universal order of hunger, socializing, fun and work. Ordinary bodily discomfort may wait briefly; a serious supported threat retains its physical consequences regardless of attention. Personality does not immunize a body against danger.

Use these product distinctions when choosing an interruption:

| Situation | Intended decision behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Minor new cue during useful work | Continue, note it or answer briefly; no compulsory replanning |
| A direct question that can be answered while working | Speak if desired while preserving the actual activity |
| A request requiring the same body, tool or destination | Choose whether to suspend, stop, decline or defer; do not pretend both incompatible acts run |
| A material shortage or vanished target | Stop the invalid means and retain/reconsider the underlying purpose |
| Immediate perceived danger | Apply actual world effects and native interruption rules; fresh reasoning must not delay those effects |
| Relief or completion | Reconsider whether the old reason still exists before selecting more of the same |
| A wish that remains unchanged | Keep it available without another paid check solely because it is stored |

When an interruption ends, the previous purpose remains recallable where the current owner supports it. Resumption checks actual materials, progress and present relevance. A thought saying “I will go back” does not recreate canceled work or guarantee that a partial step survived.

Technical delays are outside this fictional priority comparison. The resident does not become hostile, sleepy, forgetful or reluctant because a provider is slow.

## 9. Relationship to time, correction and memory

The current world clock advances bodily conditions and activities. Human reading time, optional presentation and provider wait remain their own concerns. Speeding the game does not require speeding up a stream of introspective prose.

Existing accepted records survive same-version restart and obey actual correction, forgetting and privacy invalidation. This first product slice does not add old-save compatibility, long-term personality development, dreams or historical re-authoring. A remembered cause that is no longer permitted cannot survive secretly in a new “current concern” summary.

Short-term adaptation is enough here: Rin can update an impression, stop an activity or choose differently later. Changing a lifelong trait or inventing a traumatic formative event belongs to a separately authored/later process, not a convenient repair to explain an inconsistent reply.

## 10. What the first slice must establish

The first whole episode must show a bodily or psychological concern influencing a real choice, an actual result, and a later choice that reflects the result. Across the existing CE flows, contact, belonging and enjoyment all need meaningful coverage. They need not all become urgent in the same afternoon.

A quiet interval must allow an existing interest or unfinished intention to matter without a fresh human prompt. The same integration must permit an appropriate social opening to matter; merely seeing someone is not proof that current low-urgency encounter evidence triggers reasoning. If the current reconsideration path cannot do that, CE03 records and fixes that specific integration gap. No new mandatory thought frequency is selected.

No paid check solely because a wish is stored does not mean the wish can never influence another choice. At the next legitimate opportunity—completion, an intentionally selected quiet interval ending, meaningful changed evidence or another supported internal opportunity—the existing concern can matter again. Current goal-only edits and routine sightings must not be turned into self-generated wake loops.

The human should be able to pursue their own invention or exploration, decline contact, observe a purposeful resident and reengage later. The resident must not require rescue from repeated loops or constant instructions. One excellent transcript or a correctly executed meal is insufficient.

The first proposal deliberately leaves numerical psychological pressure, automatic time-based loneliness, a universal satisfaction formula and new emotional-process families unselected. Add one only after a complete episode identifies behavior the current qualitative choices cannot express, with explicit causes, effects, lifetime, limits and an ordinary player benefit.
