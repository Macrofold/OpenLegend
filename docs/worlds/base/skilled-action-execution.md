# Base-world skilled combat execution

**Status: Release-time picking and body-center aiming pass focused checks; independent player acceptance remains open.** This is the **bundled world's optional action-execution policy**, not an engine law, general real-time-combat module, mandatory Dexterity/Focus system or new craft recipe authority. [Feature specification](../../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-feature-spec.md), [technical design](../../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md), [ASE tasks](../../maintainers/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md) and [limits](../../limits/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md) own the complete build and qualification.

## Why this world installs it

Make actual hunting and confrontation fun. A character’s weapon and experience influence what they can do, while a human has a reason to aim, move and choose a release moment. Physical events persist. Ordinary attacks and ordinary crafting remain useful without a compulsory skill check.

## Player movement and battle intent

- Normal selected-mode world interaction remains selection, walking, inspection and ordinary contextual actions. WASD supplies controlled ground movement: sample the camera basis when the eligible key combination changes and retain that world direction while held. Camera orbit alone does not bend the route.
- A visible, remappable **attack mode** makes a primary click attempt one admitted current weapon/strike action; a held primary press makes a **more deliberate version of that same chosen action**. Secondary menus and explicit target/work choices survive. The shared [client bindings](../../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#61-one-owner-visible-state-and-selected-bindings) supply the selected Shift+T / Shift+W defaults, WASD, targeting and retained Act/Speak controls. Those device preferences require actual qualification and do not become world-definition fields.
- Mode enters on the human's actual admitted attack or personally experienced directed assault, established by typed native personal evidence. A health change, environmental injury, testimony or nearby fight alone does not qualify. It is not a universal aggro state; previous hostility grace does not continuously force it back on after deliberate manual Off. A fresh personally perceived assault may re-enter it.
- Moving during a held aim is supported for compatible bodies/tools, through the shared steering owner; an ordinary replacement movement action still cancels preparation. Chosen guard and unrelated physical work remain incompatible, and native injury can still interrupt.
- Moving the held cursor onto the player character cancels the unreleased attempt; walking under a stationary pointer does not. Escape and a reachable Cancel control are equivalent. A quick tap performs the ordinary attack after any required native windup; release after deliberate hold captures that final subject-or-point choice and any permitted bounded timing quality. There is no auto-fire or guaranteed hit.

## Ranged point fire and intended subjects

**Selected for the bundled world's first usable attack-mode control:** a compatible ranged weapon may fire at a **visible 3D world point** under the mouse, even with no creature clicked. A recognized animal/person under the initial pointer can be recorded as the intended subject and receive exact attack/PvP review, but it is never a guaranteed shot target. The player may move the aim ahead of fleeing prey or aim into a formerly visible clearing without being given hidden animal coordinates; losing the creature's sight is not itself a reason to block a lawful point-directed shot. The actor's own weapon, source, permission, physical launch geometry, ready state and ammunition remain mandatory. An unresolvable floor or nonfinite/ambiguous ray is refused before material is spent; a melee attack still needs a currently eligible observed subject.

Point fire is **not a bypass for consequential violence**. Review covers both an originally intended person and a newly recognized person indicated by the final frozen release ray. Ground-down/person-up input or an omitted subject ID cannot bypass the existing exact lethal review. A changed indicated person or source/permission dependency needs renewed review; the preview checks only permitted recognized bodies and reveals no hidden bystander. An unselected shot carries the visible world-authored warning that **any actual physical interceptor may be injured**, without previewing hidden identities. After release, [CTR01](ranged-counterplay.md#selected-contact-consequences) owns genuine first-impact injury/collateral, and the world does not 'undo' an unintended fatality after a shot commits. Other installed worlds can disable point fire entirely.

## Concentric release interaction

The green/yellow annular target follows the current physical aim pointer while a white concentric radius expands and contracts. Continue tracking moving prey manually. Green means a positive release contribution, yellow ordinary, and outside yellow weaker. A poor score does not guarantee a physical miss, and green cannot create contact.

The shared [explicit attack controls](../../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#62-explicit-attack-controls) provide ordinary Attack and supported Prepare/Release/Attack normally/Cancel buttons. Aim is selected by the pointer, and the held ring remains animated.

Pointer-down requests native preparation before a tap/hold distinction is known. If the existing exact lethal review is required first, no preparation is admitted yet: end the old gesture, discard its queued release, and let explicit **Prepare attack** confirmation admit only the reviewed preparation. Continue with deliberate controls. A release review preserves one frozen subject-or-point choice and sample only in its own valid dialog; explicit authored release confirmation (**Release attack** for held melee, **Shoot** for ranged) uses fresh current authority. Closing or declining cancels the matching uncommitted attempt and never fires it. An early release retains one immutable intention and waits for legal native readiness only while current foreground input authorization remains valid. Cancel, pause, blur, detected disconnect and control loss disarm automatic release. Restart/Resume never recreates old held input; explicit native Release/Cancel uses the surviving action and current permission. No additional total hold timeout is selected: native interruption, vulnerability, resource claims and cancellation remain effective.

The first green center is `250` real ms after ring origin, so with the selected [150-ms client hold threshold](../../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#61-one-owner-visible-state-and-selected-bindings), an immediately admitted held gesture first reaches it `400` real ms after pointer-down. That arithmetic describes this curve, not a reaction-time guarantee: network admission may delay the visible origin. Slowing a future ring also changes the real wait for its next center, so qualify both ease and opportunity cost. Native preparation/recovery remains independent.

At `1×`, the selected default sling release-to-release floor is `(18 + 18) / 60 = 0.6` real seconds and bow floor is `(18 + 24) / 60 = 0.7` real seconds, assuming the next preparation begins immediately when recovery ends. Qualification must assess that combined benefit and complete native cadence. It must not describe this as only a ten- or fifteen-percent outcome change.

### Physical arrow and local evasion

The installed shot family releases one straight ray from the shooter's current native interaction-height anchor. An eligible visible living target uses its exact geometric center, half its current effective body height above its feet, and the [observed-motion intercept](ranged-counterplay.md) from personally seen executed movement; that rule applies to combat-mode taps and held releases as well as ordinary subject/menu/hunt/NPC actions. A ground/scenery pick or pick without an eligible body uses its exact permitted XYZ without leading. Button-up re-picks the current geometry even if the pointer never moved, replacing the initial target or point under native admission and exact review. Explicit release controls use the last world aim-cursor screen position. An accepted pending release or review then freezes that choice. Camera pitch/projection changes the pick ray, not the body center. Ordinary aim/shoot, automatic/NPC shots and practice use the same center; the shooter's interaction anchor remains unchanged. Flight continues beyond the selected point until first stopping contact or full weapon range; there is no arc, random heading deviation, hidden future-path read or homing. Native handling is captured once at release and affects only a qualified physical response at an actual crossing. Preserve the sensed near-passage alarm and its once-per-projectile/body-life evidence; it never ignores a collider or substitutes for current duck awareness. Preparation alone does not alarm an animal. [Ranged counterplay](ranged-counterplay.md) and the [technical collision owner](../../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#33-chronological-collision-one-conditional-contest-and-existing-defense) own contact and defense.

**The selected first response is one actual duck/tuck, not a cosmetic dodge over an unchanged capsule.** Install it for the bundled grounded person and deer tall-capsule profiles only. Other bodies require an explicitly supported response; inert targets, airborne birds and unselected shapes receive no automatic one. Displayed height follows the actual lower body. Custom animations for every character, broader leaning/twisting, anatomical parts and continuous skeleton animation are not required by this slice.

This section owns the complete initial response tuning. All values are installed implementation/playtest defaults, not physiological measurements or proof of play balance.

| Response input or rule   | Selected initial value                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ordinary shape           | The body's current ordinary radius `r` and height `h`; require a supported grounded tall capsule                                                                                            |
| Duck shape               | `hDuck = max(2 × r + ε, 0.45 × h)`, with the shared spatial numerical tolerance `ε`; require `hDuck < h`                                                                                    |
| Feet, radius and anchors | Same feet/support placement and radius; multiply ordinary eye, ear and interaction heights by `hDuck / h`                                                                                   |
| Transition               | One atomic native posture contraction at the genuine candidate boundary, admitted only when the new capsule actually clears that crossing and has legal support/clearance                   |
| Hold                     | `12` game seconds from successful pose commitment                                                                                                                                           |
| Response cooldown        | `60` game seconds from each qualified feasible response attempt, whether that contest succeeds or fails; unavailable responses start none                                                   |
| Return                   | At the hold deadline, restore only with full ordinary-body support/solid/body clearance; otherwise retain the small actual shape as `restore-blocked` and retry on a relevant native change |
| Travel during pose       | Current actual small body for contact/senses; conservative ordinary-body clearance for movement, so this does not grant low-tunnel travel                                                   |
| Candidate context `C`    | Exactly `1` in the initial bundled rule; no extra fleeing penalty or undefined avoidance statistic                                                                                          |

A response requires an alive participating body with native awareness of the actual incoming threat, supported ability and no restraint/unresponsiveness or incompatible chosen guard, attack/craft work or combat recovery. Idle and ordinary ground locomotion may qualify. For this first response, awareness is concretely established by the existing current `visionQuery` seeing the actual incoming point projectile at its candidate position, using the defender's current senses/eye anchor and real solid clearance. The bundled response treats that sensed incoming motion as recognizable danger; it does not require a preexisting generic danger flag, a recognized shooter or combat mode. If that sense test fails, the body cannot react from private collision coordinates. This is the current coarse vision law, not new physiological reflex/FOV modeling. This slice adds no universal Dexterity, Focus or hidden defender proficiency. A body with no feasible useful clearance is an actual interceptor even when the shot's source has low handling. In particular, a low ray can strike a crouched person, and an inert target cannot become intangible through a poor accuracy roll.

For a **qualified physically feasible response only**, use the source's captured native handling `H` and the selected contact-channel factor from the weapon table. Let `s*` be the admitted score, with the same effect for player-owned and NPC defenders, including disconnected/fading bodies. Then `p = clamp01(H × Fcontact(s*) × C)` is the chance that this available response fails. For `0 < p < 1`, take exactly one saved-world draw `u`; contact follows when `u < p`, otherwise the actual duck commits. At `p=1`, contact follows without a draw; at `p=0`, the qualified pose commits without a draw. No release deviation, off-ray roll, broad-bound roll or second miss after injury remains. Existing base `conditionAdjustment: none` stays neutral; its inactive `.85` coefficient is not an extra live penalty.

The ordinary body profile remains canonical. One saved native pose record derives the actual shorter capsule and anchors for every world query; it is not a renderer-only flag or a changed navigational location. The response changes the shape atomically, then all relevant same-time candidates are recomputed before any remaining world time advances. A current guard uses the existing directional shield/contact-defense owner and is incompatible with this automatic duck. If independent physical shield geometry is later installed, its earlier real collision cannot be skipped because the carrier could evade.

No arrow retains a permanently ignored body life. A ducked body is still hit wherever its actual shape intersects; after leaving one crossing, the same moving body may reenter that flight and be hit. Its pose/cooldown prevent repeated response attempts, not collision. The same ray can continue into another body, a wall or its source after the source's initial launch-overlap escape. A pending observed-miss cue is tied only to one continuous crossing and never suppresses later geometry. Show **Miss** only after actual clearance where the observer could perceive it; a pose occurrence alone does not prove the rest of the flight will miss.

The pose hold, return and response cooldown use game time and survive coherent saves with their exact body life/profile and the random sequence. At `1×` the hold is `0.2` real seconds and the cooldown is `1` real second; at `0.5×/3×/8×` they scale with the simulation just like native movement and attacks. The human ring continues in real interactive time. If standing restoration is obstructed, the character remains visibly and physically ducked; the renderer cannot make it stand by animation alone. Actual injury/collateral, private defender facts and personally observed evidence retain their native owners.

## Residents, observers and inventions

Autonomous characters use real competence, supplies and chosen motives through ordinary native attacks; they do not submit fabricated pointer histories or gain automatic perfect timing. Ordinary input is neutral. An admitted timed attack applies its supported score at actual contact without a player/NPC ownership exception. Observation remains limited to permitted actual events.

Inventions use the existing finite ranged-weapon families and their exact admitted support. Labels such as “legendary” and arbitrary generated `+25% accuracy` do not create mechanics. Other worlds can omit timing or select different supported body and weapon rules without fallback to this world. Ordinary woven-container manufacture retains its material-derived capacity and existing work.

## Game pace, accessibility and trust

Human input uses real interactive time. Native movement, work, flight, windup and recovery retain the existing 60 game seconds per real second at 1× and the selected speed multiplier. A held ring does not pause other actors or native consequences.

| Selected speed | Game seconds per real second | An 18-game-second preparation | A 36-game-second recovery |
| -------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| 0.5×           | 30                           | 600 ms                        | 1,200 ms                  |
| 1×             | 60                           | 300 ms                        | 600 ms                    |
| 3×             | 180                          | 100 ms                        | 200 ms                    |
| 8×             | 480                          | 37.5 ms                       | 75 ms                     |

These are direct clock conversions before latency or extra handling; the specific weapon table owns its actual values. The ring remains a one-real-second cycle at all these speeds. At 8× a target may move substantially during a single human choice; that is an explicit acceptance risk to measure, not permission to hide a slowdown or change world time. A different combat/calendar clock would require a separate product decision.

Use the [explicit attack controls](../../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#62-explicit-attack-controls), useful ordinary attacks and non-color timing cues. Physical aim remains pointer-driven. Reduced motion does not freeze the animated challenge or choose a perfect release. Desktop button activation is not evidence of touch/controller parity or physical assistive-device acceptance.

The admitted timing score has the same supported effects against actual player-owned and NPC defenders. Native permission, geometry, defense, injury and recovery remain in force. Browser timing is untrusted: recomputation bounds effects but does not establish human effort or anti-cheat certification. There is no special competitive scoring exception.

## Human interaction scoring and meaningfulness

Combat’s [deterministic evaluator](../../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#22-deterministic-evaluation-and-presentation) consumes the admitted [timing profile](#concentric-release-interaction). It is neither a collision verdict nor proof of human input authenticity.

Independent play must establish that tracking a moving threat and choosing a release is worthwhile compared with an ordinary attack, including after mastery. Mechanical correctness alone does not establish enjoyment.
