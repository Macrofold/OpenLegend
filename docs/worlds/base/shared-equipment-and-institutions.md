# Shared equipment — proposed Clearing Tool Library

This optional authored-world profile accompanies [DG32's product specification](../../projects/shared-equipment-and-institutions-feature-spec.md). It supplies a useful first institution through one real equipment loan. It is not installed content, a compulsory village, corporate property law or a monetary economy. [INV-20.5g–l](../../maintainers/inventions-and-world-evolution.md#dg32--shared-equipment-and-scoped-institutions) owns adoption and delivery; [INST-L01–INST-L08](../../limits/shared-equipment-and-institutions.md) owns proposed scope and growth gaps.

## The wanted outing

The Clearing Tool Library offers one real usable individual bow. Neri owns the bow and wants other people to try a ranged outing without permanently giving it away or attending every checkout. Sol has willingly accepted narrow lending authority. Neri has separately accepted the fallback keeper role. A known adult human borrower can ask to use the tool, understand its actual capability and return terms, accept or decline, take it on an ordinary outing and return the same object. Compatible ammunition is acquired or supplied separately through real supported actions. Borrowing supplies neither ammunition nor hunting proficiency, success, meat, harvesting or cooking.

This is a deliberately authored optional episode. It does not overwrite existing characters or imply that every starting camp already contains a bow, storage building or library. The creator must provide the actual supported tool, a reachable real depot, appropriate supplies and consenting roles through existing authoring and the newly selected lending permissions. Giving a place an institutional name creates no equipment. Owner and keeper remain real actors with ordinary needs and independent goals; a title supplies no automatic meals or free simulation.

The first loan is unsecured, without fees, dues, a default deadline or interest. Neri accepts finite exposure to this asset's unavailability or loss. The borrower can use the offered service as a guest; permanent association membership is optional for repeated cooperation. Nobody joins silently by borrowing. Someone who simply wants to give the bow away, barter for it, accompany its owner or make/acquire another tool can do that instead. The first experience must demonstrate a useful outing with less administration than benefit.

One owner, one keeper with the owner as an explicitly accepted fallback, one borrower, one bow and one depot define the first qualification. These are not a universal membership, world population or equipment limit. Wider stock, additional depots and simultaneous borrowers require actual resource, authority and workload qualification. No bag weight bonus, artificial manufacturing delay, maintenance chore or guaranteed reward is introduced to make lending seem necessary.

## What the institution means

The selected asset remains individually titled. The association is a coordination record; it cannot own property, sign for everyone or become a platform authority simply by existing. Five separate relationships remain visible where relevant: membership, individual title, actual custody, a named keeper's mandate, and the accepted return claim. A deposit is not silently a gift; a gift is not a retained loan; a keeper is not an owner.

Neri authorizes Sol to lend the exact bow on the approved terms. Sol accepts that limited role and may serve a request when willing. The mandate grants no self-promotion, general withdrawal, sale, sublending, claim waiver or authority over unrelated goods. A keeper's own borrowing needs the owner's separate approval. Other people can offer their own assets only through their own actual authority and consent. Borrowers independently accept the exact current loan; a group's decision does not consent for a member.

The depot has its own actual access authority. Neri's title to the bow cannot grant access to someone else's container or its ancestors. The current permission authority must admit the selected lending and return behavior at the real depot. Existing broad creator access lists are not already a deposit-only or ordinary officer permission system. This proposal adds a narrow consumer of the existing item owner; it does not turn every declared owner into a general grant editor.

The accepted mandate and any loan use the existing agreement and commitment owners, with no separate additional sixteen-place allowance. Pending proposals and amendments retain their existing bounds. The mandate takes one existing place for Neri and one for Sol, with Neri counted once despite accepting two roles. Neri also reserves one existing place for the one concurrently permitted loan. A borrower takes one existing place on actual checkout; keeper signing within the mandate creates no personal repayment debt. This proposed accounting must be qualified before delegation becomes available. A private permission or claim change must not reveal unrelated stock, a hidden relationship or another person's current location or knowledge.

## Checking out and using the bow

The borrower sees the actual selected item, the principal, the keeper's relevant authority, permitted use, return location, absence of a deadline/fee, and the accepted risk of loss. They see their own terms without opening the institution's private correspondence or everybody's loan history. If the bow is already lent, unavailable, altered incompatibly, committed to conflicting work or no longer covered by valid authority, the service cannot promise it again. A willing keeper and borrower complete a real exact transfer once. Repeated input or reconnection cannot produce a second bow or second loan.

The bow remains the same individual item during equipment use and storage. Normal attacks consume actual compatible ammunition and follow ordinary action, interruption and encounter rules. Its loan does not reserve an animal, force anyone to accompany the borrower or ensure a useful shot. Ordinary selected physical risks remain real. A change to the tool's condition must be represented by actual supported behavior; no invented wear charge appears after the outing.

## A return that does not wait for the keeper

Checkout reserves actual compatible room for this exact object's return at the agreed depot. Other deposits cannot consume that room. This reservation survives the keeper's current work ending and is explicitly new lending behavior, not the existing short-lived work reservation. The selected depot must support the promise before checkout is offered.

The borrower receives a narrow continuing return permission. At the real reachable depot, they may place the qualifying bow into its reserved location and receive evidence of the completed return without an online keeper accepting it again. That authority exposes no unrelated contents, permits no other withdrawal and stores no arbitrary cargo. Membership exit, keeper revocation and closure of new lending do not remove the accepted return route or its room. Accepted return and loan settlement use one actual result; a completed return cannot leave a duplicate outstanding demand.

Changing the depot or its access must preserve the actual agreed return route or obtain the affected borrower's assent to material changed terms. Posting an unseen sign or appointing a new keeper is not that assent. If the depot is destroyed, blocked by real world conditions or otherwise genuinely unusable, the borrower receives a truthful incomplete outcome from permitted evidence. The bow stays in its actual location; neither a replacement container nor a successful delivery is conjured. The parties can arrange a supported alternative or settlement. Blocked return is not automatic borrower fault.

## Departure, loss and closure

Sol can stop keeping the library. Neri can revoke future lending authority. The previously accepted fallback may serve within its own authority and willingness; absence does not appoint a new person or teach anyone private facts. With no authorized willing keeper, new checkout stops. Existing exact returns remain possible while their actual destination survives.

A borrower can leave the group or stop playing. Departure ends future participation, not the existing title or claim. Current human protection, actual detached-property risk and world-service funding remain unchanged. The first profile does not promise uninterrupted operation, time-frozen NPCs or unlimited unattended storage. Death, a Human Continue where supported, a closed world and current-format restoration must preserve the actual item and accepted obligations under their existing owners. Relatives acquire no debt or authority by implication.

Losing or destroying the bow is a real adverse outcome. A return claim is evidence of agreed responsibility, not a remote recovery power or proof of intentional wrongdoing. The rightful claimant can accept an exact replacement or waive their own claim through actual agreement; an unauthorized keeper cannot do so. Neither the group name nor a private decision seizes unrelated bags, assigns a credit score, transfers liability to relatives or charges the player's real platform account.

Closing the library stops future lending and winds down actual assets and claims. It does not delete goods, refund every contribution as a new copy, expose formerly restricted storage to the public or forgive every outstanding loan. Existing narrow returns continue; each titled asset and retained claim has a real disposition. Records retain only the actual permitted evidence of what happened. Reopening or renaming the service does not reset losses or create stock.

## Separately selected extensions

The product specification describes an optional secured loan using one exact nonperishable collateral lot in real custody. It is not part of this first episode. Before offering it, qualify the actual held goods, scoped access, once-only release on return, blocked/destroyed custody, accepted loss settlement and closure. A price guide cannot make collateral liquid, and a calendar deadline does not justify automatic forfeiture. Full collateral can make the loan useless to the person who lacks resources.

Corporate title, a pooled treasury, wages, recurring dues, interest, elections, courts and a territorial government need a wanted activity of their own. A simple charter should explain who may change this service without requiring political administration before borrowing a tool. Same-world usefulness precedes any compatible travel extension; the travel and restore owners must reconcile real external custody before goods or histories cross those boundaries.
