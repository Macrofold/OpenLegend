The ordinary world installs interpreted fear and anger through the [two-stage threat route](../../projects/combat-and-threat-responses-tech-design.md). Both automatic damage-to-fear producers are removed; bodily discomfort, physical injury and urgent evidence retain their native owners. Controlled service checks cover interpretation publication and subsequent action admission, including a failed second judgment. Independent character behavior, complete repertoire and scheduling qualification remain open. These fictional character models do not claim measured human psychology. The selected joy profile, native shock and independent review below are unimplemented consumers of the [general emotion contract](../../projects/emotional-physiology-tech-design.md). This document is the single authored owner of their base-world numerical choices; the engine implements their finite families.

## Selected first profile

They deliberately give a small interpretation a short life and a strong interpretation enough persistence to be available at a later decision. Reusing the existing linear-decay mechanism keeps that trial small; matching the current fear/discomfort rate is not evidence that these feelings ought to fade alike. Compare other rates and available amounts if the complete episode exposes an unhelpful result; do not present the initial values as optimal or universal engineering bounds.

## Interpreted threat reactions

### Selected scope and existing owners

The installed interpreted-threat route does not derive fear from damage divided by maximum health, including known-shooter projectile contact. Native injury, pain, body-condition evidence and urgency remain immediate physical facts. They do not wait for a model. The selected appraisal layer decides what those facts mean to this individual. A frightening miss, visible threatened violence, uncertainty about an unseen source, a chance of escape or another person's withdrawal can matter even with zero health loss.

Reuse `AppraisalDefinition`, `changeAppraisal`, exact source bindings, target stacking, current-format records and the immediate-operation integration already selected for joy. Do not add a global fear total, aggression meter, psychological need bar, independent appraisal daemon or second episode-history store. The [threat option profile](threat-response-options.md) owns possible purposes; current goals/plans own the person's accepted commitment. A feeling alone never authorizes an action.

The first threat profile installs interpreted **fear** and **anger** for eligible autonomous memory-capable people. Fear concerns possible harm; anger concerns perceived opposition, harm or violated values. Both may coexist. Neither is a probability of attacking. Pain/discomfort remains the existing bodily experience; this design does not turn ordinary physical sensation into a paid judgment. The general emotion slice also specifies joy and new independent review of the already installed source-linked qualitative grief below. Calm, restlessness, broader social needs and permanent personality development retain their separate owners and scope.

### Appraisal questions and source binding

Each relevant decision supplies the complete accepted identity, current body and abilities, actual work at stake, current purpose, known alternatives, personally available threat facts and the current cause-linked feelings. The shared world rubric asks the model to consider:

| Dimension                      | Actor-specific question and evidence boundary                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Stakes                         | What could this event cost this person, physically or in something they value? Current body damage is evidence, not the complete meaning.                                                         |
| Cause and intent               | What did this person actually perceive, what do they infer, and what remains unknown? An identified attacker, a suspicious person and an unseen impact are different facts.                       |
| Control and alternatives       | What can this body currently do, which actions or exits are feasible, and what costs or recovery constrain them? Confidence is personal interpretation, not authority to invent a route or skill. |
| Appraised opposition           | Does this person interpret the conduct as deliberate opposition, accident, an understandable mistake or uncertain intent? The source's private mind is unavailable.                               |
| Current commitment and values  | How does this affect a currently chosen purpose, valued person/place, bodily autonomy or actual unfinished work? No goal or relationship is generated merely to justify a reaction.               |
| Change since the last judgment | What new permitted evidence materially changes the interpretation? Re-reading, elapsed provider time, the feeling's own notification and repeated paraphrase are not new external occurrences.    |

These dimensions are prompt/question meaning, not six new stored meters. The model need not write an explanation or score every dimension. A finite question asks for the supported feeling amount or no change using this rubric; another asks whether each supplied purpose is relevant. Batch independent questions in the **first of the two threat decision stages**. Stage two then receives the admitted feeling results and independently chooses an action. Do not multiply emotion combinations by every tactic, and do not add a third appraisal-only call.

Every offered operation binds a personally eligible new source occurrence or an exact supported remembered source, its revision, a permitted subject or no identified subject, the installed policy pin and the prior active record revision where supplied. Known-opponent fear/anger uses that opponent as subject. Unidentified danger uses no guessed person; its cause still says what was felt or observed. Present all still-relevant current threats when judging an unidentified-danger interpretation so that the latest small cue cannot erase other danger by accidental omission.

Within one threat pipeline, prepare at most one question and admit at most one write for each actor, exact definition pin and target-stacking key. When several eligible causes have coalesced, bind the newest personally relevant eligible occurrence in the captured native sequence and include earlier still-relevant evidence and the current interpretation as context. Keep every original source identity; do not manufacture a combined cause or let arbitrary question order decide which fear value survives. With no eligible new cause, current runtime offers only existing no-change/resolution/reframe choices. The selected emotion extension may additionally offer an exact owner-issued reassessment opportunity under [EP-C04](../../projects/emotional-physiology-tech-design.md#ep-c04--reassessment-algebra-and-admission); it does not forge a new cause or change reframe. The binding selects a lawful source, not a feeling amount or compulsory reaction, and cannot consume newer evidence beyond the captured cut.

These values are game tuning hypotheses. At the current 60:1 clock, fear 0.5 naturally fades in 120 normal-play seconds and anger 0.5 in 240, absent a permitted same-cause reassessment, new-cause replacement or resolution; these are the corresponding wall durations only at 1×. The time-based control preference in the combat design is independent and much shorter. Changing speed or pausing uses game-time semantics; there is no wall-time catch-up after reopening. No claim of realistic human hormone timing is made.

The selected value is an **absolute current interpretation**, not an increment based on another hit. Supply the current value and still-relevant circumstances when judging a new occurrence. The model may choose a larger value after repeated deliberate blows, a lower value after seeing a credible escape, or no change. A later small injury must not automatically replace strong fear with `small damage / maxHealth`. Native admission enforces source and scale correctness, not an invented monotonic emotion law.

Use the existing target-stacking create operation for a genuinely new cause. It resolves that family's preceding active interpretation while retaining its exact historical outcome and deduplication tombstone. Its new occurrence/operation identity distinguishes an independent experience from replay. A same-cause psychological reassessment instead uses the general emotion owner's distinct operation; combat does not install its own writer. The earlier source and value remain historical evidence, never a second active record. A lower psychological interpretation must still be selected. Target departure alone does not settle anger; its installed native curve continues, and a supported reassessment/resolution can change it. A review deadline never assigns an amount or proves forgiveness. Exact resolution is not automatically relationship repair.

Reframing the same decaying record retains the existing prohibition on changing intensity or restarting its curve. Recalling the same blow, seeing an unchanged countdown, processing the same coalesced cue twice, or the feeling-change event waking attention cannot create another record or renew its lifetime. The binder checks the original occurrence through current/cold outcomes. A newly perceived withdrawal or a materially distinct later occurrence may support a new interpretation; a generic encounter label or regenerated summary cannot manufacture one. If no exact new source exists, retain the current feeling or offer permitted resolution/reframe; a separately eligible bounded reassessment can revise intensity under its original-cause horizon. None of these operations synthesizes an external cause.

### Immediate context and continuity

Both model stages receive current admitted intensity **with its meaning**, subject/cause, relevant lifetime and observation uncertainty. The current `decisionFeelings` projection includes intensity, scale, lifetime and permitted source coverage; these fields must remain in both stages. Use shared personal rendering, so the affected person receives “my” body experience and the correct actor/subject roles; another observer never receives private numerical state. A person may be visibly frightened without disclosing their private amount, and an aggressive action does not prove anger.

Required existing interpretations concerning these exact subjects/sources, including relevant targetless danger, must be acquired through the same appraisal owner's exact scoped lookup or continued pages. The first 24-record convenience/reflection prefix is not evidence that a later relevant record is absent. Preserve native privacy, live-record, decay and revision checks and existing preparation accounting. If the complete mandatory relevant set cannot be prepared within the admitted budget, keep the opportunity pending with an explicit incomplete-context disposition; do not silently infer no prior fear or anger. Unrelated optional recall remains optional.

Stage one's accepted feeling receipts remain tied to the original input cause identities and the current job's evidence cut. Their publication and resulting attention notification are already being consumed by stage two; they must not wake another two-call pipeline for that same interpretation. Revalidate from the post-publication snapshot without ignoring intervening external changes. Suppress only those exact known derived receipts, never later independent causes or all self-authored events. A new blow, meaningful communicated change, actual subsequent physical action result or a later subscribed decay/goal-condition boundary can still supply its ordinary fresh opportunity. Reading or replaying the same interpretation cannot generate another cause, refresh its lifetime or buy its own decision retry.

If stage one commits a feeling and stage two fails, the feeling remains a real accepted private result; the character has not chosen a physical action. A fresh relevant occurrence can later trigger a new ordinary decision. Preserve the stage-one receipt, charged requests and explicit stage-two failure. No automatic paid fallback, inferred attack, fictional thought or rollback of an independently accepted feeling occurs. A stale source, lost authority or changed definition refuses the affected update before publication.

No generic character prompt says “always fight,” “maximize survival,” or “please the player.” The shared world prompt supplies truthful embodied stakes, the possibility of fear and action coexisting, and the distinction between stopping immediate danger and pursuing another objective. An individual's values determine how to use them. The [Ada requirement](threat-response-options.md#4-initial-ada-disposition-and-required-behavior) must be met through her authored assertiveness, actual context and free selection; other people remain allowed to withdraw or interpret the same event differently.

### Qualification and definition changes

CTR06 must show native harm/urgency before model completion; fear from a credible zero-damage attack when independently selected; different fear/anger/no-change outcomes from different coherent people; coexisting fear and anger; lower, higher and unchanged interpretations with source evidence; exact scale in later choice; and no compulsory violence. Verify repeated same-source processing, cold duplicate lookup, simultaneous known and unknown sources, different target lives, correction/forgetting during both calls, permission loss, source correction, decay, pause and current-format reopen.

The new world definition and removal of automatic fear producers are one coordinated change. Do not leave old damage-created fear running alongside interpreted fear or retain a hidden generic fallback. Current development installs/writers/readers/validators change together; old development-save migration is out of scope. Live replacement/removal still obeys the current immutable-pin restriction for retained records. This design does not authorize rewriting historical meanings in an existing world or applying a new scale to old numbers. Character seed changes likewise affect newly authored worlds; accepted saved personalities are not silently reset.

An isolated joy installation must preserve the independently installed fear/anger profile. Integrated emotion qualification additionally exercises the selected reassessment, bodily reaction and mechanical contracts. Report native correctness, independently chosen behavior and player enjoyment separately. No runtime, provider or player evidence is supplied by these documents.

## Native startle and persistent fear integration

**Designed target; runtime pending.** The [general emotion architecture](../../projects/emotional-physiology-tech-design.md#ep-c05--native-bodily-reaction-admission-and-receipts) implements the [shared SC08–SC09 contract](../../projects/stealth-awareness-tech-design.md#emotions-and-reconsideration). Bodily startle, interpreted emotion and derived mechanics remain separate owners. The native effect applies to an eligible controlled person, autonomous inhabitant or native animal without memory or AI. It cannot force an interpretation, reveal another mind or choose an action.

### Selected bodily tuning

These are authored game-feel hypotheses, not physiological measurements. They deliberately make the initial disruption brief and make composure visible without turning it into fearlessness. The exact installed body/profile and attribute bindings supply these values; labels such as “brave” or “sensitive” are never parsed into numbers.

It need not create a memory, obtain a private fear interpretation or run emotional review. The same abrupt stimulus need not mean danger to every body. A synthetic body can omit startle while its memory-capable character retains supported interpreted fear; any direct mechanical mapping still requires an applicable installed parameter.

## Adaptive review policy

The independently selected `emotional-review` operation can reconsider supported feelings while ordinary activity continues. It produces no required thought, speech, goal or action. The appraisal owner keeps one exact review subrecord per enrolled feeling and the existing actor coordinator coalesces those reasons into one character ticket. No periodic job scans every character or buys a model check each tick.

### Authored appraisal time and review profiles

The selected current-schema authoring fields are `durationNormalSeconds` and `decayPerNormalSecond`. Runtime/save continuation retains the pin, conversion, simulation anchor/rate/deadline; it never reapplies the speed multiplier to a stored duration. This is an explicit coordinated schema/definition update, not a relabel of the current `perHour` field. The selected conversions preserve installed fear/anger and discomfort decay at the base 60:1 clock. Discomfort's selected rate is 1/240 per normal second and it remains the existing native producer with no independent review enrollment. Existing wilderness:calm's 30-simulation-second expiry converts to durationNormalSeconds 0.5; its disposition process interval remains in the existing simulated-second units. Neither field is merely relabeled into a 60-times-longer duration.

The separately installed `wilderness:authored-grief` v1 remains authored-only with decision/reflection disabled and no AI review. It is not converted into experience-linked grief. Source-linked grief creation still uses its existing supported reflection/admission path; independent review changes an already lawful feeling without requiring another deliberate reflection. Initial fear/anger creation schedules an enabled first review interval, while joy/grief creation establishes a dormant epoch with no immediate review. Creation itself is not a review attempt.

Fear/anger review bands enter low at 0.2 and high at 0.5. They leave high below 0.45 and leave low below 0.15. A changed band must remain stable for 1 normal second before it can make a review eligible. Mechanical fear itself remains continuous and immediate; this hysteresis controls scheduling only. Actual fear/anger below 0.2 starts no timed follow-up even if the hysteresis band is retained down to 0.15. Keep band/allowance state but park its timer; a native crossing alone cannot wake dormant support. Dispatches for the same feeling are at least 1 normal second apart across support epochs, and standalone review dispatches for the character are also at least 1 normal second apart. A compatible already authorized ordinary request does not wait for the character standalone spacing, but any included review must satisfy its own record spacing and exact eligibility. A new urgent physical threat uses ordinary urgent cognition independently and never waits for this optional-review spacing.

Interval entries are delays from the last relevant admission or accepted material reassessment, indexed by the epoch's already consumed attempt ordinal. Only a meaningfully changed accepted result can earn the next unchanged-support timed follow-up. Retain, uncertainty, a submitted failure or exhaustion makes that unchanged support dormant immediately. A later timer, native decay crossing, the body's expected startle recovery or a derived feeling notification cannot reset the allowance or wake that dormant epoch.

Admitted material independent support can start a new bounded epoch: actually acquired new evidence, a newly experienced bodily condition, a known safety/escape/separation transition, or a real useful activity/result changing the relevant concern. Continued unseen safety is not evidence. Routine footsteps, a countdown, rephrased recall, a model's own conclusion and repeated sampling of one unchanged condition are not new epochs. A still-active episode can reach a review deadline without a new external cause, using its original evidence and remaining finite allowance.

Hysteresis crossings and elapsed recovery make an eligible review possible, never mandatory paid work. Select only relevant active feelings and batch them per character through existing cognition admission. Quiet stable grief, joy and mild fear/anger have no periodic requests. Provider availability, budgets and coalescing cannot clear the feeling. Actual configured intervals and attempt counts are world tuning; engine envelopes and overflow behavior are recorded in [feelings limits](../../limits/feelings.md).

### Finite review choices

For scaled fear/anger/joy, author at most four absolute review levels including scale maximum; the selected levels are 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1. Preparation filters infeasible values against current scale/horizon, removes values equal to current intensity and deduplicates. It can also supply the exact remaining feasible ceiling and the halfway-to-minimum lower value when distinct. Retain, permitted resolve and unknown are separate options. There are at most eight total choices: a submaximum ceiling necessarily removes the authored maximum, so numeric alternatives remain at most five. These are exact prepared native options, not clamped model outputs or rounded display numbers. An already authorized generative route may propose any feasible finite value without buying another call.

Qualitative source-linked grief offers retain/resolve/unknown and, only with genuinely newly material permitted support, a provenance-only reframe using the existing interpretationSource. It adds no qualitative stage, freeform emotion payload, intensity or extra timed follow-up. Current-source reuse, another alias of the occurrence, and cycling already consumed support are retain. The independent review permission never grants the authored-grief profile new model authority.

### Personality, coping and current relevance

Composure's installed native recovery effect does not dictate courage. Courage is choosing a valued risk while fear may remain high. Sensitivity concerns what this person notices or finds meaningful, including beauty and kindness; it is not necessarily jumpiness. Temperament, persistence, expression and preferred coping remain attributed traits and accepted personal history supplied as context. Existing prose traits such as steadfastness do not silently become mechanical coefficients. The selected composure value is explicit authored mechanical data; this slice adds no autonomous model writer, training rule or automatic long-term mutation of that value.

Appraisal questions use the person's actual remembered values, uncertainty, current work and feasible alternatives. Helping someone reach shelter, a successful repair, rest, company, a meal or chosen solitude can supply actual experience and make review eligible. None guarantees relief, gratitude, trust or a relationship change. Another person's intent to comfort, facial presentation or claim that the danger has ended is testimony/observation, never permission to assign the recipient's private feeling.

A retained grief may become relevant around its known subject, reminder or value without dominating every unrelated task. Fading fear is not proof of safety. The ordinary source/retrieval owners select context; the mechanical query separately includes the complete active applicable set. Long-term belief/trait development, broader psychological needs and process authoring retain DG23, D69 and ACT09 rather than becoming an automatic dashboard or per-tick needs engine.

### Directly controlled characters

[SA-D01](../../../archive/05-project/open-decisions.md#sa-d01--psychological-fear-for-controlled-characters) remains unresolved for all longer-term interpreted emotions. Native bodily reactions and physical conditions apply equally. The recommended initial option is native bodily effects with no autonomous psychological writer for the directly controlled character. If persistent interpreted fear is selected for normal player mechanics, deterministic authored native interpretation remains the shared conditional recommendation; opt-in bounded AI is another explicit option. Exact alternatives, control-transfer handling and acceptance are in the [feature specification](../../projects/emotional-physiology-feature-spec.md). No conditional option is implicitly installed by this document.

A profile needing an unsupported kind of change, new need, curve or cause still needs a reviewed native capability; natural-language authoring alone cannot supply execution. The pilot adds no social-need bar and does not enroll an internal condition/disposition process. Wider need selection under D69 and process authoring under ACT09 remain separate.

Compact aliases are optional request-local vocabulary. A request may define `j = joy` alongside its meaning, units and exact permitted choices. That spelling grants no authority and is not assumed to occupy one tokenizer token. With no alias, use the installed full label and a bound handle. With no installed definition or eligible source, omit its operations; never interpret an unknown code as joy, guess a policy from a label or substitute a bundled default. Definitions with no numerical scale remain qualitative.

Installed meaning is pinned before selection. An edit/removal makes prepared choices stale before publication; it cannot reinterpret an old number under a different scale. The first profile refuses replacement or removal while **any retained canonical feeling or creation outcome** depends on that exact definition pin, including cold, resolved and invalidated records. Resolving/retiring an active feeling is not sufficient: current-format restoration still validates the historical pin, and a changed pin must not let the same original occurrence create joy again under a different identity. A separately supported live definition transition would have to preserve both historical meaning and original-occurrence deduplication across definition revisions before this restriction can change. No such transition, compatibility reader or migration is included in this slice; preserve the immutable installed pin and current same-world integrity.

## Privacy and evidence

Each interpreted feeling is private to its owner and separately authorized inspection. Saying something cheerful or choosing a smile is an independent supported expression; it neither reveals the numerical value nor proves a corresponding private feeling. Nearby people receive only the actual words, supported compact expressive contribution or observed conduct that their installed senses disclose, and interpret it for themselves. A cause handle cannot disclose an unheard conversation or another person's private measurement.

Source correction, forgetting, current privacy overlays and same-format restoration follow the existing [appraisal and experience owners](../../projects/continuing-lives-tech-design.md#55-numerical-personal-reactions-without-required-prose), including the current broad invalidation of that actor's feelings when memory is corrected/erased. This trial does not narrow that conservative rule or recover a forgotten cause from shared history.

## Acceptance and revisiting the hypothesis

Qualification must show a silent numerical creation through the ordinary actor path; no-change selection; two independently informed people assigning different interpretations to the same permitted contribution; later choice receiving the actual scale/intensity/cause; correct decay during game time; and duplicate/cold-source, stale-definition, correction-during-await, forgetting and current-format reopen behavior. Verify refusal of definition replacement/removal while active or cold terminal records retain its pin, a rejected amount and omission of the profile, with the existing baseline feelings unchanged. Provider fixtures prove binding/admission, not naturally chosen or psychologically plausible reactions. Controlled live comparisons and a complete CE episode must distinguish voluntary subsequent conduct from a forced happy response. No such runtime or live evidence is supplied by this design.

Revisit the vocabulary, available amounts, rate and notification bands when that evidence shows poor expressiveness, unnecessary churn, no useful later influence or excessive preparation/cost. Broader psychological needs and permanent trait changes are not implied. The [feelings inventory](../../limits/feelings.md#fl21--compact-numerical-personal-reactions) records this profile's constraints and remaining evidence.
