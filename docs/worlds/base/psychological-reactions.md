# Compact personal reactions — proposed wilderness profile

| Status      | Current progress                                                                                                                         | Last updated |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| In progress | The first numerical reaction profile is selected for design; installation, runtime integration and behavioral qualification remain open. | 2026-10-15   |

**Selected design, not installed behavior.** A person may privately experience joy after interpreting something they actually perceived or recalled, without composing a thought or saying anything. This profile is the smallest first consumer of the [continuing-life reaction contract](../../projects/continuing-lives-tech-design.md#55-numerical-personal-reactions-without-required-prose). It describes fictional character state, not measured human psychology. Existing fear/discomfort, grief, calm and restlessness behavior remains unchanged until a separately admitted definition change says otherwise.

## Selected first profile

These are adjustable authored hypotheses for a first end-to-end trial. They deliberately give a small interpretation a short life and a strong interpretation enough persistence to be available at a later decision. Reusing the existing linear-decay mechanism keeps that trial small; matching the current fear/discomfort rate is not evidence that these feelings ought to fade alike. Compare other rates and available amounts if the complete episode exposes an unhelpful result; do not present the initial values as optimal or universal engineering bounds.

The pilot's own feeling creation/change/result, its numerical read/display and the resulting reaction wake are not new independent stimuli for that same feeling. Derived notifications retain the original permitted cause through existing aliases and cannot refresh or recursively stack joy. This is a native source check, with no extra classification call.

A hypothetical thanking encounter illustrates the difference from a reward script: a tired person may interpret the same words as welcome appreciation, insincerity or an interruption. The output may be joy of a chosen intensity, no joy, or another separately supported reaction. There is no rule mapping the word “thanks,” a successful trade or a shared outing to points. The current actor's private knowledge matters; the speaker cannot assign the listener's amount.

## Installation, vocabulary and removal

The future implementation places this profile's supported definition, scale descriptions, finite amounts and optional compact aliases beside `packages/domain/src/worlds/base/appraisals.ts`, with the existing selected-world manifest and validation owning installation. The label and scale must reach ordinary actor context, request schema/legend, native admission, private inspection and current-format saves from that one source. This document owns the proposed authored choices; engine/server code must not contain fallback joy rules.

The existing qualitative/scaled value and persistent/expiry/linear-decay families can be installed as data without authoring a new executable evaluator. A profile needing an unsupported kind of change, new need, curve or cause still needs a reviewed native capability; natural-language authoring alone cannot supply execution. The pilot adds no social-need bar and does not enroll an internal condition/disposition process. Wider need selection under D69 and process authoring under ACT09 remain separate.

Compact aliases are optional request-local vocabulary. A request may define `j = joy` alongside its meaning, units and exact permitted choices. That spelling grants no authority and is not assumed to occupy one tokenizer token. With no alias, use the installed full label and a bound handle. With no installed definition or eligible source, omit its operations; never interpret an unknown code as joy, guess a policy from a label or substitute a bundled default. Definitions with no numerical scale remain qualitative.

Installed meaning is pinned before selection. An edit/removal makes prepared choices stale before publication; it cannot reinterpret an old number under a different scale. The first profile refuses replacement or removal while **any retained canonical feeling or creation outcome** depends on that exact definition pin, including cold, resolved and invalidated records. Resolving/retiring an active feeling is not sufficient: current-format restoration still validates the historical pin, and a changed pin must not let the same original occurrence create joy again under a different identity. A separately supported live definition transition would have to preserve both historical meaning and original-occurrence deduplication across definition revisions before this restriction can change. No such transition, compatibility reader or migration is included in this slice; preserve the immutable installed pin and current same-world integrity.

## Privacy and evidence

The joy record is private to its owner and separately authorized inspection. Saying something cheerful or choosing a smile is an independent supported expression; it neither reveals the numerical value nor proves a corresponding private feeling. Nearby people receive only the actual words, supported compact expressive contribution or observed conduct that their installed senses disclose, and interpret it for themselves. A cause handle cannot disclose an unheard conversation or another person's private measurement.

Source correction, forgetting, current privacy overlays and same-format restoration follow the existing [appraisal and experience owners](../../projects/continuing-lives-tech-design.md#55-numerical-personal-reactions-without-required-prose), including the current broad invalidation of that actor's feelings when memory is corrected/erased. This trial does not narrow that conservative rule or recover a forgotten cause from shared history.

## Acceptance and revisiting the hypothesis

Qualification must show a silent numerical creation through the ordinary actor path; no-change selection; two independently informed people assigning different interpretations to the same permitted contribution; later choice receiving the actual scale/intensity/cause; correct decay during game time; and duplicate/cold-source, stale-definition, correction-during-await, forgetting and current-format reopen behavior. Verify refusal of definition replacement/removal while active or cold terminal records retain its pin, a rejected amount and omission of the profile, with the existing baseline feelings unchanged. Provider fixtures prove binding/admission, not naturally chosen or psychologically plausible reactions. Controlled live comparisons and a complete CE episode must distinguish voluntary subsequent conduct from a forced happy response. No such runtime or live evidence is supplied by this design.

Revisit the vocabulary, available amounts, rate and notification bands when that evidence shows poor expressiveness, unnecessary churn, no useful later influence or excessive preparation/cost. Broader psychological needs and permanent trait changes are not implied. The [feelings inventory](../../limits/feelings.md#fl21--compact-numerical-personal-reactions) records this profile's constraints and remaining evidence.
