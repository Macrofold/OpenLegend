# Perception and attention in the base world

**Specified target; implementation and gameplay qualification pending.** These are reversible game settings for the [common feature](../../features/perception-and-attention/README.md). They are not measurements of human psychology or claims about current runtime capacity.

This profile owns common attention priority and interruption tuning. [Attention and investigation](attention-and-investigation.md) retains physical methods and their deadlines; [vision and gaze](vision-and-gaze.md) retains actual looking; [psychological reactions](psychological-reactions.md) and [combat](combat.md) retain startle, warning and preparation. No setting below replaces those laws.

## Reasons and priority

Use a 0–100 priority scale. The score orders the next useful observation; it does not choose an action or probability. For each candidate take the maximum supported current reason. The following named bands supply initial defaults; a specifically authored concern can choose another value within the scale and must disclose its basis.

| Supported reason | Priority | Demand and qualification |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Immediate supported serious danger to self or a currently affected protectee | 100 | Prompt native protection remains independent; useful danger observation outranks optional work. An actual threat/response opportunity is required. |
| Clearly supported serious harm happening nearby | 95 | Continuing attention while the danger is personally supported or a chosen finite response needs it. A person visibly burning is an example if that physical mechanic exists. |
| Possible serious incident supported by independent compatible emergency cues | 90 | Prompt assessment; maintain a bounded unresolved concern. The conclusion remains tentative. |
| Understood urgent help request connected to an actual duty, relationship or accepted concern | 85 | Prompt consideration and finite follow-through; a request remains testimony. Unknown speaker identity does not forbid understanding the request. |
| Important supported opportunity is about to close | 80 | Attend while the owner's real response window applies. Ordinary tasks with no such window do not qualify. |
| Strong unexpected cue whose consequence is not yet known | 75 | Brief check. Intensity at the receiver and supported unexpectedness are required; distant source energy alone does not qualify. |
| Relevant unexpected change to the subject of an accepted watch | 70 | Register/inspect the change; continue only for an unresolved purpose. Seeing access to watched supplies does not establish theft. |
| Current chosen pursuit or work needs useful continuing information | 60 | Maintain information while the accepted work remains applicable. A pursuit of an imminent attacker also has the stronger actual danger reason. |
| Expected relevant work change, ordinary direct request or personally expected result | 50 | Register or handle the result; no automatic continuous tracking of the source. |
| Useful positive interest or unfamiliar phenomenon | 25 | Finite optional examination when capacity permits. Interest does not grant knowledge or a reward. |
| Background movement with no further demand | 0 | Broad observation may change; no sustained-detail entitlement is created. |

There is no separate clarity, distance, number-of-observations or familiarity addition. Supported familiarity and knowledge can establish who is asking, what a signal means, or why a concern applies. They cannot identify an unknown source through hidden truth.

A known expected authorized access can use the expected-work band or satisfy its watch immediately. If the person was explicitly asked to report every opening, the opening is still reported. The absence of a theft interpretation does not cancel that accepted obligation.

An unsupported hypothetical harm does not use the serious-danger band. An uncertain but acquired alarming cue can still receive the brief-check band or support a finite possible-incident concern. A directly experienced injury and an actually observed new threat remain eligible despite prior harmless alarms.

## Selection and detail

Use the installed Focus attribute on its 0–100 scale. An ordinary human-like profile has capacity round(5 + effective Focus / 10), or 5–15 detailed subjects; missing authored Focus uses 50, giving ten. An explicit actor profile override must be finite and admitted under the same capability/resource rules. This is a detail allowance, not proof of ten simultaneous directions of sight, a visibility cap or a memory promise.

Required personal evidence and native effects are outside optional slot competition. Allocate useful currently supported demands by priority, preserving actual bodily compatibility. A brief register-only demand needs no sustained slot once served.

An ordinary challenger replaces a lower-priority optional incumbent when its priority is at least **5 points higher**. At equal priority retain an incumbent while its demand remains useful; after a brief demand is served or an ordinary demand ends, admit the oldest comparable pending demand, with stable personal acquisition order as the final tie-break.

A supported reason at **85 or higher**, or an independently supported response deadline that would be missed by waiting for ordinary stability, bypasses the replacement margin against a lower-priority incumbent. Equal-priority emergencies use the earliest supported response deadline, then continuation and stable personal acquisition order. A known deadline precedes an unknown one only among equal-priority candidates; an unknown deadline is not infinite safety.

There is **no compulsory minimum hold** before replacement. Received urgent evidence is considered at its actual acquisition boundary. This does not promise that a physical turn, a fresh sensory sample, a model call or a new action completes at that instant.

Quiet discovery uses compatible spare capacity and the existing genuine-free-interval policy. No discovery slot is protected against an immediate emergency. A waiting novelty gains no unbounded age bonus. Ordinary independently justified thought can include unfamiliar material even if it never won continuous detail.

## Brief demands and bounded assessment

| Policy | Starting value | Exact meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unexplained striking-cue demand | Up to **1 normal-play second** from receipt, ending earlier when the requested check is served | Reserves no permanent priority. If useful inspection is selected, that accepted work owns its continuation. Expiry means the brief opportunity ended, not that the source was harmless. |
| Brief assessment | Default **1 normal-play second** from actual admission | May finish early on useful answer/response. Includes the actual attempt to orient/check; no complete inspection is promised if alignment or dwell could not finish. |
| Explicit brief-assessment maximum | **5 normal-play seconds** | Longer inquiry uses the existing finite search/investigation methods. A chosen listening or examination method retains its actual duration unless an explicitly supported shorter variant was accepted. |
| Native emergency-cue combination window | **3 normal-play seconds** between the required personally acquired occurrences | Either cue order is allowed for the help-plus-battle pattern. Delayed delivery does not move occurrence times to make them fit. |
| Unresolved possible-incident demand | Up to **10 normal-play seconds** after the newest materially different supporting cue | A finite opportunity to assess, not continuously renewed by identical samples or repeated unresolved thought. Chosen search and ongoing supported danger retain their own lifetimes. |
| Approach-condition clearance | Default **2 normal-play seconds** of supported clear evidence, unless the observation family supplies a different qualified value | Unknown or incomplete coverage never clears. This is rearming, not attention-switch or thinking delay. |

Convert these normal-play values using the installed world clock at admission and retain the resulting game-time deadlines. At the current 60:1 clock, one normal-play second is 60 game seconds. Pause freezes game-time duration; acceleration changes the wall time required to reach it. Provider deadlines are separate.

For a nuisance-pursuit demonstration, the test guard explicitly accepts this contingency: an acquired unrelated striking/possible-emergency cue at priority **75 or above** activates assessment when it exceeds the current pursuit's strongest reason by the ordinary replacement margin, or qualifies for the urgent bypass against a lower-priority pursuit. Activate once per materially new cue/incident. Stop at the earliest permitted safe movement boundary, assess for the default duration, and resume the original pursuit if no different response was accepted and continuation remains valid. A pursuit supported by immediate danger at 100 is not displaced by a 75-point blast. If the target is lost, use the accepted last-seen continuation, never hidden live coordinates. If that continuation is no longer valid, finish the assessment unresolved rather than invent a destination.

This is an example accepted pursuit method, not an automatic rule for all guards. A different accepted method may keep running while listening or prioritize immediate interception. Directly controlled characters receive no such involuntary motor policy from attention.

Startle retains its own supported effects and duration. It does not become this assessment timer and is not a mandatory freeze. The shared preparation owner's warning-registration and settling periods also retain their own meanings.

## Corroboration and learned repetition

The initial emergency pattern requires an understood help request and a distinct received sound classified as consistent with fighting. Their supported regions/bearings must overlap compatibly, or the words must explicitly refer to the same personally known place/incident. If neither relationship is available, retain separate cues. Do not assign a common event because the server knows both sources.

The combination supplies a possible-incident reason at 90, not proof of guilt, combatants or exact location. A statement from an unfamiliar person can still matter; recognized identity or duty may independently supply the 85 request reason. A performer making expected battle sounds does not acquire an unseen emergency through raw audio classification alone.

Use one native reason for equivalent continuing disturbance. Repeated harmlessness reduces future interruption only when the character acquired or formed that interpretation. No universal “three decoys and become immune” counter is selected. Different position, a new consequence or meaningful escalation is new evidence; a fresh technical occurrence ID without a distinguishable change is insufficient.

When a request has been answered, declined or incorporated into an adequate accepted response, equivalent repetition does not buy another immediate choice. A materially changed request, new danger or failed response can reopen it. Distinct speech occurrences remain independently recorded under the hearing owner.

## Responsive pursuit

An admitted fast pursuit requests compact current observations of its actual target. The initial qualification target is **at most 0.10 normal-play second between due changed-target observation opportunities** while sensing and work are eligible. At 60:1 this is six game seconds. This faster demand belongs to that active pursuit and relevant dependencies; it is not a global update rate for everyone in view.

The sense still decides what was acquired at each opportunity. Samples state their time and uncertainty; no continuous intervening trajectory is inferred. Actual unsupported discontinuities, identity loss and occlusion obey the sensing owner's rules. After loss, the guard receives no hidden position or destination.

This is a provisional responsiveness target, not measured service capacity. Qualification must compare useful pursuit, sight loss, capture/escape fairness and complete CPU cost with the ordinary sensing profile. If it cannot be met within the admitted workload, the implementation is not qualified; silently following private coordinates or changing all sensing rates is unacceptable.

## Relationships to existing world content

The [first-playable people](first-playable-people.md) and [contested watchpost](contested-watchpost.md) supply actual motives and situations, not universal guard/shopkeeper scripts. [Gameplay priorities](../../repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md) favors useful making, survival, independent encounters and recoverable conflict.

An existing clay process does not gain an overheating deadline; fishing does not acquire a bite minigame; bodies and clothing do not become thermal targets through an attention example. [Heat and materials](heat-and-materials.md), [river fishing](river-fishing.md) and [skilled action execution](skilled-action-execution.md) retain their scope. When another supported mechanic supplies such a cue, this attention profile must handle it through the common inputs.
