# Visiting camps: a small, worthwhile journey

**Status: proposed optional authored profile, October 8, 2026.** This is a prepared product-qualification scenario for [DG29](../../projects/participants-and-world-travel-feature-spec.md), not a change to the default opening or a claim that cross-world character transfer is implemented. [PT01–PT06](../../maintainers/participants-and-world-travel.md) consumes PS05–PS08; the [product-scalability inventory](../../limits/product-scalability.md) owns the proposed scope and service settings.

## Why visit

One person wants to see how another has arranged a camp, meet a particular resident, or demonstrate a known useful method. The host offers a real activity or conversation in an existing place. The visitor can decline, leave early, or arrange another visit. No attendance reward, compulsory task, completed conversation, rare item or resident cooperation is guaranteed.

Start with two prepared places in the same world and ordinary possessions. That useful outing can finish before any cross-world transfer. The subsequent narrow social proof connects two explicitly opted-in, compatible worlds under one operator. It moves one existing person; signing in, switching to a different bound character or making a duplicate is not this journey. No companion, animal population or copied village travels automatically.

The profile does not impose a magical portal or a universal travel law. The authored connection explains which actual departure and arrival sites permit the visit. An application control can request the supported transition where that world offers it. Ordinary walking across a ready same-world region boundary needs no travel-review modal.

## Prepare real places and participants

Use the same supported base-world definitions and current normal mechanical speed at both ends. Normal speed currently advances 60 game seconds per real second of uninterrupted simulation; the two places need not have the same calendar label or accumulated history. Their clocks retain their existing meaning. There is no independent calendar, hidden catch-up, extra hunger during service downtime or automatic production multiplier.

Each end has an actual authored departure/arrival site with supported standing geometry and a valid existing fallback. Prepare its current state and relevant interaction work before admitting arrival. Do not search for an arbitrary convenient location, place the visitor on a roof, repair the world by narration or clear a resident out of the way. Where a protected arrival is promised, its supported rules and physical state must satisfy that promise. A hidden hazard is not disclosed by printing the private reason for refusal.

The traveler already has a valid account/person binding and current control. The destination supplies the specific visit authority; no creator, inspect, save, access-management or spending permission follows. A host may invite the traveler to interact with separately identified common-use objects. That invitation grants neither building rights nor custody of everything in the camp. Existing private-human restrictions still apply to every host, resident and derivative of the visit.

The two human participants and any actual already-admitted resident supply the social case. Do not manufacture a second version of a companion or require another NPC population to fill the scene. A resident's current work, knowledge, willingness and resources govern their response. The advertised live-invention/resident experience still needs its own qualification; an empty native scene does not establish the full game offer.

## First cross-world social scope

The first proof carries the selected compatible person's actual body, state, native presentation and supported personal continuity. It introduces no clothing or equipment primitive. If appearance depends on detachable physical items, those are goods and need the later carried-bundle qualification; they are not an unexamined exception.

This deliberately restricted first cross-world proof admits no detachable goods or accompanying actors. The person uses actual permitted destination objects and resources for any demonstration. Knowing a supported method is not a second grant to install definitions, teach another person, spend materials or perform a forbidden action. Their ordinary condition does not reset on arrival or return.

Any goods left behind remain in actual source-world custody. The player can use existing permitted storage or knowingly accept its ordinary disclosed risks; protected storage cannot be promised merely because the owner leaves. Do not silently deposit items, grant a magical locker or make detached property inactive. If the proposed storage cannot honor the specific chosen arrangement, the player keeps the goods and chooses a supported alternative or does not depart. An already empty-handed visit does not require constructing a house.

This restriction must earn its inconvenience. Prefer the same-world outing when it provides the desired experience with ordinary belongings. If cross-world stowing feels like the main activity, keep the narrow proof internal and qualify the feature specification's complete compatible carried-bundle journey before advertising regular physical travel. That next journey leaves only genuinely incompatible possessions behind and preserves the whole admitted container/dependency closure under its actual rights and world rules.

## Leave and return deliberately

The readable review states the destination, participation rights, actual clock relationship, belongings policy, service funding and return arrangement. Still-valid grants are reused. While waiting for readiness, the person stays in the source and can continue ordinary supported play. A later ready offer needs an intentional departure from a valid site; it does not interrupt a new activity automatically.

Current actions must complete or be explicitly interrupted through their own rules before a transfer that cannot carry them. Keep consumed resources and release only genuinely unused holds. A current unsupported conflict or effect does not become safe because travel is requested. The profile uses current [human absence and return](lifecycle-and-protection.md#human-logout-and-return), rather than enabling future dangerous-logout continuation or a new PvP mode.

After departure there is one continuous person with one actual location/custody result. The return review concerns current conditions. If home is unavailable, the person may continue valid destination play or choose the supported inactive retained state while recovery proceeds. Inactivity supplies no free actions, new autonomous commitments or duplicate body. Returning restores no spent object, health, time, expired authority or erased information.

Both operators must have a complete funded custody/recovery arrangement before the route is enabled. It distinguishes retained recovery of the person from immediate physical admission to the home site, and follows actual service expiry, notice and retrieval terms. A canceled hosting plan does not create indefinite free simulation, while congestion or revocation does not authorize deletion or confiscation. A route with no supported resolution for these cases remains unavailable.

Even a social visit changes another world's records and possibly another person's knowledge. The profile therefore needs the existing external-outcome/restore boundary before actual cross-world travel. A pre-trip checkpoint cannot simply reactivate the source person or erase an externally retained visit. [DG25](../../projects/corrections-and-shared-restoration-feature-spec.md#10-protected-storage-export-and-unsupported-histories) retains reconciliation or refusal; the isolated DG17 pause does not supply a connected-world recovery protocol.

## Experience and expansion gate

Qualify a complete invitation, chosen arrival, useful voluntary exchange, departure and later return, including a genuinely occupied destination and changed source geometry. Ask whether the visit was worth its preparation and whether the traveler understands where their body, belongings and home are now. Cost includes both ends, failed preparation, retained custody and recovery, not only the few minutes of visible conversation.

The prepared small case is not a replacement for the accepted shared-world release or growth workloads. More visitors, goods, companions, independent operators, protected-domain rules, different clocks, crowds and campaigns expand only through their specified consumers when they improve wanted play and meet their complete obligations. Keep useful ordinary camp play available while those features remain unqualified.
