# Knowledge and observer identity

The base world uses editable notepads for current understanding and observer-assigned given names for individuated subjects. [Engine knowledge](../../knowledge.md) owns document validation, references, persistence and privacy; [Memory architecture](../../memory-architecture.md) owns experiential memory and cognition integration.

The accepted [speech and social perception feature specification](../../projects/speech-and-social-perception-feature-spec.md) and [technical design](../../projects/speech-and-social-perception-tech-design.md) define the current evidence-dependent recognition contract. The [shared stealth and awareness contract](../../projects/stealth-awareness-tech-design.md#shared-contracts) supersedes their former reliable first-hearing simplification with evidence-dependent association and learned familiarity, keeping names separate. The [observer-specific perception design](../../projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md) supplies the detailed contract, with [PP](../../maintainers/perspective-perception.md) and [SPK](../../maintainers/speech-and-social-perception.md) owning implementation through the [shared tracker](../../maintainers/stealth-awareness.md); the current-channel implementation below retains the separate physical visual/acoustic producers and full integrated qualification as open work.

## Notepads

`packages/domain/src/worlds/base/config/knowledge.yaml` is the authored policy source. Its generated JSON stays beside it; the domain reads generated data without filesystem or YAML I/O. `knowledge.policy: editable-notepads` selects the bundled native policy. `knowledge.maxCharacters.general` is **5,000** and `knowledge.maxCharacters.subject` is **1,000**. They count Unicode code points. Changing these definitions cannot bypass engine request/storage limits.

General knowledge describes the actor's external understanding. Subject pads describe what it knows or believes about one individual or object. Keep uncertainty and contrary evidence. Do not copy live inventory, body state, executable techniques, operational goals or event histories into a competing authority. At capacity, rewrite rather than append; no automatic paid rewrite is authorized. Existing self-description workspace quotas are separate.

Fresh base-world construction installs these defaults once. Ada's initial understanding of her camp and practical background is an explicitly authored general knowledge document, preserving the previous wording without presenting it as an observed memory. The missing-belonging opening likewise supplies Mara's and Tavin's authored starting understanding with explicit origin and no fabricated experiential evidence. Ordinary changed edits replace that source with an ordinary publication; restart never reseeds accepted knowledge or converts older self-description files.

Immediate cognition may propose optional `note` and `name` operations. Reflection returns optional-in-purpose `knowledgeChanges` and `nameChanges` arrays, normally empty. Each edit replaces a pad under its supplied revision; creator edits use the same engine mutation through god-only inspection. Accepting a name or writing a note is not required for speaking or acting.

## Identity and recognition

### Supported recognition and current-channel boundary

The authored [perception and recognition policy](perception-recognition.md) owns the selected base-world matching laws, signal-quality interpretation, familiarity thresholds and the Perception attribute shared with the installed human visual policy. The current human constructor installs Perception and a saved four-feature voice profile. Current direct-path speech supplies actually received voice evidence, the selected matching/learning rules and observer-local learned individuals. The reusable visual matching/learning adapter requires exact already-acquired body and feature facts; its future physical producer remains B work. PP03 and VG01 coordinate its installation through the existing authored attribute/creation owner whenever either recognition or human visual policy requires it, independently of memory capability. A owns recognition and B owns visual gain; hearing thresholds stay with C. The generic engine does not impose this world's numeric law.

The [shared subject-familiarity design](perception-recognition.md#shared-subject-familiarity-target) is an unimplemented extension of this owner: one evidence-backed acquaintance score supplies a bounded optional-attention preference and an early-saturating recognition benefit. It is not affection, trust, a name or a second relationship record. Required modality evidence and current-body permissions remain independent. Routine reinforcement updates native preparation without creating a paid-thinking cause; actual knowledge/name changes keep their own admission.

The selected base-world target preserves a learned individual association when the observer's permitted evidence and familiarity support it. Reliable voice recognition requires prior familiarity, sufficient current evidence quality and the selected Perception rule; detecting an unfamiliar first voice alone does not reveal its canonical actor. Detection, classification, location, intelligibility and identity can be available in different combinations. A sufficiently familiar voice may be recognized without sight or understood words; weak or conflicting evidence can remain anonymous or tentative. The [perception design](../../projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md) applies the base policy's familiarity and quality laws through the existing observer identity owner, without a separate voice registry. Once supported, remembered identity can survive leaving sight, closing Talk or speaking to someone else while its source and policy remain valid. Recognition does not depend on conversational enrollment, a paid naming decision or creating a subject notepad. Preserve permitted familiarity and its support through save/load and memory/context transformations.

Keep three facts separate: the actual subject's stable engine identity, the observer's permitted reference to that subject, and the observer's known or chosen name. Cognition uses a scoped exact reference with a permitted label such as “a person”; recognizing that person does not disclose the global entity name. Repeated species labels can refer to distinct people without name matching. A later name or introduction attaches to the existing recognized subject, preserving its earlier experiences and their event-time labels. Current labels may change without rewriting old quotations or pretending the name was known earlier.

This is selected world policy, not an engine-wide biometric model or perfect-knowledge rule. A reference grants no hidden current position, unseen activity, unheard words, unlearned name or another observer's knowledge. The [hearing contract](../../hearing-and-speech.md#received-direct-addressing) lets an audible recipient know it was addressed without sight or speaker identification; a first unknown voice may therefore address someone while remaining unassociated. A suspected identity is a belief about permitted evidence, not authority to resolve the sound to that person's current body or open their subject pad as verified source knowledge. Ordinary nonlinguistic animal sound retains its own source-disclosure rule. Other worlds can select supported recognition, disguise and mistaken-identity laws through the same owner. [SSP-M01](../../limits/memory.md#ssp-m01--stable-recognition-and-known-names) records the current supported-channel boundary and outstanding qualification.

Under the [shared exposure and freshness contract](../../projects/stealth-awareness-tech-design.md#shared-contracts), continuity of an exposure episode does not prolong live sensing during a gap. Continued physical observation can strengthen evidence; replaying the same account cannot create fresh exposure or familiarity. A later recognized voice or learned name does not update old position, equipment or activity facts. Their original times, precision and support remain available as historical evidence, subject to correction and forgetting; no hidden current path is inferred from the remembered binding.

The [observer-specific perception feature specification](../../projects/perspective-perception-feature-spec.md) and [technical design](../../projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md) supply the selected detailed policy. [PP03](../../maintainers/perspective-perception.md) owns familiarity, ambiguous matching and supported later association through this existing identity owner; SPK consumes it for speech and name learning. A current low-quality voice can suggest Ada without resolving the sound to Ada's live body. Two similar voices remain distinguishable only to the degree supported by personally acquired evidence. Obscured faces, a different context or unfamiliar clothing cannot be repaired by comparing hidden IDs.

A communicated name is a claim or chosen label, not a biometric match. An unidentified voice saying “I am Ada” may supply that claimed label for its local contact; it cannot reveal which stored Ada, if any, spoke. Learning a supported individual's name changes permitted present labels without rewriting old quotations. The observer can retain a known person's history while the person's present location, possessions, activity or even continued life is unknown. Unseen death or replacement invalidates physical bindings internally without granting that fact to the observer.

### Learned individuals without a canonical association

Sufficient personally acquired history can establish a distinguishable unnamed voice or face as an observer-local learned individual. Its canonical entity association remains optional and evidence-supported; ordinary detection alone is insufficient. Familiarity attaches through this same observer identity owner, never through hidden emitter-ID equality. The selected [knowledge subject variant](../../knowledge.md#selected-stealth-evidence-and-familiarity-consumer) lets a deliberately written note describe that learned individual through the existing notepad owner, with no fake person entity or separate note store. A later association must preserve exact earlier support and reconcile any existing note under the [perception design](../../projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md); it cannot silently merge unrelated voices or overwrite another subject's document.

### Current delivered binding

`observerIdentity.policy: observer-given-names` selects the bundled naming behavior. `givenName` is the observer's preferred individual label, limited to 120 characters as a short label, not additional prose storage. Ada may call someone “the beggar” while Mike calls the same person “Tomas.” Accepting an introduction may update the preferred given name. Its canonical subject association preserves earlier memories; no global entity rename or unbounded alias list is required.

Merely seeing “a beggar” or “a deer” does not individuate or name it. Unnamed or name-unknown actors have bare canonical observer labels such as “person” and “deer,” with a species and a scoped exact reference. Profiles show “a person” or “a deer”; compact menus use bare labels; prose chooses its article through the shared [naming contract](../../entity-names.md). An explicitly assigned name “Deer” is still a name. Self-name comes from the actor's authored identity; other global entity names are not revealed by visibility. Deliberate naming or creation of a subject pad establishes an individual record.

Current body correspondence uses the actual acquired body and its uninterrupted encounter. A newly seen unfamiliar person gets a fresh observer-local subject for notes and private names, without a canonical acquaintance association or invented visual features. Generic outward labels are frozen when observed; later hidden name or species changes cannot relabel that old observation. A received reliable voice match can associate the exact current body lease with the listener's remembered individual, including a fallible match. The body's original local note/name subject remains available through that same continuous observation; an independently chosen local label keeps its precedence, and the association does not manufacture a second modality profile. Authored voice familiarity alone does not identify a merely seen body. Learned voice familiarity and eligible authored voice exemplars survive sensory loss independently; remembered names and communication references grant no current body.

Saved exposure episodes can reconnect within **30 game seconds (0.5 normal-speed seconds)** of actual sensory loss, independently of narrative departure quotas. Current body and detail permissions end immediately during the gap. A later return starts a new encounter-local subject unless actual permitted recognition reconnects it. Restoration preserves exact active observations, saved loss deadlines and separate local subjects; it does not reseed forgotten acquaintance.

Targeted teaching, attacks and gestures keep the target's global name out of their shared event text. When an actor witnesses one, the event owner writes that actor's own target label into its stored perspective: a learned name, a species label, or “me” for the target itself. Later name learning does not rewrite what the actor originally witnessed. Words inside attributed quotations are preserved. The same perspective feeds World Events, conversation presentation, memory search and cognition.

This is the current v1 specificity: the policy lives in `packages/domain/src/worlds/base/knowledge.ts`, separate from generic documents. The selected shared A/SPK work extends its episode-only restriction with the evidence-dependent rule above; it does not introduce reliable first-hearing identity. Detailed biometric analysis, distinguishing marks, disguise and mistaken identity remain separate consumers of the perception owner. Current continuity supports the selected direct-path voice recognition and supplied-feature visual adapter, without claiming B's physical feature production or perfect identity tracking. No plugin loader or knowledge-graph framework is introduced.

## Spoken introductions

Generated speech includes nullable `talk.selfIntroduction`: the exact name or preferred label the speaker explicitly introduces in that utterance. It is a communicated claim, potentially an alias, not the speaker's authoritative global name. Mentions of other people, quotations and ordinary self-descriptions are not introductions. The same generation supplies this annotation; no second model call or English-pattern parser is needed.

Committed speech carries the annotation in its event. The current reception policy uses the listener's actually understood name and supported local individual. An unfamiliar first voice retains a name claim on its anonymous exposure; a later eligible voice promotion can retain that label without a canonical identity. Sight is not a universal additional prerequisite. It uses the normal observer-name mutation, including for player-controlled listeners who do not run autonomous cognition. Repeated identical introductions do not advance revisions. An invalid, oversized or absent-from-speech annotation is discarded without rejecting speech. An unheard speaker cannot teach a name, and an unidentified introduction cannot reveal a canonical entity; introduction metadata does not bypass recognition or supply missing words.

The public entity and conversation labels update from the listener's knowledge. A private `name` operation changes the acting observer's labels and is not a way to introduce oneself to another actor. Historical speech without an introduction annotation remains unchanged; a fresh supported introduction can teach the name. Raw player speech remains ordinary text, which NPC cognition can interpret through its existing observer-name operation.
