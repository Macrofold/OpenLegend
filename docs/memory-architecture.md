# Memory architecture

[Precise history and general causes](engine-and-world-boundaries.md#preserve-precise-history-and-solve-the-general-cause) governs every memory producer, transformation and consumer: preserve exact permitted source facts, derive summaries without silently replacing the record, and render personal perspective through shared owners.

## Product-scalability extension — accepted target, not current execution

The [product-scalability principles](product-scalability/README.md) add a future cross-cutting contract for persistent lives at bounded cost. [Attention and scenes](product-scalability/attention-and-scenes.md) owns the detailed product scenarios; [PS03–PS05](maintainers/product-scalability.md) tracks integration and [the product limits](limits/product-scalability.md) retains unresolved policy choices. This document remains the owner of memory, recall and mental privacy. The extension does not silently replace the current hard-query baselines, speech guarantees, routing defaults, no-cooldown policy or consolidation rules below.

**Attention before expensive description.** Under an explicitly admitted sensory policy, a character can perceive aggregate commotion or a moving crowd while distinguishing a few sources. Grouping must happen before unnecessary per-object paid judgments, not after generating complete descriptions and transcripts for everyone. Distinguishable sources, stable focus, goal relevance and justified interruptions influence prospective awareness. Group evidence must retain uncertainty and observer-permitted features; hidden identity, allegiance and intention cannot become perceived group properties. Current promised individual evidence remains required until the new policy is implemented through its sensory and perception owners.

**Attention is not physical authority.** An unconsidered body still obstructs movement and an unnoticed arrow still hits. A real acquired danger can interrupt focus; an unseen opponent's private plan cannot. Focus changes cannot retroactively recover words or identities the character never acquired. Aggregate sources and their derived summaries need correction, forgetting and revocation support through the existing owners, not an untracked cache of private prose.

**Low-power cognition preserves agency.** Retain chosen intentions, supported methods, identity, relationships and native commitments while reducing optional initiatives under a declared world policy. Continue valid work without requiring a new thought at every step. Missing optional conversations or reflections are not a backlog to drain when a player returns. Required reaction and accounting failures remain explicit; lack of budget is not proof of deliberate silence. A new controller must not quietly replace the character's personality or invent new goals to make background execution cheap.

**Scenes preserve evidence precision.** A coarse scene can establish a completed social beat and gist without generating all words. Such a source must remain distinguishable from retained exact speech, testimony, interpretation and imagined content. A later request for verbatim wording can receive an honest admission of imperfect recall; a generated reconstruction is not a new historical transcript. Recording, exact-recall or pivotal-language mechanics must retain the actual required words at occurrence. Existing quoted utterances and listener-specific partial fragments remain protected under their current rules.

**Character quality and permissions remain separate.** Important or heavily scrutinized characters may have a higher evaluated baseline model tier even for short interactions, with task-sensitive escalation. This is a future routing policy, not a claim that expensive models guarantee correct personality or replace domain validation. Institutional briefing must not masquerade as autobiographical experience. Joint scene generation cannot acquire every participant's secrets or author a human's consent merely because it is economical to use one call.

**Time and absence remain explicit.** The proposed separate calendar in [participation and clocks](product-scalability/participation-and-protection.md) must not be inferred from the existing game-time recall windows or current speed control. Each affected retention, reminder and maintenance rule needs an explicit clock assignment before that feature lands. Preserve bounded coalescing and no automatic paid catch-up across all modes.

## 1. Intent and ownership

A short exchange should receive a short, promptly generated answer. Speaking or making an immediate decision does not require the character to rewrite its relationships, beliefs, goals and concerns. Reflection is a separate background activity. Routine cleanup of remembered experience is lighter still.

A simple greeting's optimization target is a few hundred input tokens and roughly 10–50 visible output tokens. This is a target, not a measured result or a promise for every character/context. Count the complete workflow, including Jev, tool responses and reasoning tokens, when assessing cost and latency.

The domain remains pure, deterministic and authoritative for simulation transitions. The server owns awareness, context, scheduling, spending, workspace publication and admission. The AI package supplies generic typed execution; the client displays permitted data and sends intentions. Free prose, generated JSON fields and edited inner-world files cannot acquire executable meaning automatically.

### What belongs in a character's mind

The character has a durable inner life, not just a log of events. Its authored files can express the following subjects without requiring one file, JSON record or mandatory checklist per category.

| Content                        | Intended behavior                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Identity and self-concept      | Background, traits, values, preferences and the character's understanding of itself                                     |
| Beliefs                        | Interpretations of people, events and the world; beliefs may be mistaken or uncertain                                   |
| Relationships                  | Freely revisable, memory-based descriptions of other people; directional, without points or score thresholds            |
| Feelings and appraisals        | How experiences felt and what they mean to the character; numerical effects require supported native rules              |
| Goals, intentions and concerns | Narrative aspirations and unresolved concerns; maintained operational pursuit belongs to the agency store               |
| Commitments                    | Remembered promises, debts and appointments; actual obligations and their lifecycle remain native authoritative records |
| Knowledge                      | Learned skills and recipes; thinking about a capability does not teach or admit it mechanically                         |
| Reflections and dreams         | Reinterpretation, associations and imagined possibilities retained when useful                                          |

“I saw someone take berries” is an experience; “they stole from me” is an interpretation. A later explanation can change the belief without rewriting the original experience. The character may be wrong. Validation should protect scope and game rules, not demand objectively correct beliefs or another model call to judge every interpretation.

New actors receive an authored identity and permitted starting knowledge, goals and possessions atomically with their initial saved state. Their own workspace and initial accepted About me text provide continuity from the first decision. Seed background is not a fabricated witnessed event. Essential identity and active obligations must survive ordinary compaction; new experiences can still change the character's opinions and priorities.

World state, remembered experience, authored inner world and thought presentation are separate. Inventory, health and location belong to the simulation. Raw/consolidated experiences supply recall. Reflection edits the inner world. God-only reflection thought history remains presentation, not another recall store. Under the [agency admission contract](../archive/07-technical-architecture/agent-agency-runtime.md#42-receipts-are-not-interchangeable), an accepted intentional `think` is an actor-owned raw experience with `self_thought` acquisition, source component identity and entity targets. It is recallable and consolidatable, never a world event or proof of the thought's subject. Player-private Narrator prose is a separate derived presentation and does not become actor memory.

Inner-world organization remains freeform: no mandatory goals file is required. Actor decisions and reflection deliberately changing operational pursuit submit the same [typed goal/plan mutation](../archive/07-technical-architecture/agent-agency-runtime.md#32-single-ownership-and-existing-fields). A sentence in an edited file cannot authorize crafting, cancel work or change a protected obligation. Creator-authored initial goals seed once; later actor choices do not rewrite identity or reapply the seed.

### Integrated character experience

**Accepted product target; integrated delivery and qualification remain open.** A character's context is their situated perspective on the world and relevant inner life. Actual bodily conditions can become experienced hunger or fatigue; psychological concerns can include contact, belonging, enjoyment, curiosity and room to choose. Memories, feelings and unfinished intentions should matter through attention, interpretation, real choices and later consequences. These concerns need not all become numerical meters or require a written thought.

The [compelling-character specification](projects/compelling-characters-feature-spec.md) owns this integrated target and its complete episode protocols; [CE03/CE05](maintainers/character-experience.md) coordinates with CR03/CR04/CR08/CR12 and the existing agency/appraisal owners. Complete accepted About me, required evidence and numeric body meaning remain protected by the current context contract. Inner salience does not authorize an omniscient view, fabricated sensation, hidden identity truncation or a second writable mind. The [perspective rationale](../archive/03-design-proposals/character-experience-and-inner-life.md) distinguishes the intended functional character experience from an empirical claim of consciousness.

When implementing changes to identity, prompts, attention, feelings or recall for this project, identify and exercise the affected [complete flows](projects/compelling-characters-feature-spec.md#complete-behavioral-flows), including later conduct. A field appearing in context or a persuasive private thought is insufficient evidence that the experience changes the character's life. Existing current runtime and CR acceptance states remain as recorded.

### Character continuity and learning

The accepted [character relationship design](../archive/03-design-proposals/agents-and-social-simulation.md#social-continuity) separates structured objective facts such as blood relations from unstructured person-to-free-text assessments such as “friend,” “good friend,” or “lover.” Each person may freely revise their own assessment at any point based on memories and perception, without relationship points, thresholds or a fixed progression. Assessments are sparse and directional; they cannot rewrite another person's view or objective kinship. Exact editing/publication mechanics remain future work.

Narrative assessments of others live in actor-owned subject [knowledge](knowledge.md); About me remains self-understanding. Any queryable relationship projection derives from its owning document revision, not another writable biography. Group membership, protected commitments and learned capabilities retain separate authority; revising an assessment does not erase an obligation.

Conversation preserves speaker, actual per-turn audience, interruption and topic continuity. Characters can decline, respond only through a gesture or private thought, keep working while talking, ask clarifying questions and return to unfinished plans. A promise and its fulfillment are distinct events; exhausting inference allowance must not erase accepted obligations. The future [conversation lifecycle](narration-and-conversations.md#6-durable-conversation-lifecycle) supports changing membership, overhearing without joining, self-talk and atomic merges. Membership never grants earlier speech awareness. One-on-one text precedes group text and voice, with captions retained for accessibility.

Stable traits, values and habits are distinct from temporary feelings and practical skills. Supported appraisals carry cause, target, intensity, decay and stacking keys so repeated sightings do not multiply the same fear. Native thresholds use hysteresis; stable traits change only at admitted rates. Fictional characterization must not infer sensitive traits of the human player.

Learning can follow observation, testimony, teaching and actual practice without a research minigame. Seeing part of a method cannot reveal hidden steps; proficiency changes require native evidence. Learned technique identity and compatible version mappings survive upgrades, while genuinely new steps must be learned.

### Creator edits and memories

Creator edits to family, names, objects or world rules do not automatically rewrite or delete character memories to match the edited world. Outdated beliefs may remain. Computed current descriptions refresh separately. Explicitly requested memory edits/forgetting and privacy revocation retain their existing source-dependency and invalidation obligations. [Creator edit propagation](projects/creator-edit-propagation-feature-spec.md) owns the accepted future distinction: earlier complete, approved explicit-dependency deletion previews; later optional semantic memory search with multiselect/manual or AI-assisted proposed edits. Similarity or mentioning an object never makes a memory a mandatory deletion dependency.

## 2. Semantic levels and triggers

The [events/perception/reactions contract](events-perception-and-reactions.md), accepted for implementation within a named scope on 2026-09-28, unifies how permitted external observations, owner-private internal changes, exposure changes and due reminders reach actor intake. Typed per-character intake, private departures and derived ongoing stimuli are implemented; its remaining parts stay proposed. This document continues to own Jev attention, reasoning, recall, reflection and mental privacy. An opportunity is not a required model call; native protection never waits for inference.

| Level | Executor                        | Purpose and allowed result                                                                      |
| ----- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0     | Native code                     | Routine behavior, urgent survival and ongoing actions; no semantic model required               |
| 1     | Jev                             | Attention, significance and routing; select supplied actions or escalate within offered choices |
| 2     | Mini LLM, fast thought          | Brief speech or immediate decision; default for talking to an actor                             |
| 3     | Complex LLM with low reasoning  | More complex immediate interpretation or decision                                               |
| 4     | Complex LLM with high reasoning | Difficult immediate interpretation or decision needing more deliberation                        |
| 5     | Full bounded harness            | Background reflection and multi-turn inner-world file work, including eligible dreams           |

There are three immediate execution paths: level 0 runs native routines, urgent responses and valid ongoing plan steps with zero model calls; level 1 lets Jev select a fully specified supplied action for normal engine admission and native execution, without a generative LLM; levels 2–4 generate speech, new thoughts, goal revisions, replanning, unlisted attempts or invention proposals when supplied choices cannot express the needed response. A feasible action does not suppress an independently warranted conversational or planning response. Level-5 reflection remains independent, neither a prerequisite nor an automatic consequence of a simple action selection. Every level-1 decision ends in one explicit outcome: act on the selected binding, deliberately continue, escalate once to an offered generative level through the normal spending allowance, or defer with its reason.

[NP05/NC19](maintainers/narration-and-conversations.md#nc19--progressive-display-of-structured-conversation-replies) adds private early reading for eligible direct NPC speech on the qualified Macrofold route. It changes presentation timing, never cognition, character choice or memory: only a complete validated independent first speech operation addressed to the requesting player with clear hearing and visible attribution is released. Private thoughts, notes, actions and later operations remain withheld. The final decision and native speech effects still pass their original validation/admission owners. [Configured-route and browser evidence](verification/npc-reply-preview.md) records the authorized Jev configuration correction and measured opportunity.

Jev evaluates routing for admitted semantic decision opportunities, including whether a supplied action suffices. This does not mean a paid call for each fixed step, each footstep or each object: native routine responses proceed immediately, while meaningful stimuli are coalesced into bounded actor-scoped opportunities. Level 1 may incur Jev cost even though it requires no generative LLM. The [selection-to-admission contract](../archive/07-technical-architecture/agent-agency-runtime.md#24-level-1-selection-without-generative-escalation) owns routing order, bindings and fallback. Jev attention/routing calls do not recursively create more routing calls. Unknown/unavailable judgments have an explicit deferred/escalated disposition under the spending policy; native work can continue where permitted, but missing judgment is not deliberate no intervention and never authorizes an automatic paid retry.

Talking to an agent creates a level-2 response opportunity automatically. Jev may escalate the immediate reply to level 3 or 4, or also request level-5 reflection when the circumstance warrants it. A reflection request runs in the background; the reply and simulation do not wait for it. A perceived need to develop a relationship is not a requirement to withhold speech until the mind is rewritten. Actual speech can create native evidence and supported promises through existing admission rules without a model editing a relationship record.

Environmental triggers may select any appropriate level. Significant events can be marked by trusted mechanics directly; other configured event classes ask Jev whether they warrant reflection. Downtime and eligible sleep are independent reflection opportunities. Admission, relevance, cooldowns, fair scheduling, concurrency and spending limits remain server responsibilities.

Trigger and escalation rules should be malleable through admitted in-game mechanics/inventions and world configuration. They must use finite trusted policy families; an invented description cannot grant new tools, exceed spending caps or bypass native urgent behavior. Exact models, low/high effort parameter mappings and token ceilings are configurable and must be verified for the chosen adapter. High effort is not the default for all complex thought or ordinary speech.

## 3. One compact model-facing context

Every semantic reasoning route uses the same actor-perspective projection principles, with only the information its decision requires. Keep the complete accepted inner-world text as “About me” in each decision context; other candidate sources pass through attention. Do not serialize the server's storage or execution envelope as the character's context.

Useful sections are the actual stimulus/question, About me, current relevant needs/environment, attended possessions and learned techniques, and a single Recall section. Render calendar day, time of day and known environmental conditions such as temperature in ordinary language. Raw simulation seconds, world IDs, world profile objects and the fact that self is an NPC are not useful context. A relevant world constraint can be stated briefly in English. Do not invent weather, dates or sensory knowledge that the simulation does not supply.

Include relevant operational goals, the current plan frontier and fresh results through the existing budgeted [agency projection](../archive/07-technical-architecture/agent-agency-runtime.md#72-attention-inputs). Render them once, without a duplicated plan-memory section. Complete accepted About me, required evidence, current-conversation guarantees and protected obligations retain their existing priority; narrow or defer an overflowing task.

Recent actual action outcomes now enter required decision evidence through the same personal-memory selection as other experience. Native action/control completion retains exact permitted facts, quantities, units, before/after changes, source identity and disposition. Plans retain bounded references, current status and remaining/paused work; they no longer duplicate completed-result prose or reconstruct forgotten experience from execution receipts. Existing conversation evidence covers an utterance once. Missing result records disclose incomplete coverage; mandatory context overflow refuses preparation. Optional attention failure preserves required records, and source/outcome-set validation rejects stale decisions. Correction/forgetting follows linked action/result records in both directions, including indexed cold reads. Completing steps never automatically fulfills a goal. Shared native participant rendering preserves source/lifetime, self/other/unknown attribution and literal quotes; arbitrary authored/model text retains its explicit owner. [Implementation and evidence](verification/cognition-context.md#october-5--unified-experience-and-unprescribed-choices).

Attention and generative reasoning share labeled surroundings, possessions, learned techniques, conversation and recall sections; never flatten already-included context into an unlabeled list. Current visible descriptions preserve name/species, supported observed activity, relative horizontal distance/bearing and elevation, resource contents and quantities, and observed fire state. An absent action record says nothing about stillness. Location is not a claim of reachability. The possessions section establishes ownership once; each entry gives quantity, equipped status and readable properties without repeating “I have.” These descriptions come only from the actor's permitted observation. The initial property/activity vocabulary describes the supported native families; extend its projection when a new family supplies permitted descriptive facts, rather than inferring semantics from arbitrary fields.

Spatial descriptions refresh for each observation; embeddings may omit relative geometry so motion does not continuously invalidate semantic vectors. Their revision must match the exact embedded text. Jev still sees the current spatial description when judging relevance. Body, active intentions and contact evidence provide immediate context for recall relevance. If forgetting or correction changes that evidence boundary while optional attention runs, discard the decision rather than using stale fallback evidence.

Quantifiable actor-permitted facts retain their measured values, units and scale rather than being replaced by qualitative prose. Body context includes each available numeric attribute at its current value and declared range; concerns supplement that measurement. The native food meter is fullness (lower means hungrier), not a hunger score. Unknown values remain explicitly unknown; do not invent numbers for qualitative feelings or expose another actor’s private measurements.

Inventory means owned quantities/instances; materials describe item definitions/properties. For cognition, combine the relevant facts into one description, such as “I have two lengths of cord suitable for tying.” Do not send both an inventory-ID list and a duplicated material catalogue. Invention/resolver tasks may need a different typed mechanical contract; their requirements must not inflate ordinary conversation.

Store every memory with a plain-English representation at creation or consolidation. Prefer deterministic text construction for native facts; a separately admitted small summarizer can help when needed. Model-facing recall is a list of strings, for example “Day 4, morning: I heard John say hi.” Use a person's permitted name only if recognized. Preserve meaningful testimony, inference, uncertainty and imagined attribution in the text; omit technical envelopes and empty values unless emptiness itself matters, such as “I have no food.”

Recent events are experiences, not a separate duplicated context section. One recall view combines selected consolidated memories, recent raw personal memories and recent raw world events the actor was aware of. Do not repeat the same event as a memory, a conversation transcript and a recent event.

### Metadata stays in the server binding

Entity references use `display name (ID:token)` in cognition context. The [knowledge reference contract](knowledge.md#subject-binding) defines observer/encounter-scoped opaque tokens and collision handling. No species prefix appears in a token. A server-only map for each decision resolves only its permitted references; unknown tokens fail rather than selecting a same-named actor. The domain still receives canonical IDs and rechecks authority. Every observed actor includes an explicit species (or unknown); an obvious species-only display label is rendered with an indefinite article, such as “a deer.” Observer-known names follow the [base-world identity policy](worlds/base/knowledge.md#identity-and-recognition); visibility alone does not reveal them.

Structured speech targets, expression targets and thought references use the token alone. Explicit `(ID:token)` annotations in proposed actions, private thoughts and goal objectives resolve to canonical annotations before persistence and reproject to short tokens in later context. Native attempt interpretation sees the same short references as the original decision; bare ambiguous prose never authorizes name-based target matching. Quotes, old prose and names are not rewritten by substring guesses. Memory entity references are labeled as referenced identities, not inferred subject/object roles. Short tokens and relative positions stay out of entity embedding text. Tokens identify offered references, not knowledge of names or permission to act. Observer-scoped encounter references and recognition are governed by [Knowledge](knowledge.md#subject-binding).

| Current field/content                                                  | Target treatment                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `instructionsVersion`, `policy`, `decisionId`, world/actor IDs         | Keep in execution receipts and authoritative bindings; do not ask the model to echo them                            |
| `expectedRevision`, record revisions, `processedWatermark`             | Keep for concurrency and publication validation outside ordinary model text                                         |
| `coverage`, indexes, evidence IDs, protected flags                     | Preserve internally for retrieval/admission/audit; render only useful uncertainty or missing information in English |
| `acceptedMind` documents/records                                       | Replace in model context with the accepted plain-text “About me” snapshot                                           |
| Generic request “Current situation, relationships, goals and concerns” | Replace with the actual stimulus and narrow task                                                                    |
| `quotaUsage`, file constraints                                         | Supply only in the reflection workspace instructions/tool interface                                                 |
| Empty arrays/nulls/default facets                                      | Omit unless their absence is relevant information                                                                   |
| Action identity                                                        | A short offered handle is allowed only when selecting an action; resolve its authority server-side                  |

Assemble each decision from one consistent view of current permitted state, accepted inner-world text and selected recall. Record its input boundary server-side. Historical experiences keep their own times; they must not be mistaken for current observations. Trigger facts explicitly separate event time/age, perceived speaker and intended recipient, the observer's relationship, and the source's current visibility. Unknown recipients remain unknown. Encourage a natural reply only when the triggering speech is directed to the thinking actor; this applies to statements and greetings as well as questions. Overheard speech permits deliberate participation without implying an invitation or equating another nearby individual with the speaker. If a tool explicitly retrieves newer state, recheck affected dependencies before accepting a result.

Trusted short operating instructions remain separate from untrusted character prose and observed speech. Removing metadata from model text does not remove privacy, stale-result checks, idempotency or evidence validation. IDs needed by scoped tools can be short local handles; server-owned bindings map them to permitted objects.

Autonomous cognition consumes a current opportunity snapshot, not batches of old unprocessed events. Completed direct-response jobs identify the exact speech event already handled for that actor; autonomous trigger selection excludes those events without advancing past unrelated or newer evidence. This suppression never removes conversation history or restricts what the actor may say. Its wakeup watermark covers all observed experience at admission (including native handling and failed decisions); the newest eight significant changes summarize the trigger. Retained older evidence remains queryable through the hard-query and semantic paths. It never independently forces backlog-draining calls. New evidence arriving after the snapshot, changed goals, interests or meaningful state can create a fresh opportunity under spending admission. Reflection/consolidation scheduling and retention remain separate. Autonomous cognition has no actor cooldown. New qualifying changes are eligible on the next scheduler pass; fair oldest-opportunity ordering, the shared execution slot, unchanged-opportunity suppression and spending admission still apply. Completing one actor's work does not impose another global waiting period. Scheduling diagnostics record admission reason, prior actor opportunity, trigger age in game seconds. A delayed trigger is evidence to reconsider under current conditions, not an obligation to reply.

## 4. Jev attention before context inclusion

The server first establishes what this actor can know. Jev then answers “What am I paying attention to right now?” with a yes/no judgment per eligible candidate in bounded batches. This applies to nearby entities, inventory, known recipes, conversation history, raw experiences and consolidated recall. Jev cannot authorize an unseen object, another actor's memory or a technique the actor has never learned.

Every decision starts with deterministic hard queries over actor-permitted data. These guarantees survive optional vector-search or Jev failure; they are context coverage, not a processing queue:

| Context                      | Hard-query baseline and order                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Identity and immediate state | Accepted inner-world text, body, active goals/plan/attempts, contact evidence, trigger evidence, protected commitments/corrections      |
| Current conversation         | All permitted speech through the snapshot, verbatim when it fits or as older summary plus recent exact speech, on any cognition trigger |
| Nearby actors and objects    | Nearest 16 actors and nearest 16 other perceived entities, distance then stable ID                                                      |
| Inventory                    | Up to 16 possessions, equipped item first, then stable ID                                                                               |
| Known techniques             | Up to 16 most recently learned recipes, then stable ID                                                                                  |
| Recent memory                | Newest 8 permitted memories, then stable ID                                                                                             |

Conversation history has no turn-count cutoff and is never truncated at the triggering message; later committed replies in the snapshot are included. The active conversation takes precedence, with the trigger-associated conversation used when no conversation is active. Only personally available, non-forgotten evidence is included, and existing retention still applies. The single [conversation context owner](narration-and-conversations.md#model-facing-conversation-compaction) compacts older permitted speech when needed, preserving a recent exact tail and the independent trigger. Failed compaction or exhausted preparation capacity fails explicitly rather than silently truncating. Recent-memory baseline selection does not reintroduce covered speech, including through cross-conversation duplicate groups; explicit trigger, commitment and correction sources stay independently required.

These baseline counts are explicit initial coverage policies, not provider limits. Essential trigger evidence is additional. References include baseline perceived identities and trigger participants; optional action handles bind other permitted targets server-side without duplicating every entity's metadata in the prompt. Oversized required context fails explicitly rather than silently dropping protected evidence.

Optional sections use scoped semantic top-300 retrieval when larger than 300; smaller sections go directly to Jev. Current-conversation speech bypasses optional retrieval entirely. The action library comprises all actor-permitted native and planning candidates. Above 300, index the complete library and retrieve semantic top 300; at or below 300, retain every candidate without purchasing unnecessary embeddings. An unavailable action index uses stable-ID top 300 with explicit incomplete-coverage diagnostics. Relevance only filters suggestions; it never selects or authorizes execution. Unlisted proposals remain available through the existing response contract.

Jev relevance uses a shared rubric and a compact independent Noul question for each candidate. Action relevance receives structured decision facts, without the generative response schema/instructions. Memory relevance includes a bounded saved inner-world excerpt only when nonempty; the excerpt is a keyword-selected cue, not verified belief extraction. Jev requests have no application question-count cap. Fit actual serialized state and questions under the conservative estimated token budgets in [provider limits](ai-providers.md#provider-behavior-and-limits), recording size omissions. The generative model retains its existing 100,000-byte complete-context allowance; fit action descriptions within the remaining space and report omissions. Required evidence survives optional attention failure. Retrieval admission, batch transport sizes and retention windows are separate policies; increasing question capacity does not authorize unbounded world/history disclosure or automatic paid retries.

Attention is reevaluated on meaningful changes in stimulus, intent or evidence. Cache only with valid dependencies. Record omitted candidates for diagnostics outside the reasoning prompt. During an unavailable Jev route or exhausted budget, preserve native reactions and explicitly record unavailable semantic work rather than passing the whole world/history to another model. The [perception proposal](../archive/07-technical-architecture/perception-and-attention.md) governs sensory detail; the proposed [EPR contract](events-perception-and-reactions.md) owns perception-change identity and intake, while semantic interest selection remains here.

### Every semantic decision uses an event or intent sentence

The retrieval pipeline applies to every admitted semantic decision opportunity, not only speech or explicit goals: notable events, hazards, encounters, need/goal changes and conversation all supply an actor-perspective sentence or short description. For example, “Lightning struck the tree directly in front of me” can retrieve “I recently saw someone die after being struck by lightning,” only if that actor actually retained that experience. Embed the full permitted stimulus with relevant decision context, use Jev to select recall and route reasoning, and let the recalled evidence influence the response. If the actor only hears an unidentified crash, describe that uncertainty instead of supplying hidden lightning facts.

The literal trigger names one initiating cause. At autonomous admission, choose the newest qualifying event as that cause; direct speech uses its own event, and an urgent superseding event becomes the new cause. Persist its identity separately from coalesced evidence. Other accumulated events remain required context, never concatenated into the trigger. A non-event opportunity describes its state change without appending goals or an event list.

Native templates render supported events without a separate prose-generation call. Event identity, revisions, timestamps and authority bindings remain server-side. Meaningful opportunities are coalesced and scoped caches may be reused; this is not a model call per simulation step. Native emergency responses never wait for embeddings, Jev or reflection. The lightning case specifies cognition behavior for an admitted event or labeled fixture, not new weather mechanics delivered by this design.

### Selection signals and delivery order

Memory selection must respond explicitly to **who is present, what just happened, current goals, unresolved concerns, and conflicting beliefs**. Build bounded actor-scoped signals from permitted encounters/recent experience, native plan and commitment state, and the accepted inner-world perspective. Concerns and belief conflicts can have derived retrieval cues tied to the accepted snapshot; they do not require a second writable belief store or a mandatory extraction patch on every reply. Include relevant supporting and contradictory evidence without forcing the character to adopt an objectively correct belief. Keep structured lookup signals and diagnostics server-side, with useful meaning rendered in English for Jev and reasoning context.

Deliver retrieval in order: first explicit structured signals with exact/entity/topic/commitment lookups; then semantic matching for arbitrary intents and paraphrases; then selective-recall tools when recorded cases demonstrate that an agent needs permitted information omitted from its initial context. Semantic retrieval expands candidate discovery without replacing Jev inclusion judgments. The tool gate applies to extra recall, not the file capabilities required for reflection. Record the omitted evidence, why it mattered, and whether improved initial selection or bounded tool access resolves the gap.

### Encounters, sensory detail and reminder continuity

The base world's current policy separates seeing from recording. Current visibility and exposure episodes include ordinary animals and objects, but their first appearance or routine return does not create a stored encounter. A newly seen memory-bearing person, including an unrecognized person, still creates private evidence at importance 3, urgency 0; seeing someone alone does not automatically trigger semantic reasoning. Existing interests can select visible animals/objects, and speech, attacks, death and meaningful exposed-state changes retain their own event policies. This avoids storing the bird's routine comings and goings for every observer. It also means an ordinary first sighting is not later available as an independent autobiographical memory. The policy does not infer animal intentions.

This is an owner-authorized September 27 change from retaining every ordinary animal/object onset and treating every new person encounter as an automatic social decision opportunity. The former policy preserved exhaustive first-sighting history; the new default prioritizes useful history and simulation responsiveness. `worlds/base/senses.ts` owns this bundled-world choice. It is not a universal engine law or the richer classifier: [EPR06](maintainers/events-perception-and-reactions.md#epr06--persistent-stimuli-and-bounded-actor-local-relevance) retains perceptible strength, actor relevance, novelty, urgency and narrative significance. Before adding conspicuous objects or dangerous fauna, connect their authored significance to retention/attention rather than assuming all sightings should stay quiet.

Losing sight of a memory-bearing person is recorded as private evidence at the same weight as seeing them arrive. The observer keeps at most one arrival record and one departure record per person per hour; further comings and goings stay current visibility only ([CG10](limits/cognition.md#cg10)). Exposure episodes end on loss of perception, except for a return within a short linger window after a recorded departure. So later visibility does not silently recover an unknown individual's identity, and a first brief occlusion does not reset it. The grace period counts only from the first departure recorded in the one-hour re-record window; later exits in that window write no record. A return within 30 seconds of that recorded departure keeps the episode even after further brief exits, and a return more than 30 seconds after it starts a new episode however brief the latest exit was. Preserve encounter identities, enter/exit hysteresis and restart baselines. Native immediate behavior can continue while bounded Jev routing processes meaningful opportunities. Reevaluate already exposed objects when goals change. Ordinary objects without relevant changes do not individually purchase inference.

Reserve queue capacity for hazards and directed speech, coalesce repeated episodes, and partition crowded scenes fairly with recorded coverage. A restart must not fabricate new encounters or replay paid work. Missing recognition, unintelligible speech and distant detail remain unknown; later proximity cannot retroactively upgrade historical evidence. Observation is distinct from accepting a claim as true.

Persistent critical-need reminders create reconsideration opportunities, separately from accepted hourly memory cleanup. An opportunity is a cognitive trigger point, not a required fresh thought or model call: native importance/adequacy checks may dismiss, defer or continue an adequate response, while warranted semantic consideration may use separately admitted paid Jev/model work. The initial crossing and hourly simulated reminder are proposed timing; D54 retains threshold/cadence tuning. Reminders respect real-time budgets. The example below 20/100 refers to fullness, not low hunger urgency. Apply the [agency survival boundary](agent-agency.md#8-attention-and-initiation): immediate native protection remains authoritative, while lack of an adequate native solution can permit bounded deliberation by a capable awake actor. D54 still owns threshold/reminder tuning; this does not close CR12 acceptance.

### Retrieval service and selective recall tools

Use one actor-scoped retrieval service for context assembly and reflection tools. `get_memories` accepts a purpose, topic or arbitrary intent, permitted people/entities, time range, recent/relevant/mixed strategy, and bounded record/byte limits. Scope comes from the authenticated job, never a model-selected actor. Server-side results retain selected sources, coverage, freshness and revisions; model-facing results are useful English strings.

Search recent raw experiences and eligible older consolidated memories. Use exact person/topic/commitment lookups and text indexes first, with semantic matching for arbitrary intents and paraphrases behind a replaceable interface. Rank by relevance, recency and salience, and diversify results so routine repetition or negative incidents do not crowd out other useful experiences. Jev makes the inclusion judgments described above. Relevant active promises and mandatory fresh evidence have direct lookup paths independent of similarity ranking or index freshness.

Embeddings are required for semantic retrieval of arbitrary intents and paraphrases. Embed the complete natural-language intent rather than requiring an LLM to invent search keywords. Embed the English text of recallable experiences/summaries and relevant permitted retrieval content when created or meaningfully changed; retain source identity, revision, actor/disclosure scope and embedding-model version alongside each vector. Reuse unchanged vectors and compatible cached query embeddings. Exact person/commitment lookups and full-text search remain complementary paths; Jev still selects inclusion from bounded candidates.

Batch indexing under explicit real-time resource/spending limits. Embedding inference has compute and, for paid providers, billable usage; account for both indexing and query calls without automatic paid retries. Pending/failed indexing must preserve mandatory fresh evidence through direct lookup and disclose incomplete semantic coverage rather than silently claiming equivalent keyword recall. Forgetting, corrections and permission changes remove or invalidate affected vectors and cached results. Model changes require versioned reindexing; never compare incompatible vector spaces.

The initial implementation uses OpenAI `text-embedding-3-small`, 512 dimensions, and PostgreSQL `pgvector` cosine search. The application sends a query embedding and permitted source IDs/revisions; PostgreSQL filters scope and returns only the top N IDs and scores. It never loads the stored vector collection into application memory for similarity calculation. Exact database search preserves recall; approximate indexes remain a measured scaling option. Evaluate paraphrase recall, privacy, latency, index size and total cost before changing these defaults.

Embeddings and caches are derived indexes, not canonical memory. Actor scope is enforced before ranking, snippets and counts. Corrections, forgetting and changed permissions invalidate affected entries. A lagging index cannot establish that an important new experience never happened. If required information does not fit, narrow or defer the task rather than pretend recall is complete or reveal hidden facts.

Reflection may use bounded `get_memories`, `inspect_memory` and `list_commitments` tools over this service, exposed through MCP or an equivalent adapter. Limit total calls, records, bytes, time and spending across the job; pagination cannot bypass those limits. No arbitrary SQL, world-history search or access to another actor's private mind is granted. Immediate level-2–4 calls use assembled context without requiring a tool round. Tools and database-backed recall do not require a separate database per NPC.

### Private retrieval fencing

Private evidence inspection follows the same disclosure boundary even without AI, including progress panels. Bind reads to the authenticated observer, world/control generation and current evidence/privacy revision before selecting permitted IDs. After each asynchronous or cached-promise result, recheck those bindings immediately before disclosure. Stable world identity alone does not fence a correction or forgetting committed while the read was pending. Invalidation stops later reuse; it does not by itself stop an already-running read returning old private text. Exercise that race through the actual read/commit/projection callers; reject stale results and keep missing evidence explicit. [Concurrent sling inspection](verification/practical-competence.md#continuity-and-private-inspection) demonstrates this requirement through cold SQL retrieval.

Goal changes reevaluate already perceived objects as well as new exposures. World-interest subscriptions and personal recall can share an intent, but have distinct permission scopes: remembering a tree does not establish that it is still present or currently visible.

## 5. Events, awareness and memory storage

All actors, including the player, have limited awareness. Record event-time awareness as a shared event plus an actor/event join carrying or referencing each actor's permitted perceived text, detail and attribution. Only a subset of actors may know an event; one listener might recognize a speaker while another only hears an indistinct voice. Joining an area later never grants historical witnessing.

### Personal perspective and acquisition

Static place arrival and explicit permitted item inspection attach exact observed facts to the existing private experience record. Place observations retain definition identity/revision, label, supported point, location label, description and observation time; inspected items retain exact object identity and definition pin. Possession, inventory list rendering, a visible outer bag and generated narration do not grant inspection of contents. [Known places](spatial-world.md#static-named-places-and-known-places) selects the latest retained observation for each place, including cold SQL records; it never replaces it with unseen live facts.

Forgetting the latest observation exposes an older retained observation if one exists; forgetting every observation removes that place from this view. A corrected observation stays visibly corrected, displays its eligible correcting evidence with attribution, and cannot authorize movement from the disputed location. Forgotten correcting evidence leaves an explicit correction warning. Later real observation can establish new information. These projections and their indexed eligibility are derived through the existing experience/storage owner, without a second writable memory collection or a total-place cap.

Latest-place updates batch changed observer/place pairs through the existing indexed source projection and retain the source's observer and family identity. Ordinary speech or new unrelated memories do not expire a Known places continuation. Privacy edits and restore still fence pages, and an inspection or movement selection must match the exact retained observation revision.

The [base-world place policy](worlds/base/places.md) gives its useful place observations the existing protected importance so ordinary hourly consolidation keeps the learned destination available. This retention decision is authored separately from story nomination: story importance grants no memory protection, and alternate worlds may choose ordinary forgetting for their places.

Model-facing speech history states the speaker, perceived intended recipient, and whether it was addressed to the observer or overheard. A currently continuous perception episode can bind an unnamed speaker to a supplied handle; a stored canonical source ID alone cannot identify a new exposure. Preserve quoted words and acquisition uncertainty, and keep the observer's own speech distinct from replies. Trigger facts separate the event source from its subject and report their current visibility independently.

Store individual memories in their owner's perspective, not merely as first-person text rendered by the UI. An actor's own actions and experiences use “I,” “me” and “my.” Accounts of other people use their known names and carry a qualifier explaining how the owner acquired the information. Use **observed** for witnessing and **heard** for testimony; retain **inferred** and **imagined** distinctions for interpretations and dreams. First-person wording does not replace source attribution.

| Memory owner and acquisition          | Stored wording              | Attribution                                                                |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ada gathers berries herself           | “I gathered berries.”       | Direct experience; native records currently use `observed`                 |
| Ada witnesses Mike gathering          | “Mike gathered berries.”    | `observed`                                                                 |
| Ada hears Mike describe his gathering | “Mike: I gathered berries.” | `heard`; this establishes what Mike said, not that Ada witnessed gathering |
| Mike witnesses Ada gathering          | “Ada gathered berries.”     | `observed`                                                                 |

The local character is named **Mike**. Agent-facing memories and context must refer to people by known names, never by the gameplay role “player.” Unknown people remain unidentified rather than gaining a name the observer does not know. Preserve quoted dialogue verbatim, with the speaker attributed outside the quote; the memory owner's own speech can be attributed as “I said: …”.

These rules apply to raw awareness, personal memories, consolidation and reflection. Shared world-event narration remains separate from personal memory wording. Keep acquisition metadata through storage, retrieval and inspection, and include an explicit qualifier in model-facing prose. Consolidated or authored text must preserve whether each account was witnessed, heard, inferred or imagined, including when a summary mixes sources. The [shared-presentation design](projects/compelling-characters-tech-design.md#shared-personal-presentation) makes native pronoun/name selection a common rendering responsibility; existing pattern-based conversion and locally worded feelings do not yet establish coverage for every entry.

Retain an external experiential world event when an actor was aware of it or when its trusted importance score meets the configured notable threshold. The notable-unseen exception preserves material for explicitly permitted dramatic narration without inventing awareness or making it NPC recall. Discard non-notable zero-witness experiential records after audience/scoring determination. State effects and necessary state-change, idempotency, accounting and recovery records still commit independently. Creator journal coverage must say what was never recorded; it cannot promise omniscient experiential history. Later discovery or testimony is a new experience, not retroactive awareness of the original event.

### Importance, urgency and in-flight response refresh

**Importance and urgency are separate bounded event-time scores.** Importance measures lasting significance for retention, attention and later reflection. Urgency measures how quickly newly perceived evidence should supersede an immediate response already in flight. An important event need not be urgent: learning that a close friend is visiting may matter greatly without requiring the current sentence to be discarded. Ordinary speech and routine awareness likewise do not invalidate a response merely because they arrived later. The initial runtime stores both scores on the shared world event and copies them into each permitted awareness row; legacy awareness without urgency is treated as nonurgent.

An in-flight response refreshes only when that actor receives a newer awareness entry meeting both configured thresholds. The initial thresholds are importance **8** and urgency **8** on the 0–10 scales. The director aborts the active provider stage promptly; before the one refresh it rescans from the original attempt watermark so every qualifying event that arrived before retry becomes required evidence, and provider/application stage identities distinguish the refreshed attempt. Events arriving during the refreshed attempt do not create an unbounded paid retry loop. Native emergencies still act immediately, and deterministic response admission independently rechecks action targets, range, inventory, costs and plan prerequisites. Importance alone continues to govern notability/retention; urgency alone grants neither retention nor disclosure.

Do not independently copy every aware world event into each actor's raw memory collection. The awareness view is already a raw experience source. Separate raw memories can hold other legitimate personal experiences. Both sources need stored English text and native source bindings; actor-specific projections must never reveal the shared event's hidden payload.

The production schema uses actor-scoped awareness and memory records alongside `mind.inner_world`; see the [data model](../archive/07-technical-architecture/production-data-model.md#8-minds-evidence-knowledge-and-conversation). Storage changes follow the [development save policy](../AGENTS.md#development-save-policy). This plan does not imply those tables exist today.

### Storage responsibilities

PostgreSQL is the target shared store; records are scoped by world and actor. A persistent actor workspace is the authoring surface for inner-world text, not a second simulation database.

| Record                                      | Responsibility                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Shared experiential event                   | One external occurrence retained for actor awareness or configured notability; unseen retention grants no actor knowledge                                                   |
| Actor/event awareness                       | Which actor perceived which part of the event, with stored permitted English text and event-time detail                                                                     |
| Personal memory                             | Raw personal experience (including accepted private `self_thought`) or consolidated summary, with English text, game time/range, relevant people/topics and retention state |
| `mind.inner_world`                          | One current accepted text snapshot per world/actor, with revision and publication binding stored alongside it                                                               |
| Native commitments and learned capabilities | Protected mechanical facts, status and permissions, independent of authored prose                                                                                           |
| Cognition/consolidation jobs                | Input boundary, scheduling, spending, completion and idempotent publication state                                                                                           |
| Private thought history                     | Bounded god-only presentation entries, separate from actor recall                                                                                                           |
| Player narration                            | Private source-linked prose in separate story tables; never world events or an additional actor recall source                                                               |

World commits and awareness delivery must be recoverable together; duplicate delivery must not create duplicate experiences. Game time drives age, recall windows, sleep and reminders; real time drives service deadlines, queue limits and spending. Store enough clock information to interpret saved game times after restart. The [production data model](../archive/07-technical-architecture/production-data-model.md#8-minds-evidence-knowledge-and-conversation) supplies physical table/index details for these responsibilities.

## 6. Hourly consolidation and six-hour raw recall

At every simulated-hour boundary, schedule bounded cleanup of routine raw personal memories and aware world events **older than six in-game hours**. Preserve the newest 512 linguistic speech experiences per actor as verbatim sources for current-conversation retrieval; older speech becomes eligible for consolidation as newer understood speech displaces it. This count bound avoids tying conversational continuity to accelerated simulation time while keeping storage finite. Other recent raw context uses the six-hour window. A delayed older backlog is not dumped raw into the next conversation.

Use a small summarization model, with GPT-5 nano as the requested candidate pending adapter availability and budget verification. This is light memory maintenance, not a full reflection harness. Group like entries, remove routine inconsequential details and retain or emphasize important experiences. For example, repeated berry eating can become “This morning I ate a lot of wild berries to satisfy my hunger,” and repeated observations of gathering can become “I watched John gather a bunch of fiber.” Preserve exceptions, consequential incidents, uncertain testimony and unresolved promises; do not aggressively erase character continuity.

Model request limits are separate from retained-memory capacity. Partition chronological routine sources by estimated input/output size, leaving room to return distinct singleton groups without requiring compression. Preserve original chronological positions across partitions so omitted protected incidents remain barriers. Validate every partition before atomically publishing the complete selected batch; failure, cancellation or exhausted spending allowance preserves all originals in that batch. Each partition is separately admitted against the existing background spending budget, with no automatic paid retry. Partition boundaries may leave additional compression for a later review.

The server prepares one chronological maintenance batch with at most 128 sources and 512 KiB of evidence. SQL measures encoded source bodies before hydration; both the selected raw payloads and normalized evidence must fit the byte allowance. Hourly batches stay within the existing six-hour neighborhood and calendar day; the newest 512 speech sources and native commitments remain protected. Database selection includes important incidents in order, preserving barriers. All model-request partitions of that selected batch must validate before its one atomic publication; failure preserves every original in that batch. The existing domain owner still enforces exact coverage, unchanged protected incidents and source identity, while database revision/generation checks reject stale results.

Daily review advances through the completed day in these bounded batches. Its durable cursor fixes an upper source revision, preventing its own replacement summaries from being reviewed again, and only marks the day complete after the prefix is exhausted. Previously accepted batches remain committed if a later batch fails; no partial model output within a batch publishes. A failed or interrupted batch is recorded before dispatch and cannot automatically retry unchanged. Legacy completed/attempted daily markers retain that no-retry meaning. This bounds work without a new grouping algorithm, evidence expiry or compression target; cross-batch grouping is left for a later eligible review.

Consolidation consumes the union of raw memories and raw aware events once per actor. Save source coverage/watermarks and atomically publish summaries before retiring covered detail from active recall. Shared source retention must respect other observers still awaiting consolidation. Never replace observations that arrived during a job or silently turn an imagined dream into witnessed history.

Consolidation preserves the [personal perspective and acquisition rules](#personal-perspective-and-acquisition): the owner's activity remains first person, other people retain their names, and testimony cannot become an unqualified factual recollection. A generic summary label alone does not explain how its constituent claims were acquired.

Every semantic decision context is selected consolidated memories plus selected recent raw memories plus selected recent aware events, woven into one recall section. Summaries remain subject to attention and longer-term retention limits; “consolidated” does not mean send every lifetime summary each time.

Failures leave the last accepted summaries usable and preserve unprocessed evidence. Consolidation pressure is a background scheduling signal, never a native simulation or movement gate. Missing credentials, exhausted AI budgets, busy cognition and failed maintenance must not freeze physical time or be reported as save failures. Crossing the pressure threshold does not delete, truncate or fabricate summaries of retained experience. Hourly eligibility uses simulation time; real-time concurrency, deadlines and caps control actual dispatch at accelerated speeds. Pause/offline behavior follows the existing world clock policy. Missed work is coalesced, not replayed as an unbounded series of paid catch-up calls.

### Conversation speech pool

Verbatim protection and automatic dialogue use the same actor-local set: actual self speech or heard speech containing understood words, including partial fragments. Merely seeing someone speak or detecting unintelligible speech remains ordinary perceptual evidence, available to required triggers, attention, history and later summaries. Such cues must not displace dialogue from the verbatim pool, consume the automatic recent-dialogue window, or create a separate speech-only embedding request. Main’s general eligible-memory index may index such cues as ordinary evidence under its existing policy. Explicit promises keep their separate commitment protection; an obligation record is not a duplicate speech turn. Select the pool from permitted, unforgotten awareness, not raw world events or a missing-fields fallback.

Background memory indexing uses the durable eligible-source queue across main’s supported memory families, with one bounded batch per actor per round. There is no parallel speech-only queue or completion cache. Publication revisions wake indexing; unchanged retained bodies are not repeatedly materialized. A denied actor allowance cannot prevent another actor from using its allowance. Provider failure stops the pass, and dispatched/uncertain attempts remain fenced against automatic repurchase.

Plan embedding requests against the adapter's count, per-text UTF-8 and encoded-JSON aggregate byte limits, reserving space for an uncached query when applicable. Share admission limits with the execution adapter. Never truncate evidence or label an oversized batch's sources as successfully indexed. An individually oversized optional recall source remains available to structured retrieval; semantic chunking is a separate extension, not invented by batch packing.

Provider work stays outside the world's mutation lane. Recheck current eligible source revisions, actor ownership and authority generation at the actual dispatch boundary after any awaited preparation; a cancelled-before-dispatch reservation settles without spend. Recall publication then enters a fresh serialized mutation turn. Background memory indexing revalidates the offered source revisions against current permitted evidence; foreground mixed recall rechecks its generation and forgetting/correction snapshot before publishing vectors and its query cache. A callback inheriting an old reentrancy marker must not bypass this turn. This orders publication with forgetting, correction and restore; prechecking outside the lane is insufficient. Do not imply that a cancellation can erase a provider request already dispatched. Fine-grained actor wakeups and other derived-publication families retain their own qualification work.

### Retention, corrections and protected commitments

Keep raw experience, the newest-512 verbatim speech pool, consolidated-memory retention, authored inner-world text and god-only history under separate policies. Consolidated memory has no fixed record-count ceiling; remaining storage slots must not determine whether distinct experiences can be consolidated. The six-hour routine window does not imply unlimited rows during a busy hour, and consolidated summaries do not imply unlimited lifetime retention. Tune record and byte budgets together. Native exact deduplication and supported decay work without a model; semantic summarization needs its separately admitted call.

Preserve actual unresolved promises through routine noise and compaction. Their creation allowance is finite, and native rules own fulfillment, deadlines, cancellation and other supported lifecycle changes. A model cannot create an obligation from an unsupported paraphrase, erase an active obligation to make room, or claim a promised action already happened. Narrative can be compressed without losing the required native fact.

Corrections and contradictory testimony should update later interpretation without rewriting witnessed history. Reconsider affected derived summaries or beliefs when their supporting material changes; invalidating a search entry alone is insufficient. The character may retain a mistaken belief, but the system must not silently present corrected or unavailable supporting detail as established evidence. Compacted source detail may become unavailable; keep only enough attribution to distinguish observation, testimony and imagination honestly. Forgotten details leave active indexes, cached contexts and actor-accessible files. Old provider sessions and backups must not secretly restore them. Creator retention is separately bounded and does not grant an NPC unrestricted recollection.

## 7. Minimal outputs and independent commits

Offer only the actor's supported speech and expression capabilities in both prose and the request-scoped output schema. Cognition does not grant a new body or gesture family. Validate the complete operation envelope, including mutually exclusive operation kinds and backward dependencies, before optional interpretation or admission; malformed output is a failed parsing stage with its input preserved, never a successful parse followed by unexplained admission failure.

A simple conversation may need only speech or no new response. [Agent agency](agent-agency.md) owns optional repeated operations; its [admission contract](../archive/07-technical-architecture/agent-agency-runtime.md#4-admission-ordering-and-physical-execution) owns independent outcomes, exactly-once private experience and receipts. Current response bounds belong in [Architecture](architecture.md#actor-agency-foundation), not a second target schema here.

Context uses English sections and durable event-time attribution. The conservative speech action-context gate and optional action-relevance fallback follow the [conversation context contract](narration-and-conversations.md#explicit-triggers-and-readable-context); omitted suggestions do not remove open attempts. Acquisition labels describe evidence and need not be recited in dialogue. Ordinary responses exclude mind patches and server metadata, and intentional thought remains fictional authored experience rather than provider reasoning. Native memory ingestion does not require an LLM; immediate response and background reflection have independent commit boundaries.

Reflection changes files through scoped workspace capabilities. Its final response returns **one or more short presentation thoughts, each no more than 20 words**. Choose a finite per-job count during implementation. These are authored character narration for the game, not provider private deliberation. It must not echo the entire edited inner world or its database metadata in JSON. Invalid output/publication preserves the previous accepted state; there is no automatic paid repair.

### Validation and recovery

Provider response schemas encode exactly one non-null operation kind per entry, including body/speech capability restrictions. This prevents mixed speech/action entries before admission; local validation still checks cross-operation dependencies and current authority. Heard non-speech events remain observations, not quoted speech.

Validation is deterministic code: check shape and size, the admitted actor/job, relevant revisions, offered action handles, current resources/targets/knowledge and already-committed results. Recheck relevant dependencies rather than rejecting a historical reflection merely because an unrelated world tick advanced. A delayed action must not act on a dead actor, moved target or consumed item.

Within each commit boundary, coupled changes publish atomically and duplicate completion is harmless. Preserve native observations arriving during AI work. Provider refusal, invalid output, missing credentials, cancellation and uncertain completion remain distinct. Uncertain paid execution retains its accounting reservation until reconciled; a restart must not resend it automatically.

Complete validated output is the initial publication boundary. Streaming thoughts or early action execution can follow only with explicit typed accepted messages and independent commit boundaries. Partial JSON or provisional text cannot execute an action or publish part of an inner world.

## 8. Reflection, workspaces and publication

Editable external understanding follows [Knowledge](knowledge.md); its bundled notepad and observer-name rules belong to [base-world knowledge](worlds/base/knowledge.md). General knowledge enters context directly; relevant subject documents enter scoped recall. Memories retain events and provenance. About me retains accepted self-understanding rather than duplicating those canvases.

Reflection runs a bounded multi-turn harness during safe downtime, eligible dreams or significant events. Its self-understanding workspace remains staged authoring under the existing ten-file/500-word/8000-byte per-file ceiling, with protected identity preserved. External understanding is edited through revisioned knowledge proposals with the separately admitted world limits. The server validates and atomically publishes the accepted self text and knowledge changes through the database commit boundary. Failed, partial or stale work retains the preceding accepted state. There is no per-actor YAML state file and no second independently writable narrative authority.

Store snapshot/version/job provenance beside the text, not inside the prompt. Atomic publication updates that actor's accepted snapshot and short thought history without replacing concurrent raw experience. A failed, partial, stale or canceled export cannot publish half a mind. Keep the previous accepted snapshot available while reflection runs; reconcile workspace/database disagreement explicitly, with one accepted revision rather than two competing authorities.

The simulation and interactive conversation **never wait for reflection** or remote cancellation cleanup. Use a bounded background queue, separate admission/priorities and shared spend reservations. Safe immediate behavior and the current accepted inner world remain usable while files are being edited. Reflection receives no authority to change physical state, grant a recipe, erase native obligations or elevate invented prose into observed facts.

Every NPC cognition job starts with fresh model conversation state and current trusted instructions, including immediate reasoning as well as reflection and dreams. Immediate calls use the assembled context; a reflection may share conversation state across its bounded within-job tool/model turns. Compatible compute can remain warm. Cross-job continuity comes from accepted inner-world files and permitted memories, not old model transcripts, checkpoints or audit exports. Scoped recall tools may retrieve more permitted experiences within the already admitted budget. World-agent chat tabs retain their separate conversation policy. No per-tick workspace hydration or full-harness call is required for level 2–4 reasoning.

### Why multi-turn execution matters

The harness lets an agent inspect a result before choosing its next tool call: read beliefs, examine permitted evidence, edit its chosen files, then review consistency before publication. Agent-selected names and organization are useful, but the important capability is iterative tool use; a database-backed document API could offer the same freedom with a suitable harness. Database commits remain the accepted authority and workspace edits remain staged. Knowledge documents use indexed rows rather than a combined external-knowledge text. This rationale does not establish a measured behavioral advantage over one-call reflection.

Later difficult planning, discovery and teaching may also benefit from bounded tool-using cognition. Keep these separate from reflection's file-write grants and immediate speech contracts. A character can inspect its permitted evidence and alternatives within a job, then submit a supported action proposal. Building, waiting for rain or hearing another person's reply happens in the simulation; save the objective and resume a fresh scoped job on meaningful feedback instead of keeping a harness running while waiting. Ordinary social turns need continuity, not automatically a tool loop. Initial-context recall tools retain their demonstrated-omission gate. [Later harness tasks](maintainers/cognition-redesign.md#later-harness-extensions) track this future scope; creator investigation and authoring use the separate [invention task queue](maintainers/inventions-and-world-evolution.md#inv-4--give-the-creator-useful-scoped-world-investigation-and-workshop-tools).

### Safe downtime and reflection scheduling

Downtime eligibility requires no immediate survival emergency or responsibility demanding attention. Purposeful native work continues; introspection must not displace it. Meaningful unprocessed experiences, unresolved concerns, a supported periodic review or approaching authored capacity can create a reflection opportunity. Jev can choose reflection alongside continuing ordinary activity.

Track the actor's experience boundary, idle/sleep episode and last accepted reflection. Coalesce repeated triggers, stagger actors and use real-time cooldowns and shared caps. Completing a reflection while still idle must not immediately schedule another over unchanged evidence. Mark processing progress only after accepted publication. Failure may defer a future opportunity, but never launch an automatic paid retry of the same work.

Urgent events, waking, actor death, canceled jobs or changed relevant rules may supersede pending work. Preserve the last accepted snapshot and native observation ingestion. Decide acceptance using the affected dependencies; a past experience remains usable even when its stimulus is no longer present.

### Instructions, file organization and quotas

Open Legend owns versioned master instructions, model bindings, permissions and character seeds. Skip Macrofold agent presets initially. Supply the current instructions at each fresh job; store their version in server receipts. Trusted instructions are not editable memory. Policy changes govern new jobs and can revoke incompatible in-flight work.

The harness chooses its own file names and subjects. An illustrative layout is:

```text
AGENTS.md        trusted reflection instructions, if needed by the harness
context/         bounded read-only permitted experience for this job
mind/            agent-organized persistent inner-world files
```

Workspace implementation may stage edits in an isolated snapshot or proposal directory before acceptance. Do not mount obsolete snapshots, transcripts or unrestricted creator exports. No mandatory file per psychological category or per remembered event is required.

The initial authored quota is at most ten files, each at most 500 normalized whitespace-delimited words, including titles, and a finite UTF-8 byte bound. Count all retained authored narrative, including relationship descriptions and durable scratch; metadata cannot be used as hidden prose storage. Engine-produced experiences and routine memory summaries have separate bounded stores. Trusted instructions and temporary tool results have request limits rather than consuming authored mind capacity.

Expose deterministic counts and a bounded preview/validation tool during reflection. Near capacity, the harness chooses what to summarize, merge or remove within the admitted run. Publish the final file set atomically only if it fits and preserves required identity/obligations. Reject an unresolved overflow while retaining the old snapshot; do not silently truncate text or purchase a repair call.

### Execution adapter requirements

Macrofold supplies generic bounded inference/harness execution, scoped file access, snapshots and compatible warm compute; Open Legend owns the meaning of memory and publication. Keep persistence and execution behind independently replaceable interfaces. Direct inference, fixtures or shared workers can implement the appropriate execution contract without changing memory semantics; worker lifetime, job lifetime and persistent actor identity remain separate. Verify independent model routes for levels 2–4 and the small summarizer, actor-scoped workspace reads/writes, fresh sessions, cancel/close behavior and reliable snapshot export. A catalogue entry or provisioned workspace alone does not establish those capabilities.

The game can call retrieval directly; harness tools reuse the same service. Hosted tool access needs authenticated reachability rather than assuming the worker can access the game's localhost. Keep secrets out of context and files visible to the model. PostgreSQL owns storage and workspace publication for every supported deployment.

## 9. Sleep, dreams, forgetting and consolidation

Physical sleep follows the authored [default content](worlds/base/sleep.md). Cognition policy names its eligible status-effect ID in `dream.statusEffectId` and the continuous elapsed-time minimum in `dream.afterSeconds` (two game hours by default). Dream reflection is deduplicated per effect episode. Waking resets continuous eligibility. Pause, speed changes and same-version restart preserve episode identity and cannot duplicate dreams. Dreams remain optional paid work; missing credentials or budget never prevent native sleep.

Dreamed scenes remain imagined and cannot prove an actual theft, encounter or invention. God-mode thought history receives short presentation thoughts; it is not another recall channel. Forgotten detail must leave actor-accessible indexes, workspace exports and later contexts, while creator-only audit retention stays separately scoped.

Hourly routine consolidation remains independent of sleep and reflection. A dreaming harness can reinterpret permitted experiences, but it does not replace the six-hour/hourly maintenance process.

### Future memory-transformation ideas — not implemented

- Once each week, review the thirty-day period ending seven days before the current day. Older low-salience memories may become progressively fuzzier through further consolidation, while high-salience memories retain their important detail. Any transformation may reflect the person's current perception and inner world while preserving source attribution and never rewriting imagined material as witnessed fact.
- A future dream-specific transformation may receive the person's accepted inner world as context, allowing remembered experience to be reinterpreted through current beliefs, concerns and relationships. This remains separate from intentional idle reflection and from the implemented daily mini-model consolidation pass.

## 10. Privacy, inspection and verification

Thought history and inner-world inspection remain god-only, enforced by the backend. Ordinary conversation narration exposes permitted speech and behavior, never another actor's private thoughts, reflection files, reasoning or About me text. A future explicitly authorized single-actor Narrator mode may use that actor's private fictional thoughts for a permitted recipient; choosing a perspective or voice alone grants no access. The player is also an actor for awareness filtering; creator privileges and dramatic story cutaways do not widen their character's knowledge.

The character profile exposes inner-world text, relationships/beliefs and short Thoughts only to an authorized god observer. Include an experience timeline, active promises and learned skills so continuity can be inspected alongside prose. Thoughts do not appear over avatars; public action labels are separate. Enforce the same access in responses and any future streams, not merely hidden UI controls.

Record routes, attention coverage, prompt/schema/response size, reasoning tokens, queue/provider latency and actual usage separately. Include all Jev, summarizer and harness costs per real hour at each game speed. Also measure recall/attribution errors, missed commitments, retained bytes and cost per simulated day. Compare executor or retrieval alternatives on the same character histories and workload; fluent narration alone is not acceptance. Keep diagnostic storage server-side; expose scoped detail only through authorized god inspection, avoiding bulk whole-mind exports. No observability vendor or new SDK is required by this design.

### Named and generic memory subjects

Recalled episodes about another individually named actor retain that actor's identity and are not text-deduplicated. Unnamed actors are represented by species without individual entity handles; equivalent generic episodes may be grouped, retaining original source evidence internally. The observer's own identity does not prevent grouping encounters with unnamed animals. Conversation turns and mandatory evidence remain separate regardless of generic naming. This is a recall projection, not deletion of underlying events or authoritative attribution.

Individual naming follows the observer's assigned given name under the [base-world identity policy](worlds/base/knowledge.md#identity-and-recognition), independently of the entity's global name. An explicitly assigned “Deer” is individual even though it matches a species noun. Remembered named episodes remain separate after losing the current recognition binding; they do not thereby identify a newly visible individual. Current perception and an event's still-valid exposure binding can distinguish unnamed bodies with scoped handles for targeting. Generic recollection does not identify which presently visible animal was involved.

## 11. Migration, delivery and acceptance

Initialize new actors' accepted text/workspaces from authored identity, relationships, beliefs, concerns and aspirations; operational goals use the agency seeding boundary. Preserve native commitments, learned capabilities and source attribution independently. Same-version restore must preserve these records and current forgetting rules without fabricating audiences or replaying paid work. Development compatibility follows the [root policy](../AGENTS.md#development-save-policy). Current implementation facts and historical conversions remain in Architecture and the documentation changelog.

Deliver the system through the [CR01–CR12 build tasks](maintainers/cognition-redesign.md): compact contracts and immediate conversation; attention and awareness; hourly memory cleanup; editable workspaces and accepted snapshots; background reflection and sleep; admitted trigger configuration; migration and inspection; then integrated acceptance. The task list is execution tracking, not a second architecture specification.

Acceptance must demonstrate:

- A greeting uses a short immediate response without compulsory reflection or mind-patch JSON; difficult decisions can escalate through Jev under explicit budgets.
- A promise survives repetitive routine activity; an older relevant memory can be retrieved; rumors, uncertainty and dreams remain distinguishable from witnessed facts.
- Different actors remember only what they perceived, including different detail of one shared event; unseen events still affect saved simulation state.
- The same action is first person in its actor's memory and named, source-qualified third person in a witness's memory. Names, quoted speech and acquisition survive consolidation and restart; agent-facing prose does not call a person “player.”
- Hourly cleanup summarizes routine material older than six game hours once, preserves important exceptions, the bounded newest speech pool and concurrent observations, and prevents repeated raw/consolidated copies in context.
- Reflection edits flexible files, publishes one accepted PostgreSQL text snapshot, and influences a later decision after restart without blocking the simulation or conversation.
- Sleep follows the saved energy policy, dreams cannot run before its configured continuous-sleep threshold, and provider failure never prevents sleep or survival.
- Cross-actor access, forgotten-session leakage, duplicate completion, stale publication, cancellation and partial failures cannot corrupt or disclose the mind.
- God inspection shows accepted inner-world content and short thoughts; ordinary clients do not receive them.

Behavioral acceptance must show the right memory influencing a subsequent decision, utterance or action, not merely appearing in the prompt. Use labeled cases for each selection signal and compare matched situations with different permitted histories: a present person recalls an old promise, a fresh event revives an unresolved concern, or conflicting testimony leads to a clarifying question or changed plan. Record the selected evidence and observable outcome without requiring private deliberation. Recall need not always change behavior; unchanged behavior can be justified, but the suite must demonstrate meaningful changes where the remembered evidence matters. Fixture routing proves plumbing; model-driven behavioral usefulness remains a separately capped live gate.

Use no-cost fixtures for deterministic contracts and separately capped live execution for provider compatibility, useful behavior, latency and actual cost. Do not call the complete system delivered from fixtures alone.

## 12. Remaining implementation choices

The [production recall records](../archive/07-technical-architecture/production-data-model.md#recall-sources-indexes-and-revision-changes) and [retrieval contract](../archive/07-technical-architecture/data-queries-and-mcp.md#relevance-retrieval-without-whole-history-preparation) now have a supported-gameplay implementation: SQL eligibility/ranking precede bounded text hydration. Required evidence, corrections, commitments and the bounded projection of the personally available current conversation have direct paths. Conversation lookup and awareness watermarks use indexed records. Source persistence, embedding readiness and context selection remain separate. The arbitrary 20 ms target is withdrawn; [component measurements](verification/data-foundation.md#data-foundation-runtime) are evidence, not a full-game latency guarantee. CR04 behavioral acceptance remains open.

The data foundation supplies [semantic source and grouping backing records](../archive/07-technical-architecture/production-data-model.md#semantic-history-and-grouping-support) for later classification, consolidation and dreams. It does not introduce even simple new semantic policies such as deciding that a punch matters more than a movement. Versioned optional classification, source-to-group links, uncertain/imagined attribution and correction dependencies remain extensible. Storage's ability to express overlapping groups does not change current consolidation chronology or turn a dream into factual evidence. Existing semantic behavior is preserved; new behavior stays in CR, with future omission/expiry policies recorded in the [retention ledger](../archive/07-technical-architecture/data-delivery-and-scale.md#retention-decision-ledger).

The 512-source speech preselection cap no longer bounds database recall. Eligibility preserves current semantics: correct world/actor/generation, retained awareness, non-inferred episodes not superseded by awareness of the same event, commitments, and summaries whose evidence has not been forgotten/corrected. Required evidence is selected independently of optional top N. Vector absence means index lag, not absence of evidence. Retired source versions preserve provenance but are excluded from ordinary recall and cannot bypass forgetting.

Initial choices are explicit and configurable: mini/low at level 2, complex/low or high at levels 3/4, nano cleanup, and the configured reflection harness; 1,024/4,096/8,192 immediate output tokens; a complete accepted-text context ceiling of 100,000 bytes; one size-bounded native Jev map for optional context finalists plus an action-rating map, carried in the routing request for non-speech triggers and after the gate for speech; and one to three presentation thoughts of at most twenty words. Required evidence survives attention failure. Relevance includes a Noul yes probability of at least 0.5; route choice uses a winning probability of at least 0.5, while uncertain addressed speech stays level 2. Level-1 selection acts at 0.7 and treats 0.5–0.7 as uncertain ([CG05](limits/cognition.md#cg05)); per-level request, size and monetary limits are in [CG08](limits/cognition.md#cg08). These are tuning policies, not calibrated correctness guarantees.

### Implemented retrieval and storage

Decision and reflection preparation share one request-owned actor/generation scope, conversation metadata and revision-keyed body cache. SQL excludes conversation-covered optional sources before selection limits and body hydration, so duplicate grouping cannot reintroduce those turns. Required triggers, commitments and corrections remain independently admitted. Reused bodies count toward the existing hydration byte limit; cache eviction fetches again without truncating evidence. Fresh validation checks conversation membership/corrections and other selected sources in one snapshot, deduplicating overlapping revisions. Every post-await/publication boundary remains checked; there is no cross-request freshness cache. [PF14](maintainers/performance.md#pf14--shared-cognition-preparation-and-complete-path-cost) owns measurements.

The current memory index uses the configured model (initially OpenAI `text-embedding-3-small`, 512 dimensions) and exact PostgreSQL pgvector cosine ranking across eligible actor/model/generation rows. It returns at most 300 optional sources, then hydrates source text. Exact search preserves ranking quality but grows with corpus size; measured 100,000-source latency is materially higher than structured lookup. No approximate index or arbitrary searchable-history cap is silently substituted. Incomplete vector coverage retains structured importance/recency lookup, with eligible/indexed/missing counts in diagnostics.

Committed source changes maintain a durable indexing queue. The background worker takes at most 32 sources per actor per round. A transaction rechecks current source revisions and generation, claims the batch and reserves spending before dispatch; ambiguous/failed claims survive restart and save rewind and are not automatically repurchased. Publication requires the same current eligible source version and generation. Reusable vector versions are retained outside gameplay rewind and in operational backups. They re-enter the searchable index only when their world/actor/source hash/model match current eligible evidence. This avoids repurchasing an already indexed source after correction or restore; archive cache presence alone grants no recall. No paid work runs inside the commit transaction. Each actor's cached query is bound to query text, generation, model and dimensions. Other optional candidate families retain their existing vector path. Selected source versions are rechecked after attention, before they enter reasoning.

Grouped context candidates retain all contributing source IDs. The post-attention check validates each source version, so an edit to a secondary member cannot leave stale grouped text eligible for reasoning.

`mind_source_versions` retains replaced/removed raw bodies and hashes; `mind_summary_sources` preserves current many-to-many summary provenance; `mind_source_annotations` accepts revision-checked, namespaced JSON metadata. Saves capture this backing. No new classifier, punch/walk rule, dream grouping or expiry policy is implemented. Existing consolidation and forgetting remain the semantic owners. Archive retention and eventual compaction remain explicit future policy decisions, never an implicit consequence of indexing.

The accepted requirements include embedding retrieval across all admitted semantic decision triggers, actor-perspective event/intent sentences, and the semantic levels, level-2 speech default, Jev escalation and attention, compact English context, actor awareness, hourly cleanup of routine experiences older than six hours with bounded verbatim speech continuity, background file reflection, PostgreSQL text publication, energy-based sleep and the configured continuous-sleep minimum for dreams. Implementation choices must preserve these behaviors.

### Residency and maintenance inputs

Inactive source bodies remain queryable in SQL rather than in each simulation snapshot. [Performance](performance.md#inactive-history-residency) owns residency thresholds and the explicit slow paths. Eviction never means forgotten, consolidated or ineligible. Memory selection, native maintenance input preparation, owner edits and gameplay saves all retain access to cold evidence. Consolidation prepares its existing semantic batch from an actor-scoped durable snapshot, then reacquires current sources under the mutation owner before accepting changes. No new classifier, grouping algorithm or retention policy is introduced. The player's latest-memory panel and legacy invention/world-assistant lexical contexts now use scoped SQL selection instead of treating resident history as complete. Pending decisions also retrieve their explicit triggering awareness from SQL for speech routing, event-time attribution and reference roles; those sources are revalidated after attention even if optional recall did not select them. Private inspection tracks changes to the inspected actor, plus restore and permission boundaries. Reflection checks unresolved obligations inside the publication mutation; resolved history leaving RAM does not cancel it.

## Owner-editor forgetting and raw JSON

The current god-mode Person editor lists raw personal memories, awareness of shared events, and consolidated summaries. A consolidated summary is derived text with `sourceIds` pointing to its evidence; it is not an independent world event. The World Events tab projects the same actor awareness subset, while the separate global editor changes shared event records. Deleting actor awareness does not remove the global event for other observers.

Person-editor deletion now uses the authoritative forgetting transition in the same atomic character save: it records forgotten evidence, removes dependent summaries transitively, invalidates derived mind text and marks workspace reconsideration, then clears derived vector/interest caches. Unresolved commitments cannot be deleted. Recall still filters against the authoritative ledger if cache invalidation fails. Raw JSON edits use bounded schema/hash validation and permit only prose and importance changes; identifiers, ownership, evidence links and mechanical fields must remain unchanged. Batched mutations validate before applying changes. Importance-only edits refresh retrieval metadata without deleting dependent summaries or accepted mind prose; prose changes still invalidate their dependents. Person memory pages cache actor-local ordering and hash only the selected page. ### Retrieval preparation admission

Exact retained-history eligibility has no newest-record prefilter. Required/correction source preparation is limited to 8,192 unique rows and 4 MiB of serialized source bodies, checked in the same SQL snapshot before hydration. Conversation metadata retains the row admission boundary, while only uncovered speech bodies are hydrated under the byte boundary; valid older compaction replaces repeat hydration. Oversize input returns an explicit preparation error; it never supplies an incomplete required set or deletes evidence. Existing paged history remains available. Optional selection still has its separate top-result bound; exact vector and lexical database work can grow with the eligible corpus.

Coverage reuse depends on the character’s source changes and its exact world/generation/model/dimension vector publications. Another character’s evidence or vectors and a different model’s vectors preserve warm reuse. Lexical selection reuse depends on the character’s sources, normalized query and limit, with current source bodies revalidated in the database snapshot before hydration. Restore, global eligibility and unknown mutation scope reset both caches; explicit caller transactions bypass shared reuse. Vector stamps live only while bounded coverage entries or outstanding readers reference them. Current-world/note candidate formatting checks 8,192 input items and 4 MiB of note text before formatting. These preparation allowances are operational protection, not a semantic-memory classifier or a new retention policy. [MH01–MH05/KG01](limits/memory.md#mh01) record remaining tradeoffs.

Optional legacy invention/HUD text selection now uses an inverted database index over a rebuildable `recall_sources.search_text` projection: explicitly serialized positioned `search_vector` values with GIN. Index and query words share history search’s NFKC normalization, lowercase, dotted-I folding and Unicode letter/mark/number boundaries. Quoted bound full-text values bypass PostgreSQL’s locale-dependent text parser, while original wording remains unchanged. Existing PostgreSQL byte/repetition/position edges remain; query words use OR token-prefix matching; native lexical rank replaces arbitrary substring-count scoring. Required records precede optional matches; indexed importance/recent fallback fills remaining slots. This is not semantic classification and does not change exact vector ranking or the separate complete required-evidence admission. All selections still enforce actor/world/generation/eligibility. Indexes follow atomic source changes, forgetting and rebuilds; canonical bodies remain the source of truth. Broad terms can still match a whole corpus; [MH03](limits/memory.md#mh03) retains that tradeoff.

Before any recall schema mutation, startup checks an existing text projection against the current explicit non-generated, non-null text/tsvector columns with empty defaults. A preceding generated or otherwise unsupported layout is refused unchanged even when its database marker is `3`; the shared marker alone does not prove component-layout compatibility. No conversion, index replacement, backfill or deletion is performed. The [current save contract](save-and-load.md#playable-week-format-gate) and development save policy remain authoritative.

### Maintenance storage failures

An advisory scheduling read can fail without changing the world or dispatching a provider. The maintenance scheduler reports a failed diagnostic, waits 60 real seconds, then checks again. Native simulation continues while its own saves succeed. No memories are deleted and no failed paid attempt is retried; durable attempt/source guards still apply. Failed scheduling writes, source preparation and world publication retain their existing authoritative failure handling. Advisory SQL inspections run at most once per actor per real second, with simulation deadlines and changed-source wakes retained; they do not fetch a separate world header. Actual source selection retains generation validation. Scheduling also skips reflection/review metadata when no cleanup, completed dream day, new reflection or executable queued reflection can be due.
