# Game interface research atlas

**98 distinct digital UI screenshots across 12 games, plus one display photograph excluded from that count.** Research and visual inspection: October 3–4, 2026 UTC. The initial 71-image atlas concentrated on inventory, activities and conversation; that was too narrow for Mike's request to rethink the whole game interface. This expanded atlas studies exploration, HUDs, object interaction, action selection and feedback, character information, navigation, reading, conversation, entry and help as well as inventory. Research coverage and implementation completion remain separate.

## Why these games

Selection is based on interactions Open Legend needs to learn from: embodied role-playing, physical storage, gathering/crafting, world orientation, character and action management, context-sensitive controls, readable history, conversation, multi-person play and entry into a persistent world. Similarity is at the relevant mechanic, not a claim that an MMO account bank or factory shares Open Legend's world rules. BG3 receives the deepest single-game walkthrough because Mike specifically requested it.

The research deliberately includes interfaces people criticize. A popular or highly rated game is not automatically a good inventory reference. First-person praise is tied to specific features: DOS2 equipment-slot selection, WoW combined bags, FFXIV resizing and alternative gauges, GW2 storage conveniences, Factorio stable quickbars, Stardew matching-stack deposit and a Zomboid player's fast unobtrusive inventory. Each dossier includes contrary reports or limits; no small discussion thread is presented as a survey.

## How to read a dossier

Every image has a source page and original image URL, version/platform context or explicit uncertainty, visible control/layout observations, documented player interaction, strengths, weaknesses and an Open Legend application. The manifests add exact bytes/dimensions/checksums and visual-inspection status. Controls too small or ambiguous to identify are marked uncertain; a static image cannot prove a drag gesture, hidden shortcut, interaction latency or live permission rule.

Developer guides and original developer explanations establish intended mechanisms. Firsthand player reports establish that person's experience at the stated time. Screenshots establish visible layout. Our recommendations explain how those observations might transfer. None of this is a hands-on playtest, representative preference survey, accessibility certification or current-patch completeness claim.

Known qualifications are preserved rather than smoothed away: the DOS2 alpha trade screen and optional organizer, BG3 launch-era trade versus later patches and unknown modification status, Minecraft Creative/Bedrock/controller differences, WoW's Dragonflight preview, Factorio's historical previews, Zomboid's prototypes/developer controls, Re-Logic's WIP control diagrams and the Stardew recipe-reference screen that search mislabeled as cooking.

## What changes in Open Legend

The persistent [inventory](../inventory.md), [world interaction](../world-interaction.md), [controls](../controls.md), [foundations](../foundations.md) and [chat](../chat-and-invention.md) chapters own adopted guidance. This atlas is supporting evidence, not a second handbook. The [single research ledger](../research.md#game-interface-screenshot-atlas) indexes these dossiers and the [UI work tracker](../../maintainers/ui-ux.md#uiux07) distinguishes completed research, implemented interactions and open gameplay acceptance.

The [58-surface inventory](../interface-coverage.md) connects the research to Open Legend's whole interface: world HUD and selection, character/action controls, collections and activities, conversation and reading, settings and recovery, and creator work. It records current capabilities and gaps; a screenshot in this atlas does not by itself satisfy a surface's design or acceptance requirements.

The proposed direction keeps the world and current task legible, binds commands to the selected subject, exposes the missing consequential choice, and shows what happened. Inventory opens the actual object beside the player's belongings; character and action views explain available choices; journals preserve reading context; conversation preserves speaker and audience; entry and recovery explain ownership and state; creator tools distinguish editing, review and consequential publication. The [nine layouts](../wireframes/README.md) illustrate selected arrangements, not every state of those surfaces. Keep native reach, permission, custody, capacity and real consequences. Do not import unlimited remote storage, hidden NPC inventories, a foreign game's combat rules, mandatory spatial packing or its entire aesthetic.

The expanded feature and technical designs organize the proposed changes and their [acceptance journeys](../../projects/game-interaction-redesign-feature-spec.md#end-to-end-acceptance-journeys). Those journeys must be qualified against actual supported behavior; the screenshot collection, original wireframes and design documents do not qualify the running game or imply the whole redesign is implemented. The [implementation verification report](../../verification/game-interaction-redesign.md) records actual native/component checks and remaining coverage.
