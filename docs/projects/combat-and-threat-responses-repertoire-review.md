## Purpose

Conflict should serve a playable adventure with independent people, useful inventions, comprehensible danger and worthwhile lives outside combat. Efficient native animals and independently thinking people can both produce unscripted encounters while using different decision costs and authority.

The [feature specification](combat-and-threat-responses-feature-spec.md) defines the selected player experience, the [technical design](combat-and-threat-responses-tech-design.md) defines integration, and [CTR01–CTR10](../maintainers/combat-and-threat-responses.md) owns implementation and acceptance. This document relates that scope to the broader repertoires; catalogue possibilities are not automatically implementation requirements.

Technical sophistication or a scripted resident demonstration does not establish fun or independent character behavior.

## World and future-scope boundaries

Bundled-world PvP, animal policy and Ada's disposition are authored choices. The optional [living fantasy](../repertoires/worlds/living-fantasy.md), [medieval survival](../repertoires/worlds/medieval-survival.md), [modern life](../repertoires/worlds/modern-life.md) and [planetary science-fiction](../repertoires/worlds/planetary-scifi.md) proposals keep their own laws and adoption decisions. Dragons, transformed people, object persons and collective minds do not lose agency because of body shape. Ordinary native prey needs no magical cognition or human decision calls.

The repertoires preserve these further possibilities without making them dependencies of the selected first delivery:

- Offspring and nest protection, packs and herds, predation, stalking, habituation, learning, migration, aging and generations; see the [animal scope](../worlds/base/animal-responses.md) and ecology owners.
- Exceptional creature cognition, richer quarry memory and continuing tracking, subject to their actual perception and evidence capabilities.
- Lasting grievances, forgiveness, personality and relationship change, independently accepted surrender, long-lived revenge and social aftermath.
- Rescue, capture, ghost continuity, institutions, sanctuary, political or economic consequences and advanced combat techniques.
- Broader public-world fairness, indirect hazards, recommendation counting, remapping and device controls.

[ND11](../maintainers/needs-design.md#nd11--human-conflictrecovery-and-npc-ghost-continuity) is **Done — designed for the selected direct-PvP, projectile-contact and immediate-response slice**. [ND13](../maintainers/needs-design.md#nd13--stable-action-recommendations-and-complete-control-customization) is **Done — designed for the selected combat-sensitive Command-click slice**. DG01/DG07 inherit those precise dispositions; DG02 has scoped immediate-response and encounter-continuity designs. Ada's wider life and the other portions of those design groups retain their own open acceptance requirements.

## Encounter continuity and character quality contrasts

These contrasts reuse existing repertoire patterns without adding another cast, catalogue or compulsory subsystem. The value is a complete playable change of circumstances, not breadth of unrelated prompts. [Continuing lives §17](continuing-lives-feature-spec.md#17-believable-encounter-pacing-through-chosen-intentions) owns accepted pacing behavior; [its technical contract](continuing-lives-tech-design.md#10-encounter-continuity-without-a-second-planner) and CE05 own integration and quality qualification.

These are contrasting episode families, not a newly authorized paid matrix. Freeze an exact applicable row list before execution. Start with installed capabilities; mark a variant blocked if a necessary mechanic is missing. Preserve real native action ordering and actor-permitted sources. Expected reactions and grading stay outside character context. No scene text tells a character that contact is “uninvited,” a threat “must be convincing,” or a person “should remember” something.

Review each complete episode from the player's permitted view: what they saw/heard, which choices they could make, what actually changed, and whether the next exchange follows intelligibly. Record confusing repetition, unjustified omniscience, artificial waiting, ignored real concessions and useful surprises separately. Predictable obedience is not the target. Independent cruel, suspicious, generous and playful choices can all be appropriate; evidence, character, physical possibility and consequences must agree. Human enjoyment remains a separate observation, not something a judge score proves.

## Research grounding

The [COMBAT synthesis](../../archive/02-research/game-inspiration/synthesis/combat-and-encounters.md) compares native creature behavior, purposeful pursuit, separate targeting displays and deliberate attack defaults in Rain World, Kenshi, Final Fantasy XIV and Old School RuneScape. Its [assessment records](../../archive/02-research/game-inspiration/synthesis/assessment-records.md#combat-pass--october-17-2026) own primary sources, exact dossier revisions, dates and evidence limits. Wider questions about bosses, mastery, morale, raids and surrender/capture remain open.

These comparisons support design distinctions; they do not establish OpenLegend's speeds, safety scoring, model latency or enjoyment. The animal profile's National Park Service locomotion examples are plausibility references, not biological calibration of fictional species. Native mechanics, independent Ada choice, player understanding and enjoyable real-time encounters require the separate acceptance gates in [CTR10](../maintainers/combat-and-threat-responses.md).
