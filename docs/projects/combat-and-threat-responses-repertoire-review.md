## Scope and method

This records the repertoire review supporting the [feature specification](combat-and-threat-responses-feature-spec.md), [technical design](combat-and-threat-responses-tech-design.md) and [CTR01–CTR10 delivery](../maintainers/combat-and-threat-responses.md). The reviewed base is `Macrofold/OpenLegend@0eb7358f251d1ab706592ccb6e9829e98c729fe9`, tree `61758a4016221f8899ddda37f4e51e26611f9574`.

All **42 files** under `docs/repertoires/` were retrieved at that revision. Every file received a scope/structure review and a semantic review of this design's relevant entries, headings, selection guidance and worked situations. Catalogue inventories and stable-ID structure were checked; complete changed rows and surrounding relevant context were read. The table records actual depth and deliberate keep/change decisions. This is not a word-for-word recertification of every unrelated paragraph, optional cast biography, historical research ledger or external source. A retained file is a reviewed decision, not an unexamined omission.

The governing goal is a playable adventure with independent people, useful inventions, comprehensible danger and worthwhile lives outside combat. The review strengthens existing purposeful defense, retreat, refuge, restraint and resumed activity instead of importing a large conventional combat progression system. Ordinary native animals and independently thinking people can both produce unscripted encounters while using different decision costs and authority.

## Changes and preserved possibilities

- One useful body entry is added: **BSP-260 Woodland hare**, a Core fast/small prey body. The body catalogue now has **260** entries and the non-action repertoire total is **7,872**. Existing action count is **385**. No old ID is removed or renumbered.
- **31 existing inventory rows** are clarified: fifteen action/mechanic/combat/adventure/ability rows and sixteen psychology/trait/character/relationship/conflict/history/knowledge/leisure rows. Their IDs, classifications, priorities, source cells and unique useful alternatives are preserved. Additional scope/navigation prose connects the canonical selected design.
- The living-fantasy proposal loses duplicated cast-default prose while retaining its unique qualifications. Optional modern, medieval and science-fiction worlds retain their own meanings; bundled-world PvP, animal behavior and Ada's disposition do not become laws of every reality.
- Complete relevant choices during physical work, real contact on protectors, observed versus inferred hostility, source-limited pursuit, achievable safety and reasons to stop now have explicit consumer designs. Richer animal ecology, exceptional creature cognition, institutions, surrender agreements, revenge and longer-term relationships remain useful future possibilities with named dependencies.

## Research transfer

The [COMBAT synthesis](../../archive/02-research/game-inspiration/synthesis/combat-and-encounters.md) now contains four narrow source assessments and lessons: distinct selection/threat/offense meanings; native escape with destinations; aggression with purposes and reconsideration; and stable contextual input. Its [assessment register](../../archive/02-research/game-inspiration/synthesis/assessment-records.md) retains primary sources, exact dossier pins, dates, inference boundaries and incorporation evidence. The wider guide remains **In progress**; unassessed bosses, mastery, morale, raids, surrender/capture and other original questions remain open.

The reviewed examples are the Rain World developer discussion of configurable native behavior, historical Kenshi Hold/sight/friendly-fire behavior, FFXIV's separate target/focus/enmity presentation, and OSRS's explicit attack-click defaults. These sources support design distinctions; they do not establish OpenLegend's speed, safety scoring, model latency or enjoyment. The animal profile separately cites National Park Service locomotion examples as plausibility references, not biological calibration of fictional species.

The source matrix changes exactly four COMBAT cells from U to I, for Rain World, Kenshi, FFXIV and OSRS. The 176 × 35 matrix still has **6,160 cells**: **4,928 U, 526 W, 672 X and 34 I**. COMBAT has **4 I, 155 U and 17 W**; NPC remains at 30 I and all other cells retain their previous dispositions. Historical shell-review claims are labeled as their original baseline rather than rewritten as fresh full-corpus research.

## Verification and evidence limits

Structural comparisons checked stable IDs, ordering, uniqueness, row arity, classifications, source cells and affected counts. Complete affected repertoire and research diffs were reviewed. New relative links and anchors were checked against the fetched tree and current destination headings; the final combined branch review also checks introduced links and formatting. These are document checks, not runtime qualification.

No game execution, browser playtest, paid Decision API call, latency/population benchmark or psychological validation was performed for this design. Additional Jev cost **$0**, cumulative task total **$0**. Native mechanics, independent Ada choice, player understanding and enjoyable real-time encounters have separate acceptance gates in CTR10.
