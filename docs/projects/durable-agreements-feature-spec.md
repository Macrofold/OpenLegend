# Agreements worth coming back to

| Status      | Current progress                                                                                                                   | Last updated |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| In progress | DG22 researched product proposal complete; adoption, technical work and runtime qualification remain open under INV-20.5 and BW17. | 2026-10-06   |

This develops DG22, ND09 and the modest cooperative part of ND10. It recommends a coherent disposition of the relevant D11/D18/D64 questions without marking those decisions adopted. The [world profile](../worlds/base/durable-agreements.md) owns the proposed first arrangement and defaults. [INV-20.5](../maintainers/inventions-and-world-evolution.md#dg22--durable-commissions-and-small-cooperation) remains the implementation owner; this is not a second promise or settlement system.

## 1. The experience we want

Two people want different things and can help each other. One plans an outing; the other wants food and knows how to make a useful object. They agree on a small commission, continue their separate activities, and later meet to exchange the actual result. On returning to the game, each can remember the accepted terms without reconstructing a long conversation. If somebody changed their mind, the game makes the remaining situation understandable.

The first complete addition is a **finite commission payable on delivery**. It adds durable agreed intent to the independently useful immediate-barter design. The maker promises an already understood, supported item; the customer promises a specified payment in goods. Nothing transfers or becomes reserved when they agree. Both later inspect the actual goods and choose the exact immediate exchange. A promise to produce is not a guarantee that a resident will act, and a seller's claim of completion is not delivery.

The point is useful cooperation among people with separate lives. A deadline, contract record and default label are support for that experience. They are not reasons to create slow manufacturing, compulsory trading, routine bill payment or a second job for the player. The first exchange must remain worthwhile if the optional institutional features are never built.

A later unsecured advance can create a meaningful trust choice: give someone a real supply now for a promised result later, with the possibility of loss. A later small association can remember people and a continuing purpose. Each is an independent extension. Money, banks, automatic escrow, market boards, employers, government and corporate ownership are unnecessary first dependencies. Eco’s work-party design grounds requests in actual player needs; EVE demonstrates that exchange can use goods directly. [DA-R01](#da-r01--exact-exchange-does-not-require-currency), [DA-R04](#da-r04--cooperation-should-arise-from-wanted-work).

## 2. Current context and existing designs

The audit inspected [main 0a3ab79b](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/commit/0a3ab79b7a698a7f1941dc23722f89220d1ba425), separately from this branch's older runtime ancestry. Current one-way handover offers an exact carried lot to a nearby person and requires that recipient's acceptance. It transfers nothing before acceptance, reserves nothing, and lapses when its current goods or participants cease to qualify. Its short game-time expiry fits holding something out, not a multi-session promise. [Current handover](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/0a3ab79b7a698a7f1941dc23722f89220d1ba425/packages/domain/src/handover.ts), [world social contract](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/0a3ab79b7a698a7f1941dc23722f89220d1ba425/docs/worlds/base/social.md).

[PX02 reciprocal barter](parallel-batch-04-expeditions-and-exchange-feature-spec.md#px02--trade-something-useful) already designs one exact lot on each side, changed-term assent and atomic settlement. It remains unimplemented on the inspected main. This proposal consumes that settlement and preserves PX02 as an independently complete earlier delivery. Extending the current gift code is future work, not an already available trading economy.

Current personal promises are protected actor memories. The narrow gathering form can be completed by a matching gathering event; it does not prove a quantity was delivered to somebody. The player's own Journal has a delivered read-only promise view. Broader management and negotiated amendment wait on D64. [Readable promises](readable-promises-feature-spec.md), [current obligation owner](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/0a3ab79b7a698a7f1941dc23722f89220d1ba425/packages/domain/src/commitments.ts).

The [repertoire foundation](../repertoire-foundation.md#6-agreements-obligations-and-fictional-institutions) already separates accepted terms, actual conduct and optional enforcement. Its agreed design calls for exact revisions, independently accepted roles, delivery evidence, unknown/disputed outcomes, amendment and release. The new contribution here is a selected product family and complete lived journeys, not another generalized contract language.

Current items distinguish physical custody, declared owner and access. A ground bag can be accessible without granting collective ownership. A membership name cannot confer access, reserve stock or create an account permission. Standing resource reservations need a supported owner beyond the current live-work claims and remain BW21 work. This proposal does not manufacture escrow from a promise-shaped record.

## 3. What exactly can be agreed first

The first commission has two identified people, one promised output and one promised payment. Each side is a known installed item definition with a positive supported quantity. The output may not exist yet. If it is an individual made object, the accepted requirement states that understood definition and the relevant known consequence, such as an empty pouch with its actual supported capacity. Merely using the same name is insufficient.

The first terms have one item requirement per side and settle through one currently selected lot per side. A requirement can be for a future item of the accepted definition; it is not a claim that an unseen lot is presently owned. Existing unique keepsakes, item loans requiring return of the same object, baskets of mixed goods, subjective service quality, open-ended labor, royalty shares and promises to invent an unspecified improvement need additional selected terms. They do not pass as equivalent first-family goods.

The parties also choose an understood meeting place or explicitly leave it to arrange later. A remembered place is not permission to teleport, reveal another person's location or enter restricted storage. There is **no deadline by default**. If timing matters, they choose an explicit future time in the offered world's game clock, with its meaning visible before agreement. “Tomorrow” cannot silently mean the customer's next real-world session.

The accepted record states the promised output, payment, quantity, any known condition requirement, parties, place/time, lack of reservation, amendment rule and what happens if either stops. It is concise enough to review beside the game. Optional conversation explains motivation; it cannot override the actual terms. A participant can decline without needing to provide a reason.

The first family admits only a known supported result. A maker who lacks a technique can discuss learning or inventing it, but the interface must not present an unsupported output as a dependable commissioned product. An agreement never installs a recipe, supplies ingredients, improves skill or authorizes creator powers.

## 4. From conversation to accepted intent

Natural conversation can lead to a draft: “Could you make me a pouch? I can bring food.” That is still discussion. The player then sees **They plan to provide**, **You plan to provide**, and **When and where**, with exact supported quantities and the absence of any present transfer stated plainly.

A draft may be revised freely without affecting the world. Sending it communicates an offer to the intended nearby person through ordinary permitted interaction. There is no world-wide request directory or automatic remote message service. The other person sees the same revision and can accept, decline or propose a change. A changed quantity, output definition, deadline, payment or participant clears assent to the old draft. No party can accept on another's behalf.

The current local reception/control route determines whether a person actually received the proposal. The structured panel is an interaction aid, not permission to write knowledge into an absent actor. NPC acceptance uses their own admitted choice with their actual known skills, resources, needs and intentions. The system does not force a fairness price, helpful personality or desire to do the job.

On the final required assent, the accepted terms become the durable agreement and each party receives its permitted commitment evidence. Admission must fit the existing unresolved-commitment budget; it cannot silently displace an older promise or create another allowance of protected memories. Until this succeeds, the interface says **Not agreed**.

An accepted commission supplies a reason the maker may act, not a queued order imposed by the customer. The maker might finish something else first, ask for help, use a different legitimate route, delay, or decide to stop. The customer likewise retains control of the proposed payment. Neither party receives a magical reservation on the other's goods or future labor.

## 5. Separate lives, return and actual delivery

The accepted commission survives closing the panel, saving and a normal later session. It does not create activity during stopped world time, simulate an absent human or buy unattended resident thinking. If the world actually continues under its chosen offer, actions and deadlines follow that real game clock. Otherwise, no manufactured work or lateness occurs.

The participant's Journal extends the existing Promises area with **Agreements**, rather than a business-management launcher. A compact row answers what the person agreed to provide, what they expect, the known due state and how to inspect terms. Current obligations stay separate from drafts and historical results.

The view reflects **what this participant knows**. It can show their own preparation, the terms they accepted, a deadline they can calculate and a counterpart's actual communicated claim. It cannot remotely reveal whether the other person has finished crafting, consumed the payment, died out of sight or privately abandoned the job. A remembered agreement is not an always-updated message terminal. An uncommunicated waiver, ending, membership change, death or changed custody cannot silently revise another character’s known status, commitment count, intention or UI explanation. Later actual settlement still uses the current authoritative claim, so a waived debt cannot be collected merely because an older view showed it.

The maker can say the item is ready when communication is actually possible. On meeting, both inspect an exact proposed exchange using PX02. The output is the actual item now offered, with relevant condition, quantity and destination; the payment is the actual lot selected now. A binding to the old future requirement does not grant a standing right to withdraw anything from either inventory.

If the goods satisfy the still-applicable accepted requirement, the successful joint exchange supplies delivery evidence for this agreement. The same transfer cannot be counted twice or applied to two different unpaid requirements. Selecting which agreement it satisfies is explicit when more than one could match. A completed gathering or crafting event alone cannot settle the customer's claim.

A changed item, consumed payment, lost reach, full destination, incompatible bag contents or changed permission can refuse settlement without changing either custody. The unsent intent remains understandable. They may select valid lots, renegotiate or leave. There is no partial intermediate trade, silent substitute, false receipt or automatic paid retry.

If an output no longer matches—another size, another definition, unexpected contents or a different supported effect—the parties can agree a new revision. A generous interpretation in generated dialogue cannot perform that amendment. The player should see the difference that matters, not a raw implementation identifier.

## 6. D64: withdrawal, amendment, ending and release

The proposed disposition is deliberately precise:

| Choice                                      | Whose choice it is                                 | What actually changes                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Withdraw an unaccepted offer                | Its proposer                                       | The offer ends; no accepted terms or transfer ever existed.                                                                                                                                  |
| Stop pursuing one's spoken promise          | The speaker                                        | Their future intention is withdrawn and recorded as such; it does not say the recipient forgave or released the promise.                                                                     |
| Amend accepted future terms                 | Every party whose obligation or claim is affected  | The new exact revision becomes effective only after the required actual assent. Earlier transfers and history remain.                                                                        |
| End the coordinated undertaking unfulfilled | Either participant                                 | The ending actor stops their own future participation and retires their active undertaking slot. Accepted terms, actual transfers and any still-outstanding claim remain visible in history. |
| Waive a claim                               | The person entitled to that claim                  | Their remaining claim is released, wholly or by an explicitly supported amount; somebody else's claim and prior events are untouched.                                                        |
| Record a late fulfillment                   | The relevant parties through the actual settlement | Real delivery is credited against the original remaining claim and labeled late where a due time existed.                                                                                    |
| Express forgiveness or accuse someone       | The character through ordinary expression          | An attributed interpretation is communicated; no mechanical payment, waiver or universal reputation change follows automatically.                                                            |

An uncommunicated ending retires only the ending actor’s active undertaking. The other person keeps their legitimately known terms, count and independently chosen work until they learn the ending or themselves choose to stop. A live badge, changed count, disabled action or automatically cancelled plan must not disclose a hidden decision. A private waiver follows the same knowledge boundary.

For payable-on-delivery terms, no delivery means no unconditional debt for the full price. The first family selects no cancellation damages or automatic payment for a maker’s private unfinished work. The original reciprocal promises can remain unfulfilled evidence without becoming fabricated money owed. An actual advance creates the specific remaining claim described below.

Ending unfulfilled is important when plans change or a counterpart never returns. An optional arrangement must not permanently consume every ordinary planning slot. Its status must say **Ended unfulfilled; claim remains** where appropriate, rather than **Cancelled and settled**. A later real repayment or waiver can still resolve the retained claim. This is a new proposed agreement lifecycle using the existing commitment/evidence owner; current narrow promise behavior does not change until adopted.

Retiring active work does not erase debt or make the person creditworthy. Historical claims can accumulate, and need bounded reading rather than an ever-growing active context. Nobody gains a right to another advance by clearing their own active slot: every new transfer requires another independent decision. No universal score, automatic lending or garnishment is selected.

An amendment itself may be withdrawn or declined. While it is pending, the prior effective terms remain the terms; an approaching deadline does not move merely because somebody proposed a new one. The first profile permits one pending amendment per deal, counted against the proposer's pending-proposal allowance. Parties can abandon that draft and submit another, retaining material communication history without presenting every keystroke as a contract event.

A claimant may waive their own claim without requiring grateful acceptance. Knowledge of that waiver still follows actual communication or an authorized later encounter with the result. The other participant does not retroactively learn an unobserved private decision. A private intention to forgive and the actual admitted waiver remain separate.

This recommends the product meaning to resolve D64. It does not broaden the existing English parser, change native cancellation into mutual release, or claim a general language model can infer legally exact contracts.

## 7. Due time, default and dispute without mind control

A selected due time makes the agreement **overdue** when the actual game clock passes it without sufficient supported delivery evidence. It does not automatically transfer a fine, seize possessions, lower a universal reputation score or decide why someone failed. A deadline can be missed because of danger, hunger, misunderstanding, absence, changed preferences or deliberate refusal; those causes are learned evidence and interpretation.

Late delivery remains possible until the coordinated undertaking ends, and a retained claim can still be settled afterward through a fresh exact exchange or accepted payment. An agreed deadline does not create a universal grace period or demand that the player check the game at a particular real-world hour. Default no-deadline commissions avoid that obligation entirely.

A removed or inaccessible meeting place prompts a new arrangement; it does not teleport delivery, destroy goods or infer the other participant caused the failure. A dying maker has not produced an item. A human who Continues keeps the same identity and history, with actual corpse/custody loss possibly affecting what they can now offer. An NPC's death does not erase the promise, revive them, transfer their debt to a relative or authorize looting.

For an unfulfilled commission with no advance, either party can end the undertaking and pursue another plan. For an advance, stopping does not return the goods already given. A disputed explanation can be retained as that person's claim beside the objective transfer record. Unsupported claims such as “equally valuable work” remain disputed or manually negotiated; no model secretly decides a binding award.

EVE’s published courier rule likewise distinguishes an overdue contract from an issuer-finalized failure; its collateral is a separate enforcement choice. [DA-R02](#da-r02--overdue-and-failed-are-different-outcomes).

The first world has no automatic court. Participants can forgive, seek repayment, refuse future work or keep cooperating for other reasons. Those choices can matter to a character's own memory and understanding without a global virtue meter. Meaningful conflict is preserved; an administrative trap is not required to make it consequential.

## 8. Optional unsecured advances

Select an advance only when a specific person wants to enable work or accept a real trust risk. It is a separate disclosed option, never a default checkbox on every commission. The first offered advance is an exact real transfer to the maker, accepted by them, tied to the exact agreement revision and remaining deliverable.

The review says what leaves now, who will control it afterward, what remains promised, and **No automatic refund or protected deposit**. The advance may be consumed in ordinary crafting or used differently. There is no phantom escrow stock in both inventories and no refund if inputs were spent. If the customer cannot afford that loss, payable-on-delivery remains available.

The first advance is one agreed transfer, not a revolving credit line, interest-bearing balance or salary system. Its immediate custody change and the agreement's actual advance evidence must succeed together. A failed or uncertain submission is reconciled before another attempt. Reopening the record does not advance a second payment.

At final delivery, show only the remaining consideration, accounting for the actual advance. If the advance was the entire payment, delivering the finished item can settle the remaining claim through its own accepted transfer. If additional payment remains, use the exact joint exchange for that remainder. The first family does not silently prorate partial goods or accept a replacement item; those require an exact amended remainder and actual assent.

Ending unfulfilled preserves the advance and the unresolved claim. The first advance terms provide two explicit voluntary resolutions: fulfill the original remaining output, or return the complete advance in the same accepted item definitions and quantities in exchange for release of the prospective commission. The claimant must accept that actual restitution and release; a dropped item or rejected gift does not settle it. A different substitute needs a mutually accepted revised remainder. Credit the real transfer once, clear only its agreed remaining claim and retain the original advance. A spoken apology is not a refund. A waiver ends only the lender's claim. No ordinary participant acquires permission to recover property from a bag, corpse or third person merely by producing a debt record.

The economy must tolerate the lender declining. Residents should evaluate advances through actual permitted evidence and their own priorities; a relationship description does not guarantee payment. Repeatedly harming, helping, trading or defaulting grants no mechanical reward that makes cycling agreements profitable.

## 9. Optional small association

A repeated cooperation problem may justify a persistent name, member list and purpose: for example, people who wish to maintain a shared outing kit. One commission does not. Two people can trade, help and remember promises without creating an organization.

When selected, the association begins with an inviter, a stated purpose and independently accepted membership. Membership is voluntary, can be declined and can be left immediately. Its record distinguishes invited, joined and departed people. No current starting character is silently enrolled, and no prebuilt government is added.

The first association is a coordination record, not a new autonomous actor, bank account or corporate owner. CCP’s ship-hangar redesign illustrates why complex group roles should earn their value rather than arrive with every shared container. [DA-R05](#da-r05--small-groups-need-clear-permissions-not-a-corporate-hierarchy). It can identify the member who accepted responsibility for a particular real container, with only its last legitimately known custody and link separately accepted agreements. A common purpose is a topic members may choose to pursue, not a new shared goal that overwrites individual intentions.

The founding inviter maintains this modest association record: they may invite or remove members, rename it and close future coordination. The founding purpose remains the one members accepted; a materially different purpose requires a new independently joined association. Transferring record stewardship requires the named successor’s assent. The steward may leave without a successor, closing new invitations and coordination while preserving historical membership, objects and agreements. No absence timeout removes an ordinary member or dissolves the group. These record powers grant no power over anyone’s work, possessions, private thoughts or platform access.

The record names a custodian without changing declared title or access. Current access lists remain explicit per object and controlled by their actual authorized owner. Joining does not gain contents access; leaving does not retroactively remove objects already taken. Where existing controls can revoke future access, that action remains separate and must be clear. No leader gains permission over another person's bag.

If ordinary member-operated sharing rights are wanted, that is a new narrow custody/access consumer to design and deliver under PO before presenting the association as shared protected storage. The first named association must not imply that current creator controls are available to any self-appointed steward. Keeping a record of who agreed to hold the kit is useful only if the group actually wants that coordination.

Each contribution remains either an actual gift, an explicit loan if that family later exists, or an ordinary retained possession. The first association cannot invent co-ownership percentages, withdrawal entitlements, inheritance, liability or a treasury from a caption. Member obligations continue only through their separate accepted agreements.

Dissolution closes future association invitations and coordination. It preserves membership history, contributions, remaining claims and real objects where they are. Custodianship does not evaporate and supplies do not automatically return to every contributor. Member departures and individual access changes must be discoverable to the people legitimately learning them; no world-wide private group directory is selected.

This is the recommended modest ND10 slice. Formal organization title, delegated signing, wages, taxation, courts, political power and public norms remain DG32 work when an actual activity needs them.

## 10. Economics and why money is deferred

**No currency is selected for the first delivery.** A few known people can exchange goods and make finite promises without adding another object they must earn, price or protect. Money becomes useful when someone wants to sell to one partner and buy from another later, and direct matching repeatedly obstructs wanted play. That future need must be demonstrated in an actual offered economy, not inferred from the word “commercial.”

D11's longer-term currency ambition remains open. Before a currency family is adopted, choose its issue authority and initial distribution, whether it is physical or recorded, who accepts it, any conversion, sinks, loss/theft/death rules, and behavior while participants are absent. An infinite merchant must not buy arbitrary invented output at a fixed reward. New money, resource renewal and item destruction require a connected accounting story. This proposal supplies no real-money exchange, billing, paid entitlement or creator revenue.

The example pouch is an inexpensive object under current rules. Making the smallest woven pouch from raw sources takes 300 game seconds, **five normal real seconds of native work**, excluding travel, discovery, commands, reading and actual character decisions. The reference larger bag takes fifteen such seconds. Bags organize things; current actors have no total carrying-load cap. A bag is not secretly a carrying-strength upgrade. [Current-source calculations](../worlds/base/durable-agreements.md#actual-production-economics).

These facts weaken a commission justified only by manufacturing delay. The proposed value must come from wanted making, available materials or techniques, different routes, less duplicate preparation, or the pleasure of cooperating with this particular person. A two-minute negotiation can cost much more real time and food than making the item. Offer the short exact review; do not force players through repeated bargaining turns.

Prices are exact mutually chosen quantities, not a built-in appraisal of fairness. The world must not add scarcity, slow recipes or withhold a basic technique simply to create a market for this feature. A resident can reasonably prefer making something independently, accepting a gift, or doing neither. If those simpler options dominate in the actual scene, keep immediate barter and postpone durable commissions.

## 11. Attention, cost and scalability

An accepted agreement is sparse durable meaning, not an instruction to think about every contract every tick. Native receipt evidence and due-time transitions can update the relevant record through existing owners. A character reconsiders when it is actually useful: a received proposal, a material change they know about, a due reminder, a meeting or a chosen review. A deadline does not create recurring paid reminders until somebody obeys.

The initial surface is the participant's own agreements and known counterpart interactions. CCP’s historical contract-search account shows how discovery can itself become a significant load and usability problem; it is not a capacity forecast for this game. [DA-R06](#da-r06--discovery-adds-product-and-operating-costs). No global auction house, live ranking, personalized route search, automatic price history or world-wide stock scan is selected. Closed history uses the current bounded-reading direction. It must remain reachable and honestly partial if storage is unavailable; an output page does not prove the prior search and hydration were bounded.

Active undertaking capacity, pending proposals and one pending amendment are deliberately finite. Historical transfers, ended undertakings, unreleased claims and memberships have no new total-retention cap. Repeated invitations, disputes and refinements can still grow events and storage; many users can grow recipient inboxes even when each sender has a limit. [DA limits](../limits/durable-agreements.md) records that exposure and the existing queue, memory, privacy and persistence owners.

Known work should use existing exact transaction and commitment paths. A general legal interpreter, organization simulator or market infrastructure is unnecessary. Generated language can explain or propose terms; only actual authorized assent and effects change the world. Missing cognition or budget leaves a proposal unanswered, not automatically accepted or secretly rerouted to another provider.

## 12. Complete journeys and boundaries

| Situation                      | Required product result                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| A wanted small commission      | Both people inspect and accept exact terms, follow separate choices, return and exchange actual matching goods. The result supports an activity they wanted. |
| Maker chooses another task     | The promise remains evidence; no compulsory craft action or invented output. A later delay or withdrawal is understandable.                                  |
| Return in another session      | Known terms and outstanding work remain readable; no new record is created merely by opening the Journal. No stopped-time work is fabricated.                |
| Goods were consumed or changed | The later exact exchange refuses or requires new assent. No private stock/location is disclosed to justify it.                                               |
| Deadline passes                | The known agreement becomes overdue, without automatic blame, fine, travel or forced work. Late settlement and ending remain possible.                       |
| New price proposed             | Prior terms remain effective until all affected parties accept the exact amendment. Declining changes no custody.                                            |
| Counterpart never returns      | End unfulfilled retires active scheduling while preserving transfers and remaining claims. Ordinary play and future planning remain available.               |
| Advance spent                  | Loss is real; cancellation does not refund it. Later repayment, waiver and dispute remain distinct outcomes.                                                 |
| Two matching commissions       | A real delivery is credited only once, to the explicitly selected agreement.                                                                                 |
| Duplicate or uncertain request | Reconcile the authoritative receipt before another transfer; a panel refresh or model retry cannot duplicate value.                                          |
| Human death or NPC loss        | Stable identities and actual custody remain controlling. No inherited debt, private death disclosure, automatic revival or remote seizure.                   |
| Leave an association           | Membership ends independently; original objects, claims and separately granted access retain their actual state and supported revocation route.              |
| No compelling economic need    | Keep current gifts and independently delivered barter useful; do not make a commission, currency or institution mandatory.                                   |

Delivery order is: existing PX02 exact immediate barter; the selected commission and D64 management meanings; separately selected advance; separately selected association. All enabled capabilities must include their actual privacy, interruption, time, durable history and failure behavior. Advanced economy scope is not a gate for the first useful transaction.

## 13. Research

These records distinguish documented behavior from the design inference drawn from it. Historical announcements establish what their authors described at that date, not current balance or demonstrated player enjoyment. Access limitations are stated where the available evidence was an indexed official extract or an abstract. All sources were retrieved on **2026-10-06**.

### DA-R01 — Exact exchange does not require currency

**Source:** CCP Games, Lead GM Carbon, [Item Exchange Contract](https://support.eveonline.com/hc/en-us/articles/206758389-Item-Exchange-Contract), updated March 1, 2024; retrieved October 6, 2026.

**Supported finding:** EVE item-exchange contracts can request ISK or other items. The issuer selects goods, requested item types and quantities, recipient scope, and reviews the contract before issuing it. Requested goods have eligibility restrictions, including repackaged items in the contract station.

**Open Legend inference:** Durable exchange can begin with goods. The terms must distinguish an exact existing object from any future item satisfying an accepted definition and quantity.

**Caveat:** EVE's packaging restrictions solve its own exchange problem. Open Legend must preserve relevant differences between made objects; a shared display name cannot establish equivalence.

### DA-R02 — Overdue and failed are different outcomes

**Source:** CCP Games, Lead GM Carbon, [Courier Contracts](https://support.eveonline.com/hc/en-us/articles/203218982-Courier-Contracts), updated August 23, 2021; retrieved October 6, 2026.

**Supported finding:** EVE couriers receive an actual package and post collateral. Success returns collateral; failure transfers it to the issuer. Passing the deadline leaves the contract in progress until the issuer explicitly fails it, allowing late delivery meanwhile. The rules also address destination destruction or removal.

**Open Legend inference:** Separate overdue, ended unfulfilled, late fulfillment and disputed explanation. A lost meeting place requires an understandable next choice.

**Caveat:** EVE's collateral and package rules are particular enforcement choices. Requiring capital or automatic forfeiture would add a substantial first-commission burden; this proposal does not import them.

### DA-R03 — Completion needs an actual result and suitable access

**Source:** Strange Loop Games, John Krajewski, [We're excited to announce Eco Alpha 5.5 is now available!](https://www.moddb.com/games/eco-global-survival-game/news/were-excited-to-announce-eco-alpha-55-is-now-available), March 23, 2017; retrieved October 6, 2026.

**Supported finding:** The developer described delivery contracts that recognized requested goods delivered to a depot and moved them into private storage. Construction contracts required a specified working table at a location and gave the contractor temporary building rights. Payments and deposits were held for the documented success or failure outcomes.

**Open Legend inference:** Recognize actual delivery and grant only the access needed for a selected contribution. Promising work does not prove completion.

**Caveat:** This historical developer post is hosted by ModDB. Its currency and held-payment mechanisms do not establish that Open Legend needs escrow or corporate ownership.

### DA-R04 — Cooperation should arise from wanted work

**Source:** Strange Loop Games, John Krajewski, [Developer Blog: Work Parties](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/382310/view/3559472394008716149), March 17, 2020; [readable official announcement feed](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=382310&enddate=1586284194&feed=steam_community_announcements); retrieved October 6, 2026.

**Supported finding:** Eco's work-party announcement describes jobs arising from citizens' actual needs, visible work and payment terms, fixed posted settings, contribution access, and joining while the issuer is offline. Payment can follow contribution at completion or occur as work proceeds.

**Open Legend inference:** Durable agreements earn their place by supporting wanted activities across separate attendance. Make accepted terms and remaining obligations readable without constant organizer attention.

**Caveat:** The designer's expected social benefits are not measured player outcomes. Weighted wages, employment hierarchies and automated labor are separate choices, not prerequisites for a small commission.

### DA-R05 — Small groups need clear permissions, not a corporate hierarchy

**Source:** CCP Games, CCP Greyscale, [Corp Hangars on ships and You](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/corp-hangars-and-you), November 30, 2012; retrieved October 6, 2026.

**Supported finding:** CCP described corporation-role access to ship storage as overly complex for most uses, inconsistent, and confusing about ownership. The announced replacement removed divisions and role dependence in favor of fleet and corporation access controls remembered per ship. The post acknowledged lost capability for uncommon uses.

**Open Legend inference:** Keep membership, custody, declared ownership and withdrawal permission distinct. A named small association should not require a role hierarchy or silently confer access.

**Caveat:** This is a historical, ship-specific simplification. It supports scrutinizing unnecessary controls, not omitting permissions that a real sharing activity needs.

### DA-R06 — Discovery adds product and operating costs

**Source:** CCP Games, CCP Atlas, [Contracts Improvements](https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/contracts-improvements-1), December 27, 2010; retrieved October 6, 2026.

**Supported finding:** CCP reported contract lookup load concentrating around Jita rather than distributing as expected. The redesign addressed page-local sorting and generic result rows with full-result sorting and contract-specific columns. It also explained the cost of personalized distance sorting and bounded returned results.

**Open Legend inference:** Begin with a participant's known agreements and relevant summaries. A global marketplace, route-aware search or live price directory deserves its own demonstrated purpose and cost assessment.

**Caveat:** EVE's historical infrastructure and limits are not Open Legend performance evidence. The transferable question is whether discovering deals helps enough wanted play to justify the added system.

### DA-R07 — Unreserved offers must acknowledge changed goods

**Source:** Valve, [Steam Trade Offers](https://help.steampowered.com/en/faqs/view/1115-91C5-050C-1D60), undated support article; retrieved October 6, 2026.

**Supported finding:** The indexed official article describes asynchronous acceptance, decline and counteroffers. Offered items remain in inventory until acceptance; multiple offers may involve the same goods. Trading or selling those goods makes incompatible pending offers inactive. Pending offers can be cancelled, and a counteroffer is a new proposal.

**Open Legend inference:** State whether goods are reserved, check actual current lots at settlement, and obtain assent to changed terms. A durable intention must not promise present stock.

**Caveat:** The direct page returned a shell; this record uses the indexed official text. Remote digital-item transfer does not justify remote physical delivery or private-inventory disclosure in Open Legend.

### DA-R08 — Money and item flows need a connected purpose

**Source:** Sandbox Interactive, Enya, [Introducing the Black Market](https://albiononline.com/news/video-black-market-feature), May 31, 2017; retrieved October 6, 2026.

**Supported finding:** The official indexed announcement describes a merchant purchasing player-crafted equipment and supplying that equipment as creature and chest loot. Its purchase budget draws on silver otherwise allocated to creature drops, connecting the buyer's demand to another existing game flow.

**Open Legend inference:** Any future currency or repeatable buyer needs an account of what creates demand, where payment comes from, and where acquired goods go. Paying indefinitely for unwanted output is not a sufficient economy design.

**Caveat:** Direct access was restricted; evidence is limited to indexed official extracts. No claim about current rates, inflation control, profitability or player satisfaction follows.

### DA-R09 — A promise can matter beyond its financial penalty

**Source:** Christoph Vanberg, [Why Do People Keep Their Promises? An Experimental Test of Two Explanations](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.3982/ECTA7673), _Econometrica_ 76(6), 1467–1480; first published November 24, 2008; retrieved October 6, 2026.

**Supported finding:** The publisher abstract describes an experiment distinguishing a preference for keeping one's word from an explanation based only on others' payoff expectations. The author reports that expectations alone do not explain the promise effect.

**Open Legend inference:** Preserve who promised what, actual conduct, and each person's attributed interpretation. An automatic monetary penalty or universal reputation score is not required to make a promise legible.

**Caveat:** This record relies on the abstract. A human laboratory result does not validate AI character behavior, establish Open Legend enjoyment, or specify how strongly any resident should value a promise.

## 14. Critique and retained ambition

The strongest reason to retain a deal is that two people will otherwise lose a useful arrangement while doing separate things. The weakest reason is that the simulation “needs an economy.” This design chooses the former, and explicitly confronts how quickly current small goods can be made.

The main simplifications are no default deadline, no currency, no material reservation while merely promising, fresh exact consent at exchange, and a clear unfulfilled ending. These preserve the possibility of a broken promise without turning the record into forced work or permanent planning-slot loss. Actual default can matter through goods already lost and how people interpret it.

A named association earns delivery only when repeated coordination needs a durable social fact. It must not hide the absence of ordinary shared-property powers behind a convincing group panel. An advance earns delivery only when somebody wants to take that specific risk. Neither extension should be required to qualify the first two-person commission.

The fuller ambition remains: useful specialization, invented goods, longer journeys, shared places, voluntary economic organization and norms that develop through play. Achieve those by selecting real consumers as they become valuable. Automatic markets, obligations and procedures are not substitutes for people having reasons to cooperate.
