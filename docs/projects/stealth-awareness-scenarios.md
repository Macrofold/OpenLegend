## Authority and reading rules

These traces exercise the [shared contracts](stealth-awareness-tech-design.md) and [J01–J11 journeys](stealth-awareness-feature-spec.md#representative-journeys). They are acceptance specifications, not reports of executed gameplay. [SAI-I](../maintainers/stealth-awareness.md#future-implementation-dependency-graph) owns combined delivery; each [focused tracker](../maintainers/stealth-awareness.md#specialist-design-ownership) owns its producer and consumer checks.

A is evidence/recognition; B vision/gaze; C posture/acoustics; D bodily effects/appraisals; E attention/actions; F preparedness/contact; G player projection. Their paired designs and world policies own the detailed algorithms. The twelve numbered aspects below always mean world, perception, evidence, recognition, preparedness, choices, admission, emotion/body, combat, player disclosure, persistence and play value.

Let τ denote elapsed **normal-speed seconds**, converted once to simulation time (60 simulation seconds per τ in the base world). B's canonical dynamic visual cells are 0.05 τ with fixed epoch; actual native changes split cells without rephasing. Capture/view requests introduce no extra sensing samples. A held value keeps its actual measurement and acquisition times; availability is separate. Every attack uses already acquired evidence, actual work and complete mechanics at its own immutable cut. Unknown, unsupported and incomplete are distinct; required incomplete work holds the coherent native prefix before an outcome.

A fixture that states a cue was acquired must satisfy its installed physical policy; the scenario does not grant that result from prose. Check both the stated positive condition and its specified negative contrast. Ordinary player interpretation never selects an SA-D01 alternative by implication.

## A — Quietly approaching a hare

1. **World:** An awake hare and a crouched player occupy supported ground, initially separated by opaque cover. The player chooses a quiet route across authored soft terrain.
2. **Perception:** C uses actual admitted travel/contact, terrain and carried equipment; B evaluates each body's installed visual rig, cover and light at due boundaries.
3. **Evidence:** The hare may acquire weak anonymous rustling without visual body detail. No detection is reported only after the required evaluation completes.
4. **Recognition:** Ordinary hare reception needs no human memory or identity association. The player knows only the hare detail their own sensing supplies.
5. **Preparedness:** Hearing routine rustle alone grants neither broad assault expectation nor an invisible danger meter. Actual native alarm/alert state has its own supported scope.
6. **Choices:** The player can stop, continue, change route, put down noisy cargo or choose an admitted attack. The hare follows its installed native policy.
7. **Admission:** Crouch must fit real geometry; movement uses the minimum applicable quiet/posture/direction cap. Entering clear sight may trigger native escape through ordinary navigation.
8. **Emotion/body:** Quiet approach creates no automatic psychological fear. If a later cue qualifies for bodily shock, D applies it to that life once.
9. **Combat:** Stalking does not guarantee surprise or a hit. F evaluates the hare's actual pre-outcome warning/preparation; damage follows ordinary reach/contact/protection.
10. **Player:** G shows accepted posture, useful own-noise feedback and permitted hare behavior; no hare detection progress or private alarm state is sent.
11. **Persistence:** Save pose, actual movement/stride progress, exposure support and native response continuation. Reload neither repeats a footstep nor erases an earned warning.
12. **Play value:** Route, load, light and patience offer different tradeoffs. Failure can become pursuit or a changed hunt rather than a scripted stealth failure.

## B — A hare hears without seeing the player

1. **World:** The player speaks behind opaque cover; the physical hearing path transmits a cue that the hare's installed policy can actually acquire.
2. **Perception:** C evaluates distance, barriers, background and receiver eligibility. B supplies no sight through the cover; sound does not require visual reception.
3. **Evidence:** A admits the received voice-like class and any supported bearing/uncertainty. It supplies no words to a language-disabled hare, human identity or exact emitter position.
4. **Recognition:** The hare's native cue remains anonymous. Internal source IDs used for propagation cannot become personal association or visible-body references.
5. **Preparedness:** C evaluates alarm eligibility before pattern-repeat handling. A repeated eligible alarm remains ineligible for curiosity even when a new alarm transition is suppressed.
6. **Choices:** The ordinary hare's native profile can escape or settle; it does not buy model cognition or acquire the optional ground-curiosity profile.
7. **Admission:** The existing controller selects safe movement from permitted danger information. An unlocalized cue uses its existing unknown-danger policy; blocked escape is reported truthfully.
8. **Emotion/body:** Native alarm is not a psychological fear appraisal. Any separate abrupt-sound bodily qualifier is evaluated from the received signal and exact onset receipt.
9. **Combat:** Hearing voice alone does not establish an attack. Later contact receives the hare's own preparation cut, without inventing the player's target or intention.
10. **Player:** The player sees escape only if their own senses permit it. A hidden hare's route, refuge and private alarm do not appear in the view.
11. **Persistence:** Preserve the original cue, independent pattern dispositions, idle expiries, exact life and actual movement. Restore cannot replay alarm onset or redraw refuge randomness.
12. **Play value:** A physically carried warning can matter without naming its source. A soft unclassified footstep provides the negative contrast: detection need not cause alarm.

## C — A noisy armored traveler

1. **World:** A traveler carrying authored rattling equipment walks through foliage, then stops. Equipment and terrain have installed physical-sound profiles rather than descriptive-only labels.
2. **Perception:** C derives contact and activity emissions from actual displacement/work, retained stride state and the affected materials; overlapping physical sources contribute under its acoustic law.
3. **Evidence:** Two listeners on different paths acquire different level, coarse class and localization. A rustle/rattle hypothesis need not identify armor, species or a person.
4. **Recognition:** Familiarity is considered only from an acquired identity-capable signature. Generic equipment noise cannot match the hidden wearer's remembered identity.
5. **Preparedness:** A listener can attend to the sound or deliberately prepare a relevant approach. Mere loudness supplies no universal combat warning.
6. **Choices:** The traveler may slow, stop, choose another surface or put down cargo. A muffling technique works only if its ordinary material/action family is installed.
7. **Admission:** Apply the actual movement caps and equipment constraints. No animation footfall fires while stationary; accepted action sources can continue if their work actually continues.
8. **Emotion/body:** D consumes only an eligible abrupt acoustic transition, not every loud sample or repeated publication. Ordinary concern remains optional supported interpretation.
9. **Combat:** Noise changes opportunities to prepare, not an independent damage penalty. An actual attack still uses F's scoped warning and preparation rules.
10. **Player:** Own movement-noise feedback explains an actionable tradeoff without revealing which hidden listeners heard it. Listeners receive only their own uncertain cues.
11. **Persistence:** Save actual source/contact progress, admitted movement and exposure accumulation. Pause, host partition and reload do not add strides or duplicate overlapping energy.
12. **Play value:** The same useful cargo becomes a situational burden. Changing load or route can preserve success without requiring a separate stealth statistic.

## D — Footsteps behind a wall

1. **World:** A walker crosses behind an opaque wall with finite sound transmission. The listener is working outside and has no current visual support for the walker.
2. **Perception:** C's direct physical path may permit footsteps while B denies body detail. A sound-blocking contrast permits neither; missing evaluation cannot be treated as that contrast.
3. **Evidence:** The listener receives anonymous contact-like sound with the acquired bearing/region precision, or no localization. Hidden exact coordinates never accompany the cue.
4. **Recognition:** Retain the actual heard time and uncertainty. Neither an old acquaintance nor a matching imagined explanation identifies the source automatically.
5. **Preparedness:** Optional attention is separate from hearing. A deliberate doorway watch can establish only its actual maintained coverage and earned lead.
6. **Choices:** Continue work, turn, listen, investigate the permitted region, warn another person or leave. Hearing cannot force a reasoning-capable person to turn.
7. **Admission:** Investigation binds a received region or a separately chosen permitted reachable vantage. A bearing without range stays a bearing; no default distance or hidden source supplies a destination. Blocked travel preserves partial work without revealing the obstruction.
8. **Emotion/body:** A supported appraisal may express concern; no fear is mechanically fabricated merely because the cue is unfamiliar. Native work continues without a model.
9. **Combat:** Footsteps alone are not a directed-attack warning. A later understood grounded warning or actual relevant guard can separately alter preparedness.
10. **Player:** G renders heard direction/uncertainty and available responses without a silhouette, name, equipment or attackable person at the wall.
11. **Persistence:** Retain the original acoustic fact and any chosen investigation's deadline, work identity and findings. A later silence does not rewrite the prior event.
12. **Play value:** Investigation has useful alternatives. An empty inspected area can leave a mystery, rather than proving the walker never existed or remains there.

## E — Recognizing Ada by voice

1. **World:** Ada speaks behind a wall. The listener has eligible prior acquaintance and voice familiarity; another listener has never heard her.
2. **Perception:** Each listener's physical path determines actual intelligibility, signature quality and localization. Recognition does not bypass a blocked hearing path.
3. **Evidence:** A separately admits words, voice signature, association and location where supported. Good recognition can coexist with a broad or absent bearing.
4. **Recognition:** Apply A's familiarity/quality/Perception policy. The familiar listener may recognize the remembered individual; the stranger gets an anonymous exposure. Name knowledge is separate.
5. **Preparedness:** Recognition alone establishes no threat or preparation. An actually understood, timely grounded danger clause can independently enter F's warning registration.
6. **Choices:** Reply, call her name if known, ask for clarification, approach a known place or continue work. No response is compulsory.
7. **Admission:** Speech and movement use ordinary authority and physical checks. A hidden Ada reference permits remembered discussion, not exact live targeting or following.
8. **Emotion/body:** NPC interpretation may use the actual recognized voice; affection, fear or relief remains private supported appraisal. SA-D01 controls any player psychological policy.
9. **Combat:** Familiarity neither prevents betrayal surprise nor proves hostility. Only independently qualifying warning or actual preparation changes F's result.
10. **Player:** G may label the heard speaker with the permitted remembered identity/name while keeping unseen pose, equipment and exact position absent.
11. **Persistence:** Keep per-listener source revisions, original word gaps, acquired association and familiarity support. Replay/recall cannot manufacture extra meetings or learning.
12. **Play value:** Knowing someone changes what a sound means without becoming an omniscient tracking ability. Uncertainty can motivate a conversation or a cautious check.

## F — An old spear observation and a fresh voice

1. **World:** At τ=0 the listener sees Ada holding a spear. Ada leaves sight and changes equipment; at τ=10 her voice is heard through cover.
2. **Perception:** The later acoustic signal may support voice recognition, but no sight reaches Ada's new equipment or current activity.
3. **Evidence:** The spear fact keeps its original measured/acquired time and source. The voice is a separate τ=10 fact with its own uncertainty and revision.
4. **Recognition:** A may associate the new voice with the same remembered individual. That association does not confirm the spear, earlier location, heading or bodily condition.
5. **Preparedness:** Old spear knowledge can inform a chosen concern; it is not a fresh attack warning. A finite actual guard must earn its own readiness.
6. **Choices:** Ask Ada, inspect the approach, recall the old observation, warn with explicit uncertainty or ignore it. Generated certainty cannot upgrade the evidence.
7. **Admission:** Historical inspect is allowed by its current source scope. A direct attack/follow offer requires fresh permitted physical binding and ordinary admission.
8. **Emotion/body:** An NPC can retain or reassess an appraisal supported by the old cause; later voice recognition cannot renew its original lifetime by itself.
9. **Combat:** F sees only actual eligible warning/preparation. It does not infer the present weapon, new attack capability or threat from a stale equipment fact.
10. **Player:** Show “Last seen” for the spear observation and separately “Last heard” for voice. A historical figure remains frozen and noninteractive as a live person.
11. **Persistence:** Store both original facts and lineage separately. Correction/forgetting invalidates dependent interpretations and projections, including late in-flight reads, without erasing committed physical harm.
12. **Play value:** Personal memory helps planning while allowing people to change unseen. Asking or checking matters because familiarity does not automatically refresh the world.

## G — Walking while looking behind

1. **World:** A traveler chooses a destination and a finite rearward watch while crossing supported ground. Body heading, travel direction, gaze and camera are distinct.
2. **Perception:** B uses the actual admitted sensory rig and turning limits. Rearward focus is relative to supported travel, with real alignment gaps at corners. Beyond head/eye limits, preview actual body assistance and its movement/guard consequences; otherwise offer an explicitly accepted limited result or refuse unchanged.
3. **Evidence:** Only due completed physical samples enter A. Current body detail remains measured, and looking/capture/view requests cannot generate bonus samples or familiarity.
4. **Recognition:** Seen people use ordinary recognition; a hidden former follower is only remembered. A relative watch cannot track that person's unseen route.
5. **Preparedness:** E records actually fulfilled scope. Earned lead starts at fulfillment for observation, not from the submitted destination or intention age.
6. **Choices:** Submit one reviewed Walk while watching request, amend compatible travel, stop the watch, change focus or cancel travel through ordinary controls.
7. **Admission:** Initial movement/watch combination commits atomically or refuses unchanged. Directional/quiet/crouch speed caps combine by minimum; gaze does not add a new action lane.
8. **Emotion/body:** Existing applicable effects constrain actual capabilities. Fear creates no automatic run slowdown; a lost pose requirement interrupts the relevant facet under E's rule.
9. **Combat:** Preparation covers only fulfilled relevant approaches. A route corner, incompatible action or ended watch can remove that coverage without resetting independent registered warning.
10. **Player:** G distinguishes requested, aligning, maintained, blocked and ended work. Camera orbit stays independent; other subjects remain at permitted measured values.
11. **Persistence:** Save destination, work/facet identity, original deadline, actual pose and method anchors. Restore does not restart lead, extend the watch or create another movement.
12. **Play value:** A traveler can watch a pursuer while making progress. The cost is physical coverage and movement tradeoffs, not repetitive menu work or magical all-direction vigilance.

## H — Guarding an entrance against an unseen attacker

1. **World:** At τ=0 a defender starts a valid shield guard aimed at a known doorway. The hidden attacker approaches through that guard's actual coverage.
2. **Perception:** Darkness/cover can prevent visual attacker evidence. The defender's own body, equipment and accepted guard state remain available through their owners.
3. **Evidence:** There need be no attacker identity or incoming attack fact before contact. The cut includes the defender's genuine active preparation and permitted doorway scope.
4. **Recognition:** Knowledge of the place can ground preparation without knowing a person. No hidden entrant is added to a subject pad or target table.
5. **Preparedness:** With method start τ=0, deliberate lead 0.20 and native shield readyAt=0.25, readiness is τ=0.25, not 0.45; current coverage and expiry still apply.
6. **Choices:** Maintain, redirect, stop or choose other work. A spoken request to guard counts only after the defender voluntarily accepts and the work actually begins.
7. **Admission:** Exact shield, body life, pose and normal equipment/resource checks bind the method. Approaching the position before τ=0 does not earn shield lead.
8. **Emotion/body:** A distinct qualifying abrupt sound can still startle a prepared defender. Expected guarded contact alone does not qualify as unexpected-contact shock; preparedness and emotional state remain separate.
9. **Combat:** A covered contact after readiness avoids surprise even if defense fails. Contact before readiness, outside coverage or exactly at ended work needs other eligible preparation.
10. **Player:** Show the defender's accepted guard and ordinary result, without previewing the hidden attacker's path, readiness or guaranteed surprise outcome.
11. **Persistence:** Preserve actual method start, ready/expiry boundaries, exact work/shield/life and any independent warning. Harmless revisions do not restart preparation.
12. **Play value:** Position and anticipation can protect against an unknown foe. Timing matters once; charging native guard preparation twice would make an honest action misleading.

## I — Darkness and firelight

1. **World:** An attacker moves from darkness through a firelit opening and behind cover. Actual world light/material profiles, not display brightness, determine optical input.
2. **Perception:** B samples affected dynamic scopes on fixed 0.05 τ cells and real native changes; conservative sweeps include supported interior geometric crossings.
3. **Evidence:** A acquires only detail whose sampled exposure/detection law qualifies. A brief unsupported subcell optical flash may be missed or held under the declared fidelity limit.
4. **Recognition:** A dim silhouette can be detected without recognition or equipment detail. Reacquisition needs actual correspondence/recognition, not hidden entity equality across ambiguity.
5. **Preparedness:** An acquired threatening configuration/motion may warn; a mere silhouette need not. A chosen guard can remain relevant after visual support ends.
6. **Choices:** Wait, move the light through an admitted action, use another route, observe the opening or continue. Renderer quality settings confer no sensing benefit.
7. **Admission:** Existing movement and light-source owners commit actual changes. Firelight does not remove physical cover or grant attacks through solid material.
8. **Emotion/body:** Darkness creates no automatic fear. A supported NPC appraisal or distinct eligible sudden cue follows D's existing sources and mechanical boundaries.
9. **Combat:** F captures already acquired warning and actual preparation. Impact cannot request an extra sight sample to retroactively discover the approaching attacker.
10. **Player:** G holds permitted measured body/detail values and removes them when support ends. Hidden light/source identity or live transforms never travel with a visual convenience cue.
11. **Persistence:** Save sampling anchors, pending intervals, measured/acquired times and detection progress. Shared daylight is sampled at its own one-second normal cadence without rephasing on load.
12. **Play value:** Lighting creates usable routes and observation windows. Qualification must expose the bounded temporal approximation honestly, including repeated aliasing; no continuous-optics precision is promised.

## J — A fast expected attack that cannot be dodged

1. **World:** A restrained but awake defender acquires an actual directed-attack warning at τ=0. The strike reaches them at τ=0.30.
2. **Perception:** The body remains eligible for general perception throughout registration, even though locomotion/evasion is unavailable. Its actual senses determine the warning.
3. **Evidence:** A records original occurrence, complete meaning and actual acquisition. F receives that personally acquired directed threat, without substituting a model's prediction.
4. **Recognition:** The attacker can remain unidentified. Recognition is unnecessary for the finite ordinary self-assault expectation earned by that warning.
5. **Preparedness:** Native registration matures at τ=0.20 without requiring movement, cognition or an available chosen action. The τ=0.30 cut therefore contains relevant expectation.
6. **Choices:** The defender may speak or attempt an actually available response. A menu cannot offer successful evasion merely because the threat was noticed.
7. **Admission:** Evasion refuses its concrete physical prerequisite without costs. No forced dodge, hidden relocation or shortened action replaces that refusal.
8. **Emotion/body:** Existing fear does not define surprise. Expected contact can cause actual injury/discomfort after resolution; startle requires a separate qualifying cue. Optional interpretation can arrive later.
9. **Combat:** Ordinary hit/protection/injury applies without the surprise multiplier. A failed or impossible defense does not reverse already earned anticipation.
10. **Player:** Show the noticed threat, actual blocked response and permitted result. Do not describe the character as surprised simply because they were hit.
11. **Persistence:** Preserve warning acquisition, registration deadline and exact body restrictions. Saving during the lead retains remaining simulation time rather than charging a fresh lead.
12. **Play value:** Awareness and physical power have different consequences. An unavoidable hit remains understandable when the system explains why it could not be avoided; player fairness still needs qualification.

## K — Sudden betrayal by a familiar companion

1. **World:** A known companion stands visibly nearby, then chooses an attack. The victim has no matching deliberate preparation or registered threat.
2. **Perception:** B may expose ordinary face, body and held equipment. The companion's private selected target, hostile intent and plan never become visual facts by themselves.
3. **Evidence:** Only actual readable preparation or received motion can warn. In the negative fixture, the forearm/weapon preparation is physically occluded while the familiar face remains visible; only the late approach becomes readable, leaving less than 0.20 τ after complete warning acquisition. Required sensing is never delayed to obtain this result.
4. **Recognition:** Familiarity establishes who is present, not that their next action is safe or anticipated. Earlier trust remains attributed personal meaning.
5. **Preparedness:** F checks actual scoped support; no general “can see attacker” exemption applies. The same attack with early unoccluded preparation is the positive no-surprise contrast; a fully readable ordinary wind-up will often warn early enough.
6. **Choices:** Before contact, actual warning can offer guard, retreat, speech or continued work. AI response latency is not substituted for the native registration law.
7. **Admission:** The attacker passes ordinary weapon, reach, control and review rules. The victim's late proposed guard grants nothing unless actual readiness is achieved.
8. **Emotion/body:** Unexpected contact can commit native startle once, followed by independently admitted NPC fear/anger or later reassessment. Human feelings are never inferred.
9. **Combat:** The first designated victim-life outcome may apply 2× nominal direct injury before protection. A block or miss still spends that attack's opportunity.
10. **Player:** Show only perceived attack/result and own bodily feedback. No advance betrayal label, enemy color change or hidden readiness score announces the companion's intention.
11. **Persistence:** Preserve exact contact/cut, cause, victim life, spent opportunity and personal aftermath. Later accusation or trust change cites acquired evidence, not a global shared account.
12. **Play value:** Relationships matter without granting immunity. Readable physical commitment still creates counterplay, so betrayal is not an automatic scripted hit.

## L — A projectile from an unseen archer

1. **World:** A released projectile travels from an unseen archer toward a victim. Its actual flight and contact exist independently of the archer's later life or participation.
2. **Perception:** B's ordinary due samples may expose the projectile itself. Merely being in camera view or intersecting the victim later does not establish prior sensing.
3. **Evidence:** A's observed-motion requires two ordered, completed location samples of one unambiguous permitted exposure, with original poses, uncertainty and actual acquisition times. One point or bearing is insufficient.
4. **Recognition:** No archer identity, private target or hidden velocity accompanies those samples. A can retain an anonymous physical object even when it cannot recognize its maker.
5. **Preparedness:** F derives conservative closing/intersection from the two received positions and their uncertainty; separately acquired form may refine support without becoming an extra mandatory gate. Registration starts when the complete qualifying meaning is actually acquired, never at the first sample.
6. **Choices:** The victim can attempt an admitted guard/evasion or continue. A native warning registration is independent of whether a model chooses before impact.
7. **Admission:** Evasion requires a currently valid destination/reach and actual time to execute. Seeing an arrow creates no instantaneous dodge or trajectory rewrite.
8. **Emotion/body:** Visual flight warning alone is not an initial D shock producer. A separate qualifying acoustic cue or unexpected contact can cause native startle under its own cause.
9. **Combat:** If the second qualifying sample is acquired at τ=0.05 and contact is at 0.30, eligible registration can mature at 0.25. Contact at 0.10 is too early without other preparation. These are conditional timing fixtures, not a claim that every base arrow is visible for that long.
10. **Player:** G may show the projectile only at permitted measured detail; a heard cue gives no flight mesh. No trace reveals its hidden origin or future path.
11. **Persistence:** Save actual flight, latest two eligible exposure samples, warning acquisition and registration. A captured cut remains immutable; restore cannot generate an extra sample or earlier warning.
12. **Play value:** A visible approach can provide real counterplay, while fast or concealed shots can still surprise. The test distinguishes sensory warning, anticipation and successful defense.

## M — Temporary loss of sight during an anticipated fight

1. **World:** Two opponents exchange an actual attack. One then moves behind cover and around the defender before attacking again within the ongoing encounter.
2. **Perception:** Current visual detail ends at B's provider-defined loss. Actual sound or later visual reacquisition can supply new evidence, but the hidden route is unavailable.
3. **Evidence:** The defender retains already acquired attack warning with its own time, life and scope. Current position and pose are separate and may be unknown.
4. **Recognition:** A remembered opponent can remain known while their body is untargetable. Reappearance needs permitted physical correspondence or actual recognition, not an omniscient ID join.
5. **Preparedness:** Mature matching expectation persists under F's six-second retention and two-second quiet settling rules; suitable actual support may renew only its declared scope.
6. **Choices:** Guard an approach, retreat, call for help, search or continue other work. A private “keep fighting” sentence does not preserve unlimited preparedness.
7. **Admission:** Follow/attack requires its current physical/evidence requirements. Investigating the last known place uses the recorded snapshot, never the opponent's unseen coordinates.
8. **Emotion/body:** Existing fear can persist or change through D. It does not directly determine whether the second attack is surprising or slow ordinary fleeing.
9. **Combat:** A rearward step or brief occlusion alone grants no new surprise bonus. Genuine expired/ended relevant expectation and absent deliberate preparation are needed for a later surprise.
10. **Player:** G removes live hidden detail and may retain a labeled historical observation. It does not animate the opponent through the obstacle or preview the reappearance.
11. **Persistence:** Preserve finite encounter warning/expiry, actual work and source eligibility independently of exposure continuity. Reconnect, Stop or a weapon swap cannot reset the opponent's evidence.
12. **Play value:** Breaking sight creates movement and escape options without becoming a repetitive backstab exploit. Real disengagement can make a later ambush meaningful again.

## N — Several attackers and contacts at the same simulation time

1. **World:** Two independently released attacks reach one victim at the same simulation timestamp; one may hit a collateral body rather than its intended target.
2. **Perception:** Every victim and witness has their own completed acquisitions. Neither a shared sensor cache nor another defender's warning creates personal knowledge.
3. **Evidence:** Native canonical occurrence order and acquired membership determine each cut. Equal timestamps, Awareness insertion sequence or later nested callbacks do not define prior evidence.
4. **Recognition:** One attacker can be known and another anonymous. A warning scoped to one approach is not automatically a named roster of all nearby attackers.
5. **Preparedness:** Match each attack to actual scope. Earlier contact can supply new broad warning, but a new uncredited scope still needs 0.20 τ; zero elapsed time does not mature it.
6. **Choices:** Optional decisions occur after required native commit. No model answer or incidental loop order selects which simultaneous contact is first.
7. **Admission:** Each real attack keeps its own costs, method and life bindings. Guard consumption, interruptions and remaining readiness follow the canonical earlier outcome.
8. **Emotion/body:** Deduplicate each shock by original cause/definition/target life. A new contribution can affect only later declared sampling boundaries, never rewrite an admitted evasion or its own hit.
9. **Combat:** Take separate actual-victim cuts and consume each designated opportunity once. Commit contact/defense/harm within the local publication fence; nested consequences cannot backfill the current cut.
10. **Player:** Publish coherent permitted outcomes. A bystander does not receive private warnings, exact hidden attacker targets or a transient half-committed shield/injury state.
11. **Persistence:** Save canonical order, spent opportunities, actual shield/body consequences and any held required continuation. A finished contact is never recalculated after reload.
12. **Play value:** Several attackers can overwhelm a defense without scheduler luck. Expected-but-unavoidable hits and genuinely unanticipated approaches remain mechanically distinct.

## O — Native startle followed by interpreted fear

1. **World:** A surprising contact or qualifying abrupt acquired sound affects an eligible body. A second delivery of the same cause arrives later through another record wrapper.
2. **Perception:** Contact provides the victim's own bodily consequence even without identifying the source. Acoustic startle requires C's actual received abruptness qualifier, not merely loud source metadata.
3. **Evidence:** Preserve original physical cause or acquired onset/rise receipt and exact target life. Injury, enclosing event and recalled account are not additional onsets.
4. **Recognition:** The character can be startled by an unknown cause. Later recognition can inform interpretation without changing the committed cause or earlier acquisition.
5. **Preparedness:** F evaluated surprise from the pre-contact cut. Newly committed startle cannot make that same attack unexpected or alter its completed defense.
6. **Choices:** The NPC may flee, guard, talk or continue under ordinary capabilities. Native reaction creates no forced chosen speech, goal, turn or movement.
7. **Admission:** D samples supported physiology/Composure and commits its bodily contribution once. Existing admitted paths retain speed/duration; later actions consume current eligible modifiers.
8. **Emotion/body:** The unmodified startle has a three-τ curve. A later supported fear appraisal can outlast it; effective mechanics use D's maximum applicable normalized contribution, not a duplicate-cause sum.
9. **Combat:** Accuracy is sampled at the declared melee-attempt/ranged-release boundary and evasion reach at new admission. Fear adds no second surprise multiplier or automatic running penalty.
10. **Player:** Show own bodily feedback without narrating the human's feelings. Persistent interpreted player mechanics/disclosure remain blocked by SA-D01; NPC interpretation has its selected policy.
11. **Persistence:** Retain cause/no-effect receipts, admitted curve and clock conversion, appraisal source/revision/lifetime and review allowance. Duplicate delivery or reload cannot restart them.
12. **Play value:** Immediate shock and lasting interpretation produce different aftermaths. Composure can matter while the player retains action choice and understandable recovery.

## P — Emotional recovery while hiding, with no new attack

1. **World:** An NPC with a supported fear appraisal hides after an encounter. No fresh attack or external event occurs during the review interval.
2. **Perception:** Existing senses continue normally. Silence or unseen movement is not evidence that the entire area is safe; actual acquired changes remain separate facts.
3. **Evidence:** D prepares the original eligible cause plus complete permitted current context; an already eligible timer does not require a new context change. An internal due opportunity is not a fabricated external event or fresh danger.
4. **Recognition:** Retained personal accounts keep their original times and exact source versions. Recalling the same account cannot manufacture corroboration or learning.
5. **Preparedness:** Encounter retention and chosen guard/watch continue under their independent finite laws. Feeling frightened alone cannot hold all-direction combat expectation open.
6. **Choices:** Bounded emotional review can reduce, resolve, retain or otherwise validly reassess the same cause without deliberate speech, thought narration or a new action choice.
7. **Admission:** ActorWork coalesces the exact due reason; current authority, source/appraisal revisions and finite allowance are checked at dispatch and commit. Failure does not start an automatic retry loop.
8. **Emotion/body:** Retain leaves the curve unchanged. A meaningful valid reassess may change its analytic anchor/current value within the immutable original horizon; no result renews that horizon or refills attempts. Retain, unknown or a submitted failure makes unchanged support dormant immediately; allowance exhaustion also ends further review.
9. **Combat:** A newly admitted attack/evasion uses the actual resulting mechanics. An already admitted action is not edited when review succeeds or a threshold is crossed.
10. **Player:** An ordinary observer sees only actual expression/behavior they perceive, not the NPC's private fear value or model reasoning. Authorized own disclosure uses its selected scope.
11. **Persistence:** Save appraisal value/curve, original cause, revisions, due identity, remaining allowance and ActorMind spacing. Reopening does not purchase another review or renew the emotion.
12. **Play value:** An inhabitant can recover quietly or remain cautious because of their interpretation. Consequences persist without compulsory periodic dialogue or continuous paid cognition.

## Q — AI failure during combat

1. **World:** An action-choice or emotional-review request is pending when a projectile contacts the NPC. The provider fails, returns late or refers to now-revoked evidence.
2. **Perception:** Required physical sensing and actual contact observation continue through native owners. A model request is not a sensory barrier or a source of warning.
3. **Evidence:** The contact cut contains only already acquired facts. A late answer cannot supply a prior observation, earlier action start or retroactive preparedness.
4. **Recognition:** Exact permitted identity/source bindings are rechecked after the wait. A hidden entity ID in a stale proposal grants neither recognition nor current targeting.
