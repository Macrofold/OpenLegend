## Purpose and scope

An inhabitant should be able to spend attention on something worth understanding while the rest of life continues. Hearing footsteps may prompt a question, a glance, a search, a warning, escape, a prepared stance or simply continued work. A builder watching for helpers and a sentry watching an entrance use the same finite, physically supported commitments. Neither requires an omniscient threat list.

This is **SAD-E**, the attention, investigation and active-preparation specialty of the [shared stealth design](stealth-awareness-tech-design.md#shared-contracts). It owns accepted observation work and its continuation through existing action, agency, native-controller and decision-intake owners. The [technical counterpart](attention-and-investigation-tech-design.md) specifies fields, transitions, subscriptions, temporal cuts and implementation order. Perception A owns evidence and recognition; B/C own physical sight, orientation and hearing; D owns bodily shock/private emotion; F owns warning interpretation and final surprise; G owns detailed controls and disclosure.

This pair completes the selected attention portions of [DG02](../maintainers/needs-design.md#dg02--one-resident-who-follows-through), [ND13](../maintainers/needs-design.md#nd13--stable-action-recommendations-and-complete-control-customization) and [ND16](../maintainers/needs-design.md#nd16--hearing-and-communication-beyond-direct-path-speech). It does not complete whole-life behavior, crowd/gist scenes, control customization or wider hearing.

**Design only.** Finite activity execution, goals, action results, interruption, actor intake and native animal alarm already provide foundations. General observation actions, selected simultaneous work, the preparation projection and their complete integration remain implementation work in [ATI01–ATI08](../maintainers/attention-and-investigation.md). No application change, executable test, measured capacity, model-quality result or demonstrated enjoyment is claimed.

A player may review **Walk while watching** once, selecting a permitted destination and finite watch or anticipation terms together. Acceptance starts one compatible walking action with the observation facet and actual orientation. The same facet can be added to an existing compatible move; its original deadline survives a later switch to direct steering. The review exposes movement and pose consequences, and refused/incomplete combinations leave current work intact.

## What should make it compelling

The payoff is a useful choice under partial information, not an obligation to investigate every noise.

- **Attention becomes a contribution.** A timid companion can provide the crucial account from a safe post while someone else takes a more dangerous role. Useful cooperation need not mean fighting, obeying or possessing the best equipment.
- **Work and awareness compete in understandable ways.** A maker can finish a precise operation, pause to check an approach, choose a vantage that permits intermittent checks, or ask a willing partner to watch. Their materials and completed work remain real.
- **Curiosity has satisfying small endings.** Discovering that a strange rhythm comes from a machine, hearing a musician, observing an animal feeding, or deciding an unanswered question is not worth pursuing can finish an activity without a quest reward.
- **Preparation changes options without solving the mystery.** Guarding a doorway can be sensible without identifying who might enter. Searching one route can help locate a companion even if the whole valley remains unknown.
- **Different witnesses matter.** A worker's brief glance, a dedicated lookout's continuing observations and a listener's approximate bearing can complement one another through actual conversation. Shared information still requires reception; private minds are not combined.
- **The environment is part of the solution.** Moving around a wall, stopping a rattling task, changing the approach or waiting for the next real machine cycle can produce better evidence through ordinary rules. No universal investigation skill check supplies hidden truth.

Compare each activity with the capable ordinary alternative: keep walking, look once, continue work, ask a person, use an already known method, take a safe route or leave. Sustained watch should earn its time through better opportunities or useful assurance about a specific interval. It must not become a mandatory vigilance tax before normal travel, social interaction or crafting. Human play must later establish that these benefits are noticeable.

## Four different meanings of attention

| Meaning | What changes | What it cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passive sensory reception | The existing senses acquire physically available evidence, including strong unexpected cues. | An action choice, hidden identity or consent to react. |
| Current cognitive interest | Existing purpose, goals, personal experience and optional context selection make permitted information relevant. | New sensing, body rotation or combat readiness. |
| Deliberate physical observation | An accepted look, scan, listen, inspect or search occupies actual time and compatible body/work resources. | Guaranteed discovery, complete absence or a second physical lane. |
| Deliberate anticipation | An accepted maintained method prepares for a stated approach or danger class within actual physical capability. | Universal alertness, successful defense or a final surprise result. |

A thought such as “Ada might be nearby,” a spoken “I'll keep watch,” a menu preview and a proposed plan are not accepted physical work. Actual admission binds the person, body, supported method, scope, current work and finite ending. A refusal preserves the previous work.

A person's focus may favor relevant evidence without erasing ordinary sensory-eligible history. The rejected [busy-worker observation-loss experiment](../worlds/base/attention.md) is not reinstated. Hearing remains possible while looking closely at a workpiece; strong unexpected evidence does not need to match the chosen inquiry.

## The selected activities

These are ordinary capabilities offered through the same action discovery, exact request and NPC choice owners. They are not a new activity screen or an unrestricted natural-language scripting language.

| Activity | Accepted behavior | Useful ending and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Look/orient once | B turns supported eyes/head/body toward the admitted direction, point or currently observed subject. | Stops at achieved pose or original deadline. Pose can remain; the ended action is not a continuing watch. |
| Scan | A finite sequence of admitted look positions and observation intervals. | Reports the intervals actually checked. Between sectors, old observations remain historical. |
| Listen | Attend for a finite interval using C/A's existing hearing provider, optionally after independently choosing to stop noise or move. | Reports received cues and the method's limited coverage. No extra range, invented polling, word recovery or silent-source guarantee. |
| Inspect | Examine one accessible object, surface, person or supported record using a declared observation method. | Answers only supported questions. Looking at a machine does not disclose hidden mechanisms or install a measurement capability. |
| Monitor/watch | Maintain a chosen observation condition over a subject, region or direction until an admitted end or meaningful condition. | Changes or findings can invite reconsideration; no mandatory pursuit, warning or attack follows. |
| Search/investigate | Compose ordinary approach, examination, supported inquiry and bounded search of permitted scope. | Stop when the chosen question is answered, the accessible frontier is checked, the effort/deadline ends, or the actor stops. |
| Anticipate/guard an approach | Maintain an explicitly selected compatible preparation method and threat scope. | Produces preparation evidence for F. Physical shield defense remains the guard owner's operation; watching alone does not equip or block. |

Investigation is a purpose expressed by existing finite activities, not a new planner. An already admitted conditional method may react natively to a supported condition; a novel destination, a new accusation or an attack still requires the appropriate new choice. Freeform “find out everything” must be narrowed to an executable scope, with the unresolved question retained honestly.

The [world profile](../worlds/base/attention-and-investigation.md) owns initial durations, observation methods and native curiosity policy. The [limits inventory](../limits/attention-and-investigation.md) distinguishes play choices from work quanta and retention. Another world can choose different bodies, senses, methods and deadlines through the same trusted families.

## What is being watched

**Person/object watch** means maintaining observation of a presently permitted physical subject. **Area/direction watch** means examining an admitted spatial scope for something that may appear. **Remembered-person concern** means the actor has a supported association or hypothesis, not that the person's current body is available.

A character who suspects Ada is behind a wall can investigate the historical bearing, watch a known doorway, call out or ask a companion. They cannot bind an invisible Ada as a moving destination or read her current activity. If a watched person disappears, the selected loss rule either ends subject tracking or keeps a fixed last-permitted point/bearing until the original finite end. It does not follow private coordinates or silently reacquire an indistinguishable person.

Each focus has its own supported source, observation time and uncertainty. Several selected concerns may share one watch region; several regions can be checked sequentially. Only the actual current focus supplies maintained scope; past/future scan positions remain historical/planned. Neither multiple concerns nor an old warning multiplies physical gaze. A body with independently steerable sense groups uses only its installed capabilities; human anatomy is not an engine law.

Anonymous danger is valid. “Watch the north doorway for a physical attack” needs no attacker identity. “Watch for Ada” can retain a permitted remembered association while the spatial coverage remains independently defined. “Watch arriving workers” is peaceful monitoring and creates no assumed combat preparation.

## Work, movement and interruption

Accepted observation uses the current primary physical action. A composed inquiry owns its finite control in the activity; a compatible amendment to a direct move/guard can keep the same control on that action without creating or restarting a plan. B supplies actual limited orientation. There is no free background lookout or general parallel-plan executor.

| Ongoing work | Selected coexistence |
| --- | --- |
| Walking a supported route | A compatible head/eye look can preserve route and purpose. Looking far behind can require explicit body-assisted travel with B/C's actual restrictions. If the body cannot look there, refuse or choose a different movement. |
| Speaking/listening | Current instantaneous speech can coexist under its existing rules. It does not become a simulated vocal-duration lock; any actual future sustained voice needs its own declared constraints. Hearing may include self-noise under C. |
| Gathering or precise tool use | Honor that phase's actual required view/pose. An incompatible glance refuses or explicitly interrupts; intermittent checks occur at admitted safe boundaries with honest gaps and unchanged production accounting. |
| Shield guarding | Head-only looking can preserve body/shield orientation. A body turn that would break guard requires ordinary refusal or explicit replacement. A different gaze direction does not rotate protection. |
| Aiming or attacking | Required view, weapon/body use and recovery remain with that action. A watch cannot auto-fire or preserve incompatible coverage during a strike. |
| Sleep, incapacity or unsupported sense loss | End or pause affected observation coverage immediately under the body's existing rules. No listening-based waking is selected. |

Starting observation does not refund materials, elapsed time or attack recovery. A whole-plan pause is available only where the existing activity supports it. Resuming restarts an unfinished physical step where that owner requires restart, keeps completed results and original limits, and establishes a new continuous preparation interval. A compatible glance does not cancel an otherwise independent accepted cooperation agreement.

Being attacked is not an unconditional “stop watching” rule. The actual contact/effect/work owner determines whether the method remains feasible. End coverage that truly loses its required body resources; retain a compatible commitment. The incoming outcome uses its already captured preparation before any impact-induced interruption or fear.

An attached watch's expiry releases only its future maintenance and leaves compatible walking/guard/work running. It must not use a whole-plan deadline that cancels that other work. Expiration does not automatically turn the head back, renew the guard, choose a successor or infer safety. Death/body replacement ends old-life physical commitments. Save/load preserves original deadlines, work, gaps and results without new sensing, new choice or renewed time.

## Choosing a response without repetitive thinking

For a person, a new cue becomes an actor-scoped opportunity. Their actual work, goals, understood evidence, relevant expertise, preferences and existing feelings can influence whether a decision is useful and which alternatives are considered. Native continuation carries out already accepted work without another model call for every footstep, scan segment or predicate check.

Keep at least the meaningful applicable alternatives: continue/defer, look/listen, ask/call out, investigate, watch/prepare, warn a particular reachable listener, seek cover/leave, and supported conflict choices when warranted. Availability is not desirability. No sound-to-turn-to-chase script, forced hostile interpretation, preferred-name branch or biography instruction chooses a reasoning person's response.

“Perception,” familiarity, fatigue, expertise and emotion act only through installed rules. Perception is consumed once by A/B; E adds no duplicate hearing/detection bonus. Familiarity can make evidence meaningful without locating its source. Expertise can suggest a useful accessible test without revealing its answer. Descriptive traits influence the person's own selection; only declared mechanical contributions change physical limits. Fear can motivate caution or sustained preparation while supplying no attack direction.

Repeated equivalent cues can remain real observations while their optional reconsideration is coalesced. A completed, declined or blocked inquiry retains its actual disposition through existing action results/intake. Only materially new evidence, changed prerequisites, an explicit new choice or an existing eligible quiet-initiative opportunity reopens it. Expiry, rereading, paraphrasing a suspicion and a newly generated thought do not create a paid investigation loop. A quiet interesting event must still be eligible even when it matches no predeclared native interest.

## Native animals

Ordinary animals receive compact sensory evidence without language, autobiography or a model. Their installed controller can ignore a harmless cue, orient, hold a brief listening/observation posture, enter its existing alarm, escape, remain blocked, settle, or use a specifically installed curiosity method.

Alarm, refuge selection and territorial priority remain the current native-response owners. C's alarm-pattern suppression remains authoritative; E's separate curiosity disposition extends that same owner without suppressing later alarm or creating another map. A sound with only a bearing can bias a legal vantage or escape preference, not disclose an emitter point.

E adds a small optional curiosity method: one admitted observation hold and, for a capable ground body, at most one bounded approach to a personally available vantage, followed by a final hold. Actual danger preempts it through the same controller. Unlocalized cues can end in observation-only; an impossible route is a physical block, while only unfinished required calculation remains technically incomplete. The initially selected optional profile and its limitations are in the world document; existing prey/territorial profiles do not automatically become curious. New calls, pack communication, scent, animal language and arbitrary flight searching remain unsupported.

## Disclosure and ordinary use

The actor's own controls must explain the selected subject/region, method, finite ending, compatible current work and consequential interruption before acceptance. Existing exact action requests support this without a new universal attention meter. G selects the final input bindings and accessible presentation.

Other people may observe a head turn, stance, visible weapon or movement when A/B permit those facets. That does not reveal “watching Ada,” a private fear value, exact readiness, a hidden destination, belief or notification condition. Even a spoken promise is a statement, not proof of present coverage. A person asked to watch may give an account of what they actually noticed without publishing their whole mind.

Audio, non-audio cues, history and action descriptions must agree on uncertainty. No predictive “this will surprise them” interface is supplied. Private activity/results and historical investigation coverage use existing observer/control/privacy fencing.
