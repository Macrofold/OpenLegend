## Purpose and reading scope

This is the selection companion to the [feature](emergency-combat-and-loadouts-feature-spec.md) and [technical design](emergency-combat-and-loadouts-tech-design.md), not another catalogue or delivery queue. It considers all areas in the [repertoire map](../repertoires/README.md#catalogue-map), using [gameplay priorities](../repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md), the combat catalogue's scope/selection and foundation rows, objects' equipment coverage, economy's ordinary-exchange/courier/fair examples, and the opening [combined situations](../repertoires/combinations.md).

This is a **catalogue-area and relevant-entry review**, not a fresh line-by-line audit or reranking of every entry in the thousands-item library. Existing stable IDs, priorities, proposal status and category ownership remain. No unrelated capability becomes required just because a named idea can be placed in a combat scenario.

The useful missing connection is **returning to a wanted life after a readable interruption**. The design connects existing work, possessions, trade, movement, defense and independent people instead of promoting an exhaustive armor catalogue, threat dashboard or reaction minigame. The new native scope is deliberately small: opening timing, emergency composition, one equipment-set operation, a brace source and one protective outfit.

## Selected connected journeys

### The fisher who keeps the catch

Begin with a real river task and wanted meal, not a combat arena wearing fishing props. A natural attacker creates the opening. The fisher can brace and move away, choose the carried protective set and fight, or use actual nearby cover. Keeping the catch and safely returning is a successful result. Finishing the fight should not delete the half-composed inventory choice, automatically recast the line or switch back to work gear.

**New value:** the player can comfortably pursue an ordinary livelihood while keeping one clear route to immediate agency. **Required scope:** existing river activity, natural attack, native movement/defense, loadouts and return continuity. Rich ecology and automatic companion protection are not dependencies.

### The bargain worth returning to

Two people negotiate over useful actual goods. An attack interrupts the moment without mysteriously completing or reversing the deal. Either participant can choose defense, flight or continued negotiation through their normal control/agency. Afterward, unchanged terms can be deliberately reviewed; changed terms cannot inherit old acceptance. A participant's actual help or refusal may matter later, without a guaranteed friendship reward.

**New value:** persistent characters and possessions make the interruption part of a relationship, rather than just a UI failure. **Required scope:** existing reciprocal barter and actual actions/outcomes. Full merchants, currencies and contracts remain separate.

### The unseen shot and the honest warning

The player receives a generic incoming-danger cue without a free nameplate through cover. Retaliate cannot identify a target that is not presently targetable. The player can defend toward a known direction, move toward real cover or seek a better view. A prepared shield and thoughtful positioning retain value; the opening is not a guarantee against every projectile or an excuse to falsify collision.

**New value:** uncertainty survives alongside a fair chance to act. **Required scope:** supported ranged preparation, anonymous assistance, ordinary perception and physical cover. The full stealth/psychology suite is not an ECL prerequisite; when installed it keeps its own evidence and surprise consequences.

### A home, a warning and a chosen contribution

A warning reaches someone doing useful work near a defended camp. One person may protect an entrance, another may move possessions, and another may decline. The new controls remove menu friction; existing capabilities determine whether these contributions actually work. A simple complete episode need not implement a tower siege, faction war or universal companion order.

This is a **reuse/qualification variant**, not extra mandatory construction or NPC-behavior implementation. The [toll-tower scene](../repertoires/combinations.md#the-toll-tower-at-dawn) preserves the larger desired context and its separate gaps.

### The quiet return

The character deliberately puts on working or social gear and returns to an unfinished exchange, a meal or a celebration. Nothing is required to attack again. The same crafted possessions and remembered events can make the ordinary scene feel different, while the interface simply restores the task the player chose.

**New value:** a successful escape, defense or victory has room to matter. This follows the library's instruction to stop generating emergencies once a scene reaches its meaningful outcome.

## Decisions carried from the conversation

- Viewing information is not itself physical work. No inventory/menu immunity or automatic global pause is selected.
- Repeated damage must not forcibly close every menu. The explicit Defend/Retaliate action provides the one-step emergency transition.
- Information and unfinished drafts can survive; current reach, consent and permissions still determine whether a physical trade/transfer can finish. Committed transfers stand.
- Keep a focused view of the same world, not a separate combat instance. Preserve camera orientation, actual equipped gear and meaningful escape.
- Safety can come from real places, barriers and willing companions. Opening a shop window is not a shield.
- Current-weapon Retaliate and automatic loadout selection conflict unless ordering is explicit. The selected order is reaction first, one cancellable convenience afterward; neither changes a source already bound to an attack.
- The initial reaction is extra native preparation, not a freeze of other people or an artificial first-hit invulnerability. The committed-projectile/collateral boundary is explicit.
- Focus/stats/effects may contribute to a world-authored allowance; no unselected mandatory Focus meter or psychological law is added.
- “Right click or something” was illustrative, not a binding replacement for existing context/camera controls. Visible controls plus named remappable actions preserve those controls.
- Full protective gear was a player goal, not evidence that armor already exists. The selected proof includes a real small outfit consumer rather than claiming current held equipment already supplies it.

Earlier discussion used RuneScape, Elden Ring, World of Warcraft, Minecraft, Terraria and Rust as comparison prompts. The retained lesson is the distinction between ordinary inventory, physical transactions, world time and actual safe places. This design does not rely on unverified claims about which particular interface closes after one hit; no external game's exact current behavior is asserted here.

## Design disposition

The selected emergency interface and mode-entry/loadout policy complete this **scoped refinement** of ND13/DG01. The first-attack opportunity completes this selected human-conflict refinement of ND11/DG07. Current-equipment reaction, input ownership and ordinary neutral execution reconcile the relevant ND38 consumer without reopening the whole ASE design. Modifier support consumes the existing authored-stat foundation rather than closing all ND03 progression work. Preserved barter is an ND09/PX02 integration case, not a new economy design.

Broader device parity/remapping, independent combat acceptance, psychological fear policy, currency/markets, NPC quality, armor variety and whole-game evidence remain with their existing owners. This review does not mark any of them shipped or solved.
