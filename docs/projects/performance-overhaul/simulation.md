## PF17.9 — Local mechanical recalculation

**Priority/status:** first substantial simulation experiment; refinement of proportional-step stage 1. **Scope:** medium-to-large, staged by boundary family. **Intended behavior:** unchanged, subject to explicitly checked existing sampling effects.

**Code fact.** `advanceWorldNative` in [kernel.ts](../../../packages/domain/src/kernel.ts) discards shared preparation after territorial reviews, some broad endpoint changes and any detected `nextId` change. A rebuild prepares participants' status rates and deadlines. `refineNativeInterval` in [temporal-boundaries.ts](../../../packages/domain/src/temporal-boundaries.ts) already supports limited local changes, principally flight. Ordinary walking displacement/waypoints still contribute shared mechanical boundaries.

**Implementation.** Have the actual mechanical owners report affected participants and dependencies. Preserve unrelated participants' rates and absolute deadlines. Update the participant plus anyone coupled through shared supplies, cross-target effects or active work. Replace “an ID was allocated” as an invalidation reason only where the actual change set proves what happened. Unsupported events retain a full rebuild.

**Selected first slice.** Route ordinary walking-only displacement stops through the existing private-motion path; retain arrival, stance/reach, interaction, status and work changes as meaningful boundaries. Restrict this first slice to an admitted `move` action following its fixed route. Follow, gathering approaches, moving-target pursuit, pending navigation and territorial reviews retain their existing preparation. Maintain the next minimum deadline with the existing interval machinery; a new heap is not part of this slice.

**Why it could help / risks.** This removes unrelated participants × rebuilds, rather than merely slowing animals. [Reports 12](../../verification/performance/12-work-counts-and-quiet-wandering.md) and [40](../../verification/performance/40-quiet-threat-wakeups.md) motivate the dependency audit but do not assign a reliable percentage. Step changes can alter reach sampling, fleeting sightings, rounding and random/event order.

**Future decision test.** One walker among many stationary workers/sleepers, then shared resources and simultaneous events. Preserve complete piecewise motion through turns/support seams for projectile and contact prediction: test a walker turning around an obstacle while another body/projectile crosses the misleading straight start-to-end chord. Include moving-target approach and an unrelated walk during a blocked bird landing. Count complete versus local forecasts and participants visited. Compare meaningful outcomes across different caller chunk sizes, brief visibility windows and work-on-arrival. Measure actual command tails and saves afterward. Reject a local shortcut with incomplete dependency coverage or a more expensive maintenance cost than the avoided preparation.

## PF17.10 — Prepare status operations and net flows once

**Priority/status:** next, or a narrow first slice within current intervals; new concrete refinement. **Scope:** medium. **Intended behavior:** unchanged.

**Code fact.** `reconcileStatusEffects`, `prepareStatusRates` and `integrateStatusRates` in [status-effects.ts](../../../packages/domain/src/status-effects.ts) repeatedly traverse installed rules and build groups of operations, rates and causes. Every actor remains eligible for substantial preparation; active episodes still need actual integration.

**Implementation.** First retain the grouping of captured net flows for the unchanged `NativeMechanics` interval. Then consider compiling immutable definitions into ordered arithmetic and dependency descriptions, bound to live participants only when relevant inputs change. Candidate selection includes all active effects and conservatively possible newly activated effects. Keep dynamic body ranges and rate predicates current.

**Reuse/invalidation.** Definition replacement invalidates compiled preparation. Activation/deactivation, source/target changes, cross-target conditions, body/attribute replacement, participant loss and restoration invalidate bindings. A mutable authoring object is never a stable definition key. Earlier rules can activate later rules, so a one-time filtered list must account for cascades.

**Why it could help / prior evidence.** Cost grows with participants × rules × repeated integrations. The roughly 1% lookup-only experiment in [report 27](../../verification/performance/27-meter-definition-lookup.md) did not remove this grouping or rule traversal; it is not a verdict on this larger design.

**Future decision test.** Opposing/clamped flows, periodic damage, sleep/wake, cross-target effects and live definition edits. Compare rule order, work accounting and complete outcomes. Count grouping allocations and rule visits, then actual CPU/latency/memory. Reject if compiled metadata, cold setup or invalidation costs outweigh reuse. Do not introduce generated executable code; prepared data stays within existing trusted rule evaluators.

## PF17.11 — Continuous values with one current-value owner

**Current save continuation — October 17, 2026.** The first-slice materialize-at-save instructions below describe the earlier experiment. The implemented [saved-calculation contract](saved-native-calculations.md) now persists the existing bounded exact window and validates its starting and calculated values at cold load. Ordinary edits still settle through the same owner. Read that current contract before changing saving or current-value consumers; do not reinstate the old save barrier as a performance fix. Its finite proof is separate from sustained capacity.

**Priority/status:** larger architecture option; existing proportional-step stage 4 remains unimplemented. **Scope:** large and cross-layer; the earlier audit estimated roughly 600–1,000 logic lines, not a new estimate or commitment.

**Code fact.** `integrateEndpoint` and final publication in [kernel.ts](../../../packages/domain/src/kernel.ts) apply pending rates across participants. [status-effects.ts](../../../packages/domain/src/status-effects.ts) advances episode time and work charges; `setAttribute` in [world-modules.ts](../../../packages/domain/src/world-modules.ts) writes values, revisions and threshold notices. Merely delaying a hunger field leaves much of this work intact.

**Implementation option.** For a supported family, represent a value at an effective time plus its prepared rate and next meaningful threshold. All reads, rate changes, interactions, disclosure and persistence use one current-value owner. Cover episode elapsed time and accounting as well as the meter itself. Materialize coupled participants before an interaction that requires exact current values. Begin with one complete linear family and a conservative eager fallback.

**Why it could help.** It removes repeated changes at their source: arithmetic writes, entity copies, bookkeeping and subsequent storage, potentially across every publication. This is broader than caching a definition lookup or batching four intervals.

**Arithmetic and lifecycle decision.** Repeated rounded/clamped addition is not necessarily equivalent to multiplication over a longer interval. Choose either an exact representation preserving the required operation sequence, or a documented new numerical contract with tested threshold/event behavior. Reading must not mutate semantic state or make results depend on reader order. Save/restore, death, changed definitions, inventory effects, UI and AI all participate. No legacy-format support is introduced under the current development policy.

**Future decision test.** Specify the full consumer inventory before code. Compare an eager reference through hunger, rest, injury, periodic effects, supply contention, cancellation and restoration. Measure publication writes, save volume, old-world CPU and command tails. Reject a meter-only win that leaves stale status/work values or moves equivalent cost to every observer read. Any changed arithmetic/fidelity needs explicit product documentation before retention.

### Implementation packet

**Selected first slice: design and implement one exact, uncoupled numeric family.** The proposed initial eligibility is a non-health numeric attribute with one linear effect, no periodic operation, no other writer or cross-target dependency, and a certified next threshold. This is an engine-supported test family, not a claim that every current hunger/energy rule qualifies. The existing world definitions must be inspected to identify a real qualifying family before claiming a production benefit. All other families remain eager.

**Proposed numerical contract.** First preserve the existing sequence of arithmetic and clamping exactly, using a bounded list of pending operation segments rather than assuming `rate × total time` is equivalent. A segment records the elapsed argument and ordered operation identity that eager integration would have applied. A pure current-value read replays only that value's pending segments from its materialized value. One transition-owned window, published immutably with the supported values, may span publications; its total reachable pending segment count is experimentally capped at 64. Before appending the next segment at that bound, materialize every value referring to the window, then clear it and start a new window. Current state cannot retain a chain of prior windows; older immutable snapshots may still retain their own unchanged data. This bounds retained/replay work without dropping work or slowing game time. The choice of 64 is a provisional finite experiment setting, not a measured optimum or adopted gameplay limit, and must be inventoried as proposed before implementation. If replay/compaction erases the benefit, reject this exact variant. A rate-and-anchor variant with changed rounding is a separate recorded numerical decision, not an invisible substitution.

## PF17.16 — Explicit coarse background activity

**Priority/status:** deliberate gameplay option, narrowed from accepted product direction rather than a new universal simulation law. **Scope:** large; coordinate PS02–PS05.

**Code fact.** `nativeParticipants` and the current mechanics in [kernel.ts](../../../packages/domain/src/kernel.ts) process active physical roots through shared simulation. [Background progression](../../product-scalability/background-progression.md) describes a future cheaper mode, not a delivered capability.

**Implementation option.** Start with one supported world-defined activity: an already chosen finite, stationary remote crafting stage whose inputs have been consumed at normal work start. Ordinary ambient travel is a later separate family. Retain identity, resources, location/progress, interruption conditions and next interaction boundary. Promote to detail before any relevant player or resident can perceive/interact, before supplies are contested, or when danger makes the coarse law insufficient. Use conservative reachable areas and existing geometry; unsupported mechanics keep detailed execution.

**Why it could help.** It avoids fine-grained work rather than only reducing each check. However, the original crowded seventeen-person/forty-animal scene may have little eligible remote activity; this is not a guaranteed fix for that scene.

**Compromise.** Paths, incidental encounters and exact timing may differ from the detailed counterfactual. The world may feel quieter. Do not silently freeze offscreen creatures, invent memories after the fact, pass through obstacles or waive resource costs. Detail is based on relevant interaction, not only the player's camera. World policy selects coarse-capable activities; the engine owns coherent promotion and saved continuation.

**Future decision test.** One isolated supported activity, an approaching observer, resource exhaustion, danger, cancellation and restart. Compare complete work/cost and explain behavioral differences. Then repeat with dense nearby activity where promotion is frequent. Reject if promotion churn or lost richness costs more than the saved simulation.
