## Purpose and required experience

A character should understand what they chose, what actually happened, what remains, and what they learned. An action can be a leaf or contain nested actions using the same understandable structure at every level. There is no special syntax that only works when brackets or one target happen to be present. There is no conceptual fixed nesting depth; finite storage, execution and display admission still apply.

A model-visible action uses compact English generated from an actor-permitted record, with optional nested brackets. Someone unfamiliar with the game engine should understand it without learning internal field names. Do not attach a duplicate JSON record; JSON is a last resort for meaningful permitted detail that existing English templates cannot express. Descriptions identify the actual target roles, tools, quantities, outcomes and perceived effects. The model can distinguish an offered possibility from a running plan and from a remembered result. A player can inspect those same distinctions through their authorized view.

An activity is a chosen composition, not a second physical action authority. Learning saves a reusable way of attempting work; it does not give the character a new goal, fabricate a capability, guarantee cooperation or automatically execute anything.

## Information needed to choose

A well-described action is not necessarily an informed choice. Prepare the relevant facts about the character, action, targets and circumstances together. What matters depends on the question: choosing whether to hunt, choosing a weapon, continuing after a miss and recalling the hunt need different detail. The same requirement applies to a single action and to a nested sequence.

| Question the character needs to consider    | Information to include when consequential and known                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Why might I do this now?                    | Relevant bodily state, chosen goals, values, commitments, trigger and recent experience; what the action could change                                              |
| Can I start, and what will I need later?    | Capabilities, tools, quantities, access, knowledge, permission, worksite conditions and independent participants; distinguish present requirements from later ones |
| What am I acting on, and with what?         | Specific targets/tools, relevant condition, distance, reach, compatibility and known surroundings or hazards                                                       |
| What might happen?                          | Intended result, supported expected effects, possible failure or partial results, affected people/objects and material side effects                                |
| What will it cost or displace?              | Time, travel, consumed resources, supported bodily costs, occupied equipment and consequences of interrupting current work or missing a commitment                 |
| What do I know, and what remains uncertain? | Current observations, qualified memories or testimony, relevant personal success/failure evidence, unknown or changing conditions                                  |
| What other choices are available?           | Relevant supported alternatives, continuing current work, inspecting, declining or waiting, without requiring a particular preference                              |

This is a relevance checklist, not a repeated questionnaire or a mandatory field list in every prompt. Shared body, goal and target facts appear once; each offer supplies the meaningful differences and consequences. Preserve known blockers and consequential costs even when only one option is offered. Do not silently infer that a need creates a goal, assign a universal usefulness score, or prescribe a sequence. Unsupported example mechanics remain absent.

For a sequence, the first step being executable does not establish that the whole activity can succeed. Show its intended end result, current next work, relevant later requirements, output-to-input dependencies, total resource demands where supported, known travel/work/wait costs, conditional branches and what remains after interruption. A possible output is not yet an owned resource. Two steps cannot both assume they consume the same last item; a fire burning now may not still burn after a hunt. Collapsing child detail must preserve such consequences at the parent. Continuation describes the remaining work against current state, with committed progress retained in authoritative records and recalled when relevant.

Being important does not make a fact knowable. Unknown health, routes, future yields or another person's willingness remain unknown; an actor may inspect, decline or act under uncertainty when the capability permits it. Distinguish that from accidentally omitting a known critical fact or failing to finish preparing the context. Estimates are labeled and do not promise success. Compare alternatives with consistent units and assumptions, without inventing a complete forecast or exposing hidden state.

## Action descriptions and effects

The displayed description uses plain action words and direct names. Optional brackets contain smaller actions in order, and each smaller action can use the same pattern. With no brackets, no smaller actions are shown; a simple sentence stands alone. Use plain role labels such as `Actor`, `Target`, `Using` and `Recipient` only when natural word order is insufficient. Internally, a role can bind zero, one or several permitted objects, people, places or resources according to the capability's schema. The reader sees names and their roles directly, never a second reference dictionary or raw object IDs. “Target” is a possible role, not a required universal one. Giving an object can distinguish giver, recipient and object; a multi-target action can list every known affected subject without pretending they share one result.

Compact text must retain decision-critical distinctions. With five knives, identify the actual selected knife using known descriptions and include the qualities needed for the current choice: for hunting, this may include damage, reach, attack time and hit chance. The choice also needs relevant known target information: in this hunting scenario, distance and observed current/maximum health, distinct from the weapon's reach. Include known movement/access constraints when consequential. Other activities use their own target conditions rather than requiring a health field everywhere. “Knife” cannot imply that a butter knife and a hunting knife have the same capabilities. A tool's relevant details can appear once beside the parent action, with clear shorter child references; a remembered child shown alone must regain enough detail to stand alone. Historical results use the historical tool and permitted facts, not current equipment.

Templates are expandable. The renderer tracks which critical permitted facts have been expressed; having a sentence template for an action does not prove that its details are complete. Render uncovered facts as readable labels where possible, then use a small JSON fragment for critical missing information with no suitable text rendering. Preserve needed relationships and units; never drop them merely to avoid JSON. If required facts cannot be safely described or fit, make the incompleteness explicit instead of presenting a misleading choice.

Keep execution state, attempt outcome and intended purpose distinct. A knife attack may finish with a miss. A hunt may be cancelled after injuring an animal. A meal may be eaten while the long-term goal of staying alive remains active. Report actual effects even when the larger activity fails. A rejected request causes no invented execution steps.

An offer states its actual commitment: one attack, a selected bounded repetition or a larger method. The word “hunt” cannot silently authorize attacks until death. Use AC's existing invoke/sequence/branch/repeat/wait contracts; execution does not parse the display syntax. A committed partial product remains usable after interruption, but default sequences stop on terminal failure and later use needs a new choice or explicit supported recovery. Failed attempts with useful committed outputs remain dependency evidence; a miss with no contributing effect is not a required recipe step.

Damage is reported as committed injury to the identified subject, with before/after health only when independently available to that observer. Status application distinguishes applied, refreshed, resisted, removed and unknown; duration/intensity appear only when supported and known. An applied status is not proof of all later consequences. Parent summaries may summarize child effects without counting them again.

For continued work, show remaining actionable steps, blocking conditions, relevant bindings and supported estimated work/distance. Completed work stays in memory and durable execution records. Explicit history inspection can reveal it; ordinary continuation should not repeat an entire completed prefix. Missing future targets/locations stay unresolved rather than receiving invented distances or object IDs.

Movement offers and nested movement steps state where to go or how close to get, the known current target distance, and a supported travel estimate or consequential unknown. `Move close` alone is insufficient; distance to the target, distance to walk and required reach are different facts. Remembered movement uses actual permitted travel/arrival facts instead of presenting an old estimate as accomplished travel.

## Learning and discovery

Native execution records facts without a paid call. Completion, failure, object production and observations make new evidence available; none is intrinsically labeled “useful.” Relevance and reusability are assessed from the actor's permitted experience, intentions and conditions. Failed attempts can teach limitations. Noncontributing misses are not installed as mandatory recipe steps; real partial outputs and contributing effects remain part of the supported dependency history.

Candidate activities may be connected by produced/consumed resources, established prerequisites, selected purpose, or evidence used in a decision. Sharing a timestamp, tool or target is insufficient on its own. A patrol and a social exchange can be coherent without material causality. Inferred grouping remains an interpretation, distinguishable from a native dependency.

Learning runs as optional, bounded background work during eligible idle/reflection periods. Long gaps, sleep and long-running actions do not break recorded relationships. Learning during an ongoing native wait is outside the initial policy. If the character never has eligible downtime or budget, learning remains pending and ordinary action continues. This project introduces no mandatory post-action model call or idle timeout that defines a sequence.

The character may retain a proposed method, decline it or remain uncertain. It may retain partial methods such as harvest → cook independently of a whole meal. A first success is tentative evidence, not established reliability; automatic numerical confidence promotion/decay is outside the initial path; a familiar trace updates personal evidence without repeatedly asking the same learning question. Alternative methods remain possible.

## Shared catalogue and personal knowledge

Within a compatible world, structurally equivalent methods share a definition. When another character independently performs the corresponding actions with matching roles, conditions and observed results, the system can recognize their experience using that definition and present it for personal learning. This saves repeated construction and comparison, without presenting a method they have never encountered.

Definitions do not expose discoverer identity, private goals, locations, secret prerequisites or another character's success statistics. Acquisition is actor-owned and evidence-backed. A known method is retrieved only when relevant to the current situation, chosen purpose, opportunity or explicit inspection; the catalogue is never appended to every cognition context. In-world teaching or observation can later use existing knowledge rules, but is not implemented merely by sharing storage.

An existing four-action method inside an eight-action experience is a reusable substructure, not the end of discovery. Keep the original action connections and continue considering eligible larger or overlapping compositions. Two activities may share a prerequisite without duplicating its execution or claiming the same consumed resource twice.

Equivalent matches may overlap during discovery, but a chosen executable tree performs each contributing occurrence once in its original dependency order. Declining a new candidate publishes no shared method. Retaining one atomically records its definition and personal acquisition; simultaneous independent learning reuses the same compatible definition without sharing private evidence. Routine memory compaction preserves permitted learned-method support. Explicit revocation of the sole support suppresses affected retrieval/use, including after loading an earlier save; independent permitted support may preserve the method.

## Scope and staged capability

The project covers records, deterministic scoped rendering, relationship capture, bounded candidate construction, personal learning backed by a shared catalogue, contextual retrieval and execution of selected supported compositions. Reuse current plan, perception, knowledge, repository and spending owners. It includes representative native and budgeted Jev-only behavioral qualification after implementation authorization under the standing spending policy.

It does not include arbitrary programs, universal causal discovery, exhaustive optimal sequence search, automatic transfer of private knowledge, unrestricted parallel body work, universal teaching mechanics or guaranteed survival. Exact definitions and guards are not learned physical laws; observations can be incomplete. A method can remain a remembered non-executable hypothesis when supported bindings/conditions are missing.

Stages follow the [project sequence](action-experience.md#delivery-sequence). The task tracker owns unfinished work rather than duplicating a second completion list here.

## Decisions and tradeoffs

Following the September 28 owner correction, compact English with optional nested brackets is the required presentation direction. Internal typed records provide exactness without making the model read those records. JSON is only a last resort for otherwise unrenderable permitted detail, and cannot expose internal metadata. Brevity must preserve clear targets, actual effects and important uncertainty. A composite may be coherent through selected purpose without a proven causal chain. Reusing a shared definition is separate from learning it personally. Reflection scheduling and processing budgets may defer learning, but cannot rewrite what happened. These are the accepted initial contracts. The tracker distinguishes delivered mechanisms and recorded evidence from the reopened generative presentation gap; hypothetical witness mechanics are still not shipped capabilities.
