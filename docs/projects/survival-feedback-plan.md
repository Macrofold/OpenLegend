## Authorized scope

Mike requested visible sight/hearing area tints and slightly brighter boundaries, health shown as integer points, percentage-based food and energy, silent routine food/energy drain, visible actual damage, and discrete starvation damage based on maximum health.

Initially the client derived every floating numeric loss from successive meter readings, so normal drain repeatedly refreshed notices and damage could disappear when healing occurred before the next publication. The quiet-drain slice first changed sparse numeric meter feedback to recorded outcomes. Before the follow-on, body health already had native points, but its installed meter scaled every body to a percentage and starvation applied a continuous rate. The completed Health follow-on now uses whole points, exact permitted outcomes and periodic need damage. The earlier guide fill existed at an opacity too low to be readily visible; its independent correction is complete.
