## Scope and review method

Review covers all 42 files' purpose, ownership and structure, with detailed reading of relevant sensory, physical, cognitive, tactical, social and control material and its surrounding context. The world profiles and research ledgers establish selection and provenance boundaries. This is not a word-for-word reread of every unrelated catalogue entry, a new external-source study, or a new reading-completion attestation for the research corpus. Catalogue build estimates and original study conclusions do not establish current implementation or measured enjoyment.

The selected minimum makes approach, observation, inquiry, deliberate preparation and recovery usable within the whole game. Broader psychology, surveillance, disguise, ecology, institutional behavior and optional worlds retain their existing scope and priority. Native startle and bounded NPC interpretation are specified; **SA-D01 remains open** for controlled-player appraisal production, retained-appraisal mechanical applicability, disclosure, revocation and control transfer. Native-body-only initial control is a recommendation, not an adopted policy.

## Selected mechanic boundaries

### Evidence, recognition and current facts

Detection, classification, localization, familiar identity, name knowledge and intelligible content are separate achievements. A familiar voice can be recognizable without clear words or a precise source location. An exposed patch can support a coarse visual fact without revealing hidden feet, equipment or an actionable whole body. [A](perspective-perception-tech-design.md), [B](vision-and-gaze-tech-design.md) and [C](acoustic-stealth-tech-design.md) own those facts; catalogue examples do not add alternate detection rules.

Base-world voice recognition needs actual familiarity and sufficient current evidence under the selected world policy. Being addressed does not identify an unfamiliar speaker. A changed public voice, imitated loved one's voice or social roster cannot force canonical recognition or belief. The [knowledge policy](../worlds/base/knowledge.md#identity-and-recognition) and [hearing contract](../hearing-and-speech.md#source-and-delivery-recognition) retain the distinction between intended behavior and the current runtime baseline.

Continued real exposure can strengthen evidence. Replaying, retransmitting or recalling one account cannot manufacture independent corroboration. B's current visual values remain frozen at their admitted 50 ms normal-play samples; later publication cannot turn them into freshly measured positions. Losing support ends the corresponding live detail. Hearing someone later does not refresh an old weapon, pose, location or activity. [G](stealth-player-experience-tech-design.md#authorized-projection-contract) carries original times and precision through history, inspection and rendering.

### Native bodies, attention and chosen actions

A suspicious sound gives a reasoning person a possible reason to look, investigate, warn, leave or continue work. It does not compel a turn. A supported animal controller may orient or flee from actual native reception without autobiographical memory or human reasoning. E's explicitly opted-in curiosity contrast does not retune ordinary hares. A synthetic or unusual body gains only its installed senses and responses.

Looking while walking uses the accepted body/head owner; crouching uses actual admitted geometry. Watching and guarding require finite maintained work. **Walk while watching** binds a permitted destination and finite observation terms in one [E admission](attention-and-investigation-tech-design.md#31-one-admission-transaction), with direct-steering amendments preserving its deadline. Watch handoffs require actual communication, acceptance and maintained coverage; a promise or menu selection gives no all-directions vigilance. A decoy can be noticed and ignored without identifying its unseen placer.

### Startle, emotion and independent choice

[D](emotional-physiology-tech-design.md) separates immediate native bodily startle, private interpretation and effective mechanics. Native startle preserves ordinary movement and eligible actions while imposing its actual accuracy/evasion consequences; it creates neither a categorical action lock nor automatic running slowdown. Courage and practical calm are not immunity, and prose traits do not install modifiers. Reasoning inhabitants may fear, fight, flee, investigate, help or continue according to their actual circumstances and choices.

Bounded reconsideration can use a retained concern without inventing a new external incident. Reusing an unchanged account cannot renew the cause indefinitely. The open [player-fear decision](stealth-awareness-feature-spec.md#player-fear-decision) covers retained-appraisal applicability as well as new production, disclosure, revocation and transfer. Catalogue scenes cannot silently choose whether interpreted NPC fear remains mechanically active under direct control.

### Preparedness, surprise and disclosure

[F](preparedness-and-surprise-tech-design.md) evaluates genuinely acquired relevant warning and completed preparation before contact. Concealment or a rear angle alone grants no surprise; a visible friendly visitor may surprise an unready victim, while a ready victim can anticipate an unseen danger. A missed defense is not proof of surprise, and contact cannot backfill its own prior warning. E supplies the actual maintained-method start; action age or client press time is insufficient.

Ordinary views expose only acquired facts and permitted own feedback. Current body targeting, remembered identity, uncertain cues and creator inspection remain separate roles. A historical marker cannot follow an unseen body, emit current light or authorize an attack. Accessible captions and visual alternatives preserve the same evidence and precision. The shared [disclosure contract](stealth-awareness-tech-design.md#player-information-disclosure) and G own these rules across views, assets, history, selection and delayed responses.

## Delivery and qualification

The integrated owner specifications determine formulas, durations, evidence revisions and lawful composition; this review adds none. [SA](../maintainers/stealth-awareness.md) coordinates the shared scenarios and [SPX](../maintainers/stealth-player-experience.md) owns the complete player journeys. Qualification must distinguish correct native sensing, timely publication, understandable feedback, successful control and actual player enjoyment. An acquired cue missed by the interface differs from a cue the body never acquired.

No priority or catalogue expansion is justified merely because a subsystem can support it. Choose a connected activity, observe whether the information changes a useful decision, include approach/preparation/recovery time, and retain ordinary alternatives and independent refusal. Implementation, accessibility, performance, player learning and the open player-emotion policy keep their actual evidence and decision status in the delivery owners.
