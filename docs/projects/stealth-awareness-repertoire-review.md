## Scope and review method

The review covers every directory file's ownership, opening scope and domain structure, including gameplay priorities, mechanics, psychology, traits, bodies/species, combat, actions, language/knowledge, ecology, other catalogues, combinations, coverage, source ledgers and world profiles. Dedicated relevant domains and their surrounding explanations were read together. All 25 combined situations were reviewed as complete scenes, including alternatives, failure, aftermath and selection limits. World profiles were reviewed for their authored systems, relevant cast roles, opening arcs and limits; they remain optional proposals.

Local text and heading searches covered stealth/concealment, body orientation and posture, sensory channels and acuity, acoustic masking, voice recognition, observer-specific facts and uncertainty, attention/interruption, native startle, appraisal and emotion, warning/readiness, surprise, decoys, camouflage, source-limited behavior and current/historical disclosure. Matching behavior and build-gap text was read with its owner context, rather than treating a research-source URL or an isolated keyword as feature evidence. Additional contradiction checks addressed first hearing, automatic reactions, spectator views, lost contact, retained identity and warning receipt.

This is a **contextual review of all 42 files and their relevant feature material**, not a claim that every sentence of all 8,257 catalogue entries was reread word for word. It does not revalidate every underlying research dossier, confer new source-reading completion marks or treat a catalogue's Compose/Extend/New estimate as evidence of current implementation. Historical source pins and reading ledgers retain their original meanings.

Static document checks confirmed the pinned source hashes, preserved IDs/metadata and unchanged 25 combination headings. The selected topical catalogue edits change **26 numbered rows**, two mechanics owner-reference rows, one retained psychology example and six existing combined situations. No executable audit tool is added to the repository; runtime changes, game tests and deployment are outside this review.

## Selected contradictions and boundaries

### Evidence, recognition and current facts

Detection, classification, source location, familiar identity, name knowledge and intelligible content are different achievements. A distant voice can be recognizable without understandable words or a precise location; a visible moving shape can remain unidentified or insufficiently detailed to reveal a small carried object. [BSP-044/045](../repertoires/bodies-species.md#sensory-worlds), ECW-004/009 in [Ecology](../repertoires/ecology-weather.md), CR-077 in [Combat](../repertoires/combat-rescue.md) and the [shared information contract](stealth-awareness-tech-design.md#shared-contracts) keep these distinctions explicit.

The former accepted guarantee of canonical speaker association on an unfamiliar first hearing is superseded. Reliable base-world voice recognition requires prior familiarity, sufficient current evidence quality and the selected Perception rule. The [base knowledge policy](../worlds/base/knowledge.md#identity-and-recognition), [hearing contract](../hearing-and-speech.md#source-and-delivery-recognition), [memory contract](../memory-architecture.md#personal-perspective-and-acquisition) and [SSP-M01](../limits/memory.md#ssp-m01--stable-recognition-and-known-names) state the selected target while preserving the current visually associated/episode-bound runtime baseline. Being directly addressed remains independently knowable under the selected linguistic policy; it does not identify an unfamiliar voice. Other worlds select their own supported sensing and recognition laws.

LKN071 in [Languages](../repertoires/languages-knowledge.md) preserves expression through a changed public voice without compelling recognition or belief. PB-325 in [Psychology](../repertoires/psychology-behavior.md) treats a loved person's apparent voice as fallible evidence, not canonical identity or that person's present location. An authored social roster likewise cannot disclose unknown first-heard participants; the [gathering profile](../worlds/base/social-gatherings.md) consumes actual participant evidence.

MEC-083 distinguishes continued real sensory exposure from replaying a saved account. Real duration and quality can strengthen weak evidence; re-rendering, rereading, retransmitting or recalling one account cannot manufacture independent corroboration or familiarity. Episode continuity can survive a supported gap while live detail stops as soon as its support is lost. M:recognition, LKN225 and OBS-12 preserve a remembered person or last-known region without following an unseen body. Hearing that person later does not refresh an old weapon, equipment, location or activity observation. Corrections and forgetting remain source-bound through the existing memory and identity owners.

### Native bodies, attention and chosen actions

OBS-02 and SEC-05 make suspicious sound a reason to consider looking, investigating, warning, leaving or continuing work. They do not compel a reasoning person to turn. BOD-01 locates independent looking while walking in the accepted physical body/head owner; camera motion does not grant that action. BOD-04 gives crouching an actual admitted collision body, with noise/concealment effects supplied only by supported world relationships. OBS-05 and CBT-11 make watch/guard actual maintained work, not a promise or menu selection that grants unlimited vigilance. See [Actions](../repertoires/actions.md) and the [shared orientation/attention contracts](stealth-awareness-tech-design.md).

An animal may orient or flee through its native controller and actual senses. A new sensor does not install human cognition, and a synthetic body need not have hearing, visible-light vision or fear. Native acoustic reception must not require autobiographical storage. The existing partial alarm/escape machinery is distinct from still-undelivered acoustic intake and qualification; [SSP-H02](../limits/hearing-and-speech.md#ssp-h02--sound-reactions-without-autobiographical-memory) and the [canonical hearing target](../hearing-and-speech.md#selected-animal-hearing-and-choices--accepted-target) preserve that current/target boundary. Finite sensory/controller continuation remains different from human-style memory.

Existing AP-245 acknowledged watch handoffs, AP-261 intermittent checks and ACR-051 relief arrangements remain useful combinations. MEC-094's physical decoy can be noticed and ignored. Detecting its source does not reveal an unseen placer or choose the recipient's reaction.

### Startle, emotion and independent choice

The selected design separates immediate native bodily startle, private interpretation and effective mechanics. PB-003 admits the native reaction before interpretation while retaining relevant defensive, offensive, escape and ongoing-work choices. PB-008 does not make a lookout who expects the wrong threat incapable of noticing a strong unexpected cue. [Psychology](../repertoires/psychology-behavior.md) owns meaning and conduct; the [shared emotion contract](stealth-awareness-tech-design.md#emotions-and-reconsideration) owns their composition with bodily effects.

PB-181's former generic phrase that fear was not a required combat penalty needed a selected-world distinction. The base-world target has real accuracy/evasion consequences without automatic running slowdown or compulsory flight. Other worlds may select different emotional vocabularies and effects. PB-188, M:appraisal and the retained “The same event two appraisals” example allow bounded reconsideration of a retained concern without a new external incident. An unchanged account cannot create another frightening occurrence or perpetually renew a feeling's lifetime.

TR-006 in [Traits](../repertoires/traits.md) separates practical calm and courage from bodily susceptibility, learned technique and danger-specific preparation. Appropriate authored traits, physiology and conditions may affect startle; a calm description alone grants neither immunity nor a mechanical modifier. The direct-player producer of longer-term interpreted fear remains the bounded [SA-D01 decision](stealth-awareness-feature-spec.md#player-fear-decision). Catalogue scenes do not settle it or block independent sensing, native/NPC or combat work.

### Preparedness, surprise and disclosure

CR-084 now states the selected rule explicitly: relevant warning and genuine readiness before contact determine surprise. Early detection need not settle readiness, and concealment or a rear angle alone does not confer an advantage. CR-078 limits perceived threat to what is discernible; CR-256 lets a received alarm prepare someone without revealing unseen identity or coordinates. Failed defense does not prove surprise. A hidden anticipated attacker may fail to surprise a ready victim; a visible apparently friendly visitor can surprise an unready victim. Continuous combat does not replenish a surprise advantage whenever someone steps behind an opponent. The [shared combat contract](stealth-awareness-tech-design.md#threat-preparedness-and-surprise) owns the before-contact evidence boundary and authored 2× application.

Source occurrence order differs from observer acquisition. The [structured-experience contract](../memory-architecture.md#structured-permitted-experience) retains both meanings; later awareness insertion, SQL hydration or interpretation cannot backfill a victim's prior evidence cut. The repertoire does not introduce another threat tracker or duplicate combat evaluation.

MEC-097 formerly described a “spectator view” of a hidden stage mechanism without specifying the audience's authority. The selected wording distinguishes an actually situated in-world spectator or creator-only rehearsal from ordinary player camera disclosure. A detached camera cannot expose a live character outside the controlled body's permitted view. Legitimate optional witness-view effects still use the witness's actual supported viewpoint. Historical identity, creator authority and present physical targeting remain separate under [player information disclosure](stealth-awareness-tech-design.md#player-information-disclosure).
