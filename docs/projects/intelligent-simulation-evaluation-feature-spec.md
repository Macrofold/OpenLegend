# Intelligent simulation: useful behavior and complete cost

| Status      | Current progress                                                                                               | Last updated |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| In progress | The comparison design is prepared; implementation, live behavior and player-experience qualification are open. | 2026-10-15   |

## Purpose

Improve the life the player encounters, while paying less for work that does not help it. A character should carry out an appropriate choice, notice an important exception, stop when satisfied and remember the consequence on a later return. Fewer model calls alone do not establish improvement. Nor does a more eloquent explanation establish a better person.

This is a proposed extension of existing qualification, not a second evaluation programme or a claim that a new controller works. It resolves the research follow-ups for **native execution and deeper interpretation**, **sharing computation before simplifying lives**, and **whole episodes and complete operating cost** in [the intelligent-simulation design register](../maintainers/needs-design.md#intelligent-individuals-and-efficient-simulation-research-derived-design-follow-up). These ordinary research headings do not receive new ND identities. [ND20](../maintainers/needs-design.md#nd20--a-bounded-richer-memory-retrieval-comparison) remains conditional on a consequential recall omission; this design neither activates nor replaces it.

The ambition is many distinct, believable people in worthwhile worlds. The first useful proof remains a few complete encounters and their later consequences, with an honest account of the computer work required. No population capacity follows from retained identities, successful isolated choices or a published research result.

## Current foundations and missing evidence

Known physical actions already execute natively. Chosen plans can continue without another model call. The existing director selects among offered routes, prepares actor-permitted information and accepts proposed actions through current game rules. Non-speech action ratings can share a routing request; speech can require a dependent action-rating request. Jev-only operation disables generation and defers explicitly when available selection is inadequate.

Shared history preparation, source-version checks, provider accounting and inspectable decision stages already exist. Their local evidence does not establish reliable personal continuity, calibrated routing, long-session stability or hosted capacity. Reuse those delivered mechanisms and evidence; qualify their uncovered behavior rather than rebuild them.

The existing [AG12](../maintainers/agent-agency.md#ag12--behavioral-value-and-cost-separately-authorized), [CR12](../maintainers/cognition-redesign.md#cr12--acceptance-and-tokenlatency-evidence), [CE05](../maintainers/character-experience.md#ce05--integrated-character-quality-and-complete-cost-qualification), [PF00/PF11/PF14](../maintainers/performance.md) and [PS08](../maintainers/product-scalability.md#ps08--broader-experience-and-scale-qualification) remain the acceptance owners. This pair supplies one compatible comparison contract for their relevant cases.

## The episode, not the preferred answer

Each comparison follows an initial opportunity, available evidence, a voluntary choice, actual execution, a relevant interruption or change, stopping and a later return. The later encounter must offer an actual opportunity for the earlier experience to matter. Merely mentioning a memory in private thought is insufficient.

The following are **proposed cases**, composed from existing mechanics and authored people. They are not claims that these outcomes have already been observed.

| Episode family                     | What the comparison must expose                                                                                      | Legitimate alternatives                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hunger with several means          | Carried food, reachable food and permitted hunting choices; actual costs and outcomes; stopping after relief         | Eat carried food, gather, hunt, wait briefly for a personally supported reason, or decline an unsafe opportunity  |
| Continuing craft or shelter work   | A real selected quantity, scarce material and a changed prerequisite; later resumption through ordinary admission    | Resume, choose an allowed substitute, revise the purpose, ask for help or abandon it                              |
| Quiet company or solitary pleasure | A modest invitation or familiar activity without a crisis; enough quiet time to choose                               | Accept, politely refuse, continue enjoyable repetition, seek solitude or remain satisfied without new work        |
| Personally important exception     | A proposed material or target has meaning supported by the character's own knowledge; an ordinary alternative exists | Preserve it, request permission, knowingly accept a supported tradeoff or choose another route                    |
| Contested resource                 | Two people act on overlapping supplies or changed assent                                                             | One succeeds; the other notices the actual refusal, adapts, negotiates or stops without fabricated success        |
| Disappointment, care or apology    | Attributed words and witnessed behavior; a later comparable opportunity                                              | Changed conduct, tentative interpretation, continued distrust or no change when the evidence does not warrant one |
| Interrupted hunt or shared outing  | A real miss, lost target, injury, withdrawal or changed condition; actual remaining work                             | Continue, retreat, suspend or end for reasons supported by this person's evidence                                 |
| Provider unavailable               | An admitted opportunity without a usable decision service                                                            | Continue already authorized work, defer, or use an existing permitted native response; never invent a choice      |

Author case details in the selected world's own fixture/content owner. Hunger, deer, weapons, social customs and sentimental meanings are not universal engine categories. A contrasting non-survival case, such as an authored machine preserving a designated component while completing a chosen maintenance task, must exercise the same authority and measurement contracts without adding human hunger semantics.

## What counts as success

Keep four judgments separate:

1. **Mechanical correctness:** supplies, time, damage, custody, consent, privacy, accepted actions and save/load outcomes match the installed rules.
2. **Personal continuity:** the person's choice is intelligible from their authored background and available experience; the system preserves a meaningful exception, satisfaction or refusal instead of optimizing it away.
3. **Social quality:** independent people can disagree, misunderstand, withdraw or cooperate without one person's generated account becoming another person's private truth.
4. **Readable play:** an observer can understand preparation, progress, interruption and aftermath without access to private thoughts or an invented explanation.

Mechanical invariants are hard requirements. Personal, social and presentation judgments use a written scenario rubric, observed evidence and explicit uncertainty. They are not a new universal score for a person, a reward meter or an instruction to maximize productivity, friendliness or novelty.

Record positive, negative and indeterminate judgments with their evidence. A missing opportunity, preparation failure, unavailable provider or truncated observation is not a successful quiet choice. Equally, a deliberate refusal or satisfied rest is not a failure merely because no item was produced. Define the episode's completion condition before running it; do not retrospectively choose the stopping point that flatters one variant.

## Comparisons and permitted experiments

Use the current deployed routing and continuation policy as the baseline. Compare a credible simpler alternative and the selected proposed change on matched worlds, opportunities, evidence availability and budget. The simpler alternative may omit an optional semantic gate or reuse exact preparation; it must preserve supported authority and disclose any narrower behavioral scope.

A deterministic fixture can establish plumbing, mechanical outcomes or the cost of a known route. It cannot establish spontaneous personal choice. More capable or more frequent generation can be an independently funded comparison, but its choices are not ground truth and it is never an automatic repair route.

Keep the agreed Jev-only survival condition intact. Such a case may compare permitted selection/preparation variants; adding generation makes it a differently scoped comparison and cannot pass the original condition. Neither the existing preference score nor a difference between top scores is an estimate of whether the selected action is personally appropriate. Two close choices may both be sensible; one strongly preferred choice may still miss an important exception.

### Conditional routing, reuse and learned shortcuts

Start with an observed consequential error or an attributed complete-path cost. Name what the current route missed, whether the necessary evidence was present, the time available and the consequence of being wrong. Do not add a model call merely to ask whether every existing model call was necessary.

An exact repeated read may reuse already prepared facts when its permissions and dependencies remain valid. Reusing a past behavioral decision is a different proposal: the earlier purpose, admissible means, current evidence, consequences and opportunity must still fit. It never becomes permission to execute. When that cannot be established cheaply and completely, use current routing or explicitly defer under the current policy.

A learned classifier or calibration model is only a conditional comparison after representative traces, labels and held-out cases exist. No training service, model/vendor, output threshold, automatic production promotion or hidden-state access is selected. An opaque API supplies only the fields it actually returns. Missing internal activations cannot be replaced by imagined confidence.

## Share useful work first

Profile repeated geometry, method validation, information preparation, queries, allocations and movement through their actual callers before approximating personal lives. Separate route search, local avoidance and execution. A cache saving a small query can still lose overall if dependency tracking, copying or rebuilding costs more.

Compare cold creation, warm reuse, meaningful invalidation, restore, burst changes and growing retained history. Count work visited before the returned shortlist. Permission-scoped private preparation must never be shared merely because two people stand near the same object. Share immutable public inputs where valid, then project each person's knowledge separately.

Removing unnecessary computation must preserve native ordering, saved randomness, current action admission and the actual evidence each observer receives. Dropped observations, silently skipped consequences, reduced history or slower decision access are behavior changes, not free optimization. Any such proposal requires its own selected fidelity promise and owning design.

## Fair and independent evidence

Compare changes to one relevant experience, relationship or condition while holding other permitted information and mechanics stable. Retain a no-change control and plausible alternative interpretation. Keep repeated rumor sources grouped so paraphrasing one claim does not manufacture corroboration.

Separate development/tuning, optional calibration and final evaluation by whole episode family and related source material. Adjacent paraphrases, related characters and later continuations of a tuning episode cannot quietly enter held-out evaluation. Record accidental overlap and rerun only under fresh explicit admission and spending authority.

Report ordinary-case frequency separately from deliberately over-sampled rare exceptions. A set rich in keepsake dilemmas is useful for exposing failures, but does not estimate their natural rate. Do not optimize a summary average while hiding harmful cases in a small category.

Counterfactual comparisons reveal sensitivity to a changed input; they do not establish a person's hidden causal reasoning. Score actual behavior from what the person could know. Privileged world facts may check mechanics, but must not become an answer key requiring knowledge the character never perceived.

## Complete cost and responsiveness

Count every admission, preparation, optional shortlist, attention judgment, recall query, embedding, route judgment, generation, reflection, validation, queue wait, commit and presentation operation attributable to the episode. Include unsuccessful, stale, cancelled, deferred and uncertain work, and share background costs explicitly rather than dropping them.

Report cost per useful completed episode, cost per real hour and cost per simulated day, with useful completion defined by the prewritten rubric. Retain numerator and denominator, failures, uncompleted work, achieved simulation progress and observation window. With no completed episode the ratio is undefined; it is not zero cost per success. Short-run extrapolations must be labeled estimates.

Distinguish provider-reported estimates, settled charges and outstanding or uncertain commitments. Preserve available decimal precision, including fractions of a cent. Never count both a reservation and its settled replacement as two charges; never treat cancellation or unavailable usage as free. [Verification and spending](../../.agents/rules/verification.md) remains authoritative. This design authorizes no paid calls; the additional Jev cost for preparing it is $0.

Show steady and burst behavior, time to first useful action, time to relevant reconsideration, stage tails, queue age, storage delay and browser responsiveness. Report hardware, source/model versions, clock settings, actual concurrency, active bodies, retained identities, decision counts, environment operations and human reading/composition time. A native profiler excludes provider, database or browser work unless its actual measurement includes them.

## Delivery stages and acceptance

1. **Reproducible existing baseline:** select existing CE05/AG12/CR12 cases and a documented observed failure or cost; define rubrics, matched inputs, complete trace/cost coverage and evidence gaps. Reuse existing diagnostics and profiling tools.
2. **Information-sharing comparison:** optimize only the attributed repeated work, or conclude no change is warranted. Preserve identical accepted effects and permitted evidence through cold/warm, mutation, contention and restore cases. Full cost must include the optimization itself.
3. **Episode and later-return comparison:** run native fixtures first, then separately authorized live cases and actual player observation where appropriate. Preserve failures and alternatives. Report which dimension improved, worsened or remains unknown.
4. **Conditional routing experiment:** only after the earlier evidence supports it, evaluate an explicit gate/cache/classifier offline or in disposable worlds. Keep current production routes and fallback policy unchanged. Production adoption requires a reviewable measured proposal, resolved product decisions and implementation authorization.

Design acceptance means these stages can be implemented without inventing another policy owner. Runtime acceptance remains with the linked owners. No case passes solely because the model chose the researcher's favorite option, one episode completed, a cached call was cheaper or a tool produced a report.

## Non-goals and tradeoffs

- No replacement mind, scheduler, rating database, telemetry vendor, model router or new benchmark framework.
- No new universal cognition interval, confidence threshold, population cap, operating price or psychological theory.
- No hidden personal-information pooling, generated rationale as proof, or high-capability model as an oracle.
- No automatic paid retries, production experimentation, training programme or new external payload permission.
- No claim that less frequent thought, more thought, a smaller model or learned behavior is inherently better. Their complete measured benefit and burden decide a selected comparison.

## Open decisions

None blocks this comparison design. A production routing change, learned-model deployment, new operating offer or materially changed fidelity promise is not selected here. Its evidence and developer-owned choices must be resolved before dependent implementation; an experiment cannot silently adopt them.
