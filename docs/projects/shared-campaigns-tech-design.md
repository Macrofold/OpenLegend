It has no campaign administrator, invitation, global quest state, paid director or scheduled-event prerequisite. Prevention is a completed outcome. The broader campaign proposal remains a later consumer.

Source inspection used `Macrofold/OpenLegend` main **d261c94f50875edd88e8eb4a69cf8f4a413563cb**. ## 2. Mechanism, world policy and authority

New reusable computation is limited to (a) a positive manual-drive interface feeding an existing checked two-endpoint translation, (b) an explicitly registered nonliving blocking-body interface consumed through existing spatial owners, and (c) a finite linked operation whose actual source edges permit one machine to operate one actual target. Names, materials, values, starting people and the road story remain in `worlds/base/`. There is no general encounter script, condition language, plot director, code-loading YAML field or callback chosen by content.

Proposed source owners are `packages/domain/src/linked-fixtures.ts` for the finite typed transition/evaluator and `packages/domain/src/worlds/base/shared-campaigns.ts` for authored composition. The manual-drive recipe family belongs beside the existing base recipe families. Extend `world-composition.ts`'s trusted host/family composition and the selected manifest; never add feature registries independently in HTTP, UI and NPC code. These paths are implementation targets, not claims that the files currently exist.

## 5. Physical drive, ordinary gates and material alternatives

### Positive interface and compiler

The proposed `fixture-drive-v1` item interface includes exact tip shape/size and usable insertion depth; finite tool solid boxes and grip anchor; supported stroke direction/range; positive rated drive load; occupied body ports; and the trusted family/producer dependency pins. Units are metres, newtons and game seconds. A drive requirement supplies its socket geometry, local frame, allowed tip clearance, required insertion/stroke/load and a fixed set of reached operating faces. The selected ratchet converts a linear back-and-forth tool stroke to the gate's admitted translation; no arbitrary rotary mechanics, gravity solver or continuous torque model is introduced.

The native `base:manual-drive-tool` family consumes actual qualified `wood` and `cord`. Its selected family-owned material adapter positively admits the **existing exact wood definition pin** with specified dimensions and 120 N load; generic material tags cannot certify strength. Store that adapter in the new family/profile, not by rewriting the existing wood definition or its old recipe dependencies. Addition appends definitions and leaves prior material/recipe/assembly pins unchanged; if a needed existing meaning is incompatible, refuse this addition rather than recompile prior possessions silently. Compile rated load from the material adapter, not a number freely claimed by a model. The v1 candidate can choose bounded shaft length 0.30–0.50 m, square tip width 0.025–0.035 m and grip length 0.12–0.20 m. The Ash Road socket admits a 0.028–0.032 m square tip with at least 0.060 m insertion and a 0.30 m stroke. The public crank and known turning bar both use the exact baseline shaft length 0.30 m, square tip width 0.030 m and grip length 0.15 m; these are selected values, not the midpoints of every admitted range. Longer/wider admitted tools can be honestly incompatible with this socket or current room, and an alternate lower-load native fixture can accept a subset that differs from Ash Road. Exact output geometry and material eligibility, not prose resemblance, determine use.

The v1 native compiler has one deterministic output shape. In the tool-local frame the insertion tip is `(0,0,0)`, positive x is insertion, and the shaft runs toward negative x. Given shaft length `L`, square width `S` and transverse grip length `G`, the shaft solid is `x=[−L,0], y=[−S/2,S/2], z=[−S/2,S/2]`; the grip solid is `x=[−L−S/2,−L+S/2], y=[−S/2,S/2], z=[−G/2,G/2]`. Their union is one manufactured item. The small intersection `x=[−L,−L+S/2], y/z=[−S/2,S/2]` is its declared shaft–grip join; it creates no duplicated mass, inventory or collision effect. The grip anchor is `(−L,0,0)`, and the usable insertion portion is the first 0.060 m behind the tip. Both public and known tools use these same two boxes and anchors. The admitted candidate fields change only L, S and G, not arbitrary vertices, geometry, extra outputs or a claimed reach. The broader eight-box interface ceiling permits another separately qualified producer; this family itself always emits exactly two. Insertion contact is allowed only against the actual admitted bore faces, with the profile’s 0.028–0.032 m fit rule; a generic body skin margin does not fill that deliberate socket clearance or ignore the surrounding housing.

Candidate validation charges all dependency and geometry work, resolves kept versus consumed inputs, and retains existing invention/known-method distinction. The reusable-library design may later preserve this qualified family and its pins; importing an artifact alone neither installs a missing host capability nor teaches a character. The entire expedition remains runnable with the known turning-bar method and no library or provider call. Fresh-player acquisition is explicitly authored; a prior existing-world player or visitor uses the outside physical turning-bar record through deliberate inspect/Learn. Addition does not grant prior people new methods automatically.

### One physical work representation

Extend the existing equipment-use discriminant with the registered drive use. Its two-hand port occupancy is checked through the equipment owner; equipping stows only actual incompatible equipment under ordinary admission. Add one qualified **working pose** to that owner's derived attached-item geometry, selected by the current drive action. The placement remains attached to the same actor/port and is the only physical attachment. The pose derives a tool transform from the actor's actual stance and fixture's exact socket frame; it is not a second saved XYZ or a temporary copy attached to a fixture. The geometry reader used by collision, perception, picking and rendering consumes the same transform and finite tool solids. Restoring a supported work pose derives it from the saved action and validates the attachment in both directions.

Approach uses the existing bounded work-point/stance query. The profile fixes the outside socket neutral frame, actual open socket box, translating carriage/stroke, grip and supported actor stance; the socket is on the workshop's outer west face at that exact work height; actor body, tool shaft insertion, grip and entire stroke must fit from a supported stance. Positive interface fit is necessary but insufficient: include current full actor envelope, other bodies, roof/walls, posts, hangers, loose objects and the selected tool. Only exact insertion contact and the tool's declared grip contact are intentional; never ignore the whole fixture, whole wall or actor body. Grip placement must keep the rest of the tool outside the actor's body. Item-centre reach and an image of a long handle prove nothing.

SH remains the leaf/runner/parking geometry owner. The drive requirement is an optional exact **instance component** on the newly authored workshop gate, interpreted by the installed drive host during the existing gate-operation admission. It references the unchanged SH physical family and a separately installed drive profile; adding it does not replace the family pin or alter any pre-existing camp gate. An absent drive component retains ordinary hand operation. A present component without its exact supported host/profile is invalid, never permission to fall back to hand opening. The drive evaluator combines the exact existing leaf/hanger translation sweep with the tool's finite linear-stroke sweep and the actual socket carriage's stroke. The profile's finite housing/rail solids are additional real attached components under the existing compound geometry owner, not an invisible reach exception. The socket translates with the inserted tool along the stroke and returns to its neutral endpoint during the coarse operation; a fixed small hole cannot allow an inserted shaft to move sideways through solid material. Each is a union of exact axial prisms for its own moving solids; empty space between parts is not turned into a box. The working tool stays at the admitted neutral pose and the leaf at its original endpoint during the coarse 120-second work phase, just as the selected SH endpoint model does. At completion, recheck socket fit, full pose, whole sweep, current authority and reservations, then apply the gate endpoint and work result atomically. Renderer animation has no collision, force or timing authority. A body entering the potential strip during work is permitted to be there; if it blocks the final move, completion refuses without crushing, spending the kept tool or inventing a fractional gate position.

Stopping, losing reach/control/life, incompatible equipment replacement or withdrawing a required grant ends the active work. Release the kept-tool claim and return its derived holding pose through the same attachment owner; it never changes custody, appears on the floor without an admitted placement, duplicates or repairs itself. If a carried container containing the tool moves, existing inventory work restrictions prevent bypassing that claim. Destroying a supported shutter part uses SH's dependency-aware breach; surviving tool and yoke remain their actual identities. A removed drive or broken gate does not leave an orphan work action.

Road gates remain ordinary hand-operable SH gates. Their two real stone shoulder abutments stop 0.01 m short of the end posts; stone/wood never overlap and no collision exception makes an apparent closed boundary porous. The workshop stone perimeter similarly clears its SH root by the exact profile offsets. Workshop hand-open/close is unavailable because its exact selected drive pin requires a tool; calling the generic SH operation through HTTP, NPC, typed language, inventory shortcut or a saved activity must recheck that same requirement. A damaged/breached shutter is actually absent or lowered under SH, so a person may walk through the real resulting gap. No special “puzzle solved” bypass is added.

## 6. Finite linked operation and phase order

Relay disconnection uses an ordinary finite native activity, tool-free at its exposed reached latch. It owns one relay edit at a time, reserves the actual attached yoke and complete required work, and commits the exact edge change plus detachment to a supported pile. Another person disconnecting first produces a truthful stale/already-severed result, not another yoke. Full or inaccessible personal inventory does not matter because the yoke is released at the site. If no supported release disposition fits, the operation is unavailable before the edge is changed. The yoke and released rights/provenance enter the world/authority transaction together.

Inspection reports the physical signals the observer is permitted to acquire. One severed relay unlocks the Warden's visible stop control; there is no magically vanishing cover or unreachable internal work point. Both severed relays while dormant transition it to terminal `prevented` with charge 0, as an actual mechanical consequence. A disconnected relay need not stay visible or live forever to preserve that result; retain exact edge history.

Local Start is an instantaneous native transaction at the reached start control. It selects among the at most two actually open and connected gate targets. A complete current route to the required inside stance, including the final gate-use feasibility, is required. Select minimum complete route length, with the profile's explicit west tie-break; never use entity insertion order or guessed straight distance. A candidate route needing preparation returns pending; zero candidates with complete proofs returns a useful no-operation result. A dormant no-target refusal spends no charge, starts no deadline and fabricates no event. Preview is pure.

Start captures the exact target/link/body/profile/authority, allocates one existing native work invocation and operation identity, spends the sole charge, and stores game-time start/deadline and path. Launching has no automatic reward. No participant count or public-service state is checked in this local path. Human launch still requires a deliberate current native action; a campaign interest flag, forecast or reconnect cannot issue it. An NPC needs its own current chosen action and exact grant.

The installed finite host positively admits only `close` on the exact linked SH gate operation family. It does not fabricate a human actor, general hand equipment or a generic remote gate command. At the profile’s inside stance it uses its selected side actuator anchor and at most 0.30 m of handling reach to the **current inside handle**, with the same ordinary SH unobstructed hand-to-handle segment and reached-work admission. This is the existing abstract handling model applied to a declared native manipulator; no independently colliding animated arm is promised. The open handle follows the parked leaf centre at section-local u = 3, so the closing stance must align with that position, not with the empty doorway at u = 1. Unsupported gate kind, a drive-only requirement, changed handle, blocked reach, bar/state mismatch or withdrawn operation right makes that target unavailable. At arrival and final commit revalidate the same capability, exact anchor/geometry and current grant; a source signal cannot waive handling or sweep requirements.

Movement advances the Warden's actual root through the shared supported-motion and occupied-body sweep owners; write through `setSpatialPosition`, with all ordinary geometry/semantic invalidation. The native host extends the existing movement phase to this supported nonliving mover instead of creating an independent tick loop or fake resident controller. It reads only its installed graph, route and current physical blockers. It never generates thoughts, acquires a human body, aims at people or shoves them.

**Current-source gap: nonliving solidity.** `occupiedBodySweepClear` in `spatial.ts` currently skips non-actors and inactive actors; ordinary move/follow/approach call `moveAlongPath` without its optional occupied-body check. A Warden BodyProfile alone therefore cannot establish the promised obstruction. Add a positively installed `physical-obstruction-v1` interface on this nonliving root: its canonical physical solid is one axis-aligned 1.00 m × 1.80 m × 1.00 m box at the current supported root, with movement, sight and projectile blocking selected by this profile. Its conservative BodyProfile has radius **0.71 m**, enough to contain the square's approximately 0.7071 m half-diagonal, and height 1.80 m. It is navigation/admission input, not a second colliding body. Current support/routes can conservatively reject a tighter passage; no general minimal-clearance box locomotion is promised. Actual published obstacle/ray/part geometry is still the one exact box. All ordinary/continuous/plan/follow/approach/evade movement and work stances, plus current projectile/sight/handling rays, must consult the existing indexed spatial candidate and exact finite-solid narrow-phase owner for these registered roots. Do not globally turn every inventory pile or resident into a wall; omission retains its owning current behavior. Extend that owner once, not a Warden-ID branch in each caller. Warden motion also checks actual eligible person envelopes and other registered physical solids so the body cannot pass through a blocker in the opposite direction.

Keep moving obstacle bounds in the existing derived spatial dependency/index ownership, keyed by actual root placement/shape revision. Do not rebuild the complete static map/navmesh on every footstep or store a second position. A returned static route is only a candidate: immediate motion checks current registered dynamic solids and can request a bounded current detour or wait through the existing route owner. Active-body changes invalidate incident contact/sight calculations at the actual occurrence; a full route cannot be declared clear from a stale point index. Terminal Warden geometry remains registered, costs no timer and continues to block through these same consumers. Qualification includes a materially different passive one-box movable cabinet with the same interface and no campaign/charge/controller, plus interface omission; that proves this native solidity is reusable rather than conditional on an encounter label.

The next interval includes current route waypoint/contact bounds, its work completion and the absolute 3,600-second game deadline. Preserve unconsumed native sampling progress across publication/load as the time owner requires. Movement occurs in the existing shared movement phase before end-of-interval action effects. At an endpoint, ordinary actor action completions settle in existing native order; reconcile exact source/target changes; then commit any still-admissible Warden closure; finally emit/reconcile observations through the common occurrence owners. Thus a relay severance or completed shutdown at the same endpoint can prevent a not-yet-committed closure, while an already committed closure remains real. Pin this ordering in the native boundary catalogue and tests; do not use callback arrival or browser animation order.

Wait for a relevant physical dependency change, then admit a fresh full 120-second close attempt at the same target only if the remaining allowance supports it; otherwise end exhausted. | Gate closure commits                                                                                                                      | End terminal `closed-target`, with exact gate/event reference. The prospective maximum moving distance is `(3600 − 120) × 0.011 = 38.28 m` when no waiting or failed closing work consumes allowance. The profile's unobstructed `sqrt(9² + 2²)` route is approximately 9.22 m and fits. Every currently prepared longer route must still leave time for closure; otherwise Start is unavailable or the ongoing attempt ends at its true deadline. Static and dynamic obstruction query incompleteness remains an operational hold, not a fictional body absence. Do not increase speed, teleport across water or silently extend time to make an authored launch work.

The initial stopped body and every admitted moved body must leave an actual bypass under the selected installed layout. The north path supplies that invariant for the campaign's own geometry; subsequent unconnected player construction can have its ordinary effects, which this feature does not undo. Readiness and qualification include a stopped Warden at each gate, the middle of the causeway and both sides of an obstructing body. No promised exit depends on an unimplemented hauling action or the machine becoming nonsolid.

## 7. Exact effects, receipts and current authority

Every operation uses current `WorldService` serialized mutation and `executeCommand` native admission, with stable command body and ID. `native-command-contracts.ts` classifies fixture/tool/target references and retains typed values for action history, finite plans, inspection, saved methods and grounding. Add one small installed command surface (inspect/operate with a closed native operation discriminant), not separate event-specific endpoint families. Exposed reference tokens resolve only through the current observer/actor scope.

For a real command, resolve an existing durable same-ID receipt **before** new-operation eligibility. An exact match returns its earlier result under current disclosure rights, even if the gate or yoke has moved; conflicting content refuses. Otherwise recheck timeline/generation, actor control/life/participation, pins/revisions, full authority, physical access, resources, work and current target. Preview neither allocates an ID nor consumes random state, invokes a provider, reserves a tool or writes an event.

Reuse the [first-playable material/site publication contract](first-playable-world-tech-design.md#exact-materialsite-authority-and-publication). A yoke detachment that makes its origins newly usable, an install/remove operation, and any phase carrying a required grant change must commit physical facts, exact event/result, original operation receipt and authority change in **one PostgreSQL transaction** through `WorldService.commit` and the existing store/authority owners. The current source's geometry-only progress shortcut is insufficient for a grant-bearing nongeometry outcome. No visible new yoke/stock right may precede its durable grant.

When several phases settle before publication, the existing native batch derives an ordered internal authority overlay for subsequent phase admission, inaccessible to observers and AI. Recheck original current rights at the durable boundary and persist all owned effects/revisions/receipts together. Before-commit failure discards candidate and overlay; after-commit acknowledgement uncertainty queries the original receipt, never redoes the detachment, payment or launch. Ordinary movement uses existing ordered progress persistence; its published current pose does not create a second operation ID or charge. The next durable boundary includes the exact current continuation.

Warden and source events retain attempted versus committed operation, exact source/target/item IDs, actor or autonomous machine attribution, occurrence time/order/position, actual before/after edge/charge/endpoint, quantity and terminal cause, plus qualifying private authority fences. Public projection exposes only permitted material facts, never grant internals or private person identities to explain an effect. An event originates where the gate/relay actually changes, which can differ from the attributed actor or machine position. Later observation and summaries cannot rewrite that source.

No new compensation service is needed. Public salvage is actual finite custody; explicit bargains use existing PX exact offers. A participant's successful action record, another person's report, public optional attribution and a resident's personal observation remain separate. The default public personal name is absent. Witnessed facts can persist under the existing memory rules even if the contributor declines public naming. No global completion flag forces gratitude, recipe learning or reward stock into a bag.

## 9. Save, load, definitions and removal

Extend `WORLD_RECORD_SCHEMA` through existing record nodes for the small installed linked-fixture/drive definition collections and the entity's finite-operation/relay components. Save exact profile/recipe/material/body/shape pins, charge, phase, selected target, source edge, actual item attachments/custody, active action/kept holds, partial route/work/deadline, earned results and personal histories at one coherent cut. Use the existing event/history tables for growing occurrences; no serialized per-campaign transcript inside one row.

Read current-format data into an isolated candidate. Validate exact definitions and interface consumers; unique IDs and nonduplicated yokes; complete live graph references; allowed historical absence after severance/terminal results; finite support/contacts; body position; gate endpoint/transform; actual tool pose/attachment and held reservations; bidirectional action ownership; work allocation and retained elapsed remainder; absolute deadline/phase/charge consistency; and no unsupported disconnected active host. Rebuild only dependency indexes, available action views and route preparation. A saved path is revalidated against actual current candidate geometry; recomputation retains target, charge and elapsed work. No fresh randomness, new yoke, overwritten gate, completed manufacture or personal observation is emitted by loading.

Use the existing current-format-only development policy. Reject incomplete/corrupt/unavailable candidates before replacing the active world. No special campaign save version, legacy shape adapter, reseeding hook or silent repair is added. Restore is the existing authority-generation transition: old jobs, selections, cursors, grants and callbacks cannot regain authority merely because restored gameplay IDs match. Clear stale derived preparations; resume paused. Restoring an earlier fictional charge does not restore a revoked operational grant or rewind actual billing/privacy/command fencing.

The initial selected scope is one actual world with no automatic exports. DG25/DG29's connected-boundary refusal applies to actual transferred goods, actors or shared fictional history whose external effects cannot be reconciled. Refuse such a pre-transfer local rewind rather than erasing another person's retained history or duplicating a unique yoke elsewhere. Existing historical receipt recovery can still report an earlier outcome without recreating its effects.

Keeping, freely acquiring or installing an immutable recipe through LIB is operational definition reuse under its own durable receipts and authority rules. Those retained copies survive a local gameplay rewind; they neither export the unique yoke nor force every source-world rewind to refuse. The yoke's actual custody/consumption remains in the world continuation, while [LIB's separately preserved definition/grant history](world-creation-tech-design.md#9-save-restore-restart-and-removal-integrity) supplies its specified reconciliation. Do not reseed a restored world from the library, withdraw an already retained/acquired definition right through gameplay restore, or apply an old-timeline plan automatically. Reusing a retained later-branch method in the restored world requires the fresh deliberate creator operation and current authority specified by LIB.

A completed place may remain physically installed without active campaign service. Dormant/terminal machinery has no scheduled ticks. Removing a live linked definition while needed refuses. Removing scenery or a spent body through authorized ordinary creator semantics preserves interpreted history and current goods; it does not restore sources. This design adds no deletion deadline, lifetime hosting promise, automatic cleanup, mandatory campaign residency or global retention quota.

## 10. Performance and bounded work

Apply the existing [performance](../performance.md) and [simulation-time](../simulation-time.md) contracts. The relevant multiplication is actual worlds × active operations × intervals, plus geometry candidates, offered tools, legitimate observers and durable results. Two authored relays do not bound unrelated people, objects or retained history. A cold installed place, a busy causeway, many completed campaigns and a long-lived resident have different costs.

| Work path and growth   | Selected computation/lifetime                                                                                                                          | Boundary and overflow behavior                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Idle installation      | One compiled immutable profile/definition graph; direct relay/target links                                                                             | No per-tick dormant/terminal entity scan or paid heartbeat. Installed allocation still counts against existing native/host work.                                                            |
| Active Warden          | One native invocation, at most two target route candidates at launch, one fixed target thereafter                                                      | Charge actual motion/contact/route/observation work through existing budgets. | Tool selection         | Existing actor-accessible indexed possessions; exact target interface selection before formatting; use existing inventory paging                       | Page/select before hydrating a growing stock corpus. No world-wide item or recipes × stock × fixture scan for each menu row. Direct chosen tool uses exact lookup and current revalidation. |
| Dependency preparation | Profile by exact immutable pins; geometry by map/parts/endpoint/pose; route by body, target, relevant geometry and current prepared request            | Request-owned mutable candidates never share committed-cache proof. Restore, changed body/attachment/pose, relevant geometry/rights or definition invalidates the affected result.          |
| Linked state changes   | At most the two explicit incident edges and their one Warden                                                                                           | Event/change-triggered reconciliation through the native owner; no all-entities search to find watchers. V1 tool geometry admits at most **eight finite axial boxes**, one socket, one grip and one linear stroke; the family rejects a more complex candidate before installing a partial interface. The 0.30–0.50 m shaft and remaining profile dimensions are world-selected ranges, not a universal invention size law. This scope avoids pretending a general rotational contact solver exists. Expansion requires a wanted different instrument and qualified collision/phase/persistence consumers, not arbitrary metadata permission.

The linked host admits at most **two source links and two target links per operation profile**, **one active operation and one work owner per fixture**, and no queued replacement. These restrictions bound this native family's mandatory dependency and operation work, not total worlds, bodies or user history. Installed world/host/work/storage admission remains independent. Many ordinary actors can still approach or observe, and all eligible effects/evidence must be accounted for; no new player/concurrency cap is asserted. The record/history owners have no new feature-specific retained-count cap; their existing retention, paging and required-work limits remain controlling.

CPU or storage exhaustion is an operational state with retained unadvanced time, not fictional waiting, hunger, renewed charge or a dropped observer. New optional work can refuse before it overloads required continuation. Reuse the host's bounded prefix/coherent publication; do not create another scheduler, event bus, DB lane or mutable cache. A source withdrawal or shutter change is an exact semantic dependency; invalidation must occur even when no optional narration/event consumer is active.

Qualification measures cold creation and restore; warm repeated inspect/use; source severance; a moving/body-blocked/closing machine; tool fit versus actual clearance; concentrated observers and unrelated inert objects; growing owned inventory and cold result history; authority revocation; and many terminal installations. Report advanced game time, caller counts, candidate counts before hydration, mandatory work, native/DB/render/command latency, retained bytes and all authorized provider/operator costs. Do not turn a small native fixture result into a shared-world scale claim or run paid/whole-game tests without their separate authorization.

## 12. Remaining decisions and evidence boundary

There is **no unresolved selected local mechanic** delegated to an implementer as a placeholder: geometry/install, tool compatibility, useful stock/methods, one operation, clocks, phase order, authority, persistence, callers and failure treatment are chosen above. Implementation may discover a concrete incompatibility; resolve it in this owner and the native owner before claiming completion, preserving the intended play and documenting the changed decision. In particular, eight-box tool/sweep support and SH's first-playable extension need actual implementation evidence; prose is not that evidence.

Local enjoyment, the value of the 36 m versus longer journey, actual independent resident behavior, comparative bow usefulness, end-to-end latency and operating cost remain qualification questions. A player who already has better gear may prefer the road, conversation, exploration or nothing; the design permits that outcome instead of manufacturing a reward need. The modest harmless Warden may prove less fun than prevention and the workshop; preserve the useful place and simplify the optional operation before inventing a damaging replacement boss.

Genuine later product decisions are public operation funding/operator responsibility; actual notice/replacement/reservation terms and demand; damaging intelligent opposition and human-risk authority; simultaneous worlds/fronts and canonical cross-world outcome/restore; wider manual-drive mechanics; and any durable commission/escrow service. Existing proposals preserve those ambitions. None blocks this selected local implementation, and none is adopted by its technical completion. No runtime, paid-model, game, player or scale test was run as part of this documentation-only design.
