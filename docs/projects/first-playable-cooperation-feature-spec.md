## Purpose and scope

The player can come to prefer particular people, do something useful with them, disagree, and encounter the consequences later. Four initially non-hostile residents have individual reasons to want security, travel, practical work, company or solitude. They need not join the player's camp or make the same choice in the same situation. A person who declines a dangerous hunt remains available for other ordinary interactions. A person who helps is doing actual work with actual materials and risks.

This completes the selected first-playable portion of [DG02](../maintainers/needs-design.md#dg02--one-resident-who-follows-through), [ND10](../maintainers/needs-design.md#nd10--persistent-groups-shared-ownership-and-in-world-institutions), and the [required people/cooperation target](../worlds/base/playable-openings.md#four-or-five-differentiated-non-hostile-characters). ND09's deferred commercial promises and ND04's personality development remain broader projects. Design completion does not qualify existing characters, implement new mechanics or authorize live provider spending.

The [world profile](../worlds/base/first-playable-people.md) owns the selected cast, exact authored additions and social defaults. The [technical design](first-playable-cooperation-tech-design.md) owns state, commands and implementation contracts. Existing [social rules](../worlds/base/social.md), [compelling characters](compelling-characters-feature-spec.md), [speech perception](speech-and-social-perception-feature-spec.md), [combat responses](combat-and-threat-responses-feature-spec.md) and ordinary gift/barter/travel owners retain their meanings.

## Why this should be worth doing

Companionship must change a playable activity, not only produce different dialogue. Two independently acting people can cover different approaches to quarry, carry more actual supplies, work on separate camp components, share a meal they really prepared, or offer another informed view of a risk. These benefits require ordinary individual capability and real contributions. Joining never applies a party damage bonus, creates extra meat or turns someone into an obedient follower.

The solo route stays viable. Cooperation adds negotiation, travel and uncertainty as well as potential help. A player may sensibly gather food alone, seek Mara's practical perspective on a repair, go exploring with the Mercenary, or choose Ada's quiet company. Those are hypothetical opportunities, not promised lines, ordered scenes or required responses. Actual value is assessed against the time, controls and supplies used by the capable solo route.

The [gameplay priorities](../repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md#whole-game-build-order) call for meaningful cooperation without contract administration as a prerequisite. Accordingly, a short invitation and one recipient decision are enough for a bounded shared activity. The player can speak normally without first opening a negotiation panel. Native consequences require an explicit structured choice; ordinary words do not secretly grant custody, compel acceptance or start an attack.

## Selected cast and starting knowledge

The four residents are Ada, the Peacock Mercenary, Mara Reed and Tavin Vale. Preserve their existing stable identities, histories, bodies, possessions and acquired knowledge. The [world profile](../worlds/base/first-playable-people.md#the-four-people) specifies distinctive interests, values, personal limits and disposition additions. These are authored personality and history, not instructions of the form “when the player does X, do Y.” The existing missing-belonging incident remains separately authored starting evidence, with Mara's suspicion and Tavin's knowledge kept different.

No resident receives a mandatory work schedule or opening goal. Each has physical needs, permitted surroundings, genuine personal interests and available activities from which to form an intention. Skills described in prose provide personal background; only admitted practical competence and learned-method records grant mechanical abilities. The selected elementary crafting methods are shared starting competence under the survival owner. The rare sling method remains an independently learned discovery.

The player learns names through existing introduction/recognition rules, belongings through deliberate permitted inspection/disclosure, and motives through speech or observable conduct. A player-facing card never exposes all biographies, private goals, hidden resources or another person's exact willingness score. The four resident identities are not universal engine roles such as healer, quest giver or mandatory companion.

## What can be agreed

| Choice                        | Exact agreement and effect                                                                                                                                          | What remains a separate choice                                                                         |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Offer company / travel        | Existing outing to one permitted destination; each person has their own admitted walk.                                                                              | Conversation, aid, fighting, gathering and giving goods.                                               |
| Ask for guidance              | A short episode to show one personally known destination; ordinary method advice remains speech or actual method-record inspection.                                 | Whether the guide walks, speaks, demonstrates, waits or leaves; the learner's movement and actions.    |
| Ask for practical help        | One named, currently supported task: prepare one declared quantity, carry one offered object to a permitted place, or perform one exact admitted camp contribution. | Choosing a plan, accepting custody, authorizing material use and executing each ordinary action.       |
| Invite to hunt                | One currently observed quarry, an agreed end time, a known meeting place and the explicit risk of harm.                                                             | Both people's positions, attacks, defense, pursuit, withdrawal and eventual division of actual yields. |
| Offer / trade / share         | Existing exact gift or reciprocal barter: current objects and quantities, acceptance, all required custody checks.                                                  | No future produce, unseen container contents or recipient acceptance is implied.                       |
| Agree to be allies            | A revocable mutual acknowledgement of an intended friendly association, recorded for those participants.                                                            | Help, property, entry, construction, information, lending and combat permissions.                      |
| Offer a ceasefire             | A finite agreement to stop initiating harm against the named counterpart; acceptance cancels each signer's still-cancellable attacks on that counterpart.           | Friendship, forgiveness, disarmament, custody, reparations and staying nearby.                         |
| Renegotiate / decline / leave | Exact revised terms need fresh assent; either person can decline or revoke their own future participation.                                                          | No refund of actual spent materials, injury, completed work or released projectiles.                   |

Alliances make a mutual statement available to later personal interpretation; they are not a numeric affinity or an automatic helpfulness switch. An ally can refuse, disappoint, become afraid or end the association. A non-ally may help, trade or cooperate. No hidden reputation threshold unlocks these choices.

Hunts support pairwise agreements that can coexist around the same quarry; there is no party leader or roster that authorizes anyone else's actions. A hunter may take part in one purposeful physical episode at a time, while multiple allies independently cooperate on that same hunt. Another hunter cannot silently amend the terms of the first pair. Exact selected scope and the restriction's rationale are in [CPL03](../limits/first-playable-cooperation.md#cpl03--physical-episodes-and-pairwise-coordination).

## Player and resident journeys

### A useful guide without omniscience

The player asks about food or a useful place. The resident's ordinary answer uses personally known places/methods and current observations. If a native guidance invitation is offered, it identifies one known destination or method; a remembered destination is labeled with its last observation, not claimed current safety or stock. The recipient can accept, decline or propose another destination they actually know.

Accepting a guidance episode creates no travel or learning event. Each person chooses their own walk or speech. Guidance to a place ends when both actually observe their own arrival, either leaves, or the deadline arrives. If one loses sight, their view says the guide's current location is unknown; the agreed destination remains a usable remembered point. Ordinary method advice needs no guidance agreement: it can point to an already known elementary recipe, state practical advice, or use an actual admitted method record. Hearing arbitrary instructions does not silently install a recipe. Method-learning effects retain the [knowledge contract](../knowledge.md#physical-production-method-records).

### Two people make a camp useful

The player requests help with a named component, material preparation or carrying task. The helper can review the actual intended result, quantity, destination, risks and time limit. Acceptance starts no physical work. The helper can form an ordinary plan or state that a prerequisite is missing.

For building, a host's explicit construction grant covers the exact contribution, materials and released outputs; friendship is insufficient. Two helpers may work on different assemblies, while one assembly has one active edit. Separate stock never becomes communal by proximity. Handing a bundle to a carrier uses ordinary gift acceptance and changes actual custody; the cooperation terms are a purpose, not a hidden reservation or return guarantee. A borrowed expensive item should use the separately designed lending system when available, rather than being described as a protected loan here.

The player sees actual work and progress they can perceive. Finishing a component can fulfill the declared task; a helper saying “done” cannot. Partial fabrication, a laid post and spent cord remain if the helper stops. A new attempt rechecks present geometry, materials and permission. [Camp design](editable-shelters-tech-design.md#11-first-playable-camp-construction-and-defense) owns those physical effects.

### Hunting and sharing something real

Both people identify one visible quarry and can agree to a short hunt. If the animal moves out of sight while the recipient reads or decides, the received invitation remains usable with each person's own last sighting labeled as remembered; accepting never updates its hidden position. The invitation shows the known animal, the selected meeting place and danger; it does not disclose hidden health, guaranteed yield or an optimal tactic. One hunter may approach from a different direction, use a ranged weapon, defend, wait or abandon the hunt. Native movement and strikes execute choices without model calls for every step. No role assignment forces a charge or makes the animal run into a trap.

Losing the animal preserves only each person's own last observation. A new animal of the same definition is not the agreed target. The episode ends at its explicit deadline or withdrawal; a hunter may independently continue a different pursuit afterward. When the animal dies, the hunt closes using only each participant's permitted evidence. A hunter who has not observed the death sees no remote kill report.

Remains and harvesting follow their real owners. The first-playable agreement promises no automatic allocation of future goods. After an actual harvest, a person may offer exact available meat, hide or other selected yield through the existing gift/trade interface. The agreement view says **Division is decided from the actual yield afterward** before anyone accepts. Optional spoken intentions about sharing remain attributed statements. An actual completed transfer is remembered as giving/receiving that quantity; declining a share does not grant it anyway. The world does not appoint a universal carcass owner by last hit or invisible contribution percentages.

### Trust, conflict and another meeting

Repeated useful acts, broken spoken intentions, observed violence, honest admissions and actual reparations enter ordinary personal experience. Each person may revise their own understanding independently. The player can inspect their own remembered events and accepted agreements; nobody sees a global reputation meter or the contents of another mind.

Either actor can propose an alliance or a finite ceasefire through the same ordinary communication and consent path. A ceasefire may be accepted even while fighting, with a clear notice that already released attacks can still arrive. It is a promise and an explicit immediate cancellation choice, never invulnerability. A later deliberate hostile action against the counterpart ends the acting person's participation and is offered with that consequence. A previously released projectile arriving afterward is recorded accurately and does not falsely become a new deliberate betrayal. Interpretation of accidental harm, fear, betrayal or forgiveness stays personal.

Leaving, taking cover, lowering a weapon through existing equipment actions, returning exact possessions or offering food may provide perceived reasons to reconsider. They never produce automatic surrender, forgiveness or an imposed dialogue response. No capture, restraint, forced disarm, compelled apology or enforced submission mechanic is selected for the first playable. The opponent remains free to act. Kest and Orin use these same choices; their starting hostility gives no secret player location or exception to consent.

## Clear controls and failure

Use existing person actions, inventory offers, chat and the player's existing Promises/Character areas. A compact **Together** section in Character shows current invitations and personally known agreements; it is not a new management application. The same detail component shows terms wherever opened. It presents who, purpose, exact target or place, possible material use, deadline, risk, current known progress and how to leave.

Accept and revise are deliberate controls. Natural speech can propose a grounded structured action for the speaker to confirm; a generated statement cannot accept on another person's behalf. NPCs receive the same native choices with self/other roles rendered correctly. They can choose decline, counterproposal, separate work or no reply. An unanswered invitation expires; the record never attributes that expiry to a refusal.

Changed terms disable old acceptance and preserve drafts. Out-of-range communication, missing known destination, changed physical life, consumed items, blocked approach, unavailable capacity or insufficient permission return an understandable scoped failure before costs or interruption. A technical failure does not become rejection by the character. An unknown result stays unknown until an authoritative receipt is recovered. Withdrawal is available during deliberate manual pause under the exact authority checks; it starts no physical work and does not bypass maintenance/storage/absence holds.

## Qualification and delivery stages

Implementation is divided into [CP01–CP10](../maintainers/first-playable-cooperation.md); the integrated [FPV plan](../maintainers/first-playable-validation.md) remains the whole-game acceptance owner.

1. Preserve and extend the cast as data; qualify distinct knowledge, resources, motivations and counterfactual authorship.
2. Deliver exact short cooperation and alliance/ceasefire choices through native commands, player controls, permitted personal evidence and save/load.
3. Connect guide/help/hunt purposes to actual native work, material custody, camp grants and action outcomes. Deliver one complete useful path for each; no placeholder action that only narrates success.
4. Qualify independent willingness, refusal, changed priorities, useful completion and withdrawal through ordinary live cognition, keeping every attempt and cost. Then observe uncoached players choosing companions and using the result again.

Native checks must demonstrate a successful bounded episode, changed terms, a meaningful refusal, participant loss, current-format restoration and a second world profile using different eligibility/timing/wording. Source inspection and fixtures cannot demonstrate a compelling personality or an enjoyable hunt. CE05's complete episodes remain necessary: no cherry-picked friendly response or forced demonstration closes character acceptance.

Player observation asks whether the four people are distinguishable through conduct, whether helping reduces or enriches a real problem enough to justify coordination, whether refusal is intelligible, whether players remember a particular shared event, and whether it changes a later choice. Record confusion, inactivity, repeated begging, unwanted micromanagement, provider waits and expense alongside successful episodes. Balance remains an explicit hypothesis until these observations exist.

## Research and decisions

The [commercial research synthesis](../../archive/02-research/indie-launch-commercials/learnings.md#the-recommendation) calls for useful invention and independent characters with repeatable consequences, and separates enjoyment from novelty or a market metric. This design therefore measures real joint work and later choices, not dialogue volume. The research is a basis for hypotheses, not proof that this design is fun.

[Durable-agreement research DA-R03–DA-R05](durable-agreements-feature-spec.md#da-r03--completion-needs-an-actual-result-and-suitable-access) motivates actual delivery and narrowly scoped rights, while the gameplay-priority decision avoids importing employment, currency, escrow or institutions into the first camp. That later project's existing proposals remain useful and separate. [Compelling-character authorship](compelling-characters-feature-spec.md#character-authorship-and-changing-personality) and the recorded missing-belonging/rivals experiences require source-linked memory, personally distinct knowledge and actual independent choices.

The major choices are settled for this scope: four existing friendly identities; no additional forced role; pairwise finite cooperation through existing owners; explicit later sharing of actual yield; a revocable mutual ally statement without powers; finite ceasefire without immunity; no social score, scripted interaction or control over another person's choices. Wider group institutions, enforceable future-delivery contracts and personality transformation remain separate agreed projects rather than unresolved prerequisites.
