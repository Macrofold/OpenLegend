## 1. Purpose and audience

Enable persistent, consequential lives across very large populations without making each world, character, or minute of unattended history a permanent full-cost simulation obligation. This specification is about the player's relationship with the world: belonging, attention, absence, risk, local capacity, and participation in shared events. It is not a replacement deployment, replication, backup, or networking plan.

The primary audience is a player participating in a shared authored reality. Other roles include a world creator selecting policies, a guild or property administrator granting access, an autonomous resident maintaining its own perspective, and a campaign author creating bounded pressures. Host operators retain independent authority over security and real resource limits.

## 2. Product outcomes

A player returns to the same meaningful place and particular people. Their actions accumulate legible consequences. They can own or control a protected personal space without controlling the whole universe. They can participate at any ordinary time and join forecast local campaign activity without travelling to a duplicated parallel copy of their town.

NPCs continue choices when appropriate, react to actual acquired evidence, and remember experiences consistently across coarse and detailed execution. A quiet world can produce fewer optional interactions, but it does not lie about what occurred, discard commitments, or become an expensive burst of overdue cognition when a player arrives.

A crowded experience either remains within a supported envelope or clearly declines additional load. It does not silently erase accepted effects, allow an expensive invention to bypass admission, or claim that one body can be independently defeated by every party.

## 3. Player journeys

### PS-UX01 — Return to a persistent home

**Trigger:** a player returns after an absence while the community has progressed under its disclosed background policy.

**Available information:** their own retained experiences, current permitted observations, and explicitly attributed reports of events they did not witness.

**Interaction and outcome:** they enter their actual home or a clear capacity-managed arrival path; property, relationships, current condition, and accepted commitments remain. New information explains relevant changes without inventing personal memories.

**Failure behavior:** congestion preserves the place and offers an understandable wait or safe alternative. Unavailable background detail is not replaced with fabricated exact history. Retention or archival restrictions require their separately approved policy.

### PS-UX02 — Own a home and invite a visitor

A visitor invited onto a player's land may interact only under the granted rights. Entry does not authorize building, moving protected property, reading a human's private mind, or joining the domain to a dangerous campaign. Coarse scenes and NPC helpers use the same permissions as detailed actions. Revocation and an already-present visitor have explicit handling through the access owner.

### PS-UX03 — Ada is interrupted while repairing

Ada chooses roof repair and continues it without per-step generation. An enemy reaching town is not automatic knowledge. When an alarm actually reaches Ada, her work is advanced only to that occurrence; she gains appropriate evidence and continues a chosen contingency or receives a new scoped decision. Tools, material use, partial construction, and elapsed work remain correct. A stale expected finish does not overwrite the interruption.

### PS-UX04 — Enter a partly progressed world

The first relevant player approaches an unattended area. Only the necessary detail and impending interactions are prepared. A traveler appears at the valid current point of an actual journey; a half-built structure has meaningful physical stages. Joining does not replay every old footstep or force an entire city to deliberate. A second overlapping player reuses the same preparation and history.

### PS-UX05 — Teleport into a busy district

Before successful transfer, the destination has capacity and a valid prepared arrival. The player sees technical waiting honestly. Failure leaves a coherent source-side identity and resource outcome. A concurrent arrival does not create duplicate residents or consume the same reservation twice. Departing one observer does not demote an area another still needs.

### PS-UX06 — Hear a crowd and focus

In overlapping speech, the character perceives aggregate noise plus the foreground speech it can actually distinguish. Focus can change future understanding without recovering unheard earlier words. A scream can become a justified interruption; an unnoticed body or arrow still has physical effects. No exact count, hidden identity, private motive, or transcript appears solely because server data contains it.

### PS-UX07 — Ask about a coarse conversation

A prior scene established the gist of a negotiation and a real agreement. Asked for exact words, the NPC gives the remembered meaning and acknowledges uncertainty. If an oath or recording required exact words, those retained words are available under the memory policy. A generated retelling is never retroactively stored as a witnessed exact transcript.

### PS-UX08 — Join an ongoing scene

A player enters during an NPC negotiation. Completed beats remain true, but unresolved choices become interactive. The resolver does not invent the human's consent or expose another character's private motives. A proposed resource transfer or obligation still needs native admission; conversation prose cannot execute it by itself.

### PS-UX09 — Return near a protected character

The world excludes specified background harms. Its coarse resolver cannot leave a protected character in a lethal entry trap such as surrounded by wolves solely because detailed execution has not yet run. The player finds a valid allowed continuation. Existing player-contested combat remains a separate case; leaving and returning cannot invoke background protection to undo it.

### PS-UX10 — Log out during danger

A player in an admitted ongoing encounter sees a warning based on permitted evidence and the world's disclosed continuation rule. Logout or connection loss does not reset the encounter. A bounded continuation uses standing choices or the agreed policy; reconnect restores control of the same actor. Return after resolution reflects actual aftermath, including world-specific death consequences. It does not begin unlimited new autonomous adventures or invent private human thoughts.

### PS-UX11 — Meet an important official

The player can petition, use advisors, attend a public audience, or reserve an available meeting under world rules. A heavily scrutinized character can use a higher evaluated baseline model tier. Their personal memory differs from institutional briefing. Queues do not become the only way to access meaningful relationships in the game.

### PS-UX12 — Attend a crowded concert or battle

The game distinguishes the supported activities of a venue from unrestricted combat. One canonical event can have additional actual viewing locations and nearby interactions. Admission or effect limits are explained before unsupported load is accepted. Crowd attention follows authored sensory rules, not hidden server distress. Occupancy within smaller cells cannot conceal cross-border coupling.

### PS-UX13 — Create an invention

A creator composes a new item or technique from supported capabilities without specifying a second coarse reality. Those capabilities supply progress, interruption, resource effects, and valid materialization. A genuinely unsupported capability explains its unattended restriction or alternative; neither generated prose nor claimed physical plausibility grants unlimited scope, private access, or expenditure.

### PS-UX14 — Configure time

A world creator can eventually configure calendar pacing separately from mechanical action pacing and real-time service obligations. The authoring experience explains which rules follow each clock. A fast date display need not accelerate fire-starting or produce one thought per fictional day. Current speed controls keep their existing meaning until the new feature is explicitly implemented.

### PS-UX15 — Defend home against a canonical invader

The community receives an approximate local siege forecast. Players can prepare, fight, rescue, research, negotiate, and rebuild. A destroyed power source changes the real invasion; prevention or delay revises the forecast. Another city's later siege inherits those consequences rather than replaying the same independent boss copy. Ordinary play remains available outside the forecast window.

### PS-UX16 — Contribute to a shared victory

Multiple actual fronts affect a finite common source of power. Contributions are recognized specifically and cannot be counted twice. An opted-out private domain remains outside the campaign. A creative world with incompatible powers does not submit unrestricted equivalent victories. A unique final death remains unique; late players encounter its consequences rather than receive a contradictory canonical replay.

## 4. Cross-cutting requirements

The canonical strategic rules are PS-P01 through PS-P16 in the [suite index](../product-scalability/README.md). This specification does not duplicate their policy inventory. Across all journeys, maintain stable identity, one authority over an effect, actor-scoped evidence, truthful distinction between proposal and outcome, finite computation, and explicit unsupported behavior.

Player-facing explanations must distinguish current facts, reports, estimates, predictions, private interpretation, and technical waiting. No hidden cognition score, exact private enemy location, or speculative future can be exposed merely to justify admission or a logout warning.

Worlds can select different risk, senses, clocks, and narrative policies through supported mechanisms. Security, actual spending admission, nonduplicated ownership transfer, and human privacy are not fictional settings. Presence, detail level, narrative significance, and model quality do not grant those authorities.

## 5. Product tradeoffs and non-goals

The chosen direction sacrifices a guarantee of identical microscopic offscreen history, unlimited same-location concurrency, constant autonomous novelty, and a personal killing blow for every participant. It preserves coherent history, meaningful local agency, continuous ordinary access, and bounded cost.

Do not implement a second complete body/inventory world, seamless global simulation as a prerequisite, automatic incompatible-history merges, a universal hardcoded physiology, mandatory chapter hours, or a permanently dedicated model process for every character. Private control does not imply public-economy compatibility. A meaningful shared campaign does not require every human to meet one NPC individually.

## 6. Quality and economics acceptance

Success must be demonstrated at a funded workload, not only narrated in a specification. The small community must remain compelling when background initiative is reduced. Returning players should recognize people, consequences, and opportunities. Tests must detect boundary exploits and systematic inequities even when coarse outcomes need not match a detailed trace.

Measure actual player experience, model consistency, perspective errors, transition errors, admission fairness, peak/concentrated workloads, background cost, and catch-up work. The [capacity owner](../product-scalability/capacity-and-economics.md) defines the comparison families; no numeric threshold or million-player performance is claimed here.
