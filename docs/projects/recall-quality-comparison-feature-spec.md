# A specific recall problem — product and comparison specification

| Status      | Current progress                                                                                                                                          | Last updated |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| Not started | DG04 product preparation, research and critique are complete; an observed repeatable omission, selected comparison and runtime qualification remain open. | 2026-10-04   |

**Conditional proposal.** [DG04 / ND20](../maintainers/needs-design.md#dg04--a-specific-recall-problem) asks whether one real memory failure warrants a small recall improvement. This design is complete only as a reviewable product protocol; it must not manufacture the failure that would activate it. Technical design, implementation, data transfer and paid qualification remain separate.

## 1. Product purpose

A resident should use relevant experience well enough that the player can build a history with them. The player should not repeatedly explain the same meaningful boundary, repair a resolved misunderstanding or watch an old accusation return as though its correction never happened.

This does not require photographic memory, universal recall, perfect obedience or constant references to the past. A person can forget an inconsequential detail, interpret an event differently, remain upset after an apology or choose not to talk. The problem is a preventable omission of eligible evidence that repeatedly makes an otherwise worthwhile encounter incoherent.

The intended benefit is a better later choice or response with an understandable relation to earlier play. Returning more passages, producing a fluent recap or improving a generic benchmark score is insufficient. The preferred result may be that current recall is adequate and no new feature is needed.

## 2. What the current sources establish

The [current memory contract](../memory-architecture.md#implemented-retrieval-and-storage) already has actor/world/timeline-scoped retrieval, exact vector ranking, direct required evidence, coverage diagnostics, durable indexing and rechecks after asynchronous work. Current lexical consumers and scoped preparation also exist. This is not a transcript-only baseline awaiting its first vector search.

The [September 26 Hindsight evaluation](../../archive/02-research/hindsight-memory-evaluation.md) is useful prior research with an older Open Legend code baseline. It proposes a replaceable candidate source, not replacing canonical memories or adopting a service. Its historical constants, prices and missing-feature findings are not current defaults.

One [recorded conversation-compaction run](../verification/conversation-compaction.md#conversation-compaction) provides a lead: in a synthetic 1,126-turn sequence with 14 compaction calls, an accepted apology survived early projections but disappeared from the final summary. Raw history remained intact. That report does not show a later consequential apology-related decision, a repeated occurrence or an optional-candidate omission. The later probes addressed other details.

Therefore **no qualifying current DG04 failure is established by this design task**. That lead can guide a bounded investigation. If the loss belongs to conversation compaction, its repair remains with [NC12](../maintainers/narration-and-conversations.md#nc12--remaining-compaction-acceptance) and the conditional existing compaction work, not a relabeled external-memory project. Synthetic large-history latency/recovery failures likewise establish their own performance problem, not a semantic memory omission.

## 3. Activation: identify one consequential omission

Record one concise problem statement before a comparison:

> At this actual later opportunity, this resident could lawfully recall this retained experience. It was missing at this identified stage. Its absence plausibly contributed to this visible problem. The same omission can be reproduced under these matched conditions.

Attach the permitted source, its acquisition and current eligibility, the later opportunity, the actual input/outcome and all relevant service/coverage states. Identify repeated occurrences and their denominator. Use inexpensive examination of existing authorized traces first where sufficient; reproducing a query does not require repeatedly purchasing a whole afternoon.

A disagreement or a missed social bid alone is not enough. Verify that the experience was actually acquired and retained, that the later question calls for it, and that the model did not already receive it. If required evidence was missing, fix its required path. If the source was correctly unavailable, do not revive it to improve a score.

No activation follows merely from a new memory product, a long history, expensive vector timing or an impressive vendor benchmark. The feature can remain dormant while ordinary play and the other groups proceed.

## 4. A concrete candidate encounter

### A preference that should matter, without becoming a command

Use Rin and a human in the supported personal world. During a real heard exchange, the human expresses a meaningful boundary, such as wanting to work independently and preferring to ask for help themselves. The exchange ends. Later, after ordinary intervening play, Rin has a real supported opportunity to choose an activity while the human is working on a visible or disclosed creation.

The earlier words must remain eligible evidence in the chosen snapshot. If they remain in the active conversation or a required subject/commitment path, this is a control of that path rather than an optional-retrieval case. If the source has legitimately been retired or no longer applies, record that fact and choose a different actual omission; do not alter the history until the candidate looks useful.

Useful behavior can include continuing independently, taking a quiet interest without interrupting, waiting, leaving or asking a proportionate clarification. A genuinely important new circumstance may warrant an approach despite the preference. The evaluation should ask whether the evidence is available and the resulting choice is intelligible, not whether Rin always obeys a prescribed answer.

An actual later invitation changes the situation. Rin can respond, postpone or decline for a present reason. The earlier preference must not become an indefinite no-contact rule that overrides new evidence. Equally, an invitation to discuss this tool is not blanket consent to constant future interruptions.

### What the player experiences

The human notices continuity through behavior: fewer purposeless repeated bids, a relevant acknowledgment where natural, or a choice that respects the context. No banner should announce that the resident found a memory, and no private source list should appear merely to justify a response.

If addressed about the past and genuinely uncertain, Rin can say so or ask a focused question. They should not manufacture an exact quotation, an unobserved motive or a confident invented explanation. If they have a faithful retained gist but no exact words, ordinary paraphrase is appropriate.

The candidate is illustrative until a current trace qualifies. A repeated resolved-misunderstanding or apology problem may become the first selected case if it has stronger evidence. Select one case and its real reason to matter; do not broaden the project into every possible long-term memory ambition.

## 5. Locate the failure before changing retrieval

| What the evidence shows                                                                    | Product meaning                                                    | Correct next owner or action                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Rin never heard/saw the source                                                             | They lack that experience.                                         | Preserve ignorance; inspect perception only if actual acquisition was wrong.         |
| The relevant detail was never recorded or was deliberately retired                         | Search cannot find an eligible detail that does not exist.         | Evaluate the specific acquisition/consolidation/retention decision if it harms play. |
| The source is private, forgotten, corrected away or from a discarded future                | Exclusion is required.                                             | Do not repair by expanding access or reviving it.                                    |
| A source required for the current conversation, trigger or obligation is absent            | A coverage guarantee failed.                                       | Repair the native required-evidence or conversation owner.                           |
| An eligible optional source never became a candidate                                       | Retrieval may have omitted useful evidence.                        | Compare current selection with a modest native improvement.                          |
| The candidate arrived but attention rejected it                                            | The selection judgment may be wrong.                               | Inspect the attention rubric/context before adding another search service.           |
| Selected evidence was omitted, compressed incorrectly or misattributed before the decision | Preparation/presentation lost meaning.                             | Repair that boundary; preserve source modality and uncertainty.                      |
| Complete correct evidence reached the model but the choice was incoherent                  | Recall succeeded; interpretation or decision quality may not have. | Use the character/agency evaluation owner.                                           |
| Correct evidence informed a disagreeable but coherent choice                               | The resident has their own judgment.                               | Evaluate the encounter as play, not an automatic retrieval failure.                  |

For diagnosis, an explicitly labeled offline comparison may supply the already permitted omitted source through the existing projection to see whether it matters. That is a diagnostic condition, not a deployable strategy or proof that a future retriever will find it. Never insert the desired answer, another actor's knowledge or a fabricated source.

## 6. Three possible comparators, in economical order

### A. Current Open Legend

Pin the actual build, policies, history and model configuration at activation. Preserve existing direct guarantees, complete accepted About me, current conversation, body facts, intentions and normal downstream attention. Record index coverage and cache conditions.

Do not present old missing indexes or old preselection limits as the baseline. The current [retrieval preparation contract](../memory-architecture.md#retrieval-preparation-admission) explicitly keeps eligible older history reachable while bounding admitted preparation. A small actor history may already reach all relevant candidates without buying a query embedding.

### B. The smallest native improvement that addresses the cause

Choose one targeted change after diagnosis. Examples include a relevant exact person/topic route, better use of an existing lexical or temporal clue, a corrected relevance prompt, or a narrowly repaired old-topic projection. Those examples belong to different owners; choose the one supported by the trace rather than implementing all of them.

For a true candidate omission, compare a modest native candidate selection improvement seriously. It must retain the same evidence, scope, downstream model and overall allowance. Do not handicap this arm so a richer service wins by construction.

Diagnosis can recommend a repair in another existing owner, but only an actual optional-candidate omission proceeds to the three-way retrieval comparison. Fixing the apology's loss in conversation compaction can be a successful investigation outcome under NC12. An external service rediscovering its raw words does not become the preferred repair to a required conversation contract.

If the current approach or this repair supplies the needed behavior affordably, stop. A third system is not required merely to fill an evaluation table.

### C. Optional richer retrieval

Only if a meaningful gap remains, consider a version-qualified richer candidate source such as Hindsight. Its first role is to propose which permitted originals deserve attention. It must not become a second character memory, invent a belief, write About me or decide what the player is allowed to know.

Use the same permitted sources and later question. Exclude automatic generated observations, maintained opinion pages and additional reflection from the first live comparison. If one is later worth testing, give it a separate hypothesis and full cost/authority review; do not bundle it into a claimed retrieval improvement.

An offline investigator can answer whether the candidate source finds missed originals before any live integration. Operator access remains limited to legitimately inspectable material. A useful investigator does not thereby become a suitable NPC memory path.

Identify the corpus and local/external processing before transferring anything. Permission to inspect a trace does not automatically permit sending it to another processor. Prefer disposable fictional traces or specifically authorized actor-scoped evidence, with selected access, retention/deletion and spending boundaries. An investigator's broader permitted view must not enter the NPC comparison.

Supply only eligible actor-scoped material before external processing, not an omniscient world history filtered afterward. Resolve candidate references and every contributing source of a grouped result, then load current canonical permitted wording. A generated fact with one plausible citation or truncated lineage is insufficient. Missing mappings make the candidate unusable; they do not authorize treating vendor text as original memory.

## 7. Matched evidence and comparison procedure

### Fix what must remain the same

Use the same initial world and character, actual source revisions, recognition/acquisition, retained history, current scene, opportunity, required evidence, downstream model/instructions and available actions. Keep the same complete context and spending envelope unless a deliberately separate comparison names the change.

The service, models, prompts and dataset version must be identified. A moving package release, changing history corpus or a stronger downstream model cannot silently become part of the candidate. Required identity must not be shortened to pay for optional search.

General knowledge and notes for directly involved recognized subjects retain their required paths, alongside current conversation and obligations. Do not remove an accepted subject note, known preference or required conversation record to manufacture an optional-retrieval omission. If the relevant meaning already reached the model, diagnose its use instead.

Separate a developmental set used to choose the repair from held-out variations used to evaluate it. Before opening held-out results, record the number of runs, acceptance judgments, cost/time ceiling and the intended tradeoff. Those values depend on the observed failure and available budget; this design invents no universal success score or latency target.

### Use realistic histories before synthetic growth

Start with the actual permitted history where the problem occurred, a short ordinary control and a relevant changed-condition case. Preserve intervening routine and important events as they really occurred. A synthetic long-history stress case is useful only as a labeled scale/robustness test; thousands of generated filler events should not manufacture the prerequisite for a new dependency.

Keep absent-memory and contradictory-evidence controls. A richer system that always returns the old preference may look impressive on a positive recall question while behaving badly when the human has changed their mind.

### Evaluate source selection and behavior separately

First ask whether the right eligible sources can be found and mapped, with complete attribution and coverage. Then ask whether the ordinary decision uses them in a worthwhile later encounter. A retrieved paragraph is not proof of useful behavior.

Record accepted speech, actual actions, legitimate silence/abstention and later consequences. A fluent after-the-fact explanation is weak evidence of why a prior choice occurred. Compare observed behavior and source availability, and explicitly retain cases in which the right decision is unchanged.

Repeat only enough to resolve the stated uncertainty within the selected envelope. If the problem localizes to another owner, route it there and stop the retrieval comparison. If a candidate fails an authority or forgetting boundary, do not continue buying attractive dialogue around that defect.

## 8. Lifecycle behavior and evidence boundaries

### New speech and changing circumstances

Current trigger evidence, required conversation and active obligations remain usable before optional indexing finishes. A delayed index is not proof that Rin never heard something. An incomplete search should carry that status to authorized diagnostics and use the existing permitted fallback where safe.

A normal in-game clarification is a new event. “I meant you can interrupt when it is important” does not rewrite the earlier words. It changes what Rin can now understand. A repeated rumor remains attributed testimony; repeating it does not create independent corroboration.

Recognition is also separate from retrieval. Knowing a story about a named person does not identify a newly visible stranger or a same-named actor. A result must preserve the observer's actual name/identity knowledge and how the information was acquired.

### Authorized correction

Use the current source editor/correction owner. On a genuine source correction, old wording and dependent stale interpretations must not enter later cognition through a search result, a generated aggregate or an already prepared response. Recheck the current source before use; an older successful query is not permanent permission.

Current conservative invalidation can clear more derived understanding than an ideal selective repair. This design does not quietly claim narrower correction or a selective preservation algorithm. If selective repair is desired, qualify it through the existing knowledge/feeling/memory owners.

### Forgetting

Forgetting an eligible experience removes its use from the actor's active recall paths, including derived search material, cached context, exported workspaces and old provider sessions under the current contract. A retrieval service cannot bring it back through a source chunk, extracted fact or summary.

Forgetting is not a refund or world rewind. It does not restore food, change possessions, erase another witness's memory or cancel protected unresolved obligations. Ordinary human players gain no new right to delete an NPC's mind.

If all derivatives of a forgotten source cannot be reliably excluded, stop using the affected optional candidate collection until it is rebuilt from currently eligible evidence. Native required evidence and otherwise safe recall remain under their existing rules. Do not let a vendor's successful delete response stand in for this end-to-end result.

### Save, restore, restart and service replacement

Same-version restart preserves actual source eligibility and accepted understanding. Restoring an earlier world does not permit discarded-future information, even if a candidate service can still find it by date. Optional indexes are disposable; rebuilding them must never reimport forgotten or future material.

Turning off the optional provider retains the character, canonical experiences, learned methods and active obligations. It may reduce optional recall quality. The operator can observe that degradation without a fictional personality change or an on-screen explanation of database products.

This proposal requires no old-save compatibility, bank portability promise or new archive retention policy. The existing development and operational owners remain controlling.

### Delay, failure and cancellation

Native movement and already chosen activity should not stall merely because optional memory search is slow. Existing decision deadlines and budgets determine whether a response can proceed with sufficient required evidence or must defer. An optional timeout must not silently switch to a more expensive model or send the entire lifetime history.

Distinguish no matching evidence, incomplete indexing, unavailable retrieval, stale results and required-context overflow. If current required evidence cannot be supplied, fail or defer explicitly. If optional evidence is incomplete, retain that diagnostic; the resident may reasonably be uncertain without being told that an event never happened.

Cancellation stops waiting and future publication where supported; it need not cancel every external charge. Reconcile uncertain attempts before a deliberate new paid request. A reconnect or refresh is not a retry instruction.

## 9. Complete cost and performance

The product goal is a useful, timely encounter at an affordable operating cost. [D62](../../archive/05-project/open-decisions.md#d62--recall-latency-and-search-quality) withdrew the arbitrary 20 ms retrieval target. This design introduces no replacement isolated-query guarantee.

Measure time from the actual opportunity to useful speech or action, including admission, source preparation, search, result loading, attention, generation and final acceptance. Report provider time separately and also in the complete result. A faster search can be irrelevant when preparation dominates; a better answer can be unusable when it arrives after the encounter has ended.

Count ingestion/extraction, query and source embeddings, reranking, any explicitly selected synthesis, downstream cognition, retained storage, failed/uncertain work, rebuilds and operator maintenance. Separate cold preparation, warm repeat use and restore/correction rebuilding. If one world event yields several private experiences, count that fan-out without combining private actors into an omniscient shared synthesis.

Reconcile all accounting paths: current recall/action-embedding reservations can be charged and traced outside the per-decision ledger. A comparison of that ledger alone can undercount. Record unavailable pricing and reserved/uncertain exposure instead of treating missing amounts as zero.

Compare total episode cost and cost per offered real play hour, with actual game speed and history size visible. A service's search-depth or returned-token setting is not a dollar allowance. Self-hosting includes its resources and maintenance; a cheap API result may hide expensive ongoing ingestion.

Existing source-row/body admission, optional result limits and durable storage policies remain. Limiting the returned passages does not bound how much history was scanned, formatted, expanded or retained first. The technical follow-up must identify those growing paths without arbitrarily making older memories undiscoverable.

Adopt richer retrieval only when its additional behavioral value over both reasonable baselines justifies complete latency, spending and operation. Prefer a small native repair when it captures the benefit. Do not import current vendor prices into a long-term product promise or create always-running background interpretation to solve an occasional old-topic question.

## 10. Future acceptance cases

| Case                                                    | Required outcome                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Eligible old boundary in a relevant later encounter     | The source can reach the normal decision with correct attribution; the actual choice remains coherent without a prescribed sentence.  |
| Later explicit invitation                               | Both old and new evidence keep their scope; no permanent no-contact rule appears.                                                     |
| Accepted apology or resolved misunderstanding           | A relevant later response distinguishes the earlier allegation from the heard correction/apology, while permitting lingering feeling. |
| No source or legitimately unavailable exact wording     | Abstain, ask or use the faithful retained gist; do not fabricate a quote or recover retired detail.                                   |
| Current conversation, trigger or native promise         | Required coverage succeeds independently of optional search; recalling a promise does not complete it.                                |
| Third-party claim and repeated rumor                    | Preserve who said what and uncertainty; repetition is not proof.                                                                      |
| Same name, unrecognized return or different actor/world | No unauthorized identity merge or cross-scope recall.                                                                                 |
| Correction/forgetting during work                       | Stale candidates and every contributing derivative fail current eligibility before cognition/publication.                             |
| Restore and old delayed completion                      | No discarded-future evidence or repeated paid work enters the restored character.                                                     |
| Index lag or provider outage                            | Required fresh evidence remains on its real path; safe fallback is labeled incomplete where appropriate, with no invented experience. |
| Small adequate history                                  | The candidate earns no credit merely for doing more work; retaining the current path is a successful decision.                        |
| Growing/cold history                                    | Show complete preparation/search/latency/resource costs and explicit overflow, with no new newest-record cutoff.                      |

These cases are proposed evaluations, not executed results. Use the ones relevant to the selected first omission, while all applicable privacy, correction and lifecycle obligations remain release requirements.

## 11. Delivery and stopping decisions

1. **Diagnose:** examine an actual permitted trace, reproduce the consequential omission and identify its stage. Stop if the current approach is sufficient or the problem is another subsystem.
2. **Repair locally:** compare the smallest relevant native change under matched conditions. Stop if it produces the useful behavior within the envelope.
3. **Investigate optionally:** use an authorized offline richer candidate source if a meaningful gap remains. Pin its scope/version and account for complete processing; do not send private production histories by default.
4. **Qualify live only if justified:** separately authorize and cap the smallest integrated behavioral comparison. Demonstrate source boundaries, actual later choices, correction/forgetting/restore and degradation.
5. **Decide:** retain current behavior, adopt the native repair, adopt a replaceable qualified candidate provider, or defer. Record contrary cases, costs and remaining uncertainty. No default fork, replacement memory engine or population rollout follows.

The first deliverable can be a documented decision not to add anything. Character quality work, action improvements and a truthful demo continue while this conditional investigation remains dormant.

## 12. Research and its product implications

Primary sources reviewed October 4, 2026. Published benchmarks and current vendor documentation make richer retrieval worth considering; they do not establish this game's missing evidence, enjoyment, privacy or operating economics.

**LongMemEval, Wu and colleagues, ICLR 2025.** The [March 4, 2025 paper revision](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10813v2) tests information extraction, cross-session reasoning, temporal reasoning, updates and abstention, and distinguishes indexing, retrieval and reading. It reports that correctly finding information does not guarantee using it correctly. This supports stage diagnosis and controls for changed preferences/no answer. Its constructed assistant histories and question-answering measures are not live game choices, nor do they test Open Legend's permission and restore semantics.

**LongMemEval dataset maintenance.** The [authors' repository](https://github.com/xiaowu0162/LongMemEval) records cleaned histories in September 2025 and a separate LongMemEval-V2 direction in May 2026. A paper labeled v2 is not the same thing as that later benchmark. This motivates pinning actual data, evaluator and model versions and refusing cross-version score comparisons. The first game experiment still uses its actual permitted case; running a large public benchmark is optional additional evidence.

**LoCoMo, Maharana and colleagues, ACL 2024.** The [final published paper](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.747.pdf) evaluates long multi-session conversational memory, including temporal and multi-hop questions. Its final dataset reports ten conversations and 1,986 QA questions; these differ from earlier preprint descriptions. Its reported QA measures must not be relabeled as the percentage of NPC memories remembered correctly. The useful implication is to test relevant details across time and related episodes, while retaining this game's own acquisition, interpretation and action tests.

**Lost in the Middle, Liu and colleagues.** The [original study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172) finds position-dependent performance in multi-document question answering and key-value retrieval, including poorer use of information in the middle of long inputs for the tested models. This supports distinguishing presence in context from effective use and testing a larger context instead of assuming it solves recall. It is a dated result on particular models/tasks, not proof that current Open Legend models necessarily fail in the same way.

**Hindsight original research and ACL 2026 system demonstration.** The [December 2025 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12818) and [July 2026 publication](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-demo.27.pdf) report strong results on their conversational-memory benchmarks using structured retrieval and reasoning. The later paper explicitly notes dependence on model extraction quality, propagating errors, English-only evaluation, temporal ambiguity and a lack of formal user-study validation for opinion evolution. These findings support an optional matched candidate comparison, not adopting its opinions as character psychology or treating benchmark success as faithful in-world experience.

**Hindsight release and moving documentation.** The official [v0.10.2 release](https://github.com/vectorize-io/hindsight/releases/tag/v0.10.2) was available at review, later than the archived investigation's inspected v0.10.1 package. Current documentation and a release tag are separate evidence. Select and qualify the actual service/model/configuration only if the conditional experiment proceeds; this design neither installs that version nor assumes every moving documentation example exists unchanged in it.

**Hindsight memory curation.** The [current Memories API documentation](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/developer/api/memories) supports editing, invalidating and restoring extracted facts. It also explains that the original document remains unchanged and reprocessing it resets fact curation. Consequently, invalidating a vendor fact alone cannot establish game-level forgetting across rebuilds. Canonical source eligibility, complete derivative handling and late-result exclusion remain necessary. This is a documented lifecycle boundary, not a claim that the service cannot support a properly scoped application.

**Hindsight recall scope and budgets.** The [current Recall documentation](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/developer/api/recall) distinguishes relevance ranking from strict exclusion: its temporal window steers one search component and can return material outside that period. Returned-text budgeting is separate from surrounding material, and some expanded source material can be truncated. The product implication is to verify complete eligible originals and total preparation, rather than equating a date argument with a restored timeline or a result allowance with complete cost.

**Generative Agents, Park and colleagues, 2023.** The [original paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.03442v2) connects memory, reflection and planning while reporting missed retrieval and invented elaboration in a small simulated community. It informs the central acceptance question: does remembered experience improve a later situated choice? Its short study does not establish persistent-world reliability, economical long-term operation or a requirement for constant reflection.

## 13. Game-first critique and revisions

The main risk was treating a plausible memory improvement as an inevitable infrastructure project. The finished protocol can stop after diagnosis or a small native repair. Richer retrieval only enters after a demonstrated optional-candidate omission; it is not a prize for completing the research.

The documented apology loss is retained honestly as a lead, with its synthetic history and missing behavioral/repetition evidence. The Rin scenario gives the later player consequence a concrete form without asserting that it has already failed. Required conversation and subject notes cannot be removed to manufacture the need.

The other risk was equating memory with compliance. A resident can remember a refusal and still make an exceptional approach, remember an apology and remain wary, or remember an invitation and prefer solitude. Acceptance is grounded, worthwhile continuity, not a prescribed agreeable transcript.

Finally, the design avoids adding memory dashboards or recall celebrations to ordinary play. The player benefits through a more coherent encounter. If the evidence cannot show that benefit, keeping the existing system is the economical and product-correct outcome.
