## Question and evidence boundary

How can a character understand unfamiliar situations, retain an attended route, notice a quiet portrait and react to a person approaching a ground edge without recording a permanent diary of everything nearby? Mike selected reliable recent **ordinary attention**, not a required follow action; a few-real-second latency aim after justified interpretation, with possible misses; and useful lasting accounts only when warranted. Bounded background AI is permitted when its complete benefit justifies cost.

The [feature specification](attention-and-scenes-feature-spec.md), [technical design](attention-and-scenes-tech-design.md) and [base-world profile](../worlds/base/attention.md) own the proposed contract. This study compares mechanisms and records review reasoning. It does not establish runtime delivery, implementation approval, a biological model or qualification for hundreds of densely interacting actors over ten simulated years.

## Primary-source ledger

Each entry distinguishes a useful mechanism from assumptions OpenLegend cannot import.

1. **Soar episodic memory — architecture and implementation.** The [manual](https://soar.eecs.umich.edu/soar_manual/07_EpisodicMemory/) describes automatic recording of symbolic experience and deliberate, time-relative retrieval. [Derbinsky and Laird (2009)](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~soar/sitemaker/docs/pubs/iccbr09.pdf) analyze incremental storage and retrieval. Reusing unchanged structure is useful; symbolic input already supplied to Soar does not solve physical perception. The manual explicitly retains potentially linear history searches and combinatorial structural matching, rather than promising bounded lifetime cost.

2. **Structured Event Memory — Franklin et al. (2020).** [SEM](https://gershmanlab.com/pubs/Franklin20.pdf) learns event dynamics over structured scenes and uses prediction to infer boundaries and reconstruct experience. This supports separating later event interpretation from individual measurements. Its reconstruction deliberately combines incomplete traces with learned expectations. That is a model of fallible recollection, not a suitable replacement for exact evidence about what this character actually received.

3. **Unreal Engine perception.** Epic's [stimulus record](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/API/Runtime/AIModule/FAIStimulus) separates sensed locations, age, expiration and loss of sensing. Its [sight implementation documentation](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/API/Runtime/AIModule/UAISense_Sight) describes splitting in-range and out-of-range work to reduce sorting of observer–target queries. These are useful current-perception lifecycle and scheduling examples. Latest stimuli do not provide reliable recent chronology, and the documented pair-growth problem remains relevant even with bounded attention.

4. **F.E.A.R. — Orkin (2005).** [Agent Architecture Considerations for Real-Time Planning in Games](https://cs.engr.uky.edu/~sgware/reading/papers/orkin2005agent.pdf) combines event-driven and polled sensors, cached facts and incremental expensive calculations. Planning reads prepared results instead of repeatedly performing geometry. The transferable lesson is to separate inexpensive fact maintenance from expensive decisions and account for reaction delay. Its tactical facts and authored categories are not autobiographical memory or open-ended semantic understanding.

5. **The Sims — Forbus and Wright (2001).** [Programming objects](https://users.cs.northwestern.edu/~forbus/c95-gd/2001/Programming%20Objects%20in%20The%20Sims.pdf) places behavior and advertised benefits with authored objects, allowing new objects to participate without rewriting every person. This supports world-owned meanings and affordances. It does not show that a character can infer the function or personal significance of an arbitrary new portrait, plant or anvil from an identifier or a generic native ranking formula.

6. **Comme il Faut / Prom Week — McCoy et al. (2014).** [Social Story Worlds With Comme il Faut](https://cs.uky.edu/~sgware/reading/papers/mccoy2014cif.pdf) queries timestamped social history using authored categories and temporal relationships. It demonstrates useful reasoning about prior exchanges. Its core social-history account assumes events immediately become known to every character. OpenLegend must apply such queries to individually permitted evidence; importing that shared knowledge would disclose private encounters.

7. **Talk of the Town — Ryan et al.** [Toward Characters Who Observe, Tell, Misremember, and Lie](https://expressiveintelligence.github.io/papers/ryanEtAl_TowardCharactersWhoObserveTellMisrememberLie.pdf) distinguishes personal observation, communication, belief and memory fallibility. It describes a simulated town reaching approximately 200 residents over decades. This is useful evidence for personal knowledge and social propagation, but its coarse simulation and then-unimplemented gameplay do not qualify dense, continuously sampled physical encounters or this project's complete runtime costs.

8. **Generative Agents — Park et al. (2023).** [Smallville](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.03442) stores textual observations, scores importance with an LLM, embeds memories and retrieves using relevance, recency and importance. Reflections cite earlier records. Its 25-agent, two-day experiment illustrates coherent social behavior alongside substantial historical inference expense. The per-observation semantic work and growing memory stream are unsuitable as an assumed cheap default; source-citing reflection remains useful as optional interpretation.

9. **AgentSociety.** The [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.08691v2) separates chronological event descriptions from linked personal interpretations and describes grouped asynchronous execution and batched persistence. Large-population experiments demonstrate engineering for many communicating agents under specified workloads. They do not demonstrate per-observer recording of a dense physical world, faithful lifelong source retention or OpenLegend's latency target. Population count alone cannot substitute for encounters, event rates and complete cost.

10. **Voyager — Wang et al. (2023).** [Voyager](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.16291) combines current structured Minecraft information, execution feedback, a success critic and retrieval of verified program skills through embedded descriptions. This supports separating actual results from generated interpretations and indexing permitted descriptions. Its high-level game APIs and familiar Minecraft concepts do not solve unfamiliar authored realities; generated executable programs are also outside OpenLegend's trusted action boundary.

11. **LongMemEval.** The [benchmark and experiments](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10813) distinguish extraction, temporal reasoning, knowledge updates and abstention. Replacing original conversation material with summaries or extracted facts often loses detailed answers; augmenting retrieval keys while keeping original values can help. This supports retaining exact recent material separately from search descriptions. Chat-history experiments do not establish streaming physical-world capacity, privacy or a lifetime deletion policy.

12. **EmbodiedRAG — Booker et al. (2024).** [Dynamic scene subgraph retrieval](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.23968) reduces the graph supplied to a robot planner using task-aware retrieval and feedback. It supports selection before full graph serialization. Its task-conditioned filtering can still miss unexpected relevance; the paper's robot tasks and reported savings do not establish personal significance or crowd-wide game capacity.

13. **Structured Interfaces — Ray et al. (2025).** [Querying 3D scene graphs](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.16643) moves retrieval and quantitative spatial operations behind tools instead of serializing a rich graph wholesale. This motivates OpenLegend's bounded typed geometry/expansion queries. It does not justify adopting a graph database or generated Cypher: existing spatial indexes and explicit permitted inputs fit this engine's narrower boundary.

14. **SpatialText — Jiang et al. (2026 preprint).** The [pure-text spatial benchmark](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.03002) reports difficulty with reference-frame changes and integrated spatial reasoning despite stronger direct fact retrieval. Treat that as a reason to calculate geometry natively and qualify model interpretation, not proof that every present model fails every coordinate task. Its results do not measure OpenLegend's selected model or prompts.

## Ground-up candidate comparison

| Candidate                                                                                            | What it does well                                                                                                      | Material limitation                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| All received data to a model, with a broad recent journal                                            | Broad retrospective detail; relatively little early relevance filtering                                                | Observer/source writes and model input grow with density; models still must interpret geometry and choose significance. Finite age alone does not bound volume.                                                |
| Native important-situation classifiers and selected latest facts                                     | Cheap timely responses for authored physical situations                                                                | Arbitrary personal relevance becomes a catalogue of special cases; latest positions lose intermediate chronology. It cannot explain the general kitchen/portrait/family examples without additional machinery. |
| Broad received scene, automatic detailed capture, native geometry and bounded semantic opportunities | Keeps coarse surrounding context and exact attended chronology, while bounding detailed preparation and lasting memory | Coarse views can hide important distinctions; unfamiliar meaning needs real interpretation capacity, and recent overload must disclose shorter coverage.                                                       |

The third is selected, with shared immutable source fragments plus exact personal receipt references. A separate sampled background-history cache is removed: its extra copying/coverage policy is unnecessary for the chosen current-broad-plus-attended-history promise. Current broad evidence and bounded current motion support remain available; longer exact capture follows ordinary detailed attention automatically. An unattended past route cannot be read merely because another character retained the common source. Useful lasting impressions are optional and explicitly less precise. Existing evidence/experience owners retain these lifetimes; there is no second writable scene graph, journal service or semantic scheduler.

The previous selective-watch recommendation had insufficient broad context: a stationary cliff could be absent before cognition, and a fixed list of people could hide overflow. Merely renaming a slot a situation would conceal unbounded work. The redesign instead specifies spatial aggregation, separate static/unlocated coverage, bounded expansion and the exact limits of what those mechanisms preserve. This also supersedes per-watch historical reservations: one aggregate pool retains former subjects' detail while allowing normal attention to move on.

## What actually generalizes

Native code can compute received separation/change, supported boundary clearance and explicit geometric predictions. It cannot infer a destination from the nearest object, or infer personal relevance from distance. Geometry should therefore answer finite questions over exact received facts; existing cognition interprets those answers using the actor's actual context.

The broad view is a disjoint spatial cover with counts/bounds and explicit unexpanded detail, plus nonlocalized sources. It preserves presence at a coarse level, not every semantic distinction. Fair expansion supplies unfamiliar descriptions over time without precompiling a list of objects associated with a character's interests. A task-conditioned embedding rank alone would retain the very portrait-miss problem the developer identified. No examined technique removes the finite-input versus arbitrary-relevance tradeoff.

The daughter/cliff case requires received terrain geometry. Current body positions and place anchors do not provide it. A visible clipping boundary is not necessarily a physical edge. A hypothetical constant-motion crossing is not an observed future event. These are source and geometric correctness requirements, not bespoke rules about daughters. Likewise, room category can be inferred from actual furnishings/activity while proper names and unseen room extents remain unknown.

Generative Agents offers a useful contrast: its server supplies nearby object/agent observations and an authored containment tree with semantic locations. Agents begin with individually initialized familiar subtrees and update their partial world view as they observe. A kitchen or cafe is therefore largely supplied semantic environment structure, not a demonstrated inference from raw spatial samples. That avoids some grounding work OpenLegend must specify; it does not qualify this design's perception problem.

## Costs that the architecture cannot hide

Physical reception still costs work per relevant observer/source pair, especially in a dense mutually visible group. Derived indexes, static reuse and change delivery reduce unnecessary work; an attention cap does not remove that lower-layer burden. Source sharing saves duplicate values but preserves genuine observer receipts and variants. Longer history is limited to admitted attended/required coverage; there is no extra sampler. Lossless chunking reduces row/header overhead; it does not by itself bound receiver work or writes. The [technical cost model](attention-and-scenes-tech-design.md#71-growth-shared-preparation-and-invalidation) explicitly accounts for this path, including a dense hypothetical write-rate example.

A three-second private interpretation cadence for 300 people would require 100 decisions/second; the selected design explicitly rejects that policy. Dirty sensing/familiarity causes cheap inspection, not paid admission. Native relation latches and reusable quiet/active continuations prevent ordinary proximity from purchasing repeated thought. Directed communication, real consequences, blocked chosen work or other justified unresolved causes can still create a legitimate burst; shared capacity then governs latency. Finite free-interval discovery adds at most one optional dispatched attempt per actual agency interval. The complete cost includes mode selection, tools, continuations, retained uncertain spending and meaningful turn rate, not only population or shorter output.

Long-term summaries are insufficient while retaining every original and intermediate source. The proposal adds an explicit remembered-impression variant, exact sparse support for real obligations/knowledge, complete quota accounting and physical deletion. Approximate provenance can conservatively invalidate an optional recollection; it cannot prove a protected fact false. Old opaque IDs cannot remain arbitrarily resolvable after deleting every mapping. Privacy erasure across old snapshots requires sanitizing/disabling those snapshots, rather than an allegedly constant-size forgetting ledger over an infinite archive.

The world defaults are hypotheses with defined failure behavior. Native sensing, semantic quality, actor-local storage, global event/audit retention and provider capacity require separate qualification. No cited game or research system establishes their combined performance for hundreds of dense actors over ten game years.

## Design challenge record

The earlier three-round assessment completed at 18:06 EDT on October 25, 2026 addressed selective recent chronology, but subsequent daughter/cliff/crowd examples exposed missing scene coverage and overly narrow watch semantics. That assessment does not qualify this redesign.

**New round 1 — independent construction.** Separate source/geometry, semantic selection and memory/lifecycle audits reconstructed the design from actual owners. This established absent received-terrain evidence, the two-sample motion limit, finalization admission bypass, growing source closure and current director limits. Compared complete candidates rather than only refining the earlier slot scheme.

**New round 2 — written-contract challenge.** Review found that endpoints did not bound a swept route, old paths could accidentally query current terrain, occluder clipping needed depth rules, capture ordering needed an atomic boundary and nonlocalized sources needed explicit coverage. The draft was corrected. These reviews describe that proposal's construction; later decisions below supersede its remaining sampled-buffer and recurring-opportunity policies. The revision also addressed bounded-query fairness, failed-attempt redispatch and precision/privacy through fading.

**New round 3 — final contract and lifecycle review.** Independent read-only reviews checked the revised geometry, attention/fairness, scheduling and memory contracts against their source owners. The remaining retention ambiguity was resolved: queued optional thinking owns no pin; bounded preparation/in-flight leases release on expiry without acknowledging evidence. The actual walking/clock calculation confirms that a few-second interpretation target cannot guarantee immediate danger avoidance. A final cost check also replaced all-change background capture with a rate-limited sampled preview: short storage alone did not bound write throughput. Earlier sampled coverage remains explicitly incomplete when attention begins. No further concrete blockers were found at that checkpoint; the subsequent discussion rejected background copying and a potentially recurring three-second interpretation rate, requiring the reconciliation below. These are source/document reviews, not runtime, stress or live-model acceptance.

## Final reconciliation review

**Round 1 — reconstruct from actual ownership.** Independent source/history, cognition/familiarity and memory/repertoire audits mapped existing producers/callers before the design was rewritten. They found the generic fingerprint fallback that could buy AI from familiarity reinforcement, absent full motion history, and the all-unselected EC06 status. All 28 repertoire catalogues were reviewed for relevant opportunities; selected existing examples were clarified rather than expanded into a new catalogue or mandatory feature programme.

**Round 2 — adversarial review of the rewritten design.** Independent reviews challenged exact reception/sharing, cost, attention and retention from the full goals. Corrections include stationary-anchor relative movement, latches surviving attention rotation, bounded entry/newest witness proofs, reusable quiet dispositions after recurring proximity, exact discovery reserve/dispatch/failure semantics, signed physical drop tests and counting expired-but-unreclaimed storage. The familiarity implementation target was simplified to bounded original contact roots and slower repeated-contact credit, avoiding a separate duration/block subsystem.

**Round 3 — complete contract and lifecycle review.** Independent reviewers checked the final source/receipt authority, shared lifetimes, native/paid admission, familiarity, retention and affected documentation. Final corrections preserve independently retained exact speech, prevent instantaneous speech/model-job completion from renewing discovery, and reconcile the older decision register and EC06/ND19 status. No concrete design blocker remains. These are design reviews, not stress, provider, gameplay or human-enjoyment evidence; all runtime and empirical acceptance stays open.

## October 29 runtime redesign review

The [runtime redesign](attention-and-scenes-runtime-design.md#11-decisions-and-remaining-work) replaces uniform sight and repeated observation envelopes after the actual standard opening failed normal speed. Earlier research/design rounds above retain their historical scope; they do not qualify the replacement.

Three design passes were completed: independent top-down sensing, memory/storage and terrain/context proposals compared alternatives; integrated review challenged real work and information access; final cross-owner review checked current-format lifecycle, actual callers and task/limit consistency. Review corrected unique observer phases that would defeat shared moving samples, permanent unattended last-known maps, ordinary sample intervals confused with sight loss, vague terrain clipping, repeated terrain certification per anchor, broad-only provenance accidentally requiring history, lost deferred-cleanup intent and counting large frames as single query items. The selected design retains aligned phases, compact current support independent of history, exact personal frames/checkpoints, query-local feature certification and bounded existing-owner cleanup.

The [alternative table](attention-and-scenes-runtime-design.md#11-alternatives-and-selection) explains why compacting today's envelopes, omniscient shared routes, future-visibility prediction and mandatory workers were rejected. These are code/document reasoning reviews, not new research experiments, execution, measured improvement or a claim of globally optimal design.
