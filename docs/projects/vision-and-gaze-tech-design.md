## 1. Authority, dependencies and completion boundary

This is the implementation design for [vision and gaze](vision-and-gaze-feature-spec.md), specialist **B** under [SC01–SC14](stealth-awareness-tech-design.md#shared-contracts). [VG00–VG08](../maintainers/vision-and-gaze.md) own design completion and future implementation. [Base vision policy](../worlds/base/vision-and-gaze.md) owns the selected world's values, feature families and formulas; [the limits inventory](../limits/vision-and-gaze.md) owns restrictions, incomplete work and qualification gaps. Numerical examples here use those laws rather than installing another set of defaults.

Source inspection used `Macrofold/OpenLegend` contracts tip `a3d40b4d71e29d6504e9f3fcc21e76344a0a284e`. The integrated [A evidence contract](perspective-perception-tech-design.md) and [recognition policy](../worlds/base/perception-recognition.md) are the current consumer authority. Source inspection identifies implementation gaps; it does not claim delivery of the selected behavior.

B owns physical visual opportunities, available detail, optical input, accepted receptor transforms and their temporal evaluation. A owns detection acquisition, accumulated weak evidence, exposure episodes, leases, recognition, names and historical facts. C owns posture/body/contact and locomotion policy. E owns voluntary attention, look/watch/investigation choices and native animal decisions. F owns warning/preparedness criteria and combat cuts. G owns controls and authorized presentation. Existing action, spatial, time, record and world-definition owners perform their mutations. No new package hierarchy, event bus, action lane, scheduler or knowledge database is selected.

The notation below resolves the shared B-family inputs through existing owners. It preserves `SensoryEvidence`, `ObservationResult`, `Fact`, `ExposureRef`, `EvidenceCut` and the integrated exact source-revision adapters. New runtime names are design notation, not claims that exported APIs already exist.

This task delivers documents only. Future implementers must change current-format producers, consumers and validators together under the root development policy; no old-format adapter or migration is needed. Runtime tests, performance and player qualification remain unchecked.

## 3. One physical state, separate directions

### 3.1 Authoritative records

The existing spatial record owns current placement/support, numeric body, body heading and current admitted sensory joint angles. The existing action owns any unfinished instruction to change or maintain that pose. There is no second saved world-space eye position, motion heading or camera heading on the actor.

| Meaning                  | Representation and writer                                                                                                                     | Read-only consumers                                                                                   |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Body heading             | Existing heading in radians, normalized with the shared spatial angle helper; written only by admitted spatial/action transition.             | Body/attachment transforms, guard, movement constraints, visual geometry and authorized presentation. |
| Motion direction         | Derivative of the actual accepted piecewise trajectory; zero displacement means no new motion direction. Do not recover it from body heading. | Locomotion, contact/sound, relative-motion sensing, animation.                                        |
| Head/receptor joint pose | Finite list of joint ID plus yaw/pitch relative to its declared parent; pin to the body rig. Current angles live in the spatial owner.        | Eye/receptor transform, physical feature orientation and scoped rendering.                            |
| Gaze                     | Derived ray/field transform from body heading, admitted joint angles and receptor mount. An eye may steer relative to a head.                 | Physical sensing and authorized look feedback.                                                        |
| Camera                   | G's viewer/device presentation state.                                                                                                         | Rendering, picking permitted geometry and control-intention construction. No native sensing writer.   |

Position uses the existing Y-up metre convention and support anchor. A body can be upright yet face differently from travel. The selected mechanism supports body yaw and bounded yaw/pitch sensory joints; body roll, six-degree body rotation and arbitrary skeletal constraints remain unsupported. A rigid receiver has no joint and follows its actual mount. Two lateral eyes are two receptors on the same supported body, not two independent knowledge owners.

The current position setter's implicit heading write must be removed from translation semantics and replaced at callers with explicit installed movement orientation. A caller cannot preserve old behavior accidentally by writing a copied heading after an admitted independent look. One combined transition settles pose and translation, then publishes invalidations once. Spawn, teleport, body replacement and restore explicitly supply valid current orientation; teleport does not invent a traversed path.

### 3.3 Turning law and pose trajectory

An admitted turn records original start time, current actor/life, action generation, rig/locomotion pins, authored clock conversion, starting angles, goal and ending condition in its existing action continuation. The spatial current angles are the accepted result at the coherent boundary. The continuation's start anchor is progress metadata, not another independently writable current pose.

Angles follow the shortest supported arc at the installed rate, clamped at the goal and joint bounds. At an exact half-turn tie use the world's declared tie direction; do not let map iteration, camera side or random redraw choose it. Different supported joints can advance simultaneously inside one admitted pose transition; the body does not snap while the head consumes time. The solver derives their values from the original anchor and elapsed simulation time, not repeated per-callback additions. Completion and joint-limit times are native boundaries.

An eyes/head request allocates the required yaw/pitch to the eye and head according to the pinned deterministic world allocation rule. A body-assisted request may also turn the body only if that assistance is explicit and compatible. The solver reports achieved direction and remaining permitted error. A request whose required final direction is outside the supported envelope is refused before replacing work, unless the request explicitly accepts the displayed partial result. A late restriction can end the directive at the last accepted pose; it cannot leave an angle outside the new admitted body's limits.

Pose feedback distinguishes accepted-and-turning, reached, explicitly limited, blocked/incompatible, stale-target and ended. An incomplete physical preparation is a technical result through existing work handling, not a fictional blocked neck.

## 4. Existing action owner and resource compatibility

### 4.1 Narrow composition

Add one optional orientation record to the existing admitted physical action state, containing an ordered finite list of directives with at most one per installed independently steerable gaze group. A simple body has one group. Different groups share this one action, its body/resources and its cancellation; incompatible requests for the shared body heading refuse before any group changes. An idle look is an ordinary physical action. A moving, waiting, guarding or working action may accept a directive amendment only if its installed action/body policy supports that combination. There is still one actor.action, one action generation, one physical executor and one cancellation/receipt path.

Do not implement an independently ticking gaze component, background look action, generic parallel activity tree or a new claim scheduler. Existing consumable resource claims remain what they currently are. The concrete extension is an action-local pose requirement record and a shared compatibility evaluator used by offers, admission, continuation and restore.

| Directive field | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| goal            | Exactly one of observer-relative direction captured at admission, fixed permitted world point/region-derived bearing, or current-observation-bound body/part reference. No hidden canonical tracking handle.             |
| scope           | Exact installed gaze group and selected/choosable lead receptor; eyes/head-only or explicitly body-assisted. Available joints, receptor rest transforms and deterministic lead selection come from the rig/world policy. |
| ownership       | Exact actor/life, containing action generation and activity node where one exists; one admitted directive per independently steerable rig group.                                                                         |
| extent          | Orient once; maintain through this action; or maintain to an explicit simulation deadline supplied by a finite E activity. No implicit forever watch.                                                                    |
| loss            | Stop tracking, or retain the last permitted point/bearing when explicitly selected. Neither policy follows hidden source motion.                                                                                         |
| replacement     | Amend compatible current work, or ordinary explicitly authorized replacement. A failed amendment preserves current work and pose.                                                                                        |
| progress        | Original start pose/time, admitted pins/conversion, current phase and remaining goal; no provider promise or renderer completion callback.                                                                               |

An observer-relative direction is resolved once into admitted relative joint goals using the accepted pose at admission; completing it retains that relative pose rather than secretly maintaining a world-space bearing. A world point remains in world coordinates as the actor moves. A region yields a chosen bearing/point inside the permitted region under the selected action, not the private source's coordinate. Tracking consumes A's current permitted measurements at their physical boundaries; it may aim toward the last measured point between updates but cannot read a future target route to achieve perfect pursuit.

When an orient-once directive reaches its goal, it ends without resetting the accepted pose or creating another action. The current relative pose persists until another admitted pose requirement changes it. Starting subsequent ordinary work applies that family's explicit default orientation at real turn rates. Maintaining a world point is continuing work and ends with its containing action/deadline. A voluntary long watch belongs to E's finite activity and may renew only through that owner's rules.

### 4.2 Shared read-only admission and ordered mutation

1. Resolve current control/participation, exact life, action generation, installed definitions and permitted target. Classify unsupported, stale, incompatible and technical incomplete distinctly.
2. Read current body, attachments, support and action-held pose requirements. Preserve non-negotiable support/clearance under SC06. If the request changes physical volume or mount location, check the resulting body and swept supported turn before commitment.
3. Solve the requested joint/body freedom and resulting movement relationship. Check the current action's requirements, including a required viewing region, body facing, fixed tool stance and available parts. Head-only does not imply whole-body permission.
4. Return an offer with exact fixed-relative turn duration or clearly estimated world-point/tracking duration and its finite deadline, the reachable direction/alignment tolerance, any selected movement consequence and whether current work will continue or end. Offers consume the same policy and do not allocate IDs, spend resources, interrupt or acquire evidence.
5. On execution, recheck dependencies, settle old motion/exposure to the current ordered boundary, perform exactly one admitted action/pose transition, and invalidate affected physical/evidence preparation. Ordinary cancellation preserves committed effects and recovery.

The body currently has a circular movement footprint; yaw alone need not change its clearance. Attached parts can make a turn consequential. Reuse actual numeric attachment geometry and the existing physical query; do not infer that every yaw is harmless or invent an extra square collider. If supported rotational clearance is unavailable for a particular extended assembly, refuse that body-assisted turn as unsupported before mutation. Do not block free eye rotation solely because a distant unrelated object exists.

## 5. Physical visual evaluation and A adapter

### 5.1 Input and result shapes

The physical evaluator is a pure, renderer-free read of a coherent native physical snapshot with its provider-defined held interval. Required preparation lives in the existing simulation continuation. It does not mutate knowledge, allocate an exposure, read a subjective notepad or request a model.

| Input                | Required contents                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Evaluation scope     | Existing world/timeline/native phase scope; requested interval or instant; admitted definition pins and physical revision certificate. Server audience/control wrappers remain at their existing owner.                              |
| Observer             | Exact ActorLifeRef; participation and sense capability; numeric body/rig; accepted pose and complete admitted trajectories over the interval; applicable settled ability/condition values. Memory is not an eligibility requirement. |
| Candidate source     | Existing exact object/body life reference privately; actual body/part/attachment geometry, material, physical appearance/feature definitions and executed motion. It is not a permitted participant ID.                              |
| Environment          | Indexed map/assembly/dynamic optical geometry, supported light environment/sources, clock and source/material revisions.                                                                                                             |
| Required measurement | Current query, meaningful physical transition, A's due visual-learning sample, or an already ordered native cut requiring current information. Reading a UI is not a new measurement.                                                |

The B-private result has exactly these alternatives:

| Result             | Fields and meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| complete           | Ordered, disjoint held sample intervals plus actual native point measurements; each retains its evaluated range, exact dependency certificate, receptor/patch support and source binding. An empty opportunity set is a completed absence of supported opportunity for this candidate/interval only. |
| unsupported        | An optional queried optical capability is not installed or supported; supply a scoped native reason. Do not schedule required B work for that absent capability or fabricate a human fallback. Required malformed installed data uses failed preparation instead.                             |
| incomplete         | Existing work reference and remaining preparation cursor; no completed answer for the unresolved suffix. Preserve owed simulation time.                                                                                                                                                       |
| failed preparation | Existing technical failure disposition with diagnostic family/input/pins; the dependent native boundary remains held. This is not an ObservationResult.not-detected and supplies no gameplay absence.                                                                                          |

Each held physical interval supplies its private candidate/receiver binding; from/through times; sampled detection band with immediate eligibility or nonnegative support rate; available facet masks/precision; resolved permitted values with measurement and actual acquisition times; original observer frame; received patches and sampled correspondence; and definition/dependency pins. The classification is held by the selected physical law, not continuously proved from endpoints. A value keeps its actual measurement time throughout the hold. Adjacent equal runs may merge for integration, while retaining actual measurement and original-source boundaries needed by A.

This is an internal producer input to A, not a new public shared evidence type. A maps it into SC02 ObservationResult and the upstream A facet families, allocates observer-local exposure/contribution identities, integrates support once, and supplies actual acquired facts. B never returns recognized, suspected-person, knows-name, afraid, hostile, prepared or surprised. It does not create EvidenceRef revisions or change the proposed SC02 revision representation.

Capability absence and physical zero support have different dispositions. A world with no installed optical sense does not schedule this producer; an optional capability query returns unsupported. A valid installed family with no enabled receptor, including an explicitly blind or inactive body, completes with zero visual support and the applicable loss boundary for A; it does not hold simulation. Removing a previously available sense ends its supported live facets through that same accepted transition. Missing required rig, geometry, material or trajectory data instead fails installation or required preparation. Optional missing descriptors omit only those facets. These cases must not share a fallback that either freezes a valid blind body or treats missing physical data as blindness.

Body/appearance/item relation participants bind the permitted exposure unless an eligible prior association exists. A later identity match cannot be inserted as a circular prerequisite of the features that established it.

Sampled physical correspondence follows supported received patches and their permitted uncertainty across adjacent completed samples. Private source equality never proves it. Crossing, occlusion or indistinguishable overlapping candidates can make correspondence unresolved; preserve observations and let A retain ambiguity. The sampled law may miss a shorter unrepresented optical gap, as explicitly inventoried. Seeing two surfaces does not by itself establish two people.

### 5.3 Current values, measurements and negative results

Canonical visual measurements occur on the active-scope epoch grid and actual physical discontinuities specified in §9. Actual impulse/event cues use their exact native occurrence. Dynamic locations/features are measured at those same samples, not at every precision-grid crossing, learning deadline, evidence cut or render read. A static dependency-proved value can cover its interval without another sample. B does not turn the latest measured point into a continuous trajectory.

The latest permitted point, feature interval or appearance value is resolved before handing it to A. A may keep that value in its current lease with separate value-measured and availability-confirmed intervals. B supplies new values only at canonical physical boundaries; A's capture operation copies the values already acquired for its ordered cut. A request from a later display cannot acquire a previously missed event or rewrite a prior cut.

A completed no-opportunity result covers the candidate and evaluated interval. A bounded search can use a completed set of examined geometry/intervals, but B does not conclude a whole building empty or select E's search wording. Missing resident state, unresolved geometry and unsupported capabilities are not negative observations.

### 5.4 Physical correspondence without a hidden tracking identity

B can certify that a received physical part remained separately followable; A alone decides whether that supports a current body binding, an episode or a remembered individual. The initial certificate uses a **uniquely separable received patch**, not a whole-body bounding sphere, private source equality or another face matcher.

For each supporting receptor, use only positive-area patches admitted by the world's [received-location mask](../worlds/base/vision-and-gaze.md#location-precision). Give each patch a private yaw/pitch/radial uncertainty cell in that receptor's physical observation frame. Select the world's existing location tier using that receptor's own detection quality; do not borrow another receptor's finer precision. At bearing precision, use the patch direction's nearest angular grid centre plus/minus half a step (±2.5°) on each axis, with radial interval from zero to the receptor's finite range. At point precision, surround its nearest world-grid centre with a box of half a grid step on each side; at region precision, each axis spans from `floor(coordinate/step)×step` to that lower bound plus one full step. Use the world's existing grid origin and tie rule. Project that box's complete angular and radial extrema, not only corner samples where an interior extremum is possible. Split yaw intervals at the wrap seam. A cell containing the receptor covers the full field and cannot establish uniqueness. These are private resolution bounds; public location still uses the existing cluster projection, with no added precise coordinates.

A received patch is a unique witness when its cell is disjoint from every received patch cell of every rival candidate binding in at least one supporting receptor. Use the same receptor and time for each comparison, and require separation in at least one angular or radial dimension; touching uncertainty cells overlap. All rival cells are conservatively compatible in this initial family: no second appearance/identity matcher excludes a rival. Only actual sensed target candidates participate, not sky rays, illumination backgrounds or map geometry used solely for propagation. Alternative parts/receptors of one physical candidate do not compete or add support, and their native membership serves only to deduplicate work, never to prove an observed connection. Independent items remain candidates. A spear overlapping a torso patch does not defeat a separate clear head witness; an actually received background wall is usually separated by depth. Neither example grants a hidden carrier relation.

An instantaneous unique witness permits the current physical result. Between adjacent canonical samples, continue it only when the received uncertainty cells have an unambiguous compatible continuation and conservative swept competitor bounds exclude another compatible received binding from that handoff. Reuse existing finite swept bounds; an overlap with unresolved ordering withholds correspondence rather than invoking a new temporal root solver. A receptor/patch handoff additionally needs the received same-surface adjacency or authored visible seam described below at the relevant sample. Native parent/source equality cannot repair a missing bridge. A sampled loss, ambiguity, teleport or unsupported handoff ends the certificate while leaving independently supported detection intact. This is sampled continuity; it does not assert uninterrupted real optical support inside the cell.

The completed private handoff carries the evaluated interval, original frame/receptor, received/witness patch mask, instantaneous correspondence **unique or ambiguous**, sampled continuity **supported or unresolved**, and exact private support roots for deduplication. A owns all retained hypotheses and leases. It ends only bindings/relations that depended on the lost proof; anonymous surface/region facts and independently supported detail may remain. Current source-specific tracking cannot follow a hidden true source across that break. A may establish a new binding or correlate later supported facts; B neither chooses a remembered person nor exposes the rival set as a person count. B retains the existing maximum, rather than sum, across alternative body/part/receptor support for the same physical cue. It supplies support provenance for A's once-only accumulation and hypothesis rules; it does not fuse distinct cues or select A's hypothesis membership.

Build a disposable receptor-local angular interval index over completed local received candidates, followed by radial rejection. Charge candidate and overlapping-cell comparisons to the existing work allowance. Evaluate witness, precision and bridge changes at the canonical measurements and actual discontinuities; include conservative swept candidates without enumerating every possible pair. Incomplete required competitor preparation holds the coherent prefix. Ambiguous correspondence is a completed weaker result, not a technical failure and not evidence of an extra person. No new saved tracker, identity owner or correlation database is introduced.

## 6. Geometry, actual parts and material meaning

### 6.1 Candidate bounds and patch model

Use existing spatial/entity membership with conservative full body/part bounds. An index of support anchors must expand for all admitted radii, heights, offsets, attachments and their swept motion. A target centre outside range or a body's feet below the ray does not reject an exposed in-range part. Optional attention priority can influence E's choices, never suppress a physically mandatory warning/acquisition or postpone lease loss.

No per-frame all-observer × all-target scan is selected. Normal work is proportional to affected local index visits, admitted sample/receptor pairs, intersected optical primitives, relevant light/background paths and actual temporal transitions. A genuinely dense crowd in which everyone can see everyone can have quadratic real interaction count; this design does not claim otherwise. The limits inventory explicitly leaves total dense-scene capacity unqualified. Changing a cache or a renderer light cap is not an answer to that capacity question.

Use existing monotone owner revisions and actual changed regions. A same-cell test is insufficient for turning, shadows, small gaps and within-cell motion. Use those existing optical-region dependencies, or conservatively rebuild the affected region when they are unavailable, rather than add a source-specific polling path. A moving opaque body can change another pair's sight/light even if it is not their target. Source-output changes and head-only turns must issue sensory invalidation through the same accepted transition path as translation.

Cache immutable prepared physical inputs/results with complete keys. Keep instantaneous geometry, shared environment samples and due-cell preparation separate from A’s acquired state. A point cache grants only its selected held cell or dependency-proved static interval; eviction does not create another measurement. Rebuild derived inputs conservatively while retaining the saved accepted held values and original grid. No cache miss is negative evidence. Keep existing 128/512 eviction capacities initially as engineering choices, never audience or light caps.

### 10.3 Continuations and back pressure

Charge at most the selected **256 primitive-work units per continuation** through the existing native work owner, including inner primitive intersections and correspondence comparisons. Retain a serializable-or-rebuildable cursor over stable ordered inputs, captured revisions, accumulated private classifications and original canonical cell anchors. A yield has no partial evidence side effect. Resume only after dependency validation; otherwise discard and reprepare the affected accepted scope.

Coalesce repeated dirtiness by existing source/region revision and one pending invalidation per owner; use a full-region rebuild flag when detailed invalidation provenance is unavailable. Do not append an unbounded duplicate job for each frame, each eye or each light sample. A continuation's retained primitive/input bounds and bytes count toward qualification. The population/index/overlapping-light totals can still grow; there is no claim that the queue as a whole is bounded merely because each slice is.

If required vision work is incomplete, the native owner yields at its current coherent boundary before allowing a dependent attack, knowledge read or publication to observe a half-prepared result. Existing unrelated work can proceed only where its owner already proves independence; B creates no alternate clock. User-visible diagnostics belong to existing technical progress/error handling, not an in-world stealth penalty. A terminal family failure follows §9.4; ordinary work exhaustion resumes without resetting progress or duplicating acquisitions.

### 10.4 Performance qualification contract

VG06 and VG08 must measure whole native callers, not only the raw ray function. Report candidate/index visits, receiver/target/patch count, primitive intersections, sky/source/background evaluations, interval cells and depth, cache hit/eviction rate, dirty queue size/oldest age, continuation retained bytes, accepted simulation progress and total latency. Keep world setup, actual contributors, speed multiplier and cache state in the benchmark record.

Required workload families are: one moving observer in sparse terrain; the same observer in dense foliage/architecture; a moving crowd with local mutual visibility; many stationary observers while one body turns; one light changing behind a wall; many overlapping real lights; sunrise over an occupied settlement; a moving assembly/door; repeated body/attachment changes; and cold restore of the same scenes. Compare normal and accelerated clocks at equal simulation progress, including the old four-metre threshold crossing. Double spatially distant unrelated population separately from doubling real local contributors. The former should not impose an all-pairs slope; the latter must report its real growth honestly.

No millisecond, population, frame-rate or hardware pass number is invented here. Before claiming production capacity, the existing performance owner must record representative hardware/scene envelopes and measured acceptance targets under the limits policy. That explicit qualification dependency cannot be closed by a microbenchmark, fixed nearest-N selection or reducing rendered lights. Unsupported installed physical families are refused before semantic publication; the selected sampled fidelity is explicit rather than an accidental optimization.

## 11. Persistence, restore and lifecycle

Persist current admitted spatial body heading and sensory-joint angles; exact body/rig/feature/material/light/clock definition pins; actual optical/body/attachment values; and existing object/source enabled state. Persist the unfinished orientation directive in the existing action continuation, including original start/segment anchors, next canonical control tick, accepted goal, target's scoped life/reference where applicable, loss behavior, action generation and orientation amendment revision. Save A's acquisition/weak-progress/current-support state through A, not in the gaze record.

Do not save renderer camera direction as a physical gaze, derived eye world positions as another authority, cached visibility booleans, speculative future observations, independently copied recognition features, GPU light/shadow state or a second watch timer. The current accepted pose and original continuation anchor must agree at the saved simulation time. A future control tick is derived from the original grid/index; restore does not schedule it at an arbitrary fresh interval from now.

Current-format validation must reject missing/nonfinite required fields, impossible joint indices/ranges, cyclic/missing mounts, incompatible action claims, mismatched pose/trajectory anchors, stale actor or target life, unresolved mandatory definition pins, invalid material/shape family, duplicate feature/patch IDs and impossible source/attachment transforms. A missing optional gaze directive means no ongoing turn; it does not make required current rig data optional. A body explicitly authored with no optical receptors is valid. A world missing required visual definitions is invalid installation rather than universal blindness or generic human fallback.

Load the coherent native spatial/action/world state first, validate all pins and supported capability families, then rebind A's current support under its restore contract before publishing a personal view or resuming an outcome. Drop physical caches and rebuild required physical caches from saved original trajectory and sampling anchors. Pure revalidation/confirmation does not add exposure, recognition learning or another native signal; actual unprocessed time advances once through the ordinary owner. The same saved A source revision cannot be reissued as a new event.

On death/destruction/body replacement, end dependent actual optical support at the owning transition and let A finalize only its affected leases. On control handoff, G/E's stale intention requests are invalid, while the body's already admitted physical pose continues or ends according to the existing handoff/action policy. A new controller does not inherit another observer's private knowledge through a gaze field. A target life replacement at reused coordinates never resumes the old tracking target or exposure episode by ID alone.

Restore during unfinished private preparation is supported by discarding that speculation and rebuilding from the last committed boundary; no partially consumed A interval has been published. If the native framework later persists a private cursor for efficiency, validation must prove identical captured inputs and canonical anchors, and discarding it must still produce the same accepted outcome. Saving cannot turn unresolved preparation into no-sight or bypass a pre-outcome cut.

## 12. Capability and authoring contract

The physical world installs supported mechanism families through existing sense/body/action/material definitions. It can author receptor count/mounts, finite range/field, angular quality, joints/rates/limits, brightness response, appearance dimensions, optical shapes, daylight and emitters within their validated envelopes. It cannot install an executable script, arbitrary unbounded expression, renderer callback or omniscient identity extractor in place of a supported family. New families require source changes, point/interval evaluators, current-format validation and qualification together. The current world-module validator restricts ordinary sight radius to at most 32 m; retaining 28 m works within that existing restriction, while installing a longer-range family requires an explicit validator change and its qualification. A finite number in a design is not proof that current authoring accepts it.

Trait integration uses settled existing contribution readers. A wide field can come from an actual lateral-eye rig; a damaged/covered eye changes its actual enabled state; high Perception improves supported detection/detail; a nocturnal profile selects better brightness response. An adjective alone grants nothing. Transparent or luminous bodies need actual optical material/emission definitions. An eye mounted on a tool or separately owned creature uses that actual observer/control/knowledge relationship; seeing through another character's eyes is not granted by this design.

Worlds without ordinary optical sight can explicitly omit it or install a different already supported sense family. Echolocation, scent, thermal radiation, supernatural perception and shared minds do not use these visible-light equations by default. They require their own authored source/propagation/knowledge contracts. A different world may use no Perception attribute, a different body field or no day/night; it must provide an explicit supported policy and compatible units. The bundled human, grazer and fixed-receptor fixtures are qualification contrasts, not global taxonomy.

Public offers expose only physically actionable capabilities, reachable aim/region, known compatibility/refusal causes and supported feedback from the observer's allowed information. Do not expose target-private field checks, hidden face descriptors, total candidate counts or unseen obstruction identity as a helpful error. E chooses whether/when to inspect, maintain a watch or redirect its limited voluntary attention. G defines input binding, camera behavior and how limited/reached/lost feedback is presented. B's capability query must use the same read-only validation as final admission so an offered glance cannot bypass the actual action owner.

### 14.1 Exact small contracts that catch subtle failures

- **Weak accumulation:** a verified constant quality in `[0.45,0.70)` gives 2 support units per normal-speed second. From empty, 0.25 seconds adds 0.5; an unsupported-signal gap of 0.10 seconds decays 0.2 under A; another 0.20 seconds adds 0.4, leaving 0.7; a further 0.15 seconds reaches 1. B supplies intervals only. Split any supported span and verify A consumes no time twice. Here unsupported-signal means completed zero opportunity, not an unsupported mechanism family.
- **Range by actual extent:** put the private support anchor beyond 28 m and an actual object/head sample within 28 m. The sample remains a candidate; outside parts remain unavailable. Reverse the placement to prove an in-range anchor does not grant an out-of-range sample.
- **Independent facets:** keep the body detected while turning its face away and moving a hand behind cover. Received identity dimensions and held relation end independently. Reopening the view supplies a new real measurement, not the old value with a new timestamp.
- **Turning units:** a selected human head-only 60° turn at 240°/normal-speed-second takes 0.25 normal-speed seconds, or 15 simulation seconds, assuming no eye allocation in that fixture. At 8× it has the same simulation duration; only wall time changes. An eyes-plus-head fixture must use its actual allocated joint goals, not this simplified duration.
- **Transmission identity:** one glass pane's complete entry/exit costs its selected pane factor once; two independent panes cost the product. Duplicate geometry for one pane is not another attenuator. A zero-length tangent is neither a full pane crossing nor a magical opaque blocker.
- **Face diversity:** different admitted ordinary faces supply their real measured proportions; identical or near-identical authored faces can remain ambiguous under A. No observer order, actor ID or creation time appears in a received identity vector.
- **Physical continuity:** first verify that a lone, unobstructed body remains followable across canonical samples through a full turn as adjacent samples within the head or torso hand off, including detection without fine detail. Then have two indistinguishable figures lose every uniquely separable witness while crossing and separate again. End the affected bindings when overlap becomes an actual sampled/received ambiguity; no private ID selects which emerging body inherits tracking or a held-item relation. Contrast with two separated neighbours and a person holding a spear: a clear independent witness preserves their supported body correspondence without using a broad body-wide error sphere as the competition test.
- **No artificial moments:** only a canonical due measurement or actual physical occurrence can acquire a cue; arbitrary queries do not sample an isolated pose. A zero-area patch adds no signal. A sampled point satisfying the installed law uses that law’s explicit finite held meaning, without claiming continuously proved positive duration. A strong source introduced at an actual boundary need not wait for a render frame.
- **No source leakage:** a hidden lamp changes a visible wall's brightness; its coordinates, exact fuel and holder never enter public/source-free light fields. Extinguishing it changes the real field even if the observer still remembers a fire.
- **Emissive background:** with no sky or opaque lit background on the received ray, place an actual non-occluding flame behind a dark target patch. Its finite emissive surface can support silhouette contrast. A ray through only the light's influence volume cannot; a solid blocker between the patch and flame removes that contribution. A fully transmitting, non-emitting target cannot create a dark silhouette merely because its own diffuse brightness is zero. Toggle or move a flame beyond its illumination radius from the observer and target patch, while it remains within sight range: both direct flame visibility and the supported silhouette must update, including an output change with unchanged shell geometry. At a source coinciding with a surface sample, intrinsic emission remains available without an undefined direct-light direction.
- **Disabled sight and self-view:** a valid body with no enabled receptor ends its visual support without holding simulation. A missing required rig fails preparation instead. A person's own covered face/back does not acquire optical facts through source-ID equality; actually visible hands remain eligible under the normal gates.
- **No fabricated negative result:** unresolved required geometry, unsupported installed shape or yielded work never becomes not-detected, no threat or an empty room. Required work holds a coherent prefix; completed ambiguous correspondence simply withholds tracking. Ordinary optical near-tangency does not invoke a terminal numerical solver.
