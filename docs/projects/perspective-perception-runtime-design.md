## 1. Product boundary and alternatives

The player should be able to follow a briefly visible quarry, hear someone before identifying them, compare what two witnesses experienced, or choose a quieter route. A character may notice an unfamiliar useful object or enjoy a sound without generating a diary entry or buying a thought every time it is encountered. Physical signals, interpreted meaning and chosen actions remain distinct. The base world’s current ten detailed-attention places are not a visibility cap or a limit on all sources of cognition context; installed policy and actor capability retain capacity ownership.

Keep the physically different producers: sight tests visibility, hearing determines received sound/words, touch evaluates contact. Replace their repeated current-state, availability and history management with one small received-change owner. The common procedure must not contain sight/body/movement branches. Movement is a series of received values; recording it requires no approach classifier, destination guessing or model-generated episode boundaries.

| Alternative                                                                                 | Disposition                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One lifecycle/diary per sense                                                               | Reject: repeats mutation, correction, currentness, retention and persistence logic and makes new channels another complete subsystem.     |
| Replay objective world events when cognition asks                                           | Reject: does not establish actual observer reception, attention or lost opportunities; hidden truth cannot reconstruct an acquired route. |
| One arbitrary event bus/store for all mental data                                           | Reject: makes goals, beliefs and received facts interchangeable, introduces subscribers/queues and hides authority.                       |
| Typed producers → one received-change procedure → existing native/context/history consumers | Select: separates the physics from repeated bookkeeping, preserves personal differences and extends current owners.                       |

The feature specification owns play and remaining product scope. The [grouped representation](attention-and-scenes-runtime-design.md#132-b-data-and-mutation-ownership) owns compact contacts/groups; [bookkeeping](bookkeeping-simplification-tech-design.md) owns direct saves, stable shared values, inline small values and reclamation. This document supplies their shared native contract. There is one current store and one admitted personal chronology.

A channel is an installed sense-definition reference, not an engine-wide closed list of the words sight/hearing/touch. Existing trusted producer adapters still implement those three supported families. Move channel display names and authored meaning to the installed world; use the existing sense/definition identity and validation machinery, not a new registry or plugin loader. A detector identifies the particular supported measurement rule. A new channel that uses existing typed value families adds its producer/definition and relevant semantic projection; it does not edit the received-state reducer or chronological recorder. A genuinely new value kind adds a trusted typed codec, validator and permitted projection through existing family dispatch. Arbitrary JSON, callback definitions in saved data and model-invented senses are not supported.

The common procedure does not absorb private producer working state: detector accumulation, contact physics and geometric coverage/cursors retain their existing owners. Their admitted values/support use the common groups; they must not retain a second writable copy of those received facts. A coverage cursor is preparation, not evidence or an assertion that unvisited space is clear.

An event account may contain several channels. Its adapter partitions their support for admission, preserving the original occurrence and each actual receipt, then the existing experience owner assembles one permitted account for that boundary. Channel partitioning must not duplicate the utterance/outcome or award repeated learning. A genuinely later reception retains its own acquisition time without inventing another physical occurrence. Testimony/inference retain their source roles; internal action results, private condition, thought and knowledge edits keep their non-sensory entry points and share recording admission where applicable. They require no fabricated sense or physical sample.

## 3. Canonical meanings and compact data

### 3.1 Track, group and occurrence

A **track** is one observer's ongoing reception through a supported channel. It can concern a body, a contact, a feature, a continuing signal or the observer's own supported sensation; it need not be a recognized person, one known entity or a spatial point. Reuse the existing exposure/source family identities and observer life. Key a track by the observer life, local contact/exposure identity, installed sense identity and trusted detector role, using the existing structured-key helper/nested maps. Measurement time, pose, value hash and storage position are not track identity; movement cannot create a new track per sample. Definition pins and changing physical dependencies belong to support. Existing continuity rules decide whether a capability/definition change ends that track. Private producer routing and public observer-local correspondence remain separate.

BK's persistent contact collection is the storage foundation, not a second track database. A contact contains its channel tracks and received groups. An internal source bucket may optimize routing, but a reader cannot join unknown hearing and visual references just because both route to the same hidden entity. A permitted association from the existing identity owner is required to present them as one known source. Different channels can contribute to the same attended individual only through that association or an actual supported current correspondence. Unassociated signals can supply a candidate or region without a person ID. Their number and separability must come from received evidence: two hidden emitter IDs do not prove two distinguishable sounds or two people. The producer may provide one unresolved received mixture; private routing buckets cannot become public subject counts, discovery candidates or assumed correspondence.

A **group** contains field members sharing actual source/measurement/check semantics. It references exact typed values and common support once, with sparse independent exceptions. Do not mandate one group per sense: a close visual position and an older equipment inspection can differ within sight. Do not mandate one record per field either. Groups partition held variants within a track; old versions leave current state when replaced. Across channels, distinct variants remain separate: an exact old visual point and a fresh approximate heard region do not overwrite one another as a single universal `location` field.

An **occurrence** is something received that happened: words, an impact, an action outcome or an admitted event account. It has source occurrence order/time and separate receipt order/time. Ending sight does not end or erase an already heard utterance. An instantaneous utterance does not need a fabricated continuing sound track or later loss event. A future continuing sound may supply both a changing track and discrete occurrences through its physical owner.

Do not merge value identity, occurrence identity, track continuity or learned identity. Equal bytes may be shared; independent occurrences and observer receipts remain distinct. Logical source addresses identify receiver/life, original source family/revision, admitted acquisition and selected source member. Group/mask lookup resolves that meaning; regrouping or repacking cannot change its fact identity, grant a new acquisition or invalidate retained support. Use the pinned typed family’s member ordinals, not current map iteration or storage offsets. No ungranted public entity is embedded in the handle.

### 3.2 Conceptual native interface

These names specify semantic roles; extend the existing types/entry points instead of introducing wrappers solely to match the names. `TrackKey`, `FieldMask`, typed values and source references reuse the owning identities and group layout. A field mask selects already defined members; it is not a dictionary of arbitrary sensor property names.

```ts
type ReceivedUpdate =
  | {
      kind: 'sample';
      track: TrackKey;
      fields: ReceivedFieldSelection;
      values: ReceivedValues;
      support: SampleSupport;
    }
  | { kind: 'confirm'; track: TrackKey; fields: FieldMask; support: AvailabilitySupport }
  | {
      kind: 'availability';
      track: TrackKey;
      fields: FieldMask;
      state: 'unchecked' | 'ended';
      cause: SupportedAvailabilityCause;
    }
  | {
      kind: 'occurrence';
      source: ExactPersonalSource;
      evidence: ReceivedValues;
      support: OccurrenceSupport;
    };

type ReceivedBatch = {
  observer: ObserverLife;
  channel: InstalledSenseRef;
  detector: ExistingDetectorRef;
  acquisition: NativeAcquisitionKey;
  acquiredAt: SimTime;
  receiverFrame?: AcquiredReferenceFrame;
  updates: readonly ReceivedUpdate[];
};
```

The batch carries only actually received content. It has no raw world objects, caller-selected urgency override or `recordRequired: true`. Source keys remain private. Nonspatial input needs no dummy frame/position. Framed locations retain the actual acquired frame; later receiver movement cannot relabel an old bearing as a new one. Do not duplicate the frame in every member.

`InstalledSenseRef` reuses the resolved manifest pin for the sense definition; `detector` identifies its trusted implementation/received-family rule. `ReceivedFieldSelection` selects a typed payload bundle and its finite member ordinals. Whole-bundle selection must remain compact; producers need not enumerate each member just to say that an unchanged bundle is still supported. Availability causes distinguish an actual negative test, invalidated pending coverage and a terminal life/capability/dependency change; they carry their real boundary, not an inferred disappearance.

A batch groups prepared updates for one observer/channel/detector at an existing acquisition boundary, not one batch per field or subject. The same source may produce multiple ordered batches within a native prefix; do not gather all movement into one end-of-prefix snapshot. The batch boundary groups work; it is not a replacement source identity. Each update retains the existing complete acquisition key (`observationAcquisitionKey`/owning source equivalent): observer life, installed channel/detector and occurrence or exposure/sample identity. Simulation time alone is insufficient. Retry/reentry reuses those receipts; independent subjects, real occurrences and later sampled boundaries remain distinct even when values/time match. No new eternal deduplication ledger or global ordering counter is selected.

`sample`, `confirm` and `availability` operate on direct received held-state variants only; testimony/inference cannot enter through them as a current physical grant. `SampleSupport` contains actual source revision, measurement interval/precision, direct receipt role and provider-defined availability scope. `AvailabilitySupport` establishes only successful support coverage. Neither substitutes current simulation time for an unmeasured field. Opaque digest revisions remain opaque. No redundant universal version counter replaces source, observer-life, control or correction generations.

The result is a bounded, transient **received delta** containing admitted source/group references, changed-value masks, actual measurement/check changes, availability changes and occurrence references. Its fields distinguish value change from remeasurement, support refresh and semantic readiness. It is computed while the owner still has the old group; consumers must not reconstruct it from a full before/after world or hydrate all current fields. It is not another saved history or a global broadcast bus.

### 3.3 Four operations and important absences

| Operation      | Common action                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | History and consumer consequence                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `sample`       | Establish/update only received members and their measurement support. Reuse unchanged payloads; split the group only for different support. Same value measured again advances actual measurement metadata without claiming value change. | Admitted detail retains received changed values and actual sample/check timing. Equal samples may use exact check-run encoding; they are not semantic changes or independent witnesses. |
| `confirm`      | Advance actual availability support for the supplied existing group/mask. It cannot create a field, measure a value again, fill a gap or grant a new identity.                                                                            | A selected check can be encoded once for the group. No field proof/materialization, new payload, familiarity credit or narrative event merely because a check succeeded.                |
| `availability` | Mark supplied members unchecked after actual invalidation, or end their current support after a proved negative/terminal cause. Retain only already-owned historical values.                                                              | One selected availability operation; no memory per field and no compulsory thought or departure story. Other channels remain independent.                                               |
| `occurrence`   | Admit the exact receiver account through its existing source family once. It does not invent a held current fact.                                                                                                                         | Existing occurrence/required/optional admission and semantic readiness apply. Source/time/words/results stay exact; no duplicate event wrapper is manufactured for this pipeline.       |

**No update** means no new information. It does not mean absence, safety, failure to hear, or successful confirmation. A partial candidate scan cannot end unvisited tracks. Producers emit explicit supported availability changes from actual tests, capability/life loss or their complete existing coverage contract. A completed negative test concerns its stated scope, not the whole world. Do not add a generic recurring census to compensate for incomplete producer invalidation.

`unchecked` is uncertainty about continued support, not disappearance. Current readers apply the installed held-availability law and mark unresolved coverage honestly; action admission still uses its actual current checks. Ordinary movement awaiting a sample does not automatically end a track. `ended` stops current permission for that channel/mask; recognition and separately retained history can survive. Private life/dependency causes do not tell the observer that an unseen source died or why contact ended. Exposure grace correlates a returning source only under its existing rules and does not keep unsupported current detail alive.

An observed changed value creates new evidence, not a correction of true older evidence. Administrative correction, personal reinterpretation and explicit forgetting use their existing owners, not a fifth freely callable producer operation. Those owners invoke the same contact/group mutation helpers for affected support; they do not maintain an alternative current store. The four operations are the physical reception interface, not an attempt to disguise forgetting as sensory loss. Only a trusted correction can revoke old source meaning; another observer seeing something different cannot mutate this person's past.

### 3.4 Group mutation without hidden field work

For each update, find the affected contact/track by its existing keyed index. Whole-group selection uses its existing reference/mask directly. A partial update intersects only addressed current groups; do not enumerate every member or search every observer contact. Apply these rules inside the current owner:

1. New content installs the producer's already prepared typed value reference/inline value and covered member mask. Extract each new field only when its payload/validation requires that work; no original, grant and capture record per field is created merely for storage.
2. An unchanged dependency-proved bundle reuses its reference. Availability confirmation changes only its actual check context; a same-valued **measurement** also changes measurement metadata. A reference/revision is a valid shortcut only when the producing dependencies prove equality; otherwise the owning typed family compares the received value once.
3. Partial update splits the affected mask into changed and untouched members, sharing payload/support for the remainder. Reuse/merge groups only when their complete source, times, frame, precision and availability contexts match. Keep a disposable context-key lookup within the contact if needed; do not scan the whole contact to merge after every field or build a second saved index.
4. Replace the affected current group paths once per batch. The transient delta holds the needed old/new references until dependent recording/cuts finish, so publishing the new current value cannot destroy an attended intermediate value. Release unowned prior versions through the existing lifetime owner.
5. A selected field reader resolves only that member and its group support. An all-member consumer pays its actual selected expansion cost; quotas, saves, ordinary membership checks and checkpoint creation must not secretly be such consumers.

Group partitions are per held source/channel variant. The same semantic field may have separate visual and auditory variants without overlap corruption; a missing value in one cannot revoke the other. Cheap typed membership checks occur at admission/loading, not through repeated full-payload validation in every internal reader.

## 4. One ordered receiving procedure

Implement inside the existing native transition and semantic owners; no new scheduler or observer task queue.

Use the caller’s actual coherent acquisition boundary: the completed observer sensing phase in `kernel.ts`, or the received contribution of an ordered event before its dependent callback. Apply all prepared contact/group changes for that boundary before attention selection. Keep the selection state at boundary entry and one changed-key delta; do not rescore the whole scene after each contact/field. Preserve the existing broad-to-detail promotion: initial selection may acquire the newly selected subjects’ permitted detail, followed by one final selection/capture; subjects selected only during that promotion retain what they actually received. Do not recursively promote again in that boundary; remaining missing detail uses the existing due-detail owner. Distinct sample times, event orders or intervening native consequences remain distinct boundaries even within one published prefix. Yielding a continuation is not a new sample or attention opportunity. Current mutation, required recording and selection publish/save together only after that boundary completes; the isolated candidate and existing continuation own unfinished preparation. Restart cannot expose half a received batch or lose a transient delta needed by a committed effect. Do not add a durable delta log or unbounded whole-crowd message queue.

1. **Prepare affected receivers and sources.** Use current changed/due spatial, contact and event owners. Share neutral source preparation across compatible receivers. Retain real observer-dependent eligibility, clipping, uncertainty, supported language and installed capability checks. Cheap preparation must precede rich detail; no all-source loop merely to find known changed IDs.
2. **Produce actual input at the owning boundary.** Each supported producer builds the typed batch from its received result. Existing PP detection/weak accumulation must first admit that result under [its source contract](perspective-perception-tech-design.md#accumulation-equivalent-cues-and-gaps); raw subthreshold physical input is not a received fact. This conversion does not duplicate that detector policy inside each sense. Required physical work and source measurement retain their actual time. A producer with incomplete work keeps its existing deterministic continuation; it does not submit an invented complete snapshot. Full vision/acoustic propagation stays in VG/ACS.
3. **Validate once and apply current state.** At the trusted producer boundary check actor/life, installed channel, source/mask membership and typed received shape. On a resumed native continuation, use its existing dependency/correction fences before applying prepared input. An obsolete physical sample is discarded/reprepared at an actual eligible producer boundary, never applied over newer current support or remeasured at an old time against new geometry. A legitimately late occurrence instead keeps its original event time and new receipt time; it cannot backfill an earlier cut. The common owner applies the batch in native order to BK contact/groups. Internal consumers reuse admission, with checks repeated only after a new trust/lifetime boundary. Invalid/unsupported optional input returns its existing explicit outcome; required acquisition cannot silently vanish. Candidate mutation remains isolated so failed admission cannot partially install state/history or redraw randomness.
4. **Resolve supported correspondence.** Run existing recognition and grounded-claim hooks only on eligible new evidence/actual learning intervals. They receive the admitted source references, not raw hidden participants. Familiarity contact credit follows its own due rule; repeated group checks do not automatically add it. Unknown-source current state remains useful without successful recognition or memory capability.
5. **Update attention and native cues.** Pass the delta to RU's acquired-view/change consumers and the existing attention owner. Value/support/availability changes have different meanings; confirmation alone does not create novelty. Existing direct urgency, familiarity, stable ongoing choices and discovery fairness apply. A newly selected track begins from its acquired baseline and requests missing detail only from its actual producer. Preserve existing bounded selection/promotion ordering; an acknowledgment is not another acquisition.
6. **Admit recording before replacing its required support.** The recording owner consumes this delta plus the existing attention transition, retaining any needed old group reference until admission completes. Retain changes for the union of actual selected sets visited by this bounded boundary: entry, initial selection and final selection, plus independently required support. If promotion measures a briefly selected subject that final selection replaces, those actually received details remain recorded; final membership alone cannot erase them. Entry supplies only an already-acquired baseline, and exit stops future detail. This bounded recording union grants neither extra ongoing attention slots nor unreceived fields. Required support uses an existing native continuation/outcome source reason; producers cannot bypass policy by labeling their own update important. Admission retains its existing distinct outcomes: current-only/no history, admitted optional history, or exact required support. If optional detail cannot fit after eligible retirement, preserve current reception and mark the existing coverage gap; do not block walking, silently truncate or claim complete chronology. Resuming coverage captures a new baseline of permitted current references with their real times, without filling the missing interval. Required support must fit before its dependent effect commits, otherwise the existing coherent-prefix admission holds/refuses that work. No unbounded spill store is introduced.
7. **Run dependent native consequences in causal order.** Required received evidence precedes the native callback/outcome that consumes it. If a reaction causes another event, its real later native order supplies another input; do not re-enter and replay the original batch. Existing bounded zero-time/native-work limits remain controlling. Consumers that depend on real starts/ends use the admitted delta; removing routine narrative wrappers must not remove those native/task signals. Recorders and callbacks cannot initiate another sight/hearing sample merely by reading current state.
8. **Publish and save through the selected owners.** Publish compact changed contacts, actual occurrence effects and admitted chronology as one coherent native prefix. Pass changed keys/ordered suffixes to BK's ordinary-native/strict-command save boundary. Optional context, wording, recall and derived indexes can finish later. A paused/failed save retains the established live/durable distinction; required command acknowledgment and revocation barriers remain strict.

The reducer performs no world-policy classification of footsteps, danger, kinship, “approach,” workshops or memories. Trusted payload-specific hooks supply equality/revision rules and permitted projections; semantic notices remain world-owned. RU02 owns removal of routine arrival/departure wrappers and meaningful outward-change emission. Do not recreate those wrappers to furnish an original record for a sample or attention entry.

## 5. Attention, recording and cognition context

### 5.1 Attention and memory remain separate decisions

Broad acquired state is not restricted to ten subjects. Detailed attention selects richer checking and chronological retention of supported temporal changes across channels; it is not an explicit follow action. Supported correspondence lets one selected person carry their received visual and auditory details. Without that correspondence, the body and anonymous voice remain distinct candidates. A sound/region can receive attention without an entity, while independent physical-feature context continues to support native cues and model surroundings without consuming all body slots.

Explicit focus or a due attention decision can change selection with **no reception batch**. The attention owner sends its existing before/after transition to the same recorder: entry captures already acquired group references with their original measurement/check times, exit ends future detailed coverage, and a no-op creates nothing. Do not synthesize a `sample`/`confirm`, refresh currentness, credit familiarity or rerun sensing to satisfy that call. Any newly requested detail arrives only through its real producer. Selection and an actual received change at the same boundary are captured once under §4’s bounded selected-set union.

Keep native world-authored salience, actual focus/work/direct cues, permitted familiarity and current discovery/stability rules. Do not map a personality interest to a pre-enumerated inventory of object IDs, run a model per sensory update, or introduce an extra background sample list. General semantic relevance to a novel object or portrait can be considered during existing justified cognition using available properties. The framework does not solve or silently expand personality interpretation.

Recording admission remains a separate common boundary: selected temporal changes, required evidence, and otherwise admitted meaningful experiences have different reasons/lifetimes. New source families use that boundary rather than appending directly. Keep low-level append helpers internal to the experience owner where practical; audit all exports/callers during conversion. Ordinary current perception never needs a lasting-memory allocation. An actor without autobiography still receives native signals and whatever short current/controller state its policy needs, without human chronology, names or model calls.

Recent chunks retain the actual received route/change sequence at the selected cadence, not an objective path between samples. A regular successful-check run stores actual first time, interval and count only after those exact checks occurred; irregular checks store actual time deltas. Changed membership/support or missing checks split the run. The current group owns its latest actual check; a shared receiver frame does not require a second whole-observer confirmation map. Equal successful-check runs preserve only actual check times/membership. Unknown intervals split them. Group masks must be available from admission, not reconstructed by iterating all fields before each append. Current two-sample motion is a pure spatial reader over received positions; it does not own recording. Exact values, units, reference frames and uncertainty survive chunking. Reuse the existing contextual feature history; no ground diary or per-attention terrain recopy.

Older trivial sequences can disappear; useful admitted accounts and necessary sparse support remain through existing retention/consolidation. Shared-payload reclamation does not bound independently retained world events, meaningful memories or accounting; those retain their own owners. No automatic summary or AI call is required at expiry. Correctness is faithful retained evidence, not perfect lifelong recall. Active contacts grow with current/grace ownership; old ordinary contacts are not a permanent collection of everything encountered.

### 5.2 Share preparation, not all mental state

Use the existing [situation-aware context compiler](situation-aware-cognition-context-tech-design.md). Perception contributes acquired current groups, occurrence accounts and selected chronology. Other owners contribute their own typed units:

| Context source                          | Truth/permission owner                                          | Must remain distinct                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Current sense or direct occurrence      | Common received-state/source boundary                           | Supported measurement and original receiver perspective                                   |
| Testimony                               | Received words/account plus interpreted claim source            | What was said versus whether it is true; asserted time versus receipt time                |
| Memory                                  | Existing personal recall/retention owner                        | Historical coverage and missing detail versus current state                               |
| Belief/relationship interpretation      | Existing mind/knowledge publication owner                       | Fallible interpretation, evidence dependencies and explicit revisions                     |
| Goals, commitments and current work     | Existing agency/agreement/action owners                         | Desired/accepted/intended work versus completed observed outcomes                         |
| Personal condition and bodily sensation | Existing permitted self-state owner or an actual sense producer | Known own condition versus hidden physical state; a felt pain does not reveal a diagnosis |

One source unit can depend on another; selection and the existing manifest preserve those dependencies without copying the bodies into a universal observation database. Self-state already permitted by the current context contract remains available; this conversion does not force a fresh sensor sample to read a goal or one's known condition. A new felt-sensation law needs its own actual producer and disclosure policy.

Select IDs/groups and necessary support before materialization, then render once with observer-scoped handles. Shared immutable payload bytes outside the acquired mask are unavailable, even if cheaply addressable. A missing detail needs an actual producer sample/inspection under its owner, not lazy disclosure from an old full entity snapshot. Keep source time, current availability and interpretation roles intelligible. Do not fill missing words from global speech, join an anonymous cue to a hidden person, or replace incomplete surroundings with a raw entity list. Open/terminal speech eligibility remains NST/source-owned: updates to an unfinished utterance do not become repeated semantic occurrences or premature introduction learning. Current instantaneous speech is unchanged until its separate delivery. A supported streaming adapter preserves one original occurrence identity and its source-owned prefix revisions/readiness; an append is not a fresh utterance, introduction, arrival or independent witness. Current/captured prefix disclosure and terminal recording follow NST, not an added per-prefix diary.

After async preparation, the existing source/privacy/control fences govern disclosure and actions. Ordinary later motion does not falsify a historical cut; correction or loss of current action permission has a different effect. Optional interpretation latency does not stop physical time, but a late answer cannot backdate a warning or effect. No new context protocol, model prompt format, tool loop or paid retry is selected.

## 6. Persistence, correction and restoration

Persist only current contacts/groups and actual support, meaningful attention/recognition/controller state, admitted recent frames, lasting knowledge/support and existing native continuations. Derive candidate scores/descriptions, lookup maps, spatial selection, public materializations and full field proofs. Channel tracks must not become another SQL row per every update or field. BK owns contact rows, inline versus shared values, ordered immutable frame batches, exact source references and indexed cleanup; reuse those formats and update callers together.

Current state can coalesce superseded unsaved values through the save owner. Admitted chronology and occurrences cannot be coalesced into final endpoints. No second durable receipt log is introduced to drive this reducer. Tiny local pose changes need not allocate a shared-value object when BK's family adapter uses inline values; equality of larger payloads can share bytes without sharing reception or permissions.

A source correction or forgetting selects affected group masks and their actual dependent current, recent, interpretation, recognition and retained support through existing indexed owners. Unrelated siblings/observers remain usable. Withdrawal becomes effective at its existing privacy barrier even if physical bytes await bounded deletion. Retained support must select needed members, not pin the whole group, chunk or old world. An in-flight read cannot reinstall revoked support by materializing a cached value.

Cold restore reconstructs current groups, existing acquisition order and pending native boundaries before their dependent consumers run. Rebuild derived lookup/selection indexes without sensing, replaying effects, crediting familiarity or resetting discovery fairness. Validate current group partitions/masks, source variants, detector definitions, life/timeline ownership and required value references at loading. A disabled/replaced sense stops only its current scope through the common availability path; previously acquired history keeps its original sense/source meaning while legitimately retained.

Update current protocol, validators and storage atomically by implementation slice. Reset incompatible disposable development saves under AGENTS.md, preserving the intended starting-world content through its initializer. There is no compatibility alias, old-format fallback, second implementation or universal state migration requirement.

## 7. Scaling and work bounds

Reuse the [measured diagnosis](../verification/performance/2026-10/attention-runtime.md#source-level-interpretation-and-removal-targets), not a new performance claim. The historical BK snapshot contained individual `CurrentFacetRecord` values, confirmations and full candidates after contact packing. The integrated BK foundation now has grouped evidence/checks and derived candidates; PP08 must reuse those delivered owners and eliminate remaining cross-channel duplication rather than rebuild them or hide loops behind a new method name.

Let `P` be actual observer/track pairs, `U` distinct prepared received value variants, `G` distinct support groups, `F` held field variants across those pairs, `D` admitted changed values/occurrences and `R` selected reader fields. Physical reception still costs the actual candidate/exact tests, potentially quadratic in a mutually visible crowd. Shared source preparation follows `U`; current bookkeeping follows changed contact paths and actual groups, with field work only for genuinely new/partial content. Selected chronology follows admitted `D` and actual group checks, not all fields of all visible bodies every opportunity. Selected presentation follows `R`; it must not first expand all current fields. Independently supported members can require `G = F`; that honest worst case remains.

| Workload                    | Expected structural work, not measured throughput                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Crowded first exposure      | Actual observer-dependent physical tests remain. Prepare each compatible received value variant once; admit `P` contact/track relationships and `G` support groups. Different masks/uncertainty still cost their real construction; do not create original/grant/capture/SQL records for each of `F` members. Only selected/required history is admitted. |
| Unchanged held surroundings | Clean scopes do nothing until an actual due/changed producer check. Successful checks update affected group stamps; selected history can append compact exact group-check runs. No all-member comparison, new value or semantic event is required.                                                                                                        |
| Received changes            | Prepare actual changed value variants and update affected persistent contact paths/group masks. Recording follows admitted `D`; retained intermediate changes cannot be reduced to final endpoints.                                                                                                                                                       |
| Long-lived characters       | Hot work follows active/grace contacts, admitted recent history and intentionally retained knowledge/support, not every object ever encountered. Existing history/optional-knowledge allowances and reclamation bound their owners; required support still needs its real admission and accounting. Cold query/restore costs are measured separately.     |

No unchanged read, UI poll or thought-intake refresh emits a reception. No new value/source is interned for an unchanged confirmation. No generic TTL walk scans all contacts every tick: producer invalidations and existing due indexes supply keys. No public context query reruns acquisition. No callback fanout enqueues one job per field/recipient; batch within existing native/event ownership. Contact path copying and changed-key serialization reuse `OrderedRecord`/existing indexes. Reuse masks and exact payload revisions; never compare large descriptions on every check when the producer proves unchanged dependencies.

Current world rates, detail allocation, history allowances and required admission limits remain selected defaults. This design neither promises hundreds of successful active actors nor lowers fidelity to pass a profile. It also does not require background AI/embedding work for noticing, recording or compacting checks. Native work and finite read closures remain charged under their existing owners, including keys/maps and reachable shared payload bytes. Decoding a compact group into thousands of temporary objects before saving is a failed conversion even if SQL row counts fall.

## 8. Concrete traces and pressure cases

### 8.1 A received route, not a story classifier

At t=100 a person enters broad sight; the sight producer samples presence/pose and actually available outward detail. The common owner installs groups; no routine arrival memory is required. Attention chooses them at t=110, so the recorder keeps their already-acquired baseline with its real times. At t=120/130/140 actual received positions turn toward a workshop, pass a kitchen and approach the observer. Those three admitted poses remain in order; the engine does not label an anvil destination or invent a kitchen visit between unsampled endpoints. If the actor knows which room is a kitchen from its observed contents or other acquired knowledge, a later interpretation may use that label. No sign or spoken name is universally necessary.

At t=145 received speech is an occurrence; it neither closes a movement episode nor duplicates the whole contact. If sight ends at t=150, one availability operation ends its affected visual support. Already heard words and appropriately owned last-known data remain. Attention exit stops later optional detailed capture. No acquisition after the fact fills an earlier missing route.

### 8.2 A voice, a body and a touch

A listener hears an unknown speaker at t=200. The hearing adapter supplies actual words and any supported bearing, without identifying the hidden body. A visible person elsewhere remains a separate contact even if the server knows they produced the sound. A supported observed speaking association or later learned recognition can connect the accounts, preserving the earlier anonymity. At t=210 an acquired touch begins; its producer samples only supported contact details. At t=215 touch changes and at t=220 ends through the same sample/availability operations used by sight. The shared recorder requires no tactile-history implementation. A memoryless animal can consume the same supported contact/sound change through its native controller without a linguistic account.

### 8.3 Partial detail, correction and pending cognition

A coat was measured at t=300 and checked through t=310; a received position is measured at t=320. A broad position check does not refresh coat detail. A hearing-only update adds its own time/precision. A pending model request holds an exact cut. Later source correction withdraws only the coat's affected support and actual dependent claims; it does not erase the heard words or another observer's independently received coat. The existing final guard rejects or narrows affected optional context before disclosure. A later position update alone does not invalidate the old historical positions. Save/reopen preserves the same outcomes without new reception.

### 8.4 Daughter, cliff and fifteen pursuers

All actually received bodies contribute broad state; only selected subjects receive longer detail. Supported familiarity can prioritize the daughter under installed attention policy. The cliff is independently acquired feature context around relevant self/task/attended/native-cue anchors, not a competitor for all ten subject slots. Existing bounded spatial queries relate acquired anchors and nearby features/bodies; no all-pairs prose or daughter-specific handler is added. Native reactions consume supported information before optional AI; personal interpretation may still miss significance. Partial feature coverage cannot assert safety, and omitted pursuer details cannot assert that only ten enemies exist.

### 8.5 An unfamiliar supported sense

A future authored channel might report a continuing vibration and a bearing without a visible body. Its trusted producer establishes eligibility, precision and interval; the common owner accepts existing signal/location families, confirms supported groups, records selected changes and ends availability. The model may interpret machinery, an animal or an unknown cause only from its evidence. Adding the producer/definition should require no reducer/history change. This is an extension proof, not delivery of vibration physics, smell, heat perception or mind reading. A future unfamiliar value kind still needs an explicit typed adapter and permitted rendering.

## 10. Acceptance, measurements and open decisions

Required semantic passes use actual currently supported producers and player/NPC/native/cold consumers. An injected future-channel sample establishes only the extension contract, not physical behavior or model quality.

| Case                           | Required pass                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Shared lifecycle               | Sight and touch use the same sample/check/availability path; hearing/event occurrences use the same receipt/recording boundary without synthetic held state. No source path bypasses recording admission; mixed-channel accounts remain one occurrence and internal/private results require no fake sense.                                                                                                                                       |
| Independent state and times    | Simultaneous visual point, heard region, older detail and attributed claim retain separate times/roles; same-valued remeasurement differs from availability confirmation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Ordering and idempotence       | Duplicate delivery in one acquisition has no repeated effect; equal distinct occurrences remain distinct; native reaction consumes evidence before its dependent outcome; new nested event is not replayed input. A resumed stale physical batch cannot replace newer support; regrouping leaves logical fact identities unchanged.                                                                                                              |
| Batch work                     | One observer phase updates contacts together, then uses only the initial and allowed promotion/final-selection passes, independent of contact/field count. A host yield or duplicate delivery creates no new opportunity; distinct measurement/event boundaries remain ordered. Save/restart exposes only completed reception boundaries, and a subject measured during promotion keeps its admitted detail even if final selection replaces it. |
| Incomplete work                | An omitted/unvisited subject is not absent; real field-specific loss ends only supported scope; quiet held state/read polls add no samples; unknown coverage is not safety.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Attention and chronology       | Entry does not recover an unattended route; exit/loss creates no lasting-memory reason; admitted intermediate changes remain ordered; unrelated current fields stay unexpanded. Focus with no received update captures only an old-time baseline. Optional overflow leaves an explicit gap while current sensing continues; required proof precedes its effect.                                                                                  |
| Identity and nonspatial inputs | Unknown voice cannot identify a visible body or count hidden emitters through routing; a non-person/non-point signal can be represented; memoryless receiver reacts without a human account.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Correction and restoration     | Partial mask correction/forgetting, pending reads, selected support, source expiry, sense/life change and cold restore preserve privacy/order without reacquisition or duplicate learning.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Context semantics              | Beliefs/goals/self-state retain their owners; current sensory facts, testimony and historical evidence stay distinguishable through the same context compiler; model output grants no knowledge/authority by itself.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Extension and world separation | A controlled producer using another installed channel and existing payload families uses unchanged common mutation/history code. Different authored held-support rules remain producer inputs, not engine sight defaults.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Playability                    | A controlled pursuit, warning/disagreement and return-to-known-place journey works through actual interfaces. Separately record uncoached understanding, usefulness and confusion; fixture correctness is not evidence of enjoyment.                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

Measurement is staged under [SR11's existing stop conditions](attention-and-scenes-runtime-design.md#138-smallest-validation-and-stop-conditions): first count actual group operations versus expanded fields and prepared rows in one ordinary path and one dense initial exposure; then the standard five-minute normal-speed required pass (≥0.98 with existing responsiveness/debt gates), followed by the existing substantial and remaining capacity/retention work. All producer activity/defaults stay present. Dense warmup failure is not throughput. Measure unrelated-history growth separately from active reception, cold reconstruction and retained references. If removals are achieved but the game remains slow, attribute the new owners before more iteration; do not launch repeated unchanged campaigns. Generalization alone is not a measured speedup.

**Decisions:** preserve existing gameplay defaults and source semantics; choose the shared typed procedure; use BK/RU/VW rather than new stores/schedulers/tooling; support current three-channel producers first, without requiring new senses; keep attention, lasting retention and context selection separate; use current-format-only conversion.

**Open decisions:** none blocking this design. Producer-specific laws for future senses, new physical propagation, deeper personality interpretation and novel value semantics require their own selected feature. Engineering uncertainties are group fragmentation, residual source/recognition cost, cold reconstruction and complete scale; qualify them without weakening the existing acceptance. Full PP07/SAI-A, VG/ACS/SPK future portions and attention/combat remain open in their own trackers.
