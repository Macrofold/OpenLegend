## 1. Fund something people will be glad exists

The proposed fund pays for a small, useful improvement that the ordinary game or its creators need and that otherwise may not get made. The result should help someone play, understand, create or share something worthwhile. It might make recovering a rejected invention less confusing, make a useful interaction accessible without precise pointer control, or supply a small reusable authored example that people can actually adapt. Those are candidate kinds of outcome, not promises that a missing capability already works or that these particular commissions have been approved.

A fund is not itself evidence of a successful creator ecosystem. If the local game is not enjoyable, paying for a large catalog can produce more things nobody wants. If the core business cannot afford its existing customers, announcing grants creates another liability. Begin only when a named authorized payer can reserve the full cost and a responsible owner can identify a real unmet need. The user has requested this complete proposal now; that conditional execution gate is not a reason to leave its operating behavior undefined.

The recommended first program is **one finite manual pilot with at most two awards**. Both address a published theme: make the existing creative game more useful, understandable or enjoyable for actual players or creators. Do not require a multiplayer economy, new region system, clinical benefit or social-impact program. A well-designed small tool, accessible interaction, reusable example or repair to a frustrating experience can be enough. Zero awards is a valid result when no proposal is worthwhile, affordable and admissible. Unspent budget is not failure that must be hidden by funding weak work.

The first call must name a supported reference experience and a specific observed problem or opportunity. “Improve engagement” and “make AI more alive” are too broad to contract. “Help a player understand what failed and continue their existing invention draft” is bounded enough to discuss, while leaving room for an applicant to propose a better solution. Keep the problem distinct from a required implementation. A grant should not force a class structure, vendor or new subsystem merely because it sounded plausible in an application.

For uncertain creative work, the funded outcome can be an honestly evaluated prototype. It may conclude that the idea is not worth expanding. That is a useful result when the scope promised exploration, the evidence is real and the reusable work and findings are delivered. It must not be relabeled a failed grant because fewer people liked the result than hoped. Conversely, a polished report is not a finished playable feature when the agreement promised an actual interaction. Choose that distinction before work starts.

The first cohort uses existing documents, contribution review, ordinary private correspondence and existing financial records. It creates no grant website, voting engine, token, scoring currency or permanent council. A coordinator should be able to explain each decision from a concise record. Software becomes a candidate only when the operating process demonstrably works and repeated administration consumes enough time to justify it.

## 2. What is accepted, proposed and separate

[D37](../../archive/05-project/open-decisions.md) accepts recognition, a voice for useful contributors and financial supporters, and exploration of a creator fund. It does not adopt this cohort size, review weights, prices, voting rights, payout schedule or budget. [Patrons and world history](../../archive/06-marketing/patrons-contributors-and-world-history.md) describes grants as an expense for new work, distinct from purchases. [The business plan](../../archive/06-marketing/business-plan.md) defers operating commitments until the core business can support them and values founder time separately from cash flow.

This proposal preserves those distinctions:

| Flow | What it pays for | What DG30 does not infer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hosting or platform purchase | Its specifically sold service and benefits | A donation, ownership of the company or automatic grant authority |
| DG27 historical dedication | One approved recognition benefit and its stated remedies | A grant vote, compulsory future funding or permanent governance seat |
| DG28 fixed subscriber allocation | One bounded amount directed among qualifying worlds | Spare money that can be diverted into a fund when unselected |
| Pack purchase and creator earnings | An acquired release and its actual seller/collaborator proceeds | A grant, compulsory assignment or permission to spend earned balances |
| This creator grant | New work or a specified exploration under an agreed award | Guaranteed popularity, perpetual support, a financial return to supporters or an entitlement for every applicant |
| Useful unpaid contribution | Work voluntarily offered under its actual terms | An automatic invoice, retroactive grant or purchase requirement to participate |

DG27 customer refunds, paid-through service, purchased AI value and retention obligations remain owed. DG28's unallocated subscriber amount returns under its chosen policy; it never becomes the fund's default revenue. Earned creator shares, payout holds and their dispute rules retain their own owners. A creator can receive a grant and later legitimate pack or participation revenue when the rights and disclosed terms allow it. The same cost or deliverable cannot be secretly invoiced twice to two grant sponsors.

The [money-and-mission essay](../../archive/08-wellbeing-vision/13-money-and-mission.md) and [tokens exploration](../../archive/06-marketing/tokens-and-community-funding.md) are open ideation. This pilot uses ordinary real-currency commitments, not speculative assets or promised returns. It does not adopt a corporate mission lock, a platform-wide resident policy, unlimited world export, an investment offering or compulsory clinical evidence. Those larger choices remain separate decisions requiring their own authority.

## 3. The selected small operating program

### 3.1 Roles and the finite round

The accountable budget owner authorizes the actual pool, signed awards and any change that increases a commitment. The steward administers the round, answers questions, records conflicts, coordinates acceptance and keeps the public account accurate. In a small pilot those can be the same person; the role combination must be disclosed. It does not let that person adjudicate their own financial conflict or spend money beyond their mandate.

Two independent reviewers assess usefulness, feasibility, evidence, rights and operating burden. Together they need enough relevant game and contribution-review experience for the selected theme. They are named with consent before the call, compensated or explicitly volunteering under a finite time commitment, and cannot apply for an award in the same round. Reviews advise the budget owner. Ordinary acceptance later follows the signed criteria; the owner cannot replace those criteria with a new popularity test.

Seek a useful contributor's perspective and a financial supporter's perspective in the advisory process described below. A qualified reviewer can also supply one of those perspectives, but buying a benefit is not proof of review expertise. If no genuine representative is available, disclose that absence and proceed with the independently qualified reviewers; do not create a fictional patron constituency or leave applicants waiting for a council that does not exist.

The proposed intake remains open for 14 real days. Selection and explanations take the next seven days, including a seven-day advisory window on consented proposal summaries. Allow up to 14 further days for the accepted scope, rights, payment eligibility and initial payment arrangement. A recipient's six-week delivery period begins only when the agreed initial payment is confirmed available, not when a provisional selection email arrives. Milestone reviews receive a response within seven real days. One seven-day correction period and another seven-day response provide an ordinary final-resolution path. These starting values imply approximately a 14-week round, with overlapping work where appropriate; they are proposals for a small operation, not evidence of ideal timing.

Money or disputes that remain unresolved at the scheduled closing date do not vanish. Close the public round as complete, partly complete or unresolved as appropriate, keep its remaining obligations funded, and stop new awards rather than pretending an uncertain payment was made. The ordinary calendar bounds new work and routine administration. Payment-provider delay or an appeal needs an explicit continuing owner and budget, not an indefinite unpaid recipient obligation.

### 3.2 A narrow grant-specific creator standard

Adopt the following as proposed eligibility and acceptance principles for **this funded work**, with the relevant clauses written into its scope:

- It supplies a worthwhile, honestly described game or creator outcome. Fun, clarity, expression and easier access count; a therapeutic or moral lesson is unnecessary.
- It respects existing authority, private human information, rights and the distinction between current capability and a future proposal. A funded demonstration cannot fabricate NPC choices or secretly bypass gameplay rules to look successful.
- Its essential interaction has a usable accessible route appropriate to the work: readable instructions and an alternative to an unnecessarily precise gesture, for example. It need not solve every platform's accessibility needs before a small useful contribution can be accepted.
- It uses legible costs and consent. The funded result does not depend on paid randomness, attendance-pressure rewards, purchased affection or manipulating a resident's apparent attachment to obtain spending.
- It has a useful handoff, truthful limits and finite maintenance obligations. It cannot require an unbudgeted permanent hosted service, unavailable private dependency or endless unpaid author support.

This standard does not ban ordinary fictional scarcity, meaningful danger, current bodily needs, a voluntarily challenging game or an appropriate local deadline. It is not a new platform-wide licensing or moderation law. A grant review may prefer a less coercive design for the particular funded goal without asserting that every independently authored world must comply. Changes to the standard affect later calls unless a recipient explicitly agrees to a properly funded amendment.

## 4. Reserve the whole promise before inviting work

Before opening the round, the owner signs a brief naming the legal/operating payer, currency, available settled budget, eligible payment territories, actual reserve, accountable people, time allowance, recipient contract and review date. A budget number in this specification is not that authorization. A forecast of new subscriptions, promised donation, token valuation, unused customer balance or unpaid sponsor invoice is not available cash.

The reserved amount covers both maximum awards, agreed recipient-paid expenses, payment and currency-conversion costs, reviewer/steward labor, required evidence and accessibility support, ordinary correction/appeal work, taxes or reporting costs borne by the program, and an explicit contingency. It separately preserves all existing customer and creator obligations. Do not fund the grants by assuming every prepaid AI customer will leave money unused or every small creator will fail to cash out.

The first pilot uses an approved platform expense budget. Dedicated external sponsorship is a later optional funding source, with its own exact restriction and failure terms before collection. Ordinary patron purchases keep their sold benefits; their gross receipts are not silently ring-fenced grant donations. The public round can say which actually settled funding sources supported it, without publishing legal payer identities that were never consented for display.

Unused uncommitted platform budget returns to the authorized platform budget when the round closes. It is not distributed to reviewers, converted into votes or rolled into an automatic second cohort. Funds still owed for accepted work or uncertain transfers remain reserved. A future dedicated sponsor must be told in advance whether unspent restricted money is returned or may support a later named purpose; this pilot does not solicit it under an ambiguous promise.

## 5. Invite, apply and decide without an unpaid audition

### 5.1 A bounded pilot with an honest invitation

The first round is an explicitly invited cohort of at most 12 applicants or teams, all receiving the advertised review. It is not an open public application promise. Before invitations, publish the theme, actual award envelope, eligible scope and payment territories, dates, selection criteria, reviewers, conflicts policy, output rights, and why this cohort is being invited. Invite for relevant opportunity and perspective, including suitable unpaid contributors and newcomers, rather than only supporters or the founder's close collaborators. No purchase, subscription, dedication, pack listing or prior popularity is required or can buy an invitation.

The steward records a short reason for each invitation against that brief, with public reasons aggregated where naming an invitee would disclose an unaccepted invitation. The owner and independent reviewers can inspect the full selection rationale under the applicable privacy terms. A person outside the pilot receives an honest explanation that this round is bounded, plus an optional ordinary route to express future interest. That is no waitlist position, promise of review or next-round commitment. Do not market the program as open and later reveal an insider shortlist.

Use one application per lead or team, at most 600 words and three links to existing work or relevant evidence. People can describe a concise concept when no existing artifact demonstrates it. A relevant contribution can be documentation, art, accessibility feedback, a reproducible problem report, code, a useful authored world or community work. Agent assistance is permitted under [CONTRIBUTING](../../CONTRIBUTING.md); the contributor remains responsible for its whole result. Commit count, generated-output volume, paid-tool ownership and a social following are not criteria.

The first payout scope is adults able to enter the actual agreement and receive payment through the adopted supported route. Publish the selected territories and other actual enrollment requirements before invitations; this document invents no legal clearance. Useful contributions and their attribution remain valid outside grant eligibility. A team names its accountable lead, actual contributors, agreed payee arrangement and material overlapping applications. A person cannot multiply their chance by appearing as several independent lead identities. Collaborating on another person's proposal is disclosed and evaluated as actual capacity, not automatically prohibited.

The brief asks only for the intended player benefit, proposed outcome, evidence or reason to believe it useful, rough approach and effort, requested amount within the actual envelope, material costs/dependencies/rights, collaborators, and known conflicts or co-funding. Do not request legal identity documents, bank details, private saves or personal hardship narratives at application. A public alias is sufficient for review; necessary private payment identity comes after provisional selection. Accommodation can be an equivalent short assisted or recorded explanation within the same content scope, not a requirement to write polished grant prose.

No applicant must build the missing feature, pay for a live model demonstration, travel, produce bespoke art or surrender reusable work before selection. Existing links and a clear concept are enough for an uncertain proposal. One clarification request can identify a missing material point; it is not permission to ask for a free prototype. If deciding feasibility requires actual creation or paid evaluation, select a smaller explicitly funded discovery outcome or decline the uncertainty honestly.

### 5.2 Selection and an intelligible no

Review each invited complete application against the same ordered questions:

1. Is there a real useful player/creator outcome, and does it fit the round's theme and grant-specific standard?
2. Can this applicant or team deliver the specified result within the time and actual budget, including evidence, rights and handoff?
3. Is the result sufficiently independent of discretionary outside approval, unimplemented prerequisites and indefinite operating cost?
4. Does it offer a stronger use of this finite money and reviewer time than doing less, commissioning a simpler repair or funding another admissible proposal?

Reviewers give a concise reasoned recommendation, including material uncertainty and disagreement. Do not convert the questions into a precise decimal score that disguises judgment. Price matters to feasibility and value; the cheapest bid is not automatically best, and a creator is not more worthy because they spent more. Where two proposals remain genuinely comparable, prefer the one whose outcome can be completed and reused with less unfunded dependency. If still tied, the accountable independent owner chooses and records the reason rather than making applicants run a popularity campaign.

The seven-day advisory window uses applicant-approved short summaries after the application window. An applicant may withhold private material; reviewers can still assess it privately, and the public summary can describe the benefit without disclosing an unpublished technique. No private objection, identity or financial circumstance appears in the public comparison. The window closes on its announced date even if an advisory role is vacant or no comment arrives.

At decision, each applicant receives Selected provisionally, Not selected, Ineligible for this round, or Withdrawn, with an actionable brief explanation. Provisional selection states what remains: agreed scope, rights, payment readiness and actual initial payment. It does not tell someone to start funded work. A decline distinguishes budget/relative choice from a claim that the person's work is worthless. Offer correction of a factual eligibility or conflict error, without promising a new merits review because someone dislikes the judgment.

There is no automatic second application fee, blacklist for declining terms or requirement to donate after rejection. Unselected applicants keep their work and rights. With their consent, a useful public idea can remain discoverable as a proposal; it is not installed content, an approved roadmap promise or permission for the operator to appropriate the unpublished implementation.

## 6. Agree on work that can actually be accepted

The selected recipient and steward produce a short award agreement: the useful outcome, current reference version/environment, deliverables and rights, meaningful demonstrations, milestones and amounts, complete costs and permitted paid evidence, named contributors/payees, dependencies controlled by others, correction/exit rules and finite handoff. The recipient can decline or negotiate without losing existing community standing. Do not replace the public theme with a much larger company backlog once someone has been selected.

The recommended schedule is 20% for mobilization, 40% for an independently usable midpoint and 40% for the final agreed result and handoff. These fractions apply to the agreed award amount, not an additional pool. The actual monetary amount is still an adoption choice. For a sufficiently small result, one usable intermediate and a final refinement may be enough; do not invent artificial milestones merely to produce three documents.

The 20% is a disclosed nonrepayable start grant earned when the parties sign the complete scope, the recipient completes the limited agreed start briefing, and payment eligibility is ready. It supports reserving time and beginning work; it is not an unspent expense float that the recipient must account for purchase by purchase. Payment must be confirmed available before the six-week work clock starts. Good-faith noncompletion does not retroactively turn this earned payment into debt. Proven intentional deception remains a separate remedies process under the actual agreement; no automatic bank debit, public accusation or private-account seizure is authorized.

If this start allocation would be disproportionate to a tiny exploratory task, agree a smaller independently useful discovery award before signing rather than disguise an advance as completed work. A separately approved reimbursable purchase is different: require its prior ceiling and purpose, pay or reimburse its actual supported cost, and reconcile genuinely unused refundable expense money. Do not apply that reconciliation to the fixed earned grant fee. No provider key, paid test budget or production access is inherited from receiving an award.

For example, a $1,000 illustrative award would pay $200 at the accepted start, $400 for a useful tested intermediate, and $400 for the final handoff. The intermediate might let a player recover a retained invention draft with clear feedback in a qualified standalone build; the final result may add the agreed edge cases, accessible path and concise contributor documentation. Those amounts are arithmetic, not an offered grant or actual funds. The scope would have to name what “recover” means against current authoritative behavior before being signed.

A prototype award has different evidence: a runnable bounded experiment, stated observations including failures, a usable artifact where promised, and a reasoned continue/change/stop recommendation. It cannot be failed merely for recommending stop. When the operator needs a compulsory roadmap feature under close ongoing direction, consider an ordinary paid commission or employment arrangement instead of using the grant label to obscure that relationship. The actual legal form requires appropriate adoption; the product distinction is supported by [CF-R12](#cf-r12--a-grant-label-does-not-settle-the-working-relationship).

### Review without an endless moving target

The recipient submits the agreed artifact and a short note showing the behavior, current verification and known limits. The relevant reviewer checks the stated acceptance evidence, rights and usable handoff. Acceptance is not conditional on stars, sales, hours played, a patron's approval or a maintainer merging the contribution on their preferred date. Technical merge, deployment and product release remain separately authorized. A reproducible standalone contribution can satisfy an agreed milestone even while its safe upstream adoption is pending.

Within seven real days the steward returns Accepted, Specific correction requested, or a reasoned dispute requiring the independent path. Silence is neither automatic acceptance nor permission to hold the recipient indefinitely. If the program misses its response deadline, pause the recipient's next dependent work clock, escalate to the accountable owner and provide a new bounded date. After seven further days without a valid review, the recipient may use the operator-caused cancellation terms below. They need not keep improving the result for free while waiting.

One ordinary seven-day correction opportunity addresses failure to meet already agreed criteria. It can be extended once by up to 14 real days for a specific mutually agreed obstacle, within the existing award and operating reserve; the extension does not grant new money automatically. A material new feature, changed rights, larger cost or external dependency requires a written amendment approved by the authorized owner before work. Routine method changes within the same outcome, rights and budget need a short record, not another public application or advisory vote.

If the operator changes the relevant game version, no-compatibility development policy does not disappear. Keep a supported review environment for the agreed artifact or fund a prospective scope change. Do not move the target, make the old result impossible to demonstrate, and then call it recipient failure. Accepted output is not a promise that arbitrary future runtime revisions will load it forever. The handoff states the actually supported revision and any current integration blocker.

## 7. Payment, partial value and a clean exit

### 7.1 Pay the award as an award

The program pays accepted amounts within seven real days after acceptance and complete required payment instructions, subject to a disclosed provider settlement range. Show separately Awarded, Earned/accepted, Payment submitted, Payment uncertain and Settled. The promised amount is in the agreement's named currency. The program bears its normal chosen transfer costs; any recipient-side bank or conversion treatment must be clear before signing, including an offered supported alternative. It must not quietly reduce a small award through a new fee discovered at payout.

DG28's 15% marketplace fee, monthly payout calendar, ordinary 30-day hold and $25 threshold do not apply to these grants. There is no minimum accumulated grant balance that traps a small earned payment. Team payments follow the agreed named arrangement, with consent to shares totaling the applicable award amount. A lead receiving and distributing collaborators' money must have that mandate; credit and ownership do not automatically prescribe a 50/50 split. Changes affect future unearned portions by agreement, never somebody else's already earned share.

An uncertain transfer preserves the original payment obligation and its exposure. Verify its result before issuing another transfer. A recipient's missing bank notification is not proof of nonpayment; a submitted payment is not proof of settled receipt. Fees that fail to return remain program expense. If the program cannot use the selected route, offer an agreed lawful supported alternative without requiring the recipient to buy a service or donate the payment back. Unclaimed or legally blocked money stays with the appropriate accounting owner until resolved under actual terms; the round's closing date cannot silently forfeit it.

All real award, payment, conflict and rights records survive world restore. A fictional creator may destroy a sponsored artifact inside a game without resetting the real grant milestone. A new fork or restored save is not a second paid delivery. Correcting a public account changes its explanation; it does not erase a settled charge or grant a new entitlement.

### 7.2 When only part of the work is delivered

Price useful separable subparts in the agreement where feasible. If the full midpoint cannot be completed, the reviewer may accept an independently useful already delivered part at that preagreed amount. The sum comes out of the corresponding milestone, never on top of the award. When a partial result was not priced, neither side must accept a convenient retrospective number: propose a written mutual closeout amount for the actual reusable result, or retain the disputed amount while the independent reviewer resolves the existing promise. Acceptance is not inferred from the operator experimenting with an unaccepted draft.

Good-faith recipient withdrawal keeps earned mobilization and accepted amounts, pays any separately accepted partial value, and ends unearned milestones. Return only genuinely unused refundable expense advances under their own terms. The recipient supplies the agreed handoff for paid work and identifies incomplete parts, then has no continuing duty to finish the abandoned project for free. An illness or unavoidable personal interruption need not become a public explanation; the public status can say the scope closed early.

For operator-caused cancellation or a material roadmap change, preserve all earned amounts, settle accepted partial value, and pay a proposed cancellation allowance of up to 10% of the total award for reserved work not yet accepted. It comes from the still-unearned award balance and cannot exceed that balance; the total award cap stays intact. For the ordinary first contract, pay the full available 10% allowance without demanding receipts for the recipient's lost availability. No allowance is owed on already fully paid work. This is a proposed commercial rule, not a statutory remedy; the actual agreement and applicable requirements remain controlling.

If the operator cancels a $1,000 example after the $200 start and $400 midpoint were earned, it pays those $600 plus $100 cancellation allowance, unless more accepted partial value consumes that remaining headroom. If another $350 of final work has already been accepted, $950 is earned and only $50 remains available for cancellation. The operator cannot reclaim prior $600 because the roadmap changed, and the creator cannot collect $1,050 by stacking the allowance on a full award. Exact separate approved expenses remain separately accounted for.

Fraud, rights infringement or undisclosed material conflicts require a scoped review. Stop affected new work/payment where justified, preserve undisputed earned claims, show the allegation and permitted evidence to the affected person, and give the response/appeal route. Withholding is not a final debt judgment. Recovery of money follows the actual agreement and authorized legal/payment process; moderators or a patron poll cannot confiscate a recipient's unrelated pack earnings or AI credit. Good-faith disappointing results are not fraud.

### 7.3 Dispute and appeal

The recipient or applicant can request one written review within 14 real days of the challenged decision, identifying the specific factual, criteria, conflict or payment error. This is a voluntary program process, not a deadline extinguishing other applicable rights. A different unconflicted reviewer acknowledges within seven days and issues a reasoned response within 14 further days, or a specific explanation of the missing evidence and dated next step. Reserve that review effort before opening the round.

The reviewer can confirm the decision, require correction, recommend an accepted partial amount, or identify that the accountable owner must authorize a contractual remedy. They cannot invent new spending authority. If no independent person can decide within the funded reserve, stop new awards and obtain authorized review or hold the affected decision unresolved; do not declare the original reviewer independent by convenience. Undisputed accepted payments proceed where the payment authority permits them. Public reporting states a dispute is pending without exposing private allegations as established facts.

## 8. Rights, credit and evidence that others can use

The first pilot requires the funded result to have a useful public reusable form, with exact component rights agreed before award. First-party code and documentation contributed to this repository use AGPL-3.0-only under [LICENSING](../../LICENSING.md). Contributors retain their copyright; there is no blanket assignment, new CLA or engine-relicensing power granted by accepting a grant. Separately original art or content must carry an explicitly approved compatible reuse license and dependency notices. No proprietary executable exception is created by calling a deliverable a mechanics pack or database record.

Use the actual DG12/DG28 ownership and publication review. Historical inventor/world-owner attribution, contributor grants and inherited open licenses remain. A fund can support a reusable definition without acquiring the originating world's private people, memories, conversation, legal names or restricted dependencies. If the useful result cannot be released with sufficient rights, narrow the funded artifact or decline it before work; do not pay first and demand everybody sign away a private world afterward.

The grant agreement names what is published, the accepted public alias and contributor credit, which background materials remain theirs, and what the program may demonstrate or maintain. Independent collaborators must consent to the supplied rights and payout arrangement. A team lead cannot license someone else's work by signing a program form. A later ownership or company change does not rewrite original attribution or revoke already valid open grants.

Recipients may run paid hosting, sell distinct services or receive legitimate DG28 participation revenue around an openly granted result. They must not sell exclusive rights they no longer possess or charge the fund again for the same specifically financed cost. Material co-funding is disclosed privately to reviewers and summarized publicly when relevant without exposing confidential agreements. It can strengthen a feasible proposal; it is not automatically a conflict. The agreement identifies which portion and purpose this award funds.

Evidence should be proportionate: a runnable example or reviewable artifact at the agreed revision, concise instructions, actual observations/verification and known failure cases. Screenshots can illustrate a UI, but cannot prove an undisclosed live workflow succeeded. Required paid-provider evidence belongs in the approved scope and complete cost ceiling, with the actual account owner's authorization. A recipient must not expose keys, private saves, sensitive raw provider replies or unrelated player data to obtain payment. Redacted or purpose-built permitted evidence is preferable to a compelled production-world export.

Public records show the approved alias, award and status, concise useful outcome, accepted artifact links, known limits, contributor credits and material amendments. Private application details, legal identity, banking, invoices containing personal information and conflict evidence remain restricted to authorized program/payment reviewers. A reviewer receives only what is necessary for the assigned decision; no game-admin or human-private inspection authority comes with the role.

Propose keeping declined/withdrawn private application materials for 90 real days after final disposition, then deleting them unless a disclosed unresolved dispute or applicable obligation requires longer. Applicants can request earlier removal where compatible with the current decision/recovery need. Accepted agreements and real payment evidence follow the adopted financial/legal retention policy; this specification invents no universal statutory duration. Keep the public outcome record for the life of the program's published archive, with corrections, privacy moderation and an exportable static copy; promise no perpetual hosted page. The actual retention notice must be selected before intake.

At closure, provide each recipient the final agreed public record and their own authorized award/payment summary. A privacy-driven change to a display name can preserve the historical work and payment outcome without continuing to advertise the old identity. Public attribution removal does not undo an inherited license or recall third-party copies already lawfully obtained. Where privacy and necessary accounting differ, explain the separate retained record rather than promise universal deletion.

## 9. Useful contributor and supporter voice without purchased control

### 9.1 What the voice can change

For the pilot, appoint at most one useful-contributor advisor and one financial-supporter advisor for the round. Each supplies one optional written recommendation of at most 600 words during the seven-day advisory window and one clarifying reply if requested. They can recommend a feasible proposal, challenge an assumption, identify an overlooked player need or advise making no award. There is no required meeting, attendance score, campaigning assignment or monthly vote.

The advisor role is a bounded listening channel, not a claim that one person speaks for every creator or supporter. The steward publishes the selection basis and, with consent, the advisor's relevant relationship. A contributor qualifies through at least one documented useful accepted contribution; paid and unpaid work can qualify, including non-code work. A financial supporter qualifies through a settled, nonreversed voluntary support/dedication payment under its actual terms, without a minimum spend or amount-weighted influence. An ordinary product customer can give ordinary product feedback without being reclassified as a donor. If no eligible supporter exists, leave that seat vacant; do not sell an advisory position to fill it.

A person eligible through both routes chooses one role and supplies one recommendation, never two weights. An advisor cannot seek an award in the same round. Existing ordinary public feedback routes remain available, but the pilot does not promise a new unlimited grant-comment service or full individual responses outside its bounded review. The steward can surface relevant existing feedback with consent and without duplicating a person's influence through several identities.

Both recommendations receive equal standing as advice. They are considered with reviewer findings; neither counts as extra money, a corporate vote or a right to veto someone else's world. The final public decision includes a short response to material advisory points and explains a significant divergence. If both advisors favor A but the owner funds B, readers should be able to see the relevant reason—unsupported rights, different evidence or a stronger feasible outcome—without exposing private facts. “We listened” with no traceable decision response is insufficient.

The accountable unconflicted budget owner makes the award decision within the approved mandate. Reviewers determine their scoped evidence findings; authorized payment personnel execute accepted obligations. A later grant council with binding discretion over a fixed fund could be considered, but it requires an explicit delegated amount, membership, quorum, conflict fallback and remedy before being represented as binding. This pilot grants none of those powers by implication.

### 9.2 Conflicts and capture

Disclose proposal-specific financial, family, close personal, current employment, control or collaboration interests before serious discussion. A note hidden elsewhere is not sufficient. Conflicted reviewers/advisors do not review, rank, lobby other reviewers privately or receive unnecessary competing confidential proposals. A donor cannot route their preferred applicant through a second identity, condition already promised recipient money on winning, or turn a supporter badge into a veto.

Ordinary prior contribution, a properly completed past grant or general membership is not automatically disqualifying. Record why the present relationship does or does not create a material interest. The public record can say a participant recused without revealing a sensitive family or financial detail. An applicant may identify a likely conflict privately without making a public accusation.

Two independent relevant reviews and an authorized unconflicted award decision remain required. Replace a conflicted participant within the funded time/contingency or leave the affected proposal unawarded. If the budget owner is conflicted and no genuinely authorized independent decision maker exists, that award cannot proceed. An advisor vacancy does not block the whole round; a missing independent acceptance reviewer can block the affected payment decision while undisputed earned obligations remain owed. Recusal cannot be waived by a popularity poll.

Appointments end with the round. Recognition for prior useful work or support remains its historical fact, without permanent control or a promise of reappointment. The next round, if any, reconsiders voices and reviewer independence. Do not allow a small familiar group to become an implicit permanent governing body merely because arranging the first round was convenient.
