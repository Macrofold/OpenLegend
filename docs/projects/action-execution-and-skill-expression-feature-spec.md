## Purpose and product promise

A persistent, AI-native living world should be exciting **to inhabit**, not only interesting to inspect. A player who stalks a deer, tracks it with a mouse, releases a prepared shot, and later uses a carefully made pouch should feel responsible for the attempt. Their character's experience, equipment, condition and the world's physical circumstances still matter. The same world must remain coherent for independently choosing residents, who do not perform fictitious mouse minigames.

**Selected direction:** optional, responsive human execution skill on top of ordinary admitted actions; trustworthy world consequences; immediately useful quick actions; distinct weapons and craft products; reusable input/assessment mechanisms without a universal combat minigame or mandatory item-quality stat. This implements the **design** for [ND38](../maintainers/needs-design.md#nd38--player-skill-during-action-execution) and the [Sea of Stars research handoff](../../archive/02-research/game-inspiration/synthesis/art-audio-and-game-feel.md#present-l003--execution-feedback-should-clarify-a-meaningful-action). [Technical design](action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md) and [delivery tasks](../maintainers/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md) govern implementation.

The signature experience is **skillful participation in a causally persistent world**:

- Hunting favors watching animal behavior, choosing position, tracking real movement and making a shot—not waiting for a selected target's health to decrease.
- A well-timed action never grants automatic damage, automatic collision, immunity or omniscient tracking; cover, bodily evasion, armor, equipment, surprise and actual travel still matter.
- Crafted goods can carry specific, durable quality that another player or resident actually benefits from, notices or values according to permitted evidence. A player's handwork can become useful equipment, a gift, a livelihood or a future invention input.
- Later activities may expose very different skill interactions where they genuinely deepen judgment. The default remains a useful action, not a compulsory attention tax.

## Status and boundaries

Implemented today: contextual mouse selection and click-to-walk, explicit aim/shot actions with physical straight-flight projectiles, accuracy-based _trajectory deviation_, physical collision/coverage, native melee windup/recovery, real crafting, ordinary player/NPC action admission, sling competence, and a 60-game-seconds-per-real-second clock. The installed combat-response path injures the first actual vulnerable body reached by a projectile, including unintended bodily collateral; broader CTR01 qualification remains open. Current launchers have preparation but no post-release recovery, and the bundled item set has no hammer. This design therefore includes the narrow native launcher recovery and authored hammer needed for its proofs. **None of the new WASD/attack-mode/skill-challenge/craft-quality behavior is implemented by this design.** This design must be integrated with those owners, not installed as an independent combat or crafting executor.

[Combat/threat response](combat-and-threat-responses-feature-spec.md) retains physical PvP permission, exact lethal review, observed hostility, independent NPC choice, defensive responses and native animal flight. [Action capability](../action-capabilities.md) retains exact offered actions, admission, resource/cancellation semantics and persistence. [World/engine boundaries](../engine-and-world-boundaries.md) retain selected-world authority; an invented definition cannot grant arbitrary game effects. [Time](../simulation-time.md) retains simulation mechanics and clock control.

## Player controls and attack intent

1. **WASD moves the controlled character** relative to the visible ground/camera orientation, with physically valid travel, terrain and collisions. Camera orbit/pan still have their own controls. Multiple held keys combine into one normalized intended direction. Capture the camera basis when that eligible key combination changes, then retain the resulting world direction while it is held; orbiting the camera alone never bends the route. Releasing the keys stops _new movement intention_ without teleporting or denying already committed physical movement. Real navigation, placement/support and server authority still apply.
2. **Combat/attack mode** is an explicit, visible, toggleable _input interpretation_, not a fictional fact that everyone is hostile. The selected remappable default is exact **Shift+T** after inspecting current bindings. Plain **T** retains equipped targeting; **Shift+W** takes the world-agent panel off unmodified **W**, and **Shift+A/Shift+S** retain Act/Speak. In that mode, primary input begins one attack with the exact displayed compatible tool, while holding the primary button displays an optional preparation/timing challenge. Outside combat mode, existing world click/select/interact behavior remains. Secondary/context menus, deliberate speech and the game's exact contextual Command-action route remain available. No click should silently turn ordinary conversation or movement into violence.
3. **Automatic entry** into combat mode occurs when the player **actually initiates a permitted attack** or personally experiences a supported directed assault. A typed native personal-experience notice supplies that evidence; environmental injury or a health change alone does not. An unperceived model intention, speculative target, unrelated nearby fight or hidden world enemy flag cannot switch modes. The current per-opponent combat-preference and voluntary Speak override remain separate from this input mode. Ordinary noncombat work, a selectable person or bare proximity does not automatically imply attack mode.
4. **Quick tap** requests a normal attack: no timing test, bonus or deliberate penalty. **Hold** opens the concentric challenge after the hold threshold and receipt of its admitted profile, and permits the player to decide a release moment. A release before that profile has been shown remains ordinary. Early release retains one frozen intention until native readiness while foreground authorization remains valid; pause, blur or reconnect cannot later fire it automatically. It must not silently submit a tap first. Only one physical attack is submitted per gesture; holding does not autofire. A blocked/cooling-down attack is shown as unavailable, not secretly queued or replaced.
5. For a ranged weapon, **releasing uses the mouse's actual final visible world aim point**, not an opponent's old, live-hidden or automatically tracked position. The player must follow a running animal or deliberately lead their shot. They may click a permitted visible point without first selecting a creature. Off-target shots miss physically even with excellent timing; finite flight and actual interception determine consequences. A melee attempt remains bound to the particular observed opponent it was chosen against.
6. **Cancel** by deliberately moving the pointer into the character's visible cancellation region, by Escape, by leaving attack mode, or by the ordinary visible Cancel control. Escape goes to the current focused popup first under the normal UI ownership rules. Cancel before commitment does not spend ammunition, grant practice credit, start recovery or create damage. Do not cancel when the character walks underneath a stationary mouse pointer. Clearly distinguish interruption from intentional cancellation; no delayed click on pointer loss, modal opening or reconnection.
7. **Movement continues during preparation** and should make tracking and positioning fun. Preparation occupies the chosen combat handling/defense capability: a character cannot also actively guard or perform another incompatible hand action. This deliberately replaces the bundled world's current rule that movement cancels held aim. An injury or other installed interruption rule may still cancel preparation. The general concurrency restriction is not removed for unrelated actions.
8. Losing the selected weapon, actor life/control, physical prerequisites or attack permission makes the gesture invalid. A chosen **subject-specific melee attack** also loses its exact target when recognition/reach rules require it. A **point-directed ranged attack** retains the originally observed intended person, when any, only as historical/review evidence; losing that person's sight does not automatically cancel a legitimately admitted shot at the player's chosen point or reveal a new position. Releasing cannot silently choose a new target, weapon or action. Exact lethal/PvP review and the world's collateral warning still apply at the consequential admission boundary; a gesture or preview is not permission.

If an attack first requires exact lethal review, the old pointer gesture ends without starting work or releasing. **Prepare attack** confirms only the reviewed preparation. If the final release needs review, retain that exact point and input choice only in its owning dialog, and require explicit **Release attack** confirmation under current control. Decline/Escape/close cancels the matching unfinished attempt; no old button-up or focus return fires it.

The mode indicator and cursor should communicate **what will happen on a primary click**. Mode toggling is a reversible player-control choice; whether it stays active after a threat, the player switches it off, disconnects or opens a text editor must be deliberate and comprehensible. The first slice holds manual mode until explicitly toggled and auto-enters only on a new qualifying event; it must not repeatedly override a deliberate off choice because an old hostility window remains open.

## Point-directed shots and optional intended subjects

The first selected base-world ranged interaction allows shooting toward **an eligible visible world point** in attack mode, even when the pointer is not over an entity. A click on a recognized body can additionally carry that body's exact identity/life as the **intended subject** for disclosure and the world's consequential attack review; this is not automatic bullet tracking. A shooter can keep holding when a deer leaves sight and still release toward an observed/permitted point if physical tool, ammunition and point-shot permission remain valid. They must not receive the deer's new unseen coordinates. A point whose physical location is not legitimately selectable (hidden floor, ambiguous sky ray, unsupported launch geometry) is refused without spending ammunition. Melee still needs its actual selected body.

**Point fire is not a loophole for violence.** The original intended person and a newly recognized person indicated by the final release ray participate in the same exact player-target/lethality review. Pressing on ground and dragging onto a person cannot bypass review. A changed consequence requires renewed review of the frozen release; closing that review does not fire an old gesture. A shot with no selected person carries the world's explicit general warning that it can harm anyone physically intersecting the ray; there is no hidden-body lookup in the preview. A released shot still follows the selected world's collateral rules, including unintended victims and fatal consequences, without retroactive permission checks. If a world disallows free point fire, only its explicitly admitted target-bound attacks are offered there. One gesture never silently changes from an intended-subject attack to a different action.

## Optional timing interaction and weapon meaning

The initial combat interaction displays a colored concentric target band at the player's current pointer aim, with **green** for the strongest release, **yellow** for the ordinary band and outside these bands indicating a **poorly timed release**. A white ring grows and shrinks concentrically at an authored rate while held. Release position and ring timing are separate inputs: moving the mouse does not move the game's actual target or confer knowledge of its hidden location.

**A poorly timed release is a weaker attack, not an automatic physical miss.** Yellow is ordinary; green can strengthen selected attack properties. Tool-specific effects can be continuous within a region rather than only three steps, but displayed categories must agree with the actual world-authored effect. The initial ballpark is **up to about 25% of the affected base statistic for an excellent release**, potentially less or distributed differently by tool; this is _not_ a global +25% to all damage, accuracy and crit at once and is not a guaranteed hit. The final installed profile supplies exact modest tuning, clamping and poor-release penalties. It remains a changeable balance hypothesis until player evidence exists.

- Dexterity-like motor control can widen the useful timing band; focus/steadiness-like capability can change the ring's tempo. Weapon familiarity can modify the interaction where applicable. These are **possible installed world contributions**, not new universal required engine stats. A drunken/unstable condition can make maintaining a favorable release harder without adding a second hidden score.
- Since ring speed influences both ease and time to the next good release, skill should not inadvertently impose a faster/slower _mechanical attack cooldown_. Actual weapon preparation and recovery remain native world rules; release happens when chosen and admitted, not when a circle says it should. Delaying a shot has real opportunity costs as targets move and threats act.
- The selected sling and bow emphasize conditional contact; the spear combines a smaller contact contribution with a modest injury contribution, and the hammer emphasizes impact strength. No new critical mechanic is selected. The same input mechanism supports **different** families' effect mappings. A pointed weapon does not automatically justify a universal critical multiplier.
- Effects modify the attack's native, character- and equipment-derived statistics within world bounds; poor, ordinary and excellent releases go through the same action, injury and flight owners. They do not authorize an attack, supply ammunition, teleport a shot, change cover or grant invulnerability.
- A perfect green release can still miss an observed body because of motion or a **resolved local evasion**, be physically blocked, fail to penetrate useful armor, or cause little/no injury. Conversely, a badly timed arrow can still strike. Ranged **trajectory is always the submitted physical aiming direction** when admitted; character steadiness is expressed in an appropriate hit/evasion contest, not by randomly steering an arrow away from the cursor.
- A capable, aware defender may make the first slice's short **duck/tuck** without walking to another map location. Its actual shared body shape becomes lower while keeping its feet in place. This temporary response composes with the accepted standing/crouched posture through one body owner; both shapes derive from the original standing body rather than multiplying one size reduction by another and returns to that retained posture when clearance permits. The ray must truly clear that smaller shape; an impossible dodge never makes a solid body intangible. The changed shape applies to other projectiles and senses, and later re-entry can still be hit. An appropriately observed completed avoidance may show **Miss**. Armor mitigation, shield interception, evasion and world collision remain distinct.

The player's visible successful timing is not evidence that the **target was hit**. Feedback distinguishes timing quality, release, observed flight/contact, blocked injury, unknown distant result and actual physical miss. The shooter cannot inspect unseen defenses or learn whether an unseen bystander was hurt merely by scoring green.

## Representative play journeys

### Deer stalking and a moving shot (first combat proof)

The player sees a deer from reachable cover, equips a compatible sling/bow, enters combat mode and moves with WASD to a useful stance. A quick tap at the seen animal shoots normally when permitted. Alternatively, holding shows a changing timing challenge while the player tracks the deer across the screen. The animal reacts to actual sights/sounds/injury through its existing native controller; its movement changes which line might intersect. The player releases into green, but still risks cover, motion, defense or miss. The projectile travels independently after release and can strike the **first actual participant or solid** along the ray under the selected contact law, including unintended bystanders through the installed CTR01 physical-contact path, whose broader qualification remains open. No omniscient automatic correction tracks the running deer. The selected native weapon recovery prevents rapid repeat even if the player clicks again immediately; ranged recovery is a required addition, not an existing launcher behavior. A miss provides actionable feedback, not a fabricated hit.

Test the opposite: the deer becomes obscured, passes behind the character, or dies/changing physical life before release. The held gesture loses its legal target if the exact chosen targeted attack requires that recognition; it must not aim at an invisible live coordinate or harm a substitute on unreleased command admission. A legitimately released projectile is never recalled afterward.

### Reposition, strike, retreat (first melee proof)

The player holds a hammer/spear attack, keeps walking and releases when in genuine reach and with the cursor indicating the chosen visible opponent. A perfect release does not allow damage through a wall or outside reach. An on-target release may still be blocked/evaded; hammer and spear emphasize different modifiers. Starting preparation prevents simultaneous chosen guard; cancelling with Escape returns to an uncommitted stance. Recovery persists after a committed attempt, including the existing end-of-windup reach miss. A cancellation before commitment creates no attack or recovery. The first proof includes a narrowly authored, obtainable hammer alongside the existing spear. The existing knife and Punch retain one ordinary action without a timing challenge; compatible movement remains available during weapon recovery so the player can retreat. NPCs use native strike ability, timing and chosen response—not a synthetic player ring.

### Hand-finish a useful storage pouch (first distinct noncombat proof)

The player chooses an already learned, permitted pouch-making method and the actual flexible material and cord it needs. The recipe preview shows the materials, work and ordinary storage capacity. No tool or station is required by this first pouch family. **Craft** makes a useful ordinary pouch without a handwork exercise. **Finish carefully** adds one optional fitting choice to the same craft, selected before work starts.

The start choice makes the cost clear: materials are spent when the work begins, and stopping afterward leaves them spent. Careful work shows two seam marks on the pouch and asks the player to place their shared fastening halfway between them. The player moves a marker with a pointer, touch or keyboard and commits once. The marks and instruction provide the complete visible problem; there is no hidden trick location, repeated reroll or reaction-time deadline. **Finish normally** remains available for an ordinary result.

The pouch's actual work continues while the player considers the placement. If that work finishes first, this craft shows **Ready to finish**. The actor remains occupied, and the rest of the world continues. The player can complete the fitting, finish normally or cancel. No second set of materials is charged, and only one pouch can result. Real departure, injury or a replacement action can interrupt the craft under its ordinary rules, leaving the materials spent and no finished pouch. Saving and reopening retains the exact unfinished action when it has not been interrupted; returning to the session shows that actual state or its recorded outcome. A missing reply never asks the player to repeat an already accepted placement.

The finished pouch shows its real storage capacity. A good fit can put a larger useful kit inside one movable object; a poor fit can reduce what fits, while ordinary crafting remains reliable. Characters can already carry loose items directly. The value to test is organizing and moving a useful kit, including putting the loaded pouch in a larger container, without adding a character carrying-limit system. Flexible-material quantity still determines the pouch's ordinary size, and fitting preserves the recipe's material and work costs.

Capacity belongs to that particular pouch and stays with it when dropped, stored, given away, sold or reopened from a save. A loaded pouch also contributes the load of its contents to any outer container. People and residents can inspect or compare a permitted pouch's actual capacity and see that fact in disclosed trade terms without learning hidden contents, private handwork details or a secret recipe. The initial pouch shows its actual capacity without a categorical quality grade. Other supported item families may use meaningful continuous properties and optional grades of their own; an unsupported craft does not acquire a decorative quality label or a finishing option.

### Automation, care and alternate worlds

A resident can independently manufacture the same type of pouch from permitted materials without simulating mouse movements or receiving a synthetic automatic 'perfect' score. The initial bundled world has no mandatory clothworking proficiency: a resident's ordinary manufacturing yields neutral fit; another properly installed world-specific craftsmanship proficiency can legitimately affect native workmanship for residents and humans alike. A human can elect normal crafting or an accessible assisted route, without being condemned to intrinsically inferior equipment or being unable to make progress. An authored world may install different relevant qualities (e.g. seal fit instead of pouch capacity), or no quality mechanic, without changing the engine. Do not implement more variants until the first two proof cases pass.

Remembering a successful shot or careful craft does not turn that occasion's timing or placement into an automatic technique. The first slice keeps human-dependent attempts as permitted history and excludes them from automatic learned reuse through the [existing learning owner](../action-experience.md#learning-and-shared-definitions). Ordinary taps, ordinary manufacture, recipe knowledge and already learned ordinary methods retain their existing rules. A later attempt uses current native competence and actual supplies; it cannot replay the old perfect input, promise the earlier pouch's capacity or wait indefinitely for an unavailable human finisher.

A world may stop offering new **Finish carefully** choices through its existing admitted capability selection while keeping ordinary manufacture useful. That withdrawal preserves completed pouches and the exact property meaning needed to inspect and use them. Already begun work keeps its admitted finishing rules unless a genuine interruption or invalidating definition change ends it with its normal material consequences. Removing the property support that existing items still require remains invalid; no new administration interface or silent downgrade is introduced.

## Quality model: flexible C, with a real physical owner

**Selected option C:** numerical or structured **item-specific qualities**, plus **optional derived descriptive grades**. Different product families can expose distinct effective qualities; there is no engine-mandated single 'quality' float, durability bar, or universal item tier.

A quality is not a substitute for raw materials, composition, physics or fit. Use a supported exact property with an effect that existing world mechanics actually consume. The item's effective capacity, for example, is derived at the **object/containment owner** from its installed definition and an optional stamped fit contribution. The grade is a projection of that property under the current world's display policy; it cannot be independently writable or mechanically authoritative. Item characteristics and comparison views must show the real effective property rather than a fictional 'fine' adjective that does nothing.

A settled quality must never turn one crafted output into a second invented recipe or silently grant an unknown skill. If a property changes later due to a genuinely supported repair/wear process, that is another admitted mutation through the same item owner with its own evidence and rules; it is not part of this initial slice.

## Playability and accessibility commitments

- **Normal attacks, normal crafting, existing menu and text actions remain useful** without a timing or precision interaction. Skilled execution creates optional tactical upside, not mandatory survival chores.
- No timing circle while simply selecting people, reading captions, invoking a method, walking, or waiting for server/network work. A dynamic opponent or a chosen special finish should give the input a reason to exist. Don't force it onto every ordinary action.
- Clear state: combat mode, tool, target, current preparation, when releasing is allowed, timing band, cooldown, effect explanation, failure/blocked reason and cancellation. A skill grade has distinct visual feedback from actual hit/miss and item quality.
- Support keyboard alternatives to clicking/releasing, rebindable keys, one-handed and lower-precision options, reduced motion and color-independent patterns. **Skipping** the challenge is always neutral. A player choosing an **accessible alternate interaction** (**Prepare/Release** buttons, a keyboard reticle and explicit **Step timing**, plus keyboard marker placement for the pouch) can earn the **same maximum bounded quality** as mouse timing, not an inherently lower-quality item or attack. The world still uses the same character competence, costs and actual authority. Competition-specific parity remains deferred.
- Pointer/keyboard focus and popup ownership prevail over world gestures; a conversation, modal, browser tab blur, disconnected session or unsupported device must not accidentally fire. The same click that closes a popover must never shoot. UI state is disposable and cannot spend world resources.
- Combat remains native during ordinary agent cognition delays; there is no tactical freeze or repeated paid AI decision merely to evaluate a held circle. Animation/UI may update at display refresh independent of the server's current 50-ms public-state publication window.
- Human testing must assess actual fun: ease of learning, responsiveness at variable frame rate/latency, the desire to take a deliberate shot rather than quick tap, continued enjoyment after mastery, wildlife and NPC responses, and whether the finished pouch's improvement really matters. Mechanical tests alone do not establish enjoyment.

## Multiplayer trust and competitive scope

**Full PvP fairness remains deferred.** The following neutral-contact rule is a proposed first-slice safeguard selected for implementation planning, not Mike's personal approval of an anti-cheat architecture. Existing native PvP, collateral injury, attack review, defense and native recovery remain permitted by the selected world. Until a qualified competitive-input policy exists, the **entire optional human score is zero, including its negative side, at an actual human-owned body**, including a temporarily disconnected person crossing a shot nominally directed at a deer. Evaluate that boundary at **actual bodily contact** as well as at release; do not let target selection or temporary loss of foreground control change the rule. Do not reveal hidden human ownership through special feedback. A neutral score still permits the actual point-directed physical shot and its ordinary consequences. A later PvP policy must address adversarial clients, latency, server clock ownership, accessibility and replay.

**Ordinary shared-world crafting and item exchange remain valuable.** The server, not the browser, computes fitted capacity from the approved input, exact recipe, actual materials and bounded native rule; a client cannot submit a fabricated item capacity or 'legendary' grade. A resulting item retains its real quality when another player receives or trades it. Determined modified clients could maximize an optional **noncompetitive** placement choice, so this is **not anti-cheat certification**. The existing finite resource costs, native recipe prerequisites, authorization and modest property bounds still apply. High-stakes competitive crafting or ranked/economic rewards using raw manual input require a separate rule; if the installed world enables such a contest before it is qualified, that contested activity uses the ordinary neutral route **at creation**, never secretly downgrades an already traded item.

## Selected scope and staged acceptance

The first complete proof is **WASD + explicit/automatic combat mode + ordinary tap + optional held timed release + point-directed ranged shots against moving/vanishing animals + melee variant + cancellation/recovery + useful quality-sensitive pouch manufacture and exchange**. Each uses normal authorized actions and world state. Development can stage vertical slices, but the project is not complete while any of these integrated journeys or required failure paths remain undone. The existing native CTR contact-collateral revision must be reconciled as a dependency, not reimplemented by this project.

Assess the pouch's play value as soon as a player can manufacture, pack and move a real ordinary and fitted item, before broad interface polish and population qualification. Compare first use, submitting the unchanged marker and repeated use after understanding the midpoint, across recipe sizes and their actual material/work costs. A routine extra click for predictable capacity fails this gate even if the arithmetic is correct. Revise or withdraw that optional exercise while preserving ordinary production, individual properties and existing goods; do not create demand solely by increasing rewards, adding hidden randomness, introducing a carrying limit or weakening ordinary work. Withdrawal leaves the agreed noncombat proof open until a meaningful supported interaction and useful-item experience are qualified; it does not establish project completion.

**Not selected here:** an entire real-time animation/combo fighting game, automatic aim assist, hit-location anatomy, ballistic arc/wind/ricochet, every craft having grades, durability/wear simulation, a talent tree, stamina subsystem, automatic invisibility on miss, reflex requirements for speech or eating, arbitrary player-authored minigame scripts, generic physics scripting, invented quality-to-stat paths, new defense/guard input choreography, console/touch feature parity, or a PvP anti-cheat certification.

See [technical design](action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#behavior-to-implementation-contract), [task acceptance](../maintainers/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md#delivery-and-acceptance), and [limits](../limits/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md) for exact responsibilities, initial bounded candidate numbers and unqualified balancing hypotheses.

These comparisons reinforce the most important lesson: **the skill challenge must deepen the player's decisions in the real world, not replace real effects with an interface score**. Basic attacks and basic crafting remain worthwhile; future expressive handwork, fishing into moving water, pulling stubborn roots with proper leverage, timed directional defense, playing music, balancing on uncertain footing, careful hammering and material finishing may reuse the bounded attempt mechanism only when play proves a meaningful choice and real consequence. These remain repertoire possibilities, not additional requirements of ND38.

## Open decisions and deliberate deferrals

**No unanswered question blocks documenting the selected first-playable, noncompetitive contract.** Still requiring evidence/approval before different behavior is built:

1. PvP and separately ranked/competitive production input authenticity, latency tolerance, server-owned challenge evaluation and accessible-input parity; the proposed first-slice contact rule is neutral until decided. Ordinary traded fitted items retain their native quality.
2. The first-slice **numeric input curves and weapon/pouch coefficients are selected for initial implementation** in the base-world design, not verified as fun or balanced. Subsequent play-informed tuning, optional Dexterity/Focus-like status influences and further item-grade criteria remain conditional refinements, not decisions an implementing agent must invent first.
3. Actual device, accessibility and responsiveness evidence for the selected Shift+T / Shift+W defaults, keyboard reticle and Step timing. These are complete initial controls to build and test, with later refinement driven by evidence.
4. Whether to add specialized prolonged charging, fatigue, alternate precision gestures, broader item facet effects, body-part animations or extra defense actions. None is a dependency of the first selected slice.
5. Feel at 3×/8×: player input remains real-time and release-triggered, but world movement and recovery accelerate. Qualification may motivate a separately approved combat/clock policy rather than hiding speed behind the UI.
