## Purpose and product promise

A persistent, AI-native living world should be exciting **to inhabit**, not only interesting to inspect. A player who stalks a deer, selects it, releases a prepared shot, and commits to the resulting physical consequences should feel responsible for the attempt. Their character's experience, equipment, condition and the world's physical circumstances still matter. The same world must remain coherent for independently choosing residents, who do not perform fictitious mouse minigames.

**Selected direction:** optional, responsive human execution skill on top of ordinary admitted actions; trustworthy world consequences; immediately useful quick actions; distinct weapons; reusable input/assessment mechanisms without a universal combat minigame or mandatory item-quality stat. This defines the selected feature for [ND38](../maintainers/needs-design.md#nd38--player-skill-during-action-execution) and the [Sea of Stars research handoff](../../archive/02-research/game-inspiration/synthesis/art-audio-and-game-feel.md#present-l003--execution-feedback-should-clarify-a-meaningful-action). [Technical design](action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md) and [delivery tasks](../maintainers/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md) govern implementation.

The signature experience is **skillful participation in a causally persistent world**:

- Hunting favors watching animal behavior, choosing position, tracking real movement and making a shot—not waiting for a selected target's health to decrease.
- A well-timed action never grants automatic damage, automatic collision, immunity or omniscient tracking; cover, bodily evasion, directional shields, equipment, surprise and actual travel still matter.
- Later activities may expose very different skill interactions where they genuinely deepen judgment. The default remains a useful action, not a compulsory attention tax.

## Status and boundaries

The accepted feature is camera-relative WASD, visible and personally triggered attack mode, ordinary taps and optional animated held timing, subject or exact-point ranged aiming, compatible movement, bounded bodily ducking, held-ready melee, body-scoped ranged-weapon recovery and the obtainable hammer. Ordinary crafting remains useful and unchanged. Releasing over an eligible visible living target uses personally observed movement to lead a shot at its current geometric center; scenery releases retain the exact three-dimensional point under the cursor. The ring cycle is one real second. The bundled shot law adds no random trajectory deviation. Earlier checks retain their scope in [ASE evidence](../verification/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md); the current revision is tracked in [ASE](../maintainers/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md). Independent combat enjoyment and broader CTR acceptance remain separate open gates.

[Combat/threat response](combat-and-threat-responses-feature-spec.md) retains physical PvP permission, exact lethal review, observed hostility, independent NPC choice, defensive responses and native animal flight. [Action capability](../action-capabilities.md) retains exact offered actions, admission, resource/cancellation semantics and persistence. [World/engine boundaries](../engine-and-world-boundaries.md) retain selected-world authority; an invented definition cannot grant arbitrary game effects. [Time](../simulation-time.md) retains simulation mechanics and clock control.

## Player controls and attack intent

1. **WASD moves the controlled character** relative to the visible ground/camera orientation, with physically valid travel, terrain and collisions. Camera orbit/pan still have their own controls. Multiple held keys combine into one normalized intended direction. Capture the camera basis when that eligible key combination changes, then retain the resulting world direction while it is held; orbiting the camera alone never bends the route. Releasing the keys stops _new movement intention_ without teleporting or denying already committed physical movement. Real navigation, placement/support and server authority still apply.
2. **Combat/attack mode** is an explicit, visible, toggleable _input interpretation_, not a fictional fact that everyone is hostile. The selected remappable default is exact **Shift+T** after inspecting current bindings. Plain **T** retains equipped targeting; **Shift+W** takes the world-agent panel off unmodified **W**, and **Shift+A/Shift+S** retain Act/Speak. With a supported timing profile, primary press begins the exact displayed preparation. Without one, press captures the ordinary choice and release invokes it once, without a held challenge. Outside combat mode, existing world click/select/interact behavior remains. Secondary/context menus, deliberate speech and the game's exact contextual Command-action route remain available. No click should silently turn ordinary conversation or movement into violence.
3. **Automatic entry** into combat mode occurs when the player **actually initiates a permitted attack** or personally experiences a supported directed assault. A typed native personal-experience notice supplies that evidence; environmental injury or a health change alone does not. An unperceived model intention, speculative target, unrelated nearby fight or hidden world enemy flag cannot switch modes. The current per-opponent combat-preference and voluntary Speak override remain separate from this input mode. Ordinary noncombat work, a selectable person or bare proximity does not automatically imply attack mode.
4. **Quick tap** requests a normal attack: no timing test, bonus or deliberate penalty. For a supported timed tool, **Hold** opens the concentric challenge after the hold threshold and receipt of its admitted profile, and permits the player to decide a release moment. A release before that profile has been shown remains ordinary. Early release retains one frozen intention until native readiness while foreground authorization remains valid; pause, blur or reconnect cannot later fire it automatically. It must not silently submit a tap first. Only one physical attack is submitted per gesture; holding does not autofire. A blocked/cooling-down attack is shown as unavailable, not secretly queued or replaced.
5. For a ranged weapon, **button-up checks what is under the aim cursor now**, for quick taps and held timing even if the pointer has not moved. An eligible visible living target is aimed at its current geometric center, with lead from movement actually observed by the shooter. If that body has moved away, the current permitted ground/scenery point under the same pixel is selected instead; there is no hidden target lock. Ground, scenery or a pick without an eligible body uses exact 3D XYZ without leading. Explicit Release, Attack normally and recovered ranged Release check the last world aim-cursor position, not the control button. Native admission and exact review apply to that final choice. Camera tilt does not set body-center height. A prepared melee strike remains bound to its chosen opponent; releasing over another body or scenery does not strike the old target.
6. **Cancel** by deliberately moving the pointer into the character's visible cancellation region, by Escape, by leaving attack mode, or by the ordinary visible Cancel control. Escape goes to the current focused popup first under the normal UI ownership rules. Cancel before commitment does not spend ammunition, grant practice credit, start recovery or create damage. Do not cancel when the character walks underneath a stationary mouse pointer. Clearly distinguish interruption from intentional cancellation; no delayed click on pointer loss, modal opening or reconnection.
7. **Movement continues during preparation** and should make tracking and positioning fun. Preparation occupies the chosen combat handling/defense capability: a character cannot also actively guard or perform another incompatible hand action. Direct steering shares preparation; an ordinary replacement movement action still cancels held aim. An injury or other installed interruption rule may still cancel preparation. The general concurrency restriction is not removed for unrelated actions.
8. Losing the selected weapon, actor life/control, physical prerequisites or attack permission makes the gesture invalid. A chosen **subject-specific melee attack** also loses its exact target when recognition/reach rules require it. A **point-directed ranged attack** retains the originally observed intended person, when any, only as historical/review evidence; losing that person's sight does not automatically cancel a legitimately admitted shot at the player's chosen point or reveal a new position. A ranged release may deliberately choose another visible target or point; it cannot silently replace the source weapon or action, track an unseen body or inherit another target's permission. Exact lethal/PvP review and the world's collateral warning still apply at the consequential admission boundary; a gesture or preview is not permission.

If an attack first requires exact lethal review, the old pointer gesture ends without starting work or releasing. For held preparation, **Prepare attack** confirms only that preparation; ordinary attacks retain their authored confirmation and normal native progression. If the final release needs review, retain that exact subject-or-point and input choice only in its owning dialog, and require explicit confirmation of the authored release action (**Release attack** for held melee, **Shoot** for ranged) under current control. Decline/Escape/close cancels the matching unfinished attempt; no old button-up or focus return fires it.

Resolve an uncertain attack with **Check result** before retrying. A held preparation that legitimately survives input loss returns unarmed; its explicit controls permit a fresh ordinary **Release** or exact **Cancel** under current authority. Reconnection never replays or rescores the old gesture.

The mode indicator and cursor should communicate **what will happen on a primary click**. Mode toggling is a reversible player-control choice; whether it stays active after a threat, the player switches it off, disconnects or opens a text editor must be deliberate and comprehensible. The first slice holds manual mode until explicitly toggled and auto-enters only on a new qualifying event; it must not repeatedly override a deliberate off choice because an old hostility window remains open.

## Subject-directed and exact-point shots

The selected ranged interaction distinguishes a **visible living subject** from an **exact world point**. The world's ranged target rule determines eligible bodies. A subject choice uses that exact physical life and its current geometric center (half its effective body height above its feet), leading only from personally observed executed movement. A point choice preserves its picked XYZ, including elevated surfaces and solid faces. The fresh button-up pick replaces the initial aiming choice while the original intended person remains review/history evidence. If the target leaves the cursor or sight, release uses the current permitted geometry under the cursor; no unseen target coordinate is supplied. Sky, an ambiguous hidden floor or invalid launch geometry refuses without spending ammunition. Melee still needs its actual selected body.

**Point fire is not a loophole for violence.** The original intended person and a newly recognized person indicated by the final release ray participate in the same exact player-target/lethality review. Pressing on ground and dragging onto a person cannot bypass review. A changed consequence requires renewed review of the frozen release; closing that review does not fire an old gesture. A shot with no selected person carries the world's explicit general warning that it can harm anyone physically intersecting the ray; there is no hidden-body lookup in the preview. A released shot still follows the selected world's collateral rules, including unintended victims and fatal consequences, without retroactive permission checks. If a world disallows free point fire, only its explicitly admitted target-bound attacks are offered there. One gesture never silently changes from an intended-subject attack to a different action.

## Optional timing interaction and weapon meaning

The initial combat interaction displays a colored concentric target band at the player's current pointer aim, with **green** for the strongest release, **yellow** for the ordinary band and outside these bands indicating a **poorly timed release**. A white ring grows and shrinks concentrically once per real second while held. Release position and ring timing are separate inputs: moving the mouse does not move the game's actual target or confer knowledge of its hidden location.

**A poorly timed release is a weaker attack, not an automatic physical miss.** Yellow is ordinary; green can strengthen selected attack properties. Tool-specific effects can be continuous within a region rather than only three steps, but displayed categories must agree with the actual world-authored effect. The initial ballpark is **up to about 25% of the affected base statistic for an excellent release**, potentially less or distributed differently by tool; this is _not_ a global +25% to all damage, accuracy and crit at once and is not a guaranteed hit. The final installed profile supplies exact modest tuning, clamping and poor-release penalties. It remains a changeable balance hypothesis until player evidence exists.

- Dexterity-like motor control can widen the useful timing band; focus/steadiness-like capability can change the ring's tempo. Weapon familiarity can modify the interaction where applicable. These are **possible installed world contributions**, not new universal required engine stats. A drunken/unstable condition can make maintaining a favorable release harder without adding a second hidden score.
- Since ring speed influences both ease and time to the next good release, skill should not inadvertently impose a faster/slower _mechanical attack cooldown_. Actual weapon preparation and recovery remain native world rules; release happens when chosen and admitted, not when a circle says it should. Delaying a shot has real opportunity costs as targets move and threats act.
- The selected sling and bow emphasize conditional contact; the spear combines a smaller contact contribution with a modest injury contribution, and the hammer emphasizes impact strength. No new critical mechanic is selected. The same input mechanism supports **different** families' effect mappings. A pointed weapon does not automatically justify a universal critical multiplier.
- Effects modify the attack's native, character- and equipment-derived statistics within world bounds; poor, ordinary and excellent releases go through the same action, injury and flight owners. They do not authorize an attack, supply ammunition, teleport a shot, change cover or grant invulnerability.
- A perfect green release can still miss an observed body because of motion or a **resolved local evasion**, be physically blocked, have its injury reduced by native guard/body defenses, or cause little/no injury. Conversely, a badly timed arrow can still strike. Ranged **trajectory is fixed at native release** from the admitted subject-or-point choice; character steadiness affects the hit/evasion contest, not random heading deviation.
- A capable, aware defender may make the first slice's short **duck/tuck** without walking to another map location. Its actual shared body shape and displayed height become lower while keeping its feet in place. This bounded automatic response needs no custom animation for each character. The ray must truly clear that smaller shape; an impossible dodge never makes a solid body intangible. The changed shape applies to other projectiles and senses, and later re-entry can still be hit. An appropriately observed completed avoidance may show **Miss**. Body susceptibility, shield interception, evasion and world collision remain distinct.

**Selected future posture integration:** the automatic response will compose with the accepted standing/crouched posture through one body owner. Derive both shapes from the original standing construction without multiplying reductions, and return to the retained voluntary posture only when clearance permits. [The shared body contract](action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#32-one-finite-actual-body-pose-with-no-flight-life-immunity) owns that unimplemented A/B/C integration.

The player's visible successful timing is not evidence that the **target was hit**. Feedback distinguishes timing quality, release, observed flight/contact, blocked injury, unknown distant result and actual physical miss. The shooter cannot inspect unseen defenses or learn whether an unseen bystander was hurt merely by scoring green.

## Representative play journeys

### Deer stalking and a moving shot (first combat proof)

The player sees a deer from reachable cover, equips a compatible sling/bow, enters combat mode and moves with WASD to a useful stance. A quick tap at the seen animal shoots normally when permitted. Alternatively, holding shows a changing timing challenge while the selected deer moves and the player chooses when or where to release. The animal reacts to actual sights/sounds/injury through its existing native controller; its movement changes which line might intersect. The player releases into green, but still risks cover, motion, defense or miss. The projectile travels independently after release and can strike the **first actual participant or solid** along the ray under the selected contact law, including unintended bystanders through the delivered and physically qualified CTR01 contact path. ASE's native and rendered collision checks are recorded; broader combat and independent player acceptance remain separate. A body click leads the deer from movement actually seen; later turns or stops can still defeat that prediction. The selected native weapon recovery prevents rapid repeat even if the player clicks again immediately; ranged-weapon recovery uses the same native body deadline as melee and guard. A miss provides actionable feedback, not a fabricated hit.

Test the opposite: the deer becomes obscured, passes behind the character, or dies/changing physical life before release. A held ranged preparation can remain available for a deliberate switch to another visible target or permitted point, but committing the lost subject refuses. It never reads an invisible live coordinate or substitutes another physical life. A legitimately released projectile is never recalled afterward.

### Reposition, strike, retreat (first melee proof)

The player holds a hammer/spear attack, keeps walking and releases when in genuine reach and with the cursor indicating the chosen visible opponent. A perfect release does not allow damage through a wall or outside reach. An on-target release may still be blocked/evaded; hammer and spear emphasize different modifiers. Starting preparation prevents simultaneous chosen guard; cancelling with Escape returns to an uncommitted stance. Recovery persists after a committed attempt, including the existing end-of-windup reach miss. A cancellation before commitment creates no attack or recovery. The first proof includes a narrowly authored, obtainable hammer alongside the existing spear. The existing knife and Punch retain one ordinary action without a timing challenge; compatible movement remains available during weapon recovery so the player can retreat. NPCs use native strike ability, timing and chosen response—not a synthetic player ring.

### Autonomous actions and alternate worlds

Residents use real native competence, equipment and the same physical attack owners without fabricated cursor histories or automatic perfect timing. Ordinary attacks contribute neutral execution input; an admitted timed attack keeps the same supported effect whether its actual defender is controlled by a player or an NPC. Alternate installed worlds may omit timing or select different supported weapon and response laws without a base-world fallback.

Remembering a timed shot does not turn its phase into an automatic technique. Human-dependent samples remain permitted history and are excluded from automatic learned reuse through the [existing learning owner](../action-experience.md#learning-and-shared-definitions). Ordinary taps, ordinary manufacture, recipe knowledge and already learned ordinary methods retain their rules. A later attempt uses current native competence and actual supplies, never a replayed perfect phase.

## Playability and accessibility commitments

- **Normal attacks, normal crafting (including existing finite repeat-work batches), existing menu and text actions remain useful** without a timing or precision interaction. Skilled execution creates optional tactical upside, not mandatory survival chores.
- No timing circle while simply selecting people, reading captions, invoking a method, walking, or waiting for server/network work. A dynamic opponent should give the input a reason to exist. Don't force it onto every ordinary action.
- Clear state: combat mode, tool, target, current preparation, when releasing is allowed, timing band, cooldown, effect explanation, failure/blocked reason and cancellation. Timing quality has distinct visual feedback from actual hit/miss.
- Keep ordinary attacks and explicit Prepare/Release/Attack normally/Cancel buttons reachable through standard keyboard focus and activation. Physical aim is selected by the pointer. Animated timing uses the same admitted curve and real-time clock for every defender. Color-independent cues explain the bands. Reduced-motion preferences do not freeze or automatically win the challenge; ordinary neutral attacks remain available.

## Multiplayer trust and competitive scope

The same accepted animated-timing score affects supported contact and injury channels at actual player-owned and NPC contacts, including unintended and temporarily disconnected defenders. Ownership does not neutralize bonuses or penalties. Native permission, exact lethal review, geometry, collateral, defense, injury and recovery remain authoritative, without disclosing hidden ownership.

Browser timing is an untrusted claim. Server recomputation bounds the contribution but does not prove human effort, honest timing or competitive fairness. Full anti-cheat/competitive qualification remains deferred; there is no special competitive scoring exception in this feature.

## Selected scope and staged acceptance

The complete selected proof is **WASD + explicit/automatic attack mode + ordinary tap + animated held release + physical ranged shots against moving/vanishing animals + melee + cancellation/recovery**. Preserve and requalify the changed CTR contact/collateral paths, ordinary subject-directed aim, learning, crafting and shared UI ownership. Duck/tuck changes shared collision and visible height; bespoke skeletal animation for every character is not required.

General craft skill is outside the selected scope. Ordinary crafting, material costs, retained tools, named outputs, finite batches, storage and exchange remain with their existing owners.

**Not selected here:** crafting minigames or item-fit quality, a complete animation/combo fighting game, omniscient tracking, hit-location anatomy, ballistic arc/wind/ricochet, durability/wear, talent trees, stamina, arbitrary minigame scripts, new defense choreography, touch/controller parity or anti-cheat certification.

See [technical design](action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#behavior-to-implementation-contract), [task acceptance](../maintainers/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md#delivery-and-acceptance), and [limits](../limits/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md) for responsibilities, bounds and unqualified balance assumptions.

These comparisons reinforce the most important lesson: **the skill challenge must deepen the player's decisions in the real world, not replace real effects with an interface score**. Basic attacks and basic crafting remain worthwhile; future expressive handwork, fishing into moving water, pulling stubborn roots with proper leverage, timed directional defense, playing music, balancing on uncertain footing, careful hammering and material finishing may reuse the bounded attempt mechanism only when play proves a meaningful choice and real consequence. These remain repertoire possibilities, not additional requirements of ND38.

## Open decisions and deliberate deferrals

No unanswered product question blocks the accepted correction. Remaining evidence and possible expansions are distinct:

1. Independent combat enjoyment, honest hardware responsiveness and actual assistive-device use remain unqualified. Automated mechanics and scripted browser journeys do not prove them.
2. The initial timing curve and weapon coefficients are selected world defaults, not verified fun or balance. Further competence/status modifiers require an actual supported world rule.
3. Anti-cheat, latency tolerance and competitive input authenticity remain unqualified, without a player-defender score exception.
4. Specialized charging, fatigue, extra defense actions, custom body animation and general craft skill are not dependencies of this combat slice.
5. At 3×/8× the world moves faster while input remains real-time. A different combat/calendar clock requires a separate decision rather than a hidden slowdown.
