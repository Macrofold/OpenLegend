# Skilled action execution — feature specification

| Status | Current progress | Last updated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In progress | ND38's feature design is complete; implementation, browser qualification and uncoached player acceptance remain open under ASE. | 2026-10-18 |

## Purpose and product promise

A persistent, AI-native living world should be exciting **to inhabit**, not only interesting to inspect. A player who stalks a deer, tracks it with a mouse, releases a prepared shot, and later uses a carefully made pouch should feel responsible for the attempt. Their character's experience, equipment, condition and the world's physical circumstances still matter. The same world must remain coherent for independently choosing residents, who do not perform fictitious mouse minigames.

**Selected direction:** optional, responsive human execution skill on top of ordinary admitted actions; trustworthy world consequences; immediately useful quick actions; distinct weapons and craft products; reusable input/assessment mechanisms without a universal combat minigame or mandatory item-quality stat. This implements the **design** for [ND38](../maintainers/needs-design.md#nd38--player-skill-during-action-execution) and the [Sea of Stars research handoff](../../archive/02-research/game-inspiration/synthesis/art-audio-and-game-feel.md#present-l003). [Technical design](action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md) and [delivery tasks](../maintainers/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md) govern implementation.

The signature experience is **skillful participation in a causally persistent world**:
- Hunting favors watching animal behavior, choosing position, tracking real movement and making a shot—not waiting for a selected target's health to decrease.
- A well-timed action never grants automatic damage, automatic collision, immunity or omniscient tracking; cover, bodily evasion, armor, equipment, surprise and actual travel still matter.
- Crafted goods can carry specific, durable quality that another player or resident actually benefits from, notices or values according to permitted evidence. A player's handwork can become useful equipment, a gift, a livelihood or a future invention input.
- Later activities may expose very different skill interactions where they genuinely deepen judgment. The default remains a useful action, not a compulsory attention tax.

## Status and boundaries

Implemented today: contextual mouse selection and click-to-walk, explicit aim/shot actions with physical straight-flight projectiles, accuracy-based *trajectory deviation*, physical collision/coverage, weapon recovery, real crafting, ordinary player/NPC action admission, sling competence, and a 60-game-seconds-per-real-second clock. A selected but not yet fully delivered combat-response revision permits bodily collateral on actual projectile contact. **None of the new WASD/attack-mode/skill-challenge/craft-quality behavior is implemented by this design.** This design must be integrated with those owners, not installed as an independent combat or crafting executor.

[Combat/threat response](combat-and-threat-responses-feature-spec.md) retains physical PvP permission, exact lethal review, observed hostility, independent NPC choice, defensive responses and native animal flight. [Action capability](../action-capabilities.md) retains exact offered actions, admission, resource/cancellation semantics and persistence. [World/engine boundaries](../engine-and-world-boundaries.md) retain selected-world authority; an invented definition cannot grant arbitrary game effects. [Time](../simulation-time.md) retains simulation mechanics and clock control.

## Player controls and attack intent

1. **WASD moves the controlled character** relative to the visible ground/camera orientation, with physically valid travel, terrain and collisions. Camera orbit/pan still have their own controls. Multiple held keys combine into one normalized intended direction; releasing them stops *new movement intention* without teleporting or denying already committed physical movement. Real navigation, placement/support and server authority still apply.
2. **Combat/attack mode** is an explicit, visible, toggleable *input interpretation*, not a fictional fact that everyone is hostile. A configurable shortcut is suggested as **Shift+T**, subject to the actual binding audit and player remapping. In that mode, primary click requests one immediate attack with the compatible ready tool, while holding the primary button displays an optional preparation/timing challenge. Outside combat mode, existing world click/select/interact behavior remains. Secondary/context menus, deliberate speech and the game's exact contextual Command-action route remain available. No click should silently turn ordinary conversation or movement into violence.
3. **Automatic entry** into combat mode occurs when the player **actually initiates a permitted attack** or personally receives supported current hostile-attack/harm evidence. An unperceived model intention, speculative target, unrelated nearby fight or hidden world enemy flag cannot switch modes. The current per-opponent combat-preference and voluntary Speak override remain separate from this input mode. Ordinary noncombat work, a selectable person or bare proximity does not automatically imply attack mode.
4. **Quick tap** requests a normal attack: no timing test, bonus or deliberate penalty. **Hold** opens the concentric challenge for that selected action and permits the player to decide a release moment. It must not silently submit a tap first. Only one physical attack is submitted per gesture; holding does not autofire. A blocked/cooling-down attack is shown as unavailable, not secretly queued or replaced.
5. For a ranged weapon, **releasing uses the mouse's actual final visible world aim point**, not an opponent's old, live-hidden or automatically tracked position. The player must follow a running animal or deliberately lead their shot. They may click a permitted visible point without first selecting a creature. Off-target shots miss physically even with excellent timing; finite flight and actual interception determine consequences. A melee attempt remains bound to the particular observed opponent it was chosen against.
6. **Cancel** by moving the pointer onto the controlled character's own projected body/explicit cancellation area, by Escape, by leaving attack mode, or by the ordinary visible Cancel control. Escape goes to the current focused popup first under the normal UI ownership rules. Cancel before commitment does not spend ammunition, grant practice credit, start recovery or create damage. Clearly distinguish interruption from intentional cancellation; no delayed click on pointer loss, modal opening or reconnection.
7. **Movement continues during preparation** and should make tracking and positioning fun. Preparation occupies the chosen combat handling/defense capability: a character cannot also actively guard or perform another incompatible hand action. This deliberately replaces the bundled world's current rule that movement cancels held aim. An injury or other installed interruption rule may still cancel preparation. The general concurrency restriction is not removed for unrelated actions.
8. Losing the selected weapon, actor life/control, physical prerequisites or attack permission makes the gesture invalid. A chosen **subject-specific melee attack** also loses its exact target when recognition/reach rules require it. A **point-directed ranged attack** retains the originally observed intended person, when any, only as historical/review evidence; losing that person's sight does not automatically cancel a legitimately admitted shot at the player's chosen point or reveal a new position. Releasing cannot silently choose a new target, weapon or action. Exact lethal/PvP review and the world's collateral warning still apply at the consequential admission boundary; a gesture or preview is not permission.

The mode indicator and cursor should communicate **what will happen on a primary click**. Mode toggling is a reversible player-control choice; whether it stays active after a threat, the player switches it off, disconnects or opens a text editor must be deliberate and comprehensible. The first slice holds manual mode until explicitly toggled and auto-enters only on a new qualifying event; it must not repeatedly override a deliberate off choice because an old hostility window remains open.

## Point-directed shots and optional intended subjects

The first selected base-world ranged interaction allows shooting toward **an eligible visible world point** in attack mode, even when the pointer is not over an entity. A click on a recognized body can additionally carry that body's exact identity/life as the **intended subject** for disclosure and the world's consequential attack review; this is not automatic bullet tracking. A shooter can keep holding when a deer leaves sight and still release toward an observed/permitted point if physical tool, ammunition and point-shot permission remain valid. They must not receive the deer's new unseen coordinates. A point whose physical location is not legitimately selectable (hidden floor, ambiguous sky ray, unsupported launch geometry) is refused without spending ammunition. Melee still needs its actual selected body.

**Point fire is not a loophole for violence.** A shot aimed at a currently recognized person invokes the same exact player-target/lethality review even if the client calls it a point shot. A shot with no selected person carries the world's explicit general warning that it can harm anyone physically intersecting the ray; there is no hidden-body lookup in the preview. A released shot still follows the selected world's collateral rules, including unintended victims and fatal consequences, without retroactive permission checks. If a world disallows free point fire, only its explicitly admitted target-bound attacks are offered there. One gesture never silently changes from an intended-subject attack to a different action.

## Optional timing interaction and weapon meaning

The initial combat interaction displays a colored concentric target band at the player's current pointer aim, with **green** for the strongest release, **yellow** for the ordinary band and outside these bands indicating a **poorly timed release**. A white ring grows and shrinks concentrically at an authored rate while held. Release position and ring timing are separate inputs: moving the mouse does not move the game's actual target or confer knowledge of its hidden location.

**A poorly timed release is a weaker attack, not an automatic physical miss.** Yellow is ordinary; green can strengthen selected attack properties. Tool-specific effects can be continuous within a region rather than only three steps, but displayed categories must agree with the actual world-authored effect. The initial ballpark is **up to about 25% of the affected base statistic for an excellent release**, potentially less or distributed differently by tool; this is *not* a global +25% to all damage, accuracy and crit at once and is not a guaranteed hit. The final installed profile supplies exact modest tuning, clamping and poor-release penalties. It remains a changeable balance hypothesis until player evidence exists.

- Dexterity-like motor control can widen the useful timing band; focus/steadiness-like capability can change the ring's tempo. Weapon familiarity can modify the interaction where applicable. These are **possible installed world contributions**, not new universal required engine stats. A drunken/unstable condition can make maintaining a favorable release harder without adding a second hidden score.
- Since ring speed influences both ease and time to the next good release, skill should not inadvertently impose a faster/slower *mechanical attack cooldown*. Actual weapon preparation and recovery remain native world rules; release happens when chosen and admitted, not when a circle says it should. Delaying a shot has real opportunity costs as targets move and threats act.
- A bow or spear may emphasize accurate contact/critical opportunity; a hammer can emphasize resulting impact strength. The same input mechanism supports **different** families' effect mappings. A pointed weapon does not automatically justify a universal critical multiplier.
- Effects modify the attack's native, character- and equipment-derived statistics within world bounds; poor, ordinary and excellent releases go through the same action, injury and flight owners. They do not authorize an attack, supply ammunition, teleport a shot, change cover or grant invulnerability.
- A perfect green release can still miss an observed body because of motion or a **resolved local evasion**, be physically blocked, fail to penetrate useful armor, or cause little/no injury. Conversely, a badly timed arrow can still strike. Ranged **trajectory is always the submitted physical aiming direction** when admitted; character steadiness is expressed in an appropriate hit/evasion contest, not by randomly steering an arrow away from the cursor.
- A defending character can evade by a small bodily shift, lean or angle without walking to another map location. A resolved evasive miss should have a perceptible **Miss** cue when observed. The target's authoritative support anchor need not change to express that short bodily response; the existing physical volume and defensive-contact contract must be extended explicitly instead of letting geometry and a second hidden hit test contradict one another. Armor mitigation, shield interception, evasion and world collision remain distinct.

The player's visible successful timing is not evidence that the **target was hit**. Feedback distinguishes timing quality, release, observed flight/contact, blocked injury, unknown distant result and actual physical miss. The shooter cannot inspect unseen defenses or learn whether an unseen bystander was hurt merely by scoring green.

## Representative play journeys

### Deer stalking and a moving shot (first combat proof)

The player sees a deer from reachable cover, equips a compatible sling/bow, enters combat mode and moves with WASD to a useful stance. A quick tap at the seen animal shoots normally when permitted. Alternatively, holding shows a changing timing challenge while the player tracks the deer across the screen. The animal reacts to actual sights/sounds/injury through its existing native controller; its movement changes which line might intersect. The player releases into green, but still risks cover, motion, defense or miss. The projectile travels independently after release and can strike the **first actual participant or solid** along the ray under the selected contact law, including unintended bystanders when CTR01 has been implemented. No omniscient automatic correction tracks the running deer. The normal weapon recovery prevents rapid repeat even if the player clicks again immediately. A miss provides actionable feedback, not a fabricated hit.

Test the opposite: the deer becomes obscured, passes behind the character, or dies/changing physical life before release. The held gesture loses its legal target if the exact chosen targeted attack requires that recognition; it must not aim at an invisible live coordinate or harm a substitute on unreleased command admission. A legitimately released projectile is never recalled afterward.

### Reposition, strike, retreat (first melee proof)

The player holds a hammer/spear attack, keeps walking and releases when in genuine reach and with the cursor indicating the chosen visible opponent. A perfect release does not allow damage through a wall or outside reach. An on-target release may still be blocked/evaded; hammer and spear emphasize different modifiers. Starting preparation prevents simultaneous chosen guard; cancelling with Escape returns to an uncommitted stance. Recovery persists if an impact was committed. NPCs use native strike ability, timing and chosen response—not a synthetic player ring.

### Hand-finish a useful storage pouch (first distinct noncombat proof)

The player chooses an **already admitted, learned pouch-making method**, real fibers/cord and permitted tools. Normal **Craft** remains the complete, accessible path to a usable ordinary pouch. When the selected finishing family is installed, **Finish carefully** is an optional, simple *placement/fit* interaction on a visible seam/joint, **not another expanding combat circle**. The player positions a finite finishing mark relative to the workpiece's explicitly shown material/fit guide and commits; no reflex-timer spam or invisible trick position is required. The initial finish gives one optional quality contribution, not an infinite series of rerollable presses. The underlying recipe's time/material commitment is not bypassed.

The manufactured pouch keeps the existing item's identity and actual carrying behavior, but its **effective capacity** can vary modestly with a supported *fit* property. The fit value is a per-item, inspectable, immutable result of the existing manufacturing completion; a world-authored visible grade such as ordinary/fine may be derived from it, or omitted. The more useful pouch can carry more actual load subject to ordinary inventory/containment rules. Material choice, recipe, practiced craftsmanship and a non-hands-on finish can all matter; manual performance adds a bounded contribution rather than replacing those determinants. A creator cannot declare arbitrary item properties via untrusted JSON.

A future player may recognize the useful pouch, purchase it, trade it, give it to Ada, or compare it with another actual pouch. The item's quality, origin and actual ownership travel with the **same item** and are preserved across drop, containment, inspection, save/reopen, splitting rules and transfer. Neither a stranger nor an NPC receives secret craft performance or identity information unless the world's disclosure rules supply it. If a craft does not support quality, do not display a false grade or expose the finish option.

### Automation, care and alternate worlds

A resident can independently manufacture the same type of pouch with applicable character skill and material quality without simulating mouse movements or receiving a synthetic automatic 'perfect' score. A human can elect normal crafting or an accessible assisted route, without being condemned to intrinsically inferior equipment or being unable to make progress. An authored world may install different relevant qualities (e.g. seal fit instead of pouch capacity), or no quality mechanic, without changing the engine. Do not implement more variants until the first two proof cases pass.

## Quality model: flexible C, with a real physical owner

**Selected option C:** numerical or structured **item-specific qualities**, plus **optional derived descriptive grades**. Different product families can expose distinct effective qualities; there is no engine-mandated single 'quality' float, durability bar, or universal item tier.

A quality is not a substitute for raw materials, composition, physics or fit. Use a supported exact property with an effect that existing world mechanics actually consume. The item's effective capacity, for example, is derived at the **object/containment owner** from its installed definition and an optional stamped fit contribution. The grade is a projection of that property under the current world's display policy; it cannot be independently writable or mechanically authoritative. Item characteristics and comparison views must show the real effective property rather than a fictional 'fine' adjective that does nothing.

A settled quality must never turn one crafted output into a second invented recipe or silently grant an unknown skill. If a property changes later due to a genuinely supported repair/wear process, that is another admitted mutation through the same item owner with its own evidence and rules; it is not part of this initial slice.

## Playability and accessibility commitments

- **Normal attacks, normal crafting, existing menu and text actions remain useful** without a timing or precision interaction. Skilled execution creates optional tactical upside, not mandatory survival chores.
- No timing circle while simply selecting people, reading captions, invoking a method, walking, or waiting for server/network work. A dynamic opponent or a chosen special finish should give the input a reason to exist. Don't force it onto every ordinary action.
- Clear state: combat mode, tool, target, current preparation, when releasing is allowed, timing band, cooldown, effect explanation, failure/blocked reason and cancellation. A skill grade has distinct visual feedback from actual hit/miss and item quality.
- Support keyboard alternatives to clicking/releasing, rebindable keys, one-handed and lower-precision options, reduced motion and color-independent patterns. **Skipping** the challenge is always neutral. A player choosing an **accessible alternate interaction** (such as a hold-toggle with keyboard-controlled phase or a larger readable aiming guide) can earn the **same maximum bounded quality** as mouse timing, not an inherently lower-quality item or attack. The world still uses the same character competence, costs and actual authority. Competition-specific parity remains deferred.
- Pointer/keyboard focus and popup ownership prevail over world gestures; a conversation, modal, browser tab blur, disconnected session or unsupported device must not accidentally fire. The same click that closes a popover must never shoot. UI state is disposable and cannot spend world resources.
- Combat remains native during ordinary agent cognition delays; there is no tactical freeze or repeated paid AI decision merely to evaluate a held circle. Animation/UI may update at display refresh independent of the server's current 50-ms public-state publication window.
- Human testing must assess actual fun: ease of learning, responsiveness at variable frame rate/latency, the desire to take a deliberate shot rather than quick tap, continued enjoyment after mastery, wildlife and NPC responses, and whether the finished pouch's improvement really matters. Mechanical tests alone do not establish enjoyment.

## Multiplayer trust and competitive scope

**PvP fairness is deliberately deferred, not bypassed.** Existing native PvP, collateral injury, attack review, defense and ordinary cooldowns remain permitted by the selected world. Until a qualified authoritative competitive-input policy exists, a browser-reported timing score cannot improve an attack **against a human-controlled opponent**, including a human accidentally crossing the ray of a shot nominally directed at a deer. Evaluate that boundary at **actual bodily contact** as well as at release; don't let target selection launder a bonus into PvP. A neutral score still permits the actual point-directed physical shot and its ordinary consequences. A later PvP policy must address adversarial clients, latency, server clock ownership, accessibility and replay.

**Ordinary shared-world crafting and item exchange remain valuable.** The server, not the browser, computes fitted capacity from the approved input, exact recipe, actual materials and bounded native rule; a client cannot submit a fabricated item capacity or 'legendary' grade. A resulting item retains its real quality when another player receives or trades it. Determined modified clients could maximize an optional **noncompetitive** placement choice, so this is **not anti-cheat certification**. The existing finite resource costs, native recipe prerequisites, authorization and modest property bounds still apply. High-stakes competitive crafting or ranked/economic rewards using raw manual input require a separate rule; if the installed world enables such a contest before it is qualified, that contested activity uses the ordinary neutral route **at creation**, never secretly downgrades an already traded item.

## Selected scope and staged acceptance

The first complete proof is **WASD + explicit/automatic combat mode + ordinary tap + optional held timed release + point-directed ranged shots against moving/vanishing animals + melee variant + cancellation/recovery + useful quality-sensitive pouch manufacture and exchange**. Each uses normal authorized actions and world state. Development can stage vertical slices, but the project is not complete while any of these integrated journeys or required failure paths remain undone. The existing native CTR contact-collateral revision must be reconciled as a dependency, not reimplemented by this project.

**Not selected here:** an entire real-time animation/combo fighting game, automatic aim assist, hit-location anatomy, ballistic arc/wind/ricochet, every craft having grades, durability/wear simulation, a talent tree, stamina subsystem, automatic invisibility on miss, reflex requirements for speech or eating, arbitrary player-authored minigame scripts, generic physics scripting, invented quality-to-stat paths, new defense/guard input choreography, console/touch feature parity, or a PvP anti-cheat certification.

See [technical design](action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#behavior-to-implementation-contract), [task acceptance](../maintainers/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md#delivery-and-acceptance), and [limits](../limits/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md) for exact responsibilities, initial bounded candidate numbers and unqualified balancing hypotheses.

## Open decisions and deliberate deferrals

**No unanswered question blocks documenting the selected first-playable, noncompetitive contract.** Still requiring evidence/approval before different behavior is built:
1. PvP and separately ranked/competitive production input authenticity, latency tolerance, server-owned challenge evaluation and accessible-input parity; no untrusted manual PvP bonus until decided. Ordinary traded fitted items retain their native quality.
2. Final weapon-specific green/yellow/poor stat curves, skill/focus contributions, window widths and optional crafted-quality thresholds; initial bounded examples are **proposed tuning**, not playtested constants.
3. Exact default remappable toggle key after checking collisions and supported devices; **Shift+T is a suggestion**, not committed authority.
4. Whether to add specialized prolonged charging, fatigue, alternate precision gestures, broader item facet effects, body-part animations or extra defense actions. None is a dependency of the first selected slice.
5. Feel at 3×/8×: player input remains real-time and release-triggered, but world movement and recovery accelerate. Qualification may motivate a separately approved combat/clock policy rather than hiding speed behind the UI.
