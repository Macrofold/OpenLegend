This design covers the research refinements for **DG02 and DG17**, consuming **DG23, ND19 and conditional ND20**: a chosen activity can continue within its actual terms; relevant changes can invite reconsideration; retained personal experience can influence a later choice without rewriting history. These are the continuation, attention and later-conduct questions in the register, not new ND identifiers. Reliable satisfied stopping, freely chosen resumption, unfamiliar personal significance and lasting influence remain unqualified.

The complete continuing-community proposal is preserved in the feature specification. ## 3. Express the actual choice with existing composition

### 3.1 The selected structure is the execution allowance

An exact native command, requested composition or acquired method is admitted with its actual bindings. The selection must expose consequential alternatives before execution. Represent a finite selected fallback as existing `sequence`, `branch`, `repeat`, `wait` and `invoke` nodes when those nodes express it honestly. Retain the existing node/depth/expanded-work bounds and output binding semantics; do not widen them merely to fit a speculative life plan.

Examples of meaning that must stay separate:

- “Use this knife for this finite hunt” identifies an actual tool/target and an attempt; it is not permission to hunt every animal afterward.
- “Use the selected spare if this known component is unavailable” can be a finite admitted branch only if the necessary condition is supported and personally available.
- “Obtain food somehow” is a goal; it does not authorize an unbounded search, theft, spending or an unknown recipe.
- “I enjoy watching animals” is a disposition/interpretation; it is neither a standing hunting command nor a numeric satisfaction threshold.

Native `holding`, output and time conditions already express some stopping/continuation facts. A world-specific observation uses the existing installed-host condition interface and exact definition pin. Its evaluator must declare the actor-permitted input, unknown result, relevant dependencies and next time boundary. It cannot inspect another person's hidden contents or private intentions. Unsupported conditions are not silently evaluated by a model every tick.

Do not introduce a universal discretionary permission envelope. A proven missing need may extend the existing invocation control with a narrow typed field, validated and rendered by its owner. First establish that exact bindings, selected finite branches or a fresh ordinary choice cannot express it. Any new risky substitution remains a new choice unless explicitly included in the original admitted structure. Application material permissions are still checked at each actual operation, independently of what the actor was willing to do.

Use the existing [one-session fire watch](../worlds/base/camp-routines.md) as the concrete control regression: selected observed burning fire, one chosen carried/inspected supply, original stopping time, minimum personal stock and exact attempt/debit ceiling. It already refuses gathering, supply switching, relighting and following a moved cache; preserve those meanings rather than designing a new daily caretaker. `worlds/base/camp-activity.ts` supplies the terms and `fire-activity.ts` supplies the observed condition/spending support. A full starter fire needing no fuel is not a successful fuel-care trial. This regression is infrastructure evidence, not the player payoff or a mandate that a resident choose that activity.

For the integrated payoff, retain the existing creative/adventure loop and let the resident independently choose a currently supported finite making, observation, meal or social activity. Compare the activity with its ordinary direct-action route, including actual work time, approach, controls and opportunities to do something else. A composed option is worthwhile when it removes repeated instructions while preserving understandable discretion; completing more steps does not by itself make it better. Record whether the resident and player can choose a satisfying stopping point without adding a new content obligation.

### 3.2 Admission and execution flow

1. Existing discovery establishes known/permitted options and read-only native availability. Prepare tool/actor prerequisites once before target expansion; do not simulate the action merely to offer it when owning checks suffice.
2. Render the chosen purpose, exact target/tool, resource/time/attendance terms and supported stop/fallback conditions through `activityChoiceView`/native views. Keep unknown yield explicit. IDs and definition pins remain server bindings, not repetitive model prose.
3. Jev can select a fully specified offered activity. Generation is used only by the existing authorized route for text or an unlisted proposal; no new automatic escalation or repair is introduced. Jev-only uncertainty remains an explicit deferral.
4. The existing command/agency owner admits the exact invocation, rechecking live targets, access, body, knowledge, resources, selected support and current plan replacement mode before any interruption or debit.
5. `activityFrontier` resolves only the next justified native step. Actual output references bind later inputs; expected output is never inserted as inventory. `admitActivityAttempt` applies supported debit/attempt control once per admitted attempt, including restarted unfinished work.
6. Native effects and their actor-permitted result are recorded once. Continue until the next native boundary, relevant known change or terminal condition. No paid call is required per movement segment, strike preparation or already chosen method step.

### 3.3 Alternatives, stopping and goal lifecycle

An unavailable branch condition returns unknown and blocks its dependent frontier. A refused action records the actual reason and completed costs; it cannot claim “no resources exist” merely because preparation was incomplete. An explicitly selected alternative can proceed only through ordinary native admission. An unselected alternative becomes an offered new choice using current evidence.

Invocation completion means the chosen work ended. The larger `ActorGoal` keeps its independent actor-declared lifecycle; do not infer goal completion from an action label or automatically clone a completed plan. The existing shared outcome text plus required result memories explain what ended and what happened. Meaningful satisfaction or a preference to stop is a character choice using permitted current evidence, not a global detector deciding that enough fun or work occurred.

Stop, replace and interrupt retain their existing distinct meanings. An explicit stop discards matching paused work; an interrupt retains the exact remaining frontier if the native family can stop safely. Refuse unsupported suspension before altering the current work. Do not claim general resumable construction, combat or cooperative participation merely because one watch can suspend.

## 4. Reconsideration, resumption and attention

### 4.1 Exact continuation survives harmless changes

`resumeSuspended` already resumes one paused plan after interrupting work ends and revalidates its work. Keep that behavior for the selected remaining course; do not purchase another approval merely because a conversation membership revision changed. Retain original deadline, actual debit counters, completed outputs, target episodes and definition pins. A selected condition may have ended during interruption; evaluate it before another step.

Changed recipient, unselected material, expired agreement, unsupported route/risk or lost required knowledge cannot be repaired under the old selection. The native plan becomes blocked or ends according to its existing owner, and normal intake offers reconsideration. Returning to a previously selected permitted method is not equivalent to independently choosing a new purpose. Verify and report those two qualities separately.

Every actual result enters the existing actor experience path. Plan state projects current/remaining work; it does not duplicate autobiographical narrative. Unknown or missing prior evidence stays explicit while durable work receipts still establish committed effects. Neither a remembered goal nor a finished plan grants current command rights.

### 4.2 Supporting facts feed existing intake

The current interest subscription retains goal, knowledge/mind revision, expiry and selected kinds/properties/definitions. Its exact matching is useful for known materials and observed entities; it is not a complete semantic model of an interest or promise. Start by tracing an omitted cue through perception, intake, retrieval, candidate selection and final choice. A cue that reached choice but lost is not an attention delivery bug.

Extend `compileInterests` only for concrete missing dependencies of the active/paused plan and current authoritative commitments: exact subjects, required definition pins, output dependencies, selected deadline and personally known evidence support. Keep the result a derived actor-local subscription. Do not parse free prose into hidden urgency, automatically infer consent, or retain a duplicate mutable list of psychological goals.

Choose the smallest owner revision/reference that proves freshness. Existing actor plan/goal, knowledge, inner-world and evidence revisions cover their own changes; exact referenced definitions cover their meaning. Presence/access and observation episode changes affect targets. Simulation time invalidates due conditions even when object identity is unchanged. Restore and unknown mutation scope rebuild the derived subscription. Another actor's unrelated memory edit need not invalidate it.

At a meaningful change, existing `ThoughtIntakeInputs` and `ActorWork` admit one actor opportunity. Preserve required trigger evidence, personal action results, changed accepted terms, active obligations and corrections before optional attention. Coalesce unchanged circumstances and duplicate scheduling wakes, not actual distinct offers, withdrawals, results or accounts. A deferred actor keeps the reason and its permitted evidence boundary; do not consume a cue merely because the shared decision slot is busy.

### 4.3 Unfamiliar significance and quiet initiative

Known predicates catch selected dependencies, not every personally meaningful situation. The existing attention question receives permitted new evidence and relevant current identity/interests/concerns. Existing quiet opportunities allow bounded consideration outside the current plan. They remain eligible during ordinary life without inventing a crisis, productivity excuse, social reward or new cadence.

Do not add a second generative gate to decide whether to ask Jev, or run a classifier over all history every simulation interval. If current eligible quiet opportunities miss a concrete later-choice case, first compare the supplied evidence, rubric, retrieval and cost under the existing route. New opportunity tuning remains selected-world/service configuration under its existing owner and evidence, not an undocumented constant in the scheduler.

Evidence arriving during preparation follows the existing required-source capture and urgency-refresh contract. Importance and urgency stay distinct: an important invitation can wait without cancelling every funded response. At publication, compare relevant changed sources and live action prerequisites; reject stale proposals without automatic paid retry. Preserve new eligible evidence for the next legitimate opportunity when it need not interrupt the current one.

Distinguish in inspection: not perceived; personally available but not selected into optional context; considered and chose otherwise; preparation unavailable; selection deferred; accepted choice. These are diagnostic facts under current private inspection authority, not a public motive readout. Generated explanations remain attributed statements, not proof of causation.

## 5. Later conduct uses the same evidence and interpretation owners

### 5.1 Preserve facts, sources and personal meaning

An actual apology is permitted speech, not verified remorse. A fulfilled promise is a native settlement/outcome plus what this actor witnessed or learned. A quiet act of care is the actual supported action and its permitted observation; no invented appraisal is needed to retain it. Do not create a parallel “important relationship episode” store.

The existing occurrence/experience keeps exact identities, time/order, effects and observation basis. Personal subject knowledge holds a revisable assessment of another person; current self-understanding holds a revisable outlook. Protected starting identity, current goal, accepted obligation and learned method remain separate. The [DG23 proposal](experience-shaped-characters-feature-spec.md) owns deeper outlook and old-recollection behavior; this continuation design consumes available current interpretation without selecting new personality laws.

When interpretation changes, use existing supported evidence relations and revisioned publication. The original witnessed refusal remains the original refusal. Later testimony can qualify what it meant. An actor may retain uncertainty or mixed views and choose the same action afterward. Do not equate positive wording in a reflection with demonstrated later influence.

### 5.2 Retrieve for a concrete choice; do not force a sentiment

`prepareDecision` supplies current goal/plan status and exact required recent outcomes through `requiredOutcomeMemoryIds`; `RecallService` adds actor-scoped relevant older evidence through current indexes and budgets. A current commitment/correction remains required through its existing owner. A personally important older encounter is not automatically pinned forever or loaded into every decision.

For a demonstrated omission, trace source identity through recording, retention, indexing, attention and final context. Repair that existing step rather than copying text into biography. Select IDs before hydrating bodies, batch exact source reads in a consistent scope, and keep the existing complete required-set admission. Oversize required evidence defers preparation; optional retrieval failure does not erase required facts. Explicit inspection remains paged.

Repeated retellings may be separate experiences of hearing a claim; they do not become separate observations of the claimed event. **Current representation gap:** `Awareness` retains the actual listener-specific speech and speaker, `MemoryRecord` retains event/source identity, `ExperienceSummary.sourceIds` links consolidated sources, and `KnowledgeDocument.evidenceIds` supports current notes. None currently identifies the source of an individual assertion inside an utterance. `RecallService` grouping equal remembered text is not provenance and its record count cannot establish independent corroboration. Implement the following narrow extension through those existing owners rather than treating plain source IDs as a solved contract.

### 5.3 Actor-known testimony provenance

**Canonical representation.** Add optional typed `testimony` metadata to the existing actor-owned `MemoryRecord` with `kind: 'belief'` and `source: 'inferred'`. The normal record ID, actor, occurrence time and repository revision identify the interpretation. Its `summary` is the shared English rendering of the metadata, not another copy of the quotation. A supported metadata shape contains:

- `assertion`: an exact actor-permitted awareness/memory source reference, its content revision, and one `[start, end)` Unicode-code-point span in the personally heard text. References use the existing complete source key including kind, not a bare event ID. Summaries and missing/masked words cannot supply exact spans.
- `origin`: a closed first-delivery union: `unknown`; `claimed` with the exact heard attribution span and optional personally recognized alleged source; or `inferred` with exact supplied earlier assertion references and the actor's supporting source references. Do not admit a verified/recorded citation category in this slice.
- Source and subject references remain private server bindings. A source identity is nullable when the speaker only said “someone,” the listener did not recognize the name, or identifying that person would disclose unavailable information. Do not invent a universal claim ID by hashing prose or looking up the objectively matching event.

This record says what this actor knows or believes about the route of a statement. It is not a truth rating. `claimed` means the listener heard the speaker attribute it; `inferred` means the listener connected available accounts; neither verifies that the alleged origin actually said it. The native speech owner already verifies immediate acquisition—who this listener actually heard, with what words and uncertainty. That does not verify an earlier source named within those words. Absence of a relation means origin unknown, never an independent source. Different claims in one utterance have separate spans; one whole-message origin must not automatically apply to every sentence.

**First-delivery admission.** Extend the existing cognition `note`/reflection `knowledgeChanges` proposal with optional testimony-origin edits, using request-local handles for the supplied assertions and expected source/document revisions. These edits can propose only `claimed` or `inferred` links. The existing knowledge operation binder resolves handles; the domain experience owner checks actor scope, eligible exact spans/revisions, personally known subject binding, required supporting evidence and acyclic references to already committed sources. It appends the belief through `appendMemory`/`mutateExperience`, then includes that record in the note's ordinary `evidenceIds` in the same admitted operation. Failure rejects that operation atomically; prose cannot silently create a stronger link. No extra model/classifier call is added. A Jev-only selection cannot invent new source text or a generative interpretation. Ordinary speech submission and perception remain unchanged; no quotation action, citation field, quote UI or new protocol operation is a prerequisite.

**Concrete first-delivery example.** Ada hears Mara say “I saw Kest open the cache.” Ada later hears Tavin say “Mara told me Kest opened it.” A permitted interpretation can link Tavin's assertion to the attribution to Mara as `claimed`, preserving both heard episodes. Ada may separately infer that Tavin is repeating the earlier account; the link stays `inferred`, not a verified retelling. If Ada only hears Tavin, the earlier occurrence is unavailable and no exact Mara statement appears in her context. Another resident's independent direct observation remains a separate observation source; no text similarity rule makes it the same rumor. These controls qualify ordinary later-choice/rumor behavior without a new citation capability.

**Ordinary context and storage.** `memoryCandidate` and conversation/knowledge preparation render concise English such as “Tavin said Mara saw Kest open the cache; I have not verified that source” or “I think Tavin may be repeating Mara's earlier account; that does not establish another sighting.” Keep `inferred` language explicit and render unavailable origin as unavailable. The metadata is not raw JSON in every context; exact handles stay in the normal request binding. Retellings keep their individual time/speaker/acquisition facts, while source-qualified wording prevents record multiplicity being presented as corroboration. No numerical corroboration score or automatic claim merger is added. Neither a claimed/inferred link nor its absence establishes verified dependence or independence.

Persist the metadata in the existing memory payload and saved experience state. `memory-repository.ts` projects it through the existing actor-scoped `recall_sources` publication; add a rebuildable reverse-dependency projection keyed by complete `(world, actor, dependent source kind/ID, supporting source kind/ID)` only for indexed correction/forgetting closure. It contains references, not duplicated text or another authoritative claim graph. `history-edit.ts` traverses those references alongside current summary/correction/action aliases and prepares only the affected sources before the existing serialized mutation. All metadata, source bytes and dependency references count against existing record/context admission; cyclic/over-limit proposals refuse before publication. Root source selection is indexed and batched, never an entire-history text comparison.

**Correction, edits and restoration.** Correcting or editing an assertion/attribution makes the old content revision unusable for new provenance publication and invalidates the dependent interpretation/index through the existing experience owner. It does not rewrite what was historically heard. An amended origin is a new revisioned interpretation supported by the newly perceived correction; no new support is fabricated. Forgetting either assertion or supporting origin revokes the linked interpretation and its derived context/summary/vector entries using the existing closure, including memory/event aliases and cold SQL rows. Where origin detail alone is unavailable, retained unrelated speech can remain, explicitly without that provenance; its source is not silently upgraded to independent. Current knowledge clearing/reconsideration rules remain controlling. Save/load validates same-actor source keys, content revisions and acyclic support or the owner's explicit unavailable-source disposition; restore rebuilds derived edges, applies present privacy overlays and never fetches hidden raw history to repair a missing link. Pending reads/publication recheck the same evidence and generation fences as ordinary memory.

**Conditional later extension—native recorded citation.** Exact quotation/forwarding is excluded from this first delivery and is not needed for its acceptance. Add it only for a selected encounter needing a verifiable retelling route. A future world-installed capability must enter ordinary discovery, selected-world wording and supported command admission; it does not become a universal speech law. Its implementation must update the speech/protocol contract, server handle binder, native speech/`perceiveSpeech` owner, both ordinary-player and NPC offers, context/inspection rendering and current-format persistence together. A model-proposed flag cannot verify provenance.

That later capability would bind an exact personally retained source/revision/span, visibly quote/attribute its selected words, and produce a native citation occurrence only when speech actually commits. Cold sources would be prepared through the existing repository before domain admission, with current source/privacy/authority checks after waits. A listener could receive a `recorded` origin only when the citation words were intelligible and their own permitted acquisition independently matches the cited occurrence; otherwise the result remains claimed/unknown. Recorded would verify the retelling route, not the original assertion's truth. The future slice must qualify unavailable/stale source, masked citation, unknown speaker/source, unauthorized access, correction/forgetting, preview/admission agreement and save/reopen before admitting that new union member. No quote UI is included in this design assignment.

### 5.4 Correction, forgetting and unavailable support

Use the current experience invalidation and private-retrieval guards. Forgetting revokes both memory/source identities and linked derived disclosures; a pending SQL result or prepared prompt cannot expose the revoked text afterward. Revalidate evidence/privacy revision and current world/control generation immediately before disclosure or publication after waits.

Derived interpretations must not continue presenting removed support as established evidence. Their existing owner may qualify, remove or mark unsupported the affected assessment under the admitted correction/forgetting operation. This does not force a person to believe a correction or rewrite every related subjective opinion. Actual obligation/receipt records preserve their separate integrity and disclosure rules. A forgotten quotation cannot be regenerated from a public world event or an old provider session.

### 5.5 Numerical personal reactions without required prose

**Selected design; runtime unimplemented.** A person can privately interpret an experience and propose a numerical feeling directly, without a generated explanation, thought, speech or expression. Reuse the current appraisal record as the numerical psychological meter: a source-linked feeling with its own installed scale and lifetime, not a second global joy field. The [world profile](../worlds/base/psychological-reactions.md#selected-first-profile) owns the first joy vocabulary, amounts, units, decay and stacking hypotheses. Current damage reactions and qualitative definitions remain unchanged. A compact reaction is interpretation, not a fixed stimulus-to-points script or proof of another person's intent.

**One operation and source binding.** Extend the existing optional immediate cognition-operation envelope with an appraisal operation, using the existing reflection proposal/binding meanings where applicable. Update request instructions, request-local schema, decoder, operation exclusivity/dependencies, native translation, receipts and private projection together. Immediate proposals use the same `changeAppraisal` authority as accepted reflection; they do not publish an inner-world document or invoke reflection merely to change a number. Preserve reflection's coupled publication and immediate operations' existing independent receipts and `requiresAccepted` semantics. No required explanation field or minimum count is introduced.

| Bound meaning | Required proposal and server-held facts                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Create        | A request-local installed-policy handle, eligible source handle, permitted target handle or none, and an absolute numerical intensity or the installed qualitative value. The server binds actor/world, exact definition pin, source kind/ID/content revision, original occurrence, permitted coverage/subjects and operation identity. The pilot always binds no target. |
| Reframe       | A supplied active feeling handle, expected record revision, eligible current interpretation source and only changes allowed by that definition/current native owner. The current decaying-value restriction remains: reframing cannot alter intensity or restart lifetime.                                                                                                |
| Resolve       | A supplied active feeling handle and expected revision, with the request's operation allowance. It ends that feeling; it neither deletes historical cause/outcome nor resolves another person's feeling.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| No change     | Omit the operation or select the explicit no-change choice. A zero amount is not shorthand for creation, and uncertainty/unavailability is distinguishable from a considered no change.                                                                                                                                                                                   |

These fields bind existing typed definitions and evidence, not arbitrary record paths or executable model instructions. The pilot's amount is an absolute intensity in its declared dimensionless scale, never an additive reward or a delta to body health/fullness. Validate finite values and installed range because they control actual native state; reject unusable amounts rather than coercing them. Record the proposed amount, accepted before/after value, cause, units/scale pin, accepted game time and native outcome through the existing private result/receipt owner. Qualitative definitions never acquire invented numbers.

**Compact generation or finite selection.** An already authorized generative request may return only the small structured operation. For illustration, a request could define `j = joy` and accept the request-local spelling `j` with a bound source and amount; this is proposed vocabulary, not a currently supported API. Keep the full meaning, unit/range and unambiguous handle map in the same request. A short letter is not assumed to be one tokenizer token. Without compact aliases, use the installed full label/normal handle; unknown, conflicting, missing or cross-request aliases refuse. Codes do not resolve an unknown policy by name or bypass source binding.

Jev may select a fully specified installed reaction with exact source, amount, target, native operation and relevant revisions already bound. Prepare independent actor/source questions using existing byte/context and operation bounds and batch them with other independent judgments sharing that actor-permitted snapshot when the existing route supports it. Do not multiply all reactions by all physical choices into a Cartesian product. The [pilot's finite options](../worlds/base/psychological-reactions.md#selected-first-profile) are chosen world vocabulary; Jev is not inherently restricted to quarter steps. Preserve current selection/unknown handling. Unsupported novel feelings, causes or amounts defer explicitly in strict Jev-only mode; they do not trigger generation, invent a novel proposal or get replaced by a supposedly equivalent known reaction. Neither route adds a mandatory call per stimulus or a separate appraisal-thinking loop.

**Installed operations, not a new psychological framework.** Extend the existing `AppraisalDefinition` with one optional immediate-reaction declaration containing its scale meaning/units, finite offered amounts and optional alias. Its presence enables this route; omission disables it. Reuse the existing value bounds, causes, stacking, lifetime and reflection eligibility rather than copying them into a second policy registry. The exact definition pin includes the new declaration. Keep it absent on every baseline definition and present only on the explicitly installed pilot; current living/memory/cognition eligibility and human-control authority still apply, with no new actor enrollment store. Installing it offers a possibility, never automatically creates a feeling. The current manifest/definition validator checks that amounts fit the supported scale, aliases are unambiguous and metadata fits existing definition/context bounds. A qualitative definition can expose a qualitative reaction without acquiring numerical units or amounts.

Consume that same installed meaning in prompt vocabulary, schema/choice preparation, admission, inspection and saves. The pilot is opt-in and installs no need bar, condition/disposition process or new scheduler. Supported profiles within existing native families can be assembled as data without new evaluator code. A different already supported psychological-meter operation can use the same compact transport only when its own installed rule supplies exact units, supported write, source, range, lifetime/stacking and native owner; that owner, not the transport or model, changes the meter. This is an extension seam, not a requirement to build such another meter. New physiological effects, trait changes, arbitrary increments, hidden resource rewards, novel decay functions and new process enrollment remain unsupported unless separately designed and installed. Never route around an existing meter owner by editing raw actor fields.

**Private feeling and expressed meaning.** Shared compact background contributions carry what was expressed or made perceptible, not a table of participants' internal feelings. Each actual recipient's ordinary cognition independently interprets only its permitted contribution/evidence and may propose its own numerical change, none, or another supported response. The speaker cannot choose the listener's intensity. Conversely, a private numerical change neither speaks nor publishes an observable gesture. The [attention/scenes design](attention-and-scenes-tech-design.md) owns compact contribution delivery and disclosure; this section owns the receiver's optional numerical interpretation. Generate richer descriptive experience only when a selected ordinary activity needs that language. No verbal justification is needed to prove the number was published.

**Admission after waits and deduplication.** Prepare actor-scoped sources and any cold prior creation outcomes through the existing repository before domain admission; use current `AppraisalEvidence`, `AppraisalBindings` and `appraisalCreationIdentity` rather than a new reaction store. Resolve an exact existing receipt before new-effect eligibility: it reports earlier effects under current disclosure authority, never applies them again or grants a new operation. Recheck world/timeline/control and privacy after awaited receipt reads, and omit source detail no longer disclosable. New effects additionally recheck actor eligibility, current correction/forgetting, exact source versions, installed definition pin and any supplied active feeling revision after awaited preparation/provider stages and immediately before serialized publication. Revalidate relevant mutable dependencies, not every unrelated world change. Staleness refuses the affected new operation with the previous accepted feeling intact; preserve accounting and independently committed operations, with no paid retry.

Use the existing complete actor/policy/target/original-occurrence identity and exact operation receipt. The targetless pilot yields one joy creation outcome for that original occurrence. A remembered or paraphrased copy of that occurrence does not become another cause; a new request ID, source-row alias or recall episode cannot restart decay. Check the complete scoped prior outcome when history is cold; unavailable lookup means unavailable proposal, not permission to recreate. A repeated identical operation returns the prior outcome; a reused identity with conflicting meaning refuses. A resolved/invalidated source cannot be resurrected. A genuinely distinct later experience can create its own cause-linked record; distinct records coexist without additive aggregation. Do not falsely deduplicate independent occurrences merely because their words match.

The source binder must retain the exact original occurrence through current memory/event aliases. A consolidated or inferred account spanning several events cannot invent a single new cause to bypass that identity. Offer creation only when the current source relation identifies the concrete supported occurrence; otherwise defer that creation while retaining the available qualitative evidence. Do not recover the relation by comparing prose or reading ungranted history.

The pilot excludes its own feeling-created/changed/result records, numerical reads/displays and resulting reaction wakes as independent causes for refreshing or stacking that same feeling. A derived joy notification still points to the original permitted stimulus through existing aliases; it cannot become a newly joyful event just because the feeling owner recorded a change. Apply this native provenance check without another classifier or paid call, and qualify the complete notification → next decision path to rule out an automatic feedback loop.

**Correction, forgetting and current-format continuation.** Ordinary contrary testimony can support a later interpretation without rewriting the original occurrence or forcing belief. Explicit source correction/erasure retains the current conservative all-feelings invalidation for that actor ([FL17](../limits/feelings.md#fl17)); this design does not silently narrow it. Revoked causes/interpretations must leave derived contexts, source bindings and pending writes, including cold repository rows under current forgetting rules. No history fallback may reconstruct the forgotten cause. Reframing never renews the curve. Native resolution and lifetime completion remain durable; reopen cannot replay a create.

Save current pinned meanings, cause/source versions and ownership, numerical value/anchor, original game time, record revisions, lifecycle outcome and operation/creation receipts through existing appraisal persistence. Validate current actor/source/target scope, supported scale/lifetime and exact definition identity; rebuild due/source indexes without new inference, RNG draws or elapsed real-time catch-up. Pending old-timeline proposals remain cancelled or stale, and present privacy overlays still apply. The first profile refuses definition replacement/removal while any retained canonical appraisal or creation outcome depends on its exact pin, including cold resolved/invalidated records. Native resolution/retirement alone does not release that dependency. Current validation still requires the historical pin, and the existing pin-bearing creation identity cannot by itself prevent recreation of one occurrence under a changed definition revision. A separately supported live transition must preserve historical meaning and original-occurrence deduplication across definition revisions before that restriction can change. No such transition, migration or compatibility scheme is part of this slice. Update current writers/readers/validators together while preserving immutable installed meaning and current same-world integrity.

**Later choice and bounded work.** Extend existing `decisionFeelings` rendering to expose an admitted number with scale, units, current lifetime and permitted cause, rather than relying only on the label “joy.” Keep optional feeling selection/relevance under existing budgets, with exact relevant sources selected before body hydration; this does not make every feeling required context or scan all history at every decision. Existing meaningful change/deadline intake can make a change eligible. The person remains free to continue, decline, rest or choose differently; reading a feeling does not create another feeling. Work grows with eligible sources, offered amounts and active records; use current source/operation/native-work bounds and indexed actor-scoped lookup, select before formatting, and measure actual cold/warm context → choice → admission → later-choice work. No new unbounded pending queue or periodic all-actor pass is admitted.

**Concrete acceptance.** Through ordinary immediate-operation and finite-selection paths, qualify silent creation, explicit no change, resolve and refused unsupported amount/source/definition/operation. Show different independent interpretations of one permitted compact contribution, without a shared private amount. Exercise duplicate delivery after a cold lookup, repeated recall of one source, distinct occurrences with identical words, corrected/forgotten sources during awaited reads, source/feeling/definition changes during selection, refusal of definition replacement/removal with active or cold terminal dependent records, capacity refusal and save/reopen at an active and terminal lifetime. Confirm private amounts stay absent from ordinary observer projections and baseline fear/discomfort/qualitative behavior stays unchanged. Then run a complete CE episode and matched later choices with/without the permitted feeling, recording the actual number/cause that reached choice and allowing unchanged conduct. Fixtures prove authority and arithmetic; live selection and human observation qualify voluntary use and usefulness. These remain unrun acceptance requirements, not a claim of reliable psychology.

Schema additions, if a selected gap requires them, change current writers/readers/validators together under the [development save policy](../../AGENTS.md#development-save-policy). Replacement development starts preserve useful integrated authored content through [starting-world reconciliation](../save-and-load.md#starting-world-content-reconciliation). This design does not authorize discarding a compatible saved world.

Continued work and receipt recovery use the existing one semantic mutation owner. A retained result establishes what happened, never current permission to repeat it. An external inference completing after cancellation is accounted under its actual receipt but cannot regain authority. Losing a network response does not warrant a second effect or a free retry.

## 7. Performance and cost contract

### 7.1 Growth, shared preparation and invalidation

Cost grows with active actors, perceived changes per actor, exact dependencies of selected work, offered tools/targets, active commitments, and eligible retained history. Sparse quiet actors and many distinct invitations to one resident are different workloads. No bound on final prompt size establishes a bound on discovery, source selection or database work.

- Continue unchanged native steps without paid selection; reuse the existing activity frontier rather than re-expanding a full life plan.
- Share immutable method/definition compilation, target-independent prerequisite checks and request-scoped observed inventory facts. Per-person knowledge, consent, attention and accepted bindings remain private/exact.
- Schedule known chosen deadlines with existing native boundary/intake owners. Do not scan every stored method, memory, concern or inactive actor each tick.
- Reuse indexed exact outcome/support lookup and batched permitted source preparation; never hydrate whole history to find the last relevant apology or work result.
- Retain only derived actor-local subscription data within its existing lifecycle; clear on restore/removal. A long-lived cache needs complete source/definition/access/time invalidation before introduction.
- Avoid adding a timer/worker per person or interest. Current shared intake and serialized effect publication remain; bounded asynchronous preparation does not make CPU work parallel automatically.

Finite nodes and current actor-local plan/history bounds protect an invocation; they do not promise an unlimited population, sustainable community or cheap lifetime recall. Existing top-result bounds, byte admission, interest expiry and quiet scheduling are unchanged by this design. The [shared intelligent-simulation inventory](../limits/intelligent-simulation.md) links their owners, reachable growth and conditional tuning rather than copying numerical defaults here.

### 7.2 Complete-path measurement before an efficiency claim

Measure the actual perception/intake → allowed source preparation → offered choices → Jev/native admission → committed results → subsequent decision path. Count native steps, distinct cues, coalesced opportunities, visited candidates, source rows/bytes, SQL statements, model requests including “continue,” provider latency/usage/cost and time-to-meaningful-action. Report cold rebuild, warm steady state and invalidation bursts separately.

Compare matched scenarios before/after: one continuing task; many unchanged actors; concentrated invitations; a changed selected input; a quiet unfamiliar remark; long actor history; correction during a read; current-format reopen. Keep a previously cheap direct action cheap. Unknown preparation or timed-out work is not a valid negative decision. Measure source/query plans only if changed database selection is part of the implementation; new indexes require representative fresh and aged data, not merely `LIMIT`.

No universal new latency target, benchmark population, price or cognition cadence is selected. Qualification must use the offered world clock and current player/model waiting; accelerated manual time proves different mechanics. Record every Jev batch and attributable precise cumulative cost under the existing spending policy. A native continuation saving calls is useful only if the complete behavior and cost remain acceptable.

## 8. Complete journeys and contrasting world

**Current supported activity, interruption and ending.** Have the resident voluntarily select an existing finite camp activity through ordinary offers. The human pursues a separate supported activity. Observe actual inputs/results, an interruption the resident can perceive, exact suspended work and a later eligible resumption or newly chosen alternative. Repeat with a depleted resource, chosen deadline expiring during interruption, explicit stop, unknown condition and changed grant. Reopen during active and paused work. Work receipts must agree in every case; voluntary satisfied stopping remains a separate live-choice question.

**Quiet encounter and later conduct.** Use an actual permitted invitation, refusal, apology or promise outcome between independently informed residents. Deliver a relevant cue while one decision is in flight; later offer an ordinary opportunity without prescribing the response. Compare presence/absence of the earlier evidence and a contrary interpretation. Verify what reached the choice, distinguish deliberate declining from unavailable cognition, and inspect each resident's private knowledge separately. Repeat with a common-source rumor and authorized forgetting while retrieval is pending. No relationship score or required friendly ending is introduced.

**Hypothetical different world.** A nonhuman inspection machine chooses a finite test using an owned probe, one designated spare, a charge ceiling and a selected deadline. An unavailable primary probe can take the selected branch; a locked probe belonging to another machine cannot. A colleague's correction is testimony until supported testing; repeated copies of that correction do not become repeated measurements. The generic node, evidence, deadline and native admission mechanisms remain; human hunger, kinship, sleep and belonging semantics are absent. If installed supported mechanisms cannot express a selected probe condition, add a narrow trusted family to that world, not a base-world fallback in server code.

These journeys qualify engine authority and distinct choices; they do not prove unattended economic viability, universal psychological modeling, human enjoyment or an unimplemented nonhuman world. Preserve failed comparisons rather than keep changing biography until one desired action appears.

First delivery must not wait for or silently implement it.

The numerical-reaction slice has four implementation steps, each preserving the current baseline: (1) install the selected opt-in profile and its exact scale/choice vocabulary through the current manifest/validator; (2) extend the existing immediate-operation schema, binder, provider adapters and native receipt/publication path, with strict finite selection and no required prose; (3) carry current intensity/meaning through private inspection, later decisions, correction/forgetting and current-format persistence; (4) complete §5.5's native/failure/lifecycle checks and matched live CE episode before claiming behavioral value. Detailed progress remains in existing trackers.

**Tradeoffs.** Exact finite composition makes selection inspectable and cheap but cannot express unrestricted alternatives; a fresh choice is preferable to silent overreach. Change-fed dependencies avoid polling but cannot recognize all novel meaning; bounded semantic/quiet consideration remains necessary. Reusing one memory/knowledge path reduces inconsistency but makes source/retention defects consequential; repair that path and disclose unavailable evidence rather than pin every encounter forever. Automatic valid resumption preserves a real prior choice; qualifying new willingness separately avoids presenting it as fresh autonomous intention.

**Selected decisions and remaining scope.** The direct numerical-reaction contract and minimal joy profile are selected design, including their explicit tunable first-profile hypotheses; there is no remaining developer question blocking this design. They replace the earlier blanket exclusion of numerical psychological changes from this pair. Wider D69 needs, ACT09 process-authoring lifecycle, materially changed quiet cadence, clock/background-risk choices, operating offers and model/spending policies retain their existing separate scope where unresolved. Design selection is not runtime installation, implementation authorization or evidence of voluntary use. An implementation must resolve only a genuinely applicable remaining policy before its dependent work, while completing independent authorized scope.
