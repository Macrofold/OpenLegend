## Purpose and decision

Make the entire Open Legend interface understandable through the player's current place, character, object and purpose. Mike's original October 3 request covered every in-game menu and interaction; inventory was an example. The earlier research and implementation concentrated too heavily on inventory, contextual camp tasks, crafting and conversation. Calling that the completed UI research/redesign was inaccurate. The October 4 clarification corrects this document's scope; it is not a newly added requirement.

The [whole-interface coverage map](../ui-ux/interface-coverage.md) retains the 58-group audit across ordinary play, entry and settings, character and history, creator work, operations and shared interaction, and separately records [two later integrated death/attack groups](../ui-ux/interface-coverage.md#later-integrated-surfaces--october-6-2026). The research/design correction covered those 58 audited groups; the later additions use the existing lifecycle/confirmation journeys and their native owners. The subsequent authorized continuation implements the task hierarchy, Character/actions, readers/conversation, settings/lifecycle, creator/inspection and authored-world presentation described below. Existing inventory/task work is integrated into the same interface. The map distinguishes changed, reused and conditional capabilities; implementation and focused observations do not close the complete native gameplay and human-use acceptance.

The user-approved direction is **world object → relevant interaction → visible result**. The behavior below is the accepted interaction contract. The implementation and recorded focused checks do not by themselves establish completion: the real-game journeys remain required, including the integrated camp session. [Runtime evidence and limits](../verification/game-interaction-redesign.md) distinguish observed results from remaining qualification.

The [pinned pre-redesign audit](../ui-ux/current-interface-audit.md) preserves the diagnosis: one container collection and a detail pane led to a destination chooser and an additional exact-transfer submission; the inspected world object was not connected to a paired inventory view. Camp activities exposed internal parameter fields and a separate review. The runtime now connects objects to paired inventory and authored activity presentations while retaining native validation. Visual review also corrected the first care-task implementation into the compact summary described below. The audit describes its recorded revision, not the current replacement UI.

**Why the earlier UI became a form:** the pinned source audit shows a player workflow organized around existing bounded container discovery and typed action arguments. Destination eligibility and native parameter entry were exposed as steps the player had to perform, even when selecting the world object already supplied the intention and target. Those mechanisms are useful implementation building blocks; making the player operate them is the interaction mistake. The redesign retains validation and truthful scope while changing the task to select the thing, choose the relevant action and see the result. The earlier general-action worksheet repeated that mistake; its replacement now offers native actions first and an optional focused intention with an exact subject.

## One coherent interface across the whole game

### Information architecture and visual hierarchy

The world is the main view. Persistent interface space answers four questions: **Who am I? What is happening to me? What am I doing? What can I do here?** The active character and concerning conditions, current work with its target, game time/control state and a small stable action area answer those questions. The player should not need to open a management panel to discover the person they control or whether an action started. Routine success belongs beside the object or task; a failed save or lost control needs persistent, actionable status.

Navigation has three purposes. **Inventory, Character, Journal and Conversation** open ongoing player tasks. **Settings and help** expose preferences and explain controls; session/checkpoint actions retain a clearly labeled Game route. **Create, World operations and Diagnostics** appear through explicit role-appropriate entries. The current shell implements this hierarchy: Inventory, Character, Journal and Conversation on the left; In view, Crafting and Create on the right; Game and Settings/help above. Existing authorized capabilities remain reachable, including the ordinary character's invention workshop and Private mind. Owning a world must not turn every ordinary context menu into its administration menu.

Use the established theme, typography and interaction tokens. Headings name a person, object, purpose or record; a selected item is visibly selected; a primary action describes the impending verb. Green/gold decoration cannot be the only indication of permission, condition, selection, failure or new information. Icons support legible names, and unfamiliar invented objects retain readable labels. Detailed illustrations are welcome when they identify a thing; decoration must not consume the space needed to understand two items or a consequential decision. [Foundations](../ui-ux/foundations.md) and the [production UI guide](../../apps/client/src/design-system/README.md) retain token and current-layout ownership.

A primary workspace carries the current task. Opening a related detail, recipe or memory should retain a visible return path and the prior selection/reading position. A secondary picker or quantity choice appears near its context and dismisses before the parent. At narrow sizes the task becomes a readable sheet with its title, Back and primary action still reachable; it does not become a different workflow or require repeating previous choices. Existing dock/panel behavior supplies the starting implementation, not an obligation to keep every launcher or panel equally prominent.

The research supports this hierarchy through different examples, not one game copied wholesale. [BG3](../ui-ux/games/baldurs-gate-3.md) makes controlled actor, target and action resources distinguishable; [DOS2](../ui-ux/games/divinity-original-sin-2.md) separates world actions, skills and remembered conversation; [FFXIV](../ui-ux/games/final-fantasy-xiv.md) demonstrates stable HUD regions and explicit navigation; [WoW](../ui-ux/games/world-of-warcraft.md) demonstrates deliberate HUD configuration. Their crowded layouts and player disagreements also show why merely offering customization cannot excuse a poor default.

### World orientation and current work

Keep the controlled character's readable name and authored condition summary at a stable HUD anchor. Opening the summary reaches the relevant Character condition, not an unrelated overview. Ordinary stable values are quiet; a concerning change gains a label, not just a flashing color. A detail explains the known cause, applicable units/category and available response. Unknown is rendered as unknown. A proposed action's cost appears beside that action and must not masquerade as a value already lost.

The current-work strip names the action and target, for example “Carrying reeds to River bag,” with actual progress only when native work can measure it. Show interrupted/paused work distinctly from completed work. The current native cancellation stops active and suspended work, so the implemented control says **Stop all work** and discloses that consequence. A narrower **Stop this task** requires stable native task identity and scoped cancellation first; a physical-action identity alone does not cover waiting/queued plans. Selecting another object does not silently retarget ongoing work. Changing the task prepares a supported amendment or explains replacement before Start. A finished task updates its subject and remains discoverable in the appropriate retained record when one exists.

The clock shows simulation time and the actual pause/speed state. Opening a menu must not imply the world is paused unless the native state says so. Pausing simulation, pausing caption countdown, losing control to another tab and waiting for a reply are different states with different remedies. When maintenance prevents resume, name that reason and link its notice. Do not put an apparently working Play button beside an administrative pause it cannot lift. The player can return to the character/camera through a visible named control.

Speech, reaction labels, task status and narration need a coordinated attention order in a populated scene. Preserve speaker attribution; avoid laying a second status card directly over the selected actor's active task. Urgent conditions and input-blocking states remain discoverable while a reading panel is open. A missed-caption notice opens permitted event history rather than recreating speech the character never heard. [Minecraft](../ui-ux/games/minecraft.md), [Terraria](../ui-ux/games/terraria.md) and the MMO HUD examples inform status placement and optional detail; their hunger, mana, quest trackers and combat clocks are not universal Open Legend mechanics.

### World selection, camera and direct actions

Primary selection continues to inspect. Hover supplies a compact name and the most useful permitted state; selection persists for deeper reading and actions. Stationary secondary-click opens a short contextual menu. The selected detail also exposes those actions through visible named controls and the supported keyboard route. Keep ordinary verbs prominent, inspection available and creator intervention inside a separately labeled branch. Search is available when the action set needs it; a catalogue is not the first step in opening a bag or talking to a person.

An overlapping cluster needs disambiguation by visible name, type and permitted location/appearance, with the selected object emphasized in the world. **In view** provides a deliberate readable alternative to precision pointing and a recovery route when rendering fails. It represents currently perceived results with their actual bounds; it is not an omniscient world search. BG3's active search is useful evidence for this alternative entry, while the dossier's reach/selection criticism warns against hidden target switching.

Keep camera motion separate from character motion. Follow, center, zoom, orbit/tilt, projection and level controls remain named and consistent with existing bindings. Common recovery stays visible; advanced view tuning can expand from the camera group. A level change must identify the inspected level without implying the character moved there. Closing a menu, using its scrollbar or releasing a drag cannot walk the character or move the camera. A short contextual help hint explains an unfamiliar gesture at the task, and remains available later through Controls.

Sight, hearing and action reach are distinct overlays/explanations. Label the selected guide and describe what it means. Show only permitted information; a see-through preference never expands knowledge. For an attempted action, report the actual known obstruction/reach/access issue beside that object. If Approach is supported, offer **Walk to …** as a separate intention, preserve the target and check again on arrival. Do not silently auto-walk on inspection or present a perception-radius circle as exact handling admission.

The existing three pinned quick actions should stay spatially stable and distinguish execution from reassignment. Suggestions form a separate changing area and freeze while hovered/focused. Each action identifies its target or enters a visible target-selection step. A shortcut that resolves a resource must make the chosen permitted resource understandable before consequential commitment; it must not silently consume a different valuable supply. Changed eligibility gives a reachable reason and an alternative such as selecting another target. Number keys remain owned by text entry whenever a field or composition session has focus. [Factorio](../ui-ux/games/factorio.md) provides specific evidence for stable shortcuts; [BG3](../ui-ux/games/baldurs-gate-3.md) provides target and committed-resource distinctions.

### Character, abilities and general action

Organize Character around **Condition**, **Capabilities**, **Belongings/equipment**, and **Personal record**, with current work reachable from its summary. Condition contains the installed world's meters and status effects, their permitted explanations and useful native self-actions. Capabilities names known actions/learned activities and explains whether a capability is learned, currently available through an item or otherwise supplied by supported world rules. It does not add skill points, levels, preparation slots or a universal body diagram just because a reference RPG has them. The reservoir preset's Charge/Integrity must read naturally without human hunger or wilderness vocabulary leaking into the screen.

Personal identity, traits, memories, experiences and commitments remain characterful and readable, with a route to their richer history. Personal belief/name knowledge is distinguishable from objective world truth. The controlled character's Private mind remains an ordinary permitted personal route; viewing or changing someone else's private state retains its creator authority and a clear identity header. Death, exiting, away and unavailable-control states describe what the player can actually do next. Revival is shown only through a supported authorized capability, not invented as a universal respawn button.

The general **Take an action** worksheet is replaced by native contextual actions and an optional **Describe an action** task. Routine native actions stay with their relevant object, item, Character capability or stable shortcut. The selected actor/object/item binds the information already known. Choosing a targeted ability enters **Choose a target for [action]**: emphasize eligible perceived candidates, retain the source action and offer Cancel. Selection is a preview; the commitment control names the action and selected target. Known costs, duration, interruption or replacement appear there. Invalid candidates explain the native reason without losing the action. Off-screen/hidden targets cannot be selected from a fabricated list.

For open-ended intentions, keep an optional natural-language entry such as **Describe what you want to do**, with the current subject shown as a removable/editable named chip. Sending an intention is distinct from silently executing an unrelated guessed action. When native interpretation requires a meaningful choice, ask that one choice in context: choose an actual item, target, recipient or amount. Do not present Target, Item, Tool, For, Amount and Work mode as a mandatory schema worksheet. Preserve the original words through clarification/refusal. If interpretation requires unavailable or budgeted intelligence, explain that before the request and expose the appropriate setup/allowance route only to its owner.

Advanced exact control is useful for deliberate debugging or authoring; it belongs in the authorized specialist surface and must not be the default player route. The same native action owner still receives complete validated intentions. A streamlined UI does not erase real choices about valuable materials, consent, an attack, replacing work or spending a scarce capability. In combat-like situations, show actor, target, known commitment and actual result using those native semantics; BG3's initiative order, movement budget, hit percentages and turn-ending rules are references only where Open Legend has equivalent facts. [DOS2's abilities](../ui-ux/games/divinity-original-sin-2.md) informs capability provenance; [BG3's action HUD](../ui-ux/games/baldurs-gate-3.md) informs target/cost clarity.

### Making things and placement

Crafting begins with a known desired output or an appropriate station/capability. The list shows readable results with search/categories when supported knowledge warrants them. Selecting one shows the actual ingredients, usable quantities, time, output facts and known limitations. An unavailable recipe stays understandable: “Need suitable cord” is more useful than disappearing. A permitted ingredient detail can lead to the relevant inventory or known resource context, retaining the recipe on return. Search within known recipes is distinct from inventing a new one.

Only meaningful substitutions open a choice; context binds the station and actor. Immediate making has one clear commitment; longer native work exposes current progress and Stop through the shared task strip. A native one-unit operation is labeled as such. Queueing, mass craft, alternate material automation and remote material access require actual native support before controls promise them. Ingredient preview is not reservation or custody. Current output-first Crafting and compact camp work provide a starting slice, not coverage of every possible action family.

Where an installed capability supports placing/constructing something, preview the actual candidate position/orientation, permitted obstruction/fit evidence, materials and consequence before commitment. Moving a preview is not constructing. Cancel leaves the world unchanged; the native receipt determines the result. The existing creator spawn/person-position path must distinguish ground placement from carried creation and preserve its deliberate authoring role. [Valheim](../ui-ux/games/valheim.md), [Terraria](../ui-ux/games/terraria.md) and [Project Zomboid](../ui-ux/games/project-zomboid.md) supply reference placement/status patterns. A ghost building, snapping/rotation system or general construction catalogue is conditional on supported mechanics, not a feature smuggled in through a mockup.

### Conversation, captions and narrative interruptions

The conversation workspace names its person/session and who is speaking. The recipient is not confused with who can hear. Preserve the fixed header, readable transcript, one-line growing composer, vertical speech-volume control and dots-only pending reply treatment. Switching a person restores that person's appropriately scoped draft; it cannot silently send words addressed to someone else. Item mentions remain visible, editable permitted names. Sending is explicit, and editing during an outstanding request cannot let an old result erase newer text.

History preserves reading position when new words arrive; a **New messages** affordance returns to the latest point deliberately. Older-page loading retains a recognizable anchor. Failure, interruption and an unresolved send belong to the affected message, with the owner's actual recovery action. The UI must not expose provider/tool stages as dialogue or label provisional speech as the final settled record. A historical transcript is not evidence of current hearing permission. Long names and multiline prose wrap without separating the speaker from the words.

Overhead captions and narration remain complementary to the transcript. Caption preferences govern presentation time, not world time or hearing. A narrator announcement is attributable, dismissible when appropriate and recoverable from its actual retained story location. Avoid stealing typing focus or covering the active action with a nonurgent story card. Player-facing choices/topics can be suggestions where supported, while natural-language speech stays available. [Disco Elysium](../ui-ux/games/disco-elysium.md) supports attribution and narrative continuity, [DOS2](../ui-ux/games/divinity-original-sin-2.md) supports dated transcript navigation, and [FFXIV](../ui-ux/games/final-fantasy-xiv.md) separates audience from reading filters. None grants access to private thoughts or unreceived speech.

### Journal, memory and spatial information

Journal distinguishes **possible beginnings**, **the character's promises**, and **personal story**. A suggested beginning is an invitation, a promise has its actual terms/status/due evidence, and narration is an account. Do not flatten them into a quest checklist or offer cancel/edit controls unsupported by the promise owner. On return, consequential unfinished matters are easy to find, while the player can read the full story. Selection, newly received information, an optional pinned reminder and completed/resolved state have separate labels; a dot cannot stand for all four.

World Events contains what the character perceived; Memory contains retained/known personal records; Conversation contains utterances; Journal contains its established personal material. Use consistent reading controls, headers and return navigation, but label the source instead of merging their privacy/evidence meanings. Search states say which record range was searched, whether older results may exist and whether loading stopped or failed. **No matches in the searched records** is distinct from “This never happened.” Fresh updates offer a deliberate jump, preserving the older reading position and any active edit.

A referenced known person, place or object may link to its permitted current detail or explain that only the remembered description is available. A historical location is not a live tracker. If a player map is later supported, it must distinguish discovered/remembered places, current perception, player marks and uncertain locations, with a readable legend and return-to-character action. The current operations overview is a public administrative projection; it cannot be promoted to an omniscient player map. The redesigned current UI can improve navigation among existing journal/event/memory/world selection without inventing fast travel, quest coordinates or a new mapping system.

[BG3/DOS2](../ui-ux/games/README.md), [FFXIV](../ui-ux/games/final-fantasy-xiv.md), [Guild Wars 2](../ui-ux/games/guild-wars-2.md) and [Disco](../ui-ux/games/disco-elysium.md) provide journal/map/ability examples with different rules and explicit screenshot limits. Their good headings and direct topic-to-detail routes transfer more safely than copying their quest economies or hidden map knowledge. [WoW](../ui-ux/games/world-of-warcraft.md) illustrates why map legends and icon clutter need their own inspection.

### Entering, resuming and leaving

Entry explains the current world and account context while loading. **Sign in**, **Check access again**, **Change account** and **Retry connection** mean different things and appear beside the appropriate reason. A forbidden account is not an outage; changing accounts is not a retry. The current product enters its configured world and does not have a general new-world/world-browser flow. If world selection is introduced later, it needs explicit name/character/control semantics, not a decorative chooser added to this pass.

An invite link carries its result through sign-in to the appropriate character or operations destination. Accepted/already a member, expired/revoked/used, unavailable character and storage failure have distinct next steps. Avoid an undifferentiated “Something went wrong” screen or claiming an accepted invitation means a particular character is currently controllable. Dismissing the result clears only its notice/link parameter, according to the existing entry owner.

Preserve the accepted automatic sole-tab entry/reload/refocus behavior. Another controlling tab shows **Game Paused**, **Resume Here** and **Log Out** with pending/error feedback; the UI must not reintroduce mandatory manual Resume on every entry. Losing local control hides or disables private interaction as required. Reconnection can happen automatically without replaying actions. A scene-rendering failure offers **Retry scene** and **Open In view**, while a connection interruption and failed persistence remain separately explained. **Log Out** clears private presentation across tabs; simply closing a panel or becoming temporarily away does not pretend to log out.

Onboarding is contextual and recoverable. A first encounter with selection/Open, camera recovery, talking or current-work Stop can expose a short relevant hint and the current input binding. Help remains reachable afterward; dismissing a hint is not a permanent loss of access. Do not force a questionnaire before the player can act. Returning players see their character, current work and relevant retained story without needing a new-user tour. [DOS2's entry/HUD](../ui-ux/games/divinity-original-sin-2.md) and the official MMO navigation guides inform clear return routes; the complete account/permission model remains Open Legend's.

### Settings, controls, help and accessibility

Group settings by the decision: **Appearance and reading**, **Camera and graphics**, **Controls and help**, and **Account/session**, with authoring policy or World operations as explicit authorized destinations. Show the control's scope where it matters: this device, this player's profile, or the whole world. A theme or reading-size adjustment previews locally; a revisioned world policy retains explicit Save and stale/busy/error handling. Log Out and removing another account's access must not resemble harmless style toggles.

Appearance includes the supported themes/UI scale, reduced motion, caption visibility, countdown pause and reading-time multiplier. Explain that caption countdown does not pause the simulation. Graphics distinguishes Detailed/Economy shadow cost from character-visible reveal mode/radius/strength; reveal never changes permission to know hidden content. Audio/subtitle, controller or other preferences appear only when the game actually implements those capabilities. Credits/font/icon attribution stays discoverable in About/help, without competing with the player's immediate task.

Controls lists the actual movement/camera/selection/action/chat/menu bindings and the non-drag alternatives. Contextual help links to the relevant part. Show a visible focus state, reachable disabled reasons and a clear way back. Offered UI scale is not a substitute for browser/text zoom or a guarantee of assistive-device usability. Text entry, search, quantity editing and authoring must survive long names, wrapped labels, blank drafts and composition input. The full acceptance matrix covers meaningful combinations of keyboard, pointing, short/narrow layouts, enlarged text and reduced motion; it does not claim controller/touch support solely from responsive CSS.

**IME means input method editor.** It is the system used to compose characters, for example when a person types a pronunciation and chooses Chinese or Japanese characters. During that composition, Enter may confirm a candidate rather than send a chat message, and letter/number keys belong to writing rather than game shortcuts. Native IME qualification means trying that real operating-system input workflow; simulated text insertion and ordinary Latin-keyboard checks do not establish it. See [Controls](../ui-ux/controls.md) and [MDN's composition event documentation](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Element/compositionstart_event).

### Saves, checkpoints and recovery

The HUD's durable-save status and a named checkpoint are distinct. Game → Checkpoints identifies each saved world/timeline by name, relevant game/real time and compatibility, with clear catalogue paging/refresh and selected detail. **Create checkpoint** does not imply loading it. **Load** deliberately replaces the current world and explains the native pause/restart consequences plus required **Before last load** protection. The confirmation keeps the exact selected checkpoint visible. Deletion names the checkpoint and retains its existing confirmation.

Automatic checkpoint settings explain interval and retention, current writing/queued manual work, latest successful checkpoint and durable failure. Acknowledge means the notice was read, not that protection was repaired. Retention targets are not a promise that disk cannot fill. Manual request and automatic protection status remain understandable when they coexist. A failed or uncertain load/create/delete uses its existing request identity and recovery; **Check result** must not create another checkpoint or load a second time.

When a world timeline changes, old private drafts/contents cannot briefly appear as current. A successful return identifies the loaded timeline and actual paused/control state, then offers the native next action. Useful earlier save/recovery implementation is retained, including existing protection and receipt ownership. This redesign changes information hierarchy, not save format, recovery authority or development-save policy. The reference-game entry and menu research supports identification and deliberate resume; [save/load](../save-and-load.md) owns Open Legend's more consequential shared-world behavior.

### Operations, invitations and maintenance

Operations starts with **which world, which role/capabilities, current status, and what can be done here**. A characterless spectator gets a useful read-only overview and honest empty/unavailable states. A player entering operations has a visible **Return to your character** route when permitted. The existing public SVG overview shows categories/counts/omissions; it is not private character inspection, a complete spectator camera or gameplay participation. Viewing operations must not acquire character control or generate activity merely through polling.

Invitation creation remains deliberate structured administration. Explain player/spectator/operator roles in terms of their actual capabilities, show the eligible character and any delegable capabilities, and clarify expiry. Create/copy the one-time link explicitly. Revoking an unused invitation differs from removing access for an existing account; the latter keeps a named consequential confirmation. A local environment without the necessary identity service explains that limitation at the task. Long account/character labels and unavailable selections need readable reasons, not disappearing options.

Maintenance creation/editing retains date, time, timezone, ambiguous-time choice, notice and revisioned save. Show both the announced time and the operator's local interpretation. Starting, rescheduling, extending, cancelling and marking ready/resuming are distinct actions. Player notices communicate what changed, whether play is blocked and whether the expected end has passed; an estimate is not automatic readiness. Active notices retain their nondismissable meaning while narrow layouts give accessible detail. Maintenance wall time, simulation time, caption time and provider waiting cannot share one ambiguous paused indicator.

These are settings/administration tasks where a well-designed form is appropriate. The design removes irrelevant fields and explains consequence; it does not turn precise scheduling or authority delegation into an unreviewed one-click action. Existing [multiplayer/entry owners](../ui-ux/interface-coverage.md#entry-settings-and-world-administration) retain their implemented behavior and recorded evidence.

### Invention and reviewed creator work

Ordinary player invention and owner World Agent work are distinct permitted routes. The actor workshop starts from a desired technique/recipe, relevant known capability or contextual intention. **Similar inventions** clearly offers use an existing result, modify it, propose something new, retry a failed search or continue without search. An incomplete search is not proof there are no similar ideas. Historical unavailable techniques remain inspectable with an honest limitation. Optional raw proposal data is advanced authoring input, not the player's default invention form.

The World Agent workspace has an identifiable session, Conversation and saved Work. Hiding the workspace, ending a session, stopping a request, starting a new session and reading history have different labels and effects. History is readable without accidentally continuing or resubmitting paid work. Show available allowance and the consequence of a new request where relevant; opening a saved record or exact receipt must not start inference.

Typed questions keep their context in the conversation and ask only the unresolved meaningful decision. Choice/custom switching preserves appropriate drafts; **Save answer**, **Send answer and continue**, changing a previous answer and separate **Continue** retain their actual distinctions. An answer never doubles as approval of a world change. Long option text wraps, errors stay by the affected answer and an uncertain original answer offers its receipt/recovery path. Reducing ordinary game forms does not remove precise authoring choices.

Saved Work identifies **inspected revision**, **latest saved revision**, unsaved local changes and any **prepared/approved revision** separately. Browse all seven supported kinds through readable purpose/result summaries. A history comparison explains changed facts and dependencies, not only raw JSON. Native limitations and unavailable checks remain visible. Nonrecipe work returns deliberately to Conversation for supported revision; direct recipe fields come from installed metadata with units and exact-only fields distinguishable from editable ones.

Recipe editing follows select → edit → Preview → Save new revision, retaining blank drafts, local field errors and native evidence. Preview is not Save and becomes stale when relevant fields/state change. Newer saved work offers deliberate reapplication, not silent overwrite. Dirty navigation uses Keep/Discard with a visible affected-work identity. The original Save or Prepare can be recovered without erasing later local changes.

A consequential review names the exact candidate/revision, effects, affected records and current validation/dependency limitations. **Approve**, **Reject** and **Apply** retain their actual separate semantics. After Apply, show the authoritative receipt/result, not a generic success that obscures whether the world changed. Reopening an applied review inspects the result; it does not Apply again. A long proposal keeps identity and commitment reachable while evidence scrolls. This preserves the substantial existing PW11 implementation and strengthens its information hierarchy; it is not a claim PW11 was missing. [Factorio's machinery/recipe feedback](../ui-ux/games/factorio.md) and the RPG progression/compare examples inform clear proposed/current/committed states, while existing native authoring contracts govern approval.

### Direct world and character editing

Creator item/person/environment creation visibly identifies the kind, destination or world position, exact quantity where applicable and authoring purpose. Selecting a ground location is different from putting a new object in someone's inventory. Creation is not acquisition of an existing object. Preview/check evidence is separate from committing creation, and a pending original request retains its identity. Empty traits using a native random default must be explained before creation; it must not look like the resulting person has no traits.

An uncertain creation keeps its exact original details through closing/reopening and same-owner reload. **Retry original creation** explicitly recovers or performs that original request; opening a menu or reconnecting does not submit it. One unresolved creation in the current private character/world/timeline blocks fresh creator creation until its outcome and local cleanup are resolved, while unrelated play remains available. A browser that cannot retain the request sends nothing. A confirmed refusal allows editing; a refusal after an earlier unknown result does not erase that uncertainty. Item, person and environment entries share this behavior. Successful ordinary environment selection remains immediate, without an added form or confirmation. Private logout/access and timeline cleanup remain authoritative; this is not a promise to retain private creation after leaving that lifetime. The [implementation and native replay plan](game-interaction-redesign-tech-design.md#recover-item-person-and-environment-creation) owns the shared request/receipt implementation and its separately scoped controlled and [native recovery evidence](../verification/game-interaction-redesign.md#native-recovery-for-item-person-and-environment-creation); broader J40 acceptance remains open.

Authorized character interventions are labeled creator actions in every entry. Revive, grant cognition/speech, person/family editing and another character's private mind each explain their actual supported effect and refusal reason. They do not silently change what the controlled character knows. Consistent entry labels and availability prevent Character, context menus and editors from appearing to offer different authority.

The shared person editor maintains subject identity and unsaved state across Character, Inventory, Identity, Memories and World Events. The Save action identifies the actual change scope, while separately saved configuration remains visibly separate. Editing a meter, replacing goals, changing known names, editing a memory and adjusting awareness are different facts with meaningful labels/units. Fresh source changes cannot overwrite dirty fields silently. Save/Discard/unsaved-close recovery stays reachable at short/narrow sizes, and window positioning has non-drag/keyboard access. Long authored prose and lazy raw details must not push the primary action outside a reachable scroll structure.

Global event editing distinguishes the objective event from one person's awareness of it. Deletion and configuration saves explain their actual effect; future narration policy does not rewrite the already-recorded past by implication. Where broader dependency/cascade previews are not implemented, say what is and is not known at the decision. Do not represent the existence of Delete as a complete dependency-aware editor.

Family authoring retains its delivered directional preview: focal child/parent, proposed native relation, computed relationships, exact create/delete and deliberate confirmation. Objective lineage is distinguished from what a character believes or remembers; deleting a relation does not rewrite memories. Large ancestry navigation/paging and long labels need readable context, but a graph canvas is not automatically better than the existing explicit relationship view. Existing family/person/native contracts and their evidence remain controlling. ### Knowledge, dependencies and diagnostics

Knowledge/private-mind views name whose perspective is open and whether the user is reading personal knowledge or exercising creator authority. General/person knowledge, known names, About me, feelings/notepads, actions, experiences and commitments use readable sections with clear read/edit states. Search/paging while editing preserves dirty content; stale revision feedback offers a deliberate refresh/reapply decision. Authored feelings and objective state must not be presented as the same kind of evidence.

Relationship/dependency inspection begins with a permitted named definition/entity, then explains **uses**, **produces**, **depends on**, or **used by** as supported by its projection. Forward/reverse navigation retains a breadcrumb and selected context. Bounded traces/frontiers explain where inspection stopped and why a missing relation is not proved absent. Exact source revision and raw data remain available deliberately; a speculative graph cannot imply completeness. Reading is unpaid and does not rerun the underlying world action.

Intelligence is an explicit diagnostics workspace. Its history distinguishes actor/trigger/route/outcome/time; selecting a record reveals actual stages, retrieval, questions, gates, failed/skipped/partial work and optional input/output. Filtering/paging/follow-new state is obvious and does not jump the reader away from an inspected failure. Billing is fetched only through its supported deliberate route and distinguishes unknown from zero. Copying or inspecting a request does not resend it. Drag/resize supports inspection without being the only means of reaching detail.

The player-facing question is usually **Can this action work, is it waiting, and what can I do next?** Provider setup, execution internals and currency belong to the authorized owner/diagnostic route. Configured versus fixture mode and monthly allowance/reservation are accurately labeled there. A rendering-rate readout likewise belongs in deliberate performance detail by default; it measures actual render passes, not simulation speed, provider latency or GPU timing. Optional HUD exposure can be a preference. Ordinary speech and current-work status retain game language.

### Shared interaction contract

Across all surfaces, distinguish a **draft**, **selected candidate**, **preview**, **submitted request**, **committed result**, **refusal** and **unknown outcome**. The main control names its action. A reversible routine item move commits directly; an attack/consumption/work replacement explains its meaningful commitment; a timeline replacement or reviewed world edit retains consequential review. A successful network send is not proof the game changed. Unknown outcomes offer the exact owner's receipt check, not a universal Retry that may execute again.

Input belongs to the active permitted layer. Text and IME composition own their keys; a popup consumes its click; a drag owns its pointer until completion/cancel/lost capture. Escape cancels the active subtask or closes the top child first. Back returns to the originating context. Outside-click closes only an appropriate dismissible layer and never clicks the world beneath it. A blocking access/maintenance/unsaved-work decision retains its stated behavior. Returning focus follows the still-valid task, not whichever control existed before an asynchronous request began.

Task continuity is intentional. Hiding, reopening, resizing, reconnecting or a permitted same-owner Resume can preserve appropriately scoped work after fresh access. Account/grant/character/world/timeline changes invalidate the old private state according to its owner. Shared preferences, navigation, private prose and unresolved commands are different persistence classes. A stale reply cannot restore prior private content, erase newer drafts or declare a different selected object successful.

Loading is not empty; unavailable is not zero; no matches in a bounded search is not exhaustive absence. Keep useful known data explicitly marked as last inspected where allowed. Failure offers a nearby remedy and retains valid user choices. Disabled actions have reachable reasons. Do not cover a needed correction with an unhelpful toast or silently replace it with generic transport language. Retained consequential status has an appropriate home; transient feedback is supplementary.

Accessibility and usability are journey-level acceptance. Keyboard/non-drag operation, native composition, assistive devices, browser/text zoom, realistic long content, short/narrow views, reduced motion and interrupted states must be observed where applicable. Reference screenshots and semantic markup alone cannot certify them. Uncoached observation asks players to accomplish tasks and records hesitation, errors, unintended movement, lost context and successful completion without teaching the proposed solution first. [Verification guidance](../ui-ux/verification.md) owns the method; the journeys below own the work to demonstrate.

## Inventory interaction

### Open the chest you are looking at

A stationary right-click on a perceived chest opens its short contextual menu. **Open** is prominent; the same action is available from the selected object's visible details and keyboard route. A player does not need to know the right-click gesture to discover it. Primary selection continues to inspect, and dragging continues to control the camera according to the existing bindings.

Opening identifies this exact object, reads its permitted contents and opens the inventory workspace. The player's belongings are on the left and the named chest on the right. The world remains visible around the task when there is space. Both collections stay visible while moving items. A chest's title, appearance and permitted place description establish which chest is open; a dropdown of all nearby containers does not replace that relationship.

If out of reach, selecting or inspecting the chest still does not move the character. Offer **Walk to and open** only as an explicit movement-and-open intention when supported. It follows a valid route, can be cancelled and checks reach/access again on arrival. If no route exists, explain the obstruction. Opening is never a remote view into hidden contents.

The current bundled-world handling rule is 1.6 metres between interaction anchors, with an obstruction check; the audit links its authored source. The interface should say **Too far to open** or **Blocked by the wall**, with truthful permitted spatial guidance. It must not show a decorative circle that pretends ordinary feet-to-object distance is the exact admission calculation. Other installed worlds can define different reach and means of access.

### Two collections, one clear transfer

Each side has an unmistakable title, a compact item grid, relevant quantity/capacity information and local search/sort when useful. Use small item names or a readable-label mode for unfamiliar invented objects. Quantity, equipped state and a player's protected-item mark must survive icon similarity and color changes. Item details are supporting information rather than a permanent third pane consuming the room needed by the two collections.

Grid cells organize the display. They do not introduce physical slot capacity, Tetris shapes, automatic stacking, item teleportation or a new universal weight rule. The world's native object/capacity facts remain authoritative. Visual grouping of two similar objects is not a domain merge.

The implemented desktop controls follow this grammar; their complete input and layout qualification remains part of J09:

| ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Click an item                                           | Select and inspect it; keep its location visible.                                              |
| Drag to the other open collection                       | Move the chosen item or stack through native handling admission.                               |
| Shift-click or Shift+Enter on an item                   | Quick-move its exact inspected available quantity to the other open collection.                |

Quick-move, drag and the explicit Move action share native handling and keep the destination named. Show the quick-move binding in help and the relevant item action; no hidden modifier is the only route. A second click must not accidentally repeat a consequential action. Clicking outside an ordinary item drop zone cancels the drag; dropping into the world requires the explicit supported Drop route, not an accidental release over scenery. Existing application shortcuts and input ownership remain controlling.

A routine reversible transfer is the player's commitment. It does not open another review asking them to select the already-open chest or confirm the same move. Show pending state on the affected item, then actual quantities on receipt. Before completion, do not make the item usable at both ends. A failure restores the visible truthful collection and explains the reason beside the task.

An uncertain move retains its exact original request. **Check result** reads that request's native receipt without moving the item again; hiding, reopening or resuming control is not a new transfer. If the browser cannot retain the recovery record, inventory refuses the send and explains that nothing was sent. This restriction protects recovery rather than guessing whether a timed-out move happened. [Recovery contract](game-interaction-redesign-tech-design.md#direct-transfer-without-weaker-authority)

### Small choices stay small

One gesture submits the stack's **exact inspected available quantity** without another review. Reserved units stay where they are; the command does not expand to a different quantity at admission. A requested partial move adds only a quantity decision. All/Half are shortcuts, and an exact field stays available. Blank or intermediate input remains a draft. Changing an amount keeps the selected source and chest; any stale eligibility preview is refreshed without making the player choose that chest again. Changed native quantities or revisions can refuse the move rather than silently choosing another amount or item.

Equipping is a deliberate item action. The current native model has one equipped item and projected comparison facts with fixed supported eligibility; it does not provide a world-defined multi-slot schema. Show the actual item being replaced, relevant native comparison and a clear equipped marker. A compatible-slot drop is a conditional extension only where native equipment capabilities support it. A protected/favorite mark, if introduced, prevents accidental bulk disposal as a player preference; it is not permission to violate custody. Selling, consuming and destroying have different meanings and cannot share an ambiguous trash symbol.

Nested containers use readable breadcrumbs. One external object is active in the transfer workspace; opening a different chest replaces that external side deliberately. Each side keeps one finite contents page and its own search and continuation, replacing that page when navigating rather than accumulating every opened bag. This display scope is not a limit on stored possessions. A future logistics view may compare more storage, but is not necessary for ordinary chest use.

### Space, bulk handling and failure

Show native capacity in its applicable units; unknown capacity is not zero. Empty display cells are not proof that another object fits. A known invalid target is explained when approached or attempted, rather than silently disappearing from a list.

**Take all** and **Deposit matching** are useful candidates only after native multi-item semantics exist. They must state scope, protect equipped/favorite items where supported, and report actual partial/refused outcomes. Initial delivery can be complete without them; it cannot display a button that loops through untracked mutations and calls the batch successful. Terraria and Stardew provide lessons about chore reduction, not authority to move items into unopened or remote containers.

At narrow widths, stack the two named collections vertically, with a concise transfer action reachable from either one. If height makes simultaneous grids unusable, use explicit source/destination views with a persistent counterpart strip and selected-item summary; never switch to a nearby-container dropdown. Keep selection, quantity, search and reading positions. Enlarged text can reflow the task; it must not make item identity depend on hover.

Newly invented actions must use trusted semantic presentations and context binding; an unfamiliar definition is not permission to render every schema field or execute generated UI code.

## Delivery order and completion

The entire original scope remains accounted for through the coverage map and [UIUX07–UIUX18](../maintainers/ui-ux.md#uiux07). The implementation followed the task groups below; their remaining native and experience gates stay with the same owners:

1. **Finish the already-implemented everyday interaction qualification.** UIUX08–UIUX11 retain J01–J16 and current native authority/recovery gates. Fix reproduced failures through those owners; avoid a parallel legacy inventory experience.
2. **World HUD, contextual actions and Character.** UIUX12 owns the implemented navigation hierarchy, orientation, selected target, stable quick actions and replacement of the general-action worksheet. Supported world projections and existing renderer/control semantics remain authoritative.
3. **Reading and conversation continuity.** UIUX13 connects Journal, promises, memories, perceived events, captions and narration through coherent source/return behavior, retaining the original chat owner and hearing rules.
4. **Entry, preferences and session recovery.** UIUX14 covers account/invite entry, tab control, help/settings and checkpoint presentation through existing lifecycle/save owners.
5. **Player invention and creator authoring.** UIUX15 refines workshop/session/questions/Work/review and direct editors without removing meaningful revision, approval or Save boundaries. Existing family and authoring implementations remain the starting point.
6. **Operations and deliberate inspection.** UIUX16 covers public overview, access, maintenance, knowledge/dependency and Intelligence/cost/performance navigation under their actual roles.
7. **World portability and complete experience qualification.** UIUX17 retains the finite portability boundary and remaining different-world qualification after the implemented content corrections and recharge example; UIUX18 owns whole-journey input/layout and uncoached observation, drawing on each slice's real evidence.

The research, whole-interface design and coordinated implementation candidate are delivered. The project remains **In progress** while its accepted native and human-use qualification is open. Earlier inventory/task/chat code, the wider runtime changes, expanded research, nine original proposals and design documents have different evidence roles. Exact pixel dimensions, conflicting bindings and new native capabilities retain their explicit owners. They do not reopen the accepted direction of contextual ordinary play or turn source coverage and bounded checks into complete experience acceptance.
