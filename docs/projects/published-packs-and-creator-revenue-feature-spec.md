# Published packs and creator revenue

| Status      | Current progress                                                                                                                            | Last updated |

## 1. A useful invention should travel

A creator should find a useful capability, understand what it will do in their world, obtain its actual rights and reach ordinary play without recreating the same invention. An author should be credited and, where a selected offer pays them, understand what earned the money and when they can receive it. The product succeeds when reuse improves a wanted game. Catalog size, generated variations and a complicated royalty system are not substitutes.

This proposal expands ND23 and the broader ND12 publication/distribution journey. [PACK01–PACK06](../maintainers/published-packs-and-creator-revenue.md) owns delivery beneath [INV-8](../maintainers/inventions-and-world-evolution.md#inv-8--portable-inventions-and-later-algorithm-extensions), [EWF11](../maintainers/extensible-world-foundation.md#ewf11--reusable-construct-integration-and-local-portability-proof) and [PD10](../maintainers/production-deployment.md#conditional-launch-and-expansion). [PACK limits](../limits/published-packs-and-creator-revenue.md) owns new consumer choices; [WC-L06–WC-L12](../limits/world-creation.md#wc-l06--first-library-and-publication-scope) retains DG12's narrow free-reuse profile. Money, periods and recovery consume [DG27 commercial offers](customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md), not a new wallet or payment system.

[DG12](world-creation-feature-spec.md#15-dg12-expansion--a-useful-invention-follows-its-creator) already proposes exact retained versions, complete dependency closure, scoped provenance, separate installation/knowledge/materials and one free known-recipient transfer. Those product rules are not a claim that an account library or commercial importer exists. At the inspected baseline, local attribution and the cordage-to-container composition have scoped evidence; publication, cross-world account grants, payment and marketplace operation remain delivery work. Current recipe Apply teaches its initiating actor, so installation-only import needs the existing admission owner's explicit work.

The accepted [creator-economy direction](../../archive/06-marketing/creator-economy-and-mechanics-packs.md) includes free and paid packs, standalone premium worlds and a fixed amount from each platform subscriber for participating worlds. Exact amounts and formulas remain unadopted. Every numerical policy below is a reviewable proposal, not measured capacity, a current price, an actual fund or an earning promise.

## 2. The smallest useful catalog

Begin with two curated entries in the supported managed-operator environment, each leading to a known compatible destination and a real useful action. The free proof and paid-sale qualification are separate stages. An author can publish an eligible free release without completing bank/tax onboarding, joining a revenue program or operating a popular world.

| Initial entry         | Useful promise                                                                                                                               | Qualification and boundary                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Field sling reference | Reuse one exact admitted sling recipe and its complete eligible requirements                                                                 | Free reference release. The character still learns, gathers, crafts and uses it; this does not replace the first-playable live-invention proof with a canned recipe.                                                                |
| Camp kit              | Reuse one woven-container recipe with its exact cordage producer and other required definitions, then organize a small expedition's supplies | The candidate first commercial listing after a free portability proof and actual rights/price adoption. Existing freely released versions keep their grants. Its price must buy a useful reviewed package, not a renamed duplicate. |

The [authored container](../worlds/base/camp-containers.md) organizes possessions and permits ordinary packing, carrying and voluntary sharing. It gives no extra carrying strength, preservation, waterproofing or theft protection. The [cordage family](../worlds/base/items.md#cordage-manufacture-and-reuse) supports one positively checked material-production edge, not arbitrary recursive crafting. Describe these actual benefits. If arranging supplies in a kit is not useful enough to justify buying it, retain the free example and test another demonstrated need before opening paid sales.

A pack is a distribution unit. The camp kit's selected root can include a separately useful producer as a dependency; that does not retroactively turn two original qualifying inventions into one DG27 allowance unit. Unchanged authorized acquisition/import itself consumes no new invention unit. Optional adaptation, artwork and operating AI have their existing separate accounting.

Start with DG12's one-root, complete-closure envelope: at most 32 distinct definition references and one MiB of decoded definition/manifest text, one active import preparation per destination, native appearance and no arbitrary executable code or remote media. Twenty permitted results per page is a presentation rule, not a corpus-size bound. Larger multi-root packs, general templates and richer art require a named expansion with complete rights/work qualification. A two-entry catalog needs no trend algorithm, advertisement auction, star economy or paid-placement auction.

Free libraries, direct sharing and useful AGPL self-hosting remain available independently of the managed marketplace. Paying for a pack does not buy a hosted world, character-control slot, admission to a premium game, invention-lock override or unlimited inference. A creator fund is DG30's separate program. Do not make any of these products the toll gate to ordinary free reuse.

## 3. Meaning and rights before packaging

### 3.1 Keep attribution, grants and payees distinct

Under [invention governance](../../archive/03-design-proposals/invention-governance-and-ownership.md), NPC creations belong to the world creator account(s), while player creations belong jointly to the inventing player and world creator account(s). Preserve the original actor, historical owner accounts and independent discoveries even when executable mechanics deduplicate. Importing, learning or paying does not create original authorship. Transferring world ownership or changing character controllers does not rewrite that history.

This product-control rule supplies neither an automatic equal revenue split nor permission to sell every dependency. A paid release needs a named publisher authorized by the relevant rights holders, sufficient component grants and an approved payee agreement. The publisher may receive all net earnings when those parties explicitly agree; joint ownership alone does not imply 50/50. Existing sufficient recorded grants are reused rather than repeatedly asking permission for the same release scope.

Eligible account contribution history is retained automatically under DG12's target, independent of continued source-world membership. A complete reusable copy is a different result requiring the allowed dependency bytes and grants. Show pending/failed retention honestly. Do not make a marketplace listing or extra Save ceremony necessary to preserve an eligible original contribution, and do not label a source reference as an independently retained copy.

### 3.2 The selected grant proposal

For independently licensable original content, propose a nonexclusive creator-use grant attached to the acquired exact release. The customer sees this plain-language matrix beside the release; actual legal terms and component eligibility still require adoption before publication or sale.

| Permission                       | Proposed managed creator-use grant                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Play/use                         | Operate the acquired behavior in authorized compatible worlds, including paid worlds; normal participants need no individual pack purchase                      |
| Install                          | The account may propose installation in destinations where it has actual editing authority; acquisition grants no such authority by itself                      |
| Modify                           | Make permitted private adaptations, retaining provenance and inherited notices; substantial changes are separately reviewed definitions                         |
| Export/self-host                 | Obtain the complete eligible acquired release for authorized independent hosting and recovery; hosted-service availability is a separate promise                |
| Collaborate                      | Authorized collaborators may access what the named project requires under the recorded grant, without automatically receiving unrelated personal-library rights |
| General redistribution/resale    | Not granted for otherwise restricted original content; separately open components retain their actual redistribution rights                                     |
| Installed destination continuity | Each authorized admitted destination retains its recorded operating grant for that exact release if the purchasing account later leaves                         |
| Future releases/support          | Not automatically included forever; the listing states included updates and funded support/download service                                                     |

The proposal places no per-cast royalty or license meter in ordinary simulation and no extra pack fee per guest. It grants no unlimited hosting or future technical compatibility. An already admitted destination may retain authorized operational/recovery copies; that does not let its operator resell a restricted package as a standalone product. A new unrelated destination needs an appropriate acquisition or other sufficient grant. Sharing an installed world and distributing its reusable definitions are different actions.

Inherited open material is governed by its actual license. [LICENSING.md](../../LICENSING.md) applies AGPL-3.0-only to current first-party material unless separately designated; no general Apache SDK or proprietary executable-pack exception exists. A JSON/database representation does not establish independence. Selling distribution or support can coexist with open rights, but a price cannot retract those rights or promise exclusive mechanics. Review assets/notices independently. The original AGPL text and Fab's nonbinding rights summary illustrate distinct commercial and redistribution boundaries; neither supplies an automatic Open Legend package license. [PR28-01](#pr28-01--fab-license-summary) [PR28-09](#pr28-09--agpl-original-text)

The paid camp-kit candidate may only use the restricted-original part of this matrix when the actual work and grants support it. If the complete release is covered by an open grant, disclose that honestly, including the customer's redistribution rights. A later paid version cannot silently withdraw an earlier freely released version. The value must be trustworthy preparation, a wanted new capability or explicit service, not a claim that generated ideas cannot be copied.

### 3.3 Contributions and free-use worlds

For a new selected free-use world, propose requiring a nonexclusive contribution grant sufficient for hosting, participant modification, commercial operation and redistribution of the complete eligible pack under its chosen compatible release terms. Show both invention-lock settings and the contribution/sharing terms before joining, then again before the first affected contribution or an effective future change. Declining leaves ordinary permitted play and prior contribution history available; it does not create a right to add new work under rejected terms.

Do not label an existing world free-use until its full inventory and dependency closure actually support that promise. An eligible subset can be released as a named partial pack. Missing consent remains a blocker; changing a world setting cannot appropriate older contributions. Exact standard license wording and contribution agreements remain the rights owner's adoption work. Free price, joint account ownership and a world-creator title are not substitute grants.

## 4. From a working invention to an immutable release

Start from an admitted, useful invention, not a generated listing. The author selects the intended playable benefit and exact root version. The preparation view reuses its known definition, dependency and attribution evidence without requesting a fresh model-generated description on every edit. A retained draft stays separate from an admitted method and a published release.

The complete authorized inventory identifies every required dependency, relevant old version and artifact; the distributable payload contains only what may travel. Public previews reveal only permitted metadata. A private source can appear as Private source world, and a restricted dependency as a scoped missing requirement, without exposing its secret name, author discussion or recipe details. Never export human conversations, private memories, credentials, inventories, active invocations or live-world history by wrapping them in a pack.

Choose whether the offer is a runnable capability, a template needing choices or a partial collection. The first catalog accepts complete runnable selected recipes. The author sees actual dependency blockers, supported host/version scope, playable limitations, ongoing work, price/grant and required credit together. A missing required appearance blocks the promised presentation; optional art can be omitted honestly. Pack acquisition never gives an art provider new reference rights.

Curation checks that the promised action works in the stated profile, that rights and dependency closure are sufficient, and that a simple player can reach the benefit. Review the economic effect too: a food-creation rule can invalidate a survival world's effort/reward loop even when its fields are valid. An AI explanation may identify a conflict and suggest alternatives; it cannot certify compatibility, adjudicate copyright or silently replace the creator's purpose.

The author approves the exact public text, credits, grant, included content, price and supported profile. Paid releases additionally need the reviewed financial mandate and collaborator split. Publication creates one immutable version. Editing mechanics or changing licensed content creates a new version with a readable change summary; a transport retry cannot do so. Correcting a typo or withdrawing private display information follows the appropriate presentation/privacy process without secretly changing installed mechanics.

Separate direct release availability, searchable catalog inclusion, editorial recommendation and permission to accept new sales. Removing an entry from search is not revoking its license. Keep one continuing listing identity and version-specific evidence; relisting the same package must not erase buyers or inconvenient reports. Itch's quality guidance makes these distinct states particularly useful comparators. [PR28-08](#pr28-08--itch-quality-guidance)

Use a finite first intake: two curated entries, one pending submission per publisher, and a small operator-approved review cohort before broader submissions. Publication work, retained versions, dependency preparation and unresolved disputes still need actual aggregate service limits under PACK/PD; a per-publisher limit cannot bound an unlimited number of publishers. Stop new intake before its qualified review capacity is exhausted, preserving existing paid obligations and useful free releases.

## 5. Discover, inspect and acquire

From world creation or the authorized library, show the chosen destination and useful capability first. Each card gives a plain description, original credit, exact selected version, free/paid status, grant summary and compatibility status. Opening a card preserves selection and keyboard focus while results arrive. Lexical search, filters and ordinary inspection use retained evidence without generating new thumbnails or model commentary.

The detail view answers what will change, which requirements are included, which are already available, what is missing and how a character reaches actual use. Keep source-level and destination-level evidence distinct. A recorded source demonstration is labeled; an isolated destination rehearsal cannot spend live resources, create shared history or teach anyone its preview findings. No testimonial or AI confidence score replaces the actual mechanical check.

Show the complete acquisition price, including any required separately licensed dependency, before checkout. The first two entries should include their full eligible closure, avoiding chains of surprise purchases. Already acquired exact content says Available in your library; already installed content says Already available here. Neither a changed name nor another world visit creates a fresh purchase requirement under the same grant. Do not auto-purchase missing content.

For a new purchase, state the precise release, included bytes/grants, dependency closure, destination-readiness scope, price/tax, optional adaptation or art cost, continued download/support terms and remedy. Price must not imply the host, authoring allowance or ongoing AI is included. A compatible pack can still require funding to operate its advertised residents or other inference-dependent behavior; known native crafting does not become a per-use royalty.

Acquisition follows DG27's distinct processing, paid-but-unfulfilled, ready and failed states. A tab closed after payment returns to the same receipt. Payment success alone does not prove a retained reusable copy exists. Until the exact eligible content and grant are durably available, show the remaining fulfillment task and recovery/refund path. Repeated events cannot grant, charge or publish twice. Refunds and later corrections retain the original purchase and terms.

The first paid grant covers the acquired release and compatible corrective updates to that release, not every future product by the author. An independently useful new edition may be sold separately, with a clear comparison. A correction required to deliver the original advertised promise cannot be converted into a compulsory second purchase. The buyer can remain on a valid old version where its current host supports it.

## 6. Review the destination and reach play

Select the exact destination before claiming compatibility. Review body/action families, materials, semantic interfaces, permissions, clock-dependent work, actual dependency versions, presentation and aggregate operating demand. Matching names or units is insufficient: decorative rope cannot supply the certified binding role, and a charge cost cannot silently become hunger. Factorio's version/dependency tools are useful examples, while Open Legend retains explicitly accepted versions as the persistent-world default. [PR28-02](#pr28-02--factorio-modding)

Present five useful outcomes: usable unchanged; needs a supported binding; needs an explicit adaptation; forbidden by this world's policy; unsupported by this host. Each has a specific next action. Preserve the original on refusal. Missing dependencies, unknown origin or an over-limit closure must not lead to partial execution or a weaker copied invention advertised as equivalent.

The creator reviews the exact addition and any consequential difference. Current authority, invention locks, dependencies and funding are checked again before admission. A permission valid at purchase is not frozen forever. If the applicable origin lock closes and no supported creator/scoped authority permits this import independently, retain the permitted package/proposal and block admission. Changing only the other group's lock does not invalidate an otherwise permitted request. The accepted full-invention grant must not be assumed implemented; use actual supported authority. Unlocking does not automatically retry the request. Ordinary play can continue while optional preparation waits under the existing world-change policy.

Installation adds behavior. It neither creates an item nor teaches every resident, gives a player skill, supplies resources or appropriates a body. A new-world creator can separately provide reviewed initial knowledge and a finite endowment. In an existing world, learning, gathering, crafting and access follow native rules. The pack browser does not secretly run a paid NPC lesson.

For the camp kit, a player learns the admitted method, obtains real prepared fiber, manufactures cord where needed, crafts the container, packs selected supplies and takes the kit to a useful place. Another character may access it only through the actual custody/access rules. Making it larger costs more material/work under the selected family; it does not increase the actor's total carrying strength. The point is convenient organization and sharing during an expedition, not grinding container recipes to earn creator revenue.

A second world can reject the same kit because its material or body semantics differ. The owner may approve a faithful supported binding, commission a clearly changed adaptation under current authoring limits, or keep the original for another world. No AI repair is automatic, and paying cannot grant a missing host primitive. The creator's copied work and the source world remain intact.

## 7. Updates, access endings and remedies

### 7.1 Exact versions and living worlds

A new version is a choice with a behavior/dependency/cost comparison. Never update installed worlds or descendants merely because a listing says Latest. Preserve construction semantics and required old definitions for existing objects and work. A shared-law change uses INV-5's qualified live transition; unsupported migration is a blocker, not a background package refresh. The root development policy still rejects incompatible development formats and does not authorize legacy support.

Removing a library favorite, unlisting a release, ending new sales, deleting an optional retained copy and retiring a live definition are different operations. Nonempty containers, active crafting and dependent recipes can prevent live removal until the appropriate owner has a coherent disposition. A refund receipt cannot empty everybody's bags, erase consumed materials or rewind the world.

Ordinary author departure, buyer departure, subscription cancellation or commercial delisting does not withdraw previously valid grants. The buyer's eligible account copy and the destination's recorded operating rights persist under those terms; optional support, new-release acquisition and hosted service can end separately. A reference-only entry still depends on its source, so the initial paid offer must fulfill a real retained eligible copy rather than sell an undisclosed rental dependency. Minecraft's catalog-access rules illustrate why retained files, continued use and new acquisition must be explained independently. [PR28-03](#pr28-03--minecraft-marketplace-pass)

### 7.2 Finite service with durable permission

Propose downloadable fulfillment immediately and managed re-download of the acquired release for at least 12 real months after purchase. Beyond that, retain availability while the applicable funded account-library service supports it, with the actual terms visible. Existing rights and locally retained copies do not expire merely because hosted re-download ends. The operator must maintain the sold compatible delivery/recovery route, not promise every future runtime understands the file.

At catalog/service retirement, provide at least 30 real days of authorized retrieval, suspend that window during operator-caused retrieval failure, and honor any longer unfulfilled sold download term by continuing static delivery or an applicable remedy. No live world needs to keep simulating to serve an eligible pack file. Keep private dependencies and unrelated world records excluded. Service closure cannot retroactively revoke open licenses or compel independent operators to erase their lawful copies.

An exceptional substantiated privacy, rights or security issue is different from ordinary unpublication. Suspend affected new distribution/admission while reviewing it, identify the scoped effect, notify entitled affected parties without revealing private reports, and provide a contest/review route. Actual rights that never existed cannot be promised forever. If an active component is unsafe, the owning runtime applies coherent quarantine/hold behavior; it does not silently substitute physics or wipe a whole unrelated world. Current restrictions constrain restore and export.

### 7.3 Buyer remedies

Before payment, demonstrate the supported destination and offer a reviewable preview. The proposed pack remedy covers nonfulfillment, duplicate charges, material misdescription and failure of advertised compatibility in the disclosed supported profile. Recover the promised release or refund the affected price; do not force a paid adaptation to repair that promise. A buyer choosing a clearly unsupported destination or later changing its rules does not automatically create a defect in the originally fulfilled pack. Applicable consumer requirements remain separately qualified before sale.

There is no additional recurring-service trial hidden inside a pack purchase. State the selected pack cancellation/remedy terms before acquisition; DG27's first hosting/platform 14-day promise remains scoped to its named service. An ordinary remedial refund ends the refunded purchase's new commercial service entitlement where applicable, while existing valid grants and third-party history follow their recorded terms. Open rights cannot be revoked as a collection tool. Retained delivered value and unavoidable refund fees must be funded costs of the offer.

Disputed or uncertain money is not collected twice. Preserve requested, approved, processing, failed and completed refund states. A chargeback can suspend dependent new spending/distribution while reviewed, without accusing the customer publicly or destroying their world. Refund adjustments, licenses, attribution and real accounting survive fictional restore.

## 8. One fixed subscriber allocation

### 8.1 Choice instead of paying for longer sessions

Propose a fixed published allocation A from each full paid platform membership month, prorated for a partial term using DG27's real UTC calendar and actual paid benefit. A is selected before that membership launches; the historical $5 illustration is not its price. No allocation is minted by a free account, an unpaid subscription, more bodies, extra sessions, NPC activity or faster simulated time.

Here, a qualifying attended visit means ordinary authorized human participation in that world through its supported account/control path, including controlling that world's own separately bound character. It does not require DG29 same-person physical travel, joining world histories or moving goods. One account can have qualifying experiences in several independent worlds without fusing their people.

A subscriber can select up to three eligible worlds, sharing their amount equally. Prior choices persist automatically when they qualify in the new period; no monthly checklist is required. If none of the remembered worlds qualifies and exactly one world is eligible, use that world as the disclosed default, with the person able to change or disable it. If several are eligible and no valid choice remains, retain the amount for the review window rather than guessing favorites. Selection lives quietly in Billing; the game does not interrupt a conversation to solicit votes.

Qualifying participation is a real voluntary attended visit through the supported human account/control path, combined with the subscriber's current deliberate or retained/default allocation instruction. The platform records only the needed attendance/authority fact. No minimum duration, number of casts, successful inventions or spoken words is required: a short useful visit and quiet listening can matter. A background connection, automatic reconnect or NPC running while the human is absent cannot establish a new visit or add weight. Attendance alone creates no extra payment beyond A.

This is an auditable product rule, not proof that every claimed human is honest. Begin with curated recipient worlds in the same managed authority environment and qualified payment identities. Exclude a payer's own controlled payee/world interests and fabricated/bot activity; review suspicious collusion privately. Joint ownership of an ordinary contributed invention does not make its player a world operator or controlled payee. Do not invent universal cross-operator identity trust, extract private chat to score engagement or reward repeated button presses. Unsupported participation evidence remains ineligible for automatic allocation, with a scoped correction route.

### 8.2 Formula, overlap and the unallocated remainder

For subscriber s in real month m, let A(s,m) be the funded allocation after the selected proration and subscription adjustments, and Q(s,m) the final eligible selected set, containing at most three worlds. Each selected world receives A(s,m) / |Q(s,m)|. The sum never exceeds A(s,m). Divide in monetary minor units; distribute remainder units by a stable published rotation across selected worlds, preserving the period receipt so refresh/restore cannot change the result.

If A is illustratively $6 and three worlds qualify, each receives $2. Half a paid month gives an illustrative $3 allocation and $1 each. These are arithmetic examples. The amount going to creators is not reduced by another hidden marketplace percentage. Membership payment and standard payout costs must fit the platform's retained membership revenue.

Premium worlds can qualify under the same participation and recipient rules even when this subscriber separately buys their admission. Explain the two purposes before purchase and in Billing: the premium receipt buys entry; the platform allocation directs already funded creator support. There is no additional access charge and no second A. Remembered/default choices work uniformly. A standalone premium-only customer still enters what they bought without a platform subscription and creates no platform pool. A refund of one receipt adjusts that receipt's benefit/proceeds; it does not automatically refund or duplicate the other.

The period closes at the UTC month boundary with seven real days for the subscriber to correct that period's choices using its already established eligible visits. No new visit after the boundary retroactively qualifies. Valid remembered/default choices require no action. After the review window, refund any unallocated earmarked amount to the original payment method, using the existing purchase adjustment. It becomes neither platform profit nor DG30 funding and does not silently roll into another creator pool.

A later full subscription refund returns only the remaining refundable purchase amount after that prior partial return. Reversed subscription revenue reduces its original A and associated held/earned amounts through traceable adjustments; it never creates a second refund or negative new-month pool. If the payment method cannot complete the return, show a pending remedy and obtain a supported alternative rather than claiming the money arrived. Cancellation preserves the valid paid-through period; it does not erase its legitimate participation.

Ordinary world closure does not erase valid participation already earned under its period agreement. A payout-identity problem can hold the recipient's amount pending remedy. Fraudulent or otherwise invalid participation is excluded with a reviewable reason. A subscriber cannot redirect an already paid amount by restoring a world or editing next month's favorites.

## 9. Pack sales, contributor shares and payouts

### 9.1 Every earning has an identified source

Keep pack-sale proceeds, standalone premium admission, fixed subscriber allocation, hosting expense and DG30 grants separate. A pack author need not run a world to earn from a qualified sale. A world may explicitly share part of its own allocation with pack contributors through its financial agreement, but that is a split of existing money. Dependencies, downloads and ordinary use do not create a recursive royalty graph or a second subscriber pool.

For the first managed standalone-premium receipt, propose no additional marketplace commission. The named world publisher receives its tax/refund-adjusted access proceeds less the separately disclosed payment-service charges; quoted hosting and AI costs remain its distinct obligations. The 15% pack commission does not apply by analogy. Use this same qualified payout/financial-agreement owner for receipts processed here. An independent self-hoster selling access through another service is outside this payment program and needs no platform membership, marketplace listing or Open Legend commission. If quoted hosting/payment service cannot cover the managed offering, revise the future offer before sale rather than invent a deduction afterward.

Propose a marketplace fee of 15% of a pack's tax-excluded price after applicable refunds. Disclose actual payment-processing charges separately before the publisher agrees; those charges reduce the remaining creator proceeds. The platform fee, currency, tax treatment, processing basis and expected range must be visible, not merely a headline 85% share. Free releases have no sale fee. Actual offer prices and viable minimum paid purchase remain adoption/measurement gates.

Before enabling sales, obtain one financial mandate with at most five direct payees and percentages totaling 100% of the creator proceeds. Use already sufficient grants for other credited contributors; a royalty-free dependency can require credit without a new payment share. Every affected payee approves the amount basis, split, refunds and payout conditions. An unresolved required financial agreement blocks new paid sales, not unrelated free publication or prior earned rights. Steam's group-split requirement supplies a useful precedent for resolving assent before paying. [PR28-05](#pr28-05--steam-workshop-revenue-terms)

Five limits the first service's direct payouts, not credited contributors or rights holders. A larger group may designate an authorized publisher or legal entity to receive and distribute its agreed amount under a documented mandate; that does not transfer authorship or waive required grants. Without that supported mandate, paid enrollment explains the five-payee limit and remains blocked until the group chooses a valid route or expanded payout service is qualified. Eligible free publication remains available.

Record the agreement applying to each sale. A later collaborator departure, copied mechanic or world ownership transfer cannot divert earlier earnings. A participating world's agreement is fixed for its earning month; a successor takes effect prospectively at a disclosed next boundary unless the affected parties approve a precise lawful adjustment. Public contribution credit remains separate from private bank/tax identity. Aggregate small fractional creator shares before payment and carry residual entitlement rather than repeatedly rounding it away. Actual distributions use the stable residual rule and cannot mint cents.

### 9.2 Earned, held, payable and paid

Statements distinguish gross receipts, taxes, actual fees, refunds/reversals, attributed creator proceeds, ordinary hold, dispute hold, payable balance, transfer processing and confirmed paid amount. Estimates remain labeled. An available payout is not proof that a bank cannot later reverse the originating transaction. The operator preserves funds for creator obligations separately from hosting and prepaid AI.

For the initial program, earning periods are real UTC calendar months: pack and premium-access receipts use their verified collection month, while subscriber allocations use their covered membership month. Propose a 30-real-day ordinary hold after the later of verified payment settlement or that earning-period close, followed by a monthly payout run. Publish the run date before enrollment and show each balance's next scheduled run. Eligible undisputed balances of at least $25 are paid through the selected supported method. These values need actual fee/risk qualification; 30 days is not a guarantee of finality. A material dispute can hold only the affected amount with an explanation, review status and next step. Unrelated mature earnings remain payable where safe and permitted.

Below-threshold balances stay visible. At account/program exit, pay them in the first scheduled run after applicable holds resolve; pay them at least annually even if the creator stays below $25 and continues participating. The platform absorbs the standard supported transfer fee; optional extra conversion or alternative-service charges require their own disclosure and consent. Before onboarding, qualify a route for small final payments. If no lawful supported route is available, keep a clearly reported obligation and resolution path under applicable handling requirements rather than quietly treating it as platform income.

For example, a creator earning $1 a month does not have to wait more than two years or create unnecessary sales to pass $25. The annual or exit settlement gives their small contribution a complete lifecycle. The cost of that settlement belongs in the platform's economics. Itch's documented thresholds and aging make the exit problem concrete, without selecting its fee or dormant-balance policy. [PR28-04](#pr28-04--itch-payments)

If a payout loses acknowledgment, reconcile the same transfer before sending money again. A verified failed transfer preserves the amount and offers corrected details or a supported alternative. Temporary payee ineligibility does not silently award the money to collaborators; suspend new dependent sales if continuing them would create obligations the operator cannot fulfill. Account closure retains access to necessary statements and recovery under disclosed retention terms.

### 9.3 Refunds, disputes and later receipts

A pack refund proportionately reverses the platform fee and creator proceeds recorded for the original sale, preserving its collaborator shares. The operator funds the refunded portion of ordinary nonrecoverable processing cost from its disclosed reserve. A full refund of the illustrative $10 sale below reverses $7.90 of creator proceeds and $1.50 of platform fee; the reserve supplies $0.60. A half refund reverses $3.95 and $0.75 respectively, with $0.30 from the reserve. It does not recalculate zero-price proceeds and charge the creator another processing fee. After payout, a legitimate reversal appears against that payee's future creator earnings under the accepted agreement. Do not debit unrelated game subscriptions, world AI or personal bank accounts automatically.

Fraud-related additional liability requires the actual disclosed agreement and reviewed evidence; no open-ended penalty follows from an opaque score. Give affected creators a private reason category, period/amount, required next action and a review route. Propose acknowledgment within seven real days and an initial decision or specific continued-review explanation within 30; capacity to honor that service must exist before enrollment. Do not promise a definitive decision while payment or rights evidence remains unresolved.

Late receipts, refunds and restored worlds retain the original sale/period and financial agreement. Correct the statement rather than replacing history with the most recently arrived notification. If one collaborator cannot be paid, isolate their held share and honor the others' eligible shares under the fully approved agreement; never require already paid colleagues to silently adopt a new split.

## 10. Privacy, provenance and unsupported operators

The payer can inspect their own purchases and allocation choices. A creator receives aggregate earning statements and necessary transaction/support references, not a list of private players, their other worlds, messages or character knowledge. Do not display precise attendance times or per-subscriber allocation receipts to world creators. In tiny cohorts, totals can still permit inference; do not promise mathematical anonymity or publish optional small-cohort breakdowns. Public acknowledgments require separate consent.

Historical creator attribution is not a legal-identity directory. Public credit uses the selected permitted name; private source titles and account recovery details stay scoped. Corrections preserve the distinction between an original contribution, an adaptation, imported content and independently discovered equivalent mechanics. Models do not decide ownership by choosing the most convincing story. The Copyright Office's report reinforces the difference between human expression, generated material and product attribution. [PR28-10](#pr28-10--copyright-office-ai-report)

The first managed catalog accepts authenticated provenance under one operator and explicit rights records. An independently hosted file can be retained for permitted inspection without automatically trusting its claimed account author. Broader synchronization needs a supported authenticated provenance/rights path, conflict handling and a user-visible trust status. This does not require a self-hoster to purchase a marketplace service or surrender private history. Publication under sufficient known grants can remain independent of revenue enrollment.

### A complete later cross-service round trip

Mira exports her acquired eligible kit with its exact definitions, licensed dependency closure, notices and readable rights/compatibility record. The export identifies any excluded private content before she relies on it. She runs it on a compatible independently hosted world through that operator's admission rules. This grants no Open Legend hosting, automatic account enrollment or copy of the official world; the self-hosted game can remain useful without this marketplace.

If she wants that host's contribution to appear in her managed account history, association is optional and explicit. She authenticates her own account and confirms the claim being associated. The service distinguishes a verified account association, an operator-supplied authorship claim and a source admission actually supported by trusted evidence. Logging in proves neither authorship of every uploaded file nor another collaborator's consent. Unverified claims stay labeled and cannot replace trusted original credit or earn automatic marketplace payouts.

On returning, she selects a managed destination and imports her unchanged licensed release or a separately identified adaptation. A known exact release can reuse its existing trusted provenance; changed external material needs the applicable supported verification and grant review. Both need current native compatibility and destination authority. Missing evidence can block public commercial eligibility while preserving permitted inspection and whatever private import the adopted external-content policy actually allows. The initial local catalog does not claim this later policy is delivered.

If the external host disappears, her complete eligible retained copy remains usable under its terms; a reference-only private dependency reports source unavailable. If another contributor disputes the adaptation, preserve both claims and trusted ancestry, hold only the affected publication/payout decisions, and seek the actual evidence/grants through the rights review. Do not erase her unrelated library or disclose the private world's occupants. A resolved correction changes the relevant attribution/financial adjustment with history, not the original world's events. This expansion needs qualified provenance and recovery, not a universal distributed ownership registry.

## 11. Economics and growth

For an original pack sale, let S be tax-excluded collected price, f the proposed platform rate and p its actual processing cost. Initial creator proceeds are S × (1 − f) − p; the approved collaborator split divides that amount once. Refunds reverse the original recorded proceeds and fee proportionately under section 9.3, with the refunded nonrecoverable processing portion borne by the platform reserve. Other justified adjustments remain explicit separate entries. Platform contribution is its retained fee minus curation, serving, support, standard transfers, absorbed refund cost and other obligations. A 15% headline alone establishes no viable margin.

For illustration only, a $10 sale at 15% with $0.60 payment cost produces $7.90 before other direct adjustments, divided $5.53/$2.37 under an explicitly approved 70/30 creator split. The platform receives $1.50 before its own costs. Ten minutes of review valued at an assumed $30/hour costs $5; one sale cannot pay for that review and delivery. Repeated useful sales can amortize a reviewed release, but generated low-value variants can multiply review cost faster than demand. Reject an uneconomical paid SKU or retain it as free sharing; do not silently add extraction fees later.

For membership, if n valid subscribers each fund A, the total allocation is at most n × A after proration/adjustments. Twenty illustrative subscribers with A = $4 supply at most $80, whether their worlds run for an hour or contain a thousand NPCs. Sharing 20% of a recipient world's proceeds with an agreed pack author divides that world's amount; it does not add another $16 to the total pool. Host costs remain host-funded under DG27 and cannot be inferred from creator income.

Recurring runtime cost matters as much as authoring price. A cheap pack that schedules required inference for every creature can be unaffordable after installation. Its listing and destination review must distinguish measured workload, estimate, unsupported growth and optional work. Bound aggregate effects and retained history before activation; free licensing does not make computation free. Native supported actions should reuse admitted meaning without per-tick model calls or library synchronization.

Exact browsing/reopening/eligible copying should need no new generative work. Reuse shared permitted definitions and validation evidence when the exact scope still applies. Query and dependency work, retained release bytes, downloads, concurrent preparations, pending purchases, payout holds and support cases grow independently. Qualification needs finite admission/preparation envelopes and continued reconciliation capacity; a two-entry catalog or twenty-row page does not bound all of them. Close optional new sales or submissions before existing obligations become unaffordable.

Fortnite's bounded revenue envelope and Modrinth's reported change in its operating share are useful economic lessons, not rates to copy. Absolute creator income, cash arrival, complete service cost and a sustainable platform all matter. Changes apply prospectively to new agreed offers; already earned money retains its terms. ## 12. Delivery, scenarios and game-first review

First complete DG12's free round trip and actual use. Add a tiny curated catalog only when it makes that result easier to find. Next qualify complete rights/acquisition/recovery for one paid pack and actual creator proceeds. Enable the fixed membership allocation only after its selected A, supported audience/worlds, payment program and existing DG27 offer are adopted. Broader discovery, richer packs, remote synchronization and DG30 remain independent later consumers.

No sale, payout, private-player study or paid provider experiment is authorized by this specification. Before execution, the operator must adopt the real prices, budget, audience/territory, rights, release-support scope, storage/retention, payment methods, risk reserve, review capacity and evaluation date under PD10/CO. The product walkthrough is preparation, not proof that these services run.

Evaluate whole journeys rather than isolated upload success:

- An author creates and uses a real sling, retains it, publishes an eligible exact release, and another creator imports and reaches useful play without a new generation or private-history leak.
- The camp-kit buyer sees its complete price/closure, obtains a real reusable copy, learns/crafts with real resources and uses storage for an enjoyable outing; missing binding or destination authority stays an actionable blocker.
- A disconnected purchase recovers once; a permission changed before admission prevents installation; refunding a fulfilled misdescribed release preserves unrelated objects and the applicable valid grants.
- A creator leaves, changes a public name, transfers a world or unlists a pack; old credit, sale shares, acquired rights and installed-version semantics remain correctly scoped.
- A subscriber quietly listens or plays briefly, keeps remembered choices, pays a separate premium membership and cancels near renewal; the exact qualified set and one bounded allocation remain understandable without a monthly game interruption.
- A no-choice allocation is returned, then the subscription receives a larger refund; total returned never exceeds the refundable receipt and no creator amount is counted twice.
- Several collaborators approve a split, one later loses payout access, a chargeback arrives after payment and a below-threshold creator exits; held/payable/adjusted amounts stay traceable without diverting another person's share.
- A required security restriction or incompatible runtime stops affected use truthfully; restore cannot defeat it, erase money or silently change the pack's behavior.

Measure useful import completion, time/effort saved, repeated actual use, unintended economic consequences, failed compatibility, revisions/support burden, all real costs and creator understanding. Catalog growth and longer sessions are not goals. Roblox's program documentation illustrates why payout transparency and manipulated participation need explicit treatment, but its action/time incentives are not adopted here. [PR28-07](#pr28-07--roblox-creator-rewards)

The main design risk is spending more effort proving ownership of tiny variants than helping anyone play. Keep most trustworthy free reuse straightforward. A commercial grant can be complete without making every player inspect a dependency graph. If the two selected entries are already easy to recreate and rarely reused, improve the underlying game and library before building a larger shop. If remembered allocation choices still feel like administrative work, simplify selection without turning simulation time into money. If refund/curation liabilities consume the margin, change the future offer or stop it; do not weaken sold rights retroactively.

Review removed an initial draft policy excluding separately subscribed premium worlds for the whole month. A short premium term would have blocked wanted support and complicated refunds even though the member had already funded A. Inclusion preserves that deliberate choice while keeping access receipts, allocations and hosting costs distinct. This revises a draft recommendation, not an adopted project rule.

## 13. Primary research

The following primary sources were checked October 8, 2026. Observations describe their published rules; inferences are this proposal's judgments. No purchase, pack installation, payout or measured Open Legend experiment was performed. Living documentation may change. Research does not select a provider or resolve the rights of an unknown artifact.

### PR28-01 — Fab license summary

[Fab EULA page](https://www.fab.com/eula). **Observed:** the summary distinguishes commercial use, modification, project collaborators, reference-only access and standalone redistribution. **Inference:** show the actual rights beside a release rather than treating Bought as universal permission. **Access:** the page's expressly nonbinding summary was retrieved; full contractual text was not exposed. It does not establish our grant, perpetual hosting or executable-license compatibility.

### PR28-02 — Factorio modding

[Official Modding wiki](https://wiki.factorio.com/Modding). **Observed:** previous versions, required/optional dependencies, incompatibilities and exact multiplayer synchronization are distinct; save synchronization has a latest-version default and an exact option. **Inference:** explain closure and preserve deliberately accepted versions in persistent worlds. **Access:** relevant official community-maintained sections read; the displayed edit date was September 18, 2025. No Open Legend compatibility follows.

### PR28-03 — Minecraft Marketplace Pass

[Marketplace Pass FAQ](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/marketplace/marketplace-pass). **Observed:** retained local files do not necessarily retain catalog-use permission, while some claimed cosmetics persist; the FAQ warns of overlap with Realms Plus. **Inference:** distinguish stored, licensed, usable and newly obtainable content and disclose overlapping offers. **Access:** official FAQ read. Its rental policy and retention period are comparators, not the selected Open Legend promise.

### PR28-04 — Itch payments

[Accepting Payments and Getting Paid](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments). **Observed:** platform share, processor costs, transaction aging, payout eligibility and later refunds affect net earnings; minimum payout and closure rules exist. **Inference:** show full earning states and a below-threshold exit, funding settlement costs. **Access:** relevant living official documentation read; no payout requested or fee adopted.

### PR28-05 — Steam Workshop revenue terms

[Supplemental Workshop Terms](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/workshoplegalagreement/?appid=0). **Observed:** paid contributions have specified deductions and initial payout delay; all identified group contributors approve the stated split before payment. **Inference:** obtain the financial mandate before selling and preserve its applicable historical split. **Access:** full public supplemental text read, scoped to fee-bearing Workshop contributions. It does not select Open Legend shares or legal terms.

### PR28-06 — Fortnite engagement payout

[Engagement Payout documentation](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/fortnite/engagement-payout-in-fortnite-creative). **Observed:** a bounded eligible-revenue share funds payouts under stated metrics while other revenue funds operation; metrics and dated contributions can change. **Inference:** preserve a funded envelope and prospective term history without adopting session-growth incentives. **Access:** official documentation read; no claim of independent profitability evidence or adopted percentages.

### PR28-07 — Roblox Creator Rewards

[Creator Rewards documentation](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-rewards). **Observed:** the program distinguishes estimated/held earnings, permits paid-access experiences and excludes bots, alternate-account manipulation and fraudulent activity. Its account of the previous program cites poor transparency. **Inference:** select explicit overlap, human-use and contestable accounting rules without content surveillance. **Access:** relevant current official sections read; the retrospective is Roblox's explanation, not independent evaluation.

### PR28-08 — Itch quality guidance

[Content creator quality guidelines](https://itch.io/docs/creators/quality-guidelines). **Observed:** search indexing, direct availability and paid buyer access are distinct; recreated listings can lose ownership/review continuity. **Inference:** retain release/listing identity and buyer recovery while curating truthful supported behavior. **Access:** relevant official guidance read. Search inclusion does not certify rights or compatibility in Open Legend.

### PR28-09 — AGPL original text

[GNU canonical text](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.txt); [OSI original-text mirror](https://opensource.org/license/agpl-3-0). **Observed:** the license permits paid copies, preserves compliant grants and specifies corresponding-source obligations, including its network condition. **Inference:** paid distribution cannot strip inherited rights; neither a file format nor a package label resolves work boundaries. **Access:** GNU retrieval timed out; the full original license was read at OSI. Repository/component notices remain the project authority.

### PR28-10 — Copyright Office AI report

[Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 2](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-2-Copyrightability-Report.pdf). **Observed:** the January 2025 report distinguishes human expression and selection/modification from purely generated material and considers authorship case by case. **Inference:** record contribution/control honestly without promising exclusive copyright over every invention. **Access:** executive summary and introduction read; U.S. institutional analysis, not worldwide advice or a clearance for this catalog.

### PR28-11 — Modrinth sustainability report

[Quintupling Creator Revenue and Becoming Sustainable](https://modrinth.com/news/article/becoming-sustainable/). **Observed:** the September 13, 2024 report describes changing the creator/platform split and payout timing with a new advertising arrangement. **Inference:** model absolute income, funding arrival and complete operating cost, preserving prior earned terms. **Access:** full historical official report read; revenue/sustainability statements are the operator's report, and its proposed transparency page was not then delivered. No advertising model is adopted here.
