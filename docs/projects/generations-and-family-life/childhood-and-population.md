# Choosing and raising a family

| Status | Current progress | Last updated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In progress | The product proposal is complete under Mike's adult-audience and non-explicit-presentation decision; adoption, technical design, implementation and gameplay qualification remain open. | 2026-10-14 |

This is the reproductive and dependent-family chapter of [Generations and family life](../generations-and-family-life-feature-spec.md), not a separate household engine. The main specification owns adult relationships, known history, ordinary help and shared continuity. This chapter owns the additional behavior from choosing intimacy through a child's independent adulthood. [The world profile](../../worlds/base/generations-and-family-life.md#the-growing-family-profile) owns proposed content and tuning; [GEN delivery](../../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md) owns remaining work; [GEN limits](../../limits/generations-and-family-life.md) owns discretionary restrictions. Nothing here installs these mechanics.

## Owner decision — October 14, 2026

Mike selected an **adult player audience with non-explicit presentation**. Sex remains an optional activity between eligible adult characters. The game need not depict anatomical sex; an opaque heart, an obscured scene or a fade can represent the encounter. This is a content direction, not a formal rating determination, an age-verification vendor choice, or a requirement to make every relationship cheerful. Pregnancy, children, separation, care, grief and conflicting ambitions remain possible subject matter. Child characters are not sexual participants.

The audience and presentation direction is owner-approved. The detailed mechanics below are recommendations for later adoption and implementation. Audience approval does not itself launch a public service, approve a new biological clock, authorize billing or implement population growth. The separately resolved platform choice is browser-only for now; these journeys use the existing browser rather than depend on an installed client or offline mode.

## 1. The reason to play

One adult player and a particular resident have lives worth continuing. They can enjoy intimacy without wanting children, or decide that raising a child together is something they want. They prepare a modest place, continue ordinary activities during pregnancy, welcome a new person and gradually discover what that person enjoys. Later, the child wants to see the place a parent talks about, asks how a real tool works, invents a game of their own, or rejects the profession somebody expected them to inherit. The payoff is an evolving relationship and new shared activities, not another production unit.

Parenting is optional. Child-free partnerships, casual consensual adult intimacy, friendship, chosen family and solitude remain complete ways to play. Adoption or accepting a care role can create a family without a sexual relationship. A returning player should not find themselves assigned a spouse, pregnancy or child merely because somebody else's generated story needed a family.

The first complete intimate encounter is independent of reproductive simulation. The first complete child interaction can use an explicitly authored existing family rather than wait for a birth. The growing-family profile then connects a deliberate pregnancy, birth, competent care, childhood and adulthood. These are independently useful stages, not permission to claim a static family tree already demonstrates reproduction.

The first growing-family qualification uses one human adult, one independently willing adult resident and one child. These people have actual capabilities, accessible supplies and a reachable home. Existing Mara and Ivo remain the separate adult-relative example; this proposal does not turn that parent/child relationship into an intimate pair. The authored family can instead use the human and an unrelated adult partner, or an existing family the human visits. The child's provisional name is chosen after birth or supplied explicitly in a starting scenario; reopening a preview does not reroll their identity.

## 2. Intimacy, reproductive intent and care are different choices

### An invitation, not an entitlement

**Be intimate** invites a named eligible adult into one presently chosen encounter. It establishes neither marriage nor exclusivity, reproduction, inventory access, spending authority or standing consent. Both participants choose independently. A human's deliberate input supplies their choice; an NPC uses its own permitted circumstances and preferences. No relationship score guarantees acceptance. A declined invitation can be disappointing without forcing a humiliation animation, a numerical punishment or an obligatory apology.

The first family supports a pair of living, awake adults whose installed bodies support the activity and whose current control and capacity permit agreement. Being married, related to an owner, indebted, summoned or the beneficiary of revival grants no sexual authority. The profile does not use compulsion to obtain assent. Incompatible or uncertain eligibility returns a minimal permitted explanation; it does not expose private orientation, reproductive capability or a hidden family history as a diagnostic report.

The pair choose an actual permitted place with enough usable space and reasonable privacy. The first implementation can support one simple family of private resting places; it need not require an elaborate bed, a new building profession or a purchasable romance object. Reaching that place is real movement. Neither participant is teleported or forced to walk through a threat. An invitation is distinct from arrival, beginning the activity and its actual result.

The proposed short encounter occupies each participant's ordinary physical work while active. Either can stop immediately. Losing eligibility, leaving the place, beginning incompatible work, human control loss or an actual interruption stops it. A world pause preserves no continuing sexual activity: the encounter ends, and Resume does not restart it automatically. An interrupted encounter can remain a remembered event; it grants no completion reward or reproductive result. Reading a panel or changing camera view alone does not alter the fictional outcome.

NPCs may initiate eligible invitations where the feature is enabled, but a human must explicitly accept. Two NPCs may independently agree without a human selecting their feelings. No autonomous sex or pregnancy is performed for an absent human. Repeated unchanged requests after a refusal are suppressed for the current interaction; normal contact admission and shared spending limits remain controlling. A provider failure does not count as a refusal, acceptance or successful encounter, and does not trigger paid rerolls.

### Clear non-explicit presentation

The default is a brief covered scene with an opaque heart; a **Fade only** preference omits it. Blur is acceptable only when the underlying portrayal is already non-explicit. There is no explicit anatomical animation, audio or text behind a removable censor. Camera rotation, zoom, missing assets, disabled effects, captions, accessibility output, saved previews and permitted exports retain the same boundary. A failed visual effect uses the fade, never exposed imagery.

An ordinary description such as “They spend some private time together” is sufficient. The game may acknowledge that the activity is sex in an explanatory control without describing its anatomy or mechanics. It does not need erotic dialogue, realistic sounds, camera control or an extended cinematic to establish the activity. Hiding the presentation is separate from **Stop**; the latter ends actual participation, not merely the image.

The heart appears only to an observer entitled to see the relevant scene. It is not a marker identifying people through walls. No bystander receives their private conversation, motives, orientation or future reproductive outcome. If privacy is lost, the participants may stop or seek another place through their own choices. The presentation cuts away rather than depicting an encounter involving child onlookers; children are never enrolled as participants or given adult sexual commentary. Ordinary child-family play and its records stay separate.

Adult eligibility comes from the body's actual supported development and consistent presentation, not a display name or a model's assertion. The first sexual family excludes minors. An immature body, uncertain age, identity swap, old acceptance or cosmetic reskin cannot satisfy that requirement. Coming of age supplies no sex reward, automatic romantic relationship or invitation from a former caregiver.

### A separate decision about a child

**Try for a child** is a distinct proposal. Before assent, it identifies the people contributing to the proposed parentage, the person who would carry the pregnancy, the selected world's developmental pace, and the immediate care and population requirements. Agreement to ordinary intimacy is not agreement to this proposal. The first profile has no incidental pregnancy from **Be intimate**; that is a deliberate game rule, not a biological claim.

The first supported reproductive family uses compatible adult bodies under one installed species/body rule. It does not infer reproductive roles from gender, orientation, a character's name or the appearance of clothing. Intimacy can be available when this reproductive family is not. Adoption and non-biological care remain separate routes. Unsupported donor arrangements, artificial gestation or cross-species offspring remain explicit extension cases rather than being invented by narrative.

A valid completed planned-conception activity creates one pregnancy, with no fertility dice or repeated-attempt grind in this first profile. The two parties' current assent and the gestating person's actual body must still qualify at commitment. Before that point either can stop; no pregnancy results from stale agreement or an interrupted scene. This is predictable authored behavior after successful admission, not a promise that any real person or arbitrary game body is fertile. Future uncertainty would need a wanted play purpose and an honestly disclosed rule.

The proposal creates no parent-controlled slave, automatic follower or guaranteed future worker. A contributor to biological parentage is not automatically a caregiver. Conversely, an accepted caregiver need not be a biological parent. The small review asks what actually matters now; it must not require a family constitution or a registry of hypothetical future relationships.

## 3. Admit a new dependent before promising one

An accepted pregnancy commits the world to support a possible new embodied person. Admission must account for the child as an eventual adult, not just a cheap infant. The host needs an available supported actor place, the relevant body and care capabilities, adequate operating headroom, current authorized funding, and an honest continuation or coherent stopping policy. An existing per-actor AI allowance does not fund an unlimited number of children.

The first growing-family profile permits one pregnancy or dependent child in the selected household at a time. This is a qualification limit, not a universal moral rule or a lifetime fertility cap. It prevents a small care proof from silently becoming a population-growth service. When that child is independent, another planned birth still needs fresh consent, real care readiness and host capacity; adulthood does not delete the existing person to manufacture a vacancy. Adoption and authored additions consume the same actual dependent and population capacity.

The fictional and operational facts remain distinct. The parents can see and discuss their real supplies, home and willing help. The human can also receive an operational explanation that this world has not enabled growing families, is at its supported population allowance, or cannot currently fund the promised scope. An operational refusal is not infertility, a miscarriage or a character's unwillingness. The NPC does not receive an invented medical belief about a server limit.

Preparation needs a real safe resting/support place for the newborn, accessible age-appropriate nourishment and a capable person willing to perform the selected care. It does not need a decorative nursery, formal marriage, two biological parents, a rare crib or a minimum affection score. A human alone in an attended private world can deliberately choose a more hands-on role, but the offer must not promise that their absent body continues caring. A useful supported resident helper should make taking a normal outing possible without constant commands.

A care agreement is finite and specific enough to be useful: who is currently watching, where the child can safely remain, which actual supplies may be used and when the adults will reconsider. It grants only those operations. It neither assigns the whole NPC's life nor opens the parent's entire inventory. Parents can revise responsibilities through ordinary communication. The native care action needs current permission and real resources even when the conversation was affectionate.

Do not reserve an entire future lifetime of food, compute or money as a prerequisite to one child. Qualify a finite operating envelope with actual supplies and a workable renewal or stop boundary, and show its limits to the operator. On service exhaustion, the supported isolated world stops coherently before it can no longer perform required work; it does not keep consuming food while a failed provider prevents care. Accepted births are not erased, declined retroactively or charged automatically to another player.

## 4. Pregnancy should leave a life to live

The proposed condensed-family profile makes pregnancy last three advancing game days. At the existing normal rate this is 72 minutes of actual unpaused advancement, not a real-world appointment. The pregnancy is an ongoing bodily state; it does not occupy the gestating person's action lane for that whole interval. They can converse, make things, rest, travel where supported and pursue their own interests.

There is no first-profile random sickness, compulsory medical profession, extra hunger multiplier, fixed movement penalty or lethal childbirth roll. Those would add burden without yet providing a different wanted decision. The person may describe ordinary anticipation or discomfort through grounded authored experience, but a generated sentence cannot inflict a new condition. Optional deeper pregnancy health belongs with the care/body owners and needs an actual response before it is enabled.

The gestating person learns the supported pregnancy result through a private bodily/interaction outcome. The other participant does not automatically know that hidden bodily fact; it can be communicated. Later visible presentation can support an observer's permitted inference, not an omniscient diagnosis or an exact paternity label. There is no universal pregnancy announcement or family dashboard that bypasses current knowledge.

A simple optional family note can retain known plans and the anticipated stage, without a daily task list. Preparation should improve the coming encounter rather than gate it behind chores: choose a place, arrange supplies and help, discuss a name or continue ordinary play. The parents may disagree about a future ambition. The game should not create an emergency just because the player ignored an optional decorating suggestion.

Stopping intimacy or ending a partnership does not silently cancel an already committed pregnancy. The gestating person retains authority over their own participation and pregnancy-care decisions. The proposal includes an explicit, non-graphic **End pregnancy** action for that person's current controller, or their own independent NPC choice. It requires deliberate confirmation of that bodily result, not a partner's approval; it creates no child, returns no previously spent food or time, and is not available after birth. This is a proposed fictional body operation, not medical instruction. Another person cannot invoke it through a relationship role, a cancelled promise or a generic edit disguised as care.

No involuntary pregnancy-loss mechanic is introduced in this first profile. Actual death of a pregnant person remains a real lifecycle event under the selected mortality owner: it ends the pregnancy without creating a second hidden child, and a later revival does not silently restart it. The event is non-graphic and learned only through permitted evidence. No corpse-freshness rescue, fetal surgery or magical transfer is advertised. Those are distinct possible capabilities, not assumptions hidden in ordinary revival.

For a protected inactive human, pregnancy advancement and bodily consequences do not continue through their absence. The first profile is an attended, isolated world that also stops when its only human leaves; it makes no shared-public pregnancy promise. An active NPC pregnancy may advance when that world legitimately runs. Any future shared-world profile must reconcile its actual human protection, participation and birth opportunities before enabling the combination. Calendar chronology and bodily progression must remain distinct in the interface.

## 5. Birth and the first useful care

### Birth is one real arrival

Approaching the due boundary gives the gestating person a recoverable, understandable cue with time to choose a supported resting place. It is not a reflex test. At the boundary, the first profile uses a short native birth transition and a non-graphic covered presentation. An unperformed cinematic is not a delayed birth, and hiding the picture does not skip the world outcome. The child appears at an actual supported position with the parent, not in an inventory, at a remote nursery or in a duplicate body.

Birth commits one child's identity, initial compatible body, biological links and actual placement with the parent's transition. It cannot complete twice after reconnection, create a second infant when a name is edited, or leave parentage pointing at a draft rather than the actual people. If placement or required native work is unavailable, stop the isolated world coherently before crossing the unsupported transition; preserve the pregnancy and explain the operational blocker. Do not convert an overloaded host into a stillbirth, endless dangerous labor or a fabricated off-screen success.

The first birth is survivable without a clinician or material bill. The newborn has the selected ordinary starting condition, no secretly rolled diagnosis, and a finite initial food reserve that belongs in resource accounting. Full starting reserves do not establish a renewable food source. The gestating person does not gain free replenishment, youth, skills or a cleared injury merely because they gave birth. The first profile adds no mandatory postpartum disability or recovery grind. Existing unrelated conditions remain with their actual owners.

Naming is not an urgent modal. The child can receive a provisional locally known label and be named later through an ordinary supported decision. No one learns the name remotely. The parents' biological links are objective records; what the child or anyone else knows about them still requires appropriate authored background or actual learning. A new child receives no copy of parental memories, invention knowledge, private messages, debts or account grants.

### Care must work without repeated rescue by the player

A newborn can express discomfort, settle, sleep, attend to nearby people and respond in age-appropriate ways. It does not deliver an adult monologue or know the world registry. Basic bodily cues and their timing are native; there is no model request per cry, heartbeat or feeding tick. Relevant interactions may later become remembered experience through the normal bounded cognition package. The design claims neither clinical infant psychology nor literal sentience.

**Feed**, **Comfort**, **Carry** and **Put down safely** are actual supported operations, not relabeled gifts or animations. Feeding consumes an accessible age-appropriate portion and increases only the child's supported nourishment. The first world can supply finite prepared nursery portions rather than require a dairy industry. A general adult meal is not automatically edible for an infant. Producing more nursery portions requires an admitted positive-input recipe or another actual source; neither a parenting role nor a feeding description creates food.

The first native feeding is short and competent. One capable adult performs it; another cannot simultaneously consume a second portion or hold the same child. A cancelled feed spends and restores only what actually occurred under its selected action rule. The proposed first rule commits the portion and nourishment together on successful completion, so cancellation produces neither free food nor duplicate consumption. Keep the relevant supply visible through permitted inspection and preserve the chosen item after a recoverable refusal.

Carrying concerns an embodied person, never an inventory item. The carrier, child and actual support remain linked through the narrow existing body/space owners. A handoff requires an available willing recipient and changes support once; until successful, the first carrier retains support. Putting down requires a real usable surface. Stopping a conversation, closing a panel or losing a model reply cannot drop the child through the ground. An interrupted carry uses the selected native supported lowering/aftermath rule, not teleportation to the home. This narrow care capability does not establish general stretcher physics or multi-person hauling.

Caregivers may use a finite, previously accepted native care continuation while willing and capable. It notices meaningful need changes, uses authorized nearby supplies, reserves only the next actual action, and permits interruption or withdrawal. It does not spend model calls repeatedly choosing the same obvious feed or move. The resident's broader goals and personal decisions remain independent. A requested **Watch for a while** must say what the resident actually accepted, not imply permanent compliance from their biography.

A caregiver leaving first communicates the changed situation when possible and offers a real handoff; nobody becomes responsible merely by proximity. If the human must take over, the game should make that next choice simple. Repeated failed handoffs, needless pickups, stalled feeding or a helper who only works after constant prompting are failures of the selected experience, not desirable realism. EA's May 12, 2026 care update reported precisely these classes of interruption and redundant-handling problems; that precedent informs the acceptance cases, not a claim that this proposal solves them. [GF-R03](../generations-and-family-life-feature-spec.md#gf-r03--care-reliability-is-part-of-the-feature)

The first profile does not simulate diaper inventory, mandatory bathing, sterilization, teething remedies or fine nutritional categories. Occasional care detail may add warmth, but a hygiene supply chain is not required before a shared story or outing. Comfort and play are reasons for interaction, not a rigid hourly sequence. An unsettled child need not impose a settlement-wide debuff until every adult becomes miserable.

## 6. A child becomes somebody particular

### Stages change possibilities, not ownership

The optional condensed developmental profile uses actual advancing game time for one declared world-owned life-stage rule. It does not accelerate the world calendar, movement, manufacturing, conversations or adult chronology. It does not claim that a few game days contain eighteen lived human years. The proposed stages are newborn for the first day; young child from days one to five; child from five to twelve; adolescent from twelve to eighteen; and adult from day eighteen. A short permitted description shows stage and actual time since birth, never a fabricated multi-year biography.

These are fictional gameplay values to test, not a medical model or newly adopted default for every world. At normal speed, birth to independent adulthood spans 7.2 hours of unpaused play. The full pregnancy-to-adulthood example spans 8.4 hours. That is long enough to create several sessions of different activities without requiring months of continuously paid simulation. Existing authored children and adults allow a useful family encounter immediately; nobody must wait through the whole progression to try it.

A stage transition changes the child's actual supported capabilities and compatible body presentation once. It does not refill food or health, grant an adult's learned recipes, create equipment, award account access or automatically end an unfinished action successfully. Existing possessions retain identity; changed fit or reach has an explicit supported outcome before transition. A wider body that cannot fit at its current place requires a valid native placement resolution, not clipping through a wall. No ceremony or parent login is required to trigger growth.

The newborn needs feeding and safe physical support. A young child can move locally, communicate simply, choose play and begin supported self-feeding. A child can explore an appropriate known area, ask questions, watch work, remember preferences and help with a small safe activity when interested. An adolescent has greater independence and can learn more complex supported activities. Adulthood removes the childhood care grant and restrictions where appropriate, but does not end relationships or transfer guardians' property.

All pre-adult stages remain outside sexual activity. Childhood progression is not a sexual-unlock quest, and adult arrival never triggers a sexual scene, relationship assignment or inherited consent. Adult eligibility additionally requires an actual adult body, presentation and current capacity. A mere elapsed timer, changed label, saved old agreement or cosmetic edit cannot bypass that conjunction.

### Play and learning have value now

A child's first useful experiences are not miniature employment. They can ask why a real object works, invent a game using supported props, enjoy a place, choose a favorite story, disagree about going home or want company. A parent may show a real making process; learning records only what the child actually understood or acquired under the world rule. A pretend bow is not an armed attack, and a drawing is not proof of geographical knowledge. A new physical information artifact needs its actual writing/carrier support rather than a fabricated picture receipt.

Ordinary play may be its own payoff. It need not produce a combat modifier, parenting currency or permanent trait selection screen. A quiet afternoon is not a missed growth score. No narrow childhood checklist determines an irreversible adult competence ceiling. Missed lessons remain learnable later, and adult children may change professions. An authored tendency can influence interest without dictating every action or ensuring that two siblings behave alike.

The first heritable content is bounded supported physical resemblance from the actual parent/body definitions. It is sampled once for the real child and retained, not rerolled through a new preview or save callback. It grants no inherited moral worth, guaranteed personality, recipe, supernatural capability, title or skill. Any inherited functional trait must have an actually compatible admitted body rule; a gene-like name or generated explanation cannot create missing mechanics. Advanced genetics, selective breeding and hereditary stat optimization are not prerequisites for this family.

Early communication remains appropriate to the stage. A newborn can coo or cry without a fabricated explanatory transcript. A talking child can have a genuine simple purpose and limited perspective. The controller should not mechanically cycle through eat, sleep and play forever, nor generate a novel speech every few seconds to prove variety. Familiar routines, exploration, rest and chosen social moments can produce recognizable individuality with fewer meaningful decisions.

### Guidance is not remote possession

A caregiver may suggest a destination, offer help, set a supported safety boundary or explain why an action is unavailable. The child is not a freely transferable tool. The first profile excludes child combat roles, dangerous industrial work and enforced labor. They cannot be used as invulnerable shields or projectile blockers created by a censorship rule; physical and harm laws remain their explicit owners. The enabled child activity set must be matched to an actually suitable place rather than allowing unsupported danger and fabricating rescue afterward.

The adult audience does not imply that adult players must control children. The first growing-family child is an NPC with its own evolving agency. A parent cannot automatically take its account/controller place at adulthood or on the parent's death. Any later deliberate control grant uses the existing multiplayer authority and privacy rules, including protection of prior private sources. It is a separate product capability, not inheritance by surname.

## 7. Adoption, separation and changing responsibility

An existing child can enter a family without pregnancy. The first adoption consumer is a known, real child with an explicitly supported current caregiving authority and a willing prospective caregiver, not a shop of randomly generated biographies. The current responsible party must legitimately agree where required, and a child capable of understanding has an actual voice in the change. Refusal or uncertainty can defer the arrangement without making the child a failed purchase. No payment buys ownership of a person.

The accepted change establishes social parenthood or responsibility and narrowly necessary care permissions. It does not rewrite biological parentage, memories or a child's affection. A child may remain attached to former caregivers and later ask about them. Existing family facts remain distinguishable from known or disputed accounts. Where no current authority can legitimately approve the transfer, ordinary adoption is unsupported until the world supplies that responsibility; creator-authored starting families remain a distinct transparent origin, not a claim that an adoption occurred during play.

Adults can separate without a tribunal being prerequisite to continued play. Ending a romantic relationship does not erase a child's identity, automatically revoke the other caregiver's real rights or transfer everyone to one camp. They can agree on a supported new care arrangement with actual places, supplies and willingness. Until a replacement is valid, the current custody/support and responsibilities remain what actually exists. Nobody can remove another person's objects by declaring themselves the preferred parent.

Care authority narrows as the child becomes independent. Previous caregivers may still offer company, housing or help, but those are adult choices after adulthood. An adult child can move away, keep their own possessions, decline the family profession or maintain only some relationships. They inherit no platform bill, membership role, loan or political office merely through parentage. Actual gifts or later inheritance law remain their separate owners.

A caregiver's death does not automatically appoint the nearest adult or copy the parent's mind into the child. A previously accepted fallback can take its actual supported role. Otherwise, the remaining capable person must choose real assistance, temporary care or another supported arrangement. The first isolated attended profile allows a coherent pause and retained state while the human resolves an unavailable operational path. It must not promise public-world rescue or unbounded staff intervention that does not exist.

Actual fictional loss remains possible under an enabled world's explicit mortality law. This chapter adds no random child illness, neglect-based lethal timer or graphic injury presentation. It also adds no blanket magical immunity: protecting the first child experience requires suitable supported activities, actual supervision and coherent service, not a hidden shield that benefits combat. A child death, where the adopted law permits it, retains identity and learned relationships without generating an adult ghost persona. DG33's adult speaking-spirit example is not already child-afterlife support. The loss can matter through legitimate knowledge and later choices; no mandatory mourning checklist, automatic heir or replacement child is generated.

## 8. Absence and service failure are not parenting failures

The initial growing-family offer is an isolated, attended world with one human participant. When that human leaves, current departure handling completes or safely stops physical work and the whole world holds. Pregnancy, child development, needs, care, production and danger gain no catch-up during downtime. Returning does not create a missed birth, a secretly grown adult or a guilt message about months of neglect. A server that continued after its promised stopping boundary failed operationally; the story must not blame the parent.

While the human is playing, an independently willing resident can care as actually agreed, allowing the human to pursue another nearby activity or outing. A disappearing child or caregiver cannot be replaced by an omniscient summary. A finite continuation should end coherently, with a real handoff or an understandable request to reconsider. Individual goals remain independent; agreeing to watch for an afternoon is not agreeing to raise every future child forever.

Unattended family operation is a later extension of DG17, not included merely because that design supports two adults for three days. It must qualify this actual child's stages, care, supplies, safety, native execution, funding and cold return through the entire offered interval. A larger child requires more food and different actions; a funding estimate that counts only the infant is insufficient. Optional new family initiatives stop before essential admitted care lacks support.

If required service cannot operate safely within its admitted envelope, the isolated world stops as a whole before further unsupported consequences. It does not freeze only the baby while the rest of the economy produces goods, generate unperformed care, refill a pantry or keep billing repeated repair attempts. Funding exhaustion, lost credentials and provider failures are distinct from an NPC choosing not to help. Restoring service does not imply consent to an automatic top-up or renewed unattended period.

A later shared-public family profile needs a coherent alternative compatible with other participants' continued play. It cannot import a private family's whole-world pause into a public battle or use a dependent as a remote pause switch. No such public family service is claimed by this first profile. Admission should not offer that combination until its actual protection, place, service and exit contracts can be delivered; this restriction does not block intimate adult activity or an already-authored family visit.

## 9. Food, time and sustainable population

### Count consumers, not family labels

The first profile proposes child nourishment rates of one-quarter of the ordinary adult rate for a newborn, one-half for a young child, three-quarters for a child, and the adult rate for adolescents and adults. The child's reserve capacity stays explicit and age-appropriate; a birthday does not create food. These are fictional tuning proposals, not dietary or developmental advice. Nursery portions are real eligible food; later stages can use the installed age-appropriate meal families. No child works to produce their own survival entitlement.

With the inspected adult rate of 0.003 fullness per game second, one continuously active adult needs 259.2 points per game day. Two adults plus a newborn therefore consume 583.2 points/day; two adults plus an independently adult child consume 777.6. DG19's proposed four-patch renewal ceiling of 576 points/day does not cover even the first of those populations before travel, waste or missed eating. More descendants cannot be justified by assuming that people automatically become self-sufficient at adulthood.

Across the proposed three-day pregnancy and eighteen-day childhood, the two continuously active adults consume 10,886.4 fullness points. The child's staged demand is 3,499.2, for 14,385.6 combined, before waste and additional actors. This is a **full-progression workload**, not a prerequisite that the human hoard that entire amount before consenting. A finite attended episode can use explicitly authored provisions; a continuing settlement needs a real replenishment flow and viable access. First-birth qualification can start from an openly authored late-pregnancy state rather than falsely claiming all prior days were simulated.

For a shorter arithmetic fixture, three full pregnancy days plus one newborn day require 2,138.4 points for the two adults and baby at these rates, excluding any starting reserve. Sixty ordinary 38-point adult portions contain 2,280 points for adult consumption, while four proposed 25-point nursery portions provide 100 eligible child points. This is a finite provisioning example with separate food eligibility, not an optimized stock policy or proof that everyone will eat in time. Used portions, dropped items, waste and real meal choices must be measured. The profile's detailed adult-food excess is not nourishment an infant may automatically consume.

Avoid making a new agricultural system mandatory merely to test a family scene. Prepared worlds can contain honestly finite supplies, and mature-family qualification can use controlled resupply explicitly labeled as such. Neither proves a self-sustaining population. Additional real patches, trading, farming or another selected food source may later support continued life; their actual resources, time and access belong to their owners. A named household has no invisible pantry.

### Population growth must be deliberate and supportable

The first world does not automatically pair every adult, conceive on every intimate encounter, or fill an empty population allowance with a child. A household can decide not to grow. An NPC may want a child, discuss it and make independent choices under the enabled profile, but an active paid allowance or a high affection score is not reproductive intent. New admission sees all already accepted pregnancies and actual retained inhabitants, including members outside the immediate camera.

The operator can cap new supported births and admissions without deleting anyone, changing already committed ancestry or automatically killing an elder. The first one-dependent limit prevents a chain of simultaneous care demands; it does not independently cap every host or retained record. Population, active cognitive participation, pending work, storage and service recovery need their existing separate bounds. A dormant deceased person costs less active work but is not a free place to erase or clone.

Adulthood should reduce unnecessary care while increasing independent opportunity. It must not guarantee production, income, unquestioning obedience or repayment of childhood costs. A growing person can want to explore, make art, learn something impractical or leave. A settlement offer that is economical only if every child becomes a perfectly compliant worker is not economically qualified for this game.

### Pay for meaningful decisions, not biological ticks

Known stage transitions, feeding, support, elapsed-time advancement and exact custody use native mechanics. Generating an elaborate birth scene, infant monologue, daily diary or biography update is optional and generally unnecessary. Art needs a usable supported fallback; a missing bespoke portrait cannot erase the child or block ordinary care. Important observed changes reach their actual witnesses without awakening an entire family tree.

Cognitive work should follow a person's stage and current meaningful choices. Infant expressions need no full adult decision pipeline. Older children need room for interests, social choices and curiosity, not a fixed food/play loop presented as autonomy. Common unchanging context and installed family facts can be reused within their actual permission and snapshot scopes; each person's interpretation remains their own. No parent receives the child's raw private model context as a reward for caring.

For an illustrative whole-progression sensitivity, 8.4 active hours with twelve additional meaningful child workflows per hour means about 101 workflows. Adding twenty failed or otherwise billable incomplete workflows gives about 121. At hypothetical average costs of $0.002, $0.01 or $0.05, that is approximately $0.242, $1.21 or $6.05 for that extra workload alone. These are assumed counts and prices, not a cadence, rate limit, provider quote or measured family cost. Parent decisions, care, storage, native simulation, narration, transport and operator recovery are additional. If the actual experience needs far more paid reconsideration, fix redundant work or narrow the offered scope before claiming affordability.

## 10. Restore one family, not a convenient retelling

Current-format save and restart preserve the actual pregnancy or child, elapsed supported development, body stage, caregivers' current grants, carried support, reserved or consumed food, learned evidence and completed outcomes. An uncertain conception, birth, feed or handoff is reconciled before a new attempt. No duplicate child, resampled traits, second portion, new name identity or refreshed starting stock follows from reconnecting.

A restored age must reconcile capabilities, physical fit and ongoing actions rather than leaving an adult appearance over a child's rules. Birth and adulthood are meaningful lifecycle transitions, not cosmetic changes. Existing incompatible development saves remain explicitly rejected under the root no-legacy policy; this proposal authorizes no migration, automatic deletion or reset.

The family remains subject to DG25's coherent shared-restoration rules and DG29's external consequences. A child who visited another place, acquired a gift or was remembered by an outsider cannot be erased from a shared history by loading an earlier home snapshot in isolation. The first growing-family profile is same-world and makes no cross-world pregnancy or dependent-custody promise. A later transfer must preserve actual current person, developmental meaning, caregivers, supplies, bodily protection and destination capability without counting time twice.

Death and a supported later revival preserve the same person's history and supported developmental position. They do not age a child into an adult, resume an ended pregnancy, restore a former care authority or duplicate biological parentage. A source-private conversation remains protected through the child's memories, later adult choices and any changed controller. Retained family history is not permission to reverse current erasure, account access or real provider costs.

Turning new births off prevents new admission. It does not remove a current pregnancy or child. Retiring the reproductive family must preserve supported active pregnancies and subsequent child bodies until their actual dependencies are resolved; an operator cannot remove the birth capability and rely on narrative to finish its work. Service shutdown uses its actual finite retention and recovery terms, not a fictional abandonment verdict.

## 11. Acceptance and game-first sequence

First retain the ordinary adult-family visit as an independently useful experience. Next qualify a brief consensual adult intimate encounter with truthful non-explicit presentation and cancellation. Separately qualify one existing child's enjoyable interaction and real care. Only then expose planned conception through birth, the complete supported childhood and independent adulthood. Further ecology, inherited abilities, complex medicine, public unattended families and lifespan-wide mortality need their actual useful consumers; they do not block these selected smaller experiences.

| Complete journey | Required result |
| --- | --- |
| Adults enjoy intimacy but do not want children | Clear separate action; actual mutual choice; no pregnancy, marriage, ownership grant or persistent consent. |
| One adult declines or stops | No forced scene, successful result, repeated paid reroll or automatic punishment; ordinary play remains available. |
| Camera/effects/captions change | The scene remains non-explicit in every supported presentation, with a safe fallback and reachable Stop. |
| Two people deliberately plan a child | Current eligible body, exact assent, supported care and population admission produce one pregnancy; a retry cannot produce twins. |
| Pregnancy is private or a relationship ends | No remote announcement or automatic cancellation; the gestating person's choices and actual continued responsibilities remain distinct. |
| Birth occurs during ordinary play | One real child appears at valid support without a timed naming task, duplicated parentage or graphic presentation. |
| Two adults try to feed or carry at once | One actual supported action/transfer succeeds; no duplicate nourishment, dropped child, impossible custody or wasted second supply. |
| A parent takes a wanted outing | A willing capable helper actually performs accepted care; the player need not repeatedly repair it with commands. |
| The child chooses another interest | Their actual experience supports independent behavior; no imposed profession, irreversible missed-childhood score or inherited debt. |
| A body stage changes during work | Placement, equipment, current grants and action results stay coherent; no refill, free skill, hidden adult eligibility or duplicate transition. |
| An adult adopts an existing child | Real lawful caregiving authority changes; biological facts, remembered relationships and the child's perspective are preserved. |
| Client, model or funded service fails | Current-format recovery and the isolated whole-world boundary preserve actual work; no fabricated neglect or unperformed care. |
| Another birth is unaffordable | Refuse new operational admission, not the existing child's continued identity; intimacy and ordinary family play still work. |

The proof needs real human and resident paths, not only scripted choices. Observe what players voluntarily return for, how often they must issue corrective care commands, whether they can leave a scene satisfied, and whether a child develops a recognizable purpose beyond its meters. Report browser, native, fixture, live-model and uncoached evidence separately. No result is established by this design document.

Review against the simpler alternatives. An authored existing family may provide the wanted relationship more economically than simulating every early day. A short friendly interaction can be better than another childcare mechanic. A finite prepared scenario can be better than a speculative population service. Keep reproduction when choosing and seeing a new person grow is itself the wanted experience; do not require it before the game has adventure, useful invention and people worth knowing.

The final cuts are deliberate: no anatomical rendering behind the censor, no incidental pregnancy, no fertility retry grind, no mandatory marriage or nursery furnishings, no childhood trait-picking economy, no automatic worker children and no unattended family promise inherited from a smaller adult-only test. The condensed developmental profile is explicit and optional, rather than a hidden calendar rewrite. Its particular timings and food rates need measured tuning; the approved adult-audience and non-explicit-content boundary does not.

## 12. Research added for the resolved design

The main document retains GF-R01–GF-R14 with their original dates and limits. On October 14, 2026, this continuation rechecked the relevant primary descriptions of compressed development, background-family controls and reported care fixes. The recommendations above are Open Legend design judgments, not evidence of clinical accuracy or tested enjoyment.

### GF-R15 — Intimacy and wanting a child are separate interactions

[EA, The Sims 4 WooHoo guide](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/the-sims/the-sims-4/the-sims-4-woohoo-guide/), current undated help body accessed October 14, 2026. The guide describes adult intimacy, a separate Try for a Baby choice and relationships that need not be romantic partnerships. **Inference:** Offer understandable separate intentions rather than attach an undisclosed child to every intimate action. Open Legend does not import the guide's relationship-score prerequisites, locations or supernatural exceptions. The retrieved page's relative update age is not treated as a precise publication date.

### GF-R16 — More than one route to becoming a family

[EA, The Sims 4 Pregnancy Guide](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/the-sims/the-sims-4/pregnancy-guide/), current undated help body accessed October 14, 2026. It distinguishes trying for a baby, a separately supported non-romantic science-baby route and adoption. **Inference:** Biological parentage, intimate partnership and responsibility for a child should not be one automatic bundle. The proposal selects a narrow real-child adoption consumer, not an artificial-gestation system or an account entitlement to another person.

For development pacing, [Ludeon's October 15, 2022 preview](https://ludeon.com/blog/2022/10/biotech-preview-3-reproduction-children-genetic-modification-release-date/) explicitly shortened pregnancy and offered adjustable childhood aging. That is a precedent for a disclosed authored pace, not a reason to copy its numbers, birth hazards or inherited-stat economy. [The November 21, 2022 corrections](https://ludeon.com/blog/2022/11/1-4-content-update-adds-more-integration-between-expansions/) included growth credited while learning was frozen and age multipliers applied to the wrong actor family. Those findings inform pause and body-family acceptance, not a claim of current defects.

[EA's Neighborhood Stories description](https://www.ea.com/games/the-sims/the-sims-4/news/neighborhood-stories-system), rechecked October 14, distinguishes controls for autonomous household changes. Its offscreen narrative model is not adopted as evidence that Open Legend can fabricate unattended family life. Existing time, actor knowledge and service owners remain controlling.
