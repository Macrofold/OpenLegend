# Choosing and raising a family

This is the reproductive and dependent-family chapter of [Generations and family life](../generations-and-family-life-feature-spec.md), not a separate household engine or runtime implementation. Its behavior proposals extend the independently useful adult-family encounter rather than replacing that earlier delivery with a larger prerequisite.

## Owner decisions — October 14, 2026

Mike selected an **adult player audience with non-explicit presentation**. Sex remains an optional activity between eligible adult characters. The game need not depict anatomical sex; an opaque heart, an obscured scene or a fade can represent the encounter. This is a content direction, not a formal rating determination, a choice of age-verification vendor, or a requirement to make every relationship cheerful. Pregnancy, children and difficult family relationships remain possible subject matter. Child characters are not sexual participants.

Mike also selected **the existing browser platform for now**. An installed client, console port, replacement renderer and independent offline world are not selected work. The existing platform comparison remains useful research for a future specifically chosen need; it is no longer waiting for an answer to this assignment.

Only those decisions are owner-approved by this conversation. The detailed mechanics below are product recommendations for later adoption and implementation. In particular, audience approval does not adopt a new public-service launch, biological clock, child-harm rule, subscription or automatic population growth.

## 1. Three different choices

Intimacy, reproductive intent and parenting must be separately understandable. A person can want sex without wanting a child, want to raise a child without a sexual relationship, or love their existing family without wanting another dependent. Ordinary friendship, romance and a child-free life remain complete ways to play. Sex is not a required need meter, a renewable combat benefit, a skill-grinding action or the price of a useful companion's cooperation.

The recommended first intimacy action is **Be intimate**. It invites the named eligible adult into one presently chosen encounter; it does not establish marriage, exclusivity, reproduction, inventory access or a permanent agreement to future contact. Both participants choose independently. A human's actual deliberate input supplies their choice; an NPC chooses from its own permitted situation and preferences. Refusal, uncertainty, silence, a service failure and loss of eligibility remain distinct. Repeated proposals cannot reroll another person's refusal or manufacture consent.

The first reproductive action is a separate **Try for a child** proposal with explicit intended participants and the person whose body would carry the pregnancy. It does not silently reuse assent to ordinary intimacy. The first profile recommends no incidental pregnancy from the ordinary intimacy action. This preserves the user's requested activity without attaching an undisclosed population obligation. A future world that deliberately offers reproductive risk would need to disclose that different rule before the activity and provide its complete choices and consequences.

Neither action appoints another adult as a caregiver by implication. A co-parent or helper must accept a particular responsibility, and a child's actual care must work before the world admits a new dependent. This is not a demand for a long household contract screen: ordinary understandable choices can use the existing interaction and agreement surfaces.

## 2. Non-explicit throughout the experience

The default presentation is a short covered scene with an opaque heart; a fade-only preference can omit it. A blur is acceptable only when the underlying portrayal is already non-explicit. No explicit anatomical animation, audio or text is generated behind a removable censorship layer. Camera rotation, close inspection, reduced effects, missing assets, captions and accessibility output must retain the same presentation boundary. A missing effect falls back to the fade, not exposed imagery.

The player can hide the presentation without changing the world's actual outcome. Stopping the activity is a different deliberate action, immediately respected by both participants' active work. No cinematic locks the human out of cancellation. A world pause, interrupted movement, loss of control or unavailable partner cannot leave an invisible continuing encounter or create a successful reproductive result afterward.

Only an observer entitled to perceive a relevant occurrence receives its permitted non-explicit description. A heart is presentation, not a beacon identifying people behind a wall. The encounter reveals no orientation, private thought, fertility diagnosis or future pregnancy to onlookers. Public and private knowledge keep their existing owners. A family relationship, creator inspection or subsequent memory summary cannot turn a protected private source into general gossip.

The available pair must be adult under the selected body's explicit development policy, with compatible adult presentation and current capacity to agree. An uncertain age or changed body cannot be repaired by a generated assertion. A child cannot become an eligible partner through a label edit, a filter, accelerated rendering, an old acceptance or a borrowed adult body identifier. The family experience gives children ordinary play, learning and relationships, not sexual scenes or adult romantic goals.
