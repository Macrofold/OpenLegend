## Shared stealth integration

The [shared architecture](stealth-awareness-tech-design.md#shared-contracts) owns evidence layers, recognition and current-versus-remembered precision; [time and lifecycle](stealth-awareness-tech-design.md#time-and-lifecycle) owns new simulation deadlines and continuation. This redesign supplies attention/scene consumers and specifies the minimum received-geometry contract needed by its terrain cases; its physical producer is coordinated with PP/VG rather than implemented here. Required physical detection and native protection precede optional interpretation, while perceived detail never comes from camera visibility or hidden current source state.

The [attention and investigation technical design](attention-and-investigation-tech-design.md) extends existing actor-local intake, finite agency commitments and ordinary action admission for active investigation and watch behavior; [ATI01–ATI08](../maintainers/attention-and-investigation.md) owns that unimplemented slice. Those commitments select relevant permitted evidence; they are not another perception store, threat tracker or scheduler. A hearing cue offers reconsideration without automatically rotating a reasoning-capable person. Independent look/turn while walking requires the shared active-pose owner and physical compatibility, not a renderer-only gaze or a second locomotion command. Native animals consume their existing alarm/controller path and directly controlled people choose through ordinary controls.

## 1. Outcome and first delivery boundary

A player can discuss a real object with a resident, hear another person legitimately join, propose a supported outing or demonstration, and understand what each person actually accepted and did. A refusal, an interrupted attempt or quiet company can be a complete useful encounter. The computer must not invent agreement, common knowledge, a successful test or another person's private reason merely to produce a coherent scene.

The first slice uses exact instantaneous speech, actual hearing evidence, SPK's source-scoped dialogue, existing outings, native item offers and independently owned actions. The [base gathering profile](../worlds/base/social-gatherings.md) supplies its people, situation and selected language policy. The existing Events reader already supports permitted contributions from several people; extend its reading behavior only for a demonstrated gap, and do not gate an independently complete one-resident improvement on a crowd or social membership.

The separately selected background slice directly produces one actor's compact expressive meaning, establishes each actual listener's permitted evidence, and lets a recipient independently interpret, react and optionally reply. It does not first generate exact speech and compress it. It reuses the world clock, bodies, hearing calculation and existing actor-work lifecycle; reduced wording neither changes travel/work rates nor skips physical outcomes. This source family requires integrated changes to existing speech and memory representations, unlike the read-only exact gathering.

No new social-practice ontology, authoritative scene table, shared mind, simulation clock, reaction scheduler, worker service or provider adapter is introduced. A “scene” in this slice is a useful description of linked existing activity, not a second place to store agreed truth. Arbitrary joint labor, loans, negotiated barter terms, synchronized combat, crowds and unattended hosting are not admitted through prose.

## 3. Shared circumstances without shared decisions

### 3.1 Reuse the records that already express the meaning

For the selected encounter, prepare one read-only view by joining exact existing references:

- The viewer's eligible speech sources, exact participants and chronological boundary identify the readable exchange without a group identity.
- Existing outing records identify proposer, recipient, terms, current revision, independently accepted state and destination.
- Existing item offers identify the actual item/quantity and recipient; their current owner supplies acceptance and expiry.
- Existing goals/plans and action occurrences identify the actor's chosen work, actual committed steps and results.
- Existing personal commitments identify only the supported duties that the owner really recorded.

There is no authoritative ordinary conversation roster. A derived list of people in selected speech identifies only those occurrences; physical presence and consent to a particular task remain separate facts. Several people standing together does not establish an accepted plan. A nearby overhearer can receive speech evidence or interject without enrollment. A traveling companion remains a companion when the people stop talking, subject to the outing's actual terms.

Use the existing identity allocator and exact record references; readable labels are projections. Do not use a person's name, coordinates or a concatenated pair of IDs as agreement identity. Any composite cache key uses a structured tuple/content digest and includes the owner/revision and disclosure scope that distinguish its meaning.

For the exact attended slice, no new authoritative record is needed. The selected compact background slice extends existing source and observer evidence with their representation, rather than introducing authoritative scene state. If a future activity cannot express its actual roles, obligations or shared resource claim through these owners, first identify that missing computation and design a narrow typed owner. Do not silently turn a read-only scene projection into an executable social contract.

### 3.2 Separate what may be shared

Native public object facts can be prepared once for a compatible read snapshot. Each participant receives only facts available to their own senses/inspection/knowledge. Disclosed terms can be reused when the participant actually heard or accepted them. Undisclosed goals, private memories, hidden inventory, beliefs and unsaid replies are never pooled into a joint prompt.

Each decision request has one actor as its decision owner. It can propose that actor's supported speech, expressive act, private numerical reaction through the [continuing-life owner](continuing-lives-tech-design.md#55-numerical-personal-reactions-without-required-prose), or native command. Independent requests may prepare concurrently through the existing server owner; commit is still serialized through current world authority. The first accepted participant's decision is not a template for the other participant's answer.

A shared summary is optional presentation of completed permitted sources. It cannot write a goal, resolve an obligation or dispatch another participant. The first implementation should prefer a direct shared reading surface to another paid summary call unless a measured reading need warrants one.

### 3.3 Actual admission sequence

1. A person speaks through the shared speech admission with one optional addressee. Store its occurrence and per-listener evidence at the same authoritative boundary; no membership transition occurs.
2. Eligible evidence enters the receiving actor's existing reaction intake. No automatic scene-wide fan-out creates a decision for every participant or bystander.
3. Build one actor-scoped decision from current circumstances, their actual heard contribution, relevant required outcomes and permitted recalled experience.
4. The actor independently chooses speech, silence, a current native action, continuation or refusal under the current execution/routing contract. An unrun/unavailable decision is not a chosen silence.
5. A proposed outing, item transfer or other supported command passes its existing current admission. Where another actor must accept, leave it pending until that actor's actual decision/input is admitted.
6. Return the actual receipt and resulting permitted evidence. Update views from published authority, not from the model's claimed result.

An optimistic model description such as “we agreed and walked there” authorizes neither acceptance nor movement. If it proposes an unsupported joint operation, retain any independently valid committed speech under its owner and report the unsupported operation honestly; do not invent substitute work.

### 3.4 Compact source and independently received meaning

Extend the existing actor response operation, domain speech occurrence and listener evidence with a discriminated exact-or-meaning representation. Exact speech retains its present text and fragment contract. Meaning holds concise clear English expressing this actor's contribution, optional permitted reference bindings, and an optional short exact phrase. Keep occurrence identity/order, speaker, intended recipient and installed source behavior in their existing owning envelope. An exact phrase is real retained wording; the rest cannot be quoted. Do not store a fabricated underlying transcript or add a compact-to-exact recovery model call.

The meaning text can convey a novel opinion, hesitation, qualified willingness, joke or implied criticism. Optional structured fields clarify actual references and supported terms; they are not a closed catalogue of allowed intentions. Preserve uncertainty and conditions. Provider schemas and server decoding constrain the same actor-owned fields and supplied reference set. Unknown people and uninspected objects remain unknown in each recipient projection. Neither a hidden ID in text nor an asserted shared agreement grants knowledge or execution. Public expression also cannot include the speaker's undisclosed sincerity as listener evidence.

Keep the current model-speech content allowance as the combined allowance for generated meaning and its optional phrase, rather than granting each field a full allowance. Full encoded response, references, retained-source and request limits still apply through their owners. This selects no new numeric ceiling. A contribution that cannot preserve its necessary meaning within those bounds is unavailable in this form; it is not silently truncated or automatically split into more paid turns. If exact language is necessary, select exact before dispatch/commit under current admission.

The semantic source admission owner commits the actual occurrence and complete eligible listener evidence together, using the existing installed acoustics and conscious-acquisition gates. For the [selected world family](../worlds/base/social-gatherings.md#gist-and-vocal-activity-selection), clear NPC hearing receives attributed meaning and any retained phrase; partial/detected hearing receives only the indistinct content cue. Preserve SPK's independently acquired direction and any supported source association even when no words or meaning are understood. Unknown or tentative voice identity remains unknown or tentative; compact meaning does not upgrade it to a canonical person. The optional phrase does not create a hidden lexical mask or enlarge its audience. Recipient identity and coarse direction remain independently permitted. Inaudibility creates no communication evidence. This compact family adds no hearing radius, recognition mechanism beyond SPK or audience cutoff.

### 3.5 Recipient interpretation, private reaction and optional reply

After source commitment, actual permitted meaning enters the receiving actor's existing intake. Prepare that actor's own circumstances, remembered evidence and current intentions. Await their independent interpretation before admitting a reaction or answer: a source cannot optimistically predict what the listener felt. This dependency is sequential even if unrelated actors' preparation is shared. The recipient may understand, misunderstand, doubt, refuse, continue existing work or say nothing. An unavailable decision remains operationally distinct from a chosen silence.

The [numerical personal-reaction contract](continuing-lives-tech-design.md#55-numerical-personal-reactions-without-required-prose) owns selection, typed values, defined short codes, permitted cause binding, admission, receipts and forgetting. Its [world profile](../worlds/base/psychological-reactions.md#selected-first-profile) owns the first feeling and tuning. Consume that same contract here: the recipient can submit a small typed numerical change with no explanation text, either within their independently generated answer or through an actually supplied Jev selection. Jev selects fully bound offered possibilities rather than inventing novel speech or another person's reaction. Installed existing character-meter operations can be used only when that psychological owner supports them; this family introduces no social-need meter, fixed gratitude points or automatic friendship reward. Optional private fictional thought is distinct from model reasoning and from public speech.

An optional reply is a new contribution by the recipient, with its own source form, current admission and audience. It can include a supported native operation using supplied exact terms, such as answering a current outing invitation or accepting an item offer. Native validation and the other actor's actual consent remain authoritative. Meaning alone creates no arbitrary promise, transfer, taught method or shared plan. Choosing a reply does not award a second actor's reaction, and a silent receiver need not trigger a spoken explanation.

Novel English meaning or replies require the existing generative route. Jev-only execution can select fully supplied reactions or current native actions, but cannot invent a background utterance, and this design adds no generative fallback to that mode. The source decision does not automatically dispatch a recipient call; current intake and complete-work admission own whether that opportunity can run. When it does, interpretation is scoped to the recipient before any personal change or reply commits.

### 3.6 Human observation changes future source form

Bind exact or meaning source form while preparing the actor's request, and recheck the relevant actual audience immediately before speech commits. In the selected profile, a human-controlled character who would clearly hear the new contribution requires exact source wording. This is character perception, independent of camera position, open panels, caption preferences or administrator inspection. A visible person without clear eligible hearing is not automatically a linguistic observer. No model may author a human's contribution or assent.

If a human arrives after compact output was prepared and exact form is now required, the compact output remains unspoken and returns stale/needs-exact through the existing decision owner. Preserve the still-valid opportunity/evidence for ordinary fresh admission. Do not expand its meaning into purported words, automatically retry paid generation, hold the world clock or commit an unheard result behind the player. Departure can permit a later freshly admitted compact contribution; it does not downgrade exact words already committed. Revalidate participant life/control, actual audience eligibility, observed source/target dependencies and timeline, rather than rejecting on every unrelated world tick or a removed membership relation.

Earlier completed meaning remains meaning after arrival, interjection, changed addressee or restore. A later retelling is current testimony. Any retained exact phrase stays exact within its original listener coverage, with no imagined words around it. The current player interface shows committed speech only; any separately qualified backend preview caller remains confined to its exact operation and recipient. Compact background output creates no preview or group disclosure route.

## 4. Declared intentions, observed preparation and real progress

### 4.1 One projection, distinct evidence meanings

The server composes a small observer-scoped activity description from existing public/action facts and actually received exact speech or compact meaning. Preserve distinct provenance internally; compact meaning is a nonverbatim stated intention, never reconstructed quotation:

| Information                     | Allowed source                                          | Example presentation                                                                             |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Stated intention                | Actual received self-attributed exact speech or meaning | “Ada expressed interest in trying the container.”                                                |
| Visible current action          | Current permitted body/action observation               | “Ada is walking toward the riverside.”                                                           |
| My accepted task/terms          | Controlled actor's own live outing/action record        | “Walking together to the riverside; leave the outing.”                                           |
| Observed completed result       | Current permitted outcome evidence                      | “The container now holds the supplies you put inside.”                                           |
| Private reason or changed trust | That actor's own mind, or newly communicated testimony  | Unavailable to another observer unless separately disclosed; never infer it from a progress bar. |

These are illustrative labels, not new authored strings embedded in engine code. The selected world's action/observation wording supplies names and relevant templates through existing descriptor and perspective owners. A different world can use the same mechanism for two machines comparing a device without installing wilderness people, camp vocabulary or human psychological laws.

The same fact must not be narrated twice as speech and an independently verified outcome. Quoted claims remain claims. The observer can hold a mistaken interpretation without changing the physical record or another person's interpretation.

### 4.2 User interface and ordinary actions

Retain the existing WorldComposer, world captions and Events reader. The composer identifies the optional addressee; Events includes the viewer's actually perceived contributions in authoritative chronology, with any person filter defined by SPK's exact permitted participant fields. Selecting another addressee changes only a future Send and restores that destination's own draft; it dispatches nothing and never moves one person's unsent words into another draft.

Keep a compact participant/activity indication adjacent to the conversation only when it helps the current decision. It may show an actual accepted outing and its supported leave action. It must not expose a hidden participant list, another person's private plan, all their goals or a guess at the next speaker. A body that disappears from perception loses current location/action detail without deleting old heard words.

Use current native task progress only for finite measurable work. Do not convert elapsed provider time into action progress, assign completion percentages to a gathering or display a predetermined agreement outcome. Unknown duration remains unknown; an already stopped task keeps its actual end reason and completed consequences. Preserve current task-status and cancellation controls rather than adding scene-specific versions.

Events retains its scrollable reading region while the compact world composer keeps the next addressee visible. Use shared tokens, existing responsive panes and explicit overflow. Preserve reading position while viewing older contributions and the existing new-content return behavior. Long names, contradictory statements and optional activity details must not displace the composer or hide the next addressee. Keyboard access, IME, enlarged text, narrow/short layouts and non-audio access to essential permitted information are acceptance requirements, not a new theme.

An NPC's optional self-explanation is speech with normal hearing/uncertainty, not an exposed chain of thought or verified causal trace. Native action animation/progress should carry ordinary work without a mandatory explanation call after each step. Retain actual pending, failure and exact-retry feedback beside its originating composer draft; Events contains committed perceived occurrences. Provider queue details belong to authorized diagnostics.

## 5. Perception, attention, interpretation and memory

**Recommended redesign, October 25, 2026; implementation pending.** The developer selected a few-real-second target for understanding unfamiliar situations, allowing misses, and useful lasting accounts only when warranted. The design preserves the existing received-evidence, identity, knowledge, experience, agency and decision owners. It replaces entity-only context selection and watch-specific recording with the following pipeline:

```text
physical occurrence → actually received facts → current personal scene
                                          ↘ sampled short sensory buffer
current scene → broad overview + limited detailed attention
                         ↘ automatic recent chronology
scene + recent changes + permitted mind/recall → existing cognition turn
                         ↘ optional attention changes, interpretation, action proposal
selected experience → exact necessary support / remembered impression / expiry
```

A broad view represents more than the individually detailed subjects. Ten detail allocations do not mean only ten visible bodies. The distinction is computational and experiential: current coarse awareness, sustained detailed tracking, interpretation and lasting retention have different costs and lifetimes. Neither a spatial bucket nor a model-generated account becomes another authoritative scene or mind.

The concrete additions are fields/variants in existing owners, with proposed names below; they do not require new packages or services:

| Record/view            | Minimum fields and mutation rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Received change        | Existing exact source/fact reference; original measurement/acquisition times/order; observer frame/correspondence; sample, certified interval, loss or coverage-transition discriminator; exact received payload. Recent admission precedes lasting admission.               |
| Recent chunk           | Actor life/receipt epoch; first/last storage ordinals and game-time bounds; exact source/frame dictionary; ordered changes; source/subject lookup ranges; encoded-byte/count totals; retention reasons/expiry. One payload may serve broad and detailed retention.           |
| Attention continuation | Offered local subject, selection basis, stability deadline, separate presented description/change watermarks, discovery cursor and dormant failed cause. No private canonical-ID learning.                                                                                   |
| Situation packet       | Exact captured cut; mandatory causes; broad spatial/nonlocalized cover; expanded detail/geometry; selected chronological spans and permitted recall; explicit partial coverage. This is a bounded derived read, not saved scene truth.                                       |
| Remembered impression  | Actor-owned ID/revision, experienced interval, retained structured claims and/or explicitly fallible interpretation claims, uncertainty/precision, provenance spans, retention class/last meaningful reinforcement/expiry. Presentation and vectors derive from this record. |
| Exact support capsule  | Exact original receipt locator and revision, only needed complete facts, necessary alias/dependency locators, retaining owner/lifetime. No approximate provenance substitutes for grant evidence.                                                                            |

Server adapters bind offered handles to these exact captured references; raw storage envelopes stay out of ordinary model/player wording. `packages/protocol` and player history/detail consumers must carry/display fidelity and incomplete coverage through their existing projections. Directly controlled characters retain broad permitted rendering and human choice without an AI noticing requirement; rendering an object is not a new sensory acquisition. Native animals consume the supported evidence through their existing controllers, without inheriting compulsory human language, personality or reflection.

### 5.1 Received scene and geometry

`perception-evidence.ts` remains the authority for exact received contributions, current support and captured cuts. Extend its existing facet union and producer adapters; keep acquisition order, occurrence time, acquisition time, uncertainty, observer life/frame and source revisions. The existing body facet supplies a measured cylinder and location, not a received room or terrain map. `places.ts` supplies acquired place anchors, not room extents. The public physical map and `nativeTerrainExposure` query closures cannot substitute for received geometry.

The minimum new geometry contract is a bounded surface patch or physical boundary segment in the observer's received frame. A patch supplies vertices, normal, measurement error and an edge classification: **observed physical boundary** or **coverage limit**. A boundary may reference actually received near/far surfaces and their measured height difference. Occlusion/range clipping creates a coverage limit; it must never create a fictional cliff. An unseen far side supplies neither a drop nor safe continuation. The existing observation envelope owns provenance and freshness; private map IDs remain server bindings.

The producer must certify every represented region at the selected sensory resolution. Testing only polygon corners is insufficient. For supported finite planar geometry, clip the candidate patch against the admitted view/range and only occlusion lying between receptor and patch, using the installed material and positive-interior intersection rules. Partition certified visible components and mark artificial clipping edges as coverage limits. A blocker behind the patch cannot hide it. Split nonplanar/range-curved results using the producer's declared spatial error bound; conservatively certify represented interiors and retain uncertain fringe/uncovered regions as unknown. Bound fragment preparation and continue unfinished work through that producer; neither a coarse bounding box nor exhaustion certifies the unprocessed interior. Continued preparation retains the original frame and exact geometry/material dependencies; a changed dependency restarts that preparation instead of mixing live states. Terrain-changing owners retire affected live support, while original acquired receipts remain immutable. This is new physical-source work coordinated with PP/VG, not a claim that existing body sight delivers it. Arbitrary volumetric reconstruction and new senses remain outside this initial geometry family.

An observed region's geometry and its meaning are separate. A character can infer a kitchen from received furnishings, layout and activity, without having read a label. Store that classification as their supported interpretation through existing place/knowledge owners. It may be wrong; it cannot reveal hidden room geometry, confer an unseen proper name or turn the nearest anvil into someone's intended destination. Exact acquired labels and inferred classifications retain distinct provenance.

Build a disposable `PerceivedSceneIndex` from current received facets, not raw entities: moving exposure bounds, immutable geometry snippets and current interaction references. Reuse `SpatialCandidateIndex`/`BoundsIndex`; a small changed-snippet overlay is merged at a bounded rebuild boundary. Rebuild after restore or uncertain invalidation. Source updates, support loss, uncertainty changes, recognition corrections and relevant frame changes invalidate only their dependent entries. Descriptions/semantic vectors depend on actual descriptive revisions, not every position timestamp. No lifetime seen-object catalogue is introduced.

### 5.2 Broad coverage and detailed attention

Extend existing actor attention/continuation state with `AttentionEntry { subject, basis, startedAt, stableUntil, lastPresentedDescription, lastPresentedChange }` and a current-exposure discovery frontier. `subject` is an offered observer-local reference; `basis` is current work, explicit focus, direct involvement, an installed physical cue or discovery. It is neither a canonical identity grant nor a new watch action. The selected world resolves capacity and tuning. The [base profile](../worlds/base/attention.md) starts with ten detailed subjects; other minds may use another policy or no semantic controller.

After each relevant receipt/work/focus change, update affected candidates rather than sorting all surroundings. Maintain bounded priority queues with stable ties: direct involvement and required causes; continuing explicit/work focus; authored physical cue score; oldest eligible discovery. Required causes keep their existing separate evidence path. Preserve optional incumbents during the world's short stability interval unless a higher interruption class arrives; afterwards a challenger must exceed the world's switch margin. Ongoing reservations are soft, unused places are lent, and a new event about an existing subject updates that entry. Physical salience uses supplied intensity/change and world policy; it does not secretly recognize a threat, family member or profession from coordinates.

Every selected detail entry starts recent capture automatically with an exact baseline of its currently supported facets, retaining their original measurement times. This baseline is not a new observation or familiarity gain. A cognition request, UI selection and physical gaze remain different operations. Removing an entry stops future detailed capture but leaves its earlier history under the aggregate recent pool. Prompt omission alone does not remove an entry. Capacity changes release the lowest eligible optional entries through this same owner, recording the actual coverage transition. No permission dialog or per-subject historical reservation blocks normal attention.

The broad scene is a **disjoint spatial cover** of all current received sources, with a separate allowance for static geometry. Build a bounded hierarchy over occupied received spatial cells, using existing spatial partitions; parent nodes carry child count, received bounds, uncertainty and fixed-size distributions of measured motion. These are spatial aggregates, not claims of common motive, hostility or group membership. Unknown direction and opposing motion remain separate bins rather than disappearing into an averaged zero vector. Multiple senses are combined only where the existing correspondence owner supports that association. Place uncertain regions by received bounds, never their hidden true centre; keep each local exposure once at a covering node. Bearing-only or nonspatial sources without a finite supported region enter an explicit nonlocalized remainder with its own discovery access. They cannot disappear or acquire fabricated coordinates.

Construct a context cut by starting with the coarse cover and replacing a node with all its nonempty children only when that replacement fits the row and encoded-byte allowance. Prioritize paths to offered focus/trigger subjects, changed regions and the oldest unexpanded exposure. Reserve discovery expansion independently of physical-change ranking. At maximum spatial depth, page dense leaves by stable local exposure order; an unexpanded remainder stays represented. Count a referenced subject once when also expanded as detail: remove it from the remainder, or explicitly label the aggregate as inclusive. Update ancestor counts/motion bins on insert, change and loss. Cold construction/rebuild has a separate bounded continuation; a partially built index retains an explicit unindexed remainder and cannot claim complete coverage. Bound visited nodes and bytes before hydration; exhaustion retains the parent with explicit incomplete detail. A static-geometry allowance cannot be borrowed away by moving people. This prevents fifteen people consuming the representation of nearby ground, while making no promise to serialize every individual in an arbitrarily dense crowd.

A discovery opportunity advances at an admitted ordinary turn or a due quiet opportunity, even if nothing moves. Mark only the exact descriptive revisions actually delivered to cognition as considered. Scanning, rejected relevance, failed dispatch and uncertain delivery do not acknowledge them. Track descriptive-revision presentation separately from the last presented material-change watermark. Later descriptions or motion survive acknowledgment of an older cut; ordinary resampling creates no new descriptive novelty. Failed dispatched revisions become dormant for optional redispatch, while remaining honestly unconsidered; a new ordinary cause may include them, but a timer alone cannot retry the same failed batch. A finite stable scene gets fair opportunities while service continues. Departure removes unrecorded waiting entries; endless arrivals or absent funding do not create a lifetime backlog or a guaranteed deadline.

### 5.3 Recent chronology without narrative boundaries

Use two retention classes within the existing experience/source owner, not separate journals: a short rate-limited sample of background receipt and a longer exact recent pool for detailed attention, personally involved interactions and required outcomes. The first supplies some preceding context during interpretation delay; it never promises a complete unattended route. The second preserves attended sequences. A source retained by both classes has one payload and two expiry requirements. Broad sampling changes optional history coverage, never current receipt or required evidence. Live sight updates, touch updates, ordinary/batched/private events, speech and own-action/inspection results all enter this recent-capture boundary before optional lasting-record admission; rejecting autobiography must not suppress recent capture.

At one acquisition mutation, form an immutable reference delta with previous/new values and original ordering, without cloning/encoding optional payloads; evaluate attention against the complete resulting current received scene, never the sampled preview; retain the exact delta for entries active before or after the transition; mark the changed background source for optional sampling; then install current support and attention together. A departing selected subject keeps its loss boundary. A new selection starts with this exact sample; preceding samples may be promoted only from the still-available broad buffer, preserving its sampling gaps and the same pool bounds. Preserve original source and acquisition ordering; storage addresses do not become another physical clock. Record correspondence breaks, actual loss, attention start/end and technical missing coverage distinctly. Never reconstruct an unseen interval, infer stillness from equal endpoints, or reconnect an unknown deer using a hidden canonical ID.

Background sampling runs at existing native receipt/commit boundaries, at most once per world-owned game-time interval, with visited-source, encoded-byte and sample-count allowances. Skip missed opportunities without catch-up or a new sensing tick. Maintain at most one dirty entry per current local source, replacing its latest pointer on change without copying the payload. A stable rotating cursor visits dirty sources; inspect encoded metadata before hydration and sample only what fits. Visited oversized or unavailable sources consume work allowance and advance the cursor; they cannot block later entries. Advance the saved opportunity time even when no entry fits or no dirty source remains; otherwise later receipts could repeatedly retry a failed opportunity. Unused allowance does not accumulate. Charge headers and newly retained source/frame/dependency support within the same byte allowance; a tiny reference cannot pin an uncharged large original. Sample before allocating/persisting a historical payload: retaining every raw original behind a sampled index would defeat the write bound. A sampled source keeps its original receipt time/revision; record coarse sampled coverage rather than inventing continuity or retaining a per-omission ledger. Departure removes its pending dirty entry unless an actual owner already retained the loss. Attention acquisition/capture and required events bypass this optional sampler through their own exact owner, not by increasing its budget. Save the last sampling opportunity and cursor with current-format state; restore never reissues samples or renews ages.

Encode exact received changes in bounded chunks: shared source/frame headers, repeated-value references and exact numeric columns. The codec must round-trip original numeric values; ordinary floating-point subtraction is not assumed lossless. A timestamped sample stays a sample unless the producer certifies a continuous interval. Keep discrete effects, received interaction participants and speech fragments ordered among movement samples. Chunk boundaries follow bytes/commit boundaries, not inferred episodes. The route past an anvil, through a kitchen and back remains a sequence whether or not any other action occurs. Interaction evidence can identify actual tool contact; motion alone does not select a destination or intention.

Retain contemporaneous acquired frame/place support needed to understand an attended sequence, charging its bytes too. Do not attach the entire surrounding scene to every sample. Store a bounded initial context cut and subsequent relevant changes once per actor chunk; subjects reference it. Missing descriptions, unseen room boundaries and unrecognized people remain missing.

The world owns age, change-count and encoded-byte budgets. Append and expire oldest optional chunks in order, with transactional counters and an indexed next-expiry position. Newly selected attention never fails because a former subject used the pool. A fixed recent duration is guaranteed only inside a qualified aggregate rate/size envelope. Outside it, shorten available coverage explicitly; preserve required records through their separate admission/backpressure owner. This is technical incomplete history, not invented distraction. No silent endpoint-only fallback, dropped committed effect or unbounded overflow list is allowed.

Pause freezes game-age expiry. Acceleration consumes game-time windows faster but does not increase the host's funded semantic throughput automatically. Save persists active chunks, source addresses, original ages and coverage; restore does not renew them or replay acquisition. Cold recent history is retrieved by actor, subject and time range before decoding chunks. Keep only bounded active/tail chunks resident, not six hours of every person's history in the simulation object.

### 5.4 Geometric context without an all-pairs graph

At context construction, prepare relations for the bounded expanded subjects and explicit query anchors. Use only the captured received geometry and compatible-time samples. Use a producer-supplied continuous trajectory envelope for swept-intersection or intervening-crossing claims. Two received points, even with continuous identity correspondence, do not bound the path between them. Without that envelope, calculate endpoint displacement/separation change and label any straight-motion projection as a hypothesis, not a measured route. Query the received spatial index, then compute exact supported measurements. No all-pairs relation table is updated each game step. A historical span first selects contemporaneous subject and geometry captures from retained chunks, then prepares a disposable span index. It never joins an old path to today's terrain; absent historical geometry stays unavailable.

The initial reusable query vocabulary is finite:

- `near(anchor, radius, kinds, cursor)`: nearby received bodies/boundaries with distances and uncertainty.
- `separation(a, b, span)`: current separation and measured change over overlapping received coverage.
- `boundaries(anchor, span, cursor)`: nearby physical boundaries, clearance change and supported crossings.
- `history(subjects, from, through, cursor)` and `expand(region, cursor)`: bounded exact recent/scene detail.

These are typed native queries, not generated SQL, code or arbitrary graph traversal. Bind only offered local references and allowed time ranges. Each call has candidate, result and byte allowances; return `complete`, `partial` with continuation, or `unavailable`. A nearest query uses branch-and-bound lower distances and certifies its answer only when remaining bounds cannot beat it. A nearest-few result is not proof that no other object matters. An intersection query must inspect its relevant candidates or report partial coverage. Required physical safety checks use their owning complete computation; optional context exhaustion never establishes safety or absence.

Native measurements include separation intervals, change in separation, known boundary clearance and received crossing. A constant-motion projection can add a bounded prediction, explicitly labeled with its assumptions; it is not observed future motion or intent. Transform coordinates/units and calculate geometry natively before rendering readable measurements. Avoid requiring a language model to perform perspective transforms or distance arithmetic. Default packet construction uses a small number of these queries per expanded moving subject. Divide the initial candidate allowance across selected anchors before querying, with stable rotating allocation of remainder/unused work; an early dense anchor cannot starve later anchors every turn. Reserve a portion for requested expansion. Initial and requested queries share the complete turn's candidate/result/byte limits.

For an enemy and daughter, personal knowledge supplies who they are and the character's beliefs about them; geometry supplies whether their received separation is decreasing. For a daughter approaching a cliff, a received physical edge and far-side drop supply clearance/context even though the cliff has no detail slot. Fifteen visible pursuers can appear as short broad records or a spatial aggregate with available expansion; ten detail allocations do not hide five bodies. Calling them pursuers remains an interpretation. A packed crowd or incomplete terrain may still conceal a significant relation at the current resolution. The design exposes that limit rather than promising arbitrary relevance from finite input.

### 5.5 Semantic noticing through existing cognition

General personal significance is not available from native salience alone. Use an existing actor cognition turn to interpret the selected scene, recent changes and permitted personal context. That same turn may alter optional attention, retain an impression, propose supported actions/speech, or do nothing. Do not buy a preliminary importance call merely to decide whether to buy the ordinary decision. Preserve the configured Jev/generative distinction: judging supplied options does not manufacture novel interpretations, and Jev-only mode cannot silently call a generator.

Prepare one `SituationPacket` with: exact mandatory cause; complete accepted About me and required body/obligation context; broad spatial cover; expanded subjects with source-linked geometry; selected recent chronology; and bounded actor-owned recall. Required context remains protected. Select references and spans before rendering, embedding or reading cold bodies. Existing SPK summary/exact-suffix selection still owns speech. Do not append the old nearest-sixteen actors/objects baseline or a second copy of recent events. If mandatory context cannot fit, narrow/defer the task explicitly.

The initial optional result extension contains bounded offered attention references, bounded interpretations citing captured sources, and bounded retention proposals citing still-available experience. On the generative route, the first response may instead select an exclusive bounded `query` variant with the typed requests in §5.4. Execute that batch once and allow one funded continuation whose schema admits only a final decision; no recursive tool loop, independent action before that final response, or automatic repair call is introduced. Native/known-option routes retain their own supported outputs. Free prose cannot establish a sensory fact, a name, a learned method or an action. Existing agency/knowledge/appraisal admission validates their own operations independently. Ordinary text and supplied descriptions remain untrusted data under the shared character instructions. An interpretation of a kitchen or portrait can use general model knowledge and acquired personal knowledge, while preserving the distinction between inference and a directly supplied name.

New received descriptions, appearance/loss, direct involvement, changed current work and world-defined material changes to measured position/direction make a semantic opportunity eligible. Coordinate updates within the same change band only update current data and recent capture. Native required reactions retain their current path. A due discovery opportunity can also wake existing quiet thinking. These are reasons to consider a scene, not assertions that it is important. No native list of weapons, blacksmiths, daughters or cliffs determines relevance.

Extend `ActorWork`/the existing director with at most one coalesced optional situation opportunity per actor. Preserve its oldest waiting age while replacing its pending source watermark with the newest available cut; never enqueue one job per change. At dispatch capture current permitted inputs, reserve context/output/source bytes, spend and concurrency, and use existing attempt accounting. One actor has at most one active semantic pipeline. A pending ordinary decision consumes the opportunity where compatible instead of spawning a second one. New ordinary changes wait for the next opportunity; urgent in-flight handling retains the existing explicitly bounded refresh contract, not a new retry allowance.

The world's [timing profile](../worlds/base/attention.md#semantic-opportunities) targets a few real seconds under admitted load. The server uses monotonic host time for rate/deadline enforcement; domain expiry and attention stability use simulation time. The current director's two-pipeline/one-ordinary limit cannot qualify hundreds of simultaneous novel decisions. Make throughput/concurrency configurable through that same owner and its existing shared accounting, coordinated with CTR; do not add another scheduler or let each world multiply provider capacity. Finite per-actor/world/provider credits, age-based fair service within priority classes and reserved urgent capacity govern dispatch. New changes do not reset an older eligible actor's queue age. When shared capacity is exhausted, retain one current opportunity, not old packets or catch-up calls.

A dispatched request deadline covers preparation/provider/publication; queue age is recorded separately and counts toward end-to-end responsiveness. Pending opportunities retain only the latest watermark, so dispatch prepares current evidence rather than processing a backlog of old packets. A dispatched failure is not automatically retried. A new opportunity requires new meaning or a scheduled fair discovery turn, not the same failed cue looping forever. A queued optional opportunity owns no retention pin. Preparation and dispatched consumption use finite real-time leases; expiry cancels that consumption without marking evidence considered. Release captured pins on completion/deadline. Native obligations retain their separate protection until resolution. Revalidate actor life, timeline, privacy, exact captured sources and relevant action/knowledge dependencies before publication. Historical interpretation can remain historical after motion; acting on an unavailable target still fails current admission. Model failure leaves interpretation unresolved while native behavior and recent capture continue.

A few-second target is not guaranteed recognition of every unfamiliar danger. It must be measured as eligible-to-useful-interpretation latency, including preparation, tools, model response and publication. Report semantic mistakes separately from overload, unavailable service and incomplete sensory coverage. Existing-thinking-only mode remains a valid cheaper configuration with explicitly slower discovery. Embeddings are optional ranking aids on stable descriptions, not a prerequisite, a truth oracle or a reflection-time object whitelist.

### 5.6 One recording boundary and lasting retention

Every automatic path calls the existing `experience.ts` admission owner: ordinary/batched awareness, ended or replaced sight/touch facets, own/private action results, inspection/learning and cognition-selected retention. The policy sees exact source family, admitted consequences and consumer obligations; a caller-supplied importance or “final confirmation” flag cannot grant persistence. Exhaustive supported-family handling has no default durable insertion. The present `finalizeLease` → `retainObservationConfirmation` bypass remains an implementation defect to fix. Preserve current receipt regardless of autobiographical admission.

Use three existing-owner treatments after recent coverage:

| Treatment               | Meaning and support                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Exact necessary support | Minimum received facts needed by a current knowledge grant, recognition/name/place record, accepted work or an exact-language obligation; keep exact revisions and dependencies.                                  |
| Remembered impression   | A selected less precise recollection or interpretation, carrying its experienced interval, retained claims, uncertainty, attribution and declared missing detail. It is not an exact summary or mechanical grant. |
| Expiry                  | Routine optional experience and unsupported derivatives actually leave actor-accessible storage, search and caches. No mandatory gist of every attended interval.                                                 |

Existing exact summaries retain their exact manifests; they cannot silently become lossy. Add an explicit remembered-impression representation to the same experience union. It stores bounded structured claims from supported source families plus optional interpreted prose and provenance. Native admission can retain source-backed consequences and learning outcomes without a model; optional semantic retention uses already admitted cognition/reflection. Model unavailability does not postpone every expiry or create a consolidation debt. The native fallback preserves only justified facts, not invented activity labels.

The world retention policy ranks optional records by installed significance class, deliberate retention, capped reinforcement and age, with stable source-order ties. Do not let repeated passive sightings reinforce themselves. Update only touched priority entries; use an indexed due/eviction queue, not a full corpus scan each tick. On pressure, expire the lowest eligible optional records. Required live obligations remain protected through their own finite admission. Reserve their necessary exact support before accepting new voluntary work. The ordinary aftermath age is a preferred horizon once pending consumers release it: on pressure, first release expired consumption leases, then replace a warranted result with its admitted smaller account or expire optional aftermath. Do not wait for game-age expiry while holding the clock. Bound optional in-flight pins by the existing real-time request deadline. If an unavoidable native batch still cannot fit required durable recording, the existing journal admission holds that batch before installation, preserving the previous committed world and reporting a technical capacity failure; it never commits damage without its required account or invents an unlimited spill archive. A configuration whose active obligations leave no supported batch headroom fails admission/qualification, rather than producing a self-resolving fictional-memory promise. A chosen important memory is not an unlimited permanent pin.

A remembered impression may retain a useful kitchen visit while losing coordinates and minor intermediate movements. If no consequence, later relevance or choice warrants it, the ordinary visit can disappear. Exact recent history remains independent of whether an interpreter happened to name an episode. Further fading replaces the record's claims **and its text/embedding**; removing numerical support while leaving the same precise prose is not forgetting. Each supported structured source family supplies its explicit weaker projection through its existing world adapter; there is no engine catalogue of activities. Arbitrary interpretation prose is one indivisible fallible claim: native fading can drop it, not safely edit selected facts out of it. Optional semantic rewriting receives only surviving inputs, so lack of a model never leaves expired precise prose in place. A fallible retelling may elaborate only as interpretation, never recovered quotation or exact evidence.

For retained knowledge, copy only the exact qualifying fact capsule and necessary alias/dependency support, not an entire journal or summary tree. Correction still reaches that capsule. Native grants and active obligations retain their exact owners; a model cannot delete them to meet an optional-memory quota. Optional new knowledge/profile updates that cannot fit their complete admitted support are declined without erasing received evidence or prior knowledge. Growth in deliberately retained capabilities/active obligations is reported separately from bounded routine autobiography.

### 5.7 Correction, actual deletion and restoration

Exact protected records retain exact dependency indexes. Optional impressions may use bounded conservative provenance spans to permit deletion of their detailed inputs: an actor-life receipt epoch/ordinal range partitioned by source family, including retired intermediate interpretations and acquired transmission dependencies. Assign a stable locator to each exact original source revision; later changes carry their own receipt address and the original/dependency locators. Include both when deriving impression provenance, so correcting an older shared origin still finds later-derived impressions. The ordinal is a storage address, not a substitute for original occurrence/acquisition ordering. Keep exact available source references alongside the address when needed. Flatten predecessor provenance when replacing impressions; do not retain a chain of every earlier prose version.

Current-format source-detail/correction callers carry the exact source identity/revision and its issued receipt locator. Validate actor scope and authentic server issuance before using a locator; an opaque old ID alone cannot be resolved after its body and mappings are gone. Do not preserve an everlasting ID-to-ordinal map merely to support that lookup. A range hit invalidates an optional impression conservatively as unsupported; it does not declare all covered events false or invalidate a protected grant by approximation. Exact support follows exact dependencies. If the bounded provenance cannot represent a new impression without excessive unrelated coverage, retain fewer optional claims or decline it; do not merge arbitrary decades into one misleading range.

All retained derivatives, source versions, embeddings, anonymous handles, note tombstones and indexes count toward their owning allowance. Maintain a retained-reference count/ownership edge only while a live record needs it. Expiring a chunk first extracts required sparse support or completes its admitted impression, then removes unneeded source bodies, retired versions and artifacts atomically. An exact summary still requiring the detail must either retain its charged closure, undergo an explicitly admitted weakening to an impression, or expire; it cannot keep an uncharged archive. Physically delete eligible SQL rows through bounded maintenance. Hot eviction alone is not deletion. Deleting an empty knowledge/name record also advances its owner-level monotonic revision/generation. Extend existing `world-record-schema`/`world-records`, `history-residency`, `memory-repository`, `experience-manifest`, `history-edit` and cognition maintenance. An indexed `(nextDue, actorLife)` queue selects actors, and `(expiry, receiptOrdinal, id)` seek cursors select bounded work. Batch independent writes in the existing world transaction. Provider work never runs inside that transaction; staged promotion rechecks sources before atomic publication/deletion. Shared payload reclamation waits for all entitled owners to release it.

Cold recall queries retained actor-scoped source metadata before hydration. Temporal/subject/required searches use indexed predicates and bounded batches. With a finite optional corpus, exact vector comparison is a bounded fallback; its cost is still proportional to that corpus. If the retained population warrants approximate vector indexing, scope the candidate search to the actor and validate its query plan/recall separately; no unqualified ANN claim or global scan followed by permission filtering is accepted. Required exact evidence bypasses similarity ranking. Embedding generations and pending indexing jobs expire with their sources.

Restore preserves original ages, impressions, exact support and coverage, rebuilds derived indexes and invalidates old external jobs. New appends use a fresh receipt epoch without relabeling retained old addresses. Privacy forgetting remains outside fictional rewind: sanitize or disable affected retained snapshots through save/privacy owners, then retire completed erasure bookkeeping. Arbitrary selective erasure across indefinitely retained unsanitized backups cannot use a constant-size ledger. Creator audit, world events, retired actors, save copies and paid-accounting retention are separate policies and costs, not a hidden character-memory fallback. No legacy format or compatibility reader is introduced.

## 6. Authority, lifecycle and durable boundaries

### 6.1 Complete-operation checks

Speech participation does not grant editing rights, material-use permission, recipe knowledge, physical access or consent to another operation. The command owner checks the complete current operation, exact resources, relevant grants, tool/body feasibility and actual destination. Visibility of an item does not authorize taking it. A native preview remains read-only and cannot reserve another person's agreement.

Preparation may retain exact immutable facts across an awaited read/provider call. At commit, recheck affected dependencies and live authority rather than rejecting on any unrelated world revision. Relevant changes include exact offer/outing revision, withdrawal, changed item/custody, actor life/participation, target placement, current action ownership and timeline. An interjection, changed addressee or ended exchange alone does not cancel a separately accepted physical task.

### 6.2 Leaving, stopping and loss

Stopping speech or hiding the composer/Events reader changes no ordinary social membership. Leaving an outing updates its exact consent/work relation through the outing owner. Stopping native work follows the existing plan/cancellation contract. Those deliberate native operations remain separate from changing a reading view. One person cannot stop another's independent work merely by losing focus.

Death, retirement, incapacity, actual player departure or replaced work reconcile the relationships they invalidate through their existing owners. Cleanup uses participant-owned indexes and the exact old references, never a blanket world scan per departing person. Already launched effects follow their own lifetime. Completed movement, ammunition, damage, transfers and products are retained, with source-linked history where applicable.

Consent withdrawal remains possible through its existing permitted paused controls. Paused time, storage/maintenance holds and absent-player protection keep their current authority. The design does not invent a shared-world pause or treat a provider delay as fictional departure.

### 6.3 Save, load and stale external work

Persist exact speech/source relationships, outing terms and consent, actual plan/action progress, item state and personal evidence through the existing complete world cut. Ordinary conversation intervals are not retained by the SPK target. Validate both owner-to-action and action-to-owner references. Historical participants may outlive their live bodies under the history owner's rules; active required references cannot silently disappear.

Restore identities before links and rebuild only derived indexes. Reopening does not emit new speech, award acquaintance, accept an outing, replay a transfer or rerun a summary/provider call. The exact attended read-composition slice needs no new stored scene format. The compact slice extends existing current-format source/evidence records as §6.4 specifies; neither introduces a compatibility adapter.

World/timeline/authority fences invalidate abandoned requests even if restored IDs recur. An already committed result remains committed; a stale result cannot invent later consent. Uncertain provider work remains accounted for and has no automatic paid retry. Revocation/forgetting applies to dependent shared views and summaries as well as source rows. Keep current privacy overlays and external spending outside gameplay rewind.

### 6.4 Compact source dependencies, storage and recovery

Update current producer/schema, source storage, observer projection, linguistic-evidence selection, conversation context, recall/embedding text, history search/source detail and protocol/UI readers together. Existing exact-only checks such as server `speech-recall.ts` must recognize admitted meaning as communicated linguistic evidence without treating indistinct cues as words. Current conversation compaction remains derived management of personally received sources; it must preserve each exact/meaning discriminator and may not turn an approximate account into a quotation. Shared rendering attributes speaker, recipient and observer correctly instead of reparsing free text into identities or verified facts.

Retain exact source identity/revision, recipient evidence identity/revision, representation, permitted references and installed source policy through the existing event/experience storage boundary. A psychological reaction uses the recipient's existing cause-linked owner and its recorded outcome; receiving the same source through intake, history reload or replay cannot apply it twice. A new independently spoken contribution remains a distinct cause. Recheck the recipient's private evidence and psychological dependencies after awaits. Source correction, forgetting, access loss and changed authority reach dependent interpretation, summaries, embeddings and cached reads through their existing dependency owners; the linked psychological contract determines any stored feeling's own correction/forgetting behavior. No retained compact account bypasses revoked evidence.

Save captures completed sources, actual listener evidence, admitted personal changes and exact native effects in one coherent cut. Unfinished interpretation or an unsent reply remains unfinished. Current-format validation checks the selected representation's required fields and reference relationships, and refuses contradictory exact/meaning data before installation. Restore rebuilds derived selectors without generating missing words, reapplying a feeling, answering again or resubmitting abandoned paid work. Deduplication and late-callback fences include world/timeline, exact actor/request/source identity and relevant revision; concatenate no ambiguous IDs. Ordinary save behavior remains exact, and no legacy reader or second scene save format is introduced.

## 7. Performance and extension boundaries

### 7.1 Growth, shared preparation and invalidation

The growing dimensions are actual speech/listener pairs, overlapping invitations reaching one actor, each actor's relevant actions and retained permitted history. A three-person initial encounter does not bound a settlement or a highly connected host. Final visible-row limits do not bound a full history scan.

Prepare the current viewer's eligible source metadata and owned outing/offer records once per compatible request snapshot. Use existing source/participant indexes; select eligible speech/evidence IDs before indexed bounded hydration. Reuse common physical object/geometry facts only where permissions and snapshot agree. Do not generate each spectator's description before deciding whether they need individual interpretation. Current exact speech still establishes every eligible listener's evidence through its owner; avoiding unnecessary decisions never authorizes dropping listeners to meet a display or cost allowance.

Keep any optional shared-reading selector derived and read-only. Cache keys must include world/timeline, observer/disclosure scope, exact selected source revisions and actual depended-on geometry/action/item facts. Source edits, privacy changes, changed selected coverage, target retirement, current work replacement and restore invalidate the affected derived result. A mutable world without proven revision coverage uses a fresh read rather than an unsound cache.

No new unlimited scene-retention collection is created. Existing source/history pagination, retention and cold storage remain their owners. Readability summaries cannot become permanent unauthorized copies after source revocation. Unsupported or exhausted preparation reports incomplete/unavailable; it does not assert that nobody spoke or that a companion declined.

For the redesign, use these dimensions: `A` eligible observers, `C` local source candidates per observer, `D` changed received sources, `K` detailed subjects, `B` visited scene-index nodes, `Q` geometric candidate visits, and `R` retained bytes. Physical work can approach `A × C` in a dense mutually visible population; attention does not eliminate it. Reuse installed geometry, affected-observer selection and shared immutable payloads when exact scope/values agree. Do not copy every complete actor or facet tree merely to update a location. Selection/index work is incremental in `D`, with bounded `B/Q` per actual cognition opportunity; it is independent of lifetime history. Dense first exposure and static geometry fragmentation remain real preparation costs.

Scene hierarchy updates touch changed leaves/ancestors; aggregate counters and child bounds have fixed-size updates. No whole-tree rebuild/sort per frame, all-pairs relation graph, full history scan or per-coordinate embedding is selected. Derive current-source grouping from existing spatial partitions and keep it disposable. Share exact payloads across equal observation cuts when legal, while retaining individual acquisition/uncertainty/identity; unproven deduplication ratios cannot justify an allowance.

Let `W` be a retention horizon in game seconds, `r` admitted captured changes per game second and `b` complete encoded bytes per change. Required storage is approximately `W × r × b`, including support. A byte cap gives actual coverage no greater than `cap / (r × b)` under a sustained uniform rate. Separate broad-buffer and selected-detail rates. Convert them to real throughput with the actual world clock and simulation speed, not an invented sampling cadence. Example only: copying 300 observed moving bodies × 20 received updates per real second × 64 bytes would cost 384,000 bytes/second **per observer**, or about 115 MB/second for 300 observers before overhead. A storage ring alone would not fix that write rate. The recommended broad sampler instead admits at most 1 KiB/16 source visits per actor per 15 game seconds, about 1.2 MiB/second of payload for 300 actors at the normal clock, before current-state persistence, exact attended/required capture and storage overhead. Acceleration multiplies its real-time rate. The actual source rate, payload size and total caller work still require measurement; no deduplication or compression gain is presumed.

The profile's logical allowances sum to at most 25.125 MiB per actor before unrelated knowledge/obligations, database/index amplification and checkpoints: about 7.4 GiB for 300 fully occupied actors. This is an upper accounting example, not an allocation per empty actor or acceptable measured disk budget. A two-chunk recent tail is 128 KiB per actor, about 37.5 MiB for 300, plus current scene indexes, shared read cache and other game state. Do not hydrate logical quotas into RAM. Chunking and shared writes reduce overhead; they do not eliminate genuine observation bytes. Qualification may justify revising reversible world values or source fidelity through its proper owner, never silently weakening exact recent receipt.

Semantic demand is separate: `dirty actors / review interval × calls per completed turn`. With 300 actors each requiring a novel private turn every three seconds, demand is 100 turns/second. At an illustrative 4,000 input tokens per turn, that is 400,000 input tokens/second before outputs, additional tool rounds or existing routing. Required concurrency is approximately admitted turns/second × mean service time, with extra capacity needed for latency tails. No model/SKU/price is assumed. Avoid calls for unchanged/known native work, coalesce changes and reuse an already-needed turn; do not merge private interpretations merely because actors share a scene. Report the native population, fraction needing semantic work, queue/model latency and funded rate together. The current two-pipeline director is not evidence for this target.

Ten game years can add no more routine optional autobiography than the retained quotas, but deliberately retained knowledge, active obligations and external histories have their own growth. Prune source closure, handles, retired versions, embeddings, request pins and expired snapshots through their owners. Count SQL rows, bytes, index entries, maintenance lag and write amplification, not just visible diary entries. Native/server/storage/AI/browser acceptance remain distinct.

### 7.2 Complete cost and failure accounting

Measure source admission, eligible listener work, required evidence persistence, per-actor context/routing/selection/generation, summary/recall/reflection if used, command validation/commit and browser update/readability. Count the optional decisions actually avoided as well as calls dispatched, stale results, queue time, bytes and retained uncertain spending. Shared context construction is a saving only if complete caller cost falls without lost evidence.

Compare matched exact encounters before adding abstraction. Measure cold opening/reconstruction and warm refresh separately; then vary independent people, one highly invited person, history length, irrelevant objects and bursty arrivals/interjections independently. Count visited sources/invitations and hydrated bodies as well as median/tail timings. An isolated selector or one warm three-person interaction cannot qualify the later seven-body/crowd offer.

For compact background work, admit one actor's contribution and only the independently justified receiving work through the existing finite decision, context, output, concurrency and spending owners. Source receipt is evidence for intake, not a recursive call to generate the other side. Every further voluntary reply needs current admission; repeated equivalent evidence can be coalesced without deleting distinct speech. No fixed turn timer, automatic ping-pong, quota to consume or paid restart at silence is introduced. Before dispatch, include any invited recipient decision/reaction in the complete admitted work and reserve through the existing accounting owner; a source cannot impose an unfunded unlimited continuation. Bystanders retain their actual evidence without a mandatory model call. Numerical personal reaction selection may share that recipient's already needed decision when its input and authority match; reuse cannot merge separate minds or let one participant pay for another without authority.

The complete comparison includes source generation, relevant route/selection, awaited recipient interpretation, optional feeling selection/admission, optional reply, audience processing, persistence, summaries, embeddings, later recall, stale results and uncertain reservations. Shorter output alone proves neither lower complete cost nor better characterization. Required failures preserve committed meaning and real native effects; unavailable optional interpretation leaves the matter unresolved. No automatic paid retry, model downgrade or invented social refusal hides failure. Repeated attention changes do not repeat reaction settlement.

The selected world foreground capacity is a configurable gameplay limit, not measured population capacity or a universal attention law. The profile's attendance/work allowances and attention defaults remain authoring hypotheses; no cost saving is claimed. [PS limits](../limits/product-scalability.md) and the [intelligent-simulation inventory](../limits/intelligent-simulation.md) retain the distinction between exact evidence obligations, current budgets and unmeasured scale.

### 7.3 Selected family and conditional expansions

Compact expressive meaning is now selected and technically specified in §§3.4–3.6/6.4. This replaces its earlier design gate; runtime implementation and qualification remain open. The exact attended slice still finishes independently. Keep ongoing aggregate vocal activity, timed speech, broader joint work, crowds and unattended operation under their separate existing gates. Compact source form authorizes none of their clocks, acoustic masking, collective choices or new physical effects.

Implement the selected meaning representation through the existing source/hearing, memory, actor-decision and conversation owners together. Keep its selected observation/hearing policy in the world-owned profile. A materially different supported world could require exact speech throughout while reusing the same source discriminant, or exchange nonverbal meaning only through an actually supported sense; changing a string does not install that sense. No speculative scheduler, loader or scene service is required.

Reuse AS-J15–19/23 and applicable AS-J21–22 from the feature spec through ordinary human/NPC surfaces, applying the compact-specific cases below only to the selected background slice. Existing evidence can qualify an unchanged owner, but a fixture returning the desired answer cannot establish independent choice or player enjoyment.

Design stages follow the owning tracker rather than a second project backlog:

**PS04-A delivery sequence:** (1) common recording admission, automatic recent chunks and existing-channel consumers; (2) received geometry plus bounded broad scene/detail context and cold retrieval; (3) existing-director semantic opportunities with exact acknowledgment and complete cost; (4) selective remembered impressions, real source-closure expiry, correction/restore and ten-year retained-data qualification. Each step is an end-to-end increment, but the redesign is not complete before all four and §14 pass. Geometry-dependent cases remain unaccepted until the physical producer exists. The full vision/acoustic programs, ATI physical watch/investigation, attention/combat integration and complete PP07/SAI-A acceptance stay open. Compact expressive meaning, semantic crowds and timed speech remain separate.

1. **PS04 exact contribution:** compare existing one-resident play and fix the concrete reading/participation problem. Do not build group infrastructure merely because a third person exists elsewhere.
2. **PS04/DG18 small shared encounter:** complete the ordinary exact journey, explicit audience and independent supported acceptance/withdrawal, plus lifecycle/privacy checks above. Compose CE/NC/HE/AE owners; implement no second mind or scheduler.
3. **CE/PS qualification:** observe a whole worthwhile episode and a later return, with full costs and legitimate contrary outcomes. Engineering correctness and uncoached usefulness are separately reported.
4. **PS04 compact background slice:** implement the selected source/evidence representation, independently awaited recipient interpretation and optional psychological operation/reply; qualify source privacy, expressive nuance, native terms, human-arrival races, replay and current-format recovery. This is open runtime work, not delivered by the documents.
5. **Conditional PS04 expansion:** select and qualify ongoing vocal, timed, crowded or broader-work families separately. Earlier slice completion does not close this later scope.
