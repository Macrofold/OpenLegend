### Why these, and what remains valuable

The current first-playable already designs empty-handed survival, finite known manufacture, the first useful invention, defensible natural camps, four differentiated people, voluntary cooperation, animal roaming and independently chosen human travel. Repeating those topics would not add a new design. Its runtime and integrated play evidence remain open.

The reviewed catalogues consistently favor useful discoveries, alternate methods, practical creation, particular companions and rivals, meaningful opposition and lasting victories at Complete. Detailed maintenance, broad administration and service operations usually follow those experiences. A thin result-and-reward opportunity can still be Core: INS-273 explicitly proposes a few discoverable commissions with concrete verifiable results. Include a useful local instance only where its actual fulfillment and payment rules serve the expedition; this does not select the broader durable-agreements project as a prerequisite.

Magic traditions, trained animals, richer rescue, advanced combat, stronger progression and larger social institutions remain worthwhile candidates. Their relevance depends on a selected playable consumer and their real missing prerequisites. No catalogue is demoted merely because it is outside these three designs; no entry is marked implemented because it now has a technical design. The review preserves the breadth of modern, science-fiction, magical and unusual authored worlds.
