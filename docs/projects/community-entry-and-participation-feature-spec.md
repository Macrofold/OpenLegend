# Find a community, begin playing, and participate safely

| Status      | Current progress                                                                                                                                                 | Last updated |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| In progress | The researched product proposal and game-first review are complete; adoption, technical design, public-operation approval and runtime qualification remain open. | 2026-10-04   |

## Purpose and recommendation

A person finds a place that sounds enjoyable, meets a human who is ready to welcome them, does something worthwhile, and can return as the same character. They should not have to understand world administration before they can explore a trail, cook a meal or learn why the town needs a bridge. When something goes wrong, they can stop unwanted contact and reach support without persuading a fictional official to help.

This is the product proposal for **DG08, ND02 and ND37**. Start with **a curated directory and managed adult entry into one operated community**, using explicit account approval and the existing invitation/character authority. Select an actual supported activity for the listing. Do not advertise instant open enrollment, unrestricted spectating, cross-world character travel or unlimited space. The [gameplay priorities](../repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md) require that the invitation leads to a good game.

Public discovery is a new offer; this specification does not publish it. [D68](../../archive/05-project/open-decisions.md#d68--world-entry-beyond-invites), the [production release gates](../maintainers/production-deployment.md), the selected audience policy and actual operating budget need owner adoption before that offer can run. The adult pilot recommendation does not adopt the archive's optional well-being program.

### The smallest enjoyable service

The first listing describes a small survival-adventure community: a safe starting camp, a nearby useful outing, optional wildlife danger when that encounter is qualified, and people who can answer ordinary questions. The welcome offers exploration and a concrete choice before administration. A newcomer can decline conversation or danger and still do something useful.

Begin with one community, one advertised welcome at a time, and a proposed qualification profile of two simultaneously embodied humans, including the host. This is a deliberate pilot envelope, not a revision of the project's larger capacity targets. The host may be a player; support review is a separately authorized role and must not require controlling a resident. A host's ability to welcome someone is not permission to read their private notes.

Visitors receive ordinary continuing character access when admitted. “Visit” describes their social intention, not a disposable trial character. “Make this my home” privately puts the community first in their return choices; it does not grant land, authority, a new character or a reserved server slot. A person who tries the world and never presses Stay still retains their existing permitted return path.

## Foundations and decisions this adds

The [multiplayer tracker](../maintainers/multiplayer.md) records real account authentication, one account/character binding per world, single-controller authority, expiring single-use invites, characterless operations, protected absence and coherent return. A current invite binds an already existing living person who has never had a human owner. It is a bearer link; its descriptive label does not bind it to an approved applicant. A current invite also cannot redeem into an account that already has a world grant, including a characterless or revoked grant.

Therefore this proposal adds a **recipient-bound approval for the publicly requested visit**. The verified signed-in account that applied must be the account that redeems its approved invitation. Signing in as another account offers Change account without enrolling anyone or consuming the approved place. The existing private invitation flow remains its own current contract; the public listing may not silently fall back to a shareable link that bypasses approval.

Use a freshly prepared, unplayed starter person with authored starting content. The operator checks that the person is available and the applicant accepts a short, truthful character introduction before binding. Do not reassign Ada, take over a socially established resident, recycle an abandoned human character or fabricate a history of friendship with the newcomers. Automatic character creation at redemption and promotion from a characterless grant remain separate D68 capabilities.

Prepare the newcomer close to the actual agreed welcome, not as a stock of frozen residents waiting for applicants. Ordinary world time and autonomous choice still apply while an offer is pending. Immediately before binding, recheck the person's actual condition, possessions, responsibilities and brief history against the accepted introduction. A material change requires a refreshed offer and fresh assent; a dead, previously human-owned or now socially established person is no longer this starter offer. Explain the failed offer and arrange another genuine opportunity rather than erase history or silently substitute a body. Keep at most one unclaimed prepared starter for this first community; after an offer lapses, that person remains an actual world inhabitant under ordinary rules. Preparing another needs an explicit operator/content decision within the world's funded population, not automatic replenishment.

The [worlds and belonging direction](../product-scalability/worlds-and-belonging.md) protects community identity and accumulated history. This offer is local entry, not federation: selecting a different community means a different local character under its own rules. It imports no possessions, secret memories, privileges or fictional acquaintances. The directory makes that consequence visible before an application.

## Discovery and an honest listing

Browsing the approved public directory does not require a world grant. A listing contains only deliberately published information:

- The community's name, short description, authored preview image, language, supported devices/input and a concrete example of what a first visit involves.
- The official audience, content/risk summary, participation rules, who operates it, how support is reached, and whether the world is currently open, full, paused, under maintenance or accepting requests for later.
- The next actual welcome opportunity, shown with date, the viewer's local time and the host's named time zone. An estimate is labeled an estimate. The directory does not imply people are awake around the clock.
- The qualified play profile, current access arrangement, disclosed pause/absence and retention policies, any real charge before commitment, and whether native play or AI conversation is available.
- A clear **Request a visit**, **My request**, **Return** or **Requests paused** action matching the current account's actual state.

No public member directory, exact live headcount in a tiny community, resident whereabouts, private activity feed or named spectator camera is needed. “A welcome is available” can be deliberately published by the host; it is not an inferred disclosure of someone else's session. Preview images require the depicted participants' publication permission or use an authored empty scene.

Filters initially cover language, activity and compatible welcome time. Results are an understandable editorial selection, ordered by relevant available opportunities with ordinary rotation among equally suitable listings. Explain why a result appears. Do not score a person's loneliness, personality, willingness to spend or private conversations. A blocked host or known excluded account is not recommended for direct contact; do not reveal which private restriction removed an option.

With one community, show one honest choice. If nothing fits, offer its next published opportunity or let the person leave. Do not populate the page with empty worlds or generate new societies to make search results appear abundant. Offer another community only after there is a useful distinct activity, an accountable operator and a separately funded qualified place to play. A full camp is not authorization to copy its residents and history.

Discord distinguishes discoverability from community status and admission, while Final Fantasy XIV's Community Finder presents finding, contacting and joining as separate steps. Those are useful precedents for a discoverable but managed world. They do not establish that an Open Legend directory will attract or retain people. [R1], [R4]

## Request, approval and first arrival

### A request should take less effort than deciding to play

After sign-in, show the chosen community, its audience/risk summary and the actual opportunity. Ask only for confirmation of the selected audience, a chosen available time or “another time,” and an optional short note about the desired activity or access needs. No public biography, emotional questionnaire, personality test or required explanation of why the applicant deserves entry.

The applicant can submit, withdraw or edit their time before approval. Status is Received, Needs your answer, Approved, Declined, Expired or Withdrawn. Display the next review time and whether the operator is accepting work; do not promise an immediate answer from an unattended queue. An additional question is a bounded service exchange, not an invitation for arbitrary direct messages from community members.

The reviewer sees the necessary request, the applicant's chosen service display name, eligibility result and relevant service restrictions. They do not see private game conversations, relationships, payment history unrelated to this admission or other-world character aliases. Approval requires an available starter person, welcome capacity and authority to issue the corresponding ordinary play grant. Declining for capacity or fit is distinct from finding misconduct.

Discord's application flow provides pending, withdrawal and review states without exposing the server while a request is pending. Its optional interviews and privileged bypass invitations are not copied wholesale: the first Open Legend request has no required interview, and public approval cannot be bypassed by an ordinary shareable link. [R2]

### Approval, sign-in and taking control

Approval identifies this account, this world, the specific starter person and the current offer. Before redemption, show the person's name, starting situation, starter possessions and what naming/appearance choices are actually supported. The initial choice need not create a generated biography. An applicant can refuse the character and withdraw without turning an established NPC into a reserve account.

The player accepts the community rules and current material access/retention terms once. A changed audience, charge, risk or access promise requires seeing the change before redemption. Minor listing copy edits do not reset consent. Invite expiry ends only the unused offer; it does not impose an undisclosed expiry on an enrolled player's access.

A still-valid token is not a still-valid welcome. Before first redemption, recheck the agreed welcome window, host availability, starter offer and actual admission capacity. If the time passed, the host cancelled or the reserved opportunity disappeared, keep the applicant outside play and refresh/rebook the offer with their assent. Do not silently admit them to an unattended first visit. The request retains its acknowledged place for rebooking until withdrawn or expired under its disclosed offer; token validity never outranks a current controller. Once enrolled, ordinary return access is independent of that first welcome booking.

On redemption, grant creation and character binding succeed together or neither succeeds. A repeated click, network timeout or return after sign-in recovers the actual outcome. If already enrolled, offer Return to the existing character. If the account has a characterless or revoked grant, explain that operator review is required; do not pretend a second invitation can promote or restore it.

Arrival uses the current multiplayer control and safe-return owners. Sole-tab entry remains automatic; only switching between already open game tabs uses Resume Here. Full capacity, maintenance, unsafe arrival or unavailable storage produces an explicit wait/unavailable state outside physical play. It does not drop the person into danger or give them unauthorized spectator information.

The host's first job is to make the place legible: “We can explore the ridge or make a useful tool; which sounds better?” Offer one immediate ordinary action, nearby supplies whose ownership is clear, and the Leave and Help controls. A skippable compact orientation explains human/AI markers, movement/action feedback and personal contact controls. Do not force a tour, rules quiz, promise to attend again, NPC conversation or tutorial fight. Discord's onboarding guidance supports a few useful defaults and optional later customization; it is a design lesson, not proof of conversion. [R3]

## Belonging, return and capacity

The first visit can end whenever the player chooses. There is no attendance streak, contribution debt or public announcement that they rejected the community. After an enjoyable moment, offer the optional private home preference once; do not ask repeatedly after refusal. A return screen shows this person's actual character, permitted belongings/unfinished work, known changes with their sources, and current entry availability. Public news is testimony, not a memory of events the character missed.

The pilot's published continuity window and any end-of-pilot plan must be approved before admission. Until the owner selects a production retention/save policy, this document cannot promise permanent hosted custody, exports, automatic reclamation or deletion after inactivity. A quiet world is not silently merged or reset. A removed listing does not itself revoke enrolled grants; maintenance, service closure and individual exclusion are distinct notices.

Capacity must preserve the current controller before admitting another. A brief reconnect consumes its existing protection/headroom under the applicable participation policy; it does not become a new application. A scheduled welcome occupies only the explicitly reserved, qualified opportunity, never somebody else's live body slot. Do not overbook a host and newcomer on the assumption residents will leave.

For the first offering, serve existing enrolled players waiting to return before starting another unscheduled visitor. Within the same category, use the acknowledged arrival order. Keep the waiting person outside simulation with Cancel and a truthful availability state; an absent person accrues no new survival harm merely by waiting. A scheduled visitor displaced by a genuine incident gets a new offer, not a fabricated queue estimate. A queue within a promised available slot is a service failure to record, not a capacity success.

Waiting does not require a chat room, avatar, live spectator feed or AI host. No automatic eviction of a quiet but actively participating resident is proposed. Measure recurring overcrowding and change the actual service offer before opening more invitations. A host booking is not entitlement to make another player stop playing.

## Personal controls during play

Keep **Mute**, **Block contact**, **Leave world** and **Report** available from an encountered person's menu and relevant visible message/event. A compact Help menu remains usable when the target has left, changed display name or lost a visible body. Keyboard access, clear labels and confirmation only for consequential scope changes matter more than ornamental moderation panels.

| Control       | Immediate behavior                                                                                                                                                                           | What it does not imply                                                                                                             |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mute          | Hide future speech/caption presentation from that human service account for this user until undone. Existing unwanted messages can be collapsed without replay.                              | No world ban, new immunity, erased event or compulsory report.                                                                     |
| Block contact | Apply Mute; refuse new direct offers, invitations and contact in both directions within the managed service; withdraw this pair's pending direct interactions and exclude new introductions. | No invisible body, reversal of a settled exchange, automatic revocation of property access or disclosure of other account aliases. |
| Leave world   | Stop personal participation through the applicable bounded departure policy; keep support and report access outside the world.                                                               | No report requirement, punishment of another person or erasure of ordinary aftermath.                                              |
| Report        | Submit selected permitted evidence for separately authorized human review.                                                                                                                   | No automatic guilt, automatic ban or guarantee of an immediate response.                                                           |

For the selected managed service, Mute and Block follow the service account across its local characters without publishing the association. The controls do not claim enforcement on independent hosts, external chat or newly created unrelated accounts. The blocked person receives a generic unavailable interaction result, not the blocker's location, alternate names or private decision. Unblocking restores future contact eligibility; it does not resend suppressed messages or reopen withdrawn offers. If both people have blocked, either remaining block still applies.

Bodies remain visible enough to understand collision and actions. A muted person's speech text is hidden from ordinary captions and notifications, but an attack, dropped object or completed construction remains perceptible through ordinary world rules. Do not make a harasser's moving body invisible while it still blocks a doorway. Nor may a muted speaker's words be automatically repeated by the narrator as a way around the user's choice. Preserve necessary native action feedback without repeating the unwanted text.

The service can suppress attributable delivery; it cannot promise that another human will never quote something in a new message. Deliberately using another person, an NPC command, object naming or authored content to circumvent contact restrictions is reportable. Qualify the actually enabled direct-contact surfaces before offering Block. Do not invent general mind censorship or erase residents' legitimately acquired world knowledge.

A block and an exchange settling concurrently have one actual order. If settlement already happened, it remains. Otherwise blocking withdraws the pending exchange and no items transfer. Other outstanding obligations and previously granted object access retain their own owners. The user can separately change supported access; Block is not a hidden universal property permission editor.

VRChat explicitly documents that blocked users can still affect the shared world, illustrating why personal filtering and physical exclusion need separate promises. Final Fantasy XIV's later correction to account-wide blacklist data exposed a different risk: a protection feature must not reveal that multiple characters share an account. Keep the control scope broad enough to help while keeping that identity linkage private. [R5], [R6]

### Immediate relief and persistent interference

Leaving must be available before a report is completed. The player can open a report draft, leave, and finish from the service screen. A disconnected client may not receive immediate acknowledgement; reconnection recovers the real departure and report states. Do not tell them an effect committed when only their local screen changed.

The first-threat offering consumes its [bounded retreat and return proposal](first-threat-encounter-feature-spec.md#leaving-connection-loss-and-return) only after qualification. Outside that encounter, existing multiplayer absence and world rules still control. Blocking is not a new escape teleport or combat immunity toggle.

Because personal filtering cannot stop shared-world obstruction, an authorized operator can urgently remove a participant's ability to issue further commands, followed by the ordinary bounded departure of their body. **Current revocation immediately removes authority and future private disclosure; it does not immediately cancel all native work or erase a body.** The public offering needs an additional, narrowly scoped containment behavior: an operator's urgent participation restriction cancels that person's unfinished offensive or interfering work at its current committed boundary, preserving already spent inputs and actual outcomes. It does not let an accusation undo another player's action. If the selected surface cannot be contained, pause the affected activity/world through its actual maintenance authority and keep departure available.

An access manager, creator and support reviewer may be different people. The public release must name who can invoke containment and, if necessary, maintenance. A fictional guard, town vote or resident relationship score cannot supply this platform authority.

## A report that can actually be reviewed

Start from a message, visible event, person, listing or the Help menu. Allow a report about an unidentified actor using the event the reporter perceived; do not disclose the hidden identity merely to fill a form. Categories describe the problem in ordinary language: unwanted contact, targeted abuse, interference/exploit, inappropriate service content, privacy/account problem, or something else. A technical failure can be routed to support without inventing misconduct.

The reporter sees a preview of the evidence that will be submitted: selected available messages/events, a small permitted surrounding context window, relevant world/time identifiers, and their own explanation. They may omit optional context and are told when this makes the account incomplete. The reviewer may request a specific missing part. No entire conversation, character notebook, memory collection or world save attaches automatically.

For a private conversation, the reporting participant explicitly shares only the selected excerpt with separately authorized service reviewers. A nonparticipant cannot attach it through a search result, NPC memory or creator export. Any necessary adjacent private context requires the same scoped reporting permission or a separately approved, case-specific operational access decision; ordinary creator inspection never qualifies. Preserve source authorship, audience and relevant ordering, distinguish edited/redacted presentation from originals, and mark gaps. Do not use a reporter-written paraphrase as if it were a verbatim authenticated record.

If the evidence is no longer retained, accept the explanation and label the unavailable source. Never recover forgotten private content from an old save to make reporting easier. Existing memory forgetting and privacy restrictions remain effective; case evidence has its own explicitly disclosed operational retention and access rules. Reporting does not make private content world knowledge, narrator material, resident memory or a training example.

After submission, show a durable case reference, the submitted scope, current state and next review/update date. A retry after an uncertain response finds the same case. Users may add new evidence or correct their description without deleting the earlier record of what the reviewer acted on. Duplicate reports can be associated with one incident for workload purposes, but witnesses remain distinct and no majority vote establishes truth.

The signed-in support area remains reachable after leaving a world, loss of play rights or suspension. It exposes only this user's permitted cases and notices. For a lost login, provide the separately qualified account-recovery route, not an unauthenticated private transcript lookup. The reporting flow makes no promise of secret identity: reviewers restrict disclosure, but participants may infer who reported an interaction.

## Review, decisions and correction

A separately appointed service reviewer considers actual evidence, policy scope, relevant context and uncertainty. The same person may also create a world, but must use the explicit service role for case work. A host reviews ordinary entry requests; it does not receive private case contents by default. A complaint about the host or reviewer goes to the named backup. The first public offering requires both a responsible reviewer and an independent route for contested cases.

The initial service rules prohibit targeted real-person harassment, identity-based abuse, threats, sexual coercion or unwanted explicit material, private-information disclosure, impersonation of staff, account/invite abuse and deliberate circumvention of contact or participation restrictions. Good-faith disagreement, an unappealing roleplay style, refusal of a bargain and permitted fictional rivalry are not enough by themselves. Fictional violence remains subject to the world's advertised risk rules. A game bug that let a player harm a bystander needs containment and repair; a rulebook cannot substitute for a missing protection mechanism.

Available decisions are: no established violation; advice or a conduct notice; a specified contact restriction; temporary world participation suspension; world exclusion; or, by separately authorized platform staff, exclusion across the managed service. Serious supported harm may warrant immediate exclusion; the system does not promise a fixed number of warnings. Temporary precautionary containment is explicitly labeled pending review, with an expiry/review time. Do not turn a report count, model label or payment tier into a sanction.

Each affected person receives a private notice identifying the rule, enough of the incident to understand and contest it, the restriction's scope, start/end or review date, and how to request review. Redact unnecessary third-party content and do not disclose alternate accounts. The reporter receives receipt and outcome category with useful next steps, not another person's entire case or a promise of punishment. A decision can establish insufficient evidence without accusing the reporter of lying.

An appeal goes to a reviewer who did not make the contested decision and is not a subject of the complaint. It can dispute identity, evidence, context, scope or proportionality. A temporarily restricted user can still appeal; they need not regain game access first. Corrections remove unsupported restrictions and record the reason. Restore the same eligible grant/character through authorized access management, with fresh control and current capacity/safe-return checks; an old invite cannot restore a revoked grant.

Do not rewind the world, fabricate an apology from an NPC, restore already consumed resources automatically or erase other participants' memories to compensate for a moderation error. Explain what the continuing world changed. Any concrete restitution needs its own authorized, nonduplicating game operation and visible record. Reversing a service sanction does not undo another person's voluntary block.

## Evidence lifetime and service responsibility

Case material is private operational data, with the separately adopted reporting basis and retention notice shown before submission. The proposed first policy keeps selected evidence until 90 real days after final closure. Closure means the decision notice date, replaced by the final timely appeal decision notice if an appeal occurs; it does not add the appeal window to an unappealed case. Appeals may be requested for 30 real days after notice; new material showing a serious identity or authority error can still trigger staff correction later without promising that old evidence survives. The restriction's minimal account/scope/reason/date record remains while it must be enforced, separately from the more sensitive excerpt. These are proposed service choices in [CP04](../limits/community-participation.md#cp04--case-lifetime-and-review), not changes to general game-history or account retention.

An open case receives an identified owner and a review date. At 30 days unresolved, escalate to the backup and tell the affected person what is missing. Continuing an evidence hold needs a specific reason and next date; the first offering allows no open-ended “investigation” archive. A case still unresolved at the proposed 90-day open-case boundary needs an explicit supported decision on the available evidence, or the applicable authorized exceptional retention process. That exception cannot be invented by a world creator or an automated job.

A valid privacy correction or deletion request is handled by the existing privacy owner and adopted service policy. Show when evidence became unavailable or redacted and reassess conclusions that depended on it. Remove derived case summaries, search fragments and cached copies where required; do not leave a readable copy in generic logs. A case ledger can preserve that a correction happened without preserving the corrected private text forever. Current restrictions and privacy decisions survive gameplay restore.

The first service performs human review. Listing reads, applying, redeeming, mute/block, case submission and reading a decision make no model calls. An optional later summarizer would need a permitted evidence scope, cited sources, human review and a full cost case; it cannot decide guilt or expand private access. Optional diagnostics may be incomplete and are not promised as a transcript of everything that occurred.

Minecraft's reporting guide illustrates selecting conduct and supplying context. VRChat separates local rules and service enforcement, while Discord's warning system makes the scope of a decision and review path visible even to suspended users. We adopt those interaction lessons, not their complete policies or sanctions. A small retrospective moderator study identifies time and conflict within moderation teams as relevant burdens; it does not supply a staffing ratio for this game. [R7], [R8], [R9], [R10]

## Sustainable operation and honest failure

Count the complete cost of a successful newcomer: hosting during play and waits, account and character retention, asset delivery, any actually admitted resident cognition, host time, application review, incident response and appeals. Free admission is not free operation. The first pilot does not add currency, subscriptions or paid queue priority. If an existing charge applies, disclose it before acceptance; never sell relief from harassment or faster appeal review.

Keep admission work outside simulation and use deliberately published directory information. A request must not wake residents, generate a world or run a compatibility conversation. Idle applications and case queues trigger no autonomous NPC work. Reading old cases must page permitted material instead of hydrating every person's complete history. Complete identity, About Me, relevant conversation and actual obligations remain intact whenever ordinary resident cognition does run; economy comes from fewer unnecessary tasks and an honest service envelope.

The responsible operator publishes welcome and review availability. The proposed pilot gives each acknowledged case its next update date, ordinarily within 24 real hours when public intake is open; this is a staffed operating target, not continuous emergency supervision. Pausing intake never suspends an already promised case update or appeal deadline. Reports received while intake is closed receive an explicit next staffed review date and a truthful notice if that date is unavailable; they are not described as under continuous watch. A late update is visible and pauses new public intake until coverage is restored. Someone can still submit a report, block contact or leave. If required access enforcement itself fails, close the affected live offering through the existing maintenance owner rather than leave a misleading safety claim online.

Pause intake reversibly when welcome slots, pending applications, review capacity, budget or qualified live capacity are exhausted. Preserve acknowledged requests and cases. Explain whether the person should wait for a published opportunity, change account, complete an answer, or contact support. An intake pause does not by itself cancel current access or evict active people. Discord's reversible invitation pause is a relevant containment precedent. [R11]

The proposed request and evidence envelopes live in [CP02–CP04](../limits/community-participation.md). They are product starting points for qualification. No record-count bound by itself proves preparation is bounded: directory search, applicant restrictions, case context selection, actor/account relationships, archived evidence, attachment bytes and restored copies can grow separately. The technical stage must select and measure aggregate storage, query, rate and staff-work budgets before public use. A cap reached before submission is an explicit refusal with preserved local text and a smaller supported path, not a false receipt. Immediate personal controls never wait for case quota availability.

## Required scenarios and experience gates

| Scenario                                                         | Required outcome and reason                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A visitor has 20 minutes and dislikes onboarding                 | They see the actual opportunity, make a short request, enter with a useful action and can skip the tour. No pledge or personality questionnaire stands between approval and play.  |
| The host is late or the special cache is empty                   | The listing/arrival state is corrected. An ordinary worthwhile alternative exists, or the host offers another visit. The unique spear is not refilled or privately duplicated.     |
| An approved invite is forwarded                                  | The wrong account cannot enroll or consume the approved offer; the intended person can recover it through sign-in.                                                                 |
| A spectator decides to play, or an excluded player is reinstated | The service uses the specifically supported grant-management path or says operator work is needed. It never pretends a new invite supplies an unsupported promotion.               |
| Two people want the final slot                                   | The current controller and applicable reconnect claim remain protected; acknowledged wait order and the actual reserved opportunity are honored. No alternate timeline is created. |
| A muted person crosses the trail                                 | The body and relevant action feedback remain understandable without replaying their speech.                                                                                        |
| A trade settles at the moment of Block                           | There is one actual result: completed transfer or withdrawn pending exchange. Neither person's inventory is half changed.                                                          |
| A participant reports the host and leaves                        | Independent authorized review and case access work outside the world, without exposing private evidence to the host.                                                               |
| Several accounts report a disliked newcomer                      | Reports preserve their individual evidence, but volume alone produces no guilt or automatic exclusion.                                                                             |
| Staff restrict a dangerous action                                | New commands stop immediately; the selected containment cancels relevant unfinished work, preserves actual effects and ends participation through its declared lifecycle.          |
| A suspension was mistaken                                        | The same rightful character and valid access are restored with current checks; prior blocks, spent materials and other people's subsequent actions remain real.                    |
| Storage or staff coverage is unavailable                         | No false report receipt or welcome promise. Intake pauses, acknowledged work stays recoverable and personal relief remains usable.                                                 |

The product passes only when newcomers can explain what they can enjoy here, complete a meaningful self-chosen action, understand who is human, leave easily and voluntarily choose whether to return. Record time lost to admission/arrival friction, unsupported listing promises, involuntary exposure to muted content, and actual staff minutes per accepted visitor and incident. Do not use time spent waiting, total messages, compliance with a tutorial or application counts as substitutes for fun.

Qualify the selected hostile-client, privacy, reconnect, authority-revocation, duplicate-submission, old-link, capacity, accessible-input and service-outage cases under MP/PD/data owners. These are future acceptance criteria; this documentation pass does not claim those checks ran.

## Delivery sequence and game-first critique

1. **Make one supported outing worth inviting somebody to.** Reuse ordinary crafting/exploration and the optional DG07 encounter only where qualified. Test whether a finite, already visited world still has a truthful newcomer opportunity.
2. **Qualify the complete invited adult journey.** Use the current account, starter-character and return foundation. Explain actual continuity and service coverage; finish relevant authentication and operator-role gaps.
3. **Deliver personal controls, scoped reporting and correction.** Include independent review, containment and an outside-world support route. Rehearse a complaint about the host and a mistaken exclusion.
4. **Open one curated listing and managed recipient-bound entry.** Add bounded requests and honest capacity; do not require a fleet, federation or automated community generation.
5. **Expand only on observed demand and sustainable service.** A second community needs a distinct good activity and real staffing, not a directory growth target. Broader signup, automatic character creation and richer spectating remain D68 choices.

The review rejects a large social platform as the first deliverable. There is no public reputation score, mandatory community role ladder, village judiciary for real reports, paid priority, automatic relocation or predictive matchmaking. The short application exists because strangers need an actual welcome and admission decision; remove unnecessary questions when they do not change the offer. The moderation machinery is justified by an actual public service promise, not by a wish to simulate governance.

A safe but empty camp still fails. A warm welcome with no ability to stop harassment also fails. The preferred sequence earns public participation by delivering both a playable reason to visit and proportionate, functioning service controls. None of this requires the optional well-being proposals to become global game law.
