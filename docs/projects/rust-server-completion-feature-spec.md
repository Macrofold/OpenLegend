## What a player should be able to do

Start Main, meet someone at camp, borrow or obtain supplies, choose a trip together, and come home with something useful. The other person can decline. If they come, what they notice and later remember depends on where they were, what they heard and what mattered to them. Another player can join, learn a different version of the trip and act independently. Leaving the browser and coming back returns each person to their permitted view of the living world.

That journey joins physical action and cooperation with personal knowledge that outlasts the session. It is the first delivery priority. Keep the simpler Solo start, other supported authored openings, character tools and creator/operator functions too. The [playable openings](../worlds/base/playable-openings.md), [repertoire priorities](../repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md) and [feature owners](../features/README.md) supply the content. Repertoire entries proposed for future worlds remain possibilities. A server replacement does not require thousands of new mechanics.

## Scope and product decisions

- **One live server.** Rust owns all supported server behavior. The existing browser can remain TypeScript. Build-time tools can compile the browser and authored inputs. A new world, an old-enough character memory, a privileged edit or an exceptional recovery must work without starting a Node game process.
- **Preserve useful behavior through its actual callers.** Include the player, autonomous character, second observer, operator, creator, automation client and offline-to-online restart. Converting a database table, exposing a menu item or implementing a simulation function alone leaves its caller unfinished. Preserve explicit failures and uncertainty as well as successful outcomes.
- **Keep the current game, simplify the machinery.** Preserve every supported behavior of the TypeScript reference server (called Source in the evidence). Remove superseded plumbing once its callers have moved. Keep a capability that moved to a better entry point only when its intended users can still perform it with equivalent permissions and feedback. A missing or inconvenient capability is never an implicit product removal. A proposed loss of useful behavior needs an explicit owner decision.
- **World rules have one author.** The bundled world's occasions for sleep, memory, feeding, warnings and conversation belong to that world. The engine supports installed capabilities and protects access, ordering and resources. A body without human language memory can forage and flee without generating a human journal or buying a model call.

## Required journeys

| Who and when                                                                         | Observable result and meaningful failure                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A player enters a fresh Main or Solo installation, walks, reconnects or changes tabs | Correct starting inhabitants, equipment, places and affordances. Movement, reactions and new speech appear through the normal stream. A second observer sees only what they personally received. A revoked controller cannot reuse an old request. The ordinary save and cold reopen preserve the result once.                                                                                                         |
| A person chooses work, craft, trade, travel, combat or care                          | Discovery, preview, choosing, execution, interruption and outcome agree about the same tool, target and terms. Known methods work without invention. A rejected proposal leaves unrelated accepted speech or work intact. Where the world offers a family to NPCs, they can use it under the same rules.                                                                                                               |
| A resident recalls an outing later                                                   | Eligible old personal accounts may become faithful summaries. A witnessed event, testimony, an imagined dream and a promise remain distinguishable. Important exceptions and required exact words survive. Someone who never learned the story cannot acquire it from another resident's summary. Correction and forgetting reach the raw source and its derivatives.                                                  |
| Two residents hold conversations while the world changes                             | Unrelated activity can continue. A valid reply applies and survives restart once. Withdrawing relevant evidence before dispatch prevents disclosure. Cancellation, reconnect or replay cannot produce another paid attempt or a duplicate effect. A capacity refusal has a useful recorded reason. An NPC can ask about its own prior observations before answering. An ordinary direct answer needs no extra request. |
| A player inspects an item, follows a lead or returns to the journal                  | Inspection can reveal what was actually examined. Lists can be paged. Omitted contents stay unknown. The player can reopen history, methods, notes, dispositions or commitments. An authorized Journal passage resolves to private prose, an allowed authored description or an explicit failure. Withdrawn text disappears from view.                                                                                 |
| A permitted creator, administrator or tool acts                                      | Inspect and draft within granted scope. Review and apply against the current installation. Manage invitations, rights, saves and recovery with the same separation of duties. Operators can recover a scheduled checkpoint. Reconnecting players see the current running world. A stale draft or checkpoint cannot grant access, rewind spending or revive forgotten information.                                      |

Exercise installed Main and Solo, then use the existing smaller presets to activate branches absent from either one. Preserve full current starting content. Supported user-created combinations require an actual install, execution, save and reload. Unknown families fail admission explicitly. No compatibility reader for previous development formats is required.

## Completion and evidence

**Required passes.** Close all rows in the [caller and field inventory](../maintainers/native-simulation-core-coverage.md#full-server-conversion-inventory), including non-HTTP work. Complete the journeys above on the Rust release with Node unavailable to the running server. Run past the actual memory deadlines. Exercise a fresh install, a warm world with positive optional state, a cold restart and a separate administrative recovery. Change the ordinary development and production launchers only after these pass. Then retire both superseded hosts and their exclusively used interfaces.

**Required measurements.** Use the [technical comparison rules](rust-server-completion-tech-design.md#verification-and-cutover) for uncertain changes to CPU, memory, waiting or gameplay. Compare equivalent completed work. The earlier [RGS08 decision](../maintainers/completed/2026/rust-game-server.md) qualified three game hours in one opening. It established useful movement, conversation and saving evidence. That window could not establish the review of routine accounts older than six game hours. Controlled replies establish application behavior. Model quality and uncoached enjoyment keep their existing evaluation owners.
