## The game this design delivers

Arrive empty-handed in a particular woodland, understand how to feed yourself, make useful tools, choose a site and build a camp that can become a defensible home. Meet four people with different values and lives. Choose company, barter, help or solitude. Travel for useful resources, knowledge and equipment; invent an alternative that actually changes what you can do. Encounter wildlife and distant people through their real activities, and return to a world changed by those choices.

This pair completes the **design** of the initial playable world requested October 17, 2026. It integrates existing owners instead of replacing their implementations or broad long-term proposals. [Playable openings](../worlds/base/playable-openings.md) remains the scenario requirement owner; [gameplay priorities](../repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md) remains the selection owner. [FPV](../maintainers/first-playable-validation.md) coordinates implementation/evidence and [FPW](../maintainers/first-playable-world.md) owns the new shared integration work. The paired [technical design](first-playable-world-tech-design.md) defines exact contracts, dependencies and verification.

The [combined delivery sequence](first-playable-world-tech-design.md#implementation-order-and-independent-task-boundaries) assigns combat, speech and the complete world to one AI in successive phases, with early playable checks and final cross-project qualification. The subsystem pairs remain the detailed design owners; completing one phase does not complete the combined assignment. The integrated stealth/awareness contracts supply the sensing, reaction and disclosure dependencies of those same activities, without replacing the survival, building, cooperation or reward priorities.

No interactions are scripted. Authored initial people, history, geography, material properties, recipes and finite mechanical rules establish circumstances. Actual perception, independent choices, consent, physical actions and consequences determine what happens. The world never forces the player to talk, guarantees an NPC answer, assigns a companion, spawns a raid, completes a quest on a dialogue keyword or tells a hidden actor where the player built.

## The first session: opportunities, not a prescribed sequence

The following traces are **paper acceptance scenarios**. They describe available choices and required consequences, not timed triggers or promises about what a player or character will choose.

1. **Get oriented and eat.** The initial view offers accessible berries and natural sources. Inspect explains actual stock and a normal action; gathering/eating needs no generated recipe. Eat offers a deliberate meal quantity with its actual benefit and any waste, so the player need not reopen a menu for every berry. The player can spend time reading or walk to a different patch without beginning near death. A depleted patch is honestly depleted. Familiar known methods are available through ordinary Make, with inputs, kept tools, work and result.
2. **Make something that changes the next choice.** A simple stone tool can be made from hand-accessible materials. Fiber preparation, cord and the pole/reed tools shorten later gathering. A pouch or larger basket organizes and stores actual belongings; the selected open basket can also organize wettable materials while letting rain through. Current personal carrying weight is unmodeled, so neither grants a movement or carrying-capacity bonus. A spear or shield changes actual combat options. The user chooses quantities and a finite batch; the game shows current work and Stop, consumes actual inputs and retains completed outputs if interrupted.
3. **Choose home and company.** Three different terrain suggestions make food, travel, visibility and nearby people understandable, while permitting other supported sites. The player can start with cooking/storage, a roof for a chosen place or a wall and entrance for physical protection; no roof-first requirement, mandatory plot or level ladder. A resident may discuss, help, trade, decline or pursue something else. Consent concerns exact work/material/site, not generic friendship.
4. **Take a worthwhile trip.** Lookout/workshop directions and the existing missing-satchel incident remain useful. A physical recipe record can be inspected and learned; an admitted invention can solve a personal hunting/making problem. Compare the complete known and invented routes, including generation, reading and acquisition, then use the improvement in another worthwhile activity. Two token uses of a negligible yield increase cannot establish that promise. A note is a report, not current omniscience; a cache can already be empty.
5. **Meet opposition on terms the world can support.** Ordinary prey flee real perceived threats. The stag remains an optional readable territorial challenge. Wolves can hunt and encircle through local perception; people can seek supplies and discover a camp while traveling. A closed wall/gate, a companion, an offer, retreat or a different route can matter. No outcome is guaranteed by the design.
6. **Return with a reason to continue.** Actual stock, structures, learned methods, injuries, deaths, accepted offers and personally experienced relationships remain. The player can improve a weakness, retrieve lost belongings, follow another lead or stop at a satisfying point. Reloading never provides a fresh stockpile or rewrites another person's choice.

A successful first session need not construct every available item or encounter every threat. Full FPV acceptance covers the whole capability set across suitable sessions without pretending one staged route proves the game's breadth.

## Useful progression and burden

The selected progression is practical: empty hands → food/simple tools → more efficient collection and organized storage → a useful camp → better range/defense/company → a new journey where the improvement matters. Knowledge, familiarity, real materials, particular relationships and a recognizable home supply enduring gains. No mandatory skill tree, experience currency, global settlement score, daily chore or generic quest ledger is added.

The [survival profile](../worlds/base/first-playable-survival.md) gives exact recipe chains and labor/resource arithmetic, and the [camp profile](../worlds/base/editable-shelters.md) gives two attainable layouts. These include the player doing their own work; NPC assistance is optional. Actual action durations are game seconds at the existing 60:1 clock, not real-time estimates of player completion. Travel, choosing, reading, failure and provider waits dominate many moments and must be observed separately.

Design observation windows are: food/tool understanding in the first few minutes, a useful chosen camp function within roughly 5–10 minutes, a meaningful outward trip/cooperation/invention within roughly 10–20 minutes, and a reason to improve/return during 20–40 minutes. These are **diagnostic hypotheses**, not timed rewards, hard deadlines or evidence already measured. Players may converse, explore or build in a different order. Missing these windows should prompt investigation of comprehension, input burden, availability and payoff; never auto-complete an action or pressure an NPC to compensate.

Food should motivate trips without making every minute maintenance. The initial full condition, near forage and explicit finite meals support that aim: five berries restore up to 90 Food, equivalent to 500 real seconds at the unchanged normal drain. This is a paper budget, not measured time between a person's meals; reading, choice, waste and actual conditions still matter. There is no automatic future eating. The longer social offer window allows a reply. Full camp building can span nights. Cooking/storage, a small roof or an initial barrier can each be a chosen stopping point. Stationary fire light, real barriers and willing companions serve different purposes. A daily 16:00–18:00 material-rain window makes actual roof coverage observable: covered stock stays drier. It adds no health, sleep, strength or temperature bonus and does not damage materials merely for being wet. Actual daylight and stationary fire affect physical visual opportunities through the selected [vision policy](../worlds/base/vision-and-gaze.md); brightness settings and remembered fire images do not. The [stealth design](stealth-awareness-feature-spec.md) owns readable partial evidence, finite preparation and recoverable surprise. A new portable-light subsystem is not required by this opening.

## What changes from the current Main opening

- Remove the player's supplied starting kit and stocked/lit personal camp. Preserve useful old kit content in distant actual Watchpost stock, keeping the empty-handed natural route complete.
- Stock the currently empty near berries and add the selected complementary finite natural resources/known recipes.
- Expand Main's supported terrain to 128 × 112 m while retaining existing northwest landmarks and physical stories; relocate the complete Watchpost and selected outer predators coherently.
- Extend open shelters with real wall sections, a sliding/barred gate, natural material manufacture and exact damage/repair/reclaim. Barriers have physical exposure under explicit operational grants; offline body protection does not automatically protect buildings.
- Extend ordinary offers and shared activities with exact short cooperation and voluntary relationship facts. Pending ordinary social offers become 7,200 game seconds instead of 1,800; this changes the shared world policy once rather than creating different timers per UI.
- Add finite animal feeding/roaming and human opportunity inputs, preserving independent human choice and CTR's single animal movement/escape owner.

These are selected design changes. No runtime behavior or saved world has changed in this documentation task. Implementation must preserve current-format integrity and the protected development-save policy.

## Full acceptance and scope boundaries

Every row in the design map must have implementation and exact integrated evidence before the whole first playable is accepted. Preserve partial test usefulness: an earlier food/craft or camp build can be tested honestly while other capabilities remain open. Mechanical correctness, independent character choice, useful generated invention, uncoached comprehension/enjoyment and wanted return are separate claims.

The selected opening does not require reproduction/aging, regenerating ecology, institutions/taxes, general fire spread, a structural engineering simulator, vehicles, voice/media, offline service, payment, creator marketplace, advanced resurrection or a background-population abstraction. Existing authored features remain available where already installed; these broader proposals are not deleted or marked complete. Adult-audience/agent-content requirements retain their owners and cannot be waived by a world profile.
