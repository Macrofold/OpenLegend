## Planning, guidance and material risks

The task changes **zero runtime logic lines**. It needs a durable plan because family continuity, authority, private experience, obligations, campaign timing and device/offline promises cross several existing contracts. Read root instructions first and apply the repository's design, documentation, prioritization, UI, review, verification, AI, performance and engine/world guidance where relevant. Start each group with a wanted activity and a useful outcome. Preserve a complete survival-adventure with useful invention, independent people, understandable opposition, worthwhile rewards, recovery and remembered consequences. A peaceful relationship or creative pleasure can also be worthwhile. New population, administration, magic, global events and alternate clients must justify the attention and operating cost they add. Group order selects this design assignment, not the next implementation priority.

Keep one owner for each meaning. Biological ancestry is not permission or learned knowledge; a fictional office is not platform power; a mental effect is not unrestricted access to another mind; a campaign forecast is not a guarantee that every local participant will appear; an installed client is not an offline world. Preserve truthful incomplete outcomes, current permissions, exact accounting, actual retained experience and current-format save integrity. Keep replaceable world laws distinct from reusable integrity requirements.

Research records must distinguish documented behavior, developer reports, evidence of defects, marketing claims and Open Legend design inferences. Historical and living sources need clear dates and access limits. Illustrative arithmetic is not a measured performance result or a commercial promise. Each feature must consider aggregate attention, service work and retained history as well as its per-action limits.

## Reviewed product scope of group 34

[Shared campaigns with local opportunities](shared-campaigns-feature-spec.md) selects the optional Ash Road: two real local undertakings in one world, finite relay sources, two usable road gates and one unique nonliving Warden. Players can open a gate immediately, secure it by disconnecting its relay, or use the other community's actual work to shut down the same adversary. Complete prevention is a real canonical outcome. A separately qualified operation makes one fixed-target physical attempt with a finite charge and active budget; a later player meets the actual changed place, not a regenerated personal boss.

The review corrected an initially empty stake: a machine cannot meaningfully threaten to close a gate that is already locked shut. Gates are now usable while connected, making quick access, secured access and a later setback distinct. It also removed a mandatory heavy-object recovery dependency, retained a real bypass around the spent machine, accepted harmless physical counterplay and fixed the destination at launch. The first profile threatens route access, so its fiction cannot claim an unimplemented massacre or introduce new human harm.

Forecasts distinguish real notice and a finite activation-service window from the actual body's conditional arrival. Immediate local work never waits for the advertised window. One differently timed replacement does not create a second operation, an earlier advertised bound cannot be weakened by new notice, and expired windows never launch on reconnect. Resident reservations explicitly promise entry rather than witnessing the brief operation. Early prevention does not erase an independently accepted entry or hosting commitment. Public fixture states and optional attribution remain separate from private actions and NPC knowledge, with no personal name published by default.

The twelve primary records include current guides, dated developer rationale and documented historical failures, with no invented measured results. The economic comparison counts actual living needs, finite native work, failed/uncertain workflows, growing records, recovery and operator time. A staffed two-hour offer can cost far more than its short physical operation; the design therefore keeps the local cooperative adventure independently complete and allows the advertised spectacle to be omitted. Intelligent city-by-city adversaries, damaging sieges, cross-world fronts and mature-scale claims remain separately useful and qualified expansions under the existing PS07/PS08 owners.
