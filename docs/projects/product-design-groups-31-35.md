## Planning, guidance and material risks

The task changes **zero runtime logic lines**. It needs a durable plan because family continuity, authority, private experience, obligations, campaign timing and device/offline promises cross several existing contracts. Read root instructions first and apply the repository's design, documentation, prioritization, UI, review, verification, AI, performance and engine/world guidance where relevant. Read related owners and their cited requirements thoroughly; catalogue examples and historical research supply possibilities, not an adopted constitution or runtime evidence.

Start each group with a wanted activity and a useful outcome. Preserve a complete survival-adventure with useful invention, independent people, understandable opposition, worthwhile rewards, recovery and remembered consequences. A peaceful relationship or creative pleasure can also be worthwhile. New population, administration, magic, global events and alternate clients must justify the attention and operating cost they add. Group order selects this design assignment, not the next implementation priority.

Keep one owner for each meaning. Biological ancestry is not permission or learned knowledge; a fictional office is not platform power; a mental effect is not unrestricted access to another mind; a campaign forecast is not a guarantee that every local participant will appear; an installed client is not an offline world. Preserve truthful incomplete outcomes, current permissions, exact accounting, actual retained experience and current-format save integrity. Keep replaceable world laws distinct from reusable integrity requirements.

Research records must distinguish documented behavior, developer reports, evidence of defects, marketing claims and Open Legend design inferences. Historical and living sources need clear dates and access limits. Illustrative arithmetic is not a measured performance result or a commercial promise. Each feature must consider aggregate attention, service work and retained history as well as its per-action limits.
