### Admission, preparation and release

The aiming action owns source/target identity and physical life, exact weapon/ammunition lot references and definition pins, preparation deadline, current permitted aim point, selected manual/one-shot release mode and action identity. This is active work, not private intent projected to other actors. The issuer uses the existing reservation/physical-lane owner for tool/ammunition; aiming holds one available projectile but does not consume it.

Start checks current control/participation, body capability, permitted target, sensed position, usable tool, unreserved compatible ammunition and support. The existing approach mechanism reaches a valid firing position when the selected offer disclosed auto-movement. Holding aim never repeatedly chases a target: after approach/preparation, out-of-range or lost-sight ends aim through a relevant dependency update and blocks release, rather than starting another automatic move. Movement/cancel ends held aim and releases unspent claims. Injury uses the existing interruption contract. Define Aim and one-shot offers through the same read-only checks as their execution; previews consume no random values, IDs or supplies.

Preparation follows the installed launcher profile. On readiness manual mode waits for explicit Shoot; one-shot mode releases once if still valid. Holding aim has no invented time or accuracy buff. No per-tick model decision is needed. Stop or control departure cancels unreleased work. Human final-blow confirmation binds to this exact shot under the existing review owner; Aim alone is not lethal authority. Any one-shot approach must retain current lethal-review semantics rather than silently moving or firing after a stale review.

At release, recheck target/source life, current permission, sight, range, definitions, body, review and claims. Lock the point in world coordinates from the actor's last **current** permitted observation. Atomically debit one projectile, assign the flight identity and emit release evidence. A replay cannot debit or launch again. No release on stale review, missing ammunition, unavailable native work or lost target; report the specific actor-safe failure. Once released, later stop cannot refund ammunition or recall the flight. Release ends the exclusive aiming work so the shooter can choose movement or another permitted action; any already owed attack recovery survives. The flight retains its pending terminal result independently of the actor's next action, without claiming a second actor-controlled physical lane.

### Flight, accuracy and effects

One serializable flight contains immutable source/life attribution, selected target/life, launch position, direction, launch time, speed, remaining range, committed injury/definition data and any exact review permission. Reference pins preserve the committed effect even if the launcher's held position changes; current target participation and review invalidation still govern damage. Flight is native simulation state, not a second item in inventory or an actor with a mind.

Use a **straight path** for this increment. The world declares projectile speed and range. Swept geometry must cover the traveled segment against static blockers and moving supported bodies over the same elapsed interval; a large simulation slice must not tunnel or compare only final positions. Reuse existing spatial broad phase and motion-boundary segmentation. Order contact by earliest time, with deterministic identity tie-breaking; do not depend on hash iteration or browser frames. Supported support/geometry changes truncate or stop flight coherently, not teleport it through new walls.

Replace the old binary damage draw with an authored release-error policy: existing accuracy plus the applicable competence contribution governs whether the ray uses the chosen point or an offset. The world profile owns error size/distribution; domain geometry owns whether that actual path meets something. Draw only at release and store the resulting direction, never reroll during restore or each step. A displayed accuracy value becomes **base aim steadiness**, not a promised probability of injuring a moving, shielded or covered target. Preserve definition value meaning for authoring and update all explanatory consumers/PC evidence explicitly; this is a current-format change, not an old-save conversion.

At first contact: scenery stops flight; the exact eligible target can receive its ordinary body effect; another body stops flight without receiving damage or transferring target/review authority. This last boundary intentionally excludes collateral harm pending D07. It is not a trajectory planner that steers around friends. No ricochet, penetration, area effect, retained missed projectile or automatic retrieval is implied. An obstructed shot still spent ammunition. An abandoned/dead/replaced target does not retarget to a successor. Inactive humans remain protected, and a shot cannot be used to bypass direct PvP denial.

At a valid target contact, use AV02's actual-direction defense resolver, then existing body effects once. If the original unreviewed shot has become potentially lethal due to concurrent injury, existing final-blow policy still prevents unreviewed lethal damage; report a stopped effect without refunding the released projectile. Reviewed source/target/tool/effect changes follow the review owner's invalidation, not an independent weaker projectile rule. Shooter death/departure alone does not erase a released physical object, but cannot grant any new permission.

Action experience has separate release and terminal facts linked to the one selected action. Preserve exact committed time/order, ammunition debit, attempted versus actual damage, before/after health and failure/observation uncertainty in authoritative facts; observer-specific memory contains only permitted evidence. Route required personal results through PG02's common memory selection and shared perspective renderer, retaining correction/forgetting/source links rather than a second battle recap store. The shooter knows they released a projectile; hit location/damage requires actual permitted perception. Do not use unseen impact as a remote sensing channel. Own activity completion says what was attempted and what is known, never a bare “sequence complete.” Opponents see preparation/release only through ordinary sight/hearing. Do not add universal danger markers.

Restore uses saved times/directions; incompatible saves are rejected without migration or automatic reset. Live removal of a definition/source cannot leave dangling live references or free ammunition; use existing pin/removal integrity and committed flight data. Rewind/load cancels callbacks from an abandoned timeline.

Use the existing spatial index for relevant collision candidates; charge work to the current native allowance. ## CF03 — Evasion through authoritative movement

### Scope and implementation map

Use `kernel.ts`, `action-capabilities.ts`, `strikes.ts`, `spatial.ts`, `spatial-mutations.ts`, `motion-boundaries.ts`, `native-work.ts` and existing navigation/body/participation owners. Base-world action definitions/descriptions select supported bodies and tuning. Server discovery/decision context and protocol/client intentions expose the same invocation. AV02 alone owns equipment and guard consumption.

1. Add a finite short movement action with an exact supported destination, admitted duration, source life and readiness deadline. Resolve body swept-clearance and support using existing movement services, not a private dash coordinate update.
2. Add shared read-only eligibility and explicit interruption: an uncommitted strike/guard may be abandoned, but its already owed recovery/cost survives. Existing noninterruptible work still refuses correctly.
3. Schedule committed movement and contacts through current temporal/motion boundaries. Finish at the actual legal position, recording completion, obstruction or interruption. Movement cannot push another actor or cross unsupported gaps.
4. Add exact-target action offers and player point selection with an accessible directional alternative. Model candidates use a small disclosed selection of reachable points from permitted local geometry; omitted choices are not declared impossible and ordinary point movement remains available.
5. Expose only observed attack preparation/recovery and resulting miss/range loss through current action experience, inspection and character decision triggers. Do not create an automatic “counterattack” decision or attack damage multiplier.
6. Qualify against the existing stag; consume AV02 for guard/equipment coexistence and CF02 flight after it becomes available without reimplementing either.

### Chosen motion, interruption and disclosure

The command identifies actor and destination on known supported ground. It does not nominate an enemy to dodge automatically. Admission checks current body/action/participation, finite distance, current evasion cooldown, support and the same movement/obstacle permissions as ordinary walking. Resolve and freeze the selected short path at commitment; it is not a request for a repeated route search or pursuit. World-authored action availability can omit incompatible bodies without making bipeds universal engine law.

At execution, advance a real body along that path. Dynamic collision stops at the last valid location and returns a truthful blocked/interrupted result; no snapping to the requested endpoint. Existing incoming strike geometry is evaluated against this movement. Evasion grants no immunity: the player can still be inside weapon reach or move along a projectile path. Damage/interruptions preserve whatever movement actually occurred. Native budget failure before commitment moves nothing; failure during committed motion preserves an honest paused/unfinished action through existing work admission.

Commit starts the evasion readiness deadline, which survives cancellation, interruption, another chosen action and current-format restoration. Ordinary walking is not barred by that deadline; only repeated evasion is. Beginning evasion may cancel a preparing attack or a chosen guard through their current interruption rules; it never clears the attack recovery deadline or refunds a used guard/ammunition. Completing evasion does not auto-equip, counterstrike or restart the previous plan.

Show the controlled character's own evasion readiness. For another character, show preparation, miss and recovery only while observation and the authored disclosure policy permit those facts. On losing sight do not keep a live opponent countdown. A personal memory can say the opponent missed when seen; it cannot supply refreshed hidden state. Relevant result events feed ordinary reconsideration; choosing to exploit the opening remains the player's or character's choice.

### Completion and scope boundaries

Exercise evasion both before a stag impact and too late; wall/edge/occupied-destination refusals; dynamic obstruction halfway; repeated input; stop/restart; restored motion and cooldown; current controlled life change; ordinary walking during cooldown; shield cancellation after AV02; and honest observed recovery followed by an ordinary attack or escape. A second supported body/profile with different distance/duration should use the same motion owner, not another hard-coded actor branch. Include real player interaction and report fixture/model evidence separately.

No universal stamina, injury animation rewrite, parry, push/disarm, invulnerability, automatic reaction or new PvP permission. Relevant interfaces and future enemy consumers use exact motion plus normal attacks; they do not need a special counter-hit subsystem. CF02/CF03 combined qualification demonstrates a sidestep of a locked shot without claiming all ballistics, reflex accessibility or large-battle performance.
