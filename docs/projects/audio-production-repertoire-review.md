# Audio production: repertoire application

**Design review, November 6, 2026.** The [feature specification](audio-production-feature-spec.md), [technical design](audio-production-tech-design.md) and [AUD tracker](../maintainers/audio-production.md) own the selected workflow. This review covers the 42 files in the repertoire library: 28 catalogues, four world proposals and ten shared/navigation/research records. It does not turn the library into an implementation queue.

## Method and evidence boundary

Review the project's experience priorities and catalogue ownership first, then each catalogue's relevant situations, entries and dependencies for music production, sound feedback, personal musical meaning and optional performance. Review world proposals for range and conflicting assumptions. The source atlas and historical reading ledgers are checked for ownership/provenance boundaries, not recertified as fresh external research.

Coverage includes full catalogue-entry reads for a substantial subset and category/heading coverage with complete relevant entries and examples for the remainder. This is an audio-focused cross-catalogue assessment, not a fresh line-by-line re-ranking of every unrelated entry or a claim to have reopened every underlying source. No listening, gameplay or model-quality experiment is inferred from these reads.

The selected opportunities favor recognizable people and places, useful actions, invention, discovery, independent desires, earned outcomes, ordinary pleasure and memorable shared experiences. A larger soundtrack is useful when it supports those experiences. Track count, imitation of another game's middleware, and complexity of a musician simulation are not goals by themselves.

## Application and scope

- **ARL297, the learned song as a shared keepsake.** The [Arts proposal](../repertoires/arts-leisure.md#fixed-song-performance-and-a-personal-jukebox) preserves the full musician/jukebox idea and the simple fixed-recording interpretation. It is a Complete-level experience opportunity, not a new Core prerequisite or a finished gameplay design. Existing instrument skills, gigs, tours, personal themes and musical objects remain their owners.
- **Keep composition, arrangement and recording distinct.** One melody can return in different circumstances or become someone's performance; possession of a recording, knowing a song and having the skill/access to perform it are different facts. This is the pipeline's inexpensive preparation for future personal meaning.
- **Retain broad genres and coherent selected families.** No catalogue-wide genre rewrite is warranted. Different people can enjoy the same song for different reasons; preferences need not match a profession, species, nationality or moral label. The current world's tentative sci-fi/EDM mismatch is not an engine prohibition.
- **Prioritize truthful, satisfying feedback and welcome music.** Material/action variation, clean source lifecycles, understandable threats, good transitions and repeat listening matter before advanced orchestration. Actual action/hearing owners supply facts; audio does not simulate success, a predator or a private motive.
- **Keep audience agency and authored worlds.** Dancing, tipping, teaching, romance, reputation, magic, sensory transformations and narrative truth require their own capabilities. An approved track/tag creates none of them. Silence and sound can both be satisfying; neither is a mandatory solution to every scene.

The [library index](../repertoires/README.md) owns current counts; source/expansion records remain historical. Useful specialist ideas remain optional rather than prerequisites to audio production.

## Follow-through and open design boundaries

The [AUD implementation sequence](../maintainers/audio-production.md) covers all selected production/playback work and its evidence. The [needs-design register](../maintainers/needs-design.md#ai-assisted-game-production-research-and-design-integration) closes only that design slice.

Still separate: musician learning/sharing and participation, performance interruption, NPC taste/reactions, payments and progression; DG34's public-deed/witness/bard-retelling journey; richer evidence-scoped emotion/motive interpretation; speech/TTS; synchronized performances and wider distribution. Retaining these opportunities is not permission to build them now. The initial music default remains an explicit open decision: audition Suno and Eleven Music, then ask Mike before treating either as selected. Provider-independent workflow work can proceed; production-provider adoption waits for that decision.
