## Working objective

Determine whether intended adult browser survival-adventure/sandbox players understand the world, make meaningful choices, value their camp and relationships, and want another activity or session. Examine useful invention and independent character consequences within the same game, not only as separate technical demonstrations.

Use early partial tests to resolve specific uncertainties while the complete experience is being built. State the missing coverage rather than redefining the first playable. Participant feedback informs creative judgment; it does not allocate the game's direction by vote.

## Step 1 — establish the real delivery baseline

Pin the actual implementation revision and use the [capability map](../maintainers/first-playable-validation.md#required-capability-and-owner-map). Reuse valid owner evidence; distinguish missing design, undelivered behavior, unqualified integration and missing player evidence.

Record the selected activity, source requirements, available capabilities, remaining prerequisite tasks and decision the test will inform. Reuse the shared Main initializer. Earlier supplied-kit evidence and the isolated Solo ground support labeled component checks; complete-world acceptance uses the selected empty-handed Main and its actual camp-and-conflict paths.

**Output:** a named build, a worthwhile test question and explicit missing coverage under FPV01. A participant session must not rely on unfinished dependencies arriving during play.

## Step 2 — settle the session envelope

Record the responsible operator, invitation permission, adult-participant criteria, access/device/browser expectations, applicable authorized time/spend, support route and stopping conditions. Use existing authorizations where they apply; do not infer permission from a document or discovered credential.

Choose supervised testing or a properly qualified hosted invitation environment. Apply the relevant [production requirements](../maintainers/production-deployment.md), including PD01/PD03/PD07/PD08 where applicable, without requiring unrelated future services. Under the [development save policy](../../AGENTS.md#development-save-policy), resolve any necessary owner decision before promising retained progress to real users; otherwise clearly disclose a disposable test. Browser delivery and the adult audience are already selected.

Explain AI involvement, operator-borne costs, known limitations, feedback use, progress handling and the right to stop. Recording is optional and consented. Use participant codes and collect only necessary observations; contact details, raw conversations and recordings stay out of Git. No favorable review or continued participation is owed.

**Output:** the actual session arrangement and permissions, not invented budgets or dates.

## Step 3 — qualify what will be offered

Exercise the configuration participants will receive: entry, ordinary controls, selected food/material chains, construction/access, danger, stop/cancel and continuation or disclosed disposal. Check relevant permissions, privacy, accounting and recovery through existing owners. Use separately labeled technical scenarios for service failures, depleted materials, interrupted construction, blocked entrances, stale trades, departed helpers and unreachable destinations.

Keep three forms of evidence distinct:

| Session or check               | What it can establish                                                                                                              | Boundary                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Component/fixture scenario     | Geometry, accounting, interruption, visibility and other bounded mechanical behavior                                               | Authored setup or forced choices must remain visible in the record; they do not establish independent decisions or enjoyment.                                         |
| Integrated behavioral scenario | A supported invention or autonomous actor produces the claimed result through actual knowledge, decisions, admission and execution | Record the setup and causal path. Native controllers and authored goals are valid; hidden forced outcomes or canned recipes cannot stand in for the claimed behavior. |
| Ordinary player session        | Comprehension, chosen approaches, effort/payoff, attraction and reasons to continue or stop                                        | Note help and interventions. A narrated success or passing fixture is not evidence of unassisted play.                                                                |

Live-provider claims require actual permitted live execution; native checks cannot certify unrun provider behavior. No participant should be used to expose an avoidable safety, privacy or accounting failure.

**Output:** proceed or rework for the specific session, not blanket production approval.

## Step 4 — observe distinct player journeys

Choose group size and session duration for the question and operator capacity. Short sessions may reveal entry problems; camp construction, darkness, independent contact and return value require suitable longer or later observations. There is no universal participant count or completion-time threshold. Sessions follow player choices rather than a compulsory quest sequence.

| Journey                    | Observe                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| First survival             | Understanding of hunger, food/resources and movement; unassisted access to food and useful tools; discoverable methods, chain dead ends and recovery after mistakes.                       |
| First camp                 | Site/layout choices, gathering and preparation burden, useful recipe/material alternatives, actual shelter/storage/cooking/barriers/entrance, and whether the place is worth returning to. |
| People and cooperation     | Preferences among the four/five characters; grounded advice, useful sharing/trade or a joint hunt; independently accepted, refused or ended participation and its consequences.            |
| Practical invention        | A genuinely new supported method/object is realized, solves a worthwhile problem and remains usable. Known recipes are a legitimate alternative, not failed participation.                 |
| Danger outside and at home | Understandable signs, movement and tactical choices, predator/group danger, actual defensive geometry, help, escape and understandable failure.                                            |
| Independent camp contact   | Opponent purpose, available knowledge, travel, discovery and response; whether ordinary activity creates worthwhile opportunities without a scripted arrival.                              |
| Continuing consequence     | Coherent camp/stock, useful rewards and improvements, remembered interactions, and another freely chosen activity or session.                                                              |

Do not require every person to experience every outcome. Labeled scenario checks can establish uncommon branches; ordinary sessions establish their exposure and practical value. Record no-contact sessions honestly. Distinguish deliberate avoidance or changed circumstances from broken travel, unreadable danger or pacing that leaves the world uneventful. Improve world opportunities and behavior rather than secretly forcing a visit.

Where progression matters, observe whether players recognize a wanted improvement and use it while it remains relevant. Use the [selection questions](../repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md#keep-play-worthwhile-as-players-learn-and-return) to examine what changes when they choose another offered method.

Record both time spent and what happened during it. Long play can reflect enjoyment, confusion, waiting or compulsory chores. A requested QA revisit tests continuation; an optional return provides different evidence about interest.

## Step 5 — separate the questions being answered

| Evidence layer               | Useful observations                                                                           | Do not infer                                                   |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Understanding and usability  | Expectations, unassisted actions, confusion, reading/setup burden and help required           | Desirability merely from a usable interface                    |
| Enjoyment and repeat value   | Meaningful choices/payoffs, camp/character preferences, stopping reasons and voluntary return | Enjoyment merely from duration, politeness or task completion  |
| Broader demand               | Qualified actions after truthful material reaches additional relevant audiences               | A market merely from views, free signups or one friendly group |
| Offer preference and payment | Understanding of realistic proposals; later authorized purchases or eligible renewals         | Sustainable sales from stated intent or patron donations       |
| Delivery quality and cost    | Gameplay-affecting waits, useful/failed AI results, device problems, actual costs and support | An adequate service from a cheap call or prepared clip         |

Before observing results, state what evidence would justify the next commitment and what would call for rework. Retain numerator/denominator, recruitment source, build, session setup and timing. Small or selected groups can diagnose problems without estimating market demand. Measure [commercial offers](initial-commercial-offer-decision-brief.md) separately from whether the game itself is appealing.

## Step 6 — recruit and communicate in parallel

Use existing [positioning](../../archive/06-marketing/positioning-and-copy.md) and [channel experiments](../../archive/06-marketing/ideas-channels-and-experiments.md). Prepare representative gameplay material, a concise invitation, a real next action and an opt-in contact route. Select relevant players/communities manually before investing in outreach tooling.

Keep survival-adventure play distinct from authoring/developer demonstrations and personal support for the founder. Advertise only what the named build supports. Broaden relevant outreach when repeated evidence supports it; do not require a daily posting schedule, fixed contact quota, paid ads, Steam preparation or crowdfunding under the browser-only direction. Actual posting and invitations require authorization/readiness.

## Step 7 — review, correct and retest

For each consequential finding record the observation and build, likely cause and a competing explanation, effect on play, existing owner, next correction/test and commitment limit. Distinguish a missing capability from poor explanation, unreliable execution, excessive effort or weak payoff.

Retest changed comprehension with fresh people where practical and continuing value with suitable return sessions. A promising idea may justify a bounded prototype without prior proof of fun. More geography, population or administration should not substitute for fixing a tedious opening or unrewarding camp.

**Advance** when evidence and capacity support the specific next commitment. **Rework** a named limiting problem. **Pause dependent exposure/spending** when agreed limits are reached or material safety, privacy, accounting or integrity failures make participation inappropriate. Existing participant/customer obligations remain separate.
