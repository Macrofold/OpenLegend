The selected design makes those information needs and their qualification explicit while preserving the complete survival-adventure target.

## 2. Product conclusions adopted in the design

1. **Context economies must preserve opportunities for a life, not merely enough facts to execute the current chore.** The existing repertoires already value curiosity, positive anticipation, enjoyable amateurism, particular tastes, familiar places and relationships beyond utility. A threat-only or unfinished-task-only filter would systematically remove those experiences. Supply observed opportunities and the actor's actual interests; do not force them to select leisure, speak, express delight or form a new goal.
2. **A small personal fact can matter more than a long mechanics catalogue.** A joke's actual shared origin, a mug's history, a pupil's demonstrated contribution or the specific issue on which a friend disagrees changes what a situation means. Keep those facts together with current corrections or boundaries. Generic labels such as friend, guard, religious or hostile are inadequate substitutes.
3. **Necessity is situation-specific, not a product-priority score.** A Detail-ranked keepsake or a Specialist world's already-enabled rule can be required evidence now. Catalogue Priority chooses future development work; it is never a runtime attention score, capability restriction or permission to omit relevant established facts.
4. **Permission to act, knowledge of a method and reasons to choose it are different.** The retired guard can retain technique but lose office rights; a steward can retain a personality outside the office; a successful apparatus can remove work without removing a friendship. Avoid compressing these into one role/status label.
5. **Humor, mystery and disagreement depend on different people knowing different things.** Preserve actual observation, testimony, inference, quotation, age, scope and later corrections. A listener should be able to be wrong without the context builder lying, or learn a true report from an enemy without becoming the enemy's ally.
6. **Creative opportunity requires useful alternatives, not a supplied answer.** Selecting only original-recipe inputs can eliminate invention before the model reasons. Expose relevant known properties and usable inspection paths; keep method prerequisites, aesthetic preferences, existing work and actual trial outcomes. Do not feed the complete catalogue, declare a novel method executed, or inject the lantern solution to obtain a desired demonstration.
7. **Compressing operations does not reduce character agency.** One AI reply can contain speech and physical action as separate operations; speech can accompany ongoing compatible work. Shortlists remain incomplete, inspection and open attempts remain meaningful, and the exact name/description-to-reference mapping remains required.
8. **Measure informed choices, not only smaller requests or correct JSON.** Mechanical checks should establish that required facts survive, while independent model comparisons examine whether the person can recognize contributions, make a useful proposal, keep a promise, disagree, enjoy something or remain quiet. Valid alternatives and the project's explicitly required combat baseline must keep their separate acceptance rules.

## 6. Exact world/action improvements and their purpose

### Preserve opportunities that a narrow selector would erase

**MEC-001** now explicitly supplies noticed/learned object properties even when an ordinary object is absent from an action shortlist. The person still recognizes or invents the application. **ECON-015** gives substitution the same property/expertise/access basis; matching product names alone cannot identify every useful material.

**MEC-164** now covers independently choosing characters as well as the player acquiring a new attachment or objective. A noticed resemblance, useful quirk or remembered encounter may matter before a rescue goal exists. The example does not manufacture reciprocal feelings.

**AD-036** preserves the brief ridge bloom as a discoverable, voluntary detour, using a visible change, local account or past visit as possible evidence. **AD-138** makes the shared traveler group’s different purposes concrete: a deposit, overlook and familiar doorway can matter to different people. Splitting or continuing remains possible; the example does not convert all opportunities into errands.

**BSP-046** preserves what a touched material transmits, interrupted joints, noise and ambiguous silence while allowing rhythm and music alongside warning. **BSP-049** distinguishes seeing another pattern layer from understanding its language, author or hidden purpose. **ECW-058** gives the same luminous-water observation several possible meanings: beauty, exposure or a signaling experiment. Selection should preserve an observation; the character’s interpretation and decision remain open.

### Preserve continuity, alternatives and opportunity costs under pressure

**MEC-021** retains competing uses of a finite supply and relevant future commitments, including personally valued projects. Immediate-objective optimization cannot silently erase their cost. **ECON-030** makes the owner’s own memory and needs available when deciding whether to sell a particular valued object, while the buyer receives only what they learn. An attached owner can still choose to sell.

**MEC-089** and **AD-083** preserve old ordinary evidence that becomes useful after a new question. Time, source and uncertainty do not change to fit the new purpose. The witness’s forgotten face remains forgotten.

**CR-072** retains a service passage and learned way to open it during a threat, including ordinary locks/capacity/hazards. The last-known passage condition does not become knowledge of a later unseen obstruction. **CR-147** retains the fighter’s known stake in a pump, oven or repair station. Preservation, sacrifice and relocation are alternatives; the example does not force a morally approved tactic.

**ECW-161** makes tree removal change a familiar route and lookout as well as yield stock. People learn consequences through observation or reports. The change does not invent an automatic hostility penalty for harvesting.

### Preserve concurrent activity and personal returns

**MEC-102** records the conversation point directly: a character may choose speech and a physical action together, and speak during compatible ongoing work. Starting speech does not itself finish, cancel or pause work. Bodily/activity constraints can interfere; their actual outcomes remain separate.

**ACR-210** preserves a returning person’s relevant remembered connection without prescribing their response from a role or trait. Prior acknowledgment prevents endless repeat greetings. **ACR-270** preserves actual travel photographs, sketches and souvenirs as chosen perspectives. An ordinary meal or beautiful pause can become a reason to return; participants can decline sharing and missing recordings are not invented.

The two world edits preserve the same principles: useful authored personal context survives actual deduplication in Modern Life, and a rescue supplies a possible basis for trust without purchasing obedience in Planetary Sci-fi.

### Evaluate meaning and available choice, not just smaller valid output

**SIM-104** now names consequential exceptions, amounts, sources, uncertainty and personal meaning as things compressed accounts must retain. Valid syntax does not establish preserved choice. **SIM-106** tests the same old event against repair, reunion, rivalry and a newly encountered curiosity, including an initially mundane observation. **SIM-228** distinguishes missing prerequisites, unknown capabilities and usable options missing from a shortlist. Known properties can support an unexpected combination; advice does not grant progression or guarantee rewards.

These are improvements to an existing Specialist experiment catalogue, not authorization to build a general laboratory before the playable game. The context design reuses the narrowly relevant checks as validation criteria for the selected context feature.

## 10. Deliberate retention, design status and evidence

The existing coverage matrix and all 25 worked combinations remain intact. They connect readable combat, earned rewards, functional creation, independent people, mystery, material alternatives, recovery and worthwhile ordinary life. No twenty-sixth scene or generic attention catalogue was needed. A particular situation can use a Detail-ranked keepsake or an already enabled Specialist rule without promoting that entire feature category in the implementation roadmap.

The two shared changes place the central rule at its proper owner: [README](../repertoires/README.md#from-an-idea-to-a-selected-design) links the context design/delivery/coverage record; [selection and scale](../repertoires/selection-and-scale.md#complexity-budgets-that-protect-the-experience) distinguishes product selection from request relevance. Catalogue refinements express domain-specific information needs; they do not duplicate the compiler's technical contract.

The source atlas and three historical research ledgers remain unchanged. Their earlier completed research is not outstanding cognition design work, and this pass does not reopen or recertify those external sources. Optional worlds retain their own adoption questions, audience, metaphysics and scope; the science-fiction proposal still excludes playable piloting, space navigation and dogfighting. No new universal anatomy, need, emotional rule, automatic loyalty, material capability or first-playable cast is installed by these edits.

**Open owner decisions for the selected repertoire refinements: none.** The feature/technical pair settles their context implications. SC09/SC11 qualifies complete, economical construction and informed choices on supported decisions. Broader gameplay and enjoyment remain under CE/CR/IS. Examples requiring unimplemented mechanics are optional projection/design contrasts until that mechanic is deliberately selected; they are not core SC release requirements. CTR's required independent physical counterattack remains with CTR, separate from general context comparisons permitting several coherent choices.

Focused baseline comparisons verify unchanged catalogue IDs/order/headings/metadata and the exact scoped edits. This is documentation verification, not a runtime capability audit, new automated game test, provider trial, source-population study or player result. No paid OpenLegend/Jev/provider calls were made: additional Jev cost $0; cumulative task Jev cost $0.
