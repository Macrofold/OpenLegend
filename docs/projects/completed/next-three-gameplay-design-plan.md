## Scope

The next three designs build on the [first playable](../first-playable-world-tech-design.md): useful inventions that other people can make their own, people whose experiences affect later choices, and expeditions with lasting physical consequences. Each has a feature specification, technical design, authored world profile, implementation tracker and limits inventory.

Empty-handed survival is already designed with the first playable, alongside finite known manufacture, the first useful invention, natural camps, a differentiated cast, voluntary cooperation and independent roaming. Those designs remain the immediate implementation assignment. Their existence does not establish that the complete game has been implemented or qualified.

## Player value

Apply [lasting player value](../../repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md#keep-play-worthwhile-as-players-learn-and-return) to arrival, preparation, waiting, work, stopping and returning.

An invention should improve a wanted activity or enable a useful choice. A larger bag can organize and transfer a coherent kit while the smaller original remains cheaper; it does not create carrying strength. Sharing gives another person an opportunity, and the player can value their use or criticism without receiving automatic admiration.

A relationship can remain interesting after a shared task. Quiet company, respectful rivalry, a new challenge, a changed method or wanting time alone can reveal what happened. The player can enjoy the encounter without waiting for a compulsory personality update. An assessment in a character's private notes is not proof of later behavior.

A finished expedition can remain finished. The road, workshop, learned methods, spent stock and changed choices can retain practical or personal value. Prevention need not summon a replacement threat, and a satisfying one-time discovery need not become recurring loot. Familiar-route and later-arrival journeys must be judged on their actual remaining opportunities.

The [player-facing language standard](../../ui-ux/player-facing-language.md) applies to controls, explanations, narration and generated dialogue. Explain the player's practical choice and consequences; internal protocol and state names belong in technical details.

## Scope and evidence boundaries

The [needs-design register](../../maintainers/needs-design.md) marks only the selected DG12/ND12, DG23/ND04 outlook/ND19 older-interpretation, and DG34 local Ash Road slices as designed. Implementation and acceptance remain open under LIB01–LIB09, EC01–EC04 and PS07.1/PS07.3–PS07.7. The source maps in each technical design distinguish existing owners from proposed additions.

No unresolved product choice blocks these selected implementations. Individual releases still require actual sufficient rights; the design grants none. D44's general contribution/free-use terms, public or commercial distribution, cross-operator trust, EC05 dreams/human aid, EC06 deliberate coarsening, PS07.2 advertised service, broader campaigns, new native families and whole alternate worlds retain their separate owners and adoption decisions.

The world profiles and limits inventories own selected quantities, units, work, clock behavior and constraints. These are design decisions, not measured balance or capacity. Native correctness, accessible interfaces, independently chosen conduct, uncoached enjoyment, lasting value and complete-operation cost require the focused implementation and qualification work.
