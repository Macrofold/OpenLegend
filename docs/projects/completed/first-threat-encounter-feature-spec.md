## Purpose and selected encounter

Make observing the wilderness, choosing a route and making useful equipment matter in one complete, optional episode. A territorial stag guards the shortcut to a torn cache of ordinary manufacturing supplies. The player can take a longer safe route, confront it, drive it away without killing, die and return, or leave the site alone while retaining worthwhile ordinary play. No raid, faction, deep ecology, NPC negotiation or universal combat framework is required.

| Choice             | Experience and cost                                                                                                                                 | Decision                                                               |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Territorial animal | Existing deer anatomy and native movement support a readable territorial motive, distance/cover tactics and finite aftermath without paid cognition | Selected; one authored individual rather than hostility for every deer |
| Hostile person     | Offers speech, motive and relationships, but introduces social context, human enemy decisions and NPC recovery/ghost dependencies                   | Defer until that experience is itself chosen                           |
| Defer danger       | Keeps peaceful play simpler and avoids lifecycle work, but leaves little dangerous reason to observe, move or invent                                | Rejected for this slice; peaceful alternatives remain                  |

Mike authorized full runtime delivery on October 4. The original October 3 design-only assignment remains historical context. His revisions deliberately replace consent-based trail entry and guaranteed warnings with physical signs, and collapse recovery with real death, half-type possessions and removable scars. [Player Danger](player-danger-feature-spec.md) and [Player Death](player-death-feature-spec.md) capture those accepted journeys; canonical policy belongs to their authored-world owners.

## Player journey

1. **Notice or miss danger.** Ordinary observation can reveal deep hoof tracks, damaged foliage and a heavy stag's posture near the optional shortcut. Looking reveals descriptions, not hidden aggression or a live-threat detector. Missing signs does not grant immunity; there is no entry modal, consent enrollment or compulsory inspection.
2. **Avoid.** Follow the longer route outside the aggression footprint, collect the same finite supplies and return. Actual stone geometry blocks movement/sight. Turn back or break sight to stop pursuit. Avoidance is a useful outcome, not a failed quest; supplies stay ordinary and available elsewhere.
3. **Prepare and confront.** Use existing known equipment or a genuinely admitted invention. Range and compatible ammunition matter; contact tools need proximity. No hidden required recipe, free prototype, inflated health, extra upkeep tax or automatic repeated attack creates difficulty. A command is one attempt, with actual range/accuracy/cost feedback.
4. **Respond.** The stag's antler preparation is a physical action, not a guaranteed earlier warning. Walk beyond reach or use cover. A received injury interrupts its preparation and preserves recovery. Sight, current reach and exact target life are checked at impact. A missed shot neither staggers it nor reveals an unseen shooter.
5. **Disengage.** The stag follows current sight or briefly searches its last sighting. It cannot follow hidden present coordinates, cross its pursuit bound or replenish the episode's route/time allowance. Minor wounds persist on return; sufficient injury causes permanent displacement to refuge. Failed return stops at its supported location without teleportation.
6. **Face aftermath.** Cache depletion, spent ammunition, wounds, displacement and death persist. A player death leaves an independently lootable body and retains half of carried types. Continue produces the same identity in a new physical life near a usable campfire with a treatable scar. Retrieve what remains or find that someone else took it. Killing leaves existing finite harvest; no ordinary revival or extra cache reward appears.
7. **Leave deliberately.** Reach a place safe enough for five normally simulated seconds before losing control. New animal attacks and lethal effects remain possible during fading. Unfinished human work stops; there is no automatic retreat. Inactive protection begins at the simulation deadline, and return keeps the real aftermath.

### Why invent or return?

Prepared fibers, cord and stones can support existing admitted containers, equipment and projectile manufacture. A ranged tool can reduce exposure; a fast contact tool can interrupt preparation. Actual native families/materials decide usefulness, not an invented name. The safe route remains available to a player with no weapon. Native/supplied-proposal support is distinct from a successful live-generated recipe; no provider call or PG01 runtime prerequisite is claimed.

The payoff is useful supplies brought home, a shorter route made safer and persistent bodily/world consequences. This is a one-time content slice, not proof of progression or repeated enjoyment. Later uncoached human play should determine whether signs, tuning and reward encourage a changed approach; this implementation does not invent a fun score.

## Human choices and tradeoffs

The owner selected the encounter, observational danger, type-based death loss, continuity/scars, one exact potentially lethal attack confirmation, no new indirect harm and five fully simulated seconds of vulnerable departure. Remaining details were explicitly delegated. The [technical decision list](first-threat-encounter-tech-design.md#concrete-owner-decision-list) records the chosen rounding, scar/treatment and reconnection behavior with alternatives Mike can revise. Cooperative PvP, property policy and inactive protection are preserved. Broader D07 deliberate-luring/indirect harm and BW15 NPC ghosts/ordinary revival remain open.

## Player feedback and discoverability

Use ordinary In view, selected-object inspection, inventory actions, current work, command results and World Events. Essential preparation, range, ammunition, death/Continue and scar treatment are textual and connected to the actual chosen target. An unseen target receives no current marker/health/range update. Death shows the player's retained and left-behind snapshot without giving a live corpse view. Continue shows “You stumble out of the woods...”. Character inspection names scars, consequences and enabled/refused treatment.

The final-blow dialog describes one attempt and persistent death, initially focuses Cancel, supports Escape, contains keyboard input and restores focus. It neither locks the target nor pauses mechanics. Duplicate confirmation cannot spend twice or authorize another body. Desktop/compact controls scroll without losing Continue/Cancel; existing accessible selection and movement work for keyboard/touch. Existing original deer artwork and simple attack preparation are honest first visuals; there is no bespoke charge/antler collision/audio asset claim. Faster simulation shortens cues, so normal-speed readability and accelerated accessibility are different claims.

## Acceptance for subsequent implementation

Stable criteria from the original design are retained below with Mike's authorized replacements for enrollment, collapse and wall-time departure. [Actual evidence and limitations](../../verification/first-threat-encounter.md) distinguish native, service, browser and performance checks; a written criterion is not itself a pass.

| ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

Preserve peaceful play and evaluate uncoached human learning separately from scripted qualification.                                                                                           |

## Delivery boundaries

The [implementation stages](first-threat-encounter-tech-design.md#conditional-implementation-breakdown) cover the whole experience, not independently releasable half-features. No runtime prerequisite or communication with PG01–PG04 was used. No new traps, factions, ecology, body dragging, general armor/skill framework, NPC ghost play or world-wide hostility service is delivered.
