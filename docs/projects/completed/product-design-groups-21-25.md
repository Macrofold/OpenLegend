## Product rules and scope

The task changes **zero runtime logic lines**. It has substantial product coordination risk: conflating bodily healing with death recovery; making agreements manufacture resources or consent; turning interpretation into false memory; exposing remote/private messages; and rewinding current permissions or real accounting with fictional history. The [engine/world boundary](../../engine-and-world-boundaries.md), [feature-documentation rules](../../feature-documentation.md), [gameplay priorities](../../repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md), and root instructions govern every proposal.

Begin with an activity worth doing and its actual payoff. Separate a complete smaller delivery from optional later families. Correctness, privacy, identity, actual resources, interruption, current-format restoration and truthful failure apply to every enabled capability. Scarcity, uncertainty, danger and loss can be worthwhile when they create desired stakes; they are not automatically required or automatically undesirable. Do not create a chore simply to justify another mechanic.

Keep world-specific materials, rates, effects, opportunities and wording with authored content. State which existing behavior is implemented, which target has been accepted, which choice is a new proposal, and which evidence remains unperformed. Preserve independent characters, permitted knowledge and real chosen actions; no hidden fallback or narration can stand in for a resident's consent or completed life. A proposed disposition of an open choice is not adoption of a runtime or commercial policy. Resolve any actual decision requiring the owner's input before dependent work.

## Final critique and resulting sequence

The immediate game priority remains a worthwhile outing and an independent character whose company, choices and work are interesting. None of these five additions substitutes for that experience. The parent's pacing clarification keeps actual human reading, composing and waiting inside CE05's ordinary-session evaluation: a mathematically complete system can still leave too little attention for play. These proposals neither silently retune that clock nor require later unattended-community funding.

### Changes made because of the critique

**Care must restore agency without manufacturing a supply chore.** The review chose free camp recovery and retained only the optional field dressing cost. Its one fiber is spent on valid completion, not cancellation. Survived ordinary injury, need pressure, scars and death remain distinct. The selected ten-second normal-speed recovery is a proposed first profile, not a demonstrated ideal duration. A cold or lit campfire is a clear place; it does not create enemy-scanning immunity. New-main participation evidence also made the restored-human interruption explicit: another person's Resume cannot finish care for an unavailable patient or helper.

**Agreements must earn their paperwork.** Current production time is exposed before proposing an economy. The design keeps obligation, custody, payment and knowledge separate but avoids making players manage a legal simulation. Ending an unfulfilled undertaking clears only the ending actor's own active-planning place. A counterpart who has not learned that fact keeps their known plan; real advance claims are not erased. There is no automatic unpaid-price debt for goods that were never supplied. Optional association stewardship grants record powers only, not invented property or platform authority.

**Characters need interpretation and agency more than transformation machinery.** Current mutable self-understanding and reflection are reused. Reading the same event repeatedly is not fresh corroboration; later testimony can be interpreted without becoming another independent witness. A person can generalize imperfectly, but the game must not fabricate supporting events. The first ordinary episode does not wait for new cooperative-item mechanics or simultaneous second-NPC spending. Dreams, optional older-detail coarsening and human reflection suggestions remain separate consumers, each with its own useful reason.

**Communication must help the outing without becoming an inbox obligation.** The finite spare makes the selected replacement-device journey possible. Submission confirms the sender's action without revealing delivery, blocking, reading or the recipient's condition. Text does not automatically trigger a model reply, repeated inbox draining or a second paid cadence. All NPC derivatives remain within the recipient's existing complete per-actor budget and private audience. The operational review corrected an earlier phrase that could have confused that per-actor admission with aggregate world cost.

**Correction must not become retcon, and restoration must be a coherent choice.** Seeing two utterances is not evidence that another listener knows their correction relationship or the speaker's identity, so the first shortcut's history links remain private to the correcting speaker. Creator edits retain their accepted no-automatic-semantic-memory-cascade policy; the existing administrative correction remains explicitly broad and distinct. Restore preserves current control, privacy restrictions, erasure and real accounting while rewinding fictional history together. The final review also distinguishes an unsubmitted draft from an admitted unaccepted offer: the latter is already a real offer event, even though no goods moved.

**Uncertainty and recovery must be truthful.** A restore review covers ordinary progress up to admission; normal movement does not endlessly invalidate it. Changed target/history, material binding scope or authority does. Current pre-load preservation is mandatory, and missing bound people remain a refusal. Expired command receipts yield unavailable earlier outcomes, not a claim that an action never happened or permission to resend. Restored native work follows current participation rules. No all-participant acknowledgement is required; each returning human still receives their own latest relevant result before new commands.

### Economics, growth and limits

The profiles expose local service cost without claiming that a small page or a rate limit proves overall capacity. Human camp recovery, ordinary agreement transitions, human text and correction do not require model reasoning merely to complete their native effects. Resident decisions and reflection use the existing admission and cost owners; refusal, interruption and spent/uncertain provider attempts remain real. Optional later extensions need a useful outcome before adding background work.

Text is the clearest accumulated-history risk. The [DG24 cost analysis](../world-text-messages-feature-spec.md#11-economics-performance-and-delivery-order) and [DG25 accumulated-history analysis](../corrections-and-shared-restoration-feature-spec.md#11-economics-attention-and-performance) explain the complete assumptions. At the selected maximum, 100 senders each submitting twenty-four 8,000-byte bodies in one real minute produce **19.2 MB of raw bodies per minute**, before metadata or other history. Dividing the current 256 MiB checkpoint-package bound by that envelope gives roughly **fourteen minutes**, not a measured endurance result or expected traffic forecast. The specs therefore require actual accumulated-history qualification under existing NC/data/SL owners. They do not hide growth behind short inbox pages, silently expire private history or invent a lifetime message allowance. Real production traffic, complete serialized requests, checkpoint records, capture time and load memory all remain evidence to collect in implementation.

The care, commission and text numbers are explicitly authored or proposed limits with reasons and consequences. They are not universal engine laws. Existing unresolved D-series decisions retain their statuses; proposed dispositions do not silently adopt currency, privacy, retention or commercial policies. The final source audit read newer main's death/Continue/scar and participation behavior without importing its runtime or save marker into this child branch.
