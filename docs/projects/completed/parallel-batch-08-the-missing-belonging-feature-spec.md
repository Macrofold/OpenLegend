## Experience and selection

A player reaches a shared work clearing and finds a missing satchel has caused a misunderstanding. They can question two people, inspect a physical note and a torn fastening, compare what they personally saw with what somebody said, find the actual bag and decide what to do with it. Returning later brings them back to the same people, possessions and remembered conduct. The interest is figuring out what happened and choosing how to treat the people involved, without a fight or an automatic verdict.

This selects [AD-076, The missing market purse — Core](../../repertoires/adventure-discovery.md), adapting its bounded investigation to the existing wilderness. [LKN129, The note that says who actually saw it — Complete](../../repertoires/languages-knowledge.md) supports clear source attribution. [Coverage](../../repertoires/coverage.md) ranks a meaningful independent character consequence as Core and further mysteries/destinations as Complete. These repertoire entries are proposals; this allocation supplies the particular incident and delivery definition, not proof of implementation or enjoyment.

The current game already supplies two expedition sites, finite gear, physical method learning, barter, outings, practice, fishing and editable open shelters. Another chain of directions leading to loot would duplicate that benefit. Here, partial accounts and physical comparison change the player's understanding; actual custody and consent determine the result. Batch 06 already owns the higher-priority combat work. Enclosed homes and full psychological-life design retain their unresolved choices; neither is a prerequisite or hidden addition here. Recent world-library and story-perspective proposals remain separate experiences.

MY01–MY05 are internal stages for **one agent**, not five communicating assignments. That agent must finish even if Batch 06 and every other new assignment never starts. This planning task authorizes documents and a future prompt only. The incident and new interface behavior below are proposed authored content; existing privacy, knowledge and custody contracts remain controlling.

## The authored incident

[The base-world profile](../../worlds/base/missing-belonging.md) owns the incident, Mara and Tavin's distinct partial understanding, the clearing/store, exact physical clues and finite satchel contents. The past is fixed: a mistaken relocation caused suspicion. Present speech, interpretation, custody and consent remain live. These circumstances are authored starting information, never fabricated runtime observations or a future-answer script.

The note and physical route permit recovery without a witness's cooperation. Witnesses offer interpretation and social consequences, not compulsory dialogue gates. If another visitor removes the bag or all useful clues, an unresolved or partial result is legitimate; the system does not recreate the original puzzle. No crime model, guaranteed confession, relationship improvement or automatic verdict follows from the incident.

## Complete player journey

1. **Enter and notice.** Reach the clearing by normal movement. The place, people and accessible objects appear through current perception. Mara's absence or refusal does not block exploration. A marker, the abandoned fastening or an ordinary conversation can attract attention; no forced opening speech or global missing-item alert is added.
2. **Ask or inspect.** Talk normally to either person. Each decides whether and how to answer using permitted starting understanding, actual perceptions and subsequent experience. Inspect the fastening or note deliberately. Merely seeing a container, selecting a history row or hearing a claim cannot reveal nested stock or award new knowledge.
3. **Compare.** From World Events, select any two eligible personal encounters or heard utterances with **Compare sources**. Their full permitted wording, source, acquisition and occurrence order appear together; narrow layouts stack them. This is a read-only view of existing evidence, not an editable case file. The game does not highlight the correct theory, infer guilt or mark a clue checklist complete.
4. **Follow an account and find something.** Walk toward the described store. It becomes a Known place only through the current arrival/perception owner. A remembered clue is not a live tracking signal. Inspect actual stock and identify the real satchel by its exterior; opening it remains a separate permission check.
5. **Keep, leave or offer.** The player can leave the bag, pick it up through ordinary access, use its supported storage or offer it to Mara. No ownership override, automatic reclaim or special Return command bypasses gift consent. Offer quantity is the actual whole bag and current contents. A refused/ignored offer leaves custody unchanged. A successful acceptance transfers that exact bag once.
6. **Revisit and discuss.** Later, either person can discuss the player's observed conduct or heard account through ordinary memory and conversation. A recipient can remember accepting the bag; an absent Tavin cannot know that return without observation or testimony. Remembering the player's kindness need not produce gratitude, trust, friendship or a particular future action.
7. **Continue after save/load.** Keep the same bag, moved clues, accepted knowledge and memories. A save after inspection does not restore missing stock; a save during an offer does not imply acceptance. No dialogue is rerun to recreate history.

## Evidence and interpretation

Show **Inspected**, **Heard**, or the existing applicable acquisition label. For a written note, say **Inspected a written account**: the player directly read those words, but their embedded assertions remain attributed claims. For speech, retain actual speaker identity only when recognized at the time and preserve the exact heard wording, including partial intelligibility. “Mara said she saw a bundle” is different from “Mara saw this satchel.”

The comparison may show an old observed location and time; it must label them as historical. It cannot fetch the object's hidden live position, its present holder, private witness notes or the author's incident definition. Renaming, removing or moving an object does not rewrite an older valid observation. Correction and forgetting use their existing owners and immediately remove or qualify affected comparison content, including pending reads.

A player can express a theory in normal speech. That records the player's statement and what listeners actually heard; it is not a structured verdict. NPCs may revise their own understanding through existing note/reflection admission. No new psychological law, satisfaction meter, relationship score, paid comparison call or mystery-specific inference engine is introduced.

## Presentation and ordinary character behavior

Use the existing World Events panel, Talk, inventory/contents, object inspection and Known places. **Compare sources** selects two existing source references, preserving search, scroll position and the conversation draft. Each comparison shows source/time/acquisition first, readable text second and source navigation last. Current object actions remain in ordinary inspection and inventory, separately checked; a remembered row is never a live object control.

Use shared React Aria controls and current panel/sheet patterns. Preserve keyboard selection, visible focus, source navigation, enlarged text, reduced motion and narrow/short layouts. Empty, partial, loading, stale, forgotten and unavailable sources have distinct truthful states. Close returns focus without walking or sending speech. Two compared records are a display choice; all retained eligible history remains reachable through existing paging/search.

Residents use the ordinary cognition, speech, knowledge and physical action paths. No actor-ID answer table, mandatory goal sequence, forced acceptance, hidden answer prompt or automatic change of opinion is allowed. Their identities contain tendencies and personal history; current circumstances belong in actual state and admitted understanding. The independent day, new psychological needs and continuous social simulation of D69 remain outside this finite witnessed-consequence consumer.

## Completion and meaningful failure

Completion requires the entire ordinary journey above, not only seeded scenery or a correctly stored clue. MY05 owns the combined evidence:

- A player can investigate from witnessed accounts and physical evidence, recover the actual bag, make a real custody choice and revisit the result. Separately demonstrate a useful investigation when a witness refuses.
- Actual live resident choices show permitted partial knowledge, a response to new contrary evidence, voluntary acceptance or refusal, and later conversation grounded in an observed or heard result. No required outcome is supplied as a future-answer instruction. Native controls separately establish every custody/privacy branch; one fluent reply does not establish reliable character quality.
- Two observers gain different evidence; an absent listener does not receive speech, private stock, the incident's explanation or an unseen return. A moved or taken bag is not located through history. A duplicate offer cannot duplicate the bag.
- Comparison and history rereads create no new awareness, stock or dedicated model work; repeated physical inspection records actual observations without replaying the authored past. Corrected/forgotten sources, identical display names, source removal, lost access and stale asynchronous reads remain safe and understandable.
- Current-format database restart and save/load preserve existing consequences and reject malformed references without migration or silent repair. Return after a pending or refused offer remains distinct from accepted transfer.
- Compare actual complete read/command/context work, including cold evidence, search continuation, source invalidation and a longer history. Record model costs, unpriced/uncertain commitments, behavioral failures and observed limits under current verification policy. No scale, balance or human-enjoyment claim follows from fixtures.

An engineer-controlled walkthrough can establish discoverability and the usefulness of comparison. Uncoached enjoyment remains separately labeled if no such participant is available; it cannot stand in for the required browser and live-character journeys. Required implementation or verification gaps remain unfinished assignment work.

## Decisions and exclusions

The selected incident has a fixed authored past, but present conduct and outcomes are live. This reduces mystery-generation complexity while preserving player initiative and independent residents. The physical trail remains usable without compelled disclosure, at the cost of not guaranteeing that every visitor receives every account. Returning the bag uses ordinary consent, at the cost of allowing refusal or an unresolved offer.

This scope adds neither combat nor a quest engine, hidden conclusion flag, compulsory itinerary, criminal law, reputation score, locks, door motion, household psychology, generated art, world creation, invention-library import or a new narration permission. Those features do not block this assignment. No consequential owner choice is currently open for the selected existing-law incident; any newly necessary world law must be resolved before dependent work rather than smuggled into the implementation.
