## Accepted direction and purpose

The bundled world is deliberately dangerous. Mike chose observation and precautions instead of encounter consent or warning prompts: players who miss an aggressive stag or its signs can be attacked and killed. Tracks, damaged foliage, actual preparation, movement and useful equipment make the danger learnable. Add stronger warnings later only if real play finds the physical evidence too difficult. [Player danger and departure](../../worlds/base/player-danger.md) is the current policy owner.

The optional stag/cache has a real safe longer approach, finite observed pursuit and persistent aftermath. It does not force combat for starting materials. Ordinary residents are excluded from its initial territorial targets; active/fading players can be chosen independently from actual sight. Direct cooperative PvP remains denied. Incidental danger near another active player is possible; deliberate luring, new indirect damaging families and broader D07/PS policy are separate unresolved scope, not universally authorized by removing consent.

## Leaving and lethal choices

After logout/control loss, the character fades while fully vulnerable for five normally simulated seconds. New animal attacks can start and death can occur. Unfinished human work/rest stops, with no automatic retreat/defense, new absent-player ammunition spending or paid cognition. Pause/navigation preparation/downtime do not consume exposure. Expiry wins a tied discrete hit and then ordinary inactive protection applies. Returning control cancels the fade with actual wounds/location preserved; a subsequent genuine departure starts another window. Continue after death is explicit.

Potentially lethal human attacks receive one final-blow-style review. It identifies the observed target and chosen attack/tool/range/timing/ammunition, explains death, initially focuses Cancel and supports Escape/focus restoration. It does not pause the world. Confirmation covers one exact physical life and attempt, never a same-named replacement or continuing permission. No universal incapacitation/one-health clamp/separate execution framework is added. The world keeps ordinary finite death/remains and leaves BW15 ghost/summoning/ordinary revival open.

## Acceptance and tradeoffs

Check signs without hidden intent disclosure, missed signs with real harm, no consent guard, real avoidance/confrontation/death/return, a newly started animal attack and lethal result during fade, exact expiry tie, pause/navigation/restart/reconnect/multiple-tab invariants, stale target/source/control/tool, cancellation/miss/concurrent injury, keyboard/compact modal/death feedback and scoped witnesses. Enemy cognition remains native and sensing locally filtered. [Evidence](../../verification/first-threat-encounter.md) separates executed checks from later uncoached balance/hosted/lower-end qualification.

Mike delegated interrupted-human-work handling, expiry precedence and fresh exposure after a genuine return. These favor predictable finite protection and playable reconnection, with a residual deliberate-disconnect/pause advantage rather than a claim of competitive fairness. [Selected alternatives](first-threat-encounter-tech-design.md#concrete-owner-decision-list) are reviewable; no further owner permission gates block this runtime slice.
