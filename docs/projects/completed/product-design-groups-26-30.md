## Product rules and scope

This task affects **zero runtime logic lines**. Its main risks are product and contract coordination: audio disclosing words a listener cannot know; fictional time becoming dependent on playback; confusing access, invention allowance and real cost; promising unfunded service or permanence; paying twice or reallocating money without a defined basis; exporting private world history with reusable content; and making travel or creator administration more work than the experience is worth.

Read the root instructions and applicable design, documentation, prioritization, review, UI, verification and engine/world guidance. Consult each group's maintained contracts, focused trackers, limits, prior product decisions and linked research before drafting. Every new specification must include purpose, complete human/character/creator journeys, meaningful failure and recovery, supported scope, later optional families, economics and growth, primary-source research, acceptance criteria, staged delivery and maintained records.

Start from a wanted game moment or service outcome. Preserve useful invention, independent people, ordinary competence, player choice and consequences. Supporting mechanics must earn their explanation, operating cost and player effort. No group number establishes implementation priority; a small invited game may remain preferable to media, a public marketplace, large populations or a grant program.

Keep reusable runtime integrity separate from authored-world communication, travel and content rules. World-specific choices belong to their world owner. Protect permitted knowledge, current permissions, real accounting and truthful interrupted/uncertain results across retries, disconnection, revocation and current-format save/restore. Reuse existing owners rather than inventing parallel billing, privacy, cognition or task systems.

Research findings, design inferences, proposed defaults, owner-approved policies, implemented behavior and measured evidence remain distinct. Illustrative economics are assumptions for comparison, not current provider prices or commitments. No outreach, purchase, subscription, payout, public launch or binding governance change is authorized by this design assignment. Routine proposal choices are made within the task; genuinely necessary unanswered owner decisions must be resolved before dependent work.

## Final game-first critique

The central recommendation is to preserve five useful stopping points. The completed designs are not a requirement to implement an entire communications, commerce, federation and governance stack before another person can enjoy the game. A modest implementation can improve the existing play loop without first building a research platform, analytics program or proof of market demand. Actual runtime, service and commercial qualification is required when claiming those capabilities, at their offered scope.

| Group | First complete outcome worth building                                                                         | Expansion earns its place when                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| DG26  | Hear permitted committed words or compose an editable dictated sentence during an ordinary exchange.          | A live call improves coordination over notes; timed speech creates a clear physical choice; raw voice can preserve its actual disclosure and accessible participation contract.                      |
| DG27  | One host pays for an enjoyable attended world whose advertised invention and resident behavior actually work. | Customers understand the offer, voluntarily renew, and complete cost and operator effort support the service; additional memberships, continued worlds or dedications have their own wanted benefit. |
| DG28  | Reuse a useful eligible invention in another supported world without reconstructing it.                       | A curated paid pack saves worthwhile creative effort and its complete rights, recovery and payout obligations can be honored.                                                                        |
| DG29  | Walk with normal belongings to another real camp, talk or demonstrate something useful, and return normally.  | The wanted encounter repays transfer preparation; compatible bodies, belongings, clocks and funded recovery support the advertised route.                                                            |
| DG30  | Fund one or two genuinely useful contributions or honest explorations through a finite manual round.          | Their value justifies application/review burden and the full settled operating budget; another round or software addresses an observed need.                                                         |

No group number changes project priority. Small local creative play, independent resident choices and clear ordinary controls remain the central experience. A marketplace cannot make an unwanted kit useful, physical travel cannot manufacture an interesting destination, and a grant council cannot substitute for a game people want to improve. Existing open directions remain available without becoming compulsory prerequisites.

### Changes made after critique

The reviews changed concrete behavior and coverage, rather than adding a general warning about complexity:

- **Calls and local engagement:** the current native directed-speech rule can enroll an addressed human. The optional active-call profile now explicitly proposes the necessary exception: preserve the nearby utterance's ordinary heard evidence without automatic local enrollment, merging, call termination, an answer or disclosure of private call status. The player chooses to hang up and respond; earlier local speech does not replay into a new membership. The feature, world profile, NC23.2, CA-L01 and acceptance journey agree. Pending requests still withdraw when their disclosed local context changes.
- **Offer honesty and recovery:** the first sold world must actually support its advertised invention and resident experience. Invention units remain separate from supplier cost and retail AI debit authority. The first-service refund covers eligible charges spanning a partial calendar term, renewal or upgrade without refunding a prior adjustment twice. One-term buyers receive the same eligible first-service remedy. Recognition has a defined display/recovery promise instead of an undefined permanence claim.
- **Creator choice without attendance work:** premium worlds qualify uniformly for the one existing subscriber allocation when otherwise eligible. Remembered choices avoid a monthly chore; no duration or activity grind creates money. Ordinary authorized participation through a world's separate bound character qualifies without DG29 physical travel or fused histories. Refund examples preserve the platform's processor-fee exposure, agreed creator shares and monetary remainders. Grant rights, attribution and actual payee authority stay distinct.
- **A visit before a transfer process:** DG29's first named story now shows a complete same-world outing before the restricted cross-world proof. Normal belongings remain usable. The queue keeps source play useful, separates the short ready-place offer from untimed review of changed terms, and bounds repeated held-slot churn. Conflicting destination character bindings are refused rather than overwritten. Even a social visit leaves external memories/records, so unsupported pre-trip restoration remains refused.
- **Payment for honestly completed work:** DG30's start payment is an earned nonrepayable grant under its stated conditions, distinct from unused refundable expense money. An operator cannot delay review and cancel to replace a completed milestone with a cheaper allowance. Original-criteria review and the full potentially earned amount remain reserved; any allowance uses only the remaining unearned award. Payment submission, settlement and original-attempt recovery have separate meanings. Advisory roles have finite consent, withdrawal and conflict remedies.

The inherited call sending rate may still make a live exchange less useful than notes. This is visible in its acceptance and stop decision, with a shared-policy revision required before promising rapid live chat. The restricted itemless travel proof may be inconvenient enough to keep internal until the carried-bundle stage is ready. The camp kit remains free if its organization/sharing advantage is weak. The fund's invented $5,000 example sends only 40% to awards and exposes 51 paid operating hours plus separately consented advisor effort; it explicitly permits a simpler commission or no round. These are reasons to simplify or stop the optional capability, not excuses to add more mechanics around it.

### Economics, scale and enduring obligations

Each proposal accounts for the complete promise: media generation and delivery, distinct listener renderings, failed/uncertain work, live and retained worlds, creator liabilities, refunds, payment fees, recovery and operator time. A short queue or small per-turn limit does not bound aggregate work or lifetime history. Stop unsupported new optional work before required play or accepted evidence fails. Never claim scale by dropping entitled listeners, weakening people, deleting inconvenient history or counting queued users as delivered participants.

The accepted concentrated shared-world targets remain intact: the first 100 humans, 100 agents, 100 animals and 1,000 objects with half of each in one scene, and growth toward 10,000 simultaneous humans with 200 humans/agents together in a scene. The designs do not measure or certify them. Many small worlds, mature histories, converging visitors and correlated return/recovery require additional whole-workload evidence; they cannot replace the concentrated target.

The commercial comparison preserves separate service periods. A held world's retrieval, a dedication's public display and an acquired pack's managed download have different promised durations and remedies. Static authorized files or public records can outlast live simulation. Customer AI value, refund obligations, unallocated subscriber earmarks and earned creator proceeds are not discretionary fund revenue. World restore never mints a second payment, restores revoked authority, duplicates a traveler or undoes valid open rights.
