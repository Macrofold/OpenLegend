## Scope and completion

Mike requested complete design of the required [first-playable world](../../worlds/base/playable-openings.md#first-playable-game-target), including every necessary subsystem, detailed implementation tasks and reconciliation of existing architecture, design, gameplay priorities and research. The user delegates selection of the best coherent design. World policy/content should use typed YAML definitions wherever useful; narrowly reviewed native mechanics may fill missing computational capabilities. Authored personalities, backstories and traits are valid; scripted interactions, predetermined choices, forced encounters and hidden player tracking are excluded.

This is design-only work: zero executable logic lines are planned. The main risk is inconsistent cross-system contracts, duplicated world policy and falsely closing implementation or acceptance. The work is large enough to require this durable plan. ## Design principles and decisions to settle

- A useful known route and an invention route must both work through ordinary resources and actions. No canned invention disguised as generation.
- Empty-handed survival must reach a meal/tool/fire without circular recipes or guaranteed NPC aid; decisions must leave room for human reading and provider delay.
- Home construction adds useful site, layout and defensive choices. Barriers act through real geometry; there is no invisible safe zone.
- Four differentiated initially non-hostile people plus existing hostile identities preserve individual knowledge, motives, refusal and withdrawal. Relationship effects follow actual experienced events.
- Resources, routes and independent motives create encounters. No raid timer, spawn-on-player, forced alliance, compulsory dialogue or predetermined persuasion.
- World-owned YAML selects content, numerical tuning, authored affordances and registered mechanic/controller kinds. Protected authority, exact custody, identity, transactions and perspective remain engine/application responsibilities.
- Saved gameplay stays current-format only under the protected development-save policy. Restoration never regenerates content or grants stock.
- Each selected new record/process declares its owner, invalidation, bounded candidates, meaningful wake-ups, cancellation/removal and restore behavior.
