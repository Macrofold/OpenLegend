## Planning, guidance and material risks

The task changes **zero runtime logic lines**. It needed a durable plan because family continuity, authority, private experience, obligations, campaign timing and device/offline promises cross several existing contracts. Root instructions were read first, with repository design, documentation, prioritization, UI, review, verification, AI, performance and engine/world guidance applied where relevant. Related owners and their cited requirements supplied the baseline; catalogue examples and historical research supply possibilities, not an adopted constitution or runtime evidence.

Each group starts with a wanted activity and useful outcome. Preserve a complete survival-adventure with useful invention, independent people, understandable opposition, worthwhile rewards, recovery and remembered consequences. A peaceful relationship or creative pleasure can also be worthwhile. New population, administration, magic, global events and alternate clients must justify the attention and operating cost they add. Group order selects this design assignment, not the next implementation priority.

Keep one owner for each meaning. Biological ancestry is not permission or learned knowledge; a fictional office is not platform power; a mental effect is not unrestricted access to another mind; a campaign forecast is not a guarantee that every local participant will appear; an installed client is not an offline world. Preserve truthful incomplete outcomes, current permissions, exact accounting, actual retained experience and current-format save integrity. Keep replaceable world laws distinct from reusable integrity requirements.

Research records distinguish documented behavior, developer reports, evidence of defects, marketing claims and Open Legend design inferences. Historical and living sources retain clear dates and access limits. Illustrative arithmetic is not a measured performance result or a commercial promise. Each feature considers aggregate attention, service work and retained history as well as per-action limits.

The per-group process was:

1. Read current owners, accepted targets, earlier proposals, open decisions and relevant gameplay coverage; identify the smallest complete wanted experience and actual gaps.
2. Research varied primary sources, including documented failures and simplifications, and connect evidence to product choices without claiming another game's outcome proves this design works.
3. Specify player, NPC, creator and operator journeys; knowledge and authority; normal results, refusal, interruption, departure and recovery; economics, attention, scale and meaningful limitations.
4. Separate the first complete capability from optional later families. Record world-specific proposed choices in their owner and discretionary constraints in the relevant inventory. Preserve existing delivery IDs and unsatisfied acceptance.
5. Review for fun, ordinary competence, meaningful choice, unnecessary systems, unfunded promises and contradictions; fix findings and commit each reconciled group before beginning the next independent group.

## Reviewed independent scope of group 31

[Generations and family life](../generations-and-family-life-feature-spec.md) retains the complete adult-family episode, deliberately authored starting ages, ordinary voluntary help, actual food and operating arithmetic, fourteen original primary research records, failure/recovery journeys and a game-first critique. The initial scope preserved the PG generational ambition while waiting for D16 rather than replacing it with the easier adult-only encounter.

The original review removed automatic frailty from the first scenario, avoided a household administration layer, and distinguished calendar chronology from bodily participation and actual experience. Later travel must preserve originating calendars and count elapsed intervals once; dead-person descriptions preserve age at death rather than invent off-screen life. The reviewers corrected two historical source paraphrases and separated first-scene qualification from the future mature-world workload, so a small useful encounter does not wait for a new scale program. Existing family facts, checked delivery items and current protection/accounting remain intact.

The October 14 [childhood and population chapter](../generations-and-family-life/childhood-and-population.md) finishes the previously dependent design and adds two official EA research records. Sex, reproductive intent and caregiving responsibility are separate choices. Either adult can decline or stop intimacy; it creates no automatic pregnancy, future consent, affection reward or claim over another person. The presentation uses a non-explicit scene, opaque heart or fade rather than an explicit asset behind a removable censor. Children have their own family and learning experiences, never sexual roles.

The first growing-family proposal uses an isolated attended world, one human adult, an unrelated willing adult resident and one dependent child. It defines actual pregnancy, a supported once-only birth, finite newborn reserves, age-appropriate food, feeding, comfort, physical carrying and handoff, independent interests, adoption, separation and adulthood. Condensed developmental stages are a proposed authored-body option using the current game clock, not a rewritten calendar or invented years of memories. The exact durations remain untested tuning. Existing adult ages do not inherit this developmental acceleration.

The final care review made competence concrete. Observable hunger cues lead to actual food and accepted care, with no per-tick nagging or telepathic family dashboard. Feed rechecks prevent two caregivers spending portions for the same stale need. A resident may independently renew finite care rather than forcing the human to repeat a command every few minutes. Predictable native steps in an accepted plan need no separate model call. Withdrawal does not automatically assign another parent, and stopping a care plan cannot drop a physically supported child. Real lowering or handoff is required; an unsupported physical transition stops the isolated world for honest recovery rather than teleporting a child.

The proposal adds no separate neglect countdown, automatic child removal or hidden invulnerability. Actual ordinary body hazards remain distinct from unavailable execution/service, which uses the selected coherent isolated-world stop. Real food shortage or an informed NPC refusal is not a provider outage. This distinction preserves both meaningful choices and truthful recovery without a selective public-world rescue rule.

The full food analysis counts pregnancy, all selected stages and the eventual adult. Existing illustrative patch renewal does not sustain the offered mature family by itself. Finite authored provisions demonstrate only their stated interval; longer operation must have real supply and funded service. Offscreen growth, child labor, automatic supplies, a growing family of endlessly billed minds and permanent free care are not substitutes for that evidence. GEN04's design preparation is complete; GEN05–GEN07 and all unperformed implementation/experience qualification remain open.

## Reviewed product scope of group 32

[Shared equipment and scoped institutions](../shared-equipment-and-institutions-feature-spec.md) selects an optional Clearing Tool Library: one real useful bow, an individual owner, a willing delegated keeper, an expressly accepted fallback and a guest borrower. It adds exact lending and keeper-independent return through a genuine new PO consumer, with real reserved room, enduring narrow access, privacy, ordinary exit, loss and winding-down. Its twelve primary sources compare published patterns, historical tradeoffs and documented corrected failures. The first unsecured, no-deadline outing needs no currency, permanent membership, corporate title or collateral.

The review removed compulsory membership, separated bow authority from depot authority, and kept ordinary owner absence compatible with a keeper's valid mandate. It corrected a privacy leak in role-exit accounting: the owner retires their own active places uniformly, while actual claims, item restrictions and return rights remain. Optional security uses an explicitly elected, owner-agreed buyout; title to a lost bow is settled before a later recovery, and the old restriction/return-room commitment ends with actual settlement. No private role change grants hidden knowledge, no closure duplicates stock, and no fictional obligation reaches real platform funds. Game-first acceptance compares this service with simpler gift, barter or personal acquisition before expanding it.

The review removed a fixed manifestation timer and an immediate hunger/exhaustion penalty after revival. Ordinary eating need not end an attended visit. A last-known candidate list also cannot disclose unseen revival or private refusal by removing a name. The thirteen primary records preserve historical versus current rules, developer rationale versus measured results and reported corrections versus current defects. Cost examples count failed/uncertain attempts, cold recall, actual listeners, private derivatives and normal living needs. ## Reviewed product scope of group 34

[Shared campaigns with local opportunities](../shared-campaigns-feature-spec.md) selects the optional Ash Road: two real local undertakings in one world, finite relay sources, two usable road gates and one unique nonliving Warden. Players can open a gate immediately, secure it by disconnecting its relay, or use the other community's actual work to shut down the same adversary. Complete prevention is a real canonical outcome. A separately qualified operation makes one fixed-target physical attempt with a finite charge and active budget; a later player meets the actual changed place, not a regenerated personal boss.

The review corrected an initially empty stake: a machine cannot meaningfully threaten to close a gate that is already locked shut. Gates are now usable while connected, making quick access, secured access and a later setback distinct. It also removed a mandatory heavy-object recovery dependency, retained a real bypass around the spent machine, accepted harmless physical counterplay and fixed the destination at launch. The first profile threatens route access, so its fiction cannot claim an unimplemented massacre or introduce new human harm.

Forecasts distinguish real notice and a finite activation-service window from the actual body's conditional arrival. Immediate local work never waits for the advertised window. One differently timed replacement does not create a second operation, an earlier advertised bound cannot be weakened by new notice, and expired windows never launch on reconnect. Resident reservations explicitly promise entry rather than witnessing the brief operation. Early prevention does not erase an independently accepted entry or hosting commitment. Public fixture states and optional attribution remain separate from private actions and NPC knowledge, with no personal name published by default.

The twelve primary records include current guides, dated developer rationale and documented historical failures, with no invented measured results. The economic comparison counts actual living needs, finite native work, failed/uncertain workflows, growing records, recovery and operator time. A staffed two-hour offer can cost far more than its short physical operation; the design therefore keeps the local cooperative adventure independently complete and allows the advertised spectacle to be omitted. Intelligent city-by-city adversaries, damaging sieges, cross-world fronts and mature-scale claims remain separately useful and qualified expansions under the existing PS07/PS08 owners.

The original ten-source review and two independent reviews support these distinctions. ## Final critique across the batch

The finished proposals keep the smallest worthwhile experience first: adult companionship before population management; an unsecured useful loan before collateral or a government; a wanted conversation before an optional difficult revival; and useful road work before an advertised campaign operation. None requires implementing every other group first. Peaceful play, ordinary competence, a clever solution, refusal and a quiet aftermath remain valid outcomes. A local example supplies a concrete consumer, not evidence for the project's larger population and world ambitions.

The review removed automatic frailty, mandatory membership, a timed farewell, an immediate post-revival needs penalty and mandatory heavy-machine hauling. It repaired the campaign's initially empty gate threat and made full prevention a real win. The shared operation cannot manufacture another boss or erase an accepted entry promise when players solve the problem early. Late players inherit a changed place rather than a copied personal victory. The owner-selected browser direction keeps distribution expansion from delaying the existing game.

Across groups, age remains distinct from lived experience, property from custody, commitments from private knowledge, individual choice from account power and fictional progress from current permission or real bills. Costs include actual food and time, failed or uncertain model work, cold history, growing records and operator attention. Affordable small scenes do not establish affordable background populations or concentrated shared events. Review retains the ambitious later extensions with their actual selection and qualification requirements, rather than turning them into prerequisites for one useful adventure.

The October 9 independent contract and game reviews, combined cross-batch review and affected-file review found no remaining material issue in their finished independent scope. Their corrections moved ghost and telepathy notes under the actual design needs, removed wording that implied unperformed campaign proof, and aligned the gate-opening duration inventory. Those observations remain historical evidence for unchanged designs, not a new runtime assessment.

The October 14 continuation resolves the previously unfinished family and platform scope. Its final review keeps optional sex separate from parenthood; supports an already-authored family visit without waiting through pregnancy; preserves independent care rather than fixed obedient workers; and prevents care-plan expiry or a stale transfer from losing actual support or duplicating food. Condensed development, food sources and current ordinary harms remain explicit proposals. No fertility grinding, childcare furnishing tax, daily affection reward or compulsory child labor is introduced. A larger family or a longer service must earn and fund its actual added obligations.
