# Platform and offline decisions

The [Unreal Engine 5 migration](unreal-engine-5-migration-feature-spec.md) selects an installed desktop client as the direction for ordinary play. PlayCanvas remains the running player until the replacement passes its cutover gates. Retained browser tools, additional player platforms and offline simulation have separate requirements. The [completed DG35 brief](completed/platform-and-offline-decision-brief.md) preserves the earlier browser-first decision and its evidence. [ND34](../maintainers/needs-design.md#nd34--additional-distribution-platforms-and-offline-play) tracks the scope of that change.
