# Extraordinary minds and life after death

**Status:** In progress — product and behavior proposal for DG33, written 2026-10-09 UTC against the inspected `fffdafcf3f7b92d63e1a43e551627cde67708dea` baseline. Two independently selectable authored families are developed here: voluntary contact with a retained deceased NPC, including a separately gated ordinary return to life; and selective voluntary telepathy. Adoption, technical design, implementation and measured qualification remain open. Human recovery is independent of both families.

## 1. Two experiences worth having

### 1.1 Show a familiar person what happened next

Eren is a known adult NPC who died. The player knew Eren through a legitimate authored acquaintance and actual play, not a newly disclosed global name. They had spoken about a useful bag the player hoped to make and an outing they might take. At the Stillwater Cairn, an ordinary reachable place prepared for this optional world law, the player can invite Eren to appear. Eren may accept, decline or leave. A successful invitation lets the player show the real finished object, tell a story, ask a question or simply spend a little time with this particular person.

The payoff need not be a secret location, quest reward or mandatory farewell. Eren may be pleased, skeptical, distracted, still angry about something, or interested in news. The player can discover that the relationship has a future without pretending the death did not happen. Eren can learn from this conversation and remember it at a later permitted encounter. This is the same retained person acquiring a new experience, not a recording that recites a fixed final message.

Conversation is also a complete experience if neither party wants revival. The player can say goodbye, end early or never return. There is no loneliness meter ticking in an invisible afterlife, accumulating guilt for the absent player, compulsory forgiveness or reward for summoning every day. A ghost can want to be left alone. Ordinary grief, memory and enjoyment remain possibilities rather than diagnoses of a human user.

An independently selected harder undertaking can later make Eren alive again. The player chooses an actual expedition to recover one finite return resource, brings it to an eligible place and offers a return Eren can still refuse. That journey should be memorable for its route, choices and reunion. It must not become daily corpse maintenance, a timer that permanently closes the opportunity, or a long arbitrary list of interchangeable materials.

### 1.2 Coordinate with someone across an actual obstruction

In a separate episode, a player and a willing adult companion want to approach a known place from different sides. Ordinary geometry genuinely prevents them hearing one another clearly. Before separating, they agree to a temporary thought link. Each can deliberately send a short message: “I found a clear path on this side,” “Wait; I am turning back,” or “I would rather stop.” The recipient receives that chosen communication, not the sender's private thoughts, current camera, inventory or every perceived event.

This can be useful with an NPC partner, for whom DG24's first human-only slate is not a substitute. It requires no claim that current animals respond to whispers, no newly invented stealth advantage and no compulsory combat. A simple scouting or meeting problem is enough. If ordinary speech already solves it comfortably, the extra link is unnecessary. If a slate or an ordinary outing is better, use that. Magical appearance is not a reason to duplicate an existing service without a different benefit.

The families are independent. A world may allow conversation with the dead while having no telepathy, or allow willing mental messages while treating death differently. They do not establish a universal magic language, a spirit economy, a shared controller or mind-reading. A family is offered only after its actual capability, authority, costs, evidence and interruption behavior are supported.

## 2. What identity and permission mean here

[NPC lifecycle direction](../worlds/base/lifecycle-and-protection.md#npc-death-ghosts-and-revival) already accepts retained identity, history and relationships after death, dormant outside active world participation, summoning that can support conversation and a difficult ordinary revival route. Exact manifestation and revival behavior is unresolved BW15 work. This proposal selects those details; it does not claim ghosts are already implemented.

The inspected bundled branch still has the documented human collapse distinction. DG21 separately identifies newer-main human death/Continue work at `0a3ab79b7a698a7f1941dc23722f89220d1ba425`. That later source describes one retained human identity, a new physical life on Continue and a separate corpse without copied mind/control. It is a named dependency, not a silent change to this branch. Human Continue does not summon an NPC ghost, require a return ember or force the human to accept a new character, allegiance or past. Broader dangerous-logout and lethal-consent choices remain with their existing owners.

Creator revival is another distinct power. Current architecture permits an authorized creator to reconstruct a body, including after harvesting, with an explicit administrative resource-fairness exception. Ordinary revival does not inherit free item reconstruction or replenished harvest rights. The selected ordinary family must account for the original remains, belongings and prior yields even when the corpse has decayed away. A new physical life is not a second historical person.

[EX05/EX06](../extensible-world-examples.md#ex05--a-compel-spell-that-affects-an-actors-goals) and [the world-module boundary](../../archive/07-technical-architecture/world-module-runtime.md#fictional-mental-effects-and-operational-ownership) provide room for mental effects; they do not enable them. Influence, enforced compulsion and permanent mental alteration have different meanings. This proposal selects voluntary disclosure of chosen messages. It supplies no authority to mutate another person's goals, memories or human input. Later stronger effects need their own actual target-owned behavior and explicit eligibility, resistance and control policy.

Four distinctions apply throughout:

| Distinction                                 | Product meaning                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Person and embodiment                       | The same retained identity may be dormant, temporarily manifest or ordinarily living under a supported law. There is at most one active embodiment/controller of that person. |
| World occurrence and knowledge              | An event can occur without this person perceiving it. Retained identity is not surveillance or universal recall.                                                              |
| Communication and disclosure of a mind      | A chosen message is attributable testimony. It does not expose raw private context or prove what the sender thinks.                                                           |
| Fictional authority and platform permission | A spell, relationship, summon or new body cannot override current private grants, account control, forgetting, spending or world access.                                      |

The first ghost is an adult speaking NPC whose existing capability can support the conversation. A dead animal does not acquire human language, a verbal mind or a new species merely because the same summoning framework is installed. The first telepathic pair are already known people capable of deliberate message assent and composition. No absent human is silently connected, no characterless account is promoted, and no NPC privately bound to another human becomes an autonomous substitute.

Recognition must be real. [Current identity rules](../worlds/base/knowledge.md#identity-and-recognition) support continuous encounters and explicitly authored acquaintances; a saved source identifier alone does not identify a new exposure. The selected Eren episode deliberately supplies the acquaintance. Other witnesses see/hear only their own permitted label and detail. A ghost's self-introduction is a claim under the supported introduction rule, not automatically an unforgeable identity certificate. A broader supernatural recognition ability would be a separate declared disclosure rule, not an incidental side effect of summoning.

## 3. A deceased person remains a person, not a background worker

### 3.1 Dormant continuity

After actual NPC death, retain the person's permitted identity, learned capabilities, relationships, commitments and usable memory under their existing owners. Stop active bodily work, perception and ordinary cognition. No invisible ghost walks around observing later conversations, tends a shelter, earns skills, dreams, eats or pays recurring model costs. No repeated summoning opportunity schedules an automatic paid check. Dead retention consumes finite storage and applicable service costs, but not a simulated second life.

The current body's remains have their actual world lifecycle: rotting after three game days and cleanup after seven in the bundled profile. Belongings follow the real death/custody transition, not the future ghost. Neither disappearance of the corpse nor loss of a carried keepsake deletes the retained identity. Equally, retention does not guarantee every old detail survives memory policy or privacy removal. A person who has forgotten something in life does not become omniscient in death.

When Eren next has a legitimate manifestation opportunity, relevant retained context can be prepared under the normal bounded evidence and funding rules. Time outside participation is not narrated as lived waiting, loneliness, travel or off-screen observation. Eren may learn that a long interval passed from actual testimony or a permitted current clock observation. They cannot describe everything that happened while dormant. Previously acquired knowledge of an appointment is different from witnessing that appointment occur.

No complete pre-death goal snapshot is blindly resumed. Eren's former aspirations may matter, but the manifested body has limited capabilities and the present situation may have changed. A promise remains a real record with its actual status; a retained wish to make a bow cannot cause a ghost to produce one. Later ordinary revival enables new choices against the current world rather than restarting a stale physical plan.

### 3.2 Invite one particular known person

At the actual anchor the player chooses a legitimately known deceased NPC and reviews what is offered: a voluntary temporary conversation sustained by attended presence, its permitted audience, the caller's obligations and the option to stop. There is no default countdown. The candidate list comes from the actor's actual knowledge and supported relation, not a search of every retained dead person. The action does not expose strangers' deaths, hidden aliases, private biography or a revived person's current location.

An invitation identifies this caller, this anchor and this requested encounter. It grants the selected NPC no knowledge of the caller's whole history or private human notes. Eren may accept or decline on that limited basis with permitted personal context. Silence, unavailable cognition, invalid current eligibility and a withheld refusal are not all fictional rejection. The interface keeps application failure separate from a claimed character decision while disclosing no hidden reason or private dead/live state that the caller was not entitled to learn.

The selected profile proposes one outstanding outgoing invitation place shared across a caller's supported contact, telepathic-link and spirit requests. Within the spirit family a caller may hold one pending invitation **or** one active manifestation, and a retained person may have one pending invitation or one active manifestation. A caller already sustaining a spirit cannot invite another until that visit ends. A second request cannot wake a second Eren, duplicate memories or spend a second body's actions. A pending request is not an active ghost and has no sensory feed. Declined or failed invitations do not retry automatically. Actual paid-attempt exposure remains governed by existing admission and uncertainty rules, not by an assertion that a short fictional ritual is free.

New summon invitations consume the same proposed sender-wide **three new offers per sixty real seconds** used for supported contact/link invitations, rather than a separate allowance for each magical family. A duplicate request counts once. Dismissal suppresses another invitation cue during the same attended cairn encounter; crossing a boundary cannot reset the real sender allowance. Native recipient/scene admission and the existing model budget still apply before any paid consideration. A failed or uncertain provider attempt remains that attempt until reconciled; pressing the button again is not authority for an automatic unchanged paid retry. No independent summoning AI budget is added.

Before manifestation, current death/embodiment state, NPC control eligibility, actual anchor, caller participation, permission and resource/work conditions are rechecked. A person already revived, a changed human-control binding, a retired family or a lost anchor cannot produce a ghost based on an old request. A stale acceptance records no phantom conversation. No item, visible appearance or source-name string alone bypasses those checks.

## 4. The manifested encounter

### 4.1 Preparation and appearance

The first profile uses **sixty game seconds of deliberate preparation**, one normal real second at 60:1, by an awake present caller within **2.2 world units** of the actual cairn. This brief native action occupies the ordinary physical work lane. It is an intentional start, not a manufacturing grind. The review shows actual clock meaning; speed changes follow the same world clock. There is no scarce offering to renew merely to talk and no automatic repeat if preparation is interrupted.

Acceptance and valid preparation enable one actual manifestation at a supported place by the cairn. The preview must identify a real usable location; no free point is invented through a wall, inside another body or on unsupported terrain. An unavailable anchor or placement prevents appearance with the actual state retained. NPC consent is not a promise that geometry or service capacity will cooperate. If paid preparation or a response was already dispatched, its actual cost remains truthful even when no apparition appears.

Eren has one temporary, recognizable spectral appearance under the chosen world profile. This is an explicit capability set: local sight and hearing through ordinary current perception, supported speech/expression and the ability to leave. It supplies no ordinary carrying, pickup, crafting, attacks, eating, sleeping, travel beyond the anchor, possession of another body or use of a dead person's dropped equipment. The appearance does not move the old remains or take their inventory. It is not another active Eren beside a living body.

The first manifestation is non-solid and does not become a blocker, shield or moving platform. Its visual/sound behavior and absence of contact interactions must be genuinely supported before the family is offered, not inferred from translucent artwork. Ghost sight does not penetrate walls. Spectral speech uses the actual source location, hearing geometry, listener clarity and each observer's recognition. A stranger does not learn Eren's name, relationships or biography from the display label. A partial listener receives only the permitted words.

The host permits one active spirit per caller and one active manifestation per retained identity. These are separate from the finite scene/service budget. A second person cannot keep a duplicate active by calling through another tab or alias. The first encounter has one cairn as authored evidence, not a universal one-cairn world law or permission for unlimited simultaneous spirits elsewhere.

### 4.2 A conversation with the actual person

Eren enters ordinary current conversation behavior with only actual permitted new observations and retained usable experience. The player may show a real object, tell Eren what happened, ask about an old disagreement or propose returning to life. Eren can answer, ask a question, remain silent, decline the subject or leave. The caller has brought an opportunity for contact, not purchased compliance or all the contents of a mind.

Claims stay claims. Eren may remember poorly, infer a motive incorrectly or deliberately lie. A remembered attacker is not a server-certified killer label, and a death that occurred without the victim perceiving its cause supplies no omniscient murder explanation. The caller learns testimony, not direct witnessing of the death. A useful answer about a known technique can support ordinary teaching only under the actual capability/learning rules; it cannot install an unknown recipe or give missing proficiency merely because the speaker is dead.

New experience belongs to this continuing person. A second visit may refer to the first, subject to normal retained detail and private-source restrictions. Eren does not replay their initial lines each time, claim every intervening world day as lived, or forget a refusal because the appearance ended. A living acquaintance's real new report can change the relationship. No numerical grief, loyalty or gratitude award is required.

Other people may arrive and perceive the visible/audible exchange through normal geometry. They do not automatically join the conversation or acquire earlier speech. Eren and the caller can stop speaking or end the meeting when the audience changes. The first cairn is not a private chamber; the review says local speech can be heard. Neither summoning nor ordinary resurrection declassifies a prior private human–NPC message. Such content and its derivatives remain protected in speech, notes, reflection, god inspection and later ghost recall under DG24's actual source policy.

The manifestation does not freeze the living caller's needs or the surrounding world. They can inspect, speak and listen while participating; ordinary eating from accessible possessions is permitted while they remain awake and in range. Walking away, sleeping, choosing an actually incompatible bodily activity or losing control ends sustaining attendance. They should be able to enjoy the encounter without feeding the ghost or managing a second inventory. Food is still real and finite; eating permission grants neither an automatic meal nor physiological immunity. There is no need to dismiss a friend just to take an otherwise compatible bite.

### 4.3 A native end that preserves the encounter

The caller may select **End the visit** and Eren may select **Leave**. The manifestation also ends when the caller leaves the 2.2-unit anchor range, dies, becomes ineligible/asleep, loses active participation/control, or the anchor/family can no longer support it. There is no grace period that secretly keeps a ghost observing after its cause ended. Already committed speech remains real; pending uncommitted speech/action cannot publish after the manifestation's authority is gone.

An explicit world pause ends this first manifestation. Ordinary loss of caller control/participation does too, including the current tab-departure path. Ending preserves already committed evidence and spends no extra offering. Resuming does not auto-call the person or replay an unfinished reply. This is the selected family's lifecycle rule, not a change to what pausing every other world activity means.

No fixed fictional timer cuts off a wanted conversation. This avoids a five-minute normal-speed interval becoming a few seconds at high acceleration. Concurrency, actual attended participation, legitimate service admission and real spending limits bound the active encounter instead. Lack of a duration cap is a disclosed workload choice, not evidence of unlimited affordable hosting. If additional inference is unavailable, the current committed state and native ending remain usable; missing speech is not invented, and an operator can suspend the offered service honestly.

Departure removes the active manifestation and its sensory/work eligibility, while preserving that person's actual accepted new history. It does not erase what living witnesses learned or restore a pre-visit goal list over later choices. A goodbye does not permanently delete the person or revoke every future invitation. A source-authorized preference to decline further contact can stop new invitations without erasing history; another caller, renamed actor or restored UI cannot be used to bypass current applicable access restrictions.

The first profile offers no caller handoff, multiple sustaining casters, wandering spectral employment or unattended seance. If the original caller leaves, another person's continued wish does not silently become their consent to pay or sustain. A later willing visit is a new current invocation, preserving the same person and ordinary attempt admission. No background catch-up creates missed ghost conversations.

## 5. An ordinary return is a wanted expedition

### 5.1 Decide whether return is wanted before turning it into work

During a voluntary manifestation the player can ask whether Eren wants to live again. This is an actual conversation and an independently chosen answer, not a hidden affinity threshold or a guarantee bought with materials. Eren can say yes, no, perhaps, or ask what the return would mean. The player can decide that this encounter was enough. No objective rewards screen treats a refusal as failure to complete a companion.

If both want to pursue it, the known rite explains its exact supported outcome and requirements before the player leaves: one actual return ember; an eligible local return place and adult NPC body profile; fresh consent at the eventual transition; and the ordinary world they will return to, with no restored inventory or compelled future service. The player should know whether the rite can handle Eren's declared supported bodily history. A private incompatible condition is not exposed as a diagnosis; a known inability must not be concealed simply to send someone on a pointless expedition.

The first world places **one finite return ember at Windward Ledge**, explicitly authored as a special resource source. Its location is learned through legitimate local teaching/discovery, not a global registry reveal. It does not respawn when picked up, when a caller relogs, when an NPC dies again or when somebody renames the rite. The world may later author additional actual sources, but this first episode promises only this ember. Possession, pickup, loss and all permitted transfers use the ordinary object owner.

This is a difficult return because the resource requires a meaningful excursion through a separately qualified recoverable threat/route situation, not because the rite demands many copies of an ordinary crafting ingredient. The player may prepare, avoid or distract the actual threat where supported, take a genuinely different safe route, use a useful admitted invention, bring a willing companion or decide not to go. No label grants stealth, distraction or an invented passage. The selected threat must already have its complete actual behavior and applicable human participation policy; the ghost feature does not silently adopt new PvP or dangerous-logout laws.

The route should have value and interest beyond a receipt: a wanted place to reach, a real obstacle understood through perception and a decision about risk. There is no guaranteed kill, special reward for hurting Eren, or automatically assigned expedition partner. A player who solves the route cleverly should keep that success. Do not secretly respawn guards or increase the cost because an invention made the journey easier. An accepted world law can explicitly constrain revival power; a material tag called “life” cannot manufacture another ember through an unsupported recipe.

The concrete first layout is a ground-supported approach to Windward Ledge with an actual stone screen and one territorial stag. It consumes the [separately inspected newer-main threat family](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/0a3ab79b7a698a7f1941dc23722f89220d1ba425/docs/worlds/base/first-threat-encounter.md), not a claim that this site is installed on the task branch. The direct short route crosses the stag's four-unit home trigger; the ember's collection stance lies outside that footprint. The longer route takes an outer leg beyond the eight-unit pursuit bound, then approaches the collection stance without entering the four-unit trigger. That final approach can be within eight units: it avoids starting this territorial encounter, but is not a guaranteed safe destination during an already-active pursuit. Both routes must really exist on supported ground; the word “ledge” supplies no climbing or jumping mechanic.

Hoof tracks and damaged foliage give observable grounds to investigate. They do not report an unseen animal's current intent. The player can inspect the actual animal, follow the safer outer route, or risk the short crossing. The source family permits ordinary walking to outpace the stag, uses real sight and contact, and stops a preparing strike when actual sight is lost. The stone screen can therefore matter through its real geometry, while a supported ranged tool can change the encounter through actual range/ammunition/accuracy. It cannot guarantee a hit or silently add a distraction command. Injury, retreat, a missed shot and an already-displaced stag remain real outcomes rather than a reset challenge.

The newer source targets eligible human-controlled bipeds and excludes ordinary residents. A willing NPC who legitimately knows the route and can acquire the ember may therefore help differently; the design must not secretly give the animal broader targets just to make retrieval equally difficult. Asking someone to help is a genuine alternative if they choose it, not a button that forces a resident courier. Direct shared-play luring and broader indirect harm remain their existing unresolved owner. The new site must consume the actually adopted human risk/recovery package before being offered, including that newer package's true human death and 300-game-second vulnerable departure, five real seconds only at normal speed. It cannot be backported by prose into the inspected collapse baseline; pause/downtime do not consume that game-time interval.

The one real resource is placed at that cache stance, with no killing requirement, drop chance, personal copy or automatic refill. The player returns with it and chooses the wanted revival. This supplies a concrete observation/route/risk decision and finite resource choice. Whether it is a sufficiently difficult and satisfying return is a qualification question. If the actual episode reduces to a trivial known pickup, improve its genuine encounter or keep ordinary revival unoffered; do not pad it with empty walking, login deadlines or a material checklist. Ghost contact remains independently useful while that work is unresolved.

### 5.2 The ember is actual property, not a quest flag

When the player reaches it, they take the real finite item if the current location, handling, reach and permission permit. Another actor can take it first under those same rules. There is no personal duplicate behind the scenery. Its known status is current only where actually observed; a departed person is not tracked remotely through the quest. The player may carry it home, store it, deliberately give it to another legitimate user or decide to use it for another eligible return. It is not a compulsory single-person token unless an alternate world expressly selects such a law.

The first ember has no decay timer. Losing the original corpse therefore does not start a last-minute grind, and pausing or leaving before the expedition does not make revival permanently impossible. The corpse's three/seven-day changes remain real; they simply are not this rite's eligibility deadline. Lost belongings stay where the actual world placed them. The return resource does not magically gather them back.

If the ember is stolen, destroyed by a genuinely supported mechanic or irretrievably inaccessible, the game says what the player is entitled to know and offers only real remaining possibilities. There is no free replacement granted by failure, no secretly infinite vendor and no invitation to spend money for a promised resurrection. The player can seek a legitimately available source, a newly admitted supported alternative, creator help if the operator chooses it, or continue with contact alone. A world's author should not advertise repeatable ordinary revival beyond its actual resource policy.

Before the first public offering, validate that the route is genuinely reachable and the ember can be acquired and brought back under the chosen profile. A blueprint existing in a creator catalogue is not evidence that this expedition is playable. If the only possible ordinary path requires an unqualified capability or a human risk decision still unselected, the difficult-return extension remains unavailable while the independent ghost conversation can still be useful.

## 6. Bring back this person, once

### 6.1 A fresh offer at a usable place

At the cairn, with Eren voluntarily manifest and the actual ember available, the player offers the return. The review names Eren as legitimately recognized, the specific resource, the supported bodily result, the actual placement and what will not be restored. It explains that Eren's earlier interest was not irrevocable consent. The ghost can inspect permitted facts, ask a question, accept, decline or leave. A human-operated caller cannot write the target's acceptance in their own proposed action or promise loyalty on the target's behalf.

Both are free to stop. The rite takes **600 game seconds**, ten normal real seconds, of the caller's valid ordinary physical work within the same 2.2-unit anchor reach. Sustaining this visit and performing this specific return rite are compatible; an unrelated new work action remains an interruption. The target remains voluntarily present and can withdraw at any point before the transition. Their consent concerns this physical return, not future employment, companionship, forgiveness or an inventory transfer.

The ember is bound to the particular attempt so concurrent work cannot spend it elsewhere. It is consumed only at the successful coupled transition. Cancellation releases that attempt's material claim without creating a refund item, because the material was not spent. An actual prior theft, destruction or invalidation is not reversed. If the caller moves, loses control, sleeps, is harmed in a way that interrupts the work, dies or loses the current capability, the attempt stops. Target departure, changed human control, prior revival, lost anchor or impossible body placement also prevents completion. Pausing ends the manifestation and therefore the attempt; it does not keep an invisible ritual running.

Completion rechecks the current person, physical-life state, consent, exact resource, body compatibility and real placement. It consumes one real ember and creates one supported new living physical life under the same retained identity, or commits neither effect. An uncertain acknowledgement must reconcile that one attempt. Reconnecting, refreshing, an old completion callback or a second caller cannot create another Eren, another body or another spent ember.

### 6.2 The bodily result is explicit

The first Eren profile is an explicitly authored and qualified adult speaking person with **no harvest output**. This is not an assertion that every NPC or every humanoid body shares that property. The proposed revived body has full supported health and normal full food/energy needs, clears ordinary lethal injury and ended transient bodily processes, and retains the person's authored bodily age at death, supported permanent traits and existing scars. It adds no new punitive scar. Actual advancing world-calendar time since death may be shown separately under permitted chronology; dormant retention is neither lived bodily aging nor a newborn reset. No missing birthday or placeholder timestamp is converted into a reliable age. A special condition outside the selected body's supported return policy must be resolved before that profile is offered; missing condition data is not permission to remove it by default.

Full needs are deliberate. The difficult route and finite ember already price the return; beginning the reunion in immediate hunger or exhaustion would stack maintenance onto it. This restoration is a finite authored resource transformation, counted in the economics. It is not a free daily healing station, a way to refill another person's stomach by briefly dismissing a ghost, or evidence that ordinary medicine works this way. The living person resumes their actual normal needs and independent choices after return.

Existing old biological remains cease being an active or harvestable body for this person as part of the return. A historical record or permitted inert memorial trace may remain, but not a second corpse that can be revived or looted for newly created goods. Separate actual belongings are left in their real current custody, including nested contents and objects somebody else already picked up. They are not reconstructed, pulled back, erased with the remains or handed to the revived person automatically. The preview identifies any relevant known lack of equipment without exposing unseen inventories.

The physical-life boundary changes. Old attacks, routes, treatments, movement, body-targeted consents and unfinished work cannot apply to the new body merely because the person is the same. The ghost's limited capability set ends, and its uncommitted outputs are fenced. Ordinary mind, learned techniques, permitted new conversation history and personal commitments continue under their actual owners. Death and return do not settle a loan, return a borrowed bow, grant a library officer's role again or erase another person's claim. A new body is not a fresh account.

For a later harvestable-body extension, preserve the actual already-spent and remaining original harvest entitlement and reconcile every surviving old remnant before return. Do not reclaim previously distributed meat/materials, refresh yields because a physical-life identifier changed, or maintain parallel harvestable old and new bodies. That requires its own real owner support and qualification. It is not a generic lifetime-harvest prerequisite for the selected non-harvest Eren encounter. The administrative creator-revival exception remains explicitly separate.

### 6.3 A reunion with choices, not a reward follower

The arrival is an actual local event with the ordinary permitted audience. Some witnesses recognize Eren through real acquaintance; others see an unfamiliar person. The event does not broadcast private death circumstances, a human's messages, the caller's paid cost or every remembered relationship. A new self-introduction remains a communicated claim. The same rules allow a suspicious or mistaken witness; the game should not manufacture ambiguity by mislabeling its own authoritative transition as a clone.

Eren can look around, speak, ask for their old belongings, seek food later, continue a chosen concern, change their mind about the world or leave the caller. They have no debt of obedience for the resource spent. A relationship can grow from what happened, but there is no automatic gratitude score or guaranteed romance. The player may enjoy the reunion and then pursue their own activity. They need not manage Eren as a dependent to keep the return “successful.”

The old body's cleanup deadline does not kill the new life, and the revived person does not inherit a ghost countdown. Death later remains possible under actual world rules. It returns this person's actual later history to the retained lifecycle; another ordinary return requires another legitimately available resource and current consent. The first finite source does not replenish. Killing Eren again neither produces extra harvest nor improves a summoning bonus. If a world selects renewable revival later, its resource/time/physiology economy must be separately evaluated rather than assuming repeated returns are harmless.

Lethal-action presentation must consume the installed law too. The separately inspected newer threat package's current final-blow wording says ordinary animal/NPC revival is unavailable; that sentence cannot stay universally true after this family is installed. The narrow consumer should explain only the target's actual permitted known consequences and supported possibility of return, without promising consent/success or revealing hidden body eligibility, an ember's existence or its location. Keep the same exact one-attempt lethal review, current control and PvP/harm boundaries. This wording correction does not make killing reversible on demand or change who may attack whom.

## 7. Telepathy begins with a specific willing pair

### 7.1 A mental message, not a view into a mind

The independent first telepathic family connects two mutually known, awake, currently participating people. At an actual clear local encounter they review a proposed link and each independently assents. Knowing a name, being family, belonging to the same institution or holding a caller's item grants no psychic permission. The first profile excludes dormant dead people and other-world recipients. A ghost conversation does not automatically gain a private mental backchannel.

The chosen capability permits only text deliberately composed for this pair. A human types and explicitly sends; Enter remains a new line, with an advertised button/shortcut for submission and normal IME behavior. An NPC may choose the same native communication action from its own permitted context. Neither a private thought operation nor an internal goal, reflection, raw provider output or unsent draft becomes a message. A recipient cannot ask the channel to reveal what the sender almost said.

This is an asynchronous chosen-message channel, not automatic enrollment into another live NC conversation. It can coexist with the person's ordinary local activity and hearing. Receiving one message does not merge everyone nearby, take over a call slot, force an answer or interrupt native work. If the product later offers a sustained live telepathic conversation, it must explicitly consume the same active-conversation choice and admission boundaries as other such conversations; this small family does not deliver that wider promise.

The first trial uses a human and one independently choosing NPC across an actual local hearing obstruction. A human–human pair can use the same disclosure contract once qualified. Autonomous NPC-to-NPC relay chains are not part of the first proof. No language translation, shared senses, emotional certainty or numerical affinity gate is granted. Meaning uses the participants' actual supported language/communication capabilities. A message can be ambiguous or false.

### 7.2 Local range and remembered agreement

The proposed reach is **thirty world units measured between the current eligible embodiments**, ignoring ordinary walls under this expressly magical law. The initial offer is local with clear recognition and addressing; it cannot target a guessed account, a distant alias, an old source ID or an unrecognized new body. Thirty units is a reviewable local-scouting choice, not a physical claim about thought or a measured optimum. It is smaller than world-wide slate reach and is not inherited by other senses.

There is one current selected pair per participant. New invitations use the shared existing contact-offer envelope: one outgoing pending place across contact/link/spirit requests, one invitation for the pair, same-encounter cue suppression and three new offers per sender in sixty real seconds. These do not add a second allowance and do not claim to bound every other kind of inbound world work; DG26's slate-call request envelope retains its separate owner. A pair invitation expires when the eligible local exchange ends, either person withdraws or their actual eligibility changes. No queued remote assent recreates it after separation.

Leaving is easy and local. **Leave this link** immediately stops the person's future reception and retires their own selected-pair place. It does not send a private status notice, change the other person's known count/card, erase received evidence or tell them why contact stopped. The other person can retain a clearly labeled **last-agreed link**, leave it explicitly or replace their own selection during a fresh mutually consented local exchange. There is no claim that its other end is currently listening. Replacing a link ends the replacing person's old sending/reception scope; it does not forward old history to a new partner.

The real admission state permits no simultaneous active receiving pair for one participant. A stale last-agreed peer selection is not a second active grant or an unlimited monitoring subscription. An ordinary current local exchange supplies the new pair's identity and consent; it cannot infer recognition from the old channel. A change of display name never retargets previous messages.

### 7.3 Choose words, submit once, learn only what arrived

Telepathic text reuses [DG24's message meaning](world-text-messages-feature-spec.md#5-compose-submit-and-understand-the-result), private evidence owner and history surfaces with this explicitly different admission/lifetime. It does not create a second private inbox or a new account chat. The draft names the selected person, world and **Mental message** channel. The ordinary spoken composer keeps its ordinary meaning. Switching to this mode never secretly sends a spoken draft or converts a failed psychic message into audible words.

The first envelope shares DG24/NC22's **2,000 Unicode code points and 8,000 UTF-8 bytes**, **six accepted submissions to the same correspondent and twenty-four overall per rolling sixty real seconds**, across this person's applicable text channels, tabs and devices. A slate plus a link does not double the allowance. An over-limit draft stays editable; no automatic splitting, truncation or paid summarization. The person's own remaining allowance/wait is visible, never the recipient's quota. These values are proposals inherited for coherent admission, not evidence of natural live conversation; test whether short scouting messages are useful at that rate before claiming a live-chat replacement.

The sender sees **Draft**, **Sending**, **Submitted**, an own-scope **Not sent**, or **Result not confirmed**. Submitted means only durable acceptance of those exact chosen words under the sender's eligible operation. It does not certify range, peer wakefulness, continued grant, recipient arrival, reading or a reply. Resolve an uncertain original request before offering a deliberate new copy. Duplicates consume neither a second receipt nor another response opportunity.

At the current admission boundary the intended recipient receives a mental-message experience only if they are actually awake, participating, capable, within range and currently permitting this pair. The permitted source is the attributed chosen message, with its actual world time and acquisition as communication. It conveys no coordinates, senses, heartbeat, inferred emotion or death status. No nearby listener receives it through physical hearing, and it has no loud fallback. Sender receipt, timing and counts must not distinguish privately blocked, out-of-range, asleep, dead or departed peers.

There is **no offline queued delivery**. A submission that did not reach the eligible recipient is not replayed when they wake, reconnect, move back into range, unblock or become a ghost. A genuinely acquired message can remain in their permitted history even if they attend to it later; that is retained evidence of an actual eligible receipt, not delivery to an absent mind. The UI distinguishes received history from an unsent local draft without claiming human comprehension. It supplies no read receipt.

NPC consideration uses the existing required-evidence, attention and spending rules. Delivery does not force a model call, a reply, agreement or a new plan. The received text may influence a lawful choice, but a route description is testimony and cannot conjure a map, reveal hidden stock or install a recipe. If the sender says “I will distract the animal,” the recipient has heard an intention, not observed a completed distraction. An invitation to return home still requires actual movement and willingness.

## 8. Private means private after the effect ends

### 8.1 Retain actual learning without retaining live access

The first telepathic family adopts DG24's explicit private human–NPC source policy. A human-private message and content derived from it cannot be automatically quoted or paraphrased to nonparticipants through speech, other messages, inner-world inspection, subject notes, recall, plans/reasons, appraisals, narration, diagnostics, search, counts or exports. A ghost state, new body, creator role or renamed channel supplies no exception. The message may support the NPC's own lawful choice; its independently observable movement or work retains its normal audience without publishing the private motive.

The actual source restriction must follow derived material. A paragraph mixing public facts with a protected message cannot simply be relabeled public because it is now “Eren's opinion.” If the narrower projection cannot separate the protected content safely, withhold that result there. This is a delivery prerequisite, not a prompt asking the NPC to keep secrets. Model context and host-authorized inspection need the same boundary; hiding the ordinary message panel is insufficient.

Ending a link removes future access. It does not make a recipient unlearn legitimately received words or rewind decisions already made. Ordinary source correction, forgetting and platform erasure remain their existing owners and must invalidate forbidden derivatives, old contexts and restored copies. A human can deliberately retype what they are allowed to read as new communication; that does not grant automated NPC forwarding or retroactive thread access. The first profile has no declassification button, shared inbox, forwarded identity or group mind.

A newly human-controlled former NPC does not expose historical human–NPC private messages or their derivatives to the new controller. Stop fresh use of the old channel and require a new disclosed local exchange for future communication. Retain former participants' rightful access; do not erase the corpus as a convenient way to pass the transition. If a control change cannot safely separate that history, refuse it before exposure. A resurrection does not change that protection, and a character's earlier invitation cannot authorize a different human owner.

### 8.2 Interruption without a hidden status service

Local **Leave**, blocking, current authority loss, sleep, death or loss of the recipient's eligible participation stops new reception immediately. Sender-side control loss or death stops new sends. Old peer history and labels remain last-known. None of these privately occurring events supplies the other side a reason, a read indicator or a location. A mutually authorized new reply is the ordinary evidence that the other person chose to communicate.

Crossing the thirty-unit boundary stops actual reception of newly submitted messages while out of range, without queuing them. It supplies no automatic “warm/cold” meter. Returning within range can permit future submissions only while the current local pair grants still apply; explicit Leave, block, control rebinding or death is not undone by walking closer. Range alone does not turn a remembered pair into a public radar. The UI's plain help must say that submitted text can go unheard and that an explicit reply, not a delivery tick, is the useful confirmation.

Leaving a tab, losing control or becoming inactive ends that person's active-link selection; reopening does not autojoin. A peer can remain at a last-agreed selection but receives no automatic future activity feed. Both may establish a fresh local agreement when they actually meet again. Repeated reconnects are not repeated invitations, messages or paid decisions. No absence-catching backlog appears in a newly revived mind.

Manual world pause stops new sends and first mental-message experiences. Existing permitted received history remains readable as history and an unsent draft remains unsent. The first profile retires active link selections on the pause without replay on Resume; a world-wide visible pause is an own service fact, not a private peer signal. This keeps the first active-pair experience honest without inventing a second clock or always-on account messenger.

### 8.3 Failure and load are not fictional mental resistance

The selected voluntary channel has no attack roll or hidden willpower contest. Willingness and current capability are prerequisites. A refusal is an actual recipient choice; provider failure, capacity refusal, invalid state and a missed delivery are different technical outcomes with actor-safe presentation. The application can explain an own service problem in its controls, while an NPC's pending transcript retains the ordinary dots-only treatment. It must not invent “Eren resisted your mind” to dramatize a failed request.

The human can close the link, keep an editable unsent draft, read prior permitted history and continue normal play without further inference. The NPC can continue already-admitted lawful work under ordinary agency; the feature creates no automatic fallback speech or recipient-controlling directive. When required evidence cannot fit current cognition, do not truncate a message then pretend the character heard all of it. Preserve the existing bounded, honest unavailable outcome and inspectable provider disposition.

Current-format saves preserve the coupled retained person, actual life/manifestation state, resources and in-progress native work under their respective owners. A restart reconciles current participation/control before permitting further observation or completion. If caller control was lost, the visit is over; it is not recreated merely because a saved appearance existed. If an already committed revival is recovered, the new life and one ember debit remain one result. Do not show the old ghost while also reconstructing the living person.

Ordinary same-state recovery and optional [DG25 protected shared restore](corrections-and-shared-restoration-feature-spec.md#7-selected-shared-restoration-policy) are distinct. A supported fictional cut restores embodied/dormant state, actual goods, invitation/message outcomes, received evidence and derivatives coherently, subject to current private authority and erasure. It cannot combine a pre-consumption ember with the post-return person, replay discarded mental messages, or let stale model work publish into the restored world. Human knowledge of an earlier timeline cannot be erased; any later human retelling is new testimony.

Real sending/invitation allowances, actual paid calls, current blocks and platform access retain their non-rewindable owners. A changed fictional name, new physical life or restored object does not reset them. Incompatible development saves are refused under AGENTS; preservation of a beloved identity is not a promise to migrate arbitrary old formats by guessing missing state.

Disabling a family ends new use and closes its active effects through the defined native lifecycle. It does not delete retained people, make private history public or erase an already revived living person as though that person were a temporary buff. A persistent outcome and its original source are different. Definition removal must resolve actual current dependencies before retirement; if its state cannot be supported safely, refuse removal rather than orphaning bodies or evidence. Re-enabling does not restore old consent, resend a message or recreate a dismissed apparition.

The first two families are same-world. They neither carry a mind across worlds nor maintain a channel through travel, world shutdown or provider absence. A later cross-world interpretation must consume DG29's actual embodiment, clocks, rights, history, safe return and restore boundaries. There is no psychic exemption from current world-access revocation. Service retirement uses DG27's finite retention/retrieval promises and current privacy rules; “retained person” is not a promise of eternal free hosting or a right to export everyone else's history.

## 10. Stronger mental effects and shared minds are separate choices

The voluntary pair is a deliberately complete first mental family. It should not wait for every imaginable mind mechanic. If later play wants a different power, select its behavior in terms a participant can understand before extending the technical boundary.

**An influence** could make one supported concern more salient for a finite source-linked interval, while the actor can still refuse, protect themselves and pursue competing aims. Its product selection must name the useful situation, targets, perceived warning, resistance, magnitude and whether it applies to humans. It must not silently replace another person's self-authored goals or claim that a prompt reliably enforces the pressure. The current profile selects no such mood modifier merely to make the whisper feel magical.

**Compulsion** is stronger: it imposes an actually enforceable limited constraint or pursuit. Before selecting it, define the target's retained choices, incompatible or dangerous commands, escape/dispel, opposing sources, duration and current body/action policy. A suggestion to an NPC model is not proof of control. Human input restriction or speaking on a human's behalf requires its own explicit world/player-control choice; this proposal grants neither. A command to betray a private human message cannot override the platform source policy.

**Permanent alteration or memory exchange** must identify the exact fictional state it changes, whose current authority admits it, what the target recognizes and what history remains technically true. A believed memory can be false without editing the actual event, inventory or another person's knowledge. It must not restore forgotten real-private data or claim real mental treatment. A recipient agreeing to one story is not permission to expose all the sender's past. If the selected effect cannot preserve source attribution and current erasure, it remains unsupported.

For a **shared mind**, first choose whether the playable subject is one colony controlling multiple bodies or independent people using a shared channel. The voluntary pair implements the latter's communication boundary only; it is not a colony controller. A later colony would need an explicitly authored origin, individual versus collective decisions, membership/exit, private compartments, source/time attribution, partial connectivity, division/reunion and arbitration of real resources. Two bodies cannot spend the same ember or issue conflicting actions through a single invisible double budget.

A living independent NPC is not automatically made a non-sentient drone when joining. A human's current control cannot be pooled by another participant's acceptance. Departing members may retain legitimately learned shared knowledge while losing new access; permitted forgetting/erasure remains distinct. Losing a connection must have an explicit outcome for each body, without secretly continuing remote control or killing a former member as a generic cleanup rule. Those choices are central to a colony game, not small implementation details in a message feature.

For every temporary stronger effect, expiry detaches only that effect's ongoing contribution. It does not restore an old whole-person snapshot, refund spent materials, unsay speech, erase an experience, undo a voluntary change made during the interval or resume a stale plan. Overlapping effects need defined combination and interruption before use; source death/removal, target return and save/load cannot accidentally copy a compulsion onto another physical life. [EM-R08](#em-r08--resurrection-and-duplicated-minds-expose-transition-failures) provides historical evidence of exactly these fragile boundaries.

Select an extension only if it produces a wanted episode that simpler communication or ordinary character influence cannot supply, has a clear participant-control policy and can afford its complete lifecycle. A coherent alternate world could deliberately make corpse echoes, bounded involuntary thought detection or a colony protagonist its premise. It must label those differences honestly; it cannot present an echo as Eren, a group transcript as consent, or copied private context as shared consciousness.

## 11. Economics of the complete encounter

An incorporeal person still costs perception, dialogue, decisions, permitted memory, presentation and storage while manifest. Count that person as an actual active cognitive participant in the offered scene workload, not as free decorative art. Dormancy removes ordinary active ticking and scheduled inference; it does not make a lifetime history or cold preparation free. One spirit per caller does not bound all callers, anchors, dead identities or old conversations in a mature world.

Separate a complete attempt into invitation/admission, permitted cold recall, willingness, native preparation, active responses, any actual return work, authoritative transition, interruption/reconciliation, observer evidence and retained private derivatives. The payer is the current world/operator under actual service and AI funding rules. A fictional ember pays a fictional revival cost, not an external model bill. No player's subscription, creator allocation or supporter promise is silently charged or redirected because their character was summoned.

For sensitivity, suppose a wanted ghost visit needs one paid invitation consideration and six reply decisions, while two rejected/uncertain attempts also incurred cost: nine workflows at an illustrative average `c`. At `c = $0.002`, `$0.01` or `$0.05`, inference is `$0.018`, `$0.09` or `$0.45` before cold-history preparation, any separate maintenance, normal living actors, hosting and support. These are dimensional assumptions, not provider prices or measured calls. A five-minute encounter with dozens of listeners can also create far more permitted evidence than a private pair. Measure the actual audience and complete workflow; do not price only one successful sentence.

The telepathic native submission and current grant/range checks need no model call. An NPC's later choice may cost inference, but there is no automatic reply, paid ping, transcript summary or status-check loop. The shared send cap prevents a slate/call/link combination from multiplying one sender's allowance. It does not bound hundreds of senders, rejected traffic, lifetime history or a popular NPC's total workload. Three offers per sender per minute is a burst policy, not proof that three hundred offers to one retained person are affordable.

For a scale illustration, ten thousand retained people averaging twenty KiB of retained state each would already occupy about 195 MiB before indexes, backups, relationships or message history. This is an assumed storage example, not the current person's real footprint and not a new retention target. A full private message can also carry up to eight thousand body bytes plus its evidence/derivatives. Small visible pages and one active pair do not bound all of that. Existing memory, persistence, native-work and whole-service admission own measured growth and justified capacity choices.

On the world side, the first revival spends one nonrenewing ember and restores one body's supported physiology once. Full food and energy are real economic value from that authored magical source. They are not obtained by repeatedly calling/dismissing a ghost, nor by restoring a pre-use item beside a post-use person. Eren has no harvest output in this first profile, so death/return cannot produce a material yield cycle. A later renewable resource or harvestable body must account for all replenished physiology, gathering/crafting work and extracted matter before claiming sustainable repeated revival.

The living visitor still consumes ordinary food and time. At the base rate of 10.8 fullness per game hour, a five-normal-real-minute conversation spans five game hours and uses 54 fullness for a continuously active ordinary person before eating or other modifiers. The sixty-second preparation and six-hundred-second rite together span eleven game minutes, about 1.98 fullness at that same rate; actual travel, danger and conversation dominate. These are arithmetic illustrations, not new costs or guarantees of a particular duration. Allowing an ordinary bite in range is better than requiring a second summoning just to maintain the human body's current law.

Qualification begins with the complete selected one-spirit episode and one separate active pair. Before wider advertised service, measure multiple concurrent invitations, a popular anchor, many cold histories, long retained private threads, combined text traffic, return races and ordinary foreground play. Cheap current rejection must preserve rightful recovery of already committed work. Overload can stop new invitations or submissions with honest own-service feedback; it cannot make two people share a mind accidentally, disclose a hidden refusal or delete retained people to satisfy an unstated cache limit.

## 12. Acceptance is a playable episode plus truthful boundaries

The two first families and the harder revival extension have independent completion gates. The spirit conversation must work before its expedition is required. The telepathic pair needs a real advantage across an actual obstruction before widening its range or connecting more people. The ordinary return needs its actual route, resource and body transition qualified before being advertised as achievable. A source file, fixture appearance or plausible dialogue is not sufficient evidence of any of these.

Observe player effort and voluntary choices alongside integrity. Did the ghost encounter feel like meeting Eren, or a procedural information vending machine? Did the player choose a subject beyond the revival checklist? Did the return route invite observation or creativity, or merely delay a wanted reunion? Did mental messages clarify a real coordination problem, or add a forgotten mode and stale-link confusion? If ordinary speech/contact or an authored remembrance gives the better experience, retain the simpler option rather than declaring every supernatural family necessary.

## 13. Game-first review and sequence

First deliver a coherent willing apparition at an actual place with meaningful remembered context, understandable capabilities and a clean native ending. Independently deliver one consented explicit-message pair with current private evidence. Then qualify the actual return expedition and once-only living transition. Shared minds, compulsion, general memory alteration, summoned workers and afterlife geography are optional later world premises, not prerequisites to those experiences.

The review removed a fixed ghost countdown because accelerated time would rush the conversation and make the player watch a timer. It chose full needs on revival because the journey and finite resource already provide consequence. It kept corpse cleanup without a corpse-freshness requirement, allowed ordinary eating during a visit, and made the first revived body non-harvestable so a broad yield redesign does not block the desired encounter. It selected chosen messages instead of passive reading or a live sensory feed. These are substantive reductions in work the player must manage.

Those cuts do not make death meaningless. The person is absent from ordinary life until a real return, actual goods and interrupted plans remain changed, witnesses remember what happened, and the sole authored ember cannot fund unlimited returns. The ghost is free to disagree and the revived person is free to leave. The relationship supplies stakes; repeated maintenance payments, forced gratitude and profitable companion harm would weaken it. [EM-R05](#em-r05--consequence-without-clone-maintenance) and [EM-R11](#em-r11--a-mechanic-can-undermine-the-relationship) are useful external warnings, not measured validation of this choice.

Stop or narrow an expansion if every summoning becomes a paid retry for a desired answer, if private-source protection cannot survive ordinary recall, if the “difficult” return has no interesting choice, or if stale links make coordination less reliable than talking. A more complex ontology is not its own benefit. Preserve what is enjoyable about the two encounters and improve the actual point of friction; do not repair an optional power by requiring a whole new government, currency, collective controller or revival industry.

## 14. Primary research and its limits

All records were accessed on 2026-10-09 UTC. They are official rules, developer announcements, support or creator retrospectives. They establish what those sources state, not measured Open Legend enjoyment, cost, clinical benefit or a real theory of consciousness. Historical descriptions and corrected defects retain their dates; public reader comments were excluded. The observations and our design inferences are intentionally separate.

### EM-R01 — A corpse answer is not the returned person

**Source:** Wizards of the Coast, [Speak with Dead](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619068-speak-with-dead), currently published non-Legacy rule page; undated public rule body read. The spell supplies a limited corpse exchange without restoring the departed soul, does not let that speaker learn later information, and does not guarantee truthful answers to an enemy.

**Inference:** A limited echo and the same continuing person are different offerings. Eren's encounter therefore preserves a real continuing identity, new experience and agency. The rules are a fictional comparator, not evidence that a generated voice establishes identity or that a particular metaphysical account is true.

### EM-R02 — Communication is narrower than sharing a mind

**Source:** Wizards of the Coast, [Rary's Telepathic Bond](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618925-rarys-telepathic-bond), current non-Legacy rule page; undated body read. A finite willing group receives temporary communication with an explicit planar boundary and language accommodation. The rule does not grant all participants' memories or action control.

**Inference:** Keep a useful mental channel separate from a merged controller. Open Legend selects different reach, message, language and lifetime terms rather than importing the tabletop spell wholesale. Willing communication alone does not establish continued consent to future messages or raw disclosure.

### EM-R03 — Detecting, reading and probing are separate powers

**Source:** Wizards of the Coast, [Detect Thoughts](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619099-detect-thoughts), current non-Legacy rule page; undated official body read, comments excluded. The rule distinguishes detecting a thinking presence from reading current focus and deeper probing, with different resistance, disclosure and termination behavior plus material/range limits.

**Inference:** Name each permitted disclosure explicitly. One chosen message grants no identity search, location feed or memory read. The involuntary fictional access described by this comparator is not adopted here and cannot authorize inspection of private human data.

### EM-R04 — Willingness and body eligibility matter before costly preparation

**Source:** Wizards of the Coast, [Raise Dead, Legacy](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2224-raise-dead), expressly labeled Legacy/Basic Rules 2014; full rule body read. The soul must be willing and free, with material, bodily and elapsed-time conditions. Some prior conditions persist, while missing essential parts can prevent the return and temporary penalties follow success.

**Inference:** Explain the supported result and potential refusal before an expedition. Open Legend deliberately declines the corpse deadline and repeated recovery penalty while retaining fresh consent and concrete eligibility. This record is not represented as the latest edition's revival rule.

### EM-R05 — Consequence without clone maintenance

**Source:** CCP Terminus, [A new Era of Clones](https://www.eveonline.com/ko/news/view/a-new-era-of-clones), 2014-12-02 historical announcement; English body served at the official locale URL. CCP announced removing clone-grade upkeep and associated skill loss, arguing that a forgotten upgrade could erase weeks of progress and discourage wanted risk while meaningful ship loss remained.

**Inference:** Death can have consequences without an attendance chore or upkeep trap. This is a developer's rationale and expected improvement, not a measured post-change result. EVE's cloning fiction does not define Eren's personhood or body law.

### EM-R06 — Removing a death cost has an economic counterpart

**Source:** ArenaNet, [Say Goodbye to Armor Repair Costs and Hello to Free Trait Resets](https://www.guildwars2.com/en/news/say-goodbye-to-armor-repair-costs-and-hello-to-free-trait-resets/), 2014-03-27, with date corroborated by the [official feature-pack index](https://www.guildwars2.com/en-gb/the-game/releases/feature-packs/); bodies read. The announcement treats return/repair time as sufficient burden and removes monetary repair cost, while reducing selected gold inputs as sinks disappear.

**Inference:** Avoid stacking penalties without distinct purpose, but count the real economic value of full physiology and renewable return. This historical plan is not a current complete death-system description or measured evidence of its success.

### EM-R07 — Mental power changes the affected person's choices

**Source:** Ludeon Studios / Tia Young, [Anomaly preview #3: Cultists, hate chanters and rituals](https://ludeon.com/blog/2024/04/anomaly-preview-3-cultists-hate-chanters-and-rituals/), 2024-04-04 pre-release preview; relevant body read. The preview describes limited death refusal and rituals affecting motivation, memory and allegiance, including coercive uses in a horror setting.

**Inference:** These are consequential families requiring exact target, refusal/resistance and aftermath choices. Their existence is not authority to compel Open Legend humans, promise mental treatment or adopt an entire horror economy for one wanted extraordinary encounter. Described balance was not independently tested here.

### EM-R08 — Resurrection and duplicated minds expose transition failures

**Source:** Ludeon Studios, [New ambient horror setting and tribal Anomaly support](https://ludeon.com/blog/2024/05/new-ambient-horror-setting-and-tribal-anomaly-support/), 2024-05-03 released update; relevant body and changelog read. Reported corrections include hypnosis copied to duplicates, rituals persisting after caster death/disappearance, duplicated implants and needs not updating after resurrection. The update also offered lighter integration choices.

**Inference:** Body return, active effects, consent, equipment and needs must be qualified together. A supernatural option need not import the entire surrounding progression. These are historical corrected defects, not claims of current failures or proof of Open Legend correctness.

### EM-R09 — The dead still need the history that actually occurred

**Source:** Larian Studios, [Patch #6 Now Live!](https://baldursgate3.game/news/patch-6-now-live_108), 2024-02-16, version 4.1.1.4763283; relevant official sections read. Fixes concern incorrect death circumstances in dialogue, knowledge from corpse speech failing to support later conversation, living-style chatter on dead souls, corpse-inventory duplication and summoned return across regions.

**Inference:** A convincing appearance does not prove a complete afterlife encounter. Actual scoped history, new usable testimony, body eligibility and conserved goods need the same lifecycle. This record describes reported corrections rather than current defects or measured narrative quality.

### EM-R10 — Ghost continuity can become household upkeep

**Source:** Electronic Arts, [The Sims 4 Occult Guides: Ghosts](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/the-sims/the-sims-4/sims-4-ghost-guide/), current public support body read; only a relative update age was displayed, so no exact update date is assigned. It distinguishes playable/free-roaming ghosts, irreversible release and fading prevented by strengthening a connection, with analogous needs and changes to marriage/career continuity.

**Inference:** State retained and changed relationships explicitly. A maintenance action that keeps a beloved person available can become attendance pressure; this proposal instead keeps remembrance optional and dormant continuity inactive. The source does not establish which policy players prefer.

### EM-R11 — A mechanic can undermine the relationship

**Source:** Klei Entertainment, [Wendy refresh and March 25 hotfix](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=322330&enddate=1585343932), official developer feed covering 2020-03-19 and 2020-03-25; bodies read after the direct forum returned 403. Six days after refreshing Wendy/Abigail, Klei removed a low-Wendy-health damage incentive it said undermined their teamwork, replacing it with complementary attacks.

**Inference:** A companion-return mechanic should not make harming or repeatedly killing the companion the best way to cooperate. Klei's player-commanded spirit model is a different agency contract; this proposal retains Eren's independent choices. No retention effect was measured here.

### EM-R12 — Identity ambiguity should be an intentional premise

**Source:** Thomas Grip / Frictional Games, [SOMA — 6 Months Later](https://frictionalgames.com/2016-03-soma-6-months-later/), original post 2016-03-24; the page also shows 2023-10-10. Full post read. The creator reports extensive discussion of the game's “coin-flip” interpretation and differing reactions to its bleak subject.

**Inference:** Person/copy ambiguity can serve an authored story, but an ordinary action must not accidentally misstate who continues and who controls the body. This is a creator retrospective, not a representative audience study, formal transfer specification or empirical finding about consciousness; the later page date is not a new study.

### EM-R13 — A collective mind is a different control model

**Source:** Paradox Development Studio, [Government, Civics and Hive Minds](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=281990&enddate=1490340614&feed=steam_community_announcements), 2017-02-23 Banks/Utopia preview in the official developer feed; relevant body read after direct forum retrieval failed. It presents a ruling consciousness, non-sentient workers, autonomous leaders and consequential disconnection, replacing ordinary factions/happiness.

**Inference:** A colony protagonist requires explicit bodies, control, membership and separation rules. It is a contrast to independent people exchanging messages, not permission to turn an existing NPC into a drone or concatenate private human records. Current Stellaris rules are not inferred from this historical preview.
