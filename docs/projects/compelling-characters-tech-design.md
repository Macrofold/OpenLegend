## Shared personal presentation

**Implemented for native producers and consumers:** the language/observer-reference owners render explicit participants and grammatical roles. Native producers should supply facts/attribution rather than choosing “I,” “me,” a global name or an arbitrary person’s perspective. The shared renderer receives the intended observer/owner and chooses subject/object/reflexive/possessive forms and permitted names. World narration remains an explicitly different view of the same eligible fact.

Exercise the same event with actor as viewer, a knowledgeable witness, an unknown witness, self-directed feeling, another-person target and targetless feeling. Preserve names containing pronoun-like text, verb agreement and exact quoted speech. Never rewrite “I” inside someone else’s quote to mean the listener. Source labels remain separate from grammatical person: first-person testimony is still testimony.

Extend existing typed facts and shared helpers where they carry the needed meaning; do not build a universal natural-language parser. Freeform backstories, accepted understanding and model summaries remain attributed text with explicit author/owner/perspective contracts. A shared envelope cannot guarantee grammatical correctness or truth inside arbitrary text. Validate/rerender native structure centrally; handle unusable generated content at its existing admission boundary without automatic paid repair. The affected native producers/consumers are migrated, including typed listener speech and shared feelings. This does not claim grammatical truth or automatic first-person conversion inside arbitrary authored/model prose. Newly added native producers must use the shared boundary; broader generated-summary semantic reliability remains CE03/CE05 work.

## Authorship and personality lifecycle

Keep authored history, current self-understanding, current dispositions, fleeting body/appraisals, accepted beliefs and operational goals distinct. Existing creator edits are explicit edits, not fictional experiences. New worlds use the authored seed; same-version restoration preserves accepted current data. A removed camp or changed possessions updates world state/knowledge, not an immutable biography assertion. An earlier camp experience can remain historical memory. Do not invent such an experience when moving a transient sentence out of biography.

Personality development should use the existing accepted identity/understanding and appraisal owners, with actual permitted experience as support and explicit creator authority where applicable. The absence of a complete automatic personality-development mechanism is tracked in CE/DG23; this design grants no new background model loop or arbitrary trait effects. A chosen temporary intention belongs to goals/plans. Scenario-seeded intentions remain explicitly labeled author interventions, not autonomous motivation.
