# Parallel assignment batches

This is the number and navigation register for allocations created with [create-parallel-tasks](../../.agents/skills/create-parallel-tasks/SKILL.md). Numbers identify successive allocations, not priority scores, engineer counts or completion. Detailed status and acceptance belong to the linked trackers. A later batch does not imply that every earlier parent project is complete.

Numbering was introduced on October 3, 2026 at the owner's request. The three existing allocations were renamed in their original order; PW, NP and PG identifiers, substantive requirements and completion evidence were preserved. Future new allocations take the next unused batch number under the skill; revisions retain their number.
