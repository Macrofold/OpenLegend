## Purpose and authority

Make stalking a hare, listening outside a hut, watching a doorway and surviving betrayal understandable, exciting activities in the ordinary living world. The player should be able to act on what their character notices, distinguish knowledge from guesses, and recover from a mistake without wrestling the interface.

This is the **SAD-G design**, covering the selected player-control and presentation portion of [ND13](../maintainers/needs-design.md#nd13--stable-action-recommendations-and-complete-control-customization). It consumes the integrated [stealth contracts](stealth-awareness-tech-design.md) and their [canonical specialist owners](stealth-player-experience-tech-design.md#contract-authority-and-source-inspection). The [technical design](stealth-player-experience-tech-design.md) owns publication, input timing, component responsibilities and developer acceptance. Implementation and player qualification remain separate work.

The engine executes supported actions and protects each observer's information. The installed world determines bodies, senses, recognizable details, movement speeds, sounds, emotions, preparation and combat. The interface submits intentions and presents admitted results. It never decides that someone is hidden, hostile, afraid, prepared, hit or recognized.

## What makes this worth playing

The distinctive loop is **notice something → choose what it might mean → change the situation → see what actually happens**. An encounter can end in a good observation, a meal, a useful conversation, an escape, a mistaken suspicion or a new relationship consequence. Killing every subject is not the success condition.

| Opportunity                            | Useful player decision and payoff                                                                                                   | Ordinary alternative and burden to justify                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| A living animal with its own attention | Choose approach, footing, pace, cover and when to stop; get close enough to observe or make a deliberate attack                     | Walking directly is simpler. Sneaking must offer a visible positional or informational advantage, not minutes of unexplained slow movement.      |
| An uncertain person or sound           | Watch, listen, call out, investigate a remembered region, ask a companion, or leave                                                 | Immediate contact remains viable. Investigation should provide useful limited knowledge, including a clear stopping point when nothing is found. |
| A defensible home                      | Build/use an entrance and deliberately watch a relevant approach; a willing companion can take over through existing social actions | Watching costs attention and may occupy work. It should create a useful opportunity to prepare, not an all-directions immunity icon.             |
| Practical invention                    | Use an actually supported muffled container, light placement, barrier or decoy to change physical conditions                        | No new recipe or physics is granted by this design. Existing useful tools and direct action remain sufficient alternatives.                      |
| Social intrigue                        | Recognize a voice without seeing its owner, notice a threatening movement, doubt an accusation, or experience betrayal              | No visible relationship color proves innocence or hostility. Perceived consequences can support later conversation and memory.                   |
| Recovery                               | Break sight, choose a route, stop aiming, stand when possible, defend or retreat while startled                                     | A failed ambush continues ordinary play; no forced reload, input freeze or scripted detection failure is added.                                  |

These are intended benefits, not measured fun. Later uncoached play must assess the whole activity, including approach time, reading, failed actions and recovery. The [repertoire coverage and decisions](#repertoire-coverage-and-decisions) keep this scope connected to exploration, adventure, useful hunting and independent inhabitants.

## The screen and the information promise

Keep the world as the primary workspace, with the existing React HUD, camera toolbar, inspection windows and narrow-screen sheets. Add a compact **Movement & attention** control group beside current work: actual posture, actual pace, Look, Watch, and Stop. Its expanded view contains Listen, Scan, Investigate and the ordinary offered defensive actions. The existing action browser exposes the same choices and explanations; this is not a second menu system.

A small **Noticed** placement within the existing sensory/caption presentation shares its scheduling, pending references and history route; it has no separate cue queue. It presents permitted cues and a route to **Events (J)**. In view contains current permitted subjects; remembered subjects open explicit historical inspection. **View source** opens the exact eligible occurrence in Events or the owning permitted personal-record reader when available, never a current body. **Character records** retains its separate story, promises and known places; no new history panel or shortcut is introduced. No minimap is required for the first slice.

A current contact becoming unseen immediately loses live behavior and controls when that observation loss is delivered. A separately authorized historical record can replace it. A rendering fade must never delay that demotion. No expiry timer is a claim that the person left, died or became safe.

### Historical detail

Historical inspection states its channel and time beside the subject: for example, **Last seen · 2 game minutes ago** and **Carried a spear when seen**. Hover/focus exposes the exact in-world observation time. The normal display uses the existing precision policy; it does not conflate accelerated game minutes with elapsed wall seconds.

Facets retain separate times. Hearing Ada now can establish **Ada's voice · just heard**, while the equipment section still says **Spear · last seen earlier**. An unknown voice is not retroactively named because a later person appears nearby. A reported claim remains attributed testimony; an inferred identity remains a suspicion.

The default marker does not fade toward an assertion of absence. It leaves the active scene after the bounded presentation interval in the [limits inventory](../limits/stealth-player-experience.md), while its eligible record remains accessible through historical inspection and its permitted source reader. Pinned historical inspection can remain open with the label. A silhouette option uses frozen, already permitted art and no animation, light, sound, shadow, reflection, collision or live hit testing.

Show remapped hints and separate requested from actual gaze throughout.

### Admission and interruptions

| Condition                                | What the player sees                                                                                     | What is allowed next                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Input captured locally                   | Immediate pressed/request affordance, retaining confirmed actual posture/work                            | Release/change the intention; do not show physical success.                                          |
| Request pending                          | Same control label and geometry; shared delayed loading indicator if needed                              | Duplicate discrete submission is guarded; Stop/release remains available.                            |
| Accepted, still turning/changing posture | Actual current pose plus the native transition/progress; “Turning” or “Changing posture” in current work | Only compatible actions; the renderer does not finish the transition early.                          |
| Reached / completed                      | Confirmed posture, pace or gaze; actual work receipt                                                     | Ordinary next action.                                                                                |
| Refused before replacement               | Old action/pose remains, with a reachable explanation such as “Not enough room to stand”                 | Choose another route/pose or explicitly stop the old action.                                         |
| Interrupted after acceptance             | Actual ending and cause, retained completed effects/costs and current posture                            | Reissue deliberately if desired; no hidden automatic retry.                                          |
| Technically incomplete                   | “Waiting for movement information” or existing scoped unavailable explanation                            | Retain facts but disable unsupported commands; never say the place is clear or the action succeeded. |
| No longer a current target               | Historical inspection if authorized; current action disabled                                             | Look/search the old area or select a new observed body.                                              |
| Connection stale or control lost         | Visible connection/control state; live movement input released and unsafe actions disabled               | Reconnect or regain control, then press again. Held keys and attacks are not replayed.               |

Crouching is short stationary physical work under C. Preflight and replacement occur atomically; a refused crouch preserves the old walk and held movement. If movement admission is still unconfirmed, show the one posture request as pending until that exact movement result is resolved, then submit it once against confirmed current work. This is not an accepted posture or a retry after refusal. The accepted old body remains physical truth until the transition completes and clearance is rechecked. Quiet pace changes use C's compatibility rules. During committed evasion neither pace nor posture is silently changed.

Successful stationary replacement or terminal blockage ends the held movement. Keys still down at that ending are ignored until released; a genuinely fresh direction press can start a new gesture without inheriting those old keys. Thus pressing A after W runs into a wall can move sideways, while an old held W cannot restart walking when crouch finishes. A toggle control becomes inactive. Show the ending/reason and the same fresh-input requirement beside movement controls; no automatic resume follows recovery or an obstacle disappearing.

The selected human evasion requires standing. **Evade while crouched refuses with a usable Stand option; it never secretly stands, changes the endpoint or consumes a dodge.** This is an important first-encounter teaching point. Startle itself supplies no categorical input lock; other injury or body restrictions retain their own reasons.

Direct movement may replace ordinary interruptible work under that work's existing rule, with its consequence visible in current work. It never supplies a second movement lane alongside aiming, crouching or guarding. Shortcuts use the same replacement/admission result as the menu. A refused request is not kept as an undisclosed queue to execute when a blocker later disappears.

### Watching, looking and inspecting

Looking changes actual sensory orientation; watching chooses a finite activity with a scope and purpose. Opening an inspector reads currently permitted details. A deliberate Inspect action may spend time to acquire additional evidence through E; the panel itself does not perform that action.

Start Watch with a visible region/direction preview, the installed method, duration, bodily requirement and what current work it replaces or attaches to. Default **Watch** observes that scope without adding danger anticipation. The distinct **Watch for danger** preset explicitly selects supported physical-attack concerns for the chosen approach, such as a doorway, without naming a hidden attacker. Server-confirmed feedback says **Preparing to watch** or **Watching this approach**, never **Safe** or **Immune to surprise**. Physical coverage can be fulfilled while preparation for danger still needs time: show **Watching this approach · preparing for danger** until F confirms its own preparation requirements. Coverage and expected danger descriptions include only the player's own chosen intention and permitted evidence.

When a contextual Watch or offered investigation vantage already supplies valid scope and installed defaults, open the existing activity review directly: show the scope, duration, cost and work consequences, then offer one deliberate **Start**. Open **Choose/change area** only for a missing consequential choice or an explicit edit. Reuse permitted places and the focused spatial preview: click/tap known terrain or adjust its point with arrow keys, with Shift for the larger displayed step. A region uses the installed method's labeled extents and visible outline. In that selector, **Confirm selection** returns to review without starting work, and **Cancel / Escape** restores the preceding draft. Its keys cannot also move the character or camera. A sound retains its original uncertainty and never supplies an exact source-derived default point. The [technical selector contract](stealth-player-experience-tech-design.md#choose-an-area-or-vantage) owns these input details without coordinate entry.

Compatible walking or guarding may carry E's finite attached watch. **Walk while watching** reviews a permitted destination and finite watch terms together, then starts them as one ordinary move with its attached observation. A refused admission preserves the previous work. A watch can also be attached to an already continuing compatible standalone walk. Later WASD steering preserves that same move/watch and original deadline while discarding the remaining click destination; it never resumes the old route. Plan-, activity- or cooperation-owned travel uses its own replacement rules. Watch expiry or explicit **Stop watching** ends only the watch and its owned look directive; ending the parent walk ends its attached watch. A heading change can break coverage while the body turns. The UI reflects actual coverage without automatic renewal or turning the character back.

Listen, scan, inspect and investigate show the installed expected time and real interruption rules. A spatial method may report **No new cue noticed in the checked area** with its actual time/coverage. Ambient Listen instead says **No new sound noticed during this listen** unless its owner supplied a spatially checked result. Neither can say **Nobody is there**. Incomplete work, obstructed coverage and no additional evidence are distinct outcomes. Neither repeated clicking nor opening several panels accumulates extra sensory exposure.

A sound direction is anchored to where the listener was when they heard it. Turning the camera can reproject that captured bearing; walking elsewhere cannot triangulate it using hidden source data. If the old bearing is no longer useful, keep the original-position label and history route rather than drawing a false current radar arrow. A new real cue can supply a new independent observation.

Critical admitted cues have text and visual equivalents independent of audio, tiny sprites and camera framing. They appear beside the existing sensory/current-work surfaces, remain reviewable in permitted history, and are not discarded beneath routine chatter. Presenting a cue does not promise that the world provided enough time to avoid every attack.

Lethal-action review uses the maximum harm authorized by the combat preview, including a possible surprise result. Its confirmation must not change solely because a target's hidden preparedness changed. An already released projectile never pauses for a new impact-time confirmation.

### Darkness, periphery, cover and art

The world determines whether the character detects a body or a particular detail, using the selected physical vision model. The renderer depicts only the resulting permitted facets. Raising brightness, changing graphics quality, orbiting, zooming, read-through or removing aesthetic blur must not grant more facts.

Earned appearance distinctions must also be readable. If art cannot depict a supported distinguishing feature, supply its permitted qualitative description through inspection/cue text. Do not make observably different faces indistinguishable merely because they share a sprite, or invent a distinction for actually matching features. A unique ID-coded portrait is not a substitute for perceived appearance.

Keep known terrain readable enough to navigate. Use dimmer material/value treatment to suggest darkness, rather than making essential controls or already permitted information illegible. Peripheral detection can present a vague movement before recognition; moving actual gaze can improve evidence through the world. Cover and low posture can change sightlines without guaranteeing concealment. A camera-side obstruction may be made transparent for an already permitted body; a character-side obstruction never is.

A neutral contact representation must not encode distinctive clothing, species, identity, a weapon or exact animation that has not been perceived. Preserve the art direction's believable scale and proportions. Prefer a readable cue and selection affordance to enlarging heads or inventing features. A current glow perceived around a corner can be shown at its permitted surface/region; a remembered fire cannot cast live light or reveal present silhouettes.

### Accessibility and devices

Essential information is available through keyboard-accessible HUD/list/history controls as well as world graphics. Use text, shape and labels together; color, sound, translucency and animation are never the only distinction between current, remembered and uncertain information. High contrast strengthens already authorized information. It does not reveal a hidden silhouette.

Reduced motion removes shake, sudden camera motion, pulsing and animated marker fades while retaining the same event text, status and choices. Startle uses a restrained optional self-focused pulse, not blur, camera seizure, disabled input or a fabricated targeting penalty. Audio alternatives use the same semantic cue and precision as audio; stereo or haptics cannot encode hidden exact bearings.

Keyboard remapping covers every new binding and affected existing binding, with conflict detection, clear scope, a visible restore-default action and current hints. Printable-letter shortcuts can be disabled outside the focused world; typing and screen-reader navigation retain priority. Hold actions have toggle/button alternatives with explicit active state and Stop. Do not reserve browser/OS shortcuts or require a chord a user cannot perform.

The minimum scope is desktop keyboard/mouse plus operable visible controls and responsive sheets. Touch click-to-move, tap selection and explicit Look/Watch controls must retain equivalent decisions; virtual sticks, controller profiles and full assistive-device qualification are later polish owned by UIUX/ND13, not claims made by this design. No task is available only through WASD or a drag.

## Complete journeys

These are proposed ordinary-player journeys with meaningful branches, not scripts compelling inhabitants to react. Their implementation checks are [G-AC](stealth-player-experience-tech-design.md#developer-ready-acceptance).

### G-J01 — First hare in daylight

1. The player walks into a permitted sightline. The small animal appears only at the detail actually earned; a neutral motion cue can precede “Hare.” The optional first-use hint names X crouch, Z quiet and Look, using remapped bindings.
2. The player presses X. Standing remains confirmed while crouch is pending/turning into the admitted posture; accepted completion changes the body's art and status. Pressing Z selects quiet pace independently.
3. W moves toward the hare; key release stops. Looking away or orbiting does not silently reveal more of the animal. Actual heard/seen movement, not an NPC awareness bar, explains what happens next.
4. The hare may continue feeding, look up, move or flee under its native policy. These are visible behaviors only when perceived, never a predicted detection state. The player can stop, choose cover, approach differently or leave.
5. Select the currently observed body. Inspection offers the actual compatible hunting/attack choice, range/preparation/cost and any relevant refusal. The first look/click never shoots. Choosing an attack uses the ordinary weapon and contact systems; no guaranteed sneak-kill label appears.
6. A miss, flight or decision not to shoot is a complete outcome. Noticing where the hare actually feeds or which visible opening it uses to escape can inform the next approach; no unobserved route, automatic ecological reward or permanent tracking knowledge is granted. If the player tries to evade while crouched, the interface explains the standing requirement and preserves control.

### G-J02 — Approach a walking person from behind

Use quiet/crouched movement if desired, then direct movement or the existing permitted behind/proximity action. The latter explicitly ends or follows its declared last-seen behavior on contact loss; it cannot track a hidden actor.

The person's body heading and head/gaze can differ while walking. Show the head turn only if that pose detail is currently authorized; do not fake a backward glance from their hidden detection state. The player can stop, use cover, speak or prepare an attack. Facing away alone guarantees neither safety nor surprise. A neutral figure stays unnamed until recognition/name evidence supports more.

### G-J03 — Footsteps beyond a wall; wrong person or nobody

The Noticed row says, for example, **Sounds like footsteps · heard to the right from your previous position**, with only the available bearing/region. The wall remains readable scenery; no target appears behind it. A mechanism producing the same received rhythm remains a possible explanation.

Choose Investigate. If the sound has no range, choose a permitted reachable vantage or known place from which to investigate the indicated area. A useful corner/doorway can lie beside or opposite the original bearing. Show that historical bearing separately; the chosen endpoint is not a source estimate. Review the finite search scope and start. New sound/sight can update evidence; retargeting is an explicit admitted choice.

A person found there is a newly perceived individual, not automatically the source of the earlier steps. If the search ends without new evidence, show what was checked and when. The player may stop, ask the person, broaden the search through another explicit action, or leave. No “area clear” guarantee or hidden-source pursuit is allowed.

### G-J04 — A familiar voice without sight

The caption uses the actual recognized name, or **A familiar voice**, and only the words heard. A second unfamiliar listener can get **An unfamiliar voice** instead. Neither receives a body marker.

Open the caption's historical/voice inspection. Previously seen possessions retain their last-seen timestamps. Talk/Call out uses ordinary speech and actual hearing, with no private channel implied; walking to the old sound area uses an explicit location choice. Attack/follow-body remains unavailable until a current physical target is legitimately acquired.

### G-J05 — Vigilance at a doorway

Select the visible doorway/approach and choose **Watch for danger**. With valid contextual scope, review its outline, method, duration, cost, work consequences and selected attack concerns, then choose **Start watching**. If the area needs changing, use the optional selector and **Confirm selection** to return to that review; confirmation alone starts no work. Default Watch would only observe. Current work shows actual watching and separately confirmed preparation; incomplete information cannot retain an old maintaining badge.

The player can move their camera, inspect permitted records, or choose a compatible defensive action without adding an all-around awareness power. Looking away, losing the needed sense or doing incompatible work updates/ends relevant preparation through the owners. Expiry is visible and does not renew itself. An unknown attacker entering that approach can meet a prepared defender; success of defense is still separate.

### G-J06 — Sudden betrayal

A familiar, apparently friendly person remains ordinary and neutrally presented. No hostility outline changes because their private plan changed. If the player actually perceives a threatening preparation, show that cue promptly and offer ordinary reactions; familiarity does not erase it.

If an attack arrives without adequate relevant preparation, the victim's permitted result may say **Caught off guard**. Show actual injury and any native startle afterward. Do not reveal the attacker's private motive or rewrite earlier conversation as knowingly false. The player may defend, flee, speak or seek help; witnesses keep their own separate accounts.

### G-J07 — Ambush fails against a prepared target

The player deliberately attacks through the current target/weapon choice. A preview explains their own commitment and known conditions, never the target's hidden readiness or a promised multiplier.

The target blocks, evades or is hit according to native resolution. Present only perceived outcomes. A failed surprise may produce no special attacker-side banner at all: “They were secretly prepared” is not an entitled fact. Actual visible guarding or a heard warning can support a later explanation. Ordinary combat, negotiation and escape continue.

### G-J08 — Recover while startled

After an eligible sudden attack, own status shows **Startled** and the actual consequences, such as less steady aim and a shorter available evade, with remaining normal-play time. It does not disable WASD, Stop, speech or ordinary flight merely for drama.

An already admitted evade keeps its frozen path and duration; a newly chosen endpoint must satisfy the current allowed range. A too-distant request refuses before cost and refreshes the offered choice. At pause, mechanical recovery stops. At debug 3×/8× all simulation work and recovery accelerate together; reading aids may remain readable without extending the effect. When finite startle ends, remove only that effect's claim: another authorized contribution can still affect aim or evasion. Do not announce calm, safety or full mechanical recovery, or invent a timer for a persistent effect. Longer-term appraisal applicability and disclosure remain conditional on SA-D01.

### G-J09 — Lose visual contact

A person walks behind cover. Each current visual display uses B's latest admitted 50 ms normal-play sample, frozen at its original measurement time. On delivered observation loss, remove unsupported body details, targeting and live animation; never advance a person from hidden current transforms between samples. If history is authorized, show **Last seen** at retained precision: a hollow marker for an admitted point, a region/bearing cue when that is all that was observed, or a history-list entry without a world marker when no useful location remains. Never infer hidden feet or a precise region center for a ghost. An independently heard voice can continue as its own approximate cue.

The open inspector changes to historical content in place and disables current-body commands. Camera orbit, cursor hover, asset completion and a delayed inspection response cannot restore the live person. A later real observation can establish a new current contact; use the identity owner's association, not matching clothing or proximity, to connect records.

### G-J10 — Creator inspection and return to play

An authorized user explicitly enters **Creator inspection** through the existing creator workspace. Show a persistent audience banner and only the grants actually provided; creator rights still exclude ungranted human-private material.

Holding a creator permission does not change an ordinary view. Explicit entry selects a server-validated creator audience, and the server sends a fresh scoped baseline. Hidden bodies can appear only in that creator audience. Returning to **Player perspective** first clears creator-only entities, cached details, targeting, lights, audio, map changes, history pages and late loads, then installs the ordinary baseline. No creator fact becomes character memory. A user cannot unlearn what they personally saw, so a playtest that used creator inspection is not evidence of an unspoiled ordinary-player experience.

## Minimum interface and progressive polish

1. **Safe playable foundation:** permitted current/historical projection across full views, patches, inspection and maps; direct movement and stop; posture/pace controls; independent looking; truthful work feedback; keyboard access and text/audio alternatives. No feature ships first with hidden bodies still sent to ordinary clients.
2. **Complete stealth decisions:** finite watch/listen/scan/investigate, own preparation/warning/status, existing attack/defense integration, all ten journeys and revoked/stale-state behavior. This is the minimum complete assignment scope, not optional later work.
3. **Progressive polish:** optional frozen silhouettes, refined pose animation, denser-scene cue layout, touch/controller convenience and measured input/render tuning. These improve the same permitted information, without changing mechanics or authorization.

Use one integrated first-playable world, including the camp, wildlife, residents and danger already selected by FPW/RW/CTR. Do not replace it with a stealth-only tutorial island. Optional first-use hints are short, dismissible and repeatable in help; they teach the current affordance, not a scripted NPC outcome.

## Repertoire coverage and decisions

The catalogue audit covers the scope and entry inventory of all 28 canonical catalogues, the action catalogue, supporting combination/selection/design/coverage documents and four world proposals. Directly relevant entries and all specialist design/hand-off contracts receive detailed review. This is not a new external-source study, reranking of every entry, or a claim to have revalidated every unrelated catalogue paragraph. The shared [repertoire review](stealth-awareness-repertoire-review.md) maintains the larger source map and the selected slice within the whole-game priorities.

| Families considered                                                                                                 | Decision for this interface                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Actions, mechanics, adventure/discovery, combat/rescue, abilities/progression                                       | Make approach, stopping, inspection, finite vigilance, retreat and meaningful uncertain results usable through ordinary controls; no hidden-target shortcut.                                |
| Bodies/species, traits, ecology/weather, materials/resources, objects, needs, work/crafting                         | Surface admitted bodily constraints and known surface/cargo consequences; preserve useful hunting and invention without adding scent, sprint, universal silent footsteps or new physiology. |
| Psychology/behavior, characters/backstories, relationships, diplomacy/conflict, institutions/politics, history/myth | Preserve private motives, voluntary response, mistaken identification, believable betrayal and attributed consequences; no global faction/awareness/emotion oracle.                         |
| Languages/knowledge, faith/ritual, arts/leisure                                                                     | Distinguish hearing, recognition, understood words and testimony; watching a performance or recognizing a companion uses the same information rules without hostile framing.                |
| Economy/logistics, settlements/architecture, technology                                                             | Routes, noisy carried goods, real entrances, lighting and usable home defense supply decisions; inventory administration is not a stealth prerequisite.                                     |
| Magic, unusual realities, automation/creators, simulation experiments                                               | Different senses and machines consume the same allowed observation/control contracts; unsupported channels stay explicit, and creator inspection remains separate.                          |
| Combinations, selection/scale, source atlas, coverage, design foundation and world proposals                        | Compose existing owners, keep uncertainty and native inhabitants, preserve whole-game priorities and avoid promoting alternate-world elaborations into first-playable dependencies.         |

The worthwhile additions are small and reusable: a clear historical inspection route, a sensory cue with an explicit next action, a finite watch that can end, and a control profile that leaves speech/crafting usable. Richer investigations, social deception, alarm networks and authored sensor devices can build on those seams when selected; they are not required new systems here.

## Open decisions and evidence limits

- **SA-D01 remains open:** the controlled-character fear producer, whether retained appraisals mechanically apply during direct control, disclosure, revocation and control-transfer behavior. D recommends native-body-only initial control (mask interpreted appraisal mechanics while preserving records/lifetimes), with deterministic interpretation if persistent player fear is desired; neither is adopted. This design specifies native startle and already authorized own feedback. It does not choose active versus masked prior fear, schedule AI for a human-controlled character or disclose a former controller's private thoughts.
- **Implementation and qualification remain open:** use the [integrated owner contracts](stealth-player-experience-tech-design.md#integrated-dependencies-and-remaining-decision) for exact evidence references, sampled visual values, preparation timing and control admission. Runtime help must continue describing shipped controls until the new controls are implemented.
- Numerical presentation/input defaults are selected starting proposals in the limits inventory, not measured optimums. Human understanding, enjoyment, accessibility, latency, graphics and load acceptance remain unchecked in the tracker.
