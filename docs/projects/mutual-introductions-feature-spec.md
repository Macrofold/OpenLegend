# Play together now: a mutually accepted introduction

| Status      | Current progress                                                                                                                                     | Last updated |

| In progress | The researched two-adult introduction proposal and game-first review are complete; adoption, technical design and runtime qualification remain open. | 2026-10-05   |

## Purpose and selected experience

A player wants company for something worth doing in the game. They make that particular intention visible, another player asks to join, and the first player accepts. They meet, explore or try something useful together, and may part without having to become friends. The introduction has succeeded if it removes the awkward search for a willing companion and helps both people enjoy actual play.

This is the product proposal for **DG10/ND31**. Select **one immediate introduction between two already admitted adult humans in the same world**. Consume the [official adult audience](resident-conduct-feature-spec.md), [community participation proposal](community-entry-and-participation-feature-spec.md) and their applicable release gates. This is a native activity offer, not a personality-matching service, a recurring group, a family mode or a real-world gathering. The larger [together archive](../../archive/08-wellbeing-vision/05-ideas-together.md) remains a source of ideas rather than an adopted programme.

The first qualification uses CP01's two embodied humans, **including any human welcome host**. There is no third embodied facilitator. A separately authorized service reviewer is an operational role, not an extra participating character. This proposal does not increase the world's measured capacity, admit another account, allocate another character or promote an existing characterless grant.

The player-facing action is **Play together now**. It opens the current activity offers and the option to publish one. The ordinary game remains usable alongside it. A player who never opens this feature can still meet people naturally, talk, explore, give items and leave through the existing rules.

## One complete first journey

Mira wants company while exploring the nearby trail and finding supplies for a longer outing. She chooses the supported **Explore the near trail** activity, the public camp meeting point and a relaxed pace with text conversation. The preview explains the exact audience. She publishes:

> **Mira · Human player**  
> **Explore the near trail · about 15–30 minutes**  
> Meet at the camp meeting point. Relaxed pace, text conversation. No combat planned.

The duration is an estimate of human time, not a promise to stay or a game timer. The activity must be possible in the actual qualified scene: a reachable route, something interesting to inspect or try, and ordinary reasons for both participants to care. A proposed scene is not advertised as available until its content and interactions work.

Jon, another eligible adult currently playing in this world, sees Mira's deliberately published card. **Ask to join Mira** shows the activity, meeting point, duration, risk expectation and the limited information Mira will receive about him. He chooses to send that request. Mira sees Jon's disclosed local character identity and the exact same terms; she chooses **Accept Jon**. Those are the two specific consents. There is no third confirmation that both should confirm again.

Each receives a Service notice with the agreed plan and meeting landmark. They move there themselves. Jon can use ordinary Talk to greet Mira; the game does not write a greeting for him or quietly create a private conversation. They choose their route, inspect something interesting and try their own supported actions. A conversation can be brief or sparse. Shared attention to what happens in the world can carry the encounter.

For a concrete qualification scene, place actual harvestable Wild berries and native fiber along the supported route during ordinary world authoring. Mira chooses the berries for her next outing's food; Jon gathers and prepares fiber because he wants binding material for a bag. Let Jon select his own actions, inspect the output and use the already supported native cord preparation: two actual prepared flexible-fiber items become one cord. The consumed inputs are gone, the cord is his, and its supported binding use is visible through the [item owner](../worlds/base/items.md#cordage-manufacture-and-reuse). Mira can point out the route and compare what each found, but cannot complete Jon's work for him. Both choose whether to continue exploring or return. This is an initial authored example with real useful outputs, not a new recipe, a mandatory chore sequence or a resource refill per introduction.

If they return with useful materials, each keeps what they actually acquired. They may use a real gift or, after its own qualification, [DG06 barter](exchange-and-cooperation-feature-spec.md). They can continue playing, finish the introduction, or leave. If they want another outing, DG06's proposed optional remembered understanding can be available after its own qualification. No mandatory survey, friendship ceremony, home-choice prompt or future appointment interrupts their return.

Sky's published Guide feature is a useful precedent for an actual missing objective, a willing helper and temporary cooperation that does not require friendship. Open Legend borrows that limited social shape, not Sky's handholding or movement control. [R1]

## A worthwhile activity comes before matching

The initial offer serves a small authored set of **already supported activities**, with enough flexibility to state a simple preference. It does not invent missions on demand or ask a model to promise content the world cannot deliver.

The preferred first outing is exploration with optional practical interaction. It should offer a choice about where to look, an observable result from trying a tool or native action, and an easy stopping point. A newcomer needs something to do besides following an expert who performs everything. An experienced player needs a reason to enjoy the outing besides earning a badge for supervising a novice. Demonstrating a clever use, choosing a route together or discovering how the other player approaches a problem can supply that value.

Do not make routine camp upkeep the price of receiving company. Cooking or gathering can belong when the participants choose it for a concrete purpose, but neither newcomer chores nor conversation icebreakers should delay the enjoyable part. The [gameplay priorities](../repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md) remain controlling: adventure, enemies, equipment, useful crafting and recoverable mistakes must make the world worth sharing.

### What an activity may promise

| Offered activity           | What must be real before it is offered                                                                                                                    | What the introduction does not promise                                                                                 |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Explore the near trail     | An accessible, currently supported route and at least one interesting permitted interaction; the meeting point is usable.                                 | A new discovery for every veteran, fresh loot for every participant, a private instance or guaranteed harmless travel. |
| Try a useful tool together | A participant deliberately offers to demonstrate a tool or use a supported interaction; the needed item and access can be obtained through ordinary play. | Shared control, a combined crafting process, reserved inputs, lending rights or an invention outcome.                  |
| Visit the hollow           | DG07's encounter, warning, retreat and recovery have qualified; the listing states possible danger and the single finite cache reward.                    | Both people receiving a spear, consent to fight, exclusive access or restoration of an emptied cache.                  |

A human-authored plan is labeled as that person's plan. The service does not certify “there is still a spear” merely because someone typed it. System-authored availability uses only facts the service is permitted to disclose for that offering. Hidden loot contents, another player's private inventory or unseen discoveries must not become a matchmaking signal. When a permitted fact changes, show the changed circumstance rather than preserve a stale claim to improve acceptance rates.

If the hollow cache has already been emptied, the two people can still choose the encounter for its play, explore elsewhere within supported content or try a real tool. Do not generate replacement spears, participation currency or an artificial consolation reward. A finite world needs repeatable interesting choices and useful interactions; a matching feature cannot repair exhausted or dull content by misrepresenting it.

DG07 entry remains an independent choice at the actual danger boundary. Selecting a card about the hollow expresses interest in visiting; it is not bodily-risk consent. A partner who declines can remain on the trail, choose another available activity or stop. The other player cannot convert a relaxed outing into mandatory combat by accepting on their behalf.

The activity must remain possible to stop without the introduction itself stranding the remaining person. It cannot require one participant to maintain a switch, carry the other's only means of escape or hold irreplaceable property merely to keep the social flow alive. Ordinary world hazards still exist and use their actual recovery owner. A later authored cooperative puzzle with such dependencies would need its own complete departure design.

## Deliberate discovery and limited disclosure

The board is visible only to eligible, admitted adult humans currently participating in this same qualified world. A new arrival outside capacity does not browse a private people directory while waiting. Public discovery and admission remain DG08's separate journey.

Only people who deliberately publish an offer appear. Being connected, talking to an NPC, selecting a home, having played before or accepting a previous introduction does not publish availability. The board says **Available offers**, not “everyone online.” A person can remain in the community and decline this feature entirely. Donut explicitly separates introduction opt-out from continued channel membership; that is a useful control precedent, not evidence of a social outcome. [R6]

Before publication, show the proposed card and the audience in ordinary language. The first card contains the local character label deliberately shared for this purpose, **Human player** attribution, selected activity, public meeting landmark, approximate duration, language and a simple pace/conversation preference. An optional short activity note can add something useful, such as “I would like to try the tool myself.” It is not a real-life biography. The [MI inventory](../limits/mutual-introductions.md) owns length, active-offer and timing bounds.

The first offer is English-language text play under RC01. Preferences such as **Quiet is fine**, **Explain as we go** and **Relaxed pace** describe the requested interaction; they do not identify a diagnosis, prove skill or impose a rule on all future conversation. A published character label does not reveal the human's account name, other characters or real identity, and does not rewrite how in-world observers recognize that character.

Do not disclose live coordinates, hidden map regions, inventory, personal notes, private conversation, reports, age evidence, emotional assessments or another-world aliases. Do not infer loneliness, grief, relationship status or a desire for human help from resident memories. A service support request is not an invitation to be matched. RC02's personal-follow-up preference remains independent and applies exactly as its owner specifies.

At the two-human envelope there may be only one other offer. Show it honestly. An empty board says no one has an available offer, with **Offer an activity**, **Continue playing** and the ordinary exit available. It does not claim nobody else is online, identify who opted out, fabricate interest, automatically substitute an NPC or search another world.

Do not introduce popularity ordering, compatibility percentages, public rejection counts or a leaderboard. If the qualified audience later grows, filtering should use deliberately selected activity and interaction preferences; the current proposal does not authorize a private social graph or an all-person ranking calculation. That expansion must first show that finding a willing partner is a real problem at the measured larger population.

### A resident's limited role

A resident asked how to find company can point to Play together now in its own voice through ordinary admitted cognition. The complete first journey must work without that conversation. No resident is a compulsory broker or an always-on recruiter.

A private service card is not automatically something the resident heard. In this first version, residents cannot inspect the board or select humans from private service records. A person can mention their own plan aloud, creating the ordinary actually heard evidence, but a resident must not embellish it into another human's assent. “Jon would love to meet you” requires Jon's actual authorized statement; a matching guess is not evidence.

The service never ghostwrites human greetings, enthusiasm, acceptance, excuses or farewells. Human-authored text and structured Service notices retain their own attribution. Research on AI-assisted messaging shows that assistance can affect language and partner judgments, while the negative association with suspected use is correlational. It supports taking attribution seriously, not hiding assistance or predicting that all assistance is harmful. [R10]

## Offer, request and acceptance

Publishing an activity authorizes receiving a suitable request about that card. It does not accept the other person in advance. The requester consents to the identified publisher and the displayed current terms. The publisher's later acceptance completes this particular introduction.

The first version gives each account one coordination place: **Available, Pending or Accepted**, never a pending introduction alongside an accepted one. A person must end their accepted coordination or let it expire before publishing or requesting again; End is immediate and does not stop ordinary play with the previous partner. This is not a queue of possible companions. While a request is pending, the involved offers stop accepting further requests; their owners can cancel rather than juggle applications. If the requester had published their own offer, the request review explains that sending will withdraw that offer. One deliberate send performs that replacement; do not add an unrelated confirmation ceremony.

The request discloses only the requester's permitted local character label, human attribution and selected interaction preferences alongside the unchanged activity. It includes no freeform direct message. The publisher can **Accept**, **Decline** or leave it unanswered. A request notice is quiet and nonblocking: it neither pauses native work nor forces the person to stop controlling a character in danger.

The card's activity, public meeting point, stated danger, approximate length and interaction expectations are material terms. Editing them closes pending requests; the requester sees **The offer changed** and may inspect a new request. Changing only layout or harmless text formatting does not invalidate agreement. Do not change the selected partner silently or treat a casual spoken “yes” as acceptance of an unseen service card.

If both people attempt requests at once, settle one pending proposal with identified terms. The later action shows the real existing request, with its accept/decline choices; it does not create a second accepted pair or treat a request about different terms as agreement. A repeated send, old panel, reconnect or late delivery returns the actual committed outcome. Acceptance records the two actual consents once. A permitted client can recover that result after delayed or failed delivery; neither participant is told that the partner saw the notice without evidence that they did.

Before delivery and acceptance, recheck the relevant current audience, account access, human control, contact restrictions, offer terms and participation. A revoked grant, unsupported audience or blocked pair produces a truthful unavailable outcome with no new contact authority. A person who became incapacitated or entered an activity that the qualified offer cannot interrupt does not remain advertised as ready to meet. Known unavailability withdraws the pending offer; this is not a promise that connection status can detect every distracted human.

RandomCoffee's published flow shows that a participant can opt into a round before learning whom they will meet. FFXIV's event guide likewise distinguishes applications, acceptance and ordinary messages. For this voluntary game activity, choose identified mutual consent with a short native request flow. Do not import automatic participation, a calendar or a message negotiation service. [R4], [R8]

### Decline, silence and repeated requests

A decline closes the request privately. The requester receives **This introduction is unavailable** without a reason, a score or a fabricated excuse. The publisher need not explain. In a world with two humans, someone may infer who declined; do not promise anonymity that this situation cannot provide.

When a requester cancels or is declined, their withdrawn offer is not recreated. The publisher's unchanged card can become available again for its original remaining window if the publisher is still eligible and participating; a decline still excludes that requester from the same card. Publisher withdrawal, known unavailability or expiry closes the card. None of these transitions renews the original availability window.

The declined requester cannot submit again to that same offer, including edited or reloaded versions. Refresh, reconnect and a second character do not create a new permission to pester its publisher. The publisher can deliberately publish a new availability offer later; that is a new opportunity, unless a standing Block still applies. Continued unwanted contact uses DG08's account-scoped Block and review, not an expanding collection of automatic social punishments.

Unanswered and expired requests create no obligation, resident disappointment, reliability mark or public notice. No reminder is delivered through an NPC or outside the game. The person waiting can cancel and continue at any time; nobody needs to exhaust a response window before going back to play.

## Human time, interruptions and current availability

Use one clearly disclosed **30-real-minute availability window** for a published offer. Any pending request ends with that offer; it has no additional shorter response countdown. Manual publication or renewal is deliberate, with current terms and audience review. Reading, refresh, background presence and reconnect do not automatically renew availability. These are proposed qualification values, not a measured optimum.

Acceptance closes both public offers and starts a **30-real-minute coordination window** for this accepted introduction. That window keeps the agreed plan reachable through a brief connection interruption. It is not a maximum length for playing together or a new world appointment. When it ends, the record becomes **Coordination ended** and produces no further coordination notices. Actual conversation, movement, possessions, work and continued play remain untouched. Neither person has to prove they arrived or press a completion button before the world continues.

The availability and coordination windows use real time because they describe human participation. Native travel, work and bodies retain the world's clock; currently 1× advances sixty game seconds per real second. Opening this panel grants no pause. The DG07 activity remains at its selected 1× profile, with the existing ordinary pause and departure rules. A host cannot silently accelerate an accepted outing beyond the qualified offer.

These finite windows are cleanup and expectation limits, not social pressure. Show an understandable expiry time rather than a flashing countdown. If terms expire while someone is reading, preserve the readable card and explain why its action is no longer available. A fresh offer needs a fresh deliberate request; do not auto-submit the person's stale choice when another slot opens.

The window eventually ends without a no-show verdict.                                                                                                                                                                                     |

Donut documents inactivity and insufficient available participants as reasons introductions may not happen. The relevant lesson is to handle absence honestly. Its multi-round inactivity classification is not adopted as a game reputation rule. [R7]

## Meeting and ordinary human conversation

The first supported meeting point is a public camp landmark that both participants are entitled to use and that the qualified scene actually supports. The offer can deliberately disclose that landmark. It cannot disclose a hidden route, promise entry to a private site or draw a trail to the partner's moving body.

The accepted notice identifies the two deliberately disclosed local identities, the activity and the meeting point. It can show the permitted ordinary landmark view or map information; if navigation cannot provide a supported route, say so. It never teleports, starts following, cancels work, spends supplies or turns one participant into the other's passenger. A person who needs help finding the place can use the ordinary supported world controls or end the introduction. Do not invent an invisible guide operator to rescue the flow.

The coordination panel contains structured operational notices only: requested, accepted, unavailable and ended, with the agreed details. It is not a direct-message channel, a remotely audible conversation or a new private room. The first journey deliberately avoids needing freeform remote coordination: people choose a recognizable public meeting point and an available-now activity. If they cannot reliably meet that way, fix the arrival/place presentation before adding a general messenger.

Once nearby, ordinary human-to-human Talk supplies the human's actual chosen speech without an NPC reply request. Selecting a person as the addressee does not make speech private. Other actual listeners may hear. Introduction acceptance does not automatically join or merge conversations, disclose earlier conversation history or give either person the other's private narration.

The [conversation owner](../narration-and-conversations.md) permits one current conversation per actor in the initial slice and defines how existing groups can merge. Silent current members can be included in that merge; their earlier histories retain their original audiences. The introduction UI must not promise an isolated two-person conversation. If the player wants to leave an existing conversation before greeting the partner, provide the explicit native conversation-leave action through that owner. Hiding the current Talk panel is not that action.

### Finishing without a relationship debt

**End introduction** stops this feature's future coordination for both participants. It is always available to either person and requires no reason or model approval. It does not revoke world access, end a separately chosen exchange, rewrite completed work or force the other person to leave. A quiet Service notice reports that coordination ended; it does not invent a farewell.

**Leave conversation**, **Block contact**, **Report** and **Leave world** retain their separate meanings and owners. Make them reachable where relevant without hiding the quick End action behind a misconduct form. An in-world conversation can continue after introduction coordination ends. A participant who wants to stop speaking uses the explicit conversation action or ordinary movement; a person who wants all further directed contact stopped uses Block.

A later remembered understanding under DG06 requires its own exact scope and mutual acceptance. Nothing is created automatically from a pleasant outing. No continuing work, meeting obligation, access right, shared chest, attendance expectation or friend status follows from this introduction. If the people never meet again, that does not retrospectively turn an enjoyable afternoon into a failed result.

## Cooperation, property and economic incentives

Each human chooses their own supported actions. Inputs, outputs, injury, interruptions and access checks remain real. The introduction creates no shared inventory, merged crafting process, reserved workstation, permission to feed another person, contribution balance or automatic distribution of rewards. Existing consent-aware handover and ordinary permitted containers are sufficient for the initial activity; reciprocal exchange can be added after DG06 qualifies independently.

Avoid making the first social activity a contest over an advertised personal prize. The hollow's single spear can be honestly discussed, but the outing should have value even for the person who does not take it. The service does not secretly choose the more deserving participant, award a copy or record a moral debt. Their later trade or gift is an actual voluntary action.

Do not award currency, scarce materials, faster progression or public prestige for publishing, accepting, staying the whole window, producing a positive rating or accepting another introduction. Such rewards would make repeated fake meetings and rushed social exchanges economically attractive. The reward should come from ordinary useful play and the encounter itself. This is a design judgment about this economy, not a claim that all reward programmes in other games fail.

An experienced participant volunteers for this outing. Their character progression, previous visit or host role does not certify teaching skill, confer moderator powers or require them to absorb every newcomer question. Blizzard's documented guide volunteering and withdrawal provide a useful precedent for an explicit help role; the first Open Legend introduction needs no mentor rank or eligibility grind. [R3]

The cooperative-play literature motivates a testable preference for a shared objective over immediately competing with the new partner. One small laboratory study found more reciprocal behavior after cooperative than competitive play, while its comparison with a no-game control was not conventionally significant. It does not establish that a shared outing creates friendship or a health benefit. [R9]

## Problems, unwanted contact and private evidence

Consume DG08's contact controls at publication, request delivery, acceptance and later coordination. A standing Block suppresses the pair's direct contact in both directions across the managed account scope; it withdraws pending requests and ends accepted coordination. It does not reverse an already completed gift, hide colliding bodies, rewrite shared speech or erase another person's actual memory. Do not expose alternate-account associations or the private reason for the restriction.

Actual harassment remains reportable through DG08's proposed case flow once qualified, with deliberately selected permitted evidence and separately authorized human review. The new activity card, request and acceptance/ending receipt can be selected when they are available to the reporting person. Their presence is evidence of what was offered or accepted, not proof that the promised activity happened or a participant's accusation is true.

Use CP03–CP04 for evidence size, status, case deadlines, appeal and retention. A reviewer does not acquire the whole conversation, NPC mind, account history or save merely because the report mentions an introduction. Report count is not guilt, a refusal is not misconduct, and ordinary absence is not evidence of malicious intent. The case remains reachable after world access ends under its existing owner.

The feature does not solicit real addresses, external usernames, photographs, diagnoses or off-platform meetings. The selected managed offer does not provide dating, therapy, emergency intervention, real-world venues or family participation. RC01–RC05 govern the supported audience, attribution and resident conduct. Private personal disclosures cannot be repurposed into activity recommendations or intervention assignments.

If a participant is unpleasant, the other person should be able to end coordination and leave without completing the activity or submitting feedback. Actual immediate world danger follows the qualified gameplay exit, including DG07 where applicable. A report or emotional disclosure cannot itself grant a teleport, pause or protective effect that other players cannot legitimately use.

## Current foundation and changed product behavior

The current source supports admitted human characters, current control, native human-directed Talk, audience-scoped speech and ordinary voluntary item actions. The composer opens without buying a reply for a human target. This is source inspection, not fresh two-human runtime evidence. Nearby NPC reactions and independently enabled narration can still use their normal admitted model work.

New behavior here is deliberately published activity availability, narrow service disclosure, identified mutual request/acceptance, the finite coordination receipt and its end/contact lifecycle. None is claimed to exist merely because invitations, public world overview or conversation membership already exist. DG06–DG09 are related product proposals with their own open implementation and adoption gates.

Keep service coordination separate from authored world content. World authors own the actual activities, place names, tool capabilities, danger and reward facts. The service owns the voluntary disclosure/contact flow. No universal engine rule says an outing must involve a camp, a boar, a spear or this audience policy.

## Cost and performance expectations

Publishing, reading, requesting, accepting, ending, expiry and human-authored speech need **zero additional model calls**. An empty board costs no resident inference. Do not purchase a compatibility assessment, recruit through repeated NPC calls, infer a person's availability from dialogue or run an automated post-outing relationship analysis. This avoids making a quiet community expensive merely because it has no partner to offer.

Ordinary residents still make their own choices when their actual circumstances call for cognition, including eating or reacting to heard speech. Preserve their complete accepted identity, About Me, current conversation and required commitments. Their legitimate cost belongs in the complete session measurement; it cannot be omitted to advertise a free human activity. No native fake response or hidden truncation of necessary history is allowed to satisfy a budget.

At the proposed two-human profile, matching needs no all-pairs calculation. Check the current narrowly eligible offers and their consent/contact conditions before preparing the displayed information. If later population growth makes that work expensive, select the bounded permitted candidate set before materializing full cards; a short displayed list alone is not a bound on a large private search. No embeddings, psychological scores or whole-history analysis are justified by this first use.

Measure the cost of a complete offered session: native simulation, ordinary NPC cognition, delivery, retained coordination information and any actual human support time. Count failed and abandoned introductions as costs too. Report useful meetings and enjoyment separately from request volume. A tool that generates many accepted cards but sends people to wait alone is failing its purpose.

Retain a participant's limited request/acceptance/end receipt for a proposed **seven real days after the terminal outcome** for recovery and ordinary reporting, accessible only through the permitted participant/reviewer scope. Public offers disappear when withdrawn or unavailable; there is no public archive of people who declined. The receipt records the disclosed plan, selected identities, actual consent and outcome, not a transcript, inferred relationship or location trail. An explicitly submitted case preserves its selected copy under CP03–CP04, independently of ordinary receipt expiry.

Ending publication or removing the receipt cannot erase something another human already saw or ordinary in-world evidence. Do not promise that all recipients forget. Gameplay restore cannot resurrect expired service availability, an ended introduction or contact permission that a current Block forbids. This is service continuity, not a new engine save-compatibility scheme.

Active-count, expiry and per-note bounds do not bound retained total bytes, repeated publication rate, deferred delivery or all database work. MI04 records those unselected technical budgets for MP/PD/data qualification. Overload must produce a clear unavailable result before claiming a new request was sent or accepted, preserve the last true outcome and leave native End/Leave usable. Do not silently drop accepted consent, automatically retry a request or widen the audience to compensate.

Human operating time should be concentrated on real problems. The operator does not chase no-shows, schedule meetings, write excuses or require reports from every pair. Fred Wilson's firsthand discussion of double opt-in describes both the burden of unwanted introductions and the work required of an introducer. The native identified request/accept flow adopts the consent lesson without assuming a free human concierge. [R5]

Evaluate the outing before expanding the finder. Observe time spent looking for the partner, time to a useful shared action, confusion about controls/permissions, avoidable waiting, unwanted contact and whether each participant would freely choose something similar again. Invite brief optional feedback at a suitable testing moment; do not build a compulsory in-game rating form or infer enjoyment from duration alone.

Compare a small invited pair using the card with the ordinary permitted way of meeting at the same landmark. If the card adds more reading and delay than it removes, simplify it or keep natural meeting as the preferred route. If people meet quickly but the activity is dull, improve the game content. Do not respond by adding an AI host, compatibility model or compulsory icebreaker.

Health, loneliness and durable friendship are outside this qualification claim. A separate selected DG11 study would need its own question, consent, data and interpretation. Neither a higher acceptance rate nor longer playtime silently authorizes that study.

## Sequence and game-first critique

1. **Prove the actual two-human outing using current entry.** Pick the real camp landmark and a supported nearby activity, verify ordinary movement/Talk/exit and observe whether each person enjoys the play. Keep source-level foundation distinct from measured runtime evidence. Do not build discovery to conceal missing content.
2. **Add the native offer and exact mutual agreement.** Deliver publication preview, limited disclosure, request/acceptance, honest current status, cancellation and the selected real-time windows. Qualify this invited adult flow under existing access; broader public release remains gated.
3. **Complete the contact, evidence and service integration.** Consume adopted DG08 block/review/capacity and DG09 audience/attribution/privacy rules, including races, expired information, reconnect and permitted receipts. The previous steps do not authorize offering an unsafe incomplete flow to strangers.
4. **Evaluate the complete offered journey and its real costs.** Select the actual deployment, adult eligibility, native/model budgets and available support through MP/PD/PS. Only expand concurrent people, activities or discovery after the complete initial experience is enjoyable and sustainable.

DG07 is a dependency only if the hollow is offered; its unreleased danger profile need not block a qualified harmless native activity. DG06 barter and remembered arrangements are optional later additions, not prerequisites for the first hello. DG08 public-entry expansion is unnecessary for a closed invited test, while its applicable contact/authority safeguards remain prerequisites for the managed public offer. No family programme, weekly group or DG11 health study is smuggled into that release sequence.

The main risk is overdesigning a social wrapper around an underdeveloped game. The review therefore removes automatic matchmaking, a third facilitator, remote freeform chat, a second readiness ceremony, multiple response countdowns, attendance/reliability scores, introduction rewards and required relationship completion. The retained steps each protect an actual player choice: deliberately publish, request one person's plan, accept that person, meet, and freely stop.

Some awkwardness remains legitimate. A small world may have nobody available; a stranger may not be enjoyable company; a useful finite reward may already be gone. The product should make those outcomes understandable and easy to leave. It should not fabricate enthusiasm, refill the world or turn human relationships into a retention mechanism to avoid them.

Deferred ideas include larger or recurring groups, family/minor participation, calendars, cross-world matching, external venues, voice contact, human mentor ranks, generative facilitation and psychological recommendations. Each needs a selected user problem, its own permission/operating consequences and evidence that this basic experience is already worthwhile. They are not missing pieces of the chosen DG10 journey.
