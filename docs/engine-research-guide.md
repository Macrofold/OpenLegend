# Using engine research to improve OpenLegend

This guide connects the [current Unreal and Unity research](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/README.md) to OpenLegend's existing product and design documents. The engine evidence cutoff is **2026-10-23**; this design integration was reviewed on **2026-10-24**. Its [version and evidence baseline](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/01-evidence-and-version-baseline.md) owns external claims; this guide supplies **interpretation and design preparation**, not measured OpenLegend benefits.

The opportunity is to make the project's defining experiences work well together: useful invention, people with independent purposes, places worth exploring and changing, and consequences that matter on a later return. [Gameplay priorities](repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md) and the [first-playable world](worlds/base/playable-openings.md#first-playable-game-target) already select that direction. The engine study helps explain ways to support it and questions to resolve inside existing designs. It does not select an engine migration, new world laws, a population target or a new implementation programme.

## Perspectives worth carrying forward

The examples below are **design hypotheses within existing ambitions**, not reports of delivered or observed gameplay. Each perspective connects a potential benefit to the evidence and the document that can turn it into a scoped decision.

### Spend computation where it changes an encounter

Scheduled work, reusable calculations and continuing activities offer a positive possibility: a person can pursue a recognizable purpose while the computer spends more effort on circumstances that could change it. A resident continuing a chosen task, noticing a consequential interruption and voluntarily deciding what to do next can be more intelligible than constant reconsideration.

The design question is which changes require a new choice, which accepted work can continue, and how soon the difference must become apparent to the participants. Sharing geometry or unchanged definitions can leave more computation available for meaningful differences between people. Whether that improves responsiveness or operating cost needs a complete-path comparison.

Use [simulation and perception](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/02-simulation-and-perception.md), [AI execution](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/03-ai-navigation-and-crowds.md) and [data/concurrency](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/05-data-concurrency-memory.md). The [continuing-life designs](projects/continuing-lives-feature-spec.md), [situation-aware context](projects/situation-aware-cognition-context-feature-spec.md), and [intelligent-simulation comparison](projects/intelligent-simulation-evaluation-feature-spec.md) already own the relevant behavior and qualification.

### Let useful capabilities combine across activities

The study of definitions, tasks, effects and content lifetimes makes a transferable engineering opportunity visible: a capability can have a clear executable meaning while different authored worlds give it different purposes and consequences. That supports OpenLegend's ambition for an invention or learned method to be useful beyond its first demonstration.

A proposed tool becomes interesting when it changes an available approach, can be understood and used through ordinary interaction, and retains a worthwhile use afterward. The relevant question is what existing operations can express that behavior, and precisely which computation is missing if they cannot. A general plugin, graph or scripting system is not itself the player benefit.

Read [gameplay frameworks](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/11-gameplay-frameworks-and-semantic-systems.md), [content tooling](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/09-content-tools-ui-and-platforms.md) and [code execution](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/14-scripting-compilation-and-execution.md) alongside [invention foundations](invention-foundation.md), [action capabilities](action-capabilities.md), and [engine/world boundaries](engine-and-world-boundaries.md). These owners preserve the difference between reusable mechanisms and a world's particular rules.

### Make a person recognizable through conduct and expression

Independent choice, movement, timing, posture, optional speech and later behavior can reinforce one another. Animation, sound and presentation research is useful when it helps someone recognize what a character is actually doing, distinguish people, or understand a changed intention. A technically correct choice can gain emotional and practical meaning through its visible follow-through.

A useful comparison asks which visible cue carries that meaning, whether the cue survives the selected quality settings, and whether it agrees with the actual action and the observer's permitted evidence. More visual detail is one possible means. A clearer pause, orientation or completion signal may answer a different need. Private thoughts and inferred motives retain their existing disclosure boundaries.

Use [animation and audio](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/08-rendering-animation-and-audio.md) and [current motion/agent research](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/12-research-frontiers-and-evidence-boundaries.md). [Compelling characters](projects/compelling-characters-feature-spec.md), [world presentation](world-presentation.md), [production workflows](production-workflows/README.md) and [character qualification](maintainers/character-experience.md) own the complete result.

### Connect an accomplishment to what happens later

Persistent identity, recorded effects, personal memory and visible representation can have different lifetimes. Understanding those differences helps preserve the continuity that makes a place, invention or relationship worth returning to.

The positive question is what a previous activity changes about a later opportunity. A character's recollection might affect a voluntary choice; something made earlier might enable a different approach; unfinished work might remain understandable and resumable. Merely storing more history does not answer that question. The later encounter needs an actual opportunity for the earlier consequence to matter, and the player needs access to an appropriate part of that consequence.

Read [world streaming](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/06-world-streaming-and-scale.md), [persistence and replay](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/07-networking-persistence-and-replay.md) and [memory/action research](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/12-research-frontiers-and-evidence-boundaries.md) with [memory architecture](memory-architecture.md), [action experience](action-experience.md), [save/load](save-and-load.md) and the existing [episode and later-return comparison](projects/intelligent-simulation-evaluation-feature-spec.md#the-episode-not-the-preferred-answer).

### Use atmosphere and physical feedback to make places inviting and understandable

Lighting, materials, clouds, water, sound and motion can give a place a recognizable character and make travel or quiet observation rewarding. Physical feedback can also help a player understand an interaction: where an effect occurred, what changed, or why a selected activity stopped.

A world can use expressive visual detail without making every particle or ripple authoritative. When a cue implies a mechanical consequence, the design needs agreement about the actual quantity or event it presents. For a selected environmental interaction, identify the useful choice first, then the material, geometry, time and presentation needed to make that choice work. Beauty and atmosphere can have their own value; a new maintenance burden is not required to justify them.

[Rendering and audio](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/08-rendering-animation-and-audio.md), [physics/query systems](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/04-physics-and-spatial-queries.md), and [environmental representations](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/13-environment-water-and-material-interaction.md) inform [world presentation](world-presentation.md), [spatial behavior](spatial-world.md), and the selected [heat/material proposal](projects/heat-and-materials-feature-spec.md). Its [existing product-selection gate](maintainers/heat-and-materials.md) remains meaningful: the study does not establish that a particular new material activity is worth building.

### Make creator iteration part of the experience

Import, preparation, validation, loading and execution are separate stages. Their separation can make an authored place or invention easier to inspect, revise and use while preserving a recognizable source. This matters to creators who want to discover what their idea can become through iteration.

The complete creator journey includes finding a supported expression of the idea, seeing its actual result, understanding an unsupported capability, making a meaningful revision, and using the revised creation. The useful research question is which stage makes that journey slow, confusing or fragile. An impressive one-shot generation says little about revision cost or successful use.

Use [content and tools](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/09-content-tools-ui-and-platforms.md), [definitions and features](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/11-gameplay-frameworks-and-semantic-systems.md), and [execution boundaries](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/14-scripting-compilation-and-execution.md). [Production workflows](production-workflows/README.md), [world creation](projects/parallel-batch-07-playable-world-creation-feature-spec.md), [item/building workflows](projects/item-and-building-workflows-feature-spec.md), and [asset workflows](asset-workflow.md) already divide that journey among appropriate owners.

### Make scale serve an activity somebody values

Different representations can make a larger or richer world feasible: fewer expensive poses where they add little, shared unchanged calculations, or loaded presentation only where it is needed. The potential benefit is room for more interesting encounters, places and continuing consequences.

Name what an additional resident, region or retained history contributes before treating its count as success. A busy gathering, a sparsely populated journey and a familiar place revisited later stress different work. Measure the actual activity, its changing circumstances, and the transition into detailed interaction. A large visual crowd alone answers a different question.

[World scale](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/06-world-streaming-and-scale.md), [networking](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/07-networking-persistence-and-replay.md), [rendering](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/08-rendering-animation-and-audio.md), and [profiling](../research/unity-unreal-runtime/10-profiling-and-verification.md) provide mechanisms and measurement distinctions. [Product scalability](projects/product-scalability-feature-spec.md) owns the selected exact calculated advancement and ordinary bounded fallback. Research about approximate populations does not change that selected promise.

## Put the finding where it can change a decision

Start with a concrete question from the selected experience or an observed failure. Find the mechanism chapter and check its evidence date, release/package, maturity, platform and inspection depth. Then state the transferable point in the language of the consuming feature: what a participant could do, understand or retain, and what currently prevents it.

For a finding that could affect a design, the useful record contains:

- **The intended benefit and existing owner.** Name the activity, choice, expression or consequence helped. Link its current feature/technical pair or persistent contract.
- **The evidence and the inference.** Link the precise research section. Separate documented engine behavior from the proposed relevance to OpenLegend.
- **The smallest consequential decision.** Identify the actual missing contract, measured repeated work, presentation problem or unsupported authoring capability. Compare the proposed change with the existing adequate route and a credible simpler option.
- **The evidence that would change the conclusion.** State what would establish correctness, meaningful behavior, player understanding/value and complete cost for that particular question. Reuse the owner's current qualification rather than adding a parallel procedure.

Keep external mechanisms and provenance in research; selected behavior and its rationale in the feature/world owner; implementation and lifecycle details in the technical owner; unresolved design questions in the existing ND entry; delivery and qualification in the focused tracker; actual observations in the verification report. Link between them. This preserves a trace from source to decision without copying the same source summary or task into every document.

A demonstrated cost belongs with an existing implementation owner when its contract is settled. Missing player evidence belongs with qualification. Neither automatically creates a new feature-design project.

## Design readiness and remaining questions

The [engine-research questions in the design register](maintainers/needs-design.md#current-engine-research-design-questions-and-existing-owners) identify the few questions worth carrying into existing scopes. These existing documents contain the applicable design detail:

- The **intelligent-simulation evaluation pair** connects reuse, deferral and representation changes to complete activities and later returns. Player value remains a distinct judgment alongside correct mechanics, personal/social coherence and readable play.
- **Environmental work** retains its existing wanted-activity selection. Its eventual technical counterpart needs an explicit account of authoritative quantities, presentation, coupling and queried information within the selected stage.
- **Creator reuse, controls/media, platform extensions and restoration/perspective** retain their existing ND owners. Use the current research to answer their specific open questions when the relevant scope is selected.

No additional generic engine, cognition, presentation, simulation-scale or evaluation design pair is warranted merely because the research now exists. A new scoped pair becomes useful when a selected experience exposes a contract that the current owners do not cover. That is different from adding another place to summarize the engines.

## Keep the evidence useful over time

The snapshot's date is part of its meaning. Before relying on a release-sensitive claim for implementation or adoption, verify the affected feature and package against current primary documentation. Refresh the particular claim, its source register and its consuming decision together. Preserve an earlier limitation only when clearly labeled as historical or still relevant to a supported version.

Retain concrete distinctions that prevent an invalid conclusion: pending versus negative results, visual versus mechanical effects, individual knowledge versus shared data, retained population versus active work, and serialization versus continuity. These are useful tools for finding opportunities as well as avoiding mistakes. A research prototype can suggest a controlled comparison; successful engineering and an enjoyable game each still need their own evidence.
