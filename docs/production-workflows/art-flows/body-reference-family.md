# Build an adjustable body family from visual references

**Design for a bounded experiment; production qualification is open.** This procedure produces corresponding, reference-led body shapes and fits them into OpenLegend's existing character machinery. It does not resume the paused anatomy proof, install assets, change the current cast or qualify an entire character library by itself. [MHP10](../../maintainers/art/metahuman-poc.md#peacock-anatomy-controls) owns the first body/shirt acceptance; [PPL05–PPL07](../../maintainers/art/people.md#family-asset-delivery) own broader body, age and base coverage. The [family contract](../../projects/people-from-parts-authoring-contract.md) remains the runtime and file-format authority.

## 1. Outcome and boundaries

An artist supplies strong visual targets for a deliberately sampled body space. Image generation supplies consistent construction views; Meshy supplies detailed donor geometry; fitting transfers that geometry onto the selected canonical topology; the existing compiler/evaluator turns corresponding shapes into editable recipes. The visible target remains beside the work at every stage. A few measurements or a prose description cannot replace it.

The first delivery is one adult masculine family at a fixed apparent age, stature and structural frame, with two independent body sliders: **muscle mass and fullness**. How clearly the muscles show [follows from those two](#muscle-relief-follows-from-the-two-sliders) and has no slider of its own. It includes a replaceable canonical head, complete underlying anatomy, separate close-fitting baseline coverage, and the **same existing premade Peacock shirt** working across every qualified recipe and motion. It includes source-to-runtime comparisons and reconstruction evidence, not only attractive stills. No custom tunic, hair library, new viewer, character-creator UI, engine replacement or production selection belongs to this unit.

Later stages add an independently authored feminine body, adult age responses, structural/proportion controls and approved younger ranges. These are explicit staged deliverables, not capabilities implied by a successful masculine grid. The body workflow leaves underlying facial identity, separate facial-feature sliders, skin finish, hair/beard/brows and a full wardrobe with their existing owners, while deforming whole-head, facial, neck, hand and digit soft tissue coherently with `fullness` (so a fuller build never leaves a lean head, jaw or hand attached to a heavy torso) and deforming only neck, nape and lower-jaw `masseter` musculature on `headSkin` with `muscleMass` at `F = 0` so reference-sheet facial drift never pollutes face presets. One neutral head identity is sufficient for this first body-grid qualification; it is insufficient for whole-character or adult-face-age acceptance.

### What must remain reusable

- The canonical vertex order, UV correspondence, region labels, ordered neck ring, skeleton conventions and editable fitting data.
- A source catalogue relating each accepted reference, donor, corresponding body and recipe to exact files and measurements.
- One offline target conversion path feeding the existing family compiler and one runtime evaluator, rather than a new script for each physique.
- Separate clothing bindings, weights, ease and supported ranges. A named build is a preset of controls, never a special deformation branch.
- Exact rejected and accepted evidence, with their status. A technically valid export does not supersede an approved silhouette or repair a rejected source by declaration.

The body is an anatomical surface beneath clothing, not a shirt-shaped hollow shell. The donor is an artistic source, not an automatically usable rig, family or final game asset. OpenLegend must evaluate the shipped family with Unreal, MetaHuman and provider services closed.

## 3. Controls: independent inputs, interacting anatomy

Use normalized values `0…1` in manifests; percentages are display labels. They are travel through authored targets, not body-fat percentages, muscle kilograms or medical classifications. A preset name describes a sample only.

**Author 0% and 100% as visibly extreme, plausible ends of every continuous appearance slider.** Each endpoint should read as an outlier for that trait within the supported family when compared with typical interior values at the same scale, pose and lighting. Most characters should use interior values for every slider. Reserve exact endpoints for deliberately extreme characters and qualification cases. Judge each slider with the other inputs held fixed, so an extreme on one axis does not depend on moving another axis to its limit.

For fullness, 0% reads distinctly lean and 100% reads unmistakably full across the whole head and face (cranium/temple/cheekbone breadth, malar/buccal cheeks, jowls, submental under-chin pad, occiput/nape), neck, chest/pectoral hang, torso, hips and seat, upper and lower arms, wrists, palms/dorsum, fingers, thighs, lower legs, feet and toes, with natural transitions across the whole body and under gravity when arms lift. A rounded abdomen on an otherwise athletic silhouette is an intermediate appearance. Use matched front, profile and rear views to establish that range before fitting. At fixed fullness, muscle mass spans slight to exceptionally muscular. Keep the plausible combinations specified below.

The [bundled profile](../../worlds/base/people-appearance.md#control-definitions) owns exact current keys and provisional ranges. Its `frame` currently interpolates feminine/masculine anchors; it must not be relabeled as generic skeletal breadth. The selected proof may expose independent base packages and alias a single base's data at both evaluator frame endpoints. Do not change stored meaning to simplify a new UI.

**Global breadth at 100% and shoulder refinement at 0% can coexist** if that combination is inside the family's qualified envelope: the latter means the narrow residual relative to the broad structure, not the globally narrowest possible shoulders. Publish the resulting joint span and outer surface span separately. Controls are independently settable inputs, not a promise that their visible effects are disjoint. Evaluate a recipe deterministically from neutral data; never accumulate edits onto the last displayed body. Invalid combinations need a clear unsupported-range result, not an invisible clamp that changes another slider.

### Muscle relief follows from the two sliders

Muscle relief is how clearly the muscle shapes and the grooves between them show on the surface. It has no slider. Each authored body carries the relief natural to its build: more muscle makes the shapes larger and clearer, and more fullness covers and softens them. A lean body shows its muscles, a full body shows soft forms, and a full muscular body stays visibly different from a full body with little muscle.

The cost of a third slider is the reason. A separate definition slider doubles the bodies to source and fit, from six to twelve, while the [earlier proof](../../../art-direction/studies/2026-11-11-peacock-muscle-definition/README.md) measured its effect at about 1–5 mm of surface relief with waist, ribs, pelvis and seat unchanged. High fullness mutes relief in any case, and reference images could not reliably show the same build with less relief.

Two results follow. How cut a lean body looks is set by how its lean reference is drawn; the approved Peacock sheet sets that for the first family. A lean but smooth body at the same muscle and fullness is not a supported recipe; a little fullness softens the surface and also thickens the body slightly. A surface-only relief strength in skin shading would need no extra bodies; it is untested and not part of this workflow.

### Example qualification recipes

These recipes sample endpoints and their interactions. Ordinary character presets should use interior values. The selected lean Peacock recipe is a pinned transfer reference.

| Illustrative appearance       | Mass | Fullness |
| ----------------------------- | ---: | -------: |
| Slender                       |    0 |        0 |
| Selected lean athletic source |   50 |        0 |
| Large lean musculature        |  100 |        0 |
| Moderate, softer build        |   50 |       50 |
| Very full, little muscle      |    0 |      100 |
| Fuller, moderate musculature  |   50 |      100 |
| Strong and full               |  100 |      100 |

Little muscle does not mean fat, and fullness does not remove muscle. Increasing muscle mass alone must not create an expanded ribcage ledge or breast-like isolated chest extrusion. Fullness must not leave fit arms, legs and back attached to a rectangular belly. Builds are source-relative, not stereotypes about gender or age.

## 4. Sampling plan and model counts

Separate **reference targets**, **independent donor meshes**, **corresponding support shapes**, **interpolated validation bodies** and **runtime targets**. They are different counts. A Meshy retry is a job, not another required body type. A baked character is not a new support model.

For one adult base/age/frame, the review matrix is the full Cartesian product:

```text
mass      = [0, 0.5, 1]
fullness  = [0, 0.5, 1]
9 recipe coordinates; stable row ID: M{000|050|100}-F{000|050|100}
```

Plan all rows before production. Each has an intended reference or explicitly unaccepted synthesized comparison, source status, geometry status and shirt/motion result. Generate reference sheets as consistent variants of an accepted anchor, with reference inputs retained. Do not assume a label in a prompt makes a measurable 50% anatomical midpoint.

### Stages and advancement

| ------------------------------ | -------------------: | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| B: mass interaction            |              6 total | Add mass 0 and 100, each at fullness 0 and 100. Compile the complete core surface and compare all nine reference coordinates. A full, heavily muscled body is strong and full with soft surfaces, not cut abdominal blocks painted over fat.                                                                                      |
| C: fullness midpoint if needed |              9 total | If the half-fullness row fails against its references, author all three `F=.5` supports across mass, rather than chasing individual failed combinations. All nine coordinates are then independently sourced. This is a bounded escalation requiring available budget and a demonstrated error; do not generate it automatically. |

Only six of the nine initial validation shapes are independent supports. For the three half-fullness coordinates, the initial evaluator synthesizes the shape; independent reference art judges whether that interpolation is believable and faithful. Synthetic midpoint geometry is not independent validation evidence. A source image that cannot be made mutually consistent stays unresolved, not a target the fitter must distort itself to satisfy.

The six-support experiment uses MHP10's check set: **nine static recipes and six sweeps**. The nine recipes are the grid coordinates, six supports and three interpolated half-fullness bodies. The three fullness paths hold `M∈{0,.5,1}`; the three mass paths hold `F∈{0,.5,1}`. Sweep the other slider over its complete range. Inspect quarter-fullness and quarter-mass observations from the recordings; do not replace this check set with a smaller chart.

### Age, base, height and the larger matrix

The example `3 ages × 2 bases × 3 mass × 3 fullness` is **54 reference/validation coordinates**, not a requirement for 54 unique Meshy meshes. It also omits teen transitions, structural/proportion controls and head identity; therefore it does not establish complete coverage by itself.

After MHP10, use the existing **adult milestone at ages 30 and 75 with midpoint 52.5**, two independently authored bases, four specified physique recipes per age and eight age sweeps. Its 24 comparisons remain required. Expand a new age's six-shape set only if authorized for that broader range; an age slider backed only by neutral body aging is insufficient.

The broader bundled target still names adult anchors **18, 35, 60 and 80**. Do not silently replace those with the proof's 30/75 samples or claim their range from it. At six supports per adult anchor, two bases and four anchors imply **48 corresponding body supports**, before structural residuals or required corrections; the associated nine-point grids contain **72 evaluations**. This is a planning count, not a quality guarantee or spending request. Accepted supports may be reused where exactly compatible. The proof ages need their own evidence rather than relabeling an older shape's age.

Height alone does not require another donor grid: uniform scaling and existing contact/admission checks cover the declared stature range. Structural breadth and local proportions require their own authored response targets and interaction qualification; never multiply every axis into a blind full Cartesian production run. For each added control, define its neutral/endpoint sources, affected regions/landmarks and interaction rows before the batch, then check the global physique corners most likely to stress it. This is a planned coverage matrix, not ad hoc user testing.

Child/teen/adult continuity remains PPL07. Choose age-appropriate anchors and coverage only when that stage is approved; child bodies are not shrunken adult donors or extreme adult mass variants. Shared topology enables interpolation but does not guarantee plausible growth. Adjacent bands must share qualified overlap geometry, joints, fit and motion; otherwise expose separate supported ranges. No instantaneous invisible model swap at a birthday. The current design does not authorize youth image generation.

## 5. Concept generation and source admission

### Make each source visually determinate

1. Start from the accepted body image, not armour measurements or a text summary. Record exact source paths/hashes and which parts are reliable. Use ImageGen edit/reference inputs to preserve pose, frame and identity across a batch. If the source has hair/beard, request a bald neutral head or use a neck-down body donor; do not let hairstyle change the head scale used for registration.
2. Produce front, true side and back views of one adult in one declared pose, equal scale and floor line. Use the selected canonical A-pose where possible. Require a clean unobstructed side contour, separated limbs, neutral pelvis and comparable shoulder/hip landmarks. If the side arm occludes the torso, request a dedicated unobstructed side view and record the pose difference; never call mismatched poses identical.
3. Use plain neutral lighting, background and lens, no depth of field, vignette, fog, beauty blur or dramatic rim light. Keep full feet and crown visible. A clay diagnosis later removes material and normal maps; it does not intentionally blur the torso.
4. Male adult coverage is **close-fitting high-cut linen briefs** (or a **fitted brief-cut linen loincloth**, matching [baseline coverage](../../worlds/base/people-appearance.md#baseline-coverage)), smooth over the pelvis and leaving upper thighs, hip flexors and lower glutes clear, with no modern elastic/logo, baggy trunks, hanging panels, bulky seams or sculpted cloth folds. Female adult coverage follows the selected simple opaque linen chest/hip wraps, not a modern lingerie aesthetic. Coverage must remain sufficient; “minimal geometry effect” does not mean transparent or absent coverage.
5. Keep the anatomy brief explicit across the whole body: head/cheek/jowl/submental/neck proportions, deltoids, pectorals, lats/trapezius, abdominal/oblique forms, upper/lower arms, wrists/hands/fingers, seat, thighs, calves and feet. For fullness, specify coherent soft volume and transitions in front, side and rear across head, torso and extremities. Compare each endpoint sheet against a typical interior build and confirm the difference is clearly visible where the control is meant to act. If a sheet reads as an interior build, revise it before commissioning the donor. Confirm that the donor retains the approved range before fitting. A transfer trial using a mild reference can demonstrate correspondence, but it cannot qualify an extreme endpoint. For strong older references preserve age-appropriate structural shape without forcing frailty. Use the reference image to resolve contour detail rather than inventing it procedurally from those words.
6. Compare the views before Meshy. Fix contradictory chest width, pelvic tilt, limb length, hidden hand/foot or inconsistent identity. Crop individual panels without rescaling them differently. Do not send a multi-person comparison board as if it were one model.
7. For **high-fullness (`F > 0`) or peak-muscular (`M = 1`)** endpoint builds—where unrigged 3D donors cannot show raised-arm behavior—an optional matched-stature 2D front/back horizontal T-pose reference governs **qualitative contour shape and shoulder/arm/flank gravity settle** (trapezius-to-deltoid height relative to the chin, pronated top-arm flattening, settled tricep/forearm bellies vs. `F > 0` adipose drape, axillary release, and a taut armpit-to-waist V-taper) checked in `1:1` crops covering the **full raised arm and flank** (a stacked `wrist-to-wrist × head-to-waistband` pair plus an adjacent half-span `midline-to-wrist × head-to-waistband` pair), while the registered A-pose donor and canonical skeleton remain authoritative for bone lengths, stance and rest cross-sections (never commissioning a conflicting T-pose 3D donor or warping limb lengths to 2D pixel diffs).

Fill every bracket from the selected row and source record. Do not send the brackets literally or replace a reference image with this text.


A generation tool may reject an otherwise nonsexual construction request. Record the refusal and stop that rejected route; do not repeatedly rewrite to evade it. A more-covered output is a distinct silhouette reference, not fulfillment of the anatomy requirement. Obtain a permitted suitable source through an authorized alternative before donor production. Store the actual model ID if exposed; a built-in tool that hides it is recorded as **model not exposed**, never guessed from another API's default.

### Meshy donor generation

Use the existing [tool route](tools.md) and check its current operation/model/options and price before dispatch. Meshy's documented multi-image API accepts one to four views of the same object; a wrapper may expose fewer options. Supply clean front/side/back panels for one coordinate, one job per donor. Store exact request/options, image hashes, returned task ID, costs, raw geometry and materials before cleanup. Reuse existing successful jobs instead of submitting again after an uncertain timeout. [Multi-image API](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/multi-image-to-3d).

Inspect raw untextured geometry: do the muscles exist as surface volume, or only as painted shadows? Are the side and rear coherent? Reject melted hands, contradictory joints, missing limbs and fused cloth that materially controls the intended body region. Close-fitting linen still produces clothing-shaped geometry: mark covered pelvis surfaces **unknown/inferred**, retain complete canonical anatomy there and fit separate baseline cloth. Do not shrinkwrap the body onto the outer surface of shorts and call that anatomy. Hidden-region exclusion must be visible in coverage/error reports, not a way to omit failed regions from acceptance.

Each donor carries the muscle relief its reference shows. Smoothing a mesh is not a valid way to make a softer build; it may erase muscle volume or alter waist and limbs. The visual brief governs.

## 6. Measurements and whole-body acceptance

Measure **target, donor, corresponding master and final runtime body**, in that order, using the same named landmarks and coordinate system. Record value, unit, method, view/source, uncertainty and pass/fail. A section with hidden skin or perspective uncertainty is not an exact circumference measurement.

### Registration

Use the selected family reference stature, sole origin, forward axis and canonical pose. Record source-to-canonical transform. Use rigid alignment plus one uniform scale; if anatomical pose normalization is needed, record its joint rotations and preserve the unmodified donor. Do not independently scale the waist/chest or auto-frame each comparison to hide a regression. Front/back/profile renderings use one camera calibration, comparable light and a scale bar. Pixel ratios from concept art guide proportions; physical 3D measurements come from the registered mesh.

Check the connected contours at the underarm before fitting. If the donor joins the arm and trunk where the canonical cross-section shows separate surfaces, record the difference and test whether the pose change accounts for it. Freeze observed regions from the actual anatomical interface and source exclusions. At a shared neck seam, use the ordered seam and nearby surface distance so a horizontal cutoff does not also discard exposed deltoid surfaces. Keep all-exposed and shared-coverage distances together with the measured omitted area.

### Initial fidelity gates — experimental tolerances

These are proposed acceptance defaults for the first registered adult batch, not existing measured passes or universal anatomy laws. Report failures; do not loosen them merely to accept the current result. If sampling noise or source resolution invalidates a threshold, document that specific measurement limitation and retain the visual requirement.

- On reliable exposed donor regions: bidirectional sampled surface distance, per-region median/p95/max, plus signed direction; target p95 ≤ **0.2% of stature** and no unreviewed connected patch exceeding **0.5% of stature**. At 1.75 m these are 3.5 mm and 8.75 mm. Report sampling density and excluded area. Nearest-surface agreement alone cannot prove correct vertex correspondence.
- Named external width/depth sections: within **2% of the accepted target section dimension** after registration across **every** named trunk, limb, wrist, ankle and mid-thigh section—report the true worst-case `LOD0` and `LOD1` section errors and every section exceeding 2%, rather than cherry-picking passing rows. Visually important landmarks within **0.3% of stature**, unless source uncertainty is greater and explicitly recorded. Require correct source proportions even if an aggregate error is small.
- **Runtime `LOD1` governs visual acceptance:** `LOD0` is an offline authoring and normal-bake intermediate; the game loads only runtime `bodySkin` (`LOD1`). Because normal maps shade interior surfaces without changing grazing silhouettes, forms that shape silhouettes or exceed `LOD1`'s triangle spacing must retain **80–120%** of the accepted donor's measured crest-to-valley relief and feature location **on runtime `LOD1` clay geometry itself**, not merely on `LOD0`. Sparse point-sampled relief ratios are diagnostic aids, never proof of visual fidelity—washed-out primary musculature, tubular limbs or smoothed neck/clavicle landmarks on `LOD1` clay fail regardless of sampled numbers, with only shallow sub-triangle striations deferred to a consumer-verified normal-map bake.
- No cracks, inverted triangles, new self-intersections, isolated spikes, bilateral correspondence swaps or visible mask-border ledges in accepted anatomy. Use rest-pose whole-body views and motion; one numerical average cannot waive a glaring defect.
- Reject any conspicuous underarm hole, armpit pinch pocket, deltoid/tricep/elbow unposing dent or wave, upper-back fold spike, ribcage bat-wing web, rigid-skinning shoulder hump, lumpy raised-arm upper contour, caved-in latissimus flare or harsh pectoral-to-armpit crease, even when the mesh is closed and measured surface distances pass. Inspect the chest, underarm, flank, upper-back/lat contours and upper-arm junction together in front, side, rear and three-quarter clay views at rest, at horizontal T-pose, with game arms raised (`+68°`), and through motion. Always evaluate posed previews on the **exact runtime skeleton, retargeted clip and Linear Blend Skinning**—never a simplified bone subset or volume-preserving Dual-Quaternion shortcut rig, which balloons thick shoulders artificially. Where extreme soft tissue (`F > 0`) or hypertrophy (`M = 1`) still hikes, pinches, caves lats or waves the silhouette under rigid bone weights, calibrate a build-gated raised-arm corrective against the 2D T-pose reference across the **whole connected shoulder-to-wrist and shoulder-to-waist chains**—smoothly stretching the lateral flank from iliac waist through mid-ribs into the lat arch (attenuating rest-pose lower-oblique slack rather than pushing an upper-lat bulge above a pinched mid-rib S-curve), alongside gravity-settled acromion/deltoid caps, elongated palms-down bicep top contours, fuller settled tricep/forearm bellies (without camel-hump bicep peaks or loose adipose sag on `F = 0` builds), and pinch-free axillary/pectoral walls.
- The compiled/native/runtime reconstruction of the same target must agree within **1e-6 m** in canonical unposed positions, allowing a separately documented exporter precision limit if encountered. This numerical check proves transport, not attractiveness.

Review whole-body silhouette first, then each region, then repeat whole-body review. Compare to the **actual intended row**, not only the preceding failed output. The artist/agent owns catching glaring chest, waist, back, neck and limb defects; Mike should not have to discover them one at a time. A broad audit is mandatory after every local repair because shape, seam and garment changes can regress elsewhere.

## 7. Transfer the entire reliable surface

“Transfer” here means **fit the selected canonical mesh to the donor's accepted whole-body surface**, preserving large muscle volumes and local surface contours together. It does not mean paste front-torso relief onto a generic body or filter out the large forms that make the source athletic.

1. Duplicate the selected canonical master without changing connectivity, vertex order, UVs or named anatomical regions. Preserve the original and all approved targets. Freeze a canonical topology hash and source manifest before fitting.
2. **Use coupled or directional invariants instead of `0.0 mm` lean world-space hard pins.** Strictly separate **excluding defective or fused _donor_ triangles** (webbed inter-digit gaps, Meshy's solid eyeball globes and lip slits, blurred ear folds, fused axilla/thigh bridges, clothing hems) from **freezing _canonical_ vertices at lean `(X, Y, Z)` coordinates**, which causes neck/wrist/sole cliffs, narrow pinheads and buried ears when tissue thickens or slims:
   - **Stature, floor and midline (`1D` planes):** Enforce 1D crown stature (`max Z == referenceStatureMetres` in Blender) and plantar floor contact (`ΔZ = 0`) with horizontal `X/Y` free to widen or slim, and constrain sagittal midline vertices to `X = 0` (`Y/Z` free during local untangling).
   - **Welded seams and carried sub-structures:** Solve `headSkin` and `bodySkin` as one welded surface (`0.0000 mm` collar gap), carry canonical ear cartilage, five fingers and five toes via mirror-symmetric attachment similarity plus smooth styloid/ankle transition feathering (eliminating radial thumb-side wrist steps or ankle kinks while clamping inter-digit clearance against webbing), and emit matching `PersonControl.targets` offsets on separate facial submeshes (`eyeLeft`, `eyeRight`, `lashes`, `teeth`, `oral`) so open eyelid/lip apertures stay flush without frozen cheek halos.
3. Fit **whole-person usable anatomy** across the welded `headSkin` and `bodySkin` surface from cranium to insteps while keeping additive controls orthogonal: on `headSkin`, `fullness` (`F > 0`) pads whole-head/cheek/jowl/submental adipose soft tissue around carried canonical features, whereas `muscleMass` at `F = 0` hypertrophies neck, trapezius/nape, sternocleidomastoid and lower-jaw `masseter` breadth while scaling/carrying overall cranial, temple, cheekbone and jaw breadth coherently with the thicker neck (never leaving a narrow gaunt face sunk inside a wider flared neck) and keeping **local** brow, nose, eye-socket and lip feature contours canonical (`0` baked AI-sheet identity drift). Restrict correspondences by anatomical region, local bone-axis frame and side so limbs never grab across gaps, diagonal A-pose slicing never stamps rings onto arms, and unposing across multi-bone shoulder/axilla/elbow skin weights never leaves mid-deltoid dents, rear-armpit pinches or wavy tricep bumps. Unfold steep creases radially before fine projection along **donor** surface normals (preventing ghost lean grooves on soft builds), protect narrow midline clefts flanked by bilateral bellies (sternal pectoral cleavage, `linea alba`, spinal furrow, natal cleft) and clavicular/axillary insertions from symmetric midline-smoothing washout, and **gate adipose/crease smoothing by build fullness** so lean muscular builds (`F = 0`) retain 80–120% crest-to-valley muscle relief while fuller builds (`F > 0`) stay smooth.
4. Work coarse-to-fine for convergence, retaining the final full surface. **Never carve away exposed donor anatomy with synthetic curves or cones** (such as shaving away submental fullness, clavicular/lower pectoral volume or lower glute curvature), **and never hollow out voids to "remove" donor underwear or sculpt fake bare groin cones/trenches:** feather donor cloth-exclusion masks slightly past waistband and leg-hem compression ridges so elastic brief/bra dents never stamp onto bare outer glutes or hips, and reconstruct only the genuinely cloth-covered inguinal/crotch bridge as a **smooth, neutral, watertight mannequin saddle** blended `C1`-continuously into full top-to-bottom rounded `gluteus maximus` domes and surrounding lower-abdomen/hip/thigh anatomy (never a sliced-flat lower buttock, horizontal T-bar glute shelf or square doorway groin notch). Do not project garment hems or folds into skin, high-pass the donor and discard its proportions, or treat sharp muscle forms as noise. Any artifact smoothing requires a named source discrepancy, before/after crop, bounded canonical region and preserved surrounding silhouette.
5. Evaluate **close-fitting high-cut linen briefs** matching [§5](#5-concept-generation-and-source-admission)'s footprint on every fitted candidate ([§9](#9-replaceable-head-and-separate-coverage)) so Donor-vs-Fit comparison sheets match the clothed reference/donor Apples-to-Apples while leaving upper thighs, hip flexors and lower glutes exposed—**never** loose mid-thigh trunks or spiky placeholder shorts that conceal upper-thigh/glute defects. Always pair covered views with an unmasked bare-saddle check (`0` unacceptable faces, `0` self-intersections, `0` garment poke-through, intact lower-glute roundness). Repaired joints/covered regions have their own evidence rather than fabricated source-fidelity scores.
6. Compare source/donor/fitted master **and runtime `LOD1`** in identical welded-normal clay and textured views and with the measurement set. Reject a fit that hits a few circumferences while retaining smooth generic limbs, washed-out `LOD1` muscle cuts, a lean-sized head at `F > 0`, baked nose/brow drift at `F = 0`, or a shrunk waist. Fix the fitting field, reduction projection or donor, not downstream lighting that conceals the mismatch.
7. Check canonical vertex correspondence anatomically: elbows remain elbows, muscle borders are similarly located, seams agree, triangle aspect ratios remain usable and skin weights still attach to the intended bones. Build-gated raised-arm pose correctives may address both `F > 0` soft-tissue drape and `M = 1` scapulohumeral/lat-to-waist-flank/pectoral-insertion distortion where linear skinning alone bunches or caves heavy musculature.
8. Preserve high-detail master and derive runtime reduction with **one shared mapping across all targets**, not independently remeshed slider endpoints, snapping coarse runtime `LOD1` vertices onto high-master soft-tissue folds and muscular sulci/crests (rather than smooth barycentric averaging across valleys) with bounded chord-loss compensation, a minimum triangle-altitude guard (preventing opposing sulcus snaps from crushing `LOD1` quad rows into shadow-casting slivers that invert under spine or limb motion), and tangential spacing across axillary, cubital, popliteal and inguinal flexion hinges. Bake MikkTSpace tangent-space normal maps against the actual exported triangulation/tangents from un-mirrored donor detail, computing watertight combined `headSkin`+`bodySkin` collar vertex normals and attenuating cloth-excluded or self-contact creases (including higher-riding donor waistbands and pressed-armpit creases above rest A-pose). Compare high master → reduced master → export → runtime separately so loss has an identifiable owner.

MetaHuman can remain the offline topology/rig foundation. Native body-conform/template operations have input correspondence requirements; record the installed version, actual route and options. Importing an arbitrary Meshy object does not automatically give it MetaHuman's parametric slider semantics or preserve every sculpt through native body solving. Measure any conform/parametric round trip before adopting it. If it loses accepted shape, retain the qualified canonical fit for OpenLegend and diagnose the native step; do not resculpt from a lossy text brief. [Epic body conform examples](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/body-conform-examples?lang=en-US), [template mesh requirements](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/from-template-mesh?lang=en-US).

A wholly Meshy-derived topology could also support reuse if someone builds and qualifies its correspondence, UVs, skeleton, weights, shape responses, seams, maps and clothing bindings. It is not a shortcut that avoids those requirements. For this experiment the selected canonical foundation stays fixed; changing it is a consequential foundation decision, not routine cleanup.

### Transfer tooling prerequisite

Keep source-surface queries and canonical topology separate. Untangle degenerate or self-intersecting triangles **locally at the failing faces** (keeping midline `Y/Z` free while holding `X = 0`) rather than rolling an entire anatomical region back to unsmoothed raw output.

Separate and cache the base registration/correspondence solve by input digest from modular post-fit refinement so inner iterations run in seconds against **one consolidated welded-normal `1:1` close-up comparison sheet** (showing runtime `LOD1` + `headSkin` + fitted baseline coverage beside the donor, plus one unmasked bare-pelvis saddle check); run the full multi-board rendering suite on sign-off candidates.

The stage-A fit experiment must choose and record one reproducible working correspondence recipe before batch expansion: exact existing tool/script revision, landmark pairs/curves, region eligibility, source exclusions, smoothing/projection settings, convergence/stop result and measured residuals. It must preserve accepted large forms, including waist corrections, when recovering detail; reprojecting the original narrow donor after that correction is a regression. If existing permitted tooling cannot pass the pair, report the specific failure and compare an established non-rigid wrapping route before building a new solver. ## 8. Compile supports into the existing evaluator

The reusable offline conversion lives in [`scripts/art/body-grid.py`](../../../scripts/art/body-grid.py), feeding [`scripts/art/people.py`](../../../scripts/art/people.py) and the current [`person-family.ts`](../../../apps/client/src/characters/person-family.ts) contract with input/output manifests and explicit errors. Do not create a second runtime interpolation system or assume native MetaHuman sliders run in the browser.

### Inputs and output

Input: one base/age/frame's corresponding body positions for the selected support coordinates, identical topology/UVs, reference stature/pose, source records and region confidence. Validate these invariants before subtracting meshes. Output: a neutral body, ordinary bipolar target pairs, interaction correctives with named drivers, provenance and reconstruction results for every input coordinate. Baseline coverage and shirt remain independent parts. Position targets alone do not vary skin normal maps: while a single baked `.glb` embeds its endpoint normal map, the live family evaluator currently blends maps only by `age` ([material contract](../../projects/people-from-parts-authoring-contract.md#skin-age-and-material-production)), so any sub-triangle donor striations on lean muscular builds require build-conditioned normal-map blending/attenuation through that material owner (or explicit clay-level live-sweep qualification) rather than assuming a static baked `.glb` proves live slider appearance.

The study's `M/F` symbols are coordinate labels, not new runtime recipe keys. The build record maps each once to the selected family's existing declared control ID; refuse missing or duplicate mappings. Resolve physical reference units and all other neutral control values from that exact family descriptor. A third-party tool's native muscle slider value is never silently used as the normalized OpenLegend value.

For six supports, first fill the nine-node grid by linear interpolation in fullness at each fixed mass. These filled nodes are derived, not independently authored. If later supports replace a derived node, recompute the whole target decomposition from the new grid; never stack an old correction onto a revised neutral.

### Exact decomposition

Let `S(m,f)` be corresponding positions at grid nodes, and `B=S(.5,.5)`. The neutral is the grid centre, **not** the lean Peacock source recipe. The source must reconstruct at `(m=.5,f=0)`.

For each nonempty subset `A` of `{mass,fullness}` and each selection of endpoint sides `s∈{0,1}` on those axes:

```text
x = [.5,.5] with coordinates in A set to s
C[A,s] = S(x) - B
          - sum(C[T, s restricted to T] for every nonempty proper subset T of A)
process subsets in increasing size (single axes, then the pair)
```

Store single-axis `C` as that control's negative/positive targets. Store pair `C` as existing corrective records whose drivers name the corresponding negative/positive excursions. The evaluator already uses:

```text
negative(v) = max(1 - 2*v, 0)
positive(v) = max(2*v - 1, 0)
P(recipe) = B + sum(product(driver excursions) * C)
```

This reproduces the nine grid nodes and piecewise bilinear interpolation between them. It needs at most **4 single-axis + 4 pair arrays = 8 arrays** for one mesh/base/age. At a recipe only one side of each axis is active: at most two single contributions and one pair contribution. The current evaluator may scan additional inactive metadata; do not describe storage as three arrays. Alias identical frame endpoint arrays for a single base rather than duplicating them.

Do not omit the pair interactions: a full muscular body is not the sum of a muscular change and a full change, and its softened relief lives in the mass×fullness residual. Remove exact-zero targets freely; approximate pruning requires a separately measured error/cost experiment. Preserve neutral subtraction and operation order through float32 compilation.

### What interpolation proves

Corresponding vertices move continuously; it does **not** follow that the intermediate anatomy is plausible. Misaligned abdominal groups can create doubled borders, dents or sliding muscle insertions; an endpoint may be good while its midpoint fails. Registration and the independent midpoint references test this. Do not blame linear interpolation for an incorrectly fitted endpoint or accept a midpoint solely because its number is 50%.

Before asset qualification, reconstruct every support, every grid node and at least the fixed interior points `(M,F)` with each component `.25` or `.75` (four points). Compare the compiler/native/browser outputs to a direct bilinear reference calculation with the same target data. Then run the broader visual/garment matrix. Algebraic agreement establishes implementation correctness; the visual references establish whether the function is a good body model.

### Structural and age additions

Tissue-only M/F targets keep internal joints fixed. Structural breadth and length targets use the existing internal landmark controls and rebuild rest joints/binds. Preserve height compensation and avoid applying a second bone scale after geometry already changed.

At age anchor `a`, derive its own neutral `B_a` and coefficients `C_a`. Keep neutral-age `B_0,C_0`; supply age samples `B_a-B_0` and age-conditioned response corrections `C_a-C_0`, driven by the same excursions. Blend adjacent anchors using the same age weights. Applying `C_a` on top of `C_0` would double the response. Age-related changes include torso/limb shape and fat/muscle distribution, not only skin maps.

The current evaluator supports age-shaped meshes and age-conditioned corrections. It does **not yet supply dedicated age samples for internal structural landmarks/joint rests**. If an age/proportion target changes the skeleton, add that support through the existing landmark owner as its own experiment before claiming that range; surface-bound landmarks alone cannot fix an internal joint. The adult-body proof must state which proportions change and which joints stay fixed. Cross-age fit/rig/motion is a required pass, not a presumed consequence of matching topology.

## 9. Replaceable head and separate coverage

Use one canonical head/scalp for the first body grid. Keep a named ordered neck boundary with matching positions, normal-sharing groups and skin rows; solve whole-head, facial and neck soft tissue together across the welded `headSkin` and `bodySkin` surface under the coupled invariants in [§7](#7-transfer-the-entire-reliable-surface), and propagate `headSkin` collar/neck targets into the garment fit-support mesh so shirt collars track neck fullness without tearing. Record attachment transforms and anatomical crown height. Check neutral, both head turns, nod, horizontal T-pose and raised arms, inspecting head-to-body proportion, cheek/jowl/submental contour, ear placement, occipital profile, larynx, throat and nape shape alongside `0.0000 mm` seam closure. A narrow lean head inside a thick neck collar, buried ears, a rear-skull shelf or a giant neck protrusion is a failure even when every seam vertex coincides.

Do not transfer hair/beard volume into the skull or neck. Later face sources can be generated separately, registered to the same head/neck interface and qualified for eyes/lids/mouth/expressions. Their independent production does not imply arbitrary head scaling or no integration checks.

Minimal donor underwear does not replace this separate asset. Baseline coverage must be **close-fitting high-cut linen briefs** (or a **fitted brief-cut linen loincloth**) matching [§5](#5-concept-generation-and-source-admission) rather than loose mid-thigh trunks or crumpled/spiky placeholder shorts; if an existing coverage piece is baggy, angular or hides upper-thigh/lower-glute anatomy, repair or replace its cut/binding through the part owner. ## 10. The shirt is part of acceptance

Use the exact existing Peacock premade shirt source, its material data and the maintained animation sources. Keep source rights and source-specific construction intact. Body fidelity is checked before dependent fitting, but final task completion requires the shirt on **every tested body variation**, including intermediate fullness and mass. A correct naked-body graph with a broken shirt is not a completed body-system proof.

1. Evaluate final body and structural landmarks, rebuild its bind pose if needed, then fit coverage and shirt. Use the current surface/bridge/rigid part owners; no per-frame nearest-surface fit or new collision system.
2. For a soft shirt, preserve canonical triangle/barycentric bindings and surface-frame offsets. Constrain source binding by body region/side. Inspect neckline, shoulders, axillae, sleeves, chest, abdomen, back and hem. A tight local offset can amplify a body ledge; fixing the body and garment are separate diagnoses.
3. Keep intentional ease rather than wrapping the cloth onto every abdominal groove. A bridge support can span a loose hem; rigid plates need their own later sizing/attachment contract. Fit against the final evaluated surface, including its muscle relief. A few millimetres of relief can cause breakthroughs at the chest, abdomen or sleeves even when broad measurements barely change.
4. Where a demonstrated shape needs a correction, use the existing part corrective mechanism with named drivers. A changed canonical binding requires re-derived skin weights; applying stale weights after changing the support triangle is invalid. Test armhole/elbow and pelvis/hem motion before expanding the batch.
5. Inspect with body-hiding masks **off**. Check contact through rest, raised arms, Idle, Walk, Run and start/stop. Remove/reapply shirt and confirm body/coverage restoration. Masks may optimize genuinely covered surfaces only after unmasked fit passes; they cannot hide penetration at openings or repair an anatomy gap.
6. Compare source animation, retargeted export and actual game renderer for backward elbows, shoulder twist, foot sliding or knee issues. Preserve quaternion-sign continuity, joint rest-axis mapping, weights and normal/seam fixes already in the maintained path. Do not widen gait unnaturally to avoid arm contact or claim a new raw FBX fixed a runtime interpolation bug without chain comparison.

If one shirt cut cannot accommodate the declared envelope after its existing fitting controls are correctly used, report the specific unsupported range and required cut/corrective. Do not shrink the range, invent coverage masks or resume custom tunic construction without an explicit scope decision. The named garment remains an unmet acceptance requirement until it works or Mike changes that requirement.

This procedure does not qualify armour, capes or accessories merely because a shirt works. Later garments use independent pieces, layer-specific fit supports and compatible wearers. A compound shirt/shorts item remains compound; no invented torso/leg interchangeability. Established premade hair cards, if later tested, remain separate from this body stage.

## 13. Cost, performance and isolated experiments

The design increases upfront reference/transfer work to reduce repeated manual repair. That saving is **a hypothesis**, not a guarantee. Record wall time, provider jobs/cost, failed rows, repair iterations and reusable yield per stage. Stop multiplying sources if the first pair cannot transfer faithfully; more donors do not repair a broken fitter.

Rendering and slider evaluation primarily cost the **player's client CPU/GPU and memory**. The server still admits/saves recipes and handles gameplay; cosmetic targets must not expand cognition inputs or change abilities. A good Unreal screenshot does not qualify exported geometry, textures, eyes or hair in the actual renderer. MetaHuman source complexity, compressed asset bytes, decoded arrays, GPU textures and frame time are distinct quantities.

Retain current proof budgets: **40,000 triangles, 2,048 maximum texture edge, 8,388,608 total texture pixels, eight materials, 64 joints, four influences and 16 MiB encoded GLB** for the governed output. The 16 MiB is an existing payload constraint, not a limit imposed by MetaHuman, a GPU-memory estimate or a reason to erase source anatomy. Do not apply it to the sum of an offline donor archive or quietly raise it. Compiled family data also needs its own measured transfer/residency report.

A dense position target costs `12 × vertexCount` bytes in float32 before metadata/encoding. At 20,000 vertices, eight arrays are 1.92 MB decimal for one body/base/age, excluding the neutral, normals, skin, shirt, head and textures. More age samples can multiply storage. Sparse/pruned/compressed targets or selective age loading are later measured optimizations, not assumptions used to hide the initial cost. Assets should be shared per family; report actual per-actor shaped buffer costs separately.

Body refinement therefore does **not** make every garment design disposable, but neither are old fits automatically safe. Preserve the editable garment source and binding/corrective data; refit against the changed anatomy and requalify affected shapes. Do not wait for final pores, skin finish or every hairstyle before proving the reusable body and garment system.
