# Audio tool selection and qualification

Use with the [audio procedure](audio-production.md). This is a shortlist and verification route, not a declaration that any provider has passed OpenLegend listening, editing, rights or export qualification. The [November 4 research](../research/audio/README.md) preserves the broader comparison and its dated terms. Recheck the exact account/product/operation before acquisition; prices and eligibility are not workflow constants.

## Initial music provider remains unresolved

Audition **both Suno and Eleven Music** before proposing the initial music default. Neither is selected, and trial order or one successful result does not make either the default. Compare musical appeal, targeted revisions, useful exports, licensing for the intended release and total cost. After presenting the actual results and limitations, **ask Mike before treating either provider as selected**. He may select one, approve different providers for different operations, choose neither or request further evidence. A trial authorization is not a production-provider decision.

Support different providers through narrow integrations for actual operations. Reuse study records, accounting, file intake and publication; keep provider-specific inputs and capabilities local to their helpers. Do not build a generalized provider framework, plugin registry or universal editing abstraction. The selected default, when approved, will be a convenience choice rather than an exclusive dependency.

| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| Cleanup, gain/EQ, trimming, loops and export                  | Suitable existing audio editor or narrow FFmpeg/analysis recipe                             | Preserve the selected source and record processing; do not regenerate to solve an ordinary edit                                          |
| A signature piece or difficult repair                         | Recording, licensed source, composer/producer or specialist                                 | Quality can justify a manual route; actual outreach, charges and input permissions retain their own authorization                        |

Do not install unofficial wrappers, use another person's account or create a self-hosted service to avoid evaluating the actual musical result. The old art provider roster does not select an audio provider. The two-provider music audition can use existing tools/manual records; it does not require two production adapters in advance. Additional helpers need a demonstrated operation, and no successful trial silently selects a default.

## Official capability evidence

**Checked November 6, 2026; published documentation, not executed trials.**

- **Suno:** its [MCP beta FAQ](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/14486145) documents conversational generation into the account library and the endpoint `https://api.suno.com/mcp`. It does not establish that every Studio edit/export is available through that connector. The [developer platform](https://platform.suno.com/) advertises a REST route. Inspect the connected tool schema and account access before promising a particular operation. Studio/source-separation documentation in the earlier research remains capability evidence, not clean-stem qualification.
- **Stable Audio:** the official [audio-to-audio](https://docs.stability.ai/api-reference/stable-audio/audio-to-audio) and [inpainting](https://docs.stability.ai/api-reference/stable-audio/inpaint) routes accept source audio; the latter identifies a replacement time range. The documented asynchronous job ID permits retrieving the same result. Successful musical-identity preservation remains an audition question. Do not copy an unbounded polling example into the implementation; use the project's bounded network owner.
- **Eleven SFX:** the [current documentation](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/sound-effects) identifies `eleven_text_to_sound_v3`, duration/loop controls and format distinctions; it also warns that precise repeated-event counts are not always followed. Select the actual model explicitly and inspect exports. A prompt saying “one strike” is not a single-impact pass.
- **Eleven Music:** [capability documentation](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/music) describes structure, section changes and uploaded audio references. A reference can guide sound/mood/instrumentation without guaranteeing a faithful remix. Do not assume several reference recordings can be combined unless the exact selected route supports it. The [Music terms](https://elevenlabs.io/music-terms) remain the source for plan/entity/game eligibility and library-distribution scope; [Creative terms](https://elevenlabs.io/creative-terms) separately govern SFX.

Google documents [audio understanding](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/audio) and [structured responses from multimodal inputs](https://firebase.google.com/docs/ai-logic/generate-structured-output). This makes Gemini a candidate for offline descriptor suggestions, not a qualified instrument/mood recognizer. The audio-document body could not be fetched by this research tool; its official indexed description and the accessible structured-output guide establish only the candidate capability. Inspect the exact available model/API and input terms before implementation. Do not introduce Firebase or a client-side provider key just because that guide demonstrates the capability; use the existing authorized server/local provider route where suitable. A speech-only transcription model does not satisfy musical analysis.

## Streaming links and reference files

Spotify/YouTube links may be recorded with timestamps, descriptions and the qualities Mike wants. They are not automatic downloadable conditioning sources. The [Spotify developer policy](https://developer.spotify.com/policy) restricts analysis and AI ingestion of platform content. [YouTube API policies](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies) restrict downloading audiovisual material and separating audio. Use a separately permitted source for provider uploads. A local file by itself proves neither provider-input permission nor permission to redistribute it.

The agent records whether it actually listened, inspected metadata only, or relied on Mike's description. If no authorized listening route is available, keep the reference and ask only for the missing properties needed to proceed. Do not silently replace a named source with an unrelated rendition.

## Qualify the operation before scaling

1. Establish access, exact product/model/plan, input rights and intended game-use eligibility for **each** music candidate before paid dispatch. Admit a bounded comparison under the existing shared task budget, including exports and outstanding charges. If access, licensing or budget prevents one audition, record that gap and return it to Mike; do not silently skip it and select the other.
2. Use the same musical brief, protected-feature request and listening conditions for both routes. State candidate counts and any duration/input differences before dispatch. Obtain a few candidates, choose a promising recording from each, request a targeted revision and retrieve useful final exports. Existing provider tools or UI steps can supply these artifacts; full adapter implementation is unnecessary for the comparison.
3. Compare the five dimensions below using retained actual outputs and evidence. Listen to full recordings at reasonable matched level, including repeated exposure. Hide provider labels for musical comparison when practical, then inspect control, rights and cost separately. No leaderboard, impressive excerpt or successful HTTP response establishes a winner.
4. Present the recordings, comparison, recommendation and material gaps to Mike. **Ask for his selection before setting a default, describing either as the selected music provider or proceeding on that assumption.** Aesthetic acceptance of one song does not by itself select its provider. Keep the default unset until his answer is recorded.

| Dimension          | Evidence to compare                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Musical appeal     | Full-cue appeal, intended scene fit and repeated-listening comfort, with Mike's preference separate from the agent's assessment                                                                                                                                |
| Targeted revisions | An actual edit of a favored output with a named protected feature; requested change, preserved identity, collateral drift and manual repair needed                                                                                                             |
| Exports            | Actual downloadable formats and source quality, usable endings/loops for the brief, editing utility and restrictions; no mandatory stems                                                                                                                       |
| Licensing          | Exact account/product/plan/licensee and source-input terms for official browser gameplay, internal jukebox, editing and gameplay videos; optional broader rights recorded separately                                                                           |
| Total cost         | Subscription/access purchases and incremental credits/cash, rejected attempts, revision/export charges, generation/review/repair time and uncertain commitments; distinguish actual charges, estimates and allocated subscription cost without double-counting |

Record the exact operation, receipt/recovery mechanism, inspected files and remaining limitations for each route. Use the existing [rights study](../research/audio/archive/2026/2026-11-04-rights-and-delivery.md) for licensing distinctions. An unavailable edit/export or uncertain entitlement is a finding, not permission for an automatic paid retry or a false pass. If neither meets the brief, keep the decision open and propose the concrete next option to Mike.

After Mike selects a route or combination, qualify additional operations only when needed. Effects and audio analysis have their own operation checks; auditioning Eleven Music does not qualify ElevenLabs SFX. Local editing, other cleared providers and specialists remain available within the approved scope, without expanding a failed trial indefinitely or building unused integrations.

## Preserve the useful output

Download the best legitimately available source and useful final representations, with authorized-download evidence where required. Preserve exact model, prompts, source inputs, operations and human edits. A seed may aid reproducibility but is not a substitute for the actual recording. Separate source-separation artifacts from original composition parts; never promise reversible editing of a flattened mix.

No provider is selected for live speech by this page. No trial, subscription, generation, listening acceptance or licensing clearance was performed merely by writing these instructions. [AUD01/AUD02](../maintainers/audio-production.md) own the actual qualification and acquisition work.
