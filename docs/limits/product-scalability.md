# Product scalability limits and policy choices

**Status: strategic constraints and unresolved tuning, not shipped capacity limits.** [Principles](../product-scalability/README.md), [feature specification](../projects/product-scalability-feature-spec.md), [technical design](../projects/product-scalability-tech-design.md), and [tracker](../maintainers/product-scalability.md) are the maintained records.

Each entry identifies the constraint, rationale, current decision, restrictiveness, and reconsideration trigger. Numeric examples in the discussion are not adopted settings. This file is the single product-scalability inventory; implementation-specific measured limits continue to belong to their native owners.

## Status and restrictiveness

New execution policies are not current runtime limits. DG34 now has a **selected local design** in PS-L42/PS-L46–PS-L48; its advertised service in PS-L43–PS-L45 remains explicitly deferred. Other proposal/selection statuses stay as stated in their owning entries. Existing shared inventories remain controlling. Unselected numerical values are not claims of unlimited capacity. Ratings below apply to the proposed behavioral restriction, not to an unmeasured numerical allowance or a security certification.

**Medium:** PS-L01, PS-L02, PS-L03, PS-L04, PS-L07, PS-L10, PS-L11, PS-L15 and PS-L16. These permit meaningful activity but deliberately constrain admission, attention, initiative, approximation, exact ambient detail, quality tiers, clock compatibility or campaign participation; actual generosity remains workload-dependent.

**Safe:** PS-L05, PS-L06, PS-L08, PS-L09, PS-L12 and PS-L17. These favor ready arrivals, bounded scene/control scope, supported protective outcomes, explicit grants and conservative overload handling; they may exclude otherwise valid activity until it is supported.

**Not a new tunable limit:** PS-L13 selects no funding/retention amount; PS-L14 adopts no new rollback window; PS-L18 records the evidence boundary rather than a capacity restriction. Their independent existing storage, spending and durability controls still apply. Correct identity, privacy, accounting and truthful evidence are guardrails, not candidates to relax under these ratings.

Material unresolved product choices are owned by [PS-D01–PS-D06](../../archive/05-project/open-decisions.md#product-scalability-integration-choices); this inventory owns restrictions, reasons and eventual values, not a competing decision register. The canonical topic linked through the suite index defines behavior at each boundary; the **Restriction** and **Select/revisit** fields below retain the initial disposition and evidence needed before enablement.

## PS-L01 — Local concurrency

**Decision:** no universal player-per-region number is accepted. Platform-scale goals do not imply unrestricted same-square scale. **Reason:** density and allowed interactions dominate the actual work. **Restriction:** clear local admission and action limits are permitted, including open-field boundaries where necessary. **Select/revisit:** qualify concert, market, combat, dispersed, and converging-cluster envelopes with end-to-end evidence and fair player UX.

## PS-L02 — Attention and sensory grouping

**Decision:** bounded attention with retained aggregate surroundings is accepted; exact budgets and grouping parameters are unset. **Reason:** distinguishability and cognition are limited without deleting physical effects. **Restriction:** no top-X shortcut that leaks hidden membership, discards promised understood speech, or suppresses unattended damage. **Select/revisit:** test focus stability, crowd noise, meaningful exceptions, and cost before per-object inference through PS04.

## PS-L03 — Background activity density

**Decision:** lower optional initiative and narrative density are permitted under an explicit world policy. No mandatory thoughts per fictional day. **Reason:** bound unattended liability without erasing lives. **Restriction:** current obligations and viable needs remain handled; missed optional initiatives do not accumulate. **Select/revisit:** measure long absence, relationship quality, survival, and required work in PS03.

## PS-L04 — Coarse fidelity and unsupported mechanics

**Selected first design, October 15:** numerical/trajectory/event calculations preserve the installed physical outcomes and relevant ordered evidence; unsupported equivalence retains ordinary bounded native execution or explicit admission refusal. Rendering unseen frames is unnecessary. **Reason:** save redundant computation without changing what happens. **Restriction:** no expected combat result, missed interaction, stock duplication or narrative success. **Historical broader option retained:** declared approximation need not match detailed counterfactuals only if that separate family is later selected with its own effect/work/lifecycle and protection contract. Ordinary inventors should reuse supported family computation, not implement a second game. [Selected technical contract](../projects/product-scalability-tech-design.md#11-research-refinement-one-authoritative-world-under-efficient-execution).

## PS-L05 — Detail preparation and residency

**Decision:** no fixed three-to-five-times visibility radius or universal cell size. **Reason:** effect reach, approach speed, topology, and preparation cost differ. **Restriction:** interaction readiness and coherent placement precede admitted arrival; demotion cannot erase conflict. **Select/revisit:** measure cold/warm approach, teleports, overlapping observers, border cycling, and cross-boundary effects in PS05.

## PS-L06 — Scene size and resolution

**Decision:** bounded scenes and completed beats are recommended; one scene per single model call is not guaranteed. **Reason:** privacy, interruption, and complexity can require separate actor decisions. **Restriction:** no omniscient whole-city prompt, future commitment beyond intervention, or invented human consent. **Select/revisit:** choose supported scene families and byte/participant/work bounds from actual PS04 cases.

## PS-L07 — Exact speech and gist

**Decision:** gist may remain gist; pivotal exact language can coexist with coarse scenes. **Reason:** do not pay for or fabricate unnecessary transcripts. **Restriction:** later reconstructions are not historical exact evidence; recording/perfect-recall mechanics must fund and retain actual language where required. **Select/revisit:** qualify source attribution, partial hearing, user requests for verbatim quotes, and scope-safe correction.

## PS-L08 — Protected background outcomes

**Decision:** world-specific protection over specified harms must cover the state inherited at detailed entry. **Reason:** no wolf trap or death caused merely by the first observer arriving. **Restriction:** finite supported harm categories; no universal certification of all possible future harm. Already-engaged conflict is not erased. **Select/revisit:** choose policies with explicit tagging, episode ownership, uncertainty fallback, and multiple-player fairness in PS05.

## PS-L09 — Dangerous logout and absentee control

**Decision:** current contested interactions may continue coarsely after departure; warnings and return reflect actual aftermath. **Reason:** no logout-to-escape exploit. **Restriction:** no new human goals, private thoughts, purchases, or unlimited autonomous adventures without appropriate authority. Grace, allowed defenses, safe exit, retention bounds, and reconnect rules remain unset. **Select/revisit:** compare voluntary departure, network failure, grief reengagement, and world death policies before activation.

## PS-L10 — Model quality and spending

**Decision:** heavily scrutinized characters may use higher evaluated baseline quality, with task escalation. No SKU, fixed token price, or universal role ratio is selected. **Reason:** short dialogue can still have high consistency stakes. **Restriction:** model cost is not correctness proof; scope and world admission remain external. **Select/revisit:** assess actual characterization, decision accuracy, complete workflow costs, and fair background/foreground spending.

## PS-L11 — Clocks

**Decision:** independent calendar pacing is a desired future world option, not a reinterpretation of current speed controls. **Reason:** dates need not multiply movement speed or cognition. **Restriction:** every affected rule and external commitment needs an explicit clock; arbitrary required transitions are not free. **Select/revisit:** qualify a real consumer with aging/needs/work/deadline consequences and connected-world economic rules.

## PS-L12 — Property and connected-world powers

**Decision:** protected personal/guild spaces and separate entry/build/inspection/campaign grants are primary requirements. **Reason:** preserve belonging and human privacy. **Restriction:** creative/admin powers do not automatically export competitively; owning land is not authority over another human's private mind. **Select/revisit:** define compatible federation profiles with actual travel, import, and access cases in PS07.

## PS-L13 — Quiet-world funding and retention

**Decision:** retaining history and funding perpetual autonomy are separate promises. ## PS-L14 — Durability and rollback

**Decision:** no five-minute rollback window is adopted.
