# Skilled action execution — limits and constraints

**Design selected October 18, 2026; runtime not implemented or measured.** These values are **selected initial implementation parameters** for the first playable design (status **Proposed** until delivered), not installed limits, benchmarks or evidence of fun. Their exact values are intentionally testable and can be retuned after playtesting. Review as one family with [feature](../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-feature-spec.md), [technical contract](../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md), [base-world selection](../worlds/base/skilled-action-execution.md), [ASE tasks](../maintainers/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md) and the [limits methodology](README.md).

## ASE-L01 — Quick tap versus held timing

**Proposed world-selected initial value: 150 real milliseconds** separates ordinary tap and deliberate hold; hold until manual release does not automatically fire. A click shorter than the threshold invokes one ordinary action (after real installed windup), with no bonus/penalty. A hold crosses to the installed gesture only if the selected action remains legal. **Restrictiveness: Medium.** Smaller thresholds may cause accidental mini-games; larger thresholds may feel sluggish. Measure input devices, pointer movement and first play. The native action's actual preparation and cooldown are *separate*. No additional total held-aim duration cap is selected: existing resource claims, interruptions, actor lifecycle, game pause and explicit cancellation remain effective. Indefinite holding can create defensive exposure/opportunity cost; if play exposes indefinite safe camping, change the world-owned preparation rule rather than invent an input timer.

## ASE-L02 — Ring pace and visible timing bands

**Proposed installed profile:** one full expansion/contraction every **900 real milliseconds**, centered at normalized radius `0.5` with green when `d≤0.06` and yellow (including green) when `d≤0.15`; signed-score interpolation is pinned by the [technical evaluator](../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#selected-initial-timing-curve-and-accessible-parity). This is a readable starting target, not a verified physical weapon law. World-owned applicable Dexterity-like and Focus-like contributions may widen and slow the regions respectively; no mandatory engine stat or invented drunkenness attribute. **Restrictiveness: Medium**; smaller windows reward timing but may exclude many legitimate players. Color, line/pattern and assist settings remain essential; color alone is insufficient. Changeable via exactly pinned selected profile, not renderer constants.

## ASE-L03 — Scalar execution range and release quality

**Proposed supported normalized score: `[-1,1]`**, with zero equal to ordinary input, positive green and negative poor timing/placement. Yellow is normal. One accepted score per committed execution attempt; UI estimates never constitute hit/quality authority. World-installed family chooses an admissible *specific* channel; arbitrary code/stat keys are refused. **Restrictiveness: Safe**, because it prevents injected unbounded multipliers but doesn't constrain game effects absent a world-owned consumer. If richer gestures later need more components, establish a new finite qualified profile rather than abusing the scalar. A missed timing region is not an automatic attack miss.

## ASE-L04 — Initial bounded weapon contributions

**Proposed excellent relative maxima:** sling conditional contact opportunity `+25%`, other ranged launchers `+20%`, spear conditional contact opportunity `+15%` and pre-defense injury `+10%`, hammer pre-defense injury `+25%`; all from their existing actual base after normal competence, with independent *world-authored* rates and native valid-range clamp. **Proposed poor relative minimum:** about `-10%` for each installed channel. **Restrictiveness: Medium**; these are player-quality hypotheses, not universal max bounds or current world law. A multi-channel profile is not a blanket 25% to everything and may still overperform; qualify actual combined effective contact/injury and cooldown across realistic prey and equipment. A 75%-baseline shot receiving `+25%` relative becomes at most **93.75%** conditional contact odds, not a guaranteed hit or +25 percentage points. Critical-effect channels require a separately supported critical mechanic; none is installed here.

## ASE-L05 — Item-specific fit and grade

**Proposed selected `base:woven-container` finishing fit:** neutral `1.00`, best `1.25`, poor at worst `0.90`; effective `container.capacity = round(baseCapacity × fit)`, constrained to a finite positive integer by the owning item family and initial capacity bounds. Example: `p=6`, existing baseline `4p=24`, yields approximately `22 / 24 / 30` usable packing-load units. **Restrictiveness: Medium**; quality must be useful without overwhelming real input stock/size choice. Optional descriptive grades are **derived projections**, thresholds not yet validated; no general item grade requirement or universal durability numeric bound. The existing woven-family `p` range/work/consumption stays owned by [camp containers](../worlds/base/camp-containers.md) and [objects](objects.md), not duplicated here. Item fit is immutable after manufacture until a future reviewed item-mutation mechanism exists, preventing a loaded bag from suddenly losing usable capacity.

## ASE-L06 — Finite noncombat handwork

**Proposed: at most one optional point-placement commit per crafted fitted-container output.** A normal craft or **explicitly skipped** finish yields neutral quality and the same ordinary work/material result. An **accessible skilled input** may earn up to the same bounded positive bonus, rather than being locked to neutral. No extra failed-craft penalty, repeated reroll-for-perfect, speculative production step, output multiplication or non-human fake timing. **Restrictiveness: Safe**; one choice demonstrates distinct mechanics while limiting repetitive attention work. Selected recipes without this admitted family have no option. **Selected pending-finishing bound:** one accepted unfinished manual-finish choice per native craft and at most one active primary physical work claim for the actor; when ordinary work ends without input, the action waits as **Ready to finish** instead of choosing a bonus or neutral score automatically. Waiting consumes no repeated native/AI evaluation and holds no new additional resource beyond that one workpiece's existing claims. Finish carefully, Finish normally, or Cancel are the explicit legal endings; existing body, control, work and interruption rules apply. Count/score do not create material sources or new learned recipes. Broader assembly with multiple genuinely different decisions needs independent design.

## ASE-L07 — World speed and clocks

**Current normal clock:** 60 simulated game seconds per real second, available 0.5×/1×/3×/8×. **Proposed no manual-input speed scaling:** the ring/finish interaction uses actual real input time, while movement, native work, cooldown and projectile flight retain installed simulation elapsed time. **Restrictiveness: Medium**; the player's target may move very quickly at 8×, but hiding the clock change would contradict world state. Qualify all four speeds. No global slowdown, action-time freeze or invisible world pause is selected. A later independently approved clock design may distinguish calendrical time and combat simulation, without retroactively granting uncommitted extra time.

## ASE-L08 — Input/network work and pending state

**Proposed: zero per-frame gameplay-command transmissions for ring/pointer tracking; one scored release/finish submission per exact attempt.** Movement intent uses bounded state changes/renewal, not a fully hydrated world command per rendered frame. Provisional transport design target: around **10 or fewer intent renewals per real second when continuously held**, with a finite server-controlled lost-input stop window (candidate **250 real milliseconds**, latency/device testing required) and no unbounded per-player movement queue. **Restrictiveness: Safe**, with recoverability versus latency tradeoff. Existing server rate, authority, command-receipt and native work budgets remain; this is not permission to skip real movement samples or full physical collision during that interval. Growing active players × active gestures must not produce 60× regular server-side scoring; input validation occurs at exact attempts, not every rendered frame. Avoid inventing an exact max packet size when the existing protocol already bounds requests.

## ASE-L09 — Physical contact and saved per-shot state

**Proposed:** each released finite projectile evaluates actual physical candidates in canonical earliest order. A locally avoided physical life is excluded from re-contact by **that same flight only**, and the retained set grows at most with distinct actual near-path body lives contacted/avoided by the flight. Existing flight distance/work budget, tie ordering, selected projectile count and current-format persistence remain authoritative; no new unbounded spectator scan or arbitrary `max targets` cutoff may hide a valid second body. **Restrictiveness: Medium.** If crowded-body candidate work exhausts current admitted native budget, report technical incompleteness/continuation honestly and preserve physical correctness; measure sparse and dense line-of-fire cases before setting a specific per-flight bound. No extra world-coordinate displacement is mandatory for a small resolved bodily dodge. The existing first **non-avoided** actual physical impact stops the initial finite projectile.

## ASE-L10 — Competitive trust and supported absence

**Proposed initial human-PvP restriction:** untrusted manual browser performance supplies **no additional contact/damage advantage when the actual contacted body is human-controlled**, whether intended or collateral, until an approved competitive timing/aim protocol exists. Actual projectile direction, ordinary attack eligibility, native PvP, lethal review, source attribution and material effects remain authoritative. **Restrictiveness: Very safe** for manually claimed human-PvP bonuses and may reduce tactical expressiveness until a fair protocol is built. This is a separate product/security decision, not an anti-cheat achievement.

**Shared-world crafting is not automatically ranked PvP.** A player can make, inspect, give and trade an individually fitted bag in normal co-operative play, with the server deriving fit/capacity from the selected installed recipe, approved coordinates and actual spent resources. The quality persists across custody; a later trade cannot revoke it. Modified clients may choose the best noncompetitive placement, so a future **high-stakes competitive production mode** must require separately selected server-verifiable scoring or the neutral option **at creation**. This is a deliberate, bounded first-slice trust tradeoff, not permission for the browser to author arbitrary item properties. An omitted installed skill profile retains only ordinary action behavior.

## Growth, removal and next review

These **proposed** limits have no installed behavior yet and do not remove historical caps. No blanket limit on all action types or item count is introduced. Existing shared guards remain: one authoritative mutation owner, exact work and resource reservations, current permitted visibility, stable ids/pins, bounded command envelope and actual timer/persistence transitions. After implementing a vertical slice, measure actual browser responsiveness and human usefulness before settling fine percentages or treating a high-scoring press as proof of good design. [ASE delivery](../maintainers/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md) owns required qualification; [limits audit](../maintainers/limits-audit.md) should track only concrete new restrictive or dangerously liberal behavior discovered in testing.
