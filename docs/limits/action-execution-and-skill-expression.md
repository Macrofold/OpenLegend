# Skilled action execution — limits and constraints

**Design reviewed October 19, 2026; runtime not implemented or measured.** All ASE-L01–ASE-L10 entries have status **Proposed**. They inventory selected initial implementation parameters and deliberate bounds, not installed behavior, benchmarks or evidence of fun. The [base-world profile](../worlds/base/skilled-action-execution.md) owns authored mechanical tuning; the [technical contract](../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md) owns reusable mechanisms, server input lifetime and client device defaults; the [feature](../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-feature-spec.md), [ASE tasks](../maintainers/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md) and [limits methodology](README.md) own outcome and qualification. No limit is silently removed by writing this design.

## ASE-L01 — Quick tap versus held timing

**Selected initial threshold: 150 real milliseconds**, owned by the [shared client input/settings contract](../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#61-one-owner-visible-state-and-selected-bindings). A shorter tap is ordinary score zero. A longer hold can be scored only after its exact admitted profile has been displayed; start its phase at that later boundary, and keep an earlier release neutral. Native preparation begins with the one admitted attempt and remains independent. Holding never repeats an attack. Early release retains one frozen intention only while its input authorization is live; pause, cancellation, blur, detected disconnection, control loss or expired lease disarms automatic release.

**Restrictiveness: Medium.** This is device input classification, not a world/weapon/recipe property. A smaller threshold risks accidental challenges; a larger one delays a deliberate choice. Qualify actual devices, delayed admission and frame stalls. No additional maximum total hold duration is selected: actual native claims, interruption, vulnerability, control and explicit cancellation remain effective. Indefinite safe camping, if observed, needs a justified world preparation rule rather than an unexplained browser timer.

## ASE-L02 — Ring pace and visible timing bands

The [world input profile](../worlds/base/skilled-action-execution.md#concentric-release-interaction) selects a **900-real-ms** expansion/contraction cycle, target radius `0.5`, green half-width `0.06` and ordinary outer half-width `0.15`. Its [generic evaluator](../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#22-deterministic-evaluation-and-presentation) supplies a continuous signed score; exact zero boundaries display ordinary. First center-green occurs 225 ms after ring origin, or 375 ms after an immediately admitted pointer-down. Those are arithmetic properties, not measured human response.

**Restrictiveness: Medium.** Smaller bands may reward precision while excluding useful play. No new Dexterity, Focus, intoxication or clothworking attribute is installed. Future supported native contributions may visibly alter the pinned challenge; no secret mid-attempt rescoring. Color-independent cues and explicit **Step timing** are required. The [shared Step timing control](../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#62-a-complete-finite-accessible-attack-route) owns ordered stops from the `0.01` phase grid plus the admitted triangle's exact visible center crossings `c/2` and `1-c/2`; Home/End select its first/last stops. The selected `c=0.5` behavior remains Home `0`, End `0.99`, with exact maxima `.25`/`.75`. A valid alternate `c=0.43` also permits its maxima `.215`/`.785`, through at most two additional stops and no automatic release. These client controls preserve the same noncompetitive maximum without slowing the world or making keyboard resolution a world rule. Tuning requires actual keyboard, reduced-motion and player evidence.

## ASE-L03 — Scalar execution range and release quality

**Supported score: `[-1,1]`**, with zero ordinary, positive strong and negative weak. One accepted choice contributes to one native attempt/output; a new request ID cannot reroll an accepted choice. World-installed families select only supported contact/injury/fit consumers. Client score, arbitrary effect/stat keys, nonfinite coordinates/phase and stale exact meanings refuse at admission. Missing optional support is valid absence; an invalid present profile is not silently ordinary.

**Restrictiveness: Safe.** Finite input prevents unbounded supplied modifiers without creating a global stat. The family derives its actual effect, with its native valid-range clamp. Deterministic recalculation does not prove that a human performed the input. Poor timing does not force a physical miss; green does not create collision. Richer future gestures require useful finite semantics rather than overloading this scalar.

## ASE-L04 — Initial bounded weapon contributions

The [weapon consequence table](../worlds/base/skilled-action-execution.md#weapon-specific-consequences-selected-initial-testing-profiles) selects excellent relative maxima: sling contact `+25%`, bow contact `+20%`, spear contact `+15%` plus pre-defense injury `+10%`, and hammer pre-defense injury `+25%`. Each selected channel has a `-10%` poor endpoint. Apply existing native competence once before these factors. A 75% baseline with +25% relative becomes 93.75%, not a guaranteed hit. The spear's combined pre-defense expected injury increases 26.5% in the world's stated simple example; do not call that only a 10% or 15% outcome change.

The [native baseline table](../worlds/base/skilled-action-execution.md#native-weapon-baselines-and-recovery) preserves knife/spear work and recovery, adds sling/bow recovery of 18/24 game seconds, and authors the finite hammer's 24-game-second windup and 48-game-second recovery. These use the existing body deadline across shot, strike, Punch and guard admission. Stop, movement, tool changes and restore cannot reset a committed deadline. Punch remains its ordinary unphased action without an invented timing/recovery profile. The hammer is a new obtainable authored item, not an existing weapon falsely claimed as present.

**Restrictiveness: Medium.** Magnitudes and cadence are balance proposals, not universal caps or implemented weapon facts. Qualify combined channels, useful ordinary attacks, high handling/clamping, defense, native windup and total repeat cadence. There is no new critical mechanic. A failed feasible local dodge contest can matter only where real geometry permits a response; low handling never makes unchanged matter transparent.

## ASE-L05 — Item-specific fit and grade

The [fitting law](../worlds/base/skilled-action-execution.md#fit-factor-integer-capacity-and-initial-bounds) selects a creation-time factor from `0.90` to `1.25`, neutral `1`, and positive integer capacity `floor(baseCapacity × factor + 0.5)`. The ordinary woven family still supplies baseline `4p`. The six-unit pouch has exact endpoint capacities **22 / 24 / 30**. Across the existing material range, fitted capacities are **7–40**, compared with ordinary **8–32**; do not clamp a valid best-fit result back to 32.

**Restrictiveness: Medium.** Rounding means an eight-unit capacity becoming seven loses 12.5%, although the pre-round factor loss is 10%; a small positive score may leave capacity unchanged. **No categorical grade is selected initially.** Show the real item capacity; future optional grades need supported meanings. Fitting changes neither empty load nor nested content load, and characters currently have no total carrying-load cap. Qualify a useful 25–30-load kit against the ordinary pouch and direct carrying. Existing quantities/work are owned by [camp containers](../worlds/base/camp-containers.md) and [objects](objects.md).

The exact individual item retains its admitted factor through trade, nesting, death-drop and same-version restore. No global definition mutation, stack laundering or capacity recomputation from private maker history is permitted. This slice freezes fit after creation. A future deterioration/repair rule must protect existing contents and use the same item authority.

## ASE-L06 — Finite noncombat handwork

**At most one optional placement choice per native careful craft.** Select ordinary or careful work before start. Ordinary craft auto-completes. Careful work accepts one finite point or **Finish normally**; work and input can finish in either order. If work ends first, that same actor action waits at **Ready to finish**, occupying its existing physical-work claim with no recurring zero-deadline/coarse-work/AI polling. There is no live resolved action, detached workpiece, additional output or reroll loop.

The [world fixture](../worlds/base/skilled-action-execution.md#exact-visible-fixture-and-score) supplies two visible seam marks and their halfway-fastening task. It has no perfect-answer dot, live score, hidden ID randomization or fabricated textile property. Its positive radius is `0.08`, ordinary radius `0.20` and penalty-tail width `0.50`; the latter is not the square's diagonal and saturates the negative score at distance `0.70`. The [shared marker input owner](../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#63-craft-placement-and-usable-feedback) supplies keyboard coarse/fine steps `10/120` and `1/120`, with exact pointer-to-grid takeover and all seven selected recipe midpoints reachable; the world fixture does not own those device controls. Pointer and keyboard share one marker/evaluator and useful maximum.

Materials are consumed at work start. Capture exact positive consumed-lot evidence before debit; the current maximum recipe requires at most twelve contributing lots because it consumes at most twelve input units. Generated binding-producer dependencies remain exact. Final completion does not reserve, find or charge those consumed lots again. Cancellation or genuine interruption retains spent materials and creates no pouch. Same-version continuation applies only while the native action has not actually ended.

**Restrictiveness: Safe** for one bounded choice and conserved work; **playability remains unqualified**. A simple midpoint task may become repetitive free capacity. Uncoached usefulness must establish its value before acceptance; scripted arithmetic alone cannot pass that gate. Broader assembly and additional meaningful work choices require separate design.

## ASE-L07 — World speed and clocks

Current normal clock is **60 game seconds per real second**, with 0.5×/1×/3×/8×. The selected rule keeps human input in real time and native movement, preparation, recovery, flight, craft, duck hold and response deadlines in game time. The [world clock table](../worlds/base/skilled-action-execution.md#game-pace-accessibility-and-trust) states exact conversion examples. Placement has no reflex deadline; waiting does not pause other actors.

**Restrictiveness: Medium.** At 8× opponents move and native deadlines pass much faster during a fixed human ring cycle. Qualify all four speeds, latency and pending model work. No hidden slowdown, global time freeze or action-specific pause is selected. A different calendar/combat clock is a separate product decision, not an implementing agent's workaround.

## ASE-L08 — Input/network work and pending state

There are **zero per-frame gameplay transmissions for ring/pointer tracking**, and at most one accepted scored release/finish for the exact attempt. The [existing server control/presence owner](../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#13-movement-alongside-preparation-and-the-live-input-lease) selects the initial **100-real-ms** routine renewal target and **250-real-ms** lost-input expiry. These operational values are independent of fictional time and installed world/weapon/recipe definitions; a world cannot extend stale-input authority. Unchanged renewal extends an operational lease without full world preparation. Direction/stop changes enter genuine simulation boundaries. Old/equal movement or renewal sequences cannot renew authority; discrete attack sequence is independent. Existing request envelopes and budgets remain controlling.

Retain at most one bounded current attack-gesture slot per live controller and one latest movement intent, with a matching exact early-release identity where needed. The [clock/control contract](../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#13-movement-alongside-preparation-and-the-live-input-lease) permits one unsaved finite steering window and one independent window for the exact armed release, not input history. A fresh window cannot cover old simulation debt; renewal cannot grant future or duplicate game time. Direction changes cannot replace or re-date the pending release. Native offers respect window boundaries and current expiry; unadvanced world time remains owed.

Cancellation can terminally name the original begin request before its action ID is returned. Released/ended work settles the slot; no movement-destination queue, second action registry or persisted gesture database is added. Common pause/load/handoff revokes the input epoch and both fixed lane tokens. An individual expiry rotates only that lane's token before accepting another packet, so an old higher sequence cannot restart it while the independently live lane continues. Each lane has its own renewal watermark; a stale claim cannot suppress the other's valid renewal. Fresh scope requires fresh deliberate input, not automatic rebinding of held controls. Durable receipts keep their separate recovery meaning, and a review's explicit confirmation never becomes a queued action on modal dismissal.

**Restrictiveness: Safe**, with responsiveness versus lost-input travel to measure. Under backlog, input may expire before its authorized window is reached and have no effect. Qualify navigation/CPU debt, delayed host continuation and speed changes, including a pending release; expiry cannot be repaired by deleting an effect while retaining its dependent consequences. The current five-second general presence heartbeat cannot meet this short input deadline. A network partition has no instantaneous remote-cancel guarantee; already committed effects stay real. The [shared focused aim control](../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#62-a-complete-finite-accessible-attack-route) owns initial 16-CSS-pixel increments and 1-pixel fine steps; these device defaults are neither server proof of pointer authenticity nor world distance. No new packet-size cap is invented.

## ASE-L09 — Physical contact and saved per-shot state

The [world response](../worlds/base/skilled-action-execution.md#physical-arrow-and-local-evasion) selects one actual duck/tuck for supported grounded person/deer capsules: height `max(2r + spatialTolerance, 0.45h)`, strictly below the ordinary height; unchanged feet/radius with scaled body anchors. Hold is **12 game seconds** and a qualified feasible response attempt starts a **60-game-second** response cooldown. A blocked return retains the actual small shape until standing clearance becomes valid; travel still requires the conservative ordinary-body envelope. Unaware, incapable, incompatible-work/guard/recovering or geometrically uncleared candidates receive no fictional dodge.

Retain at most one pose per body and its response deadline. The [shared physical owner](../projects/action-execution-and-skill-expression-tech-design.md#3-world-physical-contact-and-the-evasion-reconciliation) derives actual geometry through one world-aware effective-body reader; ordinary clearance remains explicit. Its shared motion preparation includes actual saved-speed Evade, animal motion, corridor/landing alternatives, falling and steering rather than treating each as an ordinary straight walk. There is **no lifetime per-flight ignored-body set**. Current shape participates in every flight, and later source/defender reentry remains collidable. Sparse pending encounter evidence covers only each current continuous crossing until exit/removal/flight termination; it can delay an observed Miss but never suppress contact. Native random draws occur once only for an uncertain qualified response, in canonical global flight/collider order. Current-format saves retain exact physical life/pins, pose/deadlines, flight contribution, crossing evidence and random continuation.

**Restrictiveness: Medium.** This atomic local response is a selected abstraction, not continuous skeletal motion, unlimited evasion or physiological measurement. No arbitrary maximum candidate count may hide a real interceptor. Existing native distance/work-budget correctness remains. Reuse compatible interval membership/envelopes and update affected geometry after a pose change; blocked standing retries wake from relevant spatial changes rather than global polling. Measure cold `O(B)` preparation, path pieces/branches, broad visits, exact tests and invalidations separately, including a distant large/fast body, dense simultaneous arrows, low rays, walls, moving reentry and large-versus-sliced advances. Clearing the last blocker must wake and permit standing after the last flight ends, subject to current support and body clearance. No population scan is added per rendered frame, and the current complete projectile path is not claimed already local-only.

## ASE-L10 — Competitive trust and supported absence

The **proposed first-slice safeguard** uses the entire optional score **zero** at the actual human-owned defender, including negative timing and disconnected-player bodies, regardless of intended versus collateral contact. Native geometry, character ability, review, injury and recovery remain real. Feedback must not disclose hidden human ownership. **Restrictiveness: Very safe** for raw manual PvP contribution and potentially less expressive; full competitive timing/aim authenticity remains deferred by Mike. This is an implementation proposal, not a personal approval or an anti-cheat result.

Ordinary shared-world fitting and trade remain allowed. The native craft derives capacity from its exact admitted family, one placement, spent resources and actual work; custody never revokes the completed property. Modified clients can choose an ideal noncompetitive placement. A separately ranked/high-stakes production activity needs a qualified policy or neutral creation, with no retroactive item downgrade. Missing installed input/fit support leaves ordinary action available; corrupt present support rejects at its owner rather than turning into an invented exceptional or ordinary result.

## Growth, removal and next review

No blanket action count, item count or total hold cap is introduced. Existing exact authority, physical work, resource, visibility, identity/pin, request-envelope, native-budget and save integrity guards remain. These proposed additions do not remove an installed historical limit. [ASE delivery](../maintainers/action-execution-and-skill-expression.md) owns real qualification; [limits audit](../maintainers/limits-audit.md) should receive only a concrete restrictive or dangerously liberal finding from actual use. Revisit timing, renewal/expiry, posture/recovery and pouch-value defaults together after the vertical slice runs, with measured environment and truthful acceptance status.
