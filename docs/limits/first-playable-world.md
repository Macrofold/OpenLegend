# First-playable integration limits

**Selected design, October 17, 2026; no integrated runtime qualification.** [The world specification](../projects/first-playable-world-feature-spec.md), [technical design](../projects/first-playable-world-tech-design.md), [layout](../worlds/base/first-playable-layout.md) and [FPW tasks](../maintainers/first-playable-world.md) define the scope. [FPV](../maintainers/first-playable-validation.md) owns complete experience acceptance.

| ID     | Selected constraint and owner                                                                                                                 | Reason / tradeoff                                                                                    | Evidence and expansion trigger                                                                                                 |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| FPWL01 | Main finite 128 × 112 m terrain; preserve original northwest 62 × 54 m landmarks                                                              | Room for separate origins/trips while reusing actual content; cold navigation/scenery costs increase | Physical candidate validation, complete caller/browser measurements and observed travel burden; no larger-world capacity claim |
| FPWL02 | Four initially non-hostile residents, existing two Watchpost people, finite existing wildlife                                                 | Distinct relationships/opposition without a crowd simulation; no guarantee of survival/loyalty       | CP/RW independent behavior and real player observations; not a universal population cap                                        |
| FPWL03 | Initial player fullness/energy 100 and empty stock; finite near forage and cold public recovery hearths                                       | Human reading room with real survival/physical loss, no automatic kit/rescue                         | SV bootstrap/depletion/death and full-session observations                                                                     |
| FPWL04 | Three 6 × 6 m terrain suggestions; arbitrary other eligible sites remain possible                                                             | Readable alternatives and verified build/gate space without mandatory plots                          | SH/FPW actual geometry and ordinary build controls; suggestions grant no ownership                                             |
| FPWL05 | Build-time typed YAML, registered finite mechanics, one generated bundle                                                                      | Auditable world-specific content without runtime code loading/evaluation                             | Strict reference/schema checks, current-format restore and genuinely different supported fixture                               |
| FPWL06 | Exact same-transaction gameplay/authority/receipt publication                                                                                 | Prevent unearned rights, stranded materials and replay after unknown outcomes                        | FPW01/04 failure/revocation/restore checks through actual SQL owner; no distributed grant service                              |
| FPWL07 | Existing 60 game seconds per real second clock and current perception; unified clock presentation                                             | No accidental double conversion, hidden night penalty or modal immunity                              | Actual action/offer pacing, rendering/AI agreement and perception/source checks                                                |
| FPWL08 | Design observation windows: first basics a few minutes; useful camp 5–10; meaningful outward activity 10–20; improvement/return 20–40 minutes | Diagnose attention and payoff; not deadlines, triggers, rewards or measured completion times         | Uncoached FPV observations may revise these hypotheses; preserve legitimate different player paths                             |

Subsystem numbers have one owner: [SV](first-playable-survival.md), [SH](editable-shelters.md), [CP](first-playable-cooperation.md), [RW](first-playable-roaming.md), [combat responses](combat-and-threat-responses.md) and [speech](hearing-and-speech.md). Existing custom/solo creation envelopes remain in [world creation](world-creation.md); none is silently increased by the fixed Main template. Broader hosting, funding, storage, concurrency and UI limits retain their existing inventories.
