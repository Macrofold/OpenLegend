## RC01 — Straight, finite, single-target projectiles

**Current delivered first-flight baseline — Restrictiveness: Medium; complete encounter balance remains unqualified.** Ordinary finite release, injury, misses and real cover have recorded play evidence. A released point projectile travels straight to first solid/body contact or its installed range. Only the exact authorized target can receive damage; another body stops it without injury. No collateral harm, arc, ricochet, penetration or recoverable ammunition. These explicit supported-family restrictions preserve existing indirect-harm policy and avoid promising a universal ballistic model. Expansion requires a selected use case and any necessary D07 decision, not a new weapon name.

**Selected removal of target-only injury, October17; implementation pending.** Mike now requires actual bodily projectile collateral and permits human PvP. [CTR01](../maintainers/combat-and-threat-responses.md) and the [world contact law](../worlds/base/ranged-counterplay.md#selected-contact-consequences) supersede this particular RC01 restriction, including post-release target-specific lethal suppression. This changes a world policy; it does not qualify new runtime behavior or generic structural destruction.
