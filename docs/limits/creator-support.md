# Creator support and contributor voice — limits

The [DG30 product proposal](../projects/creator-support-and-contributor-voice-feature-spec.md) owns behavior. These are **proposed operating choices**, not a live fund, legal terms, measured administrative capacity or permission to spend. D37 retains its existing status; [PD10.7](../maintainers/production-deployment.md) owns the conditional operating gate. Current contribution, rights, privacy and financial owners remain controlling.

## GF-L01 — One invited and accountable pilot

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** One wanted-outcome brief, one award and one contracting recipient; at most three invitations in total, including declined invitations. A declared small team works through that recipient's actual responsibilities, without automatic platform splits. Two nonconflicted reviewers and an accountable final authority are required; a genuinely separate reconsideration route must be available for the actual dispute. The first paid recipient/contact is at least 18 and legally able to contract in the actual supported territories. No paid account, prior Open Legend contribution, follower count or unpaid new prototype is required. Additional candidates, awards, guardian-mediated payments and open public intake need a separately funded scope. This is intentionally a small invited pilot, not a claim of representative worldwide competition. Owner: PD10/D37; [first cycle](../projects/creator-support-and-contributor-voice-feature-spec.md#3-a-complete-first-cycle), [eligibility](../projects/creator-support-and-contributor-voice-feature-spec.md#51-who-can-be-invited).

## GF-L02 — Short private proposal

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** One proposal per responsible person/beneficiary group, a concise 1,000-word guide and at most three links to existing evidence. Equivalent concise length and accessibility help apply; no applicant byte counter or automatic rejection for a small word discrepancy. Plain text is sufficient; private-world exports, new prototypes, video and decks are not required. Corrections replace the acknowledged version during intake. At the boundary, ask for a complete shorter proposal rather than truncate acceptance, rights or costs silently. Review access does not license rejected material or enroll it in later rounds. Owner: PD10; [submission](../projects/creator-support-and-contributor-voice-feature-spec.md#52-what-to-submit).

## GF-L03 — Finite intake and selection

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Fourteen-real-day invitation window, one consolidated clarification with seven real days to respond, and at most one common extension of seven days for material changed criteria. Each candidate receives a decision within fourteen days after the response/clarification window closes; one notified extension of up to fourteen days is permitted. Close the first selection when that expires rather than leave an indefinite funding promise. These clocks budget attention, not claim an optimal duration; revise a later program if actual accessibility or review evidence warrants it. An unsigned offer expires after fourteen real days; only the original reviewed candidates can receive a replacement offer, without reopened intake. No award is a valid outcome. Owner: PD10; [proposal](../projects/creator-support-and-contributor-voice-feature-spec.md#52-what-to-submit), [selection](../projects/creator-support-and-contributor-voice-feature-spec.md#6-choose-the-actual-result-not-the-impressive-pitch).

## GF-L04 — Real appropriation and finite operating capacity

**Status: Proposed; actual amount unselected · Restrictiveness: Safe only with the real reservation.** One approved platform appropriation covers the maximum signed award, agreed cancellation exposure, operations and contingency, with actual available review/reconsideration/support hours. Existing customer, refund, creator, tax and prior-award obligations come first. No dedicated sponsor fundraising, automatic revenue percentage or DG28 unassigned allocation feeds this pilot. Native/AI/hosting expenditure uses its existing actual funding owner. Stop new commitments when capacity is unavailable; preserve owed amounts. The proposal does not set a currency, grant amount, territory or annual promise. Owner: PD10/actual financial authority; [funding](../projects/creator-support-and-contributor-voice-feature-spec.md#4-budget-the-whole-obligation-before-inviting-work).

## GF-L05 — Delivery and milestone scope

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Six real weeks from the funded start, early attempt at two weeks, and one mutually agreed extension of at most two weeks. Actual operator-controlled review/payment/resource waits shift dependent dates without consuming that extension. Early attempt earns 25% of the award; final usable result earns the remainder. The optional advance equals that early share rounded down to the currency's minor unit and is part of the same award. Initiate an agreed advance within fourteen real days of signing and valid payment details; if required advance/resources are unavailable thirty days after signing, stop unstarted work and reconcile commitments/pending money. An honest agreed negative attempt can earn the early milestone. At most two independently useful prevalued components divide the final milestone when partial acceptance makes sense; otherwise it is one whole. No hardware purchasing or general expense platform is included. Changed scope stays within the funded ceiling; a fundamentally different project closes the old one and needs new authorization. Owner: PD10/selected contribution owner; [milestones](../projects/creator-support-and-contributor-voice-feature-spec.md#7-the-agreement-and-first-funded-attempt).

## GF-L06 — Review, payment and support

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Respond to each submitted milestone within seven real days; include one ordinary corrective revision, normally returned within fourteen days, without adding new scope. A missed review escalates to the final owner for seven further days, then stops affected work under cancellation terms if still unreviewed; required waits shift dependent dates, not acceptance. Initiate accepted payment within fourteen real days after acceptance and valid payment information, with an actual explained legal/financial hold if applicable. Conforming acceptance and discretionary merge/launch timing are separate. The final handoff includes fourteen days after acceptance and at most two hours of ordinary additional clarification/correction. Further work needs its actual commissioning and funding. These are proposed promises requiring real staffing before offer, not guarantees already made. Owner: PD10/financial and contribution owners; [acceptance and payment](../projects/creator-support-and-contributor-voice-feature-spec.md#8-delivery-acceptance-and-payment).

## GF-L07 — Stopping and advance reconciliation

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Before full early acceptance, documented agreed labor/approved expense can earn up to the early 25% milestone share regardless of advance choice. Reconcile actual payments to pay an earned balance or return unused advance. Full honest early delivery earns its agreed amount. Final partial value is preagreed or mutually agreed, never a unilateral percentage. Operator cancellation settles admitted work/commitments under signed terms within the same maximum award. Request unused advance return within thirty real days of reconciliation; a reasonable written payment arrangement or authorized waiver preserves a truthful balance without unrelated debits or game penalties. For disappearance, one initial contact and one follow-up at least seven days later; after thirty days without reply close new work and reconcile known delivery, without erasing owed money. Owner: PD10/financial authority; [closure](../projects/creator-support-and-contributor-voice-feature-spec.md#9-changes-partial-work-and-stopping-without-a-fight).

## GF-L08 — Public reusable result and finite rights grant

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** First awards fund a result under its actual public-use/reuse component terms; contributor copyright remains. Review permission, agreed milestone publication and private unaccepted drafts are distinct. No blanket assignment, exclusive marketplace, mandatory new paywall, perpetual support, automatic royalty, game power or private-data access is included. Current first-party licensing and actual inherited third-party rights still apply. A demonstration's hosting interval must be actually funded and stated if offered; this program selects no indefinite demo hosting or change to the development compatibility policy. Owner: rights/contribution/PD10; [rights](../projects/creator-support-and-contributor-voice-feature-spec.md#10-rights-recognition-and-reuse).

## GF-L09 — Bounded advisory voice

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** One optional consultation during the invitation period, open for at most seven real days or twenty admitted submissions, whichever comes first. Disclose capacity/early closure; admit complete in-scope submissions in received order and read the whole admitted set within reserved time, or omit the optional consultation. At most one revisable concise submission per person, guided by 500 words with equivalent-language/accessibility help. Report actual coverage and substantial concerns without individual-reply or representative-opinion promises. Players, contributors and supporters may speak without a payment or contribution credential. Overlapping roles and alternate accounts supply no additional weight. No ballot, elected council, quorum, binding share or purchased veto is selected. Ordinary discussion/reporting keeps its existing owners. A later binding decision requires actual delegated authority and a separate published funded scope within ND25/D37. Owner: PD10/D37; [voice](../projects/creator-support-and-contributor-voice-feature-spec.md#11-contributor-and-supporter-voice).

## GF-L10 — Reconsideration and scoped review

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** One reconsideration of an affected selection, acceptance, payment or exclusion decision, requested within fourteen real days. Acknowledge within seven days and decide within thirty, or state the actual unresolved outside payment/rights fact and next update. Use nonconflicted authority separated from the original final decision; a financial connection cannot be cured by disclosure alone. A challenge does not spend another signed award, hold unrelated earnings or create a world ban. Actual serious rights/fraud authority is separate from ordinary experimental failure. Owner: PD10/financial/rights owners; [conflicts](../projects/creator-support-and-contributor-voice-feature-spec.md#12-conflicts-disputes-and-proportionate-enforcement).

## GF-L11 — Reporting, retention and next cycle

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe for intake; necessary financial history has its own actual retention.** Publish agreed award details after signing, then the early result, final outcome and material changes; no weekly performance or sponsor-update requirement. Unsuccessful private applications ordinarily remain for ninety real days after final decision/reconsideration, then remove unnecessary material. Actual financial/rights exceptions and agreement/payment retention are selected by the real operator before launch. Uncertain and owed money remains tracked through actual resolution. No second cycle without the first cycle's financial/report closure, another appropriation, available people and a wanted brief. Owner: PD10/privacy/financial authority; [reporting](../projects/creator-support-and-contributor-voice-feature-spec.md#13-reporting-privacy-and-ending-the-program).

## GF-L12 — No automatic scale or software program

**Status: Open qualification · Restrictiveness: Too liberal if small-record limits are treated as total operating bounds.** Three proposals and twenty formal feedback submissions do not cap ordinary community discussion, disputes, publication/storage, retained financial records, dependency review or future maintenance. The actual operator must admit a cycle within available hours and financial capacity, and may omit the optional consultation. No automated scoring, standing council, donor-weight engine, indefinite application form or guaranteed recurring fund is selected. If a real later cohort needs software, design it around the established process and actual failure; do not build it as a condition for the first useful contribution. Owner: PD10/actual program owner; [economics](../projects/creator-support-and-contributor-voice-feature-spec.md#14-game-first-review-and-operating-economics).
