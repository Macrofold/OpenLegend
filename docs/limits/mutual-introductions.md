# Mutual introduction limits and constraints

All MI entries are **Proposed, unimplemented and unqualified**, reviewed 4 October 2026. They scope [Play together now](../projects/mutual-introductions-feature-spec.md), the selected DG10/ND31 offering. [MP20](../maintainers/multiplayer.md#mp20--mutually-accepted-activity-introductions) owns implementation; CP/RC/PD retain their shared participation, audience and release limits.

## MI01 — Selected people and activity

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** One introduction between two already admitted adult humans currently participating in the same qualified world. Consume [CP01](community-participation.md#cp01--selected-public-offering-and-capacity): two embodied humans include any human host. Consume RC01's English-language adult offer. No additional body slot, auto-admission, third embodied facilitator, recurring group, family/minor mode, cross-world search, external venue or dating/therapy offering.

**Reason and boundary:** First prove that one voluntary activity is enjoyable and easy to leave. The supported activity set contains only actual qualified world content; an unavailable route or finite reward is not regenerated to produce a match. Empty results are honest and normal. The activity must not require remaining together merely to escape a social commitment; ordinary danger uses its actual owner.

**Qualification:** Two useful human roles, current access/control, public meeting place, no private location/history disclosure, finite content, ordinary Talk and independent exit. Larger audiences/activities need new measured qualification, not automatic expansion of this profile. No new cap on natural social interaction or how long humans may freely play together is selected.

## MI02 — Active offers, consent and time

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** At most one published offer, one pending introduction and one accepted active introduction per account; the flow does not permit these to accumulate into multiple simultaneous commitments. Sending a request withdraws the requester's published offer; pending participants accept no other request, and acceptance closes both public offers. Request/accept must concern the same actual people and material terms.

An offer is available for **30 real minutes** from deliberate publication/renewal; a pending request shares that expiry with no additional shorter countdown. Acceptance starts **30 real minutes** of coordination availability. Either participant may end sooner. End/expiry stops further coordination, not actual world play, speech, work or possessions. No minimum attendance, withdrawal fee, automatic reminder, no-show penalty, friendship quota, recurring appointment or introduction reward is selected.

**Reason and boundary:** Bound stale availability and administration while giving people time to read and act. Known departure/ineligibility closes pending coordination; an accepted outcome remains truthfully recoverable within its window after a brief disconnection and current permission checks. Refresh, reconnect, background presence, gameplay restore and late results do not renew consent. Material edits close pending requests. Decline prevents the same requester returning to that offer or its edits; a newly published offer is a new deliberate opportunity, subject to standing CP03 Block.

**Qualification:** Simultaneous requests, stale/duplicate acceptance, real-time expiry during game pause, changes while reading, no-show, disconnected accepted partner, grant/block race and restored gameplay. Exact thirty-minute values are qualification starting points, not research-derived optimums. Current game-clock work and CP01 admission/reconnect priority stay with their owners.

## MI03 — Published information and contact surface

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Explicit publication to eligible current participants only. A card contains the deliberately disclosed local character label and Human player attribution, supported activity, offered public meeting landmark, approximate duration and simple interaction preferences. Optional activity note: **240 Unicode characters**; show remaining space and refuse excess without silent truncation. No arbitrary file/image attachment, freeform remote request message, private-message channel, AI-written human speech, live partner tracking, personality score or private-history matching.

**Reason and boundary:** Give a person enough to choose the actual plan without creating a directory of everyone online or a second chat service. The first scene uses English text; publication does not reveal age evidence, account/alternate identities, private notes, NPC memories or support disclosures. Residents do not read private service cards. Ordinary spoken information retains its actual audience.

**Qualification:** Deliberate audience preview, unfamiliar character labels, current blocks, oversized/malformed notes, readable non-color labels, keyboard/focus behavior, offered-versus-certified content and nearby listeners. No private-channel guarantee follows from introduction or conversation membership. The 240-character note value is a proposed small-plan envelope, not a general speech limit.

## MI04 — Receipts, processing and cost

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium for selected retention; unselected technical growth budgets remain unqualified.** Native discovery, publication, requests, acceptance, ending and expiry make **zero additional model calls**. No automatic paid matching/retry, background resident recruiter, social-success scoring or human concierge is required. Ordinary admitted resident cognition and narration still count in the full session cost.

Participant-scoped operational receipts retain the disclosed plan, identities, true consent/outcome and relevant times for **seven real days** after terminal outcome. No ordinary transcript, psychological profile or location trail is added. Public cards disappear when unavailable. A deliberately submitted case retains its selected evidence under [CP03–CP04](community-participation.md); that does not justify preserving every expired card indefinitely. Expiry does not promise erasure of another person's prior observations or an existing case copy.

**Reason and boundary:** Support brief recovery/reporting without building a permanent social network history. Native operation does not mean zero storage or staff cost. Candidate work is scoped before preparing full cards. If an operation cannot be admitted, show unavailable before claiming send/acceptance and preserve the last real result; do not retry or expand the audience automatically.

**Unselected bounds requiring technical qualification:** repeated create/edit/request rate; total live and retained receipt bytes; all rows examined before permitted selection; concurrent and deferred delivery count/bytes/age; case support burden; total world simulation and ordinary cognition costs. Active counts, 240-character notes and seven-day age limits do not independently bound these dimensions. MP20 with PD/data/PS must select and measure the operating budget and overload behavior before the broader managed offer. Do not label this absence of a selected number an unlimited production policy.

**Qualification:** Honest empty result, repeated activity attempts, expiry cleanup, unavailable storage/delivery, recipient-private recovery, case extraction and retention, current Block after gameplay restore, stale completion, latency while ordinary simulation runs, model-disabled native flow and measured actual support time. Required identity/history/obligations in any independent resident cognition remain complete.
