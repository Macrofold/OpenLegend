## VG-L01 — Finite visual reach and field

**Current baseline / Proposed target; Medium.** The inspected base `a3d40b4d71e29d6504e9f3fcc21e76344a0a284e` uses 28 m foot-anchor range and three centreline sight heights. Retain **28 m** as the selected human/grazer/machine receptor range, but evaluate actual sample distance. World full/central fields, receptor availability and angular degradation are tabulated in [body profiles](../worlds/base/vision-and-gaze.md#body-and-receptor-profiles). Outside support gives no visual evidence; a centre outside range does not exclude an in-range limb/tool. Finite reach bounds local relevance and makes sightlines understandable. **Change** after ordinary encounter/readability evidence; **expand** for an authored long-range body only with spatial/temporal qualification. **Remove** only by selecting another bounded sensing law, never by giving high Perception unlimited radius. No play evidence establishes 28 m or the proposed fields as optimal.

## VG-L02 — Physical rig and optical shape envelope

**Proposed; Safe.** An installed body supports at most **8 visual receptors**, **8 yaw/pitch joints**, joint-chain depth **4**, and **128 optical samples** across its admitted body and attached visual assembly. Count actual samples before admission; exceeding the envelope refuses that unsupported representation, never silently drops the shield, mask or visible spear. Separately owned physical item entities remain separate target references, but count their attached contribution to the assembly's installed work envelope; splitting invented IDs cannot evade it. Person starting shape has 42 body patches and 10 facial landmarks; grazer 42; fixed machine six. The selected rig family has bounded yaw/pitch joints and box/sphere optical parts, not arbitrary skeletal physics. Qualify at least the 52-sample person with mask/helmet/cloak and two held items, including a shield and spear, before accepting this envelope. Caps keep each physical record and interval enclosure tractable; body/attachment variety will test their restrictiveness. **Expand** only after concrete anatomy/equipment fixtures and worst-case work evidence. **Change** the patch distribution when partial-cover/overhead examples expose inadequate fidelity; **remove** a count cap only with a replacement bound on installed physical work.

This is a finite quadrature approximation. It guarantees classified exposure for the modeled patches through supported temporal intervals; it cannot promise detection of every infinitely small visible fragment between patches. The selected person's missing top/bottom box samples are a known starting restriction; its head has both axial samples. Feature size gate 0.10°, full size 0.50°, body angular-size factor 0.01 rad and 0.01 m near-distance floor are world quality/numerical choices, not empirical optical limits. A misleading partial-cover case must change the authored physical representation and its qualification, not secretly substitute the renderer mesh.

## VG-L03 — Turning and independent locomotion

**Proposed; Medium.** Human body/head/eye maximum rates **180/240/480° per normal-speed second**, head yaw ±80°/pitch [−55°,65°], and eye yaw ±15°/pitch ±10° bound physical freedom. Other installed profiles supply their own rates and stops. Person forward/side/back travel factors **1/0.75/0.55** use the world’s 60°/120° motion-angle boundaries, pending the explicit C locomotion integration. The action owner refuses impossible composition or performs its already-authorized replacement semantics. Head-only looking has no extra effort cost. These restrictions preserve meaningful footing and body orientation while allowing conversational watching and retreat. **Change** after turn/readability and movement play evidence; **expand** for an authored neck/rig or locomotion family. **Remove** a limb constraint only by changing that physical body's policy, not camera control. Exact values remain unqualified.

Moving-goal/world-point tracking selects **30 orientation-control ticks per normal-speed second**, anchored at admission; current 60:1 clock means two simulation seconds per tick. Between ticks the actual trajectory is piecewise linear at admitted joint rates. Fixed relative goals do not require idle ticks. This bounds control responsiveness and supplies a certifiable actual path; it is not a 30 Hz rendering requirement or a substitute for swept sensing. Loss cannot refresh a hidden target. **Change** cadence after rapid-crossing/accelerated-clock fairness and native-work measurements; **expand/remove** only with a supported deterministic control law and equivalent temporal certification. No tracking latency or whole-world throughput has been measured.

## VG-L04 — Light/material family and illumination reach

**Proposed; Safe.** The initial family is direct sun, **9-ray** sky access, finite point/spot sources and scalar transmission. It excludes bounce, reflections/mirrors, refraction, diffraction, volumetric smoke and global dark adaptation. Unsupported authoring fails admission or declares the absent capability before play; it cannot emit guessed optical evidence. Base fire intensity **2.8**, reach **8 m**, origin **0.8 m** above its actual support and intrinsic luminous brightness **2.8** are selected display-continuity/gameplay values; emitter visibility retains ordinary sight reach. Sky/daylight formula, glass **0.85**, foliage **0.8/2.0 m⁻¹**, and profile light thresholds are canonical in the world policy. **Change** after dark-room, doorway silhouette and fire rescue qualification; **expand** a material/light family only with complete instantaneous and temporal semantics. **Remove** an approximation only with an implementable replacement. Nine sky rays can misrepresent very small roof openings; this is not a claim of photorealism.

## VG-L05 — Quantized detection and detail

**Proposed; Medium.** Detection uses bands at **0.10/0.25/0.45/0.70/0.85**, with rates **0/0.5/1/2/3** support units per normal-speed second and immediate acquisition at 0.85. Detail uses independent face gates and quality tiers, four appearance dimensions, 20-unit centre quantization and half-widths **220/160/80/30** on A's 0–1000 scale. Perception gain is **0.75+0.005P** after physical support. These choices permit deterministic constant-rate intervals and readable weak glances without equating body detection with identity. They do not own A's stored threshold/decay/grace or recognition limits; refer to the upstream A policy when integrating. **Change** if ordinary useful glances remain illegible or peripheral/dark observations become too informative. **Expand** appearance families with actual visible measurements and positive weights, not ID entropy. **Remove** quantization only with an exact supported integration/precision contract. No numerical quality tuning has player qualification.

Location bands select **5°** bearing cells with **3.536°** additional enclosing spread, **2 m** outward-rounded regions, and **0.5/0.1 m** centroid grids with explicit cluster-plus-rounding error. Ordinary detail thresholds are **0.25** coarse supported shape, **0.45** material/colour/item appearance and **0.70** visible counts/dimensions; dimension grid is **0.05 m**, with at least **0.025 m** rounding uncertainty. These bound communicated precision, not server geometry. **Change** after navigation, cover, rescue and equipment readability evidence; precise hidden anchors or contents are not an authorized replacement.

## VG-L06 — Work continuation, not semantic truncation

**Proposed; Safe per continuation; no total contributor cap.** Select **256 primitive-work units per continuation**. A candidate/index visit, one patch/material primitive intersection, one emitter-to-patch transmission segment evaluation, or one certified interval/root-refinement operation spends one unit; multi-primitive work charges its inner operations rather than hiding them in one visit. Exhaustion saves its cursor and captured dependency revisions and yields through the existing native work owner. Deterministic ordering and complete physical preparation precede evidence publication. No nearest-N candidate, light, observer or target policy may fabricate absence or darkness. **Change** the work quantum after measured latency/throughput; **expand** batching only while interruption fairness remains qualified; **remove** a primitive counter only with equivalent bounded work accounting. This is not a wall-time guarantee or a promised whole-scene frame budget.

Candidate population, overlapping emitter count, spatial index size, interval split count and pending dirty observers have **no independent total cap in this selected policy**. Their **restrictiveness is Too liberal pending qualification**: spawning bodies/lights, dense building and mass movement can grow them automatically, and daylight can dirty all eligible optical regions. Spatial relevance, swept bounds, shared region invalidation and resumable work reduce repeated work but do not bound total completion time or memory. The technical design must refuse unsupported/nonconvergent physical preparation at its declared family envelope and hold the last coherent boundary rather than repeatedly retrying forever or inventing no-sight results. Implementation must measure queue depth/age, retained continuation bytes, primitive work and total completion latency before claiming dense-scene support. **Change/expand** through native owner admission and measured envelopes; no lossy nearest-N repair is preauthorized.

## VG-L07 — Cache and renderer selection

**Current baseline / Proposed semantic boundary; Medium.** Inspected vision cache capacities are **128 observers / 512 targets**; these are eviction/recomputation choices, not the sensing audience. Retain those cache capacities initially unless implementation selects and inventories a measured replacement. Cache validity includes receiver/target trajectories, pose/gaze, light/material/geometry revisions and definition pins. Cache misses recompute; eviction cannot remove knowledge or grant an old sightline. No cache is an authority. **Change** capacities after hit-rate/memory evidence; **remove** a cache when the indexed computation is cheaper, without changing physical truth.

The current renderer selects nearest **2 or 8** fire lights depending on quality. These remain display-work limits only. All physical contributors still enter the world light result; G must project permitted aggregate appearance so a rendering cap does not contradict darkness/illumination affordances. This inventory does not re-own G's camera privacy or presentation budget. **Change/remove** the display selection through G after visual qualification, never by applying it to authoritative detection.

## VG-L08 — Time and persistence support boundaries

**Current baseline / Proposed removal of relevant approximation; Safe.** Current sensing has an experimental **4 m** displacement bound, **60 simulation-second** fallback horizon, sampled non-move approaches/falls and **0.4 m** wander impulses; some old swept sight paths discard intervals below **10⁻⁹ simulation seconds**. These facts do not certify narrow-gap sensing. Selected B preparation must use admitted actual trajectories, preserve every supported positive interval or return explicit unresolved preparation, and retain canonical trajectory/definition/dependency state across save/restore. A saved clock split cannot alter exposure. The technical design owns the numerical solver's finite installation/refinement envelope and exact boundary ordering; it must inventory any retained temporal tolerance as a visible fidelity choice rather than calling it mathematically absent.

The new control-tick phase and turn anchor are preserved through pause, acceleration, interruption and restoration; recomputing them from publication time is unsupported. Stationary fixed-goal turns use endpoint/limit boundaries only. VG-L03 owns the cadence number and revisit decision.

The selected certified solver allows subdivision depth **64** and **16,384 examined cells per observer–target/canonical physical piece**, anchored to a **one normal-speed-second epoch grid** as detailed in technical §9.4. This is an explicit finite refinement envelope, separate from the 256-work continuation slice. Exhaustion produces a scoped technical failure/hold at the last coherent boundary; it is never a nearest-N omission, an absent sight result or a surprise grant. **Restrictiveness: Safe, unqualified. Change** after near-tangency, narrow-gap, many-light and high-rate turning evidence; **expand** only with measured completion/memory cost and deterministic preservation; **remove** only for a replacement family that proves complete finite preparation. The epoch grid is an anchored solver partition, not a physical blind interval or a mandatory world update rate.

Use canonical adjacent-representable-time root brackets. An exactly representable root retains that time; otherwise select the first representable instant certified on the post-crossing side, for **both onset and loss**. Adjacent runs tile at that same chosen boundary and A integrates the resulting modeled duration. This explicit numerical fidelity convention shifts an isolated resolved root by less than one representable time step; it does not claim exact real-valued root arithmetic. If two crossings enclosing a positive interval collapse to the same representable boundary, or their order cannot be certified, preparation holds/fails explicitly instead of deleting the interval. Retire the old `10⁻⁹`-simulation-second duration cutoff for this family. **Change** the representation/convention if large clocks or supported geometry cannot preserve required event order; reducing graphics quality cannot authorize a temporal deletion.

**Remove** the relevant wander/approach approximation only with those owners' actual trajectory changes; do not retrospectively invent travel through a teleport. **Change** supported families when new turning, animation, moving geometry or illumination cannot be certified. **Expand** only with partition/restore and pre-outcome qualification. The wider world's travel fidelity and normal clock remain owned by [time](../worlds/base/time.md), not duplicated or globally reduced here. No executable qualification was run in this design-only assignment.
