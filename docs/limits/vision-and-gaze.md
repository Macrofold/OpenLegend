## VG-L01 — Finite visual reach and field

**Current baseline / Proposed target; Medium.** The inspected base `a3d40b4d71e29d6504e9f3fcc21e76344a0a284e` uses 28 m foot-anchor range and three centreline sight heights; its current validator permits radius at most **32 m**. Retain **28 m** as the selected human/grazer/hare/bird/machine receptor range, but evaluate actual sample distance. World full/central fields, receptor availability and angular degradation are tabulated in [body profiles](../worlds/base/vision-and-gaze.md#body-and-receptor-profiles). Outside support gives no visual evidence; a centre outside range does not exclude an in-range limb/tool. Finite reach bounds local relevance and makes sightlines understandable. **Change** after ordinary encounter/readability evidence; **expand** beyond the current validator only with an explicit source/validation change and spatial/temporal qualification, not a prose-only authored radius. **Remove** only by selecting another bounded sensing law, never by giving high Perception unlimited radius. No play evidence establishes 28 m or the proposed fields as optimal.

## VG-L02 — Physical rig and optical shape envelope

**Proposed; Safe.** An installed body supports at most **8 visual receptors**, **8 yaw/pitch joints**, joint-chain depth **4**, and **128 optical samples** across its admitted body and attached visual assembly. Count actual samples before admission; exceeding the envelope refuses that unsupported representation, never silently drops the shield, mask or visible spear. Separately owned physical item entities remain separate target references, but count their attached contribution to the assembly's installed work envelope; splitting invented IDs cannot evade it. Person starting shape has 42 body patches and 10 facial landmarks; grazer 42; hare 26; bird 32; fixed machine/default object/inactive box six. The selected rig family has bounded yaw/pitch joints and box/sphere optical parts, not arbitrary skeletal physics. The inactive fixture has no enabled receiver or face landmarks; that restriction needs an explicitly authored pose to expand, not an old-face lookup. Qualify at least the 52-sample person with mask/helmet/cloak and two held items, including a shield and spear, before accepting this envelope. Caps keep each physical record and finite sample evaluation tractable; body/attachment variety will test their restrictiveness. **Expand** only after concrete anatomy/equipment fixtures and worst-case work evidence. **Change** the patch distribution when partial-cover/overhead examples expose inadequate fidelity; **remove** a count cap only with a replacement bound on installed physical work.

The selected box/sphere quadrature derives at most **12 same-surface adjacency anchors per primitive**, and at most **twice the ordinary quadrature sample count** across an assembly. These zero-area auxiliary probes do not consume the 128 authored sample slots or add visual evidence; their actual ray, light and temporal work still uses VG-L06's allowance. Compile the finite topology once and evaluate only relevant witness handoffs. **Restrictiveness: Safe, unqualified. Expand** only with a supported shape/adjacency family and measured work; never infer a connection between separate parts from native parent identity.

This is a finite quadrature approximation. It evaluates modeled patches at the canonical samples and holds the declared provider support; it cannot promise detection of every infinitely small visible fragment between patches. The selected person's missing top/bottom box samples are a known starting restriction; its head has both axial samples. Feature size gate 0.10°, full size 0.50°, body angular-size factor 0.01 rad and 0.01 m near-distance floor are world quality/numerical choices, not empirical optical limits. A misleading partial-cover case must change the authored physical representation and its qualification, not secretly substitute the renderer mesh.

## VG-L03 — Turning and independent locomotion

**Proposed; Medium.** Human body/head/eye maximum rates **180/240/480° per normal-speed second**, head yaw ±80°/pitch [−55°,65°], and eye yaw ±15°/pitch ±10° bound physical freedom. Other installed profiles supply their own rates and stops. Person forward/side/back travel factors **1/0.75/0.55** use the world’s 60°/120° motion-angle boundaries, composed with C’s quiet/posture caps by their minimum against the same ordinary rate. The action owner refuses impossible composition or performs its already-authorized replacement semantics. Head-only looking has no extra effort cost. These restrictions preserve meaningful footing and body orientation while allowing conversational watching and retreat. **Change** after turn/readability and movement play evidence; **expand** for an authored neck/rig or locomotion family. **Remove** a limb constraint only by changing that physical body's policy, not camera control. Exact values remain unqualified.

Moving-goal/world-point tracking selects **30 control ticks per normal-speed second**, anchored at directive admission; the current clock gives two simulation seconds per tick. Actual turns remain rate-limited between ticks. The control reads the latest already received target estimate, without taking an extra sight sample or fetching hidden motion. Fixed relative goals need no idle ticks. Reached checks occur at control ticks, actual segment completion and deadline, with the selected .5° tolerance. **Restrictiveness: Medium, unqualified. Change** cadence/tolerance after responsiveness and native-work evidence; preserve saved original phase and the independent visual sampling law.

World-point/observed-point alignment selects **0.5°** reached tolerance and a default **2 normal-speed-second** orient-once pursuit deadline; an explicitly earlier admitted deadline takes precedence. These bound completion feedback and futile pursuit, not sensing accuracy or real pose. Fixed relative-angle goals still complete their actual admitted angles. **Restrictiveness: Medium, unqualified. Change** after close-point, moving-companion and control-responsiveness evidence; **remove** only with a terminating supported aim policy. Maintained E-owned work retains its own finite extent.

## VG-L04 — Light/material family and illumination reach

**Proposed; Safe.** The initial family is direct sun, **9-ray** sky access, finite point/spot sources and scalar transmission. It excludes bounce, reflections/mirrors, refraction, diffraction, volumetric smoke and global dark adaptation. Unsupported authoring fails admission or declares the absent capability before play; it cannot emit guessed optical evidence. Base fire intensity **2.8**, reach **8 m**, origin **0.8 m** above its actual support and intrinsic luminous brightness **2.8** are selected display-continuity/gameplay values; emitter visibility retains ordinary sight reach. Sky/daylight formula, glass **0.85**, foliage **0.8/2.0 m⁻¹**, and profile light thresholds are canonical in the world policy. **Change** after dark-room, doorway silhouette and fire rescue qualification; **expand** a material/light family only with complete instantaneous and temporal semantics. **Remove** an approximation only with an implementable replacement. Nine sky rays can misrepresent very small roof openings; this is not a claim of photorealism.

The finite optical-domain AABB is an explicit sky-boundary convention containing every admitted optical primitive; upward exit is sky, horizontal/downward exit is not. It has no selected universal metre size: the installed world's admitted domain owns the finite bounds and expands them before out-of-domain admission. Unknown required geometry fails preparation rather than becoming clear sky. Domain volume and repeated expansion have no independent policy cap here; native spatial ownership and its limits must qualify reachable growth. **Change** the boundary convention only with source/solver and world-authoring reconciliation; no render-distance substitute is authorized.

The ground flame uses a **0.12 m-radius** non-occluding emissive sphere with **six** fixed surface samples, counted in the existing assembly envelope. Its finite shell contributes background radiance once along a traversing ray; an emitter's influence volume is not a visible surface. **Restrictiveness: Medium, unqualified. Change** shape/sample distribution after flame-through-gap and silhouette qualification; **expand** emission families only with complete geometry, transmission and temporal rules. This small additive-shell model does not install volumetric flame or particle simulation.

## VG-L05 — Quantized detection and detail

**Proposed; Medium.** Detection uses bands at **0.10/0.25/0.45/0.70/0.85**, with rates **0/0.5/1/2/3** support units per normal-speed second and immediate acquisition at 0.85. Detail uses independent face gates and quality tiers, four appearance dimensions, 20-unit centre quantization and half-widths **220/160/80/30** on A's 0–1000 scale. Perception gain is **0.75+0.5p**, where `p=perceptionValue/100` after physical support. These choices permit deterministic constant-rate intervals and readable weak glances without equating body detection with identity. They do not own A's stored threshold/decay/grace or recognition limits; refer to the upstream A policy when integrating. **Change** if ordinary useful glances remain illegible or peripheral/dark observations become too informative. **Expand** appearance families with actual visible measurements and positive weights, not ID entropy. **Remove** quantization only with an exact supported integration/precision contract. No numerical quality tuning has player qualification.

Location uses only positive-projected-area patches passing local received-signal threshold **0.10**, never geometrically clear dark extents. Bands select **5°** bearing cells with **3.536°** additional enclosing spread rounded upward to **5°**, **2 m** outward-rounded regions, and **0.5/0.1 m** centroid grids with cluster-plus-rounding error radii rounded upward on the same respective grid. Ordinary detail thresholds are **0.25** coarse supported shape, **0.45** material/colour/marking appearance and **0.70** refined recognizable item form/visible counts/dimensions; dimension grid is **0.05 m**, with at least **0.025 m** rounding uncertainty. These bound communicated precision, not server geometry. **Change** after navigation, cover, rescue and equipment readability evidence; precise hidden anchors or contents are not an authorized replacement.

New ordinary person appearance selects four independent existing-world-RNG draws from **51** allowed levels per physical ratio, with no uniqueness retries. Four dimensions are a small supported recognizability family, not biometric identification. Identical/similar profiles remain permitted. **Restrictiveness: Medium. Change/expand** after ambiguity and presentation qualification; any added dimension must correspond to actual physically supported appearance. Removing collisions through hidden identity entropy is not an authorized fix.

Physical continuity uses a uniquely separable received patch at the existing location precision; all overlapping rival patch cells are conservatively compatible. Supported adjacent samples of the same convex surface can hand off a witness at detection quality through a received boundary anchor; held/contact relations retain their higher detail requirement. Across adjacent canonical measurements, conservative received/swept correspondence withholds ambiguous tracking without making a clear turn depend on one permanent patch or hidden source equality. It does not prove uninterrupted subcell visibility. **Restrictiveness: Medium, unqualified. Change** after crossing-figure, crowded-work and ordinary held-tool qualification; **expand** only with another supported physical correspondence proof, retaining A's sole ownership of correlation and recognition. It has no new saved tracking state or additional numerical precision policy.

## VG-L06 — Work continuation, not semantic truncation

**Proposed; Safe per continuation; no total contributor cap.** Select **256 primitive-work units per continuation**. A candidate/index visit, one patch/material primitive intersection, one emitter-to-patch transmission segment evaluation, or one admitted measurement or correspondence operation spends one unit; multi-primitive work charges its inner operations rather than hiding them in one visit. Exhaustion saves its cursor and captured dependency revisions and yields through the existing native work owner. Deterministic ordering and complete physical preparation precede evidence publication. No nearest-N candidate, light, observer or target policy may fabricate absence or darkness. **Change** the work quantum after measured latency/throughput; **expand** batching only while interruption fairness remains qualified; **remove** a primitive counter only with equivalent bounded work accounting. This is not a wall-time guarantee or a promised whole-scene frame budget.

Candidate population, overlapping emitter count, spatial index size and pending dirty scopes have no independent total cap. **Restrictiveness: Too liberal pending qualification:** dense visible crowds and many lights can grow real work, while daylight can affect many regions once per environment boundary. Local swept relevance, shared invalidation and resumable batches reduce repetition but do not guarantee total completion latency. Measure queue age, retained continuation bytes, primitive work and throughput. Required incomplete work yields coherently; only invalid/unsupported installed physical data fails admission. No nearest-N omission or numerical near-tangency stall is selected.

## VG-L07 — Cache and renderer selection

**Current baseline / Proposed semantic boundary; Medium.** Inspected vision cache capacities are **128 observers / 512 targets**; these are eviction/recomputation choices, not the sensing audience. Retain those cache capacities initially unless implementation selects and inventories a measured replacement. Cache validity includes receiver/target trajectories, pose/gaze, light/material/geometry revisions and definition pins. Cache misses recompute; eviction cannot remove knowledge or grant an old sightline. No cache is an authority. **Change** capacities after hit-rate/memory evidence; **remove** a cache when the indexed computation is cheaper, without changing physical truth.

The current renderer selects nearest **2 or 8** fire lights depending on quality. These remain display-work limits only. All physical contributors still enter the world light result; G must project permitted aggregate appearance so a rendering cap does not contradict darkness/illumination affordances. This inventory does not re-own G's camera privacy or presentation budget. **Change/remove** the display selection through G after visual qualification, never by applying it to authoritative detection.

## VG-L08 — Time and persistence support boundaries

**Proposed; Medium, unqualified.** Changing local visual scopes use **0.05 normal-speed-second** cells on the world-epoch grid, converted once (3 simulation seconds at 60:1). Only affected active scopes schedule them through the existing native motion/sensory continuation; static dependency-complete scopes reuse their sample. The chosen law holds the measured support band/facet availability until the next due sample or earlier actual invalidation. Values retain their original measurement and acquisition times. No render, network, memory-credit or evidence-cut read creates another sample.

Actual physical impulses and contacts retain exact native times and causal order. Existing supported analytic sweeps can add a deterministic interior measurement for a narrow geometric opening/gap. Other optical flashes/gaps shorter than a cell may be missed or held through, and repeated subcell changes can alias. This is a declared finite fidelity choice, not complete continuous optical coverage or a bound on all accumulated error. **Change** cadence after useful-glance, fast-turn, moving-light, projectile-warning and whole-caller work evidence. **Remove** it only for another explicit finite physical law. No certified transcendental root solver, recursive refinement envelope or adjacent-float crossing requirement is selected.

World daylight direction and direct/diffuse coefficients use a separate **one-normal-speed-second epoch sample**, shared once by the environment owner. Its held coefficient fidelity avoids a 20 Hz stationary-world scan; moving local light and real on/off changes use their existing due/native boundaries. **Restrictiveness: Medium, unqualified. Change** after dawn/shadow/readability and workload evidence; preserve consistent native/G clock meaning.

Persist original visual epoch/cell ordinal, separate gaze-control phase, motion/turn anchors, definition pins, last consumed through-time, accepted held values and unfinished required-work cursor through their existing owners. Speed, host partition, pause and restoration cannot rephase sampling, regain weak/learning credit or add current measurements. A cut captures already acquired evidence; incoming contact cannot create its own warning.

The selected [shared temporal receipt target](../projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md#shared-temporal-source-and-receipt-target) reuses those actual samples for admitted attended/required history; it adds neither another sight cadence nor an unattended background-history sampler. Shared source storage does not erase differences in received precision, frame, clipped terrain or acquisition coverage. Earlier detail is available only through that observer's retained receipts, never by replaying another observer's source history. Its storage/work bounds remain with PS04-A and PP; VG's physical fidelity and full qualification remain unchanged and open.

The inspected baseline’s **4 m** travel bound, **60 simulation-second** fallback horizon, sampled approaches/falls and **.4 m** wander impulses do not implement this cadence. Install genuine movement segments where intermediate observation/contact is promised; do not retrospectively interpolate a teleport. Existing analytic sweeps retain their own supported finite geometry behavior and cannot be represented as a full-light temporal proof. The broader world travel/clock policy remains with [time](../worlds/base/time.md). Runtime, performance and player qualification are open.
