# Food preparation and finite cast constraints

## AV-L03 — Finite river casts and preparation

**Current, October 7, 2026; restrictiveness: Medium.** This entry moved from the batch 05 proposal inventory when AV05 shipped. The [river profile](../worlds/base/river-fishing.md) owns initial local stocks, cast time/chance, the reusable tool and food values. Those are authored content choices, not maximum world counts. One chosen cast attempts one catch without automatic retry; success removes one finite supply unit and creates one item. Initial stocks do not replenish. This supports a peaceful finite food activity without claiming a breeding population or indefinite food. Reproduction, boats, bait, rod repair and spoilage remain excluded; a selected later gameplay consumer triggers reconsideration.

Only explicitly supported water footprints/stances permit casting. Invalid preparation, interruption and exhaustion refuse or end the attempt without a draw or stock debit. A lawful resolution draws once; an empty attempt spends time without stock consumption. Public/NPC reads hide exact remaining catch quantity and future outcomes while describing ordinary chance/time and observed exhaustion. The restriction preserves honest observation rather than simplifying choice through hidden data.

Preparation supports installed finite input/output/work definitions and **lit heat only**. Each named role binds **one exact accessible lot**; a role with insufficient free quantity is unavailable even when several smaller lots could sum to enough. Different named roles and distinct outputs are supported; same-definition outputs are consolidated. This keeps real quantities and identity explicit through commands, work and reusable methods. It retains start-time ingredient consumption and no refund on cancellation/heat loss. These chosen boundaries replace the former universal meat pair. Additional useful heat modes or multi-lot ingredient selection should extend this owner when an authored recipe actually needs them; item-name switches and arbitrary generated effects remain unsupported.

Preparation definitions retain the existing [ST04 atomic limit of 256 ingredient operations](state-effects.md#st04), rather than accepting a definition that its execution owner can never commit. Exact assignment rejects shortages, groups independent materials and skips interchangeable remaining quantities before retrying shared-material roles; a selected lot remains distinct. The input-role limit bounds recursion depth; the inherited work meter bounds attempted assignments and charges any enclosing allowance. It does not establish a new recipe-count limit. The inherited action-text bound is 500 characters per definition text, and the inherited action-output bound is 16 distinct products. Container products must fit the existing one-container-per-lot source rule. Structured ingredient groups use the record owner’s 16-entry detail bound without making ingredient roles into display labels. The complete prospective record must still fit the existing storage/display envelopes: a 48-role meal and 16-product meal passed, while the 256-role diagnostic refused before costs. These are qualified specimens, not a promise that every combination of the maxima fits. Expand representation/allowances only for a useful rejected consumer under the [record owner](action-experience.md#ael07).

Sources: [`food-preparation.ts`](../../packages/domain/src/food-preparation.ts), [`finite-casts.ts`](../../packages/domain/src/finite-casts.ts), the existing kernel work/item owners and [current contract](../food-preparation.md). [Native, SQL and browser evidence](../verification/river-meal.md) qualifies the selected two-source journey, interruption/competition and a different multi-input/multi-output preparation. It does not measure optimal tuning, sustained survival or large-world capacity.

## FP-L01 — Choice preparation and retained growth

**No new total-count cap, October 7, 2026; restrictiveness: Too liberal at unqualified authored scale.** Authoring can increase installed preparations and cast sources; crafting can increase permitted lots and compatible tools. A cooking menu or rebuilt view indexes permitted inventory and assigns exact ingredients once per unchanged read, then binds commands across perceived fires. NPC choices sort eligible fires once. Fishing indexes compatible tools once, then expands only over perceived cast sources and matching tools. Public projection caches reuse unchanged permitted inventory/definitions and invalidate through the existing possession revision owner. Gathering shortcuts group only supplied native Gather offers and choose nearest usable sources once; they neither read hidden stock nor scan/sort every resource type separately. There is no all-world source scan per tick and no new source, recipe, memory or action-history retention ceiling.

The available definition/source/tool combinations and their complete returned choices have no independent count bound here. Unequal lot/role quantities can still branch combinatorially, so cooking choice reads install the existing group work meter even outside a native transition; each attempted lot assignment is charged. Existing actor scope, enclosing work charging, action/request envelopes and storage admission remain; required work exceeding its allowance fails rather than presenting an incomplete set as complete. Those controls are owned by [native work](native-work.md), [action experience](action-experience.md), [cognition](cognition.md), [objects](objects.md), [inventions](inventions.md) and [persistence](persistence.md). They do not certify population throughput. The two authored reaches, ordinary inventories, inherited work refusal and the [review's impossible split-stack/multiple-fire diagnostics](../verification/river-meal.md#requested-implementation-review) are qualified; sustained large authored collections and many simultaneous actors remain unmeasured under the existing [performance tracker](../maintainers/performance.md). A real larger-content consumer or measured preparation bottleneck triggers bounded/paged preparation through the same owner; remaining atomic cooking-preview preflight and complete-path measurement stay with AC11/PF05.
