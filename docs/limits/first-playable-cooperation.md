# First-playable cooperation — limits and constraints

The [people profile](../worlds/base/first-playable-people.md) and [technical design](../projects/first-playable-cooperation-tech-design.md) select these first-playable policies. **Runtime implemented; combined technical, independent-behavior and player qualification remain open in [CP](../maintainers/first-playable-cooperation.md).** Existing social/gift/travel limits remain in [base-world limits](base-world.md). Exact identity, current permission, once-only effects and private evidence are integrity requirements, not discretionary restrictions to relax.

## CPL01 — Four authored initially non-hostile residents

**Selected:** Ada, Peacock Mercenary, Mara and Tavin. Kest/Orin remain separate authored opposition. Four is scenario content, not an engine population bound. **Reason:** enough different company to make choice and later consequence meaningful while reusing established identities and useful starting content. **Restrictiveness:** appropriate first-playable scope, unmeasured. **Change trigger:** actual player experience identifies a missing distinct role that an existing person's opportunities cannot provide; no filler fifth resident is required. CP01/CP10 retain implementation and independent-behavior acceptance.

## CPL02 — Pending offers and finite deadlines

**Selected:** one shared first-playable pending-offer lifetime of 7,200 game seconds across gift/barter/outings/help/alliance/ceasefire; help/guide/hunt duration 14,400 game seconds from acceptance; ceasefire duration 7,200 game seconds from acceptance. Existing world clock remains 60 game seconds per normal real second. **Changed prior choice:** pending default grows from 1,800 seconds because thirty real seconds can be consumed by reading, a model response and reviewing terms. **Reason:** provide practical response time and bound stale consent without a secret pause. **Restrictiveness:** provisional; neither duration is established by player measurement. Actual deadline/speed consequences are visible, and renewal requires fresh assent. CP02/CP10 test reading, independent replies, expiry and faster speed. Do not duplicate this constant in each family or reset deadlines during restore.

## CPL03 — Physical episodes and pairwise coordination

**Selected:** exactly two participants per agreement and one accepted purposeful physical cooperation episode per actor. Alliances and ceasefires do not occupy that physical-work slot. Multiple independent pairs can act around the same quarry; a pair does not reserve an animal or stop outsiders acting. **Reason:** deliver useful consent and separate agency with existing outing/plan ownership, avoiding a speculative group scheduler. **Restrictiveness:** deliberately restrictive; general group roles/joint lifting are later extensions, not a world population cap. **Trigger:** a verified first-playable need that pairwise cooperation plus ordinary independently chosen work cannot satisfy. Expansion must settle readiness, membership changes, work ownership and full individual assent before increasing a number.

## CPL04 — Admission and inbox growth

**Selected:** at most three outgoing and six incoming pending social offers per actor, counted across participating families by shared admission; at most one live agreement of a given kind per exact pair. **Reason:** a sender cap alone does not bound one recipient's preparation. Refuse before record creation or model dispatch, without revealing other relationships. No queue or silent eviction. **Restrictiveness:** provisional; the small cast can ordinarily receive one offer from each other person. **Growth:** accepted ally relationships have no independent total lifetime count cap; at most one current pair record per kind, participant/pair indexes and paged inspection bound ordinary reads. CP09 measures concentrated senders and large existing acquaintance sets. This is not an authorization to discard protected historical evidence.

## CPL05 — Local reception and personal knowledge

**Selected:** proposals and acceptance require mutually perceived participants at the installed speaking reach. No fixed extra metres value is copied from the hearing/interaction owner. No remote messages, shared map, unseen ally tracker or automatic global reputation. Withdrawal may occur out of range, but another person learns only through actual permitted communication/perception or a scoped native attempt refusal. **Reason:** preserve perspective and avoid inventing telepathy. **Restrictiveness:** intentionally local; a later supported remote channel may carry the same typed terms under its own audience contract. Speech privacy and identity remain [SPK](../maintainers/speech-and-social-perception.md) owners.

## CPL06 — Exact help and actual yield

**Selected:** prepare one known recipe/quantity, carry one exact offered object to a permitted destination, or perform one exact camp phase; hunting names one exact quarry life observed when proposed. That received reference remains usable after ordinary sight loss during a reply, with stale location clearly labeled; it grants no current tracking or attack. No arbitrary executable request, work-speed buff, compulsory role or automatically allocated future yield. Actual harvesting and gifts/trade remain their owners. **Reason:** small complete actions with understandable resources and outcomes, without requiring a moving animal to wait for reading/model latency. **Restrictiveness:** appropriate first slice; future delivery, lending, escrow and joint physical lifting remain separate designs. No limit is placed on ordinary speech about plans; words alone do not add native effects. CP04–CP06 qualify every supported kind and meaningful failure.

## CPL07 — Social compacts without hidden powers

**Selected:** no affinity score, required friendliness threshold, automatic relationship reward or granted property/body control. An alliance has no automatic expiry while participants remain valid; it ends by actual withdrawal or its lifecycle owner. Ceasefire supplies an exact cancellation choice and continuing intent, never invulnerability. No restraint/capture/compelled submission family is supplied. **Reason:** keep personal judgment and consequence independent. **Restrictiveness:** conscious behavior boundaries, not incomplete tuning; new coercive or institutional mechanics need their own design and authority review. CP07/CP10 measure understandable consequences, not willingness forced by native rules.

## CPL08 — Retained history, preparation and operating work

**No independent total historical event/receipt byte cap is introduced here.** Repeated supported interactions can grow canonical history, personal evidence and existing receipts; their retention/privacy/storage owners remain controlling. Completed physical episodes leave the live collection after truthful source-linked recording. No second append-only cooperation diary or permanent completed-party list is added. Live compacts can grow with real actor pairs; participant/pair indexes and paged inspection prevent whole-world scans, but do not bound total world storage.

Optional candidate/output limits never establish bounded work by themselves. Use supplied exact IDs, relevant participant indexes and current deadlines before hydration; share request-scoped preparation and refuse incomplete required preparation under existing admission. There is no new all-resident polling loop, model call per step, provider retry rule or invitation queue. CP09 measures cold/warm commands, clustered invitations, live relationship counts and accumulated history through actual server/UI callers. Scale and enjoyment remain unverified until that evidence exists.
