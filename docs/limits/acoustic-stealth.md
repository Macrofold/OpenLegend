# Physical sound and acoustic stealth — limits and constraints

**Proposed — selected design, not implemented or calibrated.** This inventory accompanies the [feature][feature], [technical design][tech], [base policy][policy] and [ACS00–ACS08 work][work]. Evidence uses the tracker's exact common/A source pins. No executable tests, runtime measurements or play/balance trials qualify these choices.

Values are fictional gameplay starting points. [Restrictiveness](README.md#restrictiveness) is not a capacity certification. Existing speech/cache/history/media bounds stay in [the hearing inventory](hearing-and-speech.md); A owns accumulation/recognition/retention [in its inventory][a-limits]. Exact provenance, no hidden-identity leakage and atomicity are correctness obligations, not tuning candidates.

## ACS-L01 — Direct-path scalar air

**Proposed reuse/extension · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** The selected provider uses broadband air SPL referenced to 20 µPa, direct three-dimensional paths, the existing **0.25 m** near-field clamp and multiplied material energy transmission. Reflections, diffraction/around-corner routes, frequency bands and travel-time delay are unsupported. Zero transmission gives blocked reception with absent level/margin, not infinity.

This bounds geometry but can miss plausible indirect sounds. Another medium/path law needs an installed provider and corresponding work/projection bounds; no fallback physics is invented. Revisit for a concrete failed listening journey. [World policy][policy], [HR01](hearing-and-speech.md#hr01--acoustic-model-and-tuning), ACS01/ACS07.

## ACS-L02 — Real impulses, intervals and supported producers

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** Two source envelopes are selected: one instantaneous occurrence, or an actual active half-open interval `[start,end)`. Independent impulses in one sealed physical phase use peak/coincidence comparison. A current utterance is one impulse; captions, tokens and playback supply no duration. A ringing tail requires an admitted interval.

Initial adapters cover supported movement/contact, handling, impact/landing, work/foliage and an active sustained-process example. Sprint, destruction, creature calls, machinery and recording/relay require their actual physical owner. Unsupported capabilities remain explicit; descriptive action prose emits nothing. This limits authoring while preserving cause. Expand through the same source/reception mechanism. [Feature][feature], [policy][policy], ACS01/ACS03.

## ACS-L03 — Admitted anatomy and deliberate movement

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** Human quiet pace caps existing effective speed at **0.25×**, crouch at **0.15×**, combined by the minimum. Posture transition takes **0.4 normal-speed seconds** of stationary ordinary work. Crouch retains radius/slope, uses **0.60×** living height/interaction height and **0.55×** standing eye/ear heights. Old geometry remains authoritative until completion; obstruction/interruption preserves it. Existing equipment compatibility still constrains admission; current ports have no geometric coordinates, so accurate long-item extension clearance is explicitly outside this slice. Preserve the `person` capability identity through numeric posture changes; committed evasion keeps its admitted speed/end and refuses pace/posture changes.

Animal crouch, prone movement, arbitrary limb articulation, new fatigue and sprint commands remain unsupported. Incompatible posture refuses through ordinary admission. The base conversion is **60 simulation seconds per normal-speed second**; 3×/8× does not multiply authored factors again. Slower travel/action time provides the tradeoff; compare its burden with ordinary travel or a farther shot before retuning. [Policy][policy], ACS02/ACS08.

## ACS-L04 — Contact-cycle envelope

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** A locomotion cycle admits at most **eight ordered contact offsets in (0,1]**; simultaneous offsets form one physical group. Oversized definitions require explicitly grouped actual contacts or another supported profile, never silent contact loss. Initial species use one group per cycle with distinct distances/levels.

This bounds one definition, not contacts per interval, emitters or audience. All contacts crossed by actual admitted travel remain owed under required-work accounting. Zero displacement emits none; command or host-interval boundaries do not reset phase or partial-contact peak/all-quiet fields. The fastest accepted piece governs the next contact, and the −4 dB technique adjustment requires all of that contact’s travel to be quiet; this conservative approximation prevents toggle-induced silence. Revisit the eight-offset choice when a concrete anatomy cannot be represented faithfully. [Policy][policy], ACS02/ACS07.

## ACS-L05 — Emission strengths and carried equipment

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** The canonical world tables select body reference levels **24–54 dB SPL at 1 m**, material adjustments **−6…+10 dB**, equipment examples **32–64 dB** and actual impacts **52–72 dB**. Foliage uses its own body/contact coupling (**−14…0 dB**), independent of foot-impact levels. The positive unmodified reference speed is pinned; the actual movement speed/reference ratio floors at **0.10**; deliberate foot contact adds **−4 dB**, without automatically quieting gear or adding another crouch bonus. Landing/fall sound is clamped to **24–96 dB**. Handling, foliage, work and fire values remain in the [policy][policy].

These choices are unmeasured. Components sum in linear energy through actual quantity/custody/transmission. No acoustic item-count cap is selected; a private bag is not automatically soundproof. Prepare carrier contributions on meaningful changes, not every footstep; ACS-L13 covers that growth. Quieter authored variants do not create missing fastening commands. Revisit after load/route play comparisons. ACS02/ACS03/ACS08.

## ACS-L06 — Finite admission and hearing opportunities

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** Physical numbers must be finite; transmission/fidelity are **[0,1]**. Keep existing speech scalar bounds under HR01. Do not silently clip a combined emission or invent another numeric maximum; invalid definitions/computations follow their explicit refusal or required-work failure contract.

Selected floors are **0 dB SPL**. Strong-detection margins are **0** human, **−3** hare/wolf, **−2** deer and **−1** bird/bear. For `delta = receivedMargin − detectorThreshold`, **delta ≥ 0** gives immediate strong reception. Discrete impulses in **−6 ≤ delta < 0** use the selected linear weight. True intervals deliberately use constant rates **1/2/3/4 support units per normal-speed second** over delta bands **[−6,−4.5), [−4.5,−3), [−3,−1.5), [−1.5,0)**; below −6 is a zero-rate gap. This authored duration law avoids numerical quadrature, rather than claiming to approximate the impulse law. Conservative discovery includes the weak boundary. A owns integration/decay; Perception does not independently alter these thresholds. Ambient remains **28 dB**, human word thresholds **6/12 dB**. These discrimination choices need joint detection/alarm/voice qualification, not physiological claims. [Policy][policy], ACS01/ACS04/ACS08.

## ACS-L07 — Received features and pattern precision

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** The physical family has four **0–1000** dimensions: impulsiveness, roughness, ringing and voicing. Voice uses A's separate four dimensions. Fixed-bin received intervals have widths **125/250/500** at quality thresholds **.85/.60/.35**; below .35 omit distinguishing features. Path/source loss removes dimensions; distinct sources may share features.

The selected classifier uses received interval bounds, with **500** bounds for coarse contact/rustle and **600** for voice/ringing detail; voice identity retains its separate stricter precision. A rhythmic-contact hypothesis needs **three acquired compatible contacts**, gaps at most **.5 normal-seconds** and maximum/minimum gap ratio at most **1.2**. Ambiguity remains indistinct or multiple-candidate evidence; no exact source feature or true action resolves it. This deliberately loses detail and permits a mechanism to sound footstep-like. Revisit after useful-uncertainty/recognition trials with A retaining interpretation ownership. [Policy][policy], ACS04/ACS08.

## ACS-L08 — Coarse direction and absent range

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** Sound supplies uncertain bearing or no location, never exact point/range or a current attackable body. Horizontal direction has **eight world-axis sectors**. Half-spread is **90°/45°/22.5°** for margins below 6, 6–12 and at least 12 dB; attenuation requires at least 45°, path fidelity below .60 at least 90°. A broad mixture can have no bearing rather than a falsely precise average.

Horizontal direction is absent below separation `max(.25 m, abs(verticalSeparation))`. For point occurrences, above/below needs margin at least **12 dB** and elevation at least **15°**; otherwise unknown. Interval reception has no initial elevation claim and widens bearing for sampled-anchor error under ACS-L11, rounded outward to the fixed **22.5°/45°/90°/135°** buckets or omitted. The unrounded correction remains private because it encodes hidden range. No same-floor/room assertion follows. These limit resolution, not identity confidence. They trade precision for honest useful uncertainty; expanding location requires supported physics and unchanged disclosure guarantees. [Policy][policy], ACS04/ACS06/ACS08.

## ACS-L09 — Native response filtering

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** Initial animals may continue or update their existing native alarm/escape controller. Preserved voice bands require A-acquired voice-like classification or understood-speech proof; private event type cannot select them. A language-disabled animal needs the classification. An indistinct utterance follows the general filter. The world selects nonlinguistic response deltas **8–30 dB** by received class/species, plus actual onset/rise of **6 dB** for unclassified/rustling or **12 dB** for mixtures. Detection/weak accumulation alone need not alarm. No universal turn, fear, curiosity approach, waking or group broadcast is selected.

Repeat keys add permitted class; the existing **6 dB** bands and [sensory/repetition lifetime owner](hearing-and-speech.md#ssp-h04--acoustic-scene-coalescing) remain authoritative. One route review consumes the sealed boundary's eligible cue set; suppression does not erase distinct evidence. The filter limits herd alarm but can miss soft warnings. Coarse contact classification is available before its selected response threshold; finer voice/ringing detail can remain unavailable. The policy compares the close approach and noisy-load choices against the existing spear approach, while close deer/bear groups remain an explicit qualification case. A pending acoustic refuge-leg obligation prevents an anonymous alarm from settling without its actual movement; presence/rise baselines survive outside the optional alarm episode. Qualify dense movement, renewed clatter and eventual settling before retuning. [Policy][policy], ACS05/ACS08.

## ACS-L10 — Complete audiences and aggregate masking

**No independent listener, nearest-N source or loudest-N masker cap selected. Restrictiveness: Too liberal for unqualified total work.** Every eligible local body remains included. Individually subthreshold sources can collectively mask or form a detected mixture; ordinary per-source detection reach alone does not prove a mixture/masker contributor irrelevant.

Use conservative selection, shared phase preparation, stable energy summaries and proof-based omission. Exclude the entire candidate group without subtracting nearly equal huge totals. Unresolved residuals may form one anonymous broad/no-direction mixture with unknown producer count; each contribution is allocated once, not also to individual accumulation. Ambient creates no synthetic occurrence.

Completeness is deliberate; capacity is unmeasured. Required work continues or holds/pauses progress through its owner, never silently truncates quiet recipients or declares unfinished work inaudible. Improve work representation before changing audience guarantees. [Policy][policy], [native-work limits](native-work.md), ACS03/ACS07.

## ACS-L11 — Continuous sensing fidelity and work budgets

**Proposed sampled provider · Restrictiveness: Medium for sensing fidelity; too liberal for unqualified accumulated work.** Continuous source and ear anchors each update after at most **.5 m of accepted travel**, with actual source/input/lifecycle discontinuities handled immediately. Their relative geometry may differ from the actual scene by up to **1 m**. The provider can miss or delay a brief continuous change between those samples, including a thin opening. Interval direction widens by its anchor-error bound and has no initial elevation claim. Actual impulses use their true occurrence and current ear geometry; their persistent maskers use the held interval scene. A integrates that supplied duration law exactly. No frame/timer polling or general continuum root solver is selected.

The [canonical schedule][tech] owns saved sampling progress and zero-duration impulse handling. Revisit the displacement bound for a concrete failed listening/hunting journey, measuring both usefulness and complete-caller cost. ACS03/ACS07/ACS08.

Selected responsiveness quanta are **128 transfer candidates** or **256 source components**, charged within existing total allowances. Complete work includes every affected listener’s group-decision scan, not just changed geometric transfers: many staggered maskers can make those scans expensive. Batch actual simultaneous changes and reuse stable preparation; if qualification fails, improve this owner’s derived aggregate/threshold preparation. Do not hide the cost with an audience cap or a declared-negative result from unfinished work. Required work holds or fails at the coherent boundary, preserving owed time and evidence. ACS03/ACS07.

## ACS-L12 — Retention, receipts and continuation

**Proposed/no acoustic lifetime-history quota · Restrictiveness: Too liberal for unqualified retained growth.** Play grows occurrences, observer facts, producer/receiver continuations and exact source receipts. Display lifetimes, feature bounds and native expiry do not cap history/queues.

A owns accumulation, episodes, leases, familiarity receipts and retained facts; the native controller owns repetition. Physical state saves through existing owners. The [technical design][tech] enumerates body/producer/source/A/native-response/owed-work fields and actual allocations. Each animal-acquired occurrence retains its compact native source/facets for its own **900 simulation seconds**, while active episode/cut/receipt obligations can retain minimal support longer; no full story row is mandatory per contact. Active intervals use leases rather than a new occurrence per evaluation. Required state reserves bytes under the existing actor/module/world owners before a coherent phase commits; no separate acoustic lifetime quota or arbitrary receipt cap is selected. Reclaim only when support permits; required evidence cannot be evicted into a false negative, replayed on restore or replaced by an unbounded history scan. [A limits][a-limits], [memory limits](memory.md), ACS06/ACS07.

## ACS-L13 — Preparation and profile growth

**Proposed bounded preparation; no independent numeric profile cap selected here. Eight contact offsets/four features do not bound this upstream corpus.

No additional acoustic profile-count cap is installed. ACS01/ACS02/ACS06/ACS07.
