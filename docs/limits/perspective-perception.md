# Observer perception and recognition limits

Module A's [feature specification](../projects/perspective-perception-feature-spec.md), [technical design](../projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md) and [PP00–PP07 tracker](../maintainers/perspective-perception.md) own the selected behavior, implementation and qualification. The [base-world policy](../worlds/base/perception-recognition.md) owns the numerical recognition/detection law. This inventory records its limits, reasons and boundaries without making them universal engine rules.

**Evidence baseline:** documentation and source inspection at contracts tip `a3d40b4d71e29d6504e9f3fcc21e76344a0a284e`, reviewed October 19, 2026 in the maintainer's local timezone. The existing-channel implementation is recorded in [the scoped evidence](../verification/personal-perception-foundation.md). Numerical policies below remain unqualified gameplay/capacity choices; physical B/C limits and full PP07 acceptance remain open. No reported throughput, model accuracy or player enjoyment is inferred from a chosen number. Existing hearing, memory and native-controller delivery retain their own measured/historical evidence.

## PP-L01 — Supported sensory and interpretation families

**Selected policy; Medium.** The initial A integration supports installed sight, hearing and touch/impact evidence; narrow permitted appearance/equipment/activity fields and bounded two-endpoint received axes/two-sample motion; committed speech and its actual listener fragments; received voice features; source-kind hypotheses; person/name association; attributed danger/detail/location claims; and explicit later inference. A world must supply a physical detector/profile before its additional sense or transformation works. The engine does not install a general odor system, arbitrary biometric analysis, magic, full disguise simulation, recording media or natural-language truth detector by admitting a generic dictionary.

The native warning grammar supports complete understood “look out/watch out/danger at <known place> now” clauses with exact unique listener-permitted place labels and an approximate two-normal-second interval anchored to the utterance. This deliberately finite language family is not general natural-language understanding. Unknown/ambiguous labels, missing words and expired intervals supply no native current danger claim; ordinary testimony remains. Retain no new place registry or general onset store. Expand only through a concrete supported producer and its source/precision qualification.

At an unsupported family, return the explicit unsupported result and preserve any independently supported facets. Do not fabricate a clean signal or silently present the unsupported outcome as a completed negative. This restriction keeps the first implementation executable through real producers and validators while permitting useful anonymous clues. B/C/SPK own physical and speech extensions; PP01–PP03 own the narrow evidence seam. Revisit when an actual selected producer needs another family, not merely because a repertoire mentions it.

## PP-L02 — Base Perception and feature precision

**Selected policy; Medium.** Selected base-world people whose visual or recognition policy requires it receive one Perception value in **0–100**, starting at **50** absent another admitted authored value. The selected voice profile has **four** equally weighted physical descriptors, each an integer in **0–1000**. Ordinary person construction without an authored voice selects each independently from **51** equally likely values at **20-unit** spacing using saved world randomness; no uniqueness retry or speech-time redraw is permitted. This is modest fictional variation, not population calibration or synthesized audio. B supplies the visual family's physical features, ranges, normalization and positive weights; it needs at least three identity dimensions for reliable matching. Both modalities use normalized **0–1000** intervals, with widths greater than **400** unusable, and the same candidate/reliable shared-feature counts and distance criteria in [the world policy](../worlds/base/perception-recognition.md#perceivable-signatures). Received visual quality uses resolution and B's identity fidelity, without an acoustic term.

These are fictional game descriptors and initial character variation, not measured human or biometric accuracy. B owns its actual finite visible-feature family; A does not invent facial visibility. Missing Perception for an actor whose installed policy requires it is an installation/preparation error. Recognition and memory remain separate capabilities. A physical-only human visual observer still installs its required value; nonhuman/machine visual fixtures explicitly use gain 1. With `p=value/100`, B uses `0.75+0.5p` and A uses its normalized recognition law. Missing features remain absent, not guessed points. No universal attribute, bonus stack, automatic progression or physical hearing-threshold change is introduced.

## PP-L03 — Recognition quality and ambiguity

**Selected policy; Safe.** Voice quality uses actual acoustic margin; both modalities use retained interval resolution and physical identity fidelity. The exact formulas, candidate radius, reliable thresholds and **0.08** distinct-candidate margin are canonical in [matching and association](../worlds/base/perception-recognition.md#matching-and-association). Match separate learned individuals, not each of one person's exemplars as competitors. All threshold boundaries are inclusive as specified there. Scores are not truth probabilities.

Failure to identify preserves detection, direction, words and useful uncertainty. An exact tie stays ambiguous even at maximum ability/familiarity. The selected low-margin direct-speech bands do not currently support reliable recognition when all same-language words are lost; the explicit converse acceptance uses preserved timbre with unavailable lexical comprehension/nonverbal sound, or a later properly supported C/SPK transform. This prevents a false promise while retaining independent speech/voice outcomes. Revisit under PP07 when measured ordinary conversation or similar-voice play shows the selected thresholds are too restrictive or too generous.

## PP-L04 — Learning rate and anonymous seed window

**Shared familiarity implemented for supported native contacts.** The [world's subject-wide policy](../worlds/base/perception-recognition.md#shared-subject-familiarity-target) supplies the single score used by attention and recognition, with bounded exact support, correction and same-format restoration. Modality samples still establish learned sensory eligibility; they are not a second acquaintance score. [Native/fixture evidence](../verification/attention-and-memory-core.md) does not qualify future physical producers or complete PP07.

**Selected policy; Safe.** Qualifying acoustic seeds remain eligible for automatic initial matching for **30 normal-speed seconds**. Voice promotion requires two qualifying samples at least **two normal-speed seconds** apart and the world-defined conservative seed/competitor rule. The same route can complete a voice first independently associated through sight without a second visible meeting. Sight instead learns from **one** qualifying actual sample with an unambiguous observed body, preserving the pre-learning result and creating a fresh local individual unless eligible association already supports an existing subject. Private body identity cannot repair failed recognition. No anonymous visual seed-clustering route is selected. Both learning routes publish their supported association without rerunning recognition with new credits. Initial learning also meets the recognition-quality requirement at the modality's starting credit count and current ability, with the existing .55 floor; voice promotion checks the pair rather than only the new utterance. At default Perception, sight requires .575 and voice .55; uniformly medium B identity quality .55 therefore preserves observation/history until a clearer view, rather than lowering all recognition thresholds. Weaker evidence is retained without an unusable learned profile. The exact formula, including authored-seed installation quality, belongs to the world policy; later established-profile learning retains its ordinary .55 floor.

Later sensory-profile learning admits at most **one new credit per two normal-speed seconds per perceived individual/modality**, with no banking or exposure-reset bonus. Continuous acoustic signal supplies completed non-overlapping two-second samples; instantaneous utterances supply one discrete sample and no invented speaking duration. A later visual credit requires an actual completed B sample or explicitly supported constant-feature interval covering its due boundary through the existing exposure continuation. Dynamic sight waits for the next canonical sample after its credit deadline; static dependency-proved feature support can cover the due interval. Elapsed time and polling alone supply no visual sample. Current body tracking remains separate and need not wait for identity learning.

At the rate boundary, an early uniquely compatible sample retains its ordinary observation and only tentative correlation, earns no deferred credit and does not create an artificial competing seed solely because it was too early. The earliest eligible seed remains available; independently observed distinct producers remain competitors. Expired seed history cannot be silently reloaded to identify a new source; deliberate later reconsideration is a separately supported inference. A sustained clear conversation can reach familiarity, and leaving/returning does not accelerate it. Original root/sample receipts suppress retransmission and repeated physical recording credit; UI replay and synthesis create no physical listening. Normal-clock conversion is pinned under SC13; runtime speed is not multiplied twice. Revisit if ordinary interactions fail to create useful acquaintance or reward mechanical repetition.

## PP-L05 — Retained recognition supports and exemplars

**Selected policy; Safe.** Retain at most **12 actual credited samples per modality/individual**, plus at most **four authored candidate exemplars** when that modality has explicitly authored starting knowledge. Select at most **four matching exact exemplars total** across that union. Actual credit and observed prototype support share the same slots. A learned profile requires **one** eligible actual sample for sight or **two** for voice; the independently supported shared score supplies its early-saturating recognition benefit. An eligible authored seed declares learned equivalence of **1** for sight or **2** for voice, or familiar equivalence of **6**. The shared score uses its separately eligible authored or actual contact support, never a sum of modality sample counts. Encounter diversity influences actual-credit retention, not a compulsory number of meetings. Exact retention/exemplar selection, authored source/quality requirements and pair quality are defined once in [learning and familiarity](../worlds/base/perception-recognition.md#learning-and-familiarity).

A newly admitted sample competes with currently retained samples under that ordering. Eviction changes future recognition capability, not historical evidence, and cannot reset persisted last-credit/rate state or forget a replay receipt. Authored knowledge has installation time/order and explicit supplied descriptors, without fake sensory samples, encounters or learning roots; a name entry alone supplies no voice familiarity. No implicit prototype averaging, interval narrowing or unbounded sufficient-support list is allowed. Native support holds exact references rather than recursively embedded histories; existing personal/native source lineage and its prepared eligibility index own historical dependencies and root receipts. No duplicate unbounded seen-root store is selected. Correction can reduce familiarity or invalidate a learned association. This modest representation intentionally does not recognize every costume, illness, age change or imitation. Revisit when a concrete supported context requires additional capacity and the complete recognition workload has been measured.

## PP-L06 — Weak evidence, loss and correlation grace

**Selected policy; Safe.** The selected accumulating detector has threshold/max **one support unit**, a full-rate crossing in **0.25 normal-speed seconds**, and gap decay of a full unit over **0.5 normal-speed seconds**. Episode reconnection grace is separately **0.5 normal-speed seconds**, anchored to actual support loss; it is currently **30 simulation seconds** at the installed normal conversion. Strong detection is immediate at an actual provider sample or impulse. B holds its completed sample across canonical 50 ms normal-time cells; C owns its separate held acoustic law. Short unsampled optical flashes may be missed. Original measured value time never advances merely because support is held. [The world owner](../worlds/base/perception-recognition.md#exposure-and-weak-evidence) defines interval integration and admissible cue correlation.

Zero-rate samples decay and incompatible/uncorrelated cues cannot fill a shared sum. Live grants end immediately at loss; neither grace nor a visible UI cue extends them. The selected [grouped support](../projects/perspective-perception-tech-design.md#continuous-confirmation-without-rewriting-history) retains independent field meaning with common timestamps/masks, rather than requiring one mutable lease per field. Keep measurement intervals separate from availability coverage. Moving-point or continuously varying feature updates do not require a durable fact per physical evaluation; retain admitted chronology, selected cuts and needed support through existing retention owners; loss alone does not require a closing capsule per field. Save/restore preserves both intervals and the latest permitted value, not just the original onset fact. Total active exposure/facet growth remains the explicit PP-L09 qualification dimension. B/C must demonstrate a physically justified ordinary-cadence weak cue that accumulates and one that correctly does not. These are initial response/readability choices, not measured stealth balance. Revisit through B/C/A qualification if individually weak ordinary cues always vanish before they can matter, or if unrelated ambient noise combines unfairly.

## PP-L07 — Candidate preview versus complete recognition

**Selected policy; Safe for display, no semantic candidate cap.** Ordinary presentation can show up to **two** best tentative learned candidates plus an explicit more-candidates indicator when applicable. This is a preview, not the matching/search cap. The native decision must complete all possibly eligible candidate and competitor comparisons before asserting a unique match. Only the observer's eligible learned individuals are candidates; hidden world beings are not a source of preview counts.

Additional permitted candidates remain available through the existing scoped continuation/inspection path when the consumer needs them. A bounded preview never changes a tentative result into recognized or maps an anonymous cue to an entity. G owns layout and readable wording; A supplies uncertainty and completeness metadata. Revisit display count only for a demonstrated usability need.

## PP-L08 — Work quanta and overload

**Proposed; Safe per slice, total work unqualified.** Initial A continuation slices process **64 physical contributions** or **128 exemplar comparisons**, using the existing native-work owner and actual accounting. These are engineering work quanta; they neither cap physically eligible listeners nor supply simulated time. Retain deterministic continuation position and original dependency scope. Other owner work is charged through its existing rules, not declared free because one comparison fits a batch.

Required incomplete work holds a coherent native prefix before a dependent outcome. Optional interpretation/context/consolidation can defer. No nearest-N observer selection, truncated uniqueness claim or budget-induced invisibility is selected. One expensive operation can still exceed a desired wall-time slice; PP07 must measure it and reconcile the owner before capacity is claimed. Do not install a second queue solely to enforce these values.

## PP-L09 — Total acquaintances, seeds, listeners and history growth

**Shared optional-knowledge allowance installed; Medium.** Ordinary retained individual/profile records, provisional voice seeds, edited subject pads and their sparse exact proof share the [base attention/retention allowance](../worlds/base/attention.md); they do not receive separate per-feature pools. Complete encoded bytes and record count are checked before new optional learning. A full allowance declines that learning or explicitly refuses a name/note edit while preserving current received evidence and previous knowledge. Current-only local bodies are not automatically lasting allocations. Authored knowledge and active required support retain their own owners and accounting.

There is no physically eligible listener cap and no truncated semantic-match candidate set. Dense current contacts still cost native source work. A provisional seed has an actual maintenance deadline; a bound on each person's samples alone is not a bound on retained people.

Keep compact active state, actor-scoped indexes, conservative candidate preparation and resumable complete queries. Prepare required cold state before a native boundary; never scan or hydrate all historical events for each cue. Existing native/save/admission envelopes still apply and can produce an explicit hold/preparation failure. Optional context has its own bounded input selection. If a future recording family can replay arbitrarily old learning roots, its existing source-lineage adapter must prepare exact root-receipt eligibility before required learning; no forever-growing duplicate global seen-ID set is selected. True forgetting and source compaction retain distinct behavior.

Known-place capsules and their exact-label index add active-actor retained-place count/bytes, source-completeness metadata and complete activation/dirty work to that same inventory. No independent total-place cap or top-N truncation is selected. Build/rebuild through bounded existing preparation before native warning intake, update only affected entries, and never query full historical accounts inside hearing or a cut. Incomplete required preparation holds; it does not mean an unknown place.

PP07 must measure retained bytes, place count/index bytes, activation/dirty work, pending work/age and real producer-driven growth under the technical workloads. The shared allowance supplies explicit refusal and source retirement rather than silently dropping required candidates or Known-place matches. Complete dense and long-history costs remain unqualified; this is not infinite scale.

## PP-L10 — Acquaintance lifetime and correction scope

**No limit on passive acquaintance age; Medium.** Learned recognition has no fixed time-to-live or calendar decay in this slice. Its eligibility follows retained support, explicit correction/forgetting and the mind/lifecycle owner. Short exposure grace, six-hour routine recall, hot history tail and existing animal sensory retention do not independently erase a legitimately retained learned profile. Ordinary unsupported local records and expired provisional seeds are pruned; retained name/profile/contact proof remains subject to the common source-retention and storage owners.

**Implemented scoped invalidation for supported sources; Very safe.** The existing correction/forgetting owner follows exact source aliases and immediate learning dependencies, including cold and evicted predecessors. It retires dependent interpretation while preserving unrelated profiles and directly received words. The [foundation evidence](../verification/personal-perception-foundation.md) demonstrates that scope; newly supported source families still require complete dependency coverage. Authored starting knowledge retains its explicit installation authority and current forgetting/revocation overlay. Restore preserves that overlay and cannot reseed forgotten familiarity from unchanged starting content. Broader natural decay/dream reinterpretation remains ND19 work.
