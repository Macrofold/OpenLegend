# Perception and attention — limits and constraints

**Specified target; runtime and capacity qualification pending.** This inventory owns discretionary bounds and removed/no-limit decisions for the [feature](../features/perception-and-attention/README.md). Values are initial authoring/service envelopes, not scientific laws or measured population promises. [EPR06/EPR08/EPR09](../maintainers/events-perception-and-reactions.md) and [ATI04/ATI08](../maintainers/attention-and-investigation.md) own delivery and evidence.

## PA-L01 — Ranking, detail and repetition

The [base-world profile](../worlds/base/perception-and-attention.md#reasons-and-priority) owns the 0–100 priorities, five-point ordinary margin, urgent bypass, Focus-derived 5–15 detail capacity and ordinary default of ten. The exact values have one owner there.

The strongest active reason determines candidate priority. This is intentionally non-additive: equivalent observations, rule duplicates and update rates cannot manufacture urgency. It does not prevent independent evidence from supporting a stronger interpretation or independent obligations from receiving their results.

No universal one-target limit, mandatory occupied slot, fixed two-second switch delay, ten-point cap on sensory contribution, clarity bonus, or protected discovery slot during emergencies is selected. These removals prevent attention from obstructing immediate danger or forcing continuous tracking of incidental subjects. They do not remove sensory limits, actual work compatibility or mandatory personal evidence.

**Restrictiveness:** medium; rank and finite detail intentionally produce understandable gaps. **Evidence:** proposed tuning. **Revisit when:** a connected pursuit/distraction/work encounter is either unstable or unreasonably insensitive, using actual player behavior and complete cost.

## PA-L02 — First continuous-condition authoring envelope

An installed continuously evaluated definition may contain at most **eight leaf conditions and 32 total composition nodes**. A multi-cue conclusion uses at most **four distinct supporting occurrences**, a finite window no greater than **30 normal-play seconds**, and no recursive inference cycle. The selected emergency window is shorter, as specified in the world profile.

These limits apply to continuously maintained expressions, not goals written in prose, ordinary reasoning inside an independently admitted decision, content count or the number of legitimate witnesses. Unsupported expressions are refused at authoring/activation with their missing capability or exceeded scope identified; no hidden full scan or per-cue model interpretation substitutes for them.

Supported equality/membership/range forms must have bounded declared evaluation. Broad catch-all predicates are permitted only in the admitted generic profile, not as an unlimited catalogue of global callbacks. Factored alternatives retain these same node/leaf counts; expansion into all combinations is not a permitted escape from the limit.

**Restrictiveness:** medium. Four cues cover the selected corroboration and signal cases; complex investigations remain ordinary finite work. **Evidence:** design envelope only. **Revisit when:** a useful real authored condition cannot be expressed without meaningless splitting and a broader form has a complete cost/lifecycle proof.

## PA-L03 — Active monitoring and optional combination state

The initial per-observer envelope admits **32 active personal concern bindings** and **eight installed generic attention programs**. A binding can address a supported group or area; it is not one binding for every possible body in the world. An ordinary exact concern or a branch of a larger composite cannot bypass this allowance by appearing under another name. The installed body's mandatory effects, required sensing and existing admitted action obligations are outside optional attention-program admission.

Each activated multi-cue binding may retain at most **16 unresolved partial combinations**, each with at most the four occurrence references permitted above. Equivalent partial support may share one state when that preserves meaning. Keep only the minimal support still within its declared window; ordinary personal evidence remains under memory, independently of optional pattern state.

Before accepting voluntary monitoring, reserve its declared active state and work allowance. Refuse additional unsupported scope explicitly; an offered narrower scope needs acceptance. Under unexpected pressure, optional combination analysis can report incomplete/unknown and stop promising full pattern coverage. It cannot report false, erase independently required evidence, fabricate clearance or silently complete a promised exact watch.

No negative record is required for every observer × definition × possible subject. Candidate discovery, sensory-query visits, local-join visits and retained reason provenance must all be charged to their owning work/memory allowance before expensive preparation. A bounded final focus list is not evidence those preceding operations are bounded.

**Restrictiveness:** medium, currently unmeasured. **Rationale:** permits several purposeful watches/interests while preventing an authored instruction from installing unlimited live work. **Revisit when:** a legitimate authored encounter reaches the envelope, with separate measurement of active bindings, candidate density, false residual candidates and retained support. Raising a count without qualifying those costs is not acceptance.

## PA-L04 — Time and responsiveness

The [base-world profile](../worlds/base/perception-and-attention.md#brief-demands-and-bounded-assessment) owns brief cue lifetime, finite assessment, corroboration window, unresolved concern lifetime, supported clearance and the [fast-pursuit target](../worlds/base/perception-and-attention.md#responsive-pursuit).

Assessment cannot renew while waiting for a provider; elapsed deadlines do not prove an observation or clearance. Strong new evidence may end an assessment early or supersede optional detail. Unknown/stale hypotheses may remain in memory without retaining indefinite high-priority monitoring.

**Restrictiveness:** medium; short deadlines can leave unanswered questions. **Rationale:** a distraction should create a useful decision opportunity without trapping an NPC in a model wait or permanent alarm. **Evidence:** provisional starting values; no responsiveness or throughput measurement performed. **Revisit when:** explicit latency, useful observation and human play show that a selected duration creates avoidable delay or unreadable reactions.

## PA-L05 — Catalogue scale and real fan-out

There is no feature-level cap on the number of installed named content definitions. Installation/validation, retained definitions and their indexes may scale with catalogue size. Resource admission can reject an installation that exceeds its actual available envelope.

Per-observation matching must have no loop over the complete installed catalogue. Definitions eliminated by supported selective dispatch receive zero predicate evaluations, including irrelevant definitions in the same broad event family. Exact-entry lookup may be expected constant time; range/spatial queries, affected active bindings, residual predicates, finite cue candidates, deadlines and actual outputs retain their stated costs in the [technical design](../projects/perception-and-attention-tech-design.md).

There is no claim that processing an unlimited number of real matches or witnesses is constant time. Physical acquisition, actual personal outcomes, retained history, byte counts, cold restoration and publication are separate costs. A hundred true witnesses cannot become one shared mind to satisfy a benchmark.

**Restrictiveness:** low for catalogue growth, high for unselective continuous authoring. **Rationale:** expressiveness should not add unrelated recurring work. **Evidence:** a design requirement, not a measured speedup. EPR09 must measure independent definition count, relevant-match count, receiver count, cue rate, spatial density and history growth.
