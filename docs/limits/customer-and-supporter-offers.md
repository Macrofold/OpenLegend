# Customer and supporter offers: limits and constraints

[Product proposal](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md) · [Commercial delivery](../maintainers/production-deployment.md) · [Entitlement delivery](../maintainers/inventions-and-world-evolution.md#inv-13--episode-budgets-and-installed-cost-enforcement) · [Tracking rules](README.md)

**Proposed DG27 terms, October 7, 2026.** These are product recommendations, not an activated offer, measured capacity, real appropriation or change to the development save policy. Actual price, currency, quantities, population, spending, retained bytes and operator authority must be selected and qualified under PD01/PD10. Existing [AI](ai-execution.md), [invention](inventions.md), [deployment](production-deployment.md), [participation](multiplayer.md) and [persistence](persistence.md) owners retain their limits.

## OF-L01 — One ordinary hosted-world offer first

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** One monthly purchase covers one named active private world under its qualified profile. Solo value is sufficient; qualified guests do not each need another hosting subscription. Additional worlds require explicit purchases and admission. Personal invention upgrades, recognition, marketplace payouts, voice, travel and grants do not hold up this first complete experience. The initial experiment proposes up to five paying hosts before review toward the existing ten-renewing-customer milestone, with at least two renewal opportunities; actual budget may admit fewer. These are learning bounds, not a statistically representative sample or measured hosting capacity. Expand from repeat play, full cost and support evidence. Owner: PD10; [scope](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#3-offer-families-and-first-release-scope).

## OF-L02 — One successful agreed objective

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** An account unit counts a distinct agreed invention objective successfully admitted. Necessary support, repair and art stay within that objective; independently usable objectives or separately retained variants require disclosed additional units. Failed/canceled/rejected work consumes no completed units but can consume real funding. No post-completion semantic repricing or root-identifier loophole is allowed. Cross-world reservations, uncertain completion and late original-period settlement remain under INV-13.8. Units cannot buy spending authority, world powers or unbounded installed behavior. Revisit the packaging only for demonstrated confusion or an excluded useful creative pattern; preserve original receipts. Owner: INV-13.8; [counting](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#51-successful-invention-allowance).

## OF-L03 — Real monthly periods and no accumulated included balance

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** One account allowance period follows the enrollment anniversary, with missing-day month-end adjustment returning to the original anchor. Hosting has a separate usable-service anniversary. Included units expire at the account boundary without rollover; cancellation does not bring expiry forward. Optional immediate personal upgrades add a disclosed prorated price difference and whole-unit allowance, rounded down, without resetting used/held units; a zero-unit upgrade is deferred without charge. Downgrades act next period; a recorded highest funded allowance prevents repeated/cross-bundle grants. Refunds remove only unused added entitlement, with held/completed work retained and no negative debt. A pending personal renewal permits normal free use, then confirmation within 24 real hours adds only the paid difference; after that unfulfilled deadline cancel/refund, with no late automatic revival. Failed personal renewal leaves the free allowance without ending independent world hosting. Overlapping included benefits supply the largest eligible account allowance, not multiplied grants. No separately purchased top-up is selected. This contains liability while avoiding daily attendance pressure. Actual counts and prices remain unselected, with useful-session qualification before sale. Owner: INV-13.8/PD10; [periods](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#6-periods-reservations-and-plan-changes).

## OF-L04 — Payment and renewal consent

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** Initial pilot renewal is off by default. One deliberate order stays pending until reconciled; no duplicate fulfillment, automatic repeated collection, overage or top-up. Service starts on confirmed payment plus usable delivery. An unfulfilled initial order is canceled/refunded after 24 real hours. Renewal notices arrive seven real days before the charge, or at purchase for a shorter first interval. Material changes require at least 30 real days' notice and explicit future acceptance; otherwise renewal ends. These customer terms constrain the selected vendor, not the reverse. Refund initiation and completion remain distinguishable. Owner: PD10; [purchase](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#42-payment-and-delivery), [renewal](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#71-renewal).

## OF-L05 — Inactivity and refund windows

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** After one fully inactive paid period, notify the payer; after a second consecutive inactive period without affirmative keep-hosting instruction, disable the following renewal. Deliberate guest play counts; automated activity alone does not. Proposed voluntary refunds cover the first purchase within seven real days of usable delivery and accidental renewal within seven real days when the new term has no deliberate human play/new paid customer work. Inspection, recovery and automatic background activity do not disqualify that remedy. Duplicates and undelivered purchases get full refunds. Applicable stronger rights are an actual launch input, not bounded by this proposal. These terms limit forgotten charges while preserving wanted group hosting. Owner: PD10; [cancellation](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#73-cancellation-and-refunds).

## OF-L06 — Coherent service hold and proposed recovery

**Status: Proposed, activation conditional · Restrictiveness: Safe for behavior; aggregate retention unqualified.** At service end, coherent world hold stops simulation and new spending without fictional punishment. The proposed post-launch recovery floor is 30 real days, with a notice seven days before expiry and extension for service-caused recovery unavailability. Planned retirement gives at least 30 real days' notice and resolves undelivered prepaid terms. Reuse current privacy, export and resume owners. These dates do not authorize actual deletion, indefinite storage or legacy support: Mike's RP02/PD01 decision must precede any live promise. No numerical total archive/backup allowance is selected here. Owner: PD01/PD10 and existing data/persistence owners; [recovery](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#8-end-of-hosting-recovery-and-retirement).

## OF-L07 — Bounded one-time recognition

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** One chronicle claim per account in the first named edition, no resale/transfer and no recurring custom reward. Display name is at most 40 Unicode code points and 160 UTF-8 bytes; optional dedication at most 120 code points and 480 bytes. Both bounds apply; plain text only. The first edition names only the purchaser's chosen pseudonym or anonymous credit; third-person named dedications and gifts are unselected. Anonymous support is permitted. This buys a reviewed historical acknowledgement, not game powers, immortality, perpetual hosting or votes. Broader physical dedications need a useful separately authored benefit and complete lifecycle. Owner: optional PD10 recognition gate; [benefit](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#91-the-benefit).

## OF-L08 — Review, unfulfilled claims and corrections

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** One submission or correction under review per claim; complete submissions reviewed within seven real days. One revised submission follows rejection; a second rejection or unavailable benefit resolves by refund. A missed review deadline offers immediate refund. Unsubmitted claims are refunded at 90 real days, with notice at day 83. Publication consumes the claim once; one ordinary post-publication display correction is included. Required privacy/moderation/factual corrections remain available separately, without granting a new claim. Operator admission must fit actual funded review/refund capacity. These bounds avoid a lifetime bespoke naming service or revenue from undelivered benefits; relax only with measured workload and clear revised future terms. Owner: PD10; [fulfillment](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#92-review-and-fulfillment).

## OF-L09 — Actual resource and liability qualification

**Status: Open operating inputs · Restrictiveness: Too liberal if treated as an unlimited service.** Per-request/root/actor caps do not bound all accounts, concurrent worlds, native effects, private histories, stored assets, financial obligations or support labor. Before sale, PD01/PD10 must select actual service capacity, included funding, real account unit quantities, payer delegations, queue admission and retention liability. Existing INV-13 owners enforce accounting and installed cost; no second wallet. Measured heavy-use sessions and full hosting/AI/storage/refund/support costs must fit the approved cash/time ceilings. No price, “hard cap,” provider cost or character-count guarantee is inferred from a historical example. Owner: PD01/PD10/INV-13; [economics](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md#10-economics-and-performance).
