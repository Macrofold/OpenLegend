# Customer and supporter offer limits

Prepared against `671d52d724db5dfe39403ebe46f7c1ee24d6ec52` on 2026-10-08. All entries are **Proposed**, not implemented or owner-adopted numerical offers. The [feature specification](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md) owns behavior and [CO01–CO06](../maintainers/commercial-offers.md) owns delivery under PD10/INV-13. Exact accounting, permission and non-rewindability are correctness obligations, not adjustable allowances. This inventory records the discretionary product scope around them.

## CO-L01 — Small first paid offer

**Proposed; Very safe.** Begin with one owner-paid hosted-world offer for a demonstrated attended game, with a named audience and supported participation profile. Do not make voice, a marketplace, world travel, a fund, a complete premium catalog or perpetual unattended life prerequisites. The accepted free recurring invention tier and later platform/premium-world directions remain. A host's permitted guest access does not require another hosting subscription. Actual price, participant/workload envelope, real-world renewal period, account quantities and operator funding/time ceiling are adoption gates, not selected by historical $39 or 10/30 examples. At an unqualified boundary, keep the offer unavailable and explain what is supported. **Reason:** test whether people want to keep playing before expanding administration. **Revisit:** observed renewals and demand justify a separately costed offer. **Owner:** CO01/CO05/CO06.

## CO-L02 — Recurring invention allowance and optional changes

**Proposed; Medium.** A finite account allowance applies across managed worlds to successful qualifying new inventions and the specification's materially new revisions; supporting declarations are included, while independent inventions in a bundle are counted separately. Reuse, crafting another instance, teaching, unchanged authorized import and existing-mechanic art do not consume another invention unit. Classification and the selected period/change policy must be shown before costly work. The concrete proposal is one Free allowance F and one larger Platform allowance P > F, in UTC calendar-month periods, with no rollover or invention-unit top-ups in the first scope. Mid-period paid activation adds only the disclosed prorated uplift, rounded down; a zero-unit uplift cannot be advertised as an immediate invention benefit. Downgrade/cancellation takes effect at the next period boundary. Unresolved holds stay with their original period until authoritative resolution; a work deadline does not turn uncertain execution into a free retry. Actual F/P, prices and these proposed policies require adoption before sale. When available units are encumbered or exhausted, stop new qualifying authoring, preserve existing work and distinguish holds from consumption; never claim that provider dollars alone restore a missing entitlement. No universal stored-definition cap is introduced. **Reason:** a comprehensible recurring offer without charging for ordinary play or letting helper records distort the unit. **Revisit:** observed creation/revision patterns, confusing classifications or unfair period outcomes. **Owner:** CO01/CO02; authoritative accounting stays with INV-13 and billing.

## CO-L03 — Explicit spending and commercial changes

**Proposed; Very safe.** No automatic paid overage, automatic top-up or paid retry follows from a subscription, supporter status, world acceleration or a failed attempt. An optional prepaid purchase requires its own named amount, purpose, payer and current consent; it does not grant unrelated invention rights, world admission or art funding. Proposed host AI credit is world-specific, used after included AI, refundable when unspent, frozen on host expiry and reconciled/refunded at world retirement. Unknown incurred exposure stays separate; an apparent unused balance is not evidence that dispatched work was free. Included unused AI ends with its term under the disclosed remedy policy. The first proposal uses USD face-value credit. Disclose the purchased credit’s currency/denomination, debit tariff and refund basis before purchase; preserve their applicable versions. Its customer charge ceiling and the operator’s supplier-cost ceiling both apply if retail and wholesale prices differ. An 8-unit wholesale estimate cannot authorize a 12-unit retail debit against only 10 units of purchased service value. Refund unused purchased value without revaluing it at a later tariff. Keep payment uncertainty and reserved exposure visible before allowing more dependent spending. Actual minimum/maximum purchase, outstanding balance, concurrent purchase and provider-request envelopes remain release inputs; a catalog without those controls is not qualified. Reject or retain a pending purchase honestly at the boundary, without asking for a duplicate checkout to recover it. **Reason:** protect a bounded paid promise and avoid hidden liabilities. **Revisit:** measured customer need and authorized exposure, independently of increasing a provider quota. **Owner:** CO02/CO03; existing INV/PD cost and provider controls remain authoritative.

## CO-L04 — Finite hosting and retained-work service

**Proposed; Safe.** Purchase covers the explicitly selected service term and scope, not indefinite storage or world operation. Cancellation, paid-through access, hosting expiry, retrieval and final service retirement must be separately disclosed before purchase. The concrete hosting proposal uses UTC calendar-month terms, explicit renewal choice and a one-term option. A first partial term prorates price and the disclosed included AI allowance only when the remaining service still supports its advertised use. After paid expiry, hold the world without free simulation for 30 real days of authorized retrieval; suspend that retrieval countdown during an operator-caused retrieval outage. Adopt the precise backup/export, reminder and retirement/deletion scope before sale. This proposed interval is not borrowed from a comparison product or a universal world-retention policy. Retained creation rights do not authorize another participant's private history or unrestricted dependency export. If required service cannot be funded, use the existing coherent stop/recovery policy; no hidden fictional catch-up, fake resident decisions or destructive reset. Optional finite unattended periods remain with [PS-L23–PS-L26](product-scalability.md), including no automatic funding-based resumption. **Reason:** preserve meaningful creations with an affordable, honest continuity promise. **Revisit:** measured retained bytes, retrieval/support burden and an explicitly funded service change. **Owner:** CO01/CO03 and PD/SL/PS for shared service behavior.

## CO-L05 — One optional dedication, bounded display promise

**Proposed; Very safe.** The first supporter benefit is one reviewed founding-chronicle acknowledgment associated with an explicitly named official project/world, with its exact review, once-only nontransferable redemption and display/retrieval terms. The selected scope permits one original entry per account in that named chronicle, external to the simulation; no in-world event or character knowledge follows automatically. It is not a recurring world-service package or a binding governance vote. A fictional representation, if selected, has its world's normal fate; transfer does not change original attribution or recreate the naming entitlement. Public-name consent, correction, privacy withdrawal and legitimate removal remain possible. CO-L08 proposes a finite minimum display promise; the separate retrieval interval follows CO-L04. No numerical text/media size, price, aggregate number of live entries or review turnaround is adopted here; qualify complete text/media bounds and finite review capacity before sale, and pause new sales when capacity is unavailable. **Reason:** recognize support without turning a keepsake into a permanent operational obligation or a game advantage. **Revisit:** demonstrated supporter demand and a funded, reviewed broader benefit. **Owner:** CO04; DG30 owns conditional governance.

## CO-L06 — Growing purchases, histories and operator work

**Proposed; no independent aggregate bound selected at this design stage; Too liberal until qualified.** Purchases, open authoring holds, delayed payment/refund cases, receipts, dedication reviews and retained worlds can grow independently of a page size or monthly unit count. Delivery must select finite admission and preparation envelopes for each relevant consumer, useful paged history, pending-age/recovery handling, storage and support capacity. Never discard required accounting or hide an unresolved paid obligation just to meet a display limit. Optional new sales, extras and recognition intake can close before existing obligations become unserviceable; required reconciliation remains funded. Exact sizes and service targets need the actual offer workload, not invented implementation numbers. **Reason:** ten ordinary subscribers can still generate disproportionate heavy use and recovery work. **Revisit:** measured arrival rates, unresolved exposure, retention and operator hours before expanding sales. **Owner:** CO01–CO06, consuming PD/INV/SL bounds rather than duplicating their queues or ledgers.

## CO-L07 — Voluntary first-purchase remedy

**Proposed; Safe.** Allow a full-refund request within 14 real days after activation of the first managed host or platform service purchase, including a one-term host purchase, once per paying account across those offers, as a voluntary commercial promise subject to approval for the actual offer and territory. State exactly which first named host/platform service the one-time remedy covers before purchase; automatic renewal is not a condition of eligibility. Refund all base-service charges for that same service paid within the 14-day window, including the first partial month, renewal or manually purchased next term, and upgrade adjustments. Thus an October 31 start followed by a November 1 full charge does not reduce a November 10 full-refund request to the tiny first payment. Unrelated services, separately settled purchased AI and dedication remedies keep their own scopes. This is not a statement of statutory rights and does not limit required remedies, duplicate-charge correction or failed-delivery recovery. Existing creations and third-party history are not clawed back, but consumed invention units and redeemed dedications do not regenerate. Include the cost of retained delivered value in the paid test. A requested, approved, initiated, pending, failed and completed refund must remain distinguishable; the operator retains responsibility for an unfinished promised remedy. Unfulfilled dedication refunds and unspent prepaid-credit refunds follow their separate scope rather than this trial window. **Reason:** make a small early purchase reversible without using fictional rewinds or opaque usage penalties. **Revisit:** actual customer understanding, remedy cost and observed abuse; never narrow an already purchased promise retroactively. **Owner:** CO01/CO03/CO05.

## CO-L08 — Finite public acknowledgment guarantee

**Proposed; Safe.** At publication deliver the authorized static keepsake and at least 90 real days of public acknowledgment. A static external chronicle can satisfy this promise after the associated game world retires; no simulation or game server must remain live for it. If the operator retires the chronicle or removes an otherwise compliant entry before the minimum, automatically refund the dedication in full, retain its consumed redemption and preserve the customer’s permitted keepsake. Privacy withdrawal and valid disclosed moderation/removal follow their own terms. After the minimum, display continues under the sold service terms; the separate 30-day protected retrieval period applies when the chronicle retires. The 90-day value is a provisional product choice, not a measured optimum: it makes acknowledgment meaningful beyond one hosting term while keeping a finite, inexpensive static-delivery obligation. Reserve early-retirement refund exposure before using dedication proceeds. **Boundary:** provide the promised display or its stated remedy, never substitute indefinite game hosting or a future feature. **Revisit:** supporter understanding, actual static-delivery/review costs and refund exposure before expanding this offer. **Owner:** CO01/CO04/CO05.
