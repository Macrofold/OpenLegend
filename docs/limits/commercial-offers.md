# Customer and supporter offer limits

Prepared against `671d52d724db5dfe39403ebe46f7c1ee24d6ec52` on 2026-10-08. All entries are **Proposed**, not implemented or owner-adopted numerical offers. The [feature specification](../projects/customer-and-supporter-offers-feature-spec.md) owns behavior and [CO01–CO06](../maintainers/commercial-offers.md) owns delivery under PD10/INV-13. Exact accounting, permission and non-rewindability are correctness obligations, not adjustable allowances. This inventory records the discretionary product scope around them.

## CO-L01 — Small first paid offer

**Proposed; Very safe.** Begin with one owner-paid hosted-world offer for a demonstrated attended game, with a named audience and supported participation profile. Do not make voice, a marketplace, world travel, a fund, a complete premium catalog or perpetual unattended life prerequisites. The accepted free recurring invention tier and later platform/premium-world directions remain. A host's permitted guest access does not require another hosting subscription. Actual price, participant/workload envelope, real-world renewal period, account quantities and operator funding/time ceiling are adoption gates, not selected by historical $39 or 10/30 examples. At an unqualified boundary, keep the offer unavailable and explain what is supported. **Reason:** test whether people want to keep playing before expanding administration. **Revisit:** observed renewals and demand justify a separately costed offer. **Owner:** CO01/CO05/CO06.

## CO-L02 — Recurring invention allowance and optional changes

**Proposed; Medium.** A finite account allowance applies across managed worlds to successful qualifying new inventions and the specification's materially new revisions; supporting declarations are included, while independent inventions in a bundle are counted separately. Reuse, crafting another instance, teaching, unchanged authorized import and existing-mechanic art do not consume another invention unit. Classification and the selected period/change policy must be shown before costly work. The concrete proposal is one Free allowance F and one larger Platform allowance P > F, in UTC calendar-month periods, with no rollover or invention-unit top-ups in the first scope. Mid-period paid activation adds only the disclosed prorated uplift, rounded down; a zero-unit uplift cannot be advertised as an immediate invention benefit. Downgrade/cancellation takes effect at the next period boundary. Unresolved holds stay with their original period until authoritative resolution; a work deadline does not turn uncertain execution into a free retry. Actual F/P, prices and these proposed policies require adoption before sale. When available units are encumbered or exhausted, stop new qualifying authoring, preserve existing work and distinguish holds from consumption; never claim that provider dollars alone restore a missing entitlement. No universal stored-definition cap is introduced. **Reason:** a comprehensible recurring offer without charging for ordinary play or letting helper records distort the unit. **Revisit:** observed creation/revision patterns, confusing classifications or unfair period outcomes. **Owner:** CO01/CO02; authoritative accounting stays with INV-13 and billing.

## CO-L03 — Explicit spending and commercial changes

**Proposed; Very safe.** No automatic paid overage, automatic top-up or paid retry follows from a subscription, supporter status, world acceleration or a failed attempt. An optional prepaid purchase requires its own named amount, purpose, payer and current consent; it does not grant unrelated invention rights, world admission or art funding. Keep payment uncertainty and reserved exposure visible before allowing more dependent spending. Actual minimum/maximum purchase, outstanding balance, concurrent purchase and provider-request envelopes remain release inputs; a catalog without those controls is not qualified. Reject or retain a pending purchase honestly at the boundary, without asking for a duplicate checkout to recover it. **Reason:** protect a bounded paid promise and avoid hidden liabilities. **Revisit:** measured customer need and authorized exposure, independently of increasing a provider quota. **Owner:** CO02/CO03; existing INV/PD cost and provider controls remain authoritative.

## CO-L04 — Finite hosting and retained-work service

**Proposed; Safe.** Purchase covers the explicitly selected service term and scope, not indefinite storage or world operation. Cancellation, paid-through access, hosting expiry, retrieval and final service retirement must be separately disclosed before purchase. A release must select storage/backup/export scope and real deadlines; none is inferred from a comparison product. Retained creation rights do not authorize another participant's private history or unrestricted dependency export. If required service cannot be funded, use the existing coherent stop/recovery policy; no hidden fictional catch-up, fake resident decisions or destructive reset. Optional finite unattended periods remain with [PS-L23–PS-L26](product-scalability.md), including no automatic funding-based resumption. **Reason:** preserve meaningful creations with an affordable, honest continuity promise. **Revisit:** measured retained bytes, retrieval/support burden and an explicitly funded service change. **Owner:** CO01/CO03 and PD/SL/PS for shared service behavior.

## CO-L05 — One optional dedication, bounded display promise

**Proposed; Very safe.** The first supporter benefit is one reviewed founding-chronicle acknowledgment associated with an explicitly named official project/world, with its exact review, once-only nontransferable redemption and display/retrieval terms. It is not a recurring world-service package or a binding governance vote. A fictional representation, if selected, has its world's normal fate; transfer does not change original attribution or recreate the naming entitlement. Public-name consent, correction, privacy withdrawal and legitimate removal remain possible. No deadline, character count, price, number of live entries or review turnaround is adopted here; qualify complete text/media bounds and finite review capacity before sale, and pause new sales when capacity is unavailable. **Reason:** recognize support without turning a keepsake into a permanent operational obligation or a game advantage. **Revisit:** demonstrated supporter demand and a funded, reviewed broader benefit. **Owner:** CO04; DG30 owns conditional governance.

## CO-L06 — Growing purchases, histories and operator work

**Proposed; no independent aggregate bound selected at this design stage; Too liberal until qualified.** Purchases, open authoring holds, delayed payment/refund cases, receipts, dedication reviews and retained worlds can grow independently of a page size or monthly unit count. Delivery must select finite admission and preparation envelopes for each relevant consumer, useful paged history, pending-age/recovery handling, storage and support capacity. Never discard required accounting or hide an unresolved paid obligation just to meet a display limit. Optional new sales, extras and recognition intake can close before existing obligations become unserviceable; required reconciliation remains funded. Exact sizes and service targets need the actual offer workload, not invented implementation numbers. **Reason:** ten ordinary subscribers can still generate disproportionate heavy use and recovery work. **Revisit:** measured arrival rates, unresolved exposure, retention and operator hours before expanding sales. **Owner:** CO01–CO06, consuming PD/INV/SL bounds rather than duplicating their queues or ledgers.
