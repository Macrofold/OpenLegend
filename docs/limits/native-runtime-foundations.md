# Native runtime foundation constraints

[Feature](../projects/native-runtime-foundations-feature-spec.md) · [Technical design](../projects/native-runtime-foundations-tech-design.md) · [NRF work](../maintainers/native-runtime-foundations.md)

These are limits of the selected candidate designs and their experiments, not implemented population or fictional-world rules. Current NSC and underlying semantic owners retain their actual limits. Do not copy this inventory into runtime constants.

## NRF-L01 — Existing proof profile, not a new memory quota

Mike's November 13 instruction selects **2 GiB (2,147,483,648 bytes)** through the existing trusted host configuration. Resident-state and pending-save accounting remain independent. Establish fresh controls and pin this same transient allowance in both arms before attributing an optimization's effect. The [completed NSC03 assessment](../maintainers/native-simulation-core.md#nsc03-reassessment--november-12-2026) and J/S failures remain evidence for their exercised 64/128 MiB profiles; they neither qualify 2 GiB nor halt the continuing implementation. Preserve actual admission, ownership, rollback, cleanup and observation detail. Reaching the new allowance requires live-owner attribution and repair of unnecessary retention or work, not disabled accounting or forced GC. Reservation bounds, actual process memory and world population remain distinct.

## NRF-L02 — Scratch scope

S owns only the sequential temporary work of one synchronous JSON encoding operation. It cannot escape, retain sources, cross an await, reopen after close or fund output/transport bodies. This intentionally excludes general arenas and request-wide pooling whose overlapping lifetimes are different. Extend only for a separately traced real consumer and an isolated comparison; never suppress actual wrapper-header custody. Count additional native-boundary calls as well as avoided wrappers.

## NRF-L03 — Capture-sharing scope

P initially covers consecutive compatible embodied periodic SSE publications, not creator inspection, bootstrap, reconnect, command responses, different observers or different permission scopes. It shares raw facts, not complete views, tokens, deltas or disclosure leases. Preserve existing stream order. One current group is prepared at a time and no capture cache survives a publication pass. This contains memory lifetime, fairness and authorization risk; broader reuse needs its own complete key and consumer evidence.

The group also binds the native handle, generation and accepted cut at acquisition. Reuse and final shared publication require that same cut; changed progress retires reuse rather than attaching a newer host revision to old facts. Use existing service scalar progress, not a fresh full capture or SQL lookup. This optimization-only restriction leaves ordinary scoped disclosure unchanged. Its lost hits and group bookkeeping count in P's cost.

P's reuse budget is the current pass's existing `nextPublicationAt` in `native-player-http.ts::schedule` (currently set from the monotonic clock plus 50 ms), not an existing hard pass timeout. Before creating a shared group and before lending it to another recipient, compare the current monotonic clock with that captured cutoff. At or beyond it, stop group reuse and serve remaining recipients through the ordinary fresh-capture path in original order. Do not refresh the cutoff for each group, add a timer, cancel an active recipient or shorten its original operation deadline. An active borrow and its independently retained output remain funded until their actual consumers finish; the cutoff bounds new reuse, not total response lifetime. Final source/permission checks still decide whether any output may be disclosed. A changed publisher cadence changes this derived cutoff, not a fictional-world rule.

## NRF-L04 — Parallel family and pool

R initially covers pure status-rate selection for the existing supported family. Effects, reductions, RNG, identity, reception and mutation remain ordered in their original owners. It uses only the existing bounded host pool and performs no blocking external work inside a range. These are execution boundaries, not claims that all other work is inherently serial. Another family requires a scoped safe decomposition and isolated comparison.

Initial experimental scheduling uses one worker inline, fewer than twice the selected worker count inline, and at most four times that count in one prepared batch. These are reversible starting values to measure, not player/cell limits. No per-actor thread, second pool or permanent one-cell/one-core mapping is introduced.

Before borrowed-state joins, R0 permits only one runnable outer bridge call per world. Waiting calls share the existing host outstanding limit and keep their input/byte ownership outside the compute pool; inner rate jobs do not reacquire it. No independently admitted queue or busy/retry loop is added. This prevents same-world lock reentry through Rayon's work stealing. [Technical section 5.4](../projects/native-runtime-foundations-tech-design.md#54-use-the-existing-host-pool-safely) owns dispatch, completion and shutdown. R0 remains separately measured prerequisite work, not an assertion of current delivery.

## NRF-L05 — Evidence and adoption

Every uncertain performance mechanism is an isolated experiment. Freeze its evaluation targets and complete-work accounting before measurement and retain failures. Under Mike's November 13 instruction, these numerical targets guide investigation and candidate decisions; missing one does not stop the remaining designed implementation:

- **J:** reproducibly lower complete affected-consumer CPU beyond repeat variation, without meaningful response/resource regression. Its small helper consolidation does not need the overall Rust-adoption improvement threshold.
- **S/P:** target at least 10% lower median complete qualifying-workload CPU, consistently beyond repeat variation, or closure of a demonstrated binding resource failure with the same profile in both arms. Select the question before timing. The resource route targets at most 5% additional complete CPU per work unit; evaluate latency and single/distinct-client or tiny-scene regressions separately.
- **R:** target existing E2's at least 10% throughput/completion-tail benefit, at most 5% additional complete CPU per work unit and no tiny-scene regression beyond variation. Compare serial extraction, R0 dispatch and R1 parallelism independently, then the entire candidate against its original baseline. Prerequisite overhead is included, not hidden by comparing only post-R0 kernels.

Actual correctness, usable latency, admission, persistence and cleanup remain required. A stalled world, refused ordinary movement, unfinished reply, failed save or hung shutdown needs diagnosis and repair; preserve the failure and distinguish a defect, real capacity limit, unsuccessful optimization and inconclusive experiment. For the resource question, rerun the previously failing journey with both arms at 2 GiB and assess cost on matched work both actually complete; extra allowance is not an optimization gain. A failed control's partial work is not a denominator. Without matched cost evidence the result is inconclusive. Live-model quality, fixture correctness and human enjoyment are distinct evidence.

[Technical section 7.2](../projects/native-runtime-foundations-tech-design.md#72-required-correctness) requires the actual cognition-boundary differential case for affected encoding/scheduling consumers. Preserve required evidence, prompt/schema/reference meaning and admitted reply effects; a different stochastic response is not itself a deterministic correctness comparison. No optimization may qualify by quietly reducing context, dropping a reply or retuning behavior.

J cannot claim improvement from helper counts; S/P cannot free still-live allocations or omit inconvenient connections. Combined gains are measured, not added. Complete each coherent implementation batch, review and check its actual callers, then run its isolated AWS comparison before another optimization batch. Investigate material regressions and repair or revert the specific unsuccessful change while continuing other work. Correct, simpler code may be retained when performance is neutral; added complexity needs justification. A concretely disproved premise supports a documented candidate rejection or non-activation, not completion or abandonment of the overall implementation. Remove rejected paths and unused scaffolding, including R-only dispatch work unless independently justified. Keep historical criteria/results frozen, with the new policy applied only to new comparisons. No compatibility switch remains to support an earlier codebase; Native-to-Native gains do not replace overall NSC qualification.

## NRF-L06 — No new gameplay approximation or scale cap

No crowd-blindness switch, weakened defense, changed perception cadence, pushing rule, admission queue or 1,000/5,000-player guarantee is selected. Physical exclusion, recipient count and connected effects remain different dimensions. Compromises can be designed explicitly by their existing world/product owners; technical overload cannot silently invent them.

## NRF-L07 — No physical eviction without a complete consumer

Current cold-history support does not permit unloading arbitrary physical entities. A partial physical-region consumer must supply exact obligations/dependencies, coverage and activation before exposing absence-like lookups. Existing complete internal reads remain fast and synchronous. This avoids premature generic residency machinery while keeping the actual future extension boundary explicit.

## NRF-L08 — Existing identity, precision and lifecycle

Preserve logical world-scoped identity, slot generations, authoritative f64 units, exact current-format continuation and scoped permission/source checks. No legacy reader, origin-rebasing project or second seed-overlay engine is added. Future data/library changes must qualify their affected conversion, not reinterpret a numerical location or machine address as identity.

## NRF-L09 — Send availability and warning interpretation

The composer's request retains the existing two temporal checks around the application allowance read. An initial refusal ends after the first check. Each read is limited to its actual observing player and requested role. Absent/self targets need no enumeration. Resolving a public token can require scanning that observer's eligible set and hashing candidate references once. Keep one funded role binding across the allowance lookup. Recheck its current lease/life or exact remembered membership, including association loss while a different role survives. Those inputs have no new fixed count limit. Truncation could reject a legitimate addressee or change which role a token proves. Apply existing admission to actual input, workspace and results and preserve cancellation and release. No global lookup by guessed identity, long-lived token map, cross-tab sharing or larger memory allowance is selected. NRF04's periodic SSE reuse is a separate experiment.

The interpretation is initially specific to complete heard speech and the existing earned place family. Grammar, time and certainty belong to the [base-world law](../worlds/base/perception-recognition.md#ordinary-spoken-warning-policy); eligibility, proof custody and chronology belong to the observation owner. Work follows actual recipients of speech and the current complete per-listener label index. Many listeners or long utterances can still cost more. Reuse existing limits and admission. Silently skipping a listener, truncating place history or broadening a place match would change play. No population promise, new sensory law, new persistent collection or compatibility format is introduced.

Both candidates require the [controlled cognition differential](../projects/native-runtime-foundations-tech-design.md#72-required-correctness) against their own unchanged controls. For NRF07, freeze the primary question as combined CPU per completed GET in the fixed request mix, reporting eligible, refused and canceled counts separately, with ordinary CPU/game-second, latency, memory and sustained progress as guards. Claim a benefit only on equivalent work, consistently outside repeat variation. If a more complex narrow route fails to earn its cost, revise or remove it. NRF08 is an ownership repair with a performance-equivalence experiment. Retain a simpler static composition with equivalent player/private records and no material reproducible regression in the affected real caller and ordinary window. If it costs materially more, locate the extra pass/copy, simplify the implementation and recompare; the requirement that installed policy owns meaning remains. Overlapping ranges or failed runs are inconclusive for speed. These rules add no arbitrary percentage target. [Technical §9.4](../projects/native-runtime-foundations-tech-design.md#94-isolate-the-cost-and-decide) specifies measurements. The 2 GiB profile, separate accounting, historical failures and overall NSC gates keep their existing owners.

## NRF-L10 — Shared bridge products

For [NRF09–NRF13](../projects/native-runtime-foundations-bridge.md), predeclare the affected caller, removed product or pass, equal-work denominator and control before timing. Preserve exact accepted reads, independently held bytes, bounded transfers, source freshness, the paid request and receipt contracts, and one-second background persistence. An optimization needs qualified equivalent gameplay and a reproducible benefit beyond repeat variation in the selected complete caller or ordinary window. Report the other window and any regressions. An ownership repair has its own correctness exit and a separate performance-equivalence comparison. A refused, stalled or incomplete run cannot support a relative speed claim. [NRF-L05](#nrf-l05--evidence-and-adoption) owns retention/removal decisions. Counts and exact bytes can qualify the narrower structural claim independently. These experiments introduce no new allowance, gameplay cap or overall Rust adoption target.

For [NRF15 and NRF16](../projects/native-runtime-foundations-bridge.md#nrf15--release-parser-workspace-after-decoding), admit the actual peak overlap before allocation. Release only bytes attributable to storage whose lifetime has ended or transfer their custody to the surviving owner. Retained backing, independent consumers and wrapper headers awaiting normal collection remain funded. Apply NRF-L01 to current controls and label any historical-profile comparison separately. Record the primary resource measure and acceptable cost variation from the control before timing. Retain a more complex handoff only for a repeatable benefit to the affected caller or ordinary play, with matching cognition, progress and no material CPU, latency or memory regression. A lower reservation on paper alone cannot justify extra recurring work. Admission headroom, completed gameplay and physical RAM each require their own evidence.

## NRF-L11 — Concurrent player publication

NRF14 is a host scheduling experiment. Freeze a control with one active projection and a candidate with **at most two** per service (also bounded by configured request capacity), across ordinary state GETs, event-stream bootstrap and periodic views. Width two is the smallest test of independent progress. Each live stream has one publication in flight, with at most one outstanding refresh indication. Preserve the existing total waiting-capacity bound, admission timeout, fairness, connection/byte limits, current transient allowance and separate pending-save accounting. The candidate may pin two full attempts with SQL overlays and ancillary reads. A freed scheduler slot does not release still-owned output, optional SQL loads or transport buffers. Record actual object, header and RSS peaks and settled/unknown residuals.

Keep the existing 50 ms publication-start cadence, 15 s heartbeat, 30 s undrained-stream timeout and one-second ordinary save scheduling. These govern server operations; they are independent of simulated time, rendering cadence and authored perception. Fifty characters exercises density; it grants no admission exception, per-world population ceiling or hosted capacity promise. Sparse audiences can still cost a full connection scan. A radius does not establish who perceived an event. Additional worker threads, query connections, spatial indexes or concurrency changes require separate evidence.

Primary comparison: p95 across actions from the mover sending input to the **last expected observer decoding the changed position**. All 49 expected observers must receive each isolated dense-case movement by the frozen deadline. A missing observer makes the run incomplete; the deliberately blocked-reader case is separate. Compare to single-player and two-person results and the ordinary gameplay targets. Before timing record acceptable variation/guard bands from the unchanged control and existing latency/resource limits. Use §7.3's three interleaved repetitions per arm on independent databases. Require matching world progress, participations, outcomes, privacy, latency witnesses and normal persistence. Report every failure. Retain a more complex scheduler only for a repeatable improvement beyond control variation without material single-player, command, game-speed, CPU, memory, save or reconnect regression. Review first-to-last spread and ordering fairness even when the aggregate improves. Privacy, stream order, bounded lifetimes, personally earned observations and the controlled NPC request/response comparison are required passes. Failed equivalence or overlapping timing evidence leaves performance inconclusive; document the failure and keep the simpler qualified path. Freeze a different width or mechanism before any new isolated comparison. [Technical recipe](../projects/native-runtime-foundations-tech-design.md#11-multiplayer-update-delivery).
