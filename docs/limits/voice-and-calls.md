# Optional voice and private calls: limits and constraints

[Product proposal](../projects/voice-and-calls-feature-spec.md) · [Listening and dictation delivery](../maintainers/hearing-and-speech.md#he07--optional-listening-and-reviewed-dictation) · [Private-call delivery](../maintainers/narration-and-conversations.md#nc23--private-submitted-turn-calls) · [Tracking rules](README.md)

**Proposed DG26 envelopes, October 7, 2026.** No audio/media route or new physical speech law is implemented by this document. The values below are selected product inputs, not measured capacity or optimal settings. Current [hearing](hearing-and-speech.md), [private text](narration.md#tx-l01--selected-affordance-and-correspondents), [participation](multiplayer.md), [spending](ai-execution.md) and [history](memory.md) retain their shared limits. Authorization, exact evidence and actual accounting are correctness obligations, not tuning candidates.

## VC-L01 — Selected media families

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** Optional reading of committed permitted text and reviewed dictation are independently complete offerings. A later two-human private submitted-turn slate call needs actual DG24 contacts/devices/private text; resident participation requires its separate private-evidence and agency qualification. Text alone completes a call. No continuous raw human voice, group call, video, voicemail, recorded-call library, speakerphone, translation, cloning or new ND16 hearing family is selected. These restrictions focus the first benefit on easier listening, entry and wanted coordination rather than a media platform. Expand only for a useful complete player journey with its own rights, privacy, cost and recovery evidence. Owner: HE07/NC23; [stages](../projects/voice-and-calls-feature-spec.md#3-selected-scope-and-delivery-order).

## VC-L02 — Permitted non-spatial reading

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** First reading is non-spatial and uses legitimate stock voices with accessible first-use disclosure of generated reading. Unknown sources use the player's generic reading voice; partial speech reads only recorded fragments with one neutral unclear cue per coalesced gap. Neither voice identity nor gap duration may expose the hidden original. Private text becomes eligible only after actual permitted presentation through NC22's current Read/device/active-play checks: arrival, an inbox row or call acceptance is insufficient, and reading old history cannot acquire new unread messages. Character reading excludes provisional NPC replies. This narrow rendition family avoids requiring new location/recognition laws; current source authority is nonnegotiable. Revisit spatial or richer performance only when useful and disclosure-equivalent. Owner: HE07.1; [reading behavior](../projects/voice-and-calls-feature-spec.md#4-listen-to-a-line-without-delaying-play).

## VC-L03 — Automatic reading and bounded backlog

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Automatic reading starts off and, when deliberately enabled, covers new direct replies in the selected conversation only. Admit one active rendition and one pending rendition; further replies remain in text with a quiet indication. Pending work expires after fifteen seconds of foreground, unpaused playback opportunity, avoiding undispatched synthesis while retaining real dispatched costs. Stop clears active/pending automatic reading; hiding the document, closing the selected conversation, changing world/control or losing source access clears the applicable stream. Return never replays a backlog. Manual history selection and local playback speed do not change evidence or world time. These bounds protect attention without discarding words. Revisit from actual missed/abandoned listening and complete processing cost; HR04 caption queues remain independent. Owner: HE07.2; [queue](../projects/voice-and-calls-feature-spec.md#42-queue-and-interruption).

## VC-L04 — Deliberate bounded capture

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Permit one unfinished dictation/candidate per account across tabs and no dictation queue. Starting another requires finishing, discarding or cancelling the current capture/candidate while preserving typed text; closing a local candidate does not release an uncertain dispatched processing obligation. Capture has hold and start/stop controls, no automatic voice activation, and ends at thirty real seconds or two MiB of admitted encoded audio, whichever comes first. Reaching the bound retains the clip and offers Transcribe rather than submitting words. Ordinary deliberate Stop completes the one transcription action already described by Dictate. Loss of foreground tab/window or device stops capture and retains only a usable bounded local clip; ordinary keyboard focus changes during editing are not a background-capture trigger. Starting capture stops game read-aloud to avoid recording its output. A cap or recovery never sends a game action. The envelope contains one short draft and its input cost; offered devices/languages still need qualification. Owner: HE07.3; [capture journey](../projects/voice-and-calls-feature-spec.md#51-capture-journey).

## VC-L05 — Temporary input and existing text envelopes

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** An unsubmitted local clip expires after five real minutes with visible notice; account/world/controller change or draft discard deletes it earlier. It is not an exportable recording library. Remote processing uses the actual disclosed route's retention, which is distinct from application retention. A transcript remains an editable candidate in its original draft context and cannot replace newer edits or appear in another recipient/call/timeline. Existing HR02 Talk/native/model and TX-L03 private-text bounds remain their sole owners; over-limit text stays editable, with no split, truncation or paid shortening. Temporary capture reduces retained sensitive input while preserving correction; extend only for demonstrated accessibility/usefulness with admitted retention costs. Owner: HE07.3/HE07.4; [draft behavior](../projects/voice-and-calls-feature-spec.md#53-what-those-words-mean).

## VC-L06 — Quiet requests and sender-owned rate

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** A request lasts thirty real seconds, with one pending outgoing request per character and at most three retained incoming requests per character. Admit at most three attempts per initiating account per rolling real minute, and at most one from that initiating account to the selected contact per rolling real minute. The latter is directional: an unseen A-to-B request cannot consume B's visible B-to-A allowance. Admitted ignored attempts count; recipient capacity, blocks or private unavailability produce the same pending/expiry outcome. Real request/rate counters survive fictional restore and changing slates. Requests carry no custom pre-answer payload and never ring or wake someone. In an installed call profile, accepted contacts may request initially; existing thread mute suppresses cues. Call requests Off clears pending request presentation and suppresses future presentation, while leaving an accepted call intact; re-enabling presents future requests only. These bounds limit attention pressure without revealing another person's state; wider incoming load remains separately qualified. Owner: NC23.2; [request lifecycle](../projects/voice-and-calls-feature-spec.md#62-request-accept-and-end).

## VC-L07 — Accepted participation and shared private turns

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** First calls have two participants, with one active call per character and one active human call per controlling account. An incoming request cannot evict accepted work; accepting another requires deliberate ending. Actual fresh acceptance intentionally discloses that participation and nothing about unrelated account/world status. Calls use the existing private transcript/composer and TX-L04's sender-owned rolling send allowance, including its per-sender–recipient and overall-per-sender scopes. There is no extra call allowance or artificial minimum spacing between turns inside an admitted burst. Submitted does not mean read, and bystanders gain no local speech evidence. These constraints avoid competing conversations and doubled message load; group participation is a separately selected product. Owner: NC23.2/NC23.3; [turns](../projects/voice-and-calls-feature-spec.md#63-turns-and-attention).

## VC-L08 — No arbitrary active-call duration

**Status: Proposed no independent duration cap · Restrictiveness: Medium; aggregate capacity unqualified.** An accepted call has no arbitrary maximum duration while both participants remain connected and authorized. This selected written-turn channel has no standing paid media/model session, but still consumes ordinary connection/state resources. It neither pauses needs nor forces attention. If an implementation requires paid idle media, the offering must be reconsidered and disclosed before use. An account-level active-call cap does not cap all accounts or worlds; aggregate hosted admission belongs to the existing platform owners. This avoids a needless conversational countdown without promising unlimited service. Owner: NC23.6; [attention and duration](../projects/voice-and-calls-feature-spec.md#63-turns-and-attention).

## VC-L09 — No resumable live session or automatic recovery purchase

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Pause, disconnection, departure, lost device/control/access or communication capability ends the active call, with no private cause disclosed. Current-format saves preserve eligible private text and actual invitation/acceptance/end history at the coherent cut, but do not resume an active call, pending request, microphone or audio backlog. Return requires a fresh deliberate request. Existing current blocks, erasure, real request/rate counters and actual costs remain effective under DG25. Explicit permitted recovery can retain a same-context draft without automatic sending or paid retry. These boundaries keep stale participation from becoming fresh consent; longer reconnection behavior needs a wanted and independently qualified change. Owner: HE07.2/HE07.3, NC23.4; [lifecycle](../projects/voice-and-calls-feature-spec.md#7-lifecycle-and-recovery).

## VC-L10 — Explicit media funding and reuse

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Remote media requires an identified world-host payer, enabled capability, finite aggregate media allowance and delegated account admission before processing. No currency amount, public price or numerical account share is chosen here; missing/zero allowance leaves remote media unavailable and text usable. Media does not silently spend an unrelated resident's cognition budget. A purchase processes one bounded recording or one rendition, not a new character reply; uncertain/cancelled dispatched work keeps its real accounting. Reuse requires matching permitted words, identity disclosure, voice rights and private scope. Local playback of an available authorized asset purchases no new synthesis; browser exposure alone does not prove processing is local. These choices contain optional cost and disclose actual responsibility. Owner: HE07.4 and existing AI/platform owners; [economics](../projects/voice-and-calls-feature-spec.md#8-economics-and-bounded-work).

## VC-L11 — Growing history, media and preparation

**Status: Proposed; independent aggregate bounds unselected · Restrictiveness: Too liberal if treated as an unrestricted service.** Small playback/request queues, short capture and per-sender rates do not cap total accounts, accepted history, private invitation records, permission-specific renditions, candidate preparation, simultaneous processing or bytes delivered to many listeners. TX-L08 and existing memory/data owners retain text/history growth; no duplicate history expiry is introduced. Apart from temporary raw captures, this product does not choose a numerical media-cache byte/count/age policy, aggregate processing concurrency or complete source-preparation allowance. Before a route is offered, HE07.4/NC23.6 must select finite operational admission and retained-media handling, qualify current erasure/rights and report complete processing/delivery/storage cost. Optional work may be declined before dispatch; required hearing evidence cannot be truncated to meet a media budget. This explicit gap is not an unlimited-hosting promise. Wider participation requires DG29's separate measured qualification.
