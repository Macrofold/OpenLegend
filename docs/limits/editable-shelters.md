# Editable shelters: selected profile and expansion limits

**Flat one/two-bay profile qualified October 6; corrected contact and single-bay lean-to qualified October 7, 2026.** The [feature specification](../projects/editable-shelters-feature-spec.md) owns behavior, [technical counterpart](../projects/editable-shelters-tech-design.md) owns contracts/source mapping, and [base-world light canopies](../worlds/base/editable-shelters.md) owns the selected authored content and tuning. [SH01–SH06](../maintainers/editable-shelters.md) is the detailed delivery breakdown under ND07/narrow ND08 and [INV-6.4](../maintainers/inventions-and-world-evolution.md#inv-6--composable-materials-assemblies-and-passive-world-processes). Existing object, spatial, state, action, invention and scalability owners retain their shared limits. Selected flat/sloping bounds below are implemented; wider consumers remain proposed. [Evidence](../verification/editable-shelters.md) qualifies a bounded native workload and the ordinary UI journey, not settlement capacity.

## SH-L01 — Initial construction family

**Current for the selected shelter profile · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Begin with a ground-supported, single-story open canopy using light supports, reversible bindings and flexible roof sections. The October 5 profile selects one or two connected bays and four orthogonal orientations. The delivered bounds allow at most 32 protected material parts per assembly, counting both installed material and unreclaimed lowered salvage, and two covering layers at any point. Actual clear space is 1.5 × 2 metres per bay; two adjacent bays have a combined footprint determined by their actual shared posts and coverings, rather than a guaranteed empty 3 × 2 metre rectangle; minimum usable clearance beneath the cover is 2.2 metres. Each actual covering is 1.8 × 2.4 metres. The current person's 1.75 × 0.56 resting envelope plus 0.10 on each side fits naturally; arrival still rechecks real occupancy. A different body must qualify before that use is offered. The roof is non-walkable. The flat bay remains first; the separately authorized four-corner cloak lean-to is qualified for one bay with actual short/tall stock under the same family. Light walls/doors, more bays, arbitrary cantilevers, heavy roofs, moving structures, digging and multistory loads require their own qualified extension. The protected count tightens PX05's installed-only proposal to bound one root's salvage without deleting material; reclaim before adding more. At the boundary, retain the plan and explain a supported alternative before material installation. Revisit using real usable area, material span, gaps, occupied edits and concentrated construction; these numbers do not measure a settlement's capacity.

## SH-L02 — Initial support failure

**Current for the selected shelter profile · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Losing required support ends the flexible roof's coverage and leaves its actual material recoverable at the site in the declared lowered/draped state. The initial lightweight failure is non-injurious and non-obstructing for every supported occupant posture/activity and valid exit; it does not automatically equip or transfer the fabric. This does not apply to heavy beams, stone roofs, occupied upper floors or stored loads; those configurations remain unsupported pending a real failure family. Revisit with coherent damage, occupancy, material, background and restoration evidence. Do not infer arbitrary collapse physics from a connection model.

## SH-L03 — Rain and material applicability

**Current for the selected shelter profile · Restrictiveness: Safe.** First exposure uses local vertical rain, actual footprint/gaps and sequential transmission through admitted intact layers. Detailed tears/damage transmission remains a later admitted consumer; unusable material cannot supply an intact cover. No wind-driven rain, runoff collection, flooding, immersion, snow load, thermal comfort or automatic fire effects. Moisture initially applies only to selected cloth/prepared-fiber materials and supported installed, ground, carried/worn forms (SH-L13); a later tinder consequence requires SH-L04's separate choice. Nested-container exposure needs an admitted container/content rule. Unsupported properties are explicit rather than waterproof/immune defaults. Revisit with a concrete supported consumer; the 50% layer example in the spec is illustrative arithmetic, not selected tuning.

## SH-L04 — Recovery before material penalties

**Current for the selected shelter profile · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Stage 1 is a wetting/coverage proof without new punitive moisture effects. The recommended complete player release adds coarse ambient drying, understandable expressive construction and actual chosen use of the space through supported activities. This revises the earlier proposal that required a dry-tinder condition for that release: useful construction must not depend on introducing a recurring chore. The dry-tinder condition remains an optional later authored survival challenge, selected only after positive shelter play and the complete protection/drying/use/recovery route are qualified. If selected, its law applies coherently to the world and explicitly revises the existing lighting family; there is no per-player exemption. This selected release adds no fire consequence. SH-L09 records the delivered scene's drying rule; any later usable-tinder boundary remains unselected. No automatic rot, illness, damage, sleep penalty, extinguishing or recurring decay is added by this scope. Revisit using actual choice, preparation burden, viable alternatives and complete use/absence cases.

## SH-L05 — Building, storage and work growth

**Current consumer policy · Restrictiveness: Medium.** No new universal maximum home count, household size or total building size is selected. This is not unlimited service: existing native/spatial/object/storage/work admission remains controlling, and SH-L01's local part/layer bounds remain controlling at admission. The first profile permits one active construction edit per assembly, without an automatic second-edit queue; unrelated ordinary actions remain governed by their actual conflicts. Pending construction uses existing action/hold lifetimes rather than indefinite reservations. Ordinary known-plan inspection, preview, construction, moisture and reclaim require no new model call; optional interpretation cannot amplify into one call per part or weather step and has no automatic paid retry. Revisit using a settlement, concentrated edits, layered covers, applicable contents and long absence.

## SH-L06 — Rearrangement, salvage and upkeep

**Current for the selected shelter profile · Restrictiveness: Medium.** Intact detachable parts can be reused as the same conditioned objects. The initial tying method returns the actual surviving cord rather than consuming one after every rearrangement; it has no automatic material loss. This clarifies the earlier general consumed-binding example. Consumed material and destructive salvage still follow any separately admitted method's finite actual outcome; no universal refund percentage is selected. A valid carrying/ground destination is selected before a reclaim phase, and already detached material remains at its actual recoverable location if later pickup fails. The initial world adds no automatic durability loss or abandonment deletion solely from absence. Real required storage/service policies remain separate from fictional wear. Revisit salvage and optional decay independently using material-flow, repair usefulness, unattended burden and service evidence.

## SH-L07 — Finite introductory material endowment

**Current for the selected shelter profile · Restrictiveness: Medium.** One new authored shelter scene supplies one large wearable cloak, one spare nonwearable cloth, six qualified light posts and eight actual reusable cords. Exactly two additional tall posts are a separate finite new-scene/material grant for the single-bay slope, never a resized or replenished kit. One flat bay uses four posts/one cover/four cords; two bays share two posts and use six posts/two covers/eight cords. No automatic replenishment, ordinary-world retroactive grant or conversion of current supple branches into structural timber. The cloak wears through AV02's qualified back port without a second equipment store. This avoids a textile/forestry industry prerequisite while preserving material conservation. Revisit quantities and material sources after players can build, use, change and reclaim both arrangements.

## SH-L08 — Initial work timing

**Current for the selected shelter profile · Restrictiveness: Medium.** Authored game-time durations are 120 seconds to place one post, 240 seconds to fasten one cover with its four bindings, 120 seconds to untie/lower a cover and 60 seconds to remove an unloaded post. Approach is separate. Preview changes are free; completed work is not undone. No new mandatory tool, real-time waiting gate, digging, leveling or cutting is implied. Revisit through ordinary action pacing, interruptions, material holds and affordable revision; these are not measured construction times.

## SH-L09 — First moisture and drying scene

**Current for the selected shelter profile · Restrictiveness: Safe.** A controlled scene uses one 20-game-minute vertical shower followed by dry weather. Intact admitted weather cloth transmits zero rain through its actual covered extent; gaps receive rain. Eligible cloth and exposed prepared fiber retain moisture from zero to one. Full incident rain saturates a fully exposed dry item in 30 game minutes; zero incident rain dries a saturated eligible item in 90 game minutes. Partial rain scales wetting; this initial simplification chooses wetting while exposed and drying when no rain reaches the material. No simultaneous evaporation, wind, heat, water collection or automatically protected nested contents. It changes no current campfire/craft/sleep consequence. Revisit using readable contrasts, actual layering, split/merge material state, complete drying and absence. Physical research supports separating processes, not these balance numbers.

## SH-L10 — First builder and later home use

**Current for the selected shelter profile · Restrictiveness: Medium.** The first prepared scene adds a site/assembly construction grant for one builder and uses carried material with actual authority for the selected use: the designated kit or qualifying builder-produced material from eligible inputs. Another person's contribution needs a transfer permitting this use or a qualified material grant; current handover alone changes custody, and unsupported grants leave that input unavailable. Carrying after ordinary pickup, physical access, a home name or declared title alone is insufficient. Every alteration/reclaim route preserves the installed parts' restriction. Visitors keep ordinary movement/rest/speech and actual container access, without automatic alteration rights. Cooperative editing, a remembered-place association and unlocked light walls/door are separate later consumers, not a first-release household package. A later preferred-home reference is one optional personal navigation association, not a cap on owned buildings and never a change to login, respawn or Recover. Revisit through real visitors, interrupted work, permission changes and useful return; preserve scoped knowledge.

The grant lives in the existing operational authority owner, outside rewindable gameplay saves. Current account/control scope and grant revision fence every phase commit/restore; revocation releases unfinished holds and preserves completed structure. This is delivered operational authority, not a claim that current container/creator access already grants construction. Missing new permission or its retention allowance refuses new work before stock changes. Wider editor/input-transfer choices remain [stage blockers](../projects/editable-shelters-tech-design.md#101-consequential-unresolved-owner-decisions).

## SH-L11 — Qualified geometry and reachable work

**Current for the selected shelter profile · Restrictiveness: Medium.** [The world geometry owner](../worlds/base/editable-shelters.md#qualified-geometry-for-px05) selects 0.10-metre post sections, 2.30-metre kit heights, two actual binding sockets per post, 0.02-metre cover collision thickness and at least 0.10-metre unused sheet edge beyond anchor lines along the plane. The single-bay lean-to uses exactly two separately granted actual 2.40-metre posts and two actual 2.30-metre kit posts; it does not resize the kit. Both physical body clearance and displayed rest extent plus 0.10 metres on each side must fit. Flexible sheets may coincide only at the admitted bounded seam/replacement layer, with stable identity ordering of equal-height transmission; this does not exempt rigid solids from collisions. These are explicit coarse family choices, not measured cloth mechanics. Current handling reach remains controlling, rather than a new unlimited reach grant. Refuse an unsupported span, lower-edge clearance, socket, stance or body before effects. Revisit using real native geometry/reach checks, body variants, part replacement and UI use evidence; revise actual materials and layout together if they fail.

The qualified joint also admits a narrow cloth/post-tip contact band and accessible exterior-face binding work points, defined in the world geometry owner. This prevents the cloth's own collision thickness from invalidating its intended attachment. Work considers at most four face-aligned stances per binding and retains actual reach, clearance, support and line-of-effect. This does not add sockets, suppress whole-part collisions, create a finer universal grid or qualify a different body's reach. Contact outside that band, an unrelated post or an obstructed stance remains a refusal. The October 6 design review replaces nominal corner-distance evidence with a proposed clear-face reach calculation; runtime contact, all four orientations, reachable/blocked/occupied stances and current restoration are qualified in the separate lean-to follow-on, while the completed flat-release evidence is retained. Exact finite prism coverage/collision and normal thickness remain distinct from the conservative minimum underside. Full supported ground includes the interior, not just four corners.

The generic work-point query accepts at most twelve supplied stance candidates to bound route preparation; this family supplies only four. The builder must also stand clear of the prospective post or covering, before it exists. A hypothetical 2.5-metre body refuses the current lean-to rather than installing cloth through that body; this is a safety refusal, not qualification of a larger builder.

## SH-L13 — Exposed material forms and homogeneous condition

**Current for the selected shelter profile · Restrictiveness: Medium.** The first moisture profile explicitly models installed cloth, directly carried folded cloth, worn cloak, non-solid lowered/reclaimed ground cloth and homogeneous prepared fiber carried or on exposed ground. [The world owner](../worlds/base/editable-shelters.md#initial-exposed-forms-and-condition-labels) selects their coarse footprints and condition labels. Ground cloth supplies no overhead protection. A fiber lot has one average moisture value; split copies its exact law/value, while merging requires equal condition and pins, compatible provenance and no held/referenced stock. Unequal wet/dry lots remain separate; there is no resetting, weighted mixing or per-fiber simulation. This deliberately narrows the current general refusal to split/merge state-bearing items only for the admitted fiber consumer.

An active eligible material cannot enter unsupported nested exposure before a real container/content transmission consumer exists. This is an implemented refusal, with direct carrying/exposed-ground alternatives, rather than freezing elapsed moisture or claiming a bag is sealed. It affects only material participating in the new process, not unrelated existing bag contents or retroactively enabled old-world stock. The full reclaim/support-failure journey includes supported ground material, so condition continues after detachment. Revisit the restriction when the existing bag owner admits concrete outer-to-inner transmission, affected contents and complete state/time integrity. Individual covers/posts/cords do not merge; ordinary weather adds no damage, repair or mechanical penalty.
