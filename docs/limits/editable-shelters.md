# Editable shelters: proposed product limits

**Baseline: researched product proposal, October 3, expanded October 5, 2026; no runtime support or capacity is changed.** The [feature specification](../projects/editable-shelters-feature-spec.md) owns behavior, and [base-world light canopies](../worlds/base/editable-shelters.md) owns the selected authored content and tuning. ND07/narrow ND08 and [INV-6.4](../maintainers/inventions-and-world-evolution.md#inv-6--composable-materials-assemblies-and-passive-world-processes) retain design/delivery routing. Existing object, spatial, state, action, invention and scalability owners retain their shared limits.

## SH-L01 — Initial construction family

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Begin with a ground-supported, single-story open canopy using light supports, reversible bindings and flexible roof sections. The October 5 profile selects one or two connected bays, four orthogonal orientations, a maximum of 32 installed material parts and two covering layers at any point. Proposed clear space is 1.5 × 2 metres per bay; two adjacent bays have a combined footprint determined by their actual shared posts and coverings, rather than a guaranteed empty 3 × 2 metre rectangle; minimum usable clearance beneath the cover is 2.2 metres. Each actual covering is 1.8 × 2.4 metres. These are qualification targets: the supported rest footprint and margin must fit naturally, or bay and cover dimensions must be revised together before offering that use. The roof is non-walkable. A leaning roof, light walls/doors, more bays, arbitrary cantilevers, heavy roofs, moving structures, digging and multistory loads require their own qualified extension. At the boundary, retain the plan and explain a supported alternative before material consumption. Revisit using real usable area, material span, gaps, occupied edits and concentrated construction; these numbers do not measure a settlement's capacity.

## SH-L02 — Initial support failure

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Losing required support ends the flexible roof's coverage and leaves its actual material recoverable at the site in the declared lowered/draped state. The initial lightweight failure is non-injurious and non-obstructing for every supported occupant posture/activity and valid exit; it does not automatically equip or transfer the fabric. This does not apply to heavy beams, stone roofs, occupied upper floors or stored loads; those configurations remain unsupported pending a real failure family. Revisit with coherent damage, occupancy, material, background and restoration evidence. Do not infer arbitrary collapse physics from a connection model.

## SH-L03 — Rain and material applicability

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** First exposure uses local vertical rain, actual footprint/gaps, damage and sequential transmission through admitted layers. No wind-driven rain, runoff collection, flooding, immersion, snow load, thermal comfort or automatic fire effects. Moisture initially applies only to selected covering/fiber/tinder materials and supported carried/worn forms. Nested-container exposure needs an admitted container/content rule. Unsupported properties are explicit rather than waterproof/immune defaults. Revisit with a concrete supported consumer; the 50% layer example in the spec is illustrative arithmetic, not selected tuning.

## SH-L04 — Recovery before material penalties

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Stage 1 is a wetting/coverage proof without new punitive moisture effects. The recommended complete player release adds coarse ambient drying, understandable expressive construction and actual chosen use of the space through supported activities. This revises the earlier proposal that required a dry-tinder condition for that release: useful construction must not depend on introducing a recurring chore. The dry-tinder condition remains an optional later authored survival challenge, selected only after positive shelter play and the complete protection/drying/use/recovery route are qualified. If selected, its law applies coherently to the world and explicitly revises the existing lighting family; there is no per-player exemption. This is a proposed change, not current fire behavior. SH-L09 now records the initial scene's drying proposal; any later usable-tinder boundary remains unselected. No automatic rot, illness, damage, sleep penalty, extinguishing or recurring decay is added by this scope. Revisit using actual choice, preparation burden, viable alternatives and complete use/absence cases.

## SH-L05 — Building, storage and work growth

**Proposed consumer policy · Restrictiveness: Medium.** No new universal maximum home count, household size or total building size is selected. This is not unlimited service: existing native/spatial/object/storage/work admission remains controlling, and SH-L01's local part/layer bounds must qualify before release. The first profile permits one active construction edit per assembly, without an automatic second-edit queue; unrelated ordinary actions remain governed by their actual conflicts. Pending construction uses existing action/hold lifetimes rather than indefinite reservations. Ordinary known-plan inspection, preview, construction, moisture and reclaim require no new model call; optional interpretation cannot amplify into one call per part or weather step and has no automatic paid retry. Revisit using a settlement, concentrated edits, layered covers, applicable contents and long absence.

## SH-L06 — Rearrangement, salvage and upkeep

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** Intact detachable parts can be reused as the same conditioned objects. The initial tying method returns the actual surviving cord rather than consuming one after every rearrangement; it has no automatic material loss. This clarifies the earlier general consumed-binding example. Consumed material and destructive salvage still follow any separately admitted method's finite actual outcome; no universal refund percentage is selected. A valid carrying/ground destination is selected before a reclaim phase, and already detached material remains at its actual recoverable location if later pickup fails. The initial world adds no automatic durability loss or abandonment deletion solely from absence. Real required storage/service policies remain separate from fictional wear. Revisit salvage and optional decay independently using material-flow, repair usefulness, unattended burden and service evidence.

## SH-L07 — Finite introductory material endowment

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** One new authored shelter scene supplies one large wearable cloak, one spare nonwearable cloth, six qualified light posts and eight actual reusable cords. One bay uses four posts/one cover/four cords; two bays share two posts and use six posts/two covers/eight cords. No automatic replenishment, ordinary-world retroactive grant or conversion of current supple branches into structural timber. The cloak's wearing action is a new consumer, not current equipment support. This avoids a textile/forestry industry prerequisite while preserving material conservation. Revisit quantities and material sources after players can build, use, change and reclaim both arrangements.

## SH-L08 — Initial work proposal

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** Authored game-time proposals are 120 seconds to place one post, 240 seconds to fasten one cover with its four bindings, 120 seconds to untie/lower a cover and 60 seconds to remove an unloaded post. Approach is separate. Preview changes are free; completed work is not undone. No new mandatory tool, real-time waiting gate, digging, leveling or cutting is implied. Revisit through ordinary action pacing, interruptions, material holds and affordable revision; these are not measured construction times.

## SH-L09 — First moisture and drying scene

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** A controlled scene uses one 20-game-minute vertical shower followed by dry weather. Intact admitted weather cloth transmits zero rain through its actual covered extent; gaps receive rain. Eligible cloth and exposed prepared fiber retain moisture from zero to one. Full incident rain saturates a fully exposed dry item in 30 game minutes; zero incident rain dries a saturated eligible item in 90 game minutes. Partial rain scales wetting; this initial simplification chooses wetting while exposed and drying when no rain reaches the material. No simultaneous evaporation, wind, heat, water collection or automatically protected nested contents. It changes no current campfire/craft/sleep consequence. Revisit using readable contrasts, actual layering, split/merge material state, complete drying and absence. Physical research supports separating processes, not these balance numbers.

## SH-L10 — First builder and later home use

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** The first prepared scene adds a new proposed site/assembly construction grant for one builder and uses carried material with actual authority for the selected use: the designated kit or qualifying builder-produced material from eligible inputs. Another person's contribution needs a transfer permitting this use or a qualified material grant; current handover alone changes custody, and unsupported grants leave that input unavailable. Carrying after ordinary pickup, physical access, a home name or declared title alone is insufficient. Every alteration/reclaim route preserves the installed parts' restriction. Visitors keep ordinary movement/rest/speech and actual container access, without automatic alteration rights. Cooperative editing, a remembered-place association and unlocked light walls/door are separate later consumers, not a first-release household package. A later preferred-home reference is one optional personal navigation association, not a cap on owned buildings and never a change to login, respawn or Recover. Revisit through real visitors, interrupted work, permission changes and useful return; preserve scoped knowledge.
