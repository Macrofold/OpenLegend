# Editable shelters: proposed product limits

**Baseline: researched product proposal, October 3, 2026; no runtime support or capacity is changed.** The [feature specification](../projects/editable-shelters-feature-spec.md) owns behavior. ND07/narrow ND08 and [INV-6.4](../maintainers/inventions-and-world-evolution.md#inv-6--composable-materials-assemblies-and-passive-world-processes) retain design/delivery routing. Existing object, spatial, state, action, invention and scalability owners retain their shared limits.

## SH-L01 — Initial construction family

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Begin with a ground-supported, single-story modular bay using light supports, admitted bindings/anchors and flexible roof sections; extend through adjoining bays. The roof is initially non-walkable. Light walls/doors follow qualified geometry and use. No arbitrary cantilevers, heavy roofs, moving structures, digging or multistory loads are promised. Exact dimensions, material spans, orientations and part/work allowances require the complete SH-J cases. At the boundary, retain the plan and explain a supported alternative before material consumption. This bounds the first useful physical example without restricting all future authored worlds.

## SH-L02 — Initial support failure

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Losing required support ends the flexible roof's coverage and leaves its actual material recoverable at the site in the declared lowered/draped state. The initial lightweight failure is non-injurious and non-obstructing for every supported occupant posture/activity and valid exit; it does not automatically equip or transfer the fabric. This does not apply to heavy beams, stone roofs, occupied upper floors or stored loads; those configurations remain unsupported pending a real failure family. Revisit with coherent damage, occupancy, material, background and restoration evidence. Do not infer arbitrary collapse physics from a connection model.

## SH-L03 — Rain and material applicability

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** First exposure uses local vertical rain, actual footprint/gaps, damage and sequential transmission through admitted layers. No wind-driven rain, runoff collection, flooding, immersion, snow load, thermal comfort or automatic fire effects. Moisture initially applies only to selected covering/fiber/tinder materials and supported carried/worn forms. Nested-container exposure needs an admitted container/content rule. Unsupported properties are explicit rather than waterproof/immune defaults. Revisit with a concrete supported consumer; the 50% layer example in the spec is illustrative arithmetic, not selected tuning.

## SH-L04 — Recovery before material penalties

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Stage 1 is a wetting/coverage proof without new punitive moisture effects. The recommended complete player release adds coarse ambient drying and a qualified dry-tinder condition to the existing lighting family. This is a proposed change, not current fire behavior. Drying rates and usable-tinder boundaries remain unselected authored tuning. No automatic rot, illness, damage, sleep penalty, extinguishing or recurring decay is added by this scope. Revisit only with a real cause, recovery route and complete use/absence cases.

## SH-L05 — Building, storage and work growth

**Proposed consumer policy · Restrictiveness: Medium.** No new universal maximum home count, household size or total building size is selected. This is not unlimited service: existing native/spatial/object/storage/work admission remains controlling, and the new family's local part/connection/overlap/exposure bounds must qualify before release. Unsupported additional complexity is refused or narrowed before reliance. Pending construction uses existing action/hold lifetimes rather than indefinite reservations; optional explanations cannot amplify into one model call per part or weather step. Revisit using a settlement, concentrated edits, layered covers, applicable contents and long absence.

## SH-L06 — Rearrangement, salvage and upkeep

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** Intact detachable parts can be reused as the same conditioned objects. Consumed bindings and destructive salvage follow the admitted method's finite actual-material outcome; no universal refund percentage is selected. The initial world adds no automatic durability loss or abandonment deletion solely from absence. Real required storage/service policies remain separate from fictional wear. Revisit salvage and optional decay independently using material-flow, repair usefulness, unattended burden and service evidence.
