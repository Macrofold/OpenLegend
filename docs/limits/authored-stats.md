# Authored stats and checks: proposed product limits

**Baseline: researched product proposal, October 3, 2026; no runtime support or capacity is changed.** The [feature specification](../projects/authored-stats-feature-spec.md) owns behavior. [ND03](../maintainers/needs-design.md#nd03--world-authored-stats-checks-and-their-effects) retains design routing; EWF/INV and action/state owners retain delivery. The game-first review makes a useful competence effect conditional on an actual player need; deterministic effects are sufficient. The roof and dice rules below remain an optional worked candidate, not a first-playable prerequisite or a minimum release bundle. These proposed reference-world bounds do not become generic engine laws.

## ST-L01 — Initial competence and curve

**Optional worked candidate · Restrictiveness: Medium.** If selected for a useful action, one enduring Practical craft competence uses integer ranks 0–3 with explicit applicability and initial values. Two fair independent d6 plus competence and situation produce 10+ clean, 7–9 finishing-required and 6-or-less setback; the total modifier is −2 to +5. There is no separate automatic critical/fumble rule; +5 has no setback. Other names can use compatible supported meanings; arbitrary profiles and temporary competence rewrites require separate qualification. These figures preserve the original candidate for comparison, without requiring dice for competence authoring. A predictable effect on an existing supported action can be the complete first feature. Missing/inapplicable state is not implicitly zero. Select a curve only for a demonstrated play benefit; do not treat exact distributions as evidence of enjoyment.

## ST-L02 — Situational causes and help

**Optional worked candidate · Restrictiveness: Safe.** The reference has two categories, equipment suitability and working conditions/support, each contributing −1, 0 or +1. Each recognizes at most one favorable and one adverse admitted influence, canceling when both exist; equivalent descriptions do not stack. It has one principal and at most one actual helper, when that role is useful.

Use actual competence and relevant conditions at one clear consequential fastening step. Ordinary preparation retains its real progress. Help must be genuinely performed for the step, but presence throughout unrelated gathering or waiting is unnecessary. Before the unresolved step, real preparation or changed conditions may legitimately improve prospects. Resumption preserves actual work/materials and one unresolved attempt; a resolved result never changes. Missing prerequisites block or interrupt rather than becoming a bypassable penalty.

**Superseded proposal, October 3 game-first review:** removed the requirement that every favorable influence persist throughout all credited work while any adverse influence counts against the whole stage, together with its inherited whole-job condition history. That approximation could erase useful near-complete help and preserve irrelevant penalties while adding bookkeeping. Real damage, spent time/materials, required participation and one-result continuity remain protected. A later method requiring sustained contributions must justify and explain its own rule.

## ST-L03 — First action and work economics

**Optional worked candidate · Restrictiveness: Medium.** The rushed roof patch is available for evaluation only after shelter play is useful; it is no longer the selected first consumer or a dependency of useful stats. Its original arithmetic remains: positive duration T; clean completion at T; partial material requiring a chosen extra T of reliable finishing; setback after T without a completed patch; careful completion at 2T when prerequisites persist. Exact T and materials require qualification. The illustrative expected times assume each fresh setback attempt requires T; retained useful preparation may require a different complete-activity calculation rather than artificial repeated work.

Before adopting it, compare the real player's goal, pause/speed controls, helper time, competing activities and useful recovery with simpler predictable work. A stat may legitimately reward competence with reliable speed instead. No existing routine action becomes uncertain by default, and no second roll is added over an action's existing resolution of the same uncertainty. At an unsupported boundary, offer an existing method or explain missing support before spending resources. Equal method usage and a permanent failure floor are not requirements.

## ST-L04 — Attempts, repetition and result continuity

**Proposed consumer policy · Restrictiveness: Safe.** A selected checked method resolves once at its declared consequential step; the optional patch candidate places fastening at the end of the first T. Previews, duplicate requests, rewording, reconnecting and technical retries do not create new chance events. Real changes before an unresolved step can change its prospects without rerolling a resolved result. New physical attempts after setbacks require real time/resources; partial work offers finishing rather than a free roll. No universal retry cooldown or fixed lifetime attempt count is selected. Existing action/lifecycle limits control bounded continuation. An explicitly chosen budgeted work plan may authorize genuine attempts when supported, but repeated mandatory retries are a reason to reconsider the design. Current rewind policy remains controlling; preserving one completed attempt does not promise that rewinding before it cannot alter the future.

## ST-L05 — Later opposition, social effects and progression

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Opposed, social-control and numerical-progression families are not requirements for the first useful competence. The two-participant physical control contest remains an optional later example, with one draw per participant, shared adjudication and ties preserving prior state, only after real participation/custody behavior exists and the activity earns inclusion. Additional participants, curves, XP, personality change and social outcomes require a concrete need and supported design, not automatic stage progression. Players can already make progress through supported learning, invention, equipment and relationships without numerical XP. No check creates human consent, ownership, secret knowledge or resources.

## ST-L06 — Formula and runtime work

**Optional worked candidate · Restrictiveness: Safe.** If a checked method is selected, the only curve detailed here is the finite reference profile with bounded effects. It is not current supported runtime behavior, and implementing its editor is not a prerequisite for a simpler useful competence. Arbitrary weighted formulas, exploding dice and unbounded pools remain outside this candidate. One check resolves a meaningful action step, not every frame, knot or observer. Ordinary effects and check resolution require no paid model decision; optional interpretation/authoring uses existing budgets. Service failure does not alter fictional odds. Expand complexity only for a worthwhile activity with explainable choices and measured complete workloads.

## ST-L07 — Stat counts, disclosure and retained history

**Proposed consumer policy · Restrictiveness: Medium.** No new universal per-character stat count, world count or result-history cap is selected. This does not promise unlimited storage, context or live evaluation: existing state, invention, action-experience, privacy and scalability limits still apply. Candidate sources and retained/exposed history need bounded qualification independent of the short result display. Exact odds and modifier detail are shown only when their inputs may be known; otherwise use a qualified estimate or permitted explanation. Refuse unsupported growth before relying on it, preserving admitted state. Revisit whole-world authoring, modifier density, long-running histories and private-input cases.
