# Authored stats and checks: proposed product limits

**Baseline: researched product proposal, October 3, 2026; no runtime support or capacity is changed.** The [feature specification](../projects/authored-stats-feature-spec.md) owns behavior. [ND03](../maintainers/needs-design.md#nd03--world-authored-stats-checks-and-their-effects) retains design routing; EWF/INV and action/state owners retain delivery. These proposed reference-world bounds do not become generic engine laws.

## ST-L01 — Initial competence and curve

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** One enduring Practical craft competence uses integer ranks 0–3 with explicit applicability and initial values. Two fair independent d6 plus competence and situation produce 10+ clean, 7–9 finishing-required and 6-or-less setback. The total modifier is −2 to +5. There is no separate automatic critical/fumble rule; +5 has no setback. Other names are possible through the same supported meaning; arbitrary profiles and temporary competence rewrites require qualification. Missing/inapplicable state is not implicitly zero. Revisit using exact distributions and complete player/work loops, not a stat-display demo.

## ST-L02 — Situational causes and help

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Two categories, equipment suitability and working conditions/support, each contribute −1, 0 or +1. Each recognizes at most one favorable and one adverse admitted influence, canceling when both exist; equivalent descriptions do not stack. The first family admits one principal and at most one actual helper. Competence is fixed at stage start. In both categories, favorable influences must qualify throughout credited work and an admitted adverse influence counts if present during any credited work. Paused time earns no credit; resumption preserves the same principal, competence and condition history. A new attempt cannot inherit old work credit while discarding those facts. This is an explicit conservative rule, not universal physical simulation. Missing prerequisites block rather than impose a bypassable penalty. Revisit with distinct techniques, duration changes and genuinely larger shared work.

## ST-L03 — First action and work economics

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** First qualify a new optional rushed roof patch after the shelter family: positive authored duration T; clean completion at T; actual partial material requiring a chosen extra T of reliable finishing; setback after T without a completed patch. The careful alternative takes 2T when prerequisites persist. Exact T and material/stage definitions require qualification; 2T is proposed tuning. No existing routine action becomes uncertain by default. At the boundary, offer an existing supported method or explain missing support before spending resources. Revisit with time pressure, helper opportunity cost, interrupts and real material outcomes.

## ST-L04 — Attempts, repetition and result continuity

**Proposed consumer policy · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Resolve once at the end of the admitted first stage. Previews, duplicate requests, rewording, reconnecting and technical retries do not create new chance events. New physical attempts after setbacks are allowed with real time/resources. Partial work offers finishing rather than a free new roll. No universal retry cooldown or fixed lifetime attempt count is selected; existing action/lifecycle limits control bounded continuation. An explicitly chosen budgeted work plan may authorize several genuine attempts only after that activity is qualified. Current rewind policy remains controlling; preserving one completed attempt is not a promise that rewinding before it cannot alter the future. Revisit actual repeated-work economics and interruption cases.

## ST-L05 — Later opposition, social effects and progression

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** The first release has no opposed, social-control or numerical-progression family. A later qualified two-participant physical control contest uses one draw per participant, shared adjudication and ties preserving the prior state; it needs real participation/custody behavior first. Additional participants, new curves, XP, personality change and social outcomes require their own supported design. No check creates human consent, ownership, secret knowledge or resource. Revisit only with an actual useful consumer and its cross-participant consequences.

## ST-L06 — Formula and runtime work

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Initial authoring uses the finite reference profile and bounded effect choices, not arbitrary weighted stat formulas, exploding dice or unbounded pools. One check resolves a meaningful work stage, not every frame, knot or observer. Native check resolution requires no paid model decision; optional interpretation/authoring uses existing budgets. Service failure does not alter fictional odds. Revisit complexity using complete measured workloads and explainable authoring examples.

## ST-L07 — Stat counts, disclosure and retained history

**Proposed consumer policy · Restrictiveness: Medium.** No new universal per-character stat count, world count or result-history cap is selected. This does not promise unlimited storage, context or live evaluation: existing state, invention, action-experience, privacy and scalability limits still apply. Candidate sources and retained/exposed history need bounded qualification independent of the short result display. Exact odds and modifier detail are shown only when their inputs may be known; otherwise use a qualified estimate or permitted explanation. Refuse unsupported growth before relying on it, preserving admitted state. Revisit whole-world authoring, modifier density, long-running histories and private-input cases.
