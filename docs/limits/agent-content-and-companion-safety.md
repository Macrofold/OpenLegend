# Agent content and companion-safety constraints

The [canonical policy](../agent-content-and-companion-safety.md) owns behavior and every-character instruction requirements. [Production deployment](../maintainers/production-deployment.md) owns implementation and release qualification under PD07, PD08 and PD10. This inventory records chosen product restrictions, not an alternative moderation backlog or a certification of safety.

**Baseline:** Mike's October 16, 2026 acceptance, preserving the existing adult-player, non-explicit and browser-first direction. No runtime implementation, model test or independent effectiveness measurement was performed for this documentation capture. Restrictiveness follows the [inventory vocabulary](README.md#restrictiveness); it is not a legal or security grade.

## ACS01 — Non-graphic mature storytelling

**Status: Current accepted product policy; runtime enforcement unqualified. Restrictiveness: Very safe for sexual-expression scope.** In OpenLegend-operated player-facing experiences, graphic interactive sexual roleplay is excluded, including text and any future voice/media. At the boundary, withhold the graphic content, respond briefly without reproducing it, and offer non-graphic continuation or fade to black. Profanity, serious adult topics and eligible consensual non-graphic romance remain possible; this is not a blanket keyword ban.

The restriction trades erotic freedom for a clearer mainstream mature-game experience and reduced legal, vendor and operational exposure. It applies to generated characters and indirect output such as narration or recalled summaries; private channels and preference toggles do not expand it. It clarifies earlier optional-sex wording rather than removing ordinary romance. No relaxation is proposed by this task. Mike's explicit approval plus renewed legal/provider/distribution review is required to reconsider it; the word initial is not an automatic expiry.

## ACS02 — Adult-player audience and independent eligibility

**Status: Current accepted audience direction; assurance method and runtime qualification remain open. Restrictiveness: Very safe for access.** The initial operated product targets adult players, not merely adult fictional avatars. Player eligibility, fictional participant age/presentation and receiving-audience preferences are separate. A dialogue claim cannot establish verified age or bypass the approved underage-user process. At uncertain or failed eligibility, apply the approved service restriction rather than inventing an adult result.

This excludes younger players while avoiding a false promise that an 18+ terms clause alone satisfies applicable law. The policy does not select an identity vendor, prescribe document collection or change the world's innocent family/child content. PD10 owns the actual launch-region assurance, privacy and incident decisions. Changes to the audience require an explicit owner decision and new legal/product qualification, not a character-authoring exception.

## ACS03 — No unnecessary player image/video sharing in the initial scope

**Status: Current accepted scope principle; no runtime removal authorized. Restrictiveness: Safe.** Avoid adding general player image/video sharing without a specific accepted use case and its rights, abuse-response and retention review. At that boundary, leave the optional sharing feature unavailable rather than promising unsupported moderation. Existing normal game art and development reference workflows are not reclassified as player-media sharing by this policy.

The tradeoff is fewer social-media features in exchange for a smaller intimate-image, deepfake and child-exploitation risk surface. PD08/PD10 retain qualification if a concrete media feature is later approved. This task adds no media feature and authorizes no deletion of an existing art pipeline.

## Invariants and unspecified implementation choices

Minor protection, real-user consent, private-audience authorization, truthful AI/service capabilities and lawful reporting/preservation are not tunable gameplay generosity. The canonical policy owns those obligations and the shared trusted instruction layer; they are not options to disable in a custom world.

This documentation chooses no new numerical conversation length, token allowance, log-retention duration, moderation queue size or retry budget. That is **not** a decision to allow unlimited growth or retention. Existing [cognition limits](cognition.md), [memory limits](memory.md) and production admission/privacy owners remain in force. Exact legal notice schedules belong to the applicable release profile, not a universal fictional-world clock or a new blanket session cap.

No item in this task proposes expanding these restrictions, so no limits-removal task is created. Future changes must update the canonical policy, this inventory, applicable release requirements and decision history together.
