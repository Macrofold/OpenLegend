# Product learning — limits and constraints

All choices below are **Proposed, October 4, 2026**, with no participant study or paid execution performed. The [feature specification](../projects/demo-and-audience-learning-feature-spec.md) owns behavior; [DL01–DL06](../maintainers/product-learning.md) owns execution and evidence. Ratings describe restrictiveness, not measured optimality.

## DL-L01 — First audience and sample

**Proposed / Medium:** adults with relevant sandbox/crafting/simulation interests; up to six fresh participants in two rounds of up to three. Expert collaborators and other audiences remain separately labeled. This bounds the initial learning workload, not product eligibility, market size or usability coverage.

**Reason / boundary:** inspect individual causes and revise economically. Preserve cancellations, incomplete attempts and unfavorable results. Do not recruit replacements until a desired success percentage appears. More people require a specific next question and a new operating envelope; no statistical demand or conversion threshold is selected.

## DL-L02 — Session and return window

**Proposed / Medium:** up to 35 real minutes for first study sessions and 15 for an optional return, normally two to seven real days later. The roughly two-minute edited demo is a presentation target, not measured time-to-invention. These are study boundaries, not simulation timers.

**Reason / boundary:** contain participant/operator burden while leaving room for a meaningful activity. A participant may stop sooner; record unfinished steps rather than force a complete itinerary. Introduction/debrief use ordinary pause. Existing clock, physiology, cognition and funding rules apply during play. No hidden need freeze or automatic feeding is introduced.

## DL-L03 — Study information and retention

**Proposed / Medium:** minimal session observations; optional separately agreed recording; scheduling contact separated from study notes. Delete identifiable contact/raw notes/recordings within 30 days after the planned cycle review, translated to a fixed disclosed date at enrollment. A later review does not extend it automatically. Separately agreed demonstration footage needs its own purpose and date.

**Reason / boundary:** retain enough evidence to decide while avoiding an accumulating private-play archive. Permit earlier withdrawal from contact and removal of identifiable study material. Keep only a concise de-identified decision report afterward. This changes neither game/provider/accounting retention nor another person's records; current data/permission owners govern those.

## DL-L04 — Actual spending and growing work

**Proposed / Safe when configured; unqualified until then:** no new inference allowance, operator-hour commitment or cash ceiling is selected by this design. Before even a paid rehearsal, name its approved ceiling/time limit. Before recruitment, set the cycle's actual external-spend, total-hours, access and review envelope using the measured complete rehearsal and the founder's limit.

**Reason / boundary:** output length or a small sample does not bound preparation, search, validation, optional art, resident work, failed attempts, stored worlds or support. Existing invention/AI/deployment admission applies to every enabled path. Exhaustion stops new paid work; uncertain attempts reconcile before deliberate retry. A new session or participant must not reset shared costs. No outreach, ad spending or paid offer is authorized.

## DL-L05 — Offered continuity and admission

**Proposed / Medium:** only the participants, mode and supported access window in the selected brief. Keep the original world and a supported pinned build available through any promised return session; otherwise omit the return claim before enrollment. Hosted/public access uses the applicable PD/MP gates.

**Reason / boundary:** a local recording is not hosted availability; a fresh replacement world is not continuity. Disclose the actual access end and world-data treatment. Pinning a short study build adds no old-save compatibility, indefinite hosting promise, offline progression or subscription entitlement.
