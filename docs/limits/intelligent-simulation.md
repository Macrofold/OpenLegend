# Intelligent simulation — design constraints and growth map

This inventory owns only the new research-refinement scope and cross-owner constraints. Existing numerical budgets remain in [cognition](cognition.md), [native work](native-work.md), [actions/activities](action-experience.md), [spatial](spatial.md), [memory](memory.md), [narration](narration.md), [diagnostic capture](observability.md) and [product scalability](product-scalability.md). If an owner is not a separate inventory, use the linked current feature inventory; this map does not introduce another copy of its numbers. [Delivery](../maintainers/intelligent-simulation.md) and the [design plan](../projects/completed/intelligent-simulation-design-plan.md) distinguish selected design from implementation.

## IS-L01 — Existing choices before broader delegation

**Proposed design; restrictiveness: Safe.** Reuse finite native invocation/control and learned-method capabilities. A selected purpose does not admit every means, material, cost, risk or partner; unsupported changed terms require another actual choice. Bounded iteration, unknown predicates and current action admission retain their existing limits. No new universal activity duration, number of goals or personality vocabulary is selected. This may interrupt a task an unconstrained planner could complete, but preserves the meaning of the actor's choice. Expand only for a demonstrated method whose complete authorization and stopping semantics fit the installed world. [Continuing-life design](../projects/continuing-lives-tech-design.md).

## IS-L02 — Attention and decision cadence

**No new limit selected; restrictiveness: Safe for preserving the current contract, broader quality unqualified.** Existing per-opportunity/request/queue bounds, exact accepted identity and required evidence remain controlling. No universal thought cooldown, maximum concerns noticed, omission of legitimate observers, automatic novelty reward or guaranteed model call per resident is added. Retained history, possible concerns, scenes and resident population can grow independently of one bounded decision; selection before hydration and overflow/defer behavior are required at each existing owner. Growth is not qualified merely because a displayed result is short. Any changed semantic filter must compare missed personally relevant evidence and quiet opportunities, not just query speed. [Attention design](../projects/attention-and-scenes-tech-design.md).

## IS-L03 — Routing, calibration and reuse

**Conditional experiment; restrictiveness: Safe.** The current native/structured/generative routes and any agreed Jev-only condition remain unchanged. A score margin, entropy, fluent rationale or API claim is not a validated error probability. No confidence threshold, trained policy, new provider, cached consequence or automatic escalation is adopted. A candidate must have a defined target error, available features, held-out scenario families and total preparation/gate cost; unsupported model internals stop that candidate. Training data and retained traces need an explicit selected finite capture, account authority and private-data handling through existing tools. [Evaluation pair](../projects/intelligent-simulation-evaluation-feature-spec.md).

## IS-L04 — Exact calculation and compact communication

**Selected design target, October 15; runtime unimplemented · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Distant physical activity preserves the same installed effects, ordering, resources, RNG and observer evidence. Rate/trajectory/event-boundary calculations may eliminate redundant steps; no guessed success, averaged combat result, missed crossing or smaller legitimate audience is permitted. Unsupported proofs fall back to the current bounded native path, not an invented outcome. Numerical tolerances preserve the existing family law rather than weakening the result contract. No distance threshold, unseen-region quota or population aggregation is added. [Scalability design](../projects/product-scalability-tech-design.md).

The separately selected background source carries compact expressive meaning instead of mandatory full dialogue. Its precision is explicit from creation: no exact transcript exists except an actually retained phrase. The [attention design](../projects/attention-and-scenes-tech-design.md) and [gathering profile](../worlds/base/social-gatherings.md) own eligible audiences, partial hearing, independently chosen replies and player-arrival transition. This reduces verbal production, not emotional significance, independent choice or physical fidelity. It supersedes the earlier unselected exact-first background recommendation; current runtime speech remains unchanged until implementation. Optional crowd sound, timed speech and unattended funding retain their existing gates.

## IS-L05 — Concurrency, growth and admission

**Current shared controls retained; restrictiveness: Safe for their qualified scope.** Reuse current work lanes, native work vectors, per-account/provider spending reservations and one committed world. No new threads/processes per actor, replica owner, retry loop, global event broker or second scheduler is selected. Candidate multiplicity, concentrated shared inputs, incoming decisions, pending external charges and retained historical records can grow beyond visible people. Bound admitted operations and their complete dependent effects using existing owners; unknown cost refuses or defers before new liability. A queued decision may expire, but an already committed effect or uncertain paid request cannot be erased by eviction. Wider capacity requires measured cold/burst/tail workloads, not a new constant justified by research headlines. [PS limits](product-scalability.md) and [integration work](../maintainers/intelligent-simulation.md).

## IS-L06 — Evaluation scope, retention and claims

**Proposed comparison scope; restrictiveness: Safe.** Use explicit scenario manifests with finite repetitions, source/history size, clock, duration, cost and concurrency admitted under existing verification/spending policy. No new quality pass percentage, population promise, universal latency target or $ allowance is adopted by writing the design. Retained traces/evidence need a finite per-run capture and existing retention owner; there is no total-history deletion policy here. Native correctness, live-model decisions, calibrated estimates and human value remain distinct gates. Report rare constructed exceptions separately from natural frequency, and all settled/outstanding/uncertain cost without rounding away fractions. Keep generated outputs outside Git unless explicitly requested. Revisit a restriction only with evidence for that precise workload. [Evaluation technical design](../projects/intelligent-simulation-evaluation-tech-design.md).

## IS-L07 — Numerical psychological reactions with optional description

**Selected design target, October 15; runtime unimplemented · Restrictiveness: Safe.** A person's stimulus interpretation may propose a direct change to an installed numerical feeling or supported psychological meter, without obligatory speech, thought narrative, rationale or a separate explanation call. Descriptive experiences remain available where their nuance matters. The current semantic owner applies the actual allowed amount, cause, lifetime, repetition and private disclosure; a label such as gratitude never awards fixed points to every recipient. This supersedes the earlier descriptive-only recommendation, not the existing no-automatic-behavior rule.

[Continuing-life design](../projects/continuing-lives-tech-design.md#55-numerical-personal-reactions-without-required-prose), [authored reaction profile](../worlds/base/psychological-reactions.md) and [feeling inventory](feelings.md) own the pilot vocabulary, numerical hypotheses and current constraints. The engine adds no universal joy/belonging law, aggregate duplicate mood store, mandatory social-need bar or fixed vocabulary of all possible feelings. A cause-linked numerical intensity already supplies the selected emotional meter. Other installed meter operations retain their distinct owners and units.

Compact aliases are optional request-bound bindings; the full-label fallback preserves intelligibility. Selection chooses an explicitly supplied operation/amount, never converts a choice score into emotion strength. Aliases and their legend/candidate cost must be measured; a letter is not assumed to be one tokenizer token. Unknown code, definition, source or unsupported operation cannot mutate state. Prior persisted causes and receipt identities prevent a replay or remembered quotation from reapplying the original update. [D69](../../archive/05-project/open-decisions.md#d69--multidimensional-character-experience) records the accepted scoped decision and broader separate questions.
