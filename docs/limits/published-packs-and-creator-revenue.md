# Published packs and creator revenue: limits and constraints

[Product proposal](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md) · [INV-8](../maintainers/inventions-and-world-evolution.md#inv-8--portable-inventions-and-later-algorithm-extensions) · [EWF11](../maintainers/extensible-world-foundation.md#ewf11--reusable-construct-integration-and-local-portability-proof) · [PD10](../maintainers/production-deployment.md) · [Tracking rules](README.md)

**Proposed DG28 terms, October 7, 2026.** No catalogue, payment, license exception or payout programme is activated. Preserve [WC-L06–WC-L12](world-creation.md#wc-l06--first-library-and-publication-scope), existing [invention](inventions.md), [AI](ai-execution.md), [offer](customer-and-supporter-offers.md), [deployment](production-deployment.md) and [persistence](persistence.md) ownership. Actual financial terms, rights and aggregate service capacity remain activation inputs.

## PK-L01 — Curated supported free publication first

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** First public catalogue uses DG12's one-root recipe and complete eligible closure: at most 32 exact definition references, 1 MiB decoded definition/manifest text, one active import preparation per destination, no automatic update/retry or generation for exact reuse. No new arbitrary executable or external-media family is selected. Larger complete packs/templates consume the same product lifecycle after a supported measured profile exists; never truncate them or call a subset complete. This extends discovery without broadening the underlying transfer claim. Owner: INV-8/EWF11; [stages](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#3-selected-stages-and-scope).

## PK-L02 — Small legible listing and review admission

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Reuse twenty results per page. Title: 80 Unicode code points/320 UTF-8 bytes; summary: 400/1,600; detailed notes: 4,000/16,000. Both bounds apply. First listings use text and already-qualified preview presentation. One pending release submission per account; complete submissions receive acceptance, specific repair or refusal within seven real days. Stop new admission when actual funded curator capacity cannot meet that service. This is not an unlimited publication/work/storage promise or a limit on historical invention ownership. No model per query/card or paid ranking is selected. Owner: INV-8; [discovery](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#4-find-a-pack-for-something-worth-doing).

## PK-L03 — Exact release, sufficient rights and private origin

**Status: Proposed correctness boundary · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** Immutable content and terms identify each release. New bytes/dependencies/rights create a new release; ordinary marketing correction does not change installed behavior. First publication uses already sufficient recorded grants, actual component licenses and notices, with complete private-dependency review. Account attribution, zero price and AI generation create no missing publication right. New general contribution/license terms require actual adoption before their dependent programme. This preserves useful free sharing without a speculative ownership authority. Owner: INV-8.3/current licensing; [rights](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#52-rights-and-prospective-contribution-terms).

## PK-L04 — No purchased fictional power or automatic world change

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** Acquisition grants stated content rights; installation uses current destination authority and complete closure. Neither teaches a character, creates items, copies history or spends unapproved money. Updates require current scope/impact review; unsafe or invalid-rights material uses existing scoped intervention owners. Current invention admission cannot be reused unchanged because it credits/teaches its initiator. This protects the ordinary game's knowledge, economy and authorship while permitting native reuse. Owner: INV-8/INV-5/EWF11; [installation](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#62-install-deliberately).

## PK-L05 — Retained access and ordinary retirement

**Status: Proposed; aggregate storage unqualified · Restrictiveness: Safe for behavior, too liberal as unlimited hosting.** Routine delisting or membership end does not revoke valid acquired-release rights. No routine age-based deletion of acquired versions is selected. Fund bounded new retained bytes before admission; if capacity is unavailable, decline the new acquisition rather than delete prior copies. Planned service retirement consumes DG27's minimum thirty-real-day recovery notice. Exceptional privacy/security/rights removal follows actual authority and license scope, not an unconditional survival promise. Root/RP02 still controls development compatibility. Owner: INV-8/data/PD10; [retention](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#63-retention-and-self-hosted-use).

## PK-L06 — Curated sale and buyer remedies

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** First paid release has one contracting seller/recipient and the offer's actually selected currency, fees and rights. Reuse the 24-real-hour unfulfilled delivery cancellation/refund; propose a seven-real-day voluntary full-refund period after successful pack delivery. Refund changes future managed download/update/support entitlements while actual surviving license rights and fictional history remain truthful. No automatic contributor split service, unbounded free support or all-future-release entitlement is included. Standalone premium-world membership remains independent of platform membership. Actual seller/territory obligations require qualification. Owner: PD10; [sales](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#8-selling-packs-and-running-premium-worlds).

## PK-L07 — One fixed allocation and three deliberate choices

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** One confirmed fixed amount per full eligible paid account period, with a disclosed prorated amount for a partial first period and at most three persistently selected worlds. Normal pending renewal uses the same 24-hour fulfillment window once. Actual human-issued accepted gameplay qualifies use once; no duration threshold, daily requirement, NPC work or repeated-install reward. Distinct contracting beneficiaries split the amount equally, then split their own world-reporting share; whole minor-unit remainders follow saved-choice order. No silent recipient enrollment. If none qualify, refund only the earmarked component without canceling other membership benefits or renewal. The actual amount and refund economics are unselected activation inputs. This bounds incentives and avoids engagement surveillance; revise only after real participant comprehension and useful-world evidence. Owner: PD10; [allocation](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#9-a-fixed-member-allocation-that-rewards-wanted-worlds).

## PK-L08 — Overlap and self-allocation

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Any valid directly paid premium access overlapping the allocation period excludes that world for that member, regardless of purchase date or renewal being off. A recipient financially controlled by the payer or a materially controlled alternate beneficiary is ineligible; being a credited inventor alone is not self-payment. Trials/subsidies supply no unfunded pool, and no pack dependency gets automatic royalties from an invocation. Fixed confirmed funding remains the loss boundary; existing financial review handles collusion and fraud. Explain changes without exposing private enforcement evidence. Owner: PD10; [qualification](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#91-what-qualifies).

## PK-L09 — Held earnings and bounded financial review

**Status: Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Thirty-real-day ordinary refund/dispute hold starts at pack delivery or member-period close. Initiate eligible monthly payouts within fifteen real days after calendar-month close; scoped unresolved disputes retain only their affected amount with a reason/review date. Actual supported currency, fees, minima and final-small-balance route must be disclosed before enrollment. Amounts owed do not expire silently. Appeals receive acknowledgement within seven real days and resolution or dated progress within thirty. Existing receipts fix the past beneficiary/formula; prospective ownership changes cannot rewrite them. These are operating targets, not already staffed guarantees. Owner: PD10; [earnings](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#10-earnings-disputes-and-operator-closure).

## PK-L10 — Broader growth and unselected machinery

**Status: Open qualification · Restrictiveness: Too liberal if used as an unlimited service.** Result pages, small packs and one pending submission do not cap all accounts, versions, dependency closure preparation, storage, cache invalidation, support, refund fees or owed payouts. Actual operating admission must bound those dimensions before each stage. Cross-operator automatic synchronization, identity federation, richer executable/media packs and automated team splits are unselected. Deliberate eligible file export remains possible under real rights; its unverified provenance does not acquire trusted account status. Owner: INV-8/PD10 and existing data/platform owners; [economics](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md#11-economics-performance-and-real-scenarios).
