# Published packs and creator revenue limits

**Proposed product consumer, 2026-10-08, against `671d52d724db5dfe39403ebe46f7c1ee24d6ec52`.** The [feature specification](../projects/published-packs-and-creator-revenue-feature-spec.md) owns behavior and [PACK01–PACK06](../maintainers/published-packs-and-creator-revenue.md) owns delivery under INV-8/EWF11/PD10. No catalog, license, fee, payout or participation program is launched by these entries. Applicable grants, privacy, accounting and current admission authority are correctness obligations; the discretionary scope around them is inventoried here.

## PACK-L01 — Small curated publication scope

**Proposed; Very safe.** Begin with known same-operator provenance and prepared supported destinations. Candidate entries are an eligible useful sling release and a cordage/woven-container release only after their complete declared behavior qualifies. A listing promises its actual useful capability, supported world profile, exact version and full eligible dependencies. Reuse [WC-L06–WC-L12](world-creation.md#wc-l06--first-library-and-publication-scope) for the one-root free-transfer proof, including native presentation and unsupported-origin behavior. No public self-service submission flood, general executable extension, automatic update, universal template or mandatory shopping step is included. Free sharing precedes paid marketplace work and does not require a payout account. **Boundary:** do not list an unreviewed or materially incomplete promise as ready; preserve eligible private/free work. **Reason/revisit:** prove enjoyable reuse and affordable review before expanding catalog breadth or provenance. **Owner:** PACK01/02/06.

## PACK-L02 — Discovery and complete preparation work

**Proposed; Safe for the inherited small transfer; broader aggregate work unqualified.** The existing first import envelope remains one root, at most 32 distinct exact definition references and 1 MiB decoded definition/manifest text, 20 displayed library entries per page, one active destination preparation, no automatic retries and no automatic installation updates. These are consumed WC limits, not enlarged transport arguments or a new lifetime invention cap. Catalog discovery must filter before expensive hydration and preserve selected versions/focus and explicit continuation. Richer multi-invention packs, asset bytes, remote fetches, reference depth, retained release history and complete catalog search need their own qualified aggregate work/storage envelope under the same owners; no independent larger allowance is selected here. **Boundary:** report incomplete preparation or unsupported scope before activation; never omit a required dependency or use a model's compatibility opinion as proof. **Reason/revisit:** useful small reuse should save work; expand for actual blocked valuable packs and measured preparation cost. **Owner:** PACK01/03/06 and existing INV/EWF/data/art owners.

## PACK-L03 — Acquired versions, operating rights and finite service

**Proposed; Medium.** The selected paid-pack product is one-time account acquisition of one exact immutable eligible release, with its complete permitted retained copy, disclosed modification/commercial-operation/self-host export rights and component-specific redistribution grants. This is not a universal license for unknown content. Prior granted use and an installed destination's recorded operating grant survive ordinary buyer departure, unpublication or later membership expiry under their actual terms. A reference-only dependency cannot be sold as an independently retained complete pack. Hosted downloads, maintenance and future compatibility are finite services with explicit terms; the selected retirement/retrieval flow consumes CO and the source grant rather than promising perpetual downloads. Exceptional rights/privacy/security restrictions require their own scoped authority and remedy. **Boundary:** block an unsupported sale/import or identify a missing grant; preserve legitimate prior copies and history without claiming power to erase another operator's server. **Reason/revisit:** avoid building a world on an undisclosed rental dependency while preserving creator choice and applicable licenses. **Owner:** PACK02/03, CO and existing rights/SL owners.

## PACK-L04 — One subscriber's bounded world allocation

**Proposed; Safe.** One paying account has one fixed allocation A for its real UTC calendar-month platform term, prorated for a partial paid term. A remains an unselected real commercial amount. Propose at most three remembered choices among eligible worlds the person actually attended voluntarily during that period, divided equally as A/k for k valid choices; exact currency rounding preserves the total. There is no minimum session duration or action count: short useful play and quiet listening can qualify, while idle connection, NPC activity, alternate bodies, accelerated time and repeated visits add no weight. A sole eligible visited world is a disclosed default; valid prior choices persist without a monthly gameplay prompt. Self-controlled receiving payees and artificial participation are excluded under the reviewable eligibility policy. A separately subscribed premium world is excluded from that payer's platform pool for the applicable period under the feature's exact overlap/reversal rules; standalone entry stays independent. **Boundary:** allow seven real days after period end to resolve a missing/invalid choice, then return the unallocated earmark to the original payer through the existing refund/reconciliation owner. It never becomes platform profit or a DG30 grant automatically. **Reason/revisit:** support wanted worlds without rewarding time farming or another recurring game chore; revise if real users cannot understand or use the choice affordably. **Owner:** PACK04/05.

## PACK-L05 — Proposed fee, shares and ordinary payout timing

**Proposed; Medium.** Propose a marketplace fee of 15% of the tax-excluded, refund-adjusted pack sale amount, with actual payment costs disclosed separately. This is a reviewable pricing policy, not adopted revenue or evidence of sustainable margin. Contributor shares must be agreed before sale and sum to 100% of the defined creator amount; neither joint account ownership nor a dependency reference implies a financial percentage. Propose a minimum 30-real-day ordinary hold, monthly payout and a USD 25 normal threshold; the precise earning/settlement anchor and exit timing are defined in the feature proposal. A threshold delays ordinary aggregation, never transfers ownership of an unpaid balance to the platform. Creator exit has a below-threshold settlement route. **Boundary:** show estimates, holds, adjustments, payable and actually paid separately; late reversals can still occur after the ordinary hold and use the disclosed reserve/adjustment process without debiting game or AI funds. **Reason/revisit:** contain transaction/support overhead while making small earnings and exit understandable; qualify actual fees, reserves, thresholds and payment availability before accepting money. **Owner:** PACK02/05 and existing CO/billing.

## PACK-L06 — Growing commercial obligations and review capacity

**Proposed; no independent aggregate allowance selected; Too liberal until the real service envelope is qualified.** Submissions, rights reviews, sales, collaborations, participation choices, small unpaid balances, disputes, refund exposure and long-lived release copies can grow independently of displayed page size or a creator's invention allowance. Adopt finite intake, active/pending work, byte/storage, reconciliation and support limits for the first catalog and payout cohort. New optional listings/sales can close before obligations exceed funded capacity; required existing recovery cannot be silently discarded. Gross receipts cannot all fund operations when they cover creator payables, refundable subscriber earmarks, unsettled receipts or already promised downloads/remedies. A sale with negative expected delivery capacity needs an explicitly funded subsidy or must remain unavailable. **Reason/revisit:** a generous headline share is useless if the operator cannot finish payments or keep bought content usable; expand from measured complete costs, including founder time and worst supported demand. **Owner:** PACK01–06, CO and PD.

## PACK-L07 — Minimal participation and financial disclosure

**Proposed; Safe.** Use the smallest account/world attendance and deliberate-choice evidence needed for the selected human participation rule. No public play history, private conversation, NPC mind, emotional inference or fine-grained behavioral scoring is needed to allocate money. The payer sees their own choices and amounts; a creator receives appropriately scoped earning totals, reasons and review paths rather than a list of other-world activity or private subscriber profiles. Keep required financial evidence independently of optional diagnostic retention, with exact operational retention and small-cohort disclosure reviewed before launch. **Boundary:** incomplete or disputed evidence remains explicitly pending/excluded for review and remedy; do not manufacture a visit or use an unknown result as proof of abuse. **Reason/revisit:** understandable payment evidence without turning play into surveillance; widen collection only for a named necessary purpose with corresponding authority. **Owner:** PACK04/05 and current human-private/billing owners.

## PACK-L08 — Cross-operator and richer-pack expansion

**Proposed; Very safe.** Broad self-hosted account synchronization, arbitrary imported provenance, richer media, general live updates and broader executable packs remain separate expansions. Self-hosting itself remains free of mandatory platform purchase. A remote file can be retained as an unverified claim; its author label cannot overwrite trusted account ownership or grant export rights. Published components retain their actual license and dependency notices; no proprietary executable exception is invented. **Boundary:** identify the unsupported authentication, rights, binding, media or runtime obligation and retain eligible source material for later review. **Reason/revisit:** a supported free/paid local round trip must work before cross-service claims expand; use actual user demand and a qualified operator-to-operator journey. **Owner:** PACK02/03/06, INV-8, EWF11 and existing data/identity owners.
