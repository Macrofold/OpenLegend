# Optional well-being evidence limits

All entries are **Proposed**, based on the DG11 product design of 5 October 2026, not runtime or statistical qualification. [Behavior](../projects/wellbeing-evidence-feature-spec.md) and [HE tasks](../maintainers/wellbeing-evidence.md) own meaning and delivery. These limits do not adopt the archive’s whole well-being programme.

## WE01 — Finite audience and recruitment

**Proposed; Very safe.** At most 32 adults in 16 independently consenting known-friend dyads, one dyad and one outing per person, one cohort, and 28 calendar days from first enrollment. The full proposed allocation is eight dyads per offered format. Close recruitment when the cohort or time bound is reached; do not silently replace withdrawals or extend until a desired result appears. A smaller actual cohort is reported as smaller. The cap contains support and coordination cost; it is not a power calculation or evidence that this sample can establish an effect. HE03/HE06.

## WE02 — Invitation and question burden

**Proposed; Very safe.** One accepted invitation per pilot participant; one baseline form with one optional item and one end form with at most three optional items. No prompts during play, no required response, no default selected answer, no reminder campaign and no survey-linked game reward. Decline/skip ends the invitation or prompt rather than rescheduling it. This excludes richer measurement to preserve the short play experience; expansion requires a selected measurement question. HE01/HE04.

## WE03 — Activity and response time

**Proposed; Safe.** Approximately 20 minutes is the advertised outing estimate, not an enforced play minimum, healthy-time rule or automatic character removal. More than 10 minutes between baseline and the offered start makes that baseline delayed for the planned change comparison; do not repeat the question to repair it. The end form may be reopened for 30 minutes after measured play ends unless collection permission has stopped; later answers do not enter the planned immediate-outcome dataset. Late timing remains visible. Collection stopping and removal remain available afterward. These values are operational proposals, not validated recall accuracy. HE04/HE05.

## WE04 — Minimal response and correction content

**Proposed; Very safe.** Four structured item responses at most per participant across the two forms; no research free text, transcript, voice, screenshots, location trail or inferred psychological profile. Keep the original submitted value and latest explicit correction with their timing, not an unlimited revision transcript. Corrections are accepted through the disclosed removal/correction date; later changed feelings do not become an original momentary answer. A narrower dataset reduces intrusion and interpretation drift. Exact field-size and export-byte qualification remains HE04; a small visible form alone does not prove every diagnostic copy is bounded.

## WE05 — Collection and removal deadline

**Proposed; Safe.** Further collection ends on confirmed collection withdrawal. First answer submission closes at the earlier of the form’s 30-minute end window or the published collection close. Removal and correction requests are accepted through 30 days after collection closes; correction never creates a missing timely answer. Accepted removal excludes the affected information from new analysis immediately; complete active-record removal within 7 days of receiving a valid request. Distinguish not received, receipt unconfirmed, pending removal, active removal and backup expiry. No silently extended deadline or assertion that ordinary game history was erased. These are proposed service promises to qualify under HE03/HE05, not a statement of universal law.

## WE06 — Raw records, backups and reports

**Proposed; Safe.** Remove all participant- and pair-level research records and working exports by 60 days after collection closes; expire research backups and minimum restricted withdrawal receipts by day 90. Current restrictions apply before any restored record becomes usable. Final irreversible aggregation waits for the 30-day removal/correction window and all timely requests to finish. A private aggregate report may remain for 12 months after close, then needs a deliberate review or removal. Dates run from the fixed collection close, including abandonment, not from an indefinitely postponed analysis. Applicable retention conflicts must be resolved before consent. HE03/HE05/HE06.

## WE07 — Access and distribution

**Proposed; Very safe.** Only the selected custodian handles the identity association; explicitly authorized analysis uses the minimal pseudonymous records. No study answers are exposed through world-creator, friend or facilitator status. No public raw data, individual/pair result table, participant quotation, subgroup outcome report or automatic publication. This is a deliberately narrow distribution policy; the underlying human-private authorization boundary is not a tuning option. A later public report needs its own decision and disclosure review. HE03–HE06.

## WE08 — Study workload and exports

**Proposed; Safe.** One current protocol and one selected activity per pilot; one operator-requested research export may execute at a time, with no automatic export backlog. A busy export offers a later manual retry. Cohort membership bounds response selection before hydration; no whole-world history query is needed. Retained export copies follow WE06. Exact byte limits, export preparation time, correction/audit payloads and private-access records are unqualified dimensions owned by HE04/HE05, not an unlimited-growth policy.

## WE09 — No research model work

**Proposed; Very safe.** Zero AI/provider calls for invitations, questionnaire interpretation, answer scoring, summaries or response-driven personalization. Ordinary enabled game cognition retains its own complete-evidence, spending and failure rules. Research service failure cannot stall native play or fabricate a saved answer. This sacrifices automated analysis to reduce copies, cost and unclear inferences. HE04.

## WE10 — Interpretation and expansion

**Proposed; Very safe.** One finite feasibility comparison, with dyads as assignment units, custom items labeled unvalidated, and no clinical, loneliness-reduction, durable-friendship or general health claim. No positive score threshold authorizes expansion. No passive or longitudinal collection is selected. This is a supported-purpose boundary rather than proof that a broader study is impossible. Another study requires a concrete question, audience, measurement and resources; ordinary play development proceeds independently. HE01/HE03/HE06.
