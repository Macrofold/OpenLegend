# Recovery and care limits and constraints

This inventory accompanies the [DG21 feature specification](../projects/recovery-and-care-feature-spec.md), [base-world profile](../worlds/base/recovery-and-care.md) and [CARE delivery tracker](../maintainers/recovery-and-care.md). Values are product proposals, not runtime changes or measured optimal tuning. The source baseline is inspected GitHub main `0a3ab79b7a698a7f1941dc23722f89220d1ba425`; the task branch is intentionally descended from an earlier design branch. Existing [death/scar rules](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/0a3ab79b7a698a7f1941dc23722f89220d1ba425/docs/worlds/base/player-death.md) are separate from these proposed living-person recovery rules.

## CARE-L01 — First supported body and activity

**Proposed — product design only, October 6 · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** The first activity supports a living, awake human body with a real ordinary health deficit **or injury greater than zero**. One finite self-care activity at a camp can complete without supplies, another person, anatomy parts, diagnosis, disease, rescue or a new profession. It is unavailable to a dead body, a person awaiting Continue, an inactive human, a sleeping person or someone whose ordinary health and injury already need no recovery. Scar-only impairment is not a reason to offer this activity; the installed scar treatment remains separate.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Restore a survivor's ability to pursue the next activity without inventing new harm to justify treatment. This first body scope excludes other species and constructed-body repair even where current native anatomy overlaps. Expand for an actual care activity under ACT04/EWF03 rather than treating this world’s human recovery as universal biology. [CARE01](../maintainers/recovery-and-care.md#care01--recover-after-surviving-an-encounter) owns delivery.

## CARE-L02 — Camp, reach and duration

**Proposed — authored base-world tuning · Restrictiveness: Medium.** **Recover at camp** takes **600 uninterrupted game seconds**, currently **10 real seconds** at the installed normal 60:1 rate, while remaining within **2.2 world units** of an actual campfire. Cold and lit fires are equally eligible. No flame, fuel, bed, roof, prepared fiber or skill is required; absence of a usable reachable camp leaves the option unavailable rather than creating a free rest spot or teleport.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Returning to a real place and occupying a brief interval makes recovery an activity without adding another supply chain or making the player pay a material merely to shorten a long wait. Ten seconds is a provisional modest commitment, not evidence of optimal challenge. A slower material-free alternative to paid recovery is not selected; there is one ordinary camp activity. Its location restriction still imposes travel, and a campfire grants no protection from nearby danger. Keep scarce-item and field convenience choices in CARE-L06. [World profile](../worlds/base/recovery-and-care.md), [CARE01](../maintainers/recovery-and-care.md#care01--recover-after-surviving-an-encounter).

## CARE-L03 — Result and unfinished progress

**Proposed — one complete ordinary recovery · Restrictiveness: Medium.** The attempt leaves health and injury unchanged until valid completion, then restores full ordinary health and sets ordinary injury to zero together. It does not refill food or energy, remove a death scar, cure an illness, extinguish burning, remove unrelated conditions, revive anyone or change the person's location. Actual damage at the completion boundary interrupts before healing. Stopping discards that attempt's uncompleted recovery time; restarting starts a new attempt. Already elapsed world time and actual incoming harm remain.

**Reason / tradeoff:** One action completes ordinary recovery rather than demanding repeated clicks for arbitrary health packets. Delaying its effect avoids healing by repeatedly starting and cancelling. The brief restart cost is clearer than adding a persistent partial-treatment record to the first activity. Full health without clearing injury would leave this world's injury-related movement loss; scar removal remains the separately accepted material-consuming mechanic. If a later condition needs staged stabilization and recovery, design its distinct behavior instead of silently generalizing this result. [CARE01–CARE02](../maintainers/recovery-and-care.md#care01--recover-after-surviving-an-encounter).

## CARE-L04 — Food, energy and continuing danger

**Proposed — eligibility and interruption choice · Restrictiveness: Medium.** Food and energy must both remain **strictly positive** during the activity, including completion. Reaching zero cancels it. Refuse the start if the actor's currently known ordinary rates already imply either reaches zero by completion, explaining to that actor that they must eat or rest first. A helper sees no private values or private patient refusal reason. This is a calculation from current rates and duration, not a new fixed well-fed threshold or a guarantee against later changes. Existing ordinary depletion continues: with the inspected body's current occupied-action rates, 600 game seconds use **1.8 fullness points** and **0.9 energy points**, before travel or other activity. “Does not refill” is not a promise to freeze those meters. A starting reserve exactly exhausted at completion does not qualify for success.

Actual movement, admitted incompatible physical work, actual incoming harm, losing the required camp reach, departure or death cancels recovery; invalid commands do not cancel it. Talking and inspection remain available. There is no hidden enemy scan, invulnerability, automatic defense, automatic feeding or automatic restart. A player can attempt recovery in physical danger and suffer its ordinary consequences. This interruption rule belongs to the selected ordinary recovery, not every imaginable treatment: a later bleeding treatment may need a different explicitly designed interaction with continuing damage.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Recovery cannot outheal active starvation or exhaustion and does not make existing bodily needs disappear. The rule adds no arbitrary well-fed or well-rested threshold. Short work limits the opportunity cost; plainly visible current needs and specific interruption feedback must make a failed attempt understandable. Existing selected departure behavior controls when the human ceases participating; this feature does not adopt unattended healing. [CARE02](../maintainers/recovery-and-care.md#care02--readable-care-life-continuity-and-interruption), [current sleep and needs](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/0a3ab79b7a698a7f1941dc23722f89220d1ba425/docs/worlds/base/survival.md#bodily-conditions-and-chosen-responses).

## CARE-L05 — Action, save and time scope

**Proposed — single active activity, current participation · Restrictiveness: Safe.** One person participates in at most one active physical care activity through their existing action slot. Camp care adds no repeating order or pending care queue. Ordinary Pause can preserve uncompleted work; player departure cancels it, and downtime supplies no elapsed recovery. Current-format restoration preserves only legitimately retained work and its still-current participants, physical life and chosen circumstances. A queued old completion cannot heal a replacement life or repeat a completed result.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Reuse understandable action ownership instead of adding parallel patient work, off-screen recovery or another scheduler. Complete elapsed-time correctness, permissions and exactly-once effects are obligations, not tunable allowances. The duration and activity restrictions above are the discretionary choices. Existing [native work](native-work.md), [persistence](persistence.md) and action owners retain their broader admission and integrity rules. [CARE02](../maintainers/recovery-and-care.md#care02--readable-care-life-continuity-and-interruption).

## CARE-L06 — Conditional field dressing and assistance

**Proposed — separately selected later activity · Restrictiveness: Safe.** A field dressing uses **one prepared fiber** and **600 uninterrupted game seconds** for the same ordinary recovery result and food/energy boundary, without a camp requirement. It can be self-applied or performed by **one consenting helper** for **one consenting patient**. Both patient and helper stay living, awake, present, capable, stationary and within **2.2 world units** during assisted work, with their applicable food and energy positive. Either may stop. The material owner and exact prepared fiber are selected; that material is spent only on successful completion. Failed or cancelled work grants no healing and spends no dressing material.

**Reason / tradeoff:** A carried resource buys recovery where the outing needs it; it does not buy a faster timer or impose a resource floor on ordinary camp recovery. A helper supplies actual material or application effort without an automatic potency, speed, gratitude or relationship bonus. One helper keeps the encounter and consent comprehensible; multiple healers, ongoing treatment rosters, forced care and casualty allocation are unsupported. Acceptance grants no general inventory rights or future care permission. Existing custody and competing-resource claims still govern the named supply. [CARE03](../maintainers/recovery-and-care.md#care03--a-useful-field-dressing-and-willing-help) owns selection and complete delivery; it is not a gate on CARE01.

## CARE-L07 — Conditional illness and diagnosis scope

**Proposed — no illness added by first care delivery · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** Camp recovery and field dressing do not introduce a disease roll, automatic wound infection, transmissible condition or secret diagnostic table. Before enabling illness, select **one** condition with a distinct wanted situation, authored cause, observable symptoms, permitted uncertainty, progression and useful response. That condition's numerical tuning must be added to this inventory when selected; it is not inferred from the current health meter.

**Reason / tradeoff:** A separate illness is worthwhile when it creates a different meaningful choice. Merely increasing the number of bodily values or causing new supply demand does not establish that benefit. This restriction delays richer medicine while retaining it as a legitimate future activity. Reuse the selected feature's supported effects without letting health recovery automatically cure every possible cause. [CARE04](../maintainers/recovery-and-care.md#care04--one-distinct-illness-when-it-improves-play).

## CARE-L08 — Discovery, pending work and retained history

**Proposed — no new independent care-count or history limit · Restrictiveness: Too liberal for unchecked aggregate growth.** The feature selects no maximum number of care attempts over a person's life, no total treatment-history age/count cap and no maximum number of eligible people or camps in a world. Repeated attempts can grow ordinary action/results, witnessed events, associated memories and saved history under their existing owners. A short active duration or one action per person does not bound that retained corpus, eligible camps, simultaneous recovering people or service requests waiting before admission.

The first selected camp or person is resolved through current perceived interactions; it does not require a medical census. Discovery still has to explain available choices without silently dropping eligible nearby camps or patients. Page or narrow presentation through existing discoverability behavior when needed; a display page is not a cap on underlying search work. No care-specific automatic retry, patient queue or paid healing tick is selected. Existing queue/work/cold-history/storage limits remain controlling; qualified performance cannot be inferred from the absence of a new feature cap.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Preserve ordinary evidence and independent participation without inventing an arbitrary number of allowed injuries or treatments. Measure complete discovery, pending requests, active progress, projections and accumulated storage when qualifying the chosen activity. If a concrete bottleneck appears, improve the existing indexed/scoped work or admission owner before weakening eligibility, deleting history or reducing the game’s consequences. [CARE05](../maintainers/recovery-and-care.md#care05--complete-gameplay-and-operating-cost-qualification), [native work](native-work.md), [action records](action-experience.md), [memory](memory.md) and [persistence](persistence.md).
