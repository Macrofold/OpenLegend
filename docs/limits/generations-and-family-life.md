# Generations and family life — limits and constraints

This inventory accompanies the [DG31 feature](../projects/generations-and-family-life-feature-spec.md), [childhood/population chapter](../projects/generations-and-family-life/childhood-and-population.md), [world profiles](../worlds/base/generations-and-family-life.md) and [GEN delivery](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md). Entries are **proposed product scope, reviewed October 14, 2026**, not installed rules or measured tuning. Mike has resolved the adult audience/non-explicit presentation direction while retaining optional sex. That approved boundary does not adopt every mechanical value below. Existing agency, observation, privacy, consent, custody, real accounting and current-format integrity are required boundaries, not discretionary limits this inventory can relax.

## GEN-L01 — First authored cast and activity

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** Qualify one human acquaintance with two adult NPC relatives in a deliberately authored optional scenario: Mara starts at 64 years, Ivo at 36. These ages are fictional scenario choices, not medical thresholds or defaults for every body. No births or additional dependents enter this first episode. The three-person scene is an evidence scope, **not a maximum world population** or a claim that those actors cost nothing.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Existing adults make intergenerational relationships immediately available without waiting years or inventing accelerated development. The scene can use ordinary companionship, a useful outing or practical invention. Another coherent world can select a different cast; re-author consistent known background rather than relabel existing people or reuse unreliable starting timestamps. Expand population only for a wanted activity with actual capacity and resources. [GEN01](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen01--a-worthwhile-adult-family-episode).

## GEN-L02 — No new automatic age penalty or control succession

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** The adult-relative scope adds no birthday health/movement/skill loss, automatic retirement, maximum lifespan, fertility threshold, involuntary death by age or automatic transfer of player control to a relative. The selected ordinary bodies retain their existing laws. New disease, nutrients, heredity, anatomy and age-derived frailty are not prerequisites for that family visit. The separately proposed growing-child profile does not impose its developmental pace on existing adults.

**Reason / tradeoff:** A changing purpose or meaningful relationship can be useful before a biological decline model. A mechanical limitation must create a worthwhile new choice, not merely waiting or maintenance. This defers part of the accepted aging ambition; it does not reject it or claim it complete. If a later episode selects a bodily change, record its actual values, evidence, consequences and removal/revisit reason here and with the body owner. [GEN02](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen02--truthful-adult-age-and-selected-bodily-consequences).

## GEN-L03 — Current time, no hidden life acceleration

**Proposed consumer of current shared clock · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Any enabled adult chronology uses actual advancing world calendar time from its authored reference; the existing normal clock is 60 game seconds per real second. The adult-relative profile selects no separate age multiplier, pregnancy timer, chapter skip or catch-up work. The later child-stage and pregnancy proposals are explicitly inventoried below, not a reinterpretation of this first profile. The first age consumer is qualified within one world; compatible travel later requires origin-labeled dates and one actual elapsed interval without age reset, double counting or invented life during a hold. If the world advances while a human is inactive, calendar chronology may advance without bodily aging or experienced history; age at death is distinct from time since death. The shared [time contract](../simulation-time.md) and PS-D03 remain the owners of time choices; this is not a second tunable rate.

**Reason / tradeoff:** One coherent clock avoids manufacturing lived history and resource production. It cannot promise a quick birth-to-adulthood arc: at a uniform 365-day illustrative year, eighteen years take 109.5 uninterrupted real days at 60:1. That arithmetic motivated an explicit optional condensed-childhood proposal, not a recommended waiting requirement or permission to invent experienced years. Human bodily protection cannot be weakened by an age display. [GEN02](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen02--truthful-adult-age-and-selected-bodily-consequences), [GEN-L09](#gen-l09--condensed-child-development-without-a-calendar-rewrite).

## GEN-L04 — Ordinary occasional help, no care administration layer

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** The first adult encounter adds no repeating care orders, household shifts, family attendance rewards, timed birthday choices, automated pantry or new help-specific bonus. Actual selected help uses ordinary supported actions. If DG21's later assisted care is enabled, [CARE-L06](recovery-and-care.md#care-l06--conditional-field-dressing-and-assistance) owns its one willing helper/patient, duration, range, supplies and stopping behavior. It does not supply dependent feeding or incapacity treatment. GEN-L10's new child-care consumer remains a separately delivered capability.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Keep relatives free to pursue ordinary play and make an occasional accepted act of help meaningful. A practical unmet care need may justify an extension; being related is not that need and grants no new authority. The new finite-childcare proposal addresses an actual dependent rather than turning the first adult visit into household management. [GEN01](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen01--a-worthwhile-adult-family-episode), [GEN03](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen03--complete-independence-continuity-and-cost).

## GEN-L05 — Attended qualification and unchanged continued-world scope

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** The first adult-family episode is an attended experience under existing participation. It adopts no new unattended duration, funded population allowance or automatic family protection. [Continuing lives](../projects/continuing-lives-feature-spec.md) and [product-scalability limits](product-scalability.md) retain their actual finite scope; a prior two-resident readiness claim does not qualify another household or births. The later growing-family profile is separately limited to an isolated attended world under GEN-L12.

**Reason / tradeoff:** A family label cannot fund service, produce meals or guarantee a caregiver is available. An optional continued world must qualify its actual cast, full food flow, available character work, service term and recovery. Count new residents honestly even when introduced as relatives. This restriction permits useful play now while preserving the larger ambition as unfinished implementation, not an unanswered audience question. [GEN03](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen03--complete-independence-continuity-and-cost), [GEN07](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen07--care-growing-independence-and-sustainable-operation).

## GEN-L06 — Retained ancestry, experience and aggregate discovery

**No new independent lifetime count/retention bound selected · Restrictiveness: Too liberal for an unqualified growing population.** The proposal sets no maximum number of lifetime relatives, ordinary family encounters, retained dead identities, witnessed experiences or age-record duration. The selected one-dependent qualification in GEN-L11 is not a lifetime cap. Existing [memory](memory.md), [world and family constraints](base-world.md), [persistence](persistence.md), [native work](native-work.md) and service limits remain controlling. The current biological-parent limit is not a bound on total descendants, connected ancestry, all possible relatives or histories.

**Reachable growth / consequence:** New authored adults and separately admitted births/adoptions can enlarge the population; repeated meetings, losses, returns and queries enlarge retained experience and processing. A short displayed family page, two selected relatives or a bounded model prompt does not bound pre-selection traversal, hydration, pending requests, storage or aggregate simultaneous observation. The first episode does not require a world-wide family search, private household dashboard or paid periodic biography generation.

**Reason / tradeoff and revisit:** Preserve meaningful actual continuity without an arbitrary lifetime family-size or memory-deletion law. Before advertising larger or mature generational worlds, qualify complete scoped lookup, active work, cold history, save/load and service cost under existing owners. Add a justified admission/work policy there when needed; do not silently truncate entitled facts, fabricate summaries or prune inconvenient relatives. [GEN03](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen03--complete-independence-continuity-and-cost) and GEN07 retain this unmet qualification. The narrower parent-count and knowledge-integrity rules remain unchanged.

## GEN-L07 — First intimate activity and safe presentation

**Proposed activity scope under an approved presentation boundary · Restrictiveness: Safe.** One currently consenting compatible adult pair and one actual private resting place; 180 game seconds of participation, three normal real seconds, after arrival. No sex need meter, mechanical completion reward, automatic pregnancy or standing consent. Stop, loss of eligibility/control, incompatible work and explicit pause end the encounter without automatic restart. The default opaque-heart presentation and optional fade retain non-explicit text/audio/assets under every supported camera, caption and failure path. A blur cannot hide an otherwise explicit anatomical asset.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Preserve optional adult sex as Mike requested without a long cinematic, anatomy simulation or attached reproductive obligation. A short duration is illustrative world tuning, not a realism claim. Existing invitation/work/budget controls still bound attempts; no special paid allowance or retry loop is added. Broader locations, bodies and participants need a real selected consumer. [GEN05](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen05--optional-non-explicit-adult-intimacy).

## GEN-L08 — Planned pregnancy and first birth

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** The first planned-conception activity creates one pregnancy on valid completion after exact separate assent and admission. No fertility roll, incidental pregnancy from ordinary intimacy, multiple birth or automatic pairing is selected. Pregnancy lasts three advancing game days, 72 normal real minutes, with no extra hunger multiplier, default illness/slowdown, mandatory clinician or lethal childbirth roll. A supported due-boundary birth commits one actual child and bodily transition. Unsupported required execution holds the isolated world rather than fabricating a medical outcome. The gestating person's deliberately confirmed non-graphic ending is a separate proposed action, not automatic cancellation from a relationship change.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Family choice, preparation and real care supply the depth; repeated failed attempts and extra conditions are not necessary first attractions. These are fictional profile rules, not universal biology. Actual mortality ends the pregnancy without a hidden birth, and revival does not resume it. Body-specific variation or a genuine uncertain-fertility experience requires separate explicit rules and useful responses. [GEN06](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen06--planned-pregnancy-birth-and-real-responsibility).

## GEN-L09 — Condensed child development without a calendar rewrite

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** Newborn until one advancing game day, young child from one to five, child from five to twelve, adolescent from twelve to eighteen, adult from eighteen with actual compatible body and presentation. This is a world-owned stage rule using current mechanical time, not a separate calendar or eighteen experienced years. At normal speed, childhood is 7.2 active real hours and pregnancy plus childhood 8.4. Pause, downtime and protected inactive-human bodily state receive no catch-up. Existing adult ages are unchanged.

**Reason / tradeoff:** A multi-session growing relationship is possible without months of uninterrupted operation; the specific pace is untested and may rush attachment. Stage changes grant only supported bodily possibilities, not memories, learned skills, food refills or control. All pre-adult stages remain outside sexual activity; adulthood gives no reward scene or inherited consent. Preserve a clearly authored existing-child alternative and revise pacing if the full sequence adds waiting or erases a valued stage. No accelerated calendar, distributed time jump or retrospective production is delivered. [GEN07](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen07--care-growing-independence-and-sustainable-operation).

## GEN-L10 — Actual food and finite care

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Child nourishment rates are 0.25/0.5/0.75/1.0 of the ordinary adult rate across newborn/young child/child/adolescent-and-adult stages. Reserve capacity is 100 with 75 initial newborn points and no stage refill. A real nursery portion supplies 25 eligible points. One adult feeds within 2.2 world units for 120 uninterrupted game seconds; portion and nourishment commit together, and feeding above 75 fullness is refused as unnecessary. Recheck need at completion; no cancellation refund item or duplicated concurrent feed. Child energy/rest uses the supported installed body policy, not a new obligatory nap/illness economy.

The [world care profile](../worlds/base/generations-and-family-life.md#feeding-withdrawal-and-physical-support) proposes observable hunger cues on downward crossings of 50 and 20 fullness, with no per-tick repeated cue. These are gameplay values, not medical thresholds. An accepted caregiver learns permitted cues through actual attendance, not remote surveillance. Newborns use nursery portions; young children can self-feed those portions; the child stage also supports ordinary cooked food under its actual eating family. No portion is created by a family role. Zero nourishment retains the chosen body's ordinary survival consequences; no extra neglect clock, automatic child removal or hidden immunity is added. The distinct operational failure boundary remains GEN-L12.

One carrier and real supported lowering/handoff preserve a person, never an inventory item. Persistent support allows compatible walking and speech but blocks incompatible handling. End care or Stop walking cannot delete that support. An unavailable lowering retains valid support and a readable blocker; an imminent loss of necessary support without a qualified local aftermath stops only the selected isolated world for explicit recovery. It supplies no public-world rescue or teleportation. Body growth, route fit, departure and a changed carrier must be qualified through the existing body/space owner.

A finite accepted care continuation lasts at most six game hours, six normal real minutes, with discrete physical actions and room for the adult's own needs. The resident may independently renew their intended care; this grants no renewed external spending or unattended service. Expiry or withdrawal does not enroll another parent automatically. Predictable steps in an already accepted plan need no per-step model call. No mandatory diapers, baths, rare crib, paid periodic baby biography or global happiness bonus is selected.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Competent short care frees time for the relationship and other play. Rates, supplies and continuation length are unmeasured authored choices; they need complete food/sleep/access and interruption qualification, not clinical interpretation. Broader care or a second carrier must have real supported benefit and authority before expansion. [GEN07](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen07--care-growing-independence-and-sustainable-operation).

## GEN-L11 — A finite growing family, not free population

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** First qualify one adult human, one willing adult resident and one pregnancy or dependent child in the selected household, with the child's eventual adult capacity admitted as well. The child is an NPC; no automatic player succession or inherited account grant. Biological reproduction, adoption and explicit authored additions count against the same actual population/dependent scope. The initial supported body has no harvest output at any stage and only supported physical resemblance inheritance. Another child after adulthood requires new consent/readiness/capacity; the existing adult child is not removed to make room.

**Reason / tradeoff:** One real family can demonstrate the promise without unbounded care, breeding optimization or a speculative gene system. It limits initial variety, not the world's permanent ambition. The four-day fixture of sixty 38-point adult portions plus four 25-point nursery portions is explicitly finite provisioning, not a renewable source or blanket stock guarantee. Full progression needs real continued supply and costs; preserving an actual child is distinct from admitting another. Extend only for wanted siblings/communities with complete service and gameplay evidence. [World profile](../worlds/base/generations-and-family-life.md#finite-provisions-and-the-wider-population-promise).

## GEN-L12 — Isolated attended service and coherent failure

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** The first growing-family offer is same-world, isolated and attended by one human. Current departure stops supported physical work and holds the whole world. Required service exhaustion likewise stops coherently before unsupported care, depletion or birth. There is no selective baby freeze, caught-up childhood, disappearing pregnancy, hidden pantry, automatic payer top-up or fictional accusation of neglect caused by client/provider failure.

**Reason / tradeoff:** This narrower service makes a viable initial relationship possible without a public-world pause exploit. It does not certify child invulnerability, automatic survival from real adverse choices, or the prior adult-only unattended envelope for children. Larger/shared/unattended families and cross-world custody need their real participation, protection, funding, supply and restoration contracts. Existing hosting/retention policies remain separate; no forever-free family is promised. [GEN07](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen07--care-growing-independence-and-sustainable-operation).
