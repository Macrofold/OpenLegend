# Generations and family life — limits and constraints

This inventory accompanies the [DG31 feature](../projects/generations-and-family-life-feature-spec.md), [adult scenario](../worlds/base/generations-and-family-life.md) and [GEN delivery](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md). Entries are **proposed product scope, October 9, 2026**, not installed rules or measured tuning. The audience-dependent reproductive/dependent-care design is still unfinished. Existing agency, observation, privacy, consent, custody, real accounting and current-format integrity are required boundaries, not discretionary limits that this inventory can relax.

## GEN-L01 — First authored cast and activity

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** Qualify one human acquaintance with two adult NPC relatives in a deliberately authored optional scenario: Mara starts at 64 years, Ivo at 36. These ages are fictional scenario choices, not medical thresholds or defaults for every body. No births or additional dependents enter this first episode. The three-person scene is an evidence scope, **not a maximum world population** or a claim that those actors cost nothing.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Existing adults make intergenerational relationships immediately available without waiting years or inventing accelerated development. The scene can use ordinary companionship, a useful outing or practical invention. Another coherent world can select a different cast; re-author consistent known background rather than relabel existing people or reuse unreliable starting timestamps. Expand population only for a wanted activity with actual capacity and resources. [GEN01](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen01--a-worthwhile-adult-family-episode).

## GEN-L02 — No new automatic age penalty or control succession

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** The independent scope adds no birthday health/movement/skill loss, automatic retirement, maximum lifespan, fertility threshold, involuntary death by age or automatic transfer of player control to a relative. The selected ordinary bodies retain their existing laws. New disease, nutrients, heredity, anatomy and age-derived frailty are not prerequisites for family play.

**Reason / tradeoff:** A changing purpose or meaningful relationship can be useful before a biological decline model. A mechanical limitation must create a worthwhile new choice, not merely waiting or maintenance. This defers part of the accepted aging ambition; it does not reject it or claim it complete. If a later episode selects a bodily change, record its actual values, evidence, consequences and removal/revisit reason here and with the body owner. [GEN02](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen02--truthful-adult-age-and-selected-bodily-consequences).

## GEN-L03 — Current time, no hidden life acceleration

**Proposed consumer of current shared clock · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Any enabled adult age progression uses actually supported elapsed life from its declared authored age; the existing normal clock is 60 game seconds per real second. No separate age multiplier, pregnancy timer, chapter skip or catch-up work is selected. The shared [time contract](../simulation-time.md) and PS-D03 remain the owners of time choices; this is not a second tunable rate.

**Reason / tradeoff:** One coherent clock avoids manufacturing lived history and resource production. It cannot promise a quick birth-to-adulthood arc: at a uniform 365-day illustrative year, eighteen years take 109.5 uninterrupted real days at 60:1. That arithmetic identifies a product choice still to make, not a recommended waiting requirement. A different authored lifecycle needs an explicit supported time policy and full consequences. Human bodily protection cannot be weakened by an age display. [GEN02](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen02--truthful-adult-age-and-selected-bodily-consequences).

## GEN-L04 — Ordinary occasional help, no care administration layer

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** The first encounter adds no repeating care orders, household shifts, family attendance rewards, timed birthday choices, automated pantry or new help-specific bonus. Actual selected help uses ordinary supported actions. If DG21's later assisted care is enabled, [CARE-L06](recovery-and-care.md#care-l06--conditional-field-dressing-and-assistance) owns its one willing helper/patient, duration, range, supplies and stopping behavior. It does not supply dependent feeding or incapacity treatment.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Keep relatives free to pursue ordinary play and make an occasional accepted act of help meaningful. This deliberately delays more extensive domestic simulation. A practical unmet care need may justify a separately selected extension; being related is not that need and grants no new authority. [GEN01](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen01--a-worthwhile-adult-family-episode), [GEN03](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen03--complete-independence-continuity-and-cost).

## GEN-L05 — Attended qualification and unchanged continued-world scope

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** The first family episode is an attended experience under existing participation. It adopts no new unattended duration, funded population allowance or automatic family protection. [Continuing lives](../projects/continuing-lives-feature-spec.md) and [product-scalability limits](product-scalability.md) retain their actual finite scope; a prior two-resident readiness claim does not qualify another household or births. Growing families remain a distinct admission and operating question after D16.

**Reason / tradeoff:** A family label cannot fund service, produce meals or guarantee a caregiver is available. An optional continued world must qualify its actual cast, full food flow, available character work, service term and recovery. Count new residents honestly even when introduced as relatives. This restriction permits useful play now while preserving the larger ambition as unfinished. [GEN03](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen03--complete-independence-continuity-and-cost), [GEN04](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen04--resolve-the-reserved-audience-choice-and-finish-generations).

## GEN-L06 — Retained ancestry, experience and aggregate discovery

**No new independent count/retention bound selected · Restrictiveness: Too liberal for an unqualified growing population.** The independent proposal sets no maximum number of lifetime relatives, ordinary family encounters, retained dead identities, witnessed experiences or age-record duration. Existing [memory](memory.md), [world and family constraints](base-world.md), [persistence](persistence.md), [native work](native-work.md) and service limits remain controlling. The current biological-parent limit is not a bound on total descendants, connected ancestry, all possible relatives or histories.

**Reachable growth / consequence:** New authored adults and later separately authorized births can enlarge the population; repeated meetings, losses, returns and queries enlarge retained experience and processing. A short displayed family page, two selected relatives or a bounded model prompt does not bound pre-selection traversal, hydration, pending requests, storage or aggregate simultaneous observation. The first episode does not require a world-wide family search, private household dashboard or paid periodic biography generation.

**Reason / tradeoff and revisit:** Preserve meaningful actual continuity without an arbitrary family-size or memory-deletion law. Before advertising larger or mature generational worlds, qualify complete scoped lookup, active work, cold history, save/load and service cost under the existing owners. Add an actual justified admission/work policy there when needed; do not silently truncate entitled facts, fabricate summaries or prune inconvenient relatives. [GEN03](../maintainers/generations-and-family-life.md#gen03--complete-independence-continuity-and-cost) retains this unmet qualification. The narrower parent-count and knowledge-integrity rules remain unchanged.
