# Extraordinary minds and afterlife — limits and constraints

This inventory accompanies [DG33's feature](../projects/extraordinary-minds-and-afterlife-feature-spec.md) and [optional world profile](../worlds/base/extraordinary-minds-and-afterlife.md). These are **proposed product choices, October 9, 2026**, not implemented mechanics, evidence of model quality or measured operating capacity. [BW15.1–BW15.6](../maintainers/base-world.md#dg33--voluntary-spirits-and-ordinary-revival) and [EWF10T.1–EWF10T.4](../maintainers/extensible-world-foundation.md#dg33--deliberate-telepathic-messages) retain actual adoption and delivery. One identity, current authority, protected source knowledge, actual custody and once-only outcomes remain integrity requirements.

## MIND-L01 — Two independent first families

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** First qualify one optional encounter with a known adult speaking NPC, Eren, at one real Stillwater Cairn, and one separately useful living telepathic pair. The first Eren body is explicitly authored without harvest output. A real prior acquaintance supplies recognition; a remembered name or identifier alone does not. This is an evidence scene, not a maximum number of dead people, anchors, magical abilities or inhabitants. Telepathy can be delivered without revival, and ghost conversation can be delivered without the expedition.

**Reason / tradeoff:** A familiar person and a wanted exchange justify the new capability. Non-speaking animals do not receive human language or a new mind. Unknown-person divination, hostile possession, human involuntary control, shared-memory colonies and general multi-body execution would each need their own worthwhile scene and supported policy. These are conditional extensions, not prerequisites or promises inferred from the family name. Keep ordinary exploration, useful invention and independent relationships playable before broad magic infrastructure.

## MIND-L02 — Attended manifestation without a countdown

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** One caller can hold one pending invitation or one active encounter; one retained person can hold one pending invitation or one active manifestation. The awake, present caller must stay within 2.2 world units of the actual cairn. Initial preparation takes 60 game seconds, one real second at the normal 60:1 clock. It consumes no scarce offering. Contact exchange, telepathy-link and spirit invitations share [TX-L02](narration.md#tx-l02--contact-admission-and-invitation-load): one outstanding outgoing request and three new offers per sender per rolling sixty real seconds, with exact duplicates counted once and same-episode dismissal cues suppressed. These are shared places/attempts, not a new allowance; CA-L02 keeps its separately recorded slate-call request scope. Failed or uncertain paid work must be reconciled before any further authorized attempt. No fixed manifestation duration is selected: willingness, actual caller presence, current authority, available family and finite world service govern continuation. Either participant may end. Departure, control loss, explicit world pause, removal of the supported family or actual revival ends the manifestation; return requires a fresh deliberate invitation.

**Reason / tradeoff:** A clock copied from a tabletop spell would rush a conversation when game time is accelerated. Anchoring attendance makes the experience legible and avoids a ghost following an absent caller indefinitely. The spirit receives only its selected current local senses and speech; no cargo, ordinary labor, harvesting, remote perception or survival depletion. These spatial and preparation values are provisional world choices, not engine laws. Qualify a natural arrival, conversation and departure before expanding range or roaming.

## MIND-L03 — One finite ordinary return

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** The optional first expedition has one nonrenewing actual return ember at Windward Ledge. One successful return consumes that exact ember once after a 600-game-second rite, ten real seconds at normal speed. Fresh willingness, an eligible retained person, real reachable anchor, usable material and valid living arrival are required. Interrupted or stale attempts do not consume the ember as an application fee. No fresh-corpse or calendar deadline is imposed. The current three-game-day rot and seven-game-day cleanup of remains therefore cannot expire the player's goal.

**Reason / tradeoff:** The journey and real scarce item make return difficult without an obligation to log in, farm random drops or pay repeated failed attempts. One ember makes a complete selected episode economical to author and inspect; it does not establish sustainable repeated revival or a world-wide one-use maximum. Lost, used or unreachable material can genuinely block this optional route. Broader supply, trade, replenishment and simultaneous claims need an actual resource economy and supported recovery before the service promises them. The first route must demonstrate useful play, including reasonable alternatives to combat, rather than merely consume travel time. The selected layout consumes the separately inspected newer-main territorial-stag encounter, including its real signs, four-unit entry footprint, eight-unit pursuit bound, ordinary resident exclusion, sight-blocking cover and actual human danger/recovery package. Its longer route and actual cache stance must remain valid geometry. These are inherited dependency values, not new generic spirit limits or delivered behavior on the pinned branch. If the outing is merely a trivial known pickup, improve the actual optional encounter before calling it difficult; do not add an attendance timer or negate clever avoidance.

## MIND-L04 — Restored physiology with conserved identity and matter

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** The supported NPC returns with full health and normal full needs while retaining age, scars, permanent authored traits, history and relationships. No new equipment, original inventory, restored title, erased debt or youth reset is included. The ghost and old body are reconciled before there is one living successor; prior physical-life bindings cannot act on it. Already separated goods keep their actual custody. The first non-harvestable body does not require a new general yield system. A later harvestable body must conserve already-used and remaining original yield, retire or reconcile old remnants, and add no yield solely because of return. Creator revival's separate resource-fairness exception is not inherited.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Full physiological restoration is finite economic value paid for by the actual ember. Requiring immediate emergency feeding would burden the reunion after the player already earned it. Conserving identity and goods preserves consequence and blocks duplication. A body family that cannot reconcile its real anatomy, conditions, old remnants or yield stays unsupported for this ordinary ritual; do not pretend that checking the actor's name proves compatibility. Human recovery and creator administration remain independently owned and available under their actual policies.

## MIND-L05 — One chosen-message pair within thirty units

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** A capable, awake, living pair consents while in actual sight and reach. Each participant has one active pair place. Eligible chosen messages can pass within 30 world units of actual straight-line separation, through ordinary walls. No passive thoughts, sensory stream, goal rewrite, group mind, raw memory access or extra local-conversation enrollment is included. No device, power drain, per-message reagent or postage resource is added to this family. The intended cost is supported capability and actual attention, not another consumable chore.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Local range makes an otherwise difficult conversation useful without creating all-world presence or remote scouting. A send is evaluated under current private eligibility and grants no read, range, blocked or availability receipt. A participant can leave their own link immediately; the peer may retain a last-agreed link until leaving it themselves. Death, participation/control loss, rebinding, explicit world pause and family removal retire the affected active selection without automatic rejoin. Temporary range loss or sleep prevents actual new reception, with no backlog, and future messages require any still-valid grant. This deliberately trades live status for privacy. Clear last-agreed wording and easy Leave/replacement are required. Increase range or support more participants only for a selected use that justifies disclosure, audience, attention and body-control consequences.

## MIND-L06 — Shared text admission and protected derivatives

**Proposed reuse · Restrictiveness: Medium.** Telepathic text uses [TX-L03–TX-L04](narration.md#tx-l03--message-content-envelope): one recipient, at most 2,000 Unicode code points and 8,000 UTF-8 bytes, six accepted submissions per contact and twenty-four overall in a rolling sixty real seconds per sender across channels, devices and tabs. Both size bounds and the actual complete transport envelope apply. Text, calls and telepathy share the sender allowance; duplicate receipts count once, while accepted submissions excluded by private recipient policy still count. Preserve an oversized draft rather than truncating or splitting it. Submitted confirms admitted sender content only. There is no unread mental inbox, deferred delivery or replay after eligibility returns.

**Reason / tradeoff:** A magical route cannot bypass the real cost and interruption allowance of another message route. These inherited rates may impede a natural rapid exchange; observe the selected use and revise the shared allowance explicitly if needed, rather than secretly creating a second quota. A received human-private message and its derived NPC memories, notes, reasons, appraisals, speech and exports retain [TX-L07](narration.md#tx-l07--disclosure-and-inherited-privacy) protection. Controller change, ghost manifestation, revival, God inspection and fictional restore do not expand access. Native delivery requires no model; an NPC's actual decision uses existing complete cognition admission, including preparation and failures.

## MIND-L07 — No unattended afterlife or automatic reopening

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Retained dead people have no recurring ordinary cognition, senses, needs, dreams, unobserved work or automatic private progress. One legitimate invitation may prepare a bounded consent decision; no unchanged waiting state creates another paid route. Current human participation, world pause, finite hosting, service restrictions and actual family availability remain controlling. Save/restart reconciles the caller and current authority before showing any encounter. It never automatically summons a ghost, resends a mental message, repeats an ember debit or resumes a stale physical-life plan.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Dormancy saves ongoing simulation and avoids a moral attendance chore while retaining the person for a meaningful return. It does not erase durable storage or the cost of cold preparation. Fictional restoration must respect DG25's coherent cut, current privacy, actual external effects and real accounting. A finite hosted world cannot promise perpetual access to every dead character. Wider continued-world or travel support needs the existing participation/recovery owners rather than a special magical exception.

## MIND-L08 — Aggregate invitations, histories and service work

**No new aggregate lifetime bound selected · Restrictiveness: Too liberal for unqualified broad deployment.** No independent maximum for retained dead identities, past manifestations, invitation records, chosen-message history, privacy derivatives or installed anchors is selected here. Per-person concurrency, per-message size and finite displayed pages do not bound many callers, repeated rejected invitations, a popular anchor, cold history preparation or every active world together. Existing [native work](native-work.md), [cognition](cognition.md), [memory](memory.md), [narration](narration.md), [persistence](persistence.md) and [product scalability](product-scalability.md) retain their actual shared admission and growth responsibilities.

**Reachable growth / consequence:** Death and repeated contact accumulate retained history even if only one spirit is visible. Explicit retries can request real consent work despite the absence of an automatic loop. A telepathic NPC response can multiply preparation, reflection and permitted derivative storage beyond the message body. Restrict candidate scope before materializing, reconcile current authority at use, reuse admitted work and record truthful capacity failure; do not silently omit entitled experience or turn a provider error into refusal.

**Reason / tradeoff and revisit:** Count invitation, cold recall, response, actual outing, return, ordinary physiology, memory retention and recovery together. Small authored proof is allowed before a mature-world campaign, but broader advertised admission requires measured whole-workload costs and justified limits through existing owners. No new per-ghost or per-channel AI allowance is selected, and no paid experiment is authorized by writing this inventory. Preserve the ambitious eventual family without implying it is free at arbitrary population or age.
