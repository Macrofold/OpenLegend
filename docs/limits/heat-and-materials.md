# Heat and materials: proposed limits and constraints

[Feature proposal](../projects/heat-and-materials-feature-spec.md) · [Authored profile](../worlds/base/heat-and-materials.md) · [HM delivery](../maintainers/heat-and-materials.md) · [Tracking rules](README.md)

**Proposed, October 6, 2026; not adopted, implemented or measured.** These selections define DG20's first useful firing activity and small material-fire family. The world profile owns the actual materials, source behavior and outcomes. The numbers below are abstract game tuning, not real material-processing or fire-safety predictions. Current object, spatial, native-work, state, time, participation and spending limits remain controlling outside an explicitly introduced consumer.

## HM-L01 — A selected personal material experiment

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** Begin with a separate personal, isolated attended profile and the ordinary human, Ada and the Peacock Mercenary. The new family affects its expressly supported materials; it adds no body-temperature damage, burns, smoke injury, asphyxia, animal damage or public contested-property mode. Material-use authority must be real and explicit, rather than inferred from carrying, visiting, an item's name or a character's request.

**Reason / tradeoff:** A useful object and understandable local material consequences can be qualified without first settling public indirect harm, medicine or every property rule. The choice applies coherently throughout the selected world. It is not a hidden exemption for a preferred actor or offline owner. MP04 and DG13's proposed construction grants retain their actual boundaries. No existing world or save is automatically enrolled, reseeded or retroactively burned; combining the family with shared or unattended play needs its own protection and continuation qualification.

## HM-L02 — Finite starting materials and a modest vessel

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** One new prepared profile supplies **two actual unfired bowl blanks, one cold stone firing hearth, two substantial wooden wall panels, two dry twig targets, four brief igniters, six ordinary fuel units and one reusable extinguishing tool**. These are finite authored materials, not recurring grants or a claim about the current starter inventory. Each blank and empty finished bowl contributes **two packing units**; a finished bowl has **eight packing units of capacity** under ordinary solid-item containment.

**Reason / tradeoff:** A small endowment lets the player make, use, organize or offer an actual object before a clay-production industry exists. It provides no liquid handling, serving action, food preservation, bodily benefit, extra carrying strength or automatic contents disclosure. Current woven containers remain a viable alternative. Inputs and output retain one actual material history, and repeat inspection, reset of a draft, return or ordinary dismantling grants no new stock. Any further production method must account for its own actual material source.

## HM-L03 — A brief source and visibly different targets

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** The selected brief igniter delivers at most **one abstract heat unit per game minute for five game minutes**. The selected dry twig/prepared-fiber target ignites after **four accumulated units**; a selected substantial wooden panel requires **thirty**. The comparison uses the same source behavior and exposure arrangement. Ordinary target cooling removes **one unit per game minute when the authored cooling condition applies**; source removal, moisture and prior exposure must follow the profile's exact continuous-history rule.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Material form and a finite source produce an understandable difference without treating every combustible object as an immediate flame switch. The selected dry twig can catch during the source's lifetime while the substantial wall does not. Wet/dry material is an additional comparison, not a substitute for the wall. These are initial game values, not calibrated ignition thresholds or a claim that any brief flame behaves the same. At 1×, a five-game-minute source lasts only five real seconds; it cannot serve as a guaranteed time allowance for reading and deciding.

## HM-L04 — Reuse moisture without a new survival penalty

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** Preserve DG13's selected cloth/prepared-fiber moisture meaning and local exposure: fully incident rain saturates a fully exposed eligible dry item in **thirty game minutes**, and no incident rain permits saturated-to-dry recovery in **ninety game minutes**. The optional heat consumer spends **thirty delivered heat units per full moisture amount removed** before that heat can warm the same moist target toward ignition. It changes the same material moisture, with actual rain, cover and elapsed time accounted for once.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Supported wet material can respond differently while retaining DG13's complete recovery route. This does not add universal wet wood, nested-bag waterproofing, climate, evaporation physics or a new campfire-lighting/crafting penalty. The current contained cooking fire keeps its existing lighting law. Heat-assisted drying and ordinary ambient recovery must not both claim the same removed moisture or create useful water as an undeclared output. Unknown exposure remains unsupported, rather than being presented as dry or fireproof.

## HM-L05 — One useful, forgiving firing process

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** The new outdoor hearth is **firing-only** in this first profile. One bowl requires **forty warming units followed by two hundred irreversible firing units**. With the full four-unit-per-minute source delivered to that one blank, completion takes **sixty game minutes**; two equally exposed blanks share the source and each takes **one hundred and twenty game minutes**. These are best-case source allocations, not unconditional recipe durations. A bowl cools from its fully warmed condition in **twenty game minutes** after heat ceases, at **two warming units per minute**.

Actual completed firing is retained through interruption; lost warmth requires reheating. The first hearth cannot overfire an already completed bowl, so late collection is not a hidden destruction timer. The blank does not coexist with a second finished copy. The selected hearth holds at most **three game hours of banked fuel**, using the current one-hour credit per eligible ordinary fuel unit. The existing contained cooking fire retains its separate **forty-eight-hour** cap and cooking behavior.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Passive finite processing gives the player time for another chosen activity. A one-hour fuel unit exactly covers the ideal single-bowl exposure only when the blank is already positioned, the full source is available and no earlier burning or competing recipient has spent that allowance. Review actual remaining fuel and delivered heat. Additional setup, interruption, reheating and nearby materials can increase costs. Current cooking and DG19 preservation do not become free extra consumers of the new hearth's same output.

## HM-L06 — Actual local exposure shares a finite source

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** The hearth releases at most **four transferable heat units per game minute**. A burning twig releases **one half-unit per minute** and a burning wooden section **two units per minute**, while their actual fuel remains. Supported contact has weight **four**; exposed nearby material within **half a metre** has weight **one**. Divide each source's budget over all actual eligible recipients by total weight with a divisor of at least four; unused output is lost to the unmodeled environment. The material/geometry owner preserves exposure weight through quantity splitting, so creating more identifiers cannot manufacture received heat.

**Reason / tradeoff:** A local fire can affect more than one thing without giving every target a copy of its full output. Actual gaps, geometry and the supported stone obstruction matter; shared building membership and visual particles do not create exposure. Several sources may contribute to one target, but source removal ends only that source's contribution. Half a metre is a declared coarse world rule to qualify, not a server-load-dependent cutoff or permission to omit expensive nearby recipients. Candidate discovery and connected propagation can still grow beyond a single source's neighborhood.

## HM-L07 — Finite burning and local surviving material

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** A selected burning twig has **twenty game minutes** of initial fuel; a selected wall panel has **sixty**. Each substantial panel is a real independently grounded construction part, **one metre wide, one and a half metres high and one tenth of a metre thick**, with actual upright obstruction. At half its original combustible material remaining, that panel breaks down to its supported low, nonblocking charred pile; the remaining **thirty-minute** fuel allowance and actual heat follow that material. The other panel does not fail by association.

**Reason / tradeoff:** The paired wall comparison has a real material and spatial consequence. It does not require a whole-building durability flag or a heavy-collapse solver. Extinguishing, picking up, splitting or rebuilding cannot restore burned material or yield both full fuel and intact salvage. The profile must name every supported residue and handling rule. DG13's flexible-cover failure remains non-injurious and non-obstructing when actually consumed by a qualified extension; that rule is not proof that arbitrary walls, beams or roofs can collapse safely.

## HM-L08 — A practical response before wider destruction

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** The selected extinguishing action takes **thirty game seconds**, actual normal reach and the reusable admitted tool. It stops combustion on the named source/material, retains remaining actual fuel and reduces its warmth to **one quarter of that material's ignition threshold**, followed by ordinary cooling. Covering the firing hearth stops that hearth's source. An independent external flame can still reheat or reignite a target; extinguishing one object is not a whole-site immunity effect.

**Reason / tradeoff:** The player has an ordinary supported response and retains honest prior loss. It is not a water, smoke, fluid, automatic firefighting or universal extinguishing system. At the current clock the work itself takes only half a real second at 1×, while approach, noticing and choosing also take time. No human reaction guarantee follows. Ordinary cooking/preservation keep their existing interruption protection on the separate contained fire. Any later combined source must explicitly define when an emergency override is permitted and account for spent raw cooking input versus the retained DG19 portion; that future combination is not a first-profile prerequisite.

## HM-L09 — Coupled time and complete operating cost

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** The reference experience uses the current **1× clock: one real second per game minute**. Fuel, warming, firing, damage, moisture, work and bodily needs use the same advancing mechanical time. Acceleration grants no extra human reaction time. The personal whole-world hold follows ordinary participation reconciliation; it does not preserve cancelled human work or freeze only the endangered object. Restart supplies no imaginary elapsed fire or free rewind of real spending.

**Reason / tradeoff:** One ideal bowl takes one real minute of heating plus up to twenty real seconds of cooling; two blanks sharing the source take two minutes of heating before cooling. Actual setup, fuel, tools, current hunger and interruptions add to the complete journey. No model call is required for every heating step, neighbor, flame or completed unit. The finite kit and local exposure rule establish neither universal population capacity nor a bound on all current/cold/history work. Required measurements include discovery, connected material changes, altered geometry, observations, independent decisions and retained aftermath. Overload uses the actual operational owner and never silently changes combustibility or ignores elapsed burning.

## HM-L10 — Expansion follows a better game

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** First establish a useful made object and a legible material comparison, then qualify local accidents, stopping, salvage and actual changed space. Broad wildfire, public property conflict, bodily fire injury, smoke/ventilation, fluids, charcoal industries, general furnaces and full structural failure remain separate extensions. They are not necessary merely because this coarse family can be described.

**Reason / tradeoff:** A bowl that the player values can justify making; an impressive spreading flame alone cannot. Compare the complete interaction with current crafting/storage and the dependable cooking fire. If it mainly asks players to watch timers, constantly extinguish accidents or acquire more supplies to replace avoidable losses, simplify or defer it. Preserve ordinary adventure, conversation, invention, refusal and leaving work behind as legitimate choices. Later expansion must state its new benefit and requalify actual materials, permissions, recovery and complete cost.
