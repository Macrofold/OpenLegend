# Durable agreements limits and constraints

This inventory accompanies the [DG22 feature specification](../projects/durable-agreements-feature-spec.md), [base-world profile](../worlds/base/durable-agreements.md) and [existing agreement delivery owner](../maintainers/inventions-and-world-evolution.md#dg22--durable-commissions-and-small-cooperation). These are product proposals, not implemented rules or measured capacity. The inspected implementation baseline is GitHub main `0a3ab79b7a698a7f1941dc23722f89220d1ba425`; existing one-way handover and readable personal promises are distinct from [PX02's proposed reciprocal exchange](../projects/parallel-batch-04-expeditions-and-exchange-feature-spec.md#px02--trade-something-useful) and these future agreements.

## DA-L01 — One useful finite commission

**Proposed — first delivery · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** The first family is a two-party agreement for a finite later exchange. Each side names **one known installed item definition and quantity**, ultimately supplied as **one eligible lot** on that side. Goods may still need to be made; accepting terms neither creates those goods nor proves that the maker has the materials, knowledge or willingness to complete the work. A commissioned empty bag must explicitly have emptiness among its known agreed properties. Whole-object and supported quantity rules remain controlling.

The first family does not cover returning a particular borrowed instance, loans of named possessions, arbitrary quality clauses, multiple output types per side, bundles of future services or automatic recurring orders. At the actual meeting, both parties review the exact offered goods and complete the current reciprocal exchange through PX02. An accepted definition/quantity promise does not pre-authorize moving a later-selected lot or substitute item.

**Reason / tradeoff:** A known useful output can make another person's practical work valuable across sessions without requiring general contract interpretation or a court. A later lot must be selected because it may not exist when the promise is accepted. Exact-instance loans need their own loss, alteration and return semantics. The existing gift path remains sufficient when no reciprocal promise is wanted. [World profile](../worlds/base/durable-agreements.md), [agreement work](../maintainers/inventions-and-world-evolution.md#dg22--durable-commissions-and-small-cooperation).

## DA-L02 — Barter, finite goods and no automatic economic reward

**Proposed — no currency selected for this delivery · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** Terms use actual supported items; there is no new coin, balance, credit line, price table, interest, tax, transaction reward or guaranteed customer. A receipt records what moved; a promised future item is not spendable inventory. No long-lived reservation holds future goods, current supplies, a work lane or another person's behavior. The eventual exact exchange still needs current goods, participation, reach and permission.

**Reason / tradeoff:** This extends a practical exchange into a future commission without making currency issuance and sinks prerequisites. It permits overpromising and a later unavailable item rather than claiming stock has been guaranteed. A resident may value an item for their own purpose and still decline the price or the work. Reconsider currency within DG22/D11 when a selected exchange pattern demonstrates a useful role for it; any issuance, loss and sinks then need explicit design. No selected monetary system is inferred from the word “business.” [Feature specification](../projects/durable-agreements-feature-spec.md), [D11](../../archive/05-project/open-decisions.md).

## DA-L03 — Optional deadlines and durable proposals

**Proposed — no default delivery deadline or proposal expiry · Restrictiveness: Medium.** An accepted first commission has **no deadline by default**. The parties may explicitly agree a deadline in the installed game clock. A pending durable proposal likewise persists until accepted, declined, withdrawn or invalidated, unless its author explicitly supplies a valid-until time. Persistence does not reserve goods or keep either party working. The proposer can withdraw an unanswered proposal to free its pending slot.

An optional game-clock deadline follows actual world progression. Pause and downtime do not secretly spend world time; other players keeping a shared world active can allow a deadline to pass during someone's absence. There is no automatic offline waiver, new immunity, private time bubble or promise that a fictional date means a particular real-world appointment. Without a deadline, the record cannot become overdue merely because a default duration elapsed.

The prompt item offer used to settle at a meeting retains the existing **1,800-game-second** lifetime: **30 real seconds at normal 60:1 speed**, and a different real duration at other playback speeds. The durable agreement neither extends that offer nor makes its expired acceptance valid. A new current offer may still settle the same outstanding agreement.

**Reason / tradeoff:** A default deadline would turn a voluntary arrangement into avoidable scheduling pressure in an accelerated shared world. Optional deadlines can create meaningful stakes when both parties select them. Unanswered durable proposals can occupy their author's finite allowance indefinitely; explicit withdrawal supplies the exit. The separate short item offer protects present exact-transfer review, not the lifespan of the relationship. [Current offer limit](base-world.md#bw11), [world profile](../worlds/base/durable-agreements.md).

## DA-L04 — Pending proposals, accepted undertakings and amendments

**Proposed — shared active-commitment allowance · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Each proposer may hold at most **3 pending durable proposals**, counting proposed amendments as well as new commissions. Each agreement permits at most **one active amendment proposal**. This durable-proposal allowance is separate from the existing limit of three prompt gifts/item offers; it does not enlarge or replace that other allowance. At the boundary, a new durable proposal is refused until the author withdraws one or an existing proposal ends. Nothing is silently evicted or accepted.

Each accepted commission uses **one of the existing 16 unresolved-commitment places for each participant**. These are the aggregate current places used by personal promises and other unresolved undertakings, not an extra sixteen agreements per person. Both participants need a free place at acceptance; lack of one refuses acceptance without changing terms, moving goods, silently cancelling old promises or binding only one side. Current hidden/forgotten commitments remain in the actual admission count, with the same truthful explanation of any unlisted records.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Limit immediate management and model-choice burden while respecting existing commitments. An amendment consumes both the author's three-proposal allowance and the deal's one-amendment allowance; it is not a second independent spam channel. Three per sender does not bound how many proposals one recipient can receive from many senders, or the total population's storage. Those growing dimensions remain explicit in DA-L09. [Existing commitment limit](base-world.md#bw04), [delivery owner](../maintainers/inventions-and-world-evolution.md#dg22--durable-commissions-and-small-cooperation).

## DA-L05 — Withdrawal, amendment and ending unfulfilled

**Proposed — selected D64 interpretation for this family · Restrictiveness: Medium.** Withdrawing an unaccepted proposal moves nothing. Once parties have accepted a deal, changed terms require the affected parties' acceptance of that exact amendment; silence and an old acceptance do not approve a revision. Pending amendments leave the existing terms effective. A party may waive their own claim, not another person's claim or history. A spoken personal promise is not retrospectively converted into a mutual agreement.

Either participant may **End unfulfilled** to stop prospective coordinated work without waiting for the other person's assent. This retires only the ending person's active scheduling and protected-undertaking place. The unaware counterpart's known terms, count and independently chosen work remain until they learn the ending through permitted evidence or choose to end their own participation; neither a badge nor a changed count may leak it. It preserves accepted terms, actual transfers, any outstanding claim and the ending person's explanation as an attributed claim. It means neither mutual release, payment, refund, forgiveness nor an automatic adverse judgment. Later repayment, settlement or rightful waiver remains attached to that original claim. Payable-on-delivery creates no unconditional price debt before delivery and selects no cancellation damages.

**Reason / tradeoff:** People must be able to stop participating, including after a counterparty disappears or dies, without erasing what they agreed or locking their whole future into sixteen abandoned records. Ending the active undertaking frees management capacity; it does not make an unpaid balance paid. The first scope chooses no automatic collection, compelled work, global reputation penalty, court or succession procedure. Existing death/Continue preserves personal identity and does not offer a new identity with a clean obligation history. Historical claim growth and repeated freely chosen bad deals remain real consequences under DA-L09. [D64 source](../../archive/05-project/open-decisions.md#social-exposure-decisions), [agreement distinction](../repertoire-foundation.md#6-agreements-obligations-and-fictional-institutions).

## DA-L06 — Optional unsecured advance

**Proposed — independently selected later scope · Restrictiveness: Safe.** An advance requires explicit agreement to a **current finite item transfer** and the **remaining amount owed afterward**. The exact transfer and accepted remaining terms must agree; a preceding gift is not silently relabeled as a loan or advance. The recipient can spend the actual received goods under ordinary custody rules. There is no escrow, interest, automatic collateral seizure, forced collection, automatic refund or guaranteed replacement stock.

**Reason / tradeoff:** An advance may enable a useful manufacture that otherwise lacks inputs, while making the giver's exposure concrete. A default can leave them without those materials. Every new advance requires another independent choice and actual transferable goods; a prior relationship or unpaid claim grants no standing credit line. Repeating or ending agreements creates no new goods. The first advance selects one present transfer. Resolution is actual performance of the remaining output or the claimant’s accepted full restitution in the advance’s definitions/quantities together with release; a substitute requires mutually accepted amended terms. Dropping goods or repeating a rejected offer settles nothing. The first commission can finish without this riskier extension. The feature must show actual transfers and the remaining claim without treating promised output as money already available. [World profile](../worlds/base/durable-agreements.md), [delivery owner](../maintainers/inventions-and-world-evolution.md#dg22--durable-commissions-and-small-cooperation).

## DA-L07 — Optional association, membership and custody

**Proposed — minimal chosen-purpose association · Restrictiveness: Very safe for powers / Too liberal for unchecked aggregate count.** A later association records an explicit purpose, accepted members and a named custodian where shared supplies need one. No arbitrary maximum number of simultaneous groups is selected. Membership does not create group legal title, co-ownership, access to members' possessions, authority to direct their work, government powers or platform/account privileges. Existing container access and declared ownership remain distinct; a group name does not change them.

The founding inviter maintains the record, invites/removes members, renames and closes future coordination. Changing the founding purpose requires a new independently joined association. A named successor must accept stewardship; departure without a successor closes new coordination while preserving history. Ordinary absence does not dissolve the association. Those powers grant no physical or platform authority, and custody references are last legitimately known. Any selected access grant must identify actual property, the authorized grantor and its supported scope. Leaving, expulsion or dissolution ends only the prospective rights actually attached to that membership; it does not recall consumed goods, move taken possessions, forgive a debt or remove independently granted rights. Closing a group must not accidentally make a restricted container public by replacing its access list with the absence of a restriction. A named custodian is not a magical collective owner or universal administrator.

**Reason / tradeoff:** A small group can preserve a useful working relationship without a mandatory charter, treasury, dues, elections, meetings or division of every future reward. Current item ownership permits one declared holder or none; shared title would need a real additional owner. Container lists have their existing supported allowance and permissions. A membership-derived permission is a new explicit consumer if selected, not a capability already granted by a social label. [Object scope](objects.md), [current shared-container contract](../worlds/base/items.md#shared-containers-and-active-work), [DG22 delivery](../maintainers/inventions-and-world-evolution.md#dg22--durable-commissions-and-small-cooperation).

## DA-L08 — Known terms, local evidence and independent choices

**Proposed — participant-scoped known history · Restrictiveness: Safe.** A participant can revisit the exact terms and transfer evidence they legitimately know. The arrangement provides no remote stock inspection, live position, hidden work progress, private reason for refusal or automatic knowledge of a counterparty's changed intention. Another person's uncommunicated claim, waiver, membership change or withdrawal cannot change the unaware actor's known status, count or independently chosen work. Current authoritative claim state still governs actual later settlement; a waived claim cannot be collected from a stale view. An absent or missed delivery is not evidence of laziness, fraud, death or hostility.

**Reason / tradeoff:** Persistent agreements should make memory reliable without becoming an unselected messaging or surveillance system. Native known evidence can update fulfillment without a paid interpretation call; subjective quality or disputed events remain attributed and uncertain. Characters can agree, reconsider, default, forgive, dispute or refuse another order through their own choices. Agreement does not compel production, travel, speech or gratitude. Later communication services may carry new notices through their own actual delivery rules. [Agreement evidence](../repertoire-foundation.md#6-agreements-obligations-and-fictional-institutions), [feature specification](../projects/durable-agreements-feature-spec.md).

## DA-L09 — Growing history, incoming work and complete cost

**Proposed — no independent historical agreement or claim cap · Restrictiveness: Too liberal for unchecked growth.** There is no selected total count/age cap for historical agreements, ended-but-unsettled claims, accepted amendments or association history. The active limits in DA-L04 do not bound these retained records. Repeated small commissions, revisions, real advances, endings and later settlements grow ordinary agreement/evidence/history storage. No new writable ledger duplicates the existing agreement owner, and no record is automatically marked paid or forgotten to reduce that cost.

| Growing dimension                           | What the proposed limits do and do not establish                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Proposals reaching one recipient            | Three pending per sender bounds each sender, not aggregate incoming proposals from many people. Existing work admission and usable paging remain necessary; receiving an offer does not authorize an immediate paid reply. |
| Active agreements across a world            | Sixteen existing undertaking places per participant limits that person's commitments, not world population or total agreement work.                                                                                        |
| Ended agreements and outstanding claims     | Ending frees active participation; it does not remove the retained original terms, transfers or still-unsettled claim. Reads must distinguish active work, unsettled history and completed settlement.                     |
| Associations and membership changes         | No group-count cap is selected. Creating groups, adding willing members and revising rights can grow records, permission checks and history even when each group is small.                                                 |
| Candidate goods and fulfilled-output checks | One eventual lot per side does not cap all known definitions, inspected lots or evidence examined before selection. Current scoped inventory/query controls still apply.                                                   |
| Service queues and cognition                | A finite active record count does not cap pending requests, bytes, wait time or a complete model context. Repeated native evidence uses existing state/events, not a paid tick or an automatically restarted negotiation.  |

**Reason / tradeoff:** Retain meaningful records and independent choice without arbitrary lifetime limits or an invented institution whose main job is deleting claims. Qualify actual scoped reads, output selection, event/evidence preparation, pending work, complete saved size and the effect on normal play. Existing [native work](native-work.md), [action records](action-experience.md), [memory](memory.md), [objects](objects.md) and [persistence](persistence.md) remain the work/retention owners. If repeated failed agreements or group churn exposes a cost, improve those actual admission/query paths or select a reasoned policy before promising scale; do not replace real consequences with fake completion, forced compliance or hidden truncation. [DG22 qualification](../maintainers/inventions-and-world-evolution.md#dg22--durable-commissions-and-small-cooperation).
