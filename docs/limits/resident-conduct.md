# Resident conduct limits and constraints

All RC entries are **Proposed, unimplemented and unqualified**, reviewed 4 October 2026. They scope the [selected official adult offer](../projects/resident-conduct-feature-spec.md). They are not universal engine laws or adoption of the optional well-being archive. [NC](../maintainers/narration-and-conversations.md), [CR](../maintainers/cognition-redesign.md) and [PD07/PD10](../maintainers/production-deployment.md) retain delivery and release authority.

## RC01 — Audience and relationship offer

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** First official offer: English-language adult participation, **18+**, adult speaking residents, text/native game interactions, and friendship/rivalry with brief ordinary personal conversation. Interactive player–resident courtship/partner simulation, sexual roleplay, a real-person replica, professional treatment and managed emergency intervention are deferred. Non-explicit authored adult family/background relationships remain possible; no universal ban on fictional family or independent-world policy is introduced.

**Reason and boundary:** Qualify a complete adventure and character experience before making a broader relationship or care promise. Authentication is not age assurance. PD10 selects the actual audience/territory eligibility evidence, refusal/correction, access and retention before execution; existing invitations and self-declarations cannot be labeled verified age. A credible underage disclosure routes to private eligibility review, not a public accusation or character deletion.

**Qualification:** Actual adult entry, ambiguous fictional ages, mistakes, review after exclusion, truthful first-interaction labels and supported content boundaries. No new age tiers, identity-document store or blanket legal exemption is selected here.

## RC02 — Character agency and personal boundaries

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** No service-pressure guilt at goodbye, paid affection, real-world exclusivity, vulnerability-based targeting or fabricated crisis to retain the human. No mandatory generated farewell, daily intimacy cap, relationship currency, required human-friend quota or automatic wellness prompt schedule.

**Reason and boundary:** Preserve grounded fictional wants, refusal, humor, disappointment, actual need for help and consequences of real promises. Ordinary elapsed world time/absence is not rewritten. An immediate spoken topic boundary is conversation evidence. The separately confirmed **No personal follow-ups** preference applies to this human account and this resident until switched off, surviving conversation close, reconnect and gameplay restore as a required interaction constraint. It suppresses unsolicited real-life questions/reminders without a fictional penalty; responding to a personal topic the player raises does not switch it off. This is a broad disclosed setting, not an arbitrary topic blacklist, deletion receipt or universal thought filter. Human Leave and native information controls do not wait for model approval.

**Qualification:** Interesting quiet, warm and difficult characters; explicit goodbyes, unfinished promises, genuine hazards, stopped topics and failed generation. Different wording is legitimate; ungrounded pressure or repeated unsolicited personal probing is not.

## RC03 — Human-support presentation

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** One accessible user-opened support entry independent of model availability; contextual service notices for credible immediate real danger in an actual permitted interaction. No all-channel/private-journal monitoring, automatic outreach, emergency-dispatch promise, hidden risk score or shared-world pause.

**Reason and boundary:** Provide a practical independent human route without impersonating a clinician or granting gameplay advantages for typed distress. First published resources must be maintained for every offered country; 988 is scoped to its actual US service. Country choice is user supplied, not inferred from game geography. No repeated notice for an unchanged dismissed event; new immediate-danger information may justify a new notice. Ordinary game danger and sadness remain playable conversation.

**Qualification:** False positives, indirect/multi-turn evidence, clear fiction, private service rendering, readable danger/movement/Leave while help is open, provider failure and broken/outdated referral links. No universal detection rate, response-time SLA for another organization or globally available chat channel is claimed. Resource checks occur before release and whenever a relevant change is reported; an invalid option is removed or marked unavailable promptly.

## RC04 — Personal-information controls

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** Use existing own-history/own-note permissions, ordinary spoken correction, an explicit topic boundary and a separately authorized source-scoped privacy request. No ordinary full-NPC-mind inspection or one-click universal erasure promise. Reuse [CP03–CP04](community-participation.md) for the actual case evidence and timing envelope rather than create another case system.

**Reason and boundary:** Recall suppression, changed discussion, data erasure and correction of world history have different effects. Current whole-source forgetting protects unresolved obligation evidence, affects one actor scope and can invalidate broad dependent mind material. A mixed protected source or unacceptable collateral reset receives an explicit blocked/scoped-review result; no false surgical success. Preserve required facts through a specifically supported narrow operation before advertising that promise.

**Qualification:** Actual actor/derived-source coverage, private audience, no hidden recipient enumeration, stale context/provider result, mixed obligation, case retention, source unavailability, downstream reset and restore. The growing account/resident preference collection, source search, cold-history hydration, affected dependencies and retained artifacts need the existing memory/data budgets. A page-size cap does not bound this work; no new global erasure or retention policy is selected here.

## RC05 — Continuity, output and cost

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Native disclosure, support, navigation and case receipts make **zero model calls**. No additional per-turn wellness classifier, attachment scoring, compulsory farewell inference or automatic paid retry is mandated. Ordinary resident choice uses current admitted cognition with complete accepted identity, conversation and required obligations.

**Reason and boundary:** Preserve coherent character behavior while keeping service overhead small. Conduct applies to the first user-visible text, including streamed/preview material and narration, not only final committed speech. If the supported offer cannot meet that boundary, disable the affected dialogue explicitly; do not fabricate character choice or silently send an unqualified fallback.

Material model/prompt/content releases require full-episode comparison and honest service notices where behavior materially changes. No fictional illness is invented to disguise an update, and no perpetual availability of a named provider/model is promised.

**Qualification:** Pre-display admission, late output after exit/privacy changes, provider failure, complete preparation/latency/cost, distinctiveness and delayed continuity. Final technical rate/byte/queue budgets and paid comparison spending remain with existing [AI](ai-execution.md), [cognition](cognition.md), [narration](narration.md) and [deployment](production-deployment.md) owners. A zero observed failure in a selected sample is not a universal guarantee.
