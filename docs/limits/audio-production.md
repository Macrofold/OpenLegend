## Growth and qualification

Manual authoring grows recordings, revisions, vocabularies and catalogue metadata. Runtime play grows only bounded recent-selection/cursor/cache state in this slice; it never creates new music files or provider jobs. History of requested studies remains with asset retention. Tag queries do not implicitly hydrate all source files. Finite output pages alone are not proof of bounded preselection work; measure index-build cost and filter candidate counts separately.

The selected measurements report build/search work, decode/cache memory, stream/voice overlap and device behavior. A failed measurement records its workload and remaining limitation; a required correctness/listening acceptance failure remains open in AUD. Do not invent capacity claims or a new stress campaign for this design-only task.
