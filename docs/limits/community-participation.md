# Community participation limits and constraints

All CP entries are **Proposed, unimplemented and unqualified**, reviewed 4 October 2026. They scope the [community participation feature](../projects/community-entry-and-participation-feature-spec.md), not private-world multiplayer generally. [MP/PD delivery](../maintainers/multiplayer.md) and [production gates](../maintainers/production-deployment.md) own implementation and release. Ratings describe restrictiveness, not proof of safety.

## CP01 — First public offering

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** One curated community and one advertised human welcome at a time; initial qualification profile of two simultaneously embodied humans including the host. A separate operator/support connection and reconnect headroom must be included in the actual measured deployment profile. Managed adult entry; no automatic public world grant, federation or public live spectator expansion.

**Reason and boundary:** Focus staff and engineering on a complete useful visit. At capacity, preserve current control and applicable reconnect claims, then admit existing-member waits before unscheduled new visits, in acknowledged order within each category. Do not overbook a scheduled welcome against active residents or evict one to honor it. Pause intake when no actual opportunity is available. Existing larger workload targets remain unchanged; expanding the offering requires measured capacity and staffing.

**Qualification:** Actual entry, late host, full return, correlated reconnect, maintenance and safe-arrival cases; useful play after finite rewards were already consumed. Audience/age policy and continuity/retention require their respective owner selection before execution.

## CP02 — Requests and invitations

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** One pending request per account/community, at most three across the managed directory, and ten pending requests per first-pilot community. Optional applicant note up to 1,000 Unicode characters. Duplicate submissions recover the same request. Received, Needs your answer and Approved-but-unredeemed all count as pending; approval does not remove an offer or its reservation from the envelope. Redeemed, Declined, Withdrawn and Expired end the pending request. Applicant withdrawal ends it immediately.

**Reason and boundary:** Enough choice without allowing unlimited host work. A full queue refuses a new request honestly and shows the next published opportunity; no hidden waitlist is created. Editing a pending note replaces its current view while retaining only necessary review history under the adopted data policy. Selected offer expiry uses the existing invite owner's permitted range, not a second timer invented here. An approved public offer is bound to the verified requesting account. Wrong-account attempts do not consume it. Redemption also rechecks the actual welcome window, available host and current capacity; a valid token with a stale welcome requires an assented rebooking, never unattended first arrival. At most one unclaimed prepared starter exists for the first community; preparation occurs near the agreed welcome and does not freeze autonomous life. Binding rechecks the accepted current situation, and a materially changed offer needs renewed assent. Ineligible or established residents are not silently substituted or erased; additional preparation is an explicit funded content decision.

**Qualification:** Measure pre-selection/search and per-account/world request checks, message bytes, all open requests, staff review time and expired-record retention. Runtime traffic/burst limits and total account/world record retention remain unselected public-release inputs; a small final list does not bound preparation. No paid matching, character generation or NPC wake-up occurs during application.

## CP03 — Personal contact and report evidence

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** Mute and Block contact persist until the user removes them, across that account's characters in the managed service. No automatic expiry or unsolicited “forgive” prompt. Account relationships remain private. Block withdraws pending direct interactions and refuses future direct contact in either direction; it does not reverse settled effects or erase physical participation.

A first report submission permits up to **20 selected plus adjacent message/event records in total**, **64 KiB total UTF-8 evidence text**, and a **4,000-Unicode-character explanation**. Preview exact included context; no automatic attachment of a whole conversation, notes, mind or world save. Arbitrary file uploads are deferred; a screenshot is not required to report. Users can submit a smaller supported excerpt and add specifically requested evidence later.

**Reason and boundary:** Make a meaningful case affordable to submit/review while preventing indiscriminate private-history collection. If selection exceeds the envelope, show what is outside it before submission; never silently truncate or claim a complete context. Incomplete or unavailable evidence is labeled. No report-count-based guilt or automatic ban. Personal relief works independently of reporting capacity.

**Qualification:** Source selection and context preparation must be bounded before loading; counts, aliases, previews, redactions, queues, caches and exports must respect audience. Account contact-list cardinality, ingress rates and aggregate case storage still need measured operational budgets. No chosen lifetime count limit deletes old blocks to make room for new ones; a funded service must qualify growth or refuse additional intake honestly.

## CP04 — Case lifetime and review

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Medium.** Next ordinary case update within **24 real hours while public intake is open**, with published staffed coverage; no promise of continuous emergency response. Late updates pause new intake. Intake closure never suspends acknowledged-case update dates or appeal deadlines. Cases received while intake is closed show their actual next staffed review date or explicitly unavailable scheduling; no emergency response is implied. Escalate an unresolved case at **30 real days**. At **90 real days open**, decide on available evidence or use a separately authorized exceptional retention process with a specific reason and next review date. No automatic indefinite hold.

Appeals are ordinarily available for **30 real days after decision notice**. Selected evidence is retained for **90 real days after final closure**, then removed under the adopted case-data policy. Closure is the decision notice date, replaced by the final timely appeal decision notice if an appeal occurs; an unappealed case does not add the appeal window to this period. Serious later identity/authority corrections remain possible without guaranteeing deleted evidence survives. A minimal restriction record lasts as long as needed to enforce that restriction, separately from private excerpts; permanent service exclusion does not justify retaining the whole conversation permanently.

**Reason and boundary:** Give people a practical review path and prevent unresolved cases becoming unlimited private archives. New evidence appended to the same incident does not silently restart its open-case deadline. Any newly opened distinct case needs its own basis, not a retention workaround. These are proposals, not legal retention conclusions or global account/history policy.

**Qualification:** PD01/PD08/PD10 and the privacy owner must approve the actual region/audience, staffing, evidence basis, retention, correction/erasure and backup restoration treatment before public operation. Qualify case outages, overdue review, staff conflicts, derived-data removal and current restrictions after gameplay restore.

## CP05 — Service authority and cost

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** A named service reviewer plus a separate reviewer for conflicts/appeals; creator and host status alone grant no human-private access or service-wide enforcement. Existing access management is broad administrative authority and must not be given to a volunteer while described as a narrow helper role. A future narrower delegation needs its own selected capability.

Ordinary discovery, application, native admission, personal controls and case reads/submissions perform **zero model calls**. Human review decides sanctions. Optional future model assistance needs separate evidence scope, quality/cost qualification and authorization; it cannot grant itself access or determine guilt.

**Reason and boundary:** Keep required safety and participation usable during provider failure, avoid manufactured model-dependent service cost, and separate operational authority from fiction. Urgent containment is an explicitly new scoped behavior beyond current grant revocation; unfinished interfering/offensive work must stop at its committed boundary while spent effects persist. If the enabled surface cannot be contained, the actual maintenance authority closes that activity/world.

**Qualification:** All enabled command/contact routes, restore/reconnect, complaint about an operator, wrong-account appeal, and full staff/storage/compute cost. No monthly spend ceiling, per-review time budget or aggregate public workload has been measured here; select them under PD01/PS06 before opening, without trimming mandatory cognition context or quietly dropping acknowledged cases.
