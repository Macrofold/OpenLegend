# Hearing, captions and perceived history limits

Current source inspection of the main/hearing integration, 2026-09-27. Values are authored choices or engineering bounds, not measured optimal settings. [HE01–HE05](../maintainers/hearing-and-speech.md) owns implementation/acceptance; [current evidence](../verification/hearing-main-integration.md) separates observations from remaining qualification. Shared [native-work](native-work.md), [memory](memory.md), [cognition](cognition.md) and [persistence](persistence.md) inventories retain their ownership.

## SSP-H01 — Received direct addressing

**Proposed — accepted SPK target; runtime not delivered. Restrictiveness: Medium — deliberate world interaction simplification.** A hearing detector using the selected linguistic social-perception policy knows whether detected speech was ambient or directed, without an additional sight or intelligible-word gate. The actual single addressee unambiguously knows it was addressed. A bystander can retain directed-to-someone-else while the intended recipient's identity remains unknown; an absent perceived target is not evidence of ambient speech. Undetected speech gives no received message. The cue supplies no missing words, hidden coordinates, name, delivery classification or extra reach. Sound-only animal evidence remains separate under SSP-H02. This trades eye-contact ambiguity for a reliable explicit interaction while preserving acoustic privacy; physical voice/gesture inference is not a prerequisite. Current visual/perceived-intent restrictions are replaced only through the coordinated [SPK implementation](../maintainers/speech-and-social-perception.md). [Canonical contract](../hearing-and-speech.md#received-direct-addressing--accepted-target) and [technical design](../projects/speech-and-social-perception-tech-design.md).

## SSP-H02 — Sound reactions without autobiographical memory

**Proposed — accepted SPK target; runtime not delivered. Restrictiveness: — (proposed removal) — removes the memory-based eligibility restriction.** Acoustic eligibility and sensory acquisition require hearing/life/perception capability, not autobiographical memory, speaking or cognition. The first production emitter is ordinary speech, with one occurrence shared by linguistic and sound-only observers. Another admitted native emission uses that same contract; a contrasting fixture proves it without silently adding footsteps, impact noise or ambient emitters. Ordinary animals obtain intensity, bearing and genuinely associated visible sources, not quoted words, human names, hidden coordinates, delivery inferred from loudness or an intended addressee. Semantic choices use independently configured cognition through the existing director/decision system; no second animal model loop is introduced, and native sound detection does not purchase inference. Skittishness is descriptive context, never a universal speech-to-flight rule. The acoustic audience remains complete under HR02, with conservative candidates and live eligibility. Sleep/waking, bodily injury and semantic work remain separately admitted; no sound damage or waking is selected. [Animal behavior contract](../hearing-and-speech.md#selected-animal-hearing-and-choices--accepted-target), [technical design](../projects/speech-and-social-perception-tech-design.md#10-animal-sound-and-cognition-integration) and [SPK07–SPK08](../maintainers/speech-and-social-perception.md) own delivery and natural-choice qualification.

## SSP-H03 — Short sensory retention and complete detection

**Proposed — selected initial base tuning, not measured optimal. Restrictiveness: Safe for individual retention; Too liberal for independently uncapped audience/event-count growth.** Sensory-only sound evidence expires **900 simulation seconds** after acquisition. Save `expiresAt = acquisitionAt + sensoryLifetimeSeconds` in the existing actor/event awareness record; it is unavailable at `simTime >= expiresAt`. Repeated later sound has its own record and never extends this individual occurrence's expiry. Do not create a transcript store or add these records to human autobiography, long-term embeddings or consolidation. Memory-bearing linguistic experience retains its existing separate policy. A currently admitted job owns its immutable prepared input, and an accepted action owns its cause/stopping facts; expiry cannot rewrite that input or cancel safe continuation by deleting a reference. New disclosure and action admission still respect current authority, relevance, privacy and physical life.

There is **no new per-animal acquired-utterance cap and no independent audible-listener cap**. Retained work grows with admitted emission rate × eligible audience × sensory lifetime; scene associations also grow with distinct perceived sources within their lifetime. A time bound or eight-source prompt selection does not bound that population, acquisition fan-out or preselection work. Reuse conservative receiver selection and the existing recording/storage budget owner. If required recording cannot commit, refuse/hold the complete operation before emission rather than silently dropping an animal or overwriting an unhandled occurrence. Existing cognition context bounds may explicitly defer incomplete reasoning, not retroactively erase acquired evidence. Qualify a loud one-to-many burst and concentrated multiple sources through the actual record/publish/storage path; no new scale acceptance is claimed. Revisit the duration after observed lost useful continuity or excessive retained sensory cost. ## SSP-H04 — Acoustic scene coalescing

**Proposed — selected initial base tuning. Restrictiveness: Safe — suppresses equivalent sound-triggered choice opportunities, not detected occurrences.** Use a **6 dB clarity-margin band**: `floor((clarityMarginDb - acousticPolicy.thresholdsDb.detect) / profile.acousticChangeBandDb)` for finite detected contact. The native margin already subtracts the greater of background and hearing floor from received level. Base bands are `[0,6)`, `[6,12)` and so on; an exact upper boundary enters the next band. The installed width must be finite and positive. There is no added rounding, epsilon or hysteresis.

Extract the existing [HR05](#hr05--localization-and-association) native direction helper for both linguistic speech and sound. From the event-time source-minus-listener vector, horizontal direction is available exactly when `hypot(dx,dz) >= max(0.25,abs(dy))`; otherwise it is null. When available, `sector = (round(atan2(dz,dx)/(pi/4)) + 8) % 8` uses the existing JavaScript rounding rule and world axes (0:+x, 2:+z, 4:−x, 6:−z). Elevation is above at `dy > 2`, below at `dy < -2`, otherwise level. These existing **eight 45° sectors**, **0.25 m** near-coincident threshold and **±2 m** elevation boundaries are shared restrictions, not new independently editable animal senses. Null direction supplies no movement heading.

The normalized scene is a map of canonical tuples `(permitted source observation episode or anonymous, direction or unlocalized, band)`. All still-active tuples coexist. Use the shared structured-key encoding; anonymous entries never include hidden emitter IDs. Each entry retains first/latest acquisition time, repetition count, latest permitted evidence reference and absolute idle expiry. Process detections in ordinary event order: prune entries with `expiresAt <= acquisitionAt` without waking; add an absent tuple and increment the actor's opportunity revision; or refresh an existing tuple's count/latest time and `expiresAt = acquisitionAt + 900` without incrementing it. SSP-H03's individual source expiries remain fixed. An identical pattern newly detected at its exact idle-expiry boundary is new; expiry alone creates no thought.

This set comparison prevents alternating A/B/A/B speakers or anonymous intensity bands from replacing a singleton scene and purchasing work every turn. A previously encountered pattern within its still-active idle window is deliberately coalesced even after another pattern was heard. Only the normalized opportunity revision feeds the animal's existing single ActorWork ticket; raw sensory insertion must not also invoke the human new-memory/new-speech path. Existing bodily/goal/action-result triggers retain separate ownership; response heading, urgency, remaining time and count refreshes do not manufacture sound opportunities or renew chosen flight.

Scene baseline, considered watermark and response binding share the coherent save cut. Restoration never resets considered identity or replays onset. There is **no new actor cooldown, per-utterance paid job or assumed habituation/learning system**. The common initial selection of eight recent trigger sources and existing prompt/provider budgets remain preparation bounds, not a source-erasure rule; account for other still-retained records with explicit coverage, and defer when required evidence cannot fit. Preserve already-chosen useful work and the existing no-automatic-paid-retry policy. Measure raw detections, active tuples, changed-scene revisions, coalesced tickets, visited/prepared records and actual admitted jobs separately; revisit the width/window after observed chattiness or missed useful change. [Contract](../hearing-and-speech.md#selected-animal-hearing-and-choices--accepted-target), [technical algorithm](../projects/speech-and-social-perception-tech-design.md#10-animal-sound-and-cognition-integration), [cognition limits](cognition.md) and [SPK07–SPK08](../maintainers/speech-and-social-perception.md).

## SSP-H05 — Admitted animal choice bounds

**Proposed — selected first response repertoire; runtime not delivered. Restrictiveness: Very safe — finite supported actions with native continuation.** The initial choices are continue, pause to attend, approach a permitted point, retreat, or choose local escape. The newly installed embodied-response action admits hold/escape through the ordinary single active-action/plan/cancel/result owners; approach/retreat use current movement admission. This is required SPK08 work, not a claim that exposing existing candidates alone completes it. A pause defaults to **120 simulation seconds** and the selected profile admits/request-binds only **1–300 simulation seconds**. Its expiry releases the hold to ordinary native routine; it grants no acuity/stealth bonus and needs no paid call to stop. A materially changed sound/body situation can invite a different choice earlier.

Local movement/escape reuses the authored native search extent, directions, clearance and movement/calming defaults under [BW12](base-world.md#bw12--remains-and-native-escape); do not duplicate those as independently editable hearing values. Sound-chosen escape starts with urgency 1, decays as `exp(-elapsed / calmSeconds)` and stops at the absolute admitted time `startedAt + calmSeconds * log(1 / settledDanger)`. Existing base values 40 and 0.04 yield approximately **128.755 simulation seconds**; the exact expression is authoritative, with ordinary TIME_EPSILON boundary handling. This applies to either heard-bearing or observed-position sound stimulus. Actual sight may update steering without renewing lifetime. Native attack protection retains its separate safety-distance/decay policy and priority.

The new `embodied-response` command and `Action.embodiedResponse` payload use the ordinary offered-action binding, actor physical life, existing plan/action generation fences, exact response profile and retained permitted cause. Hold/escape uses one active-action owner, with absolute stop/review deadlines integrated once through the existing kernel/temporal boundaries. Review reuses one clearance-sample travel time; no new reaction-frequency constant is introduced. A heard bearing is not a hidden-source destination. Blocked/unlocalized paths yield the actual refusal/stationary result rather than invented travel. The first slice omits head/ear animation, crouching/stealth effects, scent, pack behavior and long-range refuge search. These bounds provide observable finite responses without building a complete animal ecology. Revisit pause duration/repertoire after actual gameplay and live-choice evidence. [Canonical behavior](../hearing-and-speech.md#selected-animal-hearing-and-choices--accepted-target), [technical schema and admission](../projects/speech-and-social-perception-tech-design.md#10-animal-sound-and-cognition-integration) and [SPK08](../maintainers/speech-and-social-perception.md).

## SSP-H06 — Animal profiles and native continuation

**Proposed — selected authored profile and privacy restriction. Restrictiveness: Medium — explicit first-profile capability scope.** Bundled ordinary animal profiles enable cognition while autobiographical memory, inner-world writing, human-language understanding and speech remain disabled. Each species explicitly binds the existing initial hearing floor **0 dB SPL** and applicable sight baseline **28 m** through selected definitions. Equal initial values preserve existing tuning, not biological equivalence; independently configurable profiles and a contrasting hearing-floor fixture must prove that production consumers read the definition. Actor dispositions describe tendencies rather than install conditional behavior instructions. A cognition-disabled hearing profile remains supported, and existing territorial/native protection must not acquire a competing movement owner.

Native hearing still runs without AI. Optional choice uses shared admission/budgets/uncertainty, with no automatic retry and no canned speech-induced flight when unavailable. Known AI unavailability separately disables player freeform Speak/Speak-to/Act through its shared product rule; it does not disable installed native effects, attack escape or admitted movement. Last-seen escape updates require actual permitted sight of the same supported encounter/physical life. Heard-bearing escape uses its own permitted displacement/direction, never hidden source position/distance. Either sound-chosen stimulus reaches SSP-H05's finite native stop even when trapped; only a newly accepted choice renews it, and equivalent subsequent speech cannot reset flight. Save accepted response/deadlines and source evidence together; source removal permits safe already-admitted movement away, not continued tracking. Sleep/death/perception gates forbid fresh conscious acquisition, and sound never wakes a sleeper in this scope. [Contract](../hearing-and-speech.md#selected-animal-hearing-and-choices--accepted-target), [base-world limits](base-world.md), [save/load](../save-and-load.md) and [SPK08](../maintainers/speech-and-social-perception.md).

## HR01 — Acoustic model and tuning

**Current; deliberate v1 restriction.** Air SPL uses a 20 µPa reference, direct 3D paths, source ear height, a 0.25 m near-field clamp and authored uniform background. Base presets are whisper/normal/shout at 40/60/75 dB SPL at 1 m, background 28, hearing floor 0 and detection/partial/clear margins 0/6/12 dB. Admission bounds authored levels/floors to −120…200 and requires strictly ordered thresholds. Transmission is an energy ratio 0…1; zero blocks the direct path. See [acoustic contract](../hearing-and-speech.md#2-continuous-quantities-three-thresholds) and `acoustics.ts`. These finite families avoid pretending to simulate diffraction, rooms, frequencies, language or sustained sound. Broader propagation requires a supported provider, not arbitrary scripts.

## HR02 — Complete audience and partial evidence

**No independent audience cap; deliberate completeness.** Spatial receiver bounds reject only provably irrelevant listeners. A shout can reach every eligible listener on a small map. Per-utterance work and stored perspectives grow with actual audience and history; ordinary work/storage admission still applies. Exceeding a work budget rejects/pauses required work rather than granting inaudibility. The old 10 m hard radius and binary 0.65 obstruction gate are **Removed**. Partial speech exposes approximately half the lexical units, with a stable one-word reveal/gap decision and coalesced spans; native speech remains limited to 1,500 characters, model talk to 1,200 and player Talk input to 1,000. No hidden offsets/word counts/raw text enter ordinary projections. Intl segmentation portability beyond the pinned runtime is unqualified.

## HR03 — Geometry reuse

**Current; bounded optimization, not sensory restriction.** Acoustic cache admission retains up to 256 listeners and 256 source paths per admitted listener, per immutable spatial map. Sight retains its existing shared cache bounds. Overflow queries compute exact results instead of thrashing retained entries or truncating listeners. Entries invalidate on relevant pose/body/map inputs; eligibility is always live. Population churn can reduce reuse until map collection/replacement, tracked in HE. Receiver indexes have no separate count cap: their size follows admitted memory-bearing participants and installed volume families. Native supply indexes similarly follow existing root entities, with live quantity/visibility checks and roster invalidation.

**Accepted SPK integration:** SSP-H02 separates the native acoustic candidate roster from memory-bearing participants. Reuse the existing exact fallback/cache owner; do not preserve the memory gate as a performance shortcut or claim the wider roster is already delivered.

## HR04 — Caption density and lifetime

**Current; reasonable initial presentation bounds, broader layout/accessibility qualification pending.** Eight active captions, 24 pending and three total per identified speaker bound overlay work. One active caption per identified speaker preserves utterance order; self/direct pending speech has priority. Text chunks contain at most 160 graphemes, using whitespace near the boundary when possible. Reading duration is `clamp(2000 + max(words*300, codePoints*45), 4000, 12000)` ms times a 0.5…3 user multiplier; only permitted text is counted. Reading starts when fitted/visible and pauses with layout hiding, game/presentation pause or document hiding. Measured fixed controls/open panels now count as occupied layout space; neutral fallback is tried before hiding, with no per-frame DOM measurements. Popup/modal obstruction remains HE05. Pending/continuously hidden entries expire after 60 unpaused presentation seconds. Overflow/expiry preserves history. A missed-caption notice counts dropped or never-shown perceived speech from others, reports at most once per second, shows “99+” above 99, stays until dismissed or until World Events is opened, is local to one tab and caption scope, and is a lower bound (speech that left the 60-event live window before reaching the tab is flagged only as “possibly more”). Deduplication trims above 1,024 IDs to the most recent 512. See [caption behavior](../hearing-and-speech.md#7-caption-component-and-lifetime).

## HR05 — Localization and association

**Current; privacy restriction.** Only event-time visual association/self evidence supplies a speaker identity. Unknown voices stay unknown. Bearings use eight 45° horizontal sectors and coarse elevation; primarily vertical/ambiguous sources use neutral placement. The display ring is at most 3 m and 30% of current ordinary clear reach, with 1/0.75/0.5/0.25 fit attempts. Listener movement beyond 0.5 m removes the old arrow. These are presentation bounds, never source coordinates or a hearing radius. Current authorized sprite anchors may follow identified visible speakers; remembered ghosts cannot.

**Accepted SPK integration:** SSP-H01 removes sight as a received-address prerequisite; [SSP-M01](memory.md#ssp-m01--stable-recognition-and-known-names) establishes or reuses stable speaker identity on first ordinary linguistic audible detection, even unseen and without understood words or previous familiarity. This deliberately supported association grants no name, location, delivery label or hidden body facts. Nonlinguistic animals and other supported world policies retain their own source-association restrictions. Historical bearings, current-pose permission and event-time labels remain intact. The current visual association evidence above remains accurate until the coordinated target is implemented.

## HR06 — History paging and growth

**Current page bounds; no retained-history or accumulated-DOM cap.** `/api/world-events` returns 50 rows by default, maximum 100, with one extra row for continuation. `POST /api/world-events/sources` accepts one or two structured observer-bound `WorldEventSourceReference` records: `family` is `speech` or `encounter`, the HTTP `sourceId` uses the shared record-ID syntax and 120-character bound, and `revision` and `token` are each at most 200 characters. The internal repository separately admits source IDs up to 160 characters; that does not enlarge the public request. Oversized or invalid references are refused. One batched scoped lookup selects the requested event identities before hydrating their detail; the recorded warm PostgreSQL plan used `history_perspective_type_order` and `history_events_pkey` index scans (`2.27 ms` cold, `4.74 ms` warm at 2,525 retained actor perspectives; [MY-L02/MY-L04](parallel-batch-08-the-missing-belonging.md#my-l02--two-sources-compared-at-once)). Cursors are at most 2,048 characters and bind actor/account/world/authority epoch/filter/search/watermark; exact type filters are 1…64 characters. An optional `q` search (at most 200 characters, 8 words of at most 64 characters, AND word prefixes) matches only the viewer's perspective text, never raw event payloads or unheard words; one search request examines at most 2,000 filtered rows, read 250 at a time and matched in JavaScript independently of the database locale, and reports `scanLimited` with a cursor into older history when that window has no more matches ([MH08](memory.md#mh08)). Earlier Batch 08 search measurements used 500-row windows (`64.02 ms` first window + `19.40 ms` continuation at 2,525 actor perspectives), so those timings do not qualify the current 2,000-row allowance. The matches and the event bodies loaded for them come from one read snapshot. A search cursor carries a fixed-length digest of the search words, so its size does not grow with the search (about 460 characters measured with a long authority scope). The search text travels in the request URL like the other filters. SQL filters before keyset pagination on existing actor/order and actor/type/order indexes (`history_perspective_type_order`). Raw world events never substitute for absent speech evidence. The current menu lists selected existing types; All includes every permitted external/sensory type, including private encounter/contact/body sensations. Loading older pages grows panel rows without virtualization; ordinary play grows durable events/perspectives without a hearing-specific storage quota. History reads are scoped and paged, but general changed-event evidence reconciliation can traverse retained actor history. HE05 retains multi-hour long-session qualification beyond the 2,574-event Batch 08 measurement.

## HR07 — Recall and embedding admission

**Current; shared bounds owned by memory/cognition.** Main's durable eligible-memory pipeline remains the only background indexer. Verbatim speech and automatic dialogue require heard/self linguistic content; no-word cues remain ordinary evidence. Batches share adapter limits of 33 texts, 8,000 UTF-8 bytes per text and 64,000 encoded JSON bytes; recall packs at most 32 sources and reserves a query prefix when needed. Oversized optional records remain available through structured recall rather than being truncated. Durable attempt/source/generation checks, spending caps and dispatch/publication fences apply; no hearing-specific paid retry is added. These batch limits do not bound all retained sources or corpus-search work. See [memory limits](memory.md).

## VO-L02 — Playback queue and freshness

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** One active utterance and one pending utterance per listening client; a pending utterance may begin only within ten real seconds of its commitment. Stop, skip, overflow or expiry removes optional playback, never heard evidence; history remains available. Do not displace the active utterance merely to chase every new line. No automatic replay after hidden-tab return, reconnect or restore. This bounds stale conversation and optional synthesis admission, not total heard history or source population. Explicit historical reading is a separate deliberate request; current access/spending still apply. Revisit after an observed missed-useful-line or overlap problem, not to maximize audio minutes.

## VO-L03 — Microphone draft capture

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** One explicitly active capture/draft operation per controlling person; automatic capture stops at thirty real seconds, retaining the resulting editable draft. Input/submit limits remain the selected channel's existing limits; there is no auto-split or auto-send. Thirty seconds is a convenience/work envelope, not a bound on encoded request bytes. Complete encoded upload, response, concurrency and provider reservation bounds are still unselected technical gates before offering capture. At any admission boundary preserve editable text and let the player type; silence or failed transcription creates no speech. [HE07.2](../maintainers/hearing-and-speech.md#he07--optional-speech-media-and-selected-hearing-extensions) owns qualification and reconsideration if natural short dictation is repeatedly cut.

## VO-L04 — Media retention and paid reuse

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe for stored media.** No permanent recording, voice cloning, public reusable audio link or background-world synthesis in the first scope. Raw capture is transient for the expressly requested draft, removed after private dictation review completes, cancellation or abandonment and always within ten real minutes. Synthesized audio expires within ten real minutes or its originating authorized session ending, whichever comes first; this does not shorten permitted text history. Exact permitted renderings may reuse work only while authority, content, identity and licensed voice meaning match; distinct partial projections are not one unredacted asset. Concrete total cache bytes, connection deadlines, vendor retention terms and retry/uncertain-cost accounting remain required before media launch; the ten-minute game-held retention ceiling is proposed, not a vendor deletion guarantee. Existing text retention and authorized erasure are not narrowed by deleting transient media. Stop admitting optional work before the funded ceiling; queued or failed work does not become free.

## VO-L05 — Timed speech and raw voice remain separate capabilities

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** Current instantaneous speech remains usable. First selected timed consumer is a small doorway exchange with reviewed words and synthetic rendering, under PS04/HE/NC. Raw human audio, unreviewed authoritative transcripts, voice-familiarity learning, language translation, loud-sound waking and indirect-room propagation are not silently enabled by playback. Raw voice needs a world contract acknowledging actual voice-identity disclosure and an enforceable per-listener media policy. The restriction trades spontaneous vocal expression for truthful current evidence; the product specifies the later journey and its adoption/technical qualifications without claiming it has shipped.

## VO-L06 — Aggregate growth is not solved by short playback

**Proposed assessment · Restrictiveness: Too liberal until measured and admitted.** Actual utterances, complete audible audiences and retained text grow with play; potential partial audio variants can grow with listeners, while connected silent media sessions and delivery fan-out add independent costs. A two-item client queue does not bound these dimensions. Existing HE/NC/PS/spending owners must qualify total prepared work, connections, request bytes, transient media bytes, egress and retained histories. Optional media is withheld before admission at its limit; required text/evidence is not silently truncated. No universal population, lifetime-text or model-cost number is selected by this proposal.
