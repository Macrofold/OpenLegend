# Hearing, captions and perceived history limits

Current source inspection of the main/hearing integration, 2026-09-27. Values are authored choices or engineering bounds, not measured optimal settings. [HE01–HE05](../maintainers/hearing-and-speech.md) owns implementation/acceptance; [current evidence](../verification/hearing-main-integration.md) separates observations from remaining qualification. Shared [native-work](native-work.md), [memory](memory.md), [cognition](cognition.md) and [persistence](persistence.md) inventories retain their ownership.

## SSP-H01 — Received direct addressing

**Proposed — accepted SPK target; runtime not delivered. Restrictiveness: Medium — deliberate world interaction simplification.** A hearing detector using the selected linguistic social-perception policy knows whether detected speech was ambient or directed, without an additional sight or intelligible-word gate. The actual single addressee unambiguously knows it was addressed. A bystander can retain directed-to-someone-else while the intended recipient's identity remains unknown; an absent perceived target is not evidence of ambient speech. Undetected speech gives no received message. The cue supplies no missing words, hidden coordinates, name, delivery classification or extra reach. Sound-only animal evidence remains separate under SSP-H02. This trades eye-contact ambiguity for a reliable explicit interaction while preserving acoustic privacy; physical voice/gesture inference is not a prerequisite. Current visual/perceived-intent restrictions are replaced only through the coordinated [SPK implementation](../maintainers/speech-and-social-perception.md). [Canonical contract](../hearing-and-speech.md#received-direct-addressing--accepted-target) and [technical design](../projects/speech-and-social-perception-tech-design.md).

## SSP-H02 — Sound reactions without autobiographical memory

**Proposed — accepted SPK target; runtime not delivered. Restrictiveness: — (proposed removal) — removes memory-based acoustic eligibility.** Hearing/life/perception capability determines acoustic acquisition independently of autobiographical memory, language, speech or cognition. Speech is the first production emitter, with one occurrence and one permitted observer/event record. A sound-only animal record may originate from speech without containing a linguistic payload; validation/projection must support this directly. A contrasting native non-speech fixture proves the seam without installing every other emitter.

Ordinary animals use the [native acoustic policy](../worlds/base/animal-sound-responses.md) and require zero model calls. They receive intensity/bearing and legitimate visible-source facts, not words, names, addressee, hidden source location/identity or inferred distance. Installed reaction thresholds can produce continuation or shared alarm without a universal voice-to-fear law. Existing life/sleep/status boundaries remain, with no sound waking/damage. [SPK07–SPK08](../maintainers/speech-and-social-perception.md) and the [technical integration](../projects/speech-and-social-perception-tech-design.md#10-animal-sound-and-cognition-integration) own delivery. This explicitly supersedes the former SPK ordinary-animal cognition selection.

## SSP-H03 — Short sensory retention and complete detection

**Proposed — selected retention restriction; runtime not delivered. Restrictiveness: Medium — bounded lifetime without autobiography.** The canonical [world profile](../worlds/base/animal-sound-responses.md#profile-values-and-consumers) initially retains each sensory record for **900 simulation seconds** after acquisition. Save absolute expiry; repeats never extend an old record. Memory-only recall, embeddings, consolidation, inner-world maintenance and autobiographical editing exclude these rows. The native response owner retains an active episode's minimal admitted cue separately from optional sensory availability.

There is **no new per-animal acquired-utterance cap or audible-listener cap**. Work grows with admitted emission rate × eligible audience × sensory lifetime, including cold reconstruction and record publication. A duration does not bound audience density or per-transition work. Reuse conservative candidates and the existing complete native operation/recording budget. Incomplete recording refuses/holds before partial emission, never silently drops observers. Qualify concentrated multiple sources and dense memoryless listeners through the actual record/publish/storage/native-intake path. No animal prompt-size allowance or provider budget substitutes for that measurement. Revisit lifetime on demonstrated lost useful continuity or retained sensory cost. [Hearing contract](../hearing-and-speech.md#selected-animal-hearing-and-choices--accepted-target), [native-work limits](native-work.md) and [SPK07](../maintainers/speech-and-social-perception.md).

## SSP-H04 — Acoustic scene coalescing

**Proposed — selected native repeat suppression; runtime not delivered. Restrictiveness: Medium — deliberate finite pattern familiarity.** The [world-owned algorithm](../worlds/base/animal-sound-responses.md#repeated-sound-without-repeated-behavior-work) uses the initial **6 dB** intensity bands and **900 simulation-second** pattern idle window. They are independent of individual evidence expiry. Self, subthreshold and null-response-profile detections remain recorded but do not retain response patterns. Only a new qualifying canonical source-or-anonymous/direction/band pattern updates alarm. An active repeat refreshes that pattern's idle expiry without resetting safe time, replacing movement, allocating new navigation or consuming RNG. A/B/A/B adds two patterns then refreshes them. At exact idle expiry a new occurrence can qualify; expiry by itself cannot.

Reuse [HR05](#hr05--localization-and-association)'s eight world-axis sectors, localization/elevation restrictions and one native direction helper. Null remains unknown; anonymous patterns never use hidden emitter IDs. A genuinely new louder band, direction or source can matter. A newly encountered lower band that still qualifies can also trigger review. This can both miss a useful repeated cue and admit an extra review after attenuation changes; it is a transparent first policy, not learned habituation.

The map belongs to the existing native response owner and saves only each pattern's idle expiry/latest permitted cue. No actor cooldown, paid opportunity/considered revision, human cognition wake, transcript copy or per-event model job is selected. All genuinely perceived bodily dangers and distinct actual attack evidence retain their native effect. Measure detections, active patterns, qualifying novel cues, route reviews, preparation/reuse, RNG and retained state through the complete caller; no population capacity is established. [SPK07–SPK08](../maintainers/speech-and-social-perception.md) own qualification.

## SSP-H05 — Admitted animal choice bounds

**Proposed — revised initial repertoire; runtime not delivered. Restrictiveness: Medium — continuation or the existing native alarm.** Initial acoustic outcomes are continue current behavior or enter/update the one shared native alarm, which supplies actual escape, alert settling and truthful blocked behavior. The [native dependency](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/ff68a9b4035bff08025af7b0b579fe2316ed1263/docs/worlds/base/animal-responses.md) owns all-danger safety, candidate/route bounds, speed, continuous-safe duration, blocked retries and flight/territorial continuation. Speech adds no independently editable escape timer or movement path.

The [acoustic world policy](../worlds/base/animal-sound-responses.md#one-alarm-and-actual-movement) supplies a heard-bearing preference after all real-danger safety comparisons. An unseen source is never a fabricated danger point. Null localization permits ordinary heading/candidate ordering without an invented source direction; incomplete navigation remains explicit. An unsafe arrival requires another shared escape leg; a trapped animal cannot be declared calm by a fixed speech timer.

**Superseded proposal:** the independent `embodied-response` command/`Action.embodiedResponse`, **120-second** hold default, **1–300-second** hold range and approximately **128.755-second** forced sound-flight stop are removed from the selected design. None is delivered behavior. Distinct curiosity approaches/listening holds and richer repertoire are deferred because the later native-animal decision selects a smaller efficient controller. Shared settling already supplies the stationary aftermath. Revisit repertoire for a concrete desired animal interaction, without restoring ordinary paid animal cognition. [SPK08](../maintainers/speech-and-social-perception.md) owns the replacement integration.

## SSP-H06 — Animal profiles and native continuation

**Proposed — revised selected authored policy; runtime not delivered. Restrictiveness: Medium — native ordinary animals with explicit acoustic tuning.** Ordinary animals retain native control with cognition, autobiography, inner-world writing, language and speech disabled. The single installed response profile's nullable nonnegative `soundAlarmMinimumBand` supplies reaction sensitivity; it grants no hearing. Initial bindings and the preserved **0 dB SPL** hearing floor/applicable **28 m** sight baseline are canonical in the [world profile](../worlds/base/animal-sound-responses.md#profile-values-and-consumers). Reaction bands are initially **0** for ordinary hare/deer/bird, **2** for wolf/bear, and **null** for the territorial stag and the explicit nonreactive contrast. These are provisional game values, not biological equivalence. Qualify different hearing and response bindings through actual spawn, perception and native behavior.

[CTR03–CTR04](https://github.com/Macrofold/OpenLegend/blob/ff68a9b4035bff08025af7b0b579fe2316ed1263/docs/maintainers/combat-and-threat-responses.md#ctr03--one-native-animal-response-owner) is a pinned unmerged native-controller dependency; SPK08 remains incomplete until the relevant seam is implemented and qualified. This does not authorize unrelated combat integration. No second response profile selection, movement owner, provider loop or cognitive capability fallback is allowed.

Native acquisition/intake and accepted movement work through provider outage; the separate application rule still disables new player freeform Speak/Speak-to/Act. Busy, perched and airborne bodies retain actual sensing, while life/perception/status gates and physical interruption remain authoritative. No sound waking is added. Persist exact sensory expiries, pattern state, profile/body life, minimal active cue and the existing alarm/refuge/proposal/movement relationships. Restore preserves safe time and RNG without replaying intake. Historical source loss gives no hidden tracking. Validate these current relationships through the existing owner. [Technical integration](../projects/speech-and-social-perception-tech-design.md#10-animal-sound-and-cognition-integration), [save/load](../save-and-load.md) and [SPK08](../maintainers/speech-and-social-perception.md).

## HR01 — Acoustic model and tuning

**Current; deliberate v1 restriction.** Air SPL uses a 20 µPa reference, direct 3D paths, source ear height, a 0.25 m near-field clamp and authored uniform background. Base presets are whisper/normal/shout at 40/60/75 dB SPL at 1 m, background 28, hearing floor 0 and detection/partial/clear margins 0/6/12 dB. Admission bounds authored levels/floors to −120…200 and requires strictly ordered thresholds. Transmission is an energy ratio 0…1; zero blocks the direct path. See [acoustic contract](../hearing-and-speech.md#2-continuous-quantities-three-thresholds) and `acoustics.ts`. These finite families avoid pretending to simulate diffraction, rooms, frequencies, language or sustained sound. Broader propagation requires a supported provider, not arbitrary scripts.

## HR02 — Complete audience and partial evidence

**No independent audience cap; deliberate completeness.** Spatial receiver bounds reject only provably irrelevant listeners. A shout can reach every eligible listener on a small map. Per-utterance work and stored perspectives grow with actual audience and history; ordinary work/storage admission still applies. Exceeding a work budget rejects/pauses required work rather than granting inaudibility. The old 10 m hard radius and binary 0.65 obstruction gate are **Removed**. Partial speech exposes approximately half the lexical units, with a stable one-word reveal/gap decision and coalesced spans; native speech remains limited to 1,500 characters, model talk to 1,200 and player Talk input to 1,000. No hidden offsets/word counts/raw text enter ordinary projections. Intl segmentation portability beyond the pinned runtime is unqualified.

## HR03 — Geometry reuse

**Current; bounded optimization, not sensory restriction.** Acoustic cache admission retains up to 256 listeners and 256 source paths per admitted listener, per immutable spatial map. Sight retains its existing shared cache bounds. Overflow queries compute exact results instead of thrashing retained entries or truncating listeners. Entries invalidate on relevant pose/body/map inputs; eligibility is always live. Population churn can reduce reuse until map collection/replacement, tracked in HE. Receiver indexes have no separate count cap: their size follows admitted memory-bearing participants and installed volume families. Native supply indexes similarly follow existing root entities, with live quantity/visibility checks and roster invalidation.

**Accepted SPK integration:** SSP-H02 separates the native acoustic candidate roster from memory-bearing participants. Reuse the existing exact fallback/cache owner; do not preserve the memory gate as a performance shortcut or claim the wider roster is already delivered.

## HR04 — Caption density and lifetime

**Current; reasonable initial presentation bounds, broader layout/accessibility qualification pending.** Eight active captions, 24 pending and three total per identified speaker bound overlay work. One active caption per identified speaker preserves utterance order; self/direct pending speech has priority. Text chunks contain at most 160 graphemes, using whitespace near the boundary when possible. Reading duration is `clamp(2000 + max(words*300, codePoints*45), 4000, 12000)` ms times a 0.5…3 user multiplier; only permitted text is counted. Reading starts when fitted/visible and pauses with layout hiding, game/presentation pause or document hiding. Measured fixed controls/open panels now count as occupied layout space; neutral fallback is tried before hiding, with no per-frame DOM measurements. Popup/modal obstruction remains HE05. Pending/continuously hidden entries expire after 60 unpaused presentation seconds. Overflow/expiry preserves history. A missed-caption notice counts dropped or never-shown perceived speech from others, reports at most once per second, shows “99+” above 99, stays until dismissed or until World Events is opened, is local to one tab and caption scope, and is a lower bound (speech that left the 60-event live window before reaching the tab is flagged only as “possibly more”). Deduplication trims above 1,024 IDs to the most recent 512. See [caption behavior](../hearing-and-speech.md#7-caption-component-and-lifetime).

## HR05 — Localization and association

**Current; privacy restriction.** Only event-time visual association/self evidence supplies a speaker identity. Unknown voices stay unknown. Bearings use eight 45° horizontal sectors and coarse elevation; primarily vertical/ambiguous sources use neutral placement. The display ring is camera-centered, at most 280 CSS pixels and 35% of viewport width/height, further reduced for caption bounds. This replaces the old listener-centered 3 m ring. A permitted current speaker position supplies camera-relative direction; an unlocalized voice retains only its labelled approximate event-time bearing pending the localization decision. Listener movement beyond 0.5 m removes the old arrow. These are presentation bounds, never source coordinates or a hearing radius. Current authorized sprite anchors may follow identified visible speakers; remembered ghosts cannot.

**Accepted SPK integration:** SSP-H01 removes sight as a received-address prerequisite; [SSP-M01](memory.md#ssp-m01--stable-recognition-and-known-names) establishes or reuses stable speaker identity on first ordinary linguistic audible detection, even unseen and without understood words or previous familiarity. This deliberately supported association grants no name, location, delivery label or hidden body facts. Nonlinguistic animals and other supported world policies retain their own source-association restrictions. Historical bearings, current-pose permission and event-time labels remain intact. The current visual association evidence above remains accurate until the coordinated target is implemented.

## HR06 — History paging and growth

**Current page bounds; no retained-history or accumulated-DOM cap.** `/api/world-events` returns 50 rows by default, maximum 100, with one extra row for continuation. `POST /api/world-events/sources` accepts one or two structured observer-bound `WorldEventSourceReference` records: `family` is `speech` or `encounter`, the HTTP `sourceId` uses the shared record-ID syntax and 120-character bound, and `revision` and `token` are each at most 200 characters. The internal repository separately admits source IDs up to 160 characters; that does not enlarge the public request. Oversized or invalid references are refused. One batched scoped lookup selects the requested event identities before hydrating their detail; the recorded warm PostgreSQL plan used `history_perspective_type_order` and `history_events_pkey` index scans (`2.27 ms` cold, `4.74 ms` warm at 2,525 retained actor perspectives; [MY-L02/MY-L04](parallel-batch-08-the-missing-belonging.md#my-l02--two-sources-compared-at-once)). Cursors are at most 2,048 characters and bind actor/account/world/authority epoch/filter/search/watermark; exact type filters are 1…64 characters. An optional `q` search (at most 200 characters, 8 words of at most 64 characters, AND word prefixes) matches only the viewer's perspective text, never raw event payloads or unheard words; one search request examines at most 2,000 filtered rows, read 250 at a time and matched in JavaScript independently of the database locale, and reports `scanLimited` with a cursor into older history when that window has no more matches ([MH08](memory.md#mh08)). Earlier Batch 08 search measurements used 500-row windows (`64.02 ms` first window + `19.40 ms` continuation at 2,525 actor perspectives), so those timings do not qualify the current 2,000-row allowance. The matches and the event bodies loaded for them come from one read snapshot. A search cursor carries a fixed-length digest of the search words, so its size does not grow with the search (about 460 characters measured with a long authority scope). The search text travels in the request URL like the other filters. SQL filters before keyset pagination on existing actor/order and actor/type/order indexes (`history_perspective_type_order`). Raw world events never substitute for absent speech evidence. The current menu uses world-mapped semantic categories. All initially excludes authored ambient activity, with a visible inclusion switch; this changes presentation only. A game-time seek uses `history_perspective_time`, then older/newer keyset pages. A 20,000-perspective disposable PostgreSQL check on 2026-10-16 used that index and the event primary key with one row/one loop each, at 0.045 ms execution for the selected seek. This is a query-only fixture measurement, not a gameplay capacity claim. Loading older pages grows panel rows without virtualization; ordinary play grows durable events/perspectives without a hearing-specific storage quota. History reads are scoped and paged, but general changed-event evidence reconciliation can traverse retained actor history. HE05 retains multi-hour long-session qualification beyond the 2,574-event Batch 08 measurement.

## HR07 — Recall and embedding admission

**Current; shared bounds owned by memory/cognition.** Main's durable eligible-memory pipeline remains the only background indexer. Verbatim speech and automatic dialogue require heard/self linguistic content; no-word cues remain ordinary evidence. Batches share adapter limits of 33 texts, 8,000 UTF-8 bytes per text and 64,000 encoded JSON bytes; recall packs at most 32 sources and reserves a query prefix when needed. Oversized optional records remain available through structured recall rather than being truncated. Durable attempt/source/generation checks, spending caps and dispatch/publication fences apply; no hearing-specific paid retry is added. These batch limits do not bound all retained sources or corpus-search work. See [memory limits](memory.md).

## HR08 — Compatibility and editing

**Policy reference and editing restriction.** Development compatibility is governed only by the [root policy](../../AGENTS.md#development-save-policy). Current-format restart/restore must preserve recorded fragments and normal validation; unsupported input never triggers an automatic reset. Generic editors reject committed speech text/content rewrites while preserving deletion and importance updates. Dedicated re-authoring remains [D66](../../archive/05-project/open-decisions.md#d66--re-authoring-committed-speech). The old four-second discovery descriptor and mandatory one-second execution loop are **Removed/superseded** by [elapsed-time policy](../simulation-time.md), not hidden hearing controls.

## VO-L02 — Playback queue and freshness

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** One active utterance and one pending utterance per listening client; a pending utterance may begin only within ten real seconds of its commitment. Stop, skip, overflow or expiry removes optional playback, never heard evidence; history remains available. Do not displace the active utterance merely to chase every new line. No automatic replay after hidden-tab return, reconnect or restore. This bounds stale conversation and optional synthesis admission, not total heard history or source population. Explicit historical reading is a separate deliberate request; current access/spending still apply. Revisit after an observed missed-useful-line or overlap problem, not to maximize audio minutes.

## VO-L03 — Microphone draft capture

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** One explicitly active capture/draft operation per controlling person; automatic capture stops at thirty real seconds, retaining the resulting editable draft. Input/submit limits remain the selected channel's existing limits; there is no auto-split or auto-send. Thirty seconds is a convenience/work envelope, not a bound on encoded request bytes. Complete encoded upload, response, concurrency and provider reservation bounds are still unselected technical gates before offering capture. At any admission boundary preserve editable text and let the player type; silence or failed transcription creates no speech. [HE07.2](../maintainers/hearing-and-speech.md#he07--optional-speech-media-and-selected-hearing-extensions) owns qualification and reconsideration if natural short dictation is repeatedly cut.

## VO-L04 — Media retention and paid reuse

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe for stored media.** No permanent recording, voice cloning, public reusable audio link or background-world synthesis in the first scope. Raw capture is transient for the expressly requested draft, removed after private dictation review completes, cancellation or abandonment and always within ten real minutes. Synthesized audio expires within ten real minutes or its originating authorized session ending, whichever comes first; this does not shorten permitted text history. Exact permitted renderings may reuse work only while authority, content, identity and licensed voice meaning match; distinct partial projections are not one unredacted asset. Concrete total cache bytes, connection deadlines, vendor retention terms and retry/uncertain-cost accounting remain required before media launch; the ten-minute game-held retention ceiling is proposed, not a vendor deletion guarantee. Existing text retention and authorized erasure are not narrowed by deleting transient media. Stop admitting optional work before the funded ceiling; queued or failed work does not become free.

## VO-L05 — Timed speech and raw voice remain separate capabilities

**Proposed · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** Current instantaneous speech remains usable. First selected timed consumer is a small doorway exchange with reviewed words and synthetic rendering, under PS04/HE/NC. Raw human audio, unreviewed authoritative transcripts, voice-familiarity learning, language translation, loud-sound waking and indirect-room propagation are not silently enabled by playback. Raw voice needs a world contract acknowledging actual voice-identity disclosure and an enforceable per-listener media policy. The restriction trades spontaneous vocal expression for truthful current evidence; the product specifies the later journey and its adoption/technical qualifications without claiming it has shipped.

## VO-L06 — Aggregate growth is not solved by short playback

**Proposed assessment · Restrictiveness: Too liberal until measured and admitted.** Actual utterances, complete audible audiences and retained text grow with play; potential partial audio variants can grow with listeners, while connected silent media sessions and delivery fan-out add independent costs. A two-item client queue does not bound these dimensions. Existing HE/NC/PS/spending owners must qualify total prepared work, connections, request bytes, transient media bytes, egress and retained histories. Optional media is withheld before admission at its limit; required text/evidence is not silently truncated. No universal population, lifetime-text or model-cost number is selected by this proposal.
