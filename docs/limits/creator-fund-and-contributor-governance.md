# Creator-fund and contributor-governance limits

**Status: proposed operating policies, not an active fund.** The [feature specification](../projects/creator-fund-and-contributor-governance-feature-spec.md) owns complete behavior and [FUND01–FUND06](../maintainers/creator-fund-and-contributor-governance.md) owns adoption and delivery beneath D37/C08. These policies apply to the selected program; they do not change current world rules, corporate authority, licenses or customer/creator money. Actual budget, payment arrangements and operator capacity must be selected before inviting work.

Each entry records the restriction, its reason, behavior at the boundary and a revisit trigger. All entries are **Proposed**. Restrictiveness rates the program choice, not a legal certification or evidence of economical operation. Correct accounting, current authority, privacy and preserved granted rights are obligations, not tunable shortcuts.

## FUND-L01 — One useful finite pilot

**Restrictiveness: Safe.** The first program is one manual round with at most two awards for a wanted, independently useful improvement or explicitly scoped exploration within the existing creative game. One or zero awards is a valid completion. The round creates no automatic annual program, maintenance job, marketplace prerequisite or standing council. A compulsory company roadmap task uses the appropriate commissioned-work arrangement instead of relying on a grant label. This limits administration and speculative commitments while leaving space for creator-proposed solutions. Revisit only after actual outputs, creator experience, player benefit, maintainability and full operating cost justify a further funded round.

## FUND-L02 — Reserve settled funds and human capacity

**Restrictiveness: Safe.** The selected first funding source is an actually approved platform expense budget, after existing obligations. Before intake, reserve complete possible awards, recipient expenses as agreed, payment/FX and failure costs, steward/reviewer work, required evidence, corrections/disputes and closure. Forecast subscriptions, pledges, token values, gross revenue, prepaid customer balances, unused subscriber allocations and earned creator proceeds are not available grant cash. Keep uncertain payments and signed unpaid awards committed until reconciled. No actual dollar amount or unpaid founder-time allowance is adopted by this inventory. If the whole promise is unaffordable, reduce scope or do not open the round. Revisit through actual settled funds, protected customer service and a sustainable named operator-time budget.

## FUND-L03 — Invited intake with bounded application effort

**Restrictiveness: Medium.** The first round deliberately invites at most 12 applicants/teams and reviews every eligible admitted application during the published process. It is not advertised as an open public call. The proposed brief is at most 600 words and three existing links, one application per lead/team, with a 14-real-day submission window. No paid membership, purchase, fame, generated-volume count or unpaid bespoke demonstration is required. Publish the cohort's purpose and invitation criteria before invitations; suitable unpaid contributors and newcomers can be included. Receipt, eligibility, review and an award remain distinct. Larger open intake, a review lottery or a permanent interest queue needs its own measured capacity and fairness policy before being promised. Revisit application accessibility, honest effort and whether the invited scope excludes the very people whose work the game needs.

## FUND-L04 — Useful milestones and recipient cash flow

**Restrictiveness: Medium.** The candidate award allocates 20% to a nonrepayable start grant earned after complete scope signing, the limited agreed start briefing and payment eligibility; 40% to an independently useful midpoint; and 40% to the final agreed handoff. The recipient's proposed six-week delivery period begins after the initial payment is confirmed available. Good-faith noncompletion does not turn the earned start grant into debt. Reconcile genuinely unused refundable expense advances separately under their agreed purpose and ceiling. Price useful partial results where feasible; preserve original-criteria review and the full potentially earned amount for work completed before operator cancellation. After resolving that work, the proposed operator-cancellation allowance is 10% of the total award, capped by its remaining unearned balance, never an extra award. A milestone cannot depend on later popularity, arbitrary upstream merge, deployment authority or indefinite support unless that exact independently achievable work was the accepted promise. Explicitly funded exploration may earn payment for truthful negative findings. Revisit with creators who cannot pre-finance work and with projects better served by a smaller discovery award or a simpler clearly funded scope.

## FUND-L05 — Review, payment and uncertainty

**Restrictiveness: Safe.** The proposed ordinary review responds within seven real days, permits one seven-day correction period and provides a further seven-day response. A specific obstacle may receive one mutually agreed extension of up to 14 real days within the existing award and operating reserve. A missed operator review pauses dependent recipient work; seven further days without valid review permits the operator-cancellation path without forfeiting completed-work review. Submit accepted payment within seven real days after acceptance and complete payment instructions; disclose the separate expected settlement range and recover unresolved transfers. Exact currency, gross/net treatment, fees, any applicable withholding and a supported route are agreed before signing. Grants inherit no marketplace commission, hold or cash-out threshold. One program appeal may be requested within 14 real days of a challenged decision; a different unconflicted reviewer acknowledges within seven days and responds within 14 further days or states the missing evidence and a dated next step. This process does not extinguish other applicable rights. A failed or uncertain transfer retains the original obligation and actual costs; reconcile before paying again. Stop new commitments when funded review/recovery capacity is insufficient while completing existing obligations. Revisit actual review tails, creator cash-flow strain, failures and resolution effort.

## FUND-L06 — Advisory voice and real authority

**Restrictiveness: Medium.** Two independent qualified reviewers advise a named accountable budget owner; a steward coordinates the finite round and may also be that owner with the combination disclosed. At most one contributor advisor and one financial-supporter advisor each volunteer at most one hour for the round: one optional recommendation of at most 600 words in a seven-real-day window and one requested clarifying reply. A contributor qualifies through at least one useful accepted contribution, including paid, unpaid and non-code work; supporter eligibility follows a settled nonreversed voluntary support payment with no minimum spend. Ordinary customers are not automatically donors. Seek both perspectives without inventing a constituency or delaying applicants when a seat is vacant. One person chooses one role across overlapping categories; payment size and commit count add no controlling weight. Advisors cannot apply in the round and can withdraw without retaliation. Explain the authorized decision and material divergence from advice. Reviewers and patrons acquire no company equity, engine relicensing, world control, award entitlement or binding release power. Revisit a larger or partly binding program only through explicit delegation, actual community need and affordable operations.

## FUND-L07 — Conflicts that cannot vote themselves away

**Restrictiveness: Safe.** Reviewers cannot apply for an award in the same round. Material financial, family, close personal, employer and collaborator interests must be disclosed to the actual decision maker before discussion, including when the conflicted person will be absent. Recusal covers decision influence and private lobbying, not just a recorded vote. An applicant cannot recover influence through another account, supporter status or a proxy. Ordinary historical contribution or a properly completed past grant is not automatically a permanent conflict. If independent authority is missing, obtain a qualified replacement within the funded envelope or defer/leave the award unmade; do not silently weaken independence. Publish the relevant recusal result without unnecessary private personal details. Revisit through the actual tiny-community conflict cases and the cost of a workable correction/appeal path.

## FUND-L08 — Standard limited to funded work and actual rights

**Restrictiveness: Medium.** The first grant-specific standard favors useful playable/creator outcomes, honest evidence and costs, appropriate accessibility, privacy, legitimate reusable rights and finite handoff. Funded work must not rely on paid randomness, attendance coercion or selling a resident's affection. This is no universal ban on ordinary scarcity, challenge, bodily needs or supported fictional deadlines, and it adopts neither the whole wellbeing charter nor a compulsory clinical benefit. Target-component and inherited licenses control; contributors retain their copyright and an award supplies no automatic assignment or proprietary executable exception. Exact public-output grants must be agreed before work. Changes apply prospectively or through a freely accepted funded amendment. Revisit the standard with real funded examples rather than expanding it into a general moral score for creators.

## FUND-L09 — Public accountability with private evidence

**Restrictiveness: Safe.** Publish useful consented award summaries, actual status, results, approved attribution and reconciled program amounts. Distinguish approved, earned, submitted, uncertain and settled money. Legal identity, banking, confidential applications, private conversations and world history remain restricted. An alias can support public credit without publishing payment identity. Delete declined/withdrawn private applications 90 real days after final disposition, except for a disclosed unresolved dispute or applicable obligation; support earlier removal where compatible with current decision/recovery needs. Accepted agreements/payment evidence follow the actual adopted financial retention policy, without an invented statutory duration. The public outcome record lasts for the published archive, with corrections, privacy handling and an exportable static copy; no perpetual hosted page is promised. Publish the actual complete retention notice before intake. A generated account of success cannot replace agreed evidence. Revisit real reporting needs, privacy requests, disputed credit and affordable closure.

## FUND-L10 — Amendments, sponsorship and closure

**Restrictiveness: Safe.** Routine changes may stay within the original agreed outcome, rights, money and authority; material scope, price, rights, conflict or deadline changes require an explicit recorded amendment. No retroactive easier milestone may disguise failed delivery, and an operator's changed preference does not erase honestly earned payment. Dedicated external sponsorship is a later optional source with written purpose, actual settled funds, failure/remedy terms and a clear unspent-money disposition before collection. Closing intake or reaching the ordinary end date does not cancel signed obligations or uncertain transfers. Return only genuinely uncommitted money to its authorized source; finish retained commitments with a named funded owner. No second round, lifetime hosting or permanent governing seat follows. Revisit only when complete actual results and sustainable cost justify the next wanted program.
