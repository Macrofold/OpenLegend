# World creation limits

**Current scope: first-opening implementation plus the selected DG12 design, October 18, 2026; library implementation and capacity qualification remain open.** The [feature specification](../projects/world-creation-feature-spec.md) owns behavior. [ND01](../maintainers/needs-design.md#nd01--assemble-a-new-world-from-a-creators-premise), INV-4.10 and EWF12 retain delivery routing. Shared authoring, questions, retention and spending stay in [invention limits](inventions.md); continuing operation stays in [scalability limits](product-scalability.md). Broader product envelopes originated on October 3 and remain proposed where labeled below.

DG12’s October 18 implementation-ready design selects the bounded library/reuse consumer in WC-L06–WC-L12 and WC-L14–WC-L16. [LIB01–LIB09](../maintainers/invention-library.md) delegates this delivery from INV-8/EWF11; the parent portability, rights and data ownership remain. Selected limits are not measured runtime capacity.

[Batch 07](../maintainers/parallel-batch-07-playable-world-creation.md) implements the selected first-opening consumer under WC-L01–WC-L05 and WC-L13. Native/PostgreSQL and browser checks establish exercised cases, and one real language-authored inhabited opening reaches exact review and entry. Generated-tool play and complete first-play acceptance remain open; these observations establish no general capacity. It does not deliver or require the separately selected library consumer in WC-L06–WC-L12/WC-L14–WC-L16.

## WC-L01 — Reference starting population and place

**Two supplied references implemented; broader population targets remain unqualified · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Mike's October 14 instruction replaces the small inhabited supplied reference with the complete authored main world, and retains a simple solo testing ground. The main template currently contains seven people, including two distant rivals, through the existing standard initializer; solo contains one controlled person. This is a selected finite scenario, not an engine population cap or proof of autonomous community reliability. Custom described openings retain their supported one-person/optional-resident envelope. The earlier six-person social-variety hypothesis remains unqualified; merely installing seven authored people does not close operating, agency, resource-balance or human-play acceptance. No larger automatically generated population is promised. Broader atypical and empty fictional worlds remain product targets beyond this profile. [Content and selection](../worlds/base/playable-openings.md) and [measured scope](../verification/playable-world-creation.md#october-14-main-world-and-solo-testing-ground).

## WC-L02 — Initial rule-family coverage

**Bundled-family consumer implemented; expansion proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** The current profile uses supported native bodies, placements, finite resources, item definitions and learned methods; it adds no generated executable law. Add another composition only when its complete body, action, knowledge, resource and entry dependencies qualify. Essential unsupported behavior blocks that candidate; optional mechanics may remain explicitly deferred. The restriction keeps a useful first release concrete while allowing arbitrary premises to remain drafts. It does not make survival universal or establish general navigation, magic, construction or market support. Revisit with a real new opening that demonstrates the missing family.

## WC-L03 — Local detail and historical preparation

**Fresh native preparation implemented; broader history/rehearsal proposed · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Current creation builds only the selected people, scene, possessions, learning and bounded authored background, then checks actual standing, routes, material access and native action/knowledge prerequisites. It prepares initial cognition, perception and conditions without simulating invented earlier work or altering the eventual people's time, RNG, positions or experience during readiness checks. The broader product target may add targeted rehearsal, wider live scope or historical preparation only through separately admitted family/work and service qualification. It does not simulate centuries or populate every named distant place. Established facts constrain later detail; unsupported travel is refused before an impossible journey is accepted. This is not an unlimited lore-retention policy.

The custom/solo portion of the current [world-owned candidate profile](../../packages/domain/src/worlds/base/initial-opening.ts) enforces these preparation bounds:

| ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| People                                                 | One controlled person and one optional resident |
| Scene elements / possession lots / learning selections | 24 / 32 / 6                                     |
| Traits / explicitly seeded intentions per person       | 8 / 8                                           |
| Name / each authored prose field                       | 120 Unicode characters / 4,000 characters       |
| Resource patch / possession lot                        | 10,000 finite units / 512 units                 |
| Fire fuel                                              | Seven days of finite fuel                       |

These are editable small-opening admission envelopes. The main template instead selects its finite code-owned scene through a compact candidate; it accepts no caller-supplied larger cast or raw saved world. Its title and premise retain the same text bounds, and shared transport retains the 24,000-byte candidate ceiling. The [recorded complete-caller checks](../verification/playable-world-creation.md#work-bounds-and-review-fixes) activate a 58-entity opening with 24 scene elements, 32 possession lots and all six supported learning selections, and separately accept exactly 24,000 UTF-8 candidate bytes while refusing 24,001. Browser editing/entry and one live language-authored creation pass; generated-tool first play remains open in Batch 07. These are sampled local workloads, not universal fictional laws, optimal allowances or measured hosted capacity. Expand the envelopes only for a demonstrated supported opening and its complete preparation cost.

## WC-L04 — Questions, revision and waiting

**Native selection/revision implemented; conversational qualification open · Restrictiveness: Medium.** The existing creator session retains the selected opening and exact application-owned identities, seed, values, draft revisions and review. Native selection, editing and checks require no generation. Prefer one consequential question at a time within the existing QST envelopes, and ask none when known choices and suitable defaults already resolve the material question. No additional universal interview-count limit is selected. Creation sections are not mandatory screens; optional rehearsal and deeper inspection do not block a qualified start. Existing episode, turn, record and spending boundaries remain controlling; no automatic paid question loop, keystroke-triggered regeneration or indefinite paid waiting. Preserve selected parts and re-evaluate affected behavior. Offer play when a faithful useful candidate is ready; further variations are deliberate choices. At exhaustion, retain available work and offer explicit narrower scope or renewed authorization. Revisit using novice/experienced creator correction, actual play and abandonment evidence.

## WC-L07 — Complete transfer work

**Proposed — selected design; runtime capacity unqualified · Restrictiveness: Safe.** At most **32 distinct exact scoped definition references** and **1 MiB (1,048,576 bytes)** of decoded manifest/definition text per one-root artifact. Raw UTF-8 current-format file/transport also fits 1 MiB; escaped text cannot enlarge the decoded allowance. Count root, owned output and all required recipe/item/family/policy/material references; deduplicate shared exact requirements. A reciprocal producer-output ownership backlink is not a causal cycle, while a true production dependency cycle refuses. Stop incomplete on missing/changed/private/unsupported requirements, work exhaustion or overflow; never truncate a required producer or activate a partial closure.

These values contain the two chosen useful proofs with finite preparation cost and need exact-boundary/complete-caller qualification. They do not assert optimal capacity. Existing argument/candidate/session/traversal/native-work/SQL admission still applies; this aggregate retained-artifact allowance does not enlarge a 24,000-byte creator candidate or 28,000-byte tool argument. Pass immutable retained references through those small requests. Exact family/host support must already exist in the destination; imported definition bodies do not install native executors. [Technical closure and binding](../projects/world-creation-tech-design.md#4-retain-close-dependencies-and-bind).

## WC-L08 — Library presentation and search

**Proposed — selected design; complete-caller qualification open · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Display **20 permitted entries per page** with stable selected entry/version and explicit continuation. This is no lifetime invention cap. Select account-scoped summary rows before full artifact/detail hydration. Reuse the current `text-search.ts` Unicode NFKC/lowercase word-prefix conjunction: **eight distinct terms, at most 64 code points per term**, with **250-row read chunks and at most 2,000 examined summaries per search request**. These are the existing history-search owner envelopes reused by this consumer, not a second parser or locale-dependent PostgreSQL full-text approximation.

At the examined-row bound, return a cursor and **More of your library remains to search**, including when that portion has zero matches. Only complete eligible-corpus exhaustion establishes no results. A 21st match is lookahead: continue after the twentieth displayed match rather than skipping it. Keyset cursors bind account/query/filter/sort/sequence horizon; rights/index changes require an honest refresh. Native SQL deadline/admission is independently finite; timeout/unavailability is incomplete, not absence. Underlying retained corpus, index work and per-request summary bytes still require growing-history/absent/common-query/actual-reader-lane qualification. No AI search, embeddings, generation or per-card closure traversal is required. Add a normalized-word index only after measured need and with unchanged Unicode semantics/write-cost evidence.

## WC-L09 — Pending imports and updates

**Proposed — selected design; implementation open · Restrictiveness: Safe.** **One active import preparation per destination**, enforced at its operational owner; no unattended competing-import backlog and **zero automatic user-operation redispatch or installation updates**. A competing request can inspect/cancel or deliberately wait. Exact already-installed content yields Already available here without new knowledge, items or authorship. Unknown acknowledgement is read-only original-receipt recovery, not an automatic repeat; meaningful binding/draft/authority changes require current preparation/review. The library issues a **60-second real-time** one-use authorization for the exact approved artifact/plan/destination/timeline/registration before dispatch; the destination retains its own current authority/pin checks. Expiry never blocks permitted receipt reads or authorizes execution. The selected grant policy stops new authorizations; it does not promise atomic revocation of an already accepted cross-host operation. Terms requiring that stronger boundary are unsupported. This short native dispatch window is a conservative operational choice, not game time or a limit on how long someone may keep/edit a draft. Ordinary play continues while optional preparation uses existing host-work admission. A live replacement/removal remains INV-5.

The source contribution metadata outbox is a separate derived delivery consumer: **one worker per source, at most 32 identities/bodies per batch**, sequential exact sends, ordinary work-lane yield between batches and existing finite host-work/transport deadlines. It starts on commit/startup/resume signals, stops on unavailable admission/service and does not poll world ticks. It may redeliver the same minimal historical metadata after receipt reconciliation and proved non-admission on a later recovery signal; that operation cannot grant rights, install content or buy generation. Initial older-contribution reconciliation uses the same batch/cursor owner. This exception is not automatic retry of a person's retain/import/publish/acquire action.

## WC-L10 — Retained history and copies

**Proposed — selected retention contract; total service growth unqualified · Restrictiveness: Too liberal without configured storage admission.** Preserve eligible all-past contribution history and distinguish exact retained copy from source reference; no new lifetime invention/deletion/count cap or automatic expiry is selected. Contribution/index coverage must state unfinished source reconciliation. Revisions, blobs, grants, release/acquisition history, operation receipts and pending capture can grow independently; authoring-session expiry is not library deletion. A source reference makes no promise that unretained private/retired bytes remain retrievable.

New independent retention/publication requires a configured positive finite allowance in the actual operator storage owner. With none, refuse those new operations honestly while retaining available references and ordinary installed play. Actual bytes/record overhead and shared-blob references are admitted transactionally; no “saved” acknowledgement before durability and no silent deletion of prior work to fit a new copy. Existing data/operational retention policy remains controlling. A shared required artifact cannot be cascaded away by one user's hide/delete action. Qualification must include pending work, many releases/receipts, restart and simultaneous writes; no unlimited free storage or legacy-format compatibility is promised.

## WC-L11 — Media and generative work

**Proposed — selected design · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** **Zero model calls** for browsing, lexical search, exact retention/compatibility/reopen/unchanged reuse, native supported field editing or physical method writing/learning. Separately requested conversational adaptation uses existing authoring allowance, reservation, cancellation and uncertain-result controls. First transfer uses native presentation and actual applicable notices; arbitrary remote media, commissioned art, executable code and a second asset-storage budget are excluded. Required unavailable presentation blocks that promise; optional omitted illustration stays labelled. Neither package availability nor record arrival automatically initiates an NPC invention/thought.

## WC-L12 — Rights and private-origin presentation

**Proposed — selected design; broader D44 policy remains open · Restrictiveness: Safe.** Each audience receives only its permitted metadata, credit, inventory and dependency facts. Historical ownership, original/imported/adapted association, learned methods and release rights are distinct. Zero price grants no unrecorded modification, commercial-use or redistribution right. The first release has **one selected known recipient account**, sufficient actual component grants and publisher mandate; publication sends no message, acquires nothing and changes no destination world.

Lists use separately approved public origin information or Private source world. A deliberate private source read rechecks current source authority; library membership cannot reveal its title or private dependency. Retained copies and earlier acquired releases follow actual version terms; ordinary unpublish stops new acquisition after its commit, while exceptional rights/privacy/security action follows its separately authorised scope. One-invention payloads/eligible subsets cannot be labelled complete world packs. No product-wide free-use licence, compulsory contribution terms, generated legal consent, coauthor mandate or cross-operator ownership adjudication is chosen here.


## WC-L13 — Prepared empty creation targets

**Implemented boundary; complete qualification open — October 13, 2026 · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** The creator journey uses an existing authorized creator session and operator-configured local targets with independent current PostgreSQL storage, data directories and current account/operating policy. An awaiting-creation target starts no bundled world or autonomous activity before exact reviewed activation. Only the current supported creation profile is admitted; no automatic host provisioning, occupied-world replacement, library import, remote federation, arbitrary executable law or generated media is supplied. Client/model selection carries a permitted target identity, never a connection string, path, executable command or new entitlement. An unavailable, occupied, changed or unsupported target preserves the draft and refuses activation. Native/SQL cases, the admitted maximum/rejected excess, browser interaction and one real language-authored opening-to-entry have been exercised; [OC01–OC05](../maintainers/parallel-batch-07-playable-world-creation.md) still owns generated-tool play and complete creation-to-play acceptance. The one-resident reference does not establish a population capacity, and target count, retained candidates and total storage remain independently growing work under the existing authoring/hosting owners. Expansion requires a complete supported profile or hosting use case, not merely a larger numeric allowance.

Required deferred routes use the existing real navigation worker through a separate bounded preparation owner: at most 64 requests per admitted batch, one active batch, eight queued batches and a 20-second worker-batch deadline after queue admission. The worker's V8 old-generation JavaScript heap limit is 128 MiB; this is not a total process-memory bound. One worker shares map/body preparation within its batch and terminates on completion or failure. Queued admission retains the shared five-second wait limit; termination and cleanup follow the batch deadline rather than promising an exact end-to-end completion time. Overload, timeout and unavailable preparation remain incomplete; they do not establish that no route exists. Native standing/approach and an obstructed worker detour have been exercised without changing the eventual world. These operational ceilings bound the selected request, not sustained concurrency, population capacity or navigation latency; qualification and expansion follow the actual supported consumer.

## WC-L14 — Current-format parsing and metadata

**Proposed — selected design; parser/complete-caller qualification open · Restrictiveness: Safe.** The one-root file is canonical UTF-8 JSON, `openlegend-invention-v1`, with no compression, base64 attachments, embedded executable content or remote-fetch references. Apply WC-L07's raw and decoded bounds, then an iterative check of **at most 32 nesting levels** (root level one) and **16,384 JSON nodes**, counting each property name and each primitive/container value once, before recursive schema validation/canonical serialization. Canonical round-trip equality rejects duplicate keys and ambiguous/noncanonical uploaded representations; unsupported files remain available to the user without activation. Exact per-stage parser/body-work admission and peak memory still need measurement.

Retain the selected recipe schema's **80-character name / 700-character description** fields and native validators; private library nicknames are at most **80 Unicode code points**, permitted credit labels **120 code points**. The existing attribution envelope supplies at most **65 owner credits** (64 world creators plus a distinct player); the same-author proof uses one. The selected artifact has **zero or one direct ancestry parent**; inspecting ancestry does not recursively include all historical versions. Public metadata/grant/notice text and any release envelope count in the complete operation's byte admission, never as unmetered attachments. A retained artifact which does not fit a later complete release envelope is retained and the release is blocked; nothing silently truncates its required content. These bounds give short readable metadata and a finite decoder, not a general future schema or authorship limit.

## WC-L15 — Physical method-writing and voluntary learning profile

**Proposed — selected bundled world rule; implementation/play balance open · Restrictiveness: Safe.** [The authored profile](../worlds/base/invention-reuse.md) selects **one known exact method**, **one wood + one prepared-fibre unit**, an actual accessible native **knife retained as a tool**, **300 game seconds** of ordinary stationary work, and **one portable method sheet of packing load one**. At the bundled normal 60 game-seconds/real-second clock the work is five real seconds; speed/pause follow the actual clock. Inputs are debited at Start; interruption gives no sheet/refund, while save/resume preserves an ongoing attempt's exact work. No numeric proficiency, method-learning quota, copies-per-method cap or automatic reproduction is added. Each physical copy still uses real resources, custody/capacity and native work/storage admission.

Learning retains the delivered instant actor-owned exact record action: current access/inspection/pins, one method, no consumed sheet, no output item or producer-method grant, and idempotent already-known result. Record possession, a library entry or another person's learning grants nothing. Ordinary method facts are not account export rights. The existing spoken Teach command has no separate listener-assent transition; its receipt cannot qualify voluntary learning. This selected proof uses actual learner choice and independent later craft/use. A separately reviewed creator teaching copy is one explicit authored supply, default off, with its own receipt; it is not ordinary maker labour. Revisit writing costs, tool eligibility or physical media only for a demonstrated useful world/capability and real play evidence, not to mandate a paper economy.

## WC-L16 — Optional library attachment to a fresh opening

**Proposed — selected design; OC extension qualification open · Restrictiveness: Very safe.** An unstarted supported opening may attach **one exact prepared one-root recipe artifact** under WC-L07, with separately selected starting methods or **one physical teaching sheet**. The compact candidate remains within the existing **24,000-byte** OC limit and its **six starting-learning selections**; dependencies do not auto-select producer/root knowledge. Complete artifact bytes live in separately retained native preparation, not in a larger model/tool argument. Target generation, authorised initial-account mapping, exact attachment/current grants and compiled-world review are required before the same OC Start transaction commits definitions, explicit knowledge/placement and local import receipt together.

This is an optional supported profile revision, not arbitrary pack loading or world-save cloning. No library availability is required for unattached Main/Solo/custom starts, and current-world installation remains independently useful. Until the exact attachment Start/recovery path qualifies, advertise starting the ordinary world then using its selected current-world import path; do not claim authoring a knowledge checkbox delivered it. Broader attachments or larger populations remain their own opening/hosting design and capacity work.
