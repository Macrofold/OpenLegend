# Action records and learned activities

Characters record their own attempted actions, notice their actual results, and can learn connected methods during eligible downtime. A learned method is a tentative way to attempt something. It neither assigns a goal nor guarantees its outcome.

## What a character sees

The engine builds permitted structured facts, then renders compact English. Names, targets, tools, distances, observed health, costs, requirements, uncertainty and results appear where relevant. The occurrence renderer keeps internal IDs, schema tags, reference tables and result codes out of its descriptions. Typed-choice prompts use readable option labels, but the generative action list and known-plan list still expose their selection handles; [AE03](maintainers/action-experience.md#ae03--compact-english-and-remaining-work-views) tracks that remaining English-first presentation gap. Exact server-owned request bindings remain necessary to identify the selected objects without guessing from names.

The syntax explanation is: “Each action says what to do and with what. When brackets appear, they contain smaller actions in order; semicolons separate them. Results say what actually happened.” A leaf needs no brackets. Parent actions preserve the chosen purpose, including “Hunt once,” while children describe stages that actually ran. Completion of an attempt does not mean the animal died or the character obtained food.

A choice states the current straight-line target distance and the configured stopping distance separately from attack reach. Later travel is uncertain, not a prediction from today's position. The selected weapon retains its relevant known damage, speed, reach, accuracy and ammunition information. Same-named possessions receive distinct readable positions within the actor's inventory. Past records retain event-time equipment and observations; a later tool or moving target cannot rewrite them.

Known facts use templates or readable labels. Critical nested details without a template use narrow, readable JSON; ordinary actions have no duplicate JSON representation. Projection tracks covered facts. Optional information may be omitted explicitly, but required information that cannot fit prevents dispatch. Collapsed layout still carries critical descendant facts with their enclosing action names.

Shared decision context supplies body state, goals, feelings and active commitments. A method adds its remaining steps, future prerequisites, actual or still-unproduced materials, aggregate consumption and supported shortages. These summaries follow the supplied finite structure; they do not search for every plan or simulate a future world. Known blockers, unknown facts and incomplete preparation remain distinct. Other options remain available and replace or interrupt current work through ordinary agency admission.

## Ordinary player action discovery

An action appears only when the world installs its required native support. This applies to the ordinary catalogue, inventory rows, object/person menus and equipped quick actions, as well as resident offers, typed help and requested activities. An installed action can explain missing ingredients or equipment; omitted support supplies no fallback choice. Every later command still checks current support and its ordinary permissions and prerequisites.

The ordinary action menu reuses permitted native action facts, including the exact target and carried tool, known distance, observed condition, approach, duration and consumed resources. A row highlights its decisive cost or blocker; hover explanations contain the longer commitment without duplicate expandable controls. Shots pin an exact compatible projectile and show the selected launcher and any explicit auto-equip conflicts; melee uses show the same compatible-equipment prerequisite. A possible hit is never promised damage or a kill.

Inventory cooking uses **Explore uses and targets** to choose an installed preparation, exact ingredient lots and an exact perceived fire; the former implicit-fire shortcut is removed. When none is visible, an unavailable cooking choice explains the missing fire. Food preparation facts describe all declared ingredients/products, work, heat and interruption cost from that selected definition. Fiber/cord preparation detail separately says it draws from carried supplies across lots, and a small selected lot does not block an otherwise sufficient carried total. Exact-item uses retain their item identity; fiber/cord preparation retains its supply-based command rather than pretending to bind one lot.

Finite cast choices describe ordinary time/chance and the held tool, without revealing a future roll or hidden stock. An empty cast records its actual no-yield outcome. Cooking records each exact named ingredient role and every actual output port; learned-method binding reconstructs those roles instead of embedding a private ingredient map as a shared literal. [The preparation/cast contract](food-preparation.md) and [AV05 evidence](verification/river-meal.md) distinguish native effects, recorded-command reuse and separately observed live choice.

Complete discovery retains every permitted offer/fuel lot, including accessible nested possessions. Selected-item browsing binds only that exact item for these uses and refuses inaccessible contents before returning detail. Right-click quick suggestions show currently available contextual actions. The **All actions** section also includes known actions that are currently unavailable, with dimmed rows, visible blocking reasons and hover/keyboard explanations. Unknown techniques remain excluded by the server’s permitted catalogue. Category labels are returned separately from choices: a category with no currently usable action is gray. If it contains real known blocked actions, it still opens so their explanations remain reachable. A truly empty category is disabled and has no submenu or invented locked/fallback action. Categories left empty only because walking or a no-longer-visible target was omitted disappear with those choices; for example, hiding Walk here cannot leave an empty Movement category on a box. Walk here is omitted because primary click already chooses a walking destination. Explicit browsers omit walking rows too. Right-click has no complete-catalogue entry, hide-unavailable toggle or freeform-request controls. **Settings & help → Browse known actions** opens the separate action browser; inventory’s explicit **Explore uses and targets** remains an item-scoped browser. Those browsers always include permitted known unavailable actions, their reasons and editable invention drafts; the stored visibility preference no longer hides menu choices. The complete browser concerns currently visible targets and accessible possessions, not an encyclopedia of hidden or unknown techniques.

Up to five contextual shortcuts appear first. A category with one action shows that action directly unless it is already among those shortcuts; larger categories retain counted right-opening submenus. The shortlist uses the selected world’s shared action priorities; within an equal priority it retains the existing deterministic order. The same preferred exact choice leads the object shortcut. Hover, click or Right opens a submenu; Left or Escape returns to its trigger. Submenus flip left at an edge and overlap the parent if neither side fits. A larger category initially renders at most 40 choices; search shows up to 40 matching rows directly across the entire permitted result, including visible target/tool identity. Scrolling within 80px of the end automatically reveals another 40 choices without moving focus. **Show more choices** remains as a keyboard route, adding 40 and focusing the first added command. Valid refresh retains reading windows; deliberate search/scope changes reset them. Speak/Act and Look closer count toward the five contextual rows; the category windows are separate. [AP01](limits/interface.md#ap01--ordinary-action-discovery) owns display bounds and tradeoffs.

Contextual selection, selected inventory uses and the separate known-action browser share the existing preview and exact command interface. Search, reading windows and opening order survive valid refreshes. Permission/timeline changes invalidate old reads, disappeared targets lose their details, and failed reads require explicit recovery before commands return. This presentation adds no mechanical eligibility, NPC context, model ranking or universal equipment score. [PG03 evidence](verification/player-clarity-ui.md#pg03--action-discovery-and-commitments--october-3-2026) and [interface bounds](limits/interface.md#ap01--ordinary-action-discovery) distinguish the selected player journey from broader programs.

Offer previews share read-only prerequisites with actual execution and avoid constructing a disposable world when refused. Drop previews omit the action-history record that would be discarded, while still exercising structural transfer and identity/event checks. Actual commands independently check current state and record their effects as before. Complex transfers and accepted offers still use disposable execution for previews; [PG04 evidence](verification/command-frame-spikes.md#pg04-preview-work-and-larger-load-follow-up--october-4-2026) measures this scoped improvement, and [PF05](maintainers/performance.md#pf05--public-view-and-browser-responsiveness) retains remaining projection and capacity work.

Every opening benefits from selecting relevant families before preparation and from sharing exact command checks within that request. Possession menu choices reuse the inventory action owner without building unused comparison/packing/method-detail text. The HTTP read retains durable permission checks and current scope/maintenance/loading/generation guards but does not wait behind world changes or background saves. It displays the latest published world; selecting a choice always checks current conditions independently. The [first-opening plan](projects/completed/action-menu-responsiveness-plan.md) and [measurement report](verification/command-frame-spikes.md#first-opening-action-menus-and-sections--october-14-2026) distinguish removed work from remaining complete-catalogue and hardware qualification.

## What is recorded

`packages/domain/src/action-experience.ts` stores actor-owned occurrences with selected parent meaning, native command/definition pins, actual stages, terminal outcome, multiple output ports, observed effects and links to permitted evidence. Body, status and item owners supply actual committed values and quantities. A final eight-damage hit against two remaining health records two damage. Several subjects have separate effects, not one success flag. Numeric display removes floating-point noise without changing stored values.

The kernel remains the only physical executor. Recording is in the same transition as the action/effect. A miss, blocked step, cancellation and successful attempt remain different. Already spent ammunition, materials, injury and produced items survive interruption. A failed sequence stops; later use of a partial product requires a new choice or an explicitly supported control path. Duplicate command receipts and terminal occurrence checks prevent duplicate effects.

Output consumption connects actual lots across independently chosen actions. Split/merge handling preserves contributors; mixed indistinguishable units do not acquire invented per-source precision. Family-supplied state links retain contributing injuries. Missing or externally changed support makes the candidate incomplete rather than establishing that a last hit defeated a healthy animal. Purpose links connect the actor's chosen ordered work; temporal adjacency or sharing a tool alone does not prove causality.

When a learned sequence uses something an earlier step produced, it refers to that step's actual output rather than requiring the original historical item again. This includes weapons and ammunition as well as equipped items and cooking ingredients. In the verified Craft bow → Craft arrow → Equip → Hunt sequence, replay equips the newly crafted bow and consumes the newly crafted arrow. The original item identities remain facts about the original attempt.

## Scoped practice and coaching support

Eligible real sling releases record the exact tool/mechanism, projectile and quantity, applied handling, release steadiness and, after flight, only the impact/damage actually observed in private action-occurrence detail. The competence owner then references that committed occurrence/event for future improvement. Starting, observing, retelling or redisplaying an action is not another release. Hunting retains its existing separate learning eligibility; the new inert-target and coaching occurrences do not automatically create a learned activity or disclose a recipe.

Completed coaching references the learner's observed release and each participant's actual feedback occurrence. Explicit evidence correction/forgetting reconciles compact support through the existing experience owner, including current privacy overlays on restored saves. Ordinary cold history/compaction retains support; historical shot debits and material effects are never rerolled or refunded. The owner's inspection hydrates readable release records through bounded indexed lookup and keeps unavailable detail explicit. Current coaching still marks observation at release, before the later flight result is known; [PC04](maintainers/practical-competence.md#pc04--voluntary-coaching) retains the required result observation and truthful feedback as an open integration gap. ## Learning and shared definitions

`activity-learning.ts` considers a bounded repertoire: direct material pairs with native support, endpoint dependency slices, selected-purpose spans, and compatible known spans. It preserves the raw trace and larger/overlapping candidates. A recognized four-action span does not prevent learning an eight-action span, and overlapping matches do not execute the same source step twice. Misses accompany the evidence without becoming mandatory failed steps. The exact ten-action example remains in the [technical design](projects/action-experience-tech-design.md#exact-ten-action-example).

Server preparation reads actor-scoped indexed pages and bounded dependencies before description construction. Durable cursors and retained records allow later passes; the small pending list is a coalesced wake signal, not the complete history. Time controls when processing happens, not sequence membership.

Existing maintenance admits a separate typed assessment at safe idle opportunities. Current automatic learning is NPC-only; the bundled world owns its health/fullness/controller eligibility. The assessment batches retain/decline/uncertain choices over the character's own permitted evidence, including the chosen purpose when recorded. That private purpose does not enter shared definitions or their identity. It is available in strict Jev-only mode without enabling generation, embeddings or workspaces. Native retention preflight checks capacity before paid dispatch. Existing spending admission, cancellation and timeline fences apply; dispatched signatures are recorded before calling the provider, so malformed/uncertain work is not automatically paid for again. New action selection is independent of optional learning.

A retain atomically inserts or reuses an immutable world-scoped structural definition and the actor's private acquisition/support. The definition excludes private words and places; those remain in personal bindings. Exact matching includes module and item-definition pins. Existing recipe IDs remain immutable under their own authority. Independent reproduction can reuse structure while retaining independent evidence. Catalogue existence never grants knowledge. Decline/uncertain publishes no definition; capacity refuses optional retention without deleting existing support.

Each method stores a compact description of the whole current module selection, its manifest. Actual attribute, sense, body, acoustic and ranged definitions contribute their exact identity, version and content fingerprint; declared pins and all remaining manifest settings are retained. Normalization, retention, offers and current-format validation use the same description. Changing an actual definition makes old work stale even without increasing the parent revision or updating its declared pin. The existing global dependency remains: a manifest change can make a method unavailable even when its steps do not use the changed definition. This representation avoids copying full definitions into both the method and its signature while preserving [AEL07's unchanged storage bound](limits/action-experience.md#ael07).

Acquisitions remain tentative; success once is not calibrated confidence. Only personally acquired compatible methods enter relevant offers or explicit inspection. Revoking sole support suppresses use; independent lawful support may preserve it. Current forgetting overlays also apply after restoring older saves. Routine memory compaction does not revoke an acquisition.

### Fresh stock binding in finite methods

The native stock-transfer adapter moves an exact quantity of portable homogeneous units under one current item-definition pin. The actor selects their accessible possessions or the direct contents of one accessible container as the source, and themselves or one accessible container as the destination. It excludes reserved units and items needed by ongoing work. Admission proves the selected quantity and personal minimum against current stock, then preflights and commits the whole move through the existing custody owner. A refusal changes nothing; incomplete selection is neither an empty source nor partial success. Moving stock among the actor's accessible bags does not spend their own stock. [AEL09](limits/action-experience.md#ael09--fresh-stock-binding-and-finite-reuse) owns the shared 200-candidate allowance and 16-lot bound; neither limits the number of saved belongings or units in a lot.

Each occurrence records the semantic selection, exact admitted lots and revisions, and actual output receipts. Learning checks that those receipts agree with the native selection before retaining support. A learned method preserves the demonstrated definition pin, quantities, personal minimum and compatible object/place roles; it does not preserve old lot IDs or revisions as reusable authority. Every later attempt resolves fresh stock again. An eligible NPC may retain, decline or remain uncertain about a demonstrated finite method. Native completion alone installs neither a method nor a goal, and retention alone does not demonstrate later voluntary reuse.

A ground-container role can be rebound through the actor's current permitted inspection of that selected container. Changed placement, access, contents or timeline makes the inspection stale; a partial page is not a census of the cache. Method offers must keep unavailable, unknown and incomplete bindings distinct. Individual keepsakes and other exact-instance inventory operations retain their existing revision-bound contract.

## Selected execution and inspection

Changes to action selection, first-step availability or execution follow [Action availability and temporary execution](action-capabilities.md#action-availability-and-temporary-execution).

`activity-execution.ts` extends the existing `ActorPlan` with a bounded frontier. Supported controls are native invocation, sequence, branch, bounded repeat and bounded wait. Predicates are registered observations such as availability, life, equipment, fire and actual output quantity. There is no generated code or predicate evaluation from prose. Learning retains realized traces; it does not infer new stopping rules or a universal hunting loop.

Each ready step passes through the existing kernel and its current authority, access, range and resource checks. Outputs bind only when actually committed. A completed step is not replayed on continuation or same-version restore. Failed native steps require a new choice; an explicitly blocked control may be reconsidered with current bindings. Unknown predicates stop honestly. Another actor's response or cooperation cannot be executed as the learner's action.

Restoration distinguishes retained results from work that may continue. Active, paused and nonterminal blocked activities require their current installed command/observation support; a resumable learned method also requires its exact current manifest. Completed or cancelled activities and terminal watches can retain their history after that support is removed. Their command structure, personal bindings, exact trusted observation meaning and spending receipts still validate. This permits an ended watch to explain what it spent without granting another attempt or replaying its effects.

Selecting a plan checks only its next action through the same read-only native prerequisite owner used by execution. Later equipment and typed earlier-output dependencies remain deferred until their step starts; selection neither consumes materials nor promises success. Inspected learned-method pages and their continuations survive a feasibility refresh only for the same actor, inspection and timeline. Current bindings are rebuilt; revoked methods disappear and a changed catalogue offers fresh inspection rather than reusing a stale continuation.

An explicitly selected one-session watch is requested control, not a learned conditional method. Its saved execution holds the chosen observation, absolute simulation deadline, access/reach requirements, fuel-attempt and actual-spending counters, and whether attendance was interrupted. Deadline-safe cancellation alone grants no spending support. A counted action requires an installed exact support definition that declares its argument restriction and conservative maximum debit; admission checks remaining allowance, and successful completion must supply a valid actual spending receipt. Generic stock movement has no counted-spending support. Failure feedback about a selected personal stock guard remains actor-private; witnesses receive only the visible stop. No-effect admitted fuel attempts count toward the attempt limit but spend no unit; committed receipts account for actual spending once. Suspension preserves the deadline and counters, while a restarted native fuel step is a new attempt. Stopping after a cache unit was already taken leaves that real unit in the actor's possessions. Completed steps and receipts survive current-format restart without replay. Watch control, guarded fuel steps, thresholds and unseen branches are excluded from finite-method normalization. The bundled world's supported first watch and its fuel observations are owned by [camp routines](worlds/base/camp-routines.md).

Own action history and personally learned activities are available through explicit cognition inspection and the existing private-mind UI, with separate history/method pages. The private panel reports whether learning is pending, including its need for safe idle time, storage and an authorized model budget. The server rechecks actor/account/timeline scope around asynchronous reads. Creator inspection cannot access another human's private mind. Ordinary public views never publish private action history or other actors' acquisitions.

## Storage and extension boundary

Occurrences, methods, acquisitions and learning cursors use the existing PostgreSQL record repository and saved-world transaction. Cold completed history is paged independently of the small native plan window. Current-format manual saves include active control frames, actual output bindings and private evidence. Incompatible development saves are explicitly rejected under the unchanged [development save policy](../AGENTS.md#development-save-policy); no migration or automatic reset was added.

Recorded commands are plain snapshots owned by action history, including commands dispatched from a learned plan step. A temporary editing reference cannot escape into a committed occurrence. [PW01 evidence](verification/level1-decisions.md#pw01-reliability-repairs--october-2-2026) covers the repaired clone failure, validated learned execution and current-format checkpoint restore.

Repeated discovery reuses derived lookups for exact trusted implementations only when the installed pin array and its nested data are immutable. Freezing the outer container alone is insufficient; mutable nested values and accessors prevent reuse. Mutable builders, editing drafts, replaced selections and restored data resolve afresh. Current world-dependent support, permissions, equipment, resources and availability are never cached by this lookup. [The review evidence](verification/world-engine-boundaries.md#requested-full-review--october-14-2026) records reduced descriptor-resolution work through the actual catalogue caller; broader full-catalogue efficiency and population qualification remain with [AC11](maintainers/action-capabilities.md#ac11--usability-and-measured-efficiency) and [PF05](maintainers/performance.md#pf05--public-view-and-browser-responsiveness).

The engine owns representation, finite execution, provenance, projection coverage and sharing integrity. The bundled world owns hunting labels, animal-health disclosure, weapon/food/fire mechanics and safe-downtime eligibility. Future native families can supply permitted facts and actual effects without changing the English grammar or adding arbitrary executable extensions.

This delivery does not add new multi-target combat, robotics, general cooperative work, teaching of methods, approximate semantic matching, confidence promotion, suspension of every physical family, or a whole-population performance guarantee. Exact one-off revision-bound transfers/splits/merges are recorded but remain excluded from reusable invocations; the fresh semantic stock adapter is the narrow exception described above. [PW10](maintainers/parallel-batch-01-playable-week.md#pw10--chosen-camp-activities-and-reusable-finite-methods) records current native/static/restart qualification and the still-open UI, live voluntary retention/reuse and integration gates. These boundaries and expansion triggers are recorded in [AEL](limits/action-experience.md); broader action/agency work retains its own tracker.

## Consenting travel companions

[Voluntary outings](worlds/base/social.md#voluntary-outings) add exact pair/destination consent beside the installed activity requests. Acceptance starts two separately owned finite movement activities; it never makes another character a step in the inviter's plan. Ordinary movement receipts establish progress and arrival. Private invitation/arrival/separation/ending evidence enters the same personal memory and reconsideration path. Plan outcomes reference that existing evidence rather than duplicating it. Replaying or learning one's walk cannot replay the companion's consent.

Ending the trip removes live consent and stops only its exact remaining activities, preserving unrelated replacement work and already completed effects. Current-format storage retains active consent independently of both plans, verifies their linkage, and stores terminal outcomes as ordinary experience. [AEL10](limits/action-experience.md#ael10--outing-consent-and-own-movement) owns the extension boundary; [PX04 evidence](verification/voluntary-outings.md) separates mechanical checks from voluntary choices.
