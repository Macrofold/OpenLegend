# AI providers

`@open-legend/ai` is a server-only HTTP boundary with two independent methods: `judge` for Jev and `generate` for OpenAI Responses. It imports Node utilities and must never be bundled into the browser. No credentials, fabricated answers, fixture fallbacks, automatic retries, scheduled work, or budget storage are built into the package. Tests inject HTTP responses explicitly.

The game server owns routing, fresh context assembly, audience restrictions, request deduplication, durable reservations, cancellation, model eligibility, and validating proposals before world changes. A valid JSON result establishes its shape, not its truth, safety, or applicability to the current world. Native known actions need neither method. Jev can select among supplied routes or rate supplied evidence; conversation, thoughts, and novel declaration drafts require the generator. The server also implements a Macrofold backend; a configured `MACROFOLD_API_KEY` selects it ahead of direct providers. The direct client described below remains a separate adapter. Independent workspace reflection, full native deliberation and unified owner authoring require Macrofold and fail explicitly without it; immediate conversations and known-action decision inference can use the configured direct adapters.

## Jev-only execution

### Admission and cancellation

The server has one shared decision slot. A new conversation or invention request uses the existing busy refusal while an admitted decision owns it; it does not cancel another character's funded opportunity. This replaces the earlier unconditional chat-first preemption. The client preserves its draft for explicit resubmission after the current decision finishes. Invalid, paused, replayed or unauthorized requests cannot discard current work. A successfully admitted human conversation or invention cancels background reflection asynchronously without waiting for remote cleanup. The existing urgent evidence refresh, explicit cancellation, durable receipts and uncertain spending remain separate.

Resolve the validity and authority of a replacement request before any cancellation it could cause. A request's priority alone does not establish that its replacement operation is permitted or worth discarding already admitted work. Cancellation does not make dispatched work free or create an automatic retry. [Complete-operation authorization](engine-and-world-boundaries.md#complete-operation-authorization) and [continuation](save-and-load.md#continuation-and-identity) own those boundaries; [the combat qualification](verification/rivals-and-contested-ground.md#preserve-admitted-decisions-and-qualify-the-last-ammunition--october-14-2026) records the demonstrated failure and correction.

`AI_JEV_ONLY=true` permits known action selection while disabling generative replies, reflection, narration generation, conversation-summary generation and paid embeddings. Guards reject generation before provider dispatch; no automatic generative fallback repairs uncertainty. Direct Jev credentials or the configured Macrofold connection suffice. Existing native plan continuation remains zero-call.

The level-1 path, identified by `JEV_QUESTIONS_VERSION` in `apps/server/src/jev-questions.ts`, batches independent Noul judgments over permitted known actions. Non-speech triggers prepare those actions first and rate them in the routing request; speech triggers route first and rate them in a dependent second request. Questions assess concrete progress on a current need or goal, with explicit positive/negative criteria separating useful preparation from unavailable prerequisites, pointless repetition and ordinary uncertainty of success. Action descriptions identify purpose, equipment and the finite attempt; the approved comparison follow-up adds short tool descriptions, compact native combat capabilities and world-permitted visible animal health without restoring the separate game-mechanics paragraph. It selects the highest score at or above **0.7**, preserving deliberate continue separately. Below that, the decision escalates to an offered generative level when generation is enabled and otherwise defers with an explicit reason; with the decision fallback off, missing or invalid ratings defer without escalation. An explicitly enabled [fallback policy](#decision-escalation) may instead admit its one higher attempt. In this Jev-only mode every such decision therefore defers. An exact offered equipment prerequisite can become an ordinary two-step plan; unrelated ratings never compose actions. Novel goal text, speech and proposals still need generation when enabled. [The runtime contract](../archive/07-technical-architecture/agent-agency-runtime.md#24-level-1-selection-without-generative-escalation) owns admission; [CG05](limits/cognition.md#cg05) records the provisional threshold.

[Live trials](verification/embodied-survival.md#renewed-jev-only-diagnosis-and-hunting-demonstration) demonstrate eating/gathering comparisons and spontaneous knife hunting with miss-aware retries, without seeded operational goals or generation. The [final minimal-context trials](verification/embodied-survival.md#minimal-context-and-equipment-derived-hunting) retain deer hunting and a later retry, while also exposing unarmed preference and delayed reconsideration. The later [complete-meal qualification](verification/embodied-survival.md#complete-jev-only-meals) demonstrates Jev-selected hare/deer harvesting, cooking and eating with real knife/spear/bow outcomes. Individual decisions used two calls, or three when memory selection ran; native equip, movement and execution add none. This establishes working scenarios, not calibrated reliability, novel goal formation or learned activity reuse.

**Selected shared decision extension; implementation remains open:** [Agency deliberation](../archive/07-technical-architecture/agent-agency-runtime.md#25-shared-deliberation-and-prepared-choices) keeps the existing director, typed execution and per-request accounting while supporting different declared decision strategies. Combat's dependent relevance/choice calls are one selected consumer. Stage changes do not create a new refresh or spending allowance; accepted intermediate effects and paid receipts survive later-stage failure. This does not change the current ordinary routing above, force model work for exact human choices or native continuation, or qualify the selected [CTR concurrency change](projects/combat-and-threat-responses-tech-design.md#bounded-urgent-concurrency).

## Current Macrofold routes

Macrofold uses `POST /v1/inferences` with `model_binding`, native provider `input`, `limits` and optional `stream: true`. Jev input is `{state, questions}` with independent named choice/score questions; it is never encoded as a Cartesian product of choices. Inference executes immediately without an admission queue; the current service rejects `Prefer: respond-async`, so neither completed-value nor streaming requests send that header. HTTP 200 supplies a finalized result; HTTP 202 can identify an already admitted invocation awaiting completion or recovery. Both retain the same durable run identity and original request deadline; a capacity refusal never authorizes automatic resubmission. Answers come from `result.inference.value.answers`. Generative input uses native messages and strict JSON Schema. BYOK puts `provider_connection_id` inside `model_binding`; local Jev and generation use the configured saved OpenRouter connection, with no managed fallback.

The server routes level 2 to mini/low, levels 3/4 to complex/low or high, and cleanup to nano. The requested model, reasoning effort and output ceiling are forwarded unchanged: direct Responses bodies are fixture-verified, while Macrofold inference forwarding is established by code inspection only (its `execution` selects Macrofold reasoning parameters; the direct API has no equivalent field). The reflection harness ignores per-request model/effort and uses its configured model and effort. [CG08](limits/cognition.md#cg08) sets independent per-level limits. Reflection uses the full harness with fresh sessions, actor-scoped `mind/*.md` grants and independent PostgreSQL publication; shell and unrelated tools remain denied. Model aliases and actual reported revisions appear in receipts. Per-call monetary/output/time limits remain explicit, and no paid retry occurs automatically.

The retained `NpcReplyPreviews` backend can select a complete validated first speech operation before the remaining decision finishes on its qualified Macrofold fast/complex direct-player route. Its guard requires a preview-eligible job without a decision root; eligible decision-escalation jobs do not use this preview. The player transcript that displayed it was retired on October 16, 2026. This is retained backend capability and historical private-preview evidence, not current multiplayer progressive speech. The request includes `stream: true`; only the deployed direct-inference output channel is decoded. Accepted Run identity is recorded before progress, and its normal terminal inference result supplies final validation and accounting. Interrupted non-replayable delivery withdraws the preview and observes the same accepted Run through existing polling; it never submits another inference. Callback/browser errors cannot interrupt receipt observation. Stream frames are omitted from diagnostic body capture; normal final typed results/receipts remain recorded. [NP05 evidence](verification/npc-reply-preview.md) separates the passing ordinary-route gate from browser and fixture results.

Structured preview parsing uses exact-pinned [`@streamparser/json` 0.0.26](https://github.com/juanjoDiaz/streamparser-json), with partial-token/value emission disabled and only the server-selected first operation assembled. Its incremental parser owns JSON syntax and completion. A small AI-layer wrapper enforces the existing byte/depth/key bounds, rejects decoded duplicate keys and unsupported root fields throughout the document, and rejects nonfinite numbers and lone surrogates. The server still strictly validates the complete speech operation and current recipient/hearing before showing it. No JSON repair, custom alternate parser or additional inference is retained; parser references are released on decoding failure or completion. [NPV01](limits/interface.md#npv01--private-npc-reply-preview) owns the unchanged bounds.

The ordinary Macrofold generation adapter selects `stream: true` when its caller supplies progress observation; other ordinary inference calls await the completed value. Transport progress alone does not establish permitted early speech or an active player presentation. Both forms use the immediate-execution contract above. [PG02’s live repair](verification/cognition-context.md#october-5--unified-experience-and-unprescribed-choices) qualified actual welcome/refusal replies after removing the incompatible header, without a retry loop or model substitution.

Direct Responses generation, full-harness structured calls, invention/workshop explanations and private/background callers retain completed-value delivery and are not qualified by NP05. Ordinary NPC generation currently selects fast/complex inference, including levels 3/4; the full reflection/World Agent harness is not an ordinary NPC speech route. Jev uses its native question protocol; Mike authorized correcting the local decision setting to `typesafe/jev-1.13`, without changing generation models or routes. [NC19](maintainers/narration-and-conversations.md#nc19--progressive-display-of-structured-conversation-replies) retains qualification for its other inventoried callers; the accepted public-speech successor below has its own implementation owner.

`jev-questions.ts` separates relevance, immediate complexity, lasting significance and invention admissibility/mechanism. One attention request contains as many candidate questions as its serialized size budget allows; routing asks route and reflection together; invention asks admissibility and route together. Useful contradictory evidence must survive relevance selection. A provider confidence field is not the same as a selected answer's probability. Invention remains conservatively gated; routing never grants domain authority.

Reported BYOK provider costs are distinct from zero platform model charges. Complete harness usage rows supply token/cost receipts when available; missing or ambiguous billing retains the admitted reserve and can be reconciled later. Inspector reconciliation of current-timeline World Agent messages reads their exact receipt from the accounting repository when reply diagnostics omit it; complete usage and a confirmed terminal Run are still required. The grouped god debugger links actual provider input/output to trigger, recall, route and committed outcome. Live synthetic examples and limitations remain in [TODO](maintainers/TODO.md).

The workspace permissions still select only that profile's tools; a connection grant never broadens the immutable profile or permits unapproved world changes.

## Shared Worker setup and cutover

Native full cognition, reflection and creator conversations require `MACROFOLD_WORKER_ID`, selected by the application/world compute owner. The caller sends that same top-level `worker_id` with each native Run; actor Worktrees and Sessions remain independent. OpenLegend does not create, discover, resume, pause, replace or destroy Workers. A missing setting disables only native execution, not `/v1/inferences`.

```dotenv
MACROFOLD_WORKER_ID=<existing-authorized-worker-uuid>
MACROFOLD_RUN_MAX_USD=0.25
```

Create or select the Worker in Macrofold using a separate owner/admin credential. Inspect `/v1/worker-offerings`, the accepted offering/rates and effective limits before enabling traffic. Configure the trust boundary, isolation, capacity, idle policy, credit and compute-rate ceiling deliberately. Preserve `isolate_runs: true` unless the owner explicitly authorizes mutually trusted sharing; sharing a Worker does not itself require disabling isolation. Use a zero baseline for demand-driven sleep, or an explicitly funded baseline for sustained traffic. See the upstream [Worker guide](https://github.com/Macrofold/Macrofold/blob/feat/worker-economics-autoscaling/docs/features/execution/workers.md) for fields and lifecycle rules.

The runtime key needs `workers:use` for the selected Worker plus its existing Run, Workspace, Session and file permissions. Worker authorization does not grant file/tool authority. The caller does not need `workers:write` or `workers:read`: it neither manages compute nor gates submission on a Worker read. The separate operator needs `workers:read` to inspect and administrative `workers:write` to manage the Worker. Worker-ID restrictions and Workspace restrictions remain independent.

**Worker compute is a separate owner-managed expense.** `AI_BUDGET_USD` continues to admit per-agent calls and conservatively retain unpriced reservations; `MACROFOLD_RUN_MAX_USD` caps a Run, not shared capacity. Neither setting caps the Worker's idle/allocation spend. The removed `MACROFOLD_COMPUTE_MAX_USD` was a finite per-Sandbox allowance, not an hourly Worker ceiling; it is not read or converted. Set Worker limits and account credit controls in Macrofold and inspect compute usage by `worker_id`; do not count shared allocation costs once per actor or refund old holds as zero.

Submit a Run directly to an enabled sleeping Worker, then observe the accepted Run's status/result and queue deadline. Reading a Worker does not wake it. The existing `queue_if_busy: false` prevents same-Worktree follow-ups; it does **not** disable Worker-capacity queuing. Independent Worktrees may share capacity concurrently. A persistent tool-less native conversation retains its Session ID, original billing mode and model together. Continuation uses those admitted facts even after host configuration changes; missing or invalid retained facts refuse continuation before a provider POST, without opening a replacement Session. Full cognition/reflection use fresh Sessions. The current World Agent also receives a fresh remote Session for each paid turn or selected stage, with permitted application continuity; its durable conversation and funding identity persist without reusing remote model history. A changed tool profile additionally selects a fresh Worktree after earlier exposure is reconciled. Follow the [current owner-conversation contract](world-agent-runtime.md#scoped-context-and-native-save-for-review), rather than restoring the earlier retained-Session behavior.

Closing a creator conversation aborts its local wait, durably fences that conversation and cancels only its known Run. Cancellation is checked immediately before dispatch; a cancelled request is not sent and does not consume its reservation. Closure does not abort an already-sent admission request, so a late acceptance returns its Run ID and is cancelled; an admission whose response is lost remains blocked for reconciliation of the original request rather than paid replay. Another actor's Run, the shared Worker and published Worktree/Session state are not destroyed. Server shutdown also performs no Worker lifecycle operation. A manual pause, expiration or destruction needs an explicit owner action; there is no replacement Worker or automatic-execution fallback.

The Worker cutover is delivered, not a migration to repeat for every deployment. For an installation with actual pre-cutover Sandbox allocations or charges, stop those old admissions and reconcile the original uncertain launches and financial obligations through authorized operator tooling before retiring that service. Preserve operational records and verified files/Sessions; a fresh Worker installation has no hypothetical Sandbox migration prerequisite. Deploy the matching Macrofold Worker contract and this caller together, then set the approved Worker ID. [MW04](maintainers/macrofold-worker-api.md#mw04--remaining-deployment-and-qualification-gates) retains real outstanding obligations and deployment qualification without restoring legacy APIs under the development policy. Do not reset worlds, reinterpret saved Sandbox IDs as Worker IDs, clear billing history or run the removed `macrofold-resume-setup.ts` script against the new API. Existing actor provisioning keys and timeline-scoped conversation mappings are unchanged; old Sandbox metadata remains historical and is ignored by native routing.

Changing `MACROFOLD_WORKER_ID` is an explicit operator reconfiguration, not automatic recovery. Drain/reconcile outstanding work before restarting with another target; saved verified Sessions can continue on an authorized compatible Worker. Lost/ambiguous Run requests are still fenced by their original journal/fingerprint, including the originally requested target. The current server supplies one trusted configuration per world; unrelated worlds/customers must not inherit a shared target or relaxed isolation accidentally.

Implementation and remaining live-deployment gates: [MW01–MW04](maintainers/macrofold-worker-api.md). Local HTTP and build evidence: [Worker API cutover](verification/macrofold-worker.md#macrofold-worker-api-cutover).

## Configuration and interface

Construct the client in trusted server code. Read keys from the server environment or a server secret store; never pass them through game requests, browser configuration, saved world state, telemetry, or receipts. Omitting a provider or supplying an empty key returns `unavailable` without dispatching. Configured endpoints require HTTPS; redirects are rejected. Endpoint configuration is privileged, since it determines where credentials and context go.

Direct generation uses an immutable operator-supplied model-price catalogue. The existing `LLM_*_USD_PER_MILLION` fields price only `OPENAI_MODEL`; `OPENAI_MODEL_PRICES_JSON` adds records shaped as `{ "model": "exact-requested-model", "prices": { "inputUsdPerMillion": 1, "outputUsdPerMillion": 2 }, "reportedAliases": ["explicitly-equivalent-reported-model"] }` (the numbers here are illustrative, not vendor rates). Cached-input and cache-write rates are optional fields in `prices`. An identical default-model record may attach aliases and collapses into the existing default entry; conflicting default rates, repeated models/aliases, malformed records and nonfinite/negative rates fail startup. The client copies and freezes the catalogue so later caller mutation cannot change an admitted request.

Embeddings use OpenAI's direct `/v1/embeddings` endpoint independently of Macrofold. Memory indexing, action retrieval and invention search pass the same immutable model-price catalogue to the embedding adapter. Configuration adds a standard `text-embedding-3-small` input rate of **$0.02 per million tokens**, checked **2026-10-04** against [official OpenAI pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/text-embedding-3-small). An explicit `OPENAI_MODEL_PRICES_JSON` record takes precedence over that embedding default; configure other embedding models there with an input rate and zero output rate. A reported alias needs the same explicit ownership as generation; unknown identities, missing/invalid usage and uncertain completion retain exposure. Token usage and estimated cost are captured before vector validation, so an unusable completed result still records its price. Receipts retain OpenAI's request ID when provided. These are configured token estimates, not invoice reconciliation; the ledger rounds each call upward to whole microdollars. [Observed recovery and live check](verification/camp-life.md#embedding-accounting-and-readable-descriptions--october-4-2026).

Jev uses the same priced request allowance for local reservation, decision accounting, interactive preflight and the submitted Macrofold limit. `JEV_CALL_RESERVE_USD` is a minimum, not a replacement for the configured price of the supported 500,000-byte request envelope; Macrofold adds its 1,024-byte conservative input padding. Sum separately rounded per-request microdollars when reserving several steps. The configured Jev price must match the reviewed provider rate; no live price download or automatic paid retry is added. [Rivals evidence](verification/rivals-and-contested-ground.md#jev-request-reservation-correction--october-13-2026) distinguishes admission failures from an unavailable model.

Reservations select the same requested model as settlement. Before a route has chosen its model, the interactive allowance conservatively uses the most expensive configured default/mini/complex/summary model, including cached-input or cache-write premiums. A named decision or maintenance model never borrows the default model's rates. A different provider-reported identity is priced only when explicitly listed as that requested model's alias; otherwise its cost remains unknown and the existing conservative hold applies. No prices are downloaded automatically. [Catalogue and spending limits](limits/ai-execution.md#mp01--configured-model-prices) · [PW01 evidence](verification/level1-decisions.md#pw01-reliability-repairs--october-2-2026).

```ts
import { createAiClient } from '@open-legend/ai';

const ai = createAiClient({
  jev: {
    apiKey: process.env.TYPESAFE_API_KEY ?? '',
    model: 'jev-1.13.0',
    prices: { inputUsdPerMillion: 0.042, outputUsdPerMillion: 0 },
  },
  openai: {
    apiKey: process.env.OPENAI_API_KEY ?? '',
    model: 'gpt-5.6-luna',
    reasoningEffort: 'low',
    prices: {
      inputUsdPerMillion: 0.2,
      cachedInputUsdPerMillion: 0.02,
      cacheWriteInputUsdPerMillion: 0.25,
      outputUsdPerMillion: 1.2,
    },
  },
  timeoutMs: 20_000,
  maxRequestBytes: 28_000,
  maxOutputTokens: 1800,
});
```

These price examples are standard short-context API rates checked **2026-09-19**, not prices embedded in the adapter. Jev lists $0.042 per million input tokens and free output. OpenAI lists Luna input/cached-input/cache-write/output rates of $0.20/$0.02/$0.25/$1.20 per million tokens. Recheck rates when changing providers, models, context limits, endpoints, or processing tiers. [Jev models](https://docs.typesafe.ai/models), [OpenAI API pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing).

Luna is the initial configurable candidate for focused generation; model selection still requires task-specific quality evaluation. The adapter defaults to `gpt-5.6-luna` and Jev `jev-1.13.0`, and sends OpenAI `service_tier: "default"`. Regional/long-context/fast processing charges are not modeled automatically. [Luna model documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-5.6-luna).

Both methods accept `requestId`, optional `signal`, and optional `deadlineMs` as an **absolute Unix millisecond timestamp**. The earlier of that deadline and the client timeout applies, including response-body reading. The direct client defaults to 500,000 request bytes, 262,144 response bytes and 2,048 output tokens; the game explicitly selects 500,000 bytes for both request and response, and 8,192 output tokens. These are transport/output allowances, not exact model-token counts or spending permission ([LA053–LA055](limits/ai-execution.md#la053)). A request-specific output limit cannot exceed its client cap. JSON depth and node limits also apply. There is no provider-side deadline or exactly-once promise: cancellation stops waiting locally but may follow provider completion or billing.

```ts
const judgment = await ai.judge({
  requestId: 'job-123',
  state: { observation: 'A rigid metal helmet covers the target’s head.' },
  questions: {
    route: {
      type: 'choice',
      instructions:
        'Choose the applicable supplied route. Select unknown if the evidence is insufficient.',
      criteria: {
        known: 'An already declared rule fully covers this interaction.',
        novel: 'The interaction needs a new declaration.',
        unknown: 'The supplied evidence is insufficient to decide.',
      },
    },
  },
});

const generated = await ai.generate<{ text: string }>({
  requestId: 'job-124',
  task: 'thought',
  instructions: 'Write one short thought grounded in the supplied observations.',
  context: { observations: ['I am hungry.'] },
  schema: {
    type: 'object',
    properties: { text: { type: 'string' } },
    required: ['text'],
    additionalProperties: false,
  },
});
```

The routing example illustrates transport structure; production context must include declared capabilities and the actual evidence needed for that decision. A helmet description alone cannot establish native rule coverage.

## Provider behavior and limits

### Microsoft Decision 1 and standalone evaluation

The existing Macrofold judgment route also accepts `MACROFOLD_JEV_MODEL=microsoft/microsoft-decision-1`, with `MACROFOLD_BILLING_MODE=managed` or an exact OpenRouter BYOK connection. The existing Jev model remains the default. Both models use native named Noul, Choice and Score questions through `/v1/inferences`; decisions do not create a Worker or conversation. Microsoft currently lists $0.042 per million input tokens, free output and a 32,768-token context window. The provider enforces its tokenizer-specific context limits. [Microsoft model and pricing](https://openrouter.ai/microsoft/microsoft-decision-1), [native Decisions protocol](https://openrouter.ai/docs/api/api-reference/alphadecisions/submit-a-decisions-request).

`createMacrofoldDecisionClient` in `@open-legend/ai` exposes the same native question/answer contract without constructing a game world. Callers supply the exact model, billing mode, per-call USD limit and optional accepted-Run callback; they own durable dispatch journals and aggregate spending admission. Streaming judgments are refused before dispatch. Gameplay and standalone clients share exact terminal identity, completion, accounting and timing interpretation. A terminal inference POST supplies its own usage and billing; acceptance persistence remains awaited, without another Run or organization-billing request. Running or interrupted calls recover through that same Run without another inference submission. Native harness execution retains its separate Run accounting.

Managed inference uses the terminal receipt’s Macrofold charge. BYOK inference uses its frozen-rate retail usage estimate only with complete token usage and non-provisional billing; zero Macrofold charge does not establish free provider execution. `reported_micro_usd` is a usage-based estimate, not an upstream invoice. A separate `providerReportedCostUsd` preserves a supplied upstream amount without claiming final settlement. Usage, charges and provider-reported amounts are decoded independently: an unusable field does not discard a valid answer or separately known evidence. Missing raw-model tokens cannot replace absent embedded usage. Exact-Run recovery also preserves an independently available verified managed Run charge, using the greater usable observation of that same charge rather than adding overlapping observations. Unpriced or uncertain work retains its conservative reservation. Fixture checks establish protocol/accounting behavior, not live model quality or deployment qualification.

### Standalone Luna and ordinary LLM comparison

`createOpenAiDecisionClient` exposes the official direct OpenAI `/v1/decisions` endpoint with `gpt-6-luna`. It translates the game’s Noul probability into a native predicate, supplied choices into native choices, and ordered rubric entries into score levels. It preserves named question identity, raw distributions, fractional scores, absent confidence and distinct refusal/failure/uncertainty. Decoding uses a snapshot of the submitted questions, so a caller changing its pending rubric cannot reinterpret the answer. Explicit cancellation after dispatch remains cancelled with completion uncertain and its cost obligation retained. If one question refuses, the adapter retains the other usable answers and records the exact supplied IDs of the refused questions; an entirely refused batch remains a refusal. Duplicate, missing or foreign question entries remain invalid. Refusal text is not retained. Offered-choice selection uses the greatest supplied probability with offered-order tie handling, as do the other decision adapters. It submits once, bounds the request/body/deadline, and does not execute proposals. [Official Decisions guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/decisions).

Native OpenAI requests reject more than the documented 200 questions before dispatch; this provider limit does not constrain generic Jev requests. Choice distribution entries must name exact offered strings, and score entries must use integer level indices. A complete usable choice distribution owns selection even when its redundant choice echo is absent or inconsistent. Finite predicate ratings are retained like other native ratings; calibration eligibility is evaluated separately. Macrofold snapshots preserve the caller's original offered order for decoding even though wire JSON uses canonical key ordering.

Direct Responses and Decisions share one bounded UTF-8 body reader. Native Decisions also bounds the wait when an injected fetch ignores cancellation and discards a late response. Waiting for an accepted Macrofold Run callback observes the same request deadline; interruption retains that Run identity and uncertain commitment and requests cancellation of that Run. These changes do not retry admission or release spending holds.

`createMacrofoldGenerationClient` shares the standalone exact-Run lifecycle and billing observation with native decisions. It sends a strict JSON-schema generation request to the configured OpenRouter chat model. Generation may opt into `stream: true` through the existing Macrofold stream parser; the caller still receives a complete validated result. The acceptance callback runs once and must succeed before output is delivered. If persistence fails, the client retains the known Run identity and returns uncertainty without another admission. Interrupted delivery observes the same accepted Run without another admission, preserving the original deadline, cancellation and uncertain costs. Native Jev/Microsoft streaming remains unsupported. The benchmark uses the exact `meta/muse-spark-1.3-contributor` model with medium reasoning; contributor selection does not enable account data-sharing settings or silently substitute another route. Direct Luna Responses uses the existing structured-generation client at explicitly selected low/medium/high effort. The direct transport timeout ceiling is now 300 seconds; ordinary game configuration retains its narrower range and existing defaults.

Context/action relevance uses one Noul per candidate with the shared rubric in `state.attentionPolicy`; each short question explicitly references its candidate because question keys are not model input. Noul directly supplies P(yes), without repeated yes/no option descriptions. [Noul documentation](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives/noul).

Jev uses `POST https://api.typesafe.ai/v1/systemone` with bearer authentication and `{model,state,questions}`. Choice returns an option, probability distribution, and confidence; Score returns a rubric position, legend, distribution, and confidence; Noul returns a value between zero and one. Question IDs are bookkeeping, so instructions must carry the complete question. This adapter supports text instructions and string rubric descriptions, a deliberately narrower subset of the provider’s structured-instruction features. [HTTP API reference](https://docs.typesafe.ai/api).

The client requires at least one question but imposes no question-count maximum; Choice admits 2–255 options and Score 2–10 levels. Jev documents a 64k total context and a 32k state-plus-longest-question limit. We conservatively estimate four characters per token and allow 55k total and 28k state-plus-longest-question; this is a heuristic, not tokenizer-exact accounting. Oversize requests fail locally with explicit reasons before dispatch. [Model limits](https://docs.typesafe.ai/models). Score levels start at zero, and interpolation is a rubric position, not a physical constant or calibrated outcome probability. [Score documentation](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives/score).

The decoder reads each requested answer and its required numeric values; missing answers, wrong answer types and nonnumeric/nonfinite values remain invalid because consumers need numbers they can compare. Choice and Score confidence is optional: omission is preserved, while a supplied nonfinite or nonnumeric confidence remains invalid. The game’s existing routing and invention checks that require confidence continue to abstain when it is absent; they retain their 0.55 threshold and never substitute an answer probability. Native choice selection and benchmark scoring do not require that separate confidence field. It does not assert that finite decision values fall within the provider's conventional ranges. Level-1 action selection likewise accepts every finite Noul rating, including values outside 0–1; it still uses the authored selection threshold, offered handles and native admission. Extra answer/score keys are ignored. Choice selects the offered option with the highest returned score, using the first offered option on an exact tie; it does not depend on the provider's separately named choice. Supplied scores are preserved without normalization or a sum-to-one assertion. Score retains the provider's rubric position without recalculating it from the distribution; its legend comes from the supplied rubric rather than requiring the provider to echo those labels. These rules apply to both direct Jev and Macrofold. The generic transport does not impose a universal confidence threshold: callers must evaluate task-specific thresholds and explicit unknown options. An option named `unknown` remains a successful typed value so the domain can distinguish semantic abstention from service failure. Confidence does not prove correctness. [Confidence documentation](https://docs.typesafe.ai/confidence).

OpenAI uses `POST https://api.openai.com/v1/responses` with bearer authentication, `store:false`, no tools or conversation continuation, and strict `text.format` JSON Schema. The caller’s trusted schema must have an object root, require all properties, and use `additionalProperties:false` for every object. Nullable properties and nested `anyOf` branches are supported. Local Ajv validation performs no coercion, default insertion, or mutation. References and remote schema loading are rejected. Only server-authored schemas belong here; bounded schema size does not make arbitrary regular expressions or validators safe to accept from players. [Structured outputs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs).

The decoder scans message output beyond reasoning entries, handles refusals, rejects incomplete/invalid output, then validates generated JSON locally. It does not expose provider error bodies or raw exception text. `store:false` is a request setting, not a promise of zero provider retention; retention requirements must be assessed separately. [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data).

Generation and reflection capture one owned immutable schema and compiled validator before dispatch and retain both through output validation. Judgments similarly retain their exact offered question order and IDs through decoding. Macrofold mutation transport prepares strict canonical JSON once before journal waits and reuses those exact bytes for transmission and request-body hashing. The application operation fingerprint remains a separate identity with its original meaning; prepared bytes grant no execution or spending authority.

## Receipts, outcomes, and accounting

A confirmed Macrofold `run_budget_exhausted` inference reports that the request exceeded its per-call spending limit after existing receipt/usage reconciliation. Its conservative provider admission bound can exceed the expected final bill; configure `JEV_CALL_RESERVE_USD` to cover the intended admitted request size and authorized spending. This does not increase the default reserve, retry a rejected call automatically, or turn uncertain completion into a free failure.

Results are a discriminated union: `{outcome:'value',value,receipt}` or `{outcome,reason,receipt}`. Failure outcomes are `unavailable`, `invalid`, `failed`, `refused`, `cancelled`, and `uncertain`; `unknown` is reserved for adapters with explicit semantic abstention. These adapters preserve Jev’s unknown option instead. Reasons are stable short codes rather than provider messages.

Receipts include request ID, provider, requested and reported model, timestamps, latency, dispatch status, completion uncertainty, context SHA-256, and the actual JSON request SHA-256 when assembled. Hashes are canonical exact-input identifiers; they neither prove equivalent meaning nor prevent stale-state reuse. Macrofold receipts also retain the exact request’s admitted billing mode; later configuration changes cannot reinterpret that original funding. Missing or conflicting mode evidence cannot establish a new price. Receipts contain no prompts or keys. Provider-reported models are recorded as reported, including aliases; absent model metadata leaves `modelVersionStatus:'unavailable'`. Callers should reject incompatible model changes rather than interpret metadata as a guarantee of immutable weights.

Direct OpenAI Decisions and the direct structured-generation client retain sanitized error code/type identifiers and safe scalar rate-limit evidence. They read at most 8 KiB of a failed response body, then discard it; raw provider error messages and bodies are not retained. Successful responses can also report remaining requests/tokens and reset times. Valid `Retry-After` minimum delays are preserved without shortening, while malformed values are omitted. Diagnostics do not change the known HTTP outcome, establish a zero charge or cause a retry. A rejected HTTP request can still have a provider correlation ID; that ID does not prove model invocation. [HTTP diagnostic bounds](limits/ai-execution.md#hd01--bounded-provider-http-diagnostics) · [Official rate-limit header meanings](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/rate-limits#rate-limits-in-headers).

Direct embedding and native Decisions requests send `X-Client-Request-Id` as the lowercase SHA-256 hex digest of the receipt's application request ID. It is reconstructible from the retained receipt even when no response/request ID arrives; hashing avoids sending raw caller identifier text and stays within OpenAI's ASCII/512-character contract. [OpenAI's request-tracing contract](https://developers.openai.com/api/reference/overview#supplying-your-own-request-id-with-x-client-request-id) supports provider investigation with this identifier. Native Decisions also bounds the application identifier and retains only bounded safe provider correlation/model metadata. It supplies correlation, not proof of execution, cancellation or cost. [The interrupted PG01 recovery](verification/camp-life.md#interrupted-embedding-recovery--october-4-2026) distinguishes matching aggregate usage from an exact per-request bill.

Authentication/model unavailability and rate limits return `unavailable`. Rejected request schemas and malformed/oversized results return `invalid`. Transport failure, timeout after dispatch, HTTP 408/most 5xx responses, or unfinished generation can leave completion uncertain. Caller cancellation after dispatch is `cancelled` with `completionUncertain:true`. All provider and Macrofold request/file/stream paths use the shared original operation budget, including the earlier absolute deadline, body reads and required callbacks. A late response is disposed without decoding or delivery. Cleanup of an already accepted Run retains its separate bounded wait. No outcome triggers a retry or fallback inside this package.

The [shared network contract](projects/completed/bounded-network-requests-tech-design.md#81-ai-and-macrofold) makes finite budgets, body/callback waiting and cleanup ownership common across provider/gameplay/tooling paths; [NET04](maintainers/bounded-network-requests.md#net04--migrate-all-aimacrofold-and-diagnostic-consumers) records delivery. Diagnostics observe bounded already-consumed bytes without cloning or independently reading the body; private streams omit optional exchange capture. It preserves required acceptance persistence and the existing service-owned observation of an already-sent authoring admission after conversation closure, so a late accepted Run can be journaled and cancelled exactly once. Ending local waiting never proves execution stopped, restores a paid allowance or authorizes replacement inference.

Token usage is optional and validated as nonnegative integer counters. `estimatedCostUsd` is present only when usable reported usage and explicit rates are available. Cached and cache-write tokens are subsets of input tokens; reasoning tokens are already part of output tokens. Ordinary input rate is a conservative fallback for an omitted cached-input rate, but reported writes require an explicit write rate. Missing/invalid usage or prices means **unpriced**, never zero cost.

The game server keeps these outcomes distinct in job results and god-mode traces: a decision job that stops on a provider result records `refused`, `unavailable`, `invalid`, `failed` or `cancelled`, reports `uncertain` whenever the receipt marks completion uncertain (even if an adapter labelled it `failed`), and records `budget-exhausted` when a spending reservation or per-level decision limit refuses the request. Generation that is disabled or unconfigured is `unavailable`, never a substituted model or harness. A generated response that fails the director's schema, envelope or visible-size validation is `invalid` and is rejected without a paid repair request.

The server must reserve a conservative upper bound before dispatch. When usage is unavailable or completion is uncertain, its spending hold is at least that reservation and any larger usable known same-attempt charge. A weaker later observation cannot reduce that uncertain hold; verified completion still follows the existing final reconciliation rules. The floating-point estimate is not a billing ledger; the server stores conservative integer microdollars and reconciles invoices separately. Reject untrusted requests before reserving expensive work, but do not release a dispatched reservation merely because its response failed validation.

Macrofold judgment, short/native generation and reflection use the same final outcome meanings. Verified admission rejection is unavailable (credentials/model/rate/credit) or invalid (body/schema/size), with no model dispatch. Generic network errors and arbitrary 409/5xx retain possible dispatch. The original journaled admission cannot be resubmitted automatically, including after rejection; explicit continuation uses its existing new request identity. Stopping that same Run is an idempotent control operation: a rejected cancellation can be requested again without creating another generation or spending admission. Caller cancellation and deadlines retain their first cause: a pre-dispatch deadline is unavailable, a possible-dispatch deadline is uncertain, and a confirmed ended timeout is failed. Completed malformed output is invalid independently of whether its bill is known. A terminal Run identity and supported, correctly typed inference outcome are required before its output or cost can be trusted; native execution also requires its matching execution outcome. Recovery applies the same completion rule as original execution. A price alone cannot prove completion, and explicit provider uncertainty retains the hold even after pricing arrives. Malformed UTF-8, excessive JSON nesting and oversized bodies are rejected through the bounded transport; cancellation also stops a stalled body read.

Immediate-inference receipt recovery validates the original Run identity, inference kind, final result and provider outcome before using embedded accounting. Missing, provisional or malformed evidence remains unknown; another billing report does not repair the returned receipt. Current Run charge evidence can independently protect managed recovery when embedded billing is unavailable. Native harness execution still requires complete exact-Run model-usage evidence, including attributable platform charges, under its existing itemized accounting contract. Historical [PG01 evidence](verification/camp-life.md#live-attempt-and-accounting-repair) and [delayed-billing checks](verification/camp-life.md#delayed-billing-review--october-4-2026) retain their original scope; the current shared inference path is qualified in the [efficiency follow-through report](verification/macrofold-efficiency-followup.md).

Costs use one checked conversion meaning across provider normalization, reservations, settlement and decision diagnostics. Nonnegative USD numbers round their represented decimal value upward to integer microdollars without binary-multiplication artifacts. Provider microdollar strings require decimal digits, safe magnitude and lossless recovery through the public USD number. Explicit zero is valid; blanks, signs, exponent/fraction strings, negative/nonfinite USD and unsafe sums are unpriced. Provider-reported amounts remain separate from usage-based estimates. Immediate inference consumes its own embedded settlement facts; native harness accounting aggregates complete exact-Run usage without treating the zero BYOK platform model charge as free provider execution. Incomplete, truncated or wrong-Run native usage cannot produce an aggregate charge. Every model row validates input, output and cache-token subsets before checked aggregation. Known independent charges and provider-reported evidence survive missing fields in later exact-Run reconciliation. Repeated usable observations of the same charge retain the larger amount rather than adding overlapping bills or erasing known spending with a smaller later report. Native completion, authoring recovery and inspector recovery share that interpretation and skip itemized reconciliation when contradictory terminal evidence prevents settlement.

The authoritative ledger independently normalizes cost, binds late evidence to the original attempt and known external request, refuses to retract recorded possible dispatch, and preserves final priced receipts. A completed invalid/refused/failed request settles its usable price; missing/invalid billing or uncertain execution retains at least the full admitted reserve and any larger known charge. Durable spending and character-decision summaries share this calculation; it does not declare uncertain execution complete or replace the existing per-level reservation policy. Later authoritative pricing reconciles that same attempt, including a charge above its estimate, only after original-Run completion is established; receipts with known price but uncertain execution can also be checked for later completion. Gameplay restore does not rewind obligations. Monthly admission honors the configured $0–$100 range ($50 default), while episode/decision limits and background interactive headroom remain effective. Required-context overflow reports `context-exceeded` before further dispatch without truncating accepted identity or required evidence; completed earlier preparation keeps its own receipts. [NP01 evidence](verification/level1-decisions.md#np01-reliable-ai-outcomes-and-spending--october-2-2026) is synthetic execution/accounting evidence, not live model quality or deployment qualification.

## Decision escalation

Implemented on October 17, 2026, **enabled by default** at Mike’s explicit request. Without `DECISION_ESCALATION_JSON`, `apply` permits the configured primary decision origin and the existing recovery reasons. An unresolved character decision may receive **one direct OpenAI Responses request to `gpt-6-luna`, low effort**. Ordinary generation may still use Macrofold. Standard short-context Luna rates are included when no operator price catalogue is supplied ([official pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing), checked October 17, 2026). An explicit catalogue remains authoritative. Missing direct credentials, missing exact-model prices in an explicit catalogue, `AI_JEV_ONLY`, an ineligible origin/reason, or insufficient existing allowance prevents dispatch. No model or billing substitution follows a refusal. [Feature specification](projects/decision-escalation-feature-spec.md) · [technical design](projects/decision-escalation-tech-design.md) · [delivery and qualification](maintainers/cognition-redesign.md#decision-escalation).

This is the narrow server-policy exception to the ordinary prohibition on automatic paid repair. Every transport still submits once. `off` preserves ordinary routes; `observe` funds an answer for private inspection with zero gameplay effects; `apply` submits its proposal through current game admission. Neither a world-authored request nor player/model prose enables spending. The default changed from off to apply on October 17 at Mike’s request; broader release-quality and dense-workload qualification remain separate, unmet gates. An explicit off setting still disables fallback.

An explicit configuration overrides the default, including its exact eligible origin strings, such as `macrofold/typesafe/jev-1.13`, and enabled reasons: `semantic-escalation`, `abstained`, `native-rejection`, `invalid-output`, `route-unavailable`. These mean deeper reasoning requested by the existing selection policy, no suitable answer, a concretely rejected operation, unusable completed output, and definitive primary-only route limits. Refusals, shared funding failure, stale evidence, native protection and execution saturation do not become another paid request. A legal but personally poor answer can remain undetected; passing native checks is not proof of good judgment.

The immediate choice has a one-second budget from intake. Native behavior and compatible existing actions continue when a choice is deferred. Waiting for Luna holds neither the simulation nor a world transaction; other characters and permitted player commands can proceed. A still-useful late original answer may commit once before its original deadline; it does not trigger Luna merely for being slow. Useful-later work lasts at most 31 seconds from intake. A higher request must start within two seconds of its eligibility and finish publication within 30 seconds of that eligibility, bounded by the original deadline. Both elapsed-time and wall-clock fences apply; a simulation-time boundary may expire sooner. [DE-L01–DE-L04](limits/ai-execution.md#decision-escalation-limits) own exact limits.

`CognitionExecution` shares one background execution across reconsideration, paid maintenance and indexing, separately from one primary execution. Sixteen waiting reconsiderations retain identifiers and deadlines, without copied prompts. Decision and maintenance classes alternate; decisions use trusted urgency, eligibility time and stable ID. Primary saturation defers new opportunities rather than launching a second slow primary or using Luna to bypass capacity. Unknown execution retains its lease across restart; known completed work with missing pricing frees execution capacity while keeping its monetary hold.

If startup cannot reconstruct occupied executions from storage, this pool closes and cancels queued admission. It never treats missing accounting as free capacity or repeatedly schedules the failed read. Callers retain the original storage failure; a corrected restart must reconstruct capacity before dispatch can resume.

One operational root records independent answer groups and one higher-attempt entitlement. Existing monetary attempts remain the only financial ledger; `cognition_attempt_links` adds nonfinancial ownership and execution-class information. The world transaction commits native effects, operation receipts, group outcomes and the original trigger watermark together. A partially committed response group closes; its successful speech, action or feeling cannot be regenerated. Independently declared open questions may still use the one remaining entitlement. Duplicate callbacks lose their publication claim without pausing the world as a storage failure.

Fallback preparation uses current permitted native/cached evidence with **no paid embeddings, attention, summaries, grounding or compaction**. One unpaid refresh of changed source preparation is allowed before reservation, inside the original deadlines; subsequent change ends unresolved. Queued and active work is fenced by actor life/control, timeline/generation, authority, policy, intent and source proofs. Pause, shutdown and restore end optional gameplay publication. Restart ends all unfinished roots and reconciles accounting only; it never replays paid calls or publishes an old answer.
