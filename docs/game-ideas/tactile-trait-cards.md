# Tactile trait cards

**Status:** Idea captured for exploration; mechanics, interface and visual treatment need design. [Follow-up: ND04](../maintainers/needs-design.md#nd04--experience-shaped-personality-and-practical-skill-growth).

## The appeal

Make discovering something meaningful about a character feel like receiving an object worth keeping and inspecting. A trait could have a dimensional card with a distinctive frame, layered illustration or small sculptural detail, convincing lighting and a surface that catches the light when tilted or turned.

The reference is [OPERATIVES: Revolve](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4286480/OPERATIVES_Revolve_Demo/). Mike describes cards with three-dimensional structure that rotate in play, with compelling lighting, shine and holographic effects. The attached image was unavailable for inspection; these visual preferences come from his description. The [developer's explanation](https://enigma-equation.itch.io/operatives-revolve/devlog/1178108/demo-launched) explicitly connects physical-feeling 3D cards with timing, weight, anticipation and rendering.

The aim is tactile pleasure, recognition and attachment: the player wants to turn the card over and keep looking. A small playable comparison should test whether that attention adds enjoyment while keeping ordinary play fluent.

## What the player could inspect

| Surface           | Proposed purpose                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Front             | Name, distinctive visual identity, concise description and any actual supported effect; distinguish a personality tendency from a learned ability or bodily condition |
| Reverse           | A short personal story, relevant known events or authored lore that explains why this card matters to this particular character                                       |
| Close inspection  | Optional tilt, rotation and flip that reveal depth, material, engraving, wear or a restrained foil-like finish                                                        |
| Meaningful change | A brief reveal or visual evolution when the underlying character change merits attention, followed by ordinary usable inspection                                      |

The reverse is particularly valuable: two characters may share a trait while having different reasons to care about it. A card might connect a change in outlook to remembered choices and relationships, while distinguishing the character's interpretation from what actually happened.

Keep the current trait and its history distinct. A trait that changes or no longer applies should not remain displayed as an active effect merely because its original card is attractive. Its remembered significance can remain available through the existing history presentation.

## Make the appeal serve the game

Use [Open Legend's art direction](../../art-direction/final-board/creative-brief.md): grounded form, deliberate pixel treatment, meaningful materials and beautiful light. Foil, iridescence, relief, etched metal, worn leather or translucent layers are candidate treatments; the reference does not select one universal science-fiction look.

Make the front readable without rotation, and make flipping available through ordinary keyboard and pointer controls. A reduced-motion or simpler rendering should preserve the same information. Rich reveals can be brief, skippable and revisitable; routine inspection should stay fast. A painful change deserves appropriate tone rather than automatic celebratory effects.

The card presents the underlying character; it is not another source of traits or effects. Reuse the [character-authorship boundary](../projects/compelling-characters-feature-spec.md#character-authorship-and-changing-personality), [experience-shaped character proposal](../projects/experience-shaped-characters-feature-spec.md), [trait repertoire](../repertoires/traits.md) and [knowledge/privacy contract](../knowledge.md). Do not turn every incident into a permanent personality reward or make a disposition dictate the next choice. Custom lore must preserve its origin and permitted evidence.

## Related possibilities

- **A personal visual history:** a changing inscription, material detail or small illustration recalls a significant known event. The same trait can feel personal without acquiring additional power.
- **Meaningful keepsakes:** use related inspection treatment for a gift, first successful craft or repaired favorite object, including ordinary objects with strong personal significance. The [special-item cabinet](special-item-cabinet.md) develops this into player-chosen account keepsakes with personal notes and remembered moments.
- **A reveal with a story:** briefly connect a newly recognized change to its relevant moment, then let the player inspect or continue. A card can prompt recollection without replaying or inventing private events.

The [relic idea](official-world-relics.md) applies related presentation to account collectibles. Trading belongs to that separate proposal; a character-earned trait card does not make personality tradable. Repertoire examples and a small visual/usability comparison remain in ND04.
