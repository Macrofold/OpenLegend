# Showing the world's actual state

**Status:** unscheduled presentation ideas surfaced by the [art workflow](../projects/art-workflow-feature-spec.md) review of the repertoires (October 31, 2026). They are not selected designs. Follow-up owners: [world presentation](../world-presentation.md), [V3D06 state composition](../maintainers/3d-pixel-art.md#v3d06--nonhuman-and-state-composition-proof) and the animal behaviour owners.

## The player promise

The creative brief's living place has visible causes: a world worth watching because what you see is what actually happened. The art workflow already delivers each family's recorded states (intact or damaged, full or depleted, lit or cold). These three ideas would let the renderer show more of the simulation's real state without new mechanics and without painting facts the game does not have.

## Wetness you can see

Weather cloth, the outer face of the reed-mat roof, exposed prepared fibre and the contents of open baskets already track dry, damp, wet and saturated ([editable shelters](../worlds/base/editable-shelters.md)). The state appears only as text today. A material darkening driven by that existing moisture value, applied only to materials that track it, would make shelter visibly matter: a covered basket stays pale, an exposed one darkens. No new assets are needed; it is a presentation binding.

## Camp stock drawn from real items

Ground piles already draw a bounded number of representative marks ([items](../worlds/base/items.md)). Extending that to poles, fibre bundles, baskets and the camp's stored stock, drawn from the actual items, lets a returning player see their session's work at a glance. Hidden contents stay hidden; only what an observer can see is drawn.

## Animals living their actual lives

The game already runs grazing, alert, flee and settle reactions, wolves eating real remains and regrouping, and bears foraging ([roaming](../worlds/base/first-playable-roaming.md), [animal responses](../worlds/base/animal-responses.md)). Clips made through the art workflow, bound to those committed behaviours, would put that ecology on screen. They would show what the animal is doing, never decide it.

## Boundaries

- Art shows committed facts only: no wetness, wear or wound the simulation does not record.
- These are presentation bindings to existing values, not new simulation.
- Keep each one bounded and observer-scoped, like the existing ground piles.
