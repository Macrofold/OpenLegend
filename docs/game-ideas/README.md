# Game ideas

A home for promising gameplay and presentation ideas before they become selected designs. Each note preserves the attraction, concrete possibilities and relevant existing boundaries. These notes do not claim implemented features or change the current delivery order.

The narrative section owns [authored worlds and personal discovery](../narrative/authored-worlds-and-personal-discovery.md): authored lore encountered in different orders, bards spreading player legends and world news, gods guiding personal progression, region gates and dangerous frontiers, procedural caves and dungeons, mysterious people and meaningful needs. [Gods, death and personal mythology](../narrative/gods-death-and-personal-mythology.md) develops changing divine relationships, gifts after death, original personalities, religions and a personal Beyond. The reference studies distinguish observations of Trailblazers and Curse of Resthaven from OpenLegend's proposed mechanics.

The [repertoire library](../repertoires/README.md) owns developed content examples; the [design register](../maintainers/needs-design.md) owns outstanding tasks. When an idea becomes a selected project, its feature/technical design and affected specifications own the resulting behavior. Keep this index and the original note linked to that owner rather than maintaining a second specification.
