# Game ideas

A home for promising gameplay and presentation ideas before they become selected designs. Each note preserves the attraction, concrete possibilities and relevant existing boundaries. These notes do not claim implemented features or change the current delivery order.

| Idea                                                      | Why it might be worth exploring                                                                                   | Follow-up owner                                                                                       |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Tactile trait cards](tactile-trait-cards.md)             | Make character development satisfying to inspect, with a readable explanation and a personal story on the reverse | [ND04](../maintainers/needs-design.md#nd04--experience-shaped-personality-and-practical-skill-growth) |
| [Relics across official worlds](official-world-relics.md) | Give exploration lasting, usable collectibles that can accompany new characters and be traded between players     | [DG29](../maintainers/needs-design.md#dg29--more-participants-and-travel-between-worlds)              |

The narrative section owns [authored worlds and personal discovery](../narrative/authored-worlds-and-personal-discovery.md): authored lore encountered in different orders, gods guiding personal progression, region gates and dangerous frontiers, procedural caves and dungeons, mysterious people and meaningful needs. It also records related ideas inspired by Trailblazers: Into the March.

The [repertoire library](../repertoires/README.md) owns developed content examples; the [design register](../maintainers/needs-design.md) owns outstanding tasks. When an idea becomes a selected project, its feature/technical design and affected specifications own the resulting behavior. Keep this index and the original note linked to that owner rather than maintaining a second specification.
