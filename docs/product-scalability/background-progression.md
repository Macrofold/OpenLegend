# Background progression and changes of detail

**Status: accepted product direction with proposed execution mechanisms. Existing elapsed-time integration is not evidence that this complete background system is implemented.** [Simulation time](../simulation-time.md), [agency](../agent-agency.md), [performance](../performance.md), and the [technical design](../projects/product-scalability-tech-design.md) retain their respective owners.

## 1. What low-power progression promises

An unattended community can continue lives, projects, journeys, commitments, and selected new developments without executing every footstep or continuously reconsidering every goal. Its coarse outcomes need not equal a hypothetical detailed simulation. They must be legal and coherent outcomes of the admitted world rules, consistent with already-established history and actual permissions.

Low-power mode may intentionally reduce the density of new social encounters, inventions, optional excursions, reflection, and narrative developments per unit of time. This is a world-policy choice, not a claim of exact equivalence. The world may feel quieter while humans are absent. Explain that policy instead of promising uninterrupted maximum autonomous activity.

Do not accumulate missed optional initiatives as debt. A conversation never started is not overdue when someone enters. Actual obligations, current work, deadlines, danger, and accepted promises retain their declared handling. Lack of budget is not proof that a character deliberately chose silence or that an outstanding obligation no longer exists.

## 2. Four independent dimensions

| Dimension         | Detailed behavior                                    | Possible background behavior                                                                         | What remains authoritative                                                    |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mechanics         | Fine movement, collision, tool use, local encounters | Route progress, finite work stages, supported elapsed integration or declared approximate resolution | Identity, resources, bodily state, admitted effects, progress and obligations |
| Cognition         | Prompt replies and relevant reconsideration          | Continued plans, fewer optional initiatives, bounded significant decisions                           | Character perspective, values, chosen aims and actual evidence                |
| Social resolution | Individual utterances and decisions                  | Completed scene beats, structured agreements, gist with selected exact lines                         | Participants, communicated information and admitted consequences              |
| Representation    | Nearby detail and frequent permitted updates         | Sparse presentation or no active client representation                                               | No invented poses that contradict physical support or committed actions       |

A visible blacksmith may need detailed movement without fresh generation. An unseen queen may need high-quality deliberation while remaining physically inexpensive. Treating all four dimensions as one distance-based switch would waste work and damage consistency.

## 3. Continuing a character's actual choices

A continuing activity should retain the objective, selected method, current stage, necessary resources, progress, and supported interruption conditions. Its provenance identifies whether it came from a character's choice, learned habit, installed controller, or explicit player instruction. These are not interchangeable.

Ada may choose to repair a roof, fetch missing material from a known workshop, and reconsider after completion. Executing those admitted steps requires no new decision at each movement. Abandoning the work to join a militia or deciding to punish the house owner is a new intention, not an optimization of roof repair.

Use the existing goal/plan/action owners rather than creating another independently writable biography or activity queue. Maintain bounded next steps, not a predicted lifelong script. A proposed branch cannot assert another person's consent, a successful attack, or access to a resource that is not yet available.

Ordinary survival must remain viable at the selected activity rate. If feeding requires repeated new decisions, lowering cognition alone can cause starvation. Provide sufficiently useful continuation through chosen methods and supported policies, or expose the configuration as unsupported. Do not secretly install an omniscient universal survival planner to disguise that mismatch.

## 4. The roof and the invader

An expected completion is conditional, not uninterruptible.

| Time in this illustrative scenario | Occurrence and required behavior                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 10:00                              | Ada chooses to repair a roof. Work is expected to finish at 10:30 if its assumptions remain true.                                                                                           |
| 10:07                              | An enemy reaches town. This is world state, not automatic awareness for Ada.                                                                                                                |
| 10:07:05                           | A guard sounds an alarm that Ada can hear under the actual sensory policy.                                                                                                                  |
| At the alarm                       | Bring relevant work and bodies through that occurrence time; preserve completed repair work and resources. Record Ada's permitted evidence.                                                 |
| Following perception               | Continue an applicable chosen contingency, interrupt through supported native policy, or offer a new scoped decision. Do not grant knowledge of an unseen enemy's precise identity or plan. |

A coarse journey can schedule reaching the gate or an encounter boundary without simulating every footstep. The encounter can generate the alarm. Native or coarse detection still has to exist; a calendar reminder alone cannot discover an intruder.

The saving is the absence of repeated questions during unchanged work. Meaningful stimuli can still be expensive when many things actually happen. Event-driven scheduling is not a solution to unbounded causal activity; bounded attention, scene resolution, and activity policy supply additional controls.

Predicted completion at 10:30 cannot be committed before an event at 10:07 that could still affect it. Resolve coupled participants through a common occurrence boundary. Fast-forward means economical resolution of elapsed time, not irreversible speculation ahead of live commands. Unsupported timing/coupling needs conservative handling rather than a claimed infinite safe interval.

## 5. The available execution methods

### Exact or bounded elapsed integration

For supported constant rates, staged work, and known deadlines, calculate progress until the next relevant change. Respect rate changes, depletion, capacity, geometry, status, shared demand, and intervention. Some families can be exact; others have declared numerical/fidelity bounds. Do not describe sampled perception as exact continuous exposure.

### Coarse activities

Travel retains a route segment, progress, possessions, relevant bodily needs, encounters, and arrival conditions. When detail becomes necessary, the character appears at a valid current place on that journey, not a convenient spawn that contradicts earlier observations.

Construction retains a chosen design, footprint, admitted materials, workers, completed stages, and remaining work. A partly built house needs a valid physical representation. A scalar percentage is insufficient where players can enter unfinished rooms or remove a particular wall. The construction family owns the mapping and must account for shared material use and interruption.

Trade and production cannot quietly bypass inventories, ownership, scarcity, labor, or supported exceptional sources. A coarse transaction still transfers actual supported quantities through the normal owners.

### Structured scenes

Use bounded social/encounter resolution for selected developments, not a whole-world story generator invoked each minute. A scene produces admitted completed beats and perspective-correct memories. The [attention and scene design](attention-and-scenes.md) owns exact versus gist evidence and arrival during an unfinished scene.

### Low-power initiative

Reduce optional starts and reconsideration frequency while continuing valid work and retaining necessary reactions. This intentionally changes activity density. Do not translate every simulated day into a mandatory inference quota or a never-ending maintenance queue.

## 6. Generality for invented mechanics

Ordinary creators should not define a second version of every item or recipe. Supported definitions inherit background handling from reusable trusted mechanical families. A cart can inherit travel, load, and resource behavior; a recipe can inherit work stages and admitted transfers.

Each computational family must explain its clock, dependencies, progress boundaries, invalidators, scope, supported approximation, interruption, and detailed materialization. Composition must check interactions. Two individually cheap processes can become expensive when they share scarce supplies or repeatedly trigger one another.

A genuinely new capability may lack a cheap coarse executor. Explicit options are funded detailed execution, a reviewed authored approximation, restricted unattended use, or refusal to admit the unsupported configuration. Explain the restriction before a creator relies on it. Neither free prose nor a powerful model proves arbitrary new physics can be summarized safely.

This is not a requirement for a second health system or parallel universe. There is one canonical body and resource state, with admitted operations supplying different valid ways to advance it. Supported fantasy creation remains possible through explicit source operations; attribution and permission do not disappear in coarse mode.

## 7. Where detail is required

Named towns are not the unit that must all become detailed when one person enters. Use a bounded area of actual interaction, a preparation zone for impending interaction, and ongoing background execution elsewhere. Spatial cells are a useful implementation tool but interaction groups may span them.

Preparation should consider maximum relevant closing speed, effect reach, current paths, teleport rules, and measured preparation time. A heuristic is interaction reach plus closing speed multiplied by preparation duration, with a safety margin. It is not a universal geometric formula. A fixed three-to-five-times sight radius is not accepted: on flat terrain that expands area nine-to-twenty-five times, and sight alone misses long-range effects or overactivates distant scenery.

Seeing a distant tower need not wake every mind inside it. Being able to fire through a window may require additional physical preparation. Telepathy, sound, projectiles, remote resource control, and supported invented effects need declared interaction scope rather than exceptions hidden in a visual-distance switch.

## 8. Entering detailed play

Resolve background state to a coherent arrival boundary, materialize the current activity, validate physical placement and ongoing effects, and let detailed execution continue. Do not replay hours of fine motion. Do not invent completed future events after the player has gained a right to intervene.

Reuse preparations for overlapping players. One player leaving does not demote an area still required by another. An NPC approaching from outside can also demand finer interaction; humans are not the only possible coupling source.

For teleportation, reserve destination capacity and prepare a valid arrival before committing the move and its accepted costs. On failure retain a defined source-side state or the world's explicitly admitted failure outcome. Never duplicate the traveler, charge successful travel for a failed admission, or strand an account between owners. Operational handoff details are outside this suite.

Preparation delays are technical waiting, not fictional hunger or secretly elapsed combat. Where a persistent destination continues while being prepared, validate against the actual final arrival boundary and avoid presenting an earlier prepared snapshot as current truth.

## 9. Leaving detailed play

Demote only when the removed detail is no longer necessary and the coarse continuation is valid. Use residency/hysteresis to prevent border thrashing. Retain work, resources, witnessed events, obligations, action outcomes, and existing conflicts. Never treat departure as implicit completion, cancellation, rescue, or consent.

Random choices belong to stable occurrences, not to loading callbacks. Reentering cannot reroll an encounter or restore consumed resources. Irreversible completed scene beats stay committed. An ongoing encounter can continue coarsely under its declared rules, including after dangerous logout; the [participation policy](participation-and-protection.md) bounds human absentee control.

## 10. Protection and fairness at the seam

The protected-background policy applies to the state detailed play will inherit. Avoid a coarse result that technically leaves Ada alive but surrounded by wolves with no meaningful chance to survive once observed. Use allowed safe outcomes or reject the proposed background development under the world's supported protections.

Already-active conflict is different. Leaving an area cannot remove attackers or make a player immortal. Fidelity changes must not provide systematic risk, loot, resource, or scheduling advantages. Exact microscopic equality is not required, but compare distributions and player incentives. If walking away reliably makes a battle easier, the design fails even if every isolated result looks plausible.

## 11. Evidence to obtain before broad rollout

Start with a small community containing travel, work, resources, an interrupting alarm, a scene, and a protected home. Enter and leave midactivity; add a second player; teleport; restore; change a resource or geometry dependency. Deliberately cycle modes and attempt duplicate outcomes.

Measure background cost without humans, cold preparation, interaction latency, unresolved obligations, required cognition, and perceived continuity. A cheap background system that becomes an unbounded catch-up burst is not scalable. A fast system that makes residents inconsistent or inert is not a successful game. Delivery is tracked in [product scalability](../maintainers/product-scalability.md), not inferred from this specification.
