# Open Legend UI system

## Whole-interface hierarchy

The world remains the main view. The upper-left character summary names the controlled character and current location, shows the installed world's condition attributes and participation state, and opens Character at the requested condition or current work. Current work is shown from the server's `hasWork` projection, including queued or paused work; a retained completed activity alone does not create a current-work strip. Save failures remain visible with the permitted recovery route.

The left launcher rail contains **Inventory**, **Character**, **Journal** and **Conversation**. The right rail contains **In view**, **Crafting** and **Create**. The labeled **Game** entry provides return to play, live-save status, **Checkpoints** when permitted, **Settings & help**, **Intelligence & allowance**, creation, authorized diagnostics, the separate **World operations** page when permitted, and Log Out. Create retains the ordinary invention workshop and adds the owner conversation only with its existing authority. Moving an entry does not grant its capability.

Journal and World Events use wider reading panels with an explicit expand control. Journal distinguishes **Promises**, **Beginnings** and **Your story** and links to **World Events · what you perceived**. Character uses **Condition**, **Capabilities**, **Equipped** and **Record**; the equipped item and authored meters come from the current native model. There is no universal body-slot, progression, map or turn-budget model in these panels. [The UI brief](../../../../docs/ui-design-brief.md) owns the interaction direction; the [whole-interface project](../../../../docs/projects/game-interaction-redesign-feature-spec.md) and [coverage map](../../../../docs/ui-ux/interface-coverage.md) distinguish delivered surfaces from remaining acceptance.

Settings & help is a wide retained workspace with **Appearance & reading**, **Camera & graphics**, **Controls & help**, and authorized **World permissions** tabs. Inactive tabs keep their local selection and drafts while their content is hidden from interaction. Device preferences explain that they apply immediately; world permissions keep their separate native authority and save behavior. The rendering-rate display is optional and off by default.

## Contextual actions and retained tasks

Routine native actions stay with the relevant object, belonging, recipe, Character capability or shortcut. **Describe an action** opens a focused optional task with the actor, exact named subject, original words and explicit commitment. It does not expose target/item/tool/recipient/amount fields as a default worksheet. Choosing a subject temporarily hides the open panels to expose the scene; a world click or the keyboard-accessible **In view** list binds that exact perceived subject without sending. Cancel returns to the prior task and subject. Losing sight of a selected subject retains its name and explains why it must be changed or removed.

The action task keeps its primary action and Cancel beside the possible interpretation-allowance notice, while its longer fields scroll. Scheduling choices appear only when actual work exists and distinguish queueing, pausing to resume, and replacement. **Stop all work** follows native cancellation availability and explains that paused work is also discarded. Cancel leaves the words available; it does not claim to stop an already submitted request.

Keep private task identity separate from fresh permission to read or act. The server's private-draft scope, world, timeline and character identify retained drafts and supported task selections through an allowed same-owner Resume. Current access/control scope still fences requests and asynchronous replies; readers use it to refresh permitted data and reject old completions. A changed private owner/session/grant, character or timeline invalidates private work. Hiding a panel, changing a reading tab or regaining the same permitted character is not a new owner. Never read earlier unscoped drafts as a compatibility path. [The Resume contract](../../../../docs/projects/completed/tab-resume-feature-spec.md) and [current architecture](../../../../docs/architecture.md) own the authority and persistence details.

## World presentation ownership

[World presentation](../../../../docs/world-presentation.md) owns the accepted continuous right-drag orbit/pitch, Shift + right-drag or middle-drag pan, mixed sprite/mesh depth, default illumination, projected shadows and configurable local character-visible read-through. These are presentation settings, not extra bodily perception. Follow that owner where older camera-binding or unlit-art directions conflict; SW18 retains visual qualification and further art work.

## Camera and spatial interaction

The [spatial owner](../../../../docs/spatial-world.md#picking-and-controls) defines meanings rather than another camera schema here. The compact camera toolbar uses focusable icon controls with hover/focus labels, a follow toggle beside recenter and a shortcut help icon; Shift + right-drag or middle-drag pans, right-drag orbits/tilts, wheel zooms, and focused-canvas arrows/Page Up/Page Down/Home control the view. P pauses/resumes the world; Shift + ] and Shift + [ step faster/slower through the same 0.5×, 1×, 3× and 8× choices as the time buttons. Projection switches through its camera button. Time shortcuts ignore held-key repetition, typing, composition, popups and disconnected states; pause/resume remains blocked during maintenance; a speed change never resumes a paused world. Pointer capture ends without action after a drag. A level selection is not a movement command. The small renderer interface is plain TypeScript; PlayCanvas types stay in scene/art implementation. Multi-touch pinch and assistive-device qualification remain SW09/SW16 work.

Independent **Sight** and **Hearing** toggles join the floor-selection row, with eye/ear icons, pressed states, hover/focus names and solid/dashed theme accents. Thin connected outlines and very faint area tints show the displayed reach; hover labels use short sight/speech wording. [Range guides](../../../../docs/spatial-world.md#perception-range-guides) own meaning and display approximations. The camera toolbar and speech-caption layout share the existing measured HUD exclusion area; desktop right docks reserve the stack, while open narrow sheets expose only the two range controls in a separate bottom strip. Tight sheets use the remaining viewport reading area. The app root clips overflow without programmatic scrolling when a descendant receives focus; panel/text scrollers retain their own scrolling. The scene's range hint is below world labels/HUD, pointer-transparent and subordinate to object picking.

Closed narrow views place camera controls and quick actions in one bounded lower region. The region scrolls vertically when necessary, and the shortcut row scrolls horizontally with room for visible keyboard focus. Shortcut configuration and exact-source review stay in that region; their child lists and plain tooltips use the existing portals. Subject picking collapses hidden quick controls, and caption avoidance measures the visible region while omitting hidden HUD elements. Desktop positioning and the open-sheet range strip retain their existing owners. [HC01](../../../../docs/limits/interface.md#hc01--narrow-hud-controls) records the bounds and observed scope.

This is the production port of the supplied September 20 design system. React owns the HUD and world-status markup. React Aria provides buttons, toolbars, radio groups, conversation tabs and delayed tooltips. PlayCanvas still renders the world and supplies screen coordinates; it never authorizes actions.

When enabled in **Settings & help → Camera & graphics**, the bottom-left FPS counter counts actual PlayCanvas render passes against monotonic real elapsed time, sampling about once per second. It is a local preference, off by default. Foreground stalls lower the measured rate; hidden-tab time is discarded and return starts a fresh sample. It measures application rendering throughput, not GPU execution time or a guarantee of monitor presentation. The small React counter updates independently of the full HUD and does not intercept world input.

- `components.tsx`: typed shared controls, semantic icons, meters, rows, panels and explanations.
- `components.css`: supplied theme/component styling, with preview-only landscape removed.
- `layout.css`: production docking, narrow sheets, scale, status overlays and explicit product adaptations.
- `tokens/`: the supplied Wilderness, Fantasy and Sci-fi tokens, including the data catalogue.
- `fonts/`: self-hosted WOFF2 assets and their original OFL notices.
- `icons/`: curated semantic SVG data, author credits and Lucide license. Only these repository-owned SVG bodies are rendered as markup. User/model content always renders as React text.
- `../ui/`: connected game panels and workflows; keep transport and orchestration outside reusable visual controls.

Use semantic token names, a 4px spacing scale, the supplied display/UI/utility type roles, and accessible names on every icon button. Unknown content receives an initial-letter sigil. Do not use emoji or imply that generated artwork is being requested when no art job exists. Keep one primary gold action per panel; focus uses sage in action search. Game-icons.net art is CC BY 3.0, Lucide ISC, and fonts OFL; first-party code remains AGPL-3.0-only.

World/location/time labels, item/entity/action icons and grouped-shortcut labels come from installed world or native capability presentation. Resolve a supplied semantic icon through the trusted local catalogue; unknown keys keep a readable fallback. A client subtype name cannot invent a gather capability, preparation verb or wilderness label. Theme selection changes presentation, not the installed world or its laws.

Use `TextTooltip` for plain hover/focus labels such as Retry and short disabled explanations. It sizes to its text without a background, border, padding or shadow. Use `Explanation` only for richer action details and facts.

## Accepted adaptations

The attached reference instructions are design input. The user's explicit overrides govern this port:

- Work above characters is a continuous white bar, interpolated against the server's simulation rate and frozen while paused. It disappears on completion/cancellation. Keep at most three transient notices, separate from active work.
- Quick circles separate contextual **Suggestions** from three persisted **Shortcuts · 1–3**. Assignments remain stable when unavailable and expose a reachable reason instead of opening reassignment. Suggestions freeze their identities while hovered or focused. A grouped shortcut reviews the exact resolved source before committing and refuses a changed source instead of substituting another. In the quick-action bar, rings are reserved for actual action cooldowns. Current actions have no cooldown contract, so no rings are shown there; never feed work progress into them.
- Pausing does not change clock width. Create and Conversation use the wider 504px panel class (1.5 × the normal 336px), with larger tabs and 32px close/new controls. Journal, World Events and Settings & help also use wider reading space; Journal and World Events can expand without changing their record scope.
- Search retains “Search actions or invent something…” and an inline invention affordance for unmatched text. Enter opens an editable invention draft; only explicit Send dispatches. There is no separate invention row below the search.
- Look closer uses current server-projected descriptions. Hover facts use actual native work durations and material yields/costs. Installed world/native previews own those values; shared UI code must not restate bundled-world timing or cost rules.
- God actions always carry a visible **God mode** label. Ground **Add something** opens the reusable right-hand `Pullout` by hover, click or keyboard, then the Items, Actors and Environment categories. Leaf catalogues are searchable and show eight rows before scrolling. [Base-world items](../../../../docs/worlds/base/items.md) owns item creation, pile menus and drop quantities; player inventory uses the same installed-item catalogue as other-character editing. Person creation is a React Aria modal; trait search exposes descriptions and selected traits become removable tags. Character panels group applicable owner controls under **God mode**: **Revive**, **Grant cognition and speech**, **Edit Person** and **Inspect private mind**. Panel and context-menu revival/cognition visibility share one predicate; mutations use the same server handlers. Edit Person opens on Character with current statuses and applicable meters using authored labels, units, ranges and edit permissions. Fill meters stages only their applicable maxima. Inventory has searchable item-type selectors, whole-number quantities and add/remove controls. Identity contains name, description, personality, backstory, traits and goals. Edits across tabs remain drafts until Save. Narrow editor section controls retain visible icons and accessible names when their text labels collapse.
- Traits are saved domain data, sampled three without replacement from `packages/domain/src/worlds/base/config/traits.json`. They are descriptive starting dispositions, not earned bonuses. The character sheet shows the player's scoped memories and observed history; private NPC cognition stays behind god authorization.
- Conversation inputs begin at one line and grow with wrapping or explicit newlines. Active replies use an animated three-dot wave on the originating message; successful replies have no status badge, failures retain only a small red label inside the message with the reason on hover/focus, and interrupted replies use narration from the stored cause. Provider-stage and queue text stays out of the conversation UI.

Right-dragging rotates and tilts; Shift + right-dragging or middle-dragging pans. Primary dragging does not move the camera or act on release. A stationary secondary click opens actions, and a map click that dismisses a picker never walks. Keyboard shortcuts do not intercept text fields. Panels use opening order and collapse older panels when space is insufficient; narrow layouts share one sheet. The shared width-expansion button is hidden on narrow screens, where both widths have the same geometry; Close remains available and the desktop workspace preference is retained. Conversation uses the expanded workspace sheet for readable speech and reachable input, with controls scrolling around a bounded conversation. Other sheets at enlarged UI scales leave room for the launcher row and open-panel tabs; expanded sheets retain their close control and panel tabs. Theme/scale/reduced-motion preferences stay local and never alter game semantics.

## Present capability boundaries

Known recipes and invention jobs have structured cards. The qualified World Agent renders typed single/multiple/custom questions inline with explicit save/continue, immutable answer history and separate review. Other prose questions retain their ordinary answer affordance. The owner World Agent is a unified funded native-harness conversation with durable history, exact review and Apply controls. Missing connector configuration does not silently fall back to tool-less chat. Conversation drafts and selected sessions follow the private task scope described above; reads and writes retain fresh access checks. Question drafts additionally use the server-authorized conversation and exact question identity so reload does not lose them. Logout and private-owner replacement clear private drafts; unsupported earlier unscoped transcripts are not read. The recipe workshop optionally offers Review in workshop before installing, a free capability catalogue, saved native validation details, and an explicit Apply saved proposal action. This finite recipe path does not edit shared laws or existing objects. General creator-module authoring, conjure confirmation and generated artwork remain in INV; UI styling does not grant those capabilities. Ending a conversation closes its backend lane; restoring its transcript creates a new identity, never pretends to reopen a tombstoned session. Hiding a panel retains drafts and pending work.

The whole-interface runtime and focused browser/native evidence are separate from complete gameplay qualification. The [redesign verification report](../../../../docs/verification/game-interaction-redesign.md) and [UIUX tracker](../../../../docs/maintainers/ui-ux.md) retain real native integration, operating-system text composition, assistive devices, broader text/viewport combinations and uncoached player observation where unverified. A successful fixture or screenshot is not evidence for those remaining conditions.

CSS handles the modest panel transitions; reduced motion disables transitions and animations. No separate motion dependency is needed for this layout. UI layout extensions are defined alongside the imported tokens rather than requiring approval for routine spacing decisions.

## Owner editor components

`../ui/editor.tsx` owns the reusable `EditorPanel`: configurable icon tabs, dragging/stacking, viewport-bounded dimensions that account for local UI scale, fresh centering, Save without closing, validation errors, Discard changes and the unsaved-close prompt. At narrow editor widths the same named icon tabs form a horizontal scrolling row, numeric meter labels/values precede their bars, and footer controls wrap. These adaptations retain the mounted editor and its drafts. Person fields are shared with creation; applicable meters use native labels, ranges, units and presentation metadata, while categories remain read-only. Independent World Events windows open above Person editors without closing them or pausing play. Memory/awareness refresh controls show real-time query timestamps and require saving or discarding drafts first. The JSON detail pane stays outside the scrolling list; drafts update locally per keystroke and only changed records are submitted. Authoritative privacy and validation remain server responsibilities.

The action picker loads on opening, explicit refresh and possession/pause/authority/timeline changes; it does not poll. Its header/search/availability controls and full-catalogue/freeform routes surround a scrolling result region. Command buttons and native detail disclosures are siblings; unavailable reasons stay readable without hover. Exact keyed choices retain opening order, the display window and expansion across valid refreshes. The initial 40 rows expand in increments of 40 through Show more choices, which focuses the first added command. Search includes visible target/tool identity across every returned choice; deliberate search/scope changes reset the window. Collapsed detail bodies are mounted only when opened. Failed or obsolete reads cannot publish old commands/details. Inventory opens this same picker for the exact selected item and closing restores its opener. The menu sits above the clock and ordinary sheets so long details cannot cover its controls. The separate diagnostics trace page retains its three-second polling cadence.

The shared `Panel` accepts opt-in `resizable` for native vertical resizing with bounded height. Intelligence combines it with existing header dragging on desktop; narrow layouts retain the shared sheet. Its compact rows use actor and trigger icons, bottom-right time, visible trigger subtype/level, ellipsis with structured hover/focus previews, and accessible status symbols. Preview portals use the existing React Aria tooltip primitives and stay outside panel clipping. Inspection contracts live in [the cognition debugger specification](../../../../docs/memory-architecture.md#god-mode-cognition-debugger).

Player creation presents an abstract remaining-usage meter with accessible percentage text, a distinct unavailable state and an exhausted state. It includes held reservations and never shows dollars, compute/AI costs or estimated invention counts. The owner conversation exposes underlying spending only in its separate owner-details disclosure.

## Conversation reading layout

Talk/Invent and the recipient stay above the transcript. Only message history scrolls; the vertical React Aria volume slider sits beside it and the multiline input stays below. The slider retains the existing saved Whisper/Normal/Shout choices and supports pointer dragging and arrow keys; it does not change hearing rules. Detailed missing-AI reasons remain in **Intelligence & allowance** instead of filling the composer footer.

Shared body text is 14px/20px, panel titles 22px/26px and item titles 16px. Existing control targets and user scaling remain. Narrow conversation inputs retain 16px text. Conversation expands into a reading sheet on narrow/short windows rather than squeezing all controls under the condition card. Desktop drag offsets apply only while panels are draggable; they never displace narrow sheets. [CV01](../../../../docs/limits/interface.md#cv01--conversation-reading-space) owns the presentation bounds and tradeoffs.

## Creator conversation reading layout

The active creator session separates its reading region from its bounded composer/status footer. Workspaces and session/spending are optional disclosures; Conversation and Work remain direct choices. In the shortest narrow layout, the loaded session reuses the panel header for God mode, remaining allowance and Close, while retaining the accessible Create title. Hidden or covered reading stays mounted without presenting its controls as active.

The conversation component owns unread state and scrolling. Its optional control renderer places **New reply text** beside the creator's Conversation/Work choices, so it cannot cover a question or **Save answer**. The compact cue has a full accessible name and plain tooltip. Its activation returns focus to Conversation; the choice strip reveals the focused button locally, preserving outer and transcript scroll positions except for the requested jump. Inset focus outlines remain visible at the strip's edges. Other conversation readers keep their existing cue placement. [CV02](../../../../docs/limits/interface.md#cv02--creator-conversation-reading-space) records the reading/footer tradeoff and qualification limits.

## Tab Resume dialog

`ui/tab-resume.tsx` uses the existing React Aria modal boundary, shared buttons and spacing tokens. It is blocking, has no close action, and stays open on Escape/outside clicks. Exact copy: **Game Paused**, **OpenLegend is open in another tab.**, primary **Resume Here**, secondary **Log Out**. Keep pending and error text with the relevant action. The compact dialog wraps and scrolls within narrow/short windows; underlying game shortcuts and intentions are unavailable. Sole-tab entry/reload/refocus does not show this dialog. [Behavior](../../../../docs/projects/completed/tab-resume-feature-spec.md) owns when it appears.

## Entity-name grammar

Use the server's canonical permitted name in lists, inventory, scene labels and compact actions. Object profile headings use `namePhrase(entity, 'indefinite')`; prose about a selected target uses `definite`. Proper names receive no added article. Keep grammar metadata with the label and use the shared language package instead of locally concatenating articles. [Entity names](../../../../docs/entity-names.md) owns examples, mass/plural forms and authored pronunciation overrides.
