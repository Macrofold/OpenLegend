# Gildmoss design system

**Status:** accepted on 2026-10-26 as the final design of the [interface overhaul](../../../../../docs/projects/interface-art-direction-overhaul-plan.md). Gildmoss is an available runtime theme with published artwork and shared components across the application surfaces. The confirmed row-Q construction and composition differences have source corrections, and the named corrected-build visual/input checks pass. The [verification report](../../../../../docs/verification/gildmoss-interface.md#confirmed-design-fidelity-work) separates those corrections, measured desktop geometry and remaining checks. Live World Agent reply/proposal presentation retains its accepted connection blocker.

Gildmoss is row Q of the overhaul's Claude Design canvas at version 41 (`1792999021-8ee1`): eleven screens and a component sheet, chosen and refined by Mike over rounds 8–8n, with the icon family completed in round 8o. The [study record](../../../../../art-direction/studies/2026-10-18-interface-direct-method/README.md) holds the history, alternatives and decisions; the production package holds the artwork, fonts and sources.

The material study supplies Crest and Filigree artwork and 19 selectable character-panel styles, with Jade Monolith as the catalog default. Ordinary surfaces and character-panel glass regions use the approved round-16 Clear Slab recipe with Jade Cloud, natural grain, Small panels too parallax, the glass bevel and soft top glow. The shared implementation and named Chromium, Firefox and Safari checks are delivered; the [Jade Cloud evidence](../../../../../docs/verification/gildmoss-interface.md#jade-cloud-glass-and-nineteen-styles--october-30-2026) records their scope, and [UIUX22](../../../../../docs/maintainers/ui-ux.md#uiux22) retains controlled hardware/software performance qualification. The separate [UIUX21](../../../../../docs/maintainers/ui-ux.md#uiux21--gildmoss-interface-integration) World Agent connection gate remains open.

## The look

The world stays the main view. Panels are deep olive jade glass, with restrained clouds and natural grain inside it, over a soft 12 px blur of the world. A still lantern lights the top, soft pools shift within the glass with the pointer, and a shadow sits beneath. The continuous 5 px glass bevel has silver/olive upper highlights and lower shade, with a soft glow along its top and no repeating image sections or gold corner diamonds. Pale champagne gold is metal that reflects rather than glows. It appears only on what is chosen — the selected tab, row, slot and toggle — and on rims, gold labels and the ornate frames' ornaments. Everything unselected is silver. The single primary action in a panel, and the quantity slider, are a solid, slightly warmer gold so they stand out. Gelasio sets names and titles, and in italic the narrator's voice; Figtree sets everything you read and act on. Daytime controls remain unlit; at dusk the panel light turns amber and chosen elements gain a soft, wide firelight glow. Nothing is cartoony, neon or extreme.

## Colour and metal

The canonical values and their uses are in `tokens.json`. The ones that define the feel:

- **Ground and glass:** canvas `#0C110B`; the reference/fallback panel glass falls from `rgba(20, 28, 17, 0.86)` to `rgba(12, 17, 11, 0.86)`. The procedural Clear Slab base uses alpha 0.833, calibrated with Jade Cloud and its light to match that reference composite; it is not normalized to 0.86. The archived boards' HUD plates and Say/Act bars use 84 %.
- **Chosen and hover:** chosen rows, segmented options, toggles and tiles `#2C2E1D` under a 1 px gold metal edge (2 px on a selected slot); button hover `#1F2A1A`. Ordinary navigation tabs stay transparent, with a gold label and metal underline.
- **Text:** ivory `#EFE8D2`, secondary `#C3BDA5`, muted `#9A967E`, unselected silver `#B9BDB3`. Text is at least 4.5 : 1.
- **Gold:** the highlight `#DBBE7C` (rims, the narrator's timer line); gold label text from `#F9EFD8` to `#D0B47B`; the primary button `#E9C264` with dark ink `#211A0B`, hover `#F0CD77`, pressed `#D8AA46`.
- **Need bars, Cobalt pigments:** Health `#B43A31`, Food `#C1872B`, Energy `#268843`, Spirit `#2E5FB6`, on a near-black track; each is at least 3 : 1 against it. Food stays an amber, clear of the selection gold.
- **Status tones:** good `#50915E`, bad `#B0564C`, neutral `#4E74B5`, special `#A68E5B`.
- **Coral and leaf:** `#F08A76` marks something missing, an unavailable reason or damage over the world; `#A9CB8B` marks a net gain or having everything.

Metal is never a button fill, and ordinary panels have no gold border or corner caps: their continuous glass bezel uses the silver/olive palette. The narrator's caption, the world's opening introduction and special notifications take Filigree's complete gold frame, and the secondary button Crest's hairline rim ([plan decisions 21, 24 and 25](../../../../../docs/projects/interface-art-direction-overhaul-plan.md#decisions)). Gradients are copied exactly from the boards: the chosen edge (135°), the tab underline and hotbar dividers, the round rims (conic, from 210°), gold label text, and the icon metals.

## Spacing, sizes and corners

Spacing uses only 4, 8, 12, 16, 24 and 32. Each surface has one left edge, equal paddings and aligned trailing columns; nothing is clipped or wraps by accident. Board-reference sizes are: controls 32 high (28 compact); rows 32, list rows 56; slots 48; hotbar quick slots and Speak/Act/Combat tiles 52, its panel buttons 40; status icons 32; icons 16–20 (24–26 on the hotbar); every target at least 28 × 28. Board-reference corners are panel 10, window 8, control 6, slot 4. Ordinary runtime bezels follow the host radius and draw a continuous 5 px bevel over its reserved border geometry. Filigree and character artwork retain their authored frame geometry; character-panel status cells are 30 px and its other dimensions come from each style's layout.

## Icons

One family of 108 soft filled glyphs on a 24 grid: a metal-filled shape, metal lines and a dark engraved mark (`--icon-mark`). The package's `icons.css` gives each finish: gold when chosen, silver otherwise, ivory inside status frames, dark ink (with a lighter mark) on the gold primary button, and green for Crafting's Have everything checks. The catalog uses this client's existing format, so a key keeps one meaning. Gildmoss supplies new artwork for 38 of the current catalog's 72 keys and gives six more an existing glyph (fire uses the campfire, cognition the mind, In view the eye, back and next the submenu chevron, the star the sparkles). The package's `catalog-merge.json` lists every current key's outcome. Eight camera-control icons retire with the camera buttons the design removes; eight are unused; twelve name world things (resources, animals, a person) and keep their current artwork until the world's pixel-art sprites replace them. Events marks speech, actions and observations with the Speak, Act and Overview (eye) icons. Main hand and Melee weapons share one sword glyph.

Icons for things a world defines — needs (`meter.*`), statuses (`status.*`), body regions (`body.*`), recipe categories (`category.*`), the campfire and cooking — are artwork only. The installed world chooses which key its presentation data uses; interface code never names world needs, statuses, slots or categories itself. The status icons in the designs (Well fed, Winded, Stiff leg, Trail legs, Smells of smoke, Damp, Inspired, Promise kept) are examples; the bundled world defines its own statuses. Spirit appears only where a world defines it; the bundled world's Spirit is specified in [Character and map additions](../../../../../docs/projects/character-and-map-additions-feature-spec.md).

Item illustrations are separate from semantic glyphs. A world may author `presentation.itemArt[definitionId] = { path, detailPath? }`; an initializer binds constructed outputs using their returned native definition IDs. The server supplies artwork only alongside an already-permitted item, equipment or learned-recipe row. [`ItemIllustration`](../item-illustration.tsx) uses the exact selected asset release, the detail image where supplied, and the existing icon or name sigil if artwork is absent or fails. Inventory, bags, trade, recipes, equipment and quick slots share that renderer; images do not take over item dragging or duplicate accessible names. No world identity is inferred from a displayed name.

## Components

`components.reference.css` records the board construction. The runtime [`JadeGlass`](../jade-glass.tsx), [native recipes](../jade-glass-material.ts), [`gildmoss-kit.tsx`](../gildmoss-kit.tsx) and [kit stylesheet](../gildmoss-kit.css) implement the selected materials through existing surface and button owners; the reference stylesheet remains the original board reference. In words:

- **Panels, windows, menus and tooltips:** seeded Jade Cloud/Clear Slab, the continuous glass bevel, masked lantern light, soft top glow and the host's shadow/blur. Main and raised surfaces use the same slab; there is no separate raised recipe. Narrator and opening guidance retain the complete Filigree frame above the glass. No other notification surface is designated special, so that reserved treatment has no runtime consumer. Panel dividers are silver at 22 %. Names and status lines over the world never show through a panel.
- **Buttons:** primary solid gold; secondary the material study's Crest button, dark glass with a champagne hairline rim, using its normal, hover, pressed and unavailable exports. The corners scale with actual button height, and the label gains the paired ornaments only where there is room. Quiet buttons keep their existing treatment. Focus is a 2 px ivory outline 2 px outside; unavailable controls keep a reachable explanation.
- **Fields:** a dark well with a faint gold inset edge; focus adds an ivory edge and a soft halo.
- **Tabs:** the chosen tab's label is gold with a 2 px metal underline; the rest are silver. Segmented groups use the chosen fill.
- **Right-click menu:** 8 px top and bottom; rows 32 high, 4 from the panel's side, content 12 from its edge; an 18 icon in a 24 column or the item's sprite at 1× in a 40 column; the header shows the object's art in a 64 × 52 well, its name in Gelasio 17 and its state in Figtree 13. The hovered row looks like the open row: the chosen fill with a gold edge. Unavailable rows are muted, art at 45 %, with the reason in coral on one line beneath. Submenus open 4 px to the right, their first row level with the parent (left only at the screen edge).
- **Slots:** silver edges at rest, a 2 px gold ring when chosen, the count at the bottom right, a hand for what you hold, a red tint and red number when something is missing; 4 px apart in a grid.
- **Need bars:** ordinary shared meters retain their 6 px track. Character-panel rows use their layout's track, icon and value boxes and the kit's `needTrack` recessed rim, background and shaded fill, with the world's supplied colours and values.
- **Statuses:** timed statuses are round and the ring is the timer — a 2 px arc of the time left over a faint 1 px track, running down clockwise from 12 o'clock; lasting effects are square with a steady 2 px border.
- **Progress:** a smooth champagne fill on a dark track, 6 high, no tick marks; 112 wide over a working character.
- **Over the world:** text with a drop shadow only — names in Gelasio light gold, status in Figtree, changes in coral rising and fading over a second.
- **Special shine (reserved):** a 2 px champagne border that gleams in two bright spots on opposite sides. Only for rewards, awards, achievements and special items; no screen uses it yet.
- **Pieces drawn once:** the Have everything switch; the round quantity slider in primary gold; stat tiles 56 high, tinted green with a green edge when gear or a condition raises the stat (and, per Mike, red when it lowers it); the Weight bar in silver-green and a bag's Packing load bar in gold; map markers (24 round dark chips with a gold edge and dark-ink labels with a light halo), the selected-place pin and your portrait in a 48 gold ring; World Agent messages (yours on the chosen fill, its replies beside a round World avatar, proposed changes on a darker card waiting for review); the game menu's header with the seal in a 40 gold ring. `tokens.json` (`component`) has their exact values.

## The HUD

- **Top left:** the selected character-panel style is anchored at screen pixel (0, 0), scaling with the interface. Its layout places the permitted portrait, which opens Character, the installed world's condition rows, timed rings and lasting squares, and the native perspective-lock control. The current bundled world supplies Health, Food and Energy: Mike approved the first three artwork positions, leaving the fourth unused. The art supplies no extra meter. Each style supplies 30 px status cells and a 16–18 effect display capacity; wrapping and a counted overflow route retain Condition access. A world supplying more rows than the artwork supports retains the complete plain HUD. The boards' plain 284-wide plate, 24 px inset, is the earlier reference.
- **Top right:** the minimap group fully in the corner, 24 from the right and top edges — a 200 map in a 206 champagne rim, with the 20 round North badge on the top rim. The badge starts at y24 and the rim at y31 in the 1920×1080 board. The zoom in/out buttons are round magnifier controls, 28, staggered at the lower left. The place name sits under the map in Gelasio.
- **Day-and-night dial:** a 72 round dial in the gap at the minimap's upper left — not a clock. Sunrise is on the left and sunset on the right; by day a small sun crosses the top half and by night a crescent moon crosses the bottom half, both clockwise. The face is shaded by daylight with soft fades. The left point is the world's dawn and the right its dusk, each half spread evenly over its own length, so the dial fits any world's hours. "Day 1 · Year 1" and the time sit centred beneath it; the day number changes at midnight. Year is an example value until a world defines years.
- **Hotbar:** no panel behind it — ten slots, then Speak, Act and Combat mode (main's attack mode), then Character, Journal, Crafting, Build and the game menu, with thin gold metal dividers between the groups. The game-menu button is the Open Legend campfire seal in a champagne rim and opens the game menu, as Esc does. 986 wide, centred, 24 above the bottom edge; the Say and Act bars share its edges.
- **Events quick view:** a button at the bottom left, level with the hotbar, opens a quick view of what you perceived — speech, actions and notable observations, newest at the bottom. While open it replaces the button and has its own minimize button.
- **Narrator:** a caption at the top centre, like a film subtitle: the line in Gelasio italic on dark glass in the Filigree frame, up to three lines, with a thin gold timer line beneath that shortens toward the centre. The X appears only on hover. The line appears word by word, holds, then fades; with reduced motion it simply appears and disappears.
- **Opening introduction:** **Arriving here** shows the selected world's authored opening guidance in the same Filigree frame, with 16 px block/32 px inline padding to clear its corner leaves. It remains an untimed, collapsible reading disclosure with its existing height limit and scrolling; it does not enter the narrator's timed feed or gain a reveal/fade animation. Its ordinary body type and native Gildmoss glass/light remain readable.

## Night

At dusk the panel's lamp and pools turn amber, its lamp widens, and the soft top light keeps the same warm treatment. The primary button, chosen tab, open and hovered rows, chosen hotbar tiles, selected slot and focused fields gain their soft, wide firelight glow only at night. Daytime panels retain the selected soft top glow, while controls remain unlit. The minimap picture dims with the night light; its rim, North mark, the dial and the text keep their colours. Shared theme tokens own control effects; the native glass recipes and stylesheet own the slab's light.
