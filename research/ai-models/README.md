# Models for independent characters, decisions and voice

| Research status         | Progress                                                                                                   | Last updated |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| Completed desk research | A broad, source-linked candidate inventory is ready for later selection; no models were tested or adopted. | 2026-10-24   |

This collection answers which current language, decision and voice models may be useful for Open Legend's believable, imperfect characters, including permitted dark fiction. It records what the models offer, what their publishers and users actually say, how they can be deployed, and what their costs and terms mean. The initial evidence snapshot is **October 23, 2026**, with targeted primary-source review and additions on **October 24**. Individual records preserve those dates.

**No inference, model installation, weight download, benchmark, audio audition or paid model call was performed.** The shortlists express research judgment, not measured winners. Published examples are linked and their inspection limits are explicit. Missing evidence remains missing.

## Read the collection

The inventory contains **145 model or tightly scoped family entries**: **34 character/roleplay**, **47 decision/general**, and **64 voice/speech**. Related sizes or service tiers sometimes share a row; distinct derivatives and materially different services sometimes have separate rows. These are candidate records, not 145 independent research studies or 145 unrelated model families. Six older roleplay references are listed separately and are not added to that total.

## Which candidates deserve attention first?

There is no evidence-supported universal winner. A model that writes compelling dialogue may be poor at selecting exact actions, and a reliable probability-returning classifier cannot write the conversation. The following is a useful starting panel, not a requirement to test every entry or a selection of production providers.

## What the evidence actually establishes

**“Uncensored” is an inconsistent publisher/community label.** The tables distinguish a disclosed refusal measurement, a training-intent claim, a firsthand user report and an unknown. They do not transform those into a fabricated universal score. For example, Hermes 4.3's own card reports 74.60% answered without reasoning and 72.29% with reasoning on its cited benchmark; those figures do not measure how often it would portray an Open Legend antagonist correctly.

**Character independence matters as much as willingness.** The goal is an authored person who can be selfish, frightened, loyal, cruel, generous, deceptive or principled for intelligible reasons. A character declining the player's request may be exactly right. The [existing characterization research](../../archive/02-research/npc-cognition/09-low-refusal-character-simulation.md) owns the distinction between assistant refusal, quiet sanitization, character disagreement, missing evidence, service rejection and engine rejection. This inventory supplies candidates for that distinction rather than inventing a replacement evaluation design.

**Sentiment is uneven and version-specific.** Concrete reports cover SillyTavern character chat, creative writing, game-mod dialogue, local voice cloning, speech streaming and classification. Some are a single user's setup or a publisher-selected testimonial. Several very recent releases have no meaningful independent history yet. Family-level praise is labeled as such; a 2025 report is not evidence about an October 2026 revision.

**Freshness is about the underlying model and route.** A recent quantization upload does not refresh old training. A moving API alias can silently change the served checkpoint. A preview may be callable without downloadable weights. A future-effective policy is not yet the governing policy. The catalogue preserves these differences, as well as concrete lifecycle issues such as [Hume's announced November 13 TTS/EVI shutdown](https://dev.hume.ai/voice/docs/text-to-speech-tts/overview).
