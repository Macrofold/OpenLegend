## How to narrow the inventory

Start with the required role, then choose an available deployment route. Inspect the exact variant’s terms before narrowing by content evidence and samples. The cost column names the meter; current numerical rates and their conditions remain in the linked detail record. A family can contain several routes or variants, so its listed capabilities, permissions and meters must not be combined into an imaginary single model.

- **Interest** preserves the original priority in parentheses. High means a useful research lead; it is not a required first test, a measured winner or commercial clearance. Reference/control and retirement status are shown separately. License-only original labels remain unranked.
- **Access** describes published availability, not a successful download, account entitlement or API call. Weights promised, private/gated access, previews and expiring services are explicit.
- **Terms** describe weight/service categories. Standard licenses still carry obligations; custom, mixed and unresolved terms require the exact variant/route detail. These categories do not score content freedom.
- **Content evidence** identifies the kind of support, including negative reports or restrictions. Publisher measurements use different tasks; no shared refusal score or game qualification is implied. Creative intent only describes RP/style tuning without a claim of permissive behavior. A scoped fiction exception concerns a policy provision, not measured behavior.
- **Samples** distinguish attributable output artifacts, linked demos, illustrative responses, request code, documentation and absence. Linked media was not auditioned; a usage report is not an output transcript.

Each candidate name opens the owning detail row; its record ID also links to the structured record file. Model/source URLs and source IDs remain available there and in the JSON index.

**Runpod** is a documentary hosting disposition, independent of research interest. Candidate and conditional rows still require an exact profile, current offer and operating qualification. All actual deployed limits and measurements remain unknown in this research.
