## 1. Method and confidence

This panel investigated public web sources, emphasizing original research papers, official developer posts and documentation, firsthand developer talks/interviews, and an explicitly separate public code example. Sources were reviewed for what they actually establish about individuality, shared computation, simulation frequency and measured work. Marketing capacity claims and historical implementation statements were retained with their qualifications rather than converted into current guarantees.

| Evidence category                       | How it is used                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Inspectable source code                 | Describes the particular inspected snapshot; does not establish the internals of a different proprietary product            |
| Original paper with methods and timings | Supports the stated experiment and its measured subsystem; does not transfer its capacity to Open Legend                    |
| Firsthand developer explanation         | Supports an attributed implementation statement at its publication date; not independent verification of the current binary |
| Official framework documentation        | Establishes available mechanisms; not proof that a particular shipped game uses them                                        |
| Promotional or recommended population   | Evidence of a developer's claim; insufficient for a reproducible performance contract                                       |
| Inference across sources                | Explicitly labeled analysis; useful for questions and hypotheses, not a recovered specification                             |

The research did not run games or simulations, inspect UEBS2's proprietary binary, benchmark Open Legend, or reproduce paper results. Historical hardware and prices remain historical. A live documentation page's current version label can coexist with older examples; the dossiers call out material cases.

## 2. The numbers are not a leaderboard

Each row measures something different. “Individual” here can mean an individual position and numerical state, a separate task destination, a retained game character, or an LLM-driven social agent. It does not mean that all rows offer the same cognition, physical world or responsiveness.

Two further numerical traps matter. FLAME GPU 2's 16-million-agent Sugarscape example counts environment-cell agents, not 16 million mobile creatures. Its much larger memory-capacity claims lack equivalent step-latency evidence, and its approximately 500-million count for an 80 GB A100 is an extrapolation. Likewise, the September 2026 40-million-unit announcement in Brilliant Game Studios' feed concerns an engine for its next title, not a measured UEBS2 capability. The [scientific simulation](cognition-and-agent-systems.md) and [UEBS2](uebs2.md) dossiers retain the details.

## 4. Access limits and unresolved internals

- UEBS2's exact targeting, navigation representation, update rates, behavioral LOD and synchronization remain undisclosed in the accessible evidence.
- Its official making-of video was located, but usable frames/transcript were not retrieved. No claim depends on having watched it.
- Some Steam event bodies were accessible only through an official announcement feed; stable event URLs and the retrieved primary reproductions are both retained.
- Kingdom Come's 2026 conference abstract concerning thousands of NPCs was found, but the full talk/slides were not reviewed. It is a follow-up lead, not a verified performance result.
- Planetary Annihilation, Total War and Age of Empires IV technical-talk leads did not yield sufficient reviewed material to reconstruct their final implementations. They do not support the conclusions here.
- MATSim's general individual-plan model was confirmed from its official site; inaccessible book material is not used to assert specific internal algorithms or benchmark figures.

These limits do not invalidate the positive evidence elsewhere. They define where additional investigation would need new access, a more specific question, or an actual measured experiment. The panel is complete within its desk-research scope; it does not pretend to answer proprietary questions for which public evidence was not found.
