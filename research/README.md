## Current Unreal and Unity runtime mechanisms

The [Unreal/Unity runtime research collection](unity-unreal-runtime/README.md) studies current engine systems across simulation, perception, AI, navigation, physics, data/concurrency, world scale, networking/persistence, rendering/animation/audio, content/tools/UI/platforms, profiling, gameplay frameworks, and recent primary research. Its evidence cutoff is **2026-10-23**.

Start with its [OpenLegend context and coverage map](unity-unreal-runtime/00-openlegend-context-and-coverage.md) and [version/provenance baseline](unity-unreal-runtime/01-evidence-and-version-baseline.md). The collection explains mechanisms and limitations with per-chapter source registers. It does not select OpenLegend designs, change the engine/platform decision, or authorize implementation or benchmarks.

## Game engines and platforms

The [game-engine research collection](game-engines/README.md) compares browser and native engines, performance/hardware limits, controllers, networking, effects, live generated content, console/mobile deployment, licensing and production cost.

Start with the [Open Legend recommendation](game-engines/open-legend-assessment.md), then use the [comparison matrices](game-engines/comparison-matrices.md) and individual dossiers. The [Godot community and AI-development review](game-engines/godot-community-and-ai-2026.md) adds adoption evidence, concrete integrations, community perspectives and research results. The [benchmark plan](game-engines/benchmark-plan.md) describes a conditional comparison, not an already selected migration.

The [first-player milestone](../docs/maintainers/first-player-milestone.md) owns the immediate delivery sequence: complete and observe a worthwhile browser session and measure its performance/cost. Research does not authorize runtime work, paid tools or a new platform. Current behavior and accepted policy remain in the [architecture](../docs/architecture.md), [performance](../docs/performance.md), [gameplay priorities](../docs/repertoires/gameplay-priorities.md) and [licensing](../LICENSING.md) owners.
